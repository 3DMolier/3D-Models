About This Model

Pink Wine Bottle is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
