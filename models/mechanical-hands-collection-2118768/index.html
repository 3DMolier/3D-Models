About This Model

Mechanical Hands Collection is a detailed Collections & Sets asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $89. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published January 2024·Updated 20 August 2026

Questions About This Model

Which industries use the Mechanical Hands Collection model?

Film & Video Production, Advertising and Graphics Multimedia and Web Design are the primary industries on this listing, though the Royalty Free licence puts no limit on where the model is actually used.

Can the Mechanical Hands Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free licence covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Can the Mechanical Hands Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the licence. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

What is the Mechanical Hands Collection model typically used for?

Stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.
