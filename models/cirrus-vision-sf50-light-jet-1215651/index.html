About This Model

Cirrus Vision sf50 Light Jet is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. You are getting 183,195 polygons and 197,573 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 12 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use.
