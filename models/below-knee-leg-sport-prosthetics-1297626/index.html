About This Model

A running blade prosthesis for below-knee amputees, with a carbon fiber J-shaped foot, a socket and a pylon. Only 23,788 polygons and 24,282 vertices are needed, as the curved blade and the socket are smooth, continuous forms. Fitted to an athlete on a track, the blade signals Paralympic sport at first glance.

8 maps at 4096 x 4096 show the woven carbon pattern, the matte socket shell and the rubber sole. Islands sit apart on the UV layout, so a national flag or custom artwork can be painted onto the socket. TurboSquid awarded CheckMate Pro to the prosthesis after checking every mesh and name.

Available since 2018, the device fits medical technology films, rehabilitation explainers, sports documentaries and orthopedic clinic visuals. Show a runner at the starting blocks with the blade flexed, and the scene speaks about resilience. Studio close-ups of the carbon curve also suit engineering presentations about prosthetic design.
