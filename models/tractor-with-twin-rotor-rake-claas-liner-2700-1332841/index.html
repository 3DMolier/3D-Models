About This Model

Priced at $119, the Tractor with Twin Rotor Rake Claas Liner 2700 is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 254,760 polygons and 269,942 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

28 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and game assets. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current.
