About This Model

Raw Steak is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $5. You are getting 5,016 polygons and 5,018 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX.

2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
