About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Biventricular Mechanical Heart Device with Flexible Cannula is a finished, render-ready model at $49. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Geometry weighs in at 16,740 polygons over 18,807 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
