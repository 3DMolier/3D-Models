About This Model

Teen Boy Swimwear Rigged is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $179. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2021 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 10,052 polygons over 10,647 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
