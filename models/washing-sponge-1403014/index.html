About This Model

Priced at $19, the Washing Sponge is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 2,124 polygons and 2,126 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 1 maps at 2048 x 2048. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
