About This Model

An elderly African American woman in a cook's apron, seated and relaxed, a warm figure for kitchen and family scenes. The figure uses 151,158 polygons, concentrated in the wrinkles of her hands, the apron folds and her gray hair. A seated pose makes her easy to place at a kitchen table, a porch chair or a diner counter.

There are 212,042 vertices, a dense mesh that holds close-up detail on the face and fabric. Non-overlapping UVs allow aging details, flour stains or gentle color shifts to be painted directly onto the outfit. CheckMate Lite certification covers the essentials of clean geometry and dependable real-world scale.

Introduced in 2022, the character suits soul food restaurant visuals, cookbook covers, family films and community center designs. Warm kitchen light from a window behind her gives the scene a homely, inviting mood. She brings a grandmother's presence to interiors, which often turns a neutral render into a story about home.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2022 · Updated 17 November 2026
