About This Model

The Human Eye is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 992 polygons over 996 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 11 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
