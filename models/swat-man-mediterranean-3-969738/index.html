About This Model

The SWAT Man Mediterranean 3 is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $99 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 42,362 polygons over 43,440 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 18 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
