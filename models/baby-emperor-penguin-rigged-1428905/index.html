About This Model

This Animals & Creatures model - Baby Emperor Penguin - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. You are getting 9,082 polygons and 8,571 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 9 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024.
