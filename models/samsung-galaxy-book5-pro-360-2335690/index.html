About This Model

Priced at $89, the Samsung Galaxy Book5 Pro 360 is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 50,401 polygons and 53,590 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
