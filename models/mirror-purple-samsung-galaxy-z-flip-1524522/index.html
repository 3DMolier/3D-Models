Priced at $39, the Mirror Purple Samsung Galaxy Z Flip is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
