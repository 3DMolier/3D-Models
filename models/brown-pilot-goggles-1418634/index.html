About This Model

The Brown Pilot Goggles is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 18,502 polygons and 18,566 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 13 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
