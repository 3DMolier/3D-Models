About This Model

This live scan station captures the fingerprints of both hands on a glass platen, the device police and border agencies use for enrollment. Here the scanner is switched on, its 15,270 polygons and 16,472 vertices shaping the platen, the bezel and the glowing guide. The flat desktop housing slopes toward the operator, so a person can press fingers down without bending awkwardly.

Graded at CheckMate Pro, the device ships with non-overlapping UVs that keep the glass and the casing apart. Dark plastic with a lit green scanning area makes the powered state easy to read on screen.

On TurboSquid since 2022, it fits police booking rooms, airport immigration desks and visa application centers. Crime dramas show it during an arrest when an officer rolls a suspect's fingers across the glass. Security training videos and biometrics explainers rely on it to show how digital prints replaced ink cards. Office scenes in a background check agency gain realism when the station sits beside a monitor and a camera.
