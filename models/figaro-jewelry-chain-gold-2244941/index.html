About This Model

Priced at $19, the Figaro Jewelry Chain is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 12,644 polygons and 12,644 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Treat the figure as a planning number: it behaves one way in a single render and another in a level with fifty instances.

The geometry is built to be worked with: predictable loops, no doubled faces, nothing hidden inside the shell. Objects sit at the origin with clean transforms, so duplicating the asset across an environment costs nothing extra. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
