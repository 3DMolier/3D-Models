About This Model

This Food & Beverages model - Quince - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $18 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 15,526 polygons and 15,577 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for food photography and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
