About This Model

The Weight Plate Tree 3D Models Set 2 is a production-ready Sports & Recreation 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. You are getting 247,704 polygons and 254,707 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 4000 x 4000 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and architectural visualization. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
