About This Model

Full Skier Equipment Generic is a detailed Sports & Recreation asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 132,114 polygons over 136,636 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 12 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
