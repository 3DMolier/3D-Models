About This Model

A Heinz squeeze bottle with a flip cap, a compact condiment prop for diner tables, picnic scenes and fridge doors. Only 4,659 polygons shape the soft body and the cap, so many bottles fit a scene at almost no cost. The bottle stands cap side down in many kitchens, and the model works upright or inverted.

Passing CheckMate checks, the mesh holds 4,582 vertices and partial UVs that suit label decals. Its terms are Editorial Uses Only, which suits reviews, journalism and educational food content. Its proportions match a real retail bottle, slim enough to grip and squeeze with ease.

Seven versions cost $29 apiece: buttermilk ranch, lemon pepper parm, steakhouse garlic, Mayoracha, simply tomato ketchup, sweet relish and yellow mustard. Released in 2026, they suit food reporting, kitchen interiors and cooking tutorials. Line several flavors along a counter to show a full condiment station.
