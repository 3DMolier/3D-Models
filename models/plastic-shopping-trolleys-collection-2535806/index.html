About This Model

A collection of plastic shopping trolleys, the lightweight carts used in supermarkets and convenience stores, ready to fill retail aisles. Molded plastic baskets on wheeled frames give these carts a softer, rounder silhouette than classic wire trolleys.

StemCell certification means the PBR materials are prepared once and read consistently across engines and renderers. The plastic surfaces take color well, so the carts can be matched to a store's interior palette. Grouped near an entrance or scattered in an aisle, they show the scale and use of a shop at a glance.

First listed in 2026, the set serves supermarket design, retail planning presentations and shopping scenes in games. Store designers use carts in renders to check that aisles are wide enough for two shoppers passing. A shopper character pushing one through a checkout lane makes any grocery scene feel occupied. Because the carts nest visually in a row, a line of them along a wall looks natural without manual tweaking.
