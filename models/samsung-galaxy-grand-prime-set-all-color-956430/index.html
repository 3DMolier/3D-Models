About This Model

The Samsung Galaxy Grand Prime phone in all its colors, a large budget Android smartphone with rounded corners and a physical home button. Having every color in one set saves time when a scene needs several handsets of the same model.

CheckMate Pro certification stands behind the geometry, the scale and the clean naming of each phone. Separate screens let you place any interface image on the display without touching the body. Editorial Uses Only governs this branded set, limiting it to reviews, news and tech history content.

The set has been in our catalog since 2015 and costs $34 for the full color range. Tech journalists illustrate comparisons with it, and educators use it to show how phone design has changed. A phone lying face down on a desk, with another showing its screen, gives a review shot both views at once. Older handsets like this also suit retro tech articles that look back at early large-screen phones.
