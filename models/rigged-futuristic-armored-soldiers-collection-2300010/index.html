About This Model

Heavily armored soldiers of a near-future army form this rigged collection, suited to science fiction warfare and tactical games. Angular chest plates, segmented limb guards and full-face helmets hide every human feature behind hard engineered surfaces. Combat-ready stances make the troopers look like seasoned veterans of long campaigns rather than figures built for a parade.

Each soldier was produced to CheckMate specification, which keeps armor segments and skeleton controls tidy in the scene. Rigs let animators stage breaching, crouching and squad advances without fighting against the bulk of the armor geometry. Cold blue moonlight on scratched plates suggests night operations on a hostile planet far from any friendly base.

Its royalty free license has applied since 2024, so the troopers can appear in shooters, films and concept decks. Studios pitching military science fiction use squads like these to sell the tone of a world in early meetings. A line of soldiers silhouetted on a ridge at dawn creates an instantly cinematic image for a trailer.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
