About This Model

A fluffy baby chick covered in soft yellow down, with a tiny orange beak, dark eyes and thin legs. Real fur strands give the body a soft, rounded outline that catches rim light beautifully. Small enough to sit in a palm or a nest of straw, it fits farm, Easter and children's picture book scenes.

Under CheckMate Pro rules, scale and scene organization are verified even with the fur system included. The fur can be groomed shorter or recolored for a darker breed without rebuilding the bird. Backlit on straw, the down glows at the edges and makes the chick look warm and alive.

One version is sold, at $99, with the fur system already set up and groomed. Since 2015 it has picked up 12 sales from Easter campaigns, farm animations and children's storybook art. Placed next to a cracked eggshell on soft hay, it tells a hatching story in one frame.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
