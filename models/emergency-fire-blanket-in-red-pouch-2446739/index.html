This Tools model - Fire Blanket - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 9,712 polygons and 9,714 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
