About This Model

A ruined Roman-style wall with a row of round arches, columns between them and broken masonry along the top. The 145,769 polygons carve chipped stones, eroded column capitals and gaps where blocks have fallen away. Placed in a grassy field or a city square, it turns a plain set into an ancient site.

There are 140,763 vertices in the mesh, slightly fewer than faces, and the unwrap keeps UV islands apart. 2 maps at 4096 x 4096 render sun-bleached stone with darker stains in the recesses. Its StemCell rating keeps the stone material looking the same in each supplied format.

A $79 asset since 2018, the wall goes into historical films, Roman city games and archaeology visuals. Repeating the section end to end builds a long aqueduct or the outer ring of a colosseum. Ivy creeping over the arches adds the abandoned feel of ruins that have stood for centuries.
