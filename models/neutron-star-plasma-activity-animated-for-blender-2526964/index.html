About This Model

A neutron star is not a surface you can model. What can be shown is the environment around it: plasma streaming along field lines, hot spots at the magnetic poles and the twisting sheet where the field is dragged by rotation. That is a volumetric and animated problem rather than a geometric one.

This asset is prepared and animated for Blender, so the plasma behavior and the timing are already solved in the renderer they were built for. Setting up that kind of volumetric motion from scratch is expensive in both machine time and expertise.

At $49 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits astronomy and science communication, planetarium and museum content, documentary and educational video, title sequences and motion graphics, and games. On sale since 2026.
