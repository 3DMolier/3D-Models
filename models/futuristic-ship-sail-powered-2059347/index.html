Futuristic Ship Sail Powered belongs to our Ships & Boats range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 35,879 polygons over 18,389 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
