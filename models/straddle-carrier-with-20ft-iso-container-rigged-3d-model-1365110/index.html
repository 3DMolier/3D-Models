About This Model

Straddle Carrier with 20ft ISO Container Rigged is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
