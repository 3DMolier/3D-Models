About This Model

A tenderiser mallet has two different faces for a reason: one flat for flattening, one pyramided for breaking fibres. Those spikes are the identifying feature and they have to be real geometry, because a textured flat face produces none of the shadow that makes the tool recognisable.

The mesh is light at 18,592 polygons and 9,646 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with almost all of that count in the pyramid face.

At $15 it suits kitchen and culinary visualisation, cookware retail and catalogue renders, recipe and food editorial imagery, restaurant kitchens and games. On sale since 2022.
