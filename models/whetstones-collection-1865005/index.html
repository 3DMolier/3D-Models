About This Model

A collection of rectangular whetstones for sharpening knives and tools, suited to kitchen, workshop and outdoor camping and craft scenes. Whetstones Collection keeps every block simple at 1,072 polygons, so the stones cost almost nothing in a scene. The 1,074 vertices define crisp edges and flat faces that read clearly next to blades.

Overlap-free UVs make room for different grits, from coarse gray stone to fine white or orange ceramic on each block. TurboSquid gave the set its CheckMate Pro mark after verifying geometry, scale and file setup. A damp shader with a darker surface makes the stones look freshly soaked before use.

Offered since 2022, the stones suit knife making tutorials, chef kitchen interiors and cutlery store catalogs. Under Royalty Free licensing, they can feature in commercial videos, games and printed culinary guides. Wet one beside a chef knife on a wooden board, and the sharpening ritual is ready to film.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2022 · Updated 13 November 2026
