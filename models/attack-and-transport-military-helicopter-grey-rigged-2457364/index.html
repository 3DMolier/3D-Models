About This Model

Attack and Transport Military Helicopter is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $179. You are getting 371,174 polygons and 383,942 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 20.37 x 25.25 x H10.8 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
