About This Model

The Modular Storage Shelf White is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2026, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
