About This Model

An F-16 Fighting Falcon in Israeli Air Force markings, the single-engine multirole fighter seen in many modern air operations. The bubble canopy, the chin intake under the nose and the blended wing root give it the profile every aviation fan recognizes. Markings are what separate this version, placing the jet clearly in a specific air arm for documentaries and simulations.

CheckMate Pro certification guarantees real-world scale, clean geometry and consistent naming across fuselage, wings and control surfaces. That matters when the fighter shares an apron with tankers, hangars and ground crew built by other artists. A clean hierarchy makes it practical to stage a parked jet on the flight line or an aircraft banking at altitude.

Offered since 2016 at $169, the model has a long history in flight simulation and aerospace visualization projects. It also fits war-game environments, air show sequences and aviation segments in film and television. Royalty-free terms cover commercial projects too, from game trailers to training films.
