About This Model

A glazed skylight structure, this model crowns atriums, shopping galleries and office lobbies with a framed glass roof. It uses 17,702 polygons and 18,240 vertices for the steel ribs, the glass panels and the joints where the frame meets the roof. The repeating grid lets daylight fall in neat stripes, a strong visual cue in interior renders.

The skylight is a StemCell model at TurboSquid, which means its PBR materials work in several render engines from one file. Non-overlapping UVs allow dirt streaks on the frame and subtle reflections or rain spots on the glass. A dark powder-coated metal shader against clear glass gives the structure a crisp modern outline.

Introduced in 2024, the skylight suits commercial architecture renders, museum halls and transit hub concepts. Seen from below with clouds passing overhead, it gives a lobby a bright and open feeling. Urban planners also add it to rooftop views to show how daylight reaches the floors underneath.
