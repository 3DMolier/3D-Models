About This Model

Locomotive ES40DC CSX Blue is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 1,356,225 polygons and 1,392,204 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

15 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
