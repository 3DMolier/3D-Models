About This Model

Looking for a Ships & Boats asset? The Naval River Class Offshore Patrol Ship is a finished, render-ready model at $249. You are getting 436,979 polygons and 473,394 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 15.95 x 91.35 x H36.1 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation and naval simulation. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
