About This Model

The Aluminized Fire Proximity Suit is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $99 on TurboSquid. You are getting 14,430 polygons and 14,621 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 6 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
