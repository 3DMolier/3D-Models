About This Model

Counter-drone systems are mostly antennas, and that is what makes them interesting to model: a detection radar, a set of directional jammers, an optical tracker and the mast that carries them, all packaged to be mounted on a vehicle and set up quickly. This build is the system itself, and the density here is exceptional.

At 4,828,176 polygons and 2,444,600 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, this is one of the heaviest assets in the catalog relative to its physical size. Every antenna element and mounting bracket is geometry, which is what lets a close camera examine the array rather than only pass over it.

Two other builds sit on this page: the system mounted on an Oshkosh M-ATV at $169, and a rigged version of that vehicle at $199. Aimed at defence visualization, news graphics, counter-UAS presentation and training material. On sale since 2022.
