About This Model

Priced at $99, the African Giraffe is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for film production, game environments and VR experiences. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 10,448 polygons over 10,787 vertices - a light build for its class. Texture work runs to 4 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
