About This Model

The famous cathedral on Red Square in Moscow, with its cluster of onion domes twisted, striped and faceted in bright colors. Saint Basils Cathedral rebuilds the tent-roofed central tower, the smaller chapels around it and the arched galleries joining them. Each dome has its own pattern, so the building stays recognizable from any side and at any distance.

Painted brick, white stone trim and gilded crosses give the church its fairground look under daylight. Close shots of the domes reveal crisp edges on the scales and spiral ribs. Inspectors certified the landmark at CheckMate Pro, which covers clean topology, proper naming and real-world scale.

At $199 the cathedral has drawn 20 buyers since 2015, among them travel channels, history documentaries and strategy game makers. Dropped into a winter scene with snow on the domes, it tells viewers instantly that the story has moved to Russia. It also anchors a believable Moscow skyline when placed beside the red Kremlin walls.
