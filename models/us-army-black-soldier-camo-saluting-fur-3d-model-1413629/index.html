About This Model

US Soldier Fur is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. You are getting 26,879 polygons and 28,281 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

23 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2019 · Updated 4 September 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the US Soldier Fur model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

Can the US Soldier Fur model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Where can I buy the US Soldier Fur 3D model?

It is sold through TurboSquid, where this listing has been available since 2019. Purchase, download and licensing are handled by TurboSquid; delivery is immediate after checkout.

What texture resolution ships with the US Soldier Fur?

23 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.
