About This Model

A behind-the-ear Bluetooth headset with a swiveling boom microphone, modeled after the Plantronics Voyager Legend. The device needs only 5,175 polygons and 5,550 vertices, so it can sit on a character's ear without adding weight. The curved boom and the bulbous earpiece are what make it recognizable in a side profile.

The 3 maps at 2048 x 2048 cover glossy black plastic, the soft ear tip and the small control buttons. Non-overlapping UVs keep those textures sharp in a macro shot of the microphone tip or the charging contacts. CheckMate Pro certification confirms the headset is built at true size, ready for a hand or an ear.

It is licensed for Editorial Uses Only, so it suits news pieces, tech reviews and documentary scenes. Introduced in 2017 at $19, it often fills call center desks, a driver taking calls or a home office. Device comparisons and how-to guides use it to show how the earpiece hooks over the ear.
