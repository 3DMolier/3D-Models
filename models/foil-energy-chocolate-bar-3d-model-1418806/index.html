About This Model

Looking for a Containers & Storage asset? The Foil Energy Chocolate Bar is a finished, render-ready model at $29. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale.

Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It fits food advertising, packaging visualisation, restaurant interiors and game props.

Geometry weighs in at 4,260 polygons over 4,088 vertices - a light build for its class. It ships 2 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
