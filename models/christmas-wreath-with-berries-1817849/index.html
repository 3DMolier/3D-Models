About This Model

A round fir wreath studded with glossy red berries, ready to hang on front doors, mantels and seasonal greeting card scenes. The mesh is heavy at 697,425 polygons and 633,625 vertices, because every needle cluster is modeled as real geometry. That density gives the ring a soft, bristly edge instead of a flat band of painted foliage.

Separated UV islands let the green drift toward blue spruce or pick up a frosted winter tint. TurboSquid awarded it CheckMate Pro, so the decoration fits a standard door without manual rescaling. Small specular highlights on the round berries keep the whole arrangement looking fresh and natural rather than plastic.

This berry wreath costs $44, the Christmas Ball Wreath with 683,168 polygons costs $49, and the Christmas Wreath Garland is $39. The garland is the lightest of the three versions at 634,936 polygons, handy when a long mantel or staircase rail needs draping. On sale since 2021, the set suits greeting cards, festive shop windows, cozy living room interiors and winter village scenes.
