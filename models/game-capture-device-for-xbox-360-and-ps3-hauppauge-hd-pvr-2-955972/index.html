About This Model

This Hauppauge game capture box records console gameplay in high definition, a familiar gadget on streamers' desks a decade ago. It is modeled with 10,580 polygons and 10,758 vertices, enough for the angled top, ports and status light. Made for Xbox and PlayStation consoles of its time, the device sits between console and TV in a setup.

Image textures are not included, and the glossy black shell and logo are handled through editable materials instead. Non-overlapping UVs leave space for port labels, dust or fingerprints on the shiny top surface of the box.
