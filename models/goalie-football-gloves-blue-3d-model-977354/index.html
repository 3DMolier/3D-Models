About This Model

A pair of padded goalkeeper gloves in bright blue, with spread fingers and thick palm foam ready for a diving save. Stitched seams between the finger panels give the surface enough relief for close product shots. The wrist straps wrap around a wide cuff, and the backhand carries a raised protective layer that catches light along its edges.

The model passed TurboSquid CheckMate Pro inspection, so the mesh arrives clean, with sensible naming and pivots that behave when posed. Blue synthetic latex reads well against green grass, which keeps the gloves visible even when the camera pulls back to a stadium shot.

On sale since 2015, the gloves suit sports broadcast graphics, football video games and kit-shop product renders. Drop them into a locker room scene beside boots and shin guards, or hang them on a goal net for a match-day still. Coaches and sports apps also use them in training illustrations that explain how a keeper catches and parries the ball.
