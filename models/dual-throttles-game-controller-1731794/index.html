About This Model

Dual Throttles Game Controller is a twin-lever throttle unit for flight simulators, the kind home pilots mount beside a joystick. The model uses 31,771 polygons and 32,691 vertices to shape the levers, the grip buttons and the angled base. Two separate levers let a player control engines independently, which matters in multi-engine aircraft and space sims.

Hat switches and thumb buttons on the grips are modeled as distinct shapes, so close-ups read like real hardware. Rubber, matte plastic and metal finishes go onto an overlap-free unwrap without stretching across the curves. Its CheckMate Pro grade confirms the controller arrives with orderly naming and accurate proportions.

Released back in 2021, the throttle suits gaming room setups, peripheral product shots and simulator cockpit builds. Streamers' desk scenes and esports interiors look more authentic with a flight setup beside the keyboard. Tutorial videos about flight simulation can point to each control while explaining what it does.
