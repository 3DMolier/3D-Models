About This Model

A painted wooden cuckoo clock shaped like a small chalet, with a carved roof, a bird door and pine cone weights. Carved leaves and colored trim cover the front, giving the clock the folk-art look of the Black Forest. The pendulum swings below the case, and the clock face sits in the center of the gable.

A CheckMate Pro pass guarantees sensible pivots, so hanging it on a wall takes a single move. Bright colors on the roof and trim help it stand out in a cozy interior. Wooden surfaces show grain and wear, which keeps the painted details from looking plastic.

Two versions are on offer: this static clock at $49 and a Colored Wooden Cuckoo Clock Rigged version at $69 with moving parts. Released in 2022, it appears in children's animations, alpine chalet interiors, holiday greeting cards and folk art displays. Hung above a fireplace in a mountain cabin, it sets a homely mood.
