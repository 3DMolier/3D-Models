About This Model

Paper Clip Triangular Shape Color is a detailed general asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $9. The mesh carries 1,096 polygons and 552 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
