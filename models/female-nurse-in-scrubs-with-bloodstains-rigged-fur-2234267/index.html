About This Model

Female Nurse in Scrubs with Bloodstains Rigged Fur is a hospital worker in stained scrubs, made for emergency room drama and horror scenes. Blood marks on the uniform tell a story before any action begins, which is why the character suits tense medical sequences. The mesh stays light at 13,229 polygons, so the figure can share a corridor with beds and equipment.

Hair is handled as fur rather than geometry, giving soft strands that react to light more naturally than cards. Vertices total 13,322, almost matching the faces, which reflects a clean closed body without loose fragments. The UVs do not overlap, so the blood pattern can be repainted lighter or heavier to fit the tone of a project.

Available since 2024, the nurse fits hospital thrillers, zombie and survival games, trauma training content and true crime reconstructions. The figure works well under flickering fluorescent light in an empty ward, where stains read clearly against pale fabric. Hiding the stains through materials lets the same character appear in calmer clinic scenes.
