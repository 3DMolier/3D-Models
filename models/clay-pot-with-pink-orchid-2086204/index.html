About This Model

The Clay Pot with Orchid is a flowering moth orchid with arching stems of pink blooms rising from a terracotta pot. Its 297,871 polygons and 306,073 vertices are spent on delicate petals, broad leaves and the aerial roots over the rim. The arching flower spike gives a strong graceful curve that softens a hard-edged interior.

Clean, non-overlapping UVs let petals carry fine veins while the clay pot gets its own texture. CheckMate Pro certification confirms clean naming and sensible scale for close interior shots and product photography. Because of its dense geometry, the plant works best as a hero piece on a windowsill or table.

A second version, Orchid with Clay Pot, costs $39 compared with $24 for this one. The alternative arrangement gives a different stem shape, which helps rooms that need several orchids at once. Since 2023 the orchid has appeared in living room renders, spa interiors and florist product shots.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2023 · Updated 17 November 2026
