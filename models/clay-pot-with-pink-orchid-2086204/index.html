About This Model

Priced at $24, the Clay Pot with Orchid is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 297,871 polygons and 306,073 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets.

2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

Questions About This Model

How heavy is the Clay Pot with Orchid mesh?

297,871 polygons and 306,073 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

What is the Clay Pot with Orchid model typically used for?

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

Can the Clay Pot with Orchid model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Can the Clay Pot with Orchid model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
