About This Model

Priced at $19, the Apple HomeKit Idevices Thermostat is a finished Architecture Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for advertising, product visualization and film production. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 4,992 polygons over 4,998 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
