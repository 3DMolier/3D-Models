About This Model

A male bullfinch caught in a landing animation, rigged for Maya, with wings spread as it settles onto a perch. Spread primaries, a fanned tail and tucked feet are the details that make a landing read correctly in slow motion. Its red breast and dark crown stand out against snow, bark or a pale winter sky.

Following CheckMate specification, the rig and keys load cleanly, so the landing plays straight away on the Maya timeline. Wing poses open wide and then fold, a sequence that sells weight and balance as the bird arrives. The feeding version of this bullfinch sits on its own page, so the two clips can be cut together into one sequence.

First offered in 2025, this $179 clip suits wildlife films, Christmas animations and nature education apps. Game designers trigger landing clips like this when a player approaches a tree, adding life to the scene. A single bird arriving on a branch also makes a gentle opening shot for seasonal stories.
