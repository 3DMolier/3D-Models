About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Frog Sitting Pose is a finished, render-ready model at $99. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 11,768 polygons over 11,779 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
