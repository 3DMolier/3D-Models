About This Model

A moray eel with a long muscular body, a gaping mouth full of sharp teeth and a mottled skin pattern. Murena is rigged for Maya, so the serpentine body can coil, sway and strike from a reef crevice. Its permanently open jaws are how morays breathe, yet to divers the pose looks like a constant threat.

Hidden in a rocky hole with only its head showing, the eel brings tension to any coral reef scene. The file follows TurboSquid's CheckMate specification for clean structure and consistent naming.

Both versions sell at $79: this Maya rig and a Murena Rigged for Cinema 4D for animators on that package. Since 2016 aquariums, diving schools and underwater game studios have made 21 purchases of the eel. Lunging out at a passing fish, it gives a documentary or a shooter level a sudden jolt of danger. Marine biology lessons also use it to explain how reef predators ambush their prey.
