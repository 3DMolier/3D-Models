About This Model

A jade guardian lion sits on a carved base, its paw resting on a ball and its curly mane framing a fierce face. The model has no polygon figures listed, but the mane curls, the teeth and the pattern on the ball are clear enough for printing. The figure is prepared as a closed solid, ready to print in resin or filament.

TurboSquid checked the file against its CheckMate specification, so the lion opens at a correct size in a slicer. A green jade material previews the finish, and it can be changed to bronze or stone for a render. The lion and its base are one piece, which keeps printing simple and sturdy.

Royalty Free from its 2024 release, it suits Asian decor, temple scenes and collectible prints. Guardian lions traditionally stand in pairs at gates, so a mirrored copy makes a full entrance. Hobbyists also print it as a desk ornament or a gift for the Lunar New Year.
