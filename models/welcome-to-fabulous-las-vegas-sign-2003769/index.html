About This Model

Priced at $39, the Welcome Las Vegas Sign is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 68,112 polygons and 38,090 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
