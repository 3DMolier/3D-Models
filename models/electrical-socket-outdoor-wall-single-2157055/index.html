About This Model

Priced at $29, the Electrical Socket Outdoor Wall Single is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 17,068 polygons and 8,764 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
