About This Model

Chery Jelly Pudding is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. You are getting 1,640 polygons and 1,642 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
