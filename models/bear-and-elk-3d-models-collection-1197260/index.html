About This Model

This collection pairs a bear and an elk, two signature animals of northern forests, for wildlife films and outdoor scenes. The elk's wide antlers and long legs contrast with the bear's heavy, low body and thick coat. Together they tell a predator and prey story that suits taiga, mountain and national park settings.

CheckMate Pro status means TurboSquid's toughest inspection confirmed the meshes, UVs and naming for both animals. Fur and hide textures can be darkened, faded or dusted with snow to match a season or region.

Available since 2017, the pair suits nature documentaries, hunting simulators, outdoor gear visuals and wilderness adventure games. A misty dawn clearing with the elk drinking while the bear watches from the trees creates instant tension. Park services and educators use these animals to explain forest ecosystems, migration and wildlife safety for hikers. Both animals are large, so placing them beside trees or rocks immediately gives viewers a sense of real scale.
