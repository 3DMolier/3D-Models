About This Model

Locker Room Interior is one of the Furniture & Interior models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. Geometry weighs in at 1,380,664 polygons over 1,408,395 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and event visualization as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
