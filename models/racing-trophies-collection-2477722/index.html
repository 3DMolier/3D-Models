About This Model

A collection of racing trophies in several shapes, made for podium ceremonies and motorsport celebrations. Polished bowls, handles and stepped bases give each award the shine expected after a race. Each trophy stands on its own base, so you can rank them on a podium in any order.

The pieces follow CheckMate guidelines, with clean names that make it easy to swap materials between gold, silver and bronze. Reflective surfaces reward a good HDRI, which turns a simple cup into the brightest object in the shot. Matching scale across the set keeps every trophy believable in a driver's hands or on a shelf.

Introduced in 2025, the collection suits racing game menus, sports broadcasts, team garages and award night scenes. Arranged in a glass cabinet, they give a motorsport team headquarters an instant history of wins. Animators also hand them to drivers on the podium for champagne celebrations at the finish.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
