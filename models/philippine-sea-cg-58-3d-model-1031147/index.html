About This Model

The Philippine Sea CG-58 is a production-ready Ships & Boats 3D model, priced at $289 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 1,372,713 polygons over 1,522,800 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

41 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
