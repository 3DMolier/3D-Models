About This Model

A light badminton racket with a taut string bed and a feathered shuttlecock make up this sports pair, ready for court scenes. The 4,600 polygons go mostly into the oval head frame, the grip wrap and the cone of feathers on the shuttle. With 4,783 vertices the set stays light enough for mobile games and crowded gym interiors.

Neither object needs a bitmap, because color and finish come from simple materials you can restyle. A tidy unwrap with no overlaps also makes room for custom grip patterns or a printed brand on the frame. TurboSquid confirmed CheckMate Pro after reviewing the geometry, real-world size and object names.

From 2021 the pair has supported school gym renders, sports apps and backyard leisure illustrations. Hang the shuttle in mid-air above the racket strings to freeze the instant of a smash for a poster. Physical education teachers also show it when explaining grip technique and serving rules to beginners.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2021 · Updated 14 November 2026
