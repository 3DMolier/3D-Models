About This Model

The ice giant Uranus as a smooth pale cyan sphere, ready for space scenes, solar system models and astronomy lessons. Its 5,000 polygons give a round silhouette with no faceting, even when the planet fills the frame.

The 4,902 vertices are spread evenly over the surface, so a banded atmosphere texture wraps without pinching. CheckMate Pro certification confirms a clean build placed neatly at the scene origin for orbit animation. The non-overlapping unwrap lets artists paint subtle cloud bands or swap in mission imagery. Lit from one side, the soft terminator line shows the spherical volume convincingly.

This planet has been in the space range since 2016 and remains a solid building block for solar system scenes. Planetariums, educational apps and documentary animators show it with a tilted axis, rolling on its side. Add a thin ring system and a few moons, and the model becomes a complete Uranus diorama.
