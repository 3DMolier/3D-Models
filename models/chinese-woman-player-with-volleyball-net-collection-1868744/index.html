About This Model

A female volleyball player together with a regulation net gives a court scene its two essential elements at once. The Chinese Woman Player with Volleyball Net Collection pairs an athlete in team uniform with the tall net and its posts.

The player waits in a ready stance with knees bent, the posture used just before a serve arrives. Her kit includes the knee pads that indoor players wear for diving saves on a hard floor. The set carries CheckMate Pro certification, so both pieces are clean and scaled to each other.

Available since 2022, the bundle works for sports broadcast graphics and coaching material about court positions. Animators place the athlete near the net to illustrate blocking, using its height as a reference for jump timing. Event designers and educators use the pair to dress a gym interior quickly. Game developers start from it when prototyping a volleyball match before adding a full team.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
