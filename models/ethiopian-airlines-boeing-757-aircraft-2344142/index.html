The Ethiopian Boeing 757 Aircraft is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 90,621 polygons and 95,565 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
