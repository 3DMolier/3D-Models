About This Model

Priced at $349, the Boeing 737-900 with Interior United Airlines is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 5,380,756 polygons and 5,578,308 vertices, a heavy load for a scene. Texture work runs to 32 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
