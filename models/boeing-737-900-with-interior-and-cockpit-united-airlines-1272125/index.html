About This Model

Boeing 737-900 with Interior and Cockpit United is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $349. Budget for 2,908,568 polygons and 2,993,348 vertices when you plan the scene. What matters is not the number itself but how many copies of the object your scene has to carry at once.

The material set is 25 maps, painted at 4096 x 4096. The geometry is built to be worked with: predictable loops, no doubled faces, nothing hidden inside the shell. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It went on sale in 2018 and has been kept in the catalog ever since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
