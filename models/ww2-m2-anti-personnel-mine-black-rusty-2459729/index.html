About This Model

The M2 Anti Personnel Mine is a production-ready Weapons 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. You are getting 10,023 polygons and 10,206 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 19.75 x 12.84 x H40.52 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
