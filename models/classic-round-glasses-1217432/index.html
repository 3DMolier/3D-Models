About This Model

A pair of classic round eyeglasses with thin metal rims, a keyhole bridge and slim temple arms ending in curved tips. The Classic Round Glasses are made of 7,080 polygons and 7,242 vertices, which keeps the fine wire frame smooth. Lenses are modeled with real thickness, so they bend the background correctly in a render.

A single map at 4096 x 4096 covers metal finish and lens tint. A CheckMate Pro rating vouches for clean topology, and the unwrap gives each island its own room without overlaps.

There is one version on sale, priced at $19 and suitable for most faces. Since 2017 the frames have sold 18 copies to studios dressing intellectual characters, optician shop windows, vintage portraits and reading nook scenes. Folded on top of an open book, the glasses add a quiet, scholarly touch to any still life. The temples fold realistically, so they can also hang from a shirt collar or rest on a character's forehead.
