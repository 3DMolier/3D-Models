About This Model

A water polo ball differs from other sport balls in ways that matter to anyone who plays: it is textured all over for grip on a wet hand, brightly colored to stay visible against pool water, and marked with the panel pattern that distinguishes it from a football at a glance. This build is prepared for printing, which is a different requirement from rendering.

A print-ready model has to be watertight and solid rather than merely look right from a camera angle, and preparing it that way is work that a render-only asset never does. That is what this file provides.

At $19 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits 3D printing, trophy and award manufacture, sports merchandise, club and team promotional items, and physical prototyping. On sale since 2024.
