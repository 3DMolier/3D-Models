About This Model

Classic Cruiser Motorcycle is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. The mesh carries 127,021 polygons and 128,774 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
