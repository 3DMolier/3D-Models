About This Model

This big collection focuses on the male muscular system, letting an anatomy course show muscle groups from several angles and depths. Each muscle is shaped with its tendons and attachment points, which matters when students study origins and insertions. Male Muscular System Big Collection gathers these figures so a curriculum does not depend on mismatched sources.

Groups, names and scale match from figure to figure, so a lesson can switch between views without visual jumps. Separate muscles can be highlighted in color during a lesson, a technique physiotherapists use constantly in patient explanations. Deep layers such as the rotator cuff and hip stabilizers become visible once the superficial muscles are hidden.

Released in 2025 at $1199, the set suits teaching, publishing and commercial work alike, with no extra fees per textbook or video. Sports medicine, physiotherapy apps, fitness instruction videos and anatomy atlases are the obvious uses. A warm red muscle shader with pale tendons keeps each group readable on screen.
