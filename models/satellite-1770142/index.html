About This Model

Satellite belongs to our Space & Sci-Fi range and is offered at $49 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 112,230 polygons and 119,869 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX.
