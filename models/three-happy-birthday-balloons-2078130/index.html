About This Model

Three Happy Birthday Balloons is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $24. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

Geometry weighs in at 35,012 polygons over 17,520 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
