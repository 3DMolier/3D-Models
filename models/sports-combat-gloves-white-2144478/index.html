About This Model

The Sports Combat Gloves White is a production-ready Sports & Recreation 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Geometry weighs in at 28,823 polygons over 14,758 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
