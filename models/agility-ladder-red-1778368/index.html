About This Model

A flat red agility ladder with plastic rungs and nylon straps, laid out the way coaches unroll it on turf for footwork drills. The 18,076 polygons go mostly into the straps and the rung clips, which give the ladder its real, slightly flexible look. Lying flat, it reads well from a high camera, the angle fitness videos tend to use for drills.

There are no bitmap textures here, and the red and black come from simple material colors you can change in seconds. The UVs do not overlap, so adding a fabric weave or worn turf stains later takes very little preparation. With 17,948 vertices and a CheckMate Pro pass, the mesh opens cleanly in common packages without stray faces.

Released in 2021 at $29, it works as a quiet detail in gym interiors, football training scenes and sports apps. Physiotherapy explainers use it too, since the rungs show stepping patterns clearly when the camera looks down. Pair it with cones and hurdles, and a complete training field set comes together quickly.
