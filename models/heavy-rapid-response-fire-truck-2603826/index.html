About This Model

A heavy rapid response fire truck with equipment lockers and roof gear, built for emergency scenes and fire station renders. The 577,861 polygons cover the cab, pump panels, ladders and rolled hoses fitted along the body. Bright paint and reflective striping make the truck the focal point of any street rescue.

CheckMate approval means clean geometry, and the 621,693 vertices keep doors, lockers and gear as separate parts. Partial UVs suit procedural livery work, so you can change the paint to match local fire services. Open the lockers to show the tools stored inside during an incident.

You can choose between two versions here: the standard truck at $169 and a rigged edition at $199 for animation. Added to the catalog in 2026, both suit firefighting games, safety training and emergency response documentaries. Park it beside a hydrant with lights flashing and a rescue scene is ready.
