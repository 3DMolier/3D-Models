About This Model

This Military Vehicles model - French AMX-30 - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $139 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 579,089 polygons over 587,839 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

15 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags defense simulation, battlefield simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defense training material and military VFX shots.
