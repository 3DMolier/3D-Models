About This Model

Looking for a general asset? The Prayer Wheel is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 41,504 polygons and 20,940 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
