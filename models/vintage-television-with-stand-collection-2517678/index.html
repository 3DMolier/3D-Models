About This Model

A collection of old tube televisions standing on legs, the kind that held pride of place in mid-century living rooms. Curved screens, rotary channel knobs and splayed legs give each set a clear period character from across the room.

Every set in the bundle passed CheckMate specification, so geometry is tidy and naming makes sense. Sets can be pulled apart and used individually, which helps when a room needs only one receiver in the corner. Screens accept a static image or a glowing broadcast frame, and each set keeps its own knob layout.

Two versions make up this family: the first television collection and a sequel bundle with additional receivers. Both appeared in 2026 and serve retro living rooms, museum displays and period film sets where a television fixes the year at a glance. Mix two different sets in one shot to suggest an older and a newer household side by side.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2026 · Updated 13 November 2026
