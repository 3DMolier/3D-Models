About This Model

This Military Vehicles model - Concept Fantastic Military Truck Lights On - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. On this listing the stated applications are military simulation, game environments and film production. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.
