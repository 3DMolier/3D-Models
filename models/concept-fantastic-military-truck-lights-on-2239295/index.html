About This Model

This Military Vehicles model - Concept Fantastic Military Truck Lights On - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, battlefield simulation and war-game environments. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use.
