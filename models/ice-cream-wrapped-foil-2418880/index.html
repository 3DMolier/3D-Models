About This Model

Priced at $15, the Ice Cream Wrapped Foil is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 684 polygons and 684 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 4.27 x 1.45 x H12.75 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
