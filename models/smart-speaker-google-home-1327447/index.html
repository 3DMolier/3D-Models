Smart Speaker Google Home belongs to our Electronics & Gadgets range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 3,747 polygons and 3,837 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
