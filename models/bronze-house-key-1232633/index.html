About This Model

Bronze House Key belongs to our Architecture Landmarks range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
