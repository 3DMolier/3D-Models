About This Model

A wheeled armored patrol vehicle with a heavy machine gun on the roof, modeled in dense detail for military scenes. The 1,126,088 polygons cover the angled armor plates, the gun mount, the large tires and the hatches. Camouflage paint and boxy armor give it a tactical silhouette that reads at a glance in any desert or urban shot.

The vehicle measures 321.78 x 578.89 x H405.23 cm, so soldiers and buildings beside it stay in proportion. A mixed UV approach gives hero armor panels unique space and lets repeated bolts and wheel parts share it. CheckMate specification rules apply here, so the gun, wheels and body carry clear names for quick material changes.

New in 2026 at $179, the vehicle fits defense simulation, war films and shooter game levels. Its height with the gun makes it a strong focal point for a checkpoint or convoy scene. Training content also uses such models to explain vehicle layouts and crew positions.
