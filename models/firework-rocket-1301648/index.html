About This Model

Firework Rocket is a detailed Signage & Decor asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. The mesh carries 1,538 polygons and 1,580 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
