About This Model

A massive stone golem hand prepared for Cinema 4D, made for fantasy shots where a giant fist rises from the ground or crushes a wall. Its fingers are built from rough, cracked rock slabs, giving the hand the heavy, ancient look golems are known for. Thick knuckles and blunt fingertips make the silhouette read as stone even in dark scenes.

Built to CheckMate specification, its Cinema 4D scene opens with sensible names and a consistent scale. Moss, glowing runes or dust can be layered onto the rock surface to suit your fantasy world. Board game and card artists use such hands for dramatic key art without building a full creature.

Available since 2023, the Stone Golem Hand Rigged for Cinema 4D(1) suits fantasy films, game cinematics and dramatic motion graphics. Framed close to camera, the hand alone can suggest a giant creature far bigger than the frame. Artists often pair it with debris and dust simulations as it bursts out of the earth.
