About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Silver iPad Pro 2019 is a finished, render-ready model at $39. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 61,139 polygons over 61,566 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
