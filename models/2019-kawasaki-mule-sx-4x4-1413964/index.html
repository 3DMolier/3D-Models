About This Model

The 2019 Kawasaki Mule SX 4x4 is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. On import you get 141,896 polygons and 154,399 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Textures number 33, authored at 4096 x 4096 so they survive a close crop. The geometry is built to be worked with: predictable loops, no doubled faces, nothing hidden inside the shell. Parts are separated where separation is useful, so a single component can be hidden, swapped or re-shaded on its own.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. Its first release was in 2019, and it has earned its place in the catalog since then. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
