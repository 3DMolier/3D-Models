About This Model

The C-17 with its ramp down is the image of military logistics: an aircraft that can put a tank on an unpaved strip and back itself out again afterwards. The open cargo door is the whole point of the design, and a closed airframe hides what makes the type unusual.

The model holds 1,380,711 polygons and 1,468,755 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the cargo door modelled open. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification. The library also holds the closed airframe, an in flight build, and rigged versions including native rigs for Maya and Cinema 4D.

At $169 it suits defence and logistics visualization, flight simulation, documentary and film, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.
