About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Axiom 12 Deluxe Pool is a finished, render-ready model at $15. You are getting 2,202 polygons and 2,207 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

Questions About This Model

Can the Axiom 12 Deluxe Pool model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

How much does the Axiom 12 Deluxe Pool 3D model cost?

$15 USD, paid once. The Royalty Free license means no recurring cost and no extra payment when the finished work ships.

How clean is the geometry on the Axiom 12 Deluxe Pool model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.

Can the Axiom 12 Deluxe Pool model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
