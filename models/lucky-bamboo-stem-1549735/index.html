About This Model

The Lucky Bamboo Stem is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 5,300 polygons and 5,194 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets.

First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
