About This Model

An offshore supply vessel with a high forward bridge and a long open cargo deck, simplified for wide shots. The Ocean Dee Offshore Supply Ship Simplified holds 489,956 polygons and 533,497 vertices, trimmed from a heavier build yet keeping its hull form. The wide stern deck is the working area, where cargo is loaded by crane at sea.

Its hull length suits a real platform support vessel, the kind that ferries pipes and fuel to oil rigs. CheckMate certification secures sensible hierarchy and true metric scale for maritime scenes. Cranes, railings and deck winches remain readable from a distance, which is where this version earns its keep.

Priced at $179 the simplified ship is a single 2026 edition without alternates. North Sea documentaries, offshore energy explainers, harbor scenes and naval simulators use it as working traffic. Place it beside a rig or a quay and the scene instantly gains an industrial maritime character.
