About This Model

This collection gathers wine equipment for a cellar or tasting room, from bottles and glasses to corkscrews, decanters and barrels. Glass items carry clean curves for believable refraction, while wooden pieces show staves, metal hoops and grain. Mixing tools with serving items lets a single scene shift from winery production to an elegant tasting table.

CheckMate Lite certification confirms the files open cleanly with believable proportions for every item. Each piece is separate, so a sommelier's tray or a cellar shelf can be arranged freely. Deep red wine in a decanter and soft candlelight give the set its natural evening mood.

Offered since 2022, the equipment suits winery tours, restaurant menus and gourmet lifestyle renders. A barrel with glasses and a bottle on top is a quick way to dress a wine bar corner. Royalty Free terms open the set to commercial hospitality, beverage and lifestyle projects of any size.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
