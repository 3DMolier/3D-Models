About This Model

Priced at $179, the Skyscrapers Collection is a finished Model Bundles & Sets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. The set covers several related objects at once, which saves assembling a scene from separate purchases.
