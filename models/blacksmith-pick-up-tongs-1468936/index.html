About This Model

Blacksmith Pick Up Tongs is a detailed Tools asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 2,052 polygons and 2,058 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
