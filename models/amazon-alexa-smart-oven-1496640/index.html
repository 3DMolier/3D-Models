About This Model

A countertop smart oven with a glass door, a control panel and racks, the appliance Amazon paired with its Alexa voice assistant. Its 26,530 polygons and 30,571 vertices shape the door handle, the dials, the interior rack rails and the heating elements. The compact box proportions let it sit under wall cabinets without crowding the rest of the counter.

6 maps at 4096 x 4096 provide the stainless front, the dark glass and the printed panel markings. Because UV islands never overlap, grease marks or a fresh label can be painted on with no artifacts. It holds a CheckMate Pro rating, reviewed by TurboSquid for topology and file order.

Available since 2020, the oven helps kitchen designers, smart home explainers and interior renders of modern apartments. Place a tray of cookies inside, light the interior warmly, and the kitchen suddenly feels lived in. It also fits a dorm kitchenette or an office break room scene.
