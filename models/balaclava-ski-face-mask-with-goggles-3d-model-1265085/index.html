About This Model

Adding goggles to a balaclava changes the whole head. The strap compresses the knit across the back of the skull, the frame sits on the fabric rather than on skin, and the eye opening is covered rather than open - which means the figure loses its face entirely and becomes a silhouette. That anonymity is often the point.

The mesh runs 37,312 polygons and 38,019 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 14 texture maps at a wide spread of sizes carrying the knit structure, the strap webbing and the lens tint.

At $49 it suits winter sports advertising, outdoor and tactical clothing visualization, character set dressing for film, television and games, and retail renders. On sale since 2018, kept to real-world scale so it fits a standard head.
