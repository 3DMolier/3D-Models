About This Model

This Signage & Decor model - Clio Trophys in Museum Stand - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. You are getting 16,622 polygons and 16,901 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and architectural visualization.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
