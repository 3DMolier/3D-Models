About This Model

The shortest variant of this family in a low cost livery is an unusual and useful combination, and this build includes the cabin. The short fuselage changes the proportions noticeably: fewer window rows and a wing that looks large relative to the body compared with the stretched versions.

It is one of the heaviest aircraft assets in the catalog at 4,409,964 polygons and 4,567,247 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. That density is the interior, built to the same standard as the exterior.

This page carries a second static build at $349 and rigged versions at $449. Aimed at airline marketing, airport visualization, safety and training material, film and television, and news graphics. On sale since 2025.

How clean is the geometry on the Ryanair Boeing 737-600 with Interior model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.

What is the Ryanair Boeing 737-600 with Interior model typically used for?

Can the Ryanair Boeing 737-600 with Interior model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
