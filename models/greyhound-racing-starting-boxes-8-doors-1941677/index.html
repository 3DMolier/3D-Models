About This Model

Starting boxes are a piece of track furniture with a very specific job: hold eight dogs still, then open every door at exactly the same instant. That requirement shapes the object - a long steel frame, numbered colored doors, a shared release mechanism along the top and a rear loading gate for each stall. The numbering and the color order are part of the sport, and they are modelled here rather than left to a texture.

The mesh is 173,412 polygons and 89,302 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the detail concentrated in the frame, the mesh doors and the release gear.

At $49 it suits sports broadcast graphics, racing venue visualization, betting and gaming applications, film and television, and animal sport documentary. On sale since 2022.
