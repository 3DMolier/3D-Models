About This Model

Floorless camping tents, the tarp shelters and pyramid tents hikers pitch over bare ground, are grouped here for outdoor and survival scenes. Taut fabric panels, guy lines and stakes give each shelter the crisp, angular profile of a well pitched camp. The open base shows grass or snow underneath, a detail that sets these shelters apart from ordinary dome tents.

The group earned CheckMate Pro, which means TurboSquid confirmed clean meshes and logical naming for every item. Each shelter is an independent object, so you can scatter several across a clearing and vary their colors. Fabric folds near the stakes catch low sunlight nicely, which gives a camp scene a calm early morning feel.

Since 2017 the tents have pitched up in camping gear catalogs, hiking games, survival films and travel illustrations. Add a small fire and a couple of backpacks, and a wild campsite is ready for a sunset shot. Outdoor brands can also show how lightweight shelters work in tutorials for beginners.
