About This Model

A vintage film projector with two large reels mounted on its arms, the centerpiece of home movie nights and old cinema booths. The Vintage Film Projector with Reels uses 108,991 polygons for the lens barrel, reel arms, gears and housing.

Its 112,689 vertices give the round reels and the lens a smooth profile in close shots. StemCell certification supplies the projector in many native formats prepared from a single reference scene. The film path loops from the top reel through the gate to the bottom one, ready to animate.

The projector arrived in 2026 at $59 and fits retro interiors, film history documentaries and cinema themed renders. Aim it at a screen in a darkened living room, or display it on a shelf among old cameras. Spin the reels and add a beam of light through dust to evoke a classic screening. Film schools explaining analog projection can show how each frame passes the lamp.
