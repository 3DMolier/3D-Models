About This Model

An iPhone 7 paired with AirPods and their charging case, the headphone-jack-free combination that defined the lineup that year. The flip-top case, the stem-shaped earbuds and the phone together tell the wireless audio story in a single frame.

Each object passed CheckMate Pro inspection, so scale, naming and clean geometry are consistent across the bundle. With non-overlapping UVs, the phone screen can carry any interface and the white plastic of the earbuds stays crisp. The earbuds come as separate parts, so one can rest in an ear, a hand or the open case.

The license reads Editorial Uses Only, making the set a fit for tech reviews, news graphics and how-to videos. Issued in 2017, it captures a specific moment in consumer electronics that period films and retro tech features often need. A tidy flat lay of phone, case and buds on a desk also works as an establishing shot for a commuter or student character.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2017 · Updated 13 November 2026

Can the IPhone 7 and AirPods Collection model be used in a commercial project?

No. The model depicts a real branded product, so TurboSquid lists it under the Editorial Uses Only license. It may be used in news, commentary, education, personal projects and similar editorial contexts, but not in advertising, on merchandise or in any product offered for sale. See our license guide.

Can the IPhone 7 and AirPods Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself, or using the result commercially: this listing is Editorial Uses Only.

What is the IPhone 7 and AirPods Collection model typically used for?

Stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and technical visualization. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

How clean is the geometry on the IPhone 7 and AirPods Collection model?

The mesh uses organized, quad-dominant topology at real-world scale, with objects named and grouped rather than merged into one block.
