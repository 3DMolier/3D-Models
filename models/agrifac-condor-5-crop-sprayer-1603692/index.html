About This Model

A self propelled sprayer is a machine built around a boom that can be forty meters wide and must stay level over uneven ground while the wheels track between growing rows. Everything else on the vehicle exists to carry the tank and keep that boom steady.

The model is built at subdivision level, 345,656 polygons and 376,149 vertices with non-overlapping UVs, and carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $129 it suits agricultural machinery marketing, farming and agritech visualization, training material, documentary work, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2020.
