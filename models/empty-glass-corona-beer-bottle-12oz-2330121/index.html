About This Model

An empty clear glass beer bottle in the tall Corona shape, with a long neck and a crown cap finish at the top. Its 3,714 polygons and 3,658 vertices keep the glass light while preserving the subtle shoulder and the lip ring. Clear glass shows whatever stands behind it, so the bottle reads well on a bar counter or a sunny beach table.

Glass refraction is driven by materials, and a lime wedge or leftover foam can be added for story. The unwrap leaves clean space on the body for the painted label, keeping text straight when the bottle rotates. Because the design belongs to a real beer brand, use is limited to Editorial Uses Only, such as news or documentary work.

Made in 2024, the bottle fits bar interiors, beach party scenes, recycling stories and film sets about summer nights. Several empties on a table instantly suggest a long evening, a celebration or a quiet afternoon after work. For a $15 model, it gives realistic glass detail to scenes that would otherwise use a generic bottle.
