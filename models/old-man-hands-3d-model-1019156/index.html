About This Model

Old Man Hands is one of the Medical models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

The mesh carries 6,512 polygons and 6,536 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 5 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
