About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Human with Child is a finished, render-ready model at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 196,914 polygons and 116,209 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.
