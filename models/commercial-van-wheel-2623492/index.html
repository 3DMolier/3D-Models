About This Model

A light commercial van wheel with a steel rim, hubcap and sturdy tire, ready for delivery vans, ambulances and workshop scenes. The Commercial Van Wheel uses 10,017 polygons and 10,366 vertices, enough for tread blocks, sidewall detail and wheel nuts. Its reinforced tire profile is taller than a car tire, which suits loaded vans.

CheckMate certification gives tidy geometry with a centered pivot, so the wheel can spin or be swapped quickly. The plain steel rim reads as a working vehicle rather than a sporty one, which keeps fleet scenes believable. It can sit on a hub, lean against a van or wait on a tire changer.

Two versions are listed: the wheel itself at $39 and the 2020 Ford Transit Ambulance Car Simplified at $149. Both arrived in 2026 and come with Royalty Free licensing for commercial work. Delivery fleet visualizations, garage interiors, vehicle simulators and emergency service scenes are where it fits.
