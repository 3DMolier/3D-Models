About This Model

A collection of travel equipment for trips that go beyond the city, gathering the gear a traveler carries on longer journeys. The pieces vary in size and purpose, so one purchase can dress a campsite, a train compartment or a hostel locker.

Every object follows CheckMate conventions for scale and naming, which keeps mixed sizes believable side by side. Items arrive as separate meshes that can be repositioned individually without breaking the rest of the arrangement. Simple, well-split materials make it easy to recolor straps and fabrics for a specific brand-free look.

Part of the catalog since 2025, the set suits adventure games, outdoor lifestyle renders and tourism presentations. Load a few pieces into the trunk of a car and the frame suggests a road trip about to begin. Educational content about packing lists and travel safety also benefits from clean, recognizable gear. Hang a bag on a hook by the door and an ordinary hallway reads as a home of frequent travelers.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
