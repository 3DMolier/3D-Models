About This Model

Oriental Pearl Tower Snow Globe is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 9,180 polygons and 9,000 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and architectural visualization. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
