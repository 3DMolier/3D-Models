About This Model

A collection of sports nets for goals, courts and practice cages, the fine mesh that quietly makes a playing field believable. Nets are fiddly and slow to model by hand, with every cord crossing the next. Ready variants save real time when building a stadium, a gym or a backyard practice area.

Every net here holds CheckMate Pro approval for geometry, proportions and file organization. The cords hang with a natural sag between the posts, so they never look like a rigid grid painted on glass. Each net is separate, so goals and court dividers can be placed independently around the field.

On the market since 2019, the set serves sports games, stadium visualization and coaching animations. Behind a goal mouth under floodlights, a net with proper depth gives a scored ball something to hit. The pieces also suit school gyms and tennis clubs, where empty frames without mesh would look unfinished.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
