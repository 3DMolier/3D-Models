About This Model

A set of street billboards for city scenes, giving you roadside and sidewalk structures that wait for your own artwork. Billboards are frames for content, so the useful parts are the panel, the supports and the frame edge that catches light. Grouping several formats in one purchase lets a whole street carry consistent signage without mixing styles from different sellers.

StemCell certification standardizes PBR materials across formats, so the panels behave the same in a game engine and an offline renderer. Replace the face texture with a poster, and the structure becomes a mock-up for an outdoor campaign within minutes. Night scenes benefit most, where a lit panel becomes the brightest point in the frame.

Released in 2026, the collection carries a royalty free license, which covers commercial work including client presentations. It fits city environments in games, architectural visualization of streets and mock-ups for out-of-home media agencies. Spacing a few along a highway shoulder gives a long camera move steady points of rhythm.
