About This Model

Looking for a Nature & Plants asset? The Lawn Turf Roll is a finished, render-ready model at $29. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use.
