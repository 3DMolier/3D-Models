About This Model

Gobi Air Defense UAV is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $59. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Counted at the source, the model is 51,918 polygons and 52,806 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
