About This Model

Cobra RAD 480i Laser Radar Detector is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 49,391 polygons and 26,919 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
