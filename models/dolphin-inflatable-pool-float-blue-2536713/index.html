About This Model

A blue inflatable dolphin pool float with a smiling face and handles, ready for summer pool and beach scenes. The puffy body, curved dorsal fin and seam lines capture the look of thin vinyl filled with air. It uses 22,894 polygons and 23,217 vertices, keeping the inflated curves smooth and free of facets.

The float measures 149.69 x 87.05 x H62.82 cm, big enough for a child to ride across the pool. StemCell certification gives it consistent PBR materials, and its UVs mix unique and shared islands across body and fins. A glossy plastic shader with soft highlights makes the vinyl look freshly inflated under the sun.

The Dolphin Inflatable Pool Float Blue joined the catalog in 2026 for resort and backyard visualizations. Hotel pool renders, summer vacation animations and casual beach games use it to add playful color. Toy stores also show it in product scenes, floating beside beach balls and colorful swim rings.
