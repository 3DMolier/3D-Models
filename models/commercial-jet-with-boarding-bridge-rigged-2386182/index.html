About This Model

A passenger jet docked at a jet bridge, this rigged scene captures the moment travelers walk between the terminal and the aircraft. Commercial Jet with Boarding Bridge Rigged spends 3,785,657 polygons on the airliner, the telescoping bridge and the gate equipment around it. The bridge extends and swings to meet the forward door, a motion that sells the whole scene.

With 3,181,704 vertices, the jet carries cockpit windows, door seals and engine detail for close camera passes. Animated bridges and doors let a story show boarding, pushback and departure in one continuous sequence. Ground lights and window reflections reward a careful night setup at the gate.

First offered in 2025 at $469, the scene suits commercial airport and travel projects. Airport design proposals, airline service explainers, travel documentaries and simulation games are natural fits. A wide shot from the apron at dusk, with the cabin lights on, shows the bridge and jet as one working unit.
