About This Model

This launcher is a pump action grenade weapon, which is a rare combination: the operator works a slide like a shotgun to chamber the next round from a tube magazine. It was made in tiny numbers, which is exactly why it appears so often in games - it is genuinely unusual and instantly memorable.

The mesh is deliberately minimal at 5,635 polygons and 2,895 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. That budget is a game weapon budget: light enough for a first person view at high frame rates, with the detail carried by texture.

At $49 it suits games and interactive media, film and television armouries, military history visualization, illustration and print. Real-world scale is kept, so it fits a character rig. On sale since 2024.
