About This Model

Boy Digestive System shows a child's body with the digestive tract exposed, from esophagus and stomach to intestines. The 29,747 polygons model the coiled small intestine, the framing colon and the liver beside the stomach. The child's proportions and soft, rounded organ forms set it apart from adult anatomy models used in medical schools.

Vertices total 29,789, almost exactly matching faces, which reflects clean closed organ shapes. Each organ can carry its own color for clear teaching diagrams, with labels added for quizzes and printed worksheets. The CheckMate Pro setup keeps the organs as separate objects, ready to highlight in turn.

Introduced in 2021, the anatomy suits pediatric health education, school biology lessons, nutrition explainers and medical apps. Animating food through the tract makes digestion clear for children in a few seconds of animation. Pediatricians can also show worried parents where common tummy pain might start in children during a consultation.
