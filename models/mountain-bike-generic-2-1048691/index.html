About This Model

The Mountain Bike Generic 2 is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

Counted at the source, the model is 261,837 polygons and 279,293 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 22 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
