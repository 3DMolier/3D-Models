About This Model

The Mountain Bike Generic 2 is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and game environments. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 261,837 polygons and 279,293 vertices, a mid-weight load for a scene. Maps: 22 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
