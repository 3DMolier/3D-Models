About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Sony Xperia Z5 Set is a finished, render-ready model at $39. The mesh carries 36,496 polygons and 38,820 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 19 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
