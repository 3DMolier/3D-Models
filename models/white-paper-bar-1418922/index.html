About This Model

White Paper Bar is a detailed Containers & Storage asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 5,639 polygons over 5,406 vertices - a light build for its class. Texture work runs to 2 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
