About This Model

The Orlan is a suit you climb into through a hatch in the back, which is why a cosmonaut can put one on alone in minutes while an American suit needs help and an hour. That single design decision shapes the whole silhouette: rigid torso, soft limbs, a door where the backpack should be.

The figure holds 75,244 polygons and 79,060 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 21 maps, 20 at 4096x4096 and one at 512x512. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $149 it suits space programme visualisation, documentary and news graphics, museum and education work, film, game characters and editorial illustration. On sale since 2015.
