About This Model

Magnolia Flower belongs to our Nature & Plants range and is offered at $9 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 11,382 polygons and 11,484 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 4 maps at 2048 x 2048.
