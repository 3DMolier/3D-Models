About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Fireplace Poker Tool is a finished, render-ready model at $13. The mesh carries 1,832 polygons and 922 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
