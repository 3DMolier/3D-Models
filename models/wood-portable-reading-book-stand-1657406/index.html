About This Model

The Wood Portable Reading Book Stand is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. You are getting 9,024 polygons and 9,126 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.
