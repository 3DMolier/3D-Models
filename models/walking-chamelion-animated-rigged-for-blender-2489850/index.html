About This Model

Walking Chamelion is a chameleon caught mid-step, its curled tail balancing the body as the clasping feet grip a branch. The casque on its head, the bulging turret eyes and the rough scaled skin are the details that make it read as a chameleon.

Checked against CheckMate requirements, the reptile lands at believable size with neat naming in the hierarchy. Grasping feet are split into opposing pads that wrap around twigs, just as a real chameleon's do. Its skin suits layered shaders, letting artists shift color patterns from green to orange for camouflage effects. The turreted eyes can be posed looking in different directions, a charming detail for close-ups.

Two versions are provided: this walking model and Chamelion Animated Rigged for Blender, which brings a ready walk cycle. Released in 2025, the lizard suits rainforest documentaries, terrarium visuals, children's animation and jungle levels in games. Slow deliberate steps along a vine make for a calm, hypnotic shot.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
