About This Model

Mosquito with Fur is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $69. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 9,270 polygons and 9,288 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 16 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
