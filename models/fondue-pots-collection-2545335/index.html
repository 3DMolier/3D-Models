About This Model

A collection of fondue pots with their burners and long forks, ready to set a cheese or chocolate evening on any table. Each pot keeps the wide bowl and stable stand that real fondue sets use, so the silhouettes read even from across a room. Lids, handles and the small burner under each bowl are modeled, giving the set enough detail for close dining shots.

The set follows CheckMate specification, so every pot sits at a believable size next to plates and glasses. Separate objects let you mix pot colors or pick just one for an intimate dinner. Melted cheese or chocolate can be added as a simple fluid surface inside the bowls.

New for 2026, the Fondue Pots Collection suits restaurant interiors, recipe videos and winter kitchen product shots. Ski lodge scenes benefit from it too, since fondue is the classic après-ski meal in the Alps. Food brands use such props on banners for cheese and chocolate, often with bread cubes and fruit nearby.
