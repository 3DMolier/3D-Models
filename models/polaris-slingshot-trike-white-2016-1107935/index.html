About This Model

The Polaris Slingshot Trike is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 978,356 polygons over 991,600 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

47 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
