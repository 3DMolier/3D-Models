About This Model

A row of floor-standing server cabinets with perforated doors, ready to line an aisle in a data center or a network operations room. Most of the 658,354 polygons go into the vented front panels, cable trays and the stacked rack units visible behind each door.

The mesh holds 656,101 vertices and meets the CheckMate specification, so the topology already passes the marketplace inspection checklist. Doors, side panels and the hardware inside sit as separate pieces, which lets a lighting artist swing one cabinet open for a close shot. Status lights on the equipment fronts are natural spots for small emissive materials in a darkened server hall.

These racks arrived in the collection in 2024 and suit cloud computing explainers, cybersecurity films and corporate interior visualization. Repeat the cabinets along a raised floor and a whole machine room builds up quickly without extra modeling work. Technology documentaries pick them as a believable backdrop standing behind a presenter talking about data and networks.
