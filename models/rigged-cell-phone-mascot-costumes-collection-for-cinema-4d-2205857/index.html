About This Model

Walking cell phone mascot costumes, the oversized phone-shaped suits worn at store openings, fairs and street events. Each costume has a body shaped like a handset, with a screen face, arm holes and legs for a performer. Because the figures are rigged, they can wave, dance or walk in a parade without manual sculpting.

The mascots were shaped to CheckMate rules, arriving at human scale with clean naming across the whole collection. Bright screen faces can carry any graphic you like, from a smiling emoji to a menu of apps. Soft foam-like shapes read as costumes rather than devices, which keeps the humor of the mascots intact.

This version is set up for Cinema 4D, and a Maya version of the same mascots is priced at $99 too. Since 2024 the mascots have suited event animations, explainer videos, kids' content and short comedic sketches online. Put two dancing mascots outside a phone shop, and the scene gets energy and a smile at once.
