About This Model

Traditional Chinese dress is cut to hang rather than to fit, and a walking pose is the only way to show that: the fabric moves independently of the body, which is exactly what a standing figure cannot demonstrate.

The character holds 35,576 polygons and 37,159 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, light enough for crowds and background use, and carries StemCell certification.

At $149 it suits cultural and historical visualization, architectural and urban scenes, film previsualization, education and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
