The Mandarin with Water Drops is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $34 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 199,057 polygons and 103,324 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
