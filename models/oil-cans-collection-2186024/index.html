About This Model

A collection of metal oil cans in several styles, from small spouted oilers to larger canisters for the garage. Each can is a separate object, so you can line them on a garage shelf or scatter them across a workbench. Dents, seams and pour spouts give the set the look of real workshop containers.

The collection holds CheckMate Pro certification, the strictest technical grade TurboSquid awards. Painted metal shaders with chipped edges, oil stains and faded labels make the cans look properly used. Colors can differ per can, which helps a shelf of them avoid a repetitive look.

The set went on sale in 2024 and brings several related props into one purchase. Garage interiors, gas station scenes, post-apocalyptic games, farm sheds and vintage workshop renders use oil cans as essential clutter. Placed next to tools and a car on a lift, they make a repair shop feel real.
