About This Model

A powered wheelchair is a different object from a manual one, and the differences are all structural: a moulded seat on a powered base, small castors at the front, drive wheels at the middle, a joystick on an armrest and a battery pack under the seat. Those elements have to sit in the right relationship or the chair looks like a manual model with a motor added.

The mesh is 140,556 polygons and 76,634 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry concentrated in the frame, the drive assembly and the controller.

A rigged build sits on this page at $89 for when the chair has to be driven or the footplates adjusted. The static model at $69 covers medical and healthcare visualization, accessibility and inclusive design presentation, architectural renders that need real accessibility detail, and games.
