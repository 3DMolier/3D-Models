About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Marble Cross Memorial(1) is a finished, render-ready model at $19. You are getting 964 polygons and 972 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
