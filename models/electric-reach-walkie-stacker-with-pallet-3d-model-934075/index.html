About This Model

An electric walkie reach stacker carrying a loaded pallet, the compact lifting truck used in narrow warehouse aisles. The Electric Reach Walkie Stacker with Pallet 3D Model pairs the machine with a pallet, so a loading scene is ready without extra props. An operator walks behind the tiller handle while the mast and forks lift goods up to shelf height.

CheckMate Pro status confirms clean geometry and parts that are named and grouped sensibly. Safety yellow paint, black rubber wheels and a worn wooden pallet are the natural material choices. The tall mast dominates the silhouette, making the machine readable even at the far end of a warehouse.

Dating from 2015, the stacker supports logistics visualization, warehouse safety training and material handling equipment brochures. Line several along racking with boxes on pallets, and a distribution center comes together quickly. Training videos can show the correct walking position behind the handle during lifting.
