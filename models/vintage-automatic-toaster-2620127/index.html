About This Model

A chrome toaster from the mid-century era with rounded lines and twin bread slots, a cheerful accent for retro kitchen scenes. This toaster is built entirely from quads, with 53,866 polygons shaping its curved shell, levers and base.

Its 54,597 vertices let the polished body bend highlights smoothly, which is what makes chrome look convincing. CheckMate approval confirms clean geometry and realistic countertop scale for the appliance in any kitchen. Rounded edges and a heavy base give it the solid feel of appliances built to last decades.

Two versions cost $29 each: the Vintage Toaster and the Vintage Sunbeam Radiant Control Toaster. Both arrived in 2026 and fit diner sets, period films and nostalgic home interior renders. Set one on a checkered counter with a jar of jam, or pop toast from it in a breakfast animation. Choose the Sunbeam edition when a recognizable classic model matters, and the plain one for generic kitchens.
