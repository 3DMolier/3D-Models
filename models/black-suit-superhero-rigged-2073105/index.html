About This Model

Priced at $149, the Black Suit Superhero Rigged is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and VR experiences as the intended applications. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

At 32,964 polygons and 25,200 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
