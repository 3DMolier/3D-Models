About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The American National Flag is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 3,598 polygons and 3,624 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
