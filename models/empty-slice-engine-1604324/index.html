About This Model

A sliced engine with its internals removed shows the bare casing walls and passages, built for cutaway teaching visuals. Its subdivision mesh holds 313,565 polygons and 346,648 vertices, keeping the curved walls and cut edges smooth up close. With nothing inside, the section face reads as a clean cross section rather than a crowded assembly.

Under CheckMate Pro review the topology, naming and scale passed, so the cut faces render without artifacts. Non-overlapping UVs allow one color for the machined section surface and another for the rough cast exterior. Painting the cut face a bright accent color makes every internal channel easy to follow on screen.

Offered since 2020 at $99, it serves mechanics schools, engineering videos and technical illustration. Narrators can point at the empty chambers while explaining how heat and oil move through a running engine. Pair it with a complete engine model to show the same unit before and after the cut.
