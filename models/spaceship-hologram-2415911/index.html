About This Model

Spaceship Hologram is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 189,081 polygons and 197,324 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The object measures 330.63 x 330.63 x H223.13 cm, modelled at true scale. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.
