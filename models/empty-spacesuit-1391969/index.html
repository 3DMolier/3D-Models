About This Model

The Empty Spacesuit is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 49,298 polygons and 51,963 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 12 maps at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
