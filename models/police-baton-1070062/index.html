About This Model

An expandable baton is three telescoping steel sections with a knurled grip and a cap at the tip, and its whole design is about carrying: collapsed it is short enough for a belt, extended it doubles or triples. Modelled closed or open, it is a very different object in each state.

The mesh is light at 8,032 polygons and 8,034 vertices, built entirely from quads with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 4 texture maps at 4096x4096 carrying the knurling, the steel finish and the handling wear.

At $29 it suits law enforcement and security visualization, character set dressing for film, television and games, equipment presentation and training material. Real-world scale is kept, so it fits a hand correctly. On sale since 2017.
