About This Model

This Food & Beverages model - Grilled Flank Steak - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 1,246 polygons and 1,243 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
