About This Model

A service robot is only convincing in motion. Standing still it is an appliance; walking, turning its head or handing something over it becomes an assistant, and that difference is entirely animation. This set arrives rigged and animated, prepared for Maya, with the deformation already solved.

Supplying several robots as a set also lets a scene show a fleet rather than one unit, which is how the technology is actually deployed in warehouses, hotels and hospitals.

At $349 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits robotics and automation marketing, future of work and service industry presentation, technology journalism, previsualization and games. On sale since 2026.
