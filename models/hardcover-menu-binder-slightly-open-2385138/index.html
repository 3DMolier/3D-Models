Hardcover Menu Binder Slightly Open is a detailed Signage & Decor asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. The mesh carries 6,336 polygons and 6,611 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
