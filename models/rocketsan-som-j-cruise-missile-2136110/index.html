About This Model

Rocketsan SOM-J Cruise Missile belongs to our Weapons range and is offered at $49 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 5,646 polygons and 2,909 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX.
