About This Model

Banshee Jet 80 Attack Drone Light is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 37,109 polygons and 40,059 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
