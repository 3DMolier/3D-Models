About This Model

Garden Bed is a detailed Architecture & Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 73,146 polygons and 74,632 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
