About This Model

Lobster claws, spring rings and toggle clasps in polished silver, the small fasteners that finish every chain and bracelet. The Silver Jewelry Clasps Collection lets a jewelry designer complete a necklace without modeling tiny hinged parts from scratch. Placed at the back of a chain or shown open on velvet, the clasps add realism to product renders.

CheckMate Lite status confirms the files open cleanly with correct geometry for each piece. Bright silver needs a sharp reflection environment, and small rounded forms like these reward careful studio lighting. Jump rings and spring mechanisms are the details that persuade a jeweler the piece could actually be worn.

This first set leads six versions, followed by silver collections numbered two through six. The numbered sets vary in size and price, so a buyer can add only the clasp styles a design calls for. Sold since 2023, the bundle suits jewelry visualization, e-commerce catalogs and CAD presentations for goldsmiths.
