About This Model

A themed collection inspired by the Breaking Bad series, gathering several recognizable models from its world in one purchase. Bundling them keeps materials, scale and naming consistent, which matters when the pieces share one desert or lab scene. Fans of the show know the yellow protective suits and the New Mexico desert, and those cues anchor any composition.

The collection meets CheckMate specification, so every item arrives at real size and fits beside the others without adjustment. Harsh midday sun and dusty ground tones suit the set, echoing the bleached palette the series is known for. A cluttered workbench under fluorescent light works just as well when the story moves indoors.

Out since 2025, the collection suits fan art, film analysis videos, tribute animations and crime drama previsualization. Reviewers discussing the series can stage a scene for a video essay without modeling anything themselves. Keep the camera low across the desert floor, and even a still frame carries the tension of the show.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
