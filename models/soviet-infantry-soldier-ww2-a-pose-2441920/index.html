About This Model

Soviet Infantry Soldier WW2 A-Pose is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $129. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.
