About This Model

Soviet Infantry Soldier WW2 A-Pose is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 34,273 polygons and 35,234 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
