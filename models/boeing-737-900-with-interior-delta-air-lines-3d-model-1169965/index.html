About This Model

Boeing 737-900 with Interior Delta Air Lines is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $349. Counted at the source, the model is 5,380,756 polygons and 5,578,308 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 32 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
