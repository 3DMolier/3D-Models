About This Model

A rigged set of telescopic boom lifts, the self-propelled platforms that raise workers high above construction sites and warehouses. Each lift has an articulated boom, a work basket and a wheeled chassis ready to be posed or animated. Their big tires and heavy chassis let them roll across uneven site ground without outriggers.

The collection carries CheckMate Pro certification, meaning clean topology, true scale and sensibly named parts for every machine. Because the booms are rigged, extending them for a facade cleaning shot or a steel erection sequence is a matter of moving controls. The baskets have safety rails at waist height, so a worker figure fits naturally inside.

This bundle has been available since 2017 at $249 and has been purchased by 10 buyers, many of them construction firms and safety trainers. It suits building site animations, equipment rental presentations and industrial game levels. Several lifts at different heights along a facade make a busy, believable job site.
