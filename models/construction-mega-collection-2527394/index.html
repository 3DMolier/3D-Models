About This Model

Construction Mega Collection bundles a wide range of site equipment and building assets into a single purchase for large job site scenes. Excavators, cranes, trucks and site props share one visual standard, so they sit together without mismatched detail levels. A buyer staging a whole building project avoids hunting down dozens of separate files.

Topology, naming and scale stay consistent across the bundle because every asset was made to one CheckMate standard. Consistent real-world scale matters most here, because machines and site props need to stand next to each other believably.

New to the catalog in 2026, the bundle is priced at $1599 and may be used commercially in client work. It suits architectural visualization of construction phases, infrastructure presentations, simulation software and game levels set on building sites. Dusty ground textures and low morning sun turn a scene built from these assets into a convincing working site.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026
