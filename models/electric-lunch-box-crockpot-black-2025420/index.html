About This Model

This electric lunch box is a small portable crockpot that plugs in at the office and warms food in its sealed container. The 48,748 polygons shape the locking clips, the handle and the removable inner dish with its tight-fitting lid. It sits naturally on a desk, a break room table or a car seat.

A black matte body with a lighter inner tray gives clear separation between shell and food container. A short power cord coiled beside it tells viewers the box is meant to be plugged in. CheckMate Pro certification and 25,033 vertices of clean geometry let you recolor it quickly.

A blue variant called Portable Food Warmer Crockpot Blue Blue is the second version alongside the black one. Sold since 2023, the warmer suits office kitchen scenes, meal prep content, lifestyle product renders and commuter stories. Fill the dish with a stew or rice model and steam will make the lunch look freshly heated.
