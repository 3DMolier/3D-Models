About This Model

This seated elderly woman rests in a relaxed pose, wearing everyday casual clothes, ready to place on a bench, sofa or waiting room chair. At only 10,606 polygons and 11,587 vertices, the figure stays light enough to repeat several times in a crowd or a park. The resolution goes into the face, hands and fabric folds that tell the viewer her age and posture.

Skin, hair and clothing use 17 texture maps at 4096 x 4096 and 3000 x 3000, which keeps close portrait framing believable. StemCell preparation means TurboSquid converted her into several formats with matching materials, so skin tones stay consistent between renderers. Her unwrap avoids overlaps, so retouching a sweater color or adding glasses on the texture causes no mirrored errors.

Dating from 2020, the figure costs $129 and is licensed Royalty Free for commercial and personal projects. Architects seat her in hospital lobbies, cafes and public parks, where an older visitor makes a proposal feel lived in. Healthcare explainers and elder care presentations use her to show patients in calm, everyday settings at home or in a clinic.
