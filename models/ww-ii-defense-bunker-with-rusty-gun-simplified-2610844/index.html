About This Model

A concrete defense bunker from the Second World War with a rusty gun in its firing slit, built for coastal and battlefield scenes. The WW II Defense Bunker with Rusty Gun Simplified uses 299,517 polygons for the weathered concrete shell and the corroded weapon.

Its 298,474 vertices give the thick walls rounded, battered edges that show decades of exposure. The bunker is CheckMate certified, so the structure arrives with clean geometry and believable military scale. A narrow embrasure frames the barrel, showing how defenders covered beaches and roads from protected positions.

The bunker costs $59 as a 2026 release and suits war games, history documentaries and coastal ruin scenes. Set it into a dune above a beach, or half-bury it in a forest overgrown with moss. Simplified modeling keeps it suitable for open-world games where several fortifications appear at once. History teachers covering the Atlantic Wall can use it to show what soldiers saw from inside.
