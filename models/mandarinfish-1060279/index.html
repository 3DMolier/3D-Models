About This Model

Mandarinfish belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $49 through the TurboSquid marketplace. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 3,364 polygons and 3,304 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 4 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
