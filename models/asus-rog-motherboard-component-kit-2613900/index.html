About This Model

A gaming motherboard surrounded by its loose components, laid out for PC building tutorials and hardware close-ups. The ASUS ROG Motherboard Component Kit spends 388,484 polygons and 423,920 vertices on sockets, heatsinks, slots, connectors and tiny surface parts. Every header and capacitor is modeled, so macro shots of the board survive scrutiny.

The kit passes TurboSquid CheckMate review, keeping the many small objects separated and named. Angular heatsink covers and the reinforced expansion slots give the board its aggressive gaming look. Parts can be lifted out one at a time, which makes step-by-step assembly animations easy to stage.

Five versions are available, from the main kit at $59 to the ASUS ROG Strix Z370 Motherboard Parts Kit at $79. The G TECH Foxconn Motherboard Connectors Kit is also $79, and the larger builder kits cost $129 each. Released in 2026 under Editorial Uses Only because real brands appear, they suit tech reviews, assembly guides and hardware education.
