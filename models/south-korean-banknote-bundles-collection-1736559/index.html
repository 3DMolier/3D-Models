About This Model

A collection of South Korean won banknote bundles, stacked and banded, ready to fill a cash desk, a vault shelf or a briefcase. Each bundle stands alone, so stacks can be built higher, scattered across a table or packed into a bag.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms real banknote proportions and clean, consistent geometry across every bundle. Paper bands around the middle and slightly uneven edges keep the stacks from looking like solid bricks. Under a desk lamp, the printed faces and colored bands become the most readable part of the shot.

Two versions are offered: South Korean Banknote Bundles Collection plus a follow-up set with more bundle arrangements. Each set is $49, and the money was first offered in 2021. Heist films, banking scenes, Korean drama sets and crime game levels are where these stacks most often land. A briefcase lined with bundles is one of the fastest ways to tell a story about money.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
