About This Model

This Electronics & Gadgets model - Laptop with Stickers - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $69 on TurboSquid. You are getting 9,175 polygons and 10,020 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 35.7 x 25.31 x H25.15 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
