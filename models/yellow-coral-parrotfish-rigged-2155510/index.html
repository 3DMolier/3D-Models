About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Coral Parrotfish is a finished, render-ready model at $39. The mesh carries 11,112 polygons and 5,862 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
