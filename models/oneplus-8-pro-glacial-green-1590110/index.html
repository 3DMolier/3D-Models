About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The OnePlus 8 Pro is a finished, render-ready model at $24. Geometry weighs in at 8,704 polygons over 9,213 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 7 maps at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
