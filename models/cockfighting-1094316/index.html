About This Model

A pair of roosters squaring off in a fight, wings spread, hackles raised and feet ready to strike. The 20,661 polygons capture the arched necks, sharp spurs and fanned tail feathers, so the clash reads as tense and dynamic. Their bodies lean in opposite directions, which creates a strong diagonal composition.

The texture set holds 30 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 922 x 1763 for vivid plumage, combs and eyes. The UV layout is mixed, and the birds carry CheckMate Pro certification for clean topology and natural size. Glossy green tail feathers and red combs glow under warm sunlight, which heightens the drama of the fight.

Offered since 2016, the scene is used by illustrators, animal behavior educators and historical documentary teams. Set it in a village courtyard, use it in a folk tale animation, or place it in a museum display about rural traditions. It also suits poster art where the birds stand for rivalry and courage.
