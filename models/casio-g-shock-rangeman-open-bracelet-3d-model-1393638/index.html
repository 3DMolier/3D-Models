About This Model

Casio G-Shock Rangeman Open Bracelet is a detailed Clothing & Accessories asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Geometry weighs in at 52,586 polygons over 53,640 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 14 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
