About This Model

The Orlan-10 is a catapult-launched reconnaissance drone, small enough for two people to carry and plain enough that its shape is all function: a slab-sided fuselage, a high wing, a pusher propeller and a sensor turret slung underneath. The model keeps the antennae, the launch rail lugs and the parachute hatch that make it recognisable rather than a generic fixed-wing drone.

At 25,286 polygons and 26,293 vertices with non-overlapping UVs it is light enough for a swarm and detailed enough for a hero shot, and the StemCell build means it works in a real-time engine without a rebuild.

A rigged version sits on this page at $129 if the propeller or the control surfaces have to turn. The static build at $99 covers news graphics, defence briefings, documentary work and games where the aircraft is seen but not flown.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published April 2023·Updated 20 August 2026
