About This Model

This set gathers classic metal paper clips in several shapes and sizes, the small office detail that makes a desk believable. Each clip follows the familiar double loop of bent wire, with the rounded ends that hold sheets of paper together. Scattered across a desk, clipped to documents or piled in a dish, they add small honest clutter to office scenes.

TurboSquid has given the collection its CheckMate Pro status, which means the files were reviewed for clean modeling and scale. The bright steel finish works well under studio light, and its metallic material can be tinted for colored coated clips. Because each clip is so small, they render quickly even when dozens are spread across a scene.

Two larger versions of the collection exist on the page, priced at $19 and $21, while this one costs $14. Since 2022 the clips have served stationery store renders, office animations and study desk illustrations. Clip a few to a stack of printed reports beside a laptop, and the workday scene feels complete.
