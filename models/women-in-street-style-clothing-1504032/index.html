About This Model

Women in Street Style Clothing is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $199. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 8,986 polygons and 9,680 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 16 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
