About This Model

A row of three economy class airline seats joined on one frame, with tray tables, armrests and seat-back pockets. The 55,365 polygons cover the contoured cushions, the folding trays and the metal legs bolted to the floor rails. With 57,092 vertices the unit stays light, so dozens of rows can fill a cabin without straining the scene.

A single texture at 4096 x 4096 carries the fabric pattern, the plastic shells and the seat belt buckles. Because every part shares one atlas without overlaps, changing the upholstery color for a different airline takes minutes. It is a CheckMate Pro certified model, so the geometry and naming were reviewed before release.

Offered at $39 since 2017, the triple seat has been booked by 25 customers so far. Airline cabin interiors, flight training videos, travel commercials and passenger safety animations are its usual destinations. Repeating the row along an aisle builds a believable economy cabin within a few minutes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2017 · Updated 14 November 2026
