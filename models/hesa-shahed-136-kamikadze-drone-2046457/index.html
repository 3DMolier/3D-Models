About This Model

This loitering munition is a delta wing with a pusher propeller and no landing gear at all, because it is not meant to come back. It launches from a rail in batches, cruises on a small engine and flies into its target. The simplicity is deliberate: the airframe exists to be cheap enough to use in numbers.

The mesh is 250,486 polygons and 127,145 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the wing planform, the engine mount and the launch rail fittings.

A rigged build sits on this page at the same $119. Both suit defence and geopolitical visualization, news and documentary graphics, strategic analysis material, training and games. Built to StemCell specification, on sale since 2023.
