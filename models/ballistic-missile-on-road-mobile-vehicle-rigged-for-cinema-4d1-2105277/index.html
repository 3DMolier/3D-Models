About This Model

A ballistic missile on a road-mobile launcher, rigged in Cinema 4D so the vehicle can drive and the launch tube can raise. The heavy multi-axle truck carries the long missile canister along its back, a silhouette strongly associated with strategic forces. Outriggers, cab details and the erector arm are shaped clearly enough to read in wide aerial or ground shots.

The Cinema 4D rig is the key feature, letting animators stage the move from transport to launch position. Matching CheckMate specification, the parts are organized and named, so the canister, wheels and supports can be animated separately. Olive or camouflage paint and dusty tires look right on remote roads, forests and staging areas.

First released in 2023, it suits defense simulations, documentaries on nuclear deterrence and military thrillers. War games and news graphics also use such launchers to illustrate tension and strategic capability. Seen raising its missile against a gray sky, the vehicle delivers a powerful moment of suspense.
