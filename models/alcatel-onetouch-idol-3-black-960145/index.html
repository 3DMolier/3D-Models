About This Model

The Alcatel OneTouch Idol 3 is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $34 on TurboSquid. You are getting 7,146 polygons and 7,638 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

11 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
