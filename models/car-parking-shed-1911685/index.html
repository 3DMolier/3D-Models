About This Model

Parking Shed belongs to our Architecture & Landmarks range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 34,412 polygons and 17,180 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
