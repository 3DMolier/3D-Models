About This Model

This offshore drillship carries a tall derrick amidships, a helideck over the bow and deck cranes for supply work at sea. With 1,354,629 polygons and 1,529,925 vertices of subdivision geometry, the pipe racks, lifeboats and lattice tower hold up in close flyovers. Seen from a helicopter angle, the long flat deck and central tower read instantly as the deepwater energy industry.

No texture maps come with it, so the hull paint, rust and safety yellow are driven by adjustable materials. The UV layout is free of overlaps, leaving space to bake an operator name, deck markings or salt staining later. Its CheckMate Pro certificate confirms real scale, which keeps supply vessels and helicopters believable when they approach the hull.

Four versions sit under one page, three full vessels at $229 and a rescue boat davit crane at $59. The West Vela edition matches the main hull in density, the generic offshore ship is about 1% heavier, and the crane alone is 95% lighter. Available since 2020, it serves energy documentaries, offshore engineering presentations and maritime training simulators.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2020 · Updated 18 November 2026
