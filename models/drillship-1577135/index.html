Drillship belongs to our Ships & Boats range and is offered at $229 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 1,354,629 polygons over 1,529,925 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
