About This Model

A floating iceberg with a baby penguin perched on top, a small polar scene packed as one ready set. The fluffy chick contrasts with the hard, faceted ice, which is what makes the composition charming at a glance. Iceberg forms have sharp breaks and smooth melt surfaces, and both are present here for believable lighting.

Construction respects CheckMate specification, putting detail where the camera finds it and adding nothing extra beyond that. The outliner holds no stray helpers, so the chick can be moved, duplicated or removed in seconds. Because both pieces come together, the penguin already sits at the right scale on the ice.

Released in 2025, the iceberg set suits children's animation, climate change education and polar game levels. Royalty free terms open the scene to greeting cards, toy packaging, apps and nature documentaries. Surround it with dark water and a low sun and the scene becomes a quiet Antarctic postcard.
