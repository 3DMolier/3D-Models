About This Model

The Bonsai Silhouette is a production-ready general 3D model, priced at $12 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 4,246 polygons over 4,220 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
