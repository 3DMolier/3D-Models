About This Model

Container Ship Generic is a detailed Ships asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $199. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbour environments in games, and marine simulation.

The mesh carries 5,769,509 polygons and 6,021,147 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 43 of them, peaking at 8192x8192, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
