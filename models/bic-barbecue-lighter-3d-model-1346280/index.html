About This Model

BIC Barbecue Lighter is one of the Kitchen & Tableware models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and game assets. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

The mesh carries 5,608 polygons and 5,784 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 3 maps, the largest at 2048x2048, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
