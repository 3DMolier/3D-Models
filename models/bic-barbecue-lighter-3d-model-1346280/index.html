About This Model

BIC Barbecue Lighter is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. You are getting 5,608 polygons and 5,784 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

3 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and game assets. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
