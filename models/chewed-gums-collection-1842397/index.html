About This Model

Chewed Gums Collection gathers several wads of used chewing gum, each squashed and stretched into its own lumpy, irregular shape. Some pieces look freshly spat out and round, while others are pressed flat as if a shoe stepped on them. Scattered on a sidewalk or stuck under a school desk, they add instant grime to an otherwise clean scene.

The set carries the CheckMate Pro mark, which promises sensible scale and a file that loads without broken references. Because each wad is a separate object, you can rotate, recolor or hide any single piece without touching the rest. Soft pastel pinks and faded grays make the gum read as old rather than candy straight from the wrapper.

Available since 2022, the bundle is picked for urban decay shots, crime scene close ups and gross out comedy gags. A few wads under a cafe table or on a bus seat tell a story about the place in seconds. Priced at $24 the pack saves sculpting dozens of tiny blobs by hand for a single forensic macro frame.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
