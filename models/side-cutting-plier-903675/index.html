About This Model

A pair of diagonal side cutters with angled jaws ground to a sharp edge and cushioned handles shaped for a firm grip. Electricians use them to snip wire, which is why the tool looks at home next to cable spools and junction boxes. The short, beaky head and the box joint at the pivot are the details that separate cutters from ordinary pliers.

The CheckMate Pro mark from TurboSquid covers topology, scale and the way the file imports into common packages. Handle grips and steel jaws can take separate materials, so the cutters can match any tool brand palette.

The Side Cutting Plier has been on TurboSquid since 2015, costs $19 and has gone to 12 customers. It is used in electrical training material, hardware catalogs, DIY repair videos and workbench scenes in games. Laid across a stripped cable with a few copper offcuts, the cutters tell the story of a repair in progress.
