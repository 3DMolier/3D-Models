About This Model

The Dark Skinned Nurse is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. You are getting 10,739 polygons and 11,639 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
