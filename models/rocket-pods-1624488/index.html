About This Model

Looking for a Weapons asset? The Rocket Pods is a finished, render-ready model at $39. You are getting 13,024 polygons and 13,733 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
