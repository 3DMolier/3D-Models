About This Model

This Signage & Decor model - Black Cardboard Gift Box - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. You are getting 360 polygons and 358 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2021 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
