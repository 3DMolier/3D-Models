About This Model

Foil-wrapped chocolate coins gathered into one set, the small festive treat that scatters across holiday tables and spills out of gift bags. Embossed faces and crinkled foil edges catch highlights well, so the coins still read as candy when the camera stays at a distance. Piling them by hand would take hours, and the collection removes that chore before a seasonal shot.

Each piece is built to CheckMate standards, so geometry opens cleanly and accepts new materials in any common renderer. Shifting the wrapper tint from gold to silver or copper needs only a shader tweak and leaves the mesh untouched. That flexibility helps when a scene calls for a mixed pile instead of a uniform glittering heap.

The Chocolate Coins Collection went on sale in 2023 with a Royalty Free license. Artists drop it into Christmas and Hanukkah tabletop scenes, candy shop shelves and game inventories full of edible treasure. Packaging teams sometimes place it as a stand-in while a real confectionery box is still being designed.
