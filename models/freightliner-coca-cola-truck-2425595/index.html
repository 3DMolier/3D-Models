About This Model

The Freightliner Coca-Cola Truck is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $169 on TurboSquid. You are getting 1,717,925 polygons and 1,743,575 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 20.1 x 2.89 x H4.11 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
