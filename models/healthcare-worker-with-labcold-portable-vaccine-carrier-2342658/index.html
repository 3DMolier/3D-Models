About This Model

Healthcare Worker with Labcold Portable Vaccine Carrier belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and medical visualization. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbour environments in games, and marine simulation.

Geometry weighs in at 93,753 polygons over 95,646 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
