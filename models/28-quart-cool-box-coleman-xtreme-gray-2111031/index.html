About This Model

A hard-sided picnic cooler with a hinged lid and molded side grips, made for camping, beach and tailgate scenes. The 28 Quart Cool Box Coleman Xtreme Gray holds 45,008 polygons and 22,885 vertices, which keeps the lid seam and the ribbed walls well defined. Its boxy shape sits steadily on sand or a truck bed and doubles as a seat.

A non-overlapping UV layout lets dirt, stickers and sun fading land exactly where they belong on the plastic shell. CheckMate Pro approval confirms clean topology and accurate dimensions, so food and bottles placed inside will fit plausibly. The gray body pairs well with bright towels or a campfire glow behind it.

Four versions are offered: this gray cool box, a blue portable cooler, a rigged blue Xtreme cooler and a rigged gray cool box. Rigged editions let the lid swing open in animation, and the whole set dates from 2023.
