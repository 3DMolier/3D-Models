About This Model

Sign of the Horns Emoticon is a detailed general asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Geometry weighs in at 569 polygons over 558 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 5 maps at 1024 x 1024. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
