About This Model

Priced at $89, the Agricultural Corn Field is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 82,193,058 polygons and 92,516,453 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The object measures 101.49 x 101.19 x H2.46 m, modelled at true scale. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
