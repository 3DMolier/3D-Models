About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Snake with Captured Snake is a finished, render-ready model at $119. You are getting 11,247 polygons and 11,419 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 137.82 x 145.86 x H86.92 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
