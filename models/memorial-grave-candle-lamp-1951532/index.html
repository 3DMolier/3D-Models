About This Model

The Memorial Grave Candle Lamp is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 18,696 polygons and 9,338 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
