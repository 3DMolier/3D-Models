About This Model

Priced at $149, the Bell 206B 3 JetRanger III Citycopter is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. On import you get 532,336 polygons and 585,583 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Textures number 30, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 so they survive a close crop. The geometry is built to be worked with: predictable loops, no doubled faces, nothing hidden inside the shell. Parts are separated where separation is useful, so a single component can be hidden, swapped or re-shaded on its own.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Its first release was in 2016, and it has earned its place in the catalog since then. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
