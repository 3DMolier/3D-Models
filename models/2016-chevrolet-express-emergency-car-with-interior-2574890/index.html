About This Model

A 2016 Chevrolet Express van fitted out as an emergency vehicle, with a full interior for rescue and ambulance scenes. The 288,203 polygons cover the high-roof body, light bars, side doors and the equipped rear compartment.

Its footprint of 249.18 x 642.11 x H248.51 cm keeps the van true beside stretchers, crews and hospital entrances. Construction to CheckMate specification keeps 322,237 vertices in named parts, so the doors and interior are easy to inspect. Licensing is Editorial Uses Only, because the van reproduces Chevrolet's real design and badges.

Released in 2026 at $149, it suits news illustration, emergency service training and documentary reconstructions. Park it at an accident scene with emergency lights flashing at night, and the urgency comes across immediately. Training providers also use interior views to show crews where equipment is stored inside such vehicles. Opening the rear doors reveals the interior, which is the shot most emergency-themed productions need.
