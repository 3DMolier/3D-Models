About This Model

A baby girl's first birthday party in one collection, with the little guest of honor and the decorations around her. Balloons, a small cake and soft pastel colors set the mood, so the scene reads as a celebration at first glance.

Each piece was made to CheckMate specification, which keeps scale consistent between the child, furniture and party props. Buying the party as a set avoids hunting separately for a baby figure and matching decorations. Individual props can be moved around freely, which lets the table layout match any room.

Available from 2025 at $159, the bundle suits greeting cards, family photo concepts and children's product visuals. Pose the girl beside the cake with warm window light, and the image feels like a family album page. Event planners and party supply shops also use scenes like this to preview decor themes. Pastel tones can be recolored easily, so the same party works for spring, gender reveal or holiday themes.
