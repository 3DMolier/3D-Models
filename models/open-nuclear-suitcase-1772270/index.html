About This Model

The briefcase carried near a head of state is one of the most photographed objects nobody has ever seen open. Shown open, it becomes a communications and authentication case: a panel of controls, a handset, indicator lamps and a locked compartment, all packed into an ordinary looking case.

The mesh runs 31,278 polygons and 31,571 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the lid hinge, the control panel and the case hardware.

At $39 it suits political and news illustration, documentary and current affairs graphics, thriller film and television set dressing, editorial imagery and games. On sale since 2021.
