About This Model

Silver Foil Snack Bag is one of the Containers & Storage models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, architectural visualization and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

The mesh carries 5,643 polygons and 5,632 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
