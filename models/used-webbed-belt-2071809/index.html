About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Used Webbed Belt is a finished, render-ready model at $9. You are getting 10,408 polygons and 5,206 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
