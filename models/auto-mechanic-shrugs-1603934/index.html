Auto Mechanic Shrugs belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
