About This Model

Boeing 747-8I British belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 215,499 polygons and 227,477 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 7 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 2048 x 2048. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
