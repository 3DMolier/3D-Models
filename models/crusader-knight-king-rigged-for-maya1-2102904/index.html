About This Model

A crusader knight king in chain mail, surcoat and crown, rigged in Maya for medieval battles and historical drama. The surcoat carries a bold cross over layered mail, while a crown marks him clearly as a ruler among knights. His stance is upright and commanding, the posture of a leader ready to address troops or ride into battle.

The Maya rig lets animators swing a sword, raise a shield or walk him through a throne room. CheckMate Pro certification guarantees clean topology at real-world scale, so he matches horses, castles and other soldiers. Metal rings, cloth and gold details respond differently to torchlight, adding depth to night camp scenes.

Released in 2023, the king suits medieval games, historical documentaries, fantasy films and museum reconstructions. Leading a column of crusaders or seated on a throne, he gives any medieval scene a clear focal point. Educators also use him to illustrate the crusades and feudal hierarchy in a vivid, accessible way.
