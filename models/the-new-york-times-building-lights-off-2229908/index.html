About This Model

This Architecture Landmarks model - The New York Times Building Lights Off - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $79 on TurboSquid. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 863,286 polygons and 886,044 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
