About This Model

An electric fly swatter held in a hand, ready for summer pest scenes, product pages and comedy shorts. The racket head holds a fine metal grid inside a round plastic frame, attached to a long handle with a button. Its 9,900 polygons include the hand itself, so the grip already looks natural in a close shot.

The swatter meets CheckMate guidance, with 10,620 vertices laid onto UV islands that stay apart. A small blue spark on the grid is all it takes to show the device in action. Bright yellow or green plastic reads clearly against a dark kitchen at night.

Electric Fly Swatter in Hand comes with a Foldable Electric Fly Swatter in Hand version, both at $49. First listed in 2025, the gadget suits mosquito control explainers, camping gear catalogs and household comedy sketches. Swung through the air beside a buzzing mosquito, it makes a funny, relatable moment.
