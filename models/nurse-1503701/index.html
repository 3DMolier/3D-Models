About This Model

Nurse belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $199 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

At 11,240 polygons and 12,106 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Maps: 15 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
