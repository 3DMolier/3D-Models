About This Model

Priced at $49, the Child Mannequin is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 3,958 polygons and 3,958 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 2 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

Questions About This Model

Can the Child Mannequin model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How much does the Child Mannequin 3D model cost?

$49 USD, paid once. The Royalty Free license means no recurring cost and no extra payment when the finished work ships.

Does the Child Mannequin model include materials and textures?

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. As a StemCell asset it ships with PBR materials that stay consistent across the delivered formats.

What is the Child Mannequin model typically used for?

It is cataloged for visualization, advertising and 3D printing - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.
