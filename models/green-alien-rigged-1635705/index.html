About This Model

Looking for an Animals & Creatures asset? The Green Alien Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $149. Counted at the source, the model is 4,730 polygons and 4,844 vertices. How that lands depends on your renderer and on how close the camera gets.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
