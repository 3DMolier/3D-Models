About This Model

A fingerprint scanner with a rigged hand pressing a finger to the sensor, built for biometric security scenes. Its 18,848 polygons cover the scanner housing, the glass reader, the cable port and the fingers of the hand. The 19,947 vertices keep the scene light, so it fits mobile apps and interactive security demos easily.

This scanner earned CheckMate Pro after TurboSquid examined its geometry and the setup of the hand. Non-overlapping UVs leave the housing open for a login screen glow, labels or a custom color scheme. A lit sensor under a fingertip is a simple, strong image that viewers link to identity checks at once.

Prepared for general use here, the scanner has a Cinema 4D version at the identical $89 for that package. Since 2022 the device has suited banking visuals, office access control, border security explainers and cybersecurity lessons. Animate the finger pressing down and the screen turning green, and the access process reads instantly.
