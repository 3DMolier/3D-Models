About This Model

Large Business Jet belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 249,642 polygons over 253,341 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
