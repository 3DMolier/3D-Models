About This Model

A pediatric dental typodont, the jaw model students drill and fill on, with removable teeth set in an overbite. Each tooth sits in its socket and can be lifted out, so orthodontic and restorative steps can be shown one at a time. The smaller child-sized arches set it apart from the adult training jaws found in most dental schools.

CheckMate Pro inspection confirmed the geometry, scale and naming, so the jaws open cleanly in any major package. Gums and enamel need different shaders, and a soft subsurface pink brings out the realism of the gingiva. The overbite is built into the bite, which makes the model useful for explaining malocclusion.

In the range since 2023, it serves dental school training, orthodontic patient education and clinic explainer animation. Parents understand treatment faster when they can see the teeth on a model rather than an X-ray. Dental software demos benefit too, presenting scanning and treatment planning on a child's mouth.
