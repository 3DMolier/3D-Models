About This Model

A freight train is not a locomotive, it is a consist. This build supplies both: the road unit in CSX colours plus the cars behind it, which is what lets a scene show a train rather than an engine parked on a rail. The ES44AC carries the flared radiator section at the rear that identifies the model, and the model keeps the walkways, handrails and truck castings.

It is a heavy asset at 3,111,707 polygons and 3,194,707 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, which is what a multi-vehicle consist costs when every unit is built to the same standard rather than the cars being simplified.

At $179 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits rail and logistics visualisation, industrial and infrastructure renders, simulator projects, film and television, and games. On sale since 2024.
