About This Model

The Transporter Trucks Collection gathers car carrier trucks with multi-level ramps for moving new vehicles between factories and dealers. Hydraulic decks, tie-down points and long trailers are modeled with mechanical detail visible from the roadside and from above. Rolling down a highway loaded with cars, the trucks show the logistics that bring new vehicles to every town.

Matching CheckMate rules, each truck keeps true length and clean geometry along its trailer and ramps. Painted cabs and steel decks take dirt, rust and road grime convincingly after long hauls. Midday highway light with heat haze emphasizes the length of these big rigs and their cargo.

Three versions are available for transport fans: the main collection joined by a couple of extra packs holding additional trucks. Combining them populates a port or a distribution yard with varied carriers and loads of new cars. Since its 2025 debut, the trucks suit automotive logistics visuals, traffic simulations and trucking games.
