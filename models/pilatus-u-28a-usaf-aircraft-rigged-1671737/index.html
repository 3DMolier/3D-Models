About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Pilatus U-28A USAF Aircraft Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $169. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, film production and simulation as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

At 114,427 polygons and 123,220 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
