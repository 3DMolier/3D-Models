About This Model

Honey Bee Box is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 24,874 polygons and 25,000 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 6 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
