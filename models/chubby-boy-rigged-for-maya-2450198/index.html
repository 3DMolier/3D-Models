About This Model

A chubby boy character prepared for Maya, with soft rounded proportions suited to family, school and health scenes. A round face, full cheeks and a heavier build give him a distinct silhouette among generic child figures. Realistic body diversity matters in children's content, and this figure brings it without a custom character job.

Topology was planned under CheckMate rules, so joints bend cleanly when an animator moves arms and legs. Casual clothing in friendly colors keeps the character approachable in classrooms and cartoons alike. Natural daylight suits him best, since soft shadows flatter round forms better than hard studio light.

Released in 2025, he works for pediatric health campaigns, school animation, nutrition explainers and family video games. Place him at a lunch table or a playground, and the scene feels like a real group of kids. Educators discussing healthy habits can use him with care, presenting the character as an ordinary kid, not a warning.
