About This Model

The concertina mat is the only sleeping pad that cannot be punctured, which is why it survives alongside inflatables that are lighter and more comfortable in every other respect. It also unfolds in about two seconds.

The model is very light at 7,192 polygons and 7,074 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in grey, and comes rigged so it can be posed folded or open. It is built to CheckMate specification, and native rigged builds for Cinema 4D and Maya are offered separately.

At $24 it suits camping and outdoor visualisation, e-commerce and retail imagery, character accessories, advertising, film props and game environments. On sale since 2025.
