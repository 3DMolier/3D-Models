About This Model

A round red angry emoji with furrowed brows and a clenched frown, ready for chat graphics, social posts and explainer videos. It is built from just 1,034 polygons and 1,034 vertices, a clean closed ball with no stray parts. That lightness lets many faces bounce around a motion graphic or a game menu without strain.

The 5 maps at 1024 x 1024 give the face its glossy red gradient and the dark features. Resolution is modest because the shapes are simple, and a flat cartoon look needs little more. Its separated UV islands and CheckMate Pro rating make recoloring for a brand palette quick and predictable.

Priced at $9 the emoji has picked up 24 sales since 2020, cheap enough for a throwaway gag. It pops up in reaction animations, customer feedback slides and kids' games that teach emotions. Paired with a smiling face in a split screen, it turns a simple comparison into an instant visual joke.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2020 · Updated 14 November 2026
