About This Model

Priced at $24, the Ring Doorbell is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 11,824 polygons and 12,129 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 8.58 x 4.1 x H17.51 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
