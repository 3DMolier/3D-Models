About This Model

A collection of child-sized crash test dummies with posable joints, made for car seat testing and vehicle safety visuals. Yellow and black calibration markers on the head and limbs are the detail that makes these figures read instantly. Having several dummies in one set lets a scene show different seating positions at once.

Built to CheckMate specification, the dummies share consistent proportions and fit into car seats from other kits. Hard studio light against a black backdrop matches the clinical look of real crash laboratories. Slow-motion style renders benefit from the jointed bodies, since the pose shows how forces move a small frame.

Coming out in 2026, the dummies serve car seat manufacturers, road safety campaigns, insurance explainers and engineering courses. Seat the dummies in a rear-facing child seat, and parents understand the safety message at a glance. Automotive journalists also use such figures when explaining crash ratings without showing real children in a test.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026
