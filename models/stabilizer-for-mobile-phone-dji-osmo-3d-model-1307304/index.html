About This Model

Priced at $29, the Stabilizer for Mobile Phone Dji Osmo is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 19,267 polygons and 19,542 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
