About This Model

A collection of Russian aerial bombs in several types, with finned tails and painted bodies for military aviation scenes. Each bomb has the streamlined body, suspension lugs and tail fins that let it hang believably under a wing pylon. Different lengths and diameters let you arm an aircraft or fill an ammunition depot without repeating one shape.

StemCell quality checks cover every bundled format, so the bombs open cleanly whichever package your studio prefers. Matte military green paint with worn edges suits both museum displays and gritty battlefield shots. Stacked on transport carts or lined up on a hangar floor, the ordnance reads clearly in wide shots.

The Russian Air Bombs Collection is a 2026 addition with a royalty free license. Military simulators, flight games, defense documentaries and aviation museum renders are the natural settings for this ordnance. Hanging them under a fighter's wings adds weight and realism to an otherwise clean aircraft model.
