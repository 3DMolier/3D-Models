About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Navy Helicopter Sikorsky H-5 Green is a finished, render-ready model at $79. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 169,708 polygons over 175,566 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
