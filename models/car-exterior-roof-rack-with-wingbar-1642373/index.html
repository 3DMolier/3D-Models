About This Model

Aerofoil crossbars exist because the old square ones made a noise at motorway speed that drove owners to remove the rack entirely. Solving the whistle was worth more commercially than any improvement in load capacity.

The model is built at subdivision level, 84,124 polygons and 85,288 vertices with non-overlapping UVs, so it holds up close. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $39 it suits automotive accessory visualisation, aftermarket and e-commerce imagery, vehicle configurators, outdoor and travel scenes, game assets and editorial illustration. On sale since 2021.
