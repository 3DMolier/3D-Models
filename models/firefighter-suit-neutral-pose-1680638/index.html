About This Model

Firefighter Suit Neutral Pose is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. Counted at the source, the model is 105,832 polygons and 110,162 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
