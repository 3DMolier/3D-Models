About This Model

Suborbital Spaceplane SpaceShipTwo is a detailed Space & Sci-Fi asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
