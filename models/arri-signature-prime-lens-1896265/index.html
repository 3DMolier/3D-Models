About This Model

ARRI Signature Prime Lens is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 58,110 polygons and 32,080 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
