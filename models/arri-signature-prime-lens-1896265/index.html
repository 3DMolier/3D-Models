About This Model

A cinema prime is a heavy cylinder covered in scales. The focus and iris rings carry marked gear teeth for follow focus units, the front is a large diameter thread for filters, and the mount at the back is a locking bayonet rather than a screw. Those rings and the marks on them are what identify it as cinema rather than stills glass.

The mesh is efficient at 58,110 polygons and 32,080 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the gear rings, the barrel steps and the mount.

At $29 it suits camera and cinema equipment visualisation, rental house and manufacturer catalogues, film production set dressing, technical marketing and games. On sale since 2022.
