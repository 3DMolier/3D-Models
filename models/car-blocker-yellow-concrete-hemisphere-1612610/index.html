About This Model

Priced at $19, the Car Blocker Yellow Concrete Hemisphere is a finished Architecture Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and game environments. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.
