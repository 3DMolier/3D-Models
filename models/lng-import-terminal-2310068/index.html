About This Model

LNG Import Terminal is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. Geometry weighs in at 508,096 polygons over 618,705 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
