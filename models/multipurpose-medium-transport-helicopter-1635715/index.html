About This Model

Priced at $169, the Multipurpose Medium Transport Helicopter is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 247,089 polygons and 258,076 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer.
