About This Model

Skiers carve down the slope in this collection, animated and rigged for Maya with turns, tucks and poles planted in rhythm. Bright jackets, helmets and goggles make the figures pop against white snow, even when they are small on screen. Long skis extend the silhouette, so the athletes read clearly from a chairlift or a drone angle.

The characters comply with CheckMate requirements, so cloth and body meshes stay clean during fast motion. Maya scenes open with the animation already in place, saving hours of keyframing on repeated descents. Sparkling snow and a low winter sun give the runs a crisp, high-altitude feel.

Royalty free since 2026, the skiers fit winter resort visualization, alpine sports games and travel films. Ski resorts planning new runs can populate their slopes to show how busy a weekend might look. Add a little powder spray behind each turn and the motion suddenly gains real energy and speed.
