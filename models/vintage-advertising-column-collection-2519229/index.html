About This Model

Round street columns covered in posters, the kind that once stood on European boulevards to announce concerts, films and theater nights. The Vintage Advertising Column Collection brings several of these pillars together, with domed caps, ribbed bases and layered paper bills wrapped around the drums.

Each column was built to CheckMate specification, so scale, naming and file structure match across the set. The pieces therefore stand evenly on one street without any manual adjustment. The posters wrap the curve rather than floating flat, which keeps the edges believable when the camera circles around them.

Released in 2026, the set serves period film sets, historical city reconstructions and story-driven games. These columns place a scene in a specific decade the moment they appear on screen. A single pillar on a corner instantly tells the viewer they are looking at an old European city street. Swap the poster textures to move the same column from one era to another.
