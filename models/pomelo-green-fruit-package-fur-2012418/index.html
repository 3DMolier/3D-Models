About This Model

A green pomelo wrapped in its retail packaging, the giant citrus often sold in netting or film at Asian markets. The fruit has the slightly pear-shaped body and thick, dimpled rind that sets pomelo apart from grapefruit. Fur is used for fine strand detail, giving the surface a soft, fibrous quality up close.

The model holds CheckMate Pro certification, TurboSquid's highest grade for technical quality. A waxy green shader with subtle yellow patches and fine pores makes the rind look fresh, heavy and ripe. The packaging reads best with a slightly glossy, translucent material catching soft highlights from a window.

The pomelo has been on offer in our food and beverages range since 2023. Supermarket renders, Lunar New Year scenes, fruit packaging designs and Asian grocery visuals use pomelos as a seasonal centerpiece. Arranged in a wooden crate with others and a few citrus leaves, it gives a market stall a generous, colorful look.
