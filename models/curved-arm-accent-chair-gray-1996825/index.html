About This Model

A gray upholstered accent chair with sweeping curved arms and tapered legs, suited to living rooms, hotel lobbies and reading corners. The mesh uses 13,474 polygons on 6,879 vertices, spending them on the rolled arm profile, seat cushion and soft piping. Its rounded shape softens a room full of straight cabinets, which is why designers often place one beside a window.

UVs have no overlaps, so you can trade the gray fabric for velvet, linen or boucle without remapping. Its CheckMate Pro rating reflects quad topology that subdivides into smooth upholstery curves. The compact footprint fits small apartments, and two facing chairs make an easy conversation area.

Each of the three versions costs $29: this gray chair, a turquoise armchair with curved arms and a pink Coaster accent chair. Mixing the colors in one lounge gives a playful look while keeping a consistent silhouette. Released in 2022, the chair is chosen for interior visualization, furniture configurators and residential walkthroughs.
