About This Model

M142 HIMARS Multiple Rocket Launcher Desert is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $169. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
