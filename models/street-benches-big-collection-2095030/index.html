About This Model

A large bundle of street benches in different styles, enough to furnish parks, plazas and bus stops without repeating the same seat. Slatted backs, sturdy legs and armrests vary from model to model, so a promenade looks designed rather than copied.

Every bench conforms to the CheckMate spec, so scale and pivots match and each seat lands flush on a sidewalk. Separate objects inside the set can be pulled out individually, which keeps scene files light when you need only two styles. Materials split wood from metal, so a quick recolor turns a rustic park seat into a modern civic piece.

Available since 2023, the collection serves urban planning visuals, landscape architecture boards and open-world game streets. Benches are among the first things a viewer notices missing from a public space, and they sell scale immediately. Place one beside a lamp post and a bin, and an empty plaza suddenly reads as a real place.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
