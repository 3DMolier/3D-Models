About This Model

UFC Hall of Fame Trophy is a detailed Signage & Decor asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $15. Counted at the source, the model is 1,956 polygons and 986 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.
