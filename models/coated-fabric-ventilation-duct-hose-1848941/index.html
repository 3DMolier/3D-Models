About This Model

Coated Fabric is a detailed Architecture & Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Geometry weighs in at 5,204 polygons over 5,914 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags architectural visualization, technical visualization and software demos as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
