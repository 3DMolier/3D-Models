About This Model

Young coconut palms sprouting from their husks, packed upright in a brown cardboard box ready for a nursery or garden center. The 18,440 polygons and 18,320 vertices shape the fibrous nut shells, the folded box flaps and the first arching fronds. Each seedling leans at a slightly different angle, which keeps the group from looking copied and pasted.

Repeated nuts share overlapping UVs, a modest trick that keeps texture memory low while leaves stay crisp. Corrugated edges on the box give the cardboard a believable thickness when the camera looks down from above. The scene sticks to CheckMate rules, so box and plants come apart easily for rearranging on a shelf.

Released in 2026, the box fits tropical nursery scenes, agriculture explainers and eco-friendly packaging stories. Several boxes stacked on a pallet suggest a plantation shipment, while a single one works on a porch step. The fresh green fronds pop against the dull brown of the husks and cardboard.
