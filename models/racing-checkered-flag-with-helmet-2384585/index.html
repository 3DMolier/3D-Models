About This Model

The chequered flag has meant one thing for over a century and nobody is quite sure why that pattern. Paired with a helmet it becomes the standard visual for motorsport in general, used anywhere the subject is competition rather than a specific race.

The model is light at 21,240 polygons and 22,227 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplying the flag together with the helmet. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $59 it suits motorsport and event visualization, broadcast and sports graphics, advertising, signage and decor work, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
