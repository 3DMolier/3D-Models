About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The ASUS Zenbook 17 is a finished, render-ready model at $49. The mesh carries 26,620 polygons and 14,111 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.
