About This Model

A collection of shopping baskets for supermarket and retail scenes, covering the hand-carried kind shoppers grab at the entrance. Having several baskets in one set lets aisles, checkout lanes and stacks by the door look varied instead of cloned.

Plastic mesh walls and folding handles are the details that read in a store, so they deserve crisp textures and good light. Each model carries CheckMate Pro certification, which means tidy geometry at the size of real baskets. Seen from above, the open top shows whatever sits inside, which helps when a basket carries hero products.

Released in 2018, the collection fits grocery store visualization, retail design proposals and shopping scenes in games. Stack empty baskets near the entrance, then fill one with groceries in a character's hand to show a shopper mid-trip. Packaging and product renders also use a basket as a friendly frame for items on sale. Bright colors such as red, blue and green let a store brand its baskets with a simple material swap.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2018 · Updated 12 November 2026
