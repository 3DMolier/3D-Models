About This Model

Baby Projector Mobile is a detailed Clothing & Accessories asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 15,700 polygons and 15,961 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
