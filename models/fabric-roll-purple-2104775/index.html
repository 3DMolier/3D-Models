About This Model

A rolled bolt of purple fabric, the kind of stock stacked on shelves in tailor shops and textile stores. The 9,356 polygons and 4,679 vertices give the roll its layered edge and a loose flap of cloth at the end. Its rich color makes it a natural accent in a sewing room or a draper's display window.

No maps come with the roll, so velvet, satin or linen can be set up with any shader you prefer. Neat UVs follow the wrap of the cloth, which keeps woven patterns running straight along the roll. CheckMate Pro screening confirmed tidy geometry and a believable size for a retail fabric bolt.

Fabric Roll Purple was put online in 2023 for fashion studios, upholstery shops and craft tutorials. Stack it with rolls in other colors on a wall shelf to build a full fabric store in one pass. Interior design moodboards also use the purple roll to suggest curtain or cushion materials.
