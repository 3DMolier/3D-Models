About This Model

The Embraer Legacy 650E Private Jet is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 177,488 polygons and 189,946 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

28 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
