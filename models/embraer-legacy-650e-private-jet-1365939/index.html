About This Model

The Legacy 650E comes from an airliner airframe, and it shows in the best possible way for a business jet: a stand-up cabin, three distinct zones and a baggage hold you can walk into. Externally it carries the tall T-tail, the rear-mounted engines and the winglets, and the model keeps the airstair door and the belly fairing that give the aircraft its stance on the ramp.

It is built from ngons only at 177,488 polygons and 189,946 vertices with mixed UV layout, and ships with 28 texture maps - 10 at 8192x8192 and 18 at 4096x4096. Ten maps at the largest size is a serious budget, and it is what carries a gloss corporate finish over a long fuselage without banding.

A rigged build sits on this page at $249. The static model at $199 covers business aviation marketing, charter and brokerage presentation, luxury travel advertising, airport visualization and film work. On sale since 2019.
