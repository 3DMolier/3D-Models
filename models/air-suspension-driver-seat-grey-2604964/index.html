About This Model

A heavy-duty driver seat on an air suspension base, the kind found in truck cabs, city buses and farm tractors. It uses 9,625 polygons and 9,757 vertices, enough for the bellows, adjustment levers and grey upholstery panels. The lower mechanism is visible, which is exactly what separates it from an ordinary car seat.

StemCell certification means the PBR setup reads the same across different engines and renderers. Thick bolsters and a tall backrest with an integrated headrest reflect long hours spent behind the wheel. Neutral grey fabric suits a commercial cab as well as the operator station of an industrial machine.

Added in 2026, the Air Suspension Driver Seat Grey costs $39 and comes with Royalty Free rights. Truck interiors, bus driver stations, agricultural equipment and driving simulators are the natural fits. Its low count also suits configurators that show seating options for fleet vehicles and heavy machinery.
