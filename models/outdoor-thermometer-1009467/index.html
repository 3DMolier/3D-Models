About This Model

Outdoor Thermometer is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Geometry weighs in at 6,500 polygons over 6,510 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 and 1600 x 1600 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
