About This Model

This Signage & Decor model — Light Sign Boards Collection — is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $11 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
