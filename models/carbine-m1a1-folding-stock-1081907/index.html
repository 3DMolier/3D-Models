About This Model

This Weapons model - Carbine M1A1 Folding Stock - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. You are getting 28,727 polygons and 28,891 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

13 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
