About This Model

Carbine M1A1 Folding Stock is a detailed Weapons asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $59. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 28,727 polygons and 28,891 vertices, a light load for a scene. Maps: 13 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
