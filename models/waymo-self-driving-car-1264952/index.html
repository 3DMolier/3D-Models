About This Model

Waymo Self Driving Car belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game environments. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

The mesh carries 73,899 polygons and 73,959 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. Maps: 49 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
