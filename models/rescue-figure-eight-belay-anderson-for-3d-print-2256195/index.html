About This Model

A rescue figure eight descender with wide ears, the friction device rope teams use to lower and belay climbers. Its 3,944 polygons trace the large and small rings plus the flared ears that prevent the rope from girth-hitching. The 1,968 vertices come to half the faces, a quad mesh that prints with smooth curves after subdivision.

Prepared for 3D printing, the solid closed body can go straight to a slicer as a display or training replica. Modeled against CheckMate rules, the device is named and scaled correctly, with non-overlapping UVs for anodized color. Rendered in bright anodized red or gold, the eight reads instantly as climbing hardware even in small images.

A 2024 release, it suits rope rescue manuals, climbing gym renders and outdoor safety animations for teams. Thread a virtual rope through the rings to demonstrate a lowering system step by step for trainees. Printed replicas make handy teaching aids, since instructors can pass them around without exposing real gear to wear.
