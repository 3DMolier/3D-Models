About This Model

Priced at $19, the Rock Climbing Rope Blue is a finished Sports & Recreation model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

At 19,712 polygons and 19,736 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It ships 3 maps with the top set authored at 4000x4000 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
