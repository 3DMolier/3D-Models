About This Model

Garlic Collection brings together whole bulbs, separated cloves and peeled pieces, the staple ingredients of almost every kitchen scene. Papery skins, pointed tips and the root base at the bottom of each bulb make the garlic read as real. Scattered on a cutting board beside a knife, a few cloves turn an empty countertop into a cooking moment.

CheckMate specification guided the garlic, so bulbs arrive at true size next to pans and plates. Separate objects let you place a single clove close to camera while whole bulbs sit further back. Warm side light catches the thin skins and makes the ivory cloves glow softly.

Garlic Collection 2 is a follow-up version offering more pieces for an equal $59. Since 2024 the garlic has been used in food photography renders, recipe videos, grocery store scenes and kitchen game levels. Heaped in a wooden bowl, the bulbs also fill a rustic pantry or a farmers' market stall.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
