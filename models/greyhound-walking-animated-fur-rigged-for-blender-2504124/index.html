About This Model

A sleek greyhound with groomed fur, the sighthound breed built for speed, ready for racing, pet and lifestyle scenes. The deep chest, narrow waist and long thin legs capture the aerodynamic body that makes greyhounds so fast. Calm and elegant at a walk, the dog fits living rooms as naturally as a racing track.

CheckMate-ready preparation means the dog imports at real size with clean naming and no repair work. Short fur shows every muscle, so side lighting that rakes across the body gives the best definition. Thin tails and folded rose ears are the small details that separate this breed from similar dogs.

Two versions serve different pipelines: the walking Greyhound Fur and a Greyhound Fur Rigged for Blender edition. Both are 2025 releases carrying Royalty Free rights for pet food packaging, apps and films. Adoption charities and dog sport clubs use it to show retired racers settling into family homes.
