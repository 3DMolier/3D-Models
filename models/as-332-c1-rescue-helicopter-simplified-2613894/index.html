About This Model

A Super Puma family rescue helicopter with high-visibility livery, simplified for exterior shots where the cabin stays closed. The AS 332 C1 Rescue Helicopter Simplified carries 790,427 polygons and 3,185,076 vertices, a heavy mesh focused on the rotor head, fuselage and sponsons. Dropping the interior lets the budget go to rivets, hatches and the sliding rescue door outside.

CheckMate certification keeps naming and hierarchy consistent across the whole airframe, from rotor hub to tail. Its high tail boom and wheeled undercarriage define the Puma silhouette in flight or on a helipad. Bright rescue colors make the aircraft read clearly against sea, snow or mountain backgrounds.

It appeared in 2026 at $149, and buyers may use it Royalty Free in commercial work. Rescue films, coast guard documentaries, flight simulators and emergency training content are the main homes for it. Hovering above waves or parked on a hospital rooftop, the helicopter carries a whole rescue story by itself.
