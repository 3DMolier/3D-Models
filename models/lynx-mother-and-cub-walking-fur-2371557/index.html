About This Model

Lynx Mother and Cub Walking Fur is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $229. You are getting 47,546 polygons and 48,501 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
