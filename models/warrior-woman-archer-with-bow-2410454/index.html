About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Warrior Woman Archer with Bow is a finished, render-ready model at $179. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
