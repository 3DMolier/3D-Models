Young Tomato Plants in the Garden is one of the Nature & Plants models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 36,572 polygons and 52,676 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
