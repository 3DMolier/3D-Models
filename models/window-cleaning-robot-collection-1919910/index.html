About This Model

Window cleaning robots stick to glass and crawl across it, which gives them a shape unlike any other domestic machine: flat, wide, with suction or magnetic housings underneath and a safety tether trailing behind. Because the category has several competing designs, a collection is more useful than one unit for anyone showing the product class rather than a single brand.

Supplying them as a set keeps scale and finish consistent, so several can appear in the same shot or catalog spread without visible mismatch.

At $40 for the collection it suits smart home and appliance marketing, consumer technology visualization, interior and facade renders, retail and e-commerce material, and games. On sale since 2022.
