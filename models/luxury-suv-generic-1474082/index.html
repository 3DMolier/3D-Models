About This Model

Luxury SUV Generic is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. You are getting 368,366 polygons and 378,564 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
