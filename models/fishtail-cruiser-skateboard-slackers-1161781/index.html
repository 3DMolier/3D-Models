About This Model

Looking for a Sports & Recreation asset? The Fishtail Skateboard is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 21,374 polygons and 21,944 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 19 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 4000 x 4000. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
