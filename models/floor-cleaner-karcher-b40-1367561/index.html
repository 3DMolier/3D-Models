About This Model

A walk-behind scrubber dryer built for supermarkets and warehouses, keeping wide hard floors spotless in commercial interior scenes. Its body stacks fresh and recovery water tanks over a brush deck, with a rubber squeegee trailing behind and controls on the handlebar. With 459,551 polygons and 467,724 vertices, the hoses, wheels and brush housing keep their form in close views.

Nine texture maps at 4096 x 4096 cover yellow and gray plastic, the squeegee blade and printed operating symbols. Separate UV shells let you paint scuffs and water marks exactly where a busy machine would collect them. The CheckMate Pro mark means the dense mesh is tidy, closed and grouped by logical parts.

Two versions cost $69 each: the branded scrubber and an Industrial Floor Cleaning Machine without logos. The branded version, available since 2019, is limited to editorial use such as product reviews and industry news.
