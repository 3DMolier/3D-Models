About This Model

A pregnant Asian woman sitting cross-legged in leggings and a fitted top, a calm pose for prenatal and wellness scenes. The figure uses 34,014 polygons, enough for the rounded belly, hands resting on knees and soft folds of clothing. Seated on the floor, she measures 73.92 x 54.91 x H80.21 cm, so she fits a yoga mat or living room rug.

Overlapping UVs across 34,785 vertices mean the left and right halves of the outfit reuse one texture layout. The build follows CheckMate guidelines, with consistent scale and simple topology that a lighting artist can work with directly. Soft window light from one side suits the pose, since it models the belly shape without harsh shadows.

Available since 2025, this figure fits prenatal yoga content, maternity clinic visuals, health apps and family-oriented interior scenes. Representation matters in healthcare imagery, and this figure helps Asian audiences see themselves in pregnancy guidance. Surround her with cushions and plants, and the image reads as a quiet moment rather than a medical diagram.
