About This Model

Priced at $239, the Sea Launch Platform Odyssey Rigged is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 720,470 polygons and 828,786 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 27 maps at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
