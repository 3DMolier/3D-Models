About This Model

A garment fastener with interlocking teeth, a slider and a pull tab, ready to add to jackets, bags or boots. Each tooth is modeled as its own piece, so the closure reads properly in macro shots of clothing.

The slider body and pull tab are separate parts, which makes it easy to pose the zipper half open. The model has passed CheckMate Pro, meaning clean topology that holds up when bent along a seam. The metal finish can be switched to brass, nickel or plastic with a simple material change.

Clothing designers wrap it around hoodies and duffel bags, while product artists use it for fabric close-ups. Arraying the teeth along a curve lets it follow pocket outlines or the shape of a boot shaft. Game artists also use it to add tactile detail to backpacks and armor straps. On the market since 2015, it has gathered 12 purchases from fashion, footwear and accessory studios.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2015 · Updated 12 November 2026
