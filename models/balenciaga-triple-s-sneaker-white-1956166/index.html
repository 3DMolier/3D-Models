About This Model

The chunky sneaker took an aesthetic that had been considered a mistake for two decades and sold it back at luxury prices. Stacked soles, deliberately oversized proportions and a silhouette designed to be recognized from across a street.

The model is light at 30,915 polygons and 32,339 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in white, which is enough for close product work. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $59 it suits footwear and fashion visualization, e-commerce and catalog imagery, advertising, character accessories and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
