About This Model

This beige pith helmet gives a colonial era soldier or explorer the domed silhouette that reads instantly in any period scene. The shell takes 13,299 polygons and 13,340 vertices, enough for a smooth crown, a rolled brim and the chin strap that hangs below. It measures 23.0 x 27.49 x H24.54 cm, so it sits on a head of ordinary size without rescaling.

StemCell preparation means the PBR materials carry over between renderers, so the khaki cloth keeps its tone after import. The UVs are laid flat without any overlap, which leaves room to add sweat stains, sun fading or stitching along the brim. A helmet like this works on a hat stand or a campaign table as well as on a character.

Two versions come together on the page, the clean helmet and a used British variant with the same beige brim and strap. Pick the worn one when the scene needs a veteran, and the fresh one for a parade or a museum case. The helmet has been on sale since 2025 at $29 and already has a buyer behind it.
