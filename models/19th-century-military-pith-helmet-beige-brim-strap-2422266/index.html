Priced at $29, the Military Pith Helmet Brim Strap is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 13,299 polygons over 13,340 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
