About This Model

Plastic fast food trays loaded with burgers, fries, drinks and wrappers, ready to fill restaurant counters and food court tables. The trays vary in contents, from a single burger meal to a combo with nuggets and a dessert, so a busy dining room looks natural.

Each tray passed CheckMate Pro inspection, confirming clean geometry and logical object names for every food item. Burgers, cups and fry boxes sit as separate objects, so a meal can be rearranged or shown partly eaten for storytelling. Paper liners, ketchup packets and crumpled napkins add the small mess that makes a used tray believable on camera.

The collection arrived in 2023 and suits restaurant interiors, food court visualization and casual dining scenes in games. Placed across tables with seated customers and shopping bags, the trays bring life to a lunchtime rush at the mall. Franchise planners preview counter layouts and meal presentations with it before printing new menu boards.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
