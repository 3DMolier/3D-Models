About This Model

The Fast Food Trays Collection is a production-ready Collections & Sets 3D model, priced at $69 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are advertising, product visualization and film production. First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualisation, restaurant interiors and game props.
