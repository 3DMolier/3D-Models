About This Model

A fir branch decorated with a spiky virus-shaped bauble, a topical holiday image born from the pandemic years. The 94,217 polygons cover the needles along the branch and the protein spikes that cover the round ornament. There are 54,979 vertices, a dense yet manageable mesh for holiday cards, festive banners and seasonal animations.

Needle green and the glossy ornament color are created in materials, since no image maps are included with the file. UV islands are separate, so you can add glitter, frost or a metallic sheen to the bauble without stretching. TurboSquid checked the naming, the scale and the mesh integrity of both parts before rating the decoration CheckMate Pro.

Released in 2022 for $39, it fits health awareness illustrations, news graphics and humorous Christmas greetings. Medical editors, social media designers and teachers covering vaccination history all use images like this to keep topics approachable. Hang the bauble beside ordinary ornaments under warm lights, and the contrast makes a wry seasonal statement.
