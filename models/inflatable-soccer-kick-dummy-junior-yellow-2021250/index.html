About This Model

A yellow inflatable soccer dummy shaped like a defender, used by young players to practice free kicks and dribbling. Its weighted base keeps it upright, so the figure wobbles back after each touch of the ball. Junior sizing makes it shorter than an adult wall, matching the height of kids in youth training.

The model passed CheckMate Pro review, confirming tidy geometry and accurate scale on import. Bright yellow vinyl with visible seams and a printed outline reads clearly against green artificial turf. A slight lean in the silhouette suggests the dummy has just been nudged by a shot.

Out since 2023 for $24, it suits youth sports and training scenes. Football academy promotions, coaching app illustrations and sports animations use dummies like this to set up drills. Line up a few in front of a goal to recreate a classic free kick practice session.
