About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Fire Apparatus E One Quest Seattle is a finished, render-ready model at $139. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 1,439,273 polygons over 1,474,974 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 38 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
