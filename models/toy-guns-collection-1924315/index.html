About This Model

A collection of colorful toy guns, from water pistols to plastic blasters, ready for playrooms, toy shelves and backyard games. Bright plastic shells and chunky proportions make them read as toys at a glance, never as real firearms. One blaster can sit in a child's hand while the rest of the set fills a shelf behind.

Built to CheckMate Pro, the bundle keeps topology clean and scale consistent across all the toys. Seams between plastic parts and molded triggers give close-ups enough detail to feel manufactured rather than sketched.

Three versions sit together: this Toy Guns Collection along with its numbered sequels, each adding new designs. Squirt guns and foam dart blasters also make cheerful foreground props in a summer party or birthday shot. Owning the whole run stocks an entire toy store aisle, while one volume covers a bedroom or garden scene. Since 2022 the toys have suited children's animation, summer backyard scenes and toy store visualization.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
