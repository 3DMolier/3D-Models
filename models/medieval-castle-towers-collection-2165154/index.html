About This Model

A set of medieval castle towers in stone, with crenellated tops, arrow slits and heavy bases built to anchor curtain walls. Round and square towers sit together, so a fortress can mix styles the way real castles grew over centuries. Weathered masonry and uneven blocks keep the towers from looking like freshly poured concrete in close shots.

CheckMate Pro inspection confirmed clean geometry and correct scale across every tower in the set. Each tower is a separate object, ready to be linked with walls, gates and bridges into a full castle. Low sun from the side brings out the stone texture and casts long shadows across the courtyard.

Available since 2023, the towers serve fantasy games, historical documentaries and medieval film backgrounds. Strategy and city builder games can place them as defensive upgrades around a growing town. Printed at small scale, they also make good terrain for tabletop wargames.
