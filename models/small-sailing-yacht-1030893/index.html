About This Model

Small Sailing Yacht belongs to our Ships & Boats range and is offered at $79 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 91,819 polygons and 92,871 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 9 textures, authored at 2048 x 2048. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.
