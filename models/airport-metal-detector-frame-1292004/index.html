About This Model

This walk-through frame recreates the security arch every passenger steps through at an airport checkpoint, ready to stand at the head of a screening lane. Its upright side panels carry the sensor housings, and a flat crossbar on top joins them into the familiar doorway silhouette. The opening is sized for a person to walk through, so the arch reads correctly beside travelers, trays and conveyor belts.

The mesh passed TurboSquid CheckMate Pro inspection, which covers clean topology, sensible naming and a scene that opens without missing parts. Panel edges are softened rather than razor sharp, so studio lighting catches a believable highlight along the frame. Materials stay simple and neutral, which makes it easy to restyle the casing to match the gray or beige palette of a specific terminal.

Buyers have picked it up since 2018 for airport interiors, border control scenes and security training visuals. Set several frames side by side with queue barriers and an X-ray tunnel, and a whole checkpoint hall comes together in minutes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2018 · Updated 11 November 2026
