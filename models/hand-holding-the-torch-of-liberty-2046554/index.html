About This Model

Priced at $59, the Hand Holding the Torch of Liberty is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 114,922 polygons over 58,563 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
