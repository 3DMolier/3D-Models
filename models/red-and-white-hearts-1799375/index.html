About This Model

The Red and White Hearts is a production-ready general 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 1,216 polygons over 1,218 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

It has been on sale since 2021 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
