About This Model

The Panoramic Roof EV is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $99 on TurboSquid. You are getting 86,933 polygons and 88,349 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
