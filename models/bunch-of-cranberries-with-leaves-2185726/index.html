About This Model

A small cluster of glossy cranberries resting with a few green leaves, ready for holiday food and botanical scenes. The berries and leaves use 61,002 polygons and 30,657 vertices, keeping each fruit perfectly round in close-ups.

CheckMate Pro status assures true scale and tidy topology for direct use on a plate or table. Non-overlapping UVs give the deep red skins and the leaves separate texture areas for crisp color.

Introduced in 2024, it suits Thanksgiving tables, Christmas decorations, juice packaging and healthy eating content. A handful beside roast turkey or a glass of juice instantly signals autumn flavor and family gatherings. The bright red against green leaves gives a frame a strong seasonal accent even at small scale. Bakery scenes can place it on muffins or tarts as a garnish that reads clearly. Holiday packaging designers can set the cluster on a label mockup, while recipe cards can show it floating in a festive cocktail glass.
