About This Model

A set of soccer uniforms for player models and team scenes, covering jerseys, shorts and socks in coordinated club designs. Each kit keeps the loose athletic cut of real match wear, with short sleeves and wide shorts that hang naturally on a running player. Collar shapes and stripe placement differ between kits, so teams on the same pitch never look like copies of each other.

The garments are built to the CheckMate requirements, which means clean naming and proper scale for fitting on characters. Large flat chest and back panels leave room for club crests and player names painted directly on the fabric. Fabric folds around the waistband and shoulders are gentle enough to survive animation without obvious stretching in fast running clips.

This third volume arrived in 2024 and serves sports games, kit design previews and team presentations ahead of a new season. Stadium floodlights bring out the saturated colors of the kits, which helps rival teams stand apart in wide shots.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
