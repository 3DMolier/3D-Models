About This Model

The Branch Gazebo is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 844,467 polygons and 890,664 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
