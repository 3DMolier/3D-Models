About This Model

An enclosed airport smoking zone with glass walls, benches, ashtrays and ventilation, made for terminal interior scenes. The 105,422 polygons go into the glass panels, the frame, the seating and the extraction units above. Vertices come to 54,829, around half the faces, which points to triangulated detail in the fixtures.

UVs never overlap, so signage, smudges on the glass and floor wear can be painted cleanly. The booth holds a CheckMate Pro grade, awarded by TurboSquid after an examination of its structure and size. A slightly hazy light inside the glass suggests smoke without any particle simulation or fog volumes.

On sale since 2022, Airport Smoking Zone suits airport visualization, terminal planning and travel game levels about long layovers. Architects place it near departure gates to test passenger flow and sight lines in a busy hall. Film crews use it as a quiet spot where a tense conversation happens before a flight.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 14 November 2026
