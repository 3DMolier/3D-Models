About This Model

The A330 has quietly outlived most of its contemporaries by being the right size for routes that are too long for a narrow body and too thin for anything larger. Cathay Pacific built much of its regional network on exactly that gap.

The airframe is light at 78,514 polygons and 85,228 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 12 maps, 3 of them at 8192x8192 and 9 at 4096x4096. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $129 it suits flight simulation, airline and airport visualisation, travel advertising, film backgrounds and editorial illustration. On sale since 2016.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published May 2016·Updated 20 August 2026
