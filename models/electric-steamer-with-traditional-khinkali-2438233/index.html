About This Model

Electric Steamer With Traditional Khinkali pairs a home steamer with Georgian dumplings, their pleated tops twisted into the classic knot. The whole scene uses 16,704 polygons across 16,804 vertices, a modest budget for a cooker body and several dumplings.

UVs are laid out without overlaps, which lets the dough take subtle flour dusting while the appliance keeps a clean finish. CheckMate review confirmed scale and structure, so the steamer drops onto a kitchen counter at believable size. Its body measures 29.73 x 21.82 x H41.76 cm, roughly the height of a tall kettle.

The model came out in 2025 and suits Georgian restaurant menus, recipe videos and food delivery app graphics. Each khinkali shows the twisted topknot that diners hold while eating, a detail that instantly signals the dish to viewers. Kitchen designers can also leave the dumplings out and present the appliance alone in a modern kitchen render. Soft steam added in compositing completes the picture without any change to the base geometry.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2025 · Updated 19 November 2026
