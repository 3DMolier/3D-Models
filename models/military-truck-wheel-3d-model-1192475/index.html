About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Military Truck Wheel is a finished, render-ready model at $29. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and defense simulation. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

Counted at the source, the model is 23,256 polygons and 23,509 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 1 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
