About This Model

A gantry-style construction printer module that extrudes concrete walls layer by layer, made for building technology visuals. The frame rides on tall uprights with a horizontal beam carrying the print head across the site. That open structure lets the camera see the nozzle and the fresh wall forming underneath it.

Built against the CheckMate specification, the scene comes with ordered parts and clear names for beams, carriage and nozzle. Metal surfaces and the gray concrete are separate materials, so wet and cured looks can be set apart easily in lookdev.

Released in 2024, the module serves construction startups, engineering students and documentary makers covering automated housing. Animate the head along a wall outline, and the viewer understands additive building in a single shot. Architects pitching printed homes show clients how a site looks during construction with images like this. The printer works best framed at dawn on a bare foundation slab with a few workers nearby.
