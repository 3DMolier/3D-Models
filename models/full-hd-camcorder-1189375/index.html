About This Model

Full HD Camcorder is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $89. Counted at the source, the model is 70,912 polygons and 72,886 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

39 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
