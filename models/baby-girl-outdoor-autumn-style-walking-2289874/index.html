About This Model

A baby girl in a cozy autumn coat taking careful steps on her own, arms held out slightly for balance. This version is far denser at 947,983 polygons, giving the coat, hat and boots fine folds and stitching. A wobbly toddler walk is instantly endearing, and the slight forward lean captures that uncertain moment well.

Its 593,875 vertices use non-overlapping UVs, so fabric prints and colors can be adjusted for the season. Under CheckMate specification, she stands at true toddler scale, fitting beside benches, adults and park paths. Higher detail makes this the version for close shots, where stitching and fabric weave become clearly visible to viewers.

This walking toddler joined the range in 2024 at $189, and commercial use needs no royalties. Children's clothing labels, family photography mockups, parenting apps and animated shorts are typical places where she appears. Golden afternoon light filtering through autumn trees turns her wobbly steps into a heartwarming family moment.
