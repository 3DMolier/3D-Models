About This Model

Christmas Stockings Decoration belongs to our Signage & Decor range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 5,964 polygons and 6,024 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
