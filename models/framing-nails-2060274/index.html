About This Model

This Tools model - Construction Nails - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. The build totals 1,534 polygons across 769 vertices. The same count is comfortable in an offline render and demanding in a mobile game, so judge it against your own budget.

Detail is put where the camera will find it and left out where it would only cost polygons. Scene structure is flat enough to navigate and grouped enough to be useful, which is what makes reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

On the marketplace since 2023, with the listing maintained rather than left to age. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
