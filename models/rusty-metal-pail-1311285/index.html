Rusty Metal Pail is a detailed Containers & Storage asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 2,612 polygons and 2,584 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
