About This Model

Every electronics bench has one of these: a heavy base, two articulated arms ending in crocodile clips, a magnifying lens on a third arm and a coiled holder for the soldering iron. It exists because solder needs three hands and a person has two. The model keeps the ball joints, the knurled thumbscrews and the coiled wire stand that make the tool recognisable.

It is built with subdivision geometry at 51,535 polygons and 51,612 vertices with non-overlapping UVs, which is the right choice for an object made of turned and cast parts: the curves stay smooth at any camera distance without carrying the polygon cost up front.

A rigged build sits on this page at the same $39, for when the arms have to be posed rather than accepted as they come. Aimed at electronics and maker visualization, workshop scenes, repair documentation, product catalogs and games. On sale since 2020.
