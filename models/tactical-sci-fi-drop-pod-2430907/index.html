About This Model

Priced at $24, the Tactical Sci-Fi Drop Pod is a finished Space & Sci-Fi model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 164,202 polygons and 167,623 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 133.91 x 169.19 x H362.66 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
