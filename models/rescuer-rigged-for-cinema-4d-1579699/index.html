About This Model

A rigged emergency rescuer in a high visibility uniform with a helmet and gear, ready to pose in disaster and first responder scenes. This Rescuer comes with a skeleton set up for Cinema 4D, so arms and legs bend naturally when the figure carries or lifts someone. Standing beside rubble or a fire truck, the reflective stripes tell the viewer who has arrived to help.

The character is built to the CheckMate specification, which covers orderly topology, correct human scale and clean object naming. Uniform folds around the knees and elbows are shaped so they deform without tearing when the joints rotate. Bright safety colors stay readable through smoke, dust and rain effects layered on top of the shot.

Three versions are available on this page: the Cinema 4D build at $209, plus matching rigs prepared for Modo and for Maya. The character has been offered since 2020 and suits disaster response training, safety campaigns for public services, earthquake simulations and rescue themed games.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
