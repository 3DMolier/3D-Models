About This Model

Priced at $129, the Laser MFP Black with Booklet Finisher is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 497,892 polygons and 516,407 vertices, which puts it in the mid-weight bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
