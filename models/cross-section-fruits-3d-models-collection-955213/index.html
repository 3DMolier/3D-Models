About This Model

A set of fruits sliced in half to show flesh, seeds and rind, useful for nutrition charts, cooking shows and juice packaging renders. Cut faces are where the eye lands, so the interior texture of pulp and pits matters more than the outer skin.

Every piece carries the CheckMate Pro rating, which guarantees clean geometry for macro product shots. A splash of water droplets on the cut surface makes the fruit look freshly sliced under studio light. Placing whole and cut pieces side by side makes the inside detail even more readable.

Five versions of the fruit set appear here: the first collection, Collections 3, 4 and 5, and Collection 6. Together they cover a wide spread of produce, so a market table never shows the same slice twice. Halved fruit also works in health explainers that compare vitamins or sugar content across produce. The fruits have been offered since 2015 as royalty free assets for food blogs, recipe apps and education.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
