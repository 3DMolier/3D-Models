About This Model

This Lollipop Cola Chupa Chups Collection gathers round candy heads on white sticks, some wrapped in printed foil and some unwrapped. The glossy cola-colored candy contrasts with the crinkled wrappers, which twist tightly around the base of each sphere.

Each lollipop sits in the file as its own tidy, named object, as the CheckMate Pro standard requires. Stuck into a display stand, scattered on a desk or held in a cartoon hand, the candies add playful color. Separate wrapped and bare versions let an animation show the moment a wrapper comes off before the first lick. Subsurface scattering on the bare candy gives the cola flavor its slightly translucent amber glow under strong light.

Available since 2019, the collection suits candy store interiors, children's party scenes, sweets catalogs and casual games. Food artists can drop the lollipops into checkout counters and vending displays, where small treats fill shelves quickly.
