About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Bunny Ears with Fur is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 600 polygons and 626 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 2 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
