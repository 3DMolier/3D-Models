About This Model

A hexagonal chess board designed for three players, a variant board where opponents sit around the table instead of facing each other. The 3 Man Chess Desk uses 5,862 polygons and 2,937 vertices, keeping the curved ranks and the board frame crisp. Its unusual shape immediately catches the eye of board game fans and collectors.

Contrasting light and dark wood suits the playing squares, while the frame can take a glossy lacquer or a matte finish. The UV islands do not overlap, so player colors and board markings can be painted precisely. CheckMate Pro approval from TurboSquid confirms the geometry is clean and complete.

Since 2023 the board has supported board game cafes, game design presentations and quirky living rooms. Set it on a coffee table with three chairs around it, or render it from above to show the layout. Puzzle and strategy game makers also use boards like this to pitch new variants of classic games.
