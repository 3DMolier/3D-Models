Looking for a Furniture & Interior asset? The Plastic Kids Toilet is a finished, render-ready model at $19. You are getting 1,698 polygons and 1,744 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 1 textures, authored at 2048 x 2048. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
