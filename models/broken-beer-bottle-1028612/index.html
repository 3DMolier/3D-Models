About This Model

A shattered glass beer container with a jagged neck sets the tone for a bar fight, a crime scene or a back alley. Separate shards let you scatter pieces across a floor or table, so the moment after the crash looks believable. The jagged edges are sharp in silhouette, which is what makes the object read as a threat in a tense close-up.

The shattered glass earned CheckMate Pro after a TurboSquid reviewer checked its scale, its geometry and the naming of every piece. Glass color and clarity are handled in the material, so amber, green or clear glass needs only a quick shader tweak. Refraction through the broken edges gives sparkling highlights, so even a dim scene shows the danger clearly.

This one, a green glass version and a brown glass version make up the trio here, and each costs $19. Brown glass matches most lagers in a pub scene, while green suits imported brands in a street party aftermath. Around since 2016, it has served crime dramas, action games, safety training material and urban decay environments.
