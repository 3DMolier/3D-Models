About This Model

A set of metal doors in steel frames, from plain service doors to heavier industrial entries with sturdy lever handles and kick plates. Flat panels and visible hinges give each door a functional look suited to back alleys, basements and plant rooms. Different designs in one pack keep a long corridor from repeating the same door on every wall.

They follow the CheckMate specification, giving reliable sizes for door openings in architectural and game scenes. Painted steel takes scratches, rust bloom and stenciled signs well, so the same door can look new or abandoned. A single flickering light above a closed steel door is enough to build suspense.

The family counts three versions, starting at $29 for this one, then collection 2 at $34 and collection 3 at $44. Added in 2026, the doors suit warehouses, parking garages, bunkers, hospital service areas and horror levels. Leave one slightly ajar with darkness behind it, and players will want to know what waits inside.
