About This Model

Looking for an Industrial Equipment asset? The Compact Robot Arm Kuka KR 6 R900 is a finished, render-ready model at $79. On import you get 137,798 polygons and 69,892 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Parts are separated where separation is useful, so a single component can be hidden, swapped or re-shaded on its own. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and software demos.

Its first release was in 2023, and it has earned its place in the catalog since then. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
