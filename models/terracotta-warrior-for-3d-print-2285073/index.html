About This Model

A standing terracotta warrior from the Qin emperor's army, this figure is prepared for 3D printing as a statue or desk replica. Armor plates, a topknot hairstyle and calm facial features show the details that make the Xi'an soldiers instantly recognizable. Print models need closed solid shapes, and the sturdy upright pose with a flat base suits most desktop printers.

Its geometry follows the CheckMate specification, keeping scale and structure consistent in every exported format. Clay-colored filament or a stone-effect paint gives the print the dusty look of the excavated originals. Digital artists can also render it with cracked terracotta textures for museum and documentary scenes.

Released in 2024, the warrior suits history classrooms, Chinese culture exhibitions and collectors' shelves. A row of printed soldiers on a bookcase echoes the famous pit formation on a small scale. Both digital renders and home prints are allowed under its Royalty Free license.
