About This Model

This Aircraft model - Retro Airplane - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. You are getting 113,210 polygons and 59,559 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
