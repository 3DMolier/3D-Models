Priced at $149, the St Tyfi Stone Church Gray Old is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 1,136,785 polygons and 1,259,818 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
