About This Model

Priced at $12, the Craft Knife Blue is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
