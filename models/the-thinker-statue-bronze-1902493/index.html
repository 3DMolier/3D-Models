About This Model

Priced at $49, the The Thinker Statue Bronze is a finished Signage & Decor model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 26,410 polygons and 13,248 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2022 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
