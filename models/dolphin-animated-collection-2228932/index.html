About This Model

A collection of animated dolphins, sleek marine mammals ready to swim, leap and play together in ocean scenes. The streamlined bodies, curved dorsal fins and smiling beaks match the bottlenose look most viewers picture. Several swim and jump motions let a pod move together without every animal repeating the same cycle.

Packed to CheckMate specification, the dolphins are organized and scaled consistently, so a whole pod lines up naturally in the water. Smooth gray skin reflects sky and caustic light, giving the animals a wet, glossy sheen at the surface. Animations loop cleanly, which makes it easy to keep dolphins swimming beside a boat for a long shot.

First published in 2024, the pod suits marine documentaries, aquarium displays, travel visuals and ocean conservation campaigns. Game developers use leaping dolphins to bring coastlines, cruise routes and open sea levels to life. Breaking the surface beside a sailboat at sunset, the dolphins create a joyful moment that audiences remember.
