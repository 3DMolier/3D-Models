About This Model

Private Jet and Smart Follow Me Car belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $199 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Geometry weighs in at 346,859 polygons over 369,277 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
