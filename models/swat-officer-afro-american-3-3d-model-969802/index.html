About This Model

An African American SWAT officer in full tactical gear, ready to staff police raids, hostage scenes and urban security drills. The officer wears a helmet, ballistic vest with pouches, gloves and knee pads, with a rifle carried at the ready. A firm stance and focused face give the figure the alert presence expected from special response teams.

CheckMate Pro certification guarantees clean, well scaled geometry that drops into scenes without fixes. Gear and body are cleanly organized, so you can adjust badges, patches or uniform colors for any department. Dark navy and black fabrics need strong rim light to separate the officer from night backgrounds.

The SWAT Officer Afro American 3 3D Model has served the catalog since 2015 and is royalty free. Police games, crime dramas, training simulations and public safety explainers use the officer to represent tactical units. Combining the figure with other team members builds a diverse squad for breach and entry shots.
