About This Model

A base figure is not meant to be looked at. It is meant to be dressed, rigged, sculpted over or used as the thing a garment hangs on, which is why an A pose and a clean topology matter more than any detail on the surface.

The figure is deliberately light at 21,266 polygons and 18,216 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and carries StemCell certification so it moves cleanly between engines. It is supplied in an A pose, ready for your own rig.

At $149 it suits character development and retopology work, garment and apparel visualisation, anatomy reference, game pipelines and previsualisation. On sale since 2025.
