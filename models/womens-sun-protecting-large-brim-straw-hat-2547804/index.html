About This Model

A wide-brim straw sun hat for women, the classic beach and garden accessory that shades the face in summer scenes. The broad, floppy brim casts a soft shadow across the shoulders, which adds instant atmosphere in bright sunlight. Woven straw texture gives the hat a relaxed, vacation feel even from a distance.

Crafted along CheckMate lines, this hat arrives as clean geometry that fits a character head with minor adjustment. Its round crown and gently drooping edges work for both posed models and resting product shots.

A 2026 release, it suits resort advertising, fashion e-commerce, beach scenes in games and summer lifestyle renders. Hang it on a wicker chair by the pool and the frame reads as an afternoon break. Paired with sunglasses and a linen dress, it completes a seaside outfit in seconds. The hat also works on a garden bench beside a watering can, where it suggests someone tending roses.
