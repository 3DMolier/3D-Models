About This Model

A large collection of precast concrete panels, from plain wall slabs to openings for windows and doors, for building prefab structures. Each panel shows the lifting anchors, edge chamfers and joint profiles that make precast construction recognizable on site.

CheckMate Pro certification assures real-world scale, so panels line up into story heights and wall lengths without adjustment. Separate objects let you stack them on a truck, hang one from a crane or stand a row against bracing. Having many panel types in one purchase keeps the concrete finish and modeling style consistent across a site.

Available since 2021, royalty free, the collection suits construction sequencing, prefab housing presentations and engineering education. Builders and BIM teams often need panels like these to animate how a building rises over a few days. Gray concrete with subtle stains reads convincingly under overcast light on a busy construction site. Stack a few on timber dunnage beside a tower crane and the yard immediately looks active.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
