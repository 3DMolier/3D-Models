About This Model

A hybrid airship is not a blimp. The envelope is a tri-lobed shape that generates aerodynamic lift as well as buoyancy, and it lands on air cushions rather than wheels, which is what lets it work sites with no runway at all. This build includes the interior and the cockpit, and it arrives rigged.

The mesh runs 341,766 polygons and 370,458 vertices in quads and tris. The UVs overlap, a sound economy on a symmetrical tri-lobe envelope, and the rig means the control surfaces and landing system can be posed.

Two more rigged builds share this page at the same $169, prepared for Maya and for Cinema 4D. Aimed at aerospace and logistics marketing, remote operations and mining presentation, documentary work, concept and previsualization, and games. On sale since 2026.
