About This Model

Apple Watch 38mm Fluoroelastomer Black Sport Band is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $39. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags product advertising, hardware presentation and film production as the intended applications. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

At 21,589 polygons and 21,964 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene.
