About This Model

The Snow Leopard Cub is a production-ready Animals & Creatures 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

At 34,382 polygons and 34,868 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
