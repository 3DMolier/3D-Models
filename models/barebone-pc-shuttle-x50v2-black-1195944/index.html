About This Model

The PC Shuttle X50V2 is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 17,521 polygons and 18,872 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 6 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
