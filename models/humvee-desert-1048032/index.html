About This Model

Priced at $129, the Humvee Desert is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for military simulation, game environments and film production. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defence training material and military VFX shots.

The mesh carries 330,616 polygons and 337,368 vertices, which puts it in the mid-weight bracket for this category. Texture work runs to 33 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
