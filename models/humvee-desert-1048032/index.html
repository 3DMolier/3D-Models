About This Model

Humvee Desert is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. You are getting 330,616 polygons and 337,368 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 33 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and defense simulation. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
