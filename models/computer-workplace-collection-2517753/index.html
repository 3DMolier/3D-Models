About This Model

A collection of computer workplace items, from monitors and keyboards to desks and chairs, for office and home study scenes. The pieces share a clean modern style, so they combine into a single believable workstation without visual clashes. Everything sits at real proportions, so a seated character reaches the keyboard and sees the screen naturally.

TurboSquid CheckMate guidance kept the sizes consistent and every item separately named for easy arrangement. Polygon numbers are not published for the set, so check the busiest workstation before filling an open-plan office. Screens take an emissive material, letting you show code, spreadsheets or a video call on each monitor.

Released this year in 2026 for $79, the collection suits office interiors, remote work explainers and tech company presentations. Space planners use it to test desk layouts and spacing in shared offices before buying furniture. Game designers also build office levels from it, where flickering screens and cluttered desks tell small stories.
