About This Model

A collection of United States medals of honor, with ribbons, star pendants and suspension bars for military and award scenes. Each medal shows bright metal against a colored ribbon, the combination viewers associate with ceremonies and heroism.

The collection follows the CheckMate standard, so every medal arrives at real scale for a uniform chest or a display case. Gold and bronze finishes need sharp highlights, so a small hard light makes the stars sparkle in close ups. Several medals in one purchase let a veteran's uniform or a museum case show a varied set of decorations.

Available since 2024, the set suits military history documentaries, memorial visuals, war films and educational material about service and valor. Pin the medals on a dress uniform, or arrange them on velvet inside a framed shadow box. They also work as close up props in award ceremony scenes and patriotic holiday visuals.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
