About This Model

This collection pairs mug trees and holders with the mugs hanging on them, the kind of clutter that makes a kitchen look lived in. Each holder carries its own mugs on hooks or pegs, so the set covers several countertop styles at once.

CheckMate Lite certification confirms the pieces are clean, correctly scaled and ready to place. Every holder and mug sits as its own object, so you can move a single cup off its hook for a breakfast shot. Ceramic glazes and wood or metal stands give plenty of material contrast in a small space.

A second collection version is offered at $49, while this set sells for $39 on the page. Put one beside a coffee machine, and an empty counter suddenly feels used every morning. Mugs tilt slightly on their hooks, which avoids the rigid look of perfectly aligned props. Kitchen interior renders, cafe scenes and home decor catalogs have been setting it out since 2022.
