About This Model

This Aircraft model - Lockheed L1011 Stargazer - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

At 324,919 polygons and 360,433 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

Can the Lockheed L1011 Stargazer model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What is the Lockheed L1011 Stargazer model typically used for?

It is cataloged for visualization, advertising and 3D printing - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

What does StemCell mean for the Lockheed L1011 Stargazer model?
