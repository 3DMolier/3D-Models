About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Robotic Humanoid Pointing Pose is a finished, render-ready model at $169. Geometry weighs in at 15,436 polygons over 16,828 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
