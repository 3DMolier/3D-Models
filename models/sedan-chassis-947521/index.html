About This Model

This bare sedan chassis shows the floor pan, suspension, steering and exhaust, made for explaining how a car works underneath. The 84,300 polygons go into mechanical parts rather than bodywork, so springs, arms and brake discs hold up in close views. Without a shell on top, the layout reads at once as a teaching diagram in three dimensions.

Passing CheckMate Pro review means true scale and clean topology, which lets a body from another sedan align over the frame. Non-overlapping UVs leave room for metal, rubber and painted finishes to be assigned part by part. The fuel tank and exhaust run sit where they belong, useful for safety and crash explanation sequences.

Available since 2015, it serves automotive engineering decks, technical animation, mechanic training and cutaway illustrations. Exploded views work well here, since each assembly can be pulled away from the floor structure along one axis. Pair it with a separate body to animate the shell lifting off and revealing the running gear.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2015 · Updated 13 November 2026
