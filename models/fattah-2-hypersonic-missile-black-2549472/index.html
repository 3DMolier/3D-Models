About This Model

A hypersonic glide vehicle looks unlike a conventional missile because it has to fly rather than merely arc: a wedge-shaped body that generates lift at speed, control surfaces at the rear and a blunted nose sized to survive the heat of the descent. Presented in black, those planform edges read clearly, which is what a defence graphic needs.

The mesh carries 39,234 polygons with mixed UV layout, and the geometry is spent on the body chines and the control surfaces where the shape has to be exact.

At $59 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits defence and geopolitical visualization, news and documentary graphics, strategic analysis material, education and games. Real-world scale is kept, so it seats correctly on a launch vehicle. On sale since 2026.
