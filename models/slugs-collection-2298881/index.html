About This Model

A collection of slugs, the soft-bodied garden mollusks that leave glistening trails across leaves and wet stones. Eye stalks, a ridged mantle and a moist, shiny skin make each slug read correctly in macro shots. Mixing sizes and colors, the set lets a garden scene show several species side by side.

CheckMate specification keeps their tiny scale believable, so they sit naturally on leaves, mushrooms or soil. Each slug is separate, ready to be scattered across a damp garden bed or a rotting log.

Released in 2024, the slugs suit biology lessons, gardening guides, pest control explainers and creepy-crawly game levels. A glossy specular highlight and a faint slime trail are what sell the wet texture to the viewer. Morning dew and shallow depth of field turn an ordinary leaf into a striking macro composition. Children's science books can use the set to show how slugs move and what they eat.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How clean is the geometry on the Slugs Collection model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.

How heavy is the Slugs Collection mesh?
