About This Model

Priced at $79, the Realistic Ride-On Electric Car White Rigged is a finished Toys & Games model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.

The mesh carries 52,966 polygons and 57,475 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
