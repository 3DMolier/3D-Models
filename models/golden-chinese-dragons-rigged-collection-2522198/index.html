About This Model

A set of golden Chinese dragons with long serpentine bodies, antlered heads, whiskers and clawed legs coiling along their length. Unlike a winged European dragon, this kind is shaped like a river, and the curves of the body carry every pose. The gold finish turns them into festival and temple pieces rather than monsters for a fantasy battle.

Built to CheckMate specification, the collection keeps consistent scale and naming across every dragon in the set. Polished gold needs something to reflect, so a red interior or a lantern-lit night street brings out the scales. This edition is the general rigged release, for studios that are not tied to a specific animation package.

Released in 2026, the dragons fit Lunar New Year campaigns, Asian cultural documentaries, fantasy games and festival stage visuals. A dragon winding around a pillar or above rooftops is the classic composition, and the set gives several to choose from. Buying them together saves matching scale and finish across separate purchases when a scene needs more than a single beast.
