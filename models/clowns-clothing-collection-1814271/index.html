About This Model

A collection of colorful clown outfits for circus scenes, party characters and comic or horror performers in film and games. Baggy suits, oversized collars and bright patterns give each costume the exaggerated silhouette audiences recognize instantly. The garments come at real proportions, so they fit standard human figures without heavy rescaling.

Each outfit earned CheckMate Pro after TurboSquid reviewed topology, scale and the naming of every garment part. Polygon counts are not provided for the bundle, so check the fullest costume before dressing a large circus crowd. Bold primary colors and polka dots can be reworked in the materials, turning a cheerful clown into a sinister one.

Offered since 2021 for $79, the collection suits circus animations, birthday party visuals and horror game characters. Character artists mix costumes across a troupe, giving every performer a distinct look under the big top. Event planners also preview themed parties with clown characters before hiring real performers.
