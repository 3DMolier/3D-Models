About This Model

A tactical police officer in full protective kit is the core figure for any raid, standoff or security drill scene. The SWAT Officer Asian 3 shows an operator in helmet, goggles and body armor with a ready stance.

The vest carries magazine pouches, a radio and attachment straps across the chest. Knee pads, gloves and heavy boots complete the uniform, while dark fabric keeps the silhouette compact against city backgrounds. Having cleared CheckMate Pro checks, the character arrives clean and correctly proportioned for placing in groups.

Since 2015 the officer has been used in law enforcement training material, crime drama scenes and action games. Trainers group several copies at a doorway to illustrate entry formations step by step. Filmmakers place the team behind an armored vehicle while the camera pushes in. Game developers use the character as a squad member or opponent in urban tactical missions.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
