About This Model

Priced at $179, the Boeing B-17 Propeller Aircraft Bomber is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 456,212 polygons and 468,422 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation.

2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
