About This Model

A pair of carbon trekking poles in blue and black, with cork grips, adjustable wrist straps and carbide tips. Collapsing shaft sections and the locking clamps between them show how the poles pack down for travel. Small baskets above the tips stop them sinking into mud or snow on soft trail surfaces.

The model holds a CheckMate Pro certificate, which confirms clean geometry and correct scale. Carbon fiber weave on the shafts needs a fine anisotropic sheen to read correctly in close renders. Brand graphics printed along the shafts make this an Editorial Uses Only model for news and review content.

Available since 2023 for $24, the poles fit outdoor and hiking editorial work, from alpine treks to forest walks. Trail guides, outdoor gear reviews and travel magazine illustrations use poles like these to show hiking equipment on mountain routes. Lean them against a backpack on a rocky summit for a natural trail rest shot.
