About This Model

A collection of KFC fast food items, the buckets, boxes and cups that make up a recognizable takeaway meal on a table. Grouped as one product, the pieces share scale and material rules, so they combine into a meal scene without adjustment. The red and white packaging is the recognizable part, and it reads clearly even when the food sits in the background.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms clean topology, real-world dimensions and consistent material naming across the whole set. Arranging the containers around a tray quickly turns an empty table into a busy lunch moment. Fried chicken pieces have bumpy, irregular surfaces, which is what keeps them from looking like plastic toys under light.

Available since 2024, the set is limited to editorial use, because KFC owns the brand, logos and packaging designs. It fits food journalism, documentaries about fast food, film and TV set dressing and educational material on nutrition. A bucket on a car seat or a dorm desk tells the viewer a lot about a character in one prop.
