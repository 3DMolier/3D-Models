About This Model

Priced at $49, the Aquarium Plants Collection is a finished Collections & Sets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
