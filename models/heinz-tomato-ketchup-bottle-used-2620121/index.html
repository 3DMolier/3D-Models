About This Model

A used Heinz tomato ketchup squeeze bottle, partly emptied and ready for diner tables, family kitchens and fast food scenes. The 3,991 polygons shape the soft dented body and the flip cap that shows daily use. A slight squeeze in the plastic gives the bottle a lived-in look that brand new props always lack.

The CheckMate build keeps 3,922 vertices in a closed shell, with partial UVs suited to label decals. Licensing for this bottle is Editorial Uses Only, aimed at journalism, classrooms and documentary film. Its proportions follow the familiar retail squeeze bottle that sits on countless diner tables.

At $34 since 2026, the bottle suits food blogs, restaurant interiors and lifestyle scenes in home kitchens. Set it beside a burger and fries to complete a casual lunch shot at a roadside diner. Tilt it near a plate and add a ketchup drip to tell a quick mealtime story with kids.
