About This Model

This set gathers hand sanitizer bottles of the kind found on office desks, shop counters and hospital walls since the pandemic. Small bottles like these became a fixture of everyday life and now read instantly in any public interior. One bottle on a reception desk is enough to date a scene to the present decade.

The collection meets CheckMate Lite, TurboSquid's entry level of inspection for geometry and file organization. Translucent plastic and gel deserve the most attention, since light passing through them sells the realism.

Offered since 2022, the bottles may appear in commercial scenes of clinics, schools, offices and busy supermarket checkouts. Health education videos and workplace safety training use them to demonstrate hygiene routines step by step. Several designs together keep a long corridor of dispensers from looking like a single cloned bottle. Product designers can borrow the shapes as a starting point when sketching new packaging ideas.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
