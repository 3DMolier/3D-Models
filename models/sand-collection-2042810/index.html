About This Model

A collection of sand piles and surfaces, useful for beaches, construction sites and desert terrains. Soft slopes, ripples and loose grains give each piece a natural, wind-shaped look like real dunes. Mounds can sit on concrete as building material at a site or blend into dunes as open landscape.

With CheckMate Pro status, the sand meshes are clean and well organized in the scene. Material tints shift from pale coral sand to dark volcanic grains with simple color changes. Gentle edges fade into the ground plane, so piles merge with terrain instead of sitting on top like separate objects.

Dated 2023, the sand suits beach resort renders, construction visualization, desert games and children's play areas in parks. A child's spade stuck in a pile tells of a sandcastle, while a shovel suggests a building job in progress. Low raking light reveals the fine ripples that make sand look believable in close shots.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
