About This Model

A collection of bicycle front and rear lamps with handlebar clamps, ready to equip city bikes, road bikes and cycling scenes. Different body shapes and mounts in the set cover commuter, sport and retro styles of riding. Small as they are, the lamps read clearly on a bike thanks to bold lenses and glossy housings.

The pack wears CheckMate Pro status, awarded after TurboSquid reviewed its topology, scale and naming by hand. Lenses are ready for emissive materials, so the lamps can glow on a dark street or a night trail.

Released in 2024, the Bicycle Lamps Collection suits cycling shop pages, road safety campaigns and urban riding animations. Mount a lamp on a handlebar and let its beam cut across wet asphalt for a moody shot. Product designers also block out new light concepts by modifying shapes like these. Rear red lights and white front lamps together help a rider stay visible in traffic visuals.
