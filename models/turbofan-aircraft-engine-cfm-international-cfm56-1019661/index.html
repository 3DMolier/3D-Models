About This Model

The CFM56 is the most produced jet engine in history, and it hangs under a very large share of the world's narrowbody fleet. As a model it presents the classic engine challenge: fan blades at the front, the accessory gearbox and plumbing wrapped around the core, the thrust reverser cascades in the cowl and the exhaust cone at the back, all of it examined closely because an engine shown alone is a subject rather than a detail.

Built with non-overlapping surfaces so that polished, painted and heat-stained materials can each take their own treatment across the nacelle and the core.

At $149 it suits aerospace marketing and engineering presentation, maintenance and training material, trade show and technical documentation, aviation education and games. On sale since 2016.
