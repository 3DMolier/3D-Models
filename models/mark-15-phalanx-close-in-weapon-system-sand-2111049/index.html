About This Model

Priced at $69, the Mark 15 Phalanx Close-In Weapon System is a finished Weapons model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 2,142,516 polygons and 1,114,928 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
