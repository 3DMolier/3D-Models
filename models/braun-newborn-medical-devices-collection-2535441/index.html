About This Model

A collection of Braun devices for newborn and infant care, the small home health tools parents keep near the crib. The pieces share rounded, hand-friendly shapes in soft plastic, the design language typical of baby care products.

Built to CheckMate specification, the set arrives at real scale with tidy geometry and organized naming. Because the brand is visible, the collection carries the Editorial Uses Only license.

New in 2026, it suits nursery interiors, pediatric clinic scenes and parenting education material for expectant mothers and fathers. Placed on a changing table beside diapers and a soft towel, the devices make a nursery feel real. Health journalism and news graphics about infant care can show the set as an editorial illustration. Medical training apps for nurses can also include it when demonstrating routine checks on a newborn. In a hospital maternity ward, the set can sit on a bedside trolley, giving a delivery room scene the small practical details that families notice.
