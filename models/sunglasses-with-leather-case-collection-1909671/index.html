About This Model

This set pairs sunglasses with the leather cases that protect them, so eyewear can be shown both worn and stored. Stitched seams and folded flaps on the cases contrast with the thin metal or plastic frames of the glasses. Tinted lenses deserve real attention, since reflections in them make or break any close shot.

Each piece is CheckMate Pro certified, so the delicate hinges and lenses carry no broken faces. Several models together let a display tray show different shapes without repeating a single frame.

In the catalog since 2022, the collection is cleared for commercial fashion and retail work. Eyewear store interiors, fashion visualizations and summer travel scenes are natural homes for these accessories. On a car dashboard or a beach towel, a pair beside its case tells a quick story about a trip. Characters in games and animation can wear them, with the case left on a nearby table.
