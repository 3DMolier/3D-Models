About This Model

Zucchini is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. You are getting 644 polygons and 653 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 5 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
