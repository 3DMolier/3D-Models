About This Model

An oval running track with covered grandstands gives a complete athletics venue in a single placement. The Athletic Running Track Stadium with Tribunes uses 1,581,961 polygons and 1,563,512 vertices. Those counts describe the lanes, the infield and long rows of spectator seating.

Much of the budget lives in the tiered tribunes, where seats and stair aisles hold up as the camera sweeps the stands. The venue is StemCell certified, so its materials transfer between renderers without being rebuilt on a scene this large.

Since 2024 the stadium has served as a base for broadcast graphics that need a believable arena for replays. Urban planners place it inside a campus masterplan to show how sports facilities connect to streets and parking. Game studios use the track for sprint and relay events with a full crowd. VR teams build walkthroughs where visitors stand at the finish line and look back along the curved lanes.
