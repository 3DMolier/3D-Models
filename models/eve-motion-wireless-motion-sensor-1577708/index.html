About This Model

This Eve Motion Wireless Motion Sensor is a small rounded smart home detector with a frosted lens and a matte white body. Just 1,345 polygons form the soft square shell and the sensor dome, so the device sits lightly in any interior. Mounted in a hallway corner or placed on a shelf, it hints at lights that turn on as someone walks past.

Frosted plastic, the lens and fine details are handled by 7 maps at 2048 x 2048. Logos and the lens surface stay crisp in close renders, helped by the clean UVs of its 1,394 vertices. StemCell certification gives matching materials in each format, drawn from a single master scene.

Introduced in 2020, the sensor suits smart home explainers, security system guides, apartment visualizations and tech reviews. Showing light cones that spread from the lens explains detection range in a simple diagram. Its quiet design blends into modern rooms, which is exactly why interior renders can include it unobtrusively.
