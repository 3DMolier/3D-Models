About This Model

A low-speed two-stroke ship engine, the towering crosshead unit that drives a cargo vessel's propeller directly through the shaft. Its 446,555 polygons sit on 1,148,459 vertices, a ratio that points to many separate small parts rather than one welded shell. The simplified build keeps the cylinder row, turbocharger, walkways and exhaust manifold while dropping hidden internals.

StemCell certification ensures matching results across engines, from engineering renders to real-time ship simulators. Repeated cylinder covers share UV space, while the larger casings keep their own islands for grime and paint. Railings and ladders around the block give a sense of scale when a crew figure stands beside it.

The page holds two versions, this simplified unit and a green Low-Speed Marine Diesel Engine Assembly. Available since 2026, it fits shipyard presentations, marine engineering courses and engine room scenes in maritime films. Lit from above in a dim engine room, the massive block conveys the raw power behind global shipping.
