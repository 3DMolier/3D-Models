About This Model

This indoor intercom monitor is the screen unit of a video door system, letting residents see and talk to visitors outside. Its 26,767 polygons over 27,707 vertices shape the thin display frame, the touch buttons and the wall mounting plate. A flat screen dominates the front, with a row of control keys below for unlocking the gate and answering calls.

Built to CheckMate Pro, the monitor has non-overlapping UVs, so a live camera image can be mapped onto the screen. Gloss black glass with a white or silver bezel matches most modern hallways, and emissive screen content brings it alive.

Released in 2021, the display suits apartment hallway interiors, smart home tutorials and security system visualizations. Hung beside the front door at eye level, it shows the resident's side of a visitor's call. Real estate renders of new apartments often include such panels to signal that entry is secure and modern. Pairing it with the outdoor camera from the same system tells the whole story of a door call.
