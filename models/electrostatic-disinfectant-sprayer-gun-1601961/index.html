About This Model

Priced at $49, the Electrostatic Disinfectant Sprayer Gun is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 11,930 polygons and 12,124 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 2048 x 2048. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
