About This Model

A Blender planet setup that renders Earth with a lit day side, glowing city lights on the night side and a soft atmospheric rim. The terminator line moves as the planet turns, so a single turntable render shows sunrise sweeping across continents. Clouds sit in a separate volumetric layer above the surface, which gives depth when the camera skims close to the horizon.

The system is built for Blender, so the shaders, lighting and sun controls open ready to render without conversion. Scene order follows CheckMate guidelines, which keeps the planet, atmosphere and light objects easy to find. Swapping the background star field or changing the sun angle takes a few clicks rather than a shader rebuild.

On sale since 2026, it serves science documentaries, title sequences, space game menus and educational explainers about time zones. A slow orbit with the night lights fading in is a common opening shot for news and climate presentations. Because the effect is volumetric, render times climb at close range, so plan test frames before a long sequence.
