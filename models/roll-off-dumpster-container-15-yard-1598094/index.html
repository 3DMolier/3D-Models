About This Model

Roll Off Dumpster Container 15 Yard is a detailed Containers & Storage asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.

Geometry weighs in at 16,782 polygons over 17,941 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 5 maps, the largest at 2048x2048, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
