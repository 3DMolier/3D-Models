About This Model

Sit Climbing Harness Generic models the waist belt, leg loops and belay loop climbers buckle on before a route. Its 39,813 polygons and 79,463 vertices follow the padded webbing, the gear loops and the metal adjustment buckles.

Five texture maps at 4096 x 4096 carry the woven strap pattern, stitching and padding detail. The StemCell PBR setup is meant to render consistently across common engines without reworking materials. Non-overlapping UVs let you bake chalk dust or wear on the tie-in points, where a real harness shows age first. Laid flat on a crash pad or hung from a gym hook, the harness reads clearly as climbing gear.

The harness comes in two versions, both $49: this generic one and the Sit Climbing Harness Standard. It has been on sale since 2020, and a first buyer has already used it. Climbing gym scenes, outdoor equipment catalogs, rescue training visuals and mountain adventure games are typical settings.
