About This Model

Black Tank Drum is one of the Musical Instruments models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. The mesh carries 28,820 polygons and 14,332 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.
