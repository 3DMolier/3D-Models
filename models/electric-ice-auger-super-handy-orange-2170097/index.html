About This Model

Electric Ice Auger is a detailed Tools asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. You are getting 89,122 polygons and 46,016 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.

Questions About This Model

Can the Electric Ice Auger model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What is the Electric Ice Auger model typically used for?

It is cataloged for technical visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

What does StemCell mean for the Electric Ice Auger model?

It means one source asset, several delivered formats, and a PBR material setup designed to read consistently across them - no rebuilding shaders after import.
