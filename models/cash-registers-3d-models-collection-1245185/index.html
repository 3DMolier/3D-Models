About This Model

Cash registers of several styles, from compact electronic units to point of sale terminals with drawers and screens. Each register shows a keypad, receipt printer and money drawer, the parts that signal checkout to any viewer. Having several designs together lets a shopping street fill its shops with variety rather than one repeated counter.

This bundle earned a CheckMate Pro grade, which TurboSquid gives to models with tidy geometry and consistent naming. Displays can carry any total or store logo, and casings can be shaded in black, white or retro beige plastic. Open drawers filled with coins and notes make strong close shots for retail stories.

Released in 2018, the collection supports retail visualizations, supermarket games and finance explainer videos. Lined up along a checkout counter, the registers turn an empty store model into a working shop. Training videos for cashiers also picture them when introducing a new point of sale system.
