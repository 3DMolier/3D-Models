About This Model

A sit-down personal watercraft in the style of a Sea-Doo, with a sculpted hull, a padded seat and handlebar steering. It is shaped for skimming across a lake or harbor, with the nose raised and spray trailing from the stern. The low, wide stance and bright hull panels make it stand out against blue water from any distance.

The model carries CheckMate Pro certification, so the hull, seat and controls are cleanly built and correctly scaled. Adding a rider and a foam wake from a particle system turns a static file into a lively action shot.

The page also carries a rigged version, so the pair of files covers both still renders and animated rides. Out since 2016 with 16 copies sold, the jet ski belongs in beach resort promos, water sports videos and vacation scenes. Because it reproduces a real brand, its license is limited to editorial use. Moored beside a wooden dock at sunset, it also works as quiet set dressing.
