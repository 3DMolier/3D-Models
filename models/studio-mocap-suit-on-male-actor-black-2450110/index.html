About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Suit Actor is a finished, render-ready model at $199. You are getting 35,357 polygons and 36,430 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 121.71 x 33.71 x H175.08 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
