About This Model

Coatlicue is one of the most confronting sculptures to survive from Mesoamerica: a figure whose head is two facing serpents, wearing a skirt of woven snakes and a necklace of hands and hearts. Every element is symbolic rather than decorative, which means a model of it has to reproduce the iconography exactly or it becomes ornamental nonsense.

This build is prepared for printing rather than only for rendering, and those are different requirements. A print-ready model has to be watertight and structurally sound, with no self-intersecting geometry and no detail too fine to survive a nozzle - work that a render-only asset never does.

At $89 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits 3D printing, museum shop and replica manufacture, archaeology and cultural education, exhibition design and collector pieces. On sale since 2024.
