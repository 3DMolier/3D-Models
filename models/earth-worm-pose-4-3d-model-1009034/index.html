About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Earth Worm Pose 4 is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 1,750 polygons and 1,752 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 4 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
