About This Model

Strategic Heavy Bomber belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 543,313 polygons and 564,073 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation.

2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
