Priced at $149, the Adventure Compact City is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 232,830 polygons and 238,048 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
