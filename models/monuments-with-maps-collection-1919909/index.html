About This Model

Famous monuments paired with maps make up this collection, so each landmark can be shown in its place on the globe. Placing a monument on its map turns a simple model into a geography lesson about where it stands. The map layer also works as a pedestal, giving every landmark a clean base for display.

All models received CheckMate Pro certification, which ensures clean geometry and logical naming throughout the set. Stone and bronze materials respond well to warm side light that brings out their carved details. Matte map textures contrast nicely with the shinier monument surfaces placed on top of them.

Royalty free since its 2022 launch, the collection suits travel apps, educational games and tourism presentations. Teachers can animate a camera flying from the map down to each landmark during a geography lesson. Travel agencies can present a tour route by lining up its monuments along a mapped path.
