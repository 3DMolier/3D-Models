About This Model

The guided missile cruiser USS Anzio CG-68 is modeled here for naval scenes, carrying the name of a hard-fought Italian beachhead. Boxy forward and aft deckhouses carry flat radar arrays, while vertical launch cells and gun mounts line the bow and stern. Long, low and bristling with antennas, the ship has the classic profile of the Ticonderoga class.

TurboSquid certified the cruiser as CheckMate Pro, its strictest standard for topology and organization. Haze gray paint with rust streaks and salt stains suits long deployments in a believable way. Bow spray in a heavy Atlantic swell shows off the hull form and the stacked superstructure.

On sale since 2016, the cruiser is royalty free for naval documentaries, simulators and military history content. Veterans' associations and naval historians can illustrate the ship's service in presentations and printed books. A slow flyby along the starboard side, launchers in view, makes a commanding sequence.
