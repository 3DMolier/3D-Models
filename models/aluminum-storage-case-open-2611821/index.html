About This Model

An open aluminum flight case with latches and padded lining, ready to hold tools, cameras or weapons in a scene. Its 26,859 polygons and 27,213 vertices cover the riveted frame, hinges, latches and interior padding. With the lid open, the case shows its contents, so it works as a reveal prop.

StemCell certification and non-overlapping UVs make custom texturing or light baking straightforward in any engine. The ribbed metal corners and reinforced edges are the details that read as a professional equipment case. It sits well on a workbench, in a van cargo area or on a hangar floor.
