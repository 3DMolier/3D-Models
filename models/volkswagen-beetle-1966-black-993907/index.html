About This Model

Volkswagen Beetle 1966 belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 162,063 polygons and 167,451 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
