About This Model

Volkswagen Beetle 1966 Black is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $129. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 162,063 polygons and 167,451 vertices, a light load for a scene.
