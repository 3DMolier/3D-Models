About This Model

Priced at $129, the Modern City Crossover Silver Dirty is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 171,108 polygons over 194,029 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
