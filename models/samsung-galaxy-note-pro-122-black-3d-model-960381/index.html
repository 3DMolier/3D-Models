About This Model

A large black productivity tablet with a stylus, built for designers who need a big screen surface in device shots. The Samsung Galaxy Note Pro 12.2 Black 3D Model shows the thin bezel, the home button and the textured back that imitates stitched leather.

The S Pen slot sits in the lower edge, so the stylus can be posed half drawn or stowed in place. Rounded corners and the slim side profile stay faithful to the device, which matters in reviews where readers compare silhouettes. The file carries CheckMate Pro certification, confirming clean structure and real-world scale. The leather-look back with its stitched border catches light softly, so the rear view is worth showing too.

Sold since 2015 for Editorial Uses Only, the tablet fits tech news, comparison charts and classroom material on mobile devices. Place it on a desk beside a keyboard, or hold it in a character's hands, and show your own interface on its glass. Its wide bezel leaves room for thumbs, which keeps hand poses believable in close renders.
