About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The EVA Space Suit with Detailed Interior White Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $269. You are getting 218,840 polygons and 233,070 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 161.77 x 149.28 x H224.12 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
