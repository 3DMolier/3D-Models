About This Model

White Racehorse is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $169. The mesh carries 37,728 polygons and 38,302 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 7 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Questions About This Model

Can the White Racehorse model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

How heavy is the White Racehorse mesh?

37,728 polygons and 38,302 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

What texture resolution ships with the White Racehorse?

7 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.
