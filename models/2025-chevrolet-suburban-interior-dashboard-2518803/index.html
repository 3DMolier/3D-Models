About This Model

The dashboard of a 2025 Chevrolet Suburban, with steering wheel, instrument cluster and center screen for in-cabin driving views. The wide horizontal dash and large infotainment display define the modern full-size SUV cabin seen from the driver's seat.

It is very light at 12,686 polygons and 15,591 vertices, ideal for driving simulators that need a fast cockpit. StemCell delivery means the same PBR plastics, leather and trim read correctly in each supplied format. With a span of 163.89 x 142.34 x H80.94 cm, it fits behind a windshield at true cabin scale.

The model is Editorial Uses Only, so it suits automotive journalism, driver education and simulation research. Introduced in 2026, it lets reviewers illustrate dashboard layouts and screen positions without a full vehicle. Mixed UVs share texture space on repeated vents and buttons while the screen keeps a clean island for custom UI. Place a road scene beyond the glass and the view instantly becomes a first-person driving shot.
