About This Model

Folded Dress Shirt belongs to our Clothing & Accessories range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 22,300 polygons and 23,130 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.
