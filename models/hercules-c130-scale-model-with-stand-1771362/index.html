About This Model

A desk model on a stand is a different object from the aircraft it depicts. It exists to sit in an office, and the proportions are usually flattered slightly, because a scale model that is strictly accurate rarely looks as good as one that is not.

The model is very light at 9,502 polygons and 9,524 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplied with its stand. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $69 it suits interior and office visualization, product and collectible imagery, aviation and defence presentation, film set dressing, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2021.
