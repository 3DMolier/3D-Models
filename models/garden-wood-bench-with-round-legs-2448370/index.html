About This Model

Priced at $19, the Garden Wood Bench with Round Legs is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 1,522 polygons and 1,528 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 215.06 x 43.31 x H44.16 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
