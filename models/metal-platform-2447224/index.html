About This Model

The Metal Platform is a production-ready Industrial Equipment 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. You are getting 12,138 polygons and 14,544 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
