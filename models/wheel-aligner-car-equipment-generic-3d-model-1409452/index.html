About This Model

A generic wheel alignment machine for car service centers, with its cabinet, monitor and the wheel-mounted target clamps that technicians fit to each tire. Alignment rigs are instantly recognizable in a garage, because the clamps on every wheel connect a car to the computer. Being generic, the equipment carries no manufacturer branding, so it fits any workshop without legal questions.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms correct scale and clean organization, verified by a reviewer before release. That means the clamps match real wheel sizes and the cabinet stands at a believable working height. Screen content on the monitor is easy to replace with your own alignment software interface.

Available since 2019, it serves auto repair shop interiors, technician training material and car service apps. Place it beside a car on a lift and the scene immediately reads as professional maintenance. Tire shops and dealerships can use it in renders that explain what happens during a service visit.
