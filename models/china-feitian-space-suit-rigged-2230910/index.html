About This Model

A spacewalk suit built by a different program is not simply a recolored version of the familiar one: the joint bearings, the backpack shape and the helmet visor arrangement all differ, because each agency solved the same problems independently. Those differences are the whole reason to model it separately.

The mesh is efficient at 50,653 polygons and 53,436 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and it arrives rigged so the suit can be posed on a figure.

Two more rigged builds share this page at the same $199, prepared for Maya and for Cinema 4D. Aimed at space industry visualization, documentary and news graphics, museum and education material, film and television, and games. Built to CheckMate specification, on sale since 2024.
