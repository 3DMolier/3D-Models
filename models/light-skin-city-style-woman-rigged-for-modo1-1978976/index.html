About This Model

A young woman in city-style clothes, prepared for Modo and dressed for a casual day downtown in a modern city. Contemporary street fashion and relaxed posture make her look like someone heading to a cafe or an office. Her proportions are natural rather than stylized, so she blends into architectural renders without drawing too much attention.

CheckMate specification determines the file layout, keeping names, units and scale coherent when she enters a Modo scene. Neutral clothing colors let the figure sit in many palettes, and they can be adjusted for a specific brand mood. Soft daylight and reflections from shop windows suit the character and her outfit best.

Released in 2022, the woman fits street scenes, shopping mall visualizations, real estate renders and lifestyle advertising. Architects populate plazas and sidewalks with figures like this, showing how a space will feel when busy. Fashion retailers also use her as a placeholder model when planning store window displays.
