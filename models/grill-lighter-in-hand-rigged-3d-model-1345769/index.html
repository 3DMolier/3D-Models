About This Model

A hand grips a long-nosed grill lighter with the thumb on the trigger, ready to spark a barbecue, candle or gas burner. Only 8,568 polygons and 8,503 vertices build the fingers, the plastic body and the slim metal barrel, keeping it light. Hand and lighter together explain the action, which a lighter lying on a table would not.

The kit includes 11 texture maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048, giving skin pores and plastic grain sharp detail. Mixed UVs and CheckMate Pro certification confirm clean geometry and a reliable file setup. Realistic knuckle creases and nail detail hold up in macro shots near the flame.

The general build and the Grill Lighter in Hand Rigged for Maya are each $69. Maya users get a version prepared for their software, saving setup time before animating. First listed in 2018, it supports cooking videos, home safety tutorials, kitchen animations and first-person game interactions.
