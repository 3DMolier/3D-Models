About This Model

This set pairs an ice hockey stick, with a curved blade and a taped grip, and a black rubber disc for play. The shaft runs straight down to the heel, where the blade bends at the angle players rely on for wrist shots. The disc is flat vulcanized rubber with a lightly ridged edge, just like those used in real league games.

TurboSquid's CheckMate specification guided its construction, so the naming, pivots and scale follow familiar conventions in any package. White cloth tape wrapped around the blade and handle adds a hand-used look that plain carbon would lack. Ice hockey scenes look best with a cold rink reflection on the glossy shaft and the black rubber.

Introduced in 2015, the Hockey Stick and Puck suits rink renders, winter sports content and locker room scenes. Lean the shaft against the boards, drop the disc on center ice, or hang both above a bench. Sports games and fan illustrations use the pair as a quick symbol of a game on frozen ponds.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
