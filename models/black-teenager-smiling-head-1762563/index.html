About This Model

A smiling head of a Black teenager with natural features, prepared for character work, crowd faces and medical illustration. The sculpt uses 22,702 polygons and 43,054 vertices, so the open grin, cheeks and eyelids keep their soft curves in close portraits.

Approval under CheckMate Pro covers the topology, which follows facial muscles closely enough for later expression work. Non-overlapping UVs make it simple to repaint skin tones, add freckles or bake fine pores from a higher detail sculpt. The neck ends cleanly, ready to join a body or sit alone on a bust.

Available since 2021, the head suits educational anatomy scenes, diverse game casts and figures for animated shorts. A genuine smile like this one warms up a group shot in a classroom or a sports team photo. Medical artists also use young faces when showing dental work, skin conditions or facial proportions to students.
