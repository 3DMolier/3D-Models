About This Model

Infant cereal packaging carries more regulated text than almost any other food product. Age guidance, preparation instructions and nutritional declarations are all mandatory, which leaves the designer very little space to work in.

The model is extremely light at 4,028 polygons and 2,035 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $29 it suits packaging and label mockups, food and retail visualization, e-commerce imagery, kitchen and nursery scenes, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.
