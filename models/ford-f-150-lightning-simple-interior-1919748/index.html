About This Model

Ford F-150 Lightning Simple Interior is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

The mesh carries 841,520 polygons and 456,884 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
