About This Model

Priced at $19, the Heartbeat Pulse Logo is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 2,536 polygons and 2,500 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering.
