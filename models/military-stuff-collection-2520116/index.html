About This Model

Military Stuff Collection is a detailed Model Bundles & Sets asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $119. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
