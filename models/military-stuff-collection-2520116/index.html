About This Model

The Military Stuff Collection groups the kind of field equipment that fills an army camp, from storage containers to portable gear. Olive drab paint, stenciled markings and heavy latches give the props the rugged look of real service equipment. Spread around a tent or a checkpoint, they make an empty base feel occupied and busy.

Following CheckMate rules, every item keeps real scale and tidy geometry, so dropping props into a scene needs no adjustment. Painted metal and canvas surfaces respond well to dust, mud and scratches added in a texturing pass. Hazy daylight with dust in the air brings out the matte military finish.

Three versions are available, the main pack and two numbered sequels that carry still more army equipment. New in 2026, the set supports war films, tactical shooters and training simulations that need believable clutter. Using all three lets a large base be dressed without repeating the same crate.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026
