About This Model

A collection of Toyota alloy wheels with tires, covering several spoke designs found on the brand's cars and SUVs. Spoke patterns, center caps and tire sidewalls give each wheel a recognizable look when vehicles are customized. Having many designs together makes it easy to compare rim styles or swap them on a car body.

Built to CheckMate specification, wheels come as separate objects, each ready to bolt onto a vehicle hub. Because the Toyota emblem is visible, these models are restricted to Editorial Uses Only, covering news and reviews. Metallic paint, machined faces and rubber can be tuned to match real factory finishes.

Released in 2024, the wheels suit automotive journalism, tuning articles and comparison pieces about trims and models. Turning a wheel to a slight angle shows both the spoke face and the tire tread. Editorial illustrators also use them to explain wheel size options when a new model launches.
