About This Model

A tall conical clown hat with pompoms and a ruffled brim, the party headwear that turns any character into a circus act. The 4,156 polygons and 4,696 vertices cover the cone, the fluffy balls and the frilled edge without heavy density. Animation is included, so the hat can wobble or bounce on a head instead of sitting stiff.

The 6 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 give the fabric its stripes, fuzz and bright colors. Passing CheckMate Pro means clean naming, a correct head-sized scale and UVs that never overlap. The pompoms are separate pieces, which makes swapping their color or size a quick job.

Released in 2018, the Clown Hat 3D Model suits birthday party renders, circus animations and comedic game characters. Tilted on the side of a head, it adds instant mischief to any portrait. Party supply shops and animators giving a character a silly accessory both choose it for its readable silhouette.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2018 · Updated 17 November 2026
