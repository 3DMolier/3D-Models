About This Model

These tall tapered stone pillars recreate the classic Egyptian obelisk, a needle of granite capped with a small pyramid at the top. The whole set takes only 329 polygons, so a row of them can line a temple avenue without slowing the viewport. Such a light build suits distant landmarks, strategy game maps and mobile scenes where every face counts against the budget.

With 333 vertices, the shafts stay crisp and straight, and hieroglyph detail is meant to come from your own textures or normal maps. Each face of the pillar owns its own space in the unwrap, so carved inscriptions can be painted down it without stretching. TurboSquid granted it CheckMate Pro after checking that the scale, pivots and naming hold up in a production scene.

Released in 2022, the obelisks fit history documentaries, museum walkthroughs and educational animations about the pharaohs of Luxor and Karnak. Game artists place them at palace gates, desert ruins and city squares, since Rome and Paris also display obelisks from Egypt. Weathered sandstone materials make them look ancient, while polished granite suits a freshly erected monument in a reconstruction.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2022 · Updated 18 November 2026
