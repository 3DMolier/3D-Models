About This Model

Rows of metal waiting chairs are collected here, the joined seats found in airports, clinics and train stations. The metal seats and shared beams give each row a sturdy, institutional look that viewers know at once. Placed in long lines, they turn an empty hall into a believable public waiting area within minutes.

Seat colors and metal finishes are material based, so gray, black or chrome looks are quick to set. TurboSquid inspected the chairs for CheckMate Pro, verifying scale and clean geometry across the whole set. The pieces share a single design language, so mixing them in one scene always looks consistent.

Put together in 2019, the collection suits airport archviz, hospital lobbies, bus terminals and government office scenes. Game levels can line the chairs along walls to build tense waiting moments before a story event. For $9, the collection furnishes a whole waiting room or departure lounge at once without extra modeling.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026

Yes. It is sold under TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, which covers commercial use in games, film, advertising and visualization without per-use fees.

Can the Metal Waiting Chairs Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

What is the Metal Waiting Chairs Collection model typically used for?

Stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

How clean is the geometry on the Metal Waiting Chairs Collection model?
