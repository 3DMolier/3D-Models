About This Model

Priced at $9, the Craft Envelope with Address Mockup is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 244 polygons and 246 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The object measures 16.76 x 11.62 x H0.28 cm, modelled at true scale. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
