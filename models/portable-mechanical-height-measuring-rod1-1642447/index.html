About This Model

A portable stadiometer for measuring standing height, with a vertical graduated rod and a sliding head plate on a base. Clinics and school health rooms use this kind of rod because it breaks down for transport between sites. The sliding headpiece is the key visual element, showing exactly where the measurement is taken for each child.

The asset is StemCell certified, which means its materials render consistently across major engines without rebuilding shaders. The scale markings along the rod make it read as a measuring device even at a distance or in soft focus. The base plate is flat and wide, giving the patient a stable place to stand.

The Portable Mechanical Height Measuring Rod(1) costs $19 and joined the range in 2020. It fits pediatric checkups, sports physicals, nutrition surveys, school health days and medical training visuals. Standing beside a scale in an exam room, the rod completes a believable growth screening corner.
