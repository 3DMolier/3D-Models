About This Model

A lead is a communication device rather than a restraint, which is why the hardware matters so much: the clip, the width, the length and whether it gives at all decide how much the dog on the end of it can actually tell the person holding it.

This is a collection listing rather than a single object, supplying collars and leashes as static pieces. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $59 the set suits pet product and retail visualization, e-commerce imagery, advertising, character accessories, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2015.
