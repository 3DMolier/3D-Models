About This Model

The Apple AirPower Wireless Charger is the long white pad with rounded ends, designed to charge a phone, a watch and earbuds at once. Its 20,612 polygons and 21,176 vertices keep the soft edges, the slim profile and the cable port smooth. The product was famously announced and then cancelled, so the pad has a cult status among tech fans.

3 maps at 2048 x 2048 provide the glossy white top, the matte base and the subtle charging zones. UV islands sit clear of each other, and the pad has been checked as a CheckMate Pro file. Its plain surface also makes a good test object for soft studio lighting.

Since 2018 tech reviewers, accessory designers and app explainers have bought 14 copies. Lay an iPhone, an Apple Watch and AirPods on top to recreate the complete promised setup. The Apple brand means editorial use only, which suits tech blogs and product history pieces.
