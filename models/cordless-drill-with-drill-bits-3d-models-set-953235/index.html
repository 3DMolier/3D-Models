About This Model

A battery drill paired with a spread of bits covers the classic workbench shot, from DIY tutorials to hardware store promos. The drill has a pistol grip, a chunky battery pack under the handle and a keyless chuck at the nose. The bits come as separate pieces you can lay out in a row on the bench. Keeping them apart means each bit can sit in the chuck or on the table.

The set is built to the CheckMate specification, so scale, naming and pivots behave predictably when the pieces are imported together. Spiral flutes on the bits are the detail the camera notices first, and they are shaped to catch a rim light. The trigger and the direction switch are modeled as distinct forms rather than painted on.

Priced at $59 the set first went on sale in 2015 and has gone to 12 buyers. It turns up in renovation explainers, packaging renders and game levels where a garage or workshop needs believable clutter on the shelves.
