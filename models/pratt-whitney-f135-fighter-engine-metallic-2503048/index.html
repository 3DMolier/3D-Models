About This Model

The F135 is the most powerful fighter engine ever built, and a bare engine is one of the most demanding subjects in aerospace modeling: fan blades, compressor stages, accessory gearbox, external plumbing and the segmented nozzle at the back all have to be present, because an engine shown out of an airframe is being examined rather than glimpsed.

The mesh runs 676,705 polygons and 701,623 vertices in quads and tris. The UVs overlap, which is a sound economy on machinery built from repeated blades and identical fasteners, and the StemCell build means it works in a real-time engine.

This page carries a second static build and two rigged versions at $249, for when the fan or the nozzle petals have to move. The static model at $219 covers aerospace marketing and engineering presentation, trade show and investor material, technical education, documentary work and games.
