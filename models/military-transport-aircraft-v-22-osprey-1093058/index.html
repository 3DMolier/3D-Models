About This Model

The Osprey is the only production aircraft that flies as a helicopter and cruises as a turboprop, and everything odd about its shape comes from that: enormous proprotors on rotating nacelles, a wing that folds, and a fuselage sized for a squad and a ramp. The model keeps the nacelle geometry, the ramp, the sponsons and the refuelling probe.

The mesh is efficient at 91,432 polygons and 96,299 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 18 texture maps - 4 at 8192x8192 and 14 at 4096x4096. That texture weight against a light mesh is the right trade for an aircraft covered in stencils, panel lines and exhaust staining.

Three other builds share this page, including rigged versions at $179 for when the nacelles rotate. Aimed at defence visualization, documentary and news graphics, film and television, training material and games. On sale since 2017.
