About This Model

The Skeletal and Circulatory System is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 174,584 polygons and 173,414 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
