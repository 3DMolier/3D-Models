About This Model

Pizza Packaging Octagonal Black is one of the Containers & Storage models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, architectural visualization and product rendering. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.

Geometry weighs in at 8,000 polygons over 4,000 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
