About This Model

Priced at $29, the Slice Watermelon is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 1,492 polygons and 1,485 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 7 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
