About This Model

Valve Stem is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $15. Geometry weighs in at 12,292 polygons over 12,383 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and technical visualization as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
