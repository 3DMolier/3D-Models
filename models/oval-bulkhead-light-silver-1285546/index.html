About This Model

Oval Bulkhead Light Silver belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 26,551 polygons and 26,792 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 9 textures, authored at 2048 x 2048 and 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
