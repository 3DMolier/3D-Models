About This Model

Female Caucasian Surgeon 2 belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $69 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 20,025 polygons and 21,022 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 22 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.
