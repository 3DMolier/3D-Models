About This Model

Looking for a Architecture Landmarks asset? The PC Power Cord Europe is a finished, render-ready model at $19. The mesh carries 21,864 polygons and 11,180 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, advertising and virtual reality. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
