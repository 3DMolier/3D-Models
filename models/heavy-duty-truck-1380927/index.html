About This Model

This Vehicles model - Heavy Duty Truck - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $119 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

Counted at the source, the model is 259,910 polygons and 288,905 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 8 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
