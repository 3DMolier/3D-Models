About This Model

A compact bicycle tail light with a red lens and a seat post clamp, the safety lamp that makes riders visible at night. Its 7,540 polygons and 3,917 vertices define the faceted reflector lens, the rubber strap and the power button. Clipped under a saddle, it adds a small but convincing detail to any bike model.

Non-overlapping UVs give the lens facets and the housing their own areas for glow and plastic materials. The light has CheckMate Pro certification, keeping the topology clean and the parts easy to attach. A glowing red lens on a dark road is the shot this light was built for.

Riders choose between three versions at $19: the Bike Tail Light, a Sigma Blaze USB rear light and the same Sigma light turned on. The switched-on version saves the work of setting up emissive materials for night shots. Since 2023 the lights have appeared in cycling safety videos, night ride animations and bike shop renders.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2023 · Updated 16 November 2026
