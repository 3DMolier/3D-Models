About This Model

This Other model — Rhino Rigged for Cinema 4D — is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $169 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
