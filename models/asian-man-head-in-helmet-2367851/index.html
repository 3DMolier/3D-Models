About This Model

The Asian Man Head in Helmet is a realistic male head wearing a fitted helmet, created for medical and scientific illustrations. Its 21,071 polygons and 22,546 vertices go into the facial features, the skin and the helmet shell around the skull. Shown in profile against a clean backdrop, the head works as a clear subject for diagrams and explainers.

Made in line with the CheckMate specification, the head and helmet sit as separate, well named objects. Non-overlapping UVs keep facial skin detail and the helmet surface free of stretching. Neutral studio lighting reveals the facial features without distracting shadows on the helmet.

Three versions come together: this Asian man, an Asian woman head in helmet and a male head in helmet. Choosing among them lets you represent different people in the same medical presentation. Released in 2025, it suits neuroscience explainers, medical device visuals and safety research.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 14 November 2026
