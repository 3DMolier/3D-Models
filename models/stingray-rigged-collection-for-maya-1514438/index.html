About This Model

Stingray Rigged Collection for Maya brings together rigged stingrays ready to glide across a sandy seabed in Maya scenes. Their flat diamond bodies and long whip tails make them unmistakable against a blue water background. A rig lets the wide fins ripple, which is what sells the motion of a swimming ray.

The set was built to CheckMate specification, so geometry is clean and the animals sit at believable scale. Mottled backs and pale bellies give the creatures contrast when lit from the surface above. Their slow, gliding movement makes them ideal background life for calm underwater shots.

Two versions are offered at $89 each, this Maya collection and the Stingray Rigged Collection for Cinema 4D. Picking the right package saves converting the rig between programs, which rarely survives the trip intact. Available since 2020, they populate aquarium visualizations, underwater documentaries and nature education apps.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
