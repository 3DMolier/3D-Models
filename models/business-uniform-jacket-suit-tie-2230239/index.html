About This Model

A business uniform jacket worn with a collared shirt and tie, ready to dress characters in offices, banks and hotels. The Business Uniform Jacket Suit Tie keeps to 12,651 polygons and 13,091 vertices, light enough for staff crowds in large scenes. Notched lapels, a buttoned front and a neatly knotted tie define the formal outline from across a room.

Non-overlapping UVs make it easy to add a company crest, pinstripes or a subtle wool texture. No texture maps come with the suit, so color and fabric remain your decision. Following the CheckMate specification, the garment keeps clean naming and geometry for quick edits.

A 2024 addition, the uniform suits hotel reception scenes, airline and bank staff, and corporate training animations. Put it on a doorman or concierge and a lobby feels staffed and welcoming at once. Game designers dress non-player characters in it to mark security staff, managers or clerks.
