About This Model

The 757 found its second life as a freighter. Airlines retired them from passenger service and cargo operators bought them precisely because the aircraft climbs steeply, carries a heavy load and gets into short runways that nothing else of its size can use.

The airframe is light at 90,621 polygons and 95,565 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 10 maps, one at 8192x8192 and 9 at 4096x4096. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $129 it suits logistics and air freight visualization, flight simulation, airport scenes, advertising, film backgrounds and editorial illustration. On sale since 2016.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2016 · Updated 4 September 2026
