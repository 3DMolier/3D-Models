About This Model

A collection of Range Rover SUVs, the upright luxury off-roaders with the floating roof and clean slab sides. Their tall glasshouse and short overhangs read as premium and capable at the same time, which makes them easy to spot in traffic. Several vehicles in the set give variety to a driveway, a hotel entrance or a country estate.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms real-world scale and clean topology across every vehicle in the collection. Rigged wheels and steering let the cars drive, corner and park instead of sitting as display pieces. The Land Rover design and badges belong to the manufacturer, so the license is Editorial Uses Only.

Released in 2023, the set suits automotive journalism, documentaries, driving simulation and film scenes in estates or city centers. A muddy forest track or a wet London street both suit these vehicles, which live between town and country. Rigged Range Rover Landrovers Collection saves building a matched fleet when a story needs several of them on screen.
