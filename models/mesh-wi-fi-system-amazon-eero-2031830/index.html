About This Model

Mesh Wi-Fi System Amazon Eero is one of the general models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. You are getting 17,704 polygons and 9,329 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering.
