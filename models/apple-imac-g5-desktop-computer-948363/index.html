About This Model

The white all-in-one desktop from the era of the first iPods, with its display and computer merged into one slim panel on an aluminum foot. A budget of 66,919 polygons and 74,650 vertices shapes the rounded bezel, the angled stand and the vents along the lower edge.

Ten texture maps at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024 cover the glossy plastic, the screen and the small logo details. The UVs avoid overlaps, so screen content can be swapped for any interface or wallpaper inside a render. Its CheckMate Pro status guarantees tidy geometry, which keeps the curved casing smooth under studio reflections.

The Apple iMac G5 Desktop Computer has been offered since 2015 under the Editorial Uses Only license. It fits retro office interiors, technology history documentaries and editorial illustrations of early home computing. Set on a wooden desk with a keyboard nearby, the machine instantly dates a scene by its shape alone.
