About This Model

Priced at $49, the Tactical Thermal Scope with Eyecup Black is a finished Weapons model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 30,452 polygons and 31,262 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 9.0 x 33.18 x H12.1 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing.
