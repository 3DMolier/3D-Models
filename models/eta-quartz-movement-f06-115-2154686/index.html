About This Model

A Swiss quartz watch movement with a round brass plate, a battery compartment and the stem that sets the dial hands. The 81,476 polygons and 41,591 vertices capture tiny gears, jewel settings and the coil of the stepping motor. Shown without a case, the mechanism reveals the miniature engineering that keeps a wristwatch ticking.

This movement ships untextured, letting gilded plates, steel screws and red jewels be shaded with care. Clean UV islands allow engraved caliber markings and fine circular graining on the main plate. CheckMate Pro status keeps every component at true scale, ready to be pulled apart for an exploded view.

Watchmaking content has drawn on this caliber since 2023, from repair tutorials and parts catalogs to horology explainers. Placed on a watchmaker's mat beside tweezers and a loupe, it sets up a precise workbench. Animators can explode the parts outward to show how a vibrating quartz crystal drives the gear train and hands.
