About This Model

A curtain of orange pumpkin lights hanging from a wire, ready for Halloween windows, haunted house parties and spooky porches. The 21,587 polygons shape small jack-o-lantern bulbs with carved faces that glow through emissive shaders. Strands hang at varied lengths, so the curtain looks hand-hung rather than machine perfect.

StemCell certification locks the orange plastic and glow look across every format in the download. Clean, overlap-free UVs let you bake brighter faces or swap the pumpkins for ghosts or bats. With 21,929 vertices, several curtains fit across a whole house facade without slowing the viewport.

At $29 since its 2026 release, the curtain suits seasonal renders, party planning visuals and Halloween game levels. It spans 202.21 x 19.1 x H150.21 cm, a good fit for a living room window or a front door. Hang it behind a candy bowl on a porch and the night instantly feels festive and eerie.
