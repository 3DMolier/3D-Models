About This Model

A friendly cartoon Earth with rounded continents and bright blue oceans, it suits kids' science, ecology messages and app icons. The globe carries 11,633 polygons on 6,200 vertices, a triangulated build where land masses rise slightly above the sea. Clouds and continents are simplified into soft shapes, giving the planet a toy-like charm at any size.

Its unwrap keeps every region separate, so oceans, land and ice caps can be recolored without bleeding together. Materials set the playful colors, letting the planet become greener for an eco campaign or warmer for a desert theme. TurboSquid checked the globe manually and placed it at CheckMate Pro for clean geometry and naming.

Since 2022 the globe has illustrated climate lessons, Earth Day posters and geography games for young learners. Animators spin it slowly in title sequences about travel, recycling or protecting the planet. Charity and school projects also use renders of it to explain why forests and oceans matter.
