About This Model

Grunge Broken AT-AT Star Wars is a detailed Space & Sci-Fi asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 229,892 polygons and 123,167 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
