About This Model

The Freestyle LSP3B Portable Projector is a detailed Electronics & Gadgets asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $24. You are getting 30,284 polygons and 15,834 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
