About This Model

A collection of compact portable projectors, the palm-sized devices people carry to beam movies onto a bedroom wall. Small lens barrels, vent grilles and control buttons give each projector a slightly different personality within the set. Having several designs together lets a tech store shelf or a review comparison look varied without extra modeling.

Prepared to CheckMate specification, each projector arrives as a clean object that can be placed independently. Matte plastic bodies and a glass lens respond well to simple PBR shaders and a soft studio light. Add a light cone and a projected image on a wall and the scene explains itself instantly.

Available since 2024, the collection serves gadget review content, home cinema visuals and electronics catalogs. A projector on a nightstand pointed at a blank wall tells the story of a cozy movie night. Game interiors set in modern apartments can use them as small interactive props.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
