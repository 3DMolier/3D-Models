About This Model

A small sport boat resting on a road trailer with rollers, winch post and lights, built for marinas, driveways and lake trips. The Boat Trailer With Sportboat holds 824,966 polygons and 437,652 vertices, split between the glossy hull, the cockpit and the trailer frame.

The trailer reads well from the side, where the wheels, mudguards and keel rollers line up under the boat. Hull stripes and registration numbers can be added cleanly, since the unwrap keeps every piece separate. CheckMate Lite certification confirms the mesh is valid and the file opens with nothing missing.

Four versions share the page, led by this rig at $129, while a Crossover With Boat Trailer costs $299. The bare trailer costs $99, and a Crossover Generic without the boat sells for $129. On sale since 2022, it has 10 buyers in automotive, marina and summer holiday scenes. Parked at a ramp with the stern facing the water, it sets up a launch scene in one placement.
