About This Model

Priced at $29, the Nepenthes Alata in Hanging Pot is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 21,687 polygons over 21,381 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.
