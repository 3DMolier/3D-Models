About This Model

This bundle gathers flexible LED strip lights, the thin self-adhesive tapes used to light shelves, coves and the undersides of kitchen cabinets. The LED Strip Light Collection shows the tiny diodes spaced along each strip, the narrow circuit tape and the end connectors that join sections together.

CheckMate Lite certification confirms every strip opens without missing parts and with a usable scene structure. Because the diodes are modeled as separate small shapes, they can carry an emissive material. That way they actually light a scene rather than simply sitting in it. Long straight runs suit architectural lighting, while shorter pieces fit inside furniture.

First listed in 2019, the collection has sold 11 times at $49. Interior designers use it for kitchen and bedroom renders, while electronics shops and DIY tutorials use it to show the product itself. A set saves modeling each strip type by hand and keeps the diode spacing consistent across a project.
