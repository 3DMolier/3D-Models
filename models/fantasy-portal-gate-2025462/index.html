About This Model

Fantasy Portal Gate is one of the Space & Sci-Fi models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
