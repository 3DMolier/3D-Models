About This Model

Looking for a Space & Sci-Fi asset? The Fantasy Portal Gate is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 44,271 polygons and 22,734 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

Questions About This Model

What is the Fantasy Portal Gate model typically used for?

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Fantasy Portal Gate mesh?

44,271 polygons and 22,734 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

What does StemCell mean for the Fantasy Portal Gate model?

It means one source asset, several delivered formats, and a PBR material setup designed to read consistently across them - no rebuilding shaders after import.

The product page states what ships with each format. StemCell delivery means a PBR material set that reads the same across renderers. See the listing for the exact contents.
