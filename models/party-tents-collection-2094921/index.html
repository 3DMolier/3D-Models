About This Model

A collection of party tents for garden weddings, corporate picnics and outdoor fairs, gathered as a single purchase. Each tent shows the familiar frame of poles and a peaked canopy, with side panels that define the shelter. Stretched fabric and pole joints are modeled, so the silhouettes hold up against a bright sky.

CheckMate Pro certification means the tents passed inspection for clean topology, real units and tidy naming. You can line several canopies along a lawn and they will match each other in scale without manual adjustment. Interior space stays open, so tables, chairs and a dance floor fit underneath without collisions.

Part of the range since 2023, the set supports event planning previews, venue marketing and festival environments in games. String lights and a few round tables turn any of them into an evening reception in minutes. Rental companies also use such tents to show clients a layout before the trucks arrive on site.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
