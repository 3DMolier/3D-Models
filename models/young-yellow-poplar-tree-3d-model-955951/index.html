About This Model

Young Yellow Poplar Tree is one of the Nature & Plants models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 381,017 polygons and 508,375 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

20 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
