About This Model

Golden Dessert Fork is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $9. Geometry weighs in at 1,644 polygons over 824 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
