About This Model

Priced at $169, the Tactical Artillery Walker Dual Armament is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 263,799 polygons over 283,109 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
