About This Model

A collection of wine coolers for keeping bottles chilled, made for kitchens, restaurants and bar counters in interior scenes. Each cooler is its own object, so one can sit on a counter while another stands beside a dining table. Seen with a bottle in place, each piece explains its purpose instantly, even in a wide interior shot.

As a CheckMate Pro collection, it carries the top marketplace certification for clean topology and organized files. Brushed steel and glass surfaces need an environment to reflect, so an HDRI with a window works well. The models share consistent scale, so they line up neatly when you arrange several along a bar back.

Sold since 2022, these coolers fit restaurant design, home kitchen renders, wine shop interiors and catering scenes. One purchase covers several styles, so a scene can mix a classic and a modern design without hunting further. Add a few glasses and a corkscrew nearby and the table is ready for a tasting evening.
