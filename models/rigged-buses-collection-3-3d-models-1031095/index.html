About This Model

A set of rigged city buses for traffic scenes, covering the long low-floor shapes that carry commuters through most modern cities. Buses are big, slab-sided vehicles, so wheel arches, doors and window rows are what make each one read correctly. Having several in the collection keeps a street from showing the same bus at every stop.

CheckMate Pro certification ensures the buses arrive at real-world scale with clean topology and logical names. The rigs let each bus roll, steer and pull up to a stop, which is how buses usually appear on screen. Route numbers and operator liveries can be swapped on the panels to match a specific city.

Available since 2016, the collection suits urban traffic visualization, transit planning presentations, driving games and city backgrounds in film. A bus pulling into a shelter with passengers waiting tells a city story in a single frame. Rigged Buses Collection 3 3D Models saves building a fleet for bus depots, terminals and busy avenues.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
