About This Model

A blooming gerbera daisy on a slender stem, with a wide ring of bright petals around a dark center. The tall stem and soft leaves give the flower a natural lean, so it looks picked from a garden rather than molded in plastic.

The model is built to CheckMate specification, which keeps its scale and object names in order on import. Gerbera petals are long and narrow, and the shape keeps their slight curl toward the tips.

This flower arrived in 2026 and suits florist branding, spring greetings, garden scenes and interior decor shots. A lone bloom in a slim vase gives a kitchen or office table a quiet spot of color. Motion designers use it in short loops where the flower sways in a light breeze. Several stems grouped together build a flower shop bucket or a birthday bouquet. Recoloring the petals turns it into pink, orange or white varieties for bouquets and wedding tables.
