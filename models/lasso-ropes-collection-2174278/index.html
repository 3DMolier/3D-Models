About This Model

A collection of coiled lasso ropes, the looped lariats a cowboy hangs from the saddle horn or swings above a running steer. Each rope keeps its honda knot and the open noose, the details that separate a lasso from an ordinary coil. Several coil shapes in one purchase mean a ranch scene does not repeat the same rope on every post.

The set carries the CheckMate Lite rating, which confirms the files open cleanly and match their published formats. Twisted fiber is mostly a texture job, so the ropes stay light enough to hang on fences, saddles and barn walls. Natural hemp, worn tan or dyed rodeo colors all come down to a material change.

In the range since 2024, the ropes serve western games, rodeo animation and frontier film sets. A lasso on a hook does a lot of storytelling, placing a scene in cattle country before any character appears. Royalty-free licensing lets the collection go into commercial projects of any size.
