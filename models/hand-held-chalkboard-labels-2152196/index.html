About This Model

Looking for a 3D asset? The Hand-held Chalkboard Labels is a finished, render-ready model at $9. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 3,504 polygons and 1,754 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
