About This Model

Priced at $29, the Rusty Square Tub is a finished Containers & Storage model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
