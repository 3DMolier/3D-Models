About This Model

The Amenhotep III Seated Statue Black Museum Display is a production-ready Signage & Decor 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. You are getting 128,318 polygons and 127,841 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 249.33 x 248.53 x H309.29 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and architectural visualization. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
