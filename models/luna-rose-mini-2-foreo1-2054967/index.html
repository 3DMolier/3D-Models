About This Model

A compact silicone facial cleansing brush in soft rose pink, modeled as the LUNA Rose Mini 2 Foreo(1) for beauty and skincare scenes. The device has a rounded paddle shape with touch-point bristles on the face and a smooth body with a single control button. It fits in the palm, which makes it easy to place on a bathroom shelf, a vanity tray or in a hand.

The model is built to the CheckMate specification, so the geometry arrives clean for rendering in common packages. Silicone reads best with a soft subsurface or satin shader, and the pink tone can be matched to the real product. Because it is a branded product, the license is Editorial Uses Only, covering news, reviews and educational material.

This model went on sale in 2023 and sits in the medical and personal care section of the collection. Skincare blogs, dermatology explainers and editorial pieces about beauty gadgets are where a realistic cleansing device belongs. Paired with a towel and a cream jar, it builds a believable morning routine in a bathroom render.
