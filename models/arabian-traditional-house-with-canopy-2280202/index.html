About This Model

A flat-roofed desert house with a shaded canopy over the entrance, this building sets the mood of an old Arabian quarter. The model carries 167,258 polygons and 176,334 vertices, spent on the walls, the window openings and the cloth canopy stretched on poles. Thick clay walls and small windows suggest a home built to stay cool in heat.

StemCell preparation means the plaster and wood materials survive the move between renderers. The UVs are flat and free of overlaps, so cracks and sand buildup can be painted along the base of the walls. Group a few of these along a narrow street and the scene gains a believable town.

Two versions come in this set, the realistic Arabian Traditional House with Canopy and a cartoon version for stylized projects. Released in 2024, either one can be bought for $29 and dropped into a scene. Historical films, desert game levels and travel animations use houses like this.
