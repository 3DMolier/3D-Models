About This Model

Seashell Venus Comb Murex reproduces the spiny shell whose long rows of thin spikes give it its comb-like name. It needs only 7,640 polygons and 7,538 vertices, and the spines keep their sharp, delicate taper even in macro shots.

This StemCell build comes with a PBR material set intended to look consistent across popular render engines. Non-overlapping UVs keep the pale shell surface free of seams along the spiral body and the slender canal. Placed on sand or a bathroom shelf, the shell catches backlight through its spines and casts a distinctive shadow pattern. Collectors prize this species for that fragile lattice, so close-up framing shows it at its best.

Shell collectors get a choice of two versions priced at $49: this white shell and the Murex Venus Comb Seashell Beige Beige. It has been available since 2024 and has already sold to one buyer. Natural history displays, beach scenes, aquarium dressing and marine biology lessons are typical places for it.
