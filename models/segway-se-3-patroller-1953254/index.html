About This Model

Priced at $89, the Segway SE 3 Patroller is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 188,384 polygons over 102,046 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
