About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Human Small Intestines is a finished, render-ready model at $39. You are getting 8,080 polygons and 8,082 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
