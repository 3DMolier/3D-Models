About This Model

Rescuer Sitting Pose belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 9,085 polygons and 9,804 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
