About This Model

The Police Badge is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 8,416 polygons and 4,210 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
