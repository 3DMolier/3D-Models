About This Model

ChargePoint EV Charging Plug is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. The mesh carries 28,558 polygons and 29,595 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. It ships with 11 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game environments. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.
