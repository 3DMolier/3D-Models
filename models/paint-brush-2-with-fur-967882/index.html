About This Model

A flat paintbrush with a wooden handle, metal ferrule and fur-based bristles, ready for art studio and decorating scenes. The Paint Brush 2 with Fur keeps the solid parts at 1,200 polygons and 1,245 vertices, leaving the bristles to a fur system. Real strands spread and catch light at the tip, which a solid bristle block never manages.

Clean, separate UV islands allow paint drips on the handle and wear on the crimped ferrule. Its CheckMate Pro rating shows TurboSquid inspected the geometry, naming and scale before release. Tinting the tip strands with wet paint color makes the brush look freshly used.

On sale since 2015, the brush appears in painting tutorials, renovation scenes, art supply shots and artist studio interiors. Rest it across an open paint tin, stand several in a jar, or hold one against a half-painted wall. Note that fur strands need a renderer with hair support to show the bristles as intended.
