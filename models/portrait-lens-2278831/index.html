About This Model

Portrait Lens is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. The mesh carries 39,535 polygons and 42,439 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
