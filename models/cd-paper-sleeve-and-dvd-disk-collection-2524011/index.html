About This Model

Paper CD sleeves with a window and loose discs give a music, software or archive scene its everyday media props. The whole set uses only 1,749 polygons and 1,744 vertices, so dozens of them can fill a shelf without strain. The sleeves show a round see-through window, and each disc has the central hole and stacking ring of a real one.

Labels and print are left blank because no texture maps are supplied, which suits custom album or software artwork. UVs avoid overlap entirely, so a disc face and a sleeve front each take a clean slot for printed designs. Its build meets the TurboSquid CheckMate specification on proportions, so the sleeves match a real disc in size.

Added in 2026, this is one of the newest media sets, prepared for packaging previews and music release mockups. Musicians and labels use it to present a new record, while studios scatter discs across desks in office scenes. In games and films about the early digital era, a pile of sleeves dates a room immediately.
