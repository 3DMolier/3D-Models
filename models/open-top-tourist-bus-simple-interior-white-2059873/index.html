About This Model

White is the base color tour operators buy and then wrap, because the vehicle changes route, city and branding faster than it wears out. An unwrapped open top bus is therefore the most useful version for a mockup: it is what the livery goes on.

The model holds 284,722 polygons and 163,602 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, with a simple interior, and carries StemCell certification.

At $99 it suits livery mockups and fleet presentation, tourism and travel imagery, urban and architectural scenes, advertising, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2023.
