About This Model

A tablet wrapped in a folding leather case, ready for desks, classrooms and creative workspaces. The Apple Leather Case with iPad uses 44,038 polygons and 48,311 vertices on the stitched cover, hinge folds and the device itself. The soft fold of the cover gives the slim tablet a more personal, well-used look.

The model passes CheckMate inspection, and the tablet and its case remain separate named parts you can hide or swap. Rounded leather corners and a slight puff along the edges show the thickness of real hide. It sits naturally on a sofa arm, a kitchen counter or an office desk beside a keyboard.

On sale since 2026 at $49, it is covered by Editorial Uses Only because it shows genuine Apple products. Tech reviews, magazine illustrations, films and documentaries are where the tablet fits. Placed beside a laptop and a phone, it completes a believable mobile workspace for a freelancer or student.
