About This Model

Looking for a Ships & Boats asset? The Waverider 1060 GRP is a finished, render-ready model at $149. Counted at the source, the model is 239,753 polygons and 134,577 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
