About This Model

This Characters & People model - Cyborg Child Reading in Rocking Chair - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $169 on TurboSquid. You are getting 359,266 polygons and 366,386 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
