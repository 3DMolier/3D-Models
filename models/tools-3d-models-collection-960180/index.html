About This Model

Looking for a Model Bundles & Sets asset? The Tools Collection is a finished, render-ready model at $99. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. The set covers several related objects at once, which saves assembling a scene from separate purchases.
