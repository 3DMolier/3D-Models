About This Model

The X-Ray Body Scanner is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $34 on TurboSquid. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for medical education, training simulation and VR experiences. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 35,304 polygons and 17,994 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
