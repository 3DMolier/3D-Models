About This Model

Animated Crocodile Collection gathers crocodiles with ready motion, from lurking in shallows to crawling across a riverbank. Armored scutes along the back, a long snout and powerful tail give each reptile its prehistoric silhouette. Low bodies hugging the ground make them ideal for tense shots where the camera sits at water level. Rows of teeth visible along the closed jaw add menace even in calm poses.

TurboSquid certified these reptiles at CheckMate Pro level, a check of clean geometry, logical naming and dependable setup. Ready animation saves the slow work of keying reptile locomotion, where legs splay sideways from the body.

Available since 2019, the crocodiles suit wildlife documentaries, jungle adventure games and zoo education screens. Placed half-submerged near a drinking animal, a crocodile builds suspense without moving at all. Biology teachers can use the clips to explain how these reptiles hunt patiently from ambush along riverbanks.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
