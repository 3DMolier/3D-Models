About This Model

The warehouse robot did not automate picking, it automated walking. Instead of a person going to the shelf, the shelf comes to the person, and an entire building can be laid out with no aisles because nothing human has to move through it.

The model holds 114,502 polygons and 115,152 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 12 maps all at 4096x4096, supplied with the rack. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $49 it suits warehouse and logistics visualization, automation and robotics presentation, equipment marketing, training material, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2020.
