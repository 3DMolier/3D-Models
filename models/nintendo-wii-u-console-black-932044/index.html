About This Model

Nintendo Wii U Console is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 19,182 polygons and 20,794 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2015 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
