About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Hospital Bed with Mattress Flat is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos. First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection.
