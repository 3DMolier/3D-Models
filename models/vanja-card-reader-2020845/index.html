About This Model

Priced at $19, the Card Reader is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 24,580 polygons over 12,346 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.
