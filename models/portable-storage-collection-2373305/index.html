About This Model

A collection of portable storage pieces for organizing tools, supplies and personal items, useful in garages, workshops and moving scenes. Having several containers in one set keeps their style consistent when they are stacked on shelves or along a wall.

The pieces were made to CheckMate requirements, so the files open with clean structure and logical names. Stackable, boxy shapes read well at any distance and make a background feel organized rather than empty. Lids, handles and edges give each container enough detail for medium shots without overloading a scene.

Part of the range since 2025, the set serves home organization content, storage product mockups and interior game levels. Stacked in a corner, the containers suggest a household in the middle of moving or decluttering. They also fill a storage unit or a basement, where repetition looks natural rather than lazy. Mixing sizes across a shelf adds rhythm and stops a long wall of storage from looking flat.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
