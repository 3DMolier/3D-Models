About This Model

A yellow sewer auger with a large cable drum, the trailer-sized machine municipal crews tow to clear main sewer lines. Its drum, motor housing and towing frame form a compact unit that parks beside a manhole in the street. Bright yellow paint marks it as municipal or utility equipment, visible to traffic during roadside work.

The build respects CheckMate guidelines, with drum, frame and wheels separated into clearly named objects. Cable can be shown coiled on the drum or snaking into an open manhole for an action scene. Repainting the housing in orange, white or company colors takes only a material change.

Released in 2025, the auger suits city maintenance videos, utility company presentations, roadwork scenes and open-world games. Surround it with traffic cones and a lifted manhole cover, and the street repair job explains itself. Training departments also show such machines to new crews before their first day in the field.
