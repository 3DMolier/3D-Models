About This Model

Elbit Hermes 900 UAV is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. Geometry weighs in at 80,191 polygons over 85,809 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
