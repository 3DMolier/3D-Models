About This Model

Silicone Caulk Tube is one of the Tools models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. You are getting 4,209 polygons and 4,258 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

2 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
