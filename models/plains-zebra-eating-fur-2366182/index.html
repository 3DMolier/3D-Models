About This Model

A grazing zebra spends most of the day with its head down, which is the pose almost nobody models. The stripes are what everyone notices, but the useful detail here is the posture: an animal eating is an animal that has decided nothing is hunting it.

The model is very light at 11,702 polygons and 12,181 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so a herd can be built without weight. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $99 it suits wildlife and nature visualization, documentary and education work, safari and tourism imagery, game environments, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
