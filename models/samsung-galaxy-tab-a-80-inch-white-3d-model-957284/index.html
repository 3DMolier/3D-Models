About This Model

The white edition of a small Samsung tablet, built at 10,153 polygons with the bright bezel framing an 8.0 inch display. A white front makes the dark screen pop, which helps when the device rests on a pale desk or kitchen counter. Its 10,862 vertices cover the speaker grille, the camera lens and the gently curved back.

Real-world scale comes with the CheckMate Pro stamp, along with tidy topology that subdivides without pinching. Each part has its own UV space, so a cracked screen or a case scuff can be painted precisely. Lighting artists get logically named materials, which shortens setup when the glass needs a custom reflection.

Available since 2015, it fits school projects, family living rooms and retail explainers about mid-range devices. Because Samsung holds the marks and design, usage falls under Editorial Uses Only for news and teaching. Pair it with the titanium edition to show a family choosing between colors on a store shelf.
