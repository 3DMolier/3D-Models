About This Model

A burgundy tuxedo jacket with black trousers, displayed as a full outfit for formal wear and fashion scenes. Its 10,360 polygons trace lapels, buttons and trouser creases while staying light for clothing store scenes. Satin lapels contrast with the matte jacket body, the classic detail of evening wear.

The CheckMate build keeps 10,838 vertices clean, which helps if you adjust fit or sleeve length. Velvet or satin shaders on the jacket work best with procedural fabric patterns, given the mixed UVs. Jacket and trousers remain separate pieces, so either garment can appear on its own.

New in 2026 at $79, the tuxedo suits menswear catalogs, wedding visualizations and gala event renders. The outfit spans 113.78 x 30.74 x H155.62 cm, sized for an adult wearer. Place it on a mannequin in a boutique window and turn it slightly so the lapel sheen catches the camera.
