About This Model

A collection of Chinese paper lanterns in round and oval shapes, each with ribbed sides, a top cap and hanging tassels. The ribs give every lantern its pleated silhouette, and the red color ties the group to festival celebrations. Several sizes in one set let a street scene feel layered instead of evenly spaced.

The lanterns follow the CheckMate specification, which keeps scale and object names consistent across the group. Each lantern is its own object, so you can string them along a wire at different heights.

Available from 2023, the collection serves Lunar New Year scenes, restaurant interiors and night market games. Glowing above a narrow alley in a light rain, they turn a plain street into a festival. Wedding and garden party renders can hang them from tree branches for warm overhead light. Animators can sway them gently in the wind to bring a still courtyard to life.
