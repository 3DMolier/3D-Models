About This Model

A DSLR-style camera fitted with a battery grip and mounted on a full-height tripod, ready for studios and behind-the-scenes shots. The Camera with Battery Handle and Tripod uses 40,045 polygons and 41,630 vertices on the body, lens barrel, grip buttons and leg locks. The battery handle adds a vertical shutter release, a detail photographers recognize instantly.

StemCell certification keeps the rubberized grip and metal materials consistent across renderers and engines. Extended tripod legs with locking clamps give a stable, professional stance on any floor. It works in a photo studio, on a wedding venue set or in a vlogger room facing a desk and ring light.

Released in 2026 at $49, it can be used freely under Royalty Free terms. Film production scenes, photography tutorials, studio visualizations and content creator rooms are typical places for it. Placed beside softboxes and a backdrop, it completes a professional shooting set at once, ready for a portrait session.
