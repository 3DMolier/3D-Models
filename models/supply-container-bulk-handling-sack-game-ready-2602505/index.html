About This Model

A large woven bulk bag with lifting loops, the industrial sack used to move sand, grain and building materials by crane or forklift. The Supply Container Bulk Handling Sack Game Ready is efficient at 8,247 polygons, suited to filling construction yards in real time.

Its 7,238 vertices shape the bulging sides and the four corner straps that let a forklift pick it up. Thanks to StemCell delivery, the bag comes in multiple formats generated from one source with identical materials. The sagging fabric suggests a heavy load inside, which sells the weight of the bag in a scene.

Since 2026 the bag has cost $44 and suits construction games, farm scenes and logistics visualizations. Stack a few beside a cement mixer, or hang one from a crane hook during a lift. Material suppliers can show customers how bulk deliveries arrive on site without staging a photo shoot. Low geometry keeps a whole yard of these sacks practical for mobile and console games.
