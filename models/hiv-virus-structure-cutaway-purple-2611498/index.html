About This Model

A purple HIV virus cutaway that reveals the inner capsid and genetic material, built for biology teaching and medical animation. The 95,685 polygons divide between the spiked outer envelope and the cone-shaped core visible through the open section. The removed wedge exposes layered structures, so viewers see inside the virus without losing its round outline.

The CheckMate build keeps 99,953 vertices organized, with envelope, matrix and core as separate parts for teaching. Partial UVs suit translucent purple shaders that let light pass through the outer layers in soft studio light. Animate the layers sliding apart to explain how the virus is built from the outside in.

Listed for $34 from 2026, the cutaway suits immunology courses, pharmaceutical presentations and science museum displays worldwide. It measures 20.63 x 20.8 x H20.43 cm as delivered, ready to enlarge for a cinematic close-up. Pair it with a whole virus model to show the outside and the inside in one sequence.
