About This Model

The Women Handbag White is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Counted at the source, the model is 28,519 polygons and 29,152 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 6 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
