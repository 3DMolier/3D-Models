About This Model

A black solid-body electric guitar resting on a folding metal stand, ready for rehearsal rooms, stages and bedroom studios. The Electric Guitar with Metal Stand uses 85,654 polygons and 87,212 vertices on the body, frets, pickups, tuners and stand joints. Strings and hardware are real geometry, so close shots across the fretboard hold up.

StemCell certification means the gloss lacquer and chrome hardware shaders translate between renderers. The single-cutaway body shape and carved top give the instrument its classic rock silhouette. The stand cradles the neck and lower bout the way a real one does, so the pose looks stable.

Eight versions exist, and four of them come with stands at $59, including the Gibson Electric Guitar with Wood Stand. The other four are guitars alone at $49, such as the red Classic Single Cut and the blue Les Paul Standard. Music videos, studio interiors, concert stages and rhythm games are typical homes for any of them, all licensed Royalty Free since 2026.
