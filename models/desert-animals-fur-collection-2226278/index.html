About This Model

A collection of desert animals with groomed fur, gathered for dune, savanna and arid canyon scenes in one purchase. Large ears, slim legs and sandy coats are the adaptations that make desert species look right in hot landscapes. Grouping several animals together saves matching style and scale across separate purchases for a single habitat.

Each animal follows CheckMate specification, so sizes stay plausible when smaller and larger species share a dune. Fur gives the coats soft volume that catches warm low sun, the light most desert scenes use. Sand-toned coats need careful color separation from the ground, or the animals vanish into the background.

This desert pack appeared in 2024 at a price of $189, and no royalties apply. Nature documentaries, zoo exhibit planning, educational apps and desert game levels all use animals like these. A lone animal cresting a dune at sunrise, with long shadows trailing behind, makes a classic wildlife shot.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
