About This Model

The Slide Down Skier Generic is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 140,740 polygons and 145,807 vertices, a light load for a scene. Maps: 17 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
