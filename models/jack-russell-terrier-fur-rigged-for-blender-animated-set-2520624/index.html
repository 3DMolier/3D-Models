About This Model

An energetic Jack Russell Terrier with white and tan fur comes prepared for Blender with a rig and animation. This little dog gives family and pet stories a lively companion full of character. The compact body, folded ears and alert stance capture the breed's restless energy.

Fur strands follow the body shape and break up the silhouette, which keeps the dog from looking like a plastic toy. Blender animators get rig and motion together, ready for running, jumping and head tilts. Natural daylight brings out the contrast between the white coat and the tan patches on the head.

Released in 2026 at $419, the terrier works for client projects and teaching material alike. Pet store visuals, dog training explainers, family films and casual games are good fits. Catch it mid-leap after a ball on a green lawn and the shot shows exactly what the breed is about.
