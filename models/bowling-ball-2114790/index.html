About This Model

Priced at $4, the Bowling Ball is a finished Sports & Recreation model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 4,244 polygons over 2,124 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
