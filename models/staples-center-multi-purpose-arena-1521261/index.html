About This Model

The Staples Center Multi-Purpose Arena is a production-ready Architecture Landmarks 3D model, priced at $249 on TurboSquid. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

At 164,991 polygons and 200,195 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 21 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
