About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The AgustaWestland AW139 Helicopter is a finished, render-ready model at $149. You are getting 517,198 polygons and 563,447 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
