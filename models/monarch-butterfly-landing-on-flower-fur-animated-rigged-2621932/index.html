About This Model

A monarch butterfly approaches a blossom with wings spread wide, capturing the moment just before it lands. The Monarch Butterfly Landing on Flower Fur Animated Rigged holds 35,241 polygons and 39,356 vertices shared between insect and plant. Monarch migration stories often open on exactly this kind of close, hovering shot.

The arrangement spans 61.16 x 17.79 x H69.69 cm, with the flower stem setting the overall height of the piece. Fine fur on the body softens the silhouette, matching how real monarchs look in sunlight. Being CheckMate certified, the scene comes with tidy object names and real-world units for any pipeline.

Offered in 2026 at $149, this landing pose is sold as one version alongside other monarch scenes. Wildlife channels, butterfly conservation projects, spring title sequences and botanical renders use it to bring motion into a garden. Open wings show the full orange pattern, the most recognizable view of the species.
