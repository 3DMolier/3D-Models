About This Model

Mobile Pallet Stretch Wrapping Robot is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. You are getting 84,384 polygons and 87,281 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
