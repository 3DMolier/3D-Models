About This Model

Baseball caps printed with political party symbols, gathered in one set for election coverage and campaign-season scenes. The caps share one curved-brim shape, so a crowd can wear them without the hats looking mismatched. Each cap carries its emblem on the front panel, where a camera picks it up first.

Every cap follows the same CheckMate specification, so sizes and names match across the set. That makes the caps easy to place on character heads of different sizes with a quick uniform scale. The fabric reads as cotton twill, and the materials are simple enough to recolor for a local party.

Published in 2025, the caps fit news graphics, political explainers and rally crowd scenes in film. Mix a few caps into a crowd and the event reads as a political gathering without a single sign. Neutral emblem placement means rival caps can sit together in one frame for balanced coverage.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Updated 12 November 2026
