About This Model

An antique horse-drawn sleigh with curved runners and a padded seat, made for winter villages, holiday cards and period films. The body sweeps up at the front into a scrolled dash, while long shafts reach forward for the horse. At 104.52 x 186.32 x H110.86 cm the sleigh is built at true size with 35,590 polygons.

The sleigh meets the CheckMate specification, with a mixed UV layout over 37,519 vertices. Polished wood, iron runners and soft upholstery give three clear materials to dress. A dusting of snow on the seat and runners makes the vehicle look parked rather than placed.

Antique Horse Sleigh is offered with one other version, the Vintage Winter Sleigh, and each sells for $49. Published in 2026, the sleigh suits Christmas animations, folk tale illustrations and snowy game towns. Lit by lanterns at dusk, it brings a warm old-world mood to any winter scene.
