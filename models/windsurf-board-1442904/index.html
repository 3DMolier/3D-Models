About This Model

Windsurf Board belongs to our Sports & Recreation range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 4,461 polygons and 4,777 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 4 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
