About This Model

The Walking Beach Pier is a production-ready Architecture & Landmarks 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 431,895 polygons over 449,034 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
