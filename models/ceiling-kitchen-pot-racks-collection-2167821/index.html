About This Model

A set of hanging pot racks for kitchen ceilings, the metal frames that keep pans and ladles within reach above an island counter. Several rack designs come together in one purchase, so different frames can be tried over the same kitchen island before settling on one. Each rack can be placed separately, scaled to the ceiling height, or instanced along a long galley kitchen.

The bundle meets TurboSquid CheckMate Lite, the baseline check for sensible naming and files that open as expected. Hooks and chains are where these objects earn their realism, because empty racks look wrong unless something hangs from them. Leave a few hooks bare and fill the rest with copper pans to make the space feel lived in rather than staged.

Available since 2023, the collection serves interior visualization, restaurant kitchen layouts, cooking show sets and furniture catalog renders. In a wide shot the racks also break up an empty ceiling plane, adding a layer of depth between the camera and the cabinets.
