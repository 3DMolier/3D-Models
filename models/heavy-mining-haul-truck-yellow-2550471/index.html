About This Model

This Industrial Equipment model - Heavy Mining Haul Truck - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $189 on TurboSquid. You are getting 896,425 polygons and 985,870 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 8.24 x 14.88 x H7.3 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
