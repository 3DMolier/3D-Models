About This Model

Priced at $19, the Galvanized Steel Round Tub is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 6,836 polygons and 6,782 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
