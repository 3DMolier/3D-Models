About This Model

This collection brings together the Apple iPhone Xs Max in its finishes, the large stainless steel phone with a notched edge-to-edge screen. Gathering the color options in one bundle lets a render compare finishes or show a lineup side by side. The tall glass back and polished steel band are the features that make this generation recognizable at a glance.

The bundle carries CheckMate Pro certification, TurboSquid's highest check for structure and scale. As a branded product, the collection falls under Editorial Uses Only terms. Each phone sits as its own object, ready to lie on a desk, rest in a hand or stand on a dock.

These phones have been listed since 2019 and remain a reference for that design generation. Tech reviewers, repair guides and journalism about smartphones use the set for comparisons and teardown explanations. Film and game artists hand the phones to characters, where a familiar device grounds a contemporary scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2019 · Updated 12 November 2026
