About This Model

This Characters & People model - Plague Doctor - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $179 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

At 45,302 polygons and 47,067 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Maps: 22 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
