About This Model

This Lighting model - Energy Saving Light Bulb - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, event visualization and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits interior and architectural visualization, product rendering and game environment dressing.

Geometry weighs in at 9,050 polygons over 9,201 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
