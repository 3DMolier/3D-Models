About This Model

Priced at $29, the Dyson Airblade 9KJ is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 6,260 polygons and 6,553 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.
