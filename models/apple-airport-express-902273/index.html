About This Model

A compact white wireless base station with softly rounded corners and a status light on the front, sized to sit on a desk or shelf. The model spends 8,206 polygons and 8,773 vertices on the beveled shell and the ports along the back. Small hardware like this is easy to overlook, yet it makes a home office or living room desk look lived in.

The 6 maps come at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048, covering the glossy plastic, the port labels and the indicator. Non-overlapping UVs and CheckMate Pro certification mean the router imports cleanly with readable names and real-world scale. Its flat square footprint lets it sit snugly beside a modem, a speaker or a stack of books.

The Apple AirPort Express has stayed on the market since 2015 and now also works as a retro gadget for tech history pieces. It suits home network diagrams, interior renders and tech review scenes that need a believable router next to a laptop.
