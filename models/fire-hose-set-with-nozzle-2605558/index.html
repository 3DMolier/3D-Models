About This Model

A coiled red fire hose with brass couplings and a nozzle, ready for fire stations, safety cabinets and rescue scenes. The 12,480 polygons go into the flattened hose layers, coupling threads and the nozzle grip. The hose winds in a tight roll, the way crews store it for quick deployment.

This CheckMate-approved mesh carries 12,728 vertices in neat parts that separate nozzle from hose. Woven jacket patterns are easiest to add through procedural fabric shaders over the partial UVs. Metal fittings contrast with the red fabric, giving the set strong readable details.

Offered from 2026 for $34, the hose set suits firefighter training, industrial safety renders and action games. Animators can rotate the nozzle to show the spray pattern change during a rescue scene. The set measures 152.88 x 76.35 x H62.4 cm, a coil to lay beside a hydrant or hang in a station bay for authentic detail.
