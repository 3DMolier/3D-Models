About This Model

Priced at $139, the Sherp ATV with Trailer Beige is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 413,827 polygons over 423,423 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
