About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Compact Track Loader Bobcat is a finished, render-ready model at $89. You are getting 489,298 polygons and 295,132 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
