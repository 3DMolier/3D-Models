About This Model

A collection of lounge chairs for furnishing living rooms, hotel lobbies and waiting areas with seats that share a relaxed, low posture. Lounge chairs sit lower and deeper than dining chairs, and that reclined shape is what the set is about.

CheckMate specification compliance means correct scale, clean geometry and a file that opens without fixing. Each chair is its own object, so you can place one in a reading corner or line several along a lobby wall. Upholstered seats and armrests are where fabric materials matter most, and they can be retextured to match an interior palette.

The set has been offered since 2024 for $34, much less than sourcing comparable chairs separately. Interior visualization studios use such seats to soften hard architectural renders and give rooms a human scale. In games they work as background furniture in apartments, lounges and airport terminals. A pair of matching lounge chairs facing each other is often enough to define a conversation area in a plan render.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
