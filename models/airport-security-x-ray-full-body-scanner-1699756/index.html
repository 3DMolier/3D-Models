About This Model

The body scanner is the machine passengers stand inside with their arms raised, and its shape is instantly recognisable: two curved transparent panels forming a booth, a footprint mat on the floor, an operator display on one side. The transparency is the design point - security wants the person visible while the machine works - and modeling it as real panels rather than a solid box is what makes it read correctly.

The mesh is light at 27,013 polygons and 28,640 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, which suits a machine that appears in rows along a checkpoint rather than alone.

A rigged build sits on this page at $69 for when the scanner panels have to rotate. The static model at $59 covers airport and terminal visualization, security industry presentation, film and television, training material and games. On sale since 2021.
