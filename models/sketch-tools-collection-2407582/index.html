About This Model

A collection of sketching tools such as pencils, erasers and charcoal sticks, built for artist desks, classrooms and drawing studios. Sharpened tips, worn erasers and smudged charcoal give the tools a used, honest working character. Spread across an open sketchbook, the set instantly signals creative work in progress at the desk.

Following CheckMate rules, each tool is a clean, separate object that is easy to arrange in any composition. Wood, graphite and rubber materials respond well to the soft daylight of a studio window. Pencils of different lengths suggest a working artist who wears tools down, rather than a fresh box from the shop.

Listed during 2025, the tools suit art school interiors, tutorial thumbnails, stationery catalogs and creative workspace scenes. A pencil resting across an unfinished drawing invites viewers into the artist's process. Pencil shavings scattered nearby add a final, satisfying touch of authenticity to the desk.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
