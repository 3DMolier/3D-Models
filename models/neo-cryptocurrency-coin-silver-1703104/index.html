About This Model

This general model - NEO Cryptocurrency Coin - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $18 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 624 polygons and 602 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering.
