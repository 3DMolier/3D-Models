About This Model

Refrigerant oil has to be matched to the gas, and getting it wrong destroys a compressor. The newer refrigerant required a different lubricant entirely, which is why the packaging shouts the specification louder than the brand.

The model is extremely light at 954 polygons and 984 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $19 it suits automotive service visualization, packaging and label mockups, e-commerce imagery, workshop scenes, technical material and editorial illustration. On sale since 2021.
