Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Minox B Subminiature Spy Camera Silver is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
