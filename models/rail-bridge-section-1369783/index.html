About This Model

This Architecture & Landmarks model - Rail Bridge Section - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 318,540 polygons and 333,290 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

11 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
