About This Model

This male anatomy model layers skin over a full skeleton and the internal organs, so lessons can peel the body back step by step. Ribs, spine and pelvis frame the heart, lungs and digestive tract in their natural positions inside the torso. Turning the skin transparent shows how organs sit against bone, a view students struggle to picture from flat diagrams.

TurboSquid reviewed this body at the CheckMate Lite level, checking that the scene loads correctly and the mesh has no broken faces. Separate parts for skin, bones and organs make it easy to color each system differently in an educational render. Clinicians often hide groups in turn to walk a patient through a diagnosis or a planned surgery on screen.

At $469 the model serves medical schools, anatomy apps and publishers producing textbook figures and animated explainers. First offered in 2019, it also supports VR training, pharmaceutical presentations and museum displays about the human body. Fitness and physiotherapy creators use it to show how posture affects the spine and the organs packed around it.
