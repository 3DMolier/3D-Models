About This Model

Climbing Rope is one of the Sports & Recreation models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 39,424 polygons and 19,736 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
