About This Model

A set of satin pointe shoes with long ribbons, the hard-toed slippers dancers wear to rise onto the tips of their toes. The pair runs to 147,875 polygons and 163,395 vertices, spent on the pleats around the toe box and the soft folds of the ribbons.

Nine maps at 4096 x 4096 bring out satin sheen, the scuffed platform and the stitching on each sole. With nothing overlapping on the UVs, worn patches from practice can be painted exactly where they belong. The CheckMate Pro stamp means the shoes come grouped and named, ready to hang from a hook or lie on a floor.

The set costs $79, joined the catalog in 2016 and has gone into 13 dance projects since. Ballet school promotions, theater posters, dancewear shops and tender coming-of-age films are the typical uses. Tied together by their ribbons and slung over a shoulder, the shoes tell of long hours in the studio.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2016 · Updated 14 November 2026
