About This Model

A collection of children's whiteboards, the small easel and tabletop boards kids use for drawing, letters and first sums. Bright plastic frames, marker trays and rounded corners make them safe-looking and cheerful in any playroom or nursery. Some boards stand on legs like a tiny easel while others lie flat on a desk, so a classroom can mix activities.

As CheckMate Lite models, the boards open at correct scale with tidy, usable geometry for interior scenes. The white surfaces can carry drawings, alphabets or math exercises as simple texture swaps for each scene. Soft, even light keeps the glossy board readable without hot spots hiding what is written.

Offered since 2024, the set suits kindergarten design, home schooling renders, educational apps and family animations. A child's scribble on the board makes a playroom feel lived in instantly and adds a personal touch. Teachers preparing lesson visuals can use the boards to show handwriting steps or simple drawing games.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
