About This Model

A heavyset teenage boy in a casual shirt and jeans, a realistic everyday character for streets, schools and homes. The body takes 95,861 polygons, giving the belly, the shirt creases and the denim folds believable weight. He stands 93.77 x 36.54 x H143.26 cm, a young teen height that sits right among classmates and parents.

Non-overlapping UVs across 102,783 vertices allow unique wear on the jeans and prints on the shirt. Topology meets CheckMate specification, keeping clean loops where the arms and legs need to bend. Overcast daylight suits him, softening the clothing folds and keeping the scene casual and approachable.

This character arrived in 2025 and suits health education, school campaigns, urban crowds and slice-of-life games. Plus-size figures are rare in stock libraries, so he helps scenes look like real neighborhoods. Give him a backpack and a phone, and he becomes a believable student walking home from class.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2025 · Updated 19 November 2026
