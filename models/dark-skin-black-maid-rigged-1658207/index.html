About This Model

Dark Skin Black Maid Rigged is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $199. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 17,177 polygons and 18,079 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
