About This Model

A group of home and office air purifiers in assorted tower and box shapes, each with intake grilles and a simple control panel. Together they cover the shapes a modern room usually has tucked beside a sofa or under a desk. Rounded corners and soft matte finishes match the look of current appliance design.

The collection carries CheckMate Lite certification, which confirms that naming, scale and basic topology meet a clean shared standard. Grilles and vents carry most of the modeled detail, since that pattern tells a viewer the box is a purifier. Plain plastic shells keep the materials easy to recolor for any brand palette or interior scheme.

It has been offered since 2023 at $59 for the whole set. Interior designers, appliance retailers and healthy-home explainers place these units to make a space feel clean and lived in. Swapping between models also lets a product configurator show several sizes side by side without building anything new.
