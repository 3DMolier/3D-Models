About This Model

Laptop with Detailed Inner Structure is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. You are getting 945,446 polygons and 483,350 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and product rendering.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
