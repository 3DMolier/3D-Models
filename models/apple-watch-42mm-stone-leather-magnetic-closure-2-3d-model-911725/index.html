About This Model

A smartwatch wearing a stone-colored leather band with magnetic closure, the light gray strap that wraps around and holds itself shut. The band's soft padded surface hides the magnets inside, so the strap looks seamless rather than punched with holes. That pale tone works well against dark sleeves or a white desk, where the watch should not disappear.

Certified CheckMate Pro, the file met the more demanding TurboSquid requirements for geometry, naming and organization. A soft leather shader with fine grain suits the band, while the case takes a polished metallic finish. Shown curled as if worn, the strap displays its overlapping end, which explains the magnetic fastening at a glance.

Editorial Uses Only is the license here, as with any branded device, so news and review content suits it. Available since 2015, the watch costs $34 as a single download on TurboSquid. It fits reviews of wearable accessories, fashion editorials about tech and explainers comparing different band styles.
