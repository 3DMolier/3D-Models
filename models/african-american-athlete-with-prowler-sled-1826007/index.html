About This Model

An African American athlete driving a prowler sled captures the raw effort of strength and conditioning training in a gym. The model uses 58,793 polygons and 60,798 vertices, split between the muscular athlete in training gear and the weighted steel sled. His low forward lean with arms locked on the sled handles shows the exact moment of a heavy push.

StemCell certification provides native formats with matching PBR materials, so the athlete looks consistent across different render engines. Separate UV space with no overlaps lets you change clothing colors, add sweat or swap the plate finish.

Dating back to 2021, the set suits fitness app visuals, gym interior renders, sports science lessons and training videos. Turf lanes, rubber flooring and harsh overhead lights make the most convincing setting for this intense moment. Personal trainers and physiotherapists use images like this to explain safe posture during sled pushes. Coaches also like the sled for teaching acceleration, because the leg drive is so clearly visible from the side.
