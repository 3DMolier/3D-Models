About This Model

A complete skateboard paired with skate shoes, giving street culture scenes both the deck and the footwear in one set. The board shows its concave deck, trucks and wheels, while the shoes carry the flat soles and padded collars skaters prefer. Keeping them together means the gear matches in scale and style without any hunting for compatible pieces.

The set passed CheckMate Pro inspection, so topology is clean and proportions match the real gear. Each item stays separate, so the shoes can sit by a ramp while the board leans against a wall. Grip tape, rubber and canvas surfaces respond well to simple material changes for custom graphics.

First offered in 2020, the set suits skate park environments, youth lifestyle visuals and sports retail renders. Game artists use such gear as pickups or bedroom dressing, while animators drop it beside a character after a trick. A low camera angle along the deck makes the trucks and wheels the hero of the shot.
