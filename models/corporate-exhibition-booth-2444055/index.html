About This Model

Corporate Exhibition Booth is one of the Furniture & Interior models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 531,216 polygons and 573,244 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
