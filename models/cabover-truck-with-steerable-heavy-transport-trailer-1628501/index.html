About This Model

A cab-over tractor pulling an empty modular trailer with steerable axles, the type used for oversized loads such as transformers. The 551,901 polygons cover the cab, the long deck, rows of small wheels and the gooseneck connecting trailer and tractor. With 616,714 vertices, the count runs about a tenth above the faces, as expected from many separate wheel assemblies.

The trailer is empty, so any heavy cargo from a wind turbine blade to a factory press can be placed on deck. CheckMate Lite from TurboSquid confirms the essential checks, a scene that opens properly with understandable object names. UV islands are kept distinct, so mud and wear on the tires and side rails come out cleanly.

First published in 2020 for $179, the rig suits heavy haulage companies and wind energy logistics teams planning routes. Engineers simulate turning circles on narrow roads with it, and trade show visuals present the transport capacity of a fleet. Trucking games and construction documentaries also use it for scenes of huge loads crossing a bridge.
