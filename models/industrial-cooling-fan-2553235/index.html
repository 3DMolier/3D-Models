About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Industrial Cooling Fan is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 5,414 polygons and 5,705 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That sits in low-poly territory, so it stays cheap to instance across a crowd scene or a game level. 5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and game assets. It is useful for industrial and factory visualisation, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
