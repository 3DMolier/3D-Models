About This Model

A collection of traditional Chinese oil-paper umbrellas with bamboo ribs and painted paper canopies for festive and cultural scenes. Several umbrellas come together, so a lantern festival street or a tea house entrance gets variety without extra modeling. Thin radiating ribs under the canopy are the detail that makes these props instantly recognizable.

CheckMate Pro credentials mean the collection was checked for clean topology, naming and real-world scale. Swap the canopy artwork for plum blossoms, cranes or calligraphy to match a specific region or story. Backlit paper glows softly, so a little translucency in the material adds warmth to evening shots.

Traditional Chinese Paper Umbrellas Collection was introduced in 2023 for Lunar New Year scenes, wedding decor and historical dramas. Hang a few upside down above a courtyard or line them along a bridge for a festival atmosphere. Dance performances and cultural heritage exhibits also feature these umbrellas as elegant hand props.
