About This Model

This Aircraft model - DJI Inspire 2 with Zenmuse X7 Camera - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 80,973 polygons and 82,848 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 12 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
