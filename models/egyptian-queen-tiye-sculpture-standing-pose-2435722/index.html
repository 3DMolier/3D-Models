About This Model

Priced at $39, the Egyptian Queen Tiye Sculpture Standing Pose is a finished Signage & Decor model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 4,414 polygons over 4,429 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 121.34 x 115.26 x H513.68 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
