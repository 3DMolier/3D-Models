About This Model

Folding step ladders in several heights let a renovation, warehouse or painting scene show work in progress without building props from scratch. The Step Ladders 3D Models Collection gathers these ladders into one purchase, each with splayed legs, flat treads and a hinged top.

Every ladder is a separate object, so one can lean against a wall while another stands open beside a half-painted ceiling. The set carries CheckMate Pro certification, granted after a manual inspection of geometry and scene setup. Treads and rails keep their real proportions, so a character's foot lands where a real foot would.

The collection has been available since 2016 and still fits interior renovation renders, hardware store aisles and construction training visuals. Game artists use ladders like these as climbable props or simple clutter in garages, attics and storage rooms. Placed half folded against a doorway, a ladder hints at a room under repair before any other prop appears. Mixing heights in one shot gives the scene a sense of scale that a lone ladder cannot.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2016 · Updated 12 November 2026
