About This Model

Priced at $7, the Metro Direction Sign is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 3,351 polygons and 3,650 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
