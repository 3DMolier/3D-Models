About This Model

This Irish coffee glass is a stemmed footed mug with a side handle, prepared as a printable model for home makers. A short stem lifts the bowl off the table, and the handle curves out from the side for a warm drink. Its tulip-like bowl is the shape bartenders choose for layered coffee with cream floating thickly on top.

Mesh counts are not given, though the glass is a single smooth form that slices cleanly for printing. Its build matches the CheckMate standard that TurboSquid applies, so the scale and object structure are ready to use. Printed in clear resin, the shape can stand in for real glass as a prop or a display piece.

Out since 2024 at $9, it suits printable kitchenware projects, bar prop builds and cafe scene renders. Makers print it as a decorative cup holder or a miniature for a model cafe on a shelf. Bartending courses also picture it when showing how an Irish coffee is layered in the glass.
