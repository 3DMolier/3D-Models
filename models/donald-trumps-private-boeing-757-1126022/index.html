About This Model

This Aircraft model - Donald Trumps Private Boeing 757 - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $129 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 93,597 polygons over 99,779 vertices - a light build for its class. Maps: 10 of them, peaking at 8192x8192, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
