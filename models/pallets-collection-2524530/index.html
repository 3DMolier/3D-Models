About This Model

This pallets collection gathers wooden shipping pallets of the kind stacked in every warehouse, loading dock and freight yard. Deck boards, stringers and blocks give each pallet the familiar slatted look that forklift forks slide under. Having several pallet designs in one pack makes it easy to fill a storage aisle without visible copies.

The pack follows the CheckMate specification, so every pallet arrives named, scaled and ready to stack on a forklift. Rough timber textures, stamped markings and dirt can be layered to make some pallets look newer than others. Stacked crooked against a brick wall, pallets also add grit to an urban alley scene.

Two versions are available, this first collection at $29 and Pallets Collection 2 at $69. The larger second collection suits big distribution centers where dozens of loaded stacks fill the floor. New in 2026, it serves logistics visualization, warehouse planning, industrial game levels and upcycled pallet furniture concepts.
