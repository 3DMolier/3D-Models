About This Model

An iPad Air tablet paired with its folding Smart Cover, the segmented magnetic flap that also props the screen up as a stand. Ipad Air Smart Cover 3D Models Set totals 21,938 polygons and 21,651 vertices, spent mostly on the rounded edges and the cover hinge.

UVs are free of overlaps, so a custom wallpaper on the display or a different cover color is simple to apply. The set follows the CheckMate standard, keeping real proportions and named parts that separate the tablet from its cover. No texture maps are bundled, so screen content and aluminum finish come from your own materials.

Its license is Editorial Uses Only, which points it toward tech journalism, device reviews, tutorials and documentary scenes. On sale since 2015, it also helps recreate a mid-decade office or classroom where tablets sat on every desk. Folded back behind the tablet, the cover shows its triangle-roll shape, a detail that keeps the device recognizable from the side.
