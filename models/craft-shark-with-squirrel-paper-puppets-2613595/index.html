About This Model

Folded paper puppets of a blue shark and a brown squirrel, ready for a kids craft corner or a classroom scene. The 5,886 polygons keep the crisp folds of the paper, with mouths that open like a hand puppet. Fins and tails stand out as separate folds, giving each animal a recognizable silhouette from the side.

StemCell certification guarantees consistent PBR shading across every delivered format, so the colored paper looks the same in each renderer. The UV layout has no overlapping islands, which makes it easy to paint new patterns or faces onto the puppets. The pair measures 9.13 x 32.57 x H11.75 cm, a natural size for small hands at a table.

On sale since 2026 at $29, the set suits educational videos, craft tutorials and cozy children's room interiors. Set them beside scissors and glue on a desk and the scene immediately reads as an art lesson. The flat sheets and sharp creases also suit stylized games that favor a handmade look.
