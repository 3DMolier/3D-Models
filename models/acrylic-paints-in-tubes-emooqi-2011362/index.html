About This Model

This set of acrylic paint tubes shows squeezable metal tubes with screw caps, the kind artists line up beside a palette. Its 62,328 polygons over 31,896 vertices go into crimped ends, threaded caps and the slight dents of soft tubes. Each tube carries its own color band, which makes the row read as a rainbow from across a studio.

Being a StemCell product, the set keeps caps and metal shading consistent between real-time and offline rendering. Non-overlapping UVs let you swap the printed wraps or add paint smears where caps were opened.

On sale since 2023, the tubes fit art studio interiors, craft tutorials and school classroom scenes. A painting desk with brushes in a jar looks complete once a few tubes lie open with paint squeezed out. Hobby store shelves and art supply flat lays can also arrange them by hue to show the full range. Children's craft animations benefit as well, because bright tubes signal creativity before a brush touches paper.
