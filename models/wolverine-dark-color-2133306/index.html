About This Model

The Wolverine Dark Color is a production-ready Animals & Creatures 3D model, priced at $99 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 34,742 polygons over 17,675 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
