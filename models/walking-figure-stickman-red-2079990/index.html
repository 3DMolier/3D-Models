About This Model

Priced at $9, the Figure Stickman is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 1,864 polygons and 938 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2023 · Updated 4 September 2026
