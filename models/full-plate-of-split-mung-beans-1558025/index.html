About This Model

A plate of loose pulses is one of the most expensive things to model honestly, because there is no way to fake it: every bean has to be an object, sitting on the ones below at whatever angle it landed. That is why this asset carries 1,511,096 polygons and 1,418,776 vertices for what looks like a simple plate of food.

It is built in quads and tris with mixed UV layout and ships with 10 texture maps - 8 at 4096x4096 and 2 at 512x512 - carrying the split faces, the skins and the slight colour variation between individual beans.

At $29 it suits food photography and recipe visualisation, packaging and label mockups, nutrition and health material, grocery and wholesale renders, and games. On sale since 2020.
