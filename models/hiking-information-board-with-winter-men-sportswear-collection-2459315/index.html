About This Model

A trailhead information board paired with men dressed for winter hiking, in insulated jackets, hats and technical trousers. The board is the wooden map stand found at the start of a mountain route, with a roof to keep snow off the panel. Together they set up a moment every hiker knows: stopping to read the trail map before heading out.

The bundle is made to CheckMate specification, so the figures and the board share scale and read naturally side by side. The map panel can take your own artwork, turning the board into a park notice, a resort sign or a route plan. Bright jackets against white snow are the main color note, so overcast light keeps the scene calm and believable.

Released in 2025, the collection suits ski resort advertising, outdoor apparel catalogs, tourism videos and winter levels in adventure games. Pine trees, a snowy path and a mountain backdrop are all a render needs to feel like a real trailhead. National park services and hiking apps can use it to illustrate safety notices or route information without staging a photo shoot.
