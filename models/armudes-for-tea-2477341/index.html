About This Model

Pear-shaped armudu glasses are how strong black tea is served across Turkey and the Caucasus, and this set captures that narrow-waisted silhouette. The 25,104 polygons build the flared lip, the pinched middle and the matching saucer, all modeled as thick clear glass. At 15.49 x 15.49 x H12.32 cm the glass and plate import at true tabletop scale for close shots.

UVs are allowed to overlap here, which suits repeating glass and gold rim shading but means unique decals need a fresh unwrap. The StemCell mark confirms consistent PBR materials across the delivered formats, with 24,850 vertices kept closed and watertight.

Two versions come with this page: Armudes for Tea and the Crystal Armudu Tea Cup with Plate, a cut crystal take. New to the catalog in 2025, the glass fits Turkish cafe interiors, breakfast tables, travel stories and close food renders with steam. Add a small spoon and a sugar cube on the saucer, and the frame tells the whole ritual.
