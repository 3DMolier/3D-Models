About This Model

The briefcase is one of the most persistent images of the cold war and one of the least accurate. What such a case actually contains is communication equipment and authentication codes, not a weapon, but the shorthand has outlived every attempt to correct it.

This is a collection listing rather than a single object, supplying the pieces as static items. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $59 the set suits film and television props, thriller and documentary production, game environments, editorial and historical illustration. On sale since 2025.
