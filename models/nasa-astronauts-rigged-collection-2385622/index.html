About This Model

A team of astronauts in NASA style spacesuits, rigged so each figure can walk, float or salute in space scenes. Several suited crew members in one pack let a mission story show a team rather than a single lonely explorer.

The suits carry bulky life support backpacks, gold visors and the layered fabric folds of real extravehicular gear. Rigging controls the arms, legs and helmet, so the astronauts can climb ladders or hold tools during spacewalks. Mission patches and flags on the shoulders add authentic detail even in mid-distance shots. Gloved hands are shaped to grip handrails and tools, a detail close-ups of a spacewalk depend on.

Released in 2025, the collection fits space documentaries, planetarium shows, science lessons and lunar base games. Pose one astronaut planting a flag and another photographing the moment, and the scene tells itself. White suits respond well to harsh single-source light, which mimics the sun on the lunar surface.
