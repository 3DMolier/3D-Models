About This Model

Brass became the material of precision mechanisms because it machines cleanly, does not rust and wears against steel without seizing. Every clock, instrument and early calculating machine used it, which is why the colour now reads as engineering rather than as decoration.

The model holds 75,976 polygons and 74,245 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and carries StemCell certification.

At $69 it suits technology and engineering illustration, museum and exhibition work, steampunk and period scenes, advertising, film props, game environments and editorial work. On sale since 2021.
