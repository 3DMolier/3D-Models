About This Model

Priced at $19, the Left-Facing Fist Emoticon is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 639 polygons and 628 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 1024 x 1024 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Left-Facing Fist Emoticon?

5 maps at 1024 x 1024, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Left-Facing Fist Emoticon mesh?

639 polygons and 628 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the Left-Facing Fist Emoticon model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
