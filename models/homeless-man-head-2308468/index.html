About This Model

The head of a homeless man with a tangled beard and wind-burned skin, a single strong character for street scenes. Deep wrinkles around the eyes and a sunken look give him years of hard living without a word of backstory. A head on its own lets you choose the body, the coat and the posture that fit your story.

CheckMate specification rules give the head true scale and quad-dominant flow that subdivides well. The scene structure is flat and grouped, so attaching it to a character rig is straightforward. Grime in the creases of the face matters most, so give it a sharp texture at close range.

Released in 2024, the homeless head suits urban dramas, charity appeals and survival game NPCs. Royalty free terms cover feature film, commercial games and nonprofit fundraising campaigns alike. Frame him under a bridge with a small fire nearby and the scene needs no dialogue.
