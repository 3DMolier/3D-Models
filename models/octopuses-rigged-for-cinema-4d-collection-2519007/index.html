About This Model

The octopus pack prepared specifically for Cinema 4D, so motion designers can open the scene and start animating tentacles. Each animal keeps the bulbous head, the siphon and long tapered arms lined with suckers along their undersides. Tentacles that coil and uncoil are a favorite in motion graphics, and this set suits that looping style well.

Assembled under CheckMate specification, the octopuses arrive at consistent scale, which helps when MoGraph cloners scatter them around. Cinema users get the native file instead of a conversion, avoiding broken materials and lost object hierarchy. Smooth, glossy skin responds nicely to the studio lighting setups common in product and title work.

This edition arrived in 2026, priced at $190 and licensed royalty free for commercial jobs. Motion design studios, broadcast intros, aquarium promotions and science explainers are the usual homes for it. A swirl of tentacles framing a logo or title is the sort of shot these animals were prepared for.
