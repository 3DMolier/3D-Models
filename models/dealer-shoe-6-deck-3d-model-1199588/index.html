About This Model

A casino card dispenser with a slanted front and a weighted slide, built to hold several decks for blackjack. The mesh uses 3,462 polygons and 3,515 vertices to shape the lip where each card slips out. Its angled face tips toward the players, so the top card shows clearly from a seated camera.

Its 2 maps at 2048 x 2048 give the translucent acrylic body, a darker base and faint scuffs from use. With no overlapping UVs, the color can change to match any table felt or casino palette. CheckMate Pro certification marks the mesh as clean, closed and ready for close tabletop shots.

On a blackjack table the shoe sits at the dealer's left, beside chip racks and a discard tray. It is a small piece, yet without it a casino set looks staged, and its slide lets animators pose a dealer's hand mid-deal. Priced at $9 and offered since 2017, it suits casino interiors, poker games and heist film previz.
