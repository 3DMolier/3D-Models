About This Model

This wooden sign boards collection gathers several blank planks and hanging signs, ready for shop names, directions or menu text. Having a variety in one set lets a village street or a tavern show different signs without repetition. Rough edges and visible grain give the boards a handmade look that suits rustic places.

The collection carries CheckMate Lite status, meaning the files open correctly and match their previews. Blank faces are the main appeal, since any text, logo or arrow can be painted on later. Warm lantern light at dusk makes the wood glow and the painted words easier to read.

Available since 2023, the set suits medieval games, farm stands and outdoor trail visualizations. Fixed to fence posts along a path, the boards guide visitors through a campsite or park. Fantasy cities also need them above inns, blacksmiths and markets to feel properly settled.
