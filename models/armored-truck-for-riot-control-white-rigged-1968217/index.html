About This Model

A white armored riot control truck with a raised cabin, protective grilles and a heavy front bumper for crowd scenes. Detail adds up to 622,930 polygons, spent on the armor plates, window guards, roof hatches and tall off-road tires. The count of 334,920 vertices sits below the polygon figure, a sign of triangulated hard-surface panels.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms real-world scale, clean topology and a tidy scene that opens without errors. Non-overlapping UVs mean the white paint can take police markings, dirt or soot without seams repeating across the body. Steel grilles over the glass cast hard shadows, so the cab looks protected even from a distance.

Released in 2022, the truck suits news graphics, law enforcement training, film crowd sequences and urban unrest levels in games. The white finish reads as neutral, so a client can add any national police livery without repainting. Park it across a street with barriers around it, and the tension of the scene is clear immediately.
