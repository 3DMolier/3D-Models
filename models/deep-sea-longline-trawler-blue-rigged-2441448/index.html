About This Model

Deep Sea Longline Trawler Blue Rigged belongs to our Ships & Boats range and is offered at $199 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 333,401 polygons and 352,209 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 13.94 x 47.22 x H36.6 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
