About This Model

The largest submarines ever built carry two pressure hulls side by side inside one outer casing, with the missile tubes between them and forward of the sail. The Russians called the class Akula, meaning shark; NATO called it Typhoon, and the second name is the one that stuck in the west.

The model holds 426,868 polygons and 228,579 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and rigged builds are offered separately, including native rigs for Maya and Cinema 4D.

At $99 it suits naval and defence visualisation, documentary and news graphics, simulation, film and television, and game environments. On sale since 2023.
