About This Model

Priced at $129, the Urban Electric Sedan Car Blue is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and game environments. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

Geometry weighs in at 240,824 polygons over 259,689 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
