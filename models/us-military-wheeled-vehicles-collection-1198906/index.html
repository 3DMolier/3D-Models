About This Model

US Military Wheeled Vehicles Collection gathers American trucks and utility vehicles for convoys, motor pools and forward operating base scenes. Boxy armored cabs, run-flat tires and cargo beds are the features that separate military trucks from civilian ones.

Every vehicle in the bundle passed a CheckMate Pro review carried out by TurboSquid by hand. Tan or green paint with dust along the lower panels gives the convoy a believable deployed look. Mixing vehicle types also helps a scene show the variety of jobs a modern army handles on the ground.

First listed back in 2017, the collection costs $119, and its license is fully Royalty Free. Military simulators, war films, defense logistics presentations and documentaries about modern armies are its main buyers. Lined up on a desert road, the vehicles form a convoy that reads instantly at any distance. Parked in rows at a motor pool, they fill a base scene without visible duplication.
