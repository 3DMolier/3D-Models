About This Model

This Car Radiator with Fan pairs an aluminum core of thin cooling fins with an electric fan in its shroud behind. The 37,975 polygons go into the finned core, the side tanks, the hose necks and the curved fan blades. Shown in an engine bay or on a workshop bench, it explains how a car keeps its engine from overheating.

Corrosion, dust and dented fins are easy to paint because its 39,468 vertices are unwrapped cleanly. Its CheckMate Pro grade ensures tidy geometry and recognizable names for core, tanks and fan. Spinning the fan in an animation adds motion to an otherwise static technical diagram.

Available since 2019, the radiator suits auto parts catalogs, mechanic training, repair tutorials and car building games. Coolant flow arrows drawn through the tanks make it a clear teaching aid for engine cooling basics. Parts stores and repair shops can use it to show replacement procedures step by step.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2019 · Updated 16 November 2026
