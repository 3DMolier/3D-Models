About This Model

A glossy presentation case for prestige champagne, this asset dresses up celebration tables, nightclub bars and gift scenes with a single object. Its 4,947 polygons and 5,127 vertices shape the rounded box, the hinged lid line and the raised spade emblem on the front. The case sits upright like a jewelry box, which makes it read as a luxury gift rather than shipping packaging.

Built to CheckMate specification at TurboSquid, the mesh follows the rules for clean faces, sensible scale and an orderly hierarchy. Non-overlapping UVs leave room for a metallic finish, embossed lettering and soft velvet lining inside the shell. A polished chrome or gold shader with sharp reflections does most of the work, so lighting matters more than texture detail here.

Introduced in 2025, the case fits VIP lounge interiors, wedding reception tables and luxury retail displays. Placed next to an ice bucket and two flutes, it tells the viewer at once that the evening is a special occasion. Product visualization artists also pair it with gift wrap and ribbon for holiday shop windows and boutique shelves.
