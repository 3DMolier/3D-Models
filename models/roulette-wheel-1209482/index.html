About This Model

Roulette Wheel is a detailed Toys & Games asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.

Geometry weighs in at 8,099 polygons over 8,814 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 16 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
