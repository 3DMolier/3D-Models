About This Model

The Eastern Firefly Rigged is a production-ready Animals & Creatures 3D model, priced at $99 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 100,886 polygons and 52,299 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
