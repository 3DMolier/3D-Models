About This Model

A set of boxed medicines, blister packs and pill bottles, ready to stock a pharmacy shelf, a bathroom cabinet or a hospital cart. Packaging props look simple, yet filling a pharmacy by hand means modeling dozens of boxes in slightly different sizes.

Every item meets the CheckMate specification, so box sizes stay realistic when shelves are stacked with many packs. Blank faces on the cartons accept your own fictional labels, which keeps scenes free of real trademarks. Soft overhead light and glossy plastic film on the blisters give the shelves a clean, clinical feel.

Five versions of the medicine set are offered: the starter set and four further collections that widen the assortment. Combining them fills a pharmacy wall with enough variety that no two shelves look the same. A pharmacist character behind the counter turns the props into a complete story. The packs were published in 2026, and the license is royalty free for healthcare visuals, games and film.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026
