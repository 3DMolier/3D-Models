About This Model

This Fork is a plain stainless dinner utensil with four tines and a gently curved handle, ready to set any table. Its handle widens toward the end for balance, and the neck dips before the head so food sits comfortably on the tines. Clean simple surfaces let the steel reflect a room well, which matters in a close shot of a dinner table.

Polygon data is not given here, though a utensil like this stays light even when repeated across a banquet table. TurboSquid holds the piece at CheckMate Pro after a person checked its size, naming and surface quality. Its polished finish comes from a metal shader, which you can push toward brushed steel or silver plate.

Part of the range since 2016 at $9, it suits restaurant interiors, food photography and dining scenes in films. Stylists lay it beside a plate of pasta and a folded napkin to frame a simple dinner shot. Game artists also repeat it along long tables in tavern and castle feast scenes where every place needs a setting.
