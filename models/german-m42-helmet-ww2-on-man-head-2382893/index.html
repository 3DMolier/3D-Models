About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Helmet WW2 Man Head is a finished, render-ready model at $59. Geometry weighs in at 18,577 polygons over 16,021 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
