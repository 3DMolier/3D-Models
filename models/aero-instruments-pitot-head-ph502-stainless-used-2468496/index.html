About This Model

This pitot head is the L-shaped probe that sticks out under an aircraft wing, measuring air pressure so the pilot can read airspeed. The Aero Instruments Pitot Head PH502 Stainless Used spends 19,120 polygons on the tapered probe, the mounting base and the drain holes. At 16.76 cm long, it sits on a maintenance bench or a wing panel with convincing proportion.

StemCell certification covers the stainless steel material, which stays consistent between rendering engines. Some UV islands share space, so unique scratches on each side need a small unwrap before baking. Dulled steel with heat discoloration near the tip shows years of flight hours and de-icing cycles.

Four pitot versions sell for $19 each, all variations on the same heated probe. Besides this used stainless head you get a silver heated tube, a used heated sensor and an L-shaped metallic tube. Available since 2025, the probe suits aircraft maintenance training, aviation explainers and hangar scenes.
