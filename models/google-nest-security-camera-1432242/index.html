About This Model

Security Camera is one of the Tools models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 8,293 polygons and 8,752 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
