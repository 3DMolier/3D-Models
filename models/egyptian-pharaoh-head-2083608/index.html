About This Model

This Characters & People model - Egyptian Pharaoh Head - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 23,418 polygons and 14,782 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
