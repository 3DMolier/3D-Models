About This Model

A four seat light aircraft is the workhorse of general aviation: high or low wing, fixed gear, a single engine and a cabin with two rows. It is the aircraft flying clubs own, private owners fly at weekends and students learn on, which makes it the most useful shape in the sky for anyone building an airfield scene.

The mesh runs 240,634 polygons and 251,744 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 28 texture maps - 5 at 8192x8192 and 23 at 4096x4096. Five maps at the largest size is generous for an aircraft this small, and it shows in the panel lines and the cabin glazing frames.

A rigged build sits on this page at $199. The static model at $149 covers general aviation visualisation, flight school and club material, airfield scenes, advertising and games. On sale since 2018.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published October 2018·Updated 20 August 2026

Stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.
