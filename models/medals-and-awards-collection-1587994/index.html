About This Model

This bundle gathers medals on ribbons and other awards, ready for ceremonies, honor rolls and victory moments on screen. Polished metal discs, embossed faces and striped ribbons give each piece the visual weight of a real achievement. Laid on velvet or hung around a neck, the awards read clearly in both close-ups and wide shots.

TurboSquid approved the medals at CheckMate Pro once topology, scale and scene structure met its review. Gold, silver and bronze finishes all come from metal shading, so a single medal can represent any place on the podium. Engraving areas stay plain, leaving room to add your own event names, dates or symbols in texture.

Available since 2020, the medal set costs $119 and is cleared for royalty free commercial use. Sports broadcasts and esports graphics use the awards in victory screens, and military dramas pin them on uniforms. Schools and corporate award videos also show them on stages during graduation and recognition ceremonies.
