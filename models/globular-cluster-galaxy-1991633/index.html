About This Model

Looking for a Space & Sci-Fi asset? The Globular Cluster Galaxy is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Geometry weighs in at 647,992 polygons over 351,365 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
