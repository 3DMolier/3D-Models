About This Model

A collection of conference discussion devices, the desktop microphone units with push-to-talk buttons used in parliaments and boardrooms. Gooseneck mics, speaker grilles and request lights are what make these units recognizable on a long meeting table. Several models in one set let a chairman unit stand apart from the delegate units around it.

Each device is built to CheckMate specification, so units line up at one scale along a table. Matte black housings and red indicator rings are easy to restyle for a corporate color scheme. Arrays of the same unit along a curved desk quickly build a debating chamber.

On offer since 2022, the set suits government visualization, conference center design and news graphics on summits. Universities and courts use the same systems, so the collection also fits lecture halls and hearings. Light a single red ring to show who has the floor, and the meeting dynamic is clear in one image.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
