The Argos Georgia Deep Sea Longliner is a production-ready Ships & Boats 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 333,401 polygons and 352,209 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Real-world footprint is 13.94 x 47.22 x H36.6 m, so it drops into a scene at correct scale without a rescaling pass. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.
