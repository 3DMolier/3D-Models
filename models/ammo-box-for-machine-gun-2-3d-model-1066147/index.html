About This Model

The steel ammunition box is one of the most reused objects in the world. It is watertight, stackable and almost impossible to destroy, which is why so many end up holding tools, tackle and everything else long after the contents were fired.

The model is light at 22,947 polygons and 23,085 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 12 maps all at 2048x2048. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $29 it suits defence visualization, film and television set dressing, game environments, documentary graphics, workshop scenes and editorial illustration. On sale since 2016.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2016 · Updated 14 November 2026
