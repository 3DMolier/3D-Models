About This Model

Priced at $19, the Syringa Lilac 5 is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 251,880 polygons and 244,124 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

3 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
