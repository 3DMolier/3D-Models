About This Model

Tomato Frog Rigged is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. You are getting 6,060 polygons and 5,348 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 14 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
