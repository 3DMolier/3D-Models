About This Model

This general model - Binder Clip Sheets - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 15,404 polygons and 15,470 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

7 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
