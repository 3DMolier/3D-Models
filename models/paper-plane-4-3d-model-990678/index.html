About This Model

Paper Plane 4 is a detailed Toys & Games asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. The mesh carries 4,376 polygons and 4,376 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
