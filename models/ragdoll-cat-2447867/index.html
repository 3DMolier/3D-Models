Ragdoll Cat belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 19,484 polygons and 19,206 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
