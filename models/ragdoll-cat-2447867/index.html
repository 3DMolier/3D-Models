About This Model

This Animals & Creatures model - Ragdoll Cat - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film production, game environments and VR experiences. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 19,484 polygons and 19,206 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
