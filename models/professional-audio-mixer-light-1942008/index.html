About This Model

A large format desk is a wall of identical channel strips, and that repetition is the point: an engineer works by muscle memory in the dark, so every strip has to be laid out the same way and feel the same under the hand.

The model is dense at 895,972 polygons and 467,551 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, which is what the controls require, in the light finish. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $59 it suits studio and broadcast visualization, audio equipment marketing, e-commerce imagery, film and television sets, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.
