About This Model

The extended range 777 changed what a twin engined aircraft was allowed to do. Once regulators accepted that two engines could cross an ocean, the four engined fleet started disappearing, and this is the aeroplane that made the argument.

The airframe holds 1,260,204 polygons and 735,999 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and comes rigged so the gear and control surfaces can be posed or animated. Native rigged builds for Maya and Cinema 4D are offered separately.

At $149 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits flight simulation, airline and airport visualization, documentary and news graphics, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
