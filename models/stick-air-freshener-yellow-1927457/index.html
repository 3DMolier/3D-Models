About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Stick Air Freshener is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Geometry weighs in at 3,416 polygons over 1,724 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
