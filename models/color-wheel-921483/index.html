About This Model

A circular chart divided into hue segments, this painter's tool shows primary, secondary and tertiary colors and how they relate. The disc has a center pivot so an inner window can turn, revealing complementary pairs and harmonies as it rotates. Its flat, graphic face makes it read clearly in a top-down shot, while the thin edge keeps a modest profile.

TurboSquid inspected it under the CheckMate Pro program, signing off on scale, structure and clean object names in the scene. Segment colors can be adjusted in materials to match any system, from traditional artists' pigments to RGB screen values. Because the rotating layer is a separate part, animators can spin it to demonstrate color relationships in real time.

Carried by our library since 2015, it suits art school lessons, design tutorials, paint store interiors and photography courses. Lay it on a desk beside brushes and swatch cards to dress a graphic designer's or illustrator's studio. Teachers explaining complementary colors animate the inner dial to show how opposite hues balance each other on a canvas.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
