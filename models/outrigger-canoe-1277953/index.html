About This Model

Priced at $49, the Outrigger Canoe is a finished Ships & Boats model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 11,967 polygons and 12,263 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
