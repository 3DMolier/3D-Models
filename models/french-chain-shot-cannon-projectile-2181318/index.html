About This Model

Priced at $12, the French Chain Shot Cannon Projectile is a finished Weapons model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defence training material and military VFX shots.

Geometry weighs in at 8,064 polygons over 4,032 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
