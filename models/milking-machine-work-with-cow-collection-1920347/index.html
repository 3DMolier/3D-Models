About This Model

A dairy cow stands while a milking machine works, with teat cups attached and hoses running to the collection unit. The bundle lists no polygon numbers, but the cow, the cups and the pump are modeled with visible detail. The scene shows how modern milking works in a real barn, with the cow calm and the hoses neatly routed.

TurboSquid gave the collection CheckMate Pro status after reviewing geometry, names and scale. The cow and the machine are separate, so the equipment can be shown alone or with the animal. Hoses and cups can be repositioned to show attaching or removing the machine.

Royalty Free since 2022, the set suits agriculture videos, dairy industry training and farm games. A milking scene explains the dairy process clearly, from the animal to the milk tank. Equipment suppliers also present it to farmers when explaining how their machines fit into the daily milking routine.
