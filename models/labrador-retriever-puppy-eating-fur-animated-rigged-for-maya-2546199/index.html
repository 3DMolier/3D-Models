About This Model

Labrador Retriever Puppy Eating Fur Animated Rigged For Maya captures a young lab with its head lowered into a food bowl. Short legs braced wide around the bowl give the puppy a sturdy, believable stance on kitchen tiles. Soft puppy fur, floppy ears and oversized paws give the dog the clumsy charm that viewers recognize instantly. The eating cycle moves the jaw and neck in short bites, a small action that reads well in close-ups.

Produced under the CheckMate specification, the Maya scene keeps rig controls, fur and mesh separated under clear names. Animators can extend the loop or blend it with other clips without first rebuilding the skeleton.

A 2026 addition, the puppy serves pet care tutorials, veterinary explainers and family scenes set in a kitchen. Placed beside a child or owner, the feeding moment adds warmth to lifestyle renders and short films. The low pose also fits tight frames, keeping the dog fully visible under a counter or table.
