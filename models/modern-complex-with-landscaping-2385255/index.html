About This Model

Modern Complex with Landscaping is one of the Architecture Landmarks models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $119. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 639,293 polygons and 729,974 vertices, a mid-weight load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
