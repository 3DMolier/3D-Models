About This Model

Oceanic Viper Fins Violet is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $29. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
