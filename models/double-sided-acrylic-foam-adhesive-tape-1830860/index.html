About This Model

A roll of foam tape is a cylinder with a liner on one face, and the interesting part is the end of the roll: the liner tab peeled slightly back so the tape reads as usable rather than as a solid disc. Without that detail it is just a ring.

The mesh is minimal at 832 polygons and 832 vertices, built entirely from quads with non-overlapping UVs, which is exactly the right economy for a subject whose realism comes from the material rather than from geometry.

At $19 it suits industrial and DIY product visualization, tool and hardware retail renders, workshop and workbench scenes, packaging mockups and games. On sale since 2022.
