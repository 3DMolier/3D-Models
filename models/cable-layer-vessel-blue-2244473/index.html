About This Model

A cable ship is organized around the cable path: over a bow sheave, through a linear engine amidships and down into enormous turntables in the hull. Above deck that gives a clear working area aft and a sheave at the bow that no other ship type carries.

The mesh runs 1,690,680 polygons and 1,886,547 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the cable handling gear, the cranes and the deck fittings.

A rigged build sits on this page at $349. The static model at $299 covers subsea and telecommunications visualization, offshore energy presentation, maritime industry marketing, documentary work and games. On sale since 2024.
