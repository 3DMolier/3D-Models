About This Model

This collection gathers the iPhone 13 family, phones with flat aluminum or steel edges and diagonally placed camera lenses. The smaller and larger models share the same squared profile, so the set shows how one design scales across sizes. A narrower notch at the top of the screen is the quick visual clue that tells this generation apart.

Each phone carries a CheckMate Pro rating, which confirms the meshes are sound and the files open as described. Colored glass backs and polished or satin frames are the materials to match when rendering them in close-up.

Licensing is restricted to editorial use, such as journalism, reviews, explainers and classroom materials about mobile technology. Listed since 2022, the phones work as props on a desk, in a hand or lying next to a charging cable. Comparison videos can line the models up by size so viewers see the difference before reading any specification. A news piece about phone prices or repairs also benefits from a clean render instead of a stock photo.
