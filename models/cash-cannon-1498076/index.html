About This Model

Looking for a general asset? The Cash Cannon is a finished, render-ready model at $39. You are getting 8,425 polygons and 8,746 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and defense simulation. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
