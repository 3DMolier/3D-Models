About This Model

This Industrial Equipment model - Construction Printer Module - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $69 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 383,375 polygons and 416,865 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
