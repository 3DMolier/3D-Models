About This Model

The Xiaomi Cyberdog is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $79 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 251,234 polygons over 133,158 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
