About This Model

Suspension has one job that sounds simple and is not: keep the tyre on the road while the body stays level. Every layout ever built is a different set of answers about which of those two to sacrifice when the ground gets difficult.

The model holds 69,474 polygons and 37,066 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, showing the suspension assembly on its own. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $59 it suits automotive engineering visualization, technical and training material, parts catalogs, aftermarket marketing, education and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
