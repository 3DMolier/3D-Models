About This Model

Priced at $29, the Waterskis Connelly is a finished Sports & Recreation model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 37,358 polygons and 38,818 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2015 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.
