About This Model

Siemens Mobility Vectron Locomotive Red is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $99. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
