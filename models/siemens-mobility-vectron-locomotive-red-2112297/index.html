About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Siemens Mobility Vectron Locomotive is a finished, render-ready model at $99. Geometry weighs in at 623,688 polygons over 343,092 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
