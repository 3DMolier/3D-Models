About This Model

Priced at $199, the Elk Rigged is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 9,917 polygons and 9,740 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
