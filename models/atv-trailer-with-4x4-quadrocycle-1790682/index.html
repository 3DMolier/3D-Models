About This Model

The ATV Trailer with 4x4 Quadrocycle is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $189 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 255,872 polygons and 264,395 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
