About This Model

A long-range bolt-action sniper rifle with a collapsible stock and heavy fluted barrel, built for extreme distance shooting. Its long silhouette with bipod and large optic makes the rifle instantly read as a precision weapon.

The CheckMate Pro mark shows the model cleared TurboSquid's strictest inspection of mesh, mapping and scene layout. Clear detail on the bolt, magazine and muzzle brake supports reload and inspection close-ups. Matte finishes on the receiver absorb light, so a rim light helps outline the shape against dark backgrounds.

The license is Editorial Uses Only, which suits military journalism, documentary work and weapons education. Available since 2017, it has long served defense simulation and military game projects that need a recognizable rifle. Laid on a shooting mat with spent casings beside it, the rifle sets a tense sniper scene on a rooftop or ridge. Firearm reviewers can also use it to illustrate range, scale and handling in written articles.
