About This Model

Boy on Jumping Stick belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $169 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 16,468 polygons and 17,222 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
