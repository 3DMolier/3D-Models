About This Model

A collection of coffee shop signs bearing the Starbucks name and siren logo, for storefronts, mall corridors and city streets in editorial scenes. The set gathers several sign types, so a facade can carry a wall logo while a projecting sign hangs over the sidewalk.

Each sign meets the CheckMate standard of TurboSquid, which means real scale, clean pivots and a scene that opens without missing parts. Green and white signs read best at dusk, when the lettering glows against a darker street and shop windows carry warm light. Placed on a corner building, the signs instantly tell the viewer this is a busy modern shopping street.

The license is Editorial Uses Only, so the set suits news illustration, documentary reconstruction, urban studies and editorial visualization of retail districts. It has been available since 2024 and saves modeling a well known coffee chain storefront when a city scene needs one. Several signs in one purchase also let a street show more than one branch without cloning a single logo everywhere.
