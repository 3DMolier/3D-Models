About This Model

This set gathers wooden matches and matchboxes, small props that add life to kitchens, fireplaces and candlelit dinner tables. Unlit sticks carry rounded red heads, while spent ones show blackened tips curling where the flame burned down. The boxes feature a sliding tray and a striking strip along the side, ready to stand open or closed.

TurboSquid checked the set for its CheckMate Pro tier, covering geometry, believable scale and clear object naming. Box labels can be designed freely in your own shaders, so no third-party brand appears on the packaging. Because the sticks are tiny, they work best in macro shots where wood grain and head texture are visible.

Released in 2022, the set suits survival game items, cozy interior scenes and safety education illustrations. Short animations of a match catching fire start from these models, with flame effects added on top. Scattered beside a candle or a camping stove, they make a still life feel used and lived in.
