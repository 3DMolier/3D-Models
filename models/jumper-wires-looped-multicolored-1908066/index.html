About This Model

Priced at $19, the Jumper Wires Looped is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 249,480 polygons over 124,848 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
