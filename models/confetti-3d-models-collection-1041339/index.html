About This Model

Paper confetti pieces in many shapes, from rectangles and circles to curls and stars, ready to burst over a celebration. Small twisted strips catch light on both sides, the detail that makes falling confetti sparkle in motion. Gathering many shapes together lets a particle system scatter variety rather than identical paper bits.

The collection has CheckMate Pro status, meaning TurboSquid found the meshes clean and the files well organized. Colors can be randomized per piece in most renderers, from bright party tones to metallic gold and silver. Because each piece is tiny and light, thousands can rain down in a scene without heavy memory use.

First listed in 2016, the set fills birthday intros, wedding animations and award show graphics on screens. A slow motion burst over a stage turns a simple title card into a festive highlight for any event. Game developers also trigger it as a reward effect when players finish a level or win a match.
