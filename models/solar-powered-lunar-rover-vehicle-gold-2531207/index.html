About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Solar Powered Lunar Rover is a finished, render-ready model at $69. You are getting 507,073 polygons and 530,091 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 155.09 x 188.44 x H267.34 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
