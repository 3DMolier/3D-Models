About This Model

A freestanding frame that holds a hammock without trees or posts, it lets you hang a swing seat on any patio or deck. Its 106,640 polygons and 108,191 vertices define the curved arms, the wide feet and the hooks at both ends. The arched shape is clear from the side, so the stand reads well even when the hammock itself is missing.

Separate UV islands across the frame make it simple to add wood grain, powder coating or scuffs where feet touch the ground. No image maps are included, and the finish depends on a material you set for metal or timber yourself. TurboSquid confirmed that the stand meets its CheckMate specification for clean geometry and a correct real-world scale.

Brought into the range in 2024, it fits garden furniture catalogs, backyard renders and outdoor lifestyle scenes for resorts. Landscape designers place it on lawns to show how much space a hammock needs between trees that are not there. Pair it with any fabric hammock to complete a relaxed summer corner by a pool or a cabin.
