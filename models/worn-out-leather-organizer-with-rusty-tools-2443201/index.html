About This Model

Worn Out Leather Organizer with Rusty Tools is one of the Tools models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. You are getting 14,928 polygons and 15,306 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 17.34 x 7.51 x H14.46 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
