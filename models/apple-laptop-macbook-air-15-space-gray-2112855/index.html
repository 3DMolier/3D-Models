About This Model

The 15-inch MacBook Air in Space Gray, a thin wedge-free laptop with a large display and a full-size keyboard. Its 15,986 polygons go into the aluminum shell, the keys, the trackpad, the display bezel and the hinge. The 17,120 vertices keep the rounded corners and port openings clean in desk close-ups.

UVs are cleanly separated with no overlaps, and the laptop passed the CheckMate Pro review on TurboSquid. Editorial Uses Only is the license here, which points it toward tech journalism, reviews and education.

The four versions match the full color range: Space Gray, Silver, Midnight and the warm Starlight finish. Midnight reads almost black under soft window light, while Starlight adds a warm champagne tone to the scene. Released in 2023, the laptop suits workspace interiors, technology news graphics and quiet study room scenes. Opened at an angle on a wooden desk, it fits remote work and classroom stories alike.
