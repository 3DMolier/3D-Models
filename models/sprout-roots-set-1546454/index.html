About This Model

A group of young seedlings at different stages, each with its own root length and first leaves, ready to show how plants grow. Having several sprouts in one set lets an artist build a germination sequence instead of repeating one seedling. Side by side, the stages turn a still image into a small lesson about growth. Their small scale suits macro lenses, where every root hair becomes part of the story.

As a StemCell set, the seedlings come with consistent PBR materials in each file format TurboSquid supplies. Roots and leaves can be tinted separately, which helps match the sprouts to beans, peas or grain.

The Sprout Roots Set costs $49 and has been part of the catalog since 2020, with 12 sales. Teachers, agronomists and eco brands use it in science slides, crop research visuals and sustainability campaigns. Arranged in a row of glass tubes, the seedlings make a clean laboratory shot of plant development.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026

Can the Sprout Roots Set model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What does StemCell mean for the Sprout Roots Set model?

StemCell covers both geometry and shading: the model is delivered across formats with materials that stay equivalent instead of needing per-engine conversion.
