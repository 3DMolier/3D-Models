About This Model

Priced at $19, the Colored Binder Clips is a finished model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 17,152 polygons over 17,200 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 11 of them, peaking at 2048x2048, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
