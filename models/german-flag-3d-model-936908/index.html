About This Model

A German flag in black, red and gold horizontal stripes is a quick national symbol for embassies, stadiums and news graphics. The cloth ripples gently in the wind, with deeper folds near the pole and looser waves toward the free edge. Its proportions follow the national banner of Germany, so the three bands read correctly in any shot.

TurboSquid inspected the flag and awarded CheckMate Pro for clean geometry and true-to-life sizing. Folds in the fabric are modeled rather than faked, so the waves catch light naturally under a moving sun. Because the shape is simple, many copies can line a street or fill a stadium without heavy scene weight.

Present in the catalog since 2015, it suits political news, football broadcasts and history documentaries about reunification. Fans waving it in a crowd animation instantly signal which team the stadium supports. Embassy and government building renders place it at the entrance to mark official status.
