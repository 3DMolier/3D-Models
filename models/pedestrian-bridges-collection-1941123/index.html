About This Model

A collection of pedestrian bridges for parks, rivers and city streets, giving planners several footbridge styles in one set. Decks, railings and supporting structures differ from bridge to bridge, from simple park crossings to more engineered spans. Having options side by side helps a designer test which style suits a riverbank or a busy road crossing.

CheckMate Lite means the models passed the basic technical checks, enough for quick placement in planning scenes. Each bridge comes as a separate piece, ready to span a channel or link two plazas in a landscape design.

Available since 2022, the bridges suit landscape architecture, urban planning, park design and city-building games. People walking across at golden hour, with reflections in the water below, make a classic park image. Lighting the railings at night with soft LEDs turns a plain crossing into a landmark of the waterfront. Cyclists and strollers placed on the deck help reviewers judge width and accessibility at a glance.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
