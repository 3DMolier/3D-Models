About This Model

Priced at $129, the Shelter HMMWV m1037 is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 331,450 polygons and 341,361 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

55 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and defense simulation. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
