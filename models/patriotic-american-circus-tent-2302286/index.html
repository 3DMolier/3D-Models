About This Model

Priced at $29, the Circus Tent is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 58,156 polygons and 62,788 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Questions About This Model

Does the Circus Tent model include materials and textures?

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. As a StemCell asset it ships with PBR materials that stay consistent across the delivered formats.

What does StemCell mean for the Circus Tent model?

It means one source asset, several delivered formats, and a PBR material setup designed to read consistently across them - no rebuilding shaders after import.

How heavy is the Circus Tent mesh?

58,156 polygons and 62,788 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

What is the Circus Tent model typically used for?

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.
