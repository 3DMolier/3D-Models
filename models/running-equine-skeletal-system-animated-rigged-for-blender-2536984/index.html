About This Model

A horse skeleton gallops in this Blender animation, showing exactly how the bones of the legs, spine and neck move at speed. Watching the spine stretch and compress with each stride makes clear why horses are such efficient runners. The long limbs ending in hooves extend dramatically in the airborne phase of the gallop.

The bones are modeled following CheckMate guidance, so joints rotate cleanly at full running speed. Blender playback is immediate, so the gallop can be slowed for frame-by-frame study of the legs. An ivory bone material set against a dark background keeps every moving part clearly visible.

Released with royalty free rights in 2026, it suits veterinary education, equestrian sports science and natural history films. Trainers and riders can see how leg joints load at a run, which helps explain common injuries. A side-on tracking camera running alongside the gallop gives the clearest view of the whole motion.
