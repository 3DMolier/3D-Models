About This Model

A collection of colorful laundry detergent pods, the soft swirled capsules that sit beside washing machines in modern homes. Each pod shows chambers of different colored liquid twisted together inside a glossy water-soluble film. Scattering several on a counter or inside a washing machine drum instantly signals laundry day in a domestic scene.

The set was built to CheckMate specification, so the shapes are tidy and arrive at believable real size. A translucent shader lets light pass through the film, giving the pods their recognizable candy-like glow. You can tint the inner liquids to match any packaging palette without changing the geometry at all.

The Detergent Pods Collection joined the range in 2024 for household and cleaning product visuals. Laundry room interiors, home appliance renders and lifestyle games use it to dress shelves and washers. Safety campaigns also show the pods next to toys to warn parents about keeping capsules out of reach.
