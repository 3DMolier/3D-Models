About This Model

This euro banknote bundle shows neat stacks of paper money held by paper bands, mixing three denominations in one pile. Its 33,312 polygons give each stack slightly fanned edges, so the notes look counted rather than solid blocks. Dropped into a briefcase, a bank vault or a heist scene, the bundle makes the stakes obvious.

The 33,512 vertices are all quads, with note faces unwrapped flat so the printed designs stay sharp. It uses 5 maps at 4096 x 4096 showing the colored notes, the security strips and the paper bands. A CheckMate Pro endorsement confirms real banknote size and clearly named stacks for quick arrangement.

Three versions cost $29 each: this mixed bundle and separate bundles of 500 and 200 euro notes. Pairing the high value versions with the mixed pile fills a vault shelf or a ransom bag convincingly. Since 2019 the money has appeared in crime dramas, banking explainers and finance news graphics.
