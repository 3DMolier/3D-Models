About This Model

A group of autonomous electric trucks, the cabless freight pods that logistics companies test for driverless delivery between warehouses. Without a driver seat, these vehicles take on smooth, boxy shapes with sensors placed at the corners. Several designs in one bundle make a future depot or highway scene look varied.

The trucks follow CheckMate construction practice for real scale, so they line up with loading docks, roads and pedestrians. Clean surfaces leave space for fleet liveries or project logos in concept presentations. Sensor pods and light strips give each vehicle a recognizable front, even though there is no windshield. Low, flat roofs and wide wheel stances make the trucks look planted and stable on the road.

Released in 2021 and priced at $89, the collection suits smart city concepts, logistics explainers, tech conference visuals and futuristic games. Line them up at a charging station under night lights, and the image says next-generation transport at once.
