About This Model

An inflatable life raft with a canopy, carrying a tourist inside, ready for sea rescue and survival scenes. The 149,497 polygons shape the inflated tubes, the canopy arch and the figure sitting low in the boat. Bright orange fabric is designed to be seen from the air, and it reads instantly against dark water.

With 173,071 vertices on overlapping UVs, repeated tube sections and seams share texture space efficiently. StemCell certification keeps the PBR materials consistent, so the raft renders the same in game engines and offline. The tourist sits wrapped against the cold, which makes the image feel like a real rescue rather than a product shot.

On the market since 2024, the raft scene is $189 and free of royalty fees. Maritime safety training, coast guard explainers, survival games and disaster films rely on images like this. Rocked by swells under a helicopter searchlight, the raft carries the tension of the moment before rescue.
