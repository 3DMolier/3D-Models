About This Model

Classic arcade cabinets fill this big collection, with glowing screens, joysticks and colorful side art. Arcade Games Big Collection lets a retro game room, a bar or a family entertainment center be furnished in minutes. Upright cabinets, racing seats and pinball style machines give the room variety.

Every cabinet shares one scale, so rows of machines line up naturally along a wall. Screens and marquees can carry emissive textures, which makes the arcade glow in a dark scene like the real thing. Neon signs and a patterned carpet complete the nostalgic look of an eighties game hall.

Priced at $399 since its 2026 release, the bundle suits commercial entertainment and interior projects. Retro bar interiors, game development environments, nostalgia videos and amusement venue plans are typical uses. A low shot down a row of glowing cabinets captures the atmosphere of a busy arcade night with kids crowding the controls.
