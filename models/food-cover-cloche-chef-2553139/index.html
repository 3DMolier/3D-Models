About This Model

Priced at $19, the Food Cover Cloche Chef is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 36,210 polygons and 40,333 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. 4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and game assets. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
