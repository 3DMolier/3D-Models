About This Model

The ordering kiosk changed the layout of fast food restaurants more than anything since the drive through. It is a tall column with a large touchscreen at standing height, a card reader below and a receipt slot at the bottom, and it stands in the middle of the floor rather than at a counter.

The mesh is light at 13,895 polygons and 13,957 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the screen as a separate surface so an interface image goes on without masking.

At $39 it suits restaurant and retail interior visualization, hospitality technology marketing, store planning presentation, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2021.
