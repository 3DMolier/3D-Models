About This Model

A microscopic dust mite blown up to hero size, with the bulbous body, bristly legs and mouthparts that make allergy ads memorable. The model is rigged for Maya, so the legs can be animated to crawl across bedding fibers or a carpet close-up.

Under an electron microscope a mite looks ridged and armored, and the surface here leans into that slightly alien texture. Built along CheckMate lines, the scene keeps the mesh and the controls clearly named, which saves time when you start animating. A pale, translucent shader with soft backlight gives the most convincing microscope look.

Released in 2026, the mite suits allergy and cleaning product commercials, medical education and science documentaries about the home. A slow push in from a pillow to a single crawling mite is a classic way to make an invisible problem visible. The rigged mite costs $89 and saves the work of building a joint chain for all those legs.
