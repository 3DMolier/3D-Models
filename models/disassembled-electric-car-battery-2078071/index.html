About This Model

The battery is the car now. Taking one apart shows the hierarchy that decides everything about an electric vehicle: cells inside modules, modules inside a pack, cooling running between them, and a structure that is also part of the chassis.

The model holds 476,968 polygons and 243,447 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in the disassembled state. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and an opened build is offered separately.

At $49 it suits automotive engineering visualisation, technical and training material, manufacturing and recycling presentation, education and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
