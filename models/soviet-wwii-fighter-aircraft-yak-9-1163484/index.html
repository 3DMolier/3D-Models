About This Model

The Soviet WWII Fighter Aircraft Yak-9 is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
