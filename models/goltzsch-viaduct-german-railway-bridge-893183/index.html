About This Model

This Architecture & Landmarks model - Goltzsch Viaduct German Railway Bridge - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $79 on TurboSquid. You are getting 109,514 polygons and 114,712 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
