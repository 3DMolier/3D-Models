About This Model

Mummy with Egyptian Sarcophagus Collection pairs a wrapped mummy with a painted coffin, giving archaeology and horror scenes their central props. The sarcophagus follows the classic anthropoid shape, with a sculpted face, crossed arms and a lid that lifts away. Linen wrappings on the body are modeled in layered bands, so the figure holds up under close lighting.

Each piece meets the CheckMate specification, keeping the lid, coffin base and mummy as separate named objects. That separation lets an animator open the coffin slowly and reveal the body inside without rebuilding anything. Gold and painted hieroglyph surfaces respond well to torchlight, which is the light these scenes usually call for.

Available since 2024, the set suits Egyptology documentaries, museum displays, escape room design, adventure games and Halloween animation. In a dark tomb lit by flickering torches, the open coffin becomes the obvious focal point of the shot. Classrooms also rely on it to show how ancient Egyptians prepared and protected their dead.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
