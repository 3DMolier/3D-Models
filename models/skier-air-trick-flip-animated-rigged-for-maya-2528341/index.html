About This Model

Skier Air Trick Flip Animated Rigged for Maya shows a freestyle skier launching off a kicker and rotating through a full flip. Tucked knees, crossed skis and outstretched arms at the top of the rotation give the trick its spectacular shape. Bright ski clothing keeps the athlete readable against white snow and blue sky.

The Maya scene keeps skeleton, skis and clothing clearly named under the CheckMate standard. The animated flip lets artists frame slow motion replays or freeze the athlete at the peak. Goggles and helmet hide the face, so the figure can represent any athlete in the story.
