About This Model

The Luxury Car Dashboard is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $89 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 17,353 polygons and 19,788 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
