About This Model

The Fresh Red Cherry Tomato is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 431 polygons and 433 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
