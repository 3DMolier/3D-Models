About This Model

A collection of ornate baroque picture frames with carved scrolls, acanthus leaves and deep gilded moldings. Each frame has a different profile, from narrow oval borders to heavy rectangular surrounds made for large oil canvases. Hung in a salon or a museum corridor, the set brings old-world grandeur to any wall.

The bundle carries CheckMate Pro approval, which keeps naming and scale consistent across every frame. Gold leaf, aged patina and dark wood finishes reflect candlelight and spotlights in rich, uneven glints. The inner openings stay empty, so you can drop any painting, family photo or antique mirror inside each frame.

The 4 versions include this collection and the fourth at $79, plus the third at $89. The second collection costs $59 and suits budget projects that need just a handful of frames on a single wall. Since 2016 the frames have decorated gallery visualizations, period drama sets and luxury interior renders.
