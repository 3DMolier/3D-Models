About This Model

The Eiffel Tower standing on a relief map of France, a ready visual for travel, geography and culture topics. Eiffel Tower on Map France uses 522,420 polygons and 552,476 vertices, most of them on the iron lattice of the tower. The map base shows the country's outline, letting the landmark mark Paris on a stylized tabletop.

Regions, routes or a vintage paper look can be repainted on the map thanks to UVs without overlap. The model holds CheckMate Lite certification, confirming tidy geometry and sensible organization of parts. Arches at the base and the narrowing upper deck stay crisp even in close camera moves.

Released in 2022 at $219, the piece blends landmark and infographic in one object. Travel agencies, French language courses, geography lessons and tourism videos use it as an opening graphic. Spin the map slowly as warm sunset light through the lattice casts patterned shadows across it for an elegant title shot.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2022 · Updated 18 November 2026

Questions About This Model

What is the Eiffel Tower on Map France model typically used for?

Stated applications are architectural visualization, event visualization and product rendering. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

How heavy is the Eiffel Tower on Map France mesh?

522,420 polygons and 552,476 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. 522,420 polygons reads as dense next to the roughly 7,800 median of our Other range.

How clean is the geometry on the Eiffel Tower on Map France model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.

Does the Eiffel Tower on Map France model include materials and textures?

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. Where textures are included they are packaged with the download rather than linked externally.
