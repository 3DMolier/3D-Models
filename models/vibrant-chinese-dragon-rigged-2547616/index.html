About This Model

Priced at $169, the Vibrant Chinese Dragon Rigged is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 41,886 polygons and 40,518 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
