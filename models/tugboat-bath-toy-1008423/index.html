About This Model

Tugboat Bath Toy belongs to our Toys & Games range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbour environments in games, and marine simulation.

Counted at the source, the model is 34,235 polygons and 34,769 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 7 maps, the largest at 4000x4000, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
