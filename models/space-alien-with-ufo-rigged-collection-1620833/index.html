About This Model

A flying saucer alone is a prop; a saucer with its occupants is a scene. This collection supplies both, and the figures arrive rigged, which is what allows a landing, an emergence and a gesture rather than a static tableau under a hovering disc.

Buying craft and characters together also keeps the scale honest between them, which is exactly the thing that goes wrong when a vehicle and its crew come from different sources and the doorway turns out to be the wrong size.

At $119 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits science fiction film and television, games, advertising and comedy content, motion graphics, book and poster illustration, and themed events. On sale since 2021.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
