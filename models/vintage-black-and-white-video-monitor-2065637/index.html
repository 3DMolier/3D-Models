About This Model

The Video Monitor is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 35,809 polygons over 18,936 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags technical visualization, software demos and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
