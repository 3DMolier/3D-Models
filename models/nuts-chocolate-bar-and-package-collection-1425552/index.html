About This Model

This set shows a nut chocolate bar both unwrapped and in its printed package, so a snack can appear opened or sealed. Whole nuts pressed into the chocolate give the surface a lumpy texture that tells viewers the flavor at once. Glossy chocolate and crinkled foil are the two materials that sell this shot under warm light.

CheckMate Pro approval means the bar segments and wrapper folds arrive without broken geometry. A snapped piece lying beside its torn wrapper is one of the oldest and most effective confectionery compositions.

First offered in 2019, the collection can be used in paid packaging and food projects. Packaging designers preview new wrappers on bars like this, while food stylists drop them into kitchen and picnic scenes. Games and animation also use chocolate bars as pickups or as desk props in a student bedroom. Vending machine interiors and checkout counters gain instant variety from a few bars placed among other snacks.
