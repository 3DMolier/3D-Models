About This Model

A moulded case with a cut foam insert is a specific and very readable object: the outline of the pistol pressed into the foam, magazine cutouts beside it, and the ribbed shell of the case around them. That silhouette in the foam tells the viewer what is missing when the case is empty, which is why it works so well in imagery.

The mesh runs 48,771 polygons and 49,091 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the foam cutouts, the latches and the case ribbing.

At $49 and built to StemCell specification, it suits firearms retail and catalogue renders, shooting sports material, security and law enforcement visualisation, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2025.
