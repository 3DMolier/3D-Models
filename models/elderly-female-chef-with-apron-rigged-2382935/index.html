About This Model

Priced at $149, the Elderly Female Chef with Apron is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 11,213 polygons and 12,037 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
