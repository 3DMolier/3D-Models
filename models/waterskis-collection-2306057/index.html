About This Model

A collection of waterskis for lake and resort scenes, with several designs that a rider might use behind a towing boat. Each ski has its foot bindings modeled on the deck, which is what tells viewers they are not snow skis. The long, flat boards with upturned tips read clearly even in a wide shot of a dock.

Built to CheckMate specification, every ski in the set shares consistent scale and naming, so they can be mixed on one rack. Glossy top decks look best with a bright sky reflection, which also suits the summer mood of water sports. Lean a few against a boathouse wall, or float one beside a jetty, and the setting explains itself.

On sale since 2024, the collection fits sports equipment stores, resort marketing visuals, water sports games and summer camp animations. Buying the set gives matching skis for a rental shop shelf instead of cloning a single board many times. They also work as props on the deck of a speedboat in action and travel films.
