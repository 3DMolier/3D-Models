About This Model

This helmet-mount edition of the ComTac VI clips to rail adapters instead of a headband, so it belongs on combat helmets. The 3M Peltor ComTac VI Helmet Headset takes 10,168 polygons and 10,343 vertices, a lean count for first-person gear. The arms that bridge from the cups to the helmet rails are the main difference from the headband edition.

CheckMate certification means even, well-named geometry that slots into a character pipeline without any cleanup. The bulky ear shells and side buttons give a helmeted figure the profile of modern special forces. A flexible microphone arm sweeps toward the mouth, which helps sell radio chatter in cutscenes.

Released in 2026, the part costs $44 and carries a Royalty Free license. Tactical shooters, military training simulations, films and concept art are where this kind of equipment normally ends up. Fitting it to an existing helmet takes only a quick rail alignment, since the arms already follow that curve.
