About This Model

A rugged amphibious off-road vehicle with big balloon tires, built to crawl through mud and then float across a river. The Amphibious Off Road Vehicle is a triangulated 446,059 polygons over 229,188 vertices, dense in tires and hull. A sealed tub-like hull and low-pressure tires are what let these machines swim without a propeller.

StemCell certification keeps the plastic hull and rubber materials consistent between engines and offline renderers. Overlapping UVs share space across the many identical wheels, which keeps texture memory down. The open cabin lets a driver figure sit inside, which sells the scale of the vehicle instantly.

Out since 2023, the vehicle fits hunting and fishing media, rescue service visuals and off-road games. Splashing out of a swamp onto a bank, it delivers the kind of moment adventure trailers rely on. Search and rescue teams use machines like this in flood zones, which makes it useful for emergency training.
