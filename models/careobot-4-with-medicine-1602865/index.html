About This Model

A white care robot with a round display head and a tray of medicine bottles, suited to hospital, nursing and robotics scenes. The Careobot 4 with Medicine is a subdivision build of 186,238 polygons and 193,221 vertices with soft, rounded shells. Smooth transitions between the parts give the machine a friendly, non-threatening presence beside patients.

Scale and part names meet CheckMate Pro rules, so the robot is clean to pose and animate. Head, arm and torso are separate pieces, which makes it easy to show the machine handing a bottle to a patient. The glossy white shell reflects soft ward lighting and keeps the robot looking clean and clinical.

Its only version costs $119, with the medicine tray already in place. Licensed Editorial Uses Only and on offer since 2020, it fits healthcare innovation reports, elderly care concepts and robotics lectures. Rolling down a night corridor, the robot shows the future of nursing assistance at a glance.
