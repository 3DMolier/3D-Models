About This Model

A group of clear glass jugs and pitchers in different shapes, with handles and pouring lips for water, juice or lemonade. Some jugs are tall and slim while others are squat and round, so a shelf of them shows real variety. Each vessel is modeled with real wall thickness, which glass needs to refract light convincingly.

The pouring lips are shaped from the rim, a detail that makes the jugs look usable on a dining table. TurboSquid put the bundle through its CheckMate Pro review, confirming clean meshes, real sizes and a tidy scene. Every jug is its own object, so you can fill one with liquid and leave the others empty.

The page holds four versions of the jug set, at $27 for this one and others at $27, $34 and $39. Each version gathers a different selection of jugs, so combining them builds a full glassware shelf. Since 2022 the jugs have served kitchen renders, summer drink scenes and tableware store displays.
