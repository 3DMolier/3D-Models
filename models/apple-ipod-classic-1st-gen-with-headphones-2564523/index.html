About This Model

This early scroll-wheel music player comes paired with wired earbuds, a nostalgic prop for scenes set in the first years of the digital music boom. A total of 27,174 polygons and 28,312 vertices shape the rounded steel back, the scroll wheel and the tangled cable leading to the earbuds.

Quads and triangles share the mesh, and UV mapping is mixed, which leaves room to repaint the white front shell. Laid flat, the player and headphones span 22.52 x 36.67 x H2.11 cm, so the cable spread reads at true scale on a desk. The mirrored back picks up scratches and reflections nicely, so close-ups reward a careful metal shader.

Two versions share this page: the Apple iPod Classic 1st Gen with Headphones and an unbranded Retro Digital Audio Player with Headphones, each at $44. Retro bedroom sets, music history videos and era-specific game interiors are natural homes for this compact player.
