About This Model

The Cherokee is the low wing counterpart to the high wing trainers, and the difference is immediately visible: the wing sits under the cabin, there is a single door on the starboard side, and the wing itself has a constant chord that gives it a distinctly rectangular planform. Thousands of pilots learned on this shape.

The mesh is efficient at 47,445 polygons and 49,525 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, which suits an aircraft usually shown on an apron or in a circuit rather than in extreme close-up.

At $99 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits general aviation visualisation, flight school and club material, airfield scenes, aviation advertising and games. On sale since 2024.
