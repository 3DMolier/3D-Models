About This Model

This Kitchen & Tableware model - Striped Sunflower Seeds in a Bowl - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

The mesh carries 146,808 polygons and 144,984 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 10 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background.
