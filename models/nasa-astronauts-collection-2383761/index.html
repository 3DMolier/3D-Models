About This Model

Astronauts in NASA spacesuits make up this collection, ready for launch pads, spacewalks, moon landings and mission control scenes. Bulky white suits with life support backpacks, helmets and gold visors give the figures their iconic look. Reflective visors mirror the surroundings, which helps the figures sit convincingly in any environment.

Each figure meets CheckMate requirements, keeping the folds of the heavy suit fabric clean in close shots. The white fabric needs careful exposure, since harsh sunlight in orbit easily blows out the fine detail. Hoses, connectors and chest control panels give close-ups the technical detail space fans expect.

Licensed royalty free and on sale since 2025, the astronauts serve space documentaries, science education, museum displays and exploration games. Mission patches and flags can be added as decals to match a specific space program or crew. A lone astronaut against the black of space with the blue Earth below remains the most powerful image.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
