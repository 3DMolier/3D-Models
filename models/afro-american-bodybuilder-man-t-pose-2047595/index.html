About This Model

Afro American Bodybuilder Man T-Pose belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 92,072 polygons and 49,258 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
