About This Model

Hands of an African American man and an infant, rigged in Maya so the baby can grip a finger and the adult can cradle. The Afro American Man and Baby Hands Rigged for Maya Collection targets VFX and character teams that animate in Maya. The difference in scale between the two hands tells the story of trust before any face appears on screen.

Controls follow standard Maya conventions, so referencing, constraints and animation layers behave as expected. The hands meet CheckMate specification, keeping their size relationship accurate for close macro framing. Darker skin needs careful subsurface and specular settings to look alive under soft light.

Released in 2026, the collection serves family dramas, pediatric health campaigns and insurance spots that center on care. Documentaries about fatherhood use close-ups like this to open chapters without a single line of dialogue. Game cinematics can also cut to the hands for a quiet emotional beat between loud action scenes.
