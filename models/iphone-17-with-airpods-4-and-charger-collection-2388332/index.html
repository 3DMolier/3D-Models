About This Model

An iPhone 17, AirPods 4 and a charger grouped as one set, the everyday kit people carry together. The earbud case, the phone and the cable together tell a viewer this is a personal desk or bedside scene. Rounded white plastic on the earbuds contrasts with the phone glass, which gives a still life useful material variety.

Shared scale across the pieces, a CheckMate specification requirement, keeps the case correct next to the handset. Each item stays separate, which lets an artist open the earbud case or coil the charging cable differently. Soft window light suits this kind of tabletop arrangement better than hard studio flashes on glossy surfaces.

Apple's product designs and trademarks keep this 2025 collection within Editorial Uses Only licensing. It works for tech blogs, unboxing explainers, lifestyle editorial stills and film props on a nightstand. A coffee mug and an open notebook beside the trio complete a believable morning workspace on a wooden desk.
