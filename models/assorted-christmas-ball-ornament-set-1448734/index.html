About This Model

Assorted Christmas Ball Ornament Set offers glass baubles in several finishes, each with a metal cap and wire loop for hanging. The whole set uses 12,868 polygons and 12,840 vertices, so a full tree of copies stays light in the viewport. Glossy, matte and glitter surfaces sit side by side, which keeps a decorated tree from looking repetitive.

Those finishes come from 10 maps at 2048 x 2048, covering color, sparkle and fine patterns. The unwrap keeps every bauble apart, so recoloring a whole set to match a red or gold theme is quick. Sound CheckMate Pro geometry means each ball reflects the room cleanly, without seams breaking the highlight.

Since 2019 the ornaments have sold 16 times to artists preparing holiday cards, store windows and Christmas interiors. Hang them on a tree, pile them in a glass bowl or scatter them across a table runner. Product photographers also use them as background bokeh behind gifts, perfume bottles and festive drinks.
