About This Model

A carved dragon head with flaring whiskers, bulging eyes and an open jaw, the kind that leads a festival dance or a racing boat. At 6,559 polygons and 6,788 vertices the Chinese Dragon Head stays light, putting its budget into the horns and the curl of the mane.

A set of 3 maps at 4096 x 4096 paints the red lacquer, gold scales and black outlines around the teeth. StemCell certification means one PBR material set behaves alike across renderers and formats, without reshading after import. The UVs are laid out with no overlaps, so new color schemes for the lunar year can be painted straight onto them.

Raised on a pole above a crowd, the head reads well even from a distance thanks to its bold silhouette. Fixed to the bow of a long boat, it gives a regatta scene an instant cultural anchor. Released in 2019, the head has gathered 11 sales, mostly from festival, mythology and game environment projects.
