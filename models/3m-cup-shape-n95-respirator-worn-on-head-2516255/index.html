About This Model

A molded cup respirator fitted over the nose and mouth of a smooth head form, ready for clinic scenes and workplace safety explainers. Its 13,208 polygons and 13,226 vertices go into the domed filter shell, the nose clip and the two elastic straps. The head itself stays featureless, so attention lands on the mask and the way it seals against the cheeks.

Geometry mixes quads and triangles, with the curved shell kept smooth enough for close studio lighting. The UV layout combines shared and unique islands, which leaves the fibrous white surface easy to retexture with your own print or stamp. It was made to the CheckMate specification, so the file opens with clean structure and sensible naming.

Available since 2026, it suits hospital training slides, construction site safety renders and news graphics about air quality. A respirator worn the right way says more than one lying on a table.
