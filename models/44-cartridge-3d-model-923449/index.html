About This Model

This large-caliber revolver round shows the brass case, the rim and the lead bullet seated in its crimped mouth. The straight-walled case and the flat rim match the classic magnum round used in heavy revolvers. Its smooth profile looks convincing scattered on a table, standing upright on a reloading bench or loaded in a cylinder.

TurboSquid rated the round CheckMate Pro, confirming clean topology, true scale and orderly object names. Polished brass against the duller lead tip gives strong material contrast in macro renders. The primer at the base is visible as a separate disc, which helps in shots of the cartridge from below.

Introduced in 2015 at $19, it suits western and crime films, shooter games and ammunition reference visuals. Game artists use rounds like this for ammo pickups and inventory icons beside a big revolver. Firearm educators also show it in cross sections explaining case, powder charge and projectile.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
