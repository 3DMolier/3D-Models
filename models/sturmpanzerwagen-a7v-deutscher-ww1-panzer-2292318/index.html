About This Model

The Sturmpanzerwagen A7V Deutscher WW1 Panzer is a production-ready Military Vehicles 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, battlefield simulation and war-game environments. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defence training material and military VFX shots.

Geometry weighs in at 633,489 polygons over 655,685 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
