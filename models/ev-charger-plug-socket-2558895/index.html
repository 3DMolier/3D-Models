About This Model

EV Charger Plug Socket is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. The mesh carries 12,298 polygons and 12,791 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 9 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation and automotive advertising. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
