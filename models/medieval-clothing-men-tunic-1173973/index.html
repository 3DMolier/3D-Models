About This Model

Priced at $39, the Medieval Clothing Men Tunic is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 13,497 polygons and 13,670 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
