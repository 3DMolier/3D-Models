About This Model

Standing White Ermine belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $79 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 22,223 polygons and 15,802 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
