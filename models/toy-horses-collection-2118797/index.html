About This Model

Toy horses in several styles, from rocking pieces to small figurines, make up this collection for nurseries and playrooms. Rounded shapes, painted manes and friendly faces keep the toys child-safe in appearance, even when they share a frame with real furniture.

The set carries CheckMate Lite certification, confirming that files open properly and the geometry holds together. Glossy enamel paint and soft fabric surfaces call for distinct shaders, and each horse can be recolored independently. Christmas morning setups also benefit from a new pony waiting beside the tree with a ribbon around its neck.

Two versions appear on this page: Toy Horses Collection at $43 and a second herd at $59. Sold Royalty Free from 2023, the toys fit children's book illustrations, toy store interiors and family animation. A rocking horse by the window remains one of the most recognizable symbols of childhood in a scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
