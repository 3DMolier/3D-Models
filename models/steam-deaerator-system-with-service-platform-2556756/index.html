About This Model

A steam deaerator with its storage tank and service platform, the power plant unit that strips oxygen from boiler feedwater. The 391,326 polygons model the horizontal tank, the deaerating head, piping, ladders and the railings around the platform. With 393,809 vertices, flanges and pipe bends stay round even in close inspection shots.

Mixed UV mapping means shared areas need a check before painting unique insulation or rust. StemCell certification keeps the steel, insulation and painted surfaces consistent across major renderers. The unit spans 344.2 x 731.57 x H632.02 cm, a realistic footprint for an industrial boiler house.

Released in 2026, the system suits power plant visualization, engineering training and industrial process animations. Technicians on the platform give scale and show how maintenance access is planned. Steam vents and warm lighting make it feel like an active plant, and process engineers bring such models to design reviews before fabrication.
