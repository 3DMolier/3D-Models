About This Model

Whole Red Dragon Fruit is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 2,985 polygons and 2,973 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for food photography, film & TV VFX and game assets. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Whole Red Dragon Fruit?

5 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Whole Red Dragon Fruit mesh?

2,985 polygons and 2,973 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the Whole Red Dragon Fruit model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
