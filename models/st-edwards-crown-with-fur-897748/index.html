About This Model

St Edwards Crown with Fur recreates the solid gold coronation crown of the British monarchy, with its ermine band and velvet cap. Four crosses alternate with four fleurs-de-lis around the rim, and two arches meet under a jeweled monde and cross. The fur trim at the base is what separates this version from a bare metal crown on a display stand.

Gemstones set along the arches catch highlights well, so a slow turntable shows off the faceted stones nicely. The crown carries the CheckMate Pro mark, a guarantee of correct scale, tidy topology and working materials. Velvet in the cap and the soft white ermine need different shaders, so each surface is worth tuning on its own.

Released in 2015, the model is picked for coronation reenactments, royal history documentaries and fantasy court scenes. Resting on a red cushion or placed on a seated king, it becomes the focal point of any throne room. At $49 it gives museum displays and period dramas a convincing piece of regalia without building one from scratch.
