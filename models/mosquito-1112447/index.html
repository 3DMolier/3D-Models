About This Model

The Mosquito is a production-ready Animals & Creatures 3D model, priced at $69 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 9,270 polygons and 9,288 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 7 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
