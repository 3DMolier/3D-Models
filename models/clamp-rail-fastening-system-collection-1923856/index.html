About This Model

Clamp Rail Fastening System Collection supplies the clips, bolts and base plates that hold a steel rail to sleepers, a detail most track models skip. Grouping the parts together lets a railway close-up show how the rail actually stays in place.

Each component passed CheckMate Pro inspection, so scale is real-world and the hierarchy opens without stray helpers or broken normals. Spring clips and threaded bolts are modeled as distinct shapes, which keeps them readable when the camera drops to ballast level. Rusty or freshly painted materials let the same parts serve both an old branch line and a new high-speed corridor.

The fastening collection joined our catalog in 2022 and serves railway engineering visualization, infrastructure training and train simulator environments. Repeat the clamps along a long track section with instancing and the whole line gains mechanical credibility at very little cost. Maintenance animations can also isolate a single clip to show installation or inspection steps for track crews.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
