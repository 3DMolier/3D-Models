About This Model

Retro Stand Mixer is one of the Furniture & Interior models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Geometry weighs in at 25,925 polygons over 25,115 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
