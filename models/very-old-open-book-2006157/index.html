About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Old Open Book is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Geometry weighs in at 13,606 polygons over 6,900 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
