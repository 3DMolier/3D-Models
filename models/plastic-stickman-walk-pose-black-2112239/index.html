About This Model

Priced at $9, the Plastic Stickman Walk Pose is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 2,448 polygons and 1,226 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
