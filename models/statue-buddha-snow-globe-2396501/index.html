About This Model

Priced at $39, the Statue Buddha Snow Globe is a finished Signage & Decor model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 9,033 polygons and 7,275 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
