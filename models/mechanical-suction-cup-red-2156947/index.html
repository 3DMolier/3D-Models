Looking for a Tools asset? The Mechanical Suction Cup Red is a finished, render-ready model at $19. You are getting 7,996 polygons and 4,107 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
