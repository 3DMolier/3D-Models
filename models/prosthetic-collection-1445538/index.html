About This Model

A collection of prosthetic limbs for medical, rehabilitation and sports scenes, showing how artificial parts replace lost function. Sockets, pylons and joints are the parts that define a prosthesis, and they are what viewers look for in close shots. Having several devices in one set allows a clinic or a fitting room to show a real range of options.

The prosthetics carry CheckMate Pro certification, so TurboSquid reviewed their geometry and scene files. Metal and carbon surfaces on these devices catch studio light nicely, which suits clean product style renders. Medical explainers, games and films can show the devices royalty free, without extra licensing.

Sold from 2019, the limb set fits orthopedic clinic visualizations, patient education and stories about athletes with limb loss. Mounted on a character or shown on a display stand, the devices help explain how modern prosthetics restore movement. Engineering students also study such shapes when learning how a socket transfers weight to the artificial limb.
