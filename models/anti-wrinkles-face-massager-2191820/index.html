About This Model

A handheld facial massager with a rounded head and a slim grip, this device belongs in skincare routines, beauty counters and bathroom vanity shots. Its 36,319 polygons go mostly into the curved massage head and the soft transition where it meets the handle. The 35,515 vertices keep those curves smooth, so reflections slide across the glossy shell without visible faceting.

None of the UV islands overlap, which leaves clean room for a pearl finish, button icons or fine fingerprints on the casing. StemCell certification means the massager was built once and delivered to several renderers with the same PBR look.

Two versions are on offer, the Anti Wrinkles Face Massager at $15 and the Rowenta Youth Facial Massager at $12. Listed from 2024, it suits cosmetics product shots, spa interiors, self-care tutorials and morning routine scenes in lifestyle animation. Resting on a marble tray beside a serum bottle, the device instantly reads as a premium skincare tool.
