About This Model

Pyramid Calendar is one of the general models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $12. Counted at the source, the model is 253,452 polygons and 126,431 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, event visualization and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
