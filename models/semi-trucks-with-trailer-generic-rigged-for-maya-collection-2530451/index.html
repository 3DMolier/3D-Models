About This Model

Unbranded tractor units hauling trailers are prepared for Maya in this rigged collection, suited to freight and road traffic animation. Heavy front bumpers, wide mirrors, dual rear wheels and long box trailers deliver the full presence of a highway hauler. Plain liveries make the rigs a neutral canvas for fictional fleets, delivery companies or film props.

Construction adheres to CheckMate specification, so the Maya outliner lists cabs, trailers and wheels in logical groups. Animators can drive wheel rotation and trailer swing for convincing turns at intersections or truck stops. Rain-soaked asphalt reflecting the headlights gives night scenes at a truck stop a gritty, realistic look.

New for 2026, the collection is royalty free and fits feature films, game cinematics and logistics presentations. Driving schools illustrate blind spots and wide turns with trucks like these in simple top-down animations. A row of rigs idling at a truck stop at dusk makes an evocative establishing frame.
