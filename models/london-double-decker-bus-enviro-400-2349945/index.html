About This Model

London Double Decker Bus Enviro 400 belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $79 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and game environments as the intended applications. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

At 445,804 polygons and 468,395 vertices this is a mid-weight asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Texture work runs to 35 maps, the largest at 8192x8192, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
