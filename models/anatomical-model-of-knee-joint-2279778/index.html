About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Anatomical Model of Knee Joint is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Geometry weighs in at 5,467 polygons over 5,511 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
