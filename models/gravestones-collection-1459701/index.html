About This Model

A collection of gravestones in varied shapes, from rounded headstones to crosses and slabs, ready to fill a churchyard or haunted cemetery. Each stone keeps a simple, readable silhouette, so the set reads well both up close and as distant background. Mixing shapes and tilts makes a graveyard look old and organic rather than neatly repeated.

The set carries CheckMate Lite certification and comes with non-overlapping UVs for weathered stone, moss and engraved names. Lean some at angles, sink others into the grass and add ivy to suggest decades of neglect. Moonlight and fog are the classic pairing, though the stones also suit a quiet sunny memorial scene.

This collection has been in our range since 2019 under royalty-free terms, among the older sets we offer. Halloween decorations, horror games, gothic films and historical churchyards are where these stones get the most use. Level designers like sets like this because a few pieces cover a large area with variety.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2019 · Updated 13 November 2026
