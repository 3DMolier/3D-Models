About This Model

A tiny dwarf hamster with fluffy fur comes rigged with animation for Maya, ready to sniff, nibble and scurry. The character gives pet stories and children's content a small hero full of personality. Round cheeks, a stubby tail and tiny pink paws match the real animal closely.

Fur is the key to this model, because a hamster without its soft coat simply stops reading as a hamster. The rig allows cheek pouches, ear twitches and paw movements that make close-up animation charming. Warm side lighting brings out the fur texture and the gentle color shift along the back.

Released in 2026 at $469, the hamster is licensed for both paid and classroom work. Pet food visuals for shops, veterinary explainers, children's animation and casual games suit it well. Place it beside a seed or a wheel and the scale contrast immediately shows how small the animal is.
