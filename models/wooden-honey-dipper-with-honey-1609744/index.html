About This Model

The Wooden Honey Dipper Honey is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 3,863 polygons and 4,057 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer.
