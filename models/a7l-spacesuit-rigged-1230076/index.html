About This Model

Priced at $149, the A7L Spacesuit Rigged is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 30,333 polygons and 28,868 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 10 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
