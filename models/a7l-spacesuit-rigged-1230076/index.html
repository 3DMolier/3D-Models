About This Model

A7L Spacesuit Rigged is a detailed Space & Sci-Fi asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 30,333 polygons and 28,868 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 10 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
