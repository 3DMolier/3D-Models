About This Model

A Babolat Pure Drive tennis bag that holds a strung racket, the padded carry case players sling over a shoulder on court. Its 6,778 polygons shape the zipped compartment, the racket-head outline, the strap and the stitched panels. With 6,987 vertices, the bag stays light enough to scatter around a locker room or court bench.

Three 4096 x 4096 maps carry the printed graphics and fabric weave on a clean overlap-free unwrap. With StemCell certification and Editorial Uses Only terms, it suits sports journalism and equipment reviews. Leaning against a net post, it instantly says that a match is about to start.

Four versions come in this group: two Babolat Pure Drive bags plus two generic tennis racquet bags. The generic bags work well for tennis club scenes where no manufacturer should be visible. Released in 2018, the bag fits tennis court renders, sporting goods store interiors and athlete character kits.
