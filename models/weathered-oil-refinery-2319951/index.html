About This Model

A refinery is a forest of columns, drums, heat exchangers and pipe racks, and its visual character comes almost entirely from age: streaked insulation cladding, rust running down from flanges, faded paint on tank shells and stained concrete plinths. A clean refinery looks like a rendering exercise; a weathered one looks like an industry.

It is a very large asset at 3,004,941 polygons and 3,146,207 vertices in quads and tris. The UVs overlap, which is essential economy on a plant built from thousands of repeated fittings, and it keeps a site this size workable.

At $299 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits oil and gas visualisation, energy sector marketing, infrastructure and environmental presentation, documentary and news work, film and television, and games. On sale since 2025.
