About This Model

Looking for a general asset? The Forensic Linear Scale with Numbered Markings White is a finished, render-ready model at $3. You are getting 234 polygons and 236 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 10.0 x 3.0 x H0.2 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
