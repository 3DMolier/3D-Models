About This Model

A sniper rigged for Modo, armed with a Barrett 98 Bravo bolt-action rifle for long-range military scenes. The rifle's long barrel, bipod and large scope define the silhouette, the details a viewer looks for in a marksman. The rig lets you pose him prone in the grass, kneeling behind cover or walking with the weapon slung.

Built around Modo's conventions, the rig works natively there without retargeting from another animation package. Camouflage clothing and gear can be retextured for woodland, desert or ruined urban environments as needed. CheckMate guidance keeps him at true scale beside vehicles, cover objects and other soldiers.

Released in 2022, the sniper suits military games, war films, firearms training visuals and defense documentaries about precision shooting. A long lens shot from behind the scope, looking down a quiet valley at dawn, creates instant tension. Ghillie-style texturing and grass tufts on the shoulders would push the concealment further for close shots.
