About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Child Boy Street Style is a finished, render-ready model at $149. The mesh carries 18,330 polygons and 9,855 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
