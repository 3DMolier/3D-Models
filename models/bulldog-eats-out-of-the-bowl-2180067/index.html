About This Model

Bulldog Eats Out of the Bowl is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $159. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

Counted at the source, the model is 80,371 polygons and 50,165 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
