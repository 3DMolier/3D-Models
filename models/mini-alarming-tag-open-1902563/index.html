About This Model

A small anti-theft alarm tag shown open, the clip-on device stores attach to garments and bottles to stop shoplifting. Its 14,678 polygons model the plastic housing, the release mechanism and the pin socket exposed by the open position. With 14,279 vertices close to the face count, the shell is built from dense closed pieces instead of a light cage.

Non-overlapping UVs leave clean areas for a warning label, molded logo or the gray plastic grain seen in stores. TurboSquid rated the tag CheckMate Pro after reviewing it, confirming tidy geometry, genuine scale and organized naming for every part. Matte plastic with a slightly glossy release button reads well under the harsh fluorescent lighting of retail interiors.

Dating from 2022, the tag fits retail security explainers, store interior renders and training videos for checkout staff. The open state is handy for showing how the device unlocks, either next to a detacher or on a garment hem. Game artists drop it into convenience store levels as a small interactive prop on shelves and clothing racks.
