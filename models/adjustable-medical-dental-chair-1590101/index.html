About This Model

Dental Chair is an adjustable treatment chair with a reclining backrest, headrest, swing-out tray table and an overhead examination light. Its 34,541 polygons cover the padded upholstery, the hydraulic base, the instrument arm and the foot control. Smooth curves on the seat follow the body, the way modern clinic chairs keep patients comfortable for long visits.

Texture work uses 7 maps at 4096 x 4096 for leatherette, painted metal and the plastic light housing. The 35,479 vertices carry clean UVs, so the upholstery color can be changed in one pass. StemCell certification guarantees the clinic materials behave the same in any supported renderer.

Both versions cost $59, this generic chair and a Dental Planmeca Chair. Since 2020 the chair has furnished dental clinic interiors, medical training scenes and healthcare explainers. Pair it with a cabinet of instruments and an x-ray viewer to finish the treatment room.
