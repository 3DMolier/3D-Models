About This Model

The quilted Chanel flap bag in black grained calfskin, with its chain strap and turn-lock clasp, rendered for fashion scenes. The 16,828 polygons capture the diamond quilting, the interlaced chain with leather and the raised clasp on the front flap. Its 8,487 vertices, about half the face count, show a compact triangulated build that stays light on a vanity or shelf.

Leather grain, gold hardware and stitching are achieved with materials, since the file has no image texture set. Every UV island sits on its own, so a bump pattern for the grained leather can be applied without distortion. TurboSquid reviewed the scale, naming and hierarchy of the bag and granted it CheckMate Pro after a manual pass.

The piece is licensed under Editorial Uses Only, so it fits fashion journalism, style history features and museum catalogs. Out since 2023 at $39, it shows up in articles about luxury collecting, wardrobe illustrations and educational fashion lectures. Rest it on a velvet chair with the chain draped across, and the frame reads as a quiet study of design.
