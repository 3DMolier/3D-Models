About This Model

A modern cattle shed is designed around airflow more than shelter. The open sides, the ridge vent and the height of the roof are all there to keep air moving over a large number of animals, because heat and damp cause far more losses than cold ever does.

The model holds 868,145 polygons and 896,628 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, suited to the repeated structure and fittings. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $89 it suits agricultural and livestock visualization, farm building presentation, equipment and welfare material, documentary work, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2025.
