About This Model

Saola Walking belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $139 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 17,372 polygons over 13,571 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
