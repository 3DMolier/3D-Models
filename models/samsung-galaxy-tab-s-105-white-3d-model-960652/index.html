About This Model

A slim white widescreen tablet with a vivid display, aimed at video and photo browsing more than office work. This model shows the thin bronze-toned edge band and the dimpled back panel that defined the Tab S line.

Small snap points that once held the original cover are modeled on the back, helping whenever the tablet lies face down. The wide aspect suits film stills on screen as well as landscape slide presentations in a meeting room. Holding CheckMate Pro certification, the file brings clean structure, sensible part names and real scale.

It has been listed since 2015, licensed for editorial work only: tech reviews, feature stories and learning material. Stand it in landscape on a coffee table with a movie frame on the display. It also fits a commuter's hands in a train carriage scene, held sideways for watching a show. The thin profile looks especially good in a side view against a bright window.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2015 · Updated 12 November 2026
