About This Model

Belt Bucklers are hand tools that tension a metal band around a pipe or post and lock it with a buckle in one motion. The pair of tools takes 100,920 polygons and 50,572 vertices, covering the knurled crank, the cutting lever and the slotted nose.

The file respects the CheckMate structural rules that certified tools follow, although no badge is attached. Non-overlapping UVs give each handle and lever its own texture space for painted steel, rubber grips and machining marks. Laid on a workbench next to a coil of banding, the tools explain their purpose without any extra props.

Two versions are listed at $44 each: these clean tools and the Used Belt Bucklers with workshop wear. Electricians and sign installers rely on such tools daily, which makes them believable in a utility van interior. The set came onto the market in 2023 and has already been bought by a studio. Tool catalogs, trade training videos, workshop scenes and industrial supply renders are fitting uses.
