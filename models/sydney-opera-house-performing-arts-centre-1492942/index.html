About This Model

Priced at $179, the Sydney Opera House Performing Arts Centre is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 283,370 polygons and 339,184 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

67 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, event visualization and product rendering. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
