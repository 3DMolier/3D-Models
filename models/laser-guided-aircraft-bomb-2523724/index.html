About This Model

A laser guided bomb is a conventional weapon with a kit bolted to each end: a seeker and canard fins at the nose that steer toward reflected laser light, and a fin assembly at the tail for stability. That is why the shape looks like a bomb with extra hardware rather than a purpose-built missile, and the model keeps the seeker head, the canards, the lug band and the tail fins.

The mesh is 39,142 polygons and 40,551 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout. That budget goes into the fin roots and the seeker assembly, which is where a close camera lands when the weapon is shown on a pylon.

At $59 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits defence visualization, news and documentary graphics, military training material, aviation renders where an aircraft carries stores, and games. Real-world scale is kept, so it hangs correctly from a pylon. On sale since 2026.
