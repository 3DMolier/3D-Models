About This Model

The monobloc chair is the most manufactured piece of furniture in history and nobody knows who designed it. It is made in one shot from one material, stacks without hardware, and costs less than the labour to repair it.

The model is light at 15,814 polygons and 7,889 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in white. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a green build is offered separately.

At $24 it suits interior and exterior visualization, event and hospitality scenes, furniture marketing, film sets, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.
