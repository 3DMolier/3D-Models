About This Model

Napkin 2 is one of the Kitchen & Tableware models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 21,328 polygons and 21,330 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 3 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2015 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
