About This Model

Woven wicker baskets filled with groceries and household goods, gathered in one collection for markets, pantries and picnic scenes. The Wicker Baskets with Products Collection pairs the woven containers with the items inside, so each basket arrives already dressed. Interlaced strands and twisted rims give the baskets their handmade look even from across a room.

The models passed CheckMate Pro review, so geometry, naming and real-world scale are consistent across the collection. That consistency matters when baskets of different sizes sit side by side on a shelf or market table. Natural fibers look best with warm, diffuse light that brings out the weave without harsh glare.

Part of the catalog since 2022, the set works for farmers' market stalls, country kitchen interiors and gift hamper visuals. Game artists use filled baskets to make village homes and shops feel stocked and inhabited. Remove the products and the empty baskets still serve as storage props in bathrooms and laundry rooms.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
