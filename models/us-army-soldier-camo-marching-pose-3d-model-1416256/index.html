About This Model

This Characters & People model - US Army Soldier Camo Marching Pose - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $119 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 26,563 polygons and 27,927 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It ships 22 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
