About This Model

Looking for a Nature & Plants asset? The Primula Vulgaris Red Fur is a finished, render-ready model at $39. The mesh carries 122,652 polygons and 66,558 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
