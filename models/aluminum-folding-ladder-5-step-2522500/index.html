About This Model

This aluminum step ladder unfolds into an A-frame with wide treads, a top platform and a safety bar for household and workshop jobs. The Aluminum Folding Ladder 5 Step uses 38,312 polygons and 39,218 vertices for the rails, rivets, hinges and non-slip feet. Opened, it stands 186.19 cm tall within a 142.65 x 48.96 cm footprint, matching real scale beside a person.

StemCell brings several native files from one source, each keeping the same brushed aluminum and rubber materials. UVs are mixed here, so check shared islands before adding unique warning labels to the rails. Daylight from a window glints along the rails and makes the ladder look freshly unpacked.

Introduced in 2026, the ladder suits home renovation visuals, hardware store catalogs, painting scenes and DIY tutorials. Animators climb it to change a bulb, while interior designers place it beside a half-painted wall. Folded and leaned against a garage wall, it also serves as quiet background detail in workshop scenes.
