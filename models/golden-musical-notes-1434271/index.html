About This Model

The Golden Musical Notes is a production-ready general 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. You are getting 5,056 polygons and 4,956 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

1 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
