About This Model

A kitesurfing control bar with a harness belt, this kit shows the gear a rider uses to steer and hook in for long sessions. The 24,634 polygons and 25,740 vertices shape the padded grip, the center lines, the chicken loop and the wide supportive waist strap. Its long horizontal handle and trailing lines make the equipment recognizable even when it lies on the sand.

43 texture maps at 2048 x 2048 cover grippy foam, webbing straps, buckles and printed details on the harness. TurboSquid's hand check ended with a CheckMate Pro result, so topology, scale and naming are in order. The UV layout keeps all shells apart, which lets you change the colorway of the harness without stretching any print.

Arriving in 2020, the kit fits watersports shop visuals, kiteboarding tutorials, beach scenes and extreme sports games. Lay the gear on a board at the shoreline, and the frame tells a story before anyone enters the water. Kite schools can also label each part in renders to teach beginners how the safety release works.
