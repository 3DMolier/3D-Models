About This Model

Priced at $24, the Metal Stand with Umbrellas is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 93,548 polygons over 50,109 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
