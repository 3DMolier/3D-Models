About This Model

Light Aircraft Piper PA-28-161 Warrior III is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 49,029 polygons over 51,238 vertices - a light build for its class. Texture work runs to 14 maps, the largest at 8192x8192, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
