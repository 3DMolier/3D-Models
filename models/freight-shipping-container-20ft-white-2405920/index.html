About This Model

This Containers & Storage model - Container 20ft - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 42,568 polygons and 42,652 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 244.07 x 605.96 x H259.03 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing.
