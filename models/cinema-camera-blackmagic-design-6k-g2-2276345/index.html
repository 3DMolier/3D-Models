About This Model

A cinema camera body at 38,271 polygons, with the mount ring, the side grip, the control cluster, the vent fins and the cage points modelled. A camera is a prop that appears in the hands of characters on set, and the control layout is what makes it read as a real machine rather than a black box.

The build follows CheckMate specification for topology and real-world scale, so it balances on a shoulder rig or a tripod head at believable size. Non-overlapping UVs keep the lettering and the rubberised surfaces separate.

Four versions share the page, including builds with the tally light on and a more generic professional camera for work that cannot name the maker. Editorial terms apply to the branded build. Priced at $69 for film and production scenes, product visualisation and games.
