About This Model

A bundle of everyday travel accessories, the small items that make a hotel room, airport gate or packed suitcase feel real. Each piece is a separate object, so a single item can be lifted out and placed on a bed, desk or tray.

The bundle was assembled under the CheckMate specification, keeping scale and pivots consistent from item to item. That matters when small props sit next to larger luggage, where a wrong size breaks the illusion instantly. Materials stay simple and separated, so leather, fabric and plastic finishes can be swapped to match any color palette.

Released in 2025, the collection suits travel blogs, booking website illustrations and airport scenes in games. Spread a few items across an unmade bed and the viewer reads a story of arrival or departure. Product designers also use such sets as reference objects to judge the size of new luggage concepts. Place a few beside an open case on the floor for a convincing packing scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
