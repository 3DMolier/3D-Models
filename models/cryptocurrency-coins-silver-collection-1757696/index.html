About This Model

This collection of silver cryptocurrency coins turns digital currencies into physical tokens, the image every finance article reaches for. Crisp embossed symbols and ridged edges make each coin read at a glance, even when piled into a glittering heap.

Silver metal shows every dent and highlight, so the relief is modeled rather than painted for close-ups. The bundle carries a CheckMate Pro rating, meaning clean geometry, real scale and orderly object names. Thin coin profiles keep the poly count low enough to spill hundreds across a desk in a single render.

Two versions belong to this family: the original silver coins set costing $31, with a second silver collection costing $34. Since 2021 the coins have illustrated crypto news, fintech explainers and trading app visuals. A slight bevel on every rim catches a thin line of light, which separates overlapping coins in a pile. Lit from the side on a dark surface, a falling stack of coins becomes a vivid image of market swings.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
