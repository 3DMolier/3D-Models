About This Model

A slim ultrabook with a thin metal lid, a compact keyboard deck and narrow bezels, modeled for desks, cafes and travel scenes. The Laptop Samsung ATIV Book 9 12 inch captures the tapered profile and the tight hinge line of a premium business notebook. The lid can sit open on a table or closed in a bag, and both poses read as the same machine.

The ultrabook passed CheckMate Pro inspection, so its mesh is clean and its materials are complete. Brushed metal on the lid and a dark glossy screen give reflections a lot to work with under office light. Keys, ports and the trackpad edge are separated cleanly, which makes swapping screen content or keyboard color simple.

This notebook entered the catalog in 2015 and is licensed for Editorial Uses Only. That suits news graphics, tech reviews, documentaries and films that need a recognizable laptop on a desk. Interior visualizers also place it on study tables and hotel desks to give an empty room a lived-in feel.
