About This Model

Grilled Tomahawk Steak is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. You are getting 2,654 polygons and 2,655 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
