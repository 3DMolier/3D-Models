About This Model

Soviet Anti-Personnel Mine Green is one of the Weapons models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 22,659 polygons and 11,845 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
