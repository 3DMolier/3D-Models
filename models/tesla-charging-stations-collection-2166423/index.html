About This Model

A collection of electric vehicle charging stations, including tall superchargers and wall connectors for garages. The stations show their cable holders, connectors and slim pillars in clean modern forms. Glowing accent strips and coiled cables make them easy to recognize in a parking lot.

They carry CheckMate Lite certification, so every format opens without errors and renders straight away. Their license is Editorial Uses Only, covering news, reports and educational scenes about electric cars. Each station is separate, so a row of chargers can line a parking lot while a wall unit sits in a home garage.

Introduced in 2023, the collection suits parking lot scenes, urban planning visualizations and electric mobility explainers. A row of chargers lit at night with cars plugged in shows the future of driving in one image. Planners also use these stations to illustrate how charging networks grow along highways and in city centers.
