About This Model

The Smartphone Generic is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 13,015 polygons and 13,916 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

6 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
