About This Model

Mechanical Patient Weighing Scale with Height Rod is one of the Medical models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. 2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
