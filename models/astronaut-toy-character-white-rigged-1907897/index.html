About This Model

Priced at $69, the Astronaut Toy Character is a finished Toys & Games model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 15,540 polygons and 15,181 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
