About This Model

The Racing Game is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 15,549 polygons and 16,255 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2021 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
