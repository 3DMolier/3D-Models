About This Model

This Sports & Recreation model - Basketball Hoop - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 4,554 polygons and 4,828 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
