About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Loincloth is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 6,233 polygons and 6,225 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
