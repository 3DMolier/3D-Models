About This Model

Life Cycle of Sunflower Collection shows a sunflower at successive growth stages, from seed and sprout to a full blooming head. Each stage has its own leaves, stem thickness and flower size, so the sequence reads clearly in a row. The final mature plant carries a heavy seed head that bends slightly under its weight.

The plants follow TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, with clean geometry and sensibly named parts. Petals and seed patterns live in materials, so colors can shift from fresh yellow to dried brown. Each stage can be blended into the next to build a smooth time-lapse growth sequence.

Released in 2025, the collection suits biology lessons, agriculture explainers, children's science books and garden design visuals. Arranged left to right on a soil strip, the plants turn a textbook diagram into a living scene. Farmers' cooperatives and seed producers can illustrate crop development without waiting for a season.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
