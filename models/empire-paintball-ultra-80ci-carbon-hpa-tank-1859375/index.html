About This Model

This carbon fiber HPA tank from Empire Paintball stores high-pressure air that powers a paintball marker during a game. Its 5,184 polygons and 5,369 vertices shape the wrapped cylinder, the regulator head and the threaded valve at the top. The woven carbon look and compact size make it recognizable to players who know competitive gear.

TurboSquid granted it CheckMate Pro after reviewing the topology and naming for clean use in any scene. Non-overlapping UVs let the carbon weave, gauge face and warning stickers be textured with precision. A glossy clear coat over the dark weave catches studio light, giving the tank a premium sporting look.

Dating back to 2022, the tank suits paintball game inventories, sports equipment visuals and gear review illustrations. Mounted on the back of a marker or lying in a gear bag, it fits field and locker room scenes. Players and coaches also lean on visuals of this kind when explaining air systems to newcomers in the sport.
