About This Model

Prisoner on Electric Chair is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $189. You are getting 113,346 polygons and 116,161 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and architectural visualization.

The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
