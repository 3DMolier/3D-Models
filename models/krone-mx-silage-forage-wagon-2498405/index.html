About This Model

A forage wagon picks the crop up, chops it and carries it in one machine, which removes an entire vehicle from the harvest chain. On grass silage that matters enormously, because the whole operation is a race against the weather rather than against the calendar.

The model holds 526,955 polygons and 543,904 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout. It is built to CheckMate specification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $89 it suits agricultural machinery marketing, farming visualization, rural scenes, training material, documentary work, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2026.
