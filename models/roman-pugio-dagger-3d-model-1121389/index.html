About This Model

Roman Pugio Dagger is one of the Weapons models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $9. You are getting 4,822 polygons and 4,980 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 4 maps at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
