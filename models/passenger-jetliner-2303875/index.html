About This Model

Passenger Jetliner is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. Geometry weighs in at 154,849 polygons over 169,003 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Can the Passenger Jetliner model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How heavy is the Passenger Jetliner mesh?
