About This Model

A generic aerodynamic sleeper tractor fills long-haul and logistics scenes without carrying any manufacturer badge on its sloped hood. The 557,064 polygons shape a tall roof fairing, side extenders and a sleeper compartment behind the driver. Without logos, the cab can be painted in any fleet livery your story requires.

There are 597,781 vertices, and the UVs are mixed, with some repeated surfaces sharing texture space to keep maps compact. The build follows CheckMate guidelines, so objects arrive named and grouped for quick setup. Twin exhaust stacks and chrome fuel tanks give the side profile a classic American long-haul look on the open road.

Its two versions are a static tractor for $149 and a rigged heavy duty sleeper cab for $169. Rigging lets the front wheels steer, which matters when the truck must turn into a loading bay. Released in 2026, it suits freight company presentations, highway scenes and transport games.
