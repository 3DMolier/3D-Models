About This Model

This Vehicles model - Deployed TPY 2 Radar Desert - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. The polygon count is 138,741, spread over 152,250 vertices. Whether that fits depends on where it lands - an architectural still and a real-time walkthrough tolerate very different counts.

Edge flow follows the form rather than fighting it, which is what lets the silhouette hold up when the camera moves in. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
