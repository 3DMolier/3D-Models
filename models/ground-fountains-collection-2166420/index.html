About This Model

A collection of ground-level fountains, the jets that spring straight from paving in plazas, parks and children's splash areas. Several layouts come together, so a square can have a single jet, a ring of nozzles or a long row. Flush grates and hidden nozzles keep the paving walkable, which is exactly how modern plaza fountains work.

CheckMate Lite certification confirms basic scale and file sanity, so the fountains align with standard paving grids. Water columns sit apart from the stone or metal grates, letting you swap them for your own simulation. Wet paving around the jets catches reflections, which helps the scene read as fresh and cool.

On offer since 2023, the collection suits urban plaza renders, park masterplans, mall entrances and city builder games. Landscape architects use it to show playful water features in public space proposals. Add children running between the jets on a sunny day, and the plaza instantly feels alive.
