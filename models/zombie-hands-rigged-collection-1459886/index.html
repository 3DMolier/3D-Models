About This Model

A set of decayed zombie hands with torn skin, exposed bone and clawed fingers, made for horror scenes and first person creature shots. Each hand keeps a grasping gesture, which makes them perfect for bursting out of soil or reaching through a broken window.

The collection carries CheckMate Pro approval, meaning topology, naming and proportions were inspected. Rotting flesh, dirt under the nails and dried blood sit on distinct material areas, so the grime can be pushed darker or fresher. Separate hands can be scattered through a graveyard set, which builds a large undead crowd from a small asset.

These hands have been part of the catalog since 2019 and serve horror games, Halloween animations and music video effects. Directors place them coming up through cemetery earth for a classic jump scare. Escape rooms and haunted attractions also feature them in projected effects and spooky promotional clips for events.
