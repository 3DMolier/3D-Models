About This Model

Transport Cargo Helicopter belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 766,691 polygons and 417,230 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
