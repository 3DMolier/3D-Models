About This Model

The Pilatus PC-12M Eagle is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags film production, game environments and VR experiences as the intended applications. 2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
