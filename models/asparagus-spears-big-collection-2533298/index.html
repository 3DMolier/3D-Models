About This Model

A large set of asparagus spears in loose and grouped arrangements, made for food photography, recipe content and grocery scenes. Each spear has the tight scaled tip and the woody lower stem, which is how shoppers judge freshness in real life. Having many spears in one purchase means a bunch never shows the same stalk twice in a close shot.

Spears share one consistent scale under CheckMate guidelines, so they sit correctly beside plates, cutting boards and pans. Green fading to pale purple near the tips is the color cue that makes asparagus convincing under warm kitchen light. Rubber bands and twine from a market bunch are easy to add around the stems for retail shots.

Released in 2026, the set suits cooking channels, packaging mockups, supermarket visuals and healthy eating campaigns. Grilled, steamed or raw, the spears can be shaded differently to match whatever stage of the recipe the frame shows. For a spring menu or a farmers market banner, it supplies the vegetable in bulk without modeling each stalk.
