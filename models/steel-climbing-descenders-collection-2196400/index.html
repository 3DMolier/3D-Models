About This Model

This collection of steel climbing descenders gathers the friction devices climbers use to control a rope during rappels. Rings, plates and curved bars show how each device bends the rope to slow a descent. Clipped to a harness or hanging from a gear loop, they make a climbing character look properly equipped.

TurboSquid certified the collection as CheckMate Pro, confirming clean geometry, correct scale and organized files. Polished steel with worn edges where the rope rubs gives the devices a believable used finish. Sharp highlights on curved surfaces help each piece stand out when laid side by side on a mat.

Offered since 2024, the descenders suit mountaineering guides, rescue training material and outdoor gear catalogs. Rope access workers on wind turbines and cave explorers also appear in scenes where devices like these are essential. Adventure games can hand them to players as tools for reaching deep canyons, wells or cliff faces.
