About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Ultrasonic Diffuser is a finished, render-ready model at $18. You are getting 5,187 polygons and 2,783 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
