About This Model

Oil Pump Jack is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. The mesh carries 284,241 polygons and 300,037 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 21 textures, authored at 4096 x 4906 and 1024 x 1024. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and software demos. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
