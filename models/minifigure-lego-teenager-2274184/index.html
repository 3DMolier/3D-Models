About This Model

The Minifigure LEGO Teenager is a production-ready Toys & Games 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 6,899 polygons and 7,008 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
