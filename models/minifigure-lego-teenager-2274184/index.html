About This Model

Minifigure LEGO Teenager belongs to our Toys & Games range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
