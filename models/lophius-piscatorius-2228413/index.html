About This Model

The European anglerfish, or monkfish, with its huge gaping mouth, needle teeth and fleshy lure on top of the head. The 15,582 polygons shape the flattened body, the broad pectoral fins and the fringe of skin flaps along the jaw. With 15,340 vertices the fish stays light for aquarium scenes, seafood displays and seafloor environments.

As a StemCell model, its PBR materials look identical whichever supported renderer opens the file. Mottled brown and olive skin lets the predator vanish against sand, which is exactly how it ambushes prey. A non-overlapping unwrap keeps the mouth interior and the skin flaps separate when they are repainted.

Released in 2024, the anglerfish suits marine biology lessons, deep sea documentaries and seafood market scenes. Lit from below with the lure glowing faintly, the fish becomes a perfect monster for a deep water shot. Laid on crushed ice beside lemons, it also works as an unusual subject for food photography.
