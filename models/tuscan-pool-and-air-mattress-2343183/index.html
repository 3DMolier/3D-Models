About This Model

Priced at $21, the Tuscan Pool and Air Mattress is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 28,816 polygons over 28,788 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
