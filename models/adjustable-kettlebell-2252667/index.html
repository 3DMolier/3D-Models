About This Model

A cast handle sits over a stack of removable plates, so a single bell covers several training weights in a home gym scene. The Adjustable Kettlebell uses 13,518 polygons and 13,541 vertices, a light budget that still rounds the handle and the plate edges cleanly. It works on a rubber floor beside a bench or lined up in a rack with other free weights.

Built to CheckMate specification, the file follows TurboSquid's rules for naming, scale and tidy scene structure. Non-overlapping UVs give the handle, the locking pin and each disk their own space for chipped paint or knurled grip texture.

Six versions are offered: this one, gray and red adjustable kettlebells, and a kettlebell with disks in red, gray and its base finish. Color choice matters in a gym set, where a red bell stands out from black plates and gray reads as plain cast iron. Sold since 2024, it suits fitness app visuals, gym interiors and sports game props.
