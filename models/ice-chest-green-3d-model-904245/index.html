About This Model

A green Ice Chest with a white lid, side handles and a sturdy latch, the cooler that comes along to every picnic and fishing trip. The hollow body shows its insulated wall thickness, so the lid can open to reveal drinks and ice inside. Molded ridges on the lid double as a cup rest when the cooler sits on a tailgate or beach.

TurboSquid inspected the cooler and awarded CheckMate Pro after confirming tidy topology and believable proportions. Plastic materials can be tinted to other colors, while scuffs and stickers help it look like it has seen many summers. The lid is a separate piece with a hinge, which makes opening animations simple to set up.

Around since 2015, the chest suits camping scenes, beach days, fishing boat decks and backyard barbecue renders. Open it beside a folding chair with cans on ice and the image tells a story of a lazy summer afternoon. Outdoor retailers show it in guides about keeping food fresh on long camping weekends without power.
