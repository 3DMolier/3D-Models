About This Model

Priced at $19, the Instant Noodles is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits food advertising, packaging visualisation, restaurant interiors and game props.
