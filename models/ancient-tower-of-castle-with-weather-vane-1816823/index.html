About This Model

An ancient stone castle tower crowned with a conical roof and a weather vane, for medieval, fantasy and historic scenes. The 8,530 polygons shape the round walls, the arrow slits, the battlements and the iron vane on top. At 8,732 vertices, the tower is light enough to repeat along a whole fortress wall.

Stone, roof tiles and metal keep unique weathering on a UV sheet with no overlapping islands. TurboSquid rated the tower CheckMate Pro once modeling and proportions were confirmed, along with its naming. Viewed against a stormy sky, the vane and the dark roof make an unmistakable silhouette.

On sale since 2021, Ancient Tower of Castle with Weather Vane suits fantasy games, historical documentaries and storybook illustrations. Placed on a cliff above the sea, it becomes the landmark a hero travels toward. Tabletop game designers render it for box art, campaign maps and village quest posters.
