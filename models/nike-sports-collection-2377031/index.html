About This Model

The Nike Sports Collection gathers several Nike-branded sports items into one set for gym, locker room and stadium scenes. The swoosh logos and the brand's clean product shapes make the gear recognizable even on a background shelf. Buying the pieces together keeps their style and scale consistent across a single scene or a series of shots.

Object names stay tidy under CheckMate specification, so each item can be pulled out separately. Sports gear reads through silhouette first, so the shapes are kept crisp rather than lumpy. Lighting with a hard rim helps the logos and fabric edges stand out against a dark backdrop.

The license is Editorial Uses Only, so the collection belongs in news, sports journalism, documentaries and education. Available since 2025, it fits locker room interiors, sports retail reporting and athlete profile graphics. It is quicker than hunting down and matching separate branded models one at a time.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
