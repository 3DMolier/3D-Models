About This Model

Retail boxes for Apple's wireless earbuds, gathered as a set with the white packaging and product images the brand is known for. The Boxes AirPods Collection lets a tech scene show packaging without modeling each box from scratch. Clean folded edges and tight lids give the boxes the solid premium feel of the real thing.

Each piece meets CheckMate specification, with proper scale and boxes grouped as separate objects. Artwork panels can be replaced, so the boxes work in stories about any earbud model of the series.

With an Editorial Uses Only license, the set fits tech reviews, unboxing explainers and news on audio gear. It joined the catalog in 2024 and pairs with phone and laptop models for a desk setup scene. Open a box beside its case and earbuds, and the frame reads like the start of an unboxing video. Stacked boxes on a retail shelf, lit evenly, make a convincing electronics store background.
