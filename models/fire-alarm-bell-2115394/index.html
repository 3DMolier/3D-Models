About This Model

A conventional alarm bell is a red dome over a striker, mounted on a backbox with the wiring behind it. The dome is the resonator, and its size sets the pitch, which is why bells in large buildings look bigger rather than louder. It is one of the most recognisable safety fittings anywhere.

The mesh is light at 7,906 polygons and 3,960 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the dome, the striker and the mounting plate.

At $4 it suits building safety and fire system visualization, commercial and institutional interiors, architectural compliance material, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2023.
