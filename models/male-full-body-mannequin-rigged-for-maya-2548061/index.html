About This Model

A full body male mannequin rigged for Maya, the faceless display figure that poses clothing and fills shop windows. Smooth, featureless surfaces keep attention on whatever the figure wears, which is the point of any mannequin. Segmented joints at the shoulders, elbows and knees mimic the way real display mannequins are assembled.

Construction keeps to the CheckMate specification, so files open cleanly in Maya with tidy naming. Because the figure is rigged, poses can change from a confident stance to a casual lean in minutes. A glossy white or matte gray material are the usual choices, depending on how upscale the store should look.

Released in 2026, the mannequin suits retail interior visualization, fashion e-commerce, window display planning and animation tests. Dress it with clothing models to preview a whole collection before the physical samples arrive at the studio. Animators also like mannequins as neutral stand-ins for blocking shots before final characters exist.
