About This Model

Hot Glue Pen belongs to our general range and is offered at $12 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 10,886 polygons and 5,511 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
