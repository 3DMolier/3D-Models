About This Model

A collection of rigged dwarf men for Cinema 4D, stocky fantasy characters with big beards, ready for adventure scenes. Each dwarf has the broad shoulders, short legs and heavy boots that make the race recognizable at any distance. Their outfits lean toward rugged work and battle gear, so they suit miners, warriors and tavern regulars alike.

The rigs are prepared for Cinema 4D, so posing a dwarf with an axe raised or arms crossed takes minutes. Assets follow CheckMate specification, with characters placed at the origin and named so they can be swapped in a crowd quickly. Leather, metal and braided hair give the lighting artist plenty of material contrast under torchlight or bright daylight.

Released in 2026, the set fits fantasy games, animated shorts, tabletop promotional art and storybook illustration. Buying the group together means a mine, a fortress gate or a feast can be filled without hunting for matching styles. A row of dwarfs marching through a mountain pass is the kind of shot this collection handles out of the box.
