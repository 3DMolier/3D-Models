About This Model

The portable extractor is the machine that turns a spill into a job that takes ten minutes rather than a professional visit. It sprays, agitates and sucks back in one pass, and everything about the design is about being carried up stairs.

The model is built to CheckMate specification.

At $39 it suits appliance and product visualization, e-commerce and retail imagery, advertising, interior and cleaning scenes, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
