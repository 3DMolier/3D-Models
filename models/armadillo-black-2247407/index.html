About This Model

Armadillo Black is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 10,504 polygons and 10,834 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
