About This Model

This Aircraft model - Hawker Hurricane Weathered - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $129 on TurboSquid. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 184,353 polygons over 195,597 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 43 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
