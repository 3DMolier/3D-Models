About This Model

A group of plush teddy bears gathered into one collection, prepared for character animators rather than static display. Round heads, button eyes, stubby arms and soft bellies are the shapes everyone reads instantly as a cuddly toy. Having several bears together makes a toy shelf, a nursery or a picnic scene feel naturally populated.

The bundle is built to CheckMate specification, so scale and naming stay consistent across every bear. Plush fabric needs soft lighting and a gentle fuzz shader, otherwise the toys can look like carved wood. Different fur colors across the group help a camera tell each character apart in a busy frame.

The 2025 collection costs $89 and fits children's animation, toy store catalogs and holiday greeting cards. Bedtime story apps and nursery decor renders also rely on a friendly bear as the emotional center. Seat a few on a windowsill in warm evening light and the room feels instantly safe and lived in.
