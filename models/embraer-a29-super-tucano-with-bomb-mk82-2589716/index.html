About This Model

A turboprop light attack aircraft with an MK82 bomb under the wing, ready for counterinsurgency and training scenes. The airframe uses 234,250 polygons and 256,080 vertices, with detail on the tandem canopy, the propeller hub and the weapon pylons. The slim fuselage and long nose make it instantly different from a jet, which matters in a mixed air-operations sequence.

StemCell certification standardizes the PBR materials, so the paint and canopy glass read the same in a game engine and an offline renderer. UVs are mixed, so plan a quick check before repainting squadron markings on the tail. True-to-life proportions let it park beside hangars and fuel trucks without any scale correction.

Added in 2026 and priced at $169, the model is sold under Editorial Uses Only terms because of the manufacturer's design rights. The weapon load is what sets this version apart, turning a trainer silhouette into a strike aircraft. It belongs in defense simulation, documentary reconstruction, news graphics and military game environments.
