About This Model

Priced at $119, the Electric Fat Bike Mondraker E-Panzer is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 306,840 polygons and 318,906 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

23 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and defense simulation. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
