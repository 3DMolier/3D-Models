About This Model

Male Rigged Surgeons Collection for Maya is a detailed Model Bundles & Sets asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $399. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for character animation, game development and film production. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.
