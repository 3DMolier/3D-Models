About This Model

Battlefield Laser Rangefinder Tripod shows a targeting optic on a folding tripod, used by forward observers to measure distance and direct fire. The unit measures 38.97 x 36.07 x H69.77 cm, so it sits correctly beside a crouching soldier. Lenses, a control panel and adjustment knobs are modeled as clear, separate parts.

Non-overlapping UVs give room to add scratches and dust on the olive housing without repeats. Built to CheckMate specification, the 95,216 polygons keep clean edge flow across the device. The 96,610 vertices keep the lens rings round, and glass reflections stay clean in close shots.

Two versions cost $59 each, this green unit or a Target Acquisition Optics Tripod Sand for desert scenes. Released in 2025, it suits military simulations, war films and tactical games. Set on a ridge at dawn with a soldier behind it, the optic makes the mission feel real.
