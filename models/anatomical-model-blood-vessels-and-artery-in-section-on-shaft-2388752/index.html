About This Model

A teaching model of an artery cut in section, mounted on a shaft, at 10,861 polygons. The section is the whole asset: the three vessel layers, the lumen and a plaque deposit are visible because the model is cut open, which is exactly how this object exists in a classroom.

The build follows CheckMate specification for topology and real-world scale. Non-overlapping UVs keep the intima, the muscular wall and the mounting shaft as separate areas, so each layer can be coloured or labelled independently.

Four versions share the page: artery and vein sections, with and without the display shaft. At $49 for medical education, cardiology and patient-facing visualisation and science communication.
