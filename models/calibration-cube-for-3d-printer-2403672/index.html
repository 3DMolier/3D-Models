About This Model

This general model - Calibration Cube for 3D Printer - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $7 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 1,151 polygons and 1,141 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 3.15 x 3.15 x H3.15 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
