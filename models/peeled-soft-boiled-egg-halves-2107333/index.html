About This Model

These peeled soft boiled egg halves show the smooth white and a runny yolk at the center, the topping of ramen bowls and breakfast plates. Only 1,386 polygons are used, so the halves can be placed on many dishes at once.

The mesh has 1,325 vertices, nearly level with its faces, which suggests clean quads that smooth nicely. A slightly domed cut surface and a soft yolk edge make the egg look freshly sliced. Since no UV islands overlap, a glossy yolk, a matte white and a faint browning from marinade can be painted independently. TurboSquid awarded CheckMate Pro to the halves after confirming scale and clean topology for food work.

The egg halves have been on offer since 2023 and suit food photography, recipe animations and restaurant menus. Place them on ramen with noodles and green onion for an instantly recognizable bowl. Game artists also use light food props like these to fill kitchens, diners and picnic scenes without slowing anything down.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2023 · Updated 13 November 2026
