About This Model

Basketball shorts got longer in one decade and never went back, which is one of the clearest examples of a sport's uniform being decided by its stars rather than by its rules. The cut is now the same everywhere from playgrounds up.

The garment is extremely light at 3,924 polygons and 3,914 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so it costs nothing on a character. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits apparel and sports visualization, e-commerce imagery, character wardrobe work, advertising, game assets and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
