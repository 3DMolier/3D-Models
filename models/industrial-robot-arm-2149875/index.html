Industrial Robot Arm is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. Counted at the source, the model is 137,798 polygons and 69,892 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
