About This Model

Priced at $49, the Outdoor Bonfire Bowl with Active Wood Fire Steel Old is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 46,857 polygons and 46,644 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 49.5 x 49.5 x H59.29 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
