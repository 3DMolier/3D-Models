About This Model

Steam Turbine Rotor belongs to our Industrial Equipment range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 39,464 polygons and 51,128 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

2 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

Questions About This Model

Can the Steam Turbine Rotor model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
