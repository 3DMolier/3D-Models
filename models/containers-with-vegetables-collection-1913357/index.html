About This Model

A collection of food containers filled with fresh vegetables, the meal prep scene of healthy kitchens and lunch box guides. Clear lids reveal colorful contents, so carrots, greens and peppers read clearly through the plastic. Containers of different sizes let a fridge shelf or counter look organized yet varied.

This CheckMate Pro set keeps lids and vegetables as separate objects, which makes opening a box in animation easy. Transparent plastic needs careful refraction settings, while the vegetables benefit from a slight wet sheen. Stack the boxes in a fridge interior with cool light to show a week of meals ready to go.

Available since 2022, the set suits nutrition blogs, meal planning apps, kitchenware catalogs and healthy living animations. Rows of filled containers on a clean counter instantly communicate discipline, planning and care about what the family eats. Fitness coaches and dietitians can use the boxes to illustrate portion sizes and balanced meals in their lessons.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
