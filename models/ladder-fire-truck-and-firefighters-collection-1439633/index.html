About This Model

This collection pairs a ladder fire truck with firefighters in full turnout gear for emergency response scenes. The long aerial ladder, outrigger legs and equipment lockers along the body define the truck in any street shot. Firefighters in helmets and breathing gear stand ready beside the cab, so the scene looks staffed rather than abandoned.

TurboSquid placed the bundle at CheckMate Pro after reviewing its clean topology, real dimensions and naming of each part. The ladder sections and outriggers are separate parts, letting you raise the aerial toward a burning building. Matching detail between the crew and the truck keeps a close shot of the cab from exposing a cruder figure.

At $259 the set suits public safety films, training material for fire departments and city emergency planning visuals. Released in 2019, it also appears in rescue games, news reconstructions and children's animations about everyday heroes. Fire academies use such scenes to explain apparatus positioning before recruits drill with a real ladder truck.
