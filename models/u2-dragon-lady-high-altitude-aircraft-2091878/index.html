About This Model

The U-2 Dragon Lady, a high-altitude reconnaissance aircraft with very long glider-like wings, modeled for aviation and Cold War scenes. Its slender fuselage and enormous wingspan let the real aircraft cruise near the edge of space, far above ordinary airliners. The single engine intake and the narrow cockpit canopy complete a profile that aviation fans recognize immediately. Pilots wore pressure suits to fly it, so the aircraft pairs naturally with characters in full flight gear.

Built to the StemCell standard, the spy plane shades the same whether it is rendered offline or in a real-time engine. Documentaries, flight simulators and games that need a spy plane over hostile territory can use it royalty free.

Released in 2023, the model suits Cold War history programs, air force museum displays and reconnaissance mission games. Shown banking high above the clouds, the plane conveys isolation and altitude better than almost any other aircraft. Parked on an airfield ramp, the long wings dwarf the fuselage and give the scene an unusual proportion.
