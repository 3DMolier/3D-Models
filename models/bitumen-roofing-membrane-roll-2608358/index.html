About This Model

A rolled sheet of bitumen roofing membrane, the dark waterproofing material laid over flat roofs and foundations. It uses 70,382 polygons and 70,111 vertices to show the spiral of layers at each end and the slightly loose outer wrap. The roll stands upright or lies on its side, matching how it arrives on a construction pallet from the supplier.

StemCell certification and non-overlapping UVs make baking, weathering and custom texturing simple for any pipeline. A gritty mineral surface on one face contrasts with the smoother backing visible at the open end. Several rolls stacked beside a gas torch quickly say roofing work is in progress on the building.

The Bitumen Roofing Membrane Roll was released in 2026 and sells for $29. Building supply stores, renovation videos, construction site visualization and technical training content are where it belongs. Scattered rolls on a half-finished roof make a useful foreground in architectural progress renders and site reports.
