About This Model

Colosseum At Caesars Palace Interior is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $249. You are getting 13,577,206 polygons and 14,157,939 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 70.02 x 75.71 x H33.05 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
