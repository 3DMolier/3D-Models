About This Model

Priced at $29, the Cartoon Submarine is a finished Ships & Boats model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 14,570 polygons and 15,929 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, naval simulation and game environments.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
