About This Model

Strands of tiny LEDs hang in vertical lines from a horizontal wire, forming a glowing curtain for windows and party backdrops. The LED Curtain String Lights use 24,287 polygons and 24,749 vertices, spread across rows of bulbs and their thin cables. Each drop falls straight under gravity, so the strands read as a neat sheet rather than a tangle.

The curtain spans 202.21 x 16.73 x H147.7 cm, sized for a living room window or a photo wall. Non-overlapping UVs let you tint each bulb individually or drive a color gradient through an emissive map. StemCell supply means every format arrives with the same glow material already set up.

Introduced in 2026, the light curtain costs $29 and comes as one complete package. Wedding venues, cozy bedroom interiors, holiday window displays and music video sets use it for warm sparkling backgrounds. Shoot it with a shallow lens and the bulbs melt into soft circular bokeh behind your subject.
