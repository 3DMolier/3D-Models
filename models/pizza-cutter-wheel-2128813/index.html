About This Model

Pizza Cutter Wheel belongs to our Kitchen & Tableware range and is offered at $9 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 4,328 polygons and 2,226 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
