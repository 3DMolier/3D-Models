About This Model

A set of metal piping nozzles with star, round and leaf tips, the small tools a baker screws onto a pastry bag. The 4,894 polygons and 4,854 vertices go into the cut teeth of each tip so the outlines stay crisp. Scattered on a counter or standing in a row, they set a pastry kitchen scene right away.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms tidy geometry, correct scale for such small tools and no leftover objects in the scene. UVs are non-overlapping, letting you apply polished steel, brushed chrome or colored plastic to each tip. No textures are bundled, since a simple metal shader already shows the fine edges well.

Released in 2021, the Cake Decoration Nozzles fit bakery interiors, cooking tutorials and recipe app visuals. Each tip shape leaves a different frosting pattern, which makes them easy to explain in close-up. Pastry chefs planning a class and food illustrators showing frosting techniques use them most often.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2021 · Updated 16 November 2026
