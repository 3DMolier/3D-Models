About This Model

Pickup Truck Tent Grey on Pickup belongs to our Sports & Recreation range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 341,777 polygons and 390,565 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
