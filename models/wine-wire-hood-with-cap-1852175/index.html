About This Model

Wine Wire Hood with Cap belongs to our Kitchen & Tableware range and is offered at $24 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
