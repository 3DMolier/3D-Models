About This Model

A plain single bed with a metal frame, head and foot rails and a simple mattress, built for spare and functional rooms. The Metal Single Bed Frame uses 44,736 polygons and 44,658 vertices, lean enough to fill a dormitory or ward with copies. The rails are slim enough to look fragile, which suits horror games set in abandoned institutions.

It measures 209.8 x 96.77 x H77.92 cm, a standard single size for one adult sleeper. Being CheckMate certified, the frame imports with correct units and organized parts for interior work. Thin tubular rails and a low profile give it the look of hospital, hostel and boarding school furniture.

On offer since 2026, the bed is a single version costing $24 for interior scenes. Psychiatric wards, army barracks, orphanage dramas and minimalist bedroom renders use it when a room needs austerity. A crumpled blanket and a lone lamp beside it set a lonely, quiet mood instantly.
