About This Model

An elderly African American gentleman seated in traditional dress, a calm figure for porches, waiting rooms and family scenes. Age shows in the face and posture, which is what keeps an older character from reading as a younger model with grey hair.

His mesh meets CheckMate specification with clean topology and sensible naming, so clothing can be recolored without hunting materials. Because he is already seated, the pose fits chairs and benches without rebuilding knees and hips. Seated figures are scarce in most people libraries, so this one fills a common gap in crowd and interior scenes.

He joined the range in 2023 at $169 and suits community campaigns, healthcare materials and period films. Put him on a wooden porch at golden hour, and the frame tells a story about memory and family without dialogue. Architects also add such figures to senior living renders, where residents matter more than furniture. The traditional clothing helps in heritage and church settings, where a modern tracksuit would break the mood.
