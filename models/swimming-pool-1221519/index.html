About This Model

Priced at $29, the Swimming Pool is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 20,525 polygons and 23,586 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 8 maps at 8192 x 8192. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
