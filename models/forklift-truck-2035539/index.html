About This Model

Forklift Truck is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $89. Geometry weighs in at 891,220 polygons over 453,199 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
