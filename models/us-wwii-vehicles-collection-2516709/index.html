About This Model

A collection of American Second World War vehicles, gathered for battlefield, convoy, newsreel recreation and historical museum scenes. Olive drab paint, white stars and riveted armor are the period markers that make these vehicles read correctly.

Vehicles were built to CheckMate specification, so the convoy shares scale and clean topology across every machine. A mixed fleet lets a scene show supply, transport and combat roles together rather than one tank in isolation. Each vehicle stands alone too, so a single jeep or truck can dress a smaller scene.

Its 2026 debut came at $159, aimed at war documentaries, strategy games and history education. Line the vehicles along a muddy French road with soldiers walking beside them, and the scene recalls the summer of liberation. Re-enactment groups and museums plan exhibits, anniversary events and educational materials around fleets like these. Weathering such as mud, dust and chipped paint would deepen the battlefield feel in close camera shots.
