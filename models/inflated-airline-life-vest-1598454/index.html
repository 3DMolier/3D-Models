About This Model

The life vest is demonstrated on every flight and used on almost none. Inflated, it is a specific and slightly unsettling object, because the only circumstances in which anybody sees one that way are the ones nobody wants to picture.

The model holds 40,520 polygons and 80,378 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 6 maps all at 4096x4096, in the inflated state. It carries StemCell certification, and further vest builds are offered separately.

At $39 it suits aviation safety and training visualization, cabin scenes, equipment marketing, film props, game assets and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.

Questions About This Model

What does StemCell mean for the Inflated Airline Life Vest model?

StemCell is TurboSquid's multi-format standard. The model was authored once and delivered in several formats with matching PBR materials, so shading survives the move between a game engine and an offline renderer.

Where can I buy the Inflated Airline Life Vest 3D model?

It is sold through TurboSquid, where this listing has been available since 2020. Purchase, download and licensing are handled by TurboSquid; delivery is immediate after checkout.

Which industries use the Inflated Airline Life Vest model?

Simulation, Advertising and Film & Video Production are the primary industries on this listing, though the Royalty Free license puts no limit on where the model is actually used.

What is the Inflated Airline Life Vest model typically used for?

Stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.
