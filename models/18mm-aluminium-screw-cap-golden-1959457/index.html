About This Model

This golden aluminum cap is a small threaded closure for perfume samples, essential oil vials and miniature spirits bottles. The shell totals 4,828 polygons on 2,480 vertices, a triangle-heavy build that keeps the fine vertical ribbing crisp. Its warm anodized gold look sets it apart from plain silver caps and signals a premium product on the shelf.

Metallic gold is a simple shader to build, and the unwrapped top accepts an embossed monogram or brand mark. Islands in the layout never overlap, so a printed seal strip around the skirt is also an easy addition. TurboSquid graded the mesh CheckMate Pro, and its open underside slots neatly over a matching bottle neck.

Two versions cost $15 apiece: this golden cap and a Pre-threaded Aluminum Screw Cap version for plainer bottles. Since its 2022 debut, 16 buyers have used it on cosmetics packaging, fragrance mockups and small apothecary bottles. A row of tiny vials with golden tops instantly lifts a skincare flat lay above ordinary stock imagery.
