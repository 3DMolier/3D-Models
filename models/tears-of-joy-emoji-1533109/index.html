About This Model

This general model - Tears Of Joy Emoji - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 1,460 polygons and 1,464 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 5 maps at 1024 x 1024. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
