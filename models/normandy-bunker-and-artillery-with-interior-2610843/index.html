About This Model

A Normandy coastal bunker with its artillery piece in place and a furnished interior behind the thick concrete walls. The Normandy Bunker and Artillery with Interior spends 1,388,104 polygons and 1,399,533 vertices on the gun, shell racks, corridors and battered exterior. The long barrel pointing out to sea gives the exterior a menacing, instantly readable silhouette.

The interior lets cameras move inside the casemate, following the crew from the firing chamber to storage rooms. CheckMate certification keeps this dense historical asset tidy, with correct scale for walk-through shots. Pitted concrete and rusted steel tell the story of salt air, shelling and decades of neglect.

This single version costs $79, part of the 2026 releases on World War Two themes. D-Day documentaries, WW2 shooters, history classrooms and museum walkthroughs use it to show the Atlantic Wall from inside. Lighting through the embrasure creates dramatic beams across the gun when dust hangs in the air.
