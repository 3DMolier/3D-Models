About This Model

Fast food seating is designed to be comfortable for about twenty minutes. The bench is hard, the table is fixed to the floor, and the whole arrangement is calculated around turnover rather than around lingering.

The model is extremely light at 4,454 polygons and 4,723 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, so a whole dining room costs almost nothing. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits restaurant and interior visualization, hospitality presentation, furniture marketing, film and television sets, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
