About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Portable Slit Lamp is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Geometry weighs in at 20,118 polygons over 10,103 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags medical visualization, medical simulation and architectural visualization as the intended applications.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
