About This Model

Glass awards are engineered around one moment: the photograph of somebody holding one. That is why they are heavy for their size, why the edges catch light, and why the engraving is placed where a camera will find it.

This is a collection listing rather than a single object, supplying the trophies as static pieces. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $49 the set suits corporate and event visualization, product and e-commerce imagery, award ceremony scenes, advertising, film set dressing and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.
