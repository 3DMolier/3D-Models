About This Model

A single curved bull horn with a ridged base and a smooth tapering tip, useful for trophies, drinking horns and fantasy props. Only 438 polygons and 440 vertices make up the whole quad mesh, so the silhouette relies on a clean, sweeping curve. Mirror it and you have a matched pair for a skull mount or a Viking helmet.

Four maps at 4096 x 4096 supply the layered keratin grain, the darker tip and the worn surface near the base. UV coverage is mixed, so painting fresh details may call for a quick touch-up of the layout. CheckMate Pro status confirms clean quad topology that subdivides into a smoother horn when needed.

Released in 2017, the horn has found 28 buyers among game artists, set dressers and costume designers. It hangs well on a hunting lodge wall or sits on a tavern table as a drinking horn. Scaled down, it also serves as a tooth or claw stand-in for creature kitbashing.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2017 · Updated 16 November 2026
