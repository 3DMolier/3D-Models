About This Model

Two seated figures on a bench is one of the most useful groupings in architectural visualization and one of the least often available: most people libraries supply walkers and shoppers, not people who have stopped. The posture is different too - settled, leaning, turned slightly toward each other.

The mesh is efficient at 74,228 polygons and 83,312 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, which suits figures placed in a wider scene rather than examined closely.

At $249 and built to StemCell specification, it suits architectural and urban visualization, healthcare and social care material, community and public space presentation, editorial imagery and games. On sale since 2025.
