About This Model

A volumetric dust veil for Blender that fills space scenes with drifting nebula clouds behind planets and ships. Instead of a solid mesh, the veil works as a soft medium that catches light and fades at its edges. That makes it useful for depth, since layers of dust in front of a star field push the background away.

Its colors and density can be shifted in the shader, turning the same cloud into a warm nebula or a cold gas cloud. The asset follows the CheckMate specification and opens in Blender ready to render, without a separate setup step.

Published in 2025, it suits sci-fi trailers, space game backdrops, music videos and astronomy explainers. Flying a camera slowly through the veil gives a strong sense of motion even when the ships stand still. A bright star placed behind it creates glowing rim light along the dust, a classic deep space look. Render times rise with volume detail, so test at low samples before the final frame.
