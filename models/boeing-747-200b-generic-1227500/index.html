About This Model

The Boeing 747-200B Generic is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 222,361 polygons and 236,144 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

7 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
