About This Model

The Anthracite Coal Pile is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 9,705 polygons and 9,484 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
