About This Model

The Compact Mobile Crane 2 is a production-ready Industrial Equipment 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

The mesh carries 299,137 polygons and 317,034 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 30 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
