About This Model

This Aircraft model - Strategic B-21 Raider Jet White Rigged - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $229 on TurboSquid. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

At 709,862 polygons and 740,258 vertices this is a mid-weight asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
