About This Model

Boeing 737 MAX 8 is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. Counted at the source, the model is 385,071 polygons and 420,725 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.
