About This Model

Amber is fossilised tree resin, not a mineral, which is why it is warm to the touch and light enough to float in salt water. Everything trapped inside one is a snapshot of a forest that stopped existing tens of millions of years ago.

The model is extremely light at 756 polygons and 763 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so it costs nothing anywhere. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $39 it suits jewellery and product visualization, natural history and museum work, e-commerce imagery, education material, game props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2022 · Updated 14 November 2026
