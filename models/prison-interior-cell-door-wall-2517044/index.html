About This Model

A cell front is a specific piece of construction: a steel frame set into a masonry wall, a sliding or swinging door with a food hatch and an observation panel, and a locking arrangement that operates from outside only. Every one of those elements is visible and immediately readable as custodial rather than institutional.

The mesh is efficient at 21,162 polygons and 21,526 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout, with the geometry in the door frame, the hatch and the lock hardware.

At $39 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits correctional facility visualization, film and television set dressing, architectural and security presentation, documentary material and games. On sale since 2026.
