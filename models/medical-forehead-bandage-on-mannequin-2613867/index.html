About This Model

A clean gauze bandage wraps around the forehead of a mannequin, showing a standard dressing for minor head wounds. The Medical Forehead Bandage on Mannequin holds 101,524 polygons and 101,481 vertices, enough for every fold and overlap of the wrap. Unlike bloodied versions, the white gauze stays spotless, suited to instructional rather than dramatic scenes.

It measures 19.59 x 23.8 x H31.46 cm, a compact display head suited to close studio framing. CheckMate certification means accurate units and tidy hierarchy, so the dressing fits other head models of the same scale. A smooth featureless face keeps attention on the bandage, which is ideal for teaching material.

Its price is $69, and this lone version dates from 2026 for training and display. First aid manuals, nursing schools, pharmacy displays and medical animations use it to demonstrate head bandaging step by step. Seen in profile, the band sits level above the brows, the textbook placement for forehead cuts.
