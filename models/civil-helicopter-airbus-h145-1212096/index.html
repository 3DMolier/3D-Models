About This Model

The H145 is the air ambulance of choice across much of Europe, and the reasons are structural: a shrouded tail rotor that is safer around people on the ground, clamshell rear doors that let a stretcher load straight in, and a cabin flat enough to work in. The model carries the shrouded tail, the clamshell doors and the skid arrangement.

It is heavy for a light twin at 1,248,007 polygons and 1,279,235 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 19 texture maps at 4096x4096. That density goes into the rotor head and the door frames where this helicopter is usually filmed from close range.

A rigged build sits on this page at $199. The static model at $149 covers emergency services and medical aviation visualization, police and rescue presentation, corporate aviation marketing, film and television, and games. On sale since 2018.
