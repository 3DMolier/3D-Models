About This Model

A bowl heaped with strawberries, blueberries and raspberries, made for breakfast tables, dessert menus and summer kitchen scenes. The Bowl of Various Berries spends 347,238 polygons and 357,594 vertices, mostly on the drupelets and seeds that make each fruit believable. Berries spill above the bowl rim in a loose mound, so the arrangement looks freshly picked rather than stacked.

Non-overlapping UVs allow subsurface color and soft gloss to be painted on each berry without stretching. No texture maps are bundled, which leaves the ripeness and color balance in your hands. Construction follows the CheckMate specification, keeping names readable and geometry free of stray faces.

New in 2025, the bowl serves food photography mockups, recipe apps and healthy eating illustrations. Paired with yogurt or a stack of pancakes, it gives a breakfast scene bright color and texture. Macro renders show the tiny seeds on each strawberry, a detail lighter fruit models usually skip.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2025 · Updated 16 November 2026
