About This Model

This big collection gathers many snakes in one purchase, giving reptile houses, jungle floors and desert scenes a varied cast of serpents. Scale patterns, head shapes and body thickness differ from snake to snake, which keeps a crowded terrarium from looking repetitive. Curling a snake around a branch or along sand makes even a still frame feel alive.

All models follow CheckMate specification, so long bodies deform smoothly when coiled or bent along a path. Glossy scales come alive under a sharp highlight, especially on darker snakes where the pattern is subtle. Forked tongues and lidless eyes are the small details that give these reptiles their unsettling stare.

The collection dates from 2024 and is royalty free, covering wildlife documentaries, adventure games and biology lessons. Zoos and nature centers can build exhibit previews with snakes resting on branches or rocks. Horror and fantasy projects use serpents too, slithering through temple ruins or dark cellars.
