About This Model

The Avenger is a turret of short range missiles bolted onto a light vehicle, and that is the whole idea: air defence that keeps up with the units it is protecting rather than being emplaced behind them.

The model holds 77,434 polygons and 78,875 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 13 maps, 11 at 4096x4096 and 2 at 1024x1024. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $59 it suits defence visualization and presentation, documentary and news graphics, simulation, film props, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2017.
