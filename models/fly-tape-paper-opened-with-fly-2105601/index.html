About This Model

Fly Opened with Fly is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $34. The mesh carries 41,681 polygons and 42,511 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
