About This Model

The Boeing 747 Passenger Airliner Rigged is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 240,045 polygons and 255,801 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 64.4 x 70.61 x H19.29 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
