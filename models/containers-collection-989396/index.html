About This Model

This Model Bundles & Sets model - Containers Collection - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $199 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. The set covers several related objects at once, which saves assembling a scene from separate purchases.
