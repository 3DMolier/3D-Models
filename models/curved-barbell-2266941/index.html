About This Model

Curved Barbell is one of the Sports & Recreation models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. You are getting 17,734 polygons and 17,802 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
