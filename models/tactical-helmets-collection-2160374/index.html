About This Model

This collection gathers several tactical helmets in one purchase, covering modern ballistic shells for police, soldiers and airsoft players. Cut-away ears, side rails and night vision mounts mark them as current designs rather than older steel pots. Varied shapes and colors keep a squad from looking cloned when several helmets appear in the same frame.

The set passed CheckMate Lite, confirming clean files and believable scale across every helmet. Each helmet is its own object, so it can be placed on a head, a shelf or a gear table. Matte paint, cloth covers and Velcro patches give the surfaces different textures for close camera work.

Available since 2023, the helmets serve shooter games, military films and equipment catalog renders. Lined up on a store wall, they also make a tidy display for tactical shop interiors. Game releases, short films, videos and training presentations are all allowed under Royalty Free terms.
