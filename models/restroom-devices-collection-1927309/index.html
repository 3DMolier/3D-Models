About This Model

This bundle gathers the wall-mounted fixtures that complete a public or office restroom beyond the toilet and sink. Dispensers, holders and similar devices share a consistent style, so a whole washroom wall can be dressed in a single pass. Their compact shapes mount flush against tiles, which is how these units appear in real commercial bathrooms.

TurboSquid assigned CheckMate Lite to the collection, confirming clean basic geometry and a tidy scene file. Each device is a separate object, so pieces can be placed independently along a vanity or near the door. Brushed metal and white plastic finishes match most tiled interiors without heavy material work.

On sale since 2022 at $22, the set fits office, mall, airport and restaurant restroom visualizations. Architects use bundles like this to finish washroom elevations quickly once the main plumbing fixtures are placed. Game environment artists also rely on it for bathrooms in office, school and hospital levels.
