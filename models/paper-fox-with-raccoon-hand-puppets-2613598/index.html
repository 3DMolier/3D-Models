About This Model

A paper fox and a raccoon, folded as hand puppets, bring woodland characters to craft lessons and kids' stories. The Paper Fox with Raccoon Hand Puppets uses 6,250 polygons and 6,360 vertices, a featherweight pair for mobile scenes. Folded tails curl behind each animal, adding depth to otherwise flat paper forms.

The duo measures 6.42 x 41.75 x H11.72 cm when placed side by side in a row on a table. Non-overlapping UVs mean orange fox paper and grey raccoon paper can be retextured independently of each other. StemCell supply gives both puppets the same paper material in all delivered formats and engines.

This pair dates from 2026 and sells as a single bundle priced at $29 for children's content. Forest-themed storytime videos, nursery decor renders, puppet theater apps and paper craft blogs use them as cheerful hosts. The raccoon's masked face and the fox's pointed snout read clearly even when the camera pulls back.
