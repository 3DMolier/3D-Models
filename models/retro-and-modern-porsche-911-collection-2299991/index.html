About This Model

Retro and Modern Porsche 911 Collection sets classic and current generations of the rear-engine sports car side by side. The sloping roof and round headlights carry across decades, while the bodywork grows wider and more sculpted over time. Comparing generations in one frame shows how designers kept the silhouette while modernizing almost everything else.

Each car is a separate, named object ready for staging, as CheckMate construction requires. Sold under Editorial Uses Only terms, the set serves journalism, automotive history articles and educational comparisons. Wide rear haunches and the famous fastback roofline remain recognizable even in small thumbnails.

Released in 2024, the set suits car magazine features, motorsport documentaries and design school lectures. Lined up in a studio with soft reflections, the cars make a classic evolution shot for anniversary stories. Collectors and enthusiast channels can also illustrate restoration or buying guides with the matched lineup.
