About This Model

This fish skull shows the jaw, gill covers and eye sockets of a bony fish, prepared like a cleaned museum specimen. The 21,671 polygons capture the thin plates and curved jaw bones that give a fish skull its delicate, layered structure. Set on a laboratory bench or inside a curiosity cabinet, it reads immediately as a natural history object.

Built from quads only, the skull counts 21,695 vertices and a tidy unwrap without overlapping islands. Its 3 maps at 4096 x 4096 bring out the aged ivory tone, porous bone texture and darker staining in the crevices. StemCell certification means it renders consistently across several major packages without material rework.

On offer since 2018, the Fish Skull Bones model suits zoology lessons, marine biology documentaries and museum exhibit visualization. Fantasy and horror artists also place it in witch huts, pirate caves and shaman altars as an eerie decoration. Its manageable polygon budget keeps it practical even for real-time scenes and mobile applications.
