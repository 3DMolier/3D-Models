About This Model

A pump on a trailer is the answer to a problem that moves. Flooded sites, temporary bypasses, irrigation and construction dewatering all need serious flow for a few days in a place that has no power, which is why the engine and the pump travel together.

The model holds 150,610 polygons and 152,146 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and carries StemCell certification. Rigged builds are offered separately, including native rigs for Cinema 4D and Maya.

At $79 it suits construction and utilities visualization, equipment marketing, emergency and flood response material, training, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2025.
