About This Model

This Ships & Boats model - Diesel-Electric Submarine - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $119 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 20,515 polygons over 20,900 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 10.91 x 77.7 x H20.36 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags defense simulation, naval simulation and game environments as the intended applications. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
