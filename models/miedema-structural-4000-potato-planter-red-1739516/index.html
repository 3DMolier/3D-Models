About This Model

A tractor-mounted potato planter with a large hopper, cup belts and ridging bodies that cover the seed potatoes in neat rows. The model uses 375,636 polygons and 384,203 vertices on the hopper frame, planting units, discs and hydraulic hoses. Its bright red paint stands out in a spring field next to freshly formed ridges.

Paint and steel are defined by materials alone, while the unwrapped pieces sit on the layout without touching. CheckMate Pro was awarded by TurboSquid after a manual check confirmed sound topology and correct scale. Attach it to a tractor three-point hitch and the planting operation is ready for an animated pass.

Eight versions cost $49 or $59, in red or green paint, either new or used. Some carry the Miedema Structural name while others are labeled as a generic potato planter. Introduced in 2021, the implement suits farm equipment brochures, crop production lessons and agricultural simulators.
