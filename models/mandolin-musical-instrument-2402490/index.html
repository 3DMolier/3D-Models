About This Model

Mandolin Musical Instrument is one of the Musical Instruments models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. You are getting 31,983 polygons and 32,996 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It suits music-video and film sets, game props, product rendering and educational material.
