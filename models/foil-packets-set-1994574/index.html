About This Model

Blank foil packets exist so that a designer can put artwork on them. The value is in the forms: a pillow pouch, a stand up pouch, a flat sachet, each with realistic crimped seals and the creases that foil takes when it is filled. Artwork applied to a flat rectangle never sits right.

The mesh is minimal at 6,592 polygons and 6,600 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry entirely in the seals and the fill creases.

At $29 it suits food and pharmaceutical packaging mockups, brand and label design presentation, retail and e-commerce renders, packaging industry marketing and games. On sale since 2023.
