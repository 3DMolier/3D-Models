About This Model

A spiral milk splash frozen mid-motion at 2,026 polygons, shaped for dairy, cereal and dessert product shots. The ribbon twists upward, thins toward its tail and throws off droplets that break away from the main stream. With 2,182 vertices the silhouette stays clean, which is all a liquid form really needs to read.

The surface is meant for your own milk or cream shader, with subsurface scattering set to match your lighting. Non-overlapping UVs are still in place, so you can add a map for foam or color if the shot needs it. CheckMate Pro certification guarantees sensible scale and naming, so the splash sits next to a glass or bowl correctly.

It comes as a single build, and nothing else is grouped with it on the page. Available since 2022, the splash is used in packaging design, beverage and breakfast visuals, and motion graphics. Pair it with a falling cookie or strawberry to build a classic dairy hero shot.
