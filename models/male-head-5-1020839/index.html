About This Model

This adult male head is a realistic bust with neutral expression, suited to anatomy lessons, facial studies and medical illustration work. The sculpture shows the skull shape under the skin, the folds around the eyes and the soft contours of the lips. Ears, nose and jawline are modeled with clear proportions, so the head reads correctly from the side as well as the front.

Its CheckMate Pro grade means a TurboSquid inspector verified geometry, real proportions and orderly naming before publication. Without hair or clothing, the bare surface makes it easy to project wrinkles, scars or diagnostic markings onto the face. Clinicians and teachers can light it from one side to show how facial muscles shape the visible form.

Released back in 2016, the bust remains useful for medical education, VR anatomy training and patient explanation material. Film crews use it as a base for prosthetic makeup previews and forensic reconstruction sequences in crime dramas. Game artists also start character heads from it when they need a grounded, realistic adult face quickly.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
