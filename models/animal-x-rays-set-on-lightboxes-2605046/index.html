About This Model

A row of wall lightboxes showing radiographs of animal skeletons, the centerpiece of any veterinary clinic set. The Animal X Rays Set on Lightboxes uses 43,100 polygons and 43,494 vertices on the frames, clips and film sheets. Each viewer is a slim box with a glowing panel, so the images read clearly even from across the room.

CheckMate certification means clean geometry and logical names for each viewer and its film. Emissive panels backlight the bones, which gives a cold clinical glow without adding extra lights. Mounted on a wall above a desk, the set instantly identifies a veterinary or anatomy room.

It was introduced in 2026 at $49, and Royalty Free use is allowed in any project. Vet clinic interiors, medical dramas, science classrooms and educational animations are the best fits. Swapping the film images turns the same lightboxes into a human radiology office when needed.
