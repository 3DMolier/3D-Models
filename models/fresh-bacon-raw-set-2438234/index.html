About This Model

The Fresh Bacon Raw Set is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. You are getting 6,538 polygons and 6,548 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 20.34 x 9.83 x H0.83 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
