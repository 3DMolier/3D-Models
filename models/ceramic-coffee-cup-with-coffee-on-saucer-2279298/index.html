About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Ceramic Coffee Cup with Coffee on Saucer is a finished, render-ready model at $8. Counted at the source, the model is 2,615 polygons and 2,618 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
