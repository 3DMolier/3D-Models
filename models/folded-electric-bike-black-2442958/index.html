About This Model

Priced at $79, the Folded Electric Bike is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 78,844 polygons over 80,370 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 52.72 x 80.89 x H114.43 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic as the intended applications. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
