About This Model

The Marble Vase is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $13 on TurboSquid. You are getting 1,468 polygons and 1,470 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 4.55 x 4.55 x H7.92 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
