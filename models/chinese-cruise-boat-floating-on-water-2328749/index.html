About This Model

A traditional Chinese river cruise boat floats on calm water, its multi-deck cabins topped by curved pagoda-style roofs. The 869,311 polygons cover carved railings, lantern rows and the ornate upper deck where passengers gather. Red pillars and golden trim give the vessel a festive look that suits evening light.

The model holds 917,617 vertices, and the UVs never overlap, so painted dragons and lacquer textures sit cleanly on each panel. StemCell certification lets the boat render the same way across supported engines without fixing shaders. The water surface around the hull provides gentle reflections for night scenes with glowing lanterns.

This Chinese Cruise Boat Floating on Water is one of two versions at $149 each, alongside a plainer sightseeing boat. The sightseeing option works for modern city rivers where ornate decoration would look out of place. On the market since 2024, both serve tourism films, travel apps and harbor environments in games.
