About This Model

Cellular Tower Site is a complete telecom compound with a lattice mast, antenna panels, equipment cabinets and a fenced gravel pad. Most of its 1,057,154 polygons sit in the steel lattice and cable runs, so thin members stay round in tight crops. The tall mast gives a rural road shot a clear vertical landmark against an open sky.

Painted steel, galvanized fence wire and concrete come from 10 maps at 4096 x 4096 on non-overlapping UVs. The 1,063,874 vertices passed CheckMate Pro inspection, which matters in a dense yard where parts get hidden or swapped. Cabinets, antennas and the fence can be adjusted separately to match a particular network layout.

This is a single build without alternate versions, meant to drop into a landscape as a finished site. Released in 2017, it suits network rollout explainers, coverage map visuals, wireless engineering projects and infrastructure reports. As a mid-ground element, it quietly signals that a remote valley still has mobile coverage.
