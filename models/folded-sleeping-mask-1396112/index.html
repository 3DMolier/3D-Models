Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Folded Sleeping Mask is a finished, render-ready model at $29. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer.

The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing.
