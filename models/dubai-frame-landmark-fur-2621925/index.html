About This Model

A detailed landmark model of the giant golden picture frame in Dubai, with fur-based grass around its plaza. The 508,005 polygons cover the gilded facade patterns, the bridge at the top and the surrounding grounds. The open rectangle frames old and new parts of the city, a view that works well in aerial shots.

The CheckMate build stores 557,796 vertices in clean groups, keeping the frame, ground and fur separate. UVs exist on parts of the scene, and the fur system adds lawn texture without heavy modeled blades. Its towering height dominates the plaza, so a low camera angle exaggerates the scale beautifully.

Two versions share the page at $169 each: the daytime landmark and a night illumination edition with a lit facade. Added in 2026, the model suits city flyovers, tourism videos and architectural visualization of the Dubai skyline. The lit edition makes evening sequences simple, with no need to rig your own facade lights.
