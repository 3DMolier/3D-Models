About This Model

A set of dark glass champagne and wine bottles with foil-wrapped necks, cork tops and paper labels. The Champagne and Wine Bottles 3D Models Collection puts several classic bottle shapes into one file for bars and cellars. The heavy punt in the base and the sloping shoulders separate a champagne bottle from a Bordeaux or Burgundy shape.

Lined along a bar shelf behind a bartender, the bottles make a lounge feel stocked and expensive. Glass color and label art can be changed per bottle, so one set covers red, white and sparkling wines. TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro review confirmed clean geometry and sensible naming before release.

At $29 and on sale since 2018, the collection counts 21 buyers among wine shops, restaurant designers and event planners. Chilled in an ice bucket at a wedding table, a champagne bottle signals the toast is about to begin. Wine tasting tutorials and cellar walkthroughs use the set to fill racks quickly.
