About This Model

This Food & Beverages model - Animal Egg - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

Geometry weighs in at 160 polygons over 162 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 3 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
