About This Model

The Thick Pipes Bundle 2 Meters is a production-ready Model Bundles & Sets 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. You are getting 8,106 polygons and 8,112 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering.
