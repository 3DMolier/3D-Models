About This Model

Looking for a Architecture Landmarks asset? The Payphone with Base Unit is a finished, render-ready model at $19. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

Geometry weighs in at 8,963 polygons over 9,030 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
