About This Model

The Mountain Snowshoes Atlas is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. You are getting 137,210 polygons and 69,718 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and architectural visualization.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
