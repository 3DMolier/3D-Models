Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Koi Fish is a finished, render-ready model at $49. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. At 5,686 polygons and 5,703 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
