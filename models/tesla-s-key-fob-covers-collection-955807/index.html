About This Model

A set of protective covers for the Tesla Model S key fob, the small car-shaped remote that unlocks the sedan. Together the covers use 57,904 polygons, spent on the curved shells, the button cutouts and the soft edges.

Their 61,952 vertices give each cover a smooth surface that holds up in macro shots. The covers wrap the fob snugly, keeping its car silhouette visible through the material. Thanks to CheckMate Pro certification, the collection opens with consistent scale, naming and clean geometry for each item. UVs lie flat without overlaps, leaving space for silicone, leather or carbon textures on the shells.

The collection has been around since 2015 and is licensed for Editorial Uses Only because of the Tesla design. Automotive journalists, accessory reviewers and editorial illustrators show the covers next to the fob or a phone. Laid out in a row, the different finishes make it easy to compare protective options at a glance.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2015 · Updated 13 November 2026
