About This Model

A black front-mount intercooler with its side pipes, the tuning part that cools charged air before it reaches the engine. The core sits behind the bumper while the pipes curve back toward the turbo, making the shape easy to read on camera.

CheckMate Pro status confirms the part cleared TurboSquid's strict review for scale, naming and clean geometry. Its black finish suits aftermarket tuning builds, and the fins and pipe bends catch highlights in studio lighting.

Available since 2022, it fits car customization games, performance shop renders and garage scenes in film. Placed on a workbench beside clamps and hoses, it tells the viewer an upgrade is in progress. Mounted to a car model, it fills the gap behind the front grille with believable engineering detail. Technical illustrations of turbocharged engines can also use it to explain where the air goes. Tuning catalogs and parts store visuals can show it on its own against a clean backdrop.
