About This Model

The Chenowth DPV Desert Patrol Vehicle is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
