About This Model

A paired set of male and female figures showing the full muscular system, made for anatomy teaching and fitness visuals. Having both bodies in one package lets you compare proportions and muscle mass side by side in a lesson. Posed in a neutral standing stance, the figures can be rotated freely to show front, back and profile.

TurboSquid certified the collection CheckMate Pro after a manual review of geometry and scene organization. Muscle groups are modeled with clear boundaries, so a single area like the deltoids can be highlighted. Red fibrous muscle and pale tendons are the classic look here, matching textbook illustrations students already know from school.

At $209 the pair is aimed at medical educators, physiotherapy apps, gym software and sports science channels online. In circulation since 2020, it suits personal trainer apps, kinesiology lectures, injury explainers and museum displays. Show both figures mid-rotation on a dark stage, and the differences in build become clear immediately.
