About This Model

A paper bank cheque with printed fields for the payee, the amount and the signature, the traditional way to pay by written order. This model captures a thin sheet with subtle edge detail, ready for close-ups on a desk or in a wallet. Its flat format makes it easy to place on any surface, from a desk blotter to a counter.

Security patterns, a bank logo and handwritten ink can be applied to the paper, along with a slight paper texture. The cheque can be folded, torn or stamped for storytelling, adding realism to financial scenes. TurboSquid awarded it CheckMate Pro status, confirming clean geometry ready for production use.

Bank offices, business scenes and crime dramas have used this cheque since 2023. Sign it at a desk with a fountain pen, or hand it over during a contract signing. Financial literacy videos also show cheques like this when explaining traditional payment methods.
