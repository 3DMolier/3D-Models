About This Model

LED RGB Light Panel With Remote Control is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $39. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product advertising, hardware presentation and film production. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 3,827 polygons and 4,006 vertices, a light load for a scene. Maps: 5 of them, peaking at 2048x2048, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
