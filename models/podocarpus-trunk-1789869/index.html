About This Model

Podocarpus Trunk is one of the Nature & Plants models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 12,959 polygons and 13,368 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
