About This Model

The Bullet Train JR700 Locomotive Japan Railways is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $79 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 777,993 polygons and 795,242 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 17 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
