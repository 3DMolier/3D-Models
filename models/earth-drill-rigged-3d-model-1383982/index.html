About This Model

A rigged earth drill attachment with a long helical auger, a hydraulic drive head and a mounting bracket for an excavator arm. The model uses 46,718 polygons over 48,204 vertices on the auger flights, hoses and the gearbox housing. Its spiral blade makes it instantly clear that this tool bores holes for fence posts or foundations.

Eleven textures at 4096 x 4096 provide painted steel, worn edges and caked mud. TurboSquid grades the drill Built to CheckMate specification, so naming and geometry match what contributors are asked to deliver. The rig lets the auger spin and the drive head pivot, which brings digging animations to life.

Two rigged versions are on offer at $49 each: this generic earth drill and a JCB branded drill of the same type. Released in 2019, they fit construction site animations, equipment catalogs and engineering explainers. The JCB version suits scenes built around that manufacturer's familiar yellow machinery on busy building sites.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2019 · Updated 18 November 2026
