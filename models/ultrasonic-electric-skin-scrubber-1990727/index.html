About This Model

A handheld ultrasonic skin scrubber with a flat metal spatula blade, the beauty device used for deep pore cleansing. The body is only 2,942 polygons and 5,853 vertices, shaping the slim grip, the control button and the angled blade. Its compact form fits naturally in a hand pose, on a vanity tray or lying on a bathroom shelf.

No texture maps are attached, so the glossy plastic housing and steel tip are set with simple shaders. UVs are laid out without overlaps, ready for a printed brand or a soft gradient across the case. CheckMate Pro grading from TurboSquid confirms a clean, correctly scaled model with sensible pivots.

Released in 2022, the scrubber suits skincare product renders, beauty salon interiors and cosmetics app illustrations. Low geometry also makes it a good fit for AR try-on viewers and online shop spinners. Place it on a folded towel next to a serum bottle and a jade roller for a calm spa still life.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2022 · Updated 13 November 2026
