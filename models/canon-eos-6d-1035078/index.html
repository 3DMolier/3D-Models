About This Model

Priced at $79, the Canon EOS 6D is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 80,391 polygons and 87,100 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
