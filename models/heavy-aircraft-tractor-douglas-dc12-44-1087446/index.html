About This Model

Heavy Aircraft Tractor Douglas DC12 44 belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $79 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and aerospace visualization. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 453,172 polygons over 462,642 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 44 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
