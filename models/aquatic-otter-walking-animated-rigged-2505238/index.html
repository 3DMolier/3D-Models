About This Model

Aquatic Otter is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $179. Counted at the source, the model is 16,383 polygons and 16,024 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 30.27 x 117.46 x H31.8 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
