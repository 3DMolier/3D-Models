About This Model

A stiff layered ballet skirt in black, inspired by the dark swan role and ready for dancers and theater scenes. Black Swan Ballet Tutu is surprisingly lean, with only 746 polygons and 820 vertices shaping the flat platter of tulle around the waist. The skirt stands out horizontally from the hips, the way classical stage tutus do.

Because the mesh is so light, it can be duplicated across a whole corps of dancers without slowing playback. The unwrap avoids overlaps, so feathers, sequins or a ruffled tulle texture can be painted on later. CheckMate Pro status confirms clean construction and correct scale for a performer.

Since 2020 the tutu has served ballet game characters, theater costume previews and dance school promotional imagery. Dress a dancer in it for a stage scene under a spotlight, or display it on a form in a costume workshop. Mobile games favor the low count when dressing many animated dancers at once.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2020 · Updated 16 November 2026
