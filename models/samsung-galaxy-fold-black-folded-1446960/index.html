About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Samsung Galaxy Fold Black Folded is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 42,167 polygons and 22,012 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 7 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
