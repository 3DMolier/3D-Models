About This Model

Priced at $89, the Flat Corn Plantation Field Dry is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 82,193,058 polygons and 92,516,453 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 101.49 x 101.19 x H2.46 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
