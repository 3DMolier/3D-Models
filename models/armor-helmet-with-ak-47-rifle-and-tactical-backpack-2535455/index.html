About This Model

A combat kit laid out together, an armored helmet, an AK-47 rifle and a tactical backpack ready for a soldier to pick up. The Armor Helmet with Ak-47 Rifle and Tactical Backpack uses 157,390 polygons and 159,310 vertices across all pieces. The curved magazine, the helmet rails and the pack webbing give the group an instantly military silhouette.

The arrangement measures 66.36 x 83.96 x H74.88 cm, a compact pile that fits on a crate or bench. UV layout is mixed, with the rifle and helmet unique while straps and buckles share texture space. Built with CheckMate specification in mind, each item is a separate object that can be placed alone.

Released in 2026, the kit suits shooter game loadout screens, military training visuals and war film props. Placed on a cot in a barracks scene, it tells the viewer a patrol is about to begin. Security and survival content can use the backpack and helmet without the rifle for civilian settings.
