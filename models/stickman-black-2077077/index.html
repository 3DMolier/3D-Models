About This Model

This Characters & People model - Stickman Black - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 1,864 polygons and 938 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
