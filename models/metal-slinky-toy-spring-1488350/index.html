About This Model

The Toy Spring is a production-ready Toys & Games 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 47,862 polygons over 47,864 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 1024 x 1024 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
