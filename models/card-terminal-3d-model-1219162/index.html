About This Model

The Card Terminal is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 47,058 polygons over 48,245 vertices - a light build for its class. It ships 8 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
