About This Model

Orangutan Fur is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. Counted at the source, the model is 94,380 polygons and 47,747 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2022 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
