About This Model

A changing room is the highest converting square metre in any shop, and it is furnished accordingly: a mirror angled to flatter, a hook within reach of somebody half dressed, and a seat for the bag rather than the customer.

This is a collection listing rather than a single object, supplying the equipment as static pieces. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $31 the set suits retail and interior visualization, shopfitting and equipment marketing, store scenes, advertising, film sets and game environments. On sale since 2024.
