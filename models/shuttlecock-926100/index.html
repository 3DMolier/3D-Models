About This Model

This Sports & Recreation model - Shuttlecock - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 7,928 polygons and 7,898 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 6 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
