About This Model

This is a working checkpoint rather than a machine on its own: the X-ray tunnel, the roller beds either side, the operator console and an officer at it. That combination is what a security scene needs, because a scanner alone shows equipment while a scanner with an operator shows a process.

The mesh runs 593,698 polygons and 631,836 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 53 texture maps at a mix of sizes up to 4096x4096. That texture load carries the uniform, the machine markings and the console screens.

At $189 it suits airport and terminal visualization, security industry presentation, aviation training material, film and television, and games. On sale since 2018.
