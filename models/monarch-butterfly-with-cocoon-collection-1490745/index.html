About This Model

This collection follows the monarch butterfly through its life, pairing the orange and black adult with the jade green chrysalis it emerges from. The adult wings show the bold veins and white-spotted borders that make monarchs easy to recognize. The chrysalis hangs from a thin silk pad, a detail that makes close framing believable.

Each piece carries CheckMate Pro certification, keeping scale and naming consistent across the set. Because the parts share one standard, they sit together naturally on a milkweed leaf or a branch in macro shots. The collection format saves matching a separate butterfly and pupa from different modelers.

Available since 2020, the set helps biology lessons, nature documentaries and science apps explain metamorphosis. Animate the wings opening above the empty chrysalis and the hatching moment becomes a short, memorable sequence. Gardeners and conservation groups also use it to illustrate pollinator habitats and migration stories.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
