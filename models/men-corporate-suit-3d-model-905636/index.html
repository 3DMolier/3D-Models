About This Model

A two-piece business suit modelled as clothing rather than as a painted-on shell: jacket, trousers, shirt and tie exist as their own surfaces, with lapels, a collar roll, cuffs and pocket flaps built into the geometry. That is what makes a suit read as tailored, because the eye checks the lapel line and the shoulder before anything else.

It is static, which suits the work it is usually bought for. A suit that is not being animated does not need a rig, and this one drops onto a mannequin, a display stand or a still figure and holds its shape without simulation.

At $79 it earns its place in corporate and business visualization, retail and tailoring presentation, architectural interiors that need people, product mock-ups and print. On sale since 2015, which is a long service record for an asset of this kind.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
