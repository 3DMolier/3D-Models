About This Model

A folded chair is a completely different object from an open one: flat, leaning, stacked against a wall or carried under an arm. Event and venue scenes need both states, and the folded version is the one that says an event is being set up or cleared rather than in progress.

The mesh is light at 14,780 polygons and 7,462 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the frame joints and the moulded seat ribbing.

Blue and black versions sit on this page at the same $29. All suit event and venue visualisation, hospitality and community interiors, storage and back of house scenes, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2022.
