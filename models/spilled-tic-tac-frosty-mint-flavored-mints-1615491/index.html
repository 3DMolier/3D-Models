About This Model

A spilled product is a different asset from a packaged one. The box lies open, the mints scatter across the surface in a loose arc, and a few rest against each other rather than sitting in a tidy pile. That disorder is the point: it implies motion and use, which a closed box on a white background never does.

The mesh is efficient at 15,612 polygons and 15,663 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 6 texture maps at 4096x4096 carrying the box printing and the slightly translucent sugar shell of the mints.

At $29 and built to StemCell specification, it suits confectionery advertising, packaging and brand mockups, retail and e-commerce renders, food photography set dressing and games. On sale since 2021.
