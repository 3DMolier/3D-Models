About This Model

Guinness Foreign Extra Stout bottles in dark glass, made for tavern counters, beer cellars and travel food scenes. The 13,712 polygons focus on the bottle necks, the crown caps and the subtle curve where the body meets the shoulder. Bottles stand close together in a compact cluster that fits easily on a crowded table or tray.

Each StemCell format carries the same glass and label shading, so the bottles look right in any renderer you choose.
