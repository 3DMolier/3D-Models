About This Model

Crabs Collection 2 Rigged gathers several rigged crab species with jointed legs and claws ready for sideways scuttling. Each crab has its own skeleton, so pincers can snap and legs can cycle without extra rigging work. Scattered over wet sand, seaweed or a rocky tide pool, they bring coastal and underwater scenes to life.

CheckMate specification keeps hierarchies tidy and consistent, which helps animators find leg chains quickly. Hard shells with bumps and spikes catch specular light like real wet carapaces at low tide. Different shell colors and shapes let a beach crowd look natural instead of cloned.

The collection comes in two versions at $569: this one for Maya and a Cinema 4D build. Picking the version for your main animation package avoids any time lost converting rigs between programs. Since 2020 the crabs have served marine documentaries, aquarium visuals, beach games and children's animations.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
