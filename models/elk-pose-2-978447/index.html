About This Model

Elk is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $139. Budget for 9,229 polygons and 9,306 vertices when you plan the scene. What matters is not the number itself but how many copies of the object your scene has to carry at once.

The material set is 9 maps, painted at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It went on sale in 2015 and has been kept in the catalog ever since. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
