About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Female Surgeon Dress is a finished, render-ready model at $79. Counted at the source, the model is 20,640 polygons and 21,232 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 11 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2015 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
