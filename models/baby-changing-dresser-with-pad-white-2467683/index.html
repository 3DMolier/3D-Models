About This Model

A white baby changing dresser with a padded top, this piece furnishes nurseries, family apartments and parenting store displays. The 9,426 polygons and 9,776 vertices shape the drawer fronts, the raised rails around the top and the soft cushioned pad. Raised edges on three sides keep a baby safe while changing, and that detail makes the furniture instantly readable.

Its construction follows the CheckMate specification at TurboSquid, giving clean topology, true scale and a neat hierarchy. Non-overlapping UVs let you swap the white paint for wood grain or pastel colors and add a patterned pad cover. A soft satin paint shader with slightly rounded edges keeps the piece calm and friendly in nursery lighting.

Released in 2025, the dresser suits nursery interior designs, real estate staging and parenting tutorials. Topped with a diaper basket and a folded towel, it shows a ready room for a new baby. Furniture retailers also feature it in room planners beside cribs, rocking chairs and storage shelves.
