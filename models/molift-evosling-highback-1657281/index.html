About This Model

Priced at $39, the Molift EvoSling HighBack is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 10,414 polygons and 10,890 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
