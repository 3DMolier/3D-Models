About This Model

A table tennis paddle with a wooden blade, rubber faces on both sides and a flared handle shaped for a shakehand grip. The red and black rubbers follow the standard competition pattern, so the bat looks right in a sports scene. Edges are slightly rounded where the rubber meets the blade, which catches a thin rim highlight.

The handle shows layered wood at the shoulder, a detail that sells it as a real paddle rather than a toy. A CheckMate Pro rating confirms TurboSquid reviewed the geometry, materials and scene structure before release. The pimpled rubber look can be enhanced with a bump map for closer camera work.

Offered since 2015, the paddle suits games rooms, sports clubs, school gyms and table tennis animations. Animators swing it in rallies, while set dressers lay it on a green table with a white ball beside it. It also works as a playful prop in office break rooms and student dorm scenes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
