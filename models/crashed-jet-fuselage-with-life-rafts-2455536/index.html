About This Model

Crashed Jet Fuselage with Life Rafts is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $169. You are getting 375,175 polygons and 382,272 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
