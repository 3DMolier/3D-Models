About This Model

The Antique Thermometer is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 1,352 polygons and 1,388 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2018 · Updated 20 August 2026

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Antique Thermometer?

8 maps at 4096 x 4096, with mixed UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Antique Thermometer mesh?

1,352 polygons and 1,388 vertices, built as polygonal ngons used. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the Antique Thermometer model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
