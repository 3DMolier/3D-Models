About This Model

This Aircraft model - Light Business Jet Aircraft - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $199 on TurboSquid. You are getting 233,925 polygons and 255,799 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 12.04 x 13.22 x H4.38 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
