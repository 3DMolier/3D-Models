About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Light Business Jet Aircraft White Red is a finished, render-ready model at $199. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, film production and simulation as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2026 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

At 233,925 polygons and 255,799 vertices this is a mid-weight asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
