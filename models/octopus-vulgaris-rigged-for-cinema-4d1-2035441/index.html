About This Model

A common octopus with its long arms spread out, ready to drape over rocks, cling to a reef or curl around a sunken wreck. The mantle, suckers and skin folds give the silhouette its soft, boneless character, which separates it from stiff stylized sea creatures. A mollusk this flexible looks most convincing when the arms overlap and twist rather than lie in a neat star.

The asset holds TurboSquid CheckMate Pro status, so naming, pivots and topology were inspected before it went on sale. Suckers along the underside of each arm catch light in a close macro shot, which rewards a slow camera pass along the body. Underwater fog and caustics flatter the reddish brown skin tones this species shows in the wild.

It joined the catalog in 2023 and sits alongside the other marine animals in our underwater range. Typical buyers include documentary teams, aquarium visualizations, children's educational films and ocean-themed game levels. It also works well in seafood market scenes or a kitchen shot, where a single octopus rests on crushed ice.
