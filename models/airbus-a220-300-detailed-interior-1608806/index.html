About This Model

This aircraft was designed from scratch for the hundred seat class rather than being shrunk from something larger, and it shows in the cabin: a wider seat on one side of the aisle than the other, larger windows and a genuinely different section from the older narrowbodies it competes with.

The model is built with subdivision geometry at 2,760,958 polygons and 2,876,799 vertices with non-overlapping UVs. Subdivision suits an airframe of long smooth curves, and the weight is largely the detailed interior.

A rigged build sits on this page at $319. The static model at $299 covers airline marketing, cabin and product visualisation, airport scenes, aviation journalism, film and television, and games. On sale since 2020.
