About This Model

A striped popcorn bucket topped with fluffy cotton candy sums up a day at the cinema or the fair in one bright prop. The Popcorn Bucket with Cotton Candy is dense, with 437,557 polygons shaping every kernel and each strand of sugar.

That count, spread over 451,258 vertices, means the popped kernels hold individual shapes instead of blending into a single lump. It meets the CheckMate standard, so the geometry is checked for clean structure and consistent scale. Blue and pink tones on the candy contrast with the bucket stripes, giving the composition a festive palette.

Released in 2026 at $49, it works as a hero item in close food shots or concession stand scenes. Theater lobbies, amusement parks and movie night renders gain a familiar snack without extra modeling time. Placed on a cinema seat armrest, it instantly tells the viewer a film is about to start. For distant crowd scenes a lighter prop may serve better, since the detail here is meant for the camera up close.
