About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Business Jet Plane is a finished, render-ready model at $249. The mesh carries 215,453 polygons and 219,703 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
