About This Model

Business Jet Plane Rigged belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $249 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. 2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 215,453 polygons and 219,703 vertices, a mid-weight load for a scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
