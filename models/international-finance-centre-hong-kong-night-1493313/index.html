About This Model

Priced at $99, the International Finance Centre Hong Kong Night is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 889,873 polygons and 1,323,576 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 29 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
