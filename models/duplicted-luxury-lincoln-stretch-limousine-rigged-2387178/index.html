About This Model

Duplicted - Luxury Lincoln Stretch Limousine Rigged is a long formal limousine with an extended passenger section and tinted side windows. The stretched wheelbase dominates any shot, so it reads instantly as a wedding, prom or celebrity arrival vehicle. Chrome trim and a long run of side glass make the length obvious even from a distance.

The CheckMate specification shaped the scene, so body, wheels and glass are separate objects with readable names. Licensing is Editorial Uses Only because the car carries a real Lincoln design, which fits news, documentary and illustrative work. Paint and window tint live in shaders, making it easy to switch between white wedding and dark executive versions.

Released in 2025, the limousine suits red carpet scenes, hotel entrance visualization, film sequences and city traffic in driving games. It works best pulled up to a curb under hotel lights, with doors open and a crowd waiting. Parked among ordinary sedans, its length alone signals wealth without needing any extra props.
