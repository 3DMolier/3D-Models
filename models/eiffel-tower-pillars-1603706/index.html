About This Model

The base of the Eiffel Tower with its four great pillars, modeled for street-level Paris scenes beneath the iron lattice. Each leg curves inward as it rises, and the decorative arches between them frame the view toward the Champ de Mars. The 342,515 polygons and 357,399 vertices are spent on the riveted lattice, which is what visitors see when they look up.

The lattice is unwrapped without overlaps, letting the tower's bronze-brown paint and weathering be textured without seams. TurboSquid certified the pillars CheckMate Pro after checking for tidy geometry and an orderly file. Low camera angles benefit most, since the iron web overhead fills the frame with intricate pattern.

Available since 2020, the Paris base is royalty free and suits travel videos, romantic film scenes and walkthroughs in games. Crowds queuing under the arches or a couple walking at night between the legs are typical shots it supports. Because only the base is included, the scene stays efficient while still placing the viewer firmly in Paris.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2020 · Updated 18 November 2026
