About This Model

Priced at $149, the Jeep Wrangler Rubicon is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 335,191 polygons and 389,901 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 11 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
