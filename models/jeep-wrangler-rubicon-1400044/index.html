About This Model

Priced at $149, the Jeep Wrangler Rubicon is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The polygon count is 335,191, spread over 389,901 vertices. Whether that fits depends on where it lands - an architectural still and a real-time walkthrough tolerate very different counts.

You get 11 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 - enough resolution to hold a close shot. Construction is plain and deliberate - quads through the body, triangles only where they are harmless. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
