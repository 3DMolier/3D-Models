About This Model

The Peace Arch stands directly on the border between Canada and the United States, half in each country, with an unlocked gate inside it. It was built to mark a boundary that had gone unfortified for a century, and the whole point of the monument is that it does not stop anyone.

The model holds 67,403 polygons and 69,668 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and carries StemCell certification.

At $69 it suits architectural and landmark visualization, news and documentary graphics, tourism imagery, education material, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2023.
