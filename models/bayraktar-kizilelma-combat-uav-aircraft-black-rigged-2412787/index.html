About This Model

Priced at $139, the Bayraktar Kizilelma Combat UAV Aircraft Black Rigged is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 104,029 polygons and 110,108 vertices, a light load for a scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
