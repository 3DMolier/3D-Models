About This Model

A collection of outdoor picnic tables, including classic timber designs with attached bench seats and sturdy A-frame legs. Thick boards with small gaps between them let rain drain away, the detail that marks a real park table. Benches fixed to the frame keep the table stable, so families can sit along both sides without it tipping.

The tables earned the CheckMate Pro mark, which vouches for orderly meshes and accurate size. Real-world proportions mean a seated character lines up with the bench height and tabletop without manual scaling. Several designs in a single purchase give a park or campsite variety instead of repeated copies.

Introduced in 2021, the tables suit public parks, campsites, school yards and highway rest stops. Set under a shady tree with a checkered cloth and a basket, a table invites a summer lunch scene. Landscape architects can place them along paths in park plans to show seating areas.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
