About This Model

A small rat poking its head out of a rusty, half-opened food can, set up for Blender as a gritty urban vignette. The jagged lid curls back from the rim, and flaking orange corrosion covers the dented cylinder. The rat's pink nose, twitching whiskers and long tail make the little scavenger read clearly at a glance.

Rust is layered unevenly, heavier near the seams and the bottom, so the can looks forgotten in a damp alley for months. Under CheckMate structure, the can, lid and rat stay separate, which makes repositioning the animal easy. Fur on the rat contrasts with the hard, pitted metal and pulls the eye straight to the creature.

The Rusty Food Can with Small Rat For Blender came out in 2026 for environment and story artists. It suits post-apocalyptic game levels, sewer and dumpster scenes, and illustrations about urban decay. Placed in a puddle under a flickering streetlight, the vignette tells a whole story of neglect.
