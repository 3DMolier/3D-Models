About This Model

Agusta AW109S Grand is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. You are getting 203,850 polygons and 222,393 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
