About This Model

A complete vehicle brake system with discs, calipers, lines and master cylinder, laid out for technical explanation and training. The Vehicle Brake System uses 194,182 polygons to show how each component connects from pedal to wheel.

Its 198,066 vertices keep the disc faces and caliper bodies sharp in macro inspection shots. Meeting CheckMate standards, the assembly arrives with separate parts that are easy to isolate or animate. Brake lines run between components the way they would under a real car, making the hydraulic path easy to trace.

Released in 2026 for $69, the system suits automotive education, mechanic training and engineering presentations. Animate the pistons squeezing the pads, or explode the parts apart to explain each function. Parts suppliers can show customers which component they are replacing within the larger layout. Without a car body in the way, every line and fitting stays visible from any viewing angle.
