About This Model

Japanese Classic Car Black belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and game environments. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

Geometry weighs in at 326,708 polygons over 175,118 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
