About This Model

A collection of personal watercraft, the sit-down jet skis that skim across lakes and coastlines at high speed. The Personal Water Crafts 3D Models Collection shows the sculpted hulls, handlebars and padded seats that define these machines. Each craft sits on a wide planing hull, so it looks stable floating at a dock or carving a turn.

CheckMate Pro certification for the bundle guarantees clean geometry and consistent naming from one craft to the next. Glossy fiberglass bodies throw bright reflections, which makes them stand out against blue water and sky. Seats and handlebars are scaled for a rider, so a character can be placed aboard.

At $37 the collection has sold 11 times since 2019 to resort marketers, watersports rental companies and racing game developers. Several craft in one purchase make it easy to stage a group ride or a rental beach. The set also saves time for anyone building marinas, since boats and docks need company on the water.
