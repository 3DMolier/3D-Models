About This Model

Faberge Egg Silver Closed is a detailed Signage & Decor asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

Geometry weighs in at 118,170 polygons over 59,517 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
