About This Model

Ordinary Dreidel is one of the Toys & Games models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $24. You are getting 2,616 polygons and 1,312 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
