About This Model

Looking for a Containers & Storage asset? The Health Potion is a finished, render-ready model at $8. Geometry weighs in at 7,682 polygons over 3,853 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.
