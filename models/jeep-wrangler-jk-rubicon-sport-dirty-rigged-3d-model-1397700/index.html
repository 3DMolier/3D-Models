About This Model

JEEP Wrangler Rubicon is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $169. Geometry weighs in at 337,151 polygons over 391,161 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

17 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
