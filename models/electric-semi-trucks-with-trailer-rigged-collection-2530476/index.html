About This Model

A group of electric semi tractors with trailers, rigged for logistics animation and highway traffic scenes. Smooth noses and absent exhaust stacks set these battery cabs apart from the diesel trucks most libraries offer.

Each tractor pulls a long box trailer, and the hitch lets the trailer follow the cab through a turn. Wheels spin and steer from controls, so a convoy can travel a road without hand keying every tire. Large windshields and low aerodynamic skirts give the cabs a sleek profile, and mixed colors keep a fleet from looking cloned. Trailer doors and landing gear are modeled, so a loading dock scene works without extra props.

Launched in 2026, the set serves logistics firms, charging network planners and city traffic simulations. Parked at a depot beside chargers, the trucks tell a clear story about freight going electric. Clean geometry to CheckMate rules keeps the rig responsive even with a long line of trailers.
