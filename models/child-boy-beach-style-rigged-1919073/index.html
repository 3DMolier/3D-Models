About This Model

A young boy in casual beach clothing, built for family holiday, resort, pool and summer scenes. His relaxed proportions and youthful features make him read clearly as a child rather than a scaled-down adult. Standing at the waterline with a bucket nearby, the character gives a seaside render warmth and scale.

TurboSquid certified the character CheckMate Pro, confirming clean topology and a tidy scene structure ready for animation. Rigging is included, as the title states, so he can be posed building sandcastles, running or waving. Posed figures like this help architects and resort designers show how families will actually use a space.

At $179 he suits resort visualizations, beach club renders, family travel apps and summer catalog scenes. Out since 2022, the character also fits pool areas, waterparks, cruise deck scenes and coastal real estate walkthroughs. Place him beside a parent under an umbrella, and the scene feels like a real summer afternoon.
