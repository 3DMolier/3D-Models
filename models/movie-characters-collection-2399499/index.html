About This Model

A collection of ready characters styled for cinema scenes, grouped so a film-themed project can be cast in one purchase. Costume, pose and personality vary across the set, which keeps a crowd of figures from looking cloned. Each figure carries its own outfit and attitude, giving directors of virtual scenes a varied cast to stage and light.

Consistent naming and scale, set by CheckMate specification, let the characters stand together without adjustment. Separate figures can be placed individually, so you can stage a red carpet, a film set or a theater lobby. Uniform proportions across the set keep heights believable when several characters share one wide frame.

Since 2025 the collection has been offered at $179 for games, film previs and entertainment visuals. Previs teams use ready casts like this to block scenes long before actors or final models arrive. Event and premiere renders also benefit from a group of characters gathered beneath a marquee.
