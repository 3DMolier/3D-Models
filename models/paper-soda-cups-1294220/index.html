About This Model

Paper Soda Cups belongs to our Containers & Storage range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 3,570 polygons and 3,590 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

2 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.
