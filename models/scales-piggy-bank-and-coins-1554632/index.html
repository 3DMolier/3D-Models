About This Model

A balance scale weighing a piggy bank against a pile of coins, a ready visual metaphor for savings, budgeting and financial decisions. The scale keeps its central pillar, beam and two hanging pans, so the balance reads clearly at any angle.

The set comes with StemCell certification, keeping materials consistent from one renderer to another. The piggy bank and coins are independent objects, which lets an artist tip the balance one way or the other in an animation. Glossy ceramic, gold coins and brass fittings shine under bright studio light against a plain backdrop.

Available from 2020, the composition supports finance blog illustrations, banking explainers and educational videos about money. Animators tilt the pans to show how savings grow compared with spending. Teachers use visuals like this to explain budgeting to children and teenagers. Accounting and insurance presentations also rely on the image for slides about risk and balance.
