About This Model

Priced at $129, the Avatr 12 EV with Headlights On is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 183,590 polygons over 192,576 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
