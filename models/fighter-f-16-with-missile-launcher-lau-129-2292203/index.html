About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Fighter F-16 with Missile Launcher LAU-129 is a finished, render-ready model at $219. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 153,583 polygons and 162,274 vertices.
