About This Model

Priced at $159, the Teenager Skin School Uniform Walking is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 28,261 polygons and 48,949 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
