About This Model

The same stylized cartoon woman dressed in casual youth clothes, prepared for Maya and ready for lifestyle animation. Relaxed clothing changes her personality completely, turning a corporate character into a student, shopper or friend. Rounded features and big eyes keep the cartoon read consistent with the office variant of her.

Topology follows CheckMate specification, with tidy loops at the shoulders, elbows and knees for smooth bending. Bright casual colors suit outdoor scenes, cafes and college campuses where a light, upbeat tone carries the story. Simple materials mean color changes take minutes, which helps when a campaign needs several outfits quickly.

In the catalog since 2019, the character suits youth marketing, education apps, social media shorts and lifestyle explainers. Give her a phone or a backpack from another kit, and she becomes the hero of a student-life story. Animation studios use paired outfits like this to show the same person at work and at leisure.
