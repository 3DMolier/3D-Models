About This Model

Horse and Skeleton Rigged Collection pairs a realistic horse with its matching skeleton, both rigged to move in step. Seeing bones and body together shows how the long legs, deep chest and arched neck are built underneath. The skeleton can overlay the horse in animation, revealing the joints and long bones that drive each stride at walk, trot or gallop.

Both models earned CheckMate Pro, so geometry, naming and rig organization meet a strict inspection standard. Matching rigs mean a gallop on the horse can be mirrored on the skeleton for anatomy demonstrations. Hooves, fetlocks and the long cannon bones show clearly when the camera tracks low beside the moving legs.

First offered in 2020, the collection suits veterinary schools, equine science lessons and museum exhibits. Fantasy productions also use horse skeletons for undead cavalry, cursed knights and haunted battlefield scenes. Riders and trainers can study gait mechanics with the skeleton walking beside the living animal.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
