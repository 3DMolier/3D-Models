About This Model

Priced at $29, the iPhone SE is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 19,592 polygons over 20,936 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

11 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
