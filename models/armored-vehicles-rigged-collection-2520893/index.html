About This Model

A collection of rigged armored vehicles, from wheeled carriers to tracked hulls, ready to roll through military scenes. Each vehicle has angled armor plates, hatches and mounted weapons that make its role clear even in silhouette. Because every vehicle is rigged, wheels and tracks can turn and turrets can rotate toward a target.

Built under CheckMate specification, the models share consistent scale, so a convoy lines up without manual resizing. Pivots sit at axles and turret rings, which makes setting up driving, steering and aiming animation fast and straightforward. Dusty camouflage and worn metal finishes look right under harsh desert sun or the gray light of a winter front.

Released in 2026, the collection targets military games, defense simulations, war documentaries and film previsualization. Buying the vehicles together lets a battalion, a checkpoint or a parade be staged from one consistent source. A column of these machines rolling down a road establishes conflict and power in a single wide shot.
