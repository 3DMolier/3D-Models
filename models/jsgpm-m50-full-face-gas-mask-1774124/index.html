About This Model

A modern protective mask carries its filters on the cheeks rather than in front, which sounds cosmetic and is not: it clears the space a rifle sight needs. Every design decision on one of these is a compromise between breathing, seeing and being able to work.

The model holds 58,982 polygons and 60,009 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.
