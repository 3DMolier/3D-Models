About This Model

This baby kangaroo, or joey, has oversized ears, a slim tail and long hind feet that hint at the hopping adult it will become. Its 20,961 polygons and 21,188 vertices keep the young marsupial light enough for a mob of kangaroos in the outback. Joeys are usually shown near their mothers, so it pairs naturally with an adult kangaroo or a keeper.

StemCell certification ensures the PBR materials behave the same in every renderer the file supports. UVs are unwrapped without overlaps, letting the pale belly and darker back be tinted independently.

Eight versions cover plain, fur and standing editions, with or without a rig. The fur rig is the premium pick at $179, while plain rigs for Maya, Cinema 4D and general use cost $129. A standing pose edition at $99 suits still images where the animal simply watches the camera. Since 2024 the joey has served Australian wildlife films, zoo education boards and children's animation.
