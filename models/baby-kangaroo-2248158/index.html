About This Model

Baby Kangaroo belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 20,961 polygons and 21,188 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
