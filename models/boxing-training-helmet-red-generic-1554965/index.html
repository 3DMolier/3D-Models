Looking for a Sports & Recreation asset? The Boxing Training Helmet Generic is a finished, render-ready model at $59. The mesh carries 21,012 polygons and 22,508 vertices. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them.
