About This Model

This flexible packaging bag is a soft pouch with crimped seals, the kind used for snacks, coffee or pet food. Its 2,863 polygons and 2,258 vertices shape the puffed body, the flattened top seam and gentle wrinkles near the edges. Slight bulges where the contents press outward keep the pouch from looking like a stiff cardboard box.

Its construction follows the CheckMate specification, so the mesh stays light, clean and simple to retexture. Non-overlapping UVs give a large uninterrupted front panel, perfect for testing new label artwork quickly. Glossy or matte film shaders change the character completely, from premium coffee to budget chips at a corner shop.

Created for the catalog in 2024, the bag suits packaging mockups, grocery shelf scenes and product design pitches. Stand several upright on a shelf with different prints, and a supermarket aisle starts to fill with color. Startups and design students use blank pouches like this to preview branding before paying for printing.
