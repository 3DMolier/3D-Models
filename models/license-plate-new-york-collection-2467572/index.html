About This Model

This set of New York license plates lets you finish any vehicle render with the tag a viewer expects on a New York street. Each plate is a thin stamped panel with raised characters and rounded corners, the shape drivers know from the bumper. Mounting holes sit at the top edge, so the plates can be fixed to a bracket or a bumper in seconds.

As a StemCell product, the collection relies on standardized PBR materials that render alike across several of the most common engines. Polygon figures are not provided, yet flat plates like these carry little geometry and barely affect a busy street scene. Plate colors and lettering sit in the materials, which helps when a shot needs a different number on every car.

Brought out in 2025 at $11, the plates suit traffic visualizations, crime dramas set in Manhattan and urban driving games. Animators place them on taxis, police cruisers and parked sedans to tell viewers at once which city they are in. Product designers of plate frames and bumper parts also check fit with them on a vehicle model before production starts.
