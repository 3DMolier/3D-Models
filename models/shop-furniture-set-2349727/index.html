About This Model

Shop Furniture Set belongs to our Furniture & Interior range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 21,993 polygons over 23,770 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
