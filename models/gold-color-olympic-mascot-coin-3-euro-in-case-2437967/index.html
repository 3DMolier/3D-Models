About This Model

A gold-colored commemorative euro coin with a mascot design, presented in a clear capsule inside its collector case. The cheerful mascot relief on the face contrasts with the formal denomination side, a typical sports commemorative layout. The presentation case frames the coin like jewelry, which suits close-up product shots and gift catalog pages.

Its CheckMate specification approval at TurboSquid covers realistic scale and well-named objects. A warm gold shader with soft reflections works best, while the case interior takes a dark velvet look. Tilting the coin slightly toward the light brings the raised mascot and lettering to life in a render.

This coin set joined TurboSquid in 2025 at $34 and is licensed Royalty Free for commercial work. Numismatic shop catalogs, sports history features, collector apps and museum displays are suitable settings. Next to a ticket stub and a medal, it recalls a whole sporting summer in one quiet still life.
