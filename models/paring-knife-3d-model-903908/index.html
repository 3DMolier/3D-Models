About This Model

The Paring Knife is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $4 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 662 polygons over 664 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

3 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags architectural visualization, product rendering and game assets as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
