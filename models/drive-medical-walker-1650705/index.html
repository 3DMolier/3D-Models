About This Model

A walking frame is one of the most standardised objects in healthcare: a tube frame with two front glides or wheels, height-adjustable legs with spring buttons and a pair of grips at the top. That standardisation means any deviation is noticed, and it also means the object instantly signals mobility support wherever it appears in a scene.

The model is built with subdivision geometry at 17,352 polygons and 17,182 vertices with non-overlapping UVs. Subdivision suits a frame made entirely of bent tube: the curves stay clean at any camera distance.

At $29 and built to StemCell specification, it suits healthcare and elder care visualization, accessibility and inclusive design presentation, hospital and home interiors, medical equipment marketing and games. On sale since 2021.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2020 · Updated 18 November 2026
