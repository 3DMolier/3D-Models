About This Model

A sea lion mother rests beside her pup in this collection, both shown with sleek fur, long whiskers and strong front flippers. The pair comes without polygon figures, yet the folds of the neck and the shape of the flippers are clearly modeled. The pup is sized much smaller than the adult, which makes the family scene easy to read.

TurboSquid gave the pair CheckMate Pro after reviewing their topology, naming and natural proportions. Mother and pup are separate models, so they can lie together on a rock or swim apart in a harbor scene. Wet fur materials catch the light like real animals drying in the sun.

Royalty Free since 2019, the set fits wildlife documentaries, zoo visuals and coastal games. A mother and pup on a rocky shore make a strong emotional moment in a nature story. Marine centers also use the models in exhibits about protecting seals and sea lions.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
