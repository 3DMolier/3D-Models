About This Model

A pantry collection of spreads, with a Nutella jar beside peanut butter and fruit jam jars for a breakfast scene. Glass jars, printed labels and screw lids make each spread recognizable at a glance on a kitchen counter. Grouped on a breakfast table with toast, the jars set the morning mood without extra props.

CheckMate Pro inspection confirmed clean topology and accurate scale for every jar and lid in the set. Glass and label materials respond well to soft window light, which suits food photography setups. Because Nutella is a registered brand, the collection is licensed for Editorial Uses Only.

Available since 2021, the jars serve food journalism, nutrition education and kitchen scenes in film. Articles comparing sugar content often line up jars like these to make the point visual for readers. Lids and jars are separate, so a spread can be shown open with a knife resting inside.
