About This Model

Priced at $99, the Female Left Arm Anatomy Skinless is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 50,427 polygons over 50,561 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
