About This Model

A bucket racing seat with a multi-point harness and side head supports, this model belongs in a race car cockpit or a sim rig. Its 15,476 polygons and 15,614 vertices define the bolstered shell, the padded cushions, the belt buckles and the adjustment rails underneath.

At 80.12 x 76.65 x H102.68 cm the seat matches a real competition shell, so it drops into a car interior at correct scale. Mixed UVs and StemCell certification make the seat behave the same in every major renderer. Bright harness straps against black fabric help the seat stand out even in a dark cockpit.

There is a choice of two $39 versions, the Adjustable Racing Driver Seat with Harness and a reinforced racing safety seat. Out since 2025 and covered by a Royalty Free license, it fits motorsport games, sim racing setups and automotive safety explainers. Gaming streamers often show their sim rigs, and this seat makes a believable centerpiece for such a setup.
