Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Quail Eggs Box is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The mesh carries 43,512 polygons and 43,540 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them.
