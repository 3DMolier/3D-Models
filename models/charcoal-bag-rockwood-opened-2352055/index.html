About This Model

An opened bag of Rockwood lump charcoal, the top folded back so the dark chunks inside are visible to the camera. The soft, slumped paper and irregular charcoal pieces make the sack look half used after a cookout on a long summer weekend. Visible lumps at the opening add texture that a sealed bag cannot offer in a close shot.

StemCell quality means the materials come pre-balanced and consistent in every delivered format. Matte paper with a slight sheen at the creases, plus rough black charcoal, gives strong material contrast. The bag sits naturally on concrete or decking, slumping slightly under its own weight like a real paper sack.

Released for sale in 2025, it suits barbecue lifestyle renders, grilling tutorials and outdoor kitchen designs. Pair it with a ceramic grill and tongs to stage the moment the coals are loaded before a long smoke. A light dusting of black powder around the base makes the scene look honestly used.
