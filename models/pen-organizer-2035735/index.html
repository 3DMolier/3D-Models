About This Model

Priced at $12, the Pen Organizer is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 37,010 polygons and 18,622 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 4 September 2026
