About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Sewing Thimble is a finished, render-ready model at $15. You are getting 69,856 polygons and 34,932 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
