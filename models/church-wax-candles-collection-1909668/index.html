About This Model

A group of slim church wax candles of different heights, ready for altars, chapels and candlelit memorial scenes. Tall and short candles stand together, so a row of them reads like an altar that has been in use for weeks. Melted drips along the sides catch light and make the wax look soft rather than plastic.

The collection is certified CheckMate Pro, confirming clean naming, closed shapes and a real-world scale. Each candle is a separate object, so you can arrange them on stands, in sand trays or along a windowsill. Several layouts, from orderly rows to clustered memorial stands, can be built from the same set in minutes.

On sale since 2022, the set suits religious interiors, historical drama and mood lighting tests. Paired with an emissive flame or a light per wick, it fills a dark church with warm points of light. Game artists use candles like these to guide players through crypts and quiet sanctuaries.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How clean is the geometry on the Church Wax Candles Collection model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.

How heavy is the Church Wax Candles Collection mesh?

2,424 polygons and 2,385 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Against a median of about 28,000 polygons across our Model Bundles & Sets models, 2,424 puts this one below the middle of the category.

What is the Church Wax Candles Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and architectural visualization - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

Can the Church Wax Candles Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes. It is sold under TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, which covers commercial use in games, film, advertising and visualization without per-use fees.
