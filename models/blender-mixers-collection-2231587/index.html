About This Model

Blender Mixers Collection gathers kitchen blenders with tall jugs, motor bases and control dials for countertop scenes. Lined up on a kitchen counter or a store shelf, the blenders fill out an appliance scene without extra modeling. Clear jugs reward careful glass shading, and they become the focus once fruit or a smoothie is placed inside.

CheckMate specification shaped each blender, giving every appliance true size along with clean naming and checked geometry. Plastic, glass and brushed metal cover most of the mixer surfaces, which suits bright kitchen lighting. Morning breakfast scenes often need exactly this prop beside a bowl of fruit and a coffee machine.

Blender Mixers Collection 2 is an expanded version with more models for $69, while this collection costs $59. The mixers, on sale since 2024, have been bought for kitchen visualization, appliance store interiors, cooking videos and home game levels. Each base is a separate object, so a single blender can be pulled out for a close product shot.
