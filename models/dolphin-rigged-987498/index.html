About This Model

Dolphin Rigged is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 11,362 polygons over 11,550 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 3 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
