About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Plastic Photo Frames Set is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The mesh carries 11,224 polygons and 11,236 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
