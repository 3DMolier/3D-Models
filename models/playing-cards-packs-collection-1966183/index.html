About This Model

Packs of playing cards in their boxes make up this collection, a quick way to dress game tables, casinos and magic shows. The boxes are modeled with tuck flaps and slightly rounded corners, the way real card cases look after a few games. Some packs stand closed while others are opened, so a table can show both new decks and cards in use.

TurboSquid ranks the card packs at CheckMate Pro, so each box has passed a check of its mesh and its real scale. Box artwork and card backs can be replaced with your own designs, which suits custom deck and game projects. A satin paper finish with a light sheen reproduces the coated card stock that real decks are printed on.

Poker nights, casino interiors and magician performances use the packs to fill tables and display shelves. Around since 2022, the collection also fits board game stores and cozy living rooms where a deck rests beside snacks. Scattered next to poker chips under a warm table lamp, the packs instantly set up a card game scene.
