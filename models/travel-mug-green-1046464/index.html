About This Model

Priced at $19, the Travel Mug Green is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

The mesh carries 3,269 polygons and 3,302 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category.
