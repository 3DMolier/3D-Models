About This Model

Interdental brushes clean the surfaces a toothbrush physically cannot reach, which is where most gum disease begins. The color codes the size, and choosing the right one matters more than anything about the bristles.

The model is light at 22,271 polygons and 25,443 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in blue. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits dental and oral care visualization, product and packaging imagery, e-commerce and advertising work, patient education and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
