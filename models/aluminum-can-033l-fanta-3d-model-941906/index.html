About This Model

An orange Fanta soda can, this asset brings a bright fruit drink to picnic tables, convenience store coolers and editorial beverage stories. The body follows the standard can profile with a narrow neck, a stay-on tab and a shallow concave base underneath. Its vivid orange print makes it the color accent of a drinks shelf, especially next to cola and lemon soda cans.

The model carries the CheckMate Pro rating from TurboSquid, a sign of tidy geometry, believable dimensions and an orderly scene. Use is limited to Editorial Uses Only, which fits journalism, documentaries and educational material about soft drinks. A satin metal shader with small droplets and a slightly dented rim gives the can a just-opened, handled look.

Released in 2015, the can suits summer party scenes, school cafeteria interiors and street kiosk counters in illustrative work. Half buried in a cooler of ice, it immediately signals a hot afternoon and a cold refreshment. Articles about fizzy drinks or children's diets also use it as a simple, recognizable illustration of the topic.
