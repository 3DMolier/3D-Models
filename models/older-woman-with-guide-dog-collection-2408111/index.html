About This Model

An older woman with a white cane walks with her guide dog, holding the harness handle as the dog leads her calmly along the pavement. The poses show the pair walking, pausing at an obstacle and waiting at a crossing, the moments that matter most in daily life. The dog's focused stance and steady gaze ahead clearly signal to passersby that it is on duty.

Woman and dog are separate figures, so they can be placed at station platforms, curbs, shop doors or park paths independently. The set meets CheckMate standards set by TurboSquid, keeping both at a natural size and with names that make each figure easy to find. Small cues like dark glasses, the cane and the harness make the situation clear without any text.

Offered from 2025 at $149, the collection serves accessibility training, transit design presentations, healthcare material and campaigns for inclusive cities. Architects can show how ramps, tactile strips and wide doorways support blind and partially sighted residents. Guide dog associations also find such scenes helpful when describing their training programs to donors.
