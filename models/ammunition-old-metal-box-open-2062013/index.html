About This Model

This open ammunition box is a battered steel can with a hinged lid, the kind soldiers carried rounds in for decades. The lid stands raised on its latch, revealing an empty interior where cartridges or belts once sat. Dents, a folding carry handle and a rubber seal around the rim give it the look of real field equipment.

The box follows the CheckMate specification, so the files open cleanly and the geometry matches the preview images. Olive drab paint worn through to bare metal on the edges suits the shape, along with flaking stenciled markings.

On sale since 2023, the box fits war films, military games, bunker interiors and workshop storage scenes. Many people reuse these cans for tools and fishing gear, so it also belongs in a garage or shed. A loot crate in a survival game can be built around it, with the open lid inviting players to search. Military history museums also display such boxes beside rifles to show how ammunition reached the front line.
