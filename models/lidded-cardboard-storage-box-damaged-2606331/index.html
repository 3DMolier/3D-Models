About This Model

A battered cardboard storage box with a separate lid, its corners crushed and sides dented from years in an attic. The Lidded Cardboard Storage Box Damaged uses 6,871 polygons and 6,861 vertices, with the dents built into the geometry itself. Torn tape along the seams catches light and adds texture without any extra maps.

It measures 57.55 x 56.73 x H35.67 cm, a roomy box for moving house, holiday decorations or archiving papers. CheckMate certification ensures correct scale, so the box sits at believable size next to shelves and furniture. Sagging flaps and a slightly askew lid tell a story of heavy use that clean boxes cannot.

At $39 the damaged box comes as one version, first released in 2026. Abandoned buildings, moving day scenes, basement storage and post-apocalyptic games use it to add neglect and wear. Scatter several copies with different rotations and a storage room looks lived in rather than staged.
