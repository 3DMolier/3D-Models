About This Model

Dynatron Solaris 709 Plus Storage Cart is a rolling clinic trolley that carries a physical therapy device, with drawers and a top shelf. Its 86,260 polygons and 44,180 vertices outline the caster wheels, drawer pulls and the molded tray where the unit sits.

Being StemCell certified, the cart keeps its PBR look identical across engines, so plastic panels and metal trims stay believable. The unwrap places each part on its own island, which leaves space for wear marks, labels and equipment stickers. A push handle at the back makes the cart easy to animate sliding between treatment beds or out of a storage closet.

We added it in 2024 for physiotherapy clinic interiors, rehabilitation explainers and hospital equipment planning across busy outpatient departments. Park it beside a treatment table with cables draped toward a patient, and the room immediately reads as a working practice. Sports medicine centers and chiropractic offices are other natural spots for this kind of mobile station.
