About This Model

Priced at $49, the Detailed Prosthetic Arm Rigged is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 8,284 polygons and 8,553 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
