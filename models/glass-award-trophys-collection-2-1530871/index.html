About This Model

A collection of clear glass and crystal award trophies in several shapes, from tall slabs to faceted forms. Every trophy can be moved on its own, so a whole row can stand on a podium or one can sit on a desk. Engraved names and logos are easy to add, which is why these pieces suit corporate award scenes.

Glass rewards good lighting, and these trophies show crisp refraction when lit against a dark background. Beveled edges catch highlights that make the crystal sparkle in a slow camera move. The set was made under CheckMate Pro rules, so naming and scale are consistent.

Bought for $59 since 2020, the bundle serves award ceremony visuals, company recognition posts and event invitations. A spotlight shining down onto a single trophy makes a strong poster for an annual gala. Several trophies arranged on a shelf also work as background detail in an executive office.
