About This Model

A built-in kitchen dishwasher with a stainless front and a clean handle bar, ready to slide under a countertop. The Dishwasher Samsung spends 265,036 polygons and 265,429 vertices, so the control strip, door edges and feet stay crisp. That density pays off in product shots, and a flush toe kick lets the unit line up with cabinetry without a visible gap.

Brushed steel, printed icons and soft reflections along the door come from 8 maps at 4096 x 4096. CheckMate Pro inspection confirms real-world scale, non-overlapping UVs and named parts throughout. Plain variants are Royalty Free, while the Samsung-branded versions fall under Editorial Uses Only.

Since 2018 there are 4 versions of equal polygon count: this unit, an inox Samsung DW4000KM, a front control machine and a stainless steel one. Kitchen visualizations and modern apartment interiors are where it usually stands between cabinets. Keep the door shut for a tidy elevation, or surround it with stacked plates for a lifestyle scene.
