About This Model

New Honda CBR1000RR Fireblade SP 2016 belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $119 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

Counted at the source, the model is 206,387 polygons and 205,721 vertices, a mid-weight load for a scene. Texture work runs to 61 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
