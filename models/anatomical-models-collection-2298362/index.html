About This Model

A bundle of anatomical teaching models in one purchase, the kind found on shelves in medical schools and biology classrooms. Such sets usually mix bones, joints and organs on stands, giving a scene the look of a real study room. Buying them together keeps the style, scale and material approach consistent across the shelf.

Each model keeps correct size next to desks, books and lab coats, in line with CheckMate construction habits. Clean bone tones and pale organ colors suit both photoreal renders and simplified diagrams. Each piece can be moved and rotated on its own, which makes the set useful for building lesson animations.

Released in 2024 for $89, the collection fits medical education content, health explainers, school laboratory interiors and e-learning apps. Arrange the models on a lab bench under clean white light, and the scene immediately says classroom rather than clinic. A skeleton stand in the corner would finish the look of a teaching lab.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
