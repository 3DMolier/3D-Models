About This Model

This abdominal roller is a single wheel with handles on both sides, used for core workouts by rolling forward from the knees. The model spends 14,900 polygons on 14,982 vertices, keeping the tread pattern and grip foam smooth up close. A wide rubber tire and an axle through the center define the shape, with padded handles that rotate freely.

StemCell rules apply here, and the roller ships with 5 texture maps at 4096 x 4096 in PBR form. Non-overlapping UVs keep the foam grips, rubber tread and plastic hub cleanly separated for repainting.

Around since 2020, the roller suits home gym scenes, fitness app illustrations and sports equipment visualizations. Placed on a yoga mat beside a water bottle, it sets up a workout scene without any character present. Fitness instructors making exercise videos can animate a figure rolling it forward to explain proper form. Gym interiors with racks of small equipment also gain realism when a roller sits in the corner.
