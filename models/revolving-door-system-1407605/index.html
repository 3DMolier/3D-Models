About This Model

Revolving Door System belongs to our Architecture Landmarks range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags interior design visualization, advertising and architecture presentation as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 21,736 polygons and 23,845 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Maps: 2 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
