About This Model

This bundle gathers rigged cartoon characters in the classic Disney animation style, ready for posing and simple animated scenes. Big expressive eyes, rounded shapes and bright colors give each figure the friendly look of family animation from the big studios. Placed together, the characters can fill a playful scene, from a birthday party to a fairy tale adventure in the woods.

TurboSquid gave the bundle a CheckMate Lite rating, which confirms the files open properly and meet basic quality checks. Rigging lets you pose arms, legs and heads quickly, so still images and short loops are fast to produce. Simple, smooth surfaces respond well to soft toon lighting and need no complex shading to look right.

Released in 2023 at $119, the character set is covered by TurboSquid's Royalty Free license. Animation students practice posing on the figures, and hobby projects use them in fan scenes and festive renders. Character artists also study their proportions as a reference when developing their own stylized designs.
