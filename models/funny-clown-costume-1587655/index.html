About This Model

Funny Clown Costume is a detailed Clothing & Accessories asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $69. You are getting 11,213 polygons and 11,328 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers.
