About This Model

A hard cooler is a moulded double wall box with a hinged lid, a drain plug at one end and a swing handle over the top. The proportions are set by what fits inside - bottles standing upright, or ice around them - and the moulded ribs exist to stiffen a large plastic shell that gets sat on constantly.

The mesh runs 27,573 polygons and 27,959 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 7 texture maps at a mix of sizes up to 4096x4096 carrying the moulded finish, the branding and the scuffs.

At $29 it suits outdoor and camping visualization, beach and picnic scenes, beverage and lifestyle advertising, retail renders and games. On sale since 2015.
