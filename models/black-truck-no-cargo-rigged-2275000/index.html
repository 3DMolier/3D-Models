About This Model

Truck No Cargo is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $169. The mesh carries 491,726 polygons and 508,503 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
