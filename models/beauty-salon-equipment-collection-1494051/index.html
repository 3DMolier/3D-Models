About This Model

This collection equips a beauty salon with styling chairs, hair washing stations, mirrors and trolleys for tools. Each piece has adjustable bases, padded upholstery and chrome details that you would expect in a real salon. Arranged in rows, the chairs and mirrors create the familiar rhythm of a busy hair studio.

CheckMate Lite inspection confirmed the scale and basic file order of every item in the set. That helps when you lay out an entire salon floor and need chairs and basins to fit together. Bright, even lighting suits these pieces, since glossy leather and chrome reflect a clean studio look.

The page holds two versions: the Beauty Salon Equipment Collection at $103 and a second salon set at $119. The first set went live in 2020 and is free of royalties for commercial work. Interior designers use it for salon fit-out proposals, and game makers furnish town shops and makeover scenes with it.
