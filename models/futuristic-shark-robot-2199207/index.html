About This Model

Futuristic Shark Robot belongs to our Ships range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
