About This Model

The Simpson Characters Collection brings together a set of stylized cartoon figures with exaggerated, instantly readable shapes. Big eyes, simple silhouettes and bold flat colors make each character easy to read even at small sizes. Having the whole group together lets you stage family moments instead of posing a lone figure against an empty backdrop.

Every character follows CheckMate specification, keeping relative heights consistent across the whole group. Cartoon figures look best with flat, bright lighting and a cel or toon shader applied. Simple shapes also keep the set light enough for mobile games, web viewers and other real-time scenes with tight budgets.

New in 2025, the set suits animation tests, toy prototypes, party visuals and casual game scenes. Pose them around a couch or a kitchen table to build a quick sitcom-style scene. Buying the characters together keeps proportions and style matched across every shot of a short animation.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
