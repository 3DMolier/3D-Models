About This Model

A compact street sweeper is built around one constraint: it has to work a city street without blocking it. That gives it a short wheelbase, a cab set high for visibility over the brushes, a suction nozzle slung between the axles and a hopper that tips at the front. The model keeps the side brushes, the nozzle, the hopper hinge and the roof hatches that let a crew reach the filters.

It runs 487,309 polygons and 537,162 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout. The detail sits in the brush heads and the hopper mechanism, which is where a municipal vehicle is looked at when it is the subject rather than background traffic.

This page carries four other builds, including a white municipal version at $99, a simplified build at $69 and rigged versions at $129. Aimed at urban and municipal visualization, city services presentation, equipment marketing and games. On sale since 2026.
