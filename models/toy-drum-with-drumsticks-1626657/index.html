About This Model

Priced at $29, the Toy Drum with Drumsticks is a finished Toys & Games model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 9,952 polygons and 10,014 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Toy Drum with Drumsticks?

5 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Toy Drum with Drumsticks mesh?

9,952 polygons and 10,014 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the Toy Drum with Drumsticks model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How much does the Toy Drum with Drumsticks 3D model cost?

$29 USD, paid once. The Royalty Free license means no recurring cost and no extra payment when the finished work ships.
