About This Model

A handheld rebar tier wraps wire around crossing steel bars in seconds, and this model shows the reel, trigger and twisting nose clearly. The tool carries 63,775 polygons and 63,629 vertices, most of them in the wire spool housing and the ribbed grip. Its battery pack hangs under the handle, which gives the gun a familiar power tool silhouette on a site table.

StemCell packaging means the PBR materials stay matched whether you open the file in one renderer or another. The UVs do not overlap, so concrete dust, scuffs and a faded logo area are easy to add for site realism.

Pick the generic Rebar Tying gun or the Makita Rebar Tying Tool version, with both selling for $49. The Makita version suits renders where the exact machine of a known maker must appear. Released in 2024, the tool fits construction site scenes, building process animation and safety training about reinforced concrete work. It also pairs naturally with rebar grids, formwork and hard hats in a foundation pour scene.
