About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Tractor with Dump Trailer New is a finished, render-ready model at $149. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and game assets. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

Geometry weighs in at 453,284 polygons over 471,386 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 44 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
