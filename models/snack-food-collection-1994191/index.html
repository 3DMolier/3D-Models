About This Model

The Snack Food Collection fills a pantry shelf, a vending machine or a party table with packaged treats in one file. Chip bags, wrapped bars and boxed crackers give enough variety for a believable convenience store aisle. Every item is a separate object, ready to stack, scatter or line up along a counter.

As a CheckMate Pro bundle, the snacks arrive at real-world size with clean topology and readable names. Crinkled foil bags keep soft creases, while rigid boxes hold crisp corners under strong lighting. That contrast in packaging helps a shelf look realistic instead of repeating the same shape.

Two versions are available, this main pack at $49 and the Snack Food Collection 2 at $69. On sale since 2022, the bundle is cleared for royalty free production. Supermarket renders, office break rooms, movie night scenes and grocery game levels are where it gets used most.
