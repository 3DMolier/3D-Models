About This Model

A pocket-sized hot melt glue gun with its stand folded flat, modeled for craft tables, hobby rooms and DIY tutorials. The body shows the trigger, the nozzle tip and the slot at the back where a glue stick slides in. Folded, the small metal stand tucks under the barrel, so the tool lies naturally on its side on a workbench.

As a CheckMate Pro model, it passed TurboSquid's strictest file review, so naming and real scale are dependable. The plastic housing and metal tip suit two simple materials, glossy and brushed, without complex texture work. A short, chunky barrel separates it from full-size industrial glue guns used in carpentry.

It has been on sale since 2020 and suits craft blog illustrations, school project scenes, toy repair stories and maker spaces. Next to glue sticks, scissors and cardboard, the gun completes a believable handmade crafts corner. Its compact size makes it a natural prop for a child's desk or a scrapbooking session.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2020 · Updated 12 November 2026
