About This Model

This weathered tower clock dial shows a large round face with bold hour markers and ornate hands, aged by long exposure outdoors. It uses 39,022 polygons on 41,364 vertices, so cracks, chips and rust streaks hold up in a close view. The dial sits recessed in a circular frame, ready to set into the wall of a church tower or town hall.

Built to the CheckMate specification, the face has non-overlapping UVs that accept grime masks and moss in the crevices. Peeling paint and a dulled metal rim give the piece its history, and a broken glass pane would add more drama.

Released in 2025, the dial suits horror games, abandoned town scenes and historical dramas set in old city squares. A slow push toward the frozen hands is a classic way to tell viewers that time stopped here. Steampunk environments and fantasy villages can also mount it high on a crumbling facade above a market. Animators often stop the hands at a meaningful hour, letting a single prop carry part of the story.
