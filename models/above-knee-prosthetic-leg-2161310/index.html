About This Model

A modern limb replacement for people with an amputation above the knee, with a socket, mechanical knee joint, pylon and foot. Above Knee Prosthetic Leg holds 34,252 polygons and 17,288 vertices, enough to show the joint linkage and the adapters between parts. The carbon socket at the top curves to cup the residual thigh comfortably.

A clean, non-overlapping unwrap gives each component its own space for carbon weave, anodized aluminum and rubber sole materials. The knee mechanism sits clearly exposed, which helps when explaining how the device bends during walking. CheckMate Pro certification means the model was reviewed for scale and well-formed geometry.

Offered since 2023, the leg suits rehabilitation clinic visuals, medical device presentations and inclusive character design. Show it fitted to an athlete on a track, or standing on a workbench in an orthotics workshop. Patient education materials use it to walk people through each part before their first fitting.
