About This Model

This running trainer carries a sculpted midsole, a layered upper and lacing that reads clearly in close product shots. The PUMA X Pro Flight Trainer Rigged is a shoe modeled so the sole tread and heel counter hold their shape from low angles. Its curved outsole flares slightly at the forefoot, a detail that makes the silhouette read as a performance trainer rather than a casual sneaker.

It passed CheckMate Pro inspection at TurboSquid, which looks at clean topology, real-world scale and tidy naming before a model goes live. Fabric panels and the perforated toe area give a texture artist clear zones for separate colors and materials. A pair can be built by mirroring the mesh, since left and right shoes share the same construction.

The shoe has been available since 2023, with its license limited to Editorial Uses Only. That makes it a fit for sports journalism, documentary inserts, footwear reviews and editorial illustrations of running gear. Designers also set it beside a gym bag or on a locker room bench to dress a training scene.
