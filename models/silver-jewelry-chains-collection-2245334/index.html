About This Model

A collection of silver jewelry chains in different link styles, ready for necklace mockups, jewelry store displays and fashion close-ups. The Silver Jewelry Chains Collection includes several link patterns, so designers can compare chunky and delicate styles side by side. Each chain drapes in a natural curve, the way a necklace lies on velvet in a display case.

The chains follow CheckMate requirements, keeping links clean and parts named for easy selection. A bright polished silver shader suits them, while oxidized versions give a vintage feel. Macro renders reward the link geometry, since each loop catches its own highlight.

Part of the range from 2024, the chains serve jewelry e-commerce mockups, fashion editorials and gift guides. Hang a chain from a bust or coil it beside a ring box, and a jewelry scene is ready. Jewelers can preview pendants on each chain style before producing a real piece for a customer.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
