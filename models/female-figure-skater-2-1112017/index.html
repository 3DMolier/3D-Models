About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Female Figure Skater 2 is a finished, render-ready model at $149. Geometry weighs in at 24,771 polygons over 26,125 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

13 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
