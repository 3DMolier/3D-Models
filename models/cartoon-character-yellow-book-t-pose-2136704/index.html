About This Model

Priced at $39, the Cartoon Character Book is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 16,752 polygons and 8,774 vertices. The count is honest; whether it is heavy is a decision only your target platform can make.

Edge flow follows the form rather than fighting it, which is what lets the silhouette hold up when the camera moves in. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Published in 2023, it has stayed in the range while a lot of other listings came and went. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
