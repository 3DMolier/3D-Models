About This Model

Priced at $119, the Airbus A310 Multi Role Tanker Aircraft Transport Generic is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 242,845 polygons and 252,729 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

21 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog.
