About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Amber Bracelet is a finished, render-ready model at $24. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Counted at the source, the model is 6,912 polygons and 3,456 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
