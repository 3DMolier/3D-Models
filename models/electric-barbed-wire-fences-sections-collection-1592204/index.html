About This Model

Electric Barbed Wire Fences Sections Collection gives you modular fence panels topped with sharp wire, ready to secure prisons, bases and border zones. Steel posts, tensioned strands and ceramic insulators separate an electrified barrier from an ordinary farm fence at a glance. Because the pieces come as sections, a long perimeter can be assembled around any compound by repeating and rotating them.

TurboSquid awarded these sections its CheckMate Pro rating, the stricter of its two inspection levels. Galvanized metal picks up rust and dust convincingly, and warning signs hung on the wire strengthen the sense of danger.

On the shelf since 2020, the set costs $49 and suits military training visuals, security planning renders and prison dramas. Survival and stealth games use electrified fences as obstacles the player must cut, climb or switch off. Documentaries about borders and restricted research sites also rely on this kind of hostile boundary. Floodlights on tall poles behind the wire complete a tense night scene outside a guarded facility.
