About This Model

The exploding head is one of the few emoji that describes a reaction rather than an emotion, which is why it spread so fast. It has no facial expression to interpret; the top of the head is simply gone.

The model is very light at 11,116 polygons and 11,120 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits motion graphics and social content, advertising and marketing visuals, animation, editorial illustration, game assets and interface work. On sale since 2024.
