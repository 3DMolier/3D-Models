About This Model

The Empty Rusty Metal Barrel is a production-ready Containers & Storage 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. You are getting 2,220 polygons and 2,223 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 56.21 x 56.75 x H87.94 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.
