About This Model

The DJ Console with Mixer Pioneer and Purple Lighting is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $79 on TurboSquid. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.

Counted at the source, the model is 545,100 polygons and 563,199 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
