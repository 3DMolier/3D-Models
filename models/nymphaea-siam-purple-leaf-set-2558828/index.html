About This Model

This Nature & Plants model - Nymphaea Siam Purple Leaf Set - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 39,535 polygons and 40,366 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

20 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
