About This Model

This stainless pool rail is the curved pair of tubes swimmers grab when stepping down into the water from the deck. Both rails bend over the coping in a smooth arc and drop vertically into the pool, ending just above the bottom step. Round anchor flanges sit where the tubes meet the deck, which is where the eye checks whether an install looks real.

Polygon counts are not given for this one, but the tubes are simple enough to stay light in a full backyard scene. The rail carries a CheckMate Pro rating, which TurboSquid grants only after a person checks geometry, scale and file structure. Because the shape is a clean extruded profile, it reflects water and sky without the wobbles that cheap tubes often show.

Released in 2022 for $12, the Stair Rail for Pool suits villa renders, hotel spa visualizations and summer scenes in games. Landscape designers add it to pool plans so clients can picture where the entry steps and safe handholds will go. Film crews building a backyard party set in CG can duplicate it on both sides of a wide entry staircase.
