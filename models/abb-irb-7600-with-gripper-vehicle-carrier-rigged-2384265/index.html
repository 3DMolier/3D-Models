About This Model

An ABB IRB 7600 industrial robot fitted with a gripper for lifting car bodies, rigged for automotive factory scenes. Heavy payload robots like this move whole vehicle shells between stations, which makes them centerpieces of modern assembly lines.

The scene uses 569,568 polygons and 586,344 vertices, covering the robot arm, the gripper frame and the carried vehicle. The arm has the chunky links and rear counterweight of a robot designed for very heavy handling. The gripper clamps around the car body at fixed points, so the lift looks secure from every angle.

Rigged joints let animators swing the vehicle through a full transfer cycle between conveyor and workstation. Since 2025 it has appeared in factory automation explainers, plant layout studies and engineering training videos. Overlap-free UVs make it simple to change the robot's paint or the color of the car body. Safety fences and a conveyor line around it turn the robot into a complete robotic cell for a presentation.
