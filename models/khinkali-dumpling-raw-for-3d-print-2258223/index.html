About This Model

A raw Georgian khinkali dumpling with pleated dough gathered into a twisted top knot, ready for food scenes or a printed replica. Khinkali Dumpling Raw for 3D Print stays light at 1,208 polygons and 1,174 vertices while keeping the folds of the pleats readable.

It is prepared for 3D printing, so the shell is closed and a slicer can take it straight to plastic or resin. UVs without overlaps leave space for a floury dough texture, slightly translucent at the thin pleats. Following CheckMate rules, the dumpling keeps real proportions so a plate of them sits at true size on a table.

Listed since 2024, this khinkali suits Georgian restaurant menus, cooking tutorials, food illustrations and kitchen game props. Arranged in rows on a floured board, several instances show the moment before boiling better than a single dumpling. Printed and painted, the dumpling also works as a display piece for a restaurant window.
