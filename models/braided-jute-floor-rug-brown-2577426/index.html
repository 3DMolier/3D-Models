About This Model

The Braided Jute Floor Rug Brown is a flat mat of coiled natural fiber braids that adds warmth to living rooms and entryways. Its 9,898 polygons and 9,944 vertices shape the rows of braid and the soft rounded border. Spread under a coffee table or at a front door, it brings a natural, handmade texture to the floor.

StemCell certification keeps the woven fiber material consistent across the major supported engines. UVs are mixed, so the border has unique space while repeating braid sections share it. Fine fiber detail can be pushed further with a displacement map for close floor shots.

Alongside it come two other versions: an eco jute carpet with raised coil detail and a round braided jute rug, each at $15. The round rug suits a reading nook, while the coil carpet fills a larger lounge floor. Released in 2026, it serves interior visualization, boho home styling, furniture catalogs and real estate renders.
