About This Model

Surface Hub 2 is a detailed Electronics & Gadgets asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. The mesh carries 17,113 polygons and 17,962 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

3 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
