About This Model

A steel fishing hook with an eye, curved bend and sharp barbed point, ready for angling, outdoor and nature scenes. The bend forms a smooth J, and the barb sits just behind the point, as on real hooks. The eye at the top is a closed loop, so a line can be threaded through it in an animation.

Tidy geometry and a well organized file come with its CheckMate Pro certification. Polished steel, bronze or black nickel shaders turn it into different hook styles in a few clicks. Small as it is, the hook can be scaled up for macro shots without losing its shape.

The Fishing Hook 3D Model costs $9 and first appeared in 2015. It suits fishing tackle catalogs, outdoor adventure scenes, lake trips, sport fishing and survival tutorials. Paired with a line, a float and a lure, it completes a tackle box spread on a dock.
