About This Model

A set of ice hockey sticks with long shafts and curved blades, ready to lean against boards, racks and locker room walls. The Hockey Sticks Collection gathers several sticks in one purchase, so a team bench or a store rack fills quickly. The blade curve is what makes a hockey stick recognizable, and each one is shaped with a clear hook.

As a CheckMate Pro set, every stick is built to real scale with tidy names and clean topology. Tape wrapped around the blade and the grip end gives each stick the look of real game use. Different shaft colors make it easy to suggest players from rival teams in one rack.

Since 2015 the collection has had 14 buyers, mostly sports game developers and arena visualizers. Cross two sticks behind a goal for a poster, or prop one beside a pair of skates for a quiet still life. At $24 it is an affordable way to dress a whole hockey scene with authentic equipment.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
