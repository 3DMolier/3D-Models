About This Model

A red snapper from the Gulf of Mexico shown in a feeding motion, set up for Maya users building underwater scenes. The deep red back fading to pink on the belly is the species signature, and the shape carries it well. Spiny dorsal fins and a pointed snout give the fish its profile when it turns toward food.

Real-world scale and orderly naming follow CheckMate specification, so the fish settles into reef sets without tweaking. Caustic light rippling across the scales sells depth better than any fog setting alone. Feeding motions read best with small prey or particles drifting nearby, giving the action a target.

Launched in 2026, the snapper suits aquarium visuals, fisheries education, seafood brand stories and marine documentaries. Pair it with the idle snapper from the same series, and a school can mix calm and feeding behavior. Seafood restaurants and fishing guides also find use for a fish that looks right from every side.
