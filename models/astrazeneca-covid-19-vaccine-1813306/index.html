About This Model

A small glass vaccine vial with a rubber stopper and crimped cap, built for health news graphics and clinic scenes. Just 3,326 polygons cover the vial shoulders, the flip-off seal and the narrow neck under the cap. Because the mesh is so light, a whole tray of vials can fill a pharmacy fridge without strain.

Glass, liquid and the printed label are handled with materials, since no image maps come in the download. Clean UV islands sit apart from one another, making it simple to apply a dosage sticker or batch print. TurboSquid awarded the vial its CheckMate Pro mark after reviewing topology and scene setup.

You get two versions for $19 apiece, the AstraZeneca COVID 19 Vaccine and an EpiVacCorona COVID 19 Vaccine. Listed since 2021, it appears in pandemic explainers, public health videos and medical lab renders. Held between gloved fingers before an injection, the vial becomes a clear symbol of immunization.
