About This Model

An auto mechanic character together with garage equipment, the tools and machines that fill a working repair shop. Combining the worker and the workshop in one set saves assembling a believable service bay from scratch, piece by piece.

With CheckMate Pro approval on every file, the character and each piece of equipment open cleanly in every major package. Oil-stained coveralls, scuffed toolboxes and worn lifts give the scene the grime of daily repair work. Matching scale means the mechanic stands correctly beside lifts, jacks and workbenches without manual adjustment.

Costing $349, the collection dates from 2020 and fits automotive and training projects alike. It suits car service explainers, garage interior visualizations, vocational training material and driving game backdrops. Under a raised car with a work lamp glowing, the mechanic brings human focus to an industrial scene. Wide shots of the full garage also explain how a modern repair shop is organized around its lifts.
