About This Model

Looking for a Model Bundles & Sets asset? The Men Sandals Collection is a finished, render-ready model at $59. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.

The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is catalogued for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.
