About This Model

Assorted Christmas Ball Ornaments Pack fills a tree with shiny baubles in many colors and finishes, ready for festive scenes. Round glass balls with metal caps and hanging loops are the most recognizable decorations of the winter holidays. The pack totals 53,398 polygons and 53,288 vertices, so caps and loops stay crisp in close shots.

Its 16 texture maps at 2048 x 2048 give the ornaments glossy, matte, glitter and patterned surfaces. Recoloring baubles to match any interior palette takes seconds with the clean, separated unwrap. The pack earned CheckMate Pro, TurboSquid's stricter certification for well prepared assets.

Released in 2019 at $39, the ornaments suit Christmas greetings, shop window displays and holiday interior renders. Scatter some in an open gift box or hang them from branches near warm lights for a cozy shot. Holiday games and seasonal animations use baubles as collectibles or tree decorations players can place.
