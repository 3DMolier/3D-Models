About This Model

A fluffy cone of candy floss on a paper stick, the treat sold at fairs, carnivals and seaside promenades. The Cotton Candy uses 6,060 polygons and 8,960 vertices to shape the soft, wispy cloud around the stick. Its airy, irregular outline is what keeps the sweet from looking like a solid ball of foam.

StemCell certification keeps the translucent pink material consistent across renderers, engines and lighting setups. Soft subsurface scattering makes the sugar strands glow when backlit by a sunny sky. It can be held in a character hand, sit in a stand rack or wait on a vendor cart at the gate.

Three versions cost $19 each: this cone, a version on a wooden stick and a rainbow-colored edition. All of them appeared in 2026 and are free to use under Royalty Free terms. Amusement parks, carnival games, children's animations and summer festival scenes are where these sweets belong most naturally.
