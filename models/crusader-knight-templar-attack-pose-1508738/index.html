About This Model

The Crusader Knight Templar Attack Pose is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $139 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 46,338 polygons over 48,582 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 19 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
