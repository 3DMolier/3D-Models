About This Model

Housekeeping Maid Rigged belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $209 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 47,843 polygons and 46,824 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Texture work runs to 17 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
