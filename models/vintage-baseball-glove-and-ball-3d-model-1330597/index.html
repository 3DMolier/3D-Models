About This Model

This Sports & Recreation model - Vintage Baseball Glove and Ball - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $89 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 137,864 polygons and 146,299 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

17 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution.
