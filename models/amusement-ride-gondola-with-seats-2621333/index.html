About This Model

A fairground ride gondola with molded bench seats and safety bars, ready to hang from a wheel or spin on a carousel arm. The Amusement Ride Gondola with Seats spends 305,431 polygons and 306,033 vertices on the curved shell, seating and lap bars. Rounded edges and thick padding follow the look of real theme park rides built for families.

StemCell certification keeps the glossy painted shell consistent across engines and renderers. The open sides let the camera see riders inside, which suits point-of-view shots and crowd scenes. Mounting points on top allow the car to hang from a Ferris wheel or a swinging arm.

It appeared in 2026 at $49, a reasonable cost for a ride car that can be duplicated around a whole wheel. Fairground environments, theme park visualization, music videos and horror scenes in abandoned parks all use pieces like this. Copied around a hub and recolored, the gondola fills out a complete attraction quickly.
