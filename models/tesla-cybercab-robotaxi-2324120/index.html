About This Model

The Cybercab drops the parts a driver would need. No steering wheel, no pedals, two seats, butterfly doors and a body reduced to a wedge with a full-width light bar at each end. That absence is the whole design argument, and the model keeps it: the cabin reads as a lounge rather than a cockpit, which is the thing a robotaxi render has to communicate in one frame.

The mesh is economical at 63,250 polygons and 70,814 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, built to the StemCell specification so it goes into a real-time engine, a configurator or a browser scene without a rebuild.

This page carries four other builds: rigged at $149, simplified at $99, and rigged versions prepared for Maya and for Cinema 4D at $149 each. Take the static model at $99 for stills and background traffic; take a rigged one when the doors open or the car has to drive.
