About This Model

A curling sheet is mostly painted ice: the house with its concentric circles at each end, the hog lines, the centre line and the hacks the players push off from. Those markings under the ice are the whole visual, which is why the surface has to be modelled as a proper sheet rather than as a flat plane.

The model is built with subdivision geometry at 11,988 polygons and 12,553 vertices with non-overlapping UVs, which is the right approach for a venue whose interest is the surface rather than the structure.

At $39 it suits winter sports broadcast graphics, curling and olympic material, venue and event visualization, sports advertising and games. On sale since 2021.
