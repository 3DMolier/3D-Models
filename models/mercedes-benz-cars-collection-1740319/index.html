About This Model

Mercedes-Benz cars from several model lines are gathered in this bundle for street scenes, garages, showrooms and automotive journalism. Each car keeps its signature grille, three-pointed star and flowing body lines, so the brand reads correctly in wide and close shots. Collecting several cars in one purchase fills a parking lot, a showroom or a dealership floor with consistent quality.

The bundle carries CheckMate Lite certification, which confirms the files open properly and match their previews. An Editorial Uses Only license applies, so the cars suit news, reviews, documentaries and illustrative publications. Paint responds best to a layered clear coat shader, while chrome trim, lights and glass need only light adjustment.

Four versions exist: this first collection at $269 and a second static collection at $329. Two rigged collections, the first and the second, cost $349 each for animated driving sequences. On sale since 2021, the cars serve motoring editorials, city visualization, film street scenes and automotive history features.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Which industries use the Mercedes Benz Cars Collection model?

Game Development, Film & Video Production, Architecture and Simulation are the primary industries on this listing. The license, however, is Editorial Uses Only: the model depicts a real branded product, so it may appear in editorial work in those fields, but not in advertising or in products for sale.

Can the Mercedes Benz Cars Collection model be used in a commercial project?

No. The model depicts a real branded product, so TurboSquid lists it under the Editorial Uses Only license. It may be used in news, commentary, education, personal projects and similar editorial contexts, but not in advertising, on merchandise or in any product offered for sale. See our license guide.

How heavy is the Mercedes Benz Cars Collection mesh?

387,780 polygons and 397,658 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the roughly 28,000 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs high at 387,780.

Does the Mercedes Benz Cars Collection model include materials and textures?

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. Where textures are included they are packaged with the download rather than linked externally.
