About This Model

The Kaman K Max K1200 is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $159 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 213,466 polygons and 231,806 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
