About This Model

Parcel delivery by drone has been trialled for over a decade and remains mostly trials, because the hard part was never flying. It is weather, airspace, noise and what happens when a package has to be left somewhere nobody is standing.

The model is built at subdivision level, 42,872 polygons and 44,298 vertices with non-overlapping UVs, so it holds up close. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $59 it suits logistics and future mobility visualization, technology presentation, news and documentary graphics, advertising, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.

No. The model depicts a real branded product, so TurboSquid lists it under the Editorial Uses Only license. It may be used in news, commentary, education, personal projects and similar editorial contexts, but not in advertising, on merchandise or in any product offered for sale.

Can the DHL Quadcopter Drone model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What is the DHL Quadcopter Drone model typically used for?

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

Is the DHL Quadcopter Drone model quality-checked?
