About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Ghost Phantom is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 5,130 polygons and 5,221 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Ghost Phantom?

5 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Ghost Phantom mesh?

5,130 polygons and 5,221 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the Ghost Phantom model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How much does the Ghost Phantom 3D model cost?

$39 USD, paid once. The Royalty Free license means no recurring cost and no extra payment when the finished work ships.
