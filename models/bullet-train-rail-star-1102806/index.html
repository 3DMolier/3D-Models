About This Model

Bullet Train Rail Star is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.

Geometry weighs in at 7,746,725 polygons over 7,908,428 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 24 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
