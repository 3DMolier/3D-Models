About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Opabinia is a finished, render-ready model at $69. The mesh carries 14,748 polygons and 7,486 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
