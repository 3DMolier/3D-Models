About This Model

A slender air-launched cruise missile with folding wings and a tail engine, the type carried by strategic bombers. Its 20,262 polygons and 10,340 vertices shape the long cylindrical body, the deployable wings and the ventral intake. The clean torpedo-like outline works at a distance under a wing pylon or close up on a loading trolley.

UVs are laid out without overlap, leaving room for stenciled warnings, panel seams and gray military paint. The missile passed CheckMate Pro testing, so the file is organized and opens without broken references.

Three versions are provided at $24: the Air-Launched Cruise Missile, plus rigged editions prepared for Cinema 4D and for Maya. The rigged editions make wing deployment and launch sequences far quicker to animate. Available since 2024, the model serves defense documentaries, military simulations, aviation history projects and museum exhibit planning. Its plain shape also suits classroom explanations of how guided weapons fly low over terrain to avoid radar.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2024 · Updated 13 November 2026
