About This Model

Halloween Vamp Paper Decorations is a detailed Signage & Decor asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 47,918 polygons and 49,301 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.
