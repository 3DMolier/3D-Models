About This Model

The statue works because of its posture: arms straight out, head slightly bowed, standing on a plinth on top of a mountain so it is almost always seen from below and against sky. The model keeps that stance and the blocky soapstone tiling of the surface, which is the detail that makes the figure read as built rather than cast.

The mesh is deliberately light at 10,550 polygons and 10,985 vertices in quads and tris, but it ships with 6 texture maps at 8192x8192. That trade is the right one here: almost all of the statue's character lives in the mosaic surface and the weathering, not in the silhouette, so the detail belongs in the texture.

At $99 it suits travel and tourism material, city and landmark visualization, film and television establishing shots, games and print. On sale since 2020.
