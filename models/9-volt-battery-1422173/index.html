About This Model

This Electronics & Gadgets model - 9-Volt Battery - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 2,170 polygons and 2,260 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 7 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 3072 x 3072. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
