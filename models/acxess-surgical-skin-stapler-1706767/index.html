About This Model

A disposable surgical skin stapler with a pistol-style grip, a trigger lever and a narrow head that closes wounds. The 8,963 polygons and 9,092 vertices shape the ergonomic handle, the trigger pivot and the transparent staple window. Held over a sutured incision, the tool shows the moment of wound closure in a clear way.

There are no bundled texture maps, so the white and blue plastic is set up with simple shaders. The UVs are separated without overlap, which leaves room for a brand print or sterile packaging labels. A CheckMate Pro certificate confirms the device is built to scale with tidy geometry.

The page holds two $29 versions, this branded design and a generic Surgical Stapler. Since 2021, 12 medtech and training clients have used it for procedure animations and product sheets. In an operating room set, it lies naturally on an instrument table beside other disposable tools.
