About This Model

Pet Dry Food Dispenser belongs to our Food & Beverages range and is offered at $18 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

Geometry weighs in at 109,298 polygons over 55,374 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
