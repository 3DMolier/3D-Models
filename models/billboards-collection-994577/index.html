About This Model

This billboards collection bundles several roadside and street display formats, each with a flat face ready for your own artwork. The set covers large hoardings on steel poles, backlit panels and freestanding units, so a whole street can be dressed consistently.

Geometry mixes quads and triangles, and the UVs are non-overlapping, which makes swapping poster images on the display faces straightforward. CheckMate Pro certification covers scale, topology and naming, so every frame keeps the proportions a real sign would have. Support structures and frame edges add the detail that sells a billboard when the camera looks up.

On sale since 2016, the collection serves city visualization, out-of-home ad mockups, film sets and urban game levels. Designers can preview campaign artwork in context before printing, testing how it reads from passing cars. Placed along a highway or above a rooftop, a lit panel adds night-time color and a sense of busy commerce to the frame. Mixing several formats in a shot avoids the repetition that gives away a scene built from a single asset.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2016 · Updated 13 November 2026
