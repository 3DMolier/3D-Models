About This Model

This straight conveyor section is built to be copied end to end, so a factory line of any length takes minutes to assemble. Conveyor Belt Straight spends 78,556 polygons and 40,086 vertices on the rubber belt, rollers, side guards, bolts and drive housing. Steel legs hold the belt at working height, which leaves room under the frame for cables, bins or a forklift path.

Topology mixes quads with triangles around the roller ends and bolt heads, where round shapes meet flat plates. CheckMate Pro certification confirms real-world scale, and the overlap-free unwrap accepts metal and rubber materials without stretching. Sliding a texture along the belt surface is a quick trick to make parcels appear to move.

There is one version for $39, released in 2023 for warehouse, packaging and logistics scenes. Add cardboard boxes and a few workers along the guards, and the module becomes a busy sorting center. Several units in a row also read well in factory simulations and training explainers about production flow.
