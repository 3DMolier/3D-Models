About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Military Amplifier is a finished, render-ready model at $39. You are getting 19,739 polygons and 19,284 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
