About This Model

A chubby cartoon snowman built from stacked snowballs, wearing a top hat and a cheerful face, made for holiday animation. Only 7,134 polygons and 6,362 vertices are used, so several copies can fill a winter yard without slowing the viewport. The rounded body sections read clearly from any angle, which helps when the figure turns toward the camera to greet someone.

Six texture maps ship with it, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 1000 x 1000, enough for close shots of the packed snow. Clean UV islands let you repaint the hat or tint the snow without redoing the unwrap. The snowman carries the CheckMate Pro mark, so naming, scale and topology have already passed a strict marketplace inspection.

You can choose between three builds here: the base model plus rigs prepared separately for Maya and for Cinema 4D. Picking the package-specific rig skips conversion, and the controls open where an animator expects them in that software. On sale since 2019, the snowman goes into Christmas greeting animations, children's game levels and seasonal storefront scenes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2019 · Updated 28 October 2026
