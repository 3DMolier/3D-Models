About This Model

Hands flipping quickly through the pages of a book in search of something, animated in Maya for research and detective scenes. The thumb lifts each corner while the other hand holds the spine, the restless motion of someone hunting a reference. Pages bend and settle realistically, which sells the urgency of the search even without showing the reader's face.

Controls for fingers and pages live natively in Maya, so the rhythm of the search can be sped up or slowed. Short shirt cuffs frame the wrists, keeping the hands neutral enough for any character or point of view camera. Naming and scale were prepared under CheckMate guidance, so the book and hands open in a tidy, predictable hierarchy.

Released in 2026, the clip fits detective stories, exam preparation intros and busy library scenes in educational video series. Late night lamp light and a cluttered desk around the book underline the feeling of a frantic search.
