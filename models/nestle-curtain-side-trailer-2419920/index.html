About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Nestle Curtain Side Trailer is a finished, render-ready model at $69. The mesh carries 237,502 polygons and 240,926 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
