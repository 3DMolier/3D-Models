About This Model

Sewing Thimbles Bronze Set belongs to our Signage & Decor range and is offered at $24 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 8,124 polygons and 4,068 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
