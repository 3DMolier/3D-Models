About This Model

A collection of old wooden cases and crates, with rope handles, iron corners and lids that have been opened many times. Mixed sizes let the boxes stack into convincing piles in holds, cellars and dusty attics. Scratched boards and darkened edges show they have been shipped, dropped and reused for years.

CheckMate Pro inspection confirmed the scale and topology of every case and crate in the set. Lids come off each case, so bottles, maps or loot can be placed inside. Stencils and shipping marks can be added with a decal pass for a specific era or route.

On the market since 2022, the set suits pirate games, warehouse scenes, period films and treasure hunts. A stack of old cases in a ship's hold is shorthand for adventure, smuggling or a long voyage. Antique shop interiors and rustic decor renders can use single cases as coffee tables or shelves.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
