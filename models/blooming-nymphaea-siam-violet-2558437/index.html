About This Model

Blooming Nymphaea Siam Violet is one of the Nature & Plants models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $29. Geometry weighs in at 12,985 polygons over 13,274 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. 5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry — no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
