About This Model

Toyota 4Runner TRD Pro Army Green 2021 is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game environments. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.
