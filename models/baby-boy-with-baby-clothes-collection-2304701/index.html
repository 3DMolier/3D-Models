About This Model

A baby boy figure paired with a small wardrobe of infant clothing, handy for nursery interiors and family scenes. The outfits can sit folded on a shelf or dress the child, so one purchase fills a whole room.

The garments include bodysuits, rompers and soft hats, each with stitched seams and loose folds that look worn. The infant keeps true baby proportions, with chubby wrists, a round belly and a large head on a short neck. Scaled-down toddler meshes often get those proportions wrong, and buyers notice the difference immediately in close shots.

Every clothing piece comes as its own object, so the same baby can change outfits from one shot to the next. Parenting apps, baby store interiors and educational cartoons have drawn on this set since it appeared in 2024. Pastel fabrics respond nicely to soft window light, which is the usual mood for a nursery scene. A crib or changing table from another kit completes the room around the figure in minutes.
