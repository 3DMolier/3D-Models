About This Model

Smart Home Weather Display Panel is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $24. You are getting 17,068 polygons and 10,720 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
