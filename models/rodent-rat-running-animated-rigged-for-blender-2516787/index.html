About This Model

A common rat sprinting in a looped run cycle, rigged in Blender for sewer scenes, horror shots and pest control explainers. The body is low and stretched, with a pointed snout, rounded ears and the long bare tail that makes a rat instantly readable. The run cycle loops cleanly, so a single rat can cross a whole alley or several can scatter across a floor.

Blender rig controls let you change stride speed, curl the tail or adjust the head to look toward a sound. Following CheckMate specification, the scene comes organized, which saves time when dozens of instances fill a swarm. The coat and skin read well under low, raking light, which is exactly how rats tend to appear on screen.

Released in 2026, the animal is a natural fit for game creatures, horror film inserts and urban decay environments. Pest control companies and science lessons also need a believable rodent moving at speed rather than a static figure. A quick dash past the camera adds unease to a basement or subway scene without demanding a heavy character.
