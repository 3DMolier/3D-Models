About This Model

Priced at $890, the US Aircraft Carriers Collection is a finished Model Bundles & Sets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
