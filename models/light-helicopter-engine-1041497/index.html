About This Model

Light Helicopter Engine is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $69. Counted at the source, the model is 78,161 polygons and 81,330 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

26 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and aerospace visualization. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
