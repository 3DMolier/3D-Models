About This Model

This Signage & Decor model - Closed Old Book - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 5,486 polygons and 2,792 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
