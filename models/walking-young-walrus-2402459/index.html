About This Model

Walking Young Walrus belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 23,528 polygons and 24,459 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
