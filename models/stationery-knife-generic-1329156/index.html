About This Model

Stationery Knife Generic belongs to our Tools range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 18,519 polygons and 18,555 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
