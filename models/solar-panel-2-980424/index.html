About This Model

Looking for a Space & Sci-Fi asset? The Solar Panel 2 is a finished, render-ready model at $9. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

The mesh carries 15,966 polygons and 16,142 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 2 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
