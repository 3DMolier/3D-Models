About This Model

A collection of paper euro banknotes in different denominations, each note sized and colored like the bills used across the eurozone. Notes can be fanned out, stacked or laid flat, covering the common ways money appears in a shot. The distinct color of each denomination helps viewers read values instantly, even when the printing is too small to see.

TurboSquid certified the collection at CheckMate Pro, so the notes open as clean, separate objects. Thin geometry keeps each bill light, which matters when hundreds are scattered across a table or a vault floor. A slight bend applied in your software makes the paper look handled rather than perfectly flat.

In the catalog since 2019, the banknotes support finance news graphics, economics lessons and crime dramas involving cash. Spread across a desk next to a calculator, they illustrate a budget story in a single frame. Falling bills in a slow motion animation remain a classic way to show sudden wealth or a lottery win.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
