About This Model

A set of astronaut gloves for spacesuits, with thick padded fingers and locking wrist rings, ready for space mission scenes. The Space Gloves Collection offers several glove styles, so characters can match suits from training gear to full spacewalk equipment.

Bulky layered fabric and reinforced fingertips show how hard it is to grip tools in the vacuum of space. Metal wrist connectors give each glove a clear attachment point for joining a matching suit sleeve. The bundle follows CheckMate guidance, with real-world scale and readable object names throughout.

Brought to the catalog in 2026, the glove bundle costs $49 and suits sci-fi games, space documentaries and museum displays. Show a gloved hand turning a valve on a station control panel, and the scene feels authentic at once. Educators can use the gloves to explain how astronauts protect their hands during long spacewalks outside. Clean white fabric accepts mission patches, dirt or scorch marks for different story moments.
