About This Model

A hardboard clipboard with a metal spring clip, built for offices, safety inspections, hospital rounds and coaching sidelines. The clip takes much of the 18,982 polygons and 18,989 vertices, since its spring and hinge show clearly up close. The flat board is easy to fill with a printed checklist, a chart or a signed form.

The 2 maps at 2048 x 2048 cover the pressed board surface and the metal of the clip. Separate UV islands make it simple to drop a fresh paper sheet onto the board for each scene. Clean topology approved by CheckMate Pro keeps the clipboard lying flat on a desk without glitches.

Both versions are $12, this Clipboard 2 and the Recycled Wood Clipboard, which has a matching polygon count. The recycled board suits eco themes, while the hardboard one fits a classic office or clinic. Since 2015 it has rested under the arm of a foreman, a nurse or a quiz show host.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2015 · Updated 27 November 2026
