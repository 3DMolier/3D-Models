About This Model

Priced at $59, the Great Blue Shark is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 14,630 polygons and 14,733 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
