About This Model

For twenty years the Crown Victoria was the American police car, and its silhouette is burned into every viewer's memory: a big rear-drive sedan with a long bonnet, a squared roof and a light bar across it. Any contemporary saloon dressed in police livery reads as wrong to an audience that grew up on this shape.

The mesh is 153,347 polygons and 166,853 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 8 texture maps at a mix of sizes up to 4096x4096, which carries the department markings and the reflective striping.

This page is unusually deep: rigged builds at $149, including versions prepared for Cinema 4D and for Maya, a generic police car at $149 and full-interior builds at $129. This simple-interior model at $99 suits urban and law enforcement visualisation, film and television, and games. On sale since 2016.
