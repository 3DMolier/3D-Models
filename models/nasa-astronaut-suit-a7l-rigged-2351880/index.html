About This Model

NASA Astronaut Suit A7L is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $179. You are getting 37,407 polygons and 38,174 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
