About This Model

A chess set for three players on a hexagonal board, with three armies facing each other across curved rows of squares. The 181,406 polygons go mostly into the turned pieces, so crowns, mitres and horse heads stay smooth in macro shots. There are 174,223 vertices, a little fewer than faces, which suits closed pieces sharing edges along their bases.

CheckMate Pro inspection approved the board and pieces, covering topology and proportions. Separate UV space for every army lets you choose ivory, ebony and a third wood or stone tone. Each piece can be moved by hand, so any opening or famous three-way position can be staged.

Dating from 2023 and priced at $31, the board fits game café interiors, puzzle channel graphics and tabletop simulators. Three-player chess still surprises most viewers, which makes it a great conversation piece in a living room render. A low camera across the board shows how the bent ranks meet in the middle.
