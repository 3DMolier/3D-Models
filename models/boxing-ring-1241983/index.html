About This Model

Looking for a Sports & Recreation asset? The Boxing Ring is a finished, render-ready model at $79. You are getting 72,820 polygons and 74,646 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Treat the figure as a planning number: it behaves one way in a single render and another in a level with fifty instances.

The texture set counts 7 maps, delivered at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects sit at the origin with clean transforms, so duplicating the asset across an environment costs nothing extra.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
