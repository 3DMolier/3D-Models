About This Model

This Ships model - Armas High-Speed Transport Ferry Red Rigged - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $179 on TurboSquid. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
