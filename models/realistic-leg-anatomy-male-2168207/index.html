About This Model

The leg carries the whole body, and its anatomy shows it: the largest muscles in the human body sit here, layered around bones built for load rather than dexterity. Stripped of skin, the difference between the thigh and the calf becomes an argument about function rather than shape.

The model holds 64,743 polygons and 65,022 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, showing the muscular system over the bone structure of the leg and foot. It carries StemCell certification.

At $199 it suits medical education and patient explanation, physiotherapy and sports science, anatomy reference for artists, and character work that needs correct underlying structure. On sale since 2024.
