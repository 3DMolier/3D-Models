About This Model

Military Ship Mast is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $69. Geometry weighs in at 27,136 polygons over 29,373 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and defense simulation as the intended applications.

Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
