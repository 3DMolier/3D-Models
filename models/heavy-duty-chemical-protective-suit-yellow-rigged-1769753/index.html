About This Model

Priced at $169, the Heavy Duty Chemical Protective Suit Yellow Rigged is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

At 57,922 polygons and 59,902 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
