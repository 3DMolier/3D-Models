About This Model

Priced at $59, the Atlantic Salmon Fish is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 3,930 polygons and 3,900 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer.
