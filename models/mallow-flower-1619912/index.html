About This Model

The Mallow Flower is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 11,905 polygons and 10,839 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
