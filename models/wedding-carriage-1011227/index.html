About This Model

Wedding Carriage is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. The mesh carries 135,623 polygons and 144,787 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 10 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048.
