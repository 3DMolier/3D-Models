About This Model

Pirate with Vintage Beer Mug belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 45,387 polygons and 55,993 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
