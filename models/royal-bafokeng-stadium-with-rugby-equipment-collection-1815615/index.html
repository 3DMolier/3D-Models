About This Model

This collection pairs the Royal Bafokeng Stadium in South Africa with rugby equipment, ready for sports broadcasts and match visuals. The bowl of stands, the pitch and the floodlight towers create the setting, while posts and balls fill the field. Having venue and equipment together avoids hunting for matching props when building a full match day scene.

The bundle conforms to CheckMate specification, so TurboSquid checked its geometry, naming and scene organization. Pitch markings, seat colors and signage can be retextured to match a specific team, tournament or fictional league.

Listed since 2021, the set suits rugby broadcasts, sports games, stadium planning presentations and event visualization. A low camera across the grass toward the posts gives the classic kickoff composition fans recognize. Floodlit night matches look particularly striking, with the pitch glowing under the towers against a dark sky. The stadium has also hosted football matches, so the same venue can serve other field sports with small changes.
