About This Model

A fluffy pink cloud of cotton candy in a crisp waffle cone, ready for fairground scenes and dessert renders. The 27,414 polygons build the soft candy volume and the waffle grid on the cone. The candy rises well above the rim, wider at the top like a freshly spun serving.

The CheckMate build keeps 30,206 vertices in separate candy and cone parts. A subsurface shader on the candy and a warm baked tone on the cone work best over the mixed UVs. Cone and candy can take different colors, so blue or rainbow variants take minutes.

Costing $29 and new this 2026 season, the treat suits amusement parks, children's party scenes and sweet shop windows. It measures 9.93 x 9.93 x H21.65 cm, a comfortable handheld portion. Put it in a character's hand at a carnival and backlight it so the sugar fibers glow softly.
