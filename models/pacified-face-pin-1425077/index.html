About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Pacified Face Pin is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 480 polygons and 496 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 4 maps at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
