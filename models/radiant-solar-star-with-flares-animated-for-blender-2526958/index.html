About This Model

A blazing star with churning plasma on its surface and solar flares arcing from the edge, animated for Blender. Bright granules and darker spots drift across the sphere, so the surface never looks frozen. Loops of glowing gas rise and fall along the limb, the most striking part of the effect.

The scene follows CheckMate organization, with the star body and flare elements kept apart for easy timing changes. Emission-driven shading lights nearby planets or spacecraft, so the sun can act as the main light source of a shot. Color ranges from white-hot center to deep orange at the rim, which reads well on a black sky.

The Radiant Solar Star with Flares Animated For Blender became available to buyers in 2026. Astronomy lessons, space documentaries and science fiction openings rely on a living sun to set the scale of a universe. Framed behind a planet at the start of a sequence, the flares create an unforgettable sunrise.
