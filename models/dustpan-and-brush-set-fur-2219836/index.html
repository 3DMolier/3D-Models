About This Model

A matching dustpan and hand brush, with the brush bristles built from fur fibers so they look soft and splayed. The pair totals 10,226 polygons and 10,880 vertices, keeping the pan lip thin and the handles comfortable in shape. The brush rests inside the pan, the way the set usually sits under a kitchen sink.

Its construction respects CheckMate rules, so pivots and naming allow separating the brush for a sweeping animation. Non-overlapping UVs accept colored plastic, a molded logo and dust on the flat edge of the pan. Fur bristles catch rim light convincingly, which gives close shots far more realism than flat textured strips.

Ready since 2024, the set fits cleaning product advertising, home organization guides and household interior scenes. Animators use it in comedic sequences where a character sweeps up a broken plate. Stand it against a wall beside a mop bucket and the utility corner looks complete.
