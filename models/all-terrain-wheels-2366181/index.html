About This Model

All Terrain Wheels is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. The mesh carries 20,972 polygons and 22,517 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
