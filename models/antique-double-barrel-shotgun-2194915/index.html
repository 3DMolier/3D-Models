About This Model

An old side-by-side shotgun with a carved wooden stock, exposed hammers and long twin barrels, recalling hunting lodges of past centuries. The side-by-side barrels and the curved trigger guard give the gun its unmistakable profile in a gun rack or on a wall. Such a weapon was loaded by breaking it open at the hinge, a gesture that animators love to show.

TurboSquid rated the shotgun CheckMate Pro, its grade for clean geometry and a file that opens complete. Walnut grain, engraved steel plates and worn bluing can all be tuned in the materials to show age. Close shots of the hammers and lock plate reward the viewer with the mechanical detail of an antique firearm.

Released in 2024, the gun suits western films, historical games and hunting cabin interiors in architectural scenes. Hung above a stone fireplace, it gives a country house or mountain lodge a strong sense of history. Museum designers also place it in exhibits about how sporting guns looked before modern repeating weapons appeared.
