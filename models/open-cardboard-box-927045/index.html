About This Model

An empty corrugated cardboard box with its top flaps folded open, ready for moving day, deliveries and storage room scenes. The Open Cardboard Box shows the thin board edges and the creased flap folds, so it reads as real packaging up close. Its open top invites the camera to look inside, which makes it useful for unpacking and gift reveal shots.

Built under CheckMate Pro requirements, the box has true proportions, clean geometry and clear naming. Brown kraft tones and a strip of packing tape give it the familiar look of a shipped parcel. Stacked several high in a hallway, the boxes say that someone is moving in or out.

Since its 2015 debut, the box has been taken 14 times by logistics firms, interior visualizers and game artists. Fill it with packing peanuts, books or a curious cat, and a simple scene gets a story.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
