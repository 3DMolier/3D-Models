About This Model

A slim, silver gravity bomb with a pointed nose and a guided tail kit with steerable fins. B61-12 Nuclear Bomb is built from 39,215 polygons over 41,615 vertices, shaping access panels, lugs and the fin hinges. Its long, smooth body is the shape fighter jets carry under their wings, which makes it read at once as an air weapon.

Bare metal, stenciled warnings and panel lines are carried by 4 maps at 4096 x 4096. Because the UVs are separate, serial numbers and weathering can be painted for a museum display or a test range. TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro status confirms the file was hand-checked for structure and scale.

Two versions cost $39 each: this guided bomb and a B61 Silver Bullet Fusion Bomb with a polished silver finish. Since 2018 defense analysts, history channels and strategy game makers have purchased it 21 times. Hanging in a weapons bay or resting on a loading cart, it gives a Cold War or modern deterrence story real weight.
