About This Model

This Industrial Equipment model - Duck House Floating Hexagonal - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $24 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 118,426 polygons and 59,541 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.
