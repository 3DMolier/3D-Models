About This Model

A collection of rigged and animated gorillas with groomed fur, built for jungle documentaries, zoo scenes and adventure games. Great apes are hard to build convincingly, and a ready set saves weeks of grooming and rig work.

The silverback shows a massive chest, long arms and the pale saddle of hair across the back. Long arms reach the ground, so a gorilla stands on its knuckles in the posture known from wildlife films. Fur thickens around the shoulders and thins on the face and palms, matching how real gorillas look up close. Expressive brows and deep set eyes give each face the thoughtful look that makes gorillas so compelling on camera.

Released in 2024, the pack fits rainforest conservation videos, zoo planning renders and jungle levels in games. Rigs let animators pose the group as a family troop resting in a forest clearing. Dappled light through the canopy brings out the texture of the dark fur beautifully.
