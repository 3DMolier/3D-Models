About This Model

This Female Doctor in Scrubs with Stethoscope and Mask Fur is a hospital figure wearing scrubs, a mask and a stethoscope. A light 11,340 polygons and 11,557 vertices keep the character efficient, while groomed fur gives the hair a soft look. A calm, upright pose suits waiting rooms, ward corridors and consultation scenes alike. Mask and stethoscope make the profession readable even at a distance in a busy scene.

Clean topology to CheckMate specification comes with non-overlapping UVs, so scrub colors and badges are easy to change. Low polygon counts make it practical to fill a hospital ward with several staff figures. Scrubs fall in simple folds that render quickly without heavy cloth detail.
