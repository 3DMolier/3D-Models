About This Model

A digital food thermometer pushed into a burger patty, it shows the safe way to check meat on a grill or in a pan. The 10,538 polygons and 10,732 vertices describe the probe, the display housing and the textured patty surface. The probe enters at an angle into the center, exactly where a cook would test the doneness of beef.

Four texture maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 provide the seared meat, the plastic body and the readout. A non-overlapping unwrap holds them cleanly, so grill marks and display digits stay sharp in tight shots. TurboSquid placed it at CheckMate Pro after reviewing its geometry, textures and naming manually.

Dating from 2018, it supports cooking tutorials, food safety lessons and barbecue product catalogs for kitchen stores. Chefs explaining temperature rules use renders of it in videos about grilling at home. Cooking games also show it as a tool that tells players when a burger is ready to serve.
