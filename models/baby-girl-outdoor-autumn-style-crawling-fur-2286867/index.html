About This Model

A baby girl in autumn outerwear crawling on hands and knees, curious and busy, for family, home and park scenes. The figure uses just 8,611 polygons, with fur giving the knitted hat and jacket trim their soft texture. Crawling babies look natural only with weight on the palms, and this pose keeps that balance believable.

There are 9,068 vertices on a non-overlapping layout, so the outfit can be recolored without seams appearing. CheckMate specification holds the scale true, so she sits correctly on picnic blankets, floors and grass. Her raised head looking forward gives the pose purpose, as if she is heading straight toward a toy or a parent.

She has been offered since 2024 for $189 with a royalty free license. Baby product imagery, parenting blogs, insurance visuals and children's game scenes use crawling babies widely. Placed on a leaf-covered lawn with a parent kneeling nearby, she adds warmth to any autumn story.
