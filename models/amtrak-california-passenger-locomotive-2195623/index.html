About This Model

A passenger diesel locomotive at 2,089,810 polygons: the carbody, the nose, the walkways, the trucks, the brake rigging and the roof equipment. At this weight the camera can travel along the body and find real detail everywhere, which is what a hero rail shot needs.

CheckMate Pro certification means real-world scale and clean topology across two million polygons, which is what lets coaches and platforms from other assets line up.

Three versions share the page: an Amtrak California build, a Santa Fe EMD F7 and a vintage diesel. Branded liveries carry editorial terms. Priced at $99 sold twice since 2024, into rail presentation, film and documentary work and simulation.
