About This Model

The Mechanical Handheld Tally Counter is a production-ready 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 19,765 polygons over 19,861 vertices - a light build for its class. Texture work runs to 6 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
