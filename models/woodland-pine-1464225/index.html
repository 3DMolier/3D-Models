About This Model

The Woodland Pine is a production-ready Signage & Decor 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags event visualization, product rendering and architectural visualization as the intended applications. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 1,290,290 polygons and 4,851,215 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 20 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
