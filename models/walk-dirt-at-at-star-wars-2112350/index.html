About This Model

This AT-AT walker strides across dusty ground on four towering legs, its boxy armored head lowered toward the battlefield. Dirt and grime cover the lower legs and feet, as if the machine has just marched through a muddy plain. The slow, heavy gait of these walkers makes them one of the most recognizable shapes in science fiction.

The model passed CheckMate Lite, so files open cleanly and the scale holds against other vehicles. Armor plating, joint pistons and side hatches give the giant body plenty of surface detail for close passes. Weathered gray paint with darker dirt streaks keeps the walker grounded in a lived-in setting.

Offered from 2023, the walker serves fan films, sci-fi game levels and toy-scale dioramas. Shot from a low angle against snow or desert, it towers over infantry and creates instant scale. Personal renders and fan projects are covered by a Royalty Free license.

How heavy is the Walk Dirt AT-AT Star Wars mesh?
