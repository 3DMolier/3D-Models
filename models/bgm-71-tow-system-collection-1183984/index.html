About This Model

A collection built around the BGM-71 TOW anti-tank missile system, with the tripod launcher, the sight unit and the missile tubes. The parts can be assembled into a firing position or laid out separately, as they would be during setup by a crew. A carry handle on the launch tube shows how the system was moved by hand.

CheckMate Pro inspection was completed for the whole set, so every file opens cleanly and to scale. The launch tube, guidance set and tripod legs read as separate objects, which helps when animating a firing sequence. Olive drab surfaces, stenciled markings and the wire spool at the rear give it the look of a field weapon.

Priced at $149 the collection appeared in 2017 and counts 13 buyers among defense trainers, military historians and war game studios. Set up behind a low wall with a soldier at the sight, it describes infantry anti-armor tactics in a single image. It is also handy for museum displays explaining Cold War era guided weapons.
