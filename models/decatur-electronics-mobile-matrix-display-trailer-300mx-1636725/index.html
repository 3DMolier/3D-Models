About This Model

A towable message board trailer with a large matrix display panel, used by police and road crews to warn drivers. The Decatur Electronics Mobile Matrix Display Trailer 300MX sits on a compact trailer chassis with a raised display mast. The display faces traffic at eye height, so the panel reads clearly from an approaching car.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms correct scale and an organized scene, so the display can be animated separately. An emissive texture on the panel can show speed readings, detour arrows or custom safety messages. Reflective striping along the trailer body makes it stand out under headlights at night.

On offer since 2020, the trailer suits road work scenes, traffic safety training and police operations in games. Park it on a highway shoulder with cones and flashing lights, and a lane closure becomes believable. City planners can use it to illustrate temporary traffic measures in street renders and community presentations.
