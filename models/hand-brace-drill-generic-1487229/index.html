The Hand Brace Drill Generic is a production-ready Tools 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. You are getting 10,381 polygons and 10,685 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
