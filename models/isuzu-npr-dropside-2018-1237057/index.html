About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Isuzu NPR Dropside 2018 is a finished, render-ready model at $129. The mesh carries 264,405 polygons and 275,293 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
