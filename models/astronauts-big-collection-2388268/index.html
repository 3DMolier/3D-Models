About This Model

Spacesuited figures fill this big collection, covering astronauts for launch pads, space stations and lunar surface scenes. Astronauts Big Collection saves a production from sourcing each suit type separately when a crew has to appear together. Helmets, backpacks and suit folds are modeled to read clearly in both wide shots and close-ups.

Scale, naming and file layout stay the same across all figures, so a crew lineup needs no manual correction. Uniform proportions mean several astronauts can stand in one airlock without any figure looking oddly sized. Reflective visors, fabric wrinkles at the elbows and chunky boot soles are the details that read first under hard light.

Introduced in 2025 for $989, the collection works for commercial and educational projects, from museum films to mobile games. Space documentaries, planetarium shows, science textbooks and sci-fi games are typical destinations. Hard sunlight with deep black shadows sells the vacuum of space better than any soft fill light.
