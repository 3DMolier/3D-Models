About This Model

An Achillon tendon suture guide, the surgical instrument used to repair a ruptured Achilles tendon through a small incision. Its parallel arms and guide holes let the surgeon pass sutures through the tendon ends without opening the leg widely. The unusual shape stands apart from ordinary clamps, which helps viewers understand it is a specialist orthopedic tool.

Modeled to CheckMate specification, the guide keeps true instrument scale for surgical tray layouts and hand animations. Its handle sits away from the incision, so a gloved grip does not block the camera's view of the tendon. Polished stainless steel surfaces suit a cool, clinical lighting setup typical of operating room renders.

The Achillon Tendon Suture Guide became available in 2025 for orthopedic education, surgical training and medical device visuals. Surgeons explaining minimally invasive repair use animations of the guide inserted around the tendon. Sports medicine content about Achilles injuries also benefits from a clear look at how the repair is performed.
