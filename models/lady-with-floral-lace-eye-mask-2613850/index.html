About This Model

A female head wears a delicate floral lace eye mask, ready for masquerade, carnival and costume scenes. The Lady with Floral Lace Eye Mask uses 19,645 polygons and 20,037 vertices, with much of the detail spent on the lace cutouts. Ribbon ties at the temples hold the mask in place and frame the face from the side.

It measures 36.22 x 27.52 x H43.33 cm, matching a real head and neck on display. CheckMate certification ensures tidy topology and accurate scale for close portrait framing. The open lace pattern lets skin show through, which reads beautifully with soft rim lighting from behind.

This single version was released in 2026 and is priced at $69 for portrait work. Masquerade balls, Venetian carnival films, fashion lookbooks and mystery games use it for elegant, secretive and disguised characters. Swap the lace color to match a ball gown or an evening theme without touching any geometry.
