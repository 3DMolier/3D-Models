About This Model

Crossover characters are a genre of their own: a figure from one world dressed for another, so that the armour becomes a jacket and the sword becomes something with a power cell. This is a white haired swordsman rebuilt for a neon lit city rather than a forest road.

The character holds 164,436 polygons and 87,748 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. The library also carries a T pose build, a walking pose and a rigged version, and it is built to CheckMate specification.

At $139 it suits game characters, concept and fan art, animation and previsualisation, science fiction illustration and editorial work. On sale since 2024.

Can the Cyberpunk 2077 Geralt Character model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free licence allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

Can the Cyberpunk 2077 Geralt Character model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What is the Cyberpunk 2077 Geralt Character model typically used for?

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How clean is the geometry on the Cyberpunk 2077 Geralt Character model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.
