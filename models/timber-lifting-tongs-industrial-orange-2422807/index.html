About This Model

Priced at $24, the Timber Lifting Tongs Industrial is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 14,938 polygons and 15,332 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 47.22 x 13.73 x H86.11 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and software demos. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
