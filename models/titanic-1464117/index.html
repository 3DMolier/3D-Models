About This Model

Titanic belongs to our Ships & Boats range and is offered at $249 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 418,556 polygons and 465,888 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

6 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
