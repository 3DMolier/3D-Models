About This Model

A large spaceflight control room with tiered rows of consoles, operator chairs and a wall of big screens facing the floor. It recreates the atmosphere of the Russian center where flight controllers follow launches and orbital missions. The hall works for launch-day dramas, documentaries about the space station and educational explainers.

Each console desk carries monitors, keyboards and panel details, so camera moves along the rows stay convincing. Built and checked to CheckMate Pro rules, the interior has real proportions, so seated operators and walking staff fit naturally. Projected maps of orbits and mission graphics can be placed on the main screens to tell a specific story.

A single version costs $199, delivering the complete room as a ready interior set. Since 2015 the hall has found 30 buyers, mostly film producers, science broadcasters and space museum designers. Dimming the room lights and letting the screens glow gives the classic tense look of a mission countdown.
