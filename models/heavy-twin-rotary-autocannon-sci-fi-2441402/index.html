About This Model

Rotary cannon are the design fiction reaches for whenever a weapon needs to look serious. The spinning barrel cluster is instantly legible, and doubling it is the visual equivalent of shouting, which is exactly why it belongs on a vehicle that is not real.

The model is light at 28,744 polygons and 30,086 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, suited to real time use. It is built to CheckMate specification.
