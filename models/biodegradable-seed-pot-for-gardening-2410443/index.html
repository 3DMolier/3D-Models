About This Model

The Biodegradable Seed Pot for Gardening is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

Counted at the source, the model is 768 polygons and 770 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
