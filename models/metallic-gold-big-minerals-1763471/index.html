About This Model

This Nature & Plants model - Metallic Big Minerals - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. You are getting 4,867 polygons and 4,875 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

Questions About This Model

Can the Metallic Big Minerals model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

What is the Metallic Big Minerals model typically used for?

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Metallic Big Minerals mesh?

4,867 polygons and 4,875 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

What does StemCell mean for the Metallic Big Minerals model?

It means one source asset, several delivered formats, and a PBR material setup designed to read consistently across them - no rebuilding shaders after import.
