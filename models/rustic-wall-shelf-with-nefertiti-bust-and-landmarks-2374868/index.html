Priced at $149, the Rustic Wall Shelf with Nefertiti Bust and Landmarks is a finished Signage & Decor model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
