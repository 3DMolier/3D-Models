About This Model

Stanley Thermal Mugs Collection gathers insulated travel mugs and tumblers with handles, lids and rugged steel bodies. Each mug keeps the lid mechanism, the handle shape and the powder coated finish typical of outdoor drinkware.

Construction meets the CheckMate specification, so mugs arrive at realistic size for hands, desks and car holders. Lids are separate objects, letting an artist open one to show steam rising from fresh morning coffee. Textured coatings and polished rims catch light differently, which helps the mugs read in close shots.

Introduced in 2025, the collection fits outdoor lifestyle renders, camping scenes and kitchenware catalogs for online stores. A dawn campsite with a mug steaming on a log by the fire sets a cozy, adventurous mood. Office desk scenes also gain realism when a sturdy tumbler stands beside a laptop and a stack of notebooks. Several colors in one set keep a shelf display varied without repeating the same mug.
