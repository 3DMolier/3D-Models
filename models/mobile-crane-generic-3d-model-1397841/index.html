About This Model

Mobile Crane Generic is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. Counted at the source, the model is 820,429 polygons and 864,995 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

22 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
