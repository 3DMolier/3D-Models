About This Model

A set of beetroot, with whole roots, their tapering tails and the cut stalk tops left on at the crown. Several roots rather than one means a crate or a market stall can be filled from a single file. A beet is read by its rough skin and the flat where it sat in the soil.

CheckMate Pro certification covers the proportions, which matters when the vegetables sit next to a hand. The roots are separate objects, so they can be piled, scattered or shown singly on a board. A set like this saves the worst part of food work: making the same vegetable look different five times.

No alternate builds sit on the page, so the whole set is one purchase. On sale since 2017, it has been bought forty-two times at $39. It fits market and kitchen scenes, recipe visuals, packaging for produce and still life photography.
