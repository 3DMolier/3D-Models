About This Model

The wonders of the ancient world brought together, from the Great Pyramid to the Lighthouse of Alexandria, in one history set. Ancient Monuments of Seven Wonders Collection lets a documentary jump from Giza to Rhodes without changing artistic style. The Colossus, the Hanging Gardens and the Temple of Artemis are reconstructed as historians imagine them.

Each monument meets the CheckMate specification, so proportions hold when the wonders appear side by side. Sandstone, marble and bronze surfaces carry the weathered look of structures that stood for centuries. The statue of Zeus at Olympia and the Mausoleum at Halicarnassus round out the famous list.

Released in 2023 at $229, the bundle saves commissioning separate reconstructions of each wonder. History classes, museum displays, travel documentaries and strategy games set in antiquity use these monuments often. Layer them on a map for an animated tour of the ancient Mediterranean.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
