About This Model

A modern high-roof Transit ambulance with a fully modeled patient compartment, built for shots that open the rear doors and look inside. Its 279,724 polygons and 289,865 vertices cover both the exterior and the treatment space behind the cab. Because the interior exists, the camera can follow a paramedic through the back doors without cutting to a separate set.

The model passes TurboSquid CheckMate, so topology, naming and pivots follow a predictable standard across the whole file. Emergency markings and roof beacons give the van its character even when it sits parked at an accident scene. Glass panels and separate doors make it easy to light the cabin from inside for night sequences.

Two versions are offered: the static 2020 Ford Transit Ambulance with Interior at $189 and a rigged edition at $229. The rigged one suits animated driving and door sequences, while the static file stays lighter for still renders. Both arrived in 2026 under the Editorial Uses Only license, which fits documentaries, news graphics, training simulations and film work.
