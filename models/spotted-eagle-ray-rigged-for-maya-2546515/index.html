About This Model

A spotted eagle ray with the broad diamond disc, the pointed duckbill snout and the long whip tail that trails behind it in open water. The dark back is scattered with pale spots, which is the detail divers recognize first and the reason the species reads instantly on screen.

This build is set up for Maya, so the wide pectoral fins can be keyed into the slow flapping glide that eagle rays use. That wing motion is the whole performance of a ray, and a stiff body would give it away in the first second of a shot. It is built to CheckMate specification, so scale, naming and pivots follow the same rules as the rest of a production scene.

The ray was released in 2026 and suits aquarium visualization, reef documentaries, marine biology lessons and underwater game levels. Placed above a sandy bottom with caustics rippling over its back, a single ray gives an underwater scene scale and slow, calm movement. A few instances at different depths turn it into a small school gliding past the camera.
