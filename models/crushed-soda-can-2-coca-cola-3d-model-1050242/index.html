About This Model

Aluminium is worth crushing because it is worth recycling, and a flattened can takes a fraction of the space. It is also one of the most photographed pieces of litter in the world, which is a different argument entirely.

The model is very light at 6,336 polygons and 6,090 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 4 maps all at 4096x4096. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $19 it suits recycling and environmental visualization, street and interior scene dressing, film and television sets, game environments, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2016.
