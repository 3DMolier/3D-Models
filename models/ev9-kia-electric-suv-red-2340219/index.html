About This Model

The EV9 KIA Electric is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. You are getting 280,731 polygons and 302,511 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.

2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
