About This Model

Rock Crab is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. You are getting 17,088 polygons and 17,304 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 4 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
