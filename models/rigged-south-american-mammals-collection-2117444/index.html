About This Model

A rigged collection of South American mammals, the animals of the Amazon, the Andes and the open grassy pampas. From climbers to grazers, the set covers a range of body shapes and coats seen across the continent. Every animal carries a rig, so you can animate walking, feeding or alert poses without building skeletons.

CheckMate guidance keeps each species at a believable size relative to the others in a shared scene. Rich fur and hide textures stand out well against green rainforest light or dry mountain grass. One purchase fills a wildlife scene that would otherwise need many separate assets matched by hand for scale.

Released in 2023, the mammals serve nature documentaries, zoo and conservation visuals, biology lessons and adventure games. A muddy riverbank at dawn with several species drinking side by side makes a natural opening shot. Conservation groups also use such sets to illustrate habitat loss across the continent's forests.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
