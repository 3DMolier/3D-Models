About This Model

Priced at $129, the Heavy Duty Tow Truck is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 601,814 polygons and 606,743 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

20 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
