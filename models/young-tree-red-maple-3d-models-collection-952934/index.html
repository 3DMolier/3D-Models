About This Model

This collection of young red maple trees offers several saplings of different heights and shapes for parks and gardens. Using varied trees side by side keeps a street planting from looking cloned and artificial from above. Their slim trunks and bright crowns suggest newly planted landscaping in a fresh residential development.

The trees conform to the CheckMate specification, with tidy files, sensible pivots and consistent scale across every sapling in the set. Red maple foliage glows in autumn light, so the collection shines in seasonal scenes along streets and campus lawns. Swapping the leaf material toward green lets the same saplings appear in bright summer views too.

On sale since 2016, the collection suits architectural renders, residential site plans and park visualizations. Line a new sidewalk with them, and a housing project instantly looks greener, calmer and more inviting to buyers. Landscape architects also use young trees to show how planting will look in its first years.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How clean is the geometry on the Young Tree Red Maple Collection model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.

What is the Young Tree Red Maple Collection model typically used for?

Stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and architectural visualization. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

Can the Young Tree Red Maple Collection model be modified after purchase?
