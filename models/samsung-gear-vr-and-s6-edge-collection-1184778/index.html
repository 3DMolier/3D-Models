About This Model

This set pairs the Gear VR headset with the Galaxy S6 Edge phone that slots into its front, covering mobile VR at once. The headset shows its padded face cushion, head strap and side touchpad, while the phone keeps its curved glass edges. Both pieces share one scale, so the handset drops into the visor without any fitting work.

CheckMate Pro, the stricter of TurboSquid's two inspection grades, has reviewed the geometry and file structure of both objects. On a device shown close up, a warped lens ring or a soft bezel edge would give the game away immediately.

Use is limited to editorial work such as news pieces, reviews, tech explainers and lessons about virtual reality. The pair first appeared in 2017, when phone-based headsets were how most people tried VR at home. Held by a character or resting beside a coffee mug, the duo reads instantly as a period piece of consumer tech.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2017 · Updated 11 November 2026
