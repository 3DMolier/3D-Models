About This Model

A collection of women with prosthetic arms, showing modern limb technology as a natural part of everyday life. Visible prosthetic hands and sockets are the defining detail, modeled to look like real devices rather than robot parts.

The figures conform to CheckMate specification, sharing scale and naming so they mix easily with other people models. Representation matters, and having several women with limb differences helps scenes reflect real communities. Several women of different appearance in one purchase avoid reusing a single figure throughout a project.

Published in 2024 for $159, the set suits healthcare communication, inclusive campaigns and rehabilitation material. Place one at an office desk and another in a park, and the prosthesis becomes part of ordinary life. Prosthetics makers also use such figures to show how devices look when worn in daily situations. Neutral clothing keeps attention on the person and the device, which suits clinical and lifestyle settings alike.
