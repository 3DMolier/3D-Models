About This Model

A mechanical raptor has to satisfy two audiences at once: anyone who knows dinosaurs will check the leg proportions and the tail carriage, and anyone who knows machines will look for actuators, panel lines and cable runs at the joints. Satisfying only one produces either a dinosaur in armour or a robot with a dinosaur head.

The mesh is 153,733 polygons and 157,578 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the detail in the joint hardware and the panel edges where a mechanical creature is examined.

A second build sits on this page at the same $199. Both suit games, science fiction film and television, previsualization and concept work, theme park and exhibition design, and illustration. Built to StemCell specification, on sale since 2024.
