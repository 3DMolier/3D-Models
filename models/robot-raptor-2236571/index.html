About This Model

Robot Raptor is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $199. The mesh carries 153,733 polygons and 157,578 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. As a StemCell asset it ships with PBR materials that stay consistent across the delivered formats.

Which industries use the Robot Raptor model?

What does StemCell mean for the Robot Raptor model?
