About This Model

A tube of Marvis Whitening Mint toothpaste with its retro-styled box, from the Italian brand known for elegant packaging. The slim metal-look tube and the rectangular carton sit together, ready for bathroom shelves and product close-ups. Bold lettering and the mint color scheme make the pack recognizable even in a crowded bathroom cabinet.

The pack was modeled to the CheckMate specification, keeping the tube and carton proportions true and the object names clear. Tube and box are separate objects, so the tube can lie on a sink while the carton stays upright. The crimped tube end and screw cap give the pack the small details that hold up in macro shots.

At $19 it furnishes bathroom interiors, boutique hotel amenity renders and beauty shop shelves in product visualization. Since 2025 it has also appeared in dental care articles and lifestyle scenes about morning routines.
