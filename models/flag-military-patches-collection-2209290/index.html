About This Model

Embroidered flag patches dress up military uniforms, tactical vests and backpacks in this collection of insignia. Each patch has a raised stitched border, so it reads as fabric rather than a flat decal. Sewn on a sleeve or stuck to a plate carrier, they add nationality and unit flavor to a soldier.

The patches were made to the CheckMate specification and arrive at real-world size for character outfits. Their thread texture catches side light, which helps them stand out on dark camouflage or olive fabric. Swap the artwork to create custom unit badges for your own story without touching the geometry.

Two versions exist, Flag Military Patches Collection and Flag Military Patches Collection 2 with further flags. Since 2024 the sets have served war games, military character design and uniform reference renders. A close shot of a patch on a shoulder makes a strong opening frame for a war story.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Flag Military Patches Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

Where can I buy the Flag Military Patches Collection 3D model?

It is sold through TurboSquid, where this listing has been available since 2024. Purchase, download and licensing are handled by TurboSquid; delivery is immediate after checkout.

How heavy is the Flag Military Patches Collection mesh?

2,120 polygons and 2,122 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the 32,250 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs low at 2,120.

Which industries use the Flag Military Patches Collection model?
