About This Model

Rubber Band Stack of North Korea 50 Won is a detailed general asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. You are getting 26,376 polygons and 26,568 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
