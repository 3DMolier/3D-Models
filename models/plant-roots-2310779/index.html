About This Model

Plant Roots is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. You are getting 177,598 polygons and 187,665 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
