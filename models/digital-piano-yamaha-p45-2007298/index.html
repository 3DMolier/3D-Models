About This Model

Digital Piano is a detailed Musical Instruments asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. Geometry weighs in at 122,274 polygons over 65,248 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags event visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits music-video and film sets, game props, product rendering and educational material.
