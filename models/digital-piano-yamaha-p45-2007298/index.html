About This Model

A slab digital piano is defined by what it leaves out. No cabinet, no lid, no stand: just the keyboard and the action, because the whole point is that it fits in a flat, gets carried to a gig, and weighs what one person can lift.

The model holds 122,274 polygons and 65,248 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $49 it suits musical instrument marketing, e-commerce and retail imagery, studio and interior scenes, advertising, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
