About This Model

Looking for a Space & Sci-Fi asset? The Tamir Rocket is a finished, render-ready model at $59. You are getting 6,438 polygons and 6,520 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
