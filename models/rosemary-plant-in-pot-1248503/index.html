The Rosemary Plant in Pot is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 124,080 polygons and 125,291 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
