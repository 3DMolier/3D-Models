About This Model

Female Surgeon Dress 15 is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $89. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and product rendering. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Counted at the source, the model is 24,246 polygons and 25,770 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 24 of them, peaking at 2048x2048, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
