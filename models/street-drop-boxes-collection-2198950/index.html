About This Model

A collection of street drop boxes for mail, parcels and returns, the sturdy metal bins that line city sidewalks. Heavy slots, rounded tops and bolted bases reflect the vandal-proof design of real public collection boxes. Placed at a corner or outside a library, they add quiet civic detail to urban scenes.

With a CheckMate Pro rating, the boxes keep clean topology and a logically arranged scene. Paint colors and decals can be changed to fit any postal service, library or courier brand. Solid bases meet the pavement squarely, so the boxes look anchored rather than floating on the sidewalk.

Since 2024 these boxes have suited city planning renders, street games, logistics explainers and urban film sets. Snow piled on top or rain streaks down the sides make the boxes feel weathered and real. A person dropping a letter into the slot captures a small everyday ritual.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
