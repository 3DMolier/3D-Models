About This Model

Priced at $13, the Metal Case Open is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 12,101 polygons and 12,116 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Real-world footprint is 45.46 x 23.79 x H13.65 cm, so it drops into a scene at correct scale without a rescaling pass. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
