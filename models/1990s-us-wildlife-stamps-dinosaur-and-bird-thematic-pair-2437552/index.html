About This Model

A pair of 1990s US postage stamps, a dinosaur on one and a bird on the other, as collectors keep them. The 1990s US Wildlife Stamps Dinosaur and Bird Thematic Pair uses 15,913 polygons and 15,484 vertices, so the perforated edges are cut into real geometry. At 3.03 x 3.42 cm, the stamps match the proportions of genuine postal issues.

Printed artwork, faded inks and soft paper texture can sit on each stamp, because their UV layout keeps every island apart. A matte paper material with a slight curl makes them look handled rather than freshly printed. TurboSquid's CheckMate specification guided the build, so the stamps import cleanly at correct scale.

Since 2025 the pair has illustrated philately blogs, museum displays and nostalgic desk scenes. Lay them in an album under a magnifying glass, or glue them to an old envelope for a mail story. Natural history educators also use stamps like these to connect wildlife and paleontology with collecting.
