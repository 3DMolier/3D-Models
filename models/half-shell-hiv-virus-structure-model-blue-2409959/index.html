About This Model

Priced at $29, the Half-Shell HIV Virus Structure Model Blue is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 17,137 polygons and 17,138 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
