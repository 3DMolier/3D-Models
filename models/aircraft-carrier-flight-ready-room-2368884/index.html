About This Model

An aircraft carrier ready room, the briefing space where pilots sit in padded rows before launch, built for naval aviation scenes. The 678,819 polygons and 720,679 vertices fill the room with high-backed leather chairs, screens, squadron insignia and ceiling ducts. Rows of reclining chairs facing the front screen show at once how a squadron receives its orders.

The room follows the CheckMate specification, with overlapping UVs that let tiled materials repeat across the chairs and walls. Low ceilings, exposed pipes and fluorescent light make the space feel cramped and authentic, just like quarters below a flight deck. Pilots' helmets placed on the seats or a briefing on the screen can turn the empty room into a pre-mission moment.

Released in 2025, the ready room serves military films, naval aviation documentaries, flight simulator cutscenes and training content. Directors use spaces like this for tense briefing scenes before the action moves up to the deck.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 14 November 2026
