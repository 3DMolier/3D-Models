About This Model

A KUNG body is a hard shelter box mounted on a truck chassis, used as a workshop, command post, radio station or field laboratory. That is why the vehicle looks like a lorry carrying a small building: flat sides, a door at the back, roof hatches and window shutters. This build includes the interior, which is where the shelter earns its purpose.

The mesh runs 468,911 polygons and 487,197 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout, covering both the six wheeled chassis and the body inside and out.

Three rigged builds share this page at $169, including versions prepared for Cinema 4D and for Maya. Aimed at military and historical visualization, documentary work, film and television, training material and games. Built to StemCell specification, on sale since 2026.
