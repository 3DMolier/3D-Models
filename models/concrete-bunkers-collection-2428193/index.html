About This Model

A collection of concrete military bunkers, from small pillboxes to larger shelters, ready for coastlines, front lines and ruins. Thick sloped walls and narrow firing slits define a bunker, and those features read even under heavy weathering. Grouping several bunkers together lets a level designer build a whole defensive line in one consistent concrete style.

Each bunker is a standalone object, so it can be half-buried in terrain or scattered along a beach. The collection meets CheckMate specification for real-world scale and readable object names throughout. Concrete surfaces take moss, cracks, rust stains and graffiti well, which helps show decades of abandonment.

Available from 2025, the bunkers suit World War history scenes, shooter games, survival maps and documentary reconstructions. Low sun across a dune with a bunker half sunk in sand creates the classic Atlantic Wall image. Post-apocalyptic stories and urban exploration videos also use bunkers as hideouts, shelters and eerie landmarks.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
