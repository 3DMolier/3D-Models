About This Model

Priced at $149, the MEADS TOC Mobile Air Defense Command Vehicle Green is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 550,968 polygons and 581,944 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 275.4 x 833.88 x H370.07 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and defense simulation. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
