About This Model

A bundle of classic rotary dial telephones with curly handset cords, finger wheels and heavy desk bases. Gathering several designs in one purchase lets you dress offices, hallways and homes from different decades of the century. Placed on a hall table or a detective's desk, a rotary phone sets a period mood instantly for viewers.

CheckMate Pro checks apply to every phone in the set, so scale and naming match across the bundle. Bakelite gloss and chrome dial plates give strong highlights that read well in dim interiors. Each dial shows its finger holes and stop, a detail viewers notice in close shots.

Three versions are on the page: this first collection at $39, a second at $69 and a third at $99. Since 2019, the phones have rung in period dramas, retro interior renders and noir style games. Museums and history teachers also show them when explaining how calls worked before smartphones.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
