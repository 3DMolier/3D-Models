About This Model

A collection of sliced sausages, from thin salami rounds to thick smoked slices, ready to top pizzas or fill a charcuterie board. The Sliced Sausages Collection gathers several cured and cooked varieties, each showing its marbled cross section. Arranged on a board, the slices instantly create a deli atmosphere for food scenes.

Rich red meat with white fat specks suits the salami, while smoked sausage takes a darker, glossier tone. Each variety can carry its own texture, so the platter looks varied and appetizing rather than repeated. TurboSquid stamped the bundle with CheckMate Pro, verifying clean files that open without cleanup.

Pizza restaurants, delis and picnic scenes have used these slices since 2024. Fan them across a wooden board with cheese and olives, or scatter them over a pizza before baking. Butcher shop displays and recipe videos also show sliced meats like these to tempt hungry viewers.
