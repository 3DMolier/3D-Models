About This Model

Looking for a Toys & Games asset? The Barbie Doll is a finished, render-ready model at $49. The mesh carries 49,812 polygons and 36,509 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
