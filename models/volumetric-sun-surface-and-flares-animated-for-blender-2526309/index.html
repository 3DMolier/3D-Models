About This Model

A volumetric sun surface with flares for Blender, made for close passes over a boiling star in space and science films. The surface is built as glowing volume, so it has real depth when the camera skims low over it. Flares rise from the edge as loops of hot gas, which gives the sphere the restless look of the real sun.

No polygons need managing, and the look is driven by the emission and density settings of the volume shader. It follows CheckMate specification, so the Blender scene is named and grouped cleanly before you append it anywhere. Turning the camera so the flares sit against black space shows their shape best, while a frontal view reads as a glowing disc.

Added to the range in 2026, it suits solar science lessons, documentary sequences, game cinematics and space opera trailers. A spacecraft silhouette passing in front of the surface gives the viewer an immediate sense of how huge the star is. Dim the emission slightly and the same volume passes for a cooler, older star in another system.
