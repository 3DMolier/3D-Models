About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Bayraktar TB3 UCAV is a finished, render-ready model at $69. Counted at the source, the model is 249,454 polygons and 130,461 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
