About This Model

A classroom chalkboard covered in handwritten notes, complete with chalk sticks and a felt eraser on the ledge. The Written Chalkboard with Chalk and Eraser uses 18,928 polygons for the frame, chalk tray and accessories.

There are 19,520 vertices, keeping the chalk cylinders round and the eraser block softly beveled. Its CheckMate status ensures tidy geometry and real classroom proportions on import. Writing already fills the board, so the scene reads as a lesson in progress rather than an empty room.

For $39 the 2026 chalkboard suits school scenes, lecture halls and educational video backdrops. Mount it behind a teacher's desk, or lean it on an easel in a cafe as a menu board. Tutors recording lessons can replace the writing with their own text by editing the board texture. A stub of chalk left in the tray hints at a busy day of teaching and adds subtle realism.
