About This Model

The four pack is a giveaway rather than a product. Restaurants, waiting rooms and cereal boxes have handed out this exact quantity for decades, and it is probably the most widely distributed art material in the world.

The model is extremely light at 1,416 polygons and 1,409 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with maps up to 4096x4096. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $29 it suits product and packaging visualization, e-commerce and retail imagery, children's media, advertising, film set dressing and editorial illustration. On sale since 2018.
