About This Model

This personal fans collection brings together several small cooling fans, from handheld models to compact desk units built for hot summer days. Having various shapes in one set means an office, a bedroom and a busy summer street can each get a fitting fan. Their compact blades and slim protective grilles stay recognizable even when the fans sit far back on a crowded shelf.

Every fan holds CheckMate Lite status, which guarantees the files open properly and match the preview renders shown on TurboSquid. Bright plastic housings take candy colors or clean white finishes, matching anything from teen bedrooms to minimalist offices with pale furniture. Spinning the blades with a simple rotation turns any of them into a lively animated loop for short product videos.

Brought to market in 2024, the collection fits gadget catalogs, travel essentials flatlays and summer lifestyle scenes set outdoors. Clip a fan onto a stroller or place it beside a laptop, and the heat of the season becomes obvious without words. Electronics retailers and game designers also use such sets to stock shelves with believable small appliances in many colors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
