About This Model

The Mortuary Interior is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 1,028,193 polygons and 1,049,908 vertices, which puts it in the heavy bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
