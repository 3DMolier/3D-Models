About This Model

A pack of abandoned, rust-eaten vehicles for junkyards, ghost towns and roadside scenes where nothing has moved in years. Flaking paint, sagging bodywork and dull glass give each wreck its own story without extra modeling. Corrosion follows panel edges and wheel arches, the places where water would actually collect on a real car.

Each vehicle carries CheckMate Pro certification, so scale, topology and UV layout passed the full TurboSquid inspection. Wrecks line up at correct size beside fences, buildings and characters, which matters in a cluttered junkyard. Mixing several wrecks at different angles avoids the parking lot look of repeated duplicates.

These wrecks have been offered since 2024 for $199, with royalty free rights for any project. Post-apocalyptic games, horror films, music videos and photography mood boards use rusty cars like these constantly. Scatter them among weeds with a low sun behind, and a plain field turns into a forgotten scrapyard.
