About This Model

A naval helicopter with a modeled cabin interior and a shipboard tow tractor, built for carrier decks and maritime operations scenes. The 1,531,282 polygons cover the rotor head, folded blades, cockpit, cabin seats and the low tractor at the nose. The tractor hooks to the nose gear, showing how crews move helicopters on a crowded flight deck.

CheckMate review confirms a tidy build, and the 1,589,722 vertices keep helicopter, interior and tractor separate. Naval gray paint and deck grime work best procedurally, since unwrapping is limited to some parts. Open the cabin door to show the interior during a crew boarding scene.

Selling for $219 since 2026, the set suits naval simulation, defense documentaries and military games. The full rig stretches far enough to fill a carrier elevator, so plan camera paths along the deck. Tow the helicopter from the hangar bay to the landing spot to tell a complete launch story.
