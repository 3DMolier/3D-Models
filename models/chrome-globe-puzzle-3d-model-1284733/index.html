About This Model

Priced at $39, the Chrome Globe Puzzle is a finished Toys & Games model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 57,952 polygons and 57,702 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

1 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
