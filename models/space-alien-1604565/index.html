Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Space Alien is a finished, render-ready model at $99. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
