About This Model

Priced at $99, the Space Alien is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 9,938 polygons and 10,056 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
