About This Model

The Flintlock Horse Pistol is a production-ready Weapons 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 22,490 polygons over 12,034 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
