About This Model

A commercial diving helmet supplies air from the surface and protects a diver's head during long underwater work shifts. The Commercial Diving Helmet Drass D One shapes that hard shell from 107,133 polygons and 55,514 vertices.

A wide front viewport gives the diver good visibility, while valves and fittings cluster on the side for air and communications. The rounded shell sits on a neck dam and yoke that seal it to the drysuit beneath. StemCell certification lets the painted fiberglass and metal materials move between renderers without being rebuilt.

Launched in 2023, the helmet suits offshore industry training, marine engineering presentations and underwater film scenes. Dive instructors demonstrate with it where the umbilical connects and how the free-flow valve works. Visualizers place a diver wearing it beside a pipeline or ship hull in murky green water. Game developers add it to deep-sea exploration levels, where the bright yellow shell stays visible in darkness.
