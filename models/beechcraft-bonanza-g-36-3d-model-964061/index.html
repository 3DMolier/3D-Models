About This Model

The Beechcraft Bonanza G 36 is a six-seat piston plane with a long nose, low wing and a straight tail. This model captures that classic general aviation profile beside small hangars, grass strips and regional fuel stations.

The fuselage tapers smoothly from the propeller spinner back to the tail fin. Retractable gear folds into wings that hold fuel close to the root, keeping the belly clean in flight shots. CheckMate Lite certification confirms the mesh is usable and correctly scaled for airfield layouts.

Offered since 2015, the aircraft carries an Editorial Uses Only license, which suits documentaries, news graphics and educational material. Flight simulation teams fly it as a touring aircraft that many private pilots will recognize at once. Aviation films park it on a quiet apron at dawn to open a scene. VR training builders put it in a hangar, where students walk around the wing and study the control surfaces.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
