About This Model

Priced at $59, the Alphabet Balloons with Ribbons is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 134,056 polygons and 133,926 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

52 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
