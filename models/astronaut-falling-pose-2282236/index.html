About This Model

Astronaut Falling Pose is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. Geometry weighs in at 70,187 polygons over 73,895 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
