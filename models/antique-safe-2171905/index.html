About This Model

Antique Safe is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. You are getting 17,694 polygons and 9,026 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.
