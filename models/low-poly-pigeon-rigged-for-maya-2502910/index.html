About This Model

A low-poly city pigeon with the plump chest, small head and folded wings that make the bird recognizable on any rooftop or square. Its light build keeps the polygon budget small, so flocks can fill a plaza without slowing a real-time scene. Perched on a ledge or pecking at crumbs, it adds quiet life to empty streets.

The asset follows the CheckMate specification, meaning clean geometry, sensible naming and correct scale in every format. Faceted surfaces suit stylized games while still reading as a real bird from normal viewing distance. Gray plumage with darker wing bars matches the common rock dove seen in every city.

The second version, Low Poly Pigeon Flying Animated Rigged For Maya, joins the static base model for motion work. It entered the catalog in 2025 and is bought for mobile games, urban backgrounds, VR street scenes and animated shorts. Low polygon density also makes the bird a good candidate for particle-driven flocks.
