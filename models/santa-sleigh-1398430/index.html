About This Model

Santa Sleigh is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $59. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 30,002 polygons over 32,005 vertices - a light build for its class. It ships 24 maps with the top set authored at 2048x2048 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
