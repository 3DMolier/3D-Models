About This Model

Su 33 Air Superiority Fighter Flight is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. Geometry weighs in at 157,756 polygons over 166,692 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation as the intended applications.

2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
