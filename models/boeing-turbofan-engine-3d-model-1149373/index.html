About This Model

This turbofan engine shows the large front fan, the nacelle cowling and the exhaust cone of a Boeing airliner power plant. The 38,001 polygons are concentrated in the fan blades and spinner, the parts a viewer notices first when looking into the intake. Mounted under a wing or displayed on a stand, the engine gives scale to hangar and airport scenes. Fan blades sweep in a tight spiral around the spinner, which reads beautifully in a straight-on intake shot.

Only 2 maps at 4096 x 4096 handle the painted cowling, bare metal and dark interior surfaces. The engine counts 41,888 vertices, with a flat UV layout that makes wear and decal work straightforward. It carries CheckMate Pro certification, which confirms tidy scene structure and properly named engine parts.

In the catalog since 2017, the Boeing Turbofan Engine 3D Model is licensed for Editorial Uses Only. That makes it suited to aviation news, maintenance training, engineering lectures and documentary graphics about jet propulsion.
