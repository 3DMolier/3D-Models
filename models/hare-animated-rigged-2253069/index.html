About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Hare is a finished, render-ready model at $99. You are getting 21,104 polygons and 21,051 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
