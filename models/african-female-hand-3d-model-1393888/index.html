About This Model

The African Female Hand is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 1,748 polygons and 1,802 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 8 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
