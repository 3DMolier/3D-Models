About This Model

A tall steel safe cabinet with a lock panel and heavy door, built for offices, banks and secure storage rooms. The Security Safe Cabinet Simplified keeps the count to 16,201 polygons, focusing on the door, handle and lock.

There are 17,112 vertices, enough for rounded handle edges and a thick door that shows its weight when open. Its CheckMate certification ensures tidy geometry and a size that matches real office furniture. The plain painted body stays neutral, so the safe blends into a corporate interior or a back room.

Available from 2026 at $19, the safe suits heist games, office renders and bank interior scenes. Push it into a manager's office corner, or line several along the wall of a records room. The simplified build makes it easy to repeat across a large building without slowing the scene. Crime dramas can stage a break-in by leaving the door slightly ajar with papers scattered nearby.
