About This Model

This Animals & Creatures model - Elephant Fur - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $179 on TurboSquid. You are getting 10,294 polygons and 9,611 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

9 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
