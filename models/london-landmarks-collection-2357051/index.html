About This Model

Famous London landmarks are gathered in this collection, giving city scenes the silhouettes that say London at a glance. London Landmarks Collection is aimed at skylines, travel sequences and historical stories set beside the Thames. Gothic towers, stone bridges and classical facades carry the architectural detail people expect from the city.

All buildings share one real-world scale, so placing them along a river or across a skyline needs no resizing. Stonework, clock faces and ornate rooflines are modeled rather than painted, which helps when the camera moves in. Overcast skies and wet pavements suit the set, since they match how most visitors remember London.

Available since 2025 at $539, the landmarks suit commercial travel and education projects. Tourism explainers, history documentaries, city building games and language course illustrations all draw on them. A red double-decker bus passing in the foreground completes the picture instantly for any viewer.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the London Landmarks Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

Can the London Landmarks Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

What is the London Landmarks Collection model typically used for?

Stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

How heavy is the London Landmarks Collection mesh?

4,342,514 polygons and 4,317,618 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Our Model Bundles & Sets listings sit at a median of about 28,000 polygons, so 4,342,514 is heavier than the typical one.
