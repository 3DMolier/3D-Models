About This Model

A tennis ball with real fuzz on its felt surface and the familiar curved seam running all the way around it. The Tennis Ball with Fur 3D Model captures the soft fibers that make a ball look used and handled rather than plastic. That fuzzy surface scatters light the way felt does, so the ball holds its realism even in macro shots.

Its geometry and file structure passed TurboSquid's complete CheckMate Pro check, so the fuzzy ball loads without surprises. Fluorescent yellow-green works best with a slightly emissive tint, which mimics how these balls glow in strong sunlight. The seam line gives a clear orientation, useful when animating a spin in slow motion across a court.

Sports broadcast graphics, tennis court scenes and pet toy renders have relied on it since 2015. Let one rest against the strings of a racket for a still shot, or bounce it on clay in an animation. Dog owners will recognize it immediately too, so it doubles as a backyard play prop for family scenes.
