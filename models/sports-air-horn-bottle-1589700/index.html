The Sports Air Horn Bottle is a production-ready Musical Instruments 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for event visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits music-video and film sets, game props, product rendering and educational material.
