About This Model

Priced at $39, the Football Down Marker is a finished Sports & Recreation model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 73,712 polygons and 88,894 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

24 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Questions About This Model

Can the Football Down Marker model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

Can the Football Down Marker model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Where can I buy the Football Down Marker 3D model?

It is sold through TurboSquid, where this listing has been available since 2016. Purchase, download and licensing are handled by TurboSquid; delivery is immediate after checkout.

What texture resolution ships with the Football Down Marker?

24 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.
