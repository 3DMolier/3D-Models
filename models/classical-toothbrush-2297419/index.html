About This Model

A classic straight-handled toothbrush with a dense rectangular head of bristles and a slightly flared grip that fits the palm. Most of the 84,964 polygons and 91,346 vertices sit in the individual bristle tufts packed into the head. That level of detail holds up in macro shots where toothpaste sits on the bristle tips.

Bristles and handle are untextured, so translucent plastic and white nylon are shaded however you prefer. UV islands sit apart from each other, which makes printed grip patterns or a brand stripe easy to paint. Its build meets the CheckMate specification for real proportions and clean, named parts.

On sale from 2024, the brush suits dental clinic animations, bathroom vanity scenes and oral care guides. Standing in a glass cup beside a tube of paste, it completes a believable morning routine. Dental education can tilt the head toward the camera to show how angled bristles reach between teeth and along the gum line.
