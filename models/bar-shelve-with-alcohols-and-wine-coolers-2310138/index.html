About This Model

This bar shelving unit displays rows of liquor bottles above glass-front wine coolers, the back wall of a hotel or lounge bar. Its 546,082 polygons and 548,837 vertices define bottle necks, labels, glass doors, cooler racks and the shelving frame. Bottles in many shapes and colors create a rich backdrop for bartender close-ups and cocktail scenes at night.

Modeling adhered to the CheckMate rule set from TurboSquid, keeping geometry clean, scale correct and objects named clearly. Its UVs never overlap, so bottle labels can be swapped for original designs without fighting stretched textures. Glass and liquid catch backlight beautifully, which makes the shelf glow behind the counter in evening renders.

Dating from 2024, the shelf costs $119 and is available for commercial use royalty free. Bar and restaurant designers plan back bars with it, and film crews dress lounge sets for night scenes. Nightlife games and hotel visualizations also place it behind counters with stools and pendant lights overhead.
