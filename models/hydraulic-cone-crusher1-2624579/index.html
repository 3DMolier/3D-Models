A cone crusher grinds quarry rock between a gyrating mantle and a fixed bowl, and this one shows the full machine on its frame. The Hydraulic Cone Crusher(1) carries 166,200 polygons and 171,936 vertices in clean quads around the drive pulley, hoses and feed hopper. The quad topology also bends cleanly if you need a dented guard or worn housing.
