About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Robotic Horse is a finished, render-ready model at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 84,701 polygons and 86,378 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 133.2 x 55.74 x H91.48 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
