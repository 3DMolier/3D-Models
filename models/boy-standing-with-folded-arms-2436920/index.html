About This Model

A boy standing with his arms folded across his chest, a confident, slightly stubborn pose that works for school, family and street scenes. The 95,497 polygons are spent on the face, the creased sleeves where the arms cross and the casual clothes. Because the pose is static and balanced, the character can stand in a doorway or a crowd without looking frozen mid-motion.

Built to CheckMate specification, he is scaled to a real child and modeled in quads and tris across 102,386 vertices. The unwrap has no overlaps, so the shirt color or a school logo can be changed in a texture editor in minutes.

On sale since 2025, the figure fits architectural visualization, school interiors, family lifestyle renders and background characters in games. Royalty-free terms cover commercial work, which makes him a safe choice for client projects involving children. Set him slightly off center beside an adult, and the folded arms instantly suggest an argument or a waiting child.
