About This Model

Priced at $199, the HMS Prince of Wales is a finished Ships & Boats model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 795,776 polygons and 905,777 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
