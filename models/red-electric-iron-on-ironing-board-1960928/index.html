About This Model

Electric Iron on Ironing belongs to our Furniture & Interior range and is offered at $49 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 58,832 polygons and 30,256 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
