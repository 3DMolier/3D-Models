About This Model

A collection of a clear vodka bottle paired with a classic faceted drinking glass, the kind found on old kitchen tables across Eastern Europe. The glass has vertical facets around its lower half and a smooth rim band, a design many viewers recognize instantly. Bottle and glass are separate pieces, so the drink can be poured in an animation.

Clear glass surfaces and a printed bottle label are prepared for close shots with realistic refraction. The faceted glass throws sharp sparkling highlights, which helps it stand out on a dark table. CheckMate Pro certification ensures both items are clean, closed shells with sensible names.

It has been available since 2021 and suits kitchen still lifes, historical dramas and toasts at family gatherings. Filmmakers place it beside pickles, bread and a newspaper, while game artists use it in apartment and bar levels. One half-filled glass left on a windowsill at dusk sets a quiet, reflective mood.
