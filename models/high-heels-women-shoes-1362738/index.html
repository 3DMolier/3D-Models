About This Model

Priced at $39, the High Heels Women Shoes is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Geometry weighs in at 19,686 polygons over 22,320 vertices - a light build for its class. Texture work runs to 8 maps, the largest at 2048x2048, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
