About This Model

Priced at $99, the Reach Stacker Linde is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 362,802 polygons and 399,378 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 8 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
