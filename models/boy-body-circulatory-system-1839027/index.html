About This Model

Boy Body Circulatory System is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $89. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 300,460 polygons and 299,835 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
