About This Model

This Clothing & Accessories model - Masquerade Mask Purple - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Counted at the source, the model is 7,159 polygons and 7,844 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 7 maps, the largest at 2048x2048, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
