About This Model

The Japanese Karate Fighter Black Suit Pose 3 with Fur is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. You are getting 23,991 polygons and 24,744 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

15 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
