About This Model

Flies built with fur for their bristly hairs, a collection made for extreme macro shots where every tiny hair matters. Real hair strands on the thorax and legs catch light in a way texture alone cannot imitate up close.

Bundled as one purchase, the furred flies share scale and look, so a group under a macro lens matches perfectly. Their modeling follows CheckMate specification guidelines, keeping true size so hair density reads correctly against food or skin. Backlighting reveals the halo of bristles around the body, giving macro renders a sharp, scientific realism.

First offered in 2025, the set suits scientific macro visualization, pest control commercials, horror close-ups and nature documentaries. A furred fly rubbing its legs together on a sugar cube is a gross-out shot that viewers remember. Fur rendering adds time, so these are best kept for hero insects while simpler flies fill the background. Entomology museums and microscope brands can use the hairs to show what the naked eye never sees.
