About This Model

Large passenger cruise ships fill this collection, complete with stacked decks, lifeboats, funnels and balcony rows. Cruise Ships Collection gives harbor, ocean and travel scenes several liners that share a consistent level of detail. White hulls and rows of cabin windows make the vessels instantly recognizable from shore.

Hull scale and naming match from one liner to the next, so several ships can berth side by side at a terminal. Pool decks and rows of lifeboats along the hull are details passengers recognize immediately. Lit windows and deck lights turn the ships into glowing landmarks for night harbor shots.

New in 2026 at $559, the bundle fits commercial travel and port projects. Travel agency presentations, port development renders, tourism explainers and ocean games are good fits. Turquoise water and a tropical island backdrop make the classic holiday shot, with small tenders ferrying guests to shore.
