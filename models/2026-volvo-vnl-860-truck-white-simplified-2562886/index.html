About This Model

A white 2026 Volvo VNL 860 sleeper tractor, simplified for traffic and logistics scenes where many trucks share the road. The 303,583 polygons describe the tall sleeper cab, the aerodynamic nose, fuel tanks and the fifth-wheel deck behind.

The cab measures 323.34 x 903.43 x H436.37 cm, so it lines up correctly with trailers and loading docks. Its 335,213 vertices meet CheckMate specification, and the simplified build trims hidden detail for busy highway layouts. The truck carries Editorial Uses Only terms, as it reproduces Volvo's current design and markings.

New for 2026 and priced at $149, it suits freight industry reporting, logistics simulations and transport documentaries. Run it down an interstate at dawn with a long trailer, and the scale of road freight becomes tangible. A clean white finish makes the truck a neutral base for any fleet scheme in editorial imagery.
