About This Model

The Mikoyan MiG-31 Supersonic Interceptor Aircraft is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $169 on TurboSquid. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, film production and simulation as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

At 233,280 polygons and 253,430 vertices this is a mid-weight asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
