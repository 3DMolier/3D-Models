About This Model

Boeing 767-300 Condor Flugdienst is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. You are getting 446,316 polygons and 456,936 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

34 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
