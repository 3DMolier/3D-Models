About This Model

A complete female body opens up layer by layer here, from skin through muscles and organs down to the skeleton. Defect - Realistic Female Full Anatomy carries 810,568 polygons, a count that only makes sense when every system is modeled separately. The vessels and nerves follow the body contours closely, which keeps cutaway views anatomically believable.

The 817,779 vertices sit almost one to one with the faces, a sign of a closed, carefully built shell. Non-overlapping UVs allow tissue textures, labels or highlight colors to be painted on each organ independently. Geometry follows CheckMate specification, keeping the many parts named and grouped for quick isolation.

Available since 2023, the model serves medical schools, simulation software and health explainer studios. Gynecology lessons, pregnancy education and comparative anatomy videos are typical places to bring it in. Clinical trainers also use such layered figures to explain where pain originates and which structures a procedure will touch.
