About This Model

This Electronics & Gadgets model - Vintage Retro Mini Computer - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $39 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.

Geometry weighs in at 36,891 polygons over 39,252 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
