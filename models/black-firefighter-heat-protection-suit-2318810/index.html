About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Black Firefighter Heat Protection Suit is a finished, render-ready model at $99. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Geometry weighs in at 56,405 polygons over 58,245 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
