About This Model

A set of branded drink coolers with glass doors and bright orange Fanta graphics, the kind found in corner shops and convenience stores. Lined up along a shop wall, the fridges instantly tell the viewer where the scene takes place. The collection gives several cooler units in one go, so a store interior avoids copying one fridge down the aisle.

The coolers meet the CheckMate specification, so the geometry is clean and ready for interior renders without a cleanup pass. Because the graphics belong to a real brand, the files carry the Editorial Uses Only license and suit news, documentary and illustrative work.

Added in 2026, the Fanta Refrigerators Collection helps fill supermarkets, gas stations and kiosks in documentary and editorial scenes. Stand one beside a checkout and the orange glow of the doors pulls the frame together. A row of coolers also works as a background element behind a reporter or an interview subject.
