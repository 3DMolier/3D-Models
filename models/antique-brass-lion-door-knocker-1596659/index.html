About This Model

Antique Brass Lion Door Knocker is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

Counted at the source, the model is 13,142 polygons and 13,067 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 6 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
