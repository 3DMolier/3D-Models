About This Model

Raven Landing Animated Rigged is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $259. Geometry weighs in at 183,716 polygons over 183,716 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

19 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
