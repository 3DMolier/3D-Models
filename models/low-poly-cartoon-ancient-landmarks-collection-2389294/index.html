About This Model

A collection of low poly cartoon versions of ancient landmarks, ideal for stylized games, maps and educational apps. Simplified monuments keep their iconic silhouettes while dropping fine detail for a playful look. Bright, flat colors make each landmark readable at a glance on small phone and tablet screens.

Every landmark is built per CheckMate rules, with tidy geometry that stays light enough for mobile platforms. The friendly style blends with other low poly assets, so whole worlds can be assembled quickly. Flat-shaded faces need no complex textures, which keeps loading fast in browser games and lightweight learning tools.

Launched in 2025, the landmarks serve history lessons, travel apps, puzzle games and animated explainers about ancient civilizations. Placed on a stylized globe, they turn a dry geography quiz into a colorful adventure that children actually enjoy. Their chunky shapes also suit small figurines and board game pieces for classrooms and museum gift shops.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How clean is the geometry on the Low Poly Cartoon Ancient Landmarks Collection model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.

How heavy is the Low Poly Cartoon Ancient Landmarks Collection mesh?

2,910 polygons and 3,294 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the roughly 28,000 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs low at 2,910.

What is the Low Poly Cartoon Ancient Landmarks Collection model typically used for?

The listing names game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the main applications. In practice it also works anywhere a finished model bundles & sets object is needed without modeling it from scratch.
