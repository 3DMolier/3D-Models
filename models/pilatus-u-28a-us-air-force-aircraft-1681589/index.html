About This Model

The Pilatus U-28A Us Air Force Aircraft is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $239 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 114,427 polygons and 123,220 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
