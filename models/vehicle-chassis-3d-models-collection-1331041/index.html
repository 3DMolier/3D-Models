About This Model

A bundle of bare vehicle chassis with exposed frames, suspension, axles and drivetrains, showing what sits under the body panels. Without bodywork in the way, each layout of springs, shafts and wheels can be studied clearly from any angle. Set on a workshop floor or floating in an exploded view, the frames make strong technical illustrations.

All pieces carry CheckMate Pro certification, keeping naming, scale and mesh quality uniform across the set. Steel, rubber and machined aluminum surfaces give engineering renders a convincing mechanical feel under studio light. Collecting several chassis in one purchase helps when a presentation compares different vehicle platforms side by side.

Three versions are offered on this page, rising from this first collection at $279 to two larger sets. Vehicle Chassis 3D Models Collection 5 costs $299, while Vehicle Chassis Collection 6 costs $319. Since 2018, the bundle has served automotive engineering training, technical animation and vehicle design reviews.
