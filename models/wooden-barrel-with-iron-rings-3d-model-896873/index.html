About This Model

This oak barrel bound with iron hoops is the staple container of taverns, ships, wine cellars and medieval villages. Staves bulge gently toward the middle, and the hoops sit tight around the curve, just like a real cooper's work. A flat head with visible stave joints closes the top, so the barrel reads correctly from above as well.

TurboSquid certified the model as CheckMate Pro, confirming tidy topology, realistic scale and organized naming. Dark wood grain against rusted iron gives strong contrast, which helps the prop hold up in dim lighting. Because the shape is so familiar, viewers accept it instantly in both realistic and stylized scenes.

First released in 2015 at $24, it remains a dependable pick for game levels, film sets and period visualizations. Level designers stack these barrels in docks and storerooms or break them open as destructible cover. Winery visualizations also use rows of them in cellars to show aging rooms and tasting spaces.
