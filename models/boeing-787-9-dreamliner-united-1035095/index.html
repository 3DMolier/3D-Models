About This Model

The 787-9 is the stretched variant, and the extra length is immediately visible in the run of windows and in how the wing sits relative to the fuselage. Everything else that identifies a Dreamliner is there - the raked wingtips, the chevroned nacelles, the smooth composite skin - and United's scheme runs the length of it.

The mesh runs 431,697 polygons and 442,157 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 32 texture maps at 4096x4096 carrying the doors, stencils and service markings.

A rigged build sits on this page at the same $149 for when the gear retracts or the surfaces move. Aimed at airline marketing, airport visualization, aviation journalism, film and television, and print. On sale since 2016.
