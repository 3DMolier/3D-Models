About This Model

A collection of 3D scanners in several forms, the devices that capture real objects as digital point clouds and meshes. Projector lenses, camera windows and sturdy grips are what make each scanner read as precision equipment.

Every scanner in the set is CheckMate Pro certified, arriving at true scale with clean, logically named parts. The devices are separate objects, so one can sit on a tripod while another rests in an operator's hand. Separate materials for housings, lenses and grips simplify recoloring to fit a lab or workshop palette.

Available since 2017, the collection fits engineering presentations, heritage digitization stories and technology catalogs. Place a scanner beside a car part or a statue and the purpose of the device becomes clear immediately. Metrology teachers rely on these devices to compare scanning approaches in courses about quality control. Projected laser lines across an object add a striking visual cue during scanning shots.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2017 · Updated 12 November 2026
