About This Model

A crushed can is far more useful than an intact one for the same reason a crumpled bag is: bins, gutters, recycling scenes and desk clutter all need packaging that has been used. Each can folds differently depending on how it was crushed, which is why a set beats a single item repeated.

The mesh runs 18,886 polygons and 18,048 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry entirely spent on the deformation, and ships with 6 texture maps at 4096x4096 carrying the printing distorted across the folds.

At $29 it suits recycling and waste visualisation, urban and street set dressing, interior clutter, sustainability material, film and television, and games. On sale since 2016.
