About This Model

Priced at $39, the Auditorium Soft Seating Light Wood is a finished Lighting model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 15,460 polygons and 8,162 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, event visualization and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits interior and architectural visualization, product rendering and game environment dressing.
