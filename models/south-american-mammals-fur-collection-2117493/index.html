About This Model

This set gathers the fur-covered mammals of South America into one purchase, so a rainforest or pampas scene starts with a ready cast. Each animal carries groomed fur rather than a painted coat, which keeps the silhouette soft against foliage and under low evening light.

Under CheckMate Pro review every animal was checked for scale and clean topology, so species stand in honest proportion to each other. Fur is the expensive part of any mammal, and having it already styled saves days of grooming work before the first test render. Because the animals arrive together, lighting and material response match across the group without per-model tweaking.

The collection went on sale in 2023 and is priced at $169, which covers the whole group rather than one creature. Wildlife documentaries, museum displays and nature-themed games are the usual homes for a set like this, where several species share a frame. Place them along a riverbank or under a canopy edge, and the scene reads as a living habitat instead of a lone specimen.
