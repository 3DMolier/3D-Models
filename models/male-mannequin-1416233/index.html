About This Model

The Male Mannequin is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

At 4,596 polygons and 4,578 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 2 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
