About This Model

Twin Disco Mirror Ball is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 50,014 polygons and 39,363 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
