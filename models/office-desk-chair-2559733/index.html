About This Model

Priced at $39, the Office Desk Chair is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 14,211 polygons over 14,832 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. 6 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags visualization, advertising and game assets as the intended applications. It is built for interior visualisation, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

At 14,211 polygons and 14,832 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It ships 6 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
