About This Model

A Chinese family of a man, a woman and a boy in traditional clothing, gathered for cultural scenes, festivals and historical stories. Long robes with mandarin collars and wide sleeves define the silhouettes and set the figures in a clear cultural tradition. The boy stands smaller beside his parents, which gives the group the instant read of a family on an outing.

The group holds the CheckMate Pro level, which TurboSquid grants only after reviewing topology, scale and naming by hand. Silk fabrics hang in soft folds that pick up light along the shoulders and hems in warm interiors. Rich reds and deep blues in the garments look especially festive under lantern light during new year celebrations.

Dating from 2023, the collection suits Lunar New Year visuals, history lessons, museum guides and period games set in old China. Place them in a courtyard with red lanterns and stone steps, and a festive old town scene comes together within minutes.
