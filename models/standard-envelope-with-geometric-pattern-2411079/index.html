About This Model

Looking for a general asset? The Standard Envelope with Geometric Pattern is a finished, render-ready model at $9. Geometry weighs in at 244 polygons over 246 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 16.76 x 11.62 x H0.28 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
