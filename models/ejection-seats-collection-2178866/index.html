About This Model

Ejection Seats Collection gathers rocket-powered escape seats from combat jets for aviation, museum and engineering scenes. Each seat shows the pull handles, harness straps, headbox and rails that guide it out of the cockpit. Different designs side by side reveal how ejection systems evolved across aircraft generations.

The set meets TurboSquid's CheckMate Lite level, so files open cleanly and the geometry is sound. Warning stripes and yellow handles live in materials, so they stay bright and readable in close shots. Each seat can be lifted out on its own for cutaway diagrams or exploded views.

These seats were added in 2024 and suit pilot training content, aviation museums, engineering explainers and flight simulator cockpits. Shown firing from a canopy against the sky, a seat makes a gripping sequence in any jet film. Lined up under museum lights, they also give visitors a clear look at life-saving hardware.
