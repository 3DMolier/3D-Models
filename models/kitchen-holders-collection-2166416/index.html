About This Model

A collection of kitchen holders covers the racks, stands and caddies that keep utensils, towels and paper rolls in reach. Together they carry 52,164 polygons and 26,104 vertices, enough to keep wire bends and mounting brackets smooth. Every holder stands apart in the scene, so a counter or a backsplash can be dressed one piece at a time.

Finishes such as chrome, wood or painted steel are left to your shaders because no textures come in the package. Non-overlapping UV layouts give each item room for brushed metal grain, fingerprints or a painted powder coating. TurboSquid assessed the set at CheckMate Lite, a check of basic geometry and files that load correctly.

Available from 2023, the holders help kitchen designers finish cabinetry renders, small apartment layouts and storage concepts. A paper towel stand on the counter and a utensil rack on the wall make a kitchen look inhabited. Home goods stores also use the set to present organization ideas in room previews and online guides.
