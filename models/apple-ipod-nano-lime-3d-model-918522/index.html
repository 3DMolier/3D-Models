About This Model

A lime green iPod Nano, the slim music player with a click wheel that clipped to pockets and armbands. The 13,377 polygons and 13,538 vertices shape the rounded aluminum body, the screen and the circular wheel. Its thin, tall form reads instantly as a gadget from the early iPod era, even at small sizes on screen.

No texture maps are included, so the anodized lime finish and screen glow are set up as materials. Non-overlapping UVs leave room for a custom screen image such as an album cover or playlist. CheckMate Pro status confirms clean topology and true scale for close shots. The model is licensed for Editorial Uses Only, suitable for tech history articles, reviews and documentaries.

Listed since 2015, the player suits retro music features, museum displays of consumer electronics and nostalgia pieces. Paired with white earbuds on a desk, it brings back a whole era of portable music.
