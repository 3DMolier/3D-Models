About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Luxembourg Tram Urbos is a finished, render-ready model at $149. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

Geometry weighs in at 261,519 polygons over 287,658 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 16 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
