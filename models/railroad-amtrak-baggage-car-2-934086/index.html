About This Model

Railroad Amtrak Baggage Car 2 is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $59. You are getting 955,372 polygons and 966,844 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

17 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
