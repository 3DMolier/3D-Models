About This Model

Priced at $39, the Robotic Arm is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 64,143 polygons and 66,585 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and software demos.

It has been on sale since 2022 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 4 September 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Robotic Arm model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How much does the Robotic Arm 3D model cost?

$39 USD, paid once. The Royalty Free license means no recurring cost and no extra payment when the finished work ships.

Does the Robotic Arm model include materials and textures?

The product page states what ships with each format. Textures, when present, come inside the download rather than as a separate purchase. See the listing for the exact contents.

What is the Robotic Arm model typically used for?

It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and software demos - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.
