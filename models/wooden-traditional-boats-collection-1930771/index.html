About This Model

A bundle of traditional wooden boats, the hand-built fishing and river craft found along coasts, lakes and rivers around the world. Each boat arrives individually, so a harbor, a beach or a river bank can be dotted with them freely. Planked hulls, painted trim and simple oars give the set a warm, handmade character. Pulled up on sand with nets draped over the sides, they make an instant postcard composition.

All boats pass CheckMate Pro review, keeping the planking geometry clean and UVs ready for weathering and salt stains. Royalty Free terms cover travel visuals, fishing village scenes, travel documentaries and historical films.

Four versions share the title: this first collection plus sets 2, 3 and 4. Together they fill a whole fishing harbor with real variety in shape, size and paint. Since 2022 the bundle has supported seaside renders, maritime museum displays, coastal game levels and travel guides.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
