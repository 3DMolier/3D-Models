About This Model

A collection of rusted knives paired with a steel wire brush, the starting point of a restoration job. Rust reads through color and pitting, so the blades carry the story while the brush explains what comes next. Grouped as a set, the pieces can be laid out on a workbench without searching for matching props.

Each piece follows the CheckMate specification, so scale is real and every object has a clear name. A brown oxide layer over gray steel gives the knives an abandoned look suited to horror and survival scenes.

Released in 2025, the set suits game assets, film props for crime and horror, and defense simulation inventories. Scattered in an abandoned kitchen or a survival camp, the blades tell the viewer that nobody has cared for them in years. Restoration tutorials can also use the brush and a rusty blade to illustrate the first cleaning step. Crafting systems in games often need exactly this pair, a worn tool and the means to repair it.
