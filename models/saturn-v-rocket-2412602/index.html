About This Model

This Space & Sci-Fi model - Saturn V Rocket - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $179 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 3,237,563 polygons over 3,422,331 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 19.43 x 19.43 x H110.37 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
