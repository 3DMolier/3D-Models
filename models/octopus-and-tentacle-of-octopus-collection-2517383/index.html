About This Model

A collection of octopus models and separate tentacles, made for underwater scenes, seafood shots and creature effects. Curling arms with rows of suckers are the details that make an octopus believable, and the set gives them prominence. Separate tentacles can rise from water, wrap around a ship's hull or spill off a plate in a food scene.

Every piece follows the CheckMate spec for scale and organization, which makes mixed placements simple. Wet, glossy skin with mottled color reacts well to underwater caustics and bright studio light alike. The tentacles also suit horror sequences where something unseen reaches out of dark water toward a swimmer.

Dating from 2026, the octopus set serves marine documentaries, seafood restaurant imagery and fantasy or horror games. Aquarium and marine biology lessons can show how an octopus moves and grips using its arms. Combined with rocks and coral, a single octopus turns an empty seabed into a living scene.
