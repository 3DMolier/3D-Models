About This Model

Big cats share a body plan but not a build: a lion, a tiger and a leopard differ in mass distribution, limb proportion and how the spine flexes at speed. A collection built as one family gets those differences right relative to each other, which separate purchases never quite do.

Every animal here arrives rigged and with fur grooming, which are the two things that take longest on a predator and the two things that most distinguish a convincing animal from a smooth model.

At $299 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits wildlife documentary and natural history, conservation and zoo material, advertising, film and television, and games. On sale since 2025.
