About This Model

Cherry is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 880 polygons and 884 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

7 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2017 · Updated 4 September 2026

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Cherry?

7 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Cherry mesh?

880 polygons and 884 vertices, built as polygonal quads only. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the Cherry model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
