About This Model

This Musical Instruments model - Traditional Console Organ with Bench Brown - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $89 on TurboSquid. You are getting 258,751 polygons and 266,418 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 174.86 x 173.1 x H153.6 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, film & TV VFX and architectural visualization. It suits music-video and film sets, game props, product rendering and educational material.
