About This Model

An empty leather sleeve shaped for a thin laptop, with soft corners and an open top edge. It uses 35,127 polygons and 39,239 vertices, giving the leather a gentle puffiness along the seams. Without the computer inside, the sleeve can lie flat or stand leaning against furniture.

CheckMate certification means clean quads and a logical object name throughout the file. The open mouth of the sleeve shows the lining, which helps it read as a functional case rather than a slab. Neutral styling keeps it believable in offices, dorm rooms and airport lounges.

Two versions share the $19 price: the Apple MacBook Leather Sleeve and a blue Laptop Protective Sleeve. Both were added in 2026, and Editorial Uses Only is their license, which suits reviews, news, films and documentaries. Leaning the sleeve against a desk leg is a simple way to add life to a workspace render.
