About This Model

Looking for a general asset? The Clapping Hands Emoji is a finished, render-ready model at $19. The mesh carries 1,622 polygons and 1,628 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 1024 x 1024 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
