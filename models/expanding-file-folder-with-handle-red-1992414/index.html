About This Model

A red accordion file with a carry handle and pleated sides, the kind used to keep receipts, invoices and school papers in order. The folder is dense at 155,497 polygons over 134,890 vertices, mostly spent on the pleats and the curved handle. Those pleats are what make an expanding file recognizable, so the budget goes exactly where the eye will look.

With CheckMate Pro approval and clean non-overlapping UVs, it is easy to add tab labels, embossed plastic texture or wear marks. Bright red stands out on a desk, which makes it a useful color accent in an otherwise neutral office scene. A few sheets of paper peeking out of the top pocket would make it look in use rather than fresh from the store.

It went on sale in 2022 and can be used royalty free in any commercial project. Stand it upright on a shelf or lay it flat beside a laptop to tell different stories about the same desk. Office interiors, tax season illustrations, home study scenes and stationery product renders are where this folder fits best.
