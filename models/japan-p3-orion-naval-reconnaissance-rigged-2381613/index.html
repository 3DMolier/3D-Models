About This Model

A maritime patrol aircraft is a turboprop airliner rebuilt to hunt submarines: a long fuselage full of sensor operators, a magnetic anomaly boom sticking out of the tail, and a weapons bay under the belly. That tail boom is the identification, and it is unlike anything on a civil aircraft.

The mesh is efficient at 143,915 polygons and 153,856 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and it arrives rigged so the propellers turn and the gear retracts.

This page carries a simplified build at $149 and rigged versions prepared for Cinema 4D and Maya at $219. Aimed at naval aviation and defence visualization, documentary and news graphics, film and television, training material and games. On sale since 2025.
