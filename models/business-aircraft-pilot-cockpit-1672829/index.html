About This Model

A business jet cockpit seen from the pilot's seat, with glass displays, yokes and an overhead panel for flight scenes. The 73,417 polygons build the instrument screens, throttle quadrant, switches and the curved windshield frame above. The crew seats face the panel, so the camera can sit behind the pilots for a classic takeoff shot.

Its 80,832 vertices lie on non-overlapping UVs, which helps when adding screen graphics and worn buttons. Displays come without textures, so you can drop in your own flight instruments or moving maps. TurboSquid has approved this cockpit as CheckMate Pro, so its parts are cleanly modeled and named.

At $199 it suits flight training material, private aviation films and simulator interiors for pilot schools. Available since 2021, it has also been used for luxury travel visuals and aviation safety videos. With dawn light pouring through the windshield over the clouds, it captures the calm of a long flight.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2021 · Updated 16 November 2026
