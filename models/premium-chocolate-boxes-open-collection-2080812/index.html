About This Model

Premium Chocolate Boxes Open Collection gathers several gift boxes shown with lids off, so the pralines inside face the camera in neat rows. Each box keeps its own tray of molded cavities, and the candies sit slightly proud of the edge the way real ones do.

The set passed CheckMate Pro inspection, which covers clean geometry, sensible naming and correct real-world scale. Because the boxes arrive together, a confectionery counter or a holiday table can be laid out without mixing assets from different sources. Glossy chocolate and printed cardboard react differently to light, which makes the group a handy test for a studio lighting rig.

Two versions are offered: this set at $49 and Premium Chocolate Boxes Open Collection 2 at $69 with a wider spread of designs. The bundle went on sale in 2023 and has already been picked up by a buyer. It suits packaging mockups, food styling renders, Valentine's Day scenes and gift shop interiors.
