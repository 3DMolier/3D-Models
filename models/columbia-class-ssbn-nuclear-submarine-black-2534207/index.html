About This Model

A ballistic missile submarine of the Columbia class, shown in a black hull finish for naval and defense scenes. The 31,599 polygons describe a long cylindrical hull, the sail with its planes and the stern control surfaces. With 31,859 vertices the boat stays light, so a whole fleet can sit in one scene without slowing the viewport.

TurboSquid's CheckMate rules shaped the build, so the hull arrives with logical part names and a real-world scale. Its UVs mix shared and separate islands, so check the layout before painting hull numbers or anechoic tile patterns. A matte black coating reads well at night or under overcast skies, where the hull barely separates from dark water.

Choose between two versions at $99 each: this bare hull or a military submarine already set in a water surface. Appearing in 2026, the boat fits naval documentaries, defense news graphics, strategy games and nuclear deterrence explainers on television. The water version saves time when the shot needs a surfaced boat sitting naturally in the open sea.
