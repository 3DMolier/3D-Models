About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Realistic Leaf Anatomy is a finished, render-ready model at $31. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is catalogued for medical education, training simulation and VR experiences. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualisation and medical sequences in film.

Geometry weighs in at 24,879 polygons over 25,149 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
