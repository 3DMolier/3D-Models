About This Model

A collection of clothing store fixtures for retail interiors, from rails and racks to displays used in fashion boutiques. Fixtures like these combine thin metal frames, shelves and hangers, so the set reads clean and modern in a shop. Every piece sits at real proportions, letting you plan aisles and fitting areas with believable spacing.

The collection was assembled under CheckMate guidance from TurboSquid, keeping sizes consistent and every fixture separately named. Polygon totals are not published, so test a fully stocked rack before filling an entire store floor. Brushed metal and light wood finishes suit most fashion brands, and swapping them takes only a material change.

Costing $79 since 2025, the kit suits retail design proposals, fashion brand store concepts and shopping mall game levels. Store planners use it to test layouts with clients before ordering real fixtures from manufacturers. Animators also fill a boutique with it for scenes where characters browse racks and try on outfits.
