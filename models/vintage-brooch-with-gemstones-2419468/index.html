The Brooch with Gemstones is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 40,640 polygons and 40,124 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
