About This Model

The Cabri G2 is a training helicopter designed by an engineer who had worked on far larger machines and wanted the small end done properly. A composite airframe, a shrouded tail rotor and energy absorbing seats put safety features into a two seater that used to be reserved for airliners.

The airframe holds 496,082 polygons and 507,314 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 27 maps all at 4096x4096. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $129 it suits aviation training visualization, flight simulation, general aviation marketing, film backgrounds, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2017.
