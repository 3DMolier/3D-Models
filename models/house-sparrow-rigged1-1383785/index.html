About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The House Sparrow Rigged(1) is a finished, render-ready model at $199. You are getting 24,220 polygons and 34,308 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

29 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 300 x 400 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2019 · Updated 4 September 2026
