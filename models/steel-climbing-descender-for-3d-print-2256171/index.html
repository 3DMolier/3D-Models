About This Model

A steel climbing descender shaped as a compact figure eight, prepared both for rendering and for printing as a replica. Its 1,900 polygons define the large rope ring, the smaller clip ring and the thick rounded body between them. The 948 vertices are half the faces, which shows a lean quad mesh that subdivides into smooth printable curves.

The closed solid shell suits 3D printing, so a slicer can turn it into a display piece or classroom demonstrator. Under the CheckMate specification, the descender opens with true size, clear naming and non-overlapping UVs ready for texturing. A brushed steel shader with slight scuffs around the rope groove makes it look used on many rappels.

Introduced in 2024, it fits climbing gym renders, mountaineering guides and outdoor equipment store product shots and catalogs. Animate a rope running through it to show how friction controls the speed of a rappel. Printed replicas let instructors hand out safe teaching samples without risking expensive load-rated equipment in class.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2024 · Updated 13 November 2026
