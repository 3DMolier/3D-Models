About This Model

A haul truck of the kind that works an open pit around the clock - the machines that carry three hundred tonnes of ore up a ramp and are tall enough that the driver climbs a staircase to reach the cab. The body, the ladder, the tyres and the tipping ram are all modelled as separate parts, so the bed can be raised and the wheels turned rather than faked in post.

The mesh runs to 668,834 polygons and 363,353 vertices across quads and tris, with non-overlapping UVs, which means the truck takes a new livery or a coat of pit dust without being unwrapped again. That is a moderate load for a machine this size - heavy enough to hold a close shot of the tyre tread and the frame lattice, light enough to put several of them in one pit without the scene falling over.

This build is rigged, so the bed tips and the wheels steer straight out of the box. Two more versions of the same truck sit on this page under All Versions of This Model, prepared for Cinema 4D and for Maya at the same $149, so a studio can pick the one that opens cleanly in the software it already runs instead of converting a rig by hand.

It is sold under TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, which covers commercial delivery without a fee per project - useful when the same truck appears in a mine flythrough, a safety animation and a trade-show loop in the same year.
