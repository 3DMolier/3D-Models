About This Model

This refrigerated warehouse room lines steel shelving along insulated walls and closes with a heavy blue cold-store door. Its 883,501 polygons and 873,589 vertices fill the racks with boxes, crates and pallets, and add hinges, seals and floor markings. A camera can walk the aisles between shelves, since the room is fully enclosed with ceiling, walls and floor.

Modeling followed the CheckMate rule set of TurboSquid, covering clean topology, sensible object names and correct real scale. UV islands are laid apart without overlap, so frost, condensation or scuffed paint can go on the door and shelves later. The cool blue door against bare gray steel gives the space a clear industrial color scheme.

Introduced in 2025, the room costs $129 and may be used commercially under its Royalty Free terms. Logistics companies use it in warehouse process explainers, and cold chain training shows where pallets wait at controlled temperatures. Games also stage tense stealth scenes inside storage rooms like this, where the door alone seals the player in.
