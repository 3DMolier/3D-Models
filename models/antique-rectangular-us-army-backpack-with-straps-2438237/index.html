About This Model

Early military packs were rectangular because they were made from flat panels of canvas, and everything about how they carried followed from that: no frame, no shaping, and straps that had to do all the work of distributing a load the bag itself could not.

The model is very light at 7,145 polygons and 7,553 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplied with the straps. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $49 it suits historical and period visualization, film and television props, museum material, game characters, character accessories and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
