About This Model

Apple's clamshell iBook, the colorful translucent laptop with a built-in carry handle that defined late nineties computing. The model uses 59,842 polygons and 60,981 vertices on the rounded shell, hinge, keyboard and the open display. Its candy-colored plastic and toy-like curves make it instantly recognizable to anyone who remembers that era.

The open laptop measures 34.05 x 31.63 x H32.03 cm, a believable size on a desk or school table. StemCell certification ensures the translucent plastics and screen materials look consistent across renderers and engines. UVs are mixed, so adding screen content or subtle wear may need a quick layout check before rendering.

It is an Editorial Uses Only model, since Apple owns the name and the product design. That suits technology history documentaries, retro computing articles and period films set around the millennium. Released in 2026, it looks best on a desk lit by warm light beside a bulky old monitor.
