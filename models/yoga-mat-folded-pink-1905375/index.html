About This Model

Priced at $19, the Yoga Mat Folded is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 5,936 polygons and 2,970 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
