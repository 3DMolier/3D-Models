About This Model

Priced at $49, the Flexible Child Mannequin Balancing Pose is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 21,229 polygons over 21,350 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
