About This Model

An Ohio class boat is defined by the long flat run of deck behind the sail, which is where the missile tubes sit. That section gives the submarine its enormous length and its distinctive profile, quite different from an attack boat where the hull tapers away much sooner behind the fin.

The mesh is efficient at 208,584 polygons and 222,345 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 6 texture maps all at 8192x8192. A submarine hull is largely smooth, so the texture carries the plating seams, the flood ports and the anechoic tiling.

This page carries an animated build at $149 and a rigged version at $139. The static model at $119 covers naval and defence visualisation, documentary and news graphics, strategic analysis material, and games. On sale since 2017.
