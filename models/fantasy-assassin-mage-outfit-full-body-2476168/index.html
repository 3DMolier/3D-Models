About This Model

Fantasy Assassin Mage Outfit Full Body is a hooded rogue spellcaster in layered leather and cloth, built for fantasy games and illustrations. At 122.29 x 43.81 x H180.94 cm, the figure stands at adult human height with arms spread wide. The 42,855 polygons go into the hood, belts, buckles, wraps and the hidden blades at the hips.

Vertices total 42,266, almost equal to the faces, giving a clean game-ready balance for real-time engines. Some UV islands overlap, sharing texture space across mirrored parts of the costume to save memory. StemCell certification guarantees the costume shades the same in common game engines and offline renderers.

Released in 2025, the outfit suits role-playing games, fantasy book covers, tabletop miniature design and cosplay reference. Lit by magical glow in a dark alley, the character instantly reads as both dangerous and arcane. Swapping cloth colors quickly creates new guild members without modeling any extra costumes.
