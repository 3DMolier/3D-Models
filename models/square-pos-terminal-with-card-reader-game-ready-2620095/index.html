About This Model

A compact Square point-of-sale terminal with a card reader slot and touchscreen, ready for cafe counters and retail checkouts. The Square POS Terminal with Card Reader Game Ready runs on 3,423 polygons, light enough for every till in a game mall.

Its 3,070 vertices define the slim screen, angled body and the slot where a card slides in. StemCell delivery gives this terminal several native file formats, each generated from one source scene. The white housing stands out against wooden counters, so it reads instantly as the place where a customer pays.

Released in 2026, the terminal costs $39 and suits small business interiors, restaurant scenes and fintech explainers. Place it beside a coffee machine at a bakery, or at a boutique checkout next to a folded bag. Software teams demonstrating payment apps can show the device in context instead of on a plain background. Low geometry makes it easy to duplicate across a food court full of separate stalls.
