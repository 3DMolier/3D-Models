About This Model

Fitness Trampoline with Handle is a compact rebounder with a round jumping mat, short legs and a T-shaped bar to hold during workouts. Its 33,478 polygons and 34,274 vertices shape the springs around the frame, the padded handle and the taut mat surface.

Thanks to CheckMate Pro certification, the trampoline imports at real size with organized parts and clean geometry. Each UV island is separate, which helps when adding a printed pattern on the mat or foam texture on the grip. The handle lets trainers in animation hold steady while bouncing, a detail missing from plain mini trampolines sold for children.

Our catalog has carried it since 2021 for home gym visualization, fitness app content and sports store interiors. Place it in a living room corner beside a yoga mat and dumbbells to suggest an active daily routine. Exercise class scenes can line up several rebounders in neat rows for a group cardio session led by an instructor.
