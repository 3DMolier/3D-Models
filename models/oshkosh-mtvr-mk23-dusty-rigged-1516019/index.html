About This Model

A dusty military tactical truck, the MTVR MK23 cargo variant, rigged for convoy, desert and base scenes. High ground clearance, big all-terrain tires and a long cargo bed give it the stance of a serious off-road hauler. A coat of dust on the paint shows hard use, which suits desert operations better than a showroom finish.

The rig lets wheels turn and steer, so the truck can drive through a convoy instead of sliding. CheckMate Lite certification covers the basics of clean geometry, sensible naming and real-world scale. The open cargo bed accepts crates, troops or equipment props, so each truck in a column can look different.

On sale since 2020 at $179, it suits military simulation, war films and strategy games. Logistics training content uses trucks like this to show how supplies move across rough terrain. Dust clouds behind a moving column make a dramatic shot that this rigged truck handles well.
