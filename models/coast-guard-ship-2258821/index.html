About This Model

This Ships & Boats model - Coast Guard Ship - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $199 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 2,509,425 polygons and 2,598,479 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
