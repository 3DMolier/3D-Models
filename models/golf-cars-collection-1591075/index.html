About This Model

Golf cars move players and their bags between holes, and they also carry guests around resorts, campuses and retirement communities. The Golf Cars Collection gathers several of these small electric vehicles in one set for leisure scenes.

Each car has an open cabin under a flat canopy roof, with a bench seat and a bag rack at the rear. Small wheels and a short wheelbase make them nimble on narrow cart paths beside fairways. The collection carries CheckMate Pro approval, so each vehicle is clean and correctly proportioned for placement.

Offered since 2020, the set fits golf course designs, resort masterplans and hospitality presentations. Landscape architects park a row of cars beside the clubhouse to show arrival areas and parking. Animators drive one along a winding path between greens at sunrise, with long shadows across the grass. Game developers use the carts as drivable vehicles in sports games and as background traffic in resort maps.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
