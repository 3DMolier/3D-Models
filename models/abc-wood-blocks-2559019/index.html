About This Model

Priced at $29, the ABC Wood Blocks is a finished Toys & Games model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 7,936 polygons and 8,204 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That sits in low-poly territory, so it stays cheap to instance across a crowd scene or a game level. 5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and game assets. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2026 · Updated 17 August 2026
