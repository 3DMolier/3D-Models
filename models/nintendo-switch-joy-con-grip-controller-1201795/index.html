About This Model

Nintendo Switch Joy Con Grip Controller is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 28,144 polygons and 28,590 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 8 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096.
