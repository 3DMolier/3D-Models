About This Model

This Boavision dual lens security camera pairs a fixed lens with a pan and tilt head, watching a driveway or a shop entrance. The 18,538 polygons and 18,802 vertices model the two lens housings, the infrared rings, the antennas and the wall bracket. Two stacked eyes give the camera an unmistakable profile, so it reads as surveillance even at a distance.

StemCell preparation gives this camera PBR materials that transfer unchanged between the supplied file formats. Unique islands cover the lens glass while the white plastic shell reuses texture space, a mixed UV approach. Mounted under an eave with a small status light glowing, it adds quiet tension to a night exterior.

A simpler Network Security Camera Single Lens Outdoor is the second version, and both are priced at $29. Pick the single lens unit for a generic office corridor, and the dual lens model for a home security story. New to the range in 2026, it suits smart home explainers, security product pages and crime drama sets.
