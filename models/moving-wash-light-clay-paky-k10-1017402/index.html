About This Model

Moving Wash Light is a detailed Lighting asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 51,551 polygons and 54,916 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

7 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, event visualization and film & TV VFX. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits interior and architectural visualization, product rendering and game environment dressing.
