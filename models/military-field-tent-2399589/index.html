About This Model

The Military Field Tent is a production-ready Sports & Recreation 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 125,878 polygons and 128,453 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
