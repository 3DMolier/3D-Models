About This Model

A frosted layer cake topped with lit candles and piped decorations, ready to anchor birthday parties and celebration scenes. The 232,072 polygons capture the swirled cream rosettes, the drips along the side and the twisted wax of each candle. Behind the faces sit 120,141 vertices, giving a smooth, appetizing surface for close slow camera moves around the table.

Cream, sponge and wax colors come from materials you control, as no image maps arrive with the cake. The UV layout uses overlapping islands, so repeated decorations share texture space and any painted detail will mirror between them. StemCell certification means the dessert renders the same in each delivered format, with no material rebuilding after import.

Dating from 2024, it is priced at $39 and suits greeting cards, party invitations and family celebration animations. Bakeries showing custom orders, event planners and games with party levels all need a dessert like this at the center of a table. Add a soft flame glow and dim the room lights, and the moment just before the wish feels real.

Questions About This Model

What does StemCell mean for the Birthday Celebration Cake with Candles model?

StemCell covers both geometry and shading: the model is delivered across formats with materials that stay equivalent instead of needing per-engine conversion.

Which industries use the Birthday Celebration Cake with Candles model?

This listing is cataloged for Advertising, Film & Video Production, 3D Printing and Game Development. Those are the sectors the model was tagged for on TurboSquid, based on how comparable assets in the Food & Beverages range are bought.

Does the Birthday Celebration Cake with Candles model include materials and textures?

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. As a StemCell asset it ships with PBR materials that stay consistent across the delivered formats.

Can the Birthday Celebration Cake with Candles model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.
