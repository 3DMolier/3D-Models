About This Model

A collection of money storage objects, the boxes, cases and containers people use to keep cash and valuables secure. Grouping them as the Money Storages Collection gives a finance scene several related props that share one visual style. Closed lids, sturdy hinges and locking hardware make the purpose of each piece clear without explanation.

It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro certification, the detailed tier that reviews topology, UVs, materials and scene setup. Each container is a separate object, so it can be opened, placed on a desk or hidden in a drawer. Metal and leather style surfaces give a texture artist clear areas for wear, scratches and shine.

This set has been available since 2022, and finance visuals can use it Royalty Free. Banking explainers, personal finance blogs and heist games use money containers to make abstract savings feel tangible. Placed on an office desk beside a calculator, the collection signals budgeting and careful saving at a glance.
