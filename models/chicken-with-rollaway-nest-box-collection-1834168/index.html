About This Model

A hen sits beside a rollaway nest box, a design that lets eggs roll out of reach so they stay clean for collection. The bundle lists no polygon figures, yet the feathers, the comb and the red throat skin of the bird are all modeled. The nest box shows its sloped floor and collection tray, so the farm technique is clear in one frame.

TurboSquid gave the bundle CheckMate Pro after reviewing topology, names and the proportions between bird and box. The hen and the nest are separate models, letting the bird stand in a yard while the box is mounted in a coop. Egg shapes in the tray can be moved or duplicated to show a full day of laying.

Since 2021 the set has suited poultry farming videos, agricultural courses and rural game scenes under a Royalty Free license. Equipment makers use visuals like this to explain how rollaway boxes keep eggs clean. Children's books on farm life also show the hen beside its box to explain where eggs come from.
