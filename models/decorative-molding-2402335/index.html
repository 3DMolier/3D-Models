About This Model

This Furniture & Interior model - Decorative Molding - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $11 on TurboSquid. You are getting 13,515 polygons and 16,603 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
