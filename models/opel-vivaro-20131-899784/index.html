About This Model

The 2013 Opel Vivaro, a mid-size European panel van used by tradespeople, couriers and shuttle services across city streets. Its sloping nose, tall roof and sliding side door give the van the practical outline that fits delivery and service scenes. A van like this is often the background vehicle that makes a street look lived in, parked outside shops and workshops.

The Vivaro holds CheckMate Lite certification, which confirms the van opens properly in common 3D software. Clean, flat side panels leave room for invented company lettering, so the van can belong to any local business. Opel being a real carmaker, Editorial Uses Only applies, which suits journalism, films and documentaries.

Selling since 2015, the van has long served as traffic in city animations and as a work vehicle in architectural renders. Tradespeople unloading tools from the side door or a courier stopping at the curb are typical moments it supports. Driving simulations set in European towns also gain realism from a familiar commercial van among passenger cars.
