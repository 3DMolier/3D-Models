About This Model

A Jack Daniels whiskey bottle paired with a cigarette case, a moody still life for bar scenes and editorial illustrations. The square bottle with its black label sits beside a slim case, the kind of pairing seen on a bar counter late at night. The squared shoulders of the bottle catch light along the edges, which makes the glass read well in dim scenes.

Every piece in the collection meets CheckMate requirements, so the bottle and case open as clean separate objects. Amber whiskey behind dark glass needs a careful refraction setup, and a warm backlight brings out the color.

The set came out in 2024 for Editorial Uses Only, which fits magazine features, documentaries and news illustrations. Film and game set dressers place it on a poker table or a motel nightstand to sketch a character quickly. A glass with ice and a lighter beside the case complete a classic noir composition. Shot from a low angle with heavy shadows, the pair looks like a still from a detective film.
