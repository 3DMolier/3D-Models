About This Model

A compact pen computer for illustrators, with a glossy drawing surface, programmable side keys and a slim pressure stylus. 13 Inch Wacom MobileStudio Pro Tablet keeps its 21,186 polygons and 23,193 vertices focused on rounded corners, button strips and the pen grip. The bezel frames the screen with generous space for resting a palm.

Detailing comes from 13 maps at 4096 x 4096, covering the dark matte housing, the etched glass surface and small printed labels. The unwrap does not overlap anywhere, so the display can take any artwork or interface you want to show. The tablet earned CheckMate Pro status, so it imports at real-world size with tidy modeling throughout.

Available since 2019, the tablet fits design studio interiors, creative workflow videos and digital art tutorials. Lay it on a desk with the stylus beside it, or prop it on a stand next to a sketchbook and coffee mug. Artists and educators use it in videos explaining how painting on screen differs from paper.
