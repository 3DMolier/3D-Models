About This Model

Asian Man Underwear Rigged belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $199 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
