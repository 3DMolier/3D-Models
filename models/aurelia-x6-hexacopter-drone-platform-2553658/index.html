About This Model

A heavy-lift hexacopter with folding carbon arms and a central payload bay, built for survey, cinema camera and agriculture scenes. The frame spends 533,077 polygons and 543,326 vertices on motor bells, propellers, wiring and the landing gear.

Spanning 106.38 x 101.51 x H59.03 cm, it sits at true scale next to an operator or a pickup truck tailgate. StemCell certification provides native formats, and the mixed UVs pair unique carbon textures with shared space for the repeated arms. Spinning propellers can be suggested with motion blur discs, so the static blades need no rig for flight shots.

Five drones share the page: this platform, a professional aerial survey hexacopter and its simplified copy, plus two octocopters. One of the octocopters stands on landing skids, the other on a carbon frame suited to heavy camera payloads. Added in 2026, it suits drone service explainers, inspection animation and technology product renders. Seen from below, the arms form a clean star that reads well against the sky.
