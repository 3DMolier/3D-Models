About This Model

A collection featuring the Nobel Medal in Physiology or Medicine with its presentation box, for science and award scenes. The gold medal's relief and inscriptions catch light beautifully against the box's soft lining. Open and closed box variants allow a reveal moment or a quiet display shot.

The medal and box are CheckMate Pro certified, arriving as clean, well-organized objects. Polished gold rewards careful lighting, with highlights tracing every raised detail of the relief. The case lid opens on a clear hinge line, so a short animation can reveal the medal for the first time.

Since 2022 the medal set has suited science documentaries, biography films, university presentations and history of medicine content. Placed on a laboratory desk beside old notebooks, the medal tells a story of a hard-won scientific breakthrough. In a slow close-up, the engraved lettering conveys the full weight of the honor and years of research behind it.
