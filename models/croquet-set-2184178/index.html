About This Model

A croquet set is a small collection of very specific objects: mallets with long handles and cylindrical heads, hard balls in four colors, wire hoops and a centre peg. The mallets are the recognisable part, and their proportion is what separates the game from anything played with a stick and a ball.

The mesh is light at 37,430 polygons and 18,967 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the pieces modelled separately so they can be laid out, stacked or placed on a lawn.

At $39 it suits garden and outdoor leisure visualization, hospitality and country house imagery, lifestyle and summer advertising, period film and television, and games. On sale since 2024.
