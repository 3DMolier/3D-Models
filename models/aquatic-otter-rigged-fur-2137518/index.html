About This Model

Aquatic Otter Rigged Fur is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $169. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 16,620 polygons over 8,775 vertices - a light build for its class. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
