About This Model

The British fighter of the Second World War with elliptical wings, a long Merlin engine nose and a framed sliding canopy. The Royal Air Force Fighter Supermarine Spitfire LF Mk IX carries 66,592 polygons over 69,193 vertices, a sensible budget for a hero fighter. Clipped low-altitude wingtips, cannon barrels and radiator housings under the wings are all shaped with care.

Camouflage, roundels and exhaust stains come from 8 maps at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. Squadron codes can be repainted for any unit, since the UV islands never stack on one another. TurboSquid inspected the aircraft by hand and rated it CheckMate Pro for clean topology.

Six versions are offered, with static Supermarine Spitfire builds at $149 and a British WWII fighter variant at $99. Rigged copies, including one of this LF Mk IX, cost $199, and a rigged British WWII fighter is $149. Since 2017 the fighter has 22 buyers among air show promoters, war history channels and flight sim developers.
