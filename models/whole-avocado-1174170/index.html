About This Model

Whole Avocado belongs to our Food & Beverages range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 634 polygons over 635 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 6 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags food photography and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
