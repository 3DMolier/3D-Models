About This Model

Priced at $34, the Multimedia Device with Directive Microphone is a finished Musical Instruments model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 9,487 polygons and 10,016 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for event visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits music-video and film sets, game props, product rendering and educational material.
