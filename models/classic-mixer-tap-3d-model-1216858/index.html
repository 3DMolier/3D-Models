About This Model

Classic Mixer Tap is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 3,612 polygons and 3,720 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

What texture resolution ships with the Classic Mixer Tap?

2 maps at 2048 x 2048, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Classic Mixer Tap mesh?
