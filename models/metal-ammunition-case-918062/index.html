About This Model

Looking for a Weapons asset? The Metal Ammunition Case is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Geometry weighs in at 7,772 polygons over 7,820 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
