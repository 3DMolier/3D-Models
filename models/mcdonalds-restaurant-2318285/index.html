About This Model

Looking for a Furniture & Interior asset? The McDonalds Restaurant is a finished, render-ready model at $139. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

At 256,957 polygons and 276,542 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

Questions About This Model

What is the McDonalds Restaurant model typically used for?

It is cataloged for visualization, advertising and 3D printing - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

Can the McDonalds Restaurant model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

No. The model depicts a real branded product, so TurboSquid lists it under the Editorial Uses Only license. It may be used in news, commentary, education, personal projects and similar editorial contexts, but not in advertising, on merchandise or in any product offered for sale.

Which industries use the McDonalds Restaurant model?

It is tagged for Architecture, Advertising and Film & Video Production. The categorisation reflects where similar Other assets end up rather than a hard restriction - the license does not limit the field of use.
