About This Model

The Aerodynamic Sleeper Cab Tractor Unit is a long-haul semi truck with a tall streamlined cab and a bunk behind the seats. Its 401,313 polygons and 447,278 vertices go into the roof fairing, side skirts, chrome grille and twin fuel tanks. Rolling along a highway at sunset, the truck sets the scene for freight, logistics and road trips.

The cab follows CheckMate rules closely, keeping its long body tidy and correctly scaled. UVs are mixed in type, so unique company graphics may require a fresh layout on some panels. Glossy paint with sharp highlights along the fairing shows off the aerodynamic shaping.

Three versions are offered: the standard tractor at $169, a Blue Simplified edition at $149 and a Rigged edition at $199. The simplified truck suits background traffic, and the rigged version is ready for driving animation. First shown in 2026, the tractor suits logistics explainers, highway visualizations and trucking games.
