About This Model

A golden hamster with groomed fur, rigged for Blender and playing an idle animation of small sniffs and shifts. The plump body, golden coat with a pale belly and round dark eyes match the common Syrian pet hamster. Idle motion keeps the animal alive on screen while it waits, which is most of what a pet does.

CheckMate specification shapes the file, so armature, hair and actions load together without broken links. Fur strands can be hidden while you block animation, then restored for close-up renders. Twitching whiskers and a busy nose are small cues that make an idle loop read as a real rodent.

New for 2026 at $179, the hamster suits pet store displays, children's animation and veterinary education. Placed in a cage scene with bedding and a wheel, it becomes a ready home pet vignette. Games set in houses use it as a living detail on a shelf or desk.
