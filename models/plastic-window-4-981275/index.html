About This Model

Plastic Window 4 is a detailed Architecture Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $9. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

Counted at the source, the model is 14,357 polygons and 15,490 vertices.
