About This Model

USV Drone is one of the Ships models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for defense simulation, naval simulation and aerospace visualization. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

The mesh carries 28,902 polygons and 31,627 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

Questions About This Model

Which industries use the USV Drone model?

Military & Defense, Simulation, Aerospace, Film & Video Production and Game Development are the primary industries on this listing, though the Royalty Free license puts no limit on where the model is actually used.

Can the USV Drone model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Can the USV Drone model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

What is the USV Drone model typically used for?

Stated applications are aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.
