About This Model

The NYPD Cop T-Pose is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $179 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 89,765 polygons and 94,088 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 19 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
