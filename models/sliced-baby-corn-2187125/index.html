About This Model

Sliced baby corn shows young cobs cut into rounds, revealing pale kernels around a soft core. It needs only 676 polygons, so a handful can garnish many dishes in the same scene.

The mesh has 1,295 vertices, more than its faces, which happens when cut pieces carry separate edges. The unwrap has no overlaps, so a creamy yellow surface and slightly darker kernel ridges paint easily. TurboSquid listed the slices in its CheckMate Pro tier following a review of scale and polygons.

Since 2024 the slices have been ready for food visualizations, recipe videos and Asian restaurant menus. Add them to a wok with peppers and noodles for a colorful stir fry, or scatter them over a salad. Low cost geometry suits game kitchens and mobile cooking titles that need many ingredients on screen. Glossy sauce on the surface helps the slices look freshly tossed in a hot pan.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2024 · Updated 13 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Sliced Baby Corn model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

What is the Sliced Baby Corn model typically used for?

The listing names food photography and film & TV VFX as the main applications. In practice it also works anywhere a finished food & beverages object is needed without modeling it from scratch.

How heavy is the Sliced Baby Corn mesh?

676 polygons and 1,295 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. 676 polygons reads as economical next to the roughly 8,300 median of our Food & Beverages range.

How clean is the geometry on the Sliced Baby Corn model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.
