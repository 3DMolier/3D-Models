About This Model

Looking for a Food & Beverages asset? The Beef Burger with Packaging Container is a finished, render-ready model at $79. Geometry weighs in at 139,214 polygons over 141,634 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 26.89 x 14.99 x H9.2 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags food photography as the intended applications. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
