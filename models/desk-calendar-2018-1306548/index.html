About This Model

Desk Calendar 2018 is one of the models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $19. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for interior design visualization, advertising and architecture presentation. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for interior visualisation, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

The mesh carries 40,386 polygons and 39,958 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. Texture work runs to 6 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
