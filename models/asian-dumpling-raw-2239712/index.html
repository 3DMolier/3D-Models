About This Model

A raw pleated dumpling with a pinched crescent seam, this food model shows uncooked dough before steaming or frying. Only 719 polygons shape the folds and the plump filling, so a whole tray of dumplings costs almost nothing. There are 714 vertices, nearly equal to the faces, which leaves a tidy closed shell ready for subdivision.

Meeting TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, the dumpling has clean geometry, realistic size and naming that is easy to follow. An unwrap without overlaps gives the floury surface and the darker pleats their own space for flour dust. Arrange a dozen on a bamboo steamer or a floured board, and a kitchen scene shows dinner being prepared.

A cooked dumpling is the second version, so raw and steamed stages are both covered, each for $19. Available since 2024, the model fits recipe videos, Chinese New Year scenes and restaurant menus for dim sum houses. Food channels use the two versions side by side to show what steaming does to the dough.
