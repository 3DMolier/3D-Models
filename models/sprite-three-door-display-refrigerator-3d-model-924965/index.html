About This Model

Priced at $29, the Sprite Three Door Display Refrigerator is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
