About This Model

The Sukhoi Su-47 Berkut Russin Jet Fighter is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 88,307 polygons and 95,490 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 18 of them, peaking at 8192x8192, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
