About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Sukhoi Su-47 Berkut Russin Jet Fighter is a finished, render-ready model at $149. Counted at the source, the model is 88,307 polygons and 95,490 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 18 maps at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
