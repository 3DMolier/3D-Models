About This Model

Sea Shell 2 is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 2,194 polygons and 2,196 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
