About This Model

The Red Oak Young Tree Winter is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
