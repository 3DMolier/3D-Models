About This Model

Realistic Teenage Boy is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $119. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 41,863 polygons and 43,171 vertices, a light load for a scene. Maps: 13 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
