About This Model

Teenage Boy is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $119. Counted at the source, the model is 41,863 polygons and 43,171 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

13 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2018 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
