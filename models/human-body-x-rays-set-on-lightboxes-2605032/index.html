About This Model

A row of wall-mounted lightboxes displays radiographs of the skull, chest, spine, pelvis, hands and knees for radiology room scenes. The set holds 33,518 polygons and 33,819 vertices in pure quads, with the budget spent on frames, switches and panel edges.

Laid out end to end the boxes span 323.98 x 4.71 x H104.66 cm, enough to cover a whole consultation wall. Each panel is thin and flat, so an emissive material behind the film produces the familiar cold glow of a viewer. The thin profile lets the whole set hang flush against tiled or painted walls without visible gaps. CheckMate certification confirms clean topology and accurate scale for medical visualization pipelines.

The Human Body X Rays Set on Lightboxes appeared in 2026 and sells at $39 as one complete wall. It suits hospital dramas, anatomy lessons, VR training for radiographers and diagnostic room walkthroughs for clinic designers. Swapping the film textures lets you show any case history a story calls for.
