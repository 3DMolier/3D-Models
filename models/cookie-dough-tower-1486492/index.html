About This Model

A tall stacked tower of cookie dough scoops with chocolate chips pressed into every layer, this dessert is a playful food centerpiece. Its 78,350 polygons capture lumpy dough surfaces and embedded chips, which is where close food shots need the detail. There are 77,990 vertices, almost the same count as faces, describing closed organic shapes without stray points.

Five texture maps at 4096 x 4096 give the dough its grainy surface, the chips their gloss and subtle baked tones. The tower reached CheckMate Pro after TurboSquid looked over its topology, real size and naming. Dust it with powdered sugar in the shader, and the stack reads as a fresh bakery creation.

A larger cookie dough house at $69 is the other version on the page, while this tower costs $19. Launched in 2019, the tower fits bakery menus, children's party scenes and candy-themed game levels. Its whimsical silhouette also works for festive cards and seasonal kitchen scenes with a fairy tale feel.
