About This Model

This Dart Board 2 Set pairs a classic bristle board, with its numbered ring and wire spider, with darts ready to throw. The sectors alternate dark and light, while the doubles and trebles rings carry the traditional red and green. The wire spider dividing the sectors catches thin highlights, which sells realism in close shots.

Every piece meets CheckMate specification, so board and darts open clean and separately named. Hung on a pub wall above a chalk scoreboard, the board sets a relaxed evening mood instantly. Darts stuck into the board at different angles add story, showing a game already in progress.

On sale since 2015, the set suits pub interiors, game rooms, sports bars and casual games. Interior designers use it to give basements, garages and man caves a lived-in leisure corner. Animators can throw the darts along simple arcs, ending each one with a satisfying thud near the bullseye.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
