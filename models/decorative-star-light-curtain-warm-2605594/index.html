About This Model

A curtain of warm white fairy lights with hanging stars, made for cozy windows, holiday backdrops and party walls. The 24,902 polygons trace thin wires, small bulbs and star shapes that glow nicely with emissive shaders. It spans 202.21 x 21.28 x H147.84 cm, wide enough to cover a living room window.

Bulb glass and the warm emissive tint survive the trip between formats, as StemCell certification requires. The UVs are laid out without overlaps, so you can bake glow maps or swap the light color cleanly. Vertices reach 25,064, light enough to combine several curtains in one festive interior.

Released in 2026 at $29, the curtain suits Christmas interiors, wedding decor renders and cafe ambience scenes. Hang it behind a sofa or a dessert table and the background turns soft and festive. Strands fall at uneven lengths, which keeps the light pattern lively rather than grid-like.
