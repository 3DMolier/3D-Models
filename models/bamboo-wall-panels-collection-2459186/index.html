About This Model

A collection of bamboo wall panels for lining feature walls in spas, restaurants, bedrooms and tropical-style lobbies. The panels show the jointed rhythm of bamboo culms, with nodes that break the vertical lines at regular intervals. Several styles in one bundle let a designer test different cladding looks without hunting for separate assets.

StemCell certification provides one source asset with consistent PBR shading in every delivered format. Natural cane tones pair well with warm downlights, which pick out the rounded surface of each stalk. Tiling the panels side by side covers a long wall quickly and keeps the stalk rhythm continuous.

The bundle arrived in 2025 and works for interior design proposals, wellness centers and Asian-themed game levels. Behind a reception desk or headboard, a bamboo wall adds texture that flat paint simply cannot match. Darker stains give the same panels a moody, upscale feel for bars and lounges.
