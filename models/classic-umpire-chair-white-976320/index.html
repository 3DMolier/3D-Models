About This Model

The height of an umpire's chair is not about visibility, it is about the net. The official has to see both sides of a line that a player standing on the ground cannot judge, and the seat is set at exactly the height that makes that possible.

The model is very light at 7,152 polygons and 7,540 vertices, built in quads only with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 8 maps, 4 at 4096x4096 and 4 at 2048x2048. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a green build is offered separately.

At $29 it suits sports facility visualization, court and stadium scenes, equipment marketing, broadcast graphics, film sets and game environments. On sale since 2016.
