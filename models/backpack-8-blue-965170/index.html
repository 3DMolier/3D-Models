About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Backpack 8 is a finished, render-ready model at $49. You are getting 39,147 polygons and 39,165 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 12 textures, authored at 2048 x 2048. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2015 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
