About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Steel Bank Safe is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Geometry weighs in at 12,154 polygons over 12,228 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
