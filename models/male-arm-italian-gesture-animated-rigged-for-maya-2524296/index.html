About This Model

The famous pinched-fingers gesture from Italy, performed by a male hand that gathers its fingertips and shakes them, prepared as a Maya animation. It means roughly what do you want, and almost every viewer recognizes it at once from films, memes and street life. The motion includes the small repeated bounce of the wrist that sells the emotion.

All fingertips meet at a single point, so the hand's joints were set up to close neatly without fingers passing through each other. Knuckles, nails and the soft palm are shaped for close framing, because this gesture is always shown near the camera. The asset follows the CheckMate specification of TurboSquid, so the scale, the hierarchy and the joint names come in ready to use.

Introduced in 2026 at $149, the hand suits comedy, food and travel videos, and stickers or emotes celebrating Italian humor. Language schools can show it in lessons about non-verbal communication, where a single motion carries a whole sentence of meaning. Restaurant brands and chefs on social media also enjoy it when reacting to a dish that went wrong.
