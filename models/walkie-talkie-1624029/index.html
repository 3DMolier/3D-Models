About This Model

Priced at $39, the Walkie Talkie is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 9,219 polygons and 9,390 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX.

2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
