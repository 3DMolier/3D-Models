About This Model

Raw Wooden Character Rigged is a detailed Signage & Decor asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $69. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 19,978 polygons and 10,272 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
