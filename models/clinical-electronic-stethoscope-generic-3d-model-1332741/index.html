The Clinical Electronic Stethoscope Generic is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 4,101 polygons and 4,345 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
