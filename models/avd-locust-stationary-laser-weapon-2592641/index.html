About This Model

A fixed laser turret of the Locust type, a counter-drone system that disables small aircraft with a focused beam. Its 423,515 polygons cover the beam director, the optics, the gimbal yoke, cooling vents and the mounting base. At 44.77 x 77.06 x H78.68 cm the unit is compact enough to mount on a vehicle or a rooftop.

CheckMate guidelines governed the build, so turret and base arrive as separate, named parts ready for aiming. A mixed UV layout covers its 484,258 vertices, so plan the unwrap before adding stencils or heat discoloration. Dark lenses and a clean housing give the weapon a quiet, technical look, unlike the bulk of gun turrets.

For $89 each, the page offers this unit and a counter drone version of the same laser system. New in 2026, the turret suits defense news, counter-drone explainers, military games and future warfare scenes. Add a thin glowing beam and a falling quadcopter, and the purpose of the system is obvious.
