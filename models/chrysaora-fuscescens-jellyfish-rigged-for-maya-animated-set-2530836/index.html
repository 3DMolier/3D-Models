About This Model

A Pacific sea nettle jellyfish pulses through the water in this rigged Maya set, ideal for ocean and aquarium scenes. Its golden bell trails long maroon tentacles and frilly oral arms that ripple behind it with every gentle contraction. The slow, hypnotic rhythm of the swim makes the species a favorite in public aquariums and calm underwater footage.

The jellyfish satisfies CheckMate specification, so bell, arms and tentacles stay separate for animation tweaks later on. Maya rigs let the pulse speed vary between copies, so a group drifts with natural, unsynchronized motion through the frame. Translucent shading with blue backlight makes the bell glow the way it does in a darkened aquarium tank.

The jellyfish joined the catalog in 2026 under royalty free conditions for documentaries, aquarium screens and relaxation videos. Marine biologists explain jellyfish blooms and ocean food webs with visuals of species like this one in classrooms and talks. A swarm rising slowly through shafts of sunlight forms a mesmerizing underwater sequence for any film.
