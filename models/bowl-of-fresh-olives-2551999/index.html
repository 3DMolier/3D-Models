About This Model

The Bowl of Fresh Olives is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. You are getting 9,156 polygons and 4,634 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That sits in low-poly territory, so it stays cheap to instance across a crowd scene or a game level. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and game assets. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
