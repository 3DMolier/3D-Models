The Fox Valley Athletic Line Marking Applicator is a production-ready Tools 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
