About This Model

A collection of apple flavored lollipops in their twisted wrappers, the round candy on a stick sold at checkouts. Wrappers flare at the stick where they are twisted shut, a shape every child recognizes instantly. Unwrapped and wrapped versions sit together, so a candy jar or a mouth shot can show both states clearly.

TurboSquid inspection gave the candies a CheckMate Pro result for clean meshes and sensible names. Green candy shaders with a glossy sugar sheen bring out the tangy sour apple flavor. Wrapper artwork can follow the familiar printed pattern or an invented candy design.

Available since 2019, the lollipops suit candy store renders, children's party scenes and checkout counter displays. Held between fingers by a smiling character, a lollipop adds a playful detail to a portrait or a cartoon. A jar full of them on a shop counter creates an inviting splash of color for passing kids.
