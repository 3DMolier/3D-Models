About This Model

Lavender Bush is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 583,056 polygons and 940,852 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 3 maps, the largest at 1024x1024, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
