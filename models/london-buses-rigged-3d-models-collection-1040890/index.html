About This Model

A collection of red London double-deckers, the buses that define the city's streets from Oxford Street to the suburbs. Double-deckers are tall and boxy, so the upper deck windows and the curved front are what make them recognizable. Several buses in the set means a street can show traffic of the right kind without reusing the same body.

CheckMate Pro certification guarantees real-world scale, clean topology and consistent naming throughout the collection. Being rigged, the buses can be driven through a shot instead of sitting parked at the curb. The bright red paint needs gray sky and wet asphalt around it, the weather Londoners would recognize.

Available since 2016, the set suits city tourism videos, period and modern London films, driving games and urban visualization. Place a bus outside a red phone box or crossing a bridge and the location needs no caption. London Buses Rigged 3D Models Collection saves modeling a fleet for crowded streets and bus stations.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026

Can the London Buses Rigged Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

What is the London Buses Rigged Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How clean is the geometry on the London Buses Rigged Collection model?
