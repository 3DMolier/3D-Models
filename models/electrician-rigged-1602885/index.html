About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Electrician is a finished, render-ready model at $199. You are getting 15,127 polygons and 16,001 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 19 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 3000 x 3000. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
