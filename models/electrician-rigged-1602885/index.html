About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Electrician Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $199. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 15,127 polygons and 16,001 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Texture work runs to 19 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
