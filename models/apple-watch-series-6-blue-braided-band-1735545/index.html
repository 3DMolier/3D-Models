About This Model

This Apple Watch comes with a blue braided solo loop band, the stretchy woven strap that slips over the wrist without a buckle. The 63,432 polygons and 64,221 vertices model the rounded aluminum case, the digital crown, the side button and the textured weave. Seen at an angle, the curved glass and slim profile give it the clean look of a modern wearable.

TurboSquid rates the file CheckMate Pro, confirming a manual check of mesh quality and scale before release. Its UV layout keeps every shell separate, so the screen can show any watch face you design. Because it shows a real branded device, use is limited to Editorial Uses Only, such as tech news, reviews and educational material.

Released in 2021, the smartwatch suits product reviews, technology journalism, fitness articles and lifestyle illustrations about health tracking. Place it on a wrist model during a run or on a desk beside a phone for an everyday scene. Editors writing about sleep tracking or heart rate features can open articles with a crisp close-up of the display.
