About This Model

High-Rise Twin Column Complex with Sky Platform belongs to our Architecture & Landmarks range and is offered at $89 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 282,006 polygons and 290,149 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
