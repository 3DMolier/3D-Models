About This Model

Looking for a Food & Beverages asset? The Roman Bean In a Wooden Spoon is a finished, render-ready model at $39. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

Geometry weighs in at 5,852 polygons over 5,657 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 10 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
