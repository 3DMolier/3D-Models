About This Model

Looking for a Military Vehicles asset? The Bank Armored Car 2 Simple Interior is a finished, render-ready model at $99. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for military simulation, game environments and film production. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defence training material and military VFX shots.

The mesh carries 252,791 polygons and 261,902 vertices, which puts it in the mid-weight bracket for this category.
