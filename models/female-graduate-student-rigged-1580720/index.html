About This Model

Female Graduate Student Rigged is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $209. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 44,500 polygons and 43,466 vertices.
