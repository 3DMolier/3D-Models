About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Polka Dot Necktie is a finished, render-ready model at $19. You are getting 2,817 polygons and 2,802 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
