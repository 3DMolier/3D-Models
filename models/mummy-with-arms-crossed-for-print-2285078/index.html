About This Model

An Egyptian mummy wrapped in linen bandages, standing with arms crossed over the chest in the classic pose of royal burials. The model is prepared for 3D printing, with closed shapes and solid volumes that hold up as a figurine or display piece. Layered bandages and the visible face beneath them give the figure texture even without paint.

Being print-oriented, the mesh avoids paper-thin parts, so the wraps and folded arms survive slicing and support removal. The figure follows the TurboSquid CheckMate specification, which keeps the scale sensible and the file clean before it reaches a slicer. Painted with sand tones and dark washes, a printed figure looks like an artifact taken from a dusty tomb.

Priced at $149 since its 2024 release, the mummy suits Halloween decorations, tabletop gaming miniatures, museum gift shops and classroom projects about ancient Egypt. Escape rooms and haunted attractions can print it at larger sizes for props that visitors can touch. Teachers also use printed figures to make history lessons hands-on, letting students paint their own pharaoh.
