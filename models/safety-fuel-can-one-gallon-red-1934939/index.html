The Safety Fuel Can One Gallon is a production-ready Containers & Storage 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 125,432 polygons and 63,195 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.
