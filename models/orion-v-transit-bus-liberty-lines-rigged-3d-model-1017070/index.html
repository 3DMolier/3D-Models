About This Model

Orion V Transit Bus Liberty Lines Rigged 3D Model shows the boxy American city bus in Liberty Lines colors, a staple of New York routes. Flat sides, a squared roof line and big windows define the Orion V, and the shape is what makes it read as a working bus. Its tall windshield frames the driver clearly, which helps close shots from the curb.

Its CheckMate Pro certification was issued after TurboSquid checked the files by hand for clean structure. Fleet livery on long side panels needs even lighting, and a little road grime along the skirts adds believability.

Transit planning studies, films set in American suburbs, city traffic in games and commuter documentaries are its typical uses. Pulled up at a shelter with doors open, it gives a street scene a believable daily routine. A pair parked at a depot also makes a quiet background for a story about city workers.
