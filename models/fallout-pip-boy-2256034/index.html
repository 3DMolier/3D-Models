About This Model

This chunky wrist computer straps onto the forearm with a small screen, dials and switches, a retro-future gadget for wasteland stories. Its 49,092 polygons and 50,035 vertices go into the knobs, the screen housing, the cables and the hinged cuff. Worn on a character's arm, it reads instantly as survival tech from a ruined, irradiated world.

StemCell preparation keeps its painted metal and screen materials stable across renderers and real-time engines. With an unwrap free of overlaps, rust, chipped paint and a green glowing display can be layered on. A dusty orange light at sunset fits the atmosphere this kind of prop needs.

You get two versions, the Fallout Pip Boy and a Steampunk Wristband for Victorian fantasy. Since 2024 it has suited cosplay previews, post-apocalyptic game props, fan art scenes and retro tech renders. Light the screen from inside and the device becomes a soft glowing accent in dark bunker scenes.
