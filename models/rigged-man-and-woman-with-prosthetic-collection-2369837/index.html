About This Model

A man and a woman with prosthetic limbs, rigged for animation and made for healthcare, rehabilitation and inclusive everyday scenes. The prosthetics are modeled as distinct mechanical parts, with sockets and joints that clearly show how the devices attach. Casual clothing and confident stances present both people as active and independent, rather than defined by their prosthetic limbs alone.

Rigging lets the characters walk, sit and gesture naturally, so the prosthetic movement can be shown in real situations. Both figures follow the CheckMate specification, which gives correct real scale and clear names for every body part and device. Metal and carbon surfaces on the devices contrast with skin and fabric, drawing attention to the engineering under lighting.

Released in 2025, the pair suits rehabilitation clinic visuals, medical device presentations and diverse crowds in modern city scenes. A park path or a gym floor gives the characters a natural setting that celebrates movement and recovery after injury.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
