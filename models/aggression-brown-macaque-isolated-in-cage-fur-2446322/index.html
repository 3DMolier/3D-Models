About This Model

The Aggression Brown Macaque Isolated in Cage Fur shows an agitated monkey baring its teeth inside a metal enclosure. Its 43,131 polygons and 44,077 vertices shape the snarling face, the tense posture and the cage bars around it. Placed in a lab corridor or a rescue center, the scene raises serious questions about captivity.

Made in line with CheckMate guidelines, the macaque and the cage share consistent scale and orderly topology. Non-overlapping UVs keep fur, skin and metal separate, so each can be shaded precisely. Cold fluorescent light with hard shadows through the bars heightens the tension of the moment.

Three versions are sold at $119, the main scene plus fur-ready editions for Cinema 4D and for Maya. Each edition keeps the groom and materials adapted, so the coat looks right instantly. Released in 2025, the scene suits animal welfare documentaries, lab ethics content and wildlife rescue stories.
