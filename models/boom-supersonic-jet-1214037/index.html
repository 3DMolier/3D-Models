About This Model

Priced at $149, the Boom Supersonic Jet is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

At 879,446 polygons and 898,830 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 13 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
