About This Model

A royal king's costume with a fur-trimmed mantle, embroidered tunic and regal accessories, made for coronation scenes and fairy tale characters. The flowing cloak and ermine collar give the outfit the heavy, ceremonial presence expected of a monarch on a throne.

The costume carries CheckMate Pro approval, so geometry and real-world scale have been verified. Fur, velvet and gold trim each have their own material, which lets each surface respond differently to candlelight in a hall. The garment pieces sit as separate objects, making it simple to remove the cloak or dress a character in only the tunic.

This costume has been available since 2015 for theater design, historical dramas and fantasy game characters. A figure wearing it beside a throne instantly sets the scene in a palace. Costume designers use it to preview colors and layering before sewing the real garment. Holiday and pageant illustrations also borrow it for kings in Christmas plays and storybooks.
