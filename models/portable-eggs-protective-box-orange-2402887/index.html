About This Model

Looking for a Containers & Storage asset? The Portable Eggs Protective Box Orange is a finished, render-ready model at $19. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering.

Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are advertising, product visualization and film production. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current.
