About This Model

Glass Orange Decanter with Whiskey belongs to our Kitchen & Tableware range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

The mesh carries 2,664 polygons and 2,598 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
