About This Model

A chipmunk perched on a cut tree stump among scattered nuts, a small woodland vignette ready for forest and autumn scenes. The 40,347 polygons divide between the furry animal, the cracked bark and growth rings of the stump, and the nuts around it. Its upright pose with front paws held together is the alert, nibbling posture people know from park benches. Cheek pouches look slightly full, a small touch that hints at the nuts already stored away.

CheckMate guidance governs scale, so the stump and animal stay in proportion with ferns, rocks and leaves you add later. Non-overlapping UVs across 41,048 vertices allow you to age the stump with moss or shift the nuts toward acorns.

On sale since 2025, it suits nature education, children's animation, forest game environments and autumn greeting visuals. Royalty-free terms cover commercial use, from park signage and storybooks to paid educational apps. Low golden light through the trees and a few fallen leaves complete the scene in moments.
