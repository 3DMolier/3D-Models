About This Model

A launcher in the deployed configuration is a different vehicle from one on the road: outriggers down, the platform levelled, the erector raised. Everything that makes it mobile is now working to make it stable, because accuracy depends on the ground more than on the missile.

The model holds 1,053,225 polygons and 1,160,849 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, supplied deployed for combat. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $139 it suits defence visualization and presentation, documentary and news graphics, simulation, film and game environments, and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
