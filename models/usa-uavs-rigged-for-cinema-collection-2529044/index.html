About This Model

A collection of American military drones rigged for Cinema 4D, made for defense explainers, aerial surveillance scenes and motion graphics. Long slender wings, bulbous sensor noses and pusher propellers give these aircraft their recognizable unmanned profile in flight. Different airframes in the pack let a varied fleet share one hangar or flight line shot at the same time.

Rigs open natively in Cinema 4D, letting propellers spin and control surfaces move without rebuilding anything. Each drone meets the CheckMate specification, so the aircraft arrive correctly sized, with consistent names for every part. Gray low visibility finishes and antenna domes reflect the practical, sensor focused design of modern military drones in service.

New in 2026, the pack serves broadcast motion graphics, defense documentaries and tactical game cinematics made in Cinema 4D. Thermal camera overlays and a desert airstrip below make these drones feel like part of a live overwatch mission.
