About This Model

Ancient Spear is one of the Weapons models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $24. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for game environments, film production and historical visualization. First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 6,578 polygons over 3,295 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
