About This Model

The gold metallic bottle of Armand de Brignac Brut Gold, presented with its case and a pair of champagne flutes. Polished pewter labels and the bright gold finish are the features that identify this champagne, and both are carried by the shape and materials. The model uses only 17,031 polygons and 17,195 vertices, light enough for a full bar shelf.

UVs overlap in places, which suits the repeating case material but calls for care with unique labels. Made to CheckMate standards, the bottle, case and glasses are separate objects that can be arranged freely. The slim flutes have clean rims, which lets glass shaders refract the gold bottle behind them convincingly.

The license is Editorial Uses Only, so this 2025 model, priced at $59, fits magazines, news and lifestyle editorial. Luxury reviews, nightlife features and articles about celebration culture use it to set a premium scene. On a black lacquered table with candlelight, the gold bottle becomes the brightest point in the frame.
