About This Model

This Aircraft model - Leonardo Da Vinci Aerial Screw - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 53,782 polygons and 54,344 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 16 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
