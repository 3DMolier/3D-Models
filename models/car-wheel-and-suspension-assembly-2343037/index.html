About This Model

Wheel and Suspension Assembly is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. The mesh carries 19,921 polygons and 20,179 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
