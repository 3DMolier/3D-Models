About This Model

A yellow reel wound with anti-twisting steel wire rope, the braided cable used for cranes, winches and hoists. The 610,900 polygons go into the individual strands of the rope, the reel flanges and the central hub. Vertices number 305,998, near half the faces, which reflects heavy triangulation across the braid.

UV islands remain separate, so oil, rust and scuffed paint can be added without repeating seams. TurboSquid checked the reel's geometry and real scale before awarding CheckMate Pro. The tight braid catches thin highlights along every strand, which reads as real steel even up close.

Available since 2023, Anti Twisting Steel Wire Rope Per Reel Yellow suits industrial catalogs, port scenes and construction site renders. Placed on a dock beside a crane, the reel suggests heavy lifting work about to start. Hardware suppliers show it on product pages that explain how cable strands are laid for strength.
