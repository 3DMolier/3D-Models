About This Model

Looking for a Nature & Plants asset? The Pink Camellia is a finished, render-ready model at $39. The mesh carries 29,703 polygons and 30,084 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. Texturing runs to 3 maps at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game assets. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
