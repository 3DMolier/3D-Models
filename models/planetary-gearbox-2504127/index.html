About This Model

Priced at $19, the Planetary Gearbox is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 60,570 polygons over 60,736 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 31.99 x 24.96 x H31.99 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
