About This Model

Glass Futuristic Heart Concept is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $41. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

The mesh carries 22,472 polygons and 22,742 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
