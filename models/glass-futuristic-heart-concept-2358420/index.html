About This Model

Priced at $41, the Glass Futuristic Heart Concept is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 22,472 polygons and 22,742 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
