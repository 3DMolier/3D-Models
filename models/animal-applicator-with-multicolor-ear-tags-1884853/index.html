About This Model

An ear tag is a database entry made physical. The color codes the year or the group, the number identifies the animal, and the applicator exists because the tag has to go through cartilage in one movement to heal cleanly.

The model is light at 29,450 polygons and 14,783 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplied with multicolored tags. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $39 it suits agricultural and veterinary visualization, livestock equipment marketing, training material, e-commerce imagery, documentary work and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.
