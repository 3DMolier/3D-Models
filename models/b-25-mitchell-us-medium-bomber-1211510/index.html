About This Model

B-25 Mitchell US Medium Bomber is the twin-engine Second World War bomber with a glazed nose, twin tail fins and gun positions along the fuselage. The 190,166 polygons and 193,976 vertices define the radial engine cowlings, framed nose glass, turrets and gear, enough for a slow orbit. The bomber suits war documentaries, airshow visuals and flight simulation scenes set over the Pacific or North Africa.

Camouflage paint, bare metal and markings come from 20 maps at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096, so nose art repaints cleanly. CheckMate Pro certification means the airframe is a clean, carefully grouped build.

The page offers 3 versions: this static bomber, a rigged Mitchell and a second rigged bomber build. The rigged files are made for formation flying and takeoff sequences, while the static one suits airfield or museum stills. On sale since 2017, the aircraft has 40 sales behind it, largely from war documentaries, flight simulators and history channels.
