About This Model

This Tools model - Duplex Nails Old - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 1,856 polygons and 930 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
