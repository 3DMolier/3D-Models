About This Model

A water polo cap is a fabric cap with rigid ear guards moulded into it and a chin strap, and the ear protection is the whole reason it exists: the game is physical and the ears are vulnerable. The number on the side identifies the player, since a swimming cap is the only thing a spectator can read.

The mesh is light at 7,003 polygons and 6,962 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the ear cups and the strap.

A red version sits on this page at the same $29 - the two colours are how the teams are told apart. Both suit aquatic sports visualisation and broadcast graphics, sporting goods retail renders, character set dressing and games. Built to CheckMate specification, on sale since 2025.
