About This Model

A post mortem table is designed entirely around drainage and cleaning. The surface slopes, the sink is part of the table rather than beside it, and every edge is radiused because a joint that cannot be scrubbed is a joint that cannot be used.

The model holds 66,036 polygons and 67,106 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $49 it suits medical and forensic visualization, equipment marketing, education and training material, film and television sets, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
