About This Model

Priced at $349, the USS Gerald Ford CVN 78 is a finished Ships & Boats model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 923,450 polygons and 1,054,241 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, naval simulation and game environments.

2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
