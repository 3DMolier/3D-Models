About This Model

Removing the mirror removed the last mechanical link between the lens and the photographer's eye, and everything about camera design changed as a result: shorter bodies, shorter flange distances, and lenses that could finally be computed without a swinging mirror in the way.

The model holds 59,665 polygons and 61,238 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 29 maps, 25 at 4096x4096, 2 at 2048x2048 and 2 at 1024x1024, which is enough for close product work. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $79 it suits product and technology visualization, e-commerce and catalog imagery, advertising, studio and desk scenes, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2017.
