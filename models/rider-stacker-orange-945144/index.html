About This Model

Rider Stacker is one of the Tools models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. You are getting 145,663 polygons and 146,774 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
