About This Model

Several renderings of the novel coronavirus in one collection let science explainers show the particle from different angles and stylings. Each virion is a sphere studded with club-shaped spike proteins, the crown-like outline that gave the virus family its name. Having the variants together saves building a cluster of particles from separate purchases.

The collection is CheckMate Pro certified, so every piece opens with clean geometry, sensible naming and consistent scale. Spikes are separate protrusions rather than bumps in a texture, which keeps the silhouette sharp when the particle floats against a dark backdrop. The spherical bodies take subsurface and translucent shaders well for a microscopy look.

The bundle costs $43 and went on sale in 2020, when demand for virus imagery rose sharply, and it has since found 12 buyers. Typical uses include news graphics, medical animation, public health posters and lectures on how respiratory viruses attach to cells. Scattered at different depths with a soft focus falloff, a handful of particles fills a frame convincingly.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Coronavirus 2019-nCoV Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

Can the Coronavirus 2019-nCoV Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

What is the Coronavirus 2019-nCoV Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How clean is the geometry on the Coronavirus 2019-nCoV Collection model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.
