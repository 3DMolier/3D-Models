About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Aquarium Decoration Castle is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 15,539 polygons and 15,494 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and architectural visualization.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
