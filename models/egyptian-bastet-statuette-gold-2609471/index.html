About This Model

A golden statuette of the seated cat goddess Bastet, ready for museum displays, tomb treasures and ancient Egypt scenes. With 5,002 polygons the budget is spent on the elegant upright silhouette, ears and the long front legs. Smooth surfaces and a calm pose make it readable in silhouette against a dark backdrop.

The gold PBR finish holds steady across delivered formats, as StemCell requires, while mixed mapping and just 4,994 vertices keep the statue light for games. Low weight lets you place the goddess repeatedly along a temple corridor without slowing a scene.

New in 2026 at $39, the statuette suits archaeology documentaries, adventure games and history lessons. It stands 28.9 x 50.48 x H86.53 cm, a large ceremonial piece rather than a small amulet. Set it on a stone plinth beside torches and a tomb chamber instantly gains a guardian. Treasure hunters in a game could find it guarding a sealed burial room.
