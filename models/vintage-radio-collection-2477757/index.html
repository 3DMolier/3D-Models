About This Model

This collection of vintage radios spans wooden cabinet sets, rounded tabletop receivers and early portables for period interiors. Dials, tuning knobs and fabric speaker grilles give each radio a face, which makes them strong props in close shots. Their warm wood and Bakelite tones fit kitchens, living rooms and shop counters from the middle of the last century.

Prepared to CheckMate standards, the radios import at real scale with tidy object names and pivots. Each set is a separate object, so a shelf of radios can be arranged and rearranged freely. Glowing dial windows reward a small light placed inside, adding life to dim evening scenes.

Introduced in 2025, the collection suits period dramas, antique shop renders and wartime home scenes. A family gathered around a single radio is a classic image these props help recreate. Commercial films, games and retro interiors are open to them through a Royalty Free license.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
