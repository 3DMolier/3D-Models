About This Model

Axion Tractor With Potato Planter pairs a large farm tractor with a rear-mounted planter, ready for a spring field scene. Of its 1,316,489 polygons and 680,441 vertices, a big share goes into the deep tire treads, hitch linkage and the planter's hoppers and furrow openers. A tall cab with wide glass gives the operator a clear view, which also suits shots looking in from outside.

CheckMate Pro certification guarantees that TurboSquid inspected the files by hand before publishing them. Green bodywork, black rubber and dusty steel offer three distinct materials, and a light layer of dirt in shading sells the working look.

This tractor and implement pair has been listed since 2022, priced at $149 with Royalty Free terms. Agricultural equipment dealers, farming simulators, documentaries on food production and agronomy courses are the obvious buyers. Crossing a freshly tilled field at low sun, the pair makes a strong wide shot for any rural story. Because the planter is a separate unit, it can be unhitched and parked beside a barn as set dressing.
