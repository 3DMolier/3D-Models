About This Model

Priced at $129, the Lincoln Stretch Limousine is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 217,416 polygons over 229,757 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
