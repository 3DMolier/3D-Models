About This Model

This set gathers professional flight simulators, from full motion platforms to cockpit trainers, used to train pilots without leaving the ground. Enclosed cabins on hydraulic legs and detailed cockpit replicas give training centers an authentic look. Multiple screens and instrument panels show the technology that recreates a real flight for student crews.

The collection was put together to CheckMate specification, so each simulator arrives as its own clearly labeled object. You can fill a training hall with several machines or focus on one cockpit for a dramatic close-up. Cool blue lighting from the displays makes darkened simulator bays feel quiet and high-tech.

The Flight Simulators Collection is offered in two versions, an opening set for $139 and a follow-up set for $189. That follow-up brings additional simulator types, which helps when a large training facility needs many different machines. Available since 2024, the collection suits aviation school visuals, airline training videos and engineering presentations.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026

The product page states what ships with each format. Textures, when present, come inside the download rather than as a separate purchase. See the listing for the exact contents.

How clean is the geometry on the Flight Simulators Collection model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.

How heavy is the Flight Simulators Collection mesh?

297,686 polygons and 155,733 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the roughly 28,000 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs high at 297,686.

What is the Flight Simulators Collection model typically used for?
