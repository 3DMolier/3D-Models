About This Model

Fortune Teller Origami is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $9. Counted at the source, the model is 3,875 polygons and 2,094 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.
