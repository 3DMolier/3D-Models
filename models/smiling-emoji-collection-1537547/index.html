About This Model

Looking for a Model Bundles & Sets asset? The Smiling Emoji Collection is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
