About This Model

An angled flat paintbrush with fur bristles, ready for artist studios, craft tables and painting tutorials. The slanted bristle tip lets a painter cut sharp edges, and the fur strands give the brush a soft, believable volume. A wooden handle with a crimped metal ferrule completes the shape, so it reads correctly in both close and wide shots.

The brush passed CheckMate Pro review, so construction and scale are right for a hand-held tool. Fur bristles behave best with a hair shader, which brings out the fine strands catching light. A few dried paint traces near the ferrule make the brush look used rather than fresh from the store.

Art classrooms and hobby scenes have relied on this brush ever since it appeared in 2015. Painting tutorials, games and studio interiors can include it in paid projects. Stand it in a jar of water with other brushes or lay it beside a palette of wet paint.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
