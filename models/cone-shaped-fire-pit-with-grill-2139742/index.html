About This Model

A cone-shaped metal fire pit topped with a grill grate, built for backyard, patio and camping scenes. The 30,416 polygons shape the tapered bowl, the support legs, the vent cutouts and the round grill on top. Vertices count 17,866, a little over half the faces, a sign of triangulated curves around the cone.

UVs stay non-overlapping, letting soot, rust and heat discoloration be painted where the fire would really reach. TurboSquid granted CheckMate Pro to the pit once its mesh quality and dimensions were confirmed. With glowing coals inside and sparks rising, it becomes the warm center of an evening garden shot.

Released in 2023, Cone Shaped Fire Pit with Grill suits outdoor living catalogs, garden design renders and survival games. Sausages on the grate and folding chairs around it set up a friendly cookout scene. Landscape architects place it on stone patios to show how a terrace can be used after dark.
