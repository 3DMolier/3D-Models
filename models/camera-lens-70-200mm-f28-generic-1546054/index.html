About This Model

A long telephoto zoom lens with a fast constant aperture, it is a natural prop for photographers, sports sidelines and camera stores. Its 57,038 polygons and 65,430 vertices carry the ribbed zoom and focus rings, the switches and the tripod collar. A deep lens hood sits on the front, the kind used to block side light at stadiums and weddings.

Six texture maps at 4096 x 4096 provide the printed markings, rubber grip and the matte metal barrel. StemCell PBR materials are prepared to render consistently across several engines, which saves conversion work. The generic design carries no maker logos, so it can sit on any camera body in your scene.

Introduced in 2020, the lens supports photography courses, gear review graphics and press scenes at sports events. Attach it to a camera on a monopod to show a photographer waiting by the field for the right moment. Retail renders also line it up beside other lenses to plan glass display cases in a camera shop.
