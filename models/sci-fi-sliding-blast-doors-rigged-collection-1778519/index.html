About This Model

A collection of heavy sci-fi blast doors that slide open in sections, made for spaceship corridors and bunker entrances. Thick armored panels, warning stripes and frame clamps are what make a door look capable of sealing off a hull breach. Several designs in the set prevent every doorway in a level from looking identical.

CheckMate Pro certification gives each door correct real-world scale, so characters pass through openings of believable height. The panels are separate pieces, which makes sliding animations and partial openings straightforward to set up. Brushed metal and painted hazard markings respond well to red alarm lighting in tense scenes.

Available since 2021, the doors suit sci-fi shooters, space station levels and film previsualization of escape sequences. A door grinding shut just as a character slips through is a scene this set was made for. Escape rooms and theme park designers also borrow such doors to set a futuristic mood.
