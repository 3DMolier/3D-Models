About This Model

This super tall office tower climbs in clean glass bands far above a business district, built to anchor a modern skyline. Its slender proportions and repeated floor plates give the facade a strong vertical rhythm that reads well from great distances. A podium at street level and a slimmer crown frame the shaft, so aerial and ground shots both have a clear start.

The model was prepared to the CheckMate specification that TurboSquid publishes for clean, well-organized architectural files ready for production. Large glazed surfaces respond strongly to the sky, so an HDRI at sunset turns the whole tower amber and gold. Window patterns repeat cleanly along the height, which helps when lighting individual offices at night for a lived-in look.

Out since 2025, the tower costs $129 and is available royalty free for commercial architectural work of any size. Developers present it in skyline studies, and film crews use it to build fictional financial centers behind their action scenes. City games and flight simulators also place it at the heart of a downtown core as a landmark visible from miles away.
