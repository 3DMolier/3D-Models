About This Model

The Electric Can Opener is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $12 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 13,156 polygons over 6,939 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
