About This Model

A collection of cell phone mascot costumes, the walking smartphones seen at store openings and street promotions. Each costume turns a performer into a giant phone, with a screen face, rounded corners and arms and legs below. Different designs vary the color and screen artwork, so a cast of mascots can work a crowd together.

As the name says, the costumes are rigged, so they walk, wave and dance without manual skinning. Each figure is organized under CheckMate guidelines, with readable names for body, limbs and screen. The flat screen area is ideal for swapping in a custom app interface or a simple cartoon face.

In the catalog since 2023, the set suits retail promotion visuals, phone store interiors, explainer animations and festival crowds. A pair of dancing phones outside a shop door instantly signals a launch or sale event. Game designers also use mascot costumes as comic characters in city and shopping mall levels.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
