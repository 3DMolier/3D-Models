About This Model

Counter drone systems are a radar, a camera and a jammer bolted to the same mast, and they exist because a cheap quadcopter can shut down an airport. Detection is the hard part: the target is small, slow and flying where radar was never designed to look.

The model holds 335,506 polygons and 348,441 vertices, built in quads only with non-overlapping UVs, and supplies the defence system together with its radar set. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $119 it suits defence and security visualization, airport and infrastructure protection presentation, news and documentary graphics, simulation and game environments. On sale since 2021.
