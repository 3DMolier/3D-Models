About This Model

Black Child Girls Rigged Collection for Cinema 4D brings young girl characters with natural hairstyles and everyday clothes. Each girl carries a full body rig, ready for walking, playing and expressive gestures in animation. Added to a schoolyard, library, ballet class or home scene, they bring representation and liveliness to children's stories and picture books.

The CheckMate standard keeps naming, scale and hierarchy aligned, so mixing characters in one scene stays simple. Curly and braided hair shapes and varied outfits give every child her own look in group shots. Warm skin shading reacts nicely to soft daylight and bright interior lighting alike, keeping tones natural.

Two versions are priced at $399 each: this Cinema 4D build and a Maya build. Both contain the same girls and rigs, so the choice depends only on the software in your studio. Since 2024 the characters have served children's animation, school visualization, pediatric health content and family games.
