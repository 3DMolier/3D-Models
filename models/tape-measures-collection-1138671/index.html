About This Model

Retractable tape measures in several sizes and casings fill a workbench, a hardware store shelf or a builder's belt in one move. Each tool shows the familiar boxy housing, the metal hook at the blade tip and a thumb lock on the front of the case. Grouping them together means a carpenter's corner or a renovation scene can be dressed without hunting for separate files.

TurboSquid gave the set a CheckMate Lite rating, a basic review confirming that the files open and the meshes are usable. The blade exits through a narrow slot at the base of each case, a detail that reads well in close product shots. Materials can be adjusted per tool, letting you match a house color or keep the classic yellow and black look.

The bundle dates from 2017 and still suits interior renders, construction training slides and game levels set in garages or workshops. Scattered across a plywood floor next to a level and a pencil, the tapes tell a story of work in progress. Animators also hand one to a character measuring a doorway, since the compact case fits naturally in a hand.
