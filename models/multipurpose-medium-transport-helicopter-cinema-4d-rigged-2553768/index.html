About This Model

The Multipurpose Medium Transport Helicopter Cinema 4D Rigged is a utility rotorcraft with a roomy cabin and sliding doors for troops or cargo. Its 249,105 polygons and 260,092 vertices go into the main rotor head, the engine cowlings and the landing gear. Hovering over a forest clearing or a ship deck, the helicopter handles rescue and supply scenes alike.

CheckMate specification guided the build, so the fuselage panels are clean and the scale is correct. Non-overlapping UVs give space for unit markings, exhaust soot and worn paint around the doors. Motion blur on the spinning blades sells flight better than any static pose can.

Three versions are available, each costing $199, including a rig in Maya and a Multipurpose Medium Utility Transport Helicopter edition. The rigging spins the rotors and moves parts, so takeoff and landing need no extra setup. Released in 2026, the aircraft fits military films, rescue training and aviation simulators.
