About This Model

The Starliner is a capsule designed to be reused and to land on ground rather than water, which is unusual for an American spacecraft. With the hatch open it stops being a sealed object and becomes something a crew climbs into, which is a more useful image for most work.

The model is light at 53,814 polygons and 56,375 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the airlock open. It is built to CheckMate specification, and the library also holds the closed capsule and a transport configuration.

At $99 it suits space program visualization, documentary and news graphics, museum and education work, film, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.

Questions About This Model

How clean is the geometry on the Boeing Starliner Spacecraft with Open Airlock model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.

The listing names aerospace visualization, film production and simulation as the main applications. In practice it also works anywhere a finished aircraft object is needed without modeling it from scratch.
