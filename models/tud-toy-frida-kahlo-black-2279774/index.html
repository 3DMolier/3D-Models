About This Model

The TUD Toy Frida Kahlo Black is a production-ready Toys & Games 3D model, priced at $17 on TurboSquid. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.

Geometry weighs in at 5,396 polygons over 5,513 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
