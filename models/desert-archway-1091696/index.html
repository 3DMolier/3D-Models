About This Model

The Desert Archway is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 11,573 polygons and 11,575 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
