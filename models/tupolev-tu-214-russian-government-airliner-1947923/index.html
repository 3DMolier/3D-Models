About This Model

Priced at $139, the Tupolev Tu-214 Russian Government Airliner is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 493,637 polygons and 529,708 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
