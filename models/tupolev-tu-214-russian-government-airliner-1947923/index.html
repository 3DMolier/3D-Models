Tupolev Tu-214 Russian Government Airliner is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $139. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
