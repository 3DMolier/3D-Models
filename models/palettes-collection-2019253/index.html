About This Model

Several painter's palettes come together in this set, so a studio corner or an art class scene gets its mixing surfaces in one purchase. Each palette carries the familiar thumb hole and curved edge, shapes that read instantly even when the boards lie half hidden under brushes. Because the pieces arrive as separate objects, you can scatter them across a table or stack them on a shelf without cutting meshes apart.

The bundle carries CheckMate Pro certification, TurboSquid's higher inspection tier for clean geometry, sensible naming and correct scale in the scene. Flat boards like these take paint smears well, so a texture artist can layer dried color blobs over the base surface. Thin edges are modeled with real thickness, which keeps the rims from flickering when light grazes them at a low angle.

The set has been available since 2023 and sells for $34, a modest price for an entire group of studio props. Illustrators building painting tutorials, game artists dressing an attic workshop and animators staging a classroom usually reach for it.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
