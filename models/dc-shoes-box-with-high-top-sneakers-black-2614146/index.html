About This Model

An open shoe box with a pair of black high top sneakers, ideal for retail shelves, unboxing scenes and teen bedrooms. The 23,820 polygons go into the padded collars, laces and treaded soles that sell the shoes up close. Laces run in a proper criss-cross, a detail viewers notice first on footwear.

StemCell certification carries the black suede and rubber shading unchanged into every delivered format. The box lid and sneakers remain separate pieces, and UV mapping varies between them. Vertices total 23,567 for the whole set, light enough for store shelves full of boxes.

New in 2026 at $59, the model suits skate shop interiors, product catalogs and lifestyle renders for streetwear shops. The box with sneakers measures 38.55 x 36.94 x H36.71 cm, true to a real shoebox on a store shelf. Tilt one shoe out of the box to stage a fresh purchase, and the tall ankle shape stays recognizable in wide shots.
