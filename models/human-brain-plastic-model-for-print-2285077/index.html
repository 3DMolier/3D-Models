About This Model

A printable human brain in the style of a plastic classroom model, ready for an anatomy shelf or a lab desk. The hemispheres, folds and cerebellum are shaped clearly, so the print reads as a brain even at palm size. The model is closed and solid, which keeps slicing simple for resin and filament printers alike.

It was prepared to CheckMate specification, so file names stay clean and scale is set from the start. A pink or grey primer followed by a gloss coat gives it the look of a real teaching model. Scale it up for a museum demo piece or down for a desk ornament without losing the main grooves.

Since its 2024 release it has helped teachers, medical students and makers who want a physical brain model. Neuroscience outreach events print several and let visitors handle them while learning brain regions. Painted by region, the print becomes a hands-on map of lobes for school lessons.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2024 · Updated 13 November 2026
