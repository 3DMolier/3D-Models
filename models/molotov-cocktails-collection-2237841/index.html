About This Model

A collection of improvised incendiary bottles with cloth wicks stuffed into their necks, made for riot, war and action scenes. Bottles vary in shape and fill level, so a row of them on a crate never looks copied. The rag fuses hang loosely over the glass, ready for a flame effect or a particle fire at the tip.

Built to CheckMate specification, the set shares one consistent scale with clear names for each bottle. Clear and tinted glass with liquid inside calls for refraction shaders, which pay off under firelight or street lamps.

Released in 2025, the collection serves shooter games, protest scenes in film, historical war dramas and survival levels. Lined up on a table in a basement, the bottles tell the viewer that resistance or rebellion is being prepared. One bottle in a character's hand works as a hero prop, while the rest dress the background. Thrown in slow motion, a single bottle becomes the trigger for an explosion or a burning barricade.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Updated 12 November 2026
