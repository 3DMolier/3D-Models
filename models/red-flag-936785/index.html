About This Model

Red Flag belongs to our Architecture Landmarks range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

At 3,636 polygons and 3,669 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 2 of them, peaking at 2048x2048, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

Questions About This Model

What is the Red Flag model typically used for?

It is cataloged for visualization, advertising and 3D printing - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

Can the Red Flag model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Can the Red Flag model be used in a commercial project?

Yes. It is sold under TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, which covers commercial use in games, film, advertising and visualization without per-use fees.

Which industries use the Red Flag model?

It is tagged for Architecture, Film & Video Production and Advertising. The categorisation reflects where similar Signage & Decor assets end up rather than a hard restriction - the license does not limit the field of use.
