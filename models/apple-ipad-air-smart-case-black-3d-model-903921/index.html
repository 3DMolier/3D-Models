About This Model

This Apple iPad Air Smart Case in black wraps the back of the tablet and folds its front cover into a desk stand. The segmented cover hinges along its creases, so the tablet can stand for typing or lean back for watching video. The cover segments are shown folded, which makes the case read as an in-use accessory.

A CheckMate Lite rating confirms clean geometry and logical naming for the case and tablet. The black finish has a soft-touch look that contrasts with the bright screen and the silver edges of the tablet. Its matte surfaces keep reflections soft, so the screen stays the focal point.

Since 2015 mobile app studios, accessory sellers and tech illustrators have chosen it 14 times for their mockups. Apple branding keeps it within editorial uses, such as gadget reviews and tech news. Folded into a stand next to a keyboard, it sets up a compact mobile workstation for a coffee shop scene.
