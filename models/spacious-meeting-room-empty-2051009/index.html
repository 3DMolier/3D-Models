About This Model

This Furniture & Interior model - Spacious Meeting Room Empty - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $31 on TurboSquid. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers.
