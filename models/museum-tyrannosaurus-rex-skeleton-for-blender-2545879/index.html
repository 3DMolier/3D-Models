About This Model

The mounted T-rex is a piece of museum design as much as palaeontology. The skeleton is posed, braced and lit to be walked around, and generations of visitors know the animal by that mount rather than by any reconstruction of its flesh.

This build is prepared for Blender, so it opens as a native scene rather than an import, and is supplied as a static mount. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $179 it suits museum and exhibition work, palaeontology and science education, documentary and film, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
