About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Blimp Airship Generic is a finished, render-ready model at $99. You are getting 107,315 polygons and 116,816 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 24 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
