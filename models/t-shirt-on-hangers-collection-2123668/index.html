About This Model

Plain T-shirts hung neatly on hangers in several colors, ready to fill clothing racks, closets and apparel shop displays. Soft draped folds at the shoulders and hem make the fabric read as real cotton rather than stiff plastic. Several shirts in the pack let a rail look full without obvious copies side by side.

The pack passed the CheckMate Lite review, which confirms clean files and sound geometry on import. Blank fronts make the set an easy base for printing your own designs, logos or slogans for merchandise mockups.

Released in 2023, the T-Shirt on Hangers Collection suits fashion e-commerce pages, store interior renders and apparel brand presentations. Hang a row on a chrome rail under spotlights, and a boutique corner is dressed in moments. Wardrobe scenes in bedrooms and hotel rooms also gain realism from a few shirts on hangers. Print shops preview client artwork on hangers like these before running the first batch of garments.
