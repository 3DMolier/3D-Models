A young Asian girl in national costume brings cultural festivals, travel scenes and educational content a charming character. Bright layered fabrics, decorative trims and a traditional hairstyle set her apart from everyday modern children. The rig is prepared for Maya, so animators can stage her dancing, bowing or walking in a parade.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms her topology, texture layout and naming passed TurboSquid's most rigorous check.
