About This Model

Looking for a Nature & Plants asset? The Stack of Logs with Bark is a finished, render-ready model at $34. You are getting 4,380 polygons and 4,412 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The object measures 153.17 x 281.47 x H90.8 cm, modelled at true scale.
