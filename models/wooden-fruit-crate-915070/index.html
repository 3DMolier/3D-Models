About This Model

This Containers & Storage model - Wooden Fruit Crate - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
