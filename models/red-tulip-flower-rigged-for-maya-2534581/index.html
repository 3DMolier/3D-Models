About This Model

A single red tulip on a long green stem, rigged for Maya so the flower can sway, bend or open on cue. The cup-shaped bloom is built from smooth overlapping petals that curve gently inward at the tips. Two broad leaves wrap the base of the stem, giving the plant the familiar spring silhouette.

Bones run through the stem and petals, which makes wind loops or blooming sequences quick to animate. The scene adopts CheckMate naming, so every petal and control can be found without guesswork. Saturated red against a fresh green stem makes the flower pop even in a crowded bed.

Released during 2026, the Red Tulip Flower Rigged For Maya is a light spring asset. Florist apps, spring greeting animations and Dutch tulip field scenes are typical places to use it. Duplicated across a field with small random rotations, the tulip builds a vivid carpet of color in minutes.
