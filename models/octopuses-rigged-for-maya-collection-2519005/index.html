About This Model

The same family of octopuses delivered as a Maya scene, ready for animators working in film and game pipelines. Rounded heads, soft webbing between the arms and sucker rows give each creature a convincing marine anatomy. Arms are long enough to wrap around props, which makes these animals useful for playful or menacing performances.

CheckMate specification governs the build, keeping proportions steady across the whole group when they appear together. Native Maya files spare a studio the cleanup that conversions usually demand, from renamed nodes to rebuilt shaders. Wet, slightly translucent skin is the key look, and subsurface scattering in Arnold sells it best.

This Maya edition launched in 2026, costing $190 with royalty free rights for productions. Feature animation, creature VFX, marine documentaries and underwater levels in games make good use of the set. An octopus slipping out of a shipwreck porthole is a classic shot that this pack can deliver quickly.
