About This Model

A snowboarder grabs the board mid-jump in this Maya animation, caught in one of freestyle riding's signature poses. One hand reaches down to the edge while the knees pull up, turning body and board into a compact, stylish shape. The pose communicates confidence and control, exactly what viewers expect from a skilled park rider.

The athlete is assembled according to CheckMate specification, keeping the rig, board and outfit neatly organized. Maya animators can extend the hang time or combine the grab with a spin for more ambitious tricks. A low winter sun behind the rider creates a glowing rim around the board and gloves.

Available royalty free from 2026, the jumper fits winter sports games, ski resort videos and youth sports features. Coaches can step through the animation to demonstrate how riders tuck before reaching for the board. Cut between a wide shot of the jump and a close up of the grab for a stylish edit.
