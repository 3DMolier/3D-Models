About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The All Purpose Buoy A4 is a finished, render-ready model at $19. You are getting 2,104 polygons and 2,104 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
