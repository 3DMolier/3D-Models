About This Model

Flowering Pear Tree is one of the Nature & Plants models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 2,875,129 polygons and 4,547,918 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 26 maps at 300 x 300 and 512 x 950. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
