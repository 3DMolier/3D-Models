About This Model

A woven basket filled with fresh loaves and rolls, made for bakery counters, farmhouse kitchens and rustic table settings. The Basket with Bread carries 54,388 polygons on 31,551 vertices, with most of the budget in the wicker weave and crusty surfaces. Scored tops and rounded crusts rise above the basket rim, giving the arrangement a full, generous outline.

UVs are non-overlapping, so baked crust color and flour dusting can be painted without seams across the loaves. No texture maps come with it, leaving room for golden brown bread and pale straw to match your lighting. CheckMate Pro certification means the scene opens with orderly names and geometry fit for close renders.

Introduced in 2022, the basket shows up in kitchen visualization, restaurant menus and cozy breakfast scenes. Placed on a wooden table near a window, it adds warmth and a lived-in feeling to an interior. Food illustrators can also use it as a backdrop prop behind jams, butter or a pot of coffee.
