About This Model

A cocktail style arcade cabinet, a low table with a screen facing up so players sit opposite each other and play. The flat glass top doubles as a coffee table, which made these machines popular in bars and game rooms. Joysticks and buttons sit on both ends of the cabinet, so the layout itself tells viewers it is built for head-to-head play.

TurboSquid rated the machine CheckMate Pro, meaning the model arrived with clean geometry and a well-organized file. The side panels and glass top can take custom artwork, retro game screens or a plain wood finish. Glowing buttons and a lit screen give the cabinet a warm presence in dim interior shots.

Released in 2021, it furnishes game room renders, retro bars and nostalgic scenes in films and series. A couple of characters hunched over the table at night create an instant story about friendly rivalry. Interior designers also place it in basement lounges to show how a home entertainment corner can look.
