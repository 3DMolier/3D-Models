About This Model

A collection of balloons with curling ribbons, the classic floating decoration for birthdays, openings and family celebrations. Round latex balloons and foil shapes rise on thin ribbons that curl at the ends, as real gift ribbon does. Clustered in bunches, they fill empty corners of a party room and draw the eye upward.

Prepared under CheckMate rules, each balloon and ribbon is a separate object, simple to recolor or regroup. A shiny latex finish and a bright key light give the soft highlights that make balloons look inflated. Tie a few to a chair back or a mailbox, and the scene reads as a celebration from the street.

Brand new in 2026, the set suits party invitations, event planning renders, children's animation and celebration social videos. Gently swaying ribbons are easy to animate, adding motion without any physics simulation. Release a bunch into a clear blue sky, and you have a cheerful closing shot for any happy story.
