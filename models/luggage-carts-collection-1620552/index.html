About This Model

A collection of luggage carts for airports, hotels and train stations, from flat trolleys to brass bellhop carts. Each cart has its own frame and wheels, so a terminal or a lobby can show several kinds side by side. Wide platforms and tall push handles make the carts read clearly even in a busy crowd scene.

As a CheckMate Pro set, the carts arrive with sound topology and real-world proportions in each format. Chrome tubing, rubber wheels and mesh baskets give the carts the sturdy, slightly worn look of public equipment. Every cart is separate, so suitcases can be loaded onto one while the others stand in a row.

Sold since 2020, the collection fits airport terminals, hotel lobbies, railway stations and travel-themed games. A cart piled with suitcases near the check-in desk shows a family just arriving for a long holiday. Hotel designers also use the brass bellhop cart to give a lobby visualization a touch of classic service.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
