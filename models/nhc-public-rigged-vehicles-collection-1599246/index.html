About This Model

This collection brings together rigged public service vehicles for city scenes that need buses, utility trucks and municipal fleets. Boxy bodies, roof lights and large side windows make each vehicle clear even in a crowded intersection. Since they are rigged, the vehicles can turn corners and stop at curbs instead of sitting static in traffic.

Each vehicle follows the CheckMate rules TurboSquid applies to geometry, scale and naming, so files stay consistent from one to the next. One modeling style across the fleet keeps a long street shot coherent when several public vehicles share the frame. Plain liveries let you add a local transit logo or city crest without fighting existing graphics.

At $259 the bundle suits urban planning films, transit authority presentations and traffic simulations for growing districts. Out since 2020, it is used in city builder games, driving simulators and animated explainers about public transport. Architects fill bus lanes and depots with these vehicles so street renders show how a neighborhood actually moves.
