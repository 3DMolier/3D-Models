About This Model

This battery-powered rescue cutter slices through car pillars at crash sites, making it a key tool for firefighter extrication scenes. The Holmatro Pentheon PCU40 Cordless Inclined Cutter New uses 21,665 polygons and 22,323 vertices for the angled jaws and the housing. The inclined blades are set at an angle to the body, which lets rescuers reach into tight spaces.

Its UV layout is free of overlaps, so the bright factory colors or your own safety markings can be repainted. CheckMate Pro status confirms clean, well organized geometry that holds up in a close shot of gloved hands.

Each of the 4 versions sells for $49: this new cutter, a dirty Pentheon edition, and a generic rescue cutter in new or dirty finish. Released in 2021, it has gained 10 buyers among fire service trainers, rescue equipment dealers and documentary makers. Pair it with a crumpled car door and firefighters in full gear for a convincing road accident response.
