About This Model

A collection of double decker buses, the tall urban coaches that define street scenes in London, Hong Kong and many tourist cities. Upper deck windows, the rear staircase and the flat front give these vehicles an unmistakable profile. Several buses in a single purchase let a street fill up without the same vehicle repeating in every lane.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms real scale, so the buses sit correctly beside curbs, bus shelters and passing cars. Each vehicle is a separate, clean asset with predictable naming, which keeps large traffic scenes manageable. Livery can change from classic red to modern route colors, depending on the city a project needs.

Available since 2023, the set serves city planning presentations, travel promotions and period films set in busy capitals. A double decker passing in the background is often all it takes to tell a viewer which city they are in. Transport authorities also use such buses to visualize new routes and electric fleet replacements.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How clean is the geometry on the Double Decker Buses Collection model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.

How heavy is the Double Decker Buses Collection mesh?

501,737 polygons and 518,213 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the 32,250 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs high at 501,737.

What is the Double Decker Buses Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

Can the Double Decker Buses Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.
