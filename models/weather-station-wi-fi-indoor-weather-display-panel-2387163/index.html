About This Model

Priced at $24, the Weather Station Wi-Fi Indoor Weather Display Panel is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 17,068 polygons and 10,720 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
