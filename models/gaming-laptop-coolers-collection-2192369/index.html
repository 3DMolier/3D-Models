About This Model

Gaming laptop coolers in one set, the fan-packed stands that lift a notebook and blow air under its hot base. Mesh tops, glowing fans and angled stands give each cooler a recognizable gamer look on a desk. Coolers come as single objects, so you can set one under a laptop while others wait on a store shelf.

Quality reviewers at TurboSquid passed the bundle at CheckMate Pro level, checking mesh quality, naming and the overall file layout. Emissive fan rings can glow in any RGB color, matching a gaming room's keyboard, mouse and wall lighting theme for a coordinated look.

In our range since 2024 for $31, the set fits gaming room renders, electronics store displays and tech review thumbnails. Streamers' desk setups often include a cooler like this under a laptop, sitting beside a headset, a microphone arm and a mouse. Product designers also compare stand angles and fan layouts with sets like this before prototyping.
