About This Model

A wheel set from a giant mining haul truck, with an axle carrying a pair of tires taller than a grown man. The 67,240 polygons and 74,378 vertices shape the deep tread blocks, the rim bolts and the hub assembly. Massive lugs and a thick sidewall give the tires the brutal scale that defines open-pit mining equipment.

Spanning 250.44 x 818.59 x H362.31 cm, the set dwarfs any worker or pickup placed beside it. StemCell materials keep the rubber and painted steel consistent across render engines and game platforms. Mixed UVs mean a short check is useful before adding caked mud or tire markings.

Released in 2026, it serves mining industry presentations, tire maintenance training and heavy equipment visualization. Shown on its own in a workshop, the wheel set explains how mines change and service these enormous tires. Game levels set in quarries can scatter spare tires around a maintenance yard for added scale.
