About This Model

A compact range of JCB mini excavators, rigged so the boom, dipper, bucket and tracks can move as on a real machine at work. These small diggers are the workhorses of tight urban sites, garden projects and utility trenches where a full excavator would not fit. Cab glass, hydraulic rams and the dozer blade are modeled in detail for close views.

Every machine is a separate model, so a site can show several sizes working together or parked side by side. TurboSquid's CheckMate guidelines shaped these diggers, keeping their sizes realistic and the moving parts named clearly for animators. Rigged arms make it easy to animate digging, loading a truck or lifting a pipe without rebuilding the hierarchy.

Since 2019 the collection has been available for $149 under Editorial Uses Only terms, covering news, education and documentary work. Construction trade reporting and training courses can use it to explain how compact diggers handle trenches and foundations. Because it depicts real branded machines, it suits editorial articles and industry explainers rather than promotional work.
