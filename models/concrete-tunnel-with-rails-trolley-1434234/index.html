About This Model

Concrete Tunnel with Rails Trolley builds a section of underground passage with a narrow track and a small mine-style cart for utility scenes. Its 189,010 polygons and 190,762 vertices carry the curved concrete walls, rail ties and the cart's wheels and frame.

The 6 texture maps at 4096 x 4096 bring stained concrete, rusty steel and worn timber to close inspection shots. CheckMate Pro certification and clean UVs without overlaps mean the materials sit correctly as soon as the scene opens. Water stains along the lower walls and grime between the ties are easy to paint, since each surface has its own room on the maps.

Available since 2019, the tunnel suits mining and metro construction visualization, horror game levels and engineering training films. Point a headlamp down the track and the repeating ribs of the vault create strong depth with very little setup. The trolley can be moved along the rails for animation, or parked mid-tunnel loaded with tools or rubble.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2019 · Updated 17 November 2026
