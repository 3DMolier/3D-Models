About This Model

A collection of stylized avatar pieces inspired by the fantasy card game, suited to tabletop renders, fan projects and collector displays on a shelf. The Magic The Gathering Avatar Collection keeps its pieces light, with 5,472 polygons shared across the whole set of figures. Its 5,454 vertices, almost matching the face count, point to clean, closed shapes that render reliably at any angle.

It is assembled under CheckMate specification, so pieces arrive with orderly names, clean pivots and sensible scale. No overlaps in the UV layout leave space for painted details, card-inspired colors and worn metallic edges for each figure.

New in 2026, the collection suits fan videos, game night illustrations and tabletop strategy previews for streamers. Royalty Free applies to the files, while the card game franchise still calls for care in commercial releases. Arrange the avatars around a play mat under warm lamp light, and the game table comes alive.
