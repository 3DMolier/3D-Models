About This Model

A collection of heating radiators in several styles, from classic cast iron columns to slim modern panels for interiors. Each unit shows its fins, valves and pipe connections clearly, so a radiator under a window reads as real heating. Having the variety in one bundle helps match the radiator style to the age and character of a building.

TurboSquid approved the set at CheckMate Pro level after checking its topology, scale and file organization. Separate objects let an artist place a different radiator in each room without importing extra files. Bathrooms, hallways and bedrooms all look more lived in once a radiator sits on the wall below the sill.

First listed in 2020, the bundle belongs to the long-standing part of the collection and remains in steady use. Interior designers, renovation brochures, heating installers and apartment games use it to complete a room's technical details. Old buildings get the ribbed cast iron models, while new apartments and offices suit the flat panel designs best.
