About This Model

Looking for a Furniture & Interior asset? The Speaker Tribune is a finished, render-ready model at $4. Geometry weighs in at 690 polygons over 733 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 54.01 x 41.17 x H91.43 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
