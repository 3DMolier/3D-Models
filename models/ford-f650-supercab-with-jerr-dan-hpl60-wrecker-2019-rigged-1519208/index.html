About This Model

Ford F650 Supercab with Jerr-Dan HPL60 Wrecker 2019 Rigged is a medium-duty tow truck with an extended cab and an underlift wrecker body. The 605,516 polygons cover the boom, winch lines, light bar and the dense tool boxes along the bed. The tow boom and wheel lift are the parts that tell the story, and they carry most of the visible detail.

Textures arrive as 20 maps at 4096 x 4096, enough for warning decals, worn steel and chrome to hold in close shots. There are 610,431 vertices, only a little above the face count, which points to a clean hard-surface build. The scene passed CheckMate Pro review, and its non-overlapping UVs make custom fleet liveries easy to apply.

The wrecker has been available since 2020 under an Editorial Uses Only license because it carries real Ford and Jerr-Dan design. It fits roadside recovery scenes, news graphics, documentary work and driving simulators with service vehicles. Hooked to a broken-down car on a highway shoulder at night, with beacons on, it carries a whole scene.
