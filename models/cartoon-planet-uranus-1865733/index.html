About This Model

A stylized ice giant with a soft cyan surface and a thin tilted ring, ready for children's space lessons and cartoon skies. Only 2,781 polygons form the smooth sphere and its ring, keeping the planet ideal for mobile apps and quick animation. The 3,287 vertices allow a clean silhouette even in close shots, without the weight of a realistic planet model.

Color is defined entirely in materials rather than image maps, so a gentle gradient or a flat toon shade both work. The unwrap has clean separate islands, so faint cloud bands or a smiling face can be painted if the style calls for it. Its CheckMate Pro grade from TurboSquid came once tidy geometry, proper naming and sensible scale were confirmed by hand.

Published in 2022 at $39, it suits solar system posters, kids' science videos and space themed mobile games. Teachers explaining why Uranus rolls on its side and animators building cartoon solar systems both find it useful. Spin it slowly on its tilted axis beside smaller moons, and young viewers instantly grasp what makes it unusual.
