About This Model

This Aircraft model - Passenger Jetliner Flight - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

At 154,849 polygons and 169,003 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
