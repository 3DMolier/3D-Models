About This Model

A cardboard sleeve holding a dozen strawberry yogurt cups, the multipack format found in every supermarket dairy aisle. The 19,878 polygons and 20,260 vertices shape the cups with their foil lids and the printed sleeve that wraps around them. Lined up on a refrigerated shelf, a few packs make a convincing grocery display in a supermarket render.

A non-overlapping unwrap gives the sleeve and lids room for your own label art and nutrition panels. The sleeve was produced to CheckMate specification, with cups as separate parts that can be pulled out for breakfast shots. Pink strawberry graphics against white plastic keep the product bright under store lighting.

Two versions share the same name, Strawberry Yogurt 12 Paper Pack, and differ only in their packaging details. The pack arrived in 2024 and suits dairy aisle renders, breakfast table scenes, packaging mockups and nutrition explainers. Set one open on a kitchen table beside a spoon and fresh berries for a morning scene.
