About This Model

Closed Snowmobile Sleds is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $21. You are getting 95,347 polygons and 96,736 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.
