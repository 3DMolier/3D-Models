About This Model

A collection of drinking cups in several shapes alongside clear spherical ice balls, ready for bar, cafe and beverage scenes. Ice spheres are the signature of a slow-sipped whiskey or cocktail, and they melt far slower than small cubes. The cups vary in height and profile, so a table can be dressed for anything from espresso to a tall cold drink. Clear ice is shown with subtle inner cloudiness, the look of real frozen water rather than solid glass.

Everything is scaled per CheckMate guidance, so an ice ball fits inside a rocks glass at the right size. Separate objects make it easy to pick a single cup for a close shot or scatter the whole set across a bar top.

Published in 2026, the collection suits bar interiors, beverage menus, cocktail recipe visuals and hospitality renders. Royalty-free terms cover client work for bars, cafes and drinks producers alike. Backlight the ice with a warm practical lamp and every sphere glows like amber.
