About This Model

A light six-wheeled carrier with a mounted machine gun gives military scenes a fast, open vehicle for crews. The Army ATMP 6x6 with Machine Gun shows the low flatbed body, roll bar and exposed seats. Those features make this all-terrain mobility platform recognizable even at a distance.

Three axles spread the weight across soft ground, and the weapon mount sits high so the gunner can turn freely. CheckMate Lite certification confirms usable geometry, scaled for placing beside soldiers, crates and field equipment.

Listed since 2018, the vehicle suits defense training material where the shape of the platform matters most. Simulation teams use it in convoy exercises and airborne supply scenarios, since the compact frame fits inside transport aircraft. Game developers treat it as a drivable support vehicle in desert or forest maps. Filmmakers park it at checkpoints to add authentic military detail to a wide shot.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2018 · Updated 11 November 2026
