About This Model

Jaguar XF is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. Geometry weighs in at 973,815 polygons over 1,008,094 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 18 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
