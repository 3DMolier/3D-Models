About This Model

Japanese stone lanterns in one set, the toro that line temple paths and stand beside ponds in traditional gardens. Each lantern stacks a base, a pillar, a light box and a roof cap, the classic form carved from granite. Lanterns come as individual pieces, so a garden path can be lined at any spacing you like.

Its StemCell delivery gives several formats with the same PBR stone materials in each. Weathered granite looks best with moss in the crevices and rain darkening the stone, and the solid shapes survive wide shots.

Released in 2025 at $31, the collection fits zen garden designs, temple visualizations and samurai game levels. At night, a small emissive glow in each light box turns a garden path into a gentle procession. Landscape designers use these lanterns to plan Japanese garden corners in private estates and hotel grounds. Placed beside a koi pond and a maple, a single lantern sets the whole garden mood.
