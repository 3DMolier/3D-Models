About This Model

A full-size city transit bus with wide doors, large side windows and a low floor, made for urban traffic scenes. The Transit City Passenger Bus Rigged uses 629,496 polygons across the body, wheels, seats and window frames.

Its 654,908 vertices keep the rounded roofline and wheel arches smooth in close street-level shots. The bus meets CheckMate specification, so parts arrive named, organized and at real vehicle scale. Through the glass, rows of seats are visible, which matters when the camera follows the bus past a stop.

Since 2026 the bus has cost $199 and suits city animations, driving games and transport planning renders. Pull it up to a shelter with waiting passengers, or send it along a dedicated bus lane. Transit agencies presenting new routes can show the vehicle at stops and intersections in context. Its boxy profile and long wheelbase make it read as public transport at a glance, even from above.
