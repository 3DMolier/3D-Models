About This Model

A grey patterned tuxedo with a subtle woven design, the less formal cousin of black tie for weddings and evening events. The pattern runs consistently across the jacket and trousers, so the suit looks tailored from one bolt of cloth. The lapels are cut sharp and clean, framing the chest and drawing the eye toward the face in portraits. Grey reads softer than black on camera, which helps in bright daylight ceremonies where black can lose detail.

Human proportions match the CheckMate specification, which lets the suit fit a standard adult figure. Fabric and trim materials stay separate, letting the pattern scale or color change without affecting the lapels.

This 2026 suit fits fashion visualization, wedding scenes, character dressing for games and menswear presentations. Royalty-free use applies, so it can dress a groom or a guest in client work. Garden light at a summer ceremony suits the grey cloth better than any ballroom.
