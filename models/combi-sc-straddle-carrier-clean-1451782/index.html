About This Model

Combi-SC Straddle Carrier Clean belongs to our Industrial Equipment range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbour environments in games, and marine simulation.

Geometry weighs in at 243,257 polygons over 258,033 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. It ships 14 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
