About This Model

White Man Hands Base Pose is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. You are getting 13,024 polygons and 6,536 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
