About This Model

This is a deliberate joke with a technical payoff: a balloon animal rendered transparent with a skeleton inside it, borrowing the language of anatomical illustration and applying it to something that has no anatomy at all. The contrast between the glossy inflated shell and the bones within is the whole idea.

The mesh is light at 8,895 polygons and 9,036 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the shell and the internal structure as separate bodies so a transparent material can work over an opaque one.

At $49 it suits art and design presentation, gallery and exhibition visualization, editorial and conceptual illustration, interior decor renders, advertising and games. On sale since 2022.
