About This Model

The Balloon Dog Anatomy is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. You are getting 8,895 polygons and 9,036 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos.

It has been on sale since 2022 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
