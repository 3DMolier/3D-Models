About This Model

Freesia Bouquet belongs to our Nature & Plants range and is offered at $34 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 119,540 polygons and 121,150 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 10 maps at 4096 x 4096. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
