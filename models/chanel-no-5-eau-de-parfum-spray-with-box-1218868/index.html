About This Model

This perfume bottle with its carton suits beauty editorials, since the faceted glass and golden liquid catch light like the real flacon. Chanel No 5 Eau De Parfum Spray with Box spends 8,479 polygons over 8,510 vertices on the bottle, the cap and the folding box. The squared shoulders and thick glass base are what a viewer recognizes first, so a low side light flatters it most.

The 10 maps at 4096 x 4096 cover the clear glass, amber fragrance, metal collar and the printed white carton. Non-overlapping UVs keep the label and each box panel separate, which keeps small lettering sharp in macro framing. CheckMate Pro certification means a TurboSquid inspector checked scale, naming and topology before the file went on sale.

There are two versions: this spray with box at $49 and the Chanel No5 Eau De Parfum Bottle with Box at $39. Both have an identical polygon budget, so the choice comes down to which bottle the scene calls for. Released in 2017 under an Editorial Uses Only license, it fits magazine features, fragrance reviews and fashion history pieces.
