About This Model

A collection of poses featuring the black-suited web-slinger, from crouches to swings, ready for comic-style scenes. The dark suit with its white spider emblem and large white eye lenses keeps the character recognizable in silhouette. Each pose captures a different moment, so a sequence can tell a small action story without any animation work.

Each pose follows CheckMate specification, so proportions stay identical across the set when several appear together. Glossy black fabric needs rim lighting to show the muscle shapes underneath, otherwise the figure reads as a flat cutout. Dynamic stances with bent knees and extended arms suit city rooftops, walls and dramatic low camera angles.

The pose pack dates from 2026 and costs $199 under a royalty free license. Fan art, comic panels, collectible figure concepts and superhero game mockups are where these poses are most useful. Perched on a rooftop edge at night above neon streets, any of these poses makes a striking image.
