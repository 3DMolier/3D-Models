About This Model

A set of regional passenger jets from the Brazilian manufacturer, gathered for airport, aviation, travel and regional route scenes. Having several airliners in one package keeps livery quality, proportions and construction consistent across the fleet. Lined up at gates with jet bridges attached, the aircraft turn an empty apron into a working regional airport.

The name signals rigging, so landing gear, control surfaces and other moving parts can be posed for takeoff shots. Because real Embraer designs are depicted, licensing stays Editorial Uses Only for the whole bundle. That fits aviation journalism, airline industry reports, documentaries and educational material about modern air travel.

At $259 the collection suits studios that need several matching aircraft rather than one hero plane on a runway. Released in 2020, it fits flight tracking explainers, airport documentaries, regional route news and simulator scenery. Put one on short final over a runway at dusk, and the shot instantly feels like an arrival story.
