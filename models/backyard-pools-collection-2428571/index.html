About This Model

A set of backyard swimming pools in different shapes, ready to drop into residential garden and landscape designs. Pool edges, steps and coping are what sell a pool in a render, and those parts are modeled to catch light at the waterline. Having several pools in one collection means a designer can show clients alternatives without rebuilding the yard.

Each pool is its own object, so it can be placed, rotated and paired with decking or lawn independently. The set follows CheckMate specification, which keeps scale real and object names clear. Water surfaces take a refractive shader well, and caustics on the pool floor add instant summer realism.

On sale since 2025, the collection suits landscape architecture, real estate visualization, pool contractor presentations and holiday rental images. Evening shots with underwater lights switched on are a favorite for luxury property imagery. Surround a pool with loungers and potted palms, and the backyard is ready for a sales brochure.
