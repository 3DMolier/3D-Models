About This Model

A canine anatomy model showing the internal organs together with the nervous system and the network of arteries and veins. Polygon count reaches 361,527, spread over the heart, lungs, liver, intestines and the thin branching vessels that wrap them. Nerves and vessels follow the organs closely, so a cutaway reveals how the systems share space inside the body cavity.

With 361,676 vertices and a mixed UV layout, some parts carry unique unwraps while repeated vessel segments share texture space. CheckMate specification shapes the build, so each organ is a named object that can be hidden or highlighted on its own. Color coding of red arteries, blue veins and yellow nerves follows the convention veterinary textbooks use.

Released in 2026, it serves veterinary schools, pet insurance explainers and pharmaceutical presentations about canine health. Rotating the dog slowly with the skin hidden lets students follow a single nerve from the spine to a limb. Clinics also show such cutaways to owners when explaining a diagnosis or an upcoming surgery.
