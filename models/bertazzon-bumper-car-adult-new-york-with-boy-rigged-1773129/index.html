About This Model

A dodgem is a fibreglass shell on a steel floor pan, with a rubber bumper ring around it and a pole reaching up to the ceiling grid for power. That pole is the detail that makes the ride work visually, because without it the car is just a small vehicle. Including a rider gives it scale and purpose.

The mesh is efficient at 80,487 polygons and 82,400 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and it arrives rigged so the car can be posed or driven.

At $199 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits amusement park and fairground visualization, leisure industry presentation, event and family imagery, film and television, and games. On sale since 2021.
