About This Model

A traditional pub bar is a piece of joinery as much as furniture: a heavy timber counter, turned columns, an arched glass rack overhead and a bank of taps along the top. The arch is the feature that reads first across a room, and it is what separates a pub from a modern bar with a straight run of shelving.

The mesh runs 511,032 polygons and 522,488 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout, with the density in the mouldings, the turned detail and the tap assemblies where a camera at the counter lands.

At $129 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits hospitality and interior visualisation, pub and restaurant design presentation, film and television set dressing, brewery marketing and games. On sale since 2026.
