About This Model

Aerial Ski Lift is one of the Architecture Landmarks models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
