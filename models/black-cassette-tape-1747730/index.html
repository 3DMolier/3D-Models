About This Model

Cassette Tape belongs to our Signage & Decor range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 28,314 polygons over 28,726 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
