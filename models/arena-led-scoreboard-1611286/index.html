About This Model

Arena LED Scoreboard is a hanging indoor display that shows scores and replays above basketball and hockey courts. Its screens face every side of the arena, so fans in all stands can follow the game clock. The scoreboard is efficient at 13,502 polygons and 14,948 vertices, leaving budget for the crowd below.

Non-overlapping UVs let you map any score graphics, team logos or replay footage onto the screens. It holds CheckMate Pro, given by TurboSquid once the scoreboard cleared its more demanding inspection. Emissive screen materials make the board glow over a darkened court, just like a real arena at tip-off.

Available from 2020 at $39, the scoreboard suits sports venue design, broadcast graphics and arena renovation plans. Sports games hang it above the center court so players can track points during a match. Concert setups in arenas also use the same center-hung display for video and announcements.
