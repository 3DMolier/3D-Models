About This Model

Priced at $29, the Adjustable 4 Jaw Self Centering Lathe Chuck is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, medical visualization and software demos. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.

Geometry weighs in at 22,627 polygons over 23,070 vertices.
