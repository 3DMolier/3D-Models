About This Model

A yellowfin tuna with its torpedo body, bright yellow finlets and long sickle fins, built as an open ocean fish for Blender scenes. The streamlined shape is what makes this species read at a glance, even when it flashes past the camera. Its blue-black back fades to a silver belly, and the yellow stripe along the flank completes the recognizable coloring.

Clean geometry and real-world proportions come from building it to the CheckMate specification. The fins are shaped to slice water, with the tall dorsal and anal fins swept back like blades. Small finlets run toward the tail, a detail that separates a convincing tuna from a generic fish.

Released in 2026, it suits ocean documentaries, fishing visuals, seafood education and underwater game scenes. A school of them circling in blue water gives an ocean shot scale, speed and a clear subject. Royalty-free terms cover commercial work, from aquarium visuals to seafood packaging renders.
