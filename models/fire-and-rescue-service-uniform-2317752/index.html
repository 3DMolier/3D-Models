About This Model

This Weapons model - Fire and Rescue Service Uniform - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. You are getting 56,405 polygons and 58,245 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
