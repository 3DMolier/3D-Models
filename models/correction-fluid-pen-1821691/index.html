About This Model

The Correction Fluid Pen is a production-ready general 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 3,110 polygons and 3,223 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
