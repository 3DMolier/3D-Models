About This Model

A volumetric Earth for Blender with layered atmosphere and clouds that show real depth when the camera flies close to the planet. Volumetric Planet Earth 21K For Blender builds the cloud cover as a true volume, so it casts soft shadows on the land below. Thin blue haze along the limb gives the globe the glowing edge seen in orbital photographs.

The asset is made to CheckMate standards, with consistent scale, clear naming and a well-organized Blender scene. Because the clouds are a volume rather than a painted shell, light scatters through them the way it does in reality. Continents, oceans and polar ice keep natural colors, which helps the globe read clearly from far orbit and close range.

Released in 2026, the planet suits space documentaries, science lessons and opening titles for news or film. A slow dolly past the cloud layer creates a convincing descent from orbit toward the surface. Space game menus and educational apps can use the same globe as a rotating centerpiece.
