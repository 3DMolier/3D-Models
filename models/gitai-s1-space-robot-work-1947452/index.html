About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Space Robot is a finished, render-ready model at $59. The mesh carries 98,103 polygons and 51,918 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2022 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
