About This Model

Vintage Pilot in Uniform A-Pose is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $169. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 27,363 polygons and 27,005 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
