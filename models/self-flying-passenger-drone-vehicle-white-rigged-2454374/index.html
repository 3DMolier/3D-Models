About This Model

Priced at $129, the Self-Flying Passenger Drone Vehicle White Rigged is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 95,518 polygons over 99,325 vertices - a light build for its class. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
