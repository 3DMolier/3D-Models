About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Large Railway Relay Cabinet is a finished, render-ready model at $49. You are getting 30,457 polygons and 32,242 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
