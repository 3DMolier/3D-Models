About This Model

A team of SWAT officers in tactical gear, gathered in one pack for police raids and hostage rescue scenes. Helmets, ballistic vests, knee pads and rifles give each officer the heavy, armored silhouette of special units. Several officers together read as a coordinated squad, which is far more convincing than one repeated figure.

All officers share CheckMate specification, so they stand at consistent human height when stacked in a breach line. Dark uniforms and gear need careful rim light, or the team blends into night scenes and loses its shapes. Arms and weapons are the parts most often posed, letting the squad aim, advance or cover a doorway.

Around since 2016, the SWAT team costs $199 and comes with royalty free rights for productions. Police dramas, tactical shooters, training simulations and news reconstructions are the usual homes for these officers. Lined up against a wall beside a door with flashlights on, the squad sets up an instant breach moment.

Questions About This Model

Which industries use the SWAT Rigged Policemans Collection model?

Game Development, Film & Video Production and Advertising are the primary industries on this listing, though the Royalty Free license puts no limit on where the model is actually used.

Can the SWAT Rigged Policemans Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes. It is sold under TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, which covers commercial use in games, film, advertising and visualization without per-use fees.

Can the SWAT Rigged Policemans Collection model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What is the SWAT Rigged Policemans Collection model typically used for?

Stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.
