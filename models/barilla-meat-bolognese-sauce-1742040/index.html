About This Model

A glass jar of Barilla Meat Bolognese Sauce with a metal twist lid and a wraparound label, as sold on supermarket shelves. The 6,956 polygons and 6,828 vertices shape the shoulder of the jar, the lid ridges and the slight base curve. Its squat shape keeps it stable when stacked in rows, which matters when you fill a grocery shelf.

Textures are left to you, so the label artwork, glass tint and sauce color come from your own materials. Non-overlapping UVs make label placement straightforward, with the band wrapping cleanly around the jar. On TurboSquid the model holds the CheckMate Lite grade for basic structural quality.

Available since 2021, the jar fits kitchen interiors, grocery store renders and cooking scenes in games and films. Placed next to a pot of boiling pasta and a wooden spoon, it explains the dinner plan without words. Italian pantry shelves look complete with it beside olive oil bottles and boxed spaghetti.
