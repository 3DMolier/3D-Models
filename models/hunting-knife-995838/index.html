About This Model

Hunting Knife is one of the Weapons models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 1,003 polygons and 1,039 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 5 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
