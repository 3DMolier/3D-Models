About This Model

Industrial power switches and disconnect boxes are grouped here for factory walls, electrical rooms and machine control areas. Heavy lever handles, metal enclosures and warning labels give each switch the rugged look of real plant equipment. Mounted in a row on a concrete wall, they make any industrial interior feel functional and safety regulated.

Every item carries CheckMate Pro status from TurboSquid, so meshes are clean and named consistently. Each enclosure is a separate object, which lets you place them individually along conduit runs or group them on a panel. The chunky handles cast clear shadows in raking light, so the switches read well even at a distance.

First published in 2016, the set suits factory visualizations, maintenance training, industrial games and electrical safety videos. Run conduit pipes between the boxes and add a lockout tag on one handle, and a training scenario is ready. Electricians can also use the props to illustrate isolation procedures before servicing machines.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
