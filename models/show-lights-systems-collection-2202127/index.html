About This Model

A collection of stage show lighting systems, the moving heads, spotlights and trusses that shape concerts, festivals and live events. Beams, lenses and mounting yokes give each fixture the look professionals recognize from real productions. Gathering several fixture types in one set lets a stage design mix wash, spot and effect lights.

CheckMate specification keeps the fixtures at real size, so they hang correctly from trusses above a stage. Separate objects let you aim each light individually or duplicate rows along a lighting truss.

Available since 2024, the fixtures suit concert visuals, event planning, theater design and music video previs. Volumetric fog in the render turns each beam into a visible shaft of light, the signature of a live show. Colored gels and a dark audience area give the stage the drama of a headline performance. Event planners can present lighting plots to clients long before any truck arrives at the venue.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
