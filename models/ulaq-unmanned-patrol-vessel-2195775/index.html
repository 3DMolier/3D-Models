About This Model

ULAQ Unmanned Patrol Vessel is a detailed Ships asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbour environments in games, and marine simulation.

Counted at the source, the model is 128,319 polygons and 70,209 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
