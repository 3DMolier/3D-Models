About This Model

The Cactus Vase is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 2,178 polygons over 2,180 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 5 maps at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
