About This Model

A pair of Gibson Les Paul guitars on display stands, suited to vintage music rooms, collector shelves and stage wings. Most of the 158,942 polygons describe the carved maple tops, the cutaways and the metal hardware on each body and neck. The cutaway profile gives these instruments a silhouette any rock fan identifies, even in a dim room lit by a lamp.

StemCell delivery keeps sunburst lacquer and gold hardware shading consistent across all the provided formats and engines. Partial UVs and 161,986 vertices leave frets, pickups and tuners as clean geometry rather than painted detail. The license here reads Editorial Uses Only, a good match for reviews, journalism and educational video.

Priced at $69 and launched in 2026, the pair fits music history features, guitar lessons and studio documentaries. Side by side they measure 83.89 x 36.2 x H115.22 cm, compact enough for a bedroom studio corner. Place them beside a tube amplifier under soft lamplight for the feel of a late night jam session.
