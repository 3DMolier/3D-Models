About This Model

The Lynx Cub for 3D Print is a solid, watertight sculpture of a young lynx, prepared for resin or filament printers rather than rendering. Tufted ears, oversized paws and the short bobbed tail are shaped with enough depth to survive sanding and painting. Printed at desk size, the kitten makes a charming figurine for a shelf or a wildlife gift shop.

Prepared to CheckMate requirements, the mesh is closed and free of internal faces, so slicers handle it without repair. The fur is sculpted directly into the surface, which gives primer and dry brushing something real to catch. A soft side light reveals the carved tufts on the cheeks and makes the little cat look alive.

Three versions are offered as separate poses: this calm standing cub, a walking cub and an energetic jumping cub. Collecting the full trio makes a playful tabletop scene of a litter exploring the forest floor. On sale since 2025, the cubs suit collectors, model painters and educational nature kits.
