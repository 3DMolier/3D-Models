About This Model

An electro acoustic guitar with a natural spruce top, round sound hole and a pickup control panel on the upper side. The 13,811 polygons cover the bridge pins, the tuning pegs, the frets and the strings stretched along the neck. A full rounded body gives the instrument a warm, traditional silhouette on stage or in a living room.

Five maps at 4096 x 4096 provide wood grain, binding, metal hardware and fretboard detail. Its 14,283 vertices are mapped without overlap, so a different wood finish can be applied cleanly. CheckMate Pro approval confirms the strings and body are clean, named parts that open without repair.

The guitar family has five versions, including a classical acoustic, a black finish, a cutaway body and an Epiphone acoustic electric. Since 2018 it has served music school renders, concert stages, songwriter scenes and instrument shop visuals. Cozy evening scenes lean it against a sofa beside an open notebook full of chords.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2018 · Updated 28 October 2026
