About This Model

3doodler Pro belongs to our Furniture & Interior range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 6,008 polygons over 6,503 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
