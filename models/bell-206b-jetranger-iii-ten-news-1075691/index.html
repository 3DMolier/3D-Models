About This Model

Bell 206B JetRanger III Ten News is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 532,336 polygons and 585,583 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 43 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
