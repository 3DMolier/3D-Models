About This Model

Forest Camo Combat Vehicle Worn Dirty with Interior Rigged is one of the Military Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $129. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for military simulation, game environments and film production. First published in 2026, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 291,700 polygons and 307,640 vertices, which puts it in the mid-weight bracket for this category. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
