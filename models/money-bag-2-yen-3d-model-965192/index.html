About This Model

Looking for a 3D asset? The Money Bag 2 Yen is a finished, render-ready model at $19. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for product rendering, film & TV VFX and game assets. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

The mesh carries 10,672 polygons and 10,873 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 5 of them, peaking at 2048x2048, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
