About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Oval Hatch with is a finished, render-ready model at $39. You are getting 41,514 polygons and 42,680 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 134.07 x 58.94 x H189.75 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, defense simulation and game assets. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
