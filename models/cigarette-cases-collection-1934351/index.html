About This Model

The Cigarette Cases Collection gathers slim metal and leather cases that snap shut, the pocket accessories of earlier decades. Each case stands alone, so you can lay one on a table, stack a few or slip one into a jacket. Engraved lids and rounded corners give the pieces a refined, vintage look in any close shot.

The bundle passed a CheckMate Lite check, so each piece loads correctly at real scale. Collecting several designs in one purchase saves time when dressing a period lounge or a detective's desk. Polished silver next to worn leather gives the set a pleasing mix of materials under warm lamp light.

The second version, Cigarette Cases Collection 2, costs $34, while this first set is $24. Together the two bundles fill a collector's display case with plenty of variety. Sold from 2022, it suits noir films, period dramas, antique shop scenes and vintage still life renders.
