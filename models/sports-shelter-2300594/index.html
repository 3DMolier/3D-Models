About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Sports Shelter is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Geometry weighs in at 14,933 polygons over 16,536 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
