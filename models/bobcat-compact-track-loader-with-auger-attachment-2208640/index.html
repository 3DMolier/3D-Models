About This Model

A compact track loader with an auger attachment on its front arms is ready to drill post holes or tree pits on a site. The 203,751 polygons go into the rubber tracks, the lift arms, the cab cage and the spiral flights of the auger bit. The operator sits behind a protective cage, with the arms rising on both sides of the cab.

Its 208,542 vertices keep the curved auger and the drive sprockets smooth in close views. The UV layout has no overlaps, so the white body, the black tracks and the steel bit each take their own wear. It meets the CheckMate specification, a TurboSquid standard covering scale, naming and topology checks.

The Bobcat Compact Track Loader with Auger Attachment arrived in 2024 and is licensed for Editorial Uses Only. It fits construction industry news, equipment reviews, landscaping documentaries and editorial pieces about small machinery. Tilt the auger into the ground beside a fence line to show the machine halfway through a job.
