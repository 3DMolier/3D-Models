About This Model

A printable Nio statue, the fierce, muscular guardian that stands at the gates of Japanese Buddhist temples to ward off evil. Bulging muscles, a wrathful face and swirling drapery give the figure the explosive energy that defines Nio carvings. Nio figures have guarded temple gates in Japan for centuries, often carved in wood on a heroic scale.

Nio usually stand in pairs at a temple gate, one with an open mouth and one with a closed mouth. Deep muscle grooves and dramatic folds print best on resin, then come alive with a dark wash and dry brushing. The file follows CheckMate specification, though its license limits it to editorial purposes, so review the terms before commercial work.

Available from 2024, the statue suits collectors of Japanese art, museum education, temple diorama builders and documentary film sets. Placed at the entrance of a miniature temple, the guardian immediately sets a solemn, protective mood. The print-ready statue costs $89 and goes straight into your slicer for printing.
