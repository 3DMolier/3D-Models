About This Model

The industrial jug is a shape decided by stacking and by the machine that fills it. The handle is moulded into the body rather than added, which is cheaper and also the reason it can never be replaced when it cracks.

The model is extremely light at 5,549 polygons and 5,718 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 4 maps all at 2048x2048. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $19 it suits packaging and industrial visualisation, warehouse and workshop scenes, label mockups, e-commerce imagery, film set dressing and game environments. On sale since 2018.
