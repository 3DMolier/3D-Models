About This Model

A countertop dispenser stacked with green Heineken cans, it brings a bar or a fridge display into a news or review scene. The 94,868 polygons and 48,480 vertices go into the rolling rails and the rows of cans waiting to drop. Cans slide down a slope inside the frame, a mechanism that reads clearly when the camera looks from the front.

Islands across the unwrap are kept apart, so labels and condensation can be refined without mixing the metal and plastic parts. Materials drive the green and silver finish, and adjusting the gloss changes the can from new to used quickly. TurboSquid awarded it CheckMate Pro after reviewing the mesh by hand, along with its scale and object names.

Its Editorial Uses Only terms cover journalism, documentaries and educational pieces about drinks or retail. Introduced in 2022, it fits stories about bars, beverage packaging and how a kitchen counter is organized. Set designers also stand it in a dorm room scene to show student life in a recognizable way.
