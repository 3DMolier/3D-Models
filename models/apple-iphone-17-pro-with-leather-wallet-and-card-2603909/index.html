About This Model

A current iPhone with a magnetic leather wallet attached to its back and a card tucked inside, ready for everyday carry scenes. The Apple iPhone 17 Pro with Leather Wallet and Card uses 23,842 polygons and 25,499 vertices on the camera plateau, wallet pocket and card edge. The wallet adds depth to the back of the phone, which changes how the device sits on a table.

CheckMate certification ensures clean geometry, readable part names and a sensible hierarchy. Soft leather edges contrast with the hard aluminum frame, a detail that shows in close product shots. The card slips partially out of the pocket, giving a lived-in feel to desk and bag scenes.

Released in 2026 for $49, the set carries an Editorial Uses Only license as it depicts real Apple products. News features, tech reviews, editorial illustrations and films are the right places for it. On a cafe table beside a coffee cup, it makes a believable modern still life.
