About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Marble Statue of Leopard Cub is a finished, render-ready model at $59. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine.

The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 21,172 polygons over 21,291 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
