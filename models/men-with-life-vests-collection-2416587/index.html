About This Model

Men wearing life vests fill this collection, providing figures for boats, ferries, rescue drills and water sports scenes. Bulky flotation vests with straps and collars sit over everyday clothing, signaling safety at a glance on any deck. Different poses and builds let the group read as passengers, crew members or volunteers depending on the setting.

Characters follow CheckMate specification, so each man and his vest arrive as clearly organized parts of the file. Bright vests pop against blue water and gray hulls, keeping the figures readable even in very wide shots. Wet hair and spray particles added in compositing make them look freshly pulled from the waves after a rescue.

Put on sale in 2025, the collection carries a royalty free license for training, film and simulation work. Maritime safety courses use figures like these to demonstrate evacuation procedures on ferries and offshore platforms. Placed in a lifeboat or along a ship's rail, the men instantly add human scale to marine scenes.
