About This Model

The VNL 860 is a long-haul tractor with the biggest sleeper Volvo builds, and the proportions are the point: an enormous bunk section behind a conventional bonnet, roof fairings that meet the trailer cleanly and side skirts that run almost to the rear axle. All of it is about fuel burn over long distances, and it gives the truck a slab-sided profile.

The mesh is 507,865 polygons and 562,726 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 10 texture maps - 9 at 4096x4096 and one at 2048x2048. That budget carries the fleet lettering, the chrome and the road grime along the lower panels.

A rigged build sits on this page at $179 for when the wheels turn and the doors open. The static model at $149 covers logistics and highway visualization, fleet and manufacturer marketing, advertising, film and television, and games. On sale since 2018.
