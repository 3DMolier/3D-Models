About This Model

Bullfinch Female Bird is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. You are getting 45,289 polygons and 69,290 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 30.64 x 25.57 x H15.76 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
