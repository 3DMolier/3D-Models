About This Model

Narrow gauge existed because it was cheap. Tighter curves, lighter rail and smaller earthworks meant a line could be pushed into a quarry, a mine or a forest for a fraction of the cost, and almost all of them were built to serve one industry.

The model is light at 32,578 polygons and 33,911 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits industrial and heritage visualization, mine and quarry scenes, railway presentation, film backgrounds, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
