About This Model

The Electric Car Sedan is a sleek battery-powered saloon with a closed grille, flush door handles and a long glass roof in bright orange. A total of 172,716 polygons and 179,071 vertices go into the aerodynamic wheels, slim light bars and the smooth body surfaces. Gliding past glass office towers, it reads instantly as a modern zero-emission commuter car.

StemCell certification lets the sedan render the same way in several popular packages with its prepared materials. Overlap-free UVs give room for plates, charging port markings and subtle road grime on the sills. Overcast city light keeps reflections calm and lets the orange paint glow evenly across the body.

Three versions belong to this family: the orange sedan, a purple simplified edition and a black rigged edition with moving parts. The simplified purple car is light enough for parking lots, while the rigged black one drives hero shots. Out since 2024, the sedan suits EV charging visuals, city traffic scenes and mobility apps.
