About This Model

Four-Door Liftback Hybrid is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. You are getting 210,635 polygons and 233,972 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 210.95 x 482.23 x H150.61 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and architectural visualization. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
