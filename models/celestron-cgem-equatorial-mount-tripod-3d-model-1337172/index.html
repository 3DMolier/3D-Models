About This Model

Mount Tripod is one of the Space & Sci-Fi models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 97,377 polygons and 100,323 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

10 maps at 8192 x 8192 and 2048 x 2048, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.
