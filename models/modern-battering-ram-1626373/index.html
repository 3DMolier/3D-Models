About This Model

The Modern Battering Ram is a production-ready Weapons 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 5,056 polygons and 5,152 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 5 maps at 4096 x 4096. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
