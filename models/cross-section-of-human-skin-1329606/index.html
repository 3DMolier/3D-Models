About This Model

A block cutaway of human skin showing epidermis, dermis and fat, with hair follicles and sweat glands for medical teaching. It uses 16,022 polygons and 16,531 vertices, spread across the layered block, the hair shafts and the vessels. Each layer is its own shape, so a lecturer can label, hide or highlight it one at a time.

Fifteen maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 color the tissue in the conventional textbook palette. UVs never overlap, and the CheckMate Pro certificate confirms TurboSquid's quality bar was met. Red arteries, blue veins, yellow nerves and pale fat keep the diagram readable even at small size on a classroom screen.

Released in 2018, this cutaway serves dermatology clinics, cosmetic science explainers, nursing schools and university anatomy courses. Skincare educators use it to show how a cream or serum travels through the layers beneath the visible surface. At $49 it costs far less than commissioning one custom medical illustration of the same structure.
