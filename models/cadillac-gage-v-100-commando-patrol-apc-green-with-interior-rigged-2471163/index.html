About This Model

The V-100 Commando was built for patrol and convoy escort rather than for the front line: a four-wheeled armoured hull with a boat-shaped bottom to shed mine blast, firing ports along the sides and a small turret on top. This build is the green patrol version with the interior modelled, and it arrives rigged so the wheels turn and the hatches open.

The mesh runs 291,700 polygons and 307,640 vertices in quads and tris. The UVs overlap, which is a sound economy on a symmetrical armoured hull with repeated hatches and vision blocks, and it keeps the asset efficient despite carrying an interior.

At $129 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits defence and security visualization, film and television, documentary and museum material, training scenarios and games. On sale since 2026.
