About This Model

Priced at $119, the School Library Interior is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 5,831,679 polygons and 5,875,650 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
