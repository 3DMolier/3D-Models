About This Model

An electric atomizer sprayer with a tank, motor housing and long nozzle, built for disinfection and pest control work. The sprayer holds 17,179 polygons over 17,118 vertices, with detail on the trigger handle, hose fittings and vents. Its compact body is carried by hand, which makes it look practical for indoor spraying in schools and offices.

It earned CheckMate Pro certification, confirming correct scale, clean topology and consistent naming. Non-overlapping UVs give room for warning labels, a tank level window and scuffs from fieldwork. Render a fine mist particle effect from the nozzle and the sprayer immediately looks fully in operation on site.

Part of the collection since 2015, it serves sanitation service promotions, agricultural guides and public health visuals. Animators show operators in protective suits and respirators fogging classrooms, offices and hospital corridors during disinfection procedures. Pair it with masks and gloves and the scene clearly explains a professional cleaning process.
