About This Model

This Food & Beverages model - Cracked Egg Shell - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
