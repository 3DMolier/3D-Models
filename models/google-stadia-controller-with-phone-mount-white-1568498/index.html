About This Model

Google Stadia Controller with Phone Mount White is a detailed Electronics & Gadgets asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for product advertising, hardware presentation and film production. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 10,760 polygons over 10,953 vertices - a light build for its class. Maps: 5 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
