About This Model

Dwarf Fighter with Hammer Rigged belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $199 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and defense simulation. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 85,995 polygons over 89,405 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
