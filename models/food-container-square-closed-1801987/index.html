About This Model

A moulded fibre takeaway box is pressed rather than folded, which is why the corners are rounded and the walls slope slightly outward. The lid clips over a raised rim, and the whole thing has the matt speckled surface that comes from bagasse rather than from plastic.

The mesh is minimal at 3,794 polygons and 3,788 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the rim, the lid seat and the corner radii.

At $17 it suits food packaging and takeaway visualization, sustainability and compostable packaging marketing, restaurant and delivery scenes, retail renders and games. On sale since 2022.
