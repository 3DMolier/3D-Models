About This Model

Spooky Halloween decorations gathered for front porches, costume parties and haunted house scenes in late autumn. Carved shapes, skulls, cobwebs and candles are the sort of props that make a room feel haunted at once. Grouping several pieces together lets a yard or a party table look busy rather than sparse.

The decorations follow CheckMate rules, so every piece is correctly sized, cleanly modeled and sensibly named. Warm candle glow against dark surfaces flatters these props, especially with fog drifting through the frame. Each decoration stays independent, so you can scatter them along steps or crowd them on a mantel.

This version costs $89, while the larger second version of Halloween decor runs to $249 for whole houses. Out since 2024, the props suit seasonal greeting cards, party visuals, horror games and haunted attraction planning. Dim the lights and add a flickering pumpkin by the door, and trick-or-treaters will hesitate.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
