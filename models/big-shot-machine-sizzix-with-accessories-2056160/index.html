Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Big Shot Machine Sizzix with Accessories is a finished, render-ready model at $21. The mesh carries 33,162 polygons and 17,692 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
