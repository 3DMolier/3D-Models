About This Model

The automatic coupler ended one of the deadliest jobs in industry. Before it, a man stood between two moving wagons holding a pin, and standardising this knuckle across a continent is one of the great safety achievements of railways.

The model is very light at 11,534 polygons and 11,452 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits railway engineering visualization, technical documentation, rolling stock detail, equipment marketing, museum material and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
