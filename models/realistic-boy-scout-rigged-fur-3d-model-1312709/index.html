About This Model

Priced at $99, the Realistic Boy Scout Rigged Fur is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2018 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

At 37,680 polygons and 36,807 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 7 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
