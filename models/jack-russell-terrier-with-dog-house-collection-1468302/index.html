About This Model

A Jack Russell terrier paired with its wooden kennel, sold as one set for backyard scenes and pet-themed shots. The dog has the compact build, short coat and patched white body that make the breed recognizable from across a lawn. The kennel gives the animal a place to sit, so the scene reads as a home rather than a studio.

CheckMate Pro certification means both pieces arrive at real-world scale, so the terrier fits through the doorway of its house. Topology and naming have passed inspection, which helps when the dog needs to be posed, swapped or duplicated across a street of gardens. The pitched roof and plank walls of the kennel give strong silhouettes for a low camera angle.

Available since 2019, the set usually finds its way into suburban architectural renders, children's animation and pet care scenes. One purchase covers both the animal and its shelter, which saves hunting for a matching kennel in another style. Place it near a fence or a porch and a yard instantly looks lived in by a family.
