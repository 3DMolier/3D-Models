About This Model

The Flowering Hibiscus Stem Orange is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags advertising, product visualization and film production as the intended applications. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

At 42,513 polygons and 42,076 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It ships 12 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
