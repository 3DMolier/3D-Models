About This Model

Four-legged robot dogs walk, trot and pause in this set, animated and rigged for Maya with the jointed precision of real quadruped robots. Exposed actuators at the hips and knees show how the machine moves, which makes the animation easy to read. A compact sensor head replaces a muzzle, giving the robot a curious, almost animal posture.

The robots satisfy CheckMate standards, giving clean hard-surface geometry that holds sharp edges in close-ups. Maya users can open the files and scrub through the walk cycles immediately, without any retargeting work. Matte gray plastic with darker joint housings looks right under neutral studio or warehouse lighting.

Royalty free from 2026, the set fits robotics explainers, sci-fi films and security patrol simulations. Industrial inspection scenes, such as a robot walking through a plant, are a natural fit for these machines. Pair one with a human handler to show scale and the friendly side of the technology.
