About This Model

A compact aluminum flashlight with a knurled grip, tail switch and LED head, the kind kept in a glovebox or tool drawer. The LED Handheld Flashlight is built from 5,496 polygons and 5,834 vertices, all quads, so the knurling subdivides cleanly. A reflector cup behind the lens gives a spotlight cone somewhere believable to start in your scene.

It measures 5.02 x 28.2 x H5.02 cm, a full-size torch that fits a hand without looking like a toy. StemCell rules guarantee one source for every format, so the anodized blue finish matches across engines. Pair it with a volumetric beam and a dusty room to get the classic search sequence.

Three versions are on the page: this blue torch, the Maglite ML100 LED Flashlight and a Traffic Wand Flashlight. The trio dates from 2026, and this blue edition is priced at $29. Police scenes, horror games, camping shots and power outage interiors use it as a hand prop for searching dark spaces.
