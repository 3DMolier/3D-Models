About This Model

This collection sets a paper book beside an e-reader, the classic comparison between printed pages and glowing digital screens. Having both objects in one set makes it easy to stage reading nooks, study desks and travel bags. The thick paper block and slim device contrast nicely when placed together on a table.

A CheckMate Pro certificate means TurboSquid reviewed the set for topology and scene organization. The e-reader screen can display any page you render onto it, from a novel to a magazine or a textbook. Matte paper and a glare-free screen material keep the comparison honest even under bright studio lighting.

Around since 2020, the $39 set suits library interiors, education visuals and tech comparison articles. Place both on a bedside table with a warm lamp and a mug of tea to suggest late-night reading habits. Under the royalty-free license, publishers and device stores can use the renders commercially in campaigns.
