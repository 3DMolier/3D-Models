About This Model

Axess AX500 Smart Gates Collection provides ticket gates for ski lifts, stadiums and transit stations, with turnstile arms and card readers. Gathering them together keeps the housings, sensors and arms consistent across an entry line. Bright display panels on top show where a guest scans a pass.

The set passed CheckMate Pro inspection, which confirms tidy topology and accurate naming across every gate. Brushed steel housings and translucent panels take distinct materials, so the readers can glow softly. Their clean geometry makes it easy to animate a rotating arm for a crowd simulation.

Two versions appear on the page, this collection for $59 and an Axess AX500 Smart Gates Collection 2 for $69. Released in 2022, the gates fit ski resort visualization, event venue planning and access control explainers. Placed in a row with skiers queuing, they turn a resort scene into a working entrance.
