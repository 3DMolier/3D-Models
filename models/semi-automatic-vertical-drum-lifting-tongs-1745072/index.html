About This Model

Priced at $24, the Semi Automatic Vertical Drum Lifting Tongs is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 21,400 polygons and 21,524 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
