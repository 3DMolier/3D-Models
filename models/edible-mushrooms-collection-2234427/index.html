About This Model

A set of edible mushrooms with caps, gills and stems modeled in detail, useful for cooking shows, forest floors and grocery renders. Mushrooms grow in clusters, so having many ready shapes makes forest litter and market crates look natural. Damp moss and fallen leaves around the clusters finish a convincing forest floor.

Each piece is made to CheckMate specification, keeping scale right when caps sit beside knives or cutting boards. Earthy browns and creamy whites read best under soft daylight, which also flatters food photography setups. A light dusting of soil on the stems keeps the mushrooms from looking too clean and artificial.

Five mushroom versions are offered: the opening collection and four further sets of fungi. Mixed together they cover enough species for a foraging basket, a market crate or a woodland scene. The mushrooms were released in 2024 and are licensed royalty free for food content, games and education.
