About This Model

A printable dental model of teeth with ligature braces, prepared as a solid mesh for resin or filament printers. Brackets, archwire and ties are fused into one closed shape, so the model prints without loose parts. A physical print gives patients something to hold, which explains braces better than any screen.

The file follows CheckMate specification, so its scale and orientation are ready for a slicer. Fine bracket edges benefit from a high resolution resin printer and a careful support layout under the molars. Wall thickness on the gums is generous enough for handling without breaking. Painted after printing, silver brackets and colored ties make the model a striking chairside display.

Since 2025 the Ligature Braces on Teeth Model for 3D Print has served orthodontic clinics, dental students and lab technicians. It also helps a nervous patient see the whole treatment laid out in their hands before the first appointment.
