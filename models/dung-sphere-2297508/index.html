About This Model

Dung Sphere belongs to our Architecture & Landmarks range and is offered at $9 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 128 polygons and 130 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
