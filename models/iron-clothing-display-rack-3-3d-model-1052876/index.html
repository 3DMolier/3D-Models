About This Model

A wrought iron garment rail with a hanging bar, built to fill a boutique floor or a showroom corner with clothes on hangers. Iron Clothing Display Rack 3 has a dark metal frame with curved feet that keeps it steady under a full row of coats. Its open design lets shoppers see every hanging garment from the side.

Thin tubular members and welded joints give the rack an industrial loft look that suits concept stores and vintage shops. The metal parts take a matte black or a brushed bronze material equally well. CheckMate Pro certification confirms the model was inspected for correct scale, naming and clean geometry.

Since 2016 the rack has stood in fashion store interiors, wardrobe scenes and pop-up market stalls. Hang shirts and dresses along the bar for a retail render, or leave it empty in a dressing room under construction. Interior designers use it to test aisle spacing before a real store fit-out begins.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
