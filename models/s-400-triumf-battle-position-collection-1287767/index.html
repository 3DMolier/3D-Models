About This Model

The Triumf air defense system is a mobile battery of launchers, radars and command vehicles that guards airspace over long distances. This collection places those units into a battle position, so a whole site can be dropped into a scene at once. Launch canisters stand upright on truck chassis, ready for a missile to leave in a column of smoke.

The set is rated CheckMate Pro, which guarantees correct scale and clean naming across every vehicle. Separate objects let you raise, hide or move individual launchers when staging a scene.

At $139 the collection has 34 sales since 2018, mostly in defense news graphics, military documentaries and war games. Spread the vehicles across a snowy forest clearing to show a deployed position under camouflage nets. Wide aerial shots work best, because the layout of radar and launchers tells the story. Analysts often use such scenes to explain how layered air defense covers a region.
