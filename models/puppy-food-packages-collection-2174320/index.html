About This Model

Puppy food bags and boxes in a single file let a pet store shelf, kitchen pantry or online shop banner fill up quickly. Soft stand-up pouches show crimped seals at the top, while cartons keep crisp folded edges and flat printable fronts. Package fronts are the main canvas, ready to take your own branding or a generic label design.

A CheckMate Lite rating confirms reasonable scale and tidy object structure across the Puppy Food Packages Collection. Matte paper and glossy laminated film behave differently under light, so separate shaders help the pouches look real. Each package is its own object, so you can stack, lean or tip them over without detaching anything first.

Released in 2024, the collection suits pet product mockups, supermarket aisle scenes and animated commercials for pet brands. A torn bag with kibble spilling onto the floor is an easy scene to build and always gets a smile. Lined up in rows, the packages also make a quick background for a veterinary clinic reception area.
