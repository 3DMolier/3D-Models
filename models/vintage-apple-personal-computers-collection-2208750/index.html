About This Model

A collection of vintage Apple personal computers for retro tech scenes, with the beige cases and chunky keyboards of early home computing. Old computers are instantly recognized by shape alone, so even a background desk reads as a specific decade. Several machines in one bundle let you dress a museum shelf, a school lab or a garage startup scene.

Each machine was modeled to CheckMate standards for topology and size, though the bundle has not been certified. Yellowed plastic and faded keycaps sell the age, so a slightly warm material tint goes a long way. Combine them with CRT glow and a period desk lamp for a convincing early-computing office.

Available since 2024, the set falls under Editorial Uses Only because the Apple name and industrial designs are protected. That suits technology history documentaries, museum exhibits, educational videos and journalism about the personal computer era. Films set in past decades also use such props to anchor a bedroom or office in time.
