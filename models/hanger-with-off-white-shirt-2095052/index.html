About This Model

The Hanger Off Shirt is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 16,118 polygons over 8,446 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
