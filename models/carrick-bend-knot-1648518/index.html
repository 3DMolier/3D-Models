About This Model

The carrick bend joins two heavy ropes and, unusually, can still be untied after being loaded hard. That is why it was the traditional choice for towing hawsers, where the alternative was cutting the rope to free it.

The model is extremely light at 1,344 polygons and 1,404 vertices, built at subdivision level with non-overlapping UVs, so it smooths cleanly at any scale. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $29 it suits marine and outdoor education, technical illustration, equipment marketing, instructional material, game props and editorial work. On sale since 2021.
