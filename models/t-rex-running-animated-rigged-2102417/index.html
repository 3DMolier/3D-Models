About This Model

T Rex Running is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $179. Geometry weighs in at 37,736 polygons over 22,452 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
