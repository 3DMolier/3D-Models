About This Model

Tall lighting masts with floodlight clusters, the kind installed at stadiums, ports, highway interchanges and industrial yards. The masts vary in height, lamp arrangement and head design, so a large outdoor site can be lit without every pole looking the same.

Each mast was checked to CheckMate Pro standard, confirming clean geometry and orderly names for poles and lamp heads. Floodlight heads are separate parts, so they can be aimed at a pitch or animated switching on one by one. The slender poles, service platforms and access ladders look correct when viewed from ground level.

Available from 2020, the collection suits stadium visualization, port logistics scenes and night environments in open-world games. Lined along a container terminal, the masts throw convincing pools of light across stacked boxes and cranes. Lighting engineers show illumination coverage for car parks, rail yards and sports fields with them during planning.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2020 · Updated 13 November 2026
