About This Model

The Integrated Urban Megastructure With Oval Transit Hub Asphalt is a production-ready Architecture & Landmarks 3D model, priced at $299 on TurboSquid. You are getting 1,345,467 polygons and 1,414,223 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 1022.0 x 551.48 x H101.87 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
