About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Express Locomotive Generic is a finished, render-ready model at $99. The mesh carries 236,841 polygons and 264,752 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 7 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
