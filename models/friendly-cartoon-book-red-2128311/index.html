About This Model

Friendly Cartoon Book Red is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. You are getting 16,752 polygons and 8,774 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
