About This Model

The Single Butter Croissant on Plate is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $34 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 1,234 polygons and 1,238 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Real-world footprint is 14.75 x 14.75 x H6.73 cm, so it drops into a scene at correct scale without a rescaling pass. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
