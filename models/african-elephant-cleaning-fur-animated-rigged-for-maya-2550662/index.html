About This Model

A savanna elephant sprays dust or water over its back with its trunk in this furred Maya cleaning animation, rigged for further posing. Huge fan-shaped ears, curved tusks and wrinkled skin with sparse fur give the animal its unmistakable African profile. The trunk arcs overhead during the cleaning cycle, the most expressive motion an elephant performs.

CheckMate-specification construction gives the Maya scene clearly named rig controls, fur and body mesh. Bristly hairs along the back and tail tip catch rim light, revealing the scale of the body.

Arriving in 2026, the elephant fits wildlife documentaries, safari tourism films and conservation lessons about animal behavior. Game developers building African environments gain a lively background animal that does more than walk. Dusk lighting through the sprayed particles turns the cleaning action into a cinematic moment. Zoo apps and children's nature series can also use the clip to explain how elephants cool down.
