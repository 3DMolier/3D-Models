About This Model

This Vehicles model - Old Locomotive Planet by Robert Stephenson - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 857,389 polygons over 447,860 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
