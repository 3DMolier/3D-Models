About This Model

Concrete Pillars Set is a detailed Architecture & Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. You are getting 282,056 polygons and 282,778 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
