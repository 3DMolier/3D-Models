About This Model

A jet pilot's helmet is a mounting platform. The shell carries a visor track, an oxygen mask bayonet on each side, a communications lead and increasingly a mount for a display or night vision. Those attachment points are what make it read as fast jet equipment rather than as a generic flight helmet.

The mesh runs 54,763 polygons and 57,473 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the visor mechanism, the bayonet receivers and the shell edge.

At $44 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits military aviation visualization, film and television costume, character set dressing for games, equipment marketing and training material. Real-world scale is kept. On sale since 2025.
