About This Model

Worker for belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $179 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
