About This Model

A laborer figure dressed in practical workwear, made to populate factories, building sites and warehouse scenes. Proportions follow a real adult body, so the character sits naturally next to machines and vehicles at true scale. A few copies spread across a site make an otherwise empty building scene feel busy and alive.

The model is built to CheckMate specification, meaning clean topology and an organized file were part of the brief. Clothing folds and boots carry enough detail for medium shots, while a wide view still reads well. Hands and face are modeled with enough care to hold a medium close-up beside a machine control panel. A plain outfit color scheme makes it easy to adapt the figure to any company safety palette.

Two versions are offered at $179 each, the base Worker and the Worker Rigged for Maya. Since 2017 the character has earned 13 sales from industrial training videos, safety presentations and architectural renders.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2017 · Updated 11 November 2026
