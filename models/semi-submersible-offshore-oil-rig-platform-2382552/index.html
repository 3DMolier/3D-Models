About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Semi-Submersible Offshore Oil Rig Platform is a finished, render-ready model at $249. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and software demos. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current.
