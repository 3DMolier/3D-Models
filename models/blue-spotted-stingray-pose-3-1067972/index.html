About This Model

This Animals & Creatures model - Spotted Stingray Pose 3 - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. On import you get 3,043 polygons and 3,051 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Textures number 8, authored at 4096 x 4096 so they survive a close crop. The geometry is built to be worked with: predictable loops, no doubled faces, nothing hidden inside the shell. Parts are separated where separation is useful, so a single component can be hidden, swapped or re-shaded on its own.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. Its first release was in 2016, and it has earned its place in the catalog since then. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
