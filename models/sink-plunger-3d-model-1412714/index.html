About This Model

This Architecture Landmarks model - Sink Plunger - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

Geometry weighs in at 4,056 polygons over 4,058 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 4 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
