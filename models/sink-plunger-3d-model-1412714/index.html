About This Model

Priced at $19, the Sink Plunger is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 4,056 polygons and 4,058 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
