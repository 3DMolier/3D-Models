About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Boeing 747-8 Freighter is a finished, render-ready model at $149. You are getting 215,377 polygons and 227,491 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

What is the Boeing 747-8 Freighter model typically used for?

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Boeing 747-8 Freighter mesh?
