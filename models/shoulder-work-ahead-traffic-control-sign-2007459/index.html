About This Model

A temporary works sign is a diamond plate on a folding trestle rather than a fixed post, because it has to be put out in the morning and taken in at night. Orange rather than yellow marks it as temporary, and the frame legs are weighted or sandbagged so wind from passing traffic does not take it.

The mesh is efficient at 28,516 polygons and 14,894 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the plate face as its own surface so a different legend can be applied without remodeling.

At $24 it suits roadworks and highway visualization, traffic management presentation, driving simulators and games, urban and street set dressing, and safety material. On sale since 2023.
