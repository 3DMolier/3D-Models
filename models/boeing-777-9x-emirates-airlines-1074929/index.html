About This Model

The Boeing 777-9x Emirates is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 786,985 polygons and 808,553 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 12 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
