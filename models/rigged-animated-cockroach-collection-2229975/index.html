About This Model

Cockroaches that scuttle, twitch their antennae and crawl over surfaces, rigged and animated so an infestation can come alive. The Rigged Animated Cockroach Collection saves animators the fiddly work of making every jointed leg move in a believable rhythm. Glossy brown wing covers and long antennae are the details that make viewers flinch, and they are modeled here.

The set follows CheckMate specification, so each insect has clean names, sensible pivots and real-world size. Because they are tiny, cockroaches are usually scaled up for macro shots, and the geometry holds up to that. Ready animation cycles let a swarm spread across a kitchen floor by simply duplicating and offsetting a few bugs.

Since 2024 the collection has served horror films, pest control commercials and disgust-driven game moments. A handful crawling out of a drain or under a fridge says more about a room than any dirt texture. Biology lessons use the animated insects to show how cockroaches move and why they are hard to catch.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
