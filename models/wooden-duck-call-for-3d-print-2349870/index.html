About This Model

A wooden-style duck call, the short mouth-blown whistle hunters use to imitate mallard quacks from a reed blind. The 3,399 polygons shape the barrel, the tapered insert and the reed opening at the mouthpiece end. Its compact shape fits in a palm, and the flared end directs sound out across the water.

With 3,397 vertices the mesh stays watertight, which is exactly what a printable model needs to slice cleanly. It meets CheckMate standards, so the call keeps realistic proportions for printing or for rendering beside hunting gear. Clean UV islands let the barrel take a walnut or cherry grain texture in visual work.

The model appeared in 2025 for hobby printing, hunting gear and outdoor sports scenes. Hunters print it as a practice piece, while outdoor brands and game artists hang it from lanyards in hunting scenes. Next to decoys and a camouflage jacket, it completes a waterfowl hunting still life.
