About This Model

An octopus is the hardest kind of animal to model because it has no fixed shape. The mantle changes volume, the arms taper over their whole length and curl in every direction, and the suckers have to run in a double row that thins toward the tip. This build handles all three, which is what lets it read as a soft-bodied animal rather than a rubber toy.

The mesh is 60,158 polygons and 60,136 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 6 texture maps at 4096x4096. The texture work is doing heavy lifting here: skin mottling, the sheen of a wet surface and the darker sucker rims are what sell the creature at close range.

At $99 and built to StemCell specification, it suits marine documentary and education, aquarium and museum material, seafood and restaurant advertising, film and television, and games. On sale since 2017.
