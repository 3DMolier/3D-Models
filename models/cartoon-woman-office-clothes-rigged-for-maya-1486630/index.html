About This Model

A stylized cartoon woman in a neat office outfit, prepared for Maya animators building workplace and business stories. Large expressive eyes and simplified hands follow cartoon conventions, which keeps her readable on small screens. Her proportions sit between realistic and toylike, so she works in corporate explainers without feeling childish.

CheckMate specification guided the topology, giving clean edge loops around joints where an animator needs deformation. Flat, even lighting suits the style best, since cartoon shading relies on clean color blocks rather than texture detail. Her outfit colors can be recolored quickly to match a client's brand palette or a team uniform.

Available from 2019, she suits explainer videos, onboarding courses, mobile apps and animated presentations for HR teams. Place her at a desk or in front of a whiteboard, and the character carries a slide's message by herself. Pair her with the youth-clothes version of the same character, and one story can move from office to weekend.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
