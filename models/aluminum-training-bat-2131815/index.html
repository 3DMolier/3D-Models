About This Model

A light aluminum training bat with a grip handle and capped barrel, it suits batting cages and youth practice. The Aluminum Training Bat needs only 4,648 polygons, spending them on the knob, the taper and the rounded end cap. The low count makes it a good handheld prop for a character rig without hurting frame rate.

It carries CheckMate Lite certification, and 2,328 vertices make a simple cage that smooths well. Non-overlapping UVs leave clear space for grip wear, scuffs and painted stripes along the barrel.

The page has two versions: the aluminum trainer and a steel baseball bat with its own finish. Both read as metal, but the steel one gives a heavier look in a sports store shot. Lean it on a dugout fence or lay it on the grass beside a glove and a scuffed ball. Available since 2023 and free of royalty fees, it fits baseball games, coaching videos and sporting goods renders.
