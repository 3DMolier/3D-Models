About This Model

A roadside EV fast charging station with Tesla and Xiaomi cars plugged in, ready for electric mobility scenes. Cables run from each charger to a car port, so the charging moment reads clearly without any explanation.

The scene runs to 388,122 polygons and 413,992 vertices, with detail in charger cabinets, cable holsters and car bodies. A canopy shelters the bays, and painted parking lines guide each car neatly to its own post. Bright cabinet panels contrast with dark asphalt, keeping the station visible even in night renders.

EVgo, Tesla and Xiaomi are real trademarks, so this scene carries an Editorial Uses Only license. That makes it right for news coverage and energy reports, and since 2024 it has helped planners show where fast chargers fit. UVs without overlaps make it easy to weather the pavement or add puddles for a rainy evening. The Chinese and American cars side by side also illustrate how global the electric car market has become.
