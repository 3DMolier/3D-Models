About This Model

A defensive opossum guarding an ear of corn, fur raised and mouth open, built for farm and backyard wildlife scenes. At 13,968 polygons the animal stays light enough for game levels and wide farm shots. The corn cob is part of the scene, giving the pose a reason and a story at a glance.

Grooming covers the coat, while 14,543 vertices keep the underlying body simple enough for quick edits. Non-overlapping UVs let the bare tail, the pink nose and the husks of the corn take separate texture detail. CheckMate rules were followed during the build, so scale and naming stay predictable inside a larger agricultural set.

Released in 2025, it fits crop damage explainers, rural animation, educational wildlife content and survival games set in American woodland. Low evening light from behind the animal brings out the guard hairs and turns the open mouth into a warning. Placed beside a barn door or a compost heap, the opossum becomes an instantly readable piece of country life.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2025 · Updated 14 November 2026
