About This Model

Priced at $99, the Business Jet Interior is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and aerospace visualization. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 49,368 polygons and 52,522 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 4 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
