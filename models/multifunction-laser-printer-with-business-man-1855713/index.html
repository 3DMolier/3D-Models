About This Model

Priced at $189, the Multifunction Laser Printer with Business Man is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 114,925 polygons and 117,821 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.
