About This Model

A collection of cut pumpkins showing bright orange flesh and fibrous seed cavities, made for autumn kitchens and markets. Halves, wedges and slices give enough variety to build a harvest table or a cooking prep scene. The seeds and stringy pulp inside add a natural, slightly messy realism.

These pieces carry CheckMate Pro certification, so topology is clean and objects are well organized for quick placement. Waxy rind and moist flesh contrast nicely under soft daylight from a kitchen window. Thick walls on each cut face show the true depth of the rind, which keeps the slices from looking hollow or thin.

Offered from 2024, the pumpkin pieces suit Halloween scenes, soup recipe videos, farm market stalls and seasonal food advertising. Arrange a few wedges on a cutting board with a knife for a ready-made cooking moment. Surround them with dried leaves and the frame feels like late October.
