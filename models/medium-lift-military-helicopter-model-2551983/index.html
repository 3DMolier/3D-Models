About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Medium Lift Military Helicopter Model is a finished, render-ready model at $139. Geometry weighs in at 16,419 polygons over 16,400 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation as the intended applications. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
