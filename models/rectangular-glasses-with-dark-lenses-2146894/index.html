About This Model

The Rectangular Glasses with Dark Lenses is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering.
