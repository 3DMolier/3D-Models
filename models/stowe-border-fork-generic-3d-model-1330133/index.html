About This Model

Stowe Border Fork Generic is one of the Tools models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. You are getting 2,588 polygons and 2,662 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
