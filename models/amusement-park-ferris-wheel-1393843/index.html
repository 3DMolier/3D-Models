About This Model

The Ferris Wheel is a production-ready Architecture & Landmarks 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 905,905 polygons and 961,091 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

34 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
