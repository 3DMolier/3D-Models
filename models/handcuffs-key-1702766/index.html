About This Model

Priced at $9, the Handcuffs Key is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 2,215 polygons and 2,208 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2021 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
