About This Model

A decorative sculpture of a smooth rock suspended above a slim base, creating the illusion of a stone floating in the air. Only 2,758 polygons and 2,735 vertices are needed, since the shapes are simple and the rounded surfaces stay soft. The balanced, minimal look suits a modern shelf, a reception desk or a calm meditation corner.

Color and stone grain come from materials, as the model includes no image maps. Non-overlapping UVs accept marble veins, speckles or a polished finish painted directly on the surface. TurboSquid checked the file for CheckMate Pro, approving its topology, scale and well organized parts.

Two other colors are available as versions at $29: a red piece and a lilac one. Placing the colors on different shelves ties a room's palette together without repeating one object. Released in 2022, the piece styles living rooms, hotel lobbies and interior design portfolios.
