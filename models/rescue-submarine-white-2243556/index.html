About This Model

The Rescue Submarine is a production-ready Ships & Boats 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 174,674 polygons and 177,007 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for defense simulation, naval simulation and game environments.

2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
