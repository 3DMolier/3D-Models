About This Model

A dent puller works by pressing a rubber cup against a panel and levering the air out of it, so the tool is essentially a suction cup with a cam handle. Shown open, the cup is relaxed and the handle is up, which is the state it is in when it is placed rather than pulling.

The mesh is light at 8,352 polygons and 4,182 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the cup, the cam and the handle.

At $19 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits automotive repair visualization, bodyshop and workshop scenes, tool retail and catalog renders, DIY material and games. On sale since 2023.
