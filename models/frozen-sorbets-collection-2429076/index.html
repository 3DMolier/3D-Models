About This Model

This frozen sorbets collection gathers several scoops and servings of colorful fruit ice, ready for dessert menus and summer scenes. Mixing flavors and colors across the set lets a gelato counter or a cafe table look naturally abundant. The slightly rough, icy surfaces of the scoops read differently from creamy ice cream, which is true to sorbet.

All pieces follow CheckMate specification rules, keeping files tidy and scales consistent across the collection for quick assembly. Bright raspberry, mango and lemon tones pop under warm sunlight, especially against a white ceramic dish. A frosty subsurface shader adds the faint translucency that makes frozen fruit look refreshing on camera.

Debuting in 2025, the collection suits dessert menus, ice cream shop designs and food blog illustrations during hot months. Add a mint leaf and a small spoon, and a summer terrace dessert course appears in seconds. Restaurant games and kids' apps also use sorbets to fill freezers and reward players with treats after levels.
