About This Model

A print-ready model of the Guanyin statue at Nanshan, the towering white bodhisattva that stands at the edge of the sea in Hainan. The figure is prepared for 3D printing, so the form is closed and solid rather than split into render-only shells. Robes fall in long vertical folds, and the lotus base gives a broad, stable footprint on the print bed.

Fine elements such as the crown, the hands and the drapery edges are thick enough to survive a resin or filament print. It follows the CheckMate specification for clean structure, so slicing software reads it without manual repair. A small desktop print makes a calm altar piece, while a larger one works as a museum or souvenir replica.

Released in 2024, it goes to collectors, temple gift shops and teachers who want a tangible piece of Buddhist sculpture. Digital artists also use it as a landmark in coastal scenes of southern China, rendered from a distance across the water.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
