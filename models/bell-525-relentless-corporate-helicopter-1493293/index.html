About This Model

The Bell 525 was the first civil helicopter designed around fly-by-wire controls, and the airframe shows the ambition: a large cabin with airliner-style windows, a five-blade main rotor and a fuselage that looks more like a business aircraft than a utility machine. In corporate configuration it is a boardroom that lands on a roof, and the model keeps the airstair door, the sponsons and the tail rotor gearbox fairing.

The mesh is 258,121 polygons and 269,303 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 12 texture maps at 4096x4096, which is what a gloss corporate livery needs to stay clean over a large fuselage.

This page is well stocked: a paramedic version at $119, a generic corporate build at $119, and rigged versions from $169 to $199. Take a rigged one when the rotors turn. Aimed at corporate aviation marketing, emergency services visualization, film and television and games.
