Polka Dot Bikini is a detailed Clothing & Accessories asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 8,607 polygons and 8,589 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
