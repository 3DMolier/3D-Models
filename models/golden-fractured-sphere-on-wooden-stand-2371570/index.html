About This Model

Golden Fractured Sphere on Wooden Stand is one of the models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
