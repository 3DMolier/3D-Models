About This Model

Climbing Shoes Collection gathers several snug rock climbing shoes with downturned toes and sticky rubber soles. Climbing shoes look nothing like sneakers: the toe curls down and the rubber wraps over the front. Straps or laces pull the fit tight, and those shapes are what climbers recognize at a glance.

Several models in one bundle let you show a gear wall, a shop shelf or a bag spilled at the crag. A CheckMate Lite rating confirms the files open properly in common packages and come with basic organization. Separate shoes make it easy to place one pair on a boulder or line up the whole range.

On sale since 2023, the collection suits climbing gym interiors, outdoor gear catalogs and sports visualizations. Game developers use the shoes as character equipment or pickup items in adventure and mountain survival levels. Videos about climbing technique, foot placement and choosing the right footwear for a route are another natural use.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
