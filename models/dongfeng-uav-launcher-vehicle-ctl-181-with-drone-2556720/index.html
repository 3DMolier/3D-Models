About This Model

A military truck carrying a drone launch rail, built for modern defense, unmanned systems and military parade scenes with modern hardware. The 418,210 polygons and 453,178 vertices cover the cab, launcher frame, drone airframe and multi-axle chassis. The drone perched on its angled rail atop the truck looks seconds away from a catapult launch.

CheckMate methodology guided the build, and the mixed UV layout handles both pixel camouflage and smooth drone surfaces. Pixelated camouflage reads best under flat daylight on dusty terrain, where its pattern blends with the ground. The rugged military cab and high ground clearance suit off-road deployment scenes in remote terrain.

Two versions present the launcher: Dongfeng UAV Launcher Vehicle CTL-181 with Drone and a Pixel camouflage Rigged edition. Defense commentators use it to illustrate how mobile platforms launch reconnaissance and strike drones from the field without a runway.
