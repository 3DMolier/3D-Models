About This Model

A bundle of modern digital parking meters, from slim pay stations to pole-mounted units with screens and card readers for downtown streets. Solar panels, keypads and ticket slots give each meter a believable streetside function for city scenes. Lined along a curb between parked cars, the meters instantly mark a street as a paid parking zone.

The bundle earned CheckMate Pro status, so each meter arrives with clean topology and clear names. Weatherproof housings and printed instruction panels add realism when the camera passes close to the sidewalk at eye level. Bases sit flat on the pavement, so placing a row of units along a street takes seconds.

Two versions share the set: the Digital Parking Meters Collection and Digital Parking Meters Collection 2 with further models. Available since 2021, it fits urban planning visuals, smart city concepts, parking app explainers and street level game environments. A driver tapping a card at the meter adds small but useful realism to a downtown scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
