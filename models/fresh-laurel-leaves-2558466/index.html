Priced at $19, the Fresh Laurel Leaves is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 1,582 polygons and 1,496 vertices. That sits in low-poly territory, so it stays cheap to instance across a crowd scene or a game level. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and game assets.
