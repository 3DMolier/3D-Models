About This Model

A little African boy asleep in his bed, curled under the covers in a calm nursery or bedroom scene. Of the 56,408 polygons, much goes into the soft folds of the bedding and the relaxed shape of the child. Closed eyes and a slightly turned head give the pose the heavy stillness of real deep sleep.

Its 89,618 vertices sit on non-overlapping UVs, so pajama prints and sheet patterns can be changed freely. CheckMate specification keeps the bed and child in real proportions, so the scene fits standard room heights. Pillows and blankets bunched around the boy make the bed look slept in rather than freshly made.

First offered in 2024, the bedroom scene costs $189 and comes royalty free. Parenting apps, children's furniture catalogs, healthcare explainers about sleep and family interior renders suit it well. Warm night-light glow and a dim window frame the boy as the quiet center of the room.
