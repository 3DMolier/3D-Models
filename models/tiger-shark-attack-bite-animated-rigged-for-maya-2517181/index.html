About This Model

Tiger Shark Attack Bite Animated Rigged For Maya captures a tiger shark lunging with jaws wide open, built for Maya artists staging underwater attacks. The blunt snout, striped flanks and rows of serrated teeth are what make a tiger shark look different from a great white.

Its build follows CheckMate guidelines, which keeps topology clean and every part sensibly named. Faded vertical stripes over grey skin need soft caustic light from above to read correctly underwater. Maya users receive it ready for their own software, avoiding a conversion pass before scene work.
