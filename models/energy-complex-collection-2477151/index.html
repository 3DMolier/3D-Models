About This Model

A collection of energy complex structures, the buildings and equipment that together make up a working power generation site. Bundled together, the pieces share a common style, so a plant layout looks designed rather than assembled from strangers. Expect the shapes people associate with industrial power, from boxy halls to tall vertical elements.

StemCell certification provides one source with PBR materials that hold up across every delivered format. Pivots sit where you expect them, so pieces can be instanced and rearranged into a site plan quickly. Because it is a set, the scale between the pieces already agrees, which saves an evening of careful measuring.

Released in 2025, the energy collection suits utility presentations, city builder games and infrastructure animation. Royalty free terms allow commercial use in energy sector advertising and in investor presentation material. Spread the buildings across a valley, link them with transmission lines, and a whole grid region appears.
