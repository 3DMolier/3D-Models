About This Model

The Wooden Meeting Room is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $31 on TurboSquid. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 29,399 polygons and 32,718 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
