About This Model

A medical worker in a disposable protective suit pushing a hospital gurney across an automatic sole cleaning mat at a clinic entrance. The machine scrubs shoe soles and wheels as people pass, the kind of hygiene station that spread during the epidemic years. White overalls, gloves and a face shield make the figure read as infection control staff at a glance.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms the scene is at real-world scale, with clean geometry and organized naming. Here the cleaner unit is the star, presented as a fixture for hospital lobbies and clean room doors. Bright, even lighting with cool tones suits the sterile mood of a disinfection zone.

Available since 2021, it fits hygiene equipment advertising, hospital interior design, infection control training and pandemic documentaries. Manufacturers of sole cleaners can show their machine in a realistic workflow rather than as an isolated product. Clean room entrances in labs and food plants are equally good settings for the scene.
