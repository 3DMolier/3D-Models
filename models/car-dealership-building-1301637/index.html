About This Model

Car Dealership Building is a detailed Architecture Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags architectural visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
