About This Model

This Medical model - Cross-Section of the Human Eye Left Part - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $139 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 70,697 polygons and 71,363 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 45 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
