About This Model

Looking for a Architecture Landmarks asset? The Street Fragment Cobblestone is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 24,578 polygons and 27,614 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. Texture work runs to 20 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
