About This Model

A green aircrew flight suit standing upright as if worn, with pockets, zips and patches laid out for pilots and crew. The Aircrew Flight Uniform in Standing Pose Green carries 88,391 polygons and 91,202 vertices for fabric folds and fittings. Leg pockets, a sleeve pen holder and the long front zipper are the classic markers of a military flight suit.

It stands 74.09 x 44.01 x H190.98 cm, the height of a tall adult. Overlapping UVs reuse texture space across matching pockets and sleeves, keeping fabric detail dense. Constructed to CheckMate specification, the suit has sensible pivots and imports at real size.

Since 2025 the uniform has served aviation museum displays, air force recruitment visuals and flight school material. Hung in a locker room or standing in a briefing scene, it signals a pilot without showing a face. Costume departments use it to preview patch placement and fit before tailoring a real suit.
