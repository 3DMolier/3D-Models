About This Model

Priced at $149, the EOD 10 Bomb Suit is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
