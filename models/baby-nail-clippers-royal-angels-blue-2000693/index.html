About This Model

Small clippers sized for a baby's fingers, with a blue plastic grip and a gentle curved blade for safe trimming. Baby Nail Clippers Royal Angels Blue are compact enough to appear in a hand close-up or inside a newborn care kit. The lever folds over the cutting head, and that simple mechanism is clearly shaped in the geometry.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms clean topology and correct scale, which matters for an object this tiny in a scene. Metal blades and matte plastic can take separate materials, so the tool reads clearly against soft fabrics.

Available since 2022, the clippers fit pharmacy shelf renders, parenting blogs and baby care product shots. Lay them on a soft towel beside a baby lotion bottle and the scene feels calm and domestic. They also make a useful small prop for a nursery dresser in an interior visualization. Shown next to an adult hand, the scale difference instantly signals that the tool is made for infants.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

What is the Baby Nail Clippers Royal Angels Blue model typically used for?

Stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

How heavy is the Baby Nail Clippers Royal Angels Blue mesh?

How clean is the geometry on the Baby Nail Clippers Royal Angels Blue model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.
