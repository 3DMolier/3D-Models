About This Model

Floating-Pontoon belongs to our Architecture & Landmarks range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 3,064,748 polygons and 1,533,040 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
