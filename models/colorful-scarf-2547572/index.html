About This Model

This Clothing & Accessories model - Colorful Scarf - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. You are getting 13,146 polygons and 13,126 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 3 maps at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
