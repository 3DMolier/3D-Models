About This Model

A bundle of vintage vehicles that recreates the streets and country roads of the early automobile era. Each model is a separate object, so the set can fill a whole town square or appear one at a time. Lined up along a cobbled street, the vehicles take the viewer back several decades in one shot.

Spoked wheels, running boards and round headlamps are the cues that make old transport read as old. Every vehicle meets the CheckMate Pro standard for object naming and a correct sense of scale. Faded paint and soft chrome suit a warm, slightly desaturated color grade for period films.

Offered at $129 since 2020, the collection supports historical dramas, museum displays and retro games. Buying the set at once costs less than collecting comparable vehicles separately for one project. Wedding scenes and nostalgia illustrations also use vintage vehicles as charming decorative background pieces.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2020 · Updated 13 November 2026
