About This Model

Electric Semi Truck Nikola One EV is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $99. You are getting 184,144 polygons and 208,219 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That is a heavy, close-up-grade build, so plan for it as a hero object rather than background filler. 8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and game environments. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.
