About This Model

The Pantsir S1 SA-22 Desert Camo Rigged is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $179 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 644,600 polygons and 693,973 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

22 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
