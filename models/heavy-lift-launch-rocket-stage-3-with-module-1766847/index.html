About This Model

Heavy Lift Launch Rocket Stage-3 with Module is one of the Space & Sci-Fi models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 137,571 polygons and 149,172 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
