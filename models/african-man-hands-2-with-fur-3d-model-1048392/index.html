About This Model

This Medical model - African Man Hands 2 with Fur - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 6,512 polygons and 6,536 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 10 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
