About This Model

A crinkled potato chip bag in the familiar sour cream and onion flavor, ready for snack aisles and kitchen counters. The Lays Sour Cream and Onion Chips Bag Game Ready uses just 2,557 polygons and 2,345 vertices, suited to real-time scenes.

The bag measures 20.12 x 9.86 x H31.17 cm, matching a standard single bag on a store shelf. StemCell delivery provides matching materials in each format, so the green packaging looks the same in every engine. Crimped seals at top and bottom and a puffed body give it a convincing silhouette at low cost. Light reflects off the foil inside the seams, giving the packaging a slightly glossy, plastic feel.

One version is available, sold since 2026 at $29 for game and interior work. Convenience store games, party scenes, couch-potato living rooms and vending machine fills use it as everyday clutter. Repeat it on shelves with slight rotations and the low budget lets a whole aisle stay fast.
