About This Model

A bald eagle soars on outstretched wings in this animated Blender model, gliding with the effortless calm of a master flier. Broad dark wings with spread primaries, a white head and a fanned white tail form the classic silhouette of North American skies. Subtle adjustments of the tail and wingtips keep the bird balanced on rising air currents.

Its structure follows CheckMate specification, so feathers, beak and talons stay organized for later edits. Blender users can loop the glide endlessly behind titles or let the eagle circle a mountain peak. Backlit by the sun, the wing feathers glow at their edges and reveal their layered structure.

This 2026 eagle is free of royalties for patriotic documentaries, national park films and outdoor adventure games. Civic broadcasts often open with an eagle gliding across a landscape as a symbol of freedom. Pair the glide with a slow crane over canyon walls, and the shot instantly feels majestic.
