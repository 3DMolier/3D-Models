About This Model

Sled With Levers is a blue alpine coaster cart that runs on a steel rail, with brake levers the rider pulls to slow down. The 100,386 polygons and 108,522 vertices go into the seat shell, the lever pivots, the rail wheels and the frame. Set on a mountain track among pine trees, the sled brings a family amusement ride into a resort scene.

Wheels and brackets repeat across the cart, so overlapping UVs let them draw on one texture area. Under StemCell rules the cart's glossy plastic and metal look the same in each delivered format. Twin levers at the front are the detail riders remember, so frame them clearly in a close shot.

Rail-Guided Coaster Sled With Levers Green Green is the green version, costing $59 just like the blue cart. Since 2025 the sled has been chosen for theme park visualizations, ski resort scenes, ride design presentations and casual games. Several carts spaced along a winding track make a summer toboggan run feel busy and alive.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2025 · Updated 27 November 2026
