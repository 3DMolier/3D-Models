About This Model

A set of cartridge tattoo needles in clear plastic housings, each ready to click into a modern rotary pen. The 7,089 polygons describe the grip tips, the membrane housings and the fine needle groupings inside. Its 7,415 vertices keep the small parts sharp enough for a macro view on a tattoo tray.

The file holds a CheckMate Lite rating from TurboSquid, confirming basic geometry quality and a sound file. Transparent plastic and steel needle materials are easy to adjust, since there are no bitmaps to replace. Colored caps can be retinted to tell configurations apart in a lineup of supplies.

Made available in 2020, the needles suit tattoo studio interiors, artist supply catalogs and hygiene training material. Line them up on a sterile tray next to ink caps and gloves to show a station before a session. Educators in body art courses also use them when explaining needle groupings and safe disposal.
