About This Model

A dip station is a frame that has to hold a body's whole weight on two handles, and the engineering shows: a wide base for stability, uprights braced back at an angle, and grips set at shoulder width. Those proportions are what make it read as gym equipment rather than as a handrail, and this model carries them along with the foot pads and the adjustment holes.

The mesh is 25,182 polygons and 26,386 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the detail in the joints, the grip ends and the hardware.

At $19 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits gym and fitness centre visualisation, home training interiors, sports equipment retail renders, health advertising and games. On sale since 2025, kept to real-world scale so a figure can use it correctly.
