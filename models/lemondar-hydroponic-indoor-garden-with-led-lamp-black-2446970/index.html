About This Model

A countertop hydroponic unit is mostly a lamp. The water and nutrients are trivial; the difficult part is delivering enough light in a kitchen, and that is where the whole cost of the product goes.

The model is light at 37,290 polygons and 38,119 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in black, and carries StemCell certification.

At $19 it suits kitchen and interior visualisation, product and e-commerce imagery, advertising, sustainability and lifestyle scenes, film props and game environments. On sale since 2025.
