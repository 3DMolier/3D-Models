About This Model

A dried alien body lies preserved like a mummy, with an elongated skull, thin limbs and skin stretched tight over the bones. The whole creature uses only 10,643 polygons, light enough for a game while still showing ribs and sunken features. Its 10,645 vertices sit almost level with the polygon total, a sign of a clean all-quad surface.

As a StemCell asset, its materials render the same leathery look across the supported renderers. The unwrap keeps each island apart, so cracks, dust and fabric wraps can be painted onto the body. The low mesh also takes a sculpting pass well if you want more wrinkles for a close autopsy shot.

Dating from 2023 and Royalty Free, it works for horror games, conspiracy documentaries and science fiction films. A desiccated visitor on a lab table instantly sets a secret base or crash site mood. Escape rooms and museum parodies also display it in a glass case to tease a hidden story.
