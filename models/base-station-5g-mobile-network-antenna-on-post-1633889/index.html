About This Model

A cellular base station with flat panel antennas clamped to a steel post, the hardware behind 5G coverage in cities. Base Station 5G Mobile Network Antenna on Post spends 94,110 polygons over 95,189 vertices on brackets, cable runs and radio units. Cables loop down from each panel to the equipment box, as they do on real masts beside highways.

Galvanized steel and plastic radome finishes come out of 10 maps at 4096 x 4096 for the mast. The StemCell standard means the antenna looks the same across major engines, and its UV islands stay apart. Engineers can tilt each panel toward the area it serves, since the clamps are separate parts.

There is just one version at $29, and the station has 20 buyers since its 2020 release. Telecom presentations, smart city renders and tech news stories use it to explain how coverage reaches a street. Placed on a rooftop over busy traffic, the station turns invisible signal into a single readable image.
