About This Model

This plump boiled milk sausage with smooth pink casing and pinched ends fills breakfast plates, deli counters and grill scenes. Only 2,138 polygons and 2,134 vertices are needed for the gently curved body and the twisted casing tips. Whole on a board or sliced into rounds, it reads as a classic European cold cut.

Glossy skin and meat texture come from 2 maps at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024 on separate UV islands. As a StemCell asset, it ships in several formats from one master file with matching materials. A soft sheen on the casing suggests moisture, which is what makes cooked meat look appetizing on camera.

Two versions are offered, the Cooked Milk Sausage at $29 and a simpler Sausage 3D Model at $19. The cheaper sausage runs 2,202 polygons, about 3% heavier, so both sit comfortably in a scene full of food. Out since 2018, it often ends up in food blogs, supermarket flyers and cooking games.
