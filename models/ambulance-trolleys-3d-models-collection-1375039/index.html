About This Model

A stretcher trolley is a machine for moving a person who cannot help. It has to fold to enter a vehicle, lock at several heights, take a patient's full weight on one end while being lifted at the other, and do all of it in seconds with one person if necessary.

This is a collection listing rather than a single object, supplying the trolleys as static pieces. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $89 the set suits healthcare and emergency services visualization, medical equipment marketing, training material, film and television sets, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2019.
