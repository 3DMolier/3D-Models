About This Model

A box's worth of chalk sticks in several colors, this set covers classroom boards, sidewalk art and tailoring scenes at once. Each stick has the soft rounded ends and slightly worn edges of chalk that has already been used a little. Grouping them into one collection saves time when a scene needs a scattered handful rather than one perfect piece.

The pieces are separate objects, so you can tip a few over, lay them in a tray or line them up along a ledge. The collection passed CheckMate Lite inspection, which means clean naming and a file that opens without missing parts. Surfaces are simple enough to take a powdery shader, and a faint dust trail on the floor finishes the look.

First offered in 2023, the chalks fit school interiors, art class animations and children's book illustrations. On a blackboard ledge or a playground pavement they add color in a corner of the frame without stealing focus. Game artists also scatter them in abandoned classrooms where small, bright objects guide the player's eye.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
