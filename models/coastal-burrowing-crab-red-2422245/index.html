About This Model

A burrowing crab spends most of its life below the sand and only appears when the tide is right. The asymmetry is the giveaway: one claw much larger than the other, used for display and defence rather than for feeding.

The model is very light at 7,986 polygons and 8,075 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so several can populate a shoreline at once. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $99 it suits coastal and marine visualization, nature documentary and education work, game environments, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
