About This Model

A wapiti bull lowering its head to graze, with a fur coat and a Blender rig ready for calm wildlife scenes. Feeding is how these animals spend most of the day, so the loop fits naturally into meadow and forest shots.

Heavy antlers sweep back over the shoulders, and the head dips smoothly to the grass between steps. Dark fur on the neck contrasts with a lighter body and pale rump, the coloring of this large deer. Jaw movement while chewing is visible from the side, which rewards close camera framing of the head. Blender opens the groom and the animation together, so nothing breaks on the way to a render.

Released in 2026, the grazing clip suits national park films, hunting simulators and calm VR nature walks. Place a small herd with offset timing on a hillside, and the meadow comes alive without crowding. Morning frost on the grass and low sun behind the antlers make an atmospheric autumn frame.
