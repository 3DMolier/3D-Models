About This Model

Goat on Pasture - Fur is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. The mesh carries 18,450 polygons and 9,560 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
