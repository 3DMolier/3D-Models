About This Model

The Aston Martin V12 Vantage 2014 Simple Interior pairs a detailed exterior with a pared-back cabin for traffic and chase scenes. The cabin is kept simple, so seats and dashboard read through the glass without loading the scene with hidden detail. Most of the effort shows on the bodywork, from the vented hood to the wide rear haunches.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms clean geometry, consistent naming and correct scale before the car reached the catalog. Paint, glass, chrome and tire rubber are separated, which keeps material swaps quick when a project needs another color.

In the catalog since 2015, the coupe carries an editorial license, so it fits news, documentaries and editorial illustration. Racing game prototypes and film backgrounds also use it as a recognizable British sports car parked along a street. A low camera near the front wheel shows off the aggressive stance best. Driving simulation teams sometimes place it in a garage scene to judge lighting on curved bodywork.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
