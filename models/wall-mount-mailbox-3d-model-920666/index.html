About This Model

A wall mailbox is a small metal box with a hinged flap, and its whole character sits in the details: the lip that keeps rain out, the slot proportions, the mounting holes and the small lock. Those features are what date it and locate it, because mailbox design differs noticeably from country to country.

The mesh is 2,882 polygons and 3,303 vertices, built entirely from quads with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 7 texture maps - 2 at 4096x4096, 4 at 2048x2048 and one more at 2048x2048. That texture budget carries the paint, the weathering and the metal grain.

At $4 this is among the least expensive assets in the catalog, and it does the job a cheap prop should: architectural exteriors, residential visualization, street scenes, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2015.
