About This Model

Priced at $19, the Illuminated Tabletop Magnifying Glass Lamp is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 12,338 polygons and 12,581 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
