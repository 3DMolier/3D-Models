About This Model

Full Da Vinci Surgical System is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. The mesh carries 354,182 polygons and 366,909 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

42 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
