About This Model

A man warming his hands over a burning barrel in an abandoned lot, surrounded by tires and scattered debris. The scene measures 203.0 x 157.36 x H183.55 cm, a tight vignette that drops into any alley or wasteland. Its 81,595 polygons cover the rusted drum, the layered clothing of the man and the junk around his feet.

UVs are non-overlapping across 97,525 vertices, so soot, rust and grime can build up without visible repeats. Made to CheckMate specification, the figure stands at true height beside the barrel, which reads at waist level. Firelight is the whole mood here, and an orange point light inside the drum makes the scene glow.

This vignette dates from 2025 and costs $189, royalty free for any production. Urban decay games, documentaries on homelessness, crime dramas and music videos use this kind of image often. Shot at night with sparks rising, the barrel becomes the only warm spot in a cold, empty city.
