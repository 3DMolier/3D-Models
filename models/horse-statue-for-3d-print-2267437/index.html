About This Model

A horse statue prepared for 3D printing, with solid volumes and a stable base that a printer can build without collapse. The pose shows the animal's muscular neck, flowing mane and balanced legs, which makes a strong desk or garden piece. Printable models need closed, watertight shapes, and this statue is built around that requirement rather than as a render-only mesh.

Prepared to CheckMate specification, it keeps clean naming and real proportions, so scaling it for any print size is straightforward. A single material lets you preview bronze, marble or plain resin finishes before sending the file to print. Thin details are kept chunky enough to survive printing, which avoids broken ears or snapped tails.

Out since 2024, the statue serves home decor printing, equestrian gifts, interior renders and garden ornament design. Makers use it to print trophies and keepsakes for riding clubs and horse lovers. Rendered in bronze on a plinth, it also works as a dignified piece for an estate entrance.
