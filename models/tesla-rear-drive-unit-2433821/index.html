About This Model

An electric drive unit combines motor, inverter and reduction gearbox in one casing, which is why it looks so unlike an engine and transmission: a compact aluminium package with the driveshafts emerging from either side and high voltage connectors on top. The absence of an exhaust, a sump or a bellhousing is the whole point.

The mesh is efficient at 22,137 polygons and 24,090 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the casing ribs, the connectors and the mounting points.

At $59 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits automotive engineering and technology marketing, electric vehicle presentation, technical cutaways, education and training material, and games. On sale since 2025.
