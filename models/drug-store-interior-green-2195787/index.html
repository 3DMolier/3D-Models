About This Model

Priced at $79, the Drug Store Interior Green is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 108,020 polygons over 113,017 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
