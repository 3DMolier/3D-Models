About This Model

An octopus and an arrow squid, Doryteuthis plei, rigged for Cinema 4D and packed together for underwater and marine biology scenes. The octopus has a soft rounded mantle and long tentacles lined with suckers, built to curl and flow. The squid is slender and torpedo-shaped, with fins at the tip of the mantle and short arms around the head.

Cinema 4D rigs let tentacles ripple, coil around objects or push the animals through the water column. Assembled per CheckMate specification, both creatures are tidy and scaled, so they share a reef or aquarium without adjustment. Translucent skin and subtle color patterns respond nicely to caustic light filtering through the surface.

Released in 2026, the pair fits nature documentaries, aquarium displays, educational animation and underwater game levels. Seafood restaurants and marine science centers also use cephalopods to illustrate ocean life in a vivid way. Having both species together lets one scene show the contrast between a hunter on the seabed and a fast swimmer in open water.
