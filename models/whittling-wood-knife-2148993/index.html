About This Model

Whittling Wood Knife is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $5. You are getting 1,810 polygons and 916 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
