About This Model

A detailed human heart with its chambers, arteries and veins is the core visual for cardiology lessons and health explainers. The Human Heart shows the aorta arching over the top and the coronary vessels running across the muscular surface. Its slightly tilted, cone-like shape matches how the organ actually sits inside the chest.

CheckMate Pro status stands behind its clean topology and a tidy file that imports into common packages without repair. Surfaces show the fatty grooves and the red muscle tone that viewers expect from an anatomical heart.

Since 2016 it has sold to 10 buyers at $49, among them cardiologists, nursing schools and medical animators. Animate it pumping, cut it open to show valves or tint the arteries to explain a blockage. Heart health campaigns and pharmaceutical explainers rely on exactly this kind of clear, believable organ. Placed in a transparent torso, it also helps patients understand where bypass surgery or a stent will happen.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026

Can the Human Heart model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

Can the Human Heart model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.
