About This Model

A row of crushed and torn cardboard boxes for warehouse corners, alley clutter and delivery mishap scenes. Its 22,322 polygons go into dented panels, folded flaps and creased edges where the cardboard gave way. Laid out, the set covers 273.29 x 70.99 x H61.0 cm, about the length of a short loading dock pile.

The CheckMate build keeps 22,314 vertices in tidy shells, so each box can be moved or rotated on its own. UV layout varies from box to box, and corrugated paper shaders or tape decals can be projected across the flat faces. Flaps hang open on some boxes, showing the empty interiors to a curious camera.

On sale since 2026 at $99, the set suits logistics training, post-apocalyptic environments and shipping damage explainers. Scatter the boxes around a pallet jack and a storeroom looks used rather than freshly stocked. The varied damage means the boxes never look like copies of each other when grouped.
