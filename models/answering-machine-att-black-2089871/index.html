About This Model

A desktop answering machine with a row of playback buttons, this device recalls the home phone era when messages blinked on a counter. The 9,277 polygons model the sloped control panel, the speaker grille and the small display that shows the message count. Its 9,549 vertices keep button edges crisp, so the labels can be read in a close shot.

The unwrap gives button icons, a maker plate and scuffed plastic their own space on each surface, with no shared islands. CheckMate Pro review confirms realistic scale, sensible object names and geometry with no hidden faces. Glossy plastic housings like this one reward a soft reflection map rather than harsh direct light.

Two color versions are offered, the Answering Machine ATT in black and the Answering Machine ATT White White. On sale since 2023, it fits retro office desks, nineties apartment interiors, crime drama sets and period films that need a home phone. A red blinking light added to the display gives the scene a hint of an unheard message.
