About This Model

Every euro coin shares a common reverse and carries its own national obverse, and the Spanish one euro shows the king in profile with the twelve stars around the rim. Modeling a coin well is almost entirely about relief depth: too shallow and it looks printed, too deep and it looks stamped from clay. This one carries the raised rim, the milled edge and the relief portrait as real geometry.

The mesh is 11,082 polygons and 10,916 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for the lettering and the star points to catch a rim light, which is what makes metal read as metal in a close-up.

At $29 it works in financial and banking visualization, advertising, product and packaging renders, games and print. On sale since 2024, kept to real-world scale so it sits correctly beside a wallet, a hand or another coin.
