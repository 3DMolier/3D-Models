About This Model

Glasses with Non-Alcoholic Drinks Collection fills a table with poured soft drinks, juices and mocktails, liquid already inside each glass. Filled glassware saves the slow work of modeling a liquid surface that meets the inner wall without gaps or overlaps. Different glass shapes in one set help a bar counter or a party table look stocked rather than copied.

The bundle passed TurboSquid's CheckMate Lite inspection, which confirms the files open cleanly and the geometry is sound. Glass and liquid only read well under refractive shaders, so the separation between container and drink matters at render time. Each glass stays its own object, so it can be lifted out of the arrangement and placed alone on a tray.

The set went on sale in 2022 as part of our food and beverage props. It suits restaurant and cafe visualization, beverage menus, family scenes in film and casual game interiors. Because nothing here is alcoholic, it also fits children's settings and campaigns where wine or beer would be out of place.
