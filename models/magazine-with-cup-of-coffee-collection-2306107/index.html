About This Model

A collection of magazines paired with cups of coffee, the quiet coffee table vignette for living rooms, cafes and waiting areas. Each pairing places an open or folded magazine beside a full cup, suggesting someone has just paused their reading. Glossy pages and a ceramic cup give two contrasting materials, which makes even a small tabletop detail read well.

The objects keep consistent real-world scale, so a magazine never looks oversized next to the saucer. Built to CheckMate specification, it opens with tidy names for every cup, saucer and magazine. A low morning sun across the table catches steam, page edges and the rim of the cup together. Swap the cover image for your own layout, and the magazine becomes a quick editorial mockup inside an interior.

Released in 2024, the collection suits apartment renders, cafe interiors, hotel lobbies and slow lifestyle animations. Several variations in one purchase let you dress a whole row of side tables without visible repetition.
