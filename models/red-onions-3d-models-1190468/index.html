About This Model

Red Onions 3D Models offers whole and cut red onions with glossy purple skins and layered white-and-violet rings inside. The cut pieces show the onion's concentric structure, which is what makes the vegetable instantly readable on a kitchen board.

These onions earned a CheckMate Pro badge, meaning the meshes are clean, consistently scaled and properly named. Purple outer layers fade to white toward the core, a gradient that sells freshness. Papery outer skin, a dried root tuft and a narrow neck give the whole bulbs their natural look. Halves and slices can be scattered next to the whole onions to suggest chopping in progress.

Around since 2017, the set appears in recipe illustrations, grocery shelf renders, cooking show sets and restaurant menu visuals. Pile them in a wicker basket next to garlic and tomatoes for a farmers market stall. A macro shot on a wet cutting board, with a knife resting nearby, catches the shine of the sliced rings.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2017 · Updated 11 November 2026
