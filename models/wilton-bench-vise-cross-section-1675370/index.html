About This Model

Priced at $49, the Wilton Bench Vise Cross Section is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 36,584 polygons over 38,564 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags technical visualization, architectural visualization and product rendering as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
