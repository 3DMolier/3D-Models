About This Model

The faux trophy is a joke about a tradition, and it works because the silhouette is unmistakable while the material never is. It hangs in bars and flats belonging to people who would not have the real thing on any account.

The model is extremely light at 3,820 polygons and 3,789 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 12 maps all at 2048x2048. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $39 it suits interior and decor visualisation, product and e-commerce imagery, bar and hospitality scenes, film set dressing, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.
