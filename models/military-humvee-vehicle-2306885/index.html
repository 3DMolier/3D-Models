About This Model

Military Humvee belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 330,616 polygons and 337,368 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and defense simulation.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
