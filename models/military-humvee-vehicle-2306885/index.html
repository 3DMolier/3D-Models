About This Model

The Military Humvee Vehicle is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags military simulation, game environments and film production as the intended applications. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defence training material and military VFX shots.

At 330,616 polygons and 337,368 vertices this is a mid-weight asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
