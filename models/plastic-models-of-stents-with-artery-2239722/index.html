About This Model

Stents with Artery is one of the Medical models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. The mesh carries 20,640 polygons and 20,328 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
