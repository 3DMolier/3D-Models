About This Model

A mixed plate is a menu photograph rather than a dish. Different shapes, colors and thicknesses next to each other say variety before anybody reads a word, which is why the format survives on every grill menu ever printed.

The model is extremely light at 5,978 polygons and 5,166 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $34 it suits food and menu visualization, restaurant and packaging imagery, advertising, kitchen and table scenes, game props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
