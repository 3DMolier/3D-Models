About This Model

Ancient Left Side Memnon Sculpture is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. You are getting 34,044 polygons and 33,684 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 5.19 x 9.26 x H14.23 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
