About This Model

Britax Romer Child Safety Seat is a padded child car seat with deep side wings, a molded shell and a harness over the occupant. Its 27,854 polygons and 29,992 vertices go mostly into the curved headrest, stitched seams and buckle, leaving room for a full car interior.

Fabric weave, plastic grain and webbing straps come from 13 texture maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The UVs never overlap, so the cover can be recolored or worn without unwrapping again, and CheckMate Pro covers topology and scene setup.

Five versions sit together: the standalone seat, a blue variant, a generic unbranded seat and two scenes with the seat strapped onto car seats. The mounted ones save time when the shot is about installation rather than the product alone. Since 2018 the model has sold 41 times, mostly to people building family car interiors, safety guides and automotive training material.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2018 · Updated 14 November 2026
