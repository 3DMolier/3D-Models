About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The American Football Fan Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $169. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 73,926 polygons over 76,140 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
