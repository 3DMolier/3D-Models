About This Model

Wall Recessed Power Distribution Board is a detailed Architecture Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
