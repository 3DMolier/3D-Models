About This Model

A medical linear accelerator with a rotating gantry and patient couch, the machine used to deliver radiation therapy for cancer. The Oncology Radiotherapy System uses 38,378 polygons and 39,096 vertices, a light build that still shows the gantry head and imaging arms. The couch extends into the gantry opening, the position patients hold during each session.

It measures 240.26 x 331.67 x H250.47 cm, filling a treatment bunker much like the real equipment. StemCell delivery means the clinical white and grey materials look the same in every format. Smooth curved covers and a large ring-shaped gantry give the scene its unmistakable hospital technology feel.

This system came to the store in 2026 and costs $59 for one version. Cancer center visualizations, patient education videos, medical equipment training and hospital dramas use it in treatment room scenes. Add a patient figure on the couch and soft blue lighting for a calm, reassuring treatment shot.
