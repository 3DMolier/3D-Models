About This Model

Shot Glass of Tequila is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $9. Geometry weighs in at 684 polygons over 652 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 resolution. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
