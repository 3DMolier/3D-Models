About This Model

Lockable Plastic Restraint Cuff with Key belongs to our Tools range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 4,119 polygons and 4,118 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
