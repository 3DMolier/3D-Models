About This Model

The Apple iPhone 11 Pro Space Grey is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. You are getting 15,136 polygons and 15,855 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 7 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
