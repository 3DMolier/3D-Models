About This Model

Glossy green squashes with ribbed skins, stems and pale flesh make this collection perfect for farmers markets and kitchen counters. The Zucchini Vegetable Collection includes whole vegetables alongside sliced pieces, giving cooking scenes both fresh and prepared versions.

Cut faces show the seedy center and creamy flesh, so close-ups on a chopping board look appetizing. The set earned CheckMate Pro status, confirming each zucchini shares consistent scale and well-formed geometry. Slices are separate objects that can be fanned across a plate or tossed into a pan.

Introduced in 2017, the collection serves recipe videos, food packaging mockups and farm-to-table restaurant scenes. Food stylists pair zucchini with ripe tomatoes, red peppers and basil to build colorful vegetable spreads for summer menus. Stacked in crates at a market stall, the squashes add a fresh, healthy note to summer imagery. Its royalty-free license covers paid client jobs, cookbook illustrations and personal projects alike.
