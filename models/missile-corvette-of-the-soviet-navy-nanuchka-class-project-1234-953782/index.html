About This Model

A Soviet missile corvette of the Nanuchka class, a compact fast warship with a tall radar mast and launchers on its bow. The Missile Corvette of the Soviet Navy Nanuchka class Project 1234 captures the stubby hull, the boxy superstructure and the domed radar housing. Built in large numbers, these ships served many navies, so the design suits several historical settings.

Its CheckMate Pro grade means guns, radars and launchers are named parts that can be selected individually. The gray hull sits low in the water, and the high bow flare suggests a ship built for speed in rough seas. Antennas and railings are modeled as fine detail, rewarding close camera passes along the deck.

Naval history channels, Cold War strategy games and military museums account for the 11 sales since 2015. At $249 the corvette is a rare, accurate subject for anyone recreating Soviet coastal fleets or Baltic patrols. Seen from a low angle against waves, its launchers and mast create a menacing outline.
