About This Model

A compact fire extinguisher prepared for 3D printing, with the cylinder, valve head and hose combined into a printable solid. Its 2,894 polygons and 2,902 vertices keep the file small and the slicing fast, while the curved body stays round. Printed small it becomes a model railway or diorama detail, and printed large it works as a cosplay or stage prop.

Made to CheckMate specifications, the mesh has non-overlapping UVs, so it can also be painted and rendered as a regular asset. A coat of red primer and a printed label turn the plastic print into a convincing replica within an afternoon. The handle and nozzle are the most fragile areas, so a little extra infill there helps.

This model went live in 2025 with a royalty-free license and belongs to our printable props. Makers, miniature painters, escape room builders and set designers use it wherever a safety detail must look right.
