About This Model

A white wooden pulpit with paneled sides and a slanted reading desk, this stand gives a church interior its central speaking place. Its 18,071 polygons describe the molded trim, the base molding and the shelf where a Bible rests. There are 9,149 vertices, which tells you the paneling is triangulated for clean export to any renderer.

TurboSquid reviewed the pulpit's quad layout, dimensions and naming before giving it CheckMate Pro status. The panels unwrap without overlap, so wood grain under the paint and gilded trim can be added with care. Place it beside an altar under stained glass light, and a Sunday sermon scene sets itself.

Its counterpart version is a church pulpit podium in dark oak, and the two share one $19 price. Released in 2024, the pulpit suits chapel interiors, wedding ceremonies and period films with village church scenes. White paint keeps it bright in dim naves, so the speaker stays the brightest point in frame.

Questions About This Model

How heavy is the Church Furniture Pulpit White mesh?

18,071 polygons and 9,149 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Our Furniture & Interior listings sit at a median of about 34,000 polygons, so 18,071 is lighter than the typical one.

What is the Church Furniture Pulpit White model typically used for?

It is cataloged for interior visualization, product rendering and event visualization - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

Can the Church Furniture Pulpit White model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

Can the Church Furniture Pulpit White model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.
