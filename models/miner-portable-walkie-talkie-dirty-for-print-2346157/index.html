About This Model

This dirty miner's walkie talkie is a rugged handheld radio covered in grime, prepared as a printable prop. A stubby antenna rises from the top, and the chunky body is built to survive dust, knocks and wet tunnels. Large buttons and a belt clip suit gloved hands, which is why mining radios look heavier than city models.

Mesh figures are not given, but the radio is shaped as solid geometry meant to slice cleanly for a printer. It was made to the TurboSquid CheckMate specification, so scale and structure behave predictably when you import it. Grime is part of the sculpted design, so a print painted in yellow and black already looks worn.

A 2025 printable at $9, it suits mining dioramas, cosplay props and tabletop game terrain for underground maps. Makers print it for a miner costume, painting the dirt darker around the seams and the buttons. Diorama builders also place a small print of it in a tunnel scene beside a lamp and pickaxe.
