About This Model

A plain aluminum frying pan with a flat base, flared sides and a long handle, the everyday workhorse of any stove. Aluminum Frying Pan 3D Model keeps the shape honest, with a slightly rolled rim and a handle that stays straight and practical. Brushed metal on the inside catches circular highlights, which is what makes cookware read as real in a render.

The bare metal surface suits close shots of sizzling food, eggs or pancakes in the pan. TurboSquid staff certified the pan at CheckMate Pro, confirming clean topology, sensible naming and real-world scale.

Since 2015 it has gone to 19 buyers stocking kitchen interiors, cooking shows and restaurant scenes. Hung from a rail above a stove or left on a burner, it makes a kitchen feel used. Lined up with other pans on a shelf, it fills a professional kitchen quickly and convincingly. At $19 it is an easy addition to any collection of kitchen utensils and pots.
