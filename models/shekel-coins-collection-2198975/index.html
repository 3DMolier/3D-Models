About This Model

A set of Israeli shekel coins, ready for finance graphics, travel content, coin collecting pages and close-up studies of everyday money. The Shekel Coins Collection gathers several shekel coins so a pile of change can be composed without hunting for references.

Each coin carries its relief design and rim, which catch light when the camera skims across a scattered pile. Metallic shading reads best with a strong key light, letting the different metal tones separate clearly. CheckMate Pro certification keeps scale accurate, so the coins sit correctly in a hand or wallet.

Released in 2024, the bundle costs $49 and spares you from modeling every coin separately. Economics explainers, banking apps and news pieces about currency often need exactly this kind of close shot. Stack a few coins beside banknotes and a receipt, and a household budget scene comes together quickly. Game designers also use real-world currency for shop counters and market stalls set in Israel.
