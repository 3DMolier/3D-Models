About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Abstract Human Figures Black is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

The mesh carries 5,050 polygons and 5,052 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published August 2024·Updated 20 August 2026

Event Management, Advertising and Film & Video Production are the primary industries on this listing, though the Royalty Free license puts no limit on where the model is actually used.

Can the Abstract Human Figures Black model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Can the Abstract Human Figures Black model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

What is the Abstract Human Figures Black model typically used for?
