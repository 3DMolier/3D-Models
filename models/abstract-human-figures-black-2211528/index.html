About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Abstract Human Figures Black is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

Which industries use the Abstract Human Figures Black model?

Film & Video Production, Advertising and Graphics Multimedia and Web Design are the primary industries on this listing, though the Royalty Free licence puts no limit on where the model is actually used.

Can the Abstract Human Figures Black model be used in a commercial project?

Yes — the Royalty Free licence covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Yes, modification is covered by the licence. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

Stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.
