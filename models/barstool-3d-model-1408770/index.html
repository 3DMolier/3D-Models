About This Model

A tall bar stool with a round padded seat, splayed legs and a footrest ring, made for kitchen islands, pubs and cafe counters. The mesh uses 6,320 polygons and 6,542 vertices, enough for soft cushion edges and round tubular supports. A light count matters here, because stools rarely appear alone and usually stand in rows along a counter. Its slim profile also keeps sightlines open when the camera looks across a busy bar toward the bottles.

The StemCell standard gives it consistent PBR materials that look identical in Unreal, Unity or any offline renderer. Non-overlapping UVs make it easy to swap the seat to leather, fabric or plain wood. Legs and seat can be shaded separately, so a chrome frame with a dark cushion takes only minutes.

Listed since 2019, the stool fits bar interiors, open-plan kitchens and restaurant visualization projects. Architects and game environment artists like it because a single chair model fills an entire counter convincingly.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2019 · Updated 14 November 2026

What does StemCell mean for the Barstool model?

It means one source asset, several delivered formats, and a PBR material setup designed to read consistently across them - no rebuilding shaders after import.

What is the Barstool model typically used for?

The listing names interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the main applications. In practice it also works anywhere a finished furniture & interior object is needed without modeling it from scratch.

Can the Barstool model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
