About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The iPhone 6 Plus Leather Case is a finished, render-ready model at $9. Geometry weighs in at 3,968 polygons over 3,963 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 4 maps at 4096 x 4906 and 2048 x 2048. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
