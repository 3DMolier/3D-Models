About This Model

Priced at $129, the Russian Astronaut Wearing Space Suit Sokol KV2 is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

At 31,640 polygons and 32,491 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
