About This Model

A kitchen set for baking a pie, gathering the ingredients and tools that sit on a counter before the oven warms. The pieces cover the raw components and utensils of the bake, modeled as separate objects for flexible arrangement. Separate objects mean you can stage the scene before mixing, halfway through, or with the crust ready in its dish.

Each item follows CheckMate guidelines, so pivots sit on the base and items rest flat on a counter. Food surfaces keep their natural irregularity, which avoids the plastic look common in quick grocery models. Matched scale across the set means a spoon never dwarfs a bowl when you compose a close-up.

Part of the catalog since 2024, the collection suits cooking show sets, recipe blog illustrations and kitchen game levels. Arranged on a wooden table under warm window light, it tells a homey baking story without any text. Animators can also use the pieces for a step-by-step recipe sequence, swapping objects as the pie comes together.
