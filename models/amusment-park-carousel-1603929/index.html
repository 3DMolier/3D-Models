About This Model

Park Carousel is one of the Architecture & Landmarks models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $169. Geometry weighs in at 513,512 polygons over 505,276 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
