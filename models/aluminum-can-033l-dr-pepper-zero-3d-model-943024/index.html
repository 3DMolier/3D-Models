About This Model

This compact Dr Pepper Zero can matches the short European size that fits neatly into fridge doors and vending machines. Its dark body carries the familiar sugar-free design, with a ribbed neck and a pull tab sitting on the lid. Proportions are shorter and stubbier than a standard American can, which matters when several sit together on a shelf.

No polygon figure accompanies this asset, but a single can is a light object even in a crowded fridge. The CheckMate Pro grade shows TurboSquid inspected the can for correct dimensions, clean surfaces and orderly naming. Label art and metal finish are part of the material setup, and condensation or frost can be layered on top.

Dating back to 2015 and priced at $9, the can suits kitchen renders, convenience store shelves and snack scenes in films. Visualizers place it beside a burger and fries to complete a diner table or a late night desk scene. Game artists also stack it in vending machines and fridges for convenience store levels set in modern cities.
