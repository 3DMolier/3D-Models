About This Model

The Stealth Bomber B-2 Spirit is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $169 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 1,589,064 polygons and 1,670,356 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 25 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
