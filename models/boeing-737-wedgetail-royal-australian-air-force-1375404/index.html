About This Model

The Wedgetail is an airliner with a radar bolted to its spine, and the shape of that antenna is why it looks wrong at first glance. It is fixed rather than rotating, which is unusual for airborne early warning, and Australia was the first air force to make the type work operationally.

The airframe holds 623,033 polygons and 643,931 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 26 maps all at 4096x4096. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $149 it suits defence and aviation visualization, flight simulation, documentary and news graphics, film and game environments. On sale since 2019.
