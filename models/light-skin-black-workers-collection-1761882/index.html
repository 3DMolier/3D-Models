About This Model

The Light Skin Black Workers Collection gathers several Black worker figures with a lighter skin tone into one purchase. Each figure is dressed for a job rather than for a catalog shoot, which makes them useful on a site or shop floor. Buying the group together keeps faces, proportions and clothing detail consistent across every person you place in the frame.

The set carries CheckMate Pro certification, which means real-world scale, clean topology and named materials checked by a reviewer. A worker placed beside a door or a forklift reads at the right height without any rescaling on import. Diverse workforce scenes are hard to staff from stock libraries, and this group fills that gap with believable people.

The collection has been available since 2021 and is usually placed in factories, warehouses, construction sites and training material. Architectural visualization studios use figures like these to show a building in operation instead of an empty shell. Game and film crews can scatter them as background staff, where clothing and posture matter more than facial close-ups.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
