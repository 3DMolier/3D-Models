About This Model

Skin Woman Explorer is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. Geometry weighs in at 18,351 polygons over 19,871 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
