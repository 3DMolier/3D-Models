About This Model

A heavy flatbed truck carrying precast concrete bomb shelters, built for civil defense, emergency response and military logistics scenes. The 578,114 polygons cover the truck body and the thick shelter blocks with their entry openings. The concrete modules sit squarely on the deck, giving the load a believable sense of crushing weight.

Under the StemCell standard, concrete and truck paint shade identically across each provided format. UV mapping is partial, and the 619,006 vertices keep shelters and truck separate for unloading animations. Lift the shelters off with a crane to show them placed along a city street.

Priced at $179 and released in 2026, this load suits civil protection explainers, news graphics and wartime city scenes. The rig is long and tall, so give it a wide road and a clear turning area. Park it at a bus stop to show shelters arriving where civilians need them most.
