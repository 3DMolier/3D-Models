About This Model

An opened disk drive showing the platter, read arm, spindle and controller board, ready for technical explainers and data recovery scenes. It uses 126,898 polygons and 128,132 vertices to model the mirrored platter, actuator arm, magnets and screws. Seeing the head over the platter makes it clear how data is physically read and written.

StemCell certification means the polished platter and metal casing render consistently across engines. A mirror-like disk surface reflects the arm above it, which creates striking close-ups with very little lighting work. The open cover allows animations of the arm sweeping across the platter as data is accessed during a read.

Two versions cost $69 each: the open drive described here and the HP Server Hard Disk Drive. Both entered the range in 2026 and are licensed Royalty Free for commercial work. Computer science education, data center visualization, recovery service videos and tech documentaries are natural uses for them.
