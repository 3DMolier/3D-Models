About This Model

The trophy is a Victorian claret jug rather than a purpose-designed sports cup, which is why it looks like something from a dining table: a bulbous silver body, a long neck, a scroll handle and a hinged lid with a finial. The engraved names run around the base, and the whole thing is smaller in the hand than photographs suggest.

The mesh is light at 24,104 polygons and 12,298 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the handle, the lid and the base moulding where a polished silver surface needs edges to catch.

At $49 it suits golf and sports broadcast graphics, tournament and ceremony visualization, sports advertising, memorabilia and print, and games. On sale since 2023.
