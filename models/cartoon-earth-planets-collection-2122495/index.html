About This Model

A stylised planet does a job a photographic one cannot: it can be smiled at, labelled, thrown into a diagram or shrunk to an icon and still read as a world. That is why children's media and infographics both keep reinventing the same simplified globe.

This is a collection listing rather than a single object, supplying the planets as static pieces. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $49 the set suits education and children's media, animation and motion graphics, infographic and editorial illustration, advertising and game assets. On sale since 2024.
