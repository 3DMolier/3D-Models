About This Model

A striped skunk with a black coat, white stripes down the back and a bushy raised tail, ready for wildlife scenes. Its compact body, short legs and pointed snout capture the waddling shape people recognize from forest edges and suburbs. The fluffy tail is the signature feature, and its outline reads clearly even when the animal is small in frame.

Released in 2025, the skunk suits nature documentaries, cartoon films and educational material about North American wildlife. Let it wander past a campsite, nose through garbage at night, or sit in a meadow at dusk. Children's games also gain a playful character when a skunk appears and every other animal runs away.

Two versions are available: the main Skunk Animal at $129 and an animated version rigged for Blender at $169. The rigged option suits walking cycles and that famous defensive pose with the tail lifted high. The model meets CheckMate specification, keeping clean naming and a believable scale beside other woodland animals.
