About This Model

Factory Equipment Collection gathers industrial machines, tool stations and fittings for dressing a workshop or production floor. Each piece is a standalone object, so a scene can mix them along a production line or scatter them across a hall.

The set meets CheckMate Lite requirements, keeping names readable and scale consistent from one machine to the next. Painted metal housings and safety yellow details give the equipment the worn utility look of a real plant. Repeating machines in a row works well, because each object stands on its own base. Control panels and guard rails supply the small detail that makes a plant look staffed and active.

Two versions form this family: the original collection and Factory Equipment 3D Models Collection 2, which adds more machines. Since 2018 it has served manufacturing explainers, safety training, factory planning renders and industrial game levels. Arranged under high bay lights, the machines turn an empty warehouse model into a believable plant.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2018 · Updated 11 November 2026
