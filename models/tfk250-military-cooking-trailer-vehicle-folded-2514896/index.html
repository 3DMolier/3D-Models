About This Model

The TFK250 Military Cooking Trailer Vehicle Folded is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $89 on TurboSquid. You are getting 190,637 polygons and 191,064 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 457.28 x 230.69 x H265.35 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
