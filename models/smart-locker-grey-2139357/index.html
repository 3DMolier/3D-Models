About This Model

The Smart Locker Grey is a production-ready Containers & Storage 3D model, priced at $34 on TurboSquid. You are getting 37,018 polygons and 20,562 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX.
