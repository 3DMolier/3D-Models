About This Model

A collection of Ford police cruisers, the patrol cars seen across American streets in films, news and games. Light bars, push bumpers and black and white liveries make each car instantly recognizable as law enforcement. Having several cruisers in one set lets a scene stage a roadblock or pursuit without repeating one car.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms matched scale across the fleet, so the cars line up side by side. Night scenes reward red and blue emissive lights, which bounce off wet asphalt and nearby walls. The license is Editorial Uses Only, since Ford owns the vehicle designs, so plan news, film and education work.

Part of the catalog since 2020, the fleet suits crime dramas, news graphics, police training material and driving games. Arrange the cars across an intersection with doors open, and the tension of an incident is clear immediately. Documentary producers covering policing can use the set for reconstructions without filming real patrol units.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
