About This Model

Priced at $69, the McKinley Spider is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 9,446 polygons and 9,576 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
