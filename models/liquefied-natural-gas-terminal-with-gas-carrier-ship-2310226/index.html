This Industrial Equipment model - Liquefied Natural Gas Terminal with Gas Carrier Ship - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $199 on TurboSquid. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
