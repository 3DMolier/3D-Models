About This Model

A plain cardboard tube with spiral-wound walls, the kind left inside paper towels and kitchen foil rolls. It is a simple 4,628 polygons and 4,632 vertices, built entirely from quads for clean subdivision. The open ends show the wall thickness, and that edge is what sells cardboard in a close shot.

The 2 maps at 4096 x 4096 carry the brown fiber and the faint spiral seam around the tube. Clean, separate UVs allow print, tape or a handwritten label to be added without distortion. CheckMate Pro inspectors checked its proportions, so it sits right beside kitchen rolls and craft supplies.

Two versions are offered: this tube at $19 and a Cardboard Postal Tube with end caps at $29. Since 2018 the tube has been chosen 24 times, often for recycling explainers and kids' craft scenes. Empty tubes also pile up nicely in a recycling bin or on a classroom table for a crafting session.
