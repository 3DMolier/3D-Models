About This Model

This Sports & Recreation model - Lawn Bowl Jack - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 1,536 polygons and 1,538 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 4 of them, peaking at 2048x2048, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
