About This Model

Priced at $149, the Male African American Surgeon is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 17,430 polygons and 17,624 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 18 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2015 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
