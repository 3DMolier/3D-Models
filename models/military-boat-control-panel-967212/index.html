About This Model

Military Boat Control Panel is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. You are getting 102,762 polygons and 105,029 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and defense simulation. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 4 September 2026
