About This Model

Royal King Costume belongs to our Clothing & Accessories range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 494,763 polygons and 504,587 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 47 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048.
