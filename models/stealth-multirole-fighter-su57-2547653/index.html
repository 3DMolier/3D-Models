About This Model

Priced at $189, the Stealth Multirole Fighter Su57 is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 796,880 polygons over 820,586 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation as the intended applications. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
