About This Model

A collection of IKEA furnishings for a children's room, giving kids' bedroom scenes recognizable flat-pack furniture. Every piece stands alone in the scene, letting a designer rearrange the room or reuse items in a nursery or playroom. Low beds and toy boxes sit at child height, which keeps the scale believable from an adult viewpoint.

Rounded edges, soft colors and simple shapes match the Scandinavian look that parents recognize instantly. The set carries CheckMate Lite certification, confirming the files open properly with orderly objects. It is licensed as Editorial Uses Only, which suits news stories, reviews, blogs and educational material about home design.

This family spans three versions: the first set at $24, a second set at $29 and a third at $49. Combined, the three collections furnish a complete children's room without repeating items in every corner. On sale since 2023, they fit interior design journalism, room planning tutorials and family lifestyle features.
