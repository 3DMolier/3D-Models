About This Model

A quadruped robot is built to go where wheels cannot, and its legs show it: three joints each, actuators at every one, and a body slung between them carrying sensors and a payload bay. Unlike a wheeled platform it has no preferred direction of travel, which is why the sensor head is often symmetrical.

The mesh runs 485,239 polygons and 492,522 vertices in quads and tris. The UVs overlap, which is the right economy on a machine with four identical legs, and the StemCell build means it works in a real-time engine.

At $79 it suits defence and security robotics marketing, inspection and industrial automation presentation, technology journalism, research communication, and games. On sale since 2026.
