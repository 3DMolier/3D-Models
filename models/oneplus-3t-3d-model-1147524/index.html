About This Model

OnePlus 3T is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. You are getting 10,682 polygons and 11,287 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

11 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
