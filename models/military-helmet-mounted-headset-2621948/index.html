About This Model

A combat helmet fitted with an electronic hearing protection headset, the communication setup of modern special forces. The Military Helmet Mounted Headset uses 45,110 polygons and 45,902 vertices for the rail adapters, ear cups and boom microphone. Its matte finish takes coyote tan, olive or black paint for different units.

It measures 26.37 x 34.7 x H23.03 cm, matching an adult head with the cups clipped to the helmet rails. CheckMate certification ensures accurate scale, so the gear fits character heads without any manual adjustment. Side rails and arm mounts let the ear cups swing up when the operator needs open ears.

The helmet and headset assembly reached the store in 2026 at $44 as one piece. Tactical shooters, special operations films, military training videos and airsoft content use it to gear up elite soldiers. The boom microphone near the mouth signals radio traffic, a useful cue for team coordination scenes.
