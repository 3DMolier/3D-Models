About This Model

The Papers Control White is a production-ready Tools 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 28,181 polygons and 14,572 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.
