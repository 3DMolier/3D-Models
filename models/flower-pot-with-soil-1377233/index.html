About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Flower Pot With Soil is a finished, render-ready model at $19. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

At 832 polygons and 851 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 3 of them, peaking at 2048x2048, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
