About This Model

This Animals & Creatures model - Tomato Plant Pot with Flowers Fur - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architecture visualization, film production and game environments. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 118,782 polygons and 114,277 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. Texture work runs to 14 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
