About This Model

These FPV drone goggles with twin antennas give pilots an immersive first-person view, making them an essential prop for drone racing scenes. Built entirely from quads, the 78,238 polygons and 75,682 vertices form the curved visor body, the head strap, the foam face cushion and both antennas.

At 21.37 x 28.8 x H12.4 cm the headset matches a real wearable, so it fits a character's head without scaling. Mixed UVs and a geometry made to the CheckMate specification keep the glossy shell and soft strap easy to shade. A pilot wearing them while holding a controller is the defining image of modern drone flying.

Two $39 versions are offered: the Antigravity A1 FPV Drone Goggles with Dual Antenna and black UAV pilot vision goggles. Since 2026 they have been Royalty Free for tech reviews, drone racing animations and gadget unboxing videos. Hobby shops and maker spaces can display the headset next to quadcopters and spare propellers. Esports style drone leagues also make a lively setting for several pilots wearing matching goggles.
