About This Model

A set of fireworks rockets with cardboard tubes, pointed nose cones and long wooden guide sticks, ready for a celebration night. The thin stick keeps each rocket flying straight after launch, which is why it trails far below the tube. Bright wrapping on the tubes signals different colors and effects, the way packs appear in shop displays.

Materials are yours to define, so paper wraps, foil caps and fuse cords can follow any palette. It was built to the CheckMate specification, which checks real scale and clear naming of each part.

Available from 2024 at $29, the set fits New Year scenes, festival renders and celebration greeting cards. Stuck in a snowbank or a bottle at midnight, the rockets capture the seconds before the sky lights up. Safety lessons about fireworks can also use the set to show how a rocket is built and why it must never be held. A row of them in a launch rack also dresses a backyard party or a seaside promenade at night.
