About This Model

A hand-tied bunch of long-stemmed roses with layered petals, made for Valentine cards, weddings and romantic interiors. The blooms take most of the 127,768 polygons and 127,934 vertices, all built from quads for clean subdivision. Each flower keeps its curled outer petals when the camera pushes in on the heart of the bunch.

The 10 maps at 4096 x 4096 hold petal veining, soft color gradients and the matte surface of the leaves. With StemCell certification the materials move between major renderers without rebuilding the shader graph. UV islands never overlap, so the petals can be recolored for pink, white or yellow arrangements.

This bouquet first appeared in 2018 at $39 and suits florist and gift imagery. Lay it across a bed, hand it to a character at a doorway or stand it in a glass vase. Next to a handwritten card and a candle, it turns a dinner table into an anniversary scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2018 · Updated 17 November 2026
