About This Model

This Architecture & Landmarks model - Old Wooden Church - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $79 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 229,303 polygons and 254,463 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

42 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
