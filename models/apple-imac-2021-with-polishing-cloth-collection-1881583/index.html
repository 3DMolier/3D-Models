About This Model

The colorful Apple iMac 2021 all-in-one desktop with its slim display, paired with the small polishing cloth that became a curiosity. The thin aluminum body, the chin below the screen and the round stand are the shapes that make this computer recognizable. Keeping the cloth as a separate object lets it lie folded on the desk or drape over the screen corner.

Carrying CheckMate Pro status, the set passed TurboSquid's stricter review of mesh quality and scale. The screen face can take any desktop image as an emissive map, while pastel paint shaders cover the body. The pastel shades of this generation let you match the computer to a room palette easily.

On offer since 2022 at $33 under Editorial Uses Only terms, it fits tech reviews, news segments and documentary office scenes.
