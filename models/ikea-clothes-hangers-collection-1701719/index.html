About This Model

A collection of IKEA clothes hangers in several styles, from slim wooden shapes with trouser bars to plastic hooks for children's garments. The curved shoulders follow the slope of a jacket so fabric drapes naturally when garments are hung in a closet scene. Metal swivel hooks sit on top of each hanger, letting them turn to face either way along a rail.

The set passed TurboSquid CheckMate Pro inspection, which covers clean geometry, sensible naming and accurate real-world scale. Because the hangers carry a real brand, they ship under the Editorial Uses Only license for news, reviews and educational work.

Available since 2021, the collection suits wardrobe interiors, home organization tutorials and furniture review renders. Hung in a neat row inside an open wardrobe, the hangers make a bedroom feel furnished and ready to live in. Cloth simulation artists also use them as anchors for shirts and coats draped over the bar.
