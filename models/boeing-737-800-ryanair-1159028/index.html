About This Model

The 737-800 is the aircraft that built the European low cost model, and Ryanair operates more of them than anyone. The scheme is deliberately simple - white fuselage, blue belly, yellow harp on the tail - which leaves nowhere for a stretched stencil or a misplaced door outline to hide. The model carries the winglets, the flap track fairings and the engine nacelle flats.

The mesh is 738,707 polygons and 764,152 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 7 texture maps at 4096x4096.

A rigged build sits on this page at $199 for when the gear retracts or the control surfaces move. The static model at $149 covers airline marketing, airport visualisation, aviation journalism, film and television, and games. On sale since 2017.
