About This Model

A cheerful cartoon cat and dog designed as a pair, with big expressive eyes and soft rounded bodies for animation. Their simplified shapes and bright colors suit children's content, where readable silhouettes matter more than fur realism. The two animals share one design language, so they look like characters from the same show when placed together.

The pair was assembled following CheckMate guidelines, so scale and naming match between the two characters. Each animal is a separate object, letting you stage the cat on a sofa while the dog runs across the room. Their oversized heads and short legs leave plenty of room for exaggerated expressions and bouncy cartoon movement in shorts.

Priced at $75 the duo fits children's series, educational apps and story books about friendship between pets. Released in 2026, the characters also suit casual games and explainer videos made for pet owners and veterinary clinics. Royalty Free licensing allows commercial productions to use both animals on screen and in print without additional fees.
