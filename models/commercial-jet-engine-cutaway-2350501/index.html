About This Model

Commercial Jet Engine Cutaway is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $199. It comes in at 1,041,122 polygons, with 1,001,991 vertices behind them. Heavy or light is a question about the scene, not about the model, so check it against the platform you are shipping to.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
