About This Model

A generic farm tractor running dual rear wheels, the setup farmers use for extra traction and lower soil pressure in wet fields. The unbranded body keeps it neutral, with a boxy cab, a tall exhaust and the paired tires dominating the silhouette.

CheckMate Pro certification guarantees true scale, so implements and trailers attach at believable heights. Without manufacturer marks, it can wear any paint color or dealer logo in commercial farm visuals. Dual wheels spread the load, which also makes the tractor look powerful and planted in close shots.

Royalty free since 2020, it serves agricultural visualization, farm simulators and rural landscape renders. Drive it along muddy furrows at sunset with a plow behind, and the season of planting arrives on screen. Agricultural machinery dealers can show attachments in context with a tractor that represents no single brand. Its plain body is also a convenient base for game farms that need upgrades and color variants.
