About This Model

Looking for a Food & Beverages asset? The Pomegranate Cross Section 3 is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 69,271 polygons and 69,918 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
