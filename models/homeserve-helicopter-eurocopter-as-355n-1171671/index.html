About This Model

A light twin in corporate livery is a very specific object: the same airframe that does police, medical and utility work, but finished in gloss with a company scheme and no external equipment. That cleanliness is what makes it useful for marketing imagery, where sensors and winches would clutter the shape.

The mesh is heavy for its size at 1,036,212 polygons and 1,064,988 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 19 texture maps at 4096x4096. That density shows in the rotor head and the door frames.

A rigged build sits on this page at $169. The static model at $119 covers corporate aviation marketing, charter and utility operator presentation, film and television, and games. On sale since 2017.
