About This Model

Loitering munitions blur the line between a missile and an aircraft. They fly, they wait, they are steered by an operator watching a video feed, and then they do not come back, which makes them cheap in a way that has changed how small units fight.

The model is light at 36,454 polygons and 18,854 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and carries StemCell certification. A second build is offered separately.

At $59 it suits defence visualisation, news and documentary graphics, simulation, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
