About This Model

Hockey Player Crimson is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. Counted at the source, the model is 61,172 polygons and 61,909 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely.

Texturing runs to 23 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and game assets. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 61,172 polygons over 61,909 vertices - a light build for its class. Maps: 23 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
