About This Model

The Boeing 727-200F is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 274,643 polygons and 282,780 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 22 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
