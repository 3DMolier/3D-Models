About This Model

A kraft paper takeaway box with a juicy hamburger sitting inside, ready for fast food menus and delivery app visuals. Burger Box Brown with Hamburger packs 69,498 polygons and 70,393 vertices into the bun, patty, cheese and lettuce layers. The lid hinges open behind the meal, so the burger is easy to frame from the front.

Sesame seeds, melted cheese edges and crisp leaf edges give the burger appetizing detail in close shots. The non-overlapping unwrap lets the cardboard carry a restaurant logo or eco packaging print. CheckMate Pro certification confirms proper scale and well-formed geometry for both the box and food.

Since 2022 this meal has gone into food delivery promotions, packaging mock-ups and restaurant interior scenes. Set the box on a counter next to fries, or hand it to a courier character at a doorstep. Branding studios use it when presenting new takeaway packaging to a client who sells burgers.
