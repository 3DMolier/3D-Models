About This Model

A courier in a helmet and jacket riding a small motor scooter with an insulated delivery box on the rear rack. The rider and machine together use 124,748 polygons, with detail in the helmet visor, jacket folds, mirrors and front fork. A count of 132,392 vertices keeps the pose readable from the side, where a scooter in traffic is usually seen.

The unwrap avoids overlaps, so you can print a delivery company logo on the box or change the jacket color for your own brand. Rider and scooter are separate pieces, so either can be posed or reused in another urban street scene. CheckMate Pro review confirms the scale, and the wheels touch the ground plane without adjustment.

Two versions are available: this one at $239 and a Food Delivery Man Riding Scooter Fur variant at $249 with fiber-based detail. In use since 2021, the character rides through app explainers, restaurant promos, city traffic animations and logistics presentations. Stopped at a curb with the box lid open, the rider tells the story of a hot meal arriving on time.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2021 · Updated 17 November 2026
