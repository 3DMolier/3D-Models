About This Model

A collection of ancient marble busts, portraits of philosophers, emperors and gods carved in the classical Greek and Roman manner. Lined up on plinths, the busts turn a plain hallway into a museum gallery or a scholar's library. Individual heads can be scaled, rotated or swapped around a scene without disturbing their neighbors.

Under CheckMate Pro rules the busts share a common scale, so they sit evenly beside each other on a shelf. Curled beards, wreaths and worn noses add the kind of history that a smooth generic statue never shows. Soft marble surfaces catch side light well, which is how real museums show their sculpture.

Three versions are grouped here: the Ancient Busts Collection itself plus the second and third volumes with further portraits. Together they fill a whole sculpture hall, while one volume dresses a study or a garden niche. Available since 2023, the set goes into museum visualization, history documentaries and classical interiors in games.
