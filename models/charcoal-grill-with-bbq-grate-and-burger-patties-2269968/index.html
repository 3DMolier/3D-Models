About This Model

Charcoal Grill with BBQ Grate and Burger Patties is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $59. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for advertising, product visualization and film production. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualisation, restaurant interiors and game props.

The mesh carries 142,569 polygons and 144,617 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
