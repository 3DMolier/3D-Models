About This Model

Red Open Cello Case belongs to our Musical Instruments range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 42,216 polygons and 43,646 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. 10 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game assets. It suits music-video and film sets, game props, product rendering and educational material.
