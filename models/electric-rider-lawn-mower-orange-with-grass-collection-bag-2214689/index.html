About This Model

An orange electric riding mower with a grass collection bag at the rear, the machine that keeps a large lawn tidy. The seat sits over the deck, the steering wheel rises in front, and the bag catches clippings behind the driver. Electric drive means no exhaust or fuel cap, which gives the body a cleaner, smoother outline than a gas mower.

Its construction meets CheckMate specification rules, so scale and naming match those of other certified assets. As a set it groups the mower and its collection bag, so both pieces arrive together and fit each other. The bright orange paint stands out against green grass, which helps the machine read in wide shots.

First sold in 2024, the mower fits garden equipment catalogs, suburban yard scenes and landscaping company presentations. Its royalty free status allows outdoor power equipment advertising and games with backyard levels. Park it beside a garden shed with a striped lawn behind and the weekend chore is told in one frame.
