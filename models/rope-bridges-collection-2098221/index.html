About This Model

A set of suspended rope bridges with wooden planks, made for jungle crossings, canyon adventures and fantasy game levels. Each bridge in the Rope Bridges Collection hangs from rope rails, with planks spaced so the gaps show the drop below.

The sag of the deck and the knotted side lines carry most of the drama, so the bridges work well in silhouette against the sky. Rope textures and weathered wood are the main materials, which makes the bridges easy to age further. CheckMate Pro certification means clean naming and real-world scale across every piece in the set.

Released in 2023, the bundle costs $49 and saves building several crossings from scratch. Adventure game designers use such bridges for tense traversal moments, and filmmakers stage chases across them. Stretch one between two cliffs, add mist below, and a simple environment gains a clear path and a sense of height. Placing the bridges at different heights across a gorge turns a flat level into a vertical one.
