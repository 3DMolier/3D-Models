About This Model

Compass in Steel Case is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $34. The mesh carries 5,772 polygons and 3,014 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
