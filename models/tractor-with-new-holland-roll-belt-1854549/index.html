About This Model

Tractor with New Holland Roll Belt is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. You are getting 816,471 polygons and 426,893 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

40 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
