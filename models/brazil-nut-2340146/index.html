About This Model

Priced at $29, the Brazil Nut is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 571 polygons over 579 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags food photography and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.
