About This Model

An inflatable body board for snow, with side handles and a ribbed air chamber that riders lie on head first down a slope. The Sled 3D Model uses 18,094 polygons and 18,229 vertices, all quads, keeping the puffy tubes smooth and easy to subdivide. Laid flat on snow, its glossy bulges catch the bright winter light.

Four maps at 4096 x 4096 cover the blue vinyl, the printed graphics and the rubber grips. UVs do not overlap, so scuffs and frost can be painted wherever riders actually scrape the surface. Under the CheckMate Pro badge, the board comes at full size, ready for a rider character to lie on it.

Four versions share the page: this blue board, a red Big Air inflatable, a green inflatable and the Airboard Classic. On offer since 2017, the group fits ski resort scenes, winter sport games and holiday product shots on fresh powder.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2017 · Updated 14 November 2026
