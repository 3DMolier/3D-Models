About This Model

The Nike Sport Sneakers Fur is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. You are getting 60,598 polygons and 49,394 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
