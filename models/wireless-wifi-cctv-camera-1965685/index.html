About This Model

The Wireless WiFi CCTV Camera is a production-ready Tools 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 9,937 polygons and 5,140 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for technical visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
