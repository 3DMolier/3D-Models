About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Ocean Sunfish Common Mola is a finished, render-ready model at $49. You are getting 4,250 polygons and 4,211 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
