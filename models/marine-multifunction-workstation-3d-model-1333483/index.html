About This Model

Marine Multifunction Workstation is one of the Furniture & Interior models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. Counted at the source, the model is 107,234 polygons and 109,575 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 14 maps at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
