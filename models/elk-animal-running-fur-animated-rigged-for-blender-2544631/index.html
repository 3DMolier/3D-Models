About This Model

A full-grown elk in a running cycle with a groomed fur coat, prepared in Blender for wildlife films. Wide antlers, a dark shaggy neck mane and a pale rump patch give the bull the coloring of the species.

The run extends the long legs fully and lifts the head slightly, which shows weight driving forward. Fur on the neck and chest moves with the stride and keeps the animal from looking like a plastic toy. Blender controls hold both the groom and the animation, so nothing breaks in a format conversion.

This bull has galloped through national park promos, hunting games and VR forest walks since 2026. A looped run lets the elk cross a meadow or flee between trees along any curve you draw. The brown coat sits naturally against autumn grass, early snow and the gray trunks of an aspen grove. Dust kicked up by the hooves can be added in compositing, since the feet strike the ground cleanly.
