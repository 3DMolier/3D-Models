About This Model

A triceratops shown both as a living animal and as a fossil skeleton, made for museums, dinosaur documentaries and science classrooms. The broad neck frill and the brow horns give the animal its famous profile, while the skeleton reveals the bones behind it. Showing both side by side helps visitors connect fossils to a real creature.

The collection carries CheckMate Pro certification for clean geometry and true proportions between the flesh model and the bones. Skin, horns and skeleton parts are grouped clearly, so the fossil can be assembled or scattered as a dig site.

First published in 2020, it is used by museum designers, educational publishers and dinosaur game developers. Mount the skeleton in a hall, let the living animal graze by a river, or bury bones under sand for a dig scene. It also fits children's books and documentaries about the late Cretaceous world.
