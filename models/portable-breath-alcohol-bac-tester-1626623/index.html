About This Model

Priced at $39, the BAC Tester is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 18,244 polygons and 18,808 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 7 maps at 4096 x 4096. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
