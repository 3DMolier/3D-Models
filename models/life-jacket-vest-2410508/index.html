About This Model

Priced at $29, the Life Jacket Vest is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 8,539 polygons over 8,614 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Real-world footprint is 45.73 x 10.06 x H66.43 cm, so it drops into a scene at correct scale without a rescaling pass. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
