About This Model

Fresh bagels in one set, the ring-shaped breads with glossy crusts and seed toppings that anchor any breakfast table. Rounded rings are easy to recognize but hard to model with believable crust bumps, and these arrive with that work done. Each bagel sits as its own object, so you can halve the pile, fill a basket or lay a few on a board.

This collection holds CheckMate Pro certification, reflecting TurboSquid's careful review of geometry and naming. A warm subsurface shader brings out the baked crust, and sesame or poppy seeds can come through bump detail.

First listed in 2023 at $33, it works for bakery interiors, café menus and New York street food scenes. Pair them with cream cheese, smoked salmon or a coffee cup, and a brunch illustration is nearly finished. Game artists also stack bagels on deli counters to make a city shop look stocked and busy. A paper bag tipped on its side, with bagels spilling out, is another quick composition.
