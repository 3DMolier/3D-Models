About This Model

Alphabet Balloons belongs to our Signage & Decor range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 95,108 polygons and 94,926 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

52 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
