About This Model

Subaru Forester 2022 is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. The mesh carries 224,993 polygons and 244,782 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
