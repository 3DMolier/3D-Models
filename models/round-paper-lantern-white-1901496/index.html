This Lighting model - Round Paper Lantern - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $24 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 4,073 polygons over 4,105 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
