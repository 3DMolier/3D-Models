About This Model

The African American Locksmith is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 14,101 polygons and 15,071 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.
