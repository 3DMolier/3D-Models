About This Model

Planmeca Solanna Dental Unit Operating Light is one of the Medical models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $29. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags medical education, training simulation and VR experiences as the intended applications. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualisation and medical sequences in film.

At 12,767 polygons and 13,075 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Maps: 7 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
