About This Model

A spotted white-tailed fawn with groomed fur, made for woodland scenes, nature documentaries and gentle family animation. White spots along the back, big ears and slender legs give the young deer its fragile, endearing look. The fawn looks right standing in tall grass at a forest edge or lying hidden beneath ferns.

CheckMate-aligned preparation gives the animal clean geometry and a realistic size beside forest props such as logs and stumps. Dappled sunlight through leaves mirrors the camouflage of the spotted coat and shows the fur structure well. Close shots reward the soft muzzle and dark eyes, where viewers naturally look first.

Two versions are offered: a walking fawn and the White-Tailed Fawn Deer Jumping Fur Animated Rigged for Maya. Introduced in 2025, the fawn comes royalty free for documentaries, games and picture books. Wildlife parks, hunting education programs and children's storytellers use it to show the first months of a deer's life.
