About This Model

A gaming monitor with thin bezels, an angular stand and a matte screen, made for esports rooms and home setups. The 25,303 polygons shape the panel, the rear vents, the adjustable stand and the port cluster. There are 25,821 vertices, a touch above the faces, so the parts remain clean and separate.

Six texture maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 carry plastic grain, rear details and the glowing display. UV islands are separate, so a game screenshot or a desktop wallpaper can be mapped straight onto the screen. TurboSquid inspected the monitor's build and size before awarding it CheckMate Pro.

Available since 2019, 27 Inch Gaming Monitor suits gaming room renders, esports arena scenes and electronics store visuals. RGB lighting behind the desk makes the screen pop in a dark room like a real battle station. Streamer setups and office desks both benefit from a believable display in the frame.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2019 · Updated 12 November 2026
