About This Model

This collection gathers battery electric trucks in one purchase, ready for logistics animations, charging depot scenes and clean transport explainers. Electric trucks look different from diesel rigs, with flat sealed grilles, no exhaust stacks and cab shapes tuned for smoother airflow. Those details are what a viewer notices first, and the set keeps them crisp enough for close passes along the cab.

Every vehicle in the bundle passed CheckMate Pro review, the strictest TurboSquid tier for topology, naming and overall file hygiene. In practice that means clean quads, sensible pivots and no stray helper objects when the trucks open in your scene. Because the vehicles share one style, they sit together in a fleet shot without obvious jumps in detail or finish.

The collection first went on sale in 2019 and still covers the core of what an electric freight scene needs. Studios take it for sustainability reports, urban delivery animations, port and warehouse visualization, and engineering presentations. Buying the group together also saves the work of matching scale and material style across separate vehicles from different authors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
