About This Model

A large collection of transporter trucks, the long haulers that carry cars, machinery and freight between cities. A road scene needs several kinds of heavy trucks, and this set provides them with matching scale and style. Long trailers and high cabs give strong silhouettes that read well even from a helicopter camera.

Each truck is prepared to CheckMate specification, so dimensions and naming stay consistent across the set. That makes it easy to fill a highway, a port gate or a logistics yard without mixing mismatched assets. Cab colors and trailer liveries are the quickest changes for giving each vehicle its own company.

First offered in 2025, the bundle serves traffic simulations, logistics presentations and open-world driving games. Line them up at a loading dock at dawn and the scene tells a story of the day's deliveries. Toy and model makers can also study cab and trailer proportions here before designing scaled replicas for collectors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
