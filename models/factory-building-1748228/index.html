About This Model

A sprawling industrial complex with long production halls, loading docks and a flat roofline, ready to anchor a logistics scene. The Factory Building uses 80,549 polygons and 95,883 vertices for the whole site. That keeps it light enough for aerial shots and drive-by animation beside roads and parking lots.

Its UVs are non-overlapping, so brick, corrugated metal or concrete can tile across the walls without visible seams. CheckMate Lite certification confirms the file opens cleanly and the geometry is ready for scene assembly. Repeated window bands and roof vents give the long facades rhythm when the camera passes at street level.

Offered since 2021 at $129, the complex stands in for any modern plant, serving urban planners and game environment artists. Surround it with trucks and shipping containers, and it becomes a convincing backdrop for a news graphic. The exterior massing reads well from far away, which is where buildings like this usually sit in a frame.
