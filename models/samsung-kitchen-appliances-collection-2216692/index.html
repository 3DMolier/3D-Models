About This Model

A collection of Samsung kitchen appliances, including fridges, ovens and other major units for modern kitchen interiors. Flush doors, handles and control panels follow the clean design of the brand, so a rendered kitchen feels current.

The appliances were made to CheckMate specification, keeping geometry and naming orderly for every unit. Units slot singly into cabinetry or line up along a full kitchen wall with aligned door gaps. Stainless and matte finishes respond well to soft window light in interior renders.

Buyers get two versions: the first Samsung Kitchen Appliances Collection and a second volume with further units. The license is Editorial Uses Only, so the set fits product reviews, home renovation articles and news. Available since 2024, they also fit kitchen design studies and interior education material for students. Light the counters warmly and the appliances settle into a lived-in home with cooking underway.
