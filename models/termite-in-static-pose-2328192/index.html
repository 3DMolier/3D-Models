About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Termite in Static Pose is a finished, render-ready model at $49. Geometry weighs in at 16,452 polygons over 28,164 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
