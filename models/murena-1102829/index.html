About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Murena is a finished, render-ready model at $59. Counted at the source, the model is 4,246 polygons and 4,624 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
