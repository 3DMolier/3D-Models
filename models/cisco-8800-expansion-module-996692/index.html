About This Model

A sidecar key module that clips onto an office IP phone, adding a column of programmable line buttons and a small display. Its 44,481 polygons describe each key, the angled screen and the stand that matches the desk phone beside it.

There are 8 texture maps at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024, covering button labels, the plastic housing and the screen. Non-overlapping UVs and a CheckMate Pro rating vouch for a clean surface across 46,467 vertices. The key labels can be repainted for any company directory, which helps in reception scenes. Each button sits slightly raised from the panel, so side lighting picks out the whole column.

Two color versions are available: the Cisco 8800 Expansion Module in dark grey and the Cisco 8800 Expansion Module White. The model dates from 2016 and serves office visualization, call center interiors and corporate training material. Placed beside a desk phone on a reception counter, it marks the seat of whoever handles every call.
