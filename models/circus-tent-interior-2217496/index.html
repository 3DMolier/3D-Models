About This Model

Circus Tent Interior puts the camera inside the big top, with tiered seating rings around a central arena under a striped canvas roof. Its 219,385 polygons and 230,808 vertices cover the poles, benches and draped fabric, giving wide shots enough structure to feel enclosed.

Because the UV layout has no overlaps, painters can add worn patches to the canvas or unique numbers to each seat row. The scene follows the CheckMate specification, so pieces arrive named and grouped logically rather than as one tangled mesh. Striped canvas panels can be recolored for a vintage or modern troupe without touching the seating geometry.

Released in 2024, the interior suits animated films, carnival-themed games and event planning visualization for touring shows. Drop a spotlight from the rigging toward the ring and the empty arena immediately feels like the minutes before a performance. Acrobat figures, animals or a ringmaster can be staged in the middle without rebuilding any of the surrounding architecture.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2024 · Updated 17 November 2026
