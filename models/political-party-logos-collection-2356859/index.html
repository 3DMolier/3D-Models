About This Model

A collection of political party logos built as 3D emblems, ready for election coverage, debate studios and campaign graphics. Each logo is a separate object with clean extruded edges, so it can stand alone or sit in a lineup. The shapes keep their flat symbol design while gaining enough depth to catch studio light from the side.

Every emblem was built under the CheckMate specification for tidy, predictable geometry. Metallic, glossy plastic or flat color shaders all work, depending on the tone of the broadcast. Logos can be animated to rotate, scale or slide in, which suits results tables and title cards.

The collection arrived in 2025 and puts several related emblems into one purchase. Election night broadcasts, news explainers, political science lessons and documentaries use party symbols to orient viewers quickly. Placed on podiums, maps or above vote count bars, the logos make complex politics easier to follow.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
