About This Model

HMMWV TOW Missile Carrier M966 Desert Simple Interion is a detailed Weapons asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $119. You are getting 346,092 polygons and 352,357 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

41 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
