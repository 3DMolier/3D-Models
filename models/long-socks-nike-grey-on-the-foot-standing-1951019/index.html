About This Model

Socks modelled on a foot are far more useful than socks lying flat. The fabric stretches over the heel, ribs at the cuff, creases at the ankle and takes the shape of the toes - none of which happens to a flat garment, and all of which is what makes hosiery look worn rather than folded.

The mesh is light at 13,872 polygons and 13,876 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the ribbing and the heel and toe shaping.

This page carries a blue version at $24 and two further builds at $29. All suit apparel and sportswear retail renders, e-commerce imagery, character set dressing for film, television and games, and lifestyle advertising. On sale since 2022.
