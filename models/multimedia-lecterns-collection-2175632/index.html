About This Model

A collection of multimedia lecterns with built-in screens and microphone mounts, made for conference stages, lecture halls and press events. The podiums vary in shape, from slim modern stands to wider desks with room for a laptop, notes and a glass of water. Angled tops and front panels give each lectern a clear speaking side, so placement on a stage is obvious.

Built to CheckMate specification, the lecterns share one true scale, so they line up with stage risers and seating. Front panels are left plain, which suits an institution logo or event title applied as a simple decal.

On TurboSquid since 2024, the set serves university interiors, corporate keynote scenes, political debate sets and webinar backdrops. A speaker standing behind one of these podiums instantly reads as a host, professor or official addressing a crowd. Having several designs on hand lets a scene show a panel of speakers without identical furniture repeating across the stage.
