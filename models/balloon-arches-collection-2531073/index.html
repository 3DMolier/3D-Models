About This Model

A collection of balloon arches in different color combinations, the festive frames placed over doors, stages and photo corners. Clusters of round balloons in several sizes give each arch the full, organic look of a professional decorator's work. Glossy latex highlights repeat across every balloon, which is what makes the arches sparkle under party lighting.

Every arch meets the CheckMate specification, keeping scale and naming consistent so they fit doorways and stages of real proportions. Geometry stays smooth on each sphere, so a glossy or metallic shader reads cleanly without faceting. Colors can be changed per balloon group to match a theme, brand palette or season.

Introduced in 2026, the set costs $59 and covers several arch styles. Event planners, party supply stores and wedding visualizers use arches like these to preview a venue before setup. Framing a couple or a birthday cake, the arch turns an ordinary room into a celebration at once.
