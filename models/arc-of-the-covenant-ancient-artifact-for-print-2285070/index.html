About This Model

This printable Ark of the Covenant recreates the gilded chest with its carrying poles and winged cherubim facing each other on the lid. The mesh is prepared as a closed solid, so the artifact prints without extra repair in common slicers. Its rectangular chest, poles and figures form a compact silhouette that stands well on a shelf.

Topology follows CheckMate specification, which keeps the outstretched wings clean and printable at desktop scale. Fine relief on the chest sides holds detail best on resin printers, while filament prints suit larger display pieces.

Arc of the Covenant Ancient Artifact for Print has been ready for makers since 2024, used for tabletop games, Bible study props and collectibles. Painted gold with dark washes, the print looks like a relic pulled from an ancient vault. Adventure game fans also place it in dioramas of temple ruins and treasure chambers. Scaled small, it becomes a miniature treasure for role playing campaigns and board game sets.
