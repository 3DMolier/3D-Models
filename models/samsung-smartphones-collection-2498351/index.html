About This Model

A row of Samsung smartphones in one set, with the slim glass bodies and camera clusters that mark the Galaxy line. The Samsung Smartphones Collection covers several handsets, so a scene can show a lineup instead of a single device. Camera lenses, button placement and screen curvature are the details people check to identify a phone model.

The phones were modeled under CheckMate specification, sitting at true size in a hand or on a desk. Screens are best treated as emissive surfaces, so a home screen or app interface can be dropped in. Matching build quality across the set keeps reflections and glass consistent when phones sit together.

Samsung owns the designs and marks, so the collection is licensed for Editorial Uses Only. Released in 2025, it suits tech journalism, product comparison articles and phone review videos. Desk scenes and office interiors also use them as everyday props that place a story in the present.
