About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Electric Glider is a finished, render-ready model at $69. The mesh carries 128,310 polygons and 69,316 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
