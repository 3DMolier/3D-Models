About This Model

Priced at $129, the Port Container Crane with Container is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 821,810 polygons and 842,436 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

33 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
