About This Model

Kicktail Skateboard Generic is one of the Sports & Recreation models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 21,626 polygons over 22,196 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 18 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
