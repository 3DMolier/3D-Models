About This Model

This Medical model - Human Colon - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $39 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 7,184 polygons and 7,194 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 5 maps with the top set authored at 2048x2048 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
