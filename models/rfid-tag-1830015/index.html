About This Model

RFID Tag belongs to our general range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 1,888 polygons and 1,890 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2021 · Updated 20 August 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the RFID Tag model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

How much does the RFID Tag 3D model cost?

$19 USD, paid once. The Royalty Free license means no recurring cost and no extra payment when the finished work ships.

How clean is the geometry on the RFID Tag model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.

Can the RFID Tag model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
