About This Model

A collection of squeezed toothpaste tubes, rolled up and crumpled from daily use, for bathrooms that look truly lived in. Each tube is deformed differently, with dents, folded ends and caps left open, so no tube repeats another. The pieces are separate objects, which lets you lay one on a sink edge and drop others in a cup.

CheckMate Lite certification confirms clean files at real size, ready for bathroom shelves and counters. Label areas can carry your own designs or faded generic prints after many squeezes. Crinkles along the metal-look laminate catch light, which makes the wear readable from a distance.

Released in 2023, the collection suits realistic bathroom interiors, recycling explainers, dental tips and student apartment scenes. A tube squeezed flat beside an empty glass tells a small story about running out at the worst moment. Environmental illustrators also use crumpled tubes like these to talk about plastic waste and recycling habits.
