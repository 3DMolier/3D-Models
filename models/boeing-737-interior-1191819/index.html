About This Model

This Aircraft model - Boeing 737 Interior - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $279 on TurboSquid. The polygon count is 4,055,509, spread over 4,200,995 vertices. Whether that fits depends on where it lands - an architectural still and a real-time walkthrough tolerate very different counts.

You get 24 maps at 4096 x 4096 - enough resolution to hold a close shot. Edge flow follows the form rather than fighting it, which is what lets the silhouette hold up when the camera moves in. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV and VR training.
