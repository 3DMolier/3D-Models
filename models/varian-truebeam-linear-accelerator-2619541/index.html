About This Model

A Varian TrueBeam linear accelerator gantry, the machine that rotates around a patient to deliver precise radiation therapy. The Varian TrueBeam Linear Accelerator uses 52,180 polygons for the gantry ring, treatment head and imaging arms.

With 53,461 vertices, the sweeping curves of the housing stay smooth from any angle of rotation. As a StemCell product, the machine is provided in multiple native formats prepared from one master scene. The imaging arms fold out to either side, a recognizable feature of modern treatment rooms.

This 2026 accelerator is priced at $59 and serves hospital architects, medical animators and oncology educators. Animate the gantry turning around a couch to explain how beams reach a tumor from many directions. A lighter build than the full system, it suits wider room shots where many pieces of equipment appear. Clinics introducing new technology to patients can show the machine before the first appointment.
