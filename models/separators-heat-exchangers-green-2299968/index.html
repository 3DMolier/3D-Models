About This Model

Priced at $99, the Separators Heat Exchangers Green is a finished Architecture Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 222,699 polygons and 230,228 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
