About This Model

A monarch butterfly that lifts off from a swaying flower, with orange wings veined in black and edged with white spots. The animation starts with the insect resting on the bloom, then the stem springs back as the wings open. Because the flower moves too, the shot has natural follow-through rather than a stiff, mechanical departure.

Thin legs and curled antennae are modeled separately, which keeps the silhouette sharp in slow motion takeoffs. It is built to CheckMate specification, so naming, scale and scene structure follow TurboSquid's inspection rules. Translucent wing materials glow when backlit by the sun, the effect that makes macro nature shots feel alive.

Two versions are offered at $149 each: the Maya build and an equivalent rigged for Cinema 4D. Looped or played once, the takeoff adds a moment of life to still garden scenes. Since 2020 it has fluttered through garden commercials, nature documentaries, spring greeting animations and educational pieces on migration.
