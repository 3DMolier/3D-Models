About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Mr Olympia Sandow Statue is a finished, render-ready model at $49. You are getting 15,700 polygons and 15,663 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
