About This Model

Coach-style passenger seating with high padded backs and aisle armrests, built for intercity bus interiors and travel scenes. The Bus Passenger Seat Simplified holds 399,693 polygons and 417,732 vertices, a generous budget that keeps stitching, cushions and frames smooth in close shots. Reclining backs and headrest covers give the seating the comfortable look of long-distance travel rather than a city route.

Its CheckMate certification confirms clean geometry, consistent naming and a hierarchy that is easy to duplicate down an aisle. Fabric panels contrast with plastic side shells and metal floor brackets, which helps each row read in dim cabin light. Rows can be spaced to any pitch, so the same asset furnishes compact shuttles and spacious tour coaches alike.

It joined the catalog in 2026 with a price of $139 and Royalty Free terms. Bus interior visualization, travel films, transport simulators and commuter games are the places this seating serves well. A row of empty seats under flickering lights also creates a quiet, uneasy mood for night-ride thrillers.
