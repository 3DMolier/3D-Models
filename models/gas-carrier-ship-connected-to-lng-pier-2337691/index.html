Looking for a Ships & Boats asset? The Gas Carrier Ship Connected to LNG Pier is a finished, render-ready model at $189. The mesh carries 1,145,683 polygons and 1,249,108 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 56 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096.
