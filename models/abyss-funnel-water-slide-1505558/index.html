About This Model

A funnel water slide drops riders into a giant open cone where they swing up the walls before falling through the bottom. The Abyss Funnel Water Slide uses 230,480 polygons and 257,728 vertices on the tube, funnel and support tower.

Six maps at 4096 x 4096 cover glossy fiberglass, steel supports and stair treads. An enclosed tube feeds the funnel from a tall platform, and the cone flares wide enough for rafts to climb its sides. The ride meets CheckMate Pro standards, with tidy topology and realistic proportions throughout the whole structure.

Since 2020 the slide has been used in water park masterplans, resort renders and amusement design pitches. Designers place it beside a pool with sunbeds to show how the ride anchors a leisure zone. Animators send a raft spiraling around the funnel to explain the experience. Game developers build theme park levels where the bright cone becomes a landmark.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2020 · Updated 12 November 2026
