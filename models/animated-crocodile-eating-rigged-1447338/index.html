About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Animated Crocodile Eating Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $249. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is catalogued for film production, game environments and VR experiences. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 24,948 polygons over 24,739 vertices - a light build for its class. Texture work runs to 6 maps, the largest at 8192x8192, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
