About This Model

KAI T-50 Golden Eagle Republic of Korea Jet Rigged is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $179. You are getting 320,633 polygons and 352,240 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 9.49 x 13.29 x H4.83 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization and flight simulation. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
