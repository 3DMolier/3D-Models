About This Model

Xmas Bells is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 16,728 polygons and 17,448 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
