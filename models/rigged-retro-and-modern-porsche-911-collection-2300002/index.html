About This Model

Classic and current Porsche 911 coupes share this rigged collection, showing how the rear-engine sports car kept its sloping roofline across generations. Round headlights, wide rear haunches and that falling fastback make each generation instantly familiar to car enthusiasts. Rigged wheels and steering let driving shots and parked hero angles be set up quickly.

Each car meets CheckMate specification, so body panels stay smooth and reflections flow cleanly along the curves. Glossy paint with a clear coat layer brings out the subtle shoulder lines that define the shape. Chrome trim on the retro cars contrasts with the darker detailing of the modern ones.

Licensed for Editorial Uses Only and available since 2024, the set fits motoring journalism, automotive history content and editorial illustration. A lineup of old and new cars side by side explains design evolution better than paragraphs of text. Classic models suit cobblestone streets, while the newer coupes look right in clean urban settings.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
