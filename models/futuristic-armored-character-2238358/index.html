Futuristic Armored Character belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $169 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defence training material and military VFX shots.
