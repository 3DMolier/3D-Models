About This Model

This electric hydrofoil surfboard rides above the water on a mast and wing, a striking prop for watersport and lifestyle scenes. The Fliteboard PRO Series 3 Carbon packs 17,010 polygons and 8,902 vertices into the board, the mast and the foil wings. Its sleek carbon deck and thin hydrofoil make a strong silhouette against open sea, a lagoon or a sunset sky.

The deck unwrap has no overlapping islands, so a custom graphic or a different grip pad color goes on easily. Being CheckMate Pro certified, the mesh is tidy enough to animate skimming over waves.

There are 2 versions at $39 each: this carbon edition and the Fliteboard PRO Series 3 Wood Color. Released in 2023, it has attracted 10 buyers from watersport retailers, travel video creators and game developers. Put a rider on the deck, add spray behind the foil, and a holiday coast scene comes alive in seconds.
