About This Model

This Signage & Decor model - Little Snow Girl - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $39 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 87,674 polygons over 93,314 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
