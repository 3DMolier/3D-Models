About This Model

A dark naval jumper with the square blue collar and white tapes, the traditional top worn by British sailors. The Royal Navy Sailor Jumper Dark spends its 16,844 polygons on soft folds, cuffs and the broad collar flap.

With 16,836 vertices, the fabric drapes naturally over the shoulders without looking stiff or plastic. StemCell production gives the jumper consistent materials across several native formats from one master scene. The collar falls in a wide square across the back, the shape that has marked sailors for generations.

The 2026 jumper is priced at $39 and suits historical dramas, naval costumes and museum reconstructions. Dress a character standing at the rail of a destroyer, or lay it folded on a narrow bunk below decks. Combined with the cap and trousers, it builds a complete Royal Navy uniform for period scenes. Costume designers planning a stage production can check how the dark cloth reads under spotlights.
