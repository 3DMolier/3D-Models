About This Model

Astronaut Wearing Space Suit Strizh with SK-1 Helmet is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $169. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Geometry weighs in at 69,842 polygons over 71,634 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
