About This Model

These western boot spurs pair spiked star rowels with leather straps, made for cowboy characters, rodeo arenas and saloon props. The 78,492 polygons and 39,249 vertices give the rowel points, the buckles and the curved heel bands sharp edges. The star rowel sits at the back of the heel band, where it catches the eye in any low boot shot.

Polished steel meets worn leather in one small object, which makes the pair a handy test for mixed material setups. Straps and bands unwrap separately without overlaps, leaving clean room for painted engraving, tarnish or rust on the metal. A CheckMate Pro mark confirms clean topology and clearly named parts throughout the pair.

Available since 2022, the spurs suit western films, frontier games and history lessons about cattle drives. Strapped to dusty boots resting on a porch rail, they establish a character before a single line of dialogue. Hung from a nail beside a saddle, the pair also dresses a tack room or a ranch hallway wall.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2022 · Updated 18 November 2026
