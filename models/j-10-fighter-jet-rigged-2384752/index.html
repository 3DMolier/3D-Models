About This Model

The J-10 Fighter Jet is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. You are getting 130,402 polygons and 137,030 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation.

It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
