About This Model

A wild zebra caught in full gallop gives savanna chases, wildlife films and nature documentaries an energetic striped runner. Fur covers the body, so the bold black and white stripes have a soft natural texture instead of a flat painted look. Prepared for Blender as an animated set, the zebra can cross a scene at speed straight away.

TurboSquid verified the asset against CheckMate specification, so its mesh and naming are dependable. The short upright mane and tufted tail move with the gait, adding life to every stride.

Released in 2026, the runner suits safari documentaries, migration sequences, educational biology content and African wildlife games. A herd of copies kicking up dust across a dry plain creates the classic migration image in minutes. Predator chase scenes with lions or cheetahs depend on a convincing prey animal, and this zebra delivers that. Low golden light from the side brings out the stripe pattern and the muscles of the legs in motion.
