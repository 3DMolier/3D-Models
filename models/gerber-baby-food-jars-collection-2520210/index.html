About This Model

A broad range of jars for infant purees from a well-known baby brand, mixing products for a complete store shelf. Squat glass jars with colorful labels and twist lids line up into the familiar baby aisle look. Having the range together saves building a retail shelf one jar at a time, which matters for long aisles.

This collection was audited against CheckMate specification, so TurboSquid confirmed scale and naming. Labels benefit from a matte paper shader, while lids take a brushed metal finish that catches overhead light. Mixed flavors in one row give a supermarket shelf the variety a shopper expects to see.

Released in 2026 at $34, the collection may only be used under Editorial Uses Only terms. Grocery news, infant nutrition reporting, parenting blogs and documentary footage are appropriate settings for the collection. Stacked in a pantry beside formula and bottles, the jars complete a realistic, busy family kitchen corner.
