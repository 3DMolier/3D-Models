About This Model

The Four Door 4X4 SUV Exterior Only is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $89 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 192,878 polygons over 219,419 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
