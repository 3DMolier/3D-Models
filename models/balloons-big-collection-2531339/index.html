About This Model

A large set of party balloons in assorted shapes and colors, ready to dress birthdays, weddings and store openings. Round latex balloons, tied knots and trailing strings are the details that make a balloon read as real rather than as a sphere. Having many variants in one set means a bunch never looks cloned.

Each balloon meets CheckMate specification with a tidy pivot at the knot for easy placement in a scene. Glossy surfaces reflect the room around them, which helps balloons blend into interior lighting. You can cluster them into arches, float them under a ceiling or tie them to the backs of chairs.

Added in 2026 with a $179 price, the set serves event planners, greeting card artists and animation studios. Retail scenes use balloons to signal a sale or celebration without any text. Game developers also scatter them through festival levels, fairgrounds and party stages.
