About This Model

Dental Equipment with Doctor and Patient Rigged is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $209. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are medical education, training simulation and VR experiences. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualisation and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 57,814 polygons and 53,466 vertices, a light load for a scene. Maps: 32 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
