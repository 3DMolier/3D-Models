About This Model

A strip of office staples, partly broken into loose pieces, ready for macro shots of desk supplies. The Paper Staples model uses 162,004 polygons and 163,608 vertices, all quads, so every tiny wire bend stays perfectly smooth. Such a high count for a tiny object means the staples hold up even at extreme magnification.

The group measures 8.74 x 7.76 x H1.84 cm, smaller than a palm and sized exactly like real stationery. StemCell delivery keeps the bright steel material identical across every supported format and render engine. Glue lines between the individual staples show how a strip holds together before loading into a stapler.

At $19 the staples are one version, first placed in the store during 2026. Office scenes, stationery product renders, school supply explainers, desk organizers and macro photography practice use it as realistic desk clutter. Light rakes across the shiny wire and gives macro shots a crisp metallic sparkle.
