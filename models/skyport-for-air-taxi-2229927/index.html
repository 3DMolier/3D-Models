About This Model

The Skyport for Air Taxi is a production-ready Architecture Landmarks 3D model, priced at $59 on TurboSquid. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

Counted at the source, the model is 16,585 polygons and 19,276 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

Can the Skyport for Air Taxi model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

What is the Skyport for Air Taxi model typically used for?

Stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.
