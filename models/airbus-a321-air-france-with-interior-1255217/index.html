About This Model

Airbus A321 Air France with Interior is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $499. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

At 2,405,109 polygons and 2,472,953 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 55 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
