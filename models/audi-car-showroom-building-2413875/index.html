About This Model

Priced at $129, the Audi Car Showroom Building is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 1,198,036 polygons over 1,055,522 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
