About This Model

The Jumper Gauge is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 6,192 polygons over 6,176 vertices - a light build for its class. Texture work runs to 4 maps, the largest at 2048x2048, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
