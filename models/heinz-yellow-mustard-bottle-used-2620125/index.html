About This Model

A used Heinz yellow mustard squeeze bottle with dents and smudges, made for barbecue tables, hot dog stands and picnic scenes. The 6,686 polygons describe the pinched body, the nozzle cap and the creases left by many squeezes. The bright yellow plastic draws attention, even when the bottle sits at the edge of a table.

Under CheckMate review it passed with real scale, and its 6,600 vertices keep the bottle simple and light. Mixed UVs suit decals for labels, price stickers and the yellow mustard smears near the cap. The license here is Editorial Uses Only, a fit for reporting, education and documentary storytelling.

Offered at $34 from 2026, the bottle suits food photography for editorial features, kitchen interiors and summer cookout scenes. It measures 33.48 x 18.84 x H18.03 cm in its resting pose. Lay it on its side beside a grill and a few hot dogs for a relaxed backyard moment.
