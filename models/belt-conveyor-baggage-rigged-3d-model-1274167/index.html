About This Model

An airport baggage conveyor with a moving belt surface and side rails, built for check-in halls and baggage claim scenes. Its 19,772 polygons keep the frame light, while 208,437 vertices give the belt segments and rollers their density. The long, low shape slots under check-in desks or curves around a claim area.

Surfacing uses 53 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048, enough for close shots of scuffed rubber and metal. UVs are non-overlapping, so wear can be painted along the belt without repeating patterns.

In the catalog since 2018, the conveyor serves airport visualization, logistics training and film sets. Put a few suitcases on the belt, and the scene reads immediately as an arrivals hall. The rails sit at a height where passengers can lift bags off without bending low. Warehouse planners also borrow it for parcel sorting lines where a simple belt is enough.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2018 · Updated 16 November 2026
