About This Model

Traditional African instruments gathered in one collection, from hand drums to plucked and shaken pieces for music and cultural scenes. The African Music Instruments Collection brings carved wood, stretched hide and decorative cords together in a single set. Each instrument keeps its handmade character, with uneven carving and lashings that read clearly in close shots.

Every piece holds CheckMate Pro certification, which confirms clean geometry, real scale and orderly naming. Drum skins look most convincing with a slightly translucent, fibrous material under warm side light.

Since 2023 the collection has supported music education, ethnographic museum displays and cultural festival visuals. Animators use the drums for performance scenes, while game artists place them in villages and ceremony grounds. Arrange the instruments on a woven mat to create a complete still life for an article on West African rhythm. Documentary producers also cut away to such sets when explaining how each sound is produced.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
