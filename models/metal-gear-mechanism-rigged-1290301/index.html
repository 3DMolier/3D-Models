About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Metal Gear Mechanism Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $49. Geometry weighs in at 84,599 polygons over 83,762 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 3 maps at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current.
