About This Model

A single ripe cherry with a curved stem, light enough to scatter in large numbers across desserts and fruit bowls. Cherry Fruit uses only 192 polygons over 194 vertices, all quads, so subdivision rounds the skin without visible facets. The small dimple where the stem enters the fruit is modeled, and it catches a soft shadow in close framing.

Its 7 maps at 4096 x 4096 carry the deep red skin, faint speckles and the green woody stalk. Because the UV islands never overlap, recoloring it into a yellow Rainier type or a dark sour cherry takes minutes. TurboSquid granted CheckMate Pro after a manual review, an unusual badge for a mesh this small.

On the market since 2017 at $9, the cherry tops cakes, sundaes and cocktail glasses in food photography and recipe apps. Duplicating it and bending two stalks together gives the joined pair seen on market stalls. Bakery menus and juice packaging also use it as a bright accent against white whipped cream.
