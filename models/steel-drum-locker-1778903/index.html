About This Model

The Steel Drum Locker is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

Counted at the source, the model is 19,251 polygons and 19,983 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
