About This Model

Another collection of Christmas tree toys, gathering ornaments that hang from branches or sit on festive shelves. Glass baubles, figurines and small shapes catch light differently, which is what makes a decorated tree sparkle in a render. Grouping them together keeps colors and scale coordinated, so a tree looks dressed by one hand rather than assembled at random.

Each ornament is its own object, ready to be scattered across a tree with a placement tool. Every ornament in the box follows CheckMate specification for real-world size and readable names. Reflective and glossy surfaces respond beautifully to small point lights, like the glow of fairy lights.

Released in 2026, it serves holiday greeting cards, retail window design, seasonal interiors and festive game levels. A close shot of a single ornament with a blurred tree behind makes a classic holiday card image. Interior designers use sets like this to show clients a finished holiday scheme before buying decorations.
