About This Model

This Electronics & Gadgets model - Old Telephone With Rotary Dial - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $39 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 21,405 polygons and 22,066 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

24 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
