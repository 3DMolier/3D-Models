About This Model

Looking for a Food & Beverages asset? The Dry Dog Food is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 25,344 polygons and 25,740 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 3 maps at 1024 x 1024. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for food photography, film & TV VFX and game assets. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
