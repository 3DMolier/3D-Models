About This Model

A collection of ice cream vending machines, the glowing freezer kiosks found in malls, stations and office lobbies. Glass fronts, illuminated panels and product windows are the details people recognize, so each machine reads instantly.

Every machine meets CheckMate specification, and matching scale lets a row of them stand neatly along a corridor wall. Several designs in one pack let a food court or transit hall avoid repeating the same unit. Glass doors with visible product shelves reward close shots, since reflections and frost suggest the cold.

Priced at $166 and released in 2026, the set suits retail planning, vending industry presentations and shopping mall renders. Turn on the panel glow in a dim station at night, and the machines become the brightest objects in the frame. Food brands can map their own artwork onto the fronts to preview new branding on real equipment. The machines also fill background space in convenience stores and airport lounges, where vending is everywhere.
