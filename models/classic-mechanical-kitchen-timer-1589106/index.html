About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Mechanical Kitchen Timer is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 3,303 polygons and 3,420 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and event visualization. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
