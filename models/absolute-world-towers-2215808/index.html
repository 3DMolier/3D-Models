About This Model

A pair of curvy residential towers whose oval floor plates twist as they rise, giving the facade its famous wavy balconies. The 1,387,894 polygons go into each floor slab, balcony edge and glazing band, so the twist reads smoothly. Two heights side by side make the pair read like dancers turning in place.

Vertices number 1,738,237, and StemCell credentials keep the glass and concrete materials stable across engines. Continuous balconies wrap every floor, catching sunlight in bright horizontal stripes from morning to dusk. Placed in a skyline, the towers stand out because no other buildings twist quite like them.

Two versions exist: daytime towers at $129 and Absolute World Towers in the Night Lightning, also $129. The night version adds lit windows and a glowing outline for evening skyline renders. Since 2024 the towers have stood in Mississauga city scenes, architecture lectures and real estate visualizations.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
