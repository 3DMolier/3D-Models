About This Model

Priced at $99, the AMG F1 City Electric Bicycle Black is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine.
