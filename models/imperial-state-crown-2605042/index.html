About This Model

This replica of the British Imperial State Crown carries its arches, cross pattee and band of gems on a velvet cap. The mesh holds 2,424,195 polygons and 2,314,947 vertices, so even small pearls along the arches stay round in close-ups.

It measures 28.85 x 29.04 x H35.49 cm, true to the size of a crown worn at a state opening. CheckMate certification ensures consistent naming and real units, which matters when this many objects sit in one file. The ermine border at the base frames the velvet and separates gold from fabric under strong light.

Three versions are offered: the bare crown, a fur variant, and the crown inside a glass exhibition stand with fur. All three date from 2026, with the base version priced at $59. The exhibition stand variant suits a jewel house setting, complete with display lighting through the glass. Royal documentaries, palace tours, period dramas and museum exhibit previews use it for scenes of ceremony and heritage.
