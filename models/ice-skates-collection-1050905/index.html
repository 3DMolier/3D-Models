About This Model

Ice skates in several styles, with laced boots and steel blades, collected for winter sports and rink scenes. The Ice Skates Collection lets a scene show skaters of different ages and disciplines without hunting for separate models. Blades are mounted under the boot as on real skates, so a pair standing on the ice looks believable.

Every skate in the set is rated CheckMate Pro, a sign that meshes and materials were reviewed. Shiny blades against matte leather or plastic boots give contrast that reads well under arena lights. Laces and eyelets are shaped in geometry, which holds up in close shots of skaters tying their boots.

The skates have been sold since 2016 and suit both outdoor ponds and indoor arenas. Sports stores show them on product pages, and winter games give them to players on frozen lakes. Holiday animations also hang a pair over a shoulder as a quick sign of the season.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
