About This Model

A children's pop-up book, this playful prop fills nurseries, school libraries and bedtime story scenes with paper magic. The book uses 2,352 polygons on 1,224 vertices for the hard cover, the folded pages and the paper figures that rise from the spread. The cutouts stand up when the book opens wide, which makes it perfect for animated reading moments.

TurboSquid checked the book to its CheckMate Lite standard, which covers the basics of a sound and usable model. The unwrapped pages are kept apart, so colorful illustrations, story text and cover art can be painted in. A soft paper shader with slight curling at the edges gives the pages a hand-crafted feel.

Released in 2023, the book suits kids' animations, kindergarten interiors and publishing concept visuals. Opened on a bed with a toy nearby, it creates a calm bedtime scene for a family story. Libraries and bookshops in games show it on low shelves where young readers can reach it.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2023 · Updated 16 November 2026
