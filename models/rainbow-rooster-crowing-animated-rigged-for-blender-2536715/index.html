About This Model

A colorful rooster throwing its head back to crow, animated and rigged in Blender for morning scenes and farm cartoons. Crowing at dawn is the most recognizable rooster behavior, so the clip instantly signals the start of a day.

Rainbow plumage runs from orange hackles to green and blue tail sickles, giving the bird a vivid silhouette in sunlight. During the crow the chest swells, the beak opens wide and the neck stretches upward in one fluid motion. Bright red comb and dangling lobes under the beak shake with each call, which adds lively secondary motion. Blender keeps the controls and feather materials together, which simplifies edits to timing or pose.

Introduced in 2026, the rooster suits breakfast scenes, village alarm clock gags and agricultural explainers. Perch it on a fence post against a sunrise, and the colors glow as the light rises behind it. A second rooster with offset timing creates a comic duet across a farmyard without extra modeling.
