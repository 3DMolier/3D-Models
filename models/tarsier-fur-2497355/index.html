About This Model

The Tarsier Fur is a production-ready Animals & Creatures 3D model, priced at $99 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. Available since 2026, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 12,402 polygons and 12,827 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
