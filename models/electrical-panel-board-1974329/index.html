About This Model

A floor-standing distribution cabinet packed with breakers, busbars and labeled switches, built for plant rooms and utility corridors. Electrical Panel Board uses 152,562 polygons against only 82,855 vertices, a triangle-heavy mesh that spends its budget on breaker toggles and cable entries. The front doors frame the switchgear like a grid, so the cabinet still reads well when the camera stays several meters back.

UV islands are kept apart without overlap, leaving clean space for warning stickers, circuit labels and grime around the handles. CheckMate Pro certification brings correct scale and orderly naming, so the cabinet sits flush against walls, doors and conduit runs. Lined up in a row, several cabinets turn a bare concrete room into a believable switch room for data center scenes.

The model came out in 2022 and serves electrical engineers, safety trainers and level designers who build industrial maps. Lockout training clips can open the door and point at each breaker before a lesson on isolating a circuit. Under a single flickering lamp in a basement, the panel becomes the obvious focus of a blackout or thriller scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2022 · Updated 19 November 2026
