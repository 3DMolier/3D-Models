About This Model

A resting walrus rigged and animated for Blender, sprawled on its belly with long ivory tusks resting on the ground. Wrinkled, pinkish-brown skin and thick folds of blubber give the body its heavy, sleepy weight. Stiff whiskers on the broad snout catch light, a small detail that makes the face read up close.

Its rest animation adds slow breathing and small head movements, so it looks alive on an ice floe without travel. Rigged as named, the walrus can also be posed lifting its head or rolling to one side. CheckMate guidance puts it at a believable size, so it dwarfs nearby seals and people as it should.

New in 2025, the walrus suits Arctic documentaries, climate change explainers, zoo signage and polar exploration games. A herd of several hauled out on a gray beach, breath steaming, makes a striking wide shot. Marine mammal researchers rely on footage like this to show how walruses rest between feeding dives.
