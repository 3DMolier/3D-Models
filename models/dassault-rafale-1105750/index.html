About This Model

Dassault Rafale is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $159. You are getting 118,770 polygons and 132,221 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 29 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 2048 x 2048. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
