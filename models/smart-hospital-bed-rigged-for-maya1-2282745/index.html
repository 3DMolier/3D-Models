About This Model

This modern hospital bed is built for clinical scenes, with an adjustable frame, side rails and a mattress ready for patient shots. Head and knee sections fold to raise a patient, the core function any care setting needs to show. The frame sits on locking casters, which helps the bed look believable when rolled through a ward corridor.

The bed complies with CheckMate specification, so the hierarchy is tidy and parts are named for selection in Maya. Smooth plastic panels and a control pad at the foot board suggest the electronic adjustments found on newer ward beds.

Since 2024 the smart bed has served medical animation, hospital interior design and healthcare training videos. Paired with a monitor stand and a curtain track, it quickly sets up a private recovery room. White and pale gray finishes keep the bed neutral, so it fits both public hospitals and private clinics. Producers of medical drama scenes also use it as a dependable foreground prop.
