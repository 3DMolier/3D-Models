About This Model

Jack Daniels belongs to our Kitchen & Tableware range and is offered at $24 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 12,462 polygons over 6,265 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
