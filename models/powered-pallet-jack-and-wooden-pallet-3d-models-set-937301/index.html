About This Model

Powered Pallet Jack and Wooden Pallet Set belongs to our Industrial Equipment range and is offered at $49 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 101,203 polygons and 102,274 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

16 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
