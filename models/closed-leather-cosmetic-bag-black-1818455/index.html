About This Model

A zipped leather makeup pouch with a soft rounded body, a metal zipper pull and neat stitched seams along the edges. Its 9,404 polygons and 9,904 vertices capture the gentle bulge of a filled bag and the zipper teeth across the top. Placed on a vanity next to perfume bottles, it adds a personal touch to a bathroom or bedroom scene.

Backed by CheckMate Pro, the pouch is a clean mesh that sits well on any shelf or tabletop. Black leather with soft creases catches restrained highlights, which keeps the bag elegant rather than shiny. The zipper pull hangs slightly off the body, a small detail that makes close shots believable.

The pouch comes in three versions at $44 each, two in black and one in brown leather. Since 2021, the bag has appeared in beauty product scenes, travel packing visuals and fashion accessory renders. Lifestyle bloggers also use it when illustrating what to pack for a weekend away.
