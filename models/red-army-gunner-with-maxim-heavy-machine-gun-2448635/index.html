Red Army Gunner with Maxim Heavy Machine Gun is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $179. You are getting 385,671 polygons and 387,857 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
