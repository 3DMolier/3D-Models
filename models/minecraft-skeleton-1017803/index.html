About This Model

The Minecraft Skeleton is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 7,890 polygons and 7,888 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 2 textures, authored at 256 x 256. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and medical visualization. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
