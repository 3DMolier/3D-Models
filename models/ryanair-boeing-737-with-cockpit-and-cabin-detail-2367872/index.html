About This Model

Priced at $349, the Ryanair Boeing 737 with Cockpit and Cabin Detail is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 5,088,949 polygons and 5,271,641 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
