About This Model

The Metal Attic Ladder is a production-ready Tools 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is useful for industrial and factory visualisation, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

Counted at the source, the model is 66,652 polygons and 35,355 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
