About This Model

A fortified gun position with sandbag walls and a camouflage net, built for military bases, war games and battlefield dioramas. The 973,399 polygons go into individual sandbags, net mesh and the weapon tucked under the sagging cover. The net drapes over poles with realistic sag, which breaks up the silhouette exactly as camouflage should.

Passing CheckMate checks, the position holds 970,617 vertices and keeps net, walls and gun as separate objects. Its UVs have no overlapping islands, so desert tan or woodland patterns can be painted or baked cleanly. Swap the net color to move the emplacement from a sandy region to a forest setting in minutes.

Priced at $169 from its 2026 launch, the model suits strategy games, defense training and war documentary reconstructions. The footprint covers a full crew position, wide enough for soldiers to move around the gun inside. Shoot it from a drone at dusk and the sandbag rows read beautifully against the long shadows.
