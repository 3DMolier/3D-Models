About This Model

A collection of German steel helmets from the Second World War, the flared silhouette instantly tied to that period. The deep skirt, the flared rim and the side vent lugs are the details military historians look for. Several helmets in one set let a display case or battlefield scene avoid repeating one shape.

This set complies with CheckMate specification, which TurboSquid confirmed for scale and naming. Field gray paint, chipped edges and a hint of rust are the classic finish for such helmets. Placed on a crate or a rifle stock, a helmet tells the viewer exactly where and when the scene happens.

The collection costs $34, has been offered since 2025 and is open to Royalty Free use. Historical documentaries, war films, museum displays and strategy or shooter games set in the war years use them. Arranged on a museum shelf with labels, the helmets form an instant military history display.
