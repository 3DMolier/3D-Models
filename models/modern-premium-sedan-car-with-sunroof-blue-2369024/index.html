About This Model

Priced at $129, the Premium with Sunroof is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 257,665 polygons and 278,292 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.
