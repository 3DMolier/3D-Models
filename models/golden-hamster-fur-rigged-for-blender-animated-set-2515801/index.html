About This Model

A golden hamster with soft groomed fur scurries, sniffs and nibbles across this animated Blender set for pet scenes. Round cheeks, tiny pink paws, dark bead eyes and a stubby body make the little rodent irresistibly cute on camera. Its cheek pouches and twitching nose are the details that make viewers smile in close ups.

Built according to CheckMate specification, the hamster keeps fur, body and rig cleanly grouped in the file. Blender animators can blend the cycles into foraging sequences or a quick dash into a cardboard hiding tube. Warm, cozy lighting deepens the golden coat and brings out the soft white fur on the belly.

This 2026 hamster set comes free of royalties for children's animation, pet store displays and lessons about pet care. Kids' learning apps can show feeding, handling and habitat basics with an animal that children already love. A hamster stuffing seeds into its cheeks makes a charming and funny close up.
