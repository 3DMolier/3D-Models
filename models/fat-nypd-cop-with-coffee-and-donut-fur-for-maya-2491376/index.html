About This Model

A heavyset New York police officer holding coffee and a donut, a lighthearted take on the classic beat cop stereotype. Groomed fur adds a mustache and hair, while the uniform, badge and cap make the character readable at once. The coffee cup and donut give the pose a punchline, so the figure works in comedy without extra props.

Prepared for Maya and sized under CheckMate rules, he fits patrol cars, diners and city sidewalks from other kits. Warm diner lighting and a rainy window behind him set a cozy, humorous mood. Neon reflections on a wet sidewalk work too when the scene needs a classic New York night.

Ready since 2025, he suits comedy animation, crime caricatures, cartoon-style trailers and city life games. Keep the tone gentle and affectionate, and the character lands as a familiar joke rather than an insult. Cartoonists and storyboard artists can pose him quickly to test gags before final animation begins.
