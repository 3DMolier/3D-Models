About This Model

Priced at $59, the Human Fetus 12 Weeks is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 10,838 polygons and 10,842 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

18 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
