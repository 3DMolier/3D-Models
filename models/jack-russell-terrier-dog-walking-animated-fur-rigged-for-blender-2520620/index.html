About This Model

A Jack Russell terrier at an easy walk, head up and tail high, the confident trot of a small dog out with its owner. Short legs, a deep chest and a tan and white coat give the breed its sturdy, compact outline. Blender fur covers the coat, so walking shots in sunlight show soft hair rather than a painted surface.

CheckMate specification frames the file, keeping object names and real scale consistent beside people and furniture. A relaxed gait makes the terrier ideal for background life in parks, streets and living rooms. Its fur stays short enough to keep render times low across longer shots and loops.

Since 2026 the terrier has been available for pet care videos, dog food packaging and family animation. A small dog trotting beside a pedestrian adds warmth to any architectural street render or park scene. Veterinary clinics and pet insurers also show walking dogs to illustrate healthy, active companions.
