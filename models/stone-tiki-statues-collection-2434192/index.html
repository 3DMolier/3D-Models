About This Model

A collection of carved stone tiki statues with wide mouths, heavy brows and blocky bodies, inspired by Polynesian carving traditions. Each figure has its own face and pose, so a row of them never looks like copies of one idol. Weathered stone surfaces catch raking sunlight, which brings out the carved grooves in late afternoon shots.

The set follows the CheckMate rules for scale and file layout, so the statues line up at sensible sizes. Moss, lichen or volcanic rock materials suit the shapes, from fresh carving to ancient ruin. Placed along a jungle path or around a pool bar, they set a tropical tone without any dialogue.

Since 2025 the collection has served adventure games, island resort renders and tiki bar interiors. Escape room designers and theme park planners use the figures to mark a mysterious island zone. Lit by torches at night, the idols turn into a strong mood piece for horror or treasure hunt stories.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
