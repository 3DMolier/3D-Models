About This Model

A burly biker in a leather jacket with groomed hair and beard, a generic rider for road, bar and garage scenes. Generic means no club patches or brand marks, so the character fits any story without legal checks. Groomed hair on the head and face gives the character the rugged texture that a smooth mesh cannot fake.

His CheckMate Pro status guarantees true human scale and tidy topology, so he stands correctly beside motorcycles and doorways. Strong rim light catches the fur strands and leather creases, which is the classic look for a roadside portrait. Posed alone or placed near a parked bike, he reads as a rider before any vehicle enters the frame.

Dating back to 2016, the biker remains useful for crime dramas, road trip animation, roadside diner interiors and open world games. A gas station at dusk or a neon bar sign behind him sets the mood with almost no extra modeling. Background crowds at rallies also benefit, since one well groomed figure anchors a group of simpler extras.
