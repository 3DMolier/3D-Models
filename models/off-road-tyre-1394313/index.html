About This Model

Off Road Tyre is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 17,808 polygons and 17,556 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 5 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
