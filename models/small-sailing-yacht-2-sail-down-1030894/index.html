About This Model

This small sailing yacht sits with its sail lowered and furled along the boom, the way a boat rests at a mooring. With the canvas packed away, the clean deck, the cockpit and the rigging lines take center stage. The bare mast stands tall above the hull, which makes it an easy silhouette to read across a crowded harbor.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms that the topology, scale and object naming meet TurboSquid's inspection standards. The hull lines are smooth enough for close passes, and the furled sail keeps the geometry compact compared with a full spread of canvas. The low freeboard and short cabin give it the proportions of a weekend cruiser rather than a racing boat.

It has been on sale since 2016 and has been purchased 15 times by marina planners, coastal tourism designers and animation studios. Several copies moored in a row turn an empty quay into a busy yacht harbor without much setup. It also suits calm evening scenes where a boat rests on still water after a day out.
