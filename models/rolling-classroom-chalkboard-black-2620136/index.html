About This Model

A mobile chalkboard on a wheeled frame, the kind teachers roll between classrooms, workshops and lecture halls. This board has 16,532 polygons, spread over the writing panel, the support frame and the caster wheels.

Its 17,094 vertices keep the frame corners rounded and the wheels properly circular in close views. StemCell packaging means the board ships in several native formats, all built from one master scene. The dark writing surface is ideal for adding your own lessons, formulas or menu text in a texture.

Two versions share the $24 price: the black Rolling Classroom Chalkboard and a Wooden Rolling Classroom Chalkboard. The wooden version gives a warmer, old-school feel, while the black one looks modern and clean. Both arrived in 2026 and suit school interiors, art studios, cafe menu boards and educational animations. Wheel one beside a teacher's desk at the front of the room, or park it outside a cafe to announce the daily specials.
