About This Model

Beriev A 100 Rigged is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $199. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. 2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 287,723 polygons and 297,275 vertices.
