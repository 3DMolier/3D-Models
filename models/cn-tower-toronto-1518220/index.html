About This Model

A concrete communications tower at 40,468 polygons, with the tapered shaft, the pod, the observation deck and the antenna mast. The 46,876 vertices sit above the face count because the pod and the mast are rings on rings. The shape is read from kilometers away, so the budget goes into the silhouette rather than surface clutter.

Thirteen maps at 4096 x 4096 carry the weathered concrete, the glazing of the deck and the lit panels at night. Concrete streaking is what keeps a landmark from looking like a fresh render of a model kit. Delivery is to the StemCell standard, so the tower lands at real scale in any application.

Nothing else sits on the page, so the tower arrives as one model. Released in 2020, the tower has been bought forty-three times at $149. It fits skyline visuals, travel graphics, establishing shots and city studies that need a known landmark.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2020 · Updated 13 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How heavy is the CN Tower Toronto mesh?

40,468 polygons and 46,876 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Our Architecture & Landmarks listings sit at a median of about 53,000 polygons, so 40,468 is lighter than the typical one.

Can the CN Tower Toronto model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How much does the CN Tower Toronto 3D model cost?

It is listed at $149 USD on TurboSquid. That is a one-off purchase under the Royalty Free license - there is no subscription and no per-project fee afterwards.

Does the CN Tower Toronto model include materials and textures?

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. As a StemCell asset it ships with PBR materials that stay consistent across the delivered formats.
