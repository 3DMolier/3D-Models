About This Model

A chameleon fires its sticky tongue at an insect in this Blender animation, catching the lightning-fast hunt of this patient lizard. Swiveling turret eyes, a curled prehensile tail and grasping feet make the chameleon one of nature's strangest designs. The slow, rocking approach along a branch contrasts sharply with the instant strike of the tongue.

Topology upholds CheckMate standards, so the long tongue stretches and retracts without breaking the mesh. Blender is the target program, so the hunt plays back immediately and can be slowed down for detail. Bright green skin with fine, bumpy texture responds especially well to dappled jungle light.

Released in 2026 with royalty free terms, the chameleon suits nature documentaries, biology classes and jungle games. Slow-motion replays of the strike make a memorable highlight in any wildlife compilation or classroom video. A branch with leaves and a lone fly is all the scene needs to tell its story.
