About This Model

A collection of Marshall audio gear, the black-and-gold speakers and amp-styled devices that look straight out of a rock stage. Textured vinyl covering, brass-colored control knobs and the script logo give each piece its recognizable guitar-amp character. Several devices in one set let a shelf or a desk feel kitted out by a music fan.

The pieces share CheckMate specification, so scale and naming stay consistent when they sit side by side. The grille cloth wins close shots, catching light differently from the leather-look sides, while warm side lighting brings out the knobs and gold trim.

Marshall owns the brand and the design, so the license is Editorial Uses Only. Released in 2025, the set suits music-themed film sets, documentaries about recording culture and editorial illustration of home audio. Place a speaker beside a turntable and a stack of records and the scene immediately reads as a listening room. Keep the logo facing the camera, since that script is what viewers recognize first.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2025 · Updated 13 November 2026
