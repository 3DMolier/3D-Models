About This Model

This collection of potato chip bags fills snack aisles, vending machines and party tables with crinkly, colorful foil packaging. Different bags in the set let you mix flavors and styles on a store shelf without obvious repetition. Puffed, air-filled bodies and crimped seams at the top and bottom give each bag its typical look.

As a CheckMate Pro collection, it passed a thorough TurboSquid review of geometry and organization. The foil fronts are perfect canvases for your own packaging designs, nutrition panels and flavor graphics. A metallic inner layer with crinkle normal detail sells the material convincingly in close-ups.

In the catalog since 2022, these $39 bags suit supermarket scenes, snack brand mockups and movie night props. Toss an open bag on a couch with chips spilling out for a casual, lived-in weekend shot. Vending machine interiors and convenience store racks also need many of these to look properly stocked.
