About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Sukhoi Superjet 100 with Saberlets Aeroflot is a finished, render-ready model at $149. You are getting 172,695 polygons and 188,594 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2021 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
