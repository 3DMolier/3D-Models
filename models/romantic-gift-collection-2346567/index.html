About This Model

Gifts for a romantic moment collected in one set, the kind of props that sit on a table for an anniversary or Valentine's evening. The Romantic Gift Collection saves a stylist from hunting for boxes, flowers and keepsakes one by one. Ribbons, hearts and soft colors are the visual language of these props, and they read clearly even at a distance.

Every item was made to CheckMate specification, so scale and naming line up when pieces are arranged together. Consistent sizing means a gift box sits correctly beside a glass of wine or a bouquet. Materials can be retinted from red to gold, turning the set toward a birthday or wedding mood.

Released in 2025, the collection suits greeting card renders, social media visuals and restaurant table scenes. Jewelry and perfume brands often place their product among props like these to set a mood. Romantic scenes in games and animation use them to dress a dinner table or a bedroom quickly.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
