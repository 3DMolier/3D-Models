About This Model

A faceted vase and a single stem is a still life composition rather than two objects. The hexagonal body breaks the reflection into planes instead of a continuous curve, which is what makes glass or ceramic read as faceted, and the rose gives the arrangement a scale and a direction.

The mesh runs 22,048 polygons and 21,231 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 15 texture maps at a mix of sizes up to 4096x4096 - most of that budget going into the petals and the leaves.

At $39 and built to StemCell specification, it suits interior and decor visualisation, floristry and gift retail renders, still life and editorial imagery, hospitality set dressing and games. On sale since 2020.
