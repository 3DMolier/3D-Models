Aircraft Hangar is one of the Furniture & Interior models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. You are getting 642,575 polygons and 699,178 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
