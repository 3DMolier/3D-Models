About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Ford Transit Cargo 2020 Branded Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $149. You are getting 305,698 polygons and 174,527 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation and automotive advertising. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
