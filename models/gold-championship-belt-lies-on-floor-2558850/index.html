About This Model

Gold Championship Belt Lies on Floor is one of the Sports & Recreation models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 21,954 polygons and 23,210 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and game assets.
