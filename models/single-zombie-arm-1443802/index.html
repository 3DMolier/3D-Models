About This Model

Single Zombie Arm belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $49 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
