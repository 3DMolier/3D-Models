About This Model

A fresh apple shown with stainless steel slicers, the round corer tools that cut fruit into neat wedges with one press. The polished blades and the spoked frame of each slicer are what make the set useful for kitchen gadget scenes. Every item stands apart in the file, letting the apple stay whole while the slicer rests beside it or presses through.

CheckMate Lite certification confirms the files open cleanly with basic geometry and naming checks done. Stainless steel reads best with an anisotropic metal shader, and the apple skin benefits from soft subsurface scattering.

Offered since 2022 at $32, it suits recipe videos, healthy snack guides and kitchenware store displays. Lunchbox tutorials for kids often show exactly this moment, a quick slice turning an apple into easy wedges. Cut wedges fanned out on a plate make a cheerful closing shot for a cooking clip. Its simple setup also suits product mockups for new kitchen gadget designs.
