About This Model

Mobile Construction Crane with Concrete Bucket is a wheeled all-terrain crane carrying a pour bucket on its hook, ready for building site shots. The telescopic boom, outriggers and multi-axle carrier take most of the 329,997 polygons. Because the bucket already hangs from the line, the crane reads as mid-task rather than parked.

The 354,034 vertices sit a little higher than the face count, a profile common to vehicles with many mechanical joints. With no overlapping UV islands, concrete crust on the bucket and mud on tires can be painted uniquely. Naming follows the CheckMate spec, so the boom sections stay easy to select for extending or lowering.

Available since 2024, the crane suits construction planning visuals, infrastructure documentaries, heavy equipment training and building simulator games. It reads best against a half-built concrete frame, with the bucket swinging toward workers waiting at the formwork. Seen from far away, the long boom also adds a strong vertical line to any skyline.
