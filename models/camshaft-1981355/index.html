About This Model

An engine camshaft with a row of egg-shaped lobes along a polished steel shaft and bearing journals between them. The 26,856 polygons and 13,482 vertices keep each lobe profile smooth enough to read clearly in tight machining close-ups. Its long, rhythmic shape shows at a glance how valves are timed inside a motor.

Machined steel and oil sheen are set up in your shaders, because the shaft comes without maps. UVs are laid out cleanly, ready for wear marks on the lobes where followers press. CheckMate Pro certification confirms accurate scale for placement inside an engine block.

The camshaft has been available since 2022 for automotive training, engine animations and parts catalogs. Laid on a workbench beside timing gears and valve springs, it sets up a rebuild scene in a garage. Technical animations can spin it to show lobes pushing valves open in sequence as the crank turns.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2022 · Updated 16 November 2026
