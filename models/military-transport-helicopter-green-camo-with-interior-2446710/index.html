About This Model

The Military Transport Helicopter Green Camo with Interior is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is catalogued for aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
