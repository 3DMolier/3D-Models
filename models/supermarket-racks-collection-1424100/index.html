About This Model

A set of supermarket shelving units, ready to stock with products and line up into long store aisles. Retail interiors depend on repetition, and a matched family of racks keeps shelf heights and finishes consistent from aisle to aisle. Metal uprights and shelf edges catch the flat overhead light typical of store photography.

Exact polygon and texture figures are not listed, so measure the weight once before filling a large store. The racks carry CheckMate Pro certification, which vouches for clean geometry, tidy naming and believable real-world scale. Empty shelves look stark on camera, so plan to fill them with packaged goods or crates for a lived-in shop.

The collection costs $19, and 11 retail and game projects have bought it since 2019. It suits grocery store interiors, convenience shops, pharmacy layouts, warehouse clubs and shopping simulation games. One rack type repeated with varied stock is often enough to sell a full supermarket in the background.
