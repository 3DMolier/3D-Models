About This Model

This open bottle of Morning Fresh dishwashing liquid shows a squeeze bottle with its flip cap raised, ready to pour. Its 6,922 polygons and 6,903 vertices shape the curved grip waist, the dispensing nozzle and the hinged cap. The open cap suggests a moment of use, which makes kitchen sink scenes feel active and genuinely busy.

The bottle holds CheckMate Pro certification, so inspectors approved its geometry, naming and organization before release. Non-overlapping UVs leave room for labels, a translucent green liquid layer and the printed front area. A slightly transparent plastic shader shows the liquid inside, especially when backlit by a kitchen window at noon.

Uploaded in 2022, it suits kitchen interiors, cleaning tutorials and household product illustrations for retailers. Set beside a sponge and a stack of plates, it completes a dishwashing scene with little effort. Cleaning service websites and home organization guides also use bottles like this beside sinks and countertops.
