About This Model

A fixed lecture hall desk unit with a shared tabletop and two fold-down seats, built for tiered university classrooms. Its 4,844 polygons and 4,706 vertices cover the curved writing surface, the seat shells and the steel support frame. Units repeat side by side in real halls, and this one is designed to line up in long rows without gaps.

Under the CheckMate Pro grade, topology, naming and scale were all checked, so rows can be built without cleanup. Seats and desk are modeled as distinct parts, making it easy to recolor upholstery while keeping the wood top. A light count lets you fill an auditorium with dozens of units while keeping the viewport responsive.

Added in 2022 and usable without royalties, it serves campus architecture, school furniture catalogs and training simulations. Architects use rows of it to plan seat counts and sightlines in new lecture halls before construction begins. Film and game scenes set on campuses fill classrooms with it for exams, lectures and late study sessions.
