About This Model

Yamaha YZ125 2022 Dirt is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 367,238 polygons and 190,791 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.
