About This Model

A fashionable woman together with fitting room equipment, gathered as one collection for boutique and clothing store scenes. Mirrors, curtains and clothes racks frame the figure, turning a simple pose into the moment of trying something on. Her styled outfit and confident stance make her the focus while the equipment explains where she is.

Buying the figure and the fitting room pieces together keeps scale and style consistent across the scene. Every element follows CheckMate specification, with real-world size and clear object names throughout. Pieces are separate, so the mirror or rack can dress another store while the woman appears elsewhere.

Available since 2024, the set suits fashion retail visualization, boutique interior design, e-commerce imagery and lifestyle games. Warm spotlights above the mirror and a soft background blur give a premium boutique feel. Store planners use scenes like this to test fitting room layouts and lighting before a refit.
