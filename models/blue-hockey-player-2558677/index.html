About This Model

The Blue Hockey Player is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 61,172 polygons over 61,909 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. 23 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry — no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags visualization, advertising and game assets as the intended applications. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.
