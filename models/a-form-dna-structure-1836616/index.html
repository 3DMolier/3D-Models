About This Model

A Form DNA Structure is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. The mesh carries 37,176 polygons and 37,248 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos.
