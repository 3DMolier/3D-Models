About This Model

A white electric crossover fitted with radar and sensor pods, the look of a self-driving prototype out on public test routes. The AI-Driven Electric Crossover with Radars White uses 207,360 polygons and 227,936 vertices, including the roof sensor housings. Visible sensors on the roof and bumpers are what separate a test car from an ordinary family crossover.

At 206.35 x 457.87 x H203.93 cm, the body matches a mid-size crossover on the road. Non-overlapping UVs leave clean room for test fleet decals, sensor warnings and a client's own company logos. It is made to CheckMate specification, so wheels, sensors and body are separate named parts.

Released in 2025, it suits autonomous driving explainers, smart mobility presentations and tech conference keynote visuals. Driving through a city scene with scan lines overlaid, it shows what an autonomous car perceives. Since it carries no real brand, the car can represent any startup or carmaker in client work.
