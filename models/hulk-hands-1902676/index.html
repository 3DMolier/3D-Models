About This Model

Priced at $49, the Hulk Hands is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 3,950 polygons over 3,918 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.
