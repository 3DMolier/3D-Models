About This Model

A boxing collection with padded gloves and hanging punching bags, ready for gyms, fight clubs and sports training scenes. Boxing Gloves and Punching Bag Collection pairs laced and strapped gloves with heavy bags hung from chains. The bags show slight sagging at the base, the shape real leather takes after years of hard training.

The set meets CheckMate requirements for real scale and clear naming, so gloves fit character hands properly. Stitched seams, scuffed leather and bright colors help each piece read well under harsh gym lighting. Each glove and bag is a separate object, so bags can swing on their chains after a punch.

Released in 2026, the collection suits fitness apps, boxing games, sports documentaries and gym interior renders. A lone fighter working the heavy bag in a dim gym makes a powerful training montage frame. Sports retailers can show the gloves rotating on a clean background to highlight padding and closures.
