About This Model

Metal film reels, some loaded with spooled stock and some empty, recall the projection booths and editing rooms of classic cinema. The spokes and cutouts in each reel are modeled as openings, so light passes through them and casts patterned shadows. Stacked in a pile or lined along a shelf, the reels set the mood for any title sequence about the history of film.

Aluminum and dark film stock are the main materials, and a little scratching on the rims makes the reels look properly archived. The pieces follow TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, meaning tidy meshes, believable size and names you can find in the outliner. Film canisters with lids often travel with reels, so pairing the set with a few tins makes a convincing archive shelf.

Three versions of the reel bundle are listed, two at $44 and a third at $29, each with a different arrangement of reels. Added in 2026, they suit film festival graphics, cinema lobby decor and documentaries about how movies were once edited by hand. Streaming services and video schools also pick reels like these as a simple symbol of filmmaking in their intros.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2026 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

What is the Video Film Reels Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Video Film Reels Collection mesh?

21,696 polygons and 24,685 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the roughly 28,000 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs low at 21,696.

How clean is the geometry on the Video Film Reels Collection model?

The mesh uses organized, quad-dominant topology at real-world scale, with objects named and grouped rather than merged into one block.

Does the Video Film Reels Collection model include materials and textures?

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. Where textures are included they are packaged with the download rather than linked externally.
