About This Model

The Cartoon Woman is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. You are getting 12,678 polygons and 13,772 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 15 maps at 8000 x 8000 and 5000 x 5000. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
