About This Model

Priced at $39, the Cooled Incubator 340L is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 58,739 polygons and 61,107 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
