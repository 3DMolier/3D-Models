About This Model

Business Woman Caucasian is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. Geometry weighs in at 16,921 polygons over 17,513 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

16 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
