About This Model

A glowing holographic asteroid, this asset drops a sci-fi display into spaceship bridges, mission control rooms and space exploration games. The rocky body takes 18,420 polygons and 23,658 vertices, with craters and ridges that still read through the transparent glow. A hovering projection base underneath anchors the hologram so it feels like part of a real console.

TurboSquid certified the model as CheckMate Pro, confirming clean geometry, correct scale and a tidy scene setup. Six maps at 4096 x 4096 handle the rock detail, the emissive scan lines and the soft edge glow. With a non-overlapping layout, the glow color can shift from blue to green or red for different interface moods.

Since 2019 the hologram has suited science fiction films, planetarium shows and space mining game interfaces. Rotating slowly above a table while crew members point at it, the asteroid becomes the focus of a briefing scene. Educational videos about near-Earth objects also use it to show surface details in a futuristic way.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2019 · Updated 14 November 2026

Questions About This Model

What should I search for to find models like the Asteroid Hologram?

Useful search terms are hologram, projection and holographic. Browsing the Electronics & Gadgets category shows the closest alternatives at a range of prices.

What texture resolution ships with the Asteroid Hologram?

6 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Asteroid Hologram mesh?

18,420 polygons and 23,658 vertices, built as polygonal quads only. Compared with the roughly 28,000 polygon median of our Electronics & Gadgets catalog, this build runs low at 18,420.

Can the Asteroid Hologram model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.
