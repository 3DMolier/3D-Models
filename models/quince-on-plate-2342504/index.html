About This Model

This Food & Beverages model - Quince on Plate - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $28 on TurboSquid. You are getting 42,827 polygons and 43,052 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
