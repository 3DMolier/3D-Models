About This Model

A crumpled sheet of kitchen aluminum foil, useful for countertops, food preparation scenes and grill-side props. It uses only 1,516 polygons and 1,518 vertices, letting the shader do the work of the crinkled metallic surface. The sheet lies loosely with soft wrinkles and slightly lifted corners, as if just torn from a long roll.

StemCell certification means the reflective silver material looks the same across render engines and lighting setups. Foil reads mostly through highlights, so the gentle folds give enough variation to break up flat reflections. It can sit under baked food, wrap leftovers on a plate or lie flat on an oven tray.

At $15 since 2026, the Aluminum Foil Silver is one of the cheapest ways to finish a kitchen scene. Royalty Free terms allow games, food visualization, films and product shots alike. A few copies at different angles make a believable cookout, bakery or takeaway counter in minutes.
