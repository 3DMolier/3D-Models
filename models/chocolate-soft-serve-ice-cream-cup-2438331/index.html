About This Model

A swirl of chocolate soft serve piled high in a cup, with the spiraling ridges that a dispensing machine leaves on every portion. The twisted peak is the part that sells the dessert, so it deserves a slightly glossy material with soft subsurface scattering.

CheckMate compliance on this dessert means sensible topology, clear object names and correct import scale. The cup surface is a natural place for a cafe logo or a striped paper pattern added in texturing. Chocolate reads darker under cool light, so a warm key light keeps the dessert looking rich instead of muddy.

This 2025 release suits ice cream shop menus, summer advertising, dessert apps and food truck scenes. Add a plastic spoon pushed into the side and the cup looks ready to hand over the counter. A row of these cups on a counter tray makes a quick visual for a dessert bar or children's party. It also works as a playful prop in game cafes and city street scenes.
