About This Model

An African pregnant woman in A-pose offers a respectful, realistic figure for prenatal care, family planning and healthcare visuals. The 8,870 polygons shape the rounded belly, natural body proportions, hair and a gentle, calm facial expression. At 18,680 vertices, roughly double the faces, the mesh carries UV splits and separate shells for clothing.

Overlapping UVs allow mirrored body halves to share texture space efficiently, keeping the files light and fast. Made to CheckMate requirements, she has a believable height and clean naming for medical or family scenes. Soft, warm lighting suits the figure, whether in a clinic waiting room or a sunny home interior.

Four versions exist: this A-pose figure at $149 and three rigged editions at $179 each. The rigged editions cover Maya and Cinema 4D plus a general rig for other animation tools. Released in 2025, she serves maternity health education, telemedicine apps, documentary illustration and diverse family animation.
