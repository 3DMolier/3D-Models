About This Model

This pocket digital radio receiver has a small screen, a speaker grille, tuning buttons and a pull-out telescopic antenna. Its 39,899 polygons over 40,508 vertices define the rounded case, the tactile buttons and the fine holes of the grille. At 12.28 x 3.06 x H10.44 cm it is compact enough to hold in a single hand or tuck into a jacket.

Built under CheckMate specification, the receiver has non-overlapping UVs that keep buttons and display separate from the body. Matte black plastic with a glowing blue screen and silver knobs mirrors the look of budget travel radios.

Out since 2025, the radio fits kitchen counters, camping trips, emergency kits and retro bedroom scenes. Set on a windowsill with the antenna extended, the radio suggests someone listening to morning news over breakfast. Disaster preparedness guides often show such receivers as a lifeline when phone networks go down.
