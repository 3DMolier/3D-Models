About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Bamboo Matcha Tea Whisk is a finished, render-ready model at $24. Counted at the source, the model is 30,824 polygons and 30,608 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
