About This Model

Looking for a general asset? The Plastic Parallel Rolling Ruler is a finished, render-ready model at $15. You are getting 9,616 polygons and 9,531 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Real-world footprint is 30.67 x 6.71 x H2.22 cm, so it drops into a scene at correct scale without a rescaling pass. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
