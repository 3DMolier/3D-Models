About This Model

The Boxwood Square Bush is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 434,854 polygons over 934,415 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
