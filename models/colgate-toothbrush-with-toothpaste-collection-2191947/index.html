About This Model

Colgate Toothbrush with Toothpaste Collection groups toothbrushes and paste tubes for bathroom sinks, dental clinic counters and morning routine scenes. Several items in one bundle let a vanity shelf look stocked instead of holding one brush in a lonely cup.

The pieces passed CheckMate Pro review, so they import with real-world scale, clean naming and geometry ready for rendering. Bristle tufts, ribbed grips and crimped tube ends are modeled clearly enough to read in a close bathroom shot. A slightly tilted tube or a brush resting on the sink edge suggests products in daily use rather than fresh from a shelf.

This bundle arrived in our range in 2024 and fits oral hygiene explainers, dental office interiors and household lifestyle renders. Stand a brush in a glass by the mirror and lay a tube beside the faucet for an honest everyday detail. Hotel bathrooms and travel kits are other good settings, since the items stay small and light in any scene.
