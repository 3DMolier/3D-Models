About This Model

Baseball Player Generic belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 55,972 polygons and 57,265 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 23 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
