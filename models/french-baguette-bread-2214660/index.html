About This Model

Priced at $18, the French Baguette Bread is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 5,886 polygons and 5,886 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
