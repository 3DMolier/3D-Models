About This Model

This Food & Beverages model - Half Loaf of Bread - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

The mesh carries 2,588 polygons and 2,589 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
