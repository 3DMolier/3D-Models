About This Model

Priced at $49, the All Seasons Kids Wooden Dollhouse by Hape Furnished is a finished Toys & Games model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 56,926 polygons and 56,565 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
