About This Model

A full-body Captain America figure in a standing pose, shield at his side, ready for fan art and cinematic stills. The star-spangled suit, the winged helmet and the round shield make the hero recognizable at any distance. A neutral standing pose leaves room for dramatic lighting to set the tone of a scene.

Certified to CheckMate Pro, the character has accurate proportions and clean topology, so he lines up with other heroes in a group shot. Metallic paint on the shield reflects the environment, and a strong key light makes the star pop. Leather straps and fabric panels on the suit catch subtle highlights that keep the figure from looking plastic.

On sale since 2023, the figure serves fan art, comic-inspired animation, collector renders and superhero tribute videos. Stand him on a rooftop at sunset, and the shot reads as a classic comic cover moment. Cosplay designers also use figures like this to study proportions before building a suit of their own.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
