About This Model

The SUV Car Frame Structure with Chassis is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and game environments. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 416,401 polygons and 431,468 vertices, a mid-weight load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
