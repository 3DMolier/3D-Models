About This Model

Priced at $19, the Chess Clock is a finished Toys & Games model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.

At 3,268 polygons and 3,285 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 6 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background.
