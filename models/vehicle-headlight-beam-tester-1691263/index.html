About This Model

A beam tester is a tall box on a wheeled column that rolls up to a car's headlight and shows whether the beam points where regulations say it should. Every MOT bay and service workshop has one, and its shape is unmistakable: a mirror box with a lens on the front, a levelling sight on top and a heavy base on castors.

The mesh is 56,278 polygons and 58,175 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the detail in the column, the adjustment mechanism and the front lens housing.

A rigged build sits on this page at the same $59 for when the box has to be raised or rotated. Aimed at automotive workshop and service visualization, garage equipment marketing, technical and training material, and games. On sale since 2021.
