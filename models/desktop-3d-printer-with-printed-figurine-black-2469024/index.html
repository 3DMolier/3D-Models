About This Model

Showing a printer with something on the bed is the whole difference between a machine and a process. The figurine is the argument: this is what came out, this is roughly how long it took, and this is why the thing is on the desk at all.

The model holds 287,874 polygons and 291,007 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, in black, supplied with the printed figurine. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $59 it suits product and technology visualization, e-commerce and retail imagery, maker and education material, office and workshop scenes, and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
