About This Model

TTS Scarborough CG42 Coast Guard Rigged belongs to our Ships range and is offered at $189 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Counted at the source, the model is 1,762,439 polygons and 908,107 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
