About This Model

A collection of steel towers for a mountain gondola, with tall columns, crossarms and the wheel assemblies that guide the haul rope. Several tower designs in one package let a lift line climb a slope without every support looking cloned. Their rhythm of repeating masts up a hillside is what tells viewers a cable car route is nearby.

Every tower in the set earned the CheckMate Pro mark for clean topology, real scale and well-organized files. Supports can be placed at any spacing along a spline, and the cable itself can be drawn between them. Their bases sit flat, so they plant easily on rocky ground or concrete footings in any terrain.

The Ski Gondola Lift Towers 3D Models Collection costs $119 and first appeared on TurboSquid in 2019. With 12 sales behind it, it serves ski resort masterplans, mountain tourism films and winter sports games. Pair the towers with cabins and a snowy terrain, and a complete alpine transport line takes shape.
