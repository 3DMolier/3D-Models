About This Model

Priced at $59, the Golden Statue of Isis Ancient Egyptian Goddess is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 13,241 polygons and 13,223 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 4 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
