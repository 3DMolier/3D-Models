About This Model

A medical linear accelerator with its rotating gantry and patient couch, used for cancer treatment rooms and hospital visualizations. The Radiotherapy Linear Accelerator with Table carries 133,217 polygons across the gantry housing, collimator head and treatment table.

With 135,465 vertices, the curved shell of the machine keeps smooth contours under the bright, even lighting of a clinic. StemCell delivery bundles several formats prepared from a single source scene, so the clinical white materials hold up everywhere. The couch extends beneath the head of the machine, showing exactly how a patient is positioned during a session.

Priced at $89 and first offered in 2026, the system serves hospital architects, medical animation studios and patient education teams. Place it in a shielded bunker room with lasers on the walls to recreate a real treatment setting. The gantry arm frames the patient from above, a shape instantly familiar to oncology staff. Explaining a course of therapy to a nervous patient becomes easier when the room can be shown before the first visit.
