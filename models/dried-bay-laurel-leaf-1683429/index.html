About This Model

A single dried bay leaf with a curled edge and visible central vein, this is the herb cooks drop into soups and stews. With 1,562 polygons and 1,476 vertices, Dried Bay Laurel Leaf carries a gentle twist so it never looks like a flat paper cutout. Scatter a few on a cutting board and each one catches light differently along its wavy rim.

No textures are included, so the olive-green surface and faint brown spots come from your own leaf material. Its UVs do not overlap, which makes it simple to paint veins or a slight translucency mask for backlit shots. As a StemCell model, it is prepared to behave the same way across the leading 3D packages.

Two versions exist here: the lone leaf and the Dried Bay Laurel Leaf Sprig, a fuller branch priced at $19. First made available in 2021, it appears in recipe visualizations, spice packaging concepts and rustic kitchen still lifes. Drop two leaves beside a pot of simmering broth and the dish suddenly feels home cooked.
