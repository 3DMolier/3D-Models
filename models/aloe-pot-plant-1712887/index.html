About This Model

A potted aloe with thick, spiky leaves fanning out from a low rosette, the easy houseplant that fills a sunny windowsill. The plant uses 11,989 polygons and 11,646 vertices, with the serrated leaf edges and the rim of the pot taking most of them. The leaves curve outward at different angles, so the plant looks grown rather than copied.

It ships without texture maps, so the waxy green leaves and the pot glaze rely on shaders of your choice. An overlap-free unwrap allows a speckled leaf pattern to be painted in later. CheckMate Pro marks the file as inspected, clean and scaled to sit on a real table.

At $29 since 2021, the aloe has gone to 13 buyers furnishing apartments, offices and plant shop scenes. Spa interiors and wellness product shots use it too, since aloe reads instantly as healing and calm. Set on a kitchen counter in afternoon light, the plant brings a small, living touch to the room.
