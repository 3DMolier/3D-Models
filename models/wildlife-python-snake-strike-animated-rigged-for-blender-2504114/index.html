About This Model

A python coiled and lunging in a strike, animated and rigged in Blender for wildlife, jungle and horror scenes. The long body is built from smooth overlapping scale patterns that flow from the broad head down to the tapering tail. The strike animation captures the sudden uncoiling, which is the moment that makes snake footage memorable.

Because the rig is native to Blender, the strike plays immediately and can be retimed or blended with other motions. The snake meets the CheckMate checklist TurboSquid uses for naming, scale and orderly scene structure. Skin patterns are controlled in materials, so the python can shift between brown, olive or albino coloring.

First available in 2025 for $169, the python suits nature documentaries, reptile parks and educational channels about predators. Jungle games use the strike as an ambush, and horror films borrow it for sudden jump scares. Zoo signage and biology lessons also animate it to explain how constrictors catch and hold their prey.
