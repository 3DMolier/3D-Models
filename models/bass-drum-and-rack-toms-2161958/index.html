About This Model

This drum set piece combines a bass drum with the rack toms mounted above it, the core of any rock or jazz kit. Most of the 87,932 polygons go into tension rods, hoops, lugs and the hardware holding the toms aloft. Chrome fittings against a glossy lacquered shell catch stage lights on every single bolt.

With 45,151 vertices and a clean unwrap, the shells have room for a custom wrap finish or logo. Adding a snare, cymbals and a stool quickly builds a complete kit for a band scene. Kick drum heads offer a large round canvas where a band name or emblem can be painted.

Made to CheckMate specification, the drums arrived in 2023 with commercial rights included. Concert stage visualizations, music videos, rehearsal rooms and rhythm games are the obvious homes for the drums. Music schools and instrument shops can illustrate how a kit is arranged around the seated player.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2023 · Updated 14 November 2026
