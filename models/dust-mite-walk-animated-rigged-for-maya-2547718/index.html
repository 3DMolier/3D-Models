About This Model

A microscopic dust mite blown up to screen size, rigged for Maya and animated walking on its stubby legs. The rounded body and bristly legs match how the creature appears under an electron microscope. Scaled up, the mite becomes a strange character that works for science explainers and allergy campaigns.

CheckMate specification keeps the scene clean, so Maya opens the mesh and controls without leftover helpers. The walk cycle moves the legs in alternating sets, giving the creeping gait that makes viewers' skin crawl. Body ridges catch light under a rim lamp, which helps the creature stand out against fabric fibers.

Released in 2026 for $179, the mite fits medical animation, cleaning product explainers and biology lessons. Allergy and asthma education uses such models to show what lives in bedding and carpets. Macro worlds in games and short films gain an unsettling resident with this walk.
