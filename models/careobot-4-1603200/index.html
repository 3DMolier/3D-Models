About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Careobot 4 is a finished, render-ready model at $49. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 20,752 polygons and 21,914 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
