About This Model

An ENT diagnostic set gathers the instruments an ear, nose and throat doctor reaches for, with forceps and a tuning fork among them. The whole set measures 17.41 x 18.04 cm and stands 4.53 cm high, and 12,458 polygons cover it with 12,513 vertices.

Polished stainless steel is the hard part of a medical render, and the smooth shafts here give reflections a clean surface to travel along. The UV layout has no overlapping islands, and construction follows the CheckMate specification for quad-based topology and real-world scale. Each instrument is a separate piece, so a single tool can be lifted out and placed in a doctor's hand.

Two finishes are available at $34: plain stainless steel and a gold-plated version of the same diagnostic set. First offered in 2025, the set suits clinic interiors, medical training slides and close product shots of surgical equipment. On a tray beside an examination chair, the instruments tell the viewer at once which specialist works in the room.
