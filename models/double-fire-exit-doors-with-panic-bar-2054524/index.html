About This Model

Double fire exit doors with panic bars, this building element secures emergency exits in offices, schools and public spaces. The two leaves swing outward with horizontal push bars across each door at waist height. Pushing the bar releases the latch instantly, which is why these doors are required in public buildings.

These doors follow the CheckMate rules of TurboSquid, which keep an architectural asset sound and usable. Painted steel panels, an emergency sign above and metal hardware are the core materials for the set. Scuffs near the bars and slight paint chips give the doors the look of a busy exit.

Released in 2023, the doors suit commercial building renders, school corridors and safety training scenes. Placed at the end of a hallway with a green exit sign, they show a clear evacuation route. Shopping malls and hospitals in architectural renders need such exits for realism.
