About This Model

The Waterproof Outdoor Raincoat is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 7,868 polygons and 7,983 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
