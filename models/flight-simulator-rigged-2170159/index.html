About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Flight Simulator is a finished, render-ready model at $149. The mesh carries 295,825 polygons and 154,851 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
