About This Model

A set of flowering shrubs in different shapes and bloom colors, ready to line a garden path or soften a building facade. Each bush grows into a rounded crown of small leaves dotted with clusters of blossoms, the way trimmed ornamental hedges look in early summer. Having several varieties in one purchase keeps a planted border from looking copied and pasted.

TurboSquid awarded the collection a CheckMate Pro grade, which means the meshes open cleanly with tidy names and no missing files. Landscape artists can scatter the bushes with a distribution tool and vary scale slightly to break up repetition. The flower colors can be shifted in the shader to suit spring pinks, summer whites or a warmer palette.

First offered in 2019, the bushes fill architectural visualization yards, park designs and residential street scenes in games. Planted at the foot of a wall or around a fountain, they add color without demanding attention from the main subject. Real estate renders benefit most, since a blooming front yard makes a new house feel inhabited.
