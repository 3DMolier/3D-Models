About This Model

A generic high-speed passenger train with a long tapered nose, smooth sides and a chain of linked carriages. It is an extremely dense build, with 7,746,725 polygons covering bogies, pantographs, door seams and window frames. The 7,908,428 vertices make it best suited to cinematic close-ups, while distant shots can use a lighter copy.

Some 24 maps at 4096 x 4096 carry the livery stripes, metal panels, glass and the darker undercarriage. Because the livery is unbranded, it can be repainted for any fictional rail operator using the overlap-free UVs. CheckMate Pro certification confirms that a model this large is still organized and clean inside.

There are 7 versions: this generic train, a second Speed Train Generic 2, and the Rail Star and JR700 bullet trains. The generic, Rail Star and JR700 trains also come rigged at $169, while static builds cost $149. Since 2016 it has carried 26 buyers' projects, from railway documentaries to station visualizations and chase scenes in films.
