About This Model

Large Star Wars Bowcaster is one of the Space & Sci-Fi models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $12. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 20,024 polygons and 20,013 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
