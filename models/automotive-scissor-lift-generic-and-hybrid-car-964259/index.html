About This Model

A low-profile scissor lift with a generic hybrid car raised on its pads, the scene a garage or dealership service bay needs. Of the 184,649 polygons, a good share sits in the crossed arms, the hydraulic ram and the rubber lifting blocks under the sills. The car itself carries no badge, so it reads as a plausible compact hybrid without inviting a question about whose design it is.

The lift and car share 30 texture maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. They cover the painted steel, the tire rubber, the glass and the body paint. The unwrap is non-overlapping across 199,048 vertices, which makes adding grease stains or a shop logo to the lift a texture job. CheckMate Lite certification covers the basics of clean geometry and a correct, predictable file setup.

Released in 2015, the set still does steady work in service center visualization, mechanic training material and automotive retail interiors. Because the car is unbranded and the license is royalty-free, both pieces can go into commercial renders without a clearance step.
