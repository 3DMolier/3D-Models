About This Model

Looking for a Musical Instruments asset? The Grand Piano with Bench is a finished, render-ready model at $99. The mesh carries 698,213 polygons and 720,398 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 22 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for event visualization, film & TV VFX and architectural visualization. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits music-video and film sets, game props, product rendering and educational material.
