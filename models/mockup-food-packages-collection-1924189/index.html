About This Model

Blank food packages in several shapes form this collection, ready for custom label designs. The Mockup Food Packages Collection lets a designer preview how a printed label wraps around real package shapes before production. Folded edges, seams and lids give each pack enough physical detail to look convincing when they sit on a store shelf.

TurboSquid inspected the bundle to its CheckMate Lite standard, confirming the basic structure and scale are correct. Each package is a separate object, so arranging a lineup of products for a presentation takes little effort. Plain surfaces make it easy to apply artwork, nutrition panels or barcodes in any image editor.

We released this packaging bundle in 2022, and it costs $39 for the whole set. It suits packaging design studios, student portfolios, supermarket shelf renders and food startup pitch decks. Lit softly on a neutral backdrop, the packs look like a real photo shoot of new grocery goods.
