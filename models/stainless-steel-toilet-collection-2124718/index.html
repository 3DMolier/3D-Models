About This Model

A collection of stainless steel toilets, the seamless metal fixtures used in prisons, public restrooms and industrial facilities. Brushed steel surfaces resist vandalism in real life, and in a render they instantly signal a tough, institutional, no-nonsense setting. Different bowl and mounting styles in the pack let you furnish cells, stations and factory washrooms.

The pack passes TurboSquid's CheckMate Lite check, confirming the files open correctly and geometry is sound. Smooth metal forms work well with anisotropic shaders, which bring out the brushed grain. Placed in a row behind partition walls, the units give a bus terminal restroom a believable layout.

Released in 2023, the Stainless Steel Toilet Collection suits prison dramas, transit station interiors and architectural plumbing specifications. Light a cell with a single harsh lamp overhead, and the toilet's cold metallic sheen sets the mood. Facility designers also use such fixtures in proposals for parks, stadiums and other high-traffic public washrooms.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
