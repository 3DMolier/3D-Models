About This Model

Ripe mangoes cut in classic hedgehog cubes, halves and slices, showing bright orange flesh against red and green skin. The cubed halves bend outward as if pressed from behind, a detail that instantly reads as freshly prepared tropical fruit. Separate pieces let you build a fruit platter, top a dessert or show the stone removed beside a halved fruit.

Each piece keeps real proportions, so the slices sit naturally on plates, cutting boards and smoothie bowls from other scenes. A CheckMate Lite grade from TurboSquid confirms the files open properly with correct scale in the delivered formats. Juicy surfaces respond well to a little subsurface scattering, which brings out the glow of ripe flesh under strong light.

Since its 2023 debut at $39, the group has fit smoothie bars, tropical menus and healthy breakfast visuals. Food stylists, juice bar designers and nutrition apps use cut fruit like this to suggest sweetness and summer heat. Arrange the cubes beside a coconut and a few lime wedges, and the plate looks ready for a beach café.
