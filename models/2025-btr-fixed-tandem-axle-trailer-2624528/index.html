About This Model

An open utility trailer on two fixed axles, ready to hitch behind a pickup in farm, construction or road-trip scenes. The 2025 BTR Fixed Tandem Axle Trailer puts 288,840 polygons and 314,378 vertices into the frame rails, drawbar, fenders and paired wheels. Suspension parts and safety chains are modeled rather than painted on, so low angles behind the trailer still hold up.

StemCell certification means the asset ships in the TurboSquid multi-format standard with materials that carry over between renderers. The flat open deck is the useful part: load it with lumber, crates or a garden tractor and it becomes a story prop. Fenders over the wheels and the triangular drawbar give it a believable silhouette in any side view.

It came out in 2026 and costs $69, an easy price for a towable vehicle with this much mechanical detail. The Royalty Free license covers games, films, product visualization and architectural renders of yards and driveways. Transport simulators and rural environments suit the trailer best, usually coupled behind a pickup truck or a compact tractor.
