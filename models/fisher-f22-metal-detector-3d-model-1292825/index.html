About This Model

This Tools model - Fisher F22 Metal Detector - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $39 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
