About This Model

A sleeveless basketball jersey with matching loose shorts, the classic court uniform with wide armholes and a long cut. The garment is extremely light at 1,262 polygons and 1,370 vertices, so it suits mobile games and large crowds of players.

With so few faces, the outfit relies on clean shapes rather than modeled wrinkles, which keeps it easy to fit onto different bodies. CheckMate Pro certification confirms neat topology for further sculpting or cloth simulation. Non-overlapping UVs make it simple to apply team colors, numbers and sponsor patches to the front and back.

On the market since 2021, the uniform is useful for sports games, arena crowd scenes and team kit previews. Designers repaint it to show several clubs on one court without modeling a new set for each side. It also works for background fans in streetball scenes, where a jersey silhouette reads instantly as basketball.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2021 · Updated 14 November 2026
