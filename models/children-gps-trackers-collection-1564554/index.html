About This Model

This collection brings together several compact GPS trackers designed for children, the small devices parents attach to a backpack or a wrist. The bodies are rounded and friendly, with bright shells and simple buttons that suit a young owner's small hands. Each device is modeled as its own object, so one tracker can be pulled out for a close product view.

The geometry mixes quads and triangles, kept light enough to place on a character's backpack in animation. Non-overlapping UVs leave space for logos, screen graphics or the scuffs that come from a playground fall. The bundle carries CheckMate Pro, which TurboSquid grants after a manual look at topology, real size and naming.

On offer since 2020, the trackers fit wearable tech presentations, family safety explainers and school-day scenes in animation. Clip one to a schoolbag in a render, and the story of a parent keeping watch is told without words. Product designers also use such sets to compare housing shapes and colors before a physical sample is made.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2020 · Updated 13 November 2026
