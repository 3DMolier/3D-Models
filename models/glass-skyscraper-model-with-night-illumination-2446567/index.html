About This Model

A glass skyscraper lit for night, with glowing floors and facade lighting ready for evening city skylines. Curtain wall glazing wraps the whole tower, and lit interiors behind it give the building depth after dark. Illuminated bands at the crown mark the silhouette against a dark sky, the way real towers announce themselves.

Proportions follow CheckMate specification, so the tower lines up with streets and neighboring blocks. Emissive window materials do the lighting work, so the building glows without placing individual lamps. Reflections on the glass pick up surrounding lights, which ties the tower into any night environment.

Added in 2025 at $179, the skyscraper suits real estate presentations, city flyovers and night scenes in games. Developers use lit towers like this to show a project's presence on the skyline at dusk. Event visualization also benefits, since a glowing landmark sets the tone for a product launch or city festival.
