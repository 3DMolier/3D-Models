About This Model

A swirl of chocolate whipped cream modeled as a printable piece, suited to cake toppers, molds and dessert mockups. The spiral peaks carry 6,210 polygons over 6,210 vertices, so each fold of the piped rosette stays readable. Its flat base sits flush on a cupcake or a print bed without needing extra supports.

Built to CheckMate specification, the mesh keeps clean edges and real-world proportions instead of a rough scan surface. UVs are non-overlapping, so the same file can also wear a glossy cocoa material for rendering. Ridges between the star-tip grooves are deep enough to survive slicing at common layer heights.

The model came out in 2024 and is aimed at bakers, prop makers and food photographers building fake desserts. Printed in resin it becomes a reusable decoration for window displays where real cream would melt. A few copies on a tray of muffins turn a plain bake into a styled shot.
