About This Model

Mosquito forceps are the smallest clamp in the tray and the most used. They close a bleeding vessel a millimetre across, they lock so the surgeon can let go, and almost every operation involves several of them.

The model is supplied in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and is built to CheckMate specification.

At $19 it suits surgical and medical visualization, instrument marketing, clinical education, technical documentation, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
