About This Model

Looking for a Nature & Plants asset? The Blooming Strawberry Bush is a finished, render-ready model at $39. The mesh carries 50,466 polygons and 54,395 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 8 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
