About This Model

A manpack radio is a compromise between range and what a person can carry up a hill. With a satellite antenna attached it stops depending on line of sight altogether, which is the difference between talking to the next valley and talking to another continent.

The model holds 75,133 polygons and 76,124 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout, supplied with the SATCOM antenna, and carries StemCell certification.

At $59 it suits defence and communications visualization, equipment marketing, training material, character accessories, film props, game assets and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
