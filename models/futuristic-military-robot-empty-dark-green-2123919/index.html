About This Model

An empty machine is a specific kind of unsettling. A military robot with no pilot and no operator is either waiting or abandoned, and the viewer has to decide which, which is why the pose matters more here than any weapon fitted to it.

The model is listed at 747 polygons over 388,240 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, finished in dark green, and carries StemCell certification.
