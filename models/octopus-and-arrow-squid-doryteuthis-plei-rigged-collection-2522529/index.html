About This Model

A rigged pair of cephalopods, a common octopus and the arrow squid Doryteuthis plei, for underwater scenes that need soft-bodied life. The octopus brings a bulbous mantle and curling arms, while the squid adds a sleek torpedo body with side fins. Both creatures are rigged, so tentacles can coil, reach and trail in animation instead of staying stiff.

Grouping the two in one set keeps scale and naming consistent, which helps when they share a reef. CheckMate guidance shapes the geometry, so the animals import at believable sizes beside divers and coral. Suckers along the arms and the large eyes are the details a close camera will look for first.

First offered in 2026, the pair goes into marine biology lessons, aquarium displays, ocean documentaries and underwater games. A squid jetting away from an octopus in blue water makes an easy, dynamic opening shot. Seafood and fisheries explainers also use the squid to show how the species is identified.
