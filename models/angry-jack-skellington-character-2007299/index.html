About This Model

Jack Skellington is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. The mesh carries 17,561 polygons and 17,728 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
