About This Model

A Stars and Stripes banner caught mid-ripple on its pole, this model gives a patriotic accent to plazas, government buildings and parade scenes. Most of the 42,644 polygons go into the cloth, where soft folds roll across the stripes and catch light along every crest. The pole stays slim and plain, so the fabric remains the clear focal point of the shot.

Its 21,728 vertices sit on a mixed mesh of quads and triangles, with UVs laid out flat and without overlaps. That clean unwrap lets the star field and stripes be painted precisely, or swapped for a weathered, sun-faded flag variant. CheckMate Pro certification confirms the file opens tidy, with named objects and no stray geometry.

Because the folds are modeled rather than faked with a texture, the silhouette stays convincing even against a bright open sky. Out since 2022, the American Flag Waving Animated suits city squares, embassy exteriors, Fourth of July renders and stadium approaches. Plant it on a rooftop or at a memorial entrance and the scene immediately signals an American location.
