About This Model

Rectangular HEPA Air Filter Panel belongs to our Architecture & Landmarks range and is offered at $15 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 101,590 polygons and 114,313 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 32.2 x 40.42 x H0.73 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
