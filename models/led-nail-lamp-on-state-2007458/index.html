About This Model

Lamp State belongs to our Clothing & Accessories range and is offered at $24 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 12,766 polygons and 6,788 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and architectural visualization.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
