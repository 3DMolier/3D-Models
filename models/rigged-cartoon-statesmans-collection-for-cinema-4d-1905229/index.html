About This Model

A cast of cartoon politicians in suits, with exaggerated faces and proportions, rigged for speeches, handshakes and debates. Each figure is separate, so a summit table, a press conference or a parliament scene can be staged quickly. They suit satire animations, explainer videos about government, editorial cartoons and educational shorts about politics.

The cartoon cast was made to the CheckMate specification of TurboSquid, which checks clean geometry, scale and naming. Suits and ties are separate materials, so national colors or party styles can be applied in a few clicks. Simple flat lighting suits the cartoon style, keeping faces readable and the comedy of each pose clear.

There are three versions at $199 each, giving the same statesmen with rigs native to Cinema 4D, Maya or Modo. That choice lets animators start working immediately with controllers designed for their package. The cast dates from 2022 and suits political satire, social studies lessons, news explainers and comedy animation channels.
