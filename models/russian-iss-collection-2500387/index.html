About This Model

Russian modules of the International Space Station are gathered in this collection for orbital and spaceflight scenes. Cylindrical modules with solar panel wings, docking ports and antenna arrays recreate the Russian segment's distinctive outline. Seen against the curve of Earth, the hardware shows how the station grew by joining pieces launched over many years.

The set is constructed to CheckMate specification, so each module stays separate for assembly and docking sequences. Thermal blankets, radiator panels and handrails catch the hard orbital sunlight and create crisp, high contrast shadows. Docking moments between the modules give animators a natural focal point for a slow, suspenseful sequence.

Since 2025 the collection has been royalty free for space documentaries, science lessons and spaceflight simulators. Educators explaining international cooperation in orbit can highlight which modules came from Russian launches. A slow orbit around the modules, with sunrise flaring across the solar panels, creates a memorable closing shot.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
