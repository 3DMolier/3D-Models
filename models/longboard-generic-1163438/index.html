About This Model

Looking for a Sports & Recreation asset? The Longboard Generic is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 20,114 polygons and 20,744 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 20 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 4000 x 4000. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
