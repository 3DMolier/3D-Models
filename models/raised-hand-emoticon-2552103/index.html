About This Model

Raised Hand Emoticon is a detailed general asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 565 polygons and 565 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 1024 x 1024 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
