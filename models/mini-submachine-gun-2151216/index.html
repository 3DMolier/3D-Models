About This Model

Priced at $44, the Mini Submachine Gun is a finished Weapons model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 52,436 polygons and 56,026 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
