About This Model

Small floor machines exist for the spaces the big ones cannot reach. Around tables, behind doors, in a corridor that turns twice: the compact size is not a compromise, it is the entire specification.

The model is light at 24,675 polygons and 27,528 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so it drops into any interior. It is built to CheckMate specification.
