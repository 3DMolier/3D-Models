About This Model

Water Strider is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. You are getting 7,535 polygons and 7,543 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
