About This Model

A collection of carved wooden tiki totems with grimacing faces, wide eyes and stacked figures inspired by Polynesian carving traditions. Each totem has its own expression and proportions, so a group placed together looks handmade rather than copied. Deep cuts around the mouths and brows catch shadow, which keeps the faces readable even from a distance.

The set was made to CheckMate rules, with scale and naming consistent across every carved piece. Weathered hardwood and sun-faded paint are the natural finishes for these figures, and each can take a different tone. Because the totems are separate objects, you can scatter them around a bar or line them along a garden path.

This collection was published in 2025 and suits tropical resort renders, tiki bar interiors and island adventure games. Lit by torches at dusk, the figures cast long shadows that add mystery to a beach party or jungle scene. Theme park designers and set builders use totems like these to signal a Pacific island setting at a glance.
