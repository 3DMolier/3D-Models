About This Model

The iPad Air Cellular Silver is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 15,602 polygons and 16,461 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

6 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
