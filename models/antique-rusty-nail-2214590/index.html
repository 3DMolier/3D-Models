About This Model

The Antique Rusty Nail is a production-ready Other 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 557 polygons over 559 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
