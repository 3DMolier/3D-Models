About This Model

A wah pedal is a foot-operated treadle on a cast metal chassis, and its whole shape comes from that: a hinged top plate at an angle, a rubber tread on top, a heavy base to stop it sliding under pressure and jacks on both sides. Modelled flat it looks like a doorstop; modelled with the treadle angle it reads as an instrument.

It is built in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so the rubber tread, the cast finish and any control lettering land cleanly on their own surfaces.

At $15 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits music gear and instrument retail renders, recording studio and stage interiors, music advertising, band and performance visualization, and games. On sale since 2025.
