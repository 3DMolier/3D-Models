About This Model

The Wilga looks wrong until you know what it is for. Enormously long undercarriage legs, a wing that generates lift at walking pace, and a fuselage shaped for glider towing and parachute drops from a grass strip. Every awkward proportion on it has a job.

The airframe holds 240,195 polygons and 249,621 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in white, and carries StemCell certification. A yellow scheme is offered separately.

At $129 it suits general aviation visualization, flight simulation, aviation history and documentary work, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2024.
