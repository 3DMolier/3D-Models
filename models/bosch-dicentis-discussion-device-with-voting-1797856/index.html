About This Model

The Bosch Dicentis discussion device is a conference desk unit combining a gooseneck microphone, speaker and voting buttons. Units like this sit before every seat in parliaments, council chambers, courtrooms and large international meetings. A lit ring on the microphone tip, glowing while a delegate speaks, is what viewers recognize first.

Under CheckMate Pro review, the slim base, the bendable stem and the button panel proved cleanly built. A compact footprint means a whole chamber can be equipped without overwhelming the scene with heavy geometry.

First released in 2021, the unit carries an Editorial Uses Only license, suiting news and teaching content well. News graphics about votes, explainers on how assemblies work and documentaries on diplomacy are where it belongs. Placed seat after seat along curved desks, these units turn an empty hall into a working parliament. Architects planning conference rooms can show clients how seating, desks and equipment fit together.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
