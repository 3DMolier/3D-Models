About This Model

The Double Drawer Dishwasher is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $31 on TurboSquid. You are getting 363,818 polygons and 362,725 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
