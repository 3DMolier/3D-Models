About This Model

A complete tarot kit with the classic card deck, its storage box and a guide book for readers learning the spreads. The Rider Waite Tarot Set with Box and Book is modest at 12,996 polygons, keeping the cards thin and the box edges crisp.

There are 13,182 vertices, enough to give the book cover a slight thickness and the box a fitted lid. Built to CheckMate standards, the set arrives with clean geometry and consistent scale across all three pieces. The cards can be spread across a table or kept stacked, depending on whether a reading is underway.

For $39, the 2026 kit suits mystical interiors, gift shop shelves and spiritual lifestyle scenes. Place it on a nightstand or beside crystals and incense to suggest a personal practice. Online stores selling tarot accessories can also show the kit in styled room settings. Compared with a lone deck, the box and booklet tell viewers this is a starter set.
