About This Model

This mobile missile carrier puts a long transport tube on a heavy multi-axle chassis, ready for military documentaries, strategy games and defense explainers. Its 603,989 polygons and 635,303 vertices go mostly into the wheel hubs, the cab louvers and the hydraulic cradle that holds the tube. The erector arm, the outrigger jacks and the rear hatch stay readable even in a wide aerial shot of a moving convoy.

18 texture maps at 4096 x 4096 cover the camouflage paint, the rubber sidewalls and the grimy steel of the deck. Because no UV islands overlap, stencils, unit markings or extra mud can be layered in without fighting shared texture space. As a StemCell product it opens in several native formats whose PBR materials keep their look from one renderer to the next.

Eight versions are offered here: the generic truck, dusty finishes, Dongfeng-41 markings and several rigged builds meant for animation work. The family dates from 2020 and turns up in war films, simulation trainers and documentaries about nuclear deterrence policy. Pick the clean generic edition for a parade sequence and a dusty one when the vehicle should look fresh from a desert exercise.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2020 · Updated 13 November 2026
