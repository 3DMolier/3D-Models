About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The AgustaWestland AW101 Merlin Royal Air Force with Cargo Bag is a finished, render-ready model at $219. You are getting 265,057 polygons and 292,416 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
