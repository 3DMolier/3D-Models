About This Model

This golden necklace display bust gives jewelry a neck and shoulders to hang on, the way boutique counters present chains and pendants. Its smooth, stylized torso has no face or arms, which keeps attention on whatever piece is draped across it. The golden finish adds warmth beside glass cases and soft spotlights in a jewelry store.

Built against the CheckMate specification, the bust arrives with sensible pivots, real-world scale and clean object names. Its broad, gently curved chest gives a necklace room to fall naturally without clipping. Several busts at different heights create a staggered display that guides the eye along a counter.

Two versions share the same name and the $29 price, so either works as a starting point for a boutique layout. A velvet or matte black material swap turns the same bust into a more dramatic presentation piece. Since 2024 it has dressed jewelry store interiors, e-commerce product shots, fashion window displays and luxury concept renders.
