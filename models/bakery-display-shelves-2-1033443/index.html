About This Model

A wall of bakery display shelves stocked with breads, rolls and pastries, angled forward so customers can see each loaf. The unit is large at 181,318 polygons and 183,168 vertices, because every loaf and roll is modeled on its own. Place it behind a counter in a bakery or along the fresh bread aisle of a supermarket.

Six maps at 2048 x 2048 cover the baked crusts, the wooden shelves and the metal frame. The shelves carry CheckMate Pro status, granted after a check of geometry, scale and object grouping. Rows of golden bread create a warm, inviting background that fills a shop interior without extra props.

Two variants can be chosen: Bakery Display Shelves 2 and a Bakery Display Shelves 4 edition with a different layout. Combining them lines a whole bakery wall without repeating the same shelf twice. Its first year in the collection was 2016, and it has served bakery interiors, retail design and food service renders.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2016 · Updated 14 November 2026
