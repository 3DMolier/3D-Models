About This Model

This Industrial Equipment model - Epiroc Mobile Miner - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
