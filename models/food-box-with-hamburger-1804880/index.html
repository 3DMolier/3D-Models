About This Model

Food Box with Hamburger shows a takeaway carton opened to reveal a burger with a sesame bun, patty, cheese and fresh vegetables. Its 71,862 polygons and 72,731 vertices go into the bun seeds, the melted cheese edge and the folded cardboard flaps.

A pass through CheckMate Pro review means the box and burger arrive as clean separate objects at real scale. Every surface has its own UV space without overlaps, ready for a custom print on the box and juicy food textures. Lettuce leaves peeking from under the bun add the irregular detail that makes fast food look appetizing up close.

This meal has been in our catalog since 2021 for menu boards, food delivery app visuals and restaurant interior scenes. Set it on a car dashboard or a park bench for a casual lunch moment in a story. Close the lid in animation and the same box doubles as packaging for a delivery courier.
