About This Model

Priced at $139, the Shipwreck is a finished Ships & Boats model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 69,893 polygons over 77,224 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 12 maps at 8192 x 8192. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
