About This Model

Colorado potato beetles rigged for Cinema 4D, the striped yellow and black pests that strip potato fields leaf by leaf. Rigging lets the legs and antennae move, so the beetles can crawl across a plant instead of sitting as static ornaments. Several beetles in one bundle make an infestation scene look convincing without endless duplication.

True insect scale follows the CheckMate approach to building, so the beetles stay tiny next to leaves, hands and garden tools. The domed shell with its bold stripes catches light well and reads clearly even in small close-up frames. Separate wing cases and legs give animators enough parts to stage a short flight or a slow walk.

Released in 2026 and priced at $89, the collection suits agricultural education, crop protection product visuals, gardening explainers and nature documentaries. Let a few beetles march along a potato leaf in bright midday light, and farmers will recognize the threat at once.
