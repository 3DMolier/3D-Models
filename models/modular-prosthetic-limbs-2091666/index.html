About This Model

Modular Prosthetic Limbs belongs to our Medical range and is offered at $89 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualisation and medical sequences in film.

Geometry weighs in at 599,669 polygons over 313,096 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
