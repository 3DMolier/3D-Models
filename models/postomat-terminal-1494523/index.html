About This Model

Postomat Terminal belongs to our Architecture & Landmarks range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 5,166 polygons and 5,619 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

7 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
