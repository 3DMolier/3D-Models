About This Model

The Cartoon Earth with Clouds Fur is a production-ready Space & Sci-Fi 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. You are getting 29,704 polygons and 30,082 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
