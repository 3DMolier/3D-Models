About This Model

Sandstone Arches Collection provides several natural rock arches shaped by wind and water, like those found in desert canyons. Each arch shows layered strata, rough erosion and the smooth curve of rock spanning an open sky.

Construction respects the CheckMate specification, so the rocks arrive with sensible scale and clean names. Red and orange banding runs across the stone, which makes the arches read well under warm low sunlight. Bases widen into rubble and boulders, so the formations sit naturally on a sandy ground plane.

New in 2026, the collection suits desert landscapes, travel films and open world games set in the American Southwest. A road trip scene gains drama when a car drives past an arch at golden hour. Geology teachers use these formations to explain erosion and how arches form over thousands of years. Several arches spaced across a valley create a convincing national park panorama.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026
