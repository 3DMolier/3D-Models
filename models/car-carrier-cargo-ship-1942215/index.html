About This Model

This Ships & Boats model - Carrier Cargo Ship - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $199 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 173,531 polygons and 204,017 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
