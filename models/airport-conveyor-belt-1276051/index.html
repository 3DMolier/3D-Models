About This Model

A baggage claim conveyor with sloped metal plates and a steel skirt, the loop travelers watch in every arrivals hall. The 25,480 polygons and 26,348 vertices define the overlapping slat plates, the side guards and the curved end sections. That modest count lets several belts line up in a large terminal without strain.

Materials draw on 11 maps at 4096 x 4096, showing rubber, brushed steel and scuffs from countless suitcases. Being CheckMate Pro certified, the geometry is clean and real-scale, ready for a terminal floor plan. The plates repeat evenly, which makes a looping movement animation simple to set up.

The Airport Conveyor Belt and the Baggage Carousel make up the two versions, $39 apiece. Since 2018 it has furnished airport interiors, travel films, transport games and terminal design studies. Add a few suitcases on top and the arrivals scene reads at a glance.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2018 · Updated 14 November 2026
