About This Model

A domestic embroidery machine is a small industrial robot that happens to sit on a table. The hoop moves in two axes under a needle that stays put, which is the same principle as a plotter, and the screen on the front is where the design is actually chosen.

The model is light at 40,108 polygons and 21,076 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the screen on, and comes rigged so the hoop and arm can be posed or animated. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and native rigged builds for Maya and Cinema 4D are offered separately.

At $69 it suits product and craft equipment visualisation, e-commerce and retail imagery, advertising, workshop and interior scenes, and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
