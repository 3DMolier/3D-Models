About This Model

These hearing aids are a pair of small in-ear devices in a rose gold finish, shaped to sit discreetly inside the ear canal. Each unit has a rounded shell with a slim grip so the wearer can pull it out easily, and a tiny vent for airflow. Their soft metallic tone was chosen to look like jewelry rather than a medical device, which shows well in close shots.

The model holds a CheckMate Pro grade, signaling clean naming, sensible pivots and true scale for such a small object. Rose gold responds beautifully to a warm studio light, so a simple metallic shader gives the shells a premium glow. Because the devices are tiny, a macro lens and shallow focus help them fill the frame.

Released in 2023, the Signia Active Pro are Hearing Aids Rose Gold fit audiology clinic renders, health articles and accessibility visuals. Place them in an open charging case, on a bathroom shelf, or beside glasses on a bedside table. Hearing care educators like the pair for showing how modern models hide almost completely inside the ear.
