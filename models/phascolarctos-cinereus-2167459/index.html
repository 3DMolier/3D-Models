About This Model

Phascolarctos Cinereus is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $89. Counted at the source, the model is 21,084 polygons and 11,001 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
