About This Model

The Shield Pro is a small black box designed to disappear into a media cabinet, and that gives it almost no styling to hang a model on: a flat slab with a subtle logo, ports along the back and rubber feet underneath. When an object is this plain, the proportion and the port layout are all a viewer has to recognise it by, and both are exact here.

The mesh is 35,860 polygons and 19,476 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry concentrated in the connector recesses and the chamfered edges.

At $29 it suits home entertainment and interior visualisation, consumer electronics advertising, retail and e-commerce renders, product presentation and games. On sale since 2022, kept to real-world scale so it sits correctly on a shelf beside other equipment.
