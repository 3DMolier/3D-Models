About This Model

Cartoon Business Suit is a detailed Clothing & Accessories asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $89. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Geometry weighs in at 3,223 polygons over 3,450 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
