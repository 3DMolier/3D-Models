About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Chevrolet Silverado EV RST White 2024 is a finished, render-ready model at $129. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

Geometry weighs in at 250,720 polygons over 276,136 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
