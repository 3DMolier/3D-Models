About This Model

An argon gas regulator with twin gauges, a flow knob and a hose, the setup used on welding cylinders in workshops. Its 14,660 polygons shape the gauge faces, the brass body, the adjusting knob and the flexible hose with its fittings. The two round dials are the visual anchor, showing tank pressure and outlet flow side by side.

There are 14,975 vertices, a bit above the faces, as expected for threaded fittings and gauge rims. Three textures at 4096 x 4096 add the gauge markings, brass finish and rubber hose surface. CheckMate Pro certification makes the assembly clean and easy to attach to cylinder models.

In the range from 2019 at $39, it suits welding workshops, fabrication shops and industrial safety training scenes. Screwed onto a gray argon cylinder beside a TIG welder, it completes a believable welding station. It also suits technical explainers about shielding gas and how pressure regulators work in practice.
