About This Model

An exterior door with built-in blinds sealed between glass panes, the entrance style used for patios, kitchens and back gardens. The Exterior Door with Blinds uses 11,983 polygons and 12,274 vertices, shaping the frame, the handle and the slatted blinds. Light filtering through the slats adds striped shadows and realism to the rooms behind it.

White painted frames or dark aluminum both suit the door, with clear glass and fine blind slats in matching colors. Weatherstripping, scratches or a door number can go onto separate UV islands that never overlap. Constructed to TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, the door keeps accurate real-world size for architectural plans.

Suburban homes, apartment patios and architectural visualizations have used this door since 2025. Open it onto a deck with garden furniture, or close the blinds for privacy in a bedroom. Builders also present doors like this when showing homeowners energy efficient upgrade options.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 19 November 2026
