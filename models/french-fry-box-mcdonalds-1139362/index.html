About This Model

The French Fry Box McDonalds is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 72,178 polygons and 75,622 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
