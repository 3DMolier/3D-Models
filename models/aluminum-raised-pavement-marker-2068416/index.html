About This Model

A road stud is a small aluminium housing with reflective lenses set into sloping faces, bonded to the road surface. The slope is what matters: it presents the lens to oncoming headlights at the right angle and lets a tyre ride over the stud without tearing it out. Modelled as a flat disc it loses both properties.

The mesh is light at 5,072 polygons and 2,546 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the lens recesses and the sloped faces.

At $9 it suits road and highway visualization, traffic safety presentation, civil infrastructure renders, urban and motorway scenes, and games. Road studs appear in long repeating lines, so the low weight is what makes the asset usable. On sale since 2023.
