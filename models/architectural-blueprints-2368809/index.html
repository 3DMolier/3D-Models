About This Model

Architectural Blueprints is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $4. Geometry weighs in at 1,336 polygons over 1,338 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
