About This Model

Twin truck wheels mounted side by side with deep-tread tires, built for heavy vehicles, trailer axles and repair yard scenes. The 30,068 polygons go into the rim spokes, wheel nuts and chunky tread blocks that read well in close shots. The dual setup shows how heavy trucks spread load across paired tires on each axle end.

CheckMate certification confirms clean scale, and the 32,559 vertices keep rims and tires ready for separate materials. UVs are present on part of the mesh, so rubber wear and rim rust come easiest through procedural shading. Rotate the assembly for rolling animations or tilt it against a wall as a spare.

At $59 this 2026 release suits truck builds, logistics visualizations and garage environments. The assembly measures 71.12 x 53.93 x H53.93 cm, easy to fit onto a chassis of your choice. Stack it near a tire changer in a workshop and the workshop becomes a busy fleet service bay.
