About This Model

Priced at $199, the Man in Swimwear is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 9,278 polygons and 9,912 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 21 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 3000 x 3000. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
