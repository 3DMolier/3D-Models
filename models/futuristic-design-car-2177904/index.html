About This Model

The Futuristic Design is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 888,844 polygons over 467,288 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic as the intended applications.

2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
