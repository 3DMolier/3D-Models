About This Model

The Chocolate Covered Candy is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $4 on TurboSquid. You are getting 1,468 polygons and 1,472 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
