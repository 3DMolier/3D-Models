About This Model

A neon style LED bar sign glowing on its backing panel, the word bar in bright tubes for pubs and cocktail lounges. The 15,704 polygons shape the bent tube letters, the mounting standoffs and the clear acrylic board behind them. Its 15,972 vertices keep the tube curves smooth enough for close shots above a crowded bar counter at night.

Rated CheckMate Lite by TurboSquid, the sign comes with clean geometry, sensible names and a believable wall size. Tubes and backing board unwrap without overlaps, so the glow color can change while the acrylic stays clear. Hang it on dark brick behind rows of bottles, and its pink or blue light paints the whole wall.

Two versions light up this page: the sign switched on and a version with the LED tubes switched off. The off version suits daytime shots and closing time scenes, while the lit sign sets the night mood. Since 2023 it has dressed bar interiors, nightclub renders, cocktail menu graphics and city nightlife game levels.
