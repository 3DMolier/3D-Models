About This Model

A long-handled back scrubber with a fluffy head, this bath tool reaches the spots between the shoulder blades that hands cannot. The 5,384 polygons shape the curved handle and the plush scrubbing pad, which is built as fur for a soft silhouette. With 2,720 vertices the base mesh stays light even when the fur layer adds visual density in renders.

Its UV islands are kept separate, giving the handle and the pad their own space for plastic sheen and fiber color. The scrubber follows CheckMate specification on scale and naming, so it drops into a bathroom scene at realistic size.

This family has two versions: the Back Scrubber Fur in its green shade and the Back Scrubber Blue Fur Blue. Available since 2024, it fits bathroom interiors, hotel amenity scenes, personal care illustrations and shower caddy close-ups. Hang it from a hook beside the tub, and the bathroom gains the small clutter that makes it feel used.
