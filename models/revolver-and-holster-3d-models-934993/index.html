About This Model

Revolver and Holster is one of the Weapons models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 18,176 polygons and 18,605 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 12 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.
