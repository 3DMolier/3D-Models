About This Model

A garden rose shown in the process of blooming, its petals unfurling from a tight center, prepared for work in Maya. Layered petals curling outward give the flower the depth that makes roses so hard to fake with simple shapes. The stem, thorns and leaves complete the plant, so it can stand alone in a vase or rise from a garden bed.

CheckMate specification keeps the geometry orderly, so close macro shots of the petals stay clean. Since the file targets Maya, artists working in that package can open it without conversion steps.

New in 2026, the rose suits romantic film titles, wedding videos, botanical education and perfume packaging visuals. Soft backlight through the petals reveals their thin, translucent edges and a warm glow at the heart. Dew drops added to the surface give the bloom a just-opened morning freshness. Placed in a time-lapse style sequence of stills, the bloom tells a simple story of growth and renewal.
