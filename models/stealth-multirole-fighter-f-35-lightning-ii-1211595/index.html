This Aircraft model - Stealth Multirole Fighter Lightning II - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $199 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 206,388 polygons and 209,982 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
