About This Model

Card or Cash Ticket Machine Green is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
