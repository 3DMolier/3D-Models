Propulsion Jet Rotor belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $49 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 41,787 polygons and 43,084 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
