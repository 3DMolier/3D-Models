About This Model

A flying wing loitering munition has no fuselage to speak of and no landing gear at all, because it is not expected to land. The delta shape is chosen for stability at low speed with almost nothing controlling it.

The model is extremely light at 4,873 polygons and 5,102 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 5 maps all at 4096x4096. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and desert and winter camouflage builds are offered separately.

At $49 it suits defence visualisation, news and documentary graphics, simulation, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2019.
