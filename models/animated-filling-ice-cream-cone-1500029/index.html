About This Model

A waffle cone that fills with swirling soft serve ice cream in an animated sequence, built for dessert and cafe visuals. The model uses 56,334 polygons on 30,387 vertices, with the swirl of the soft serve taking most of that detail. The ice cream rises in a spiral and finishes in a pointed tip, the shape that makes a cone look freshly served.

Ten texture maps at 4096 x 4096 bring out the crisp waffle grid and the creamy surface of the dessert. The UVs are mixed, with some shared areas, which still works well for the repeated waffle pattern on the cone. It carries a CheckMate Lite rating, a basic sign from TurboSquid that the file opens and plays as expected.

Introduced in 2020, the cone works in cafe menu screens, summer social posts and food channel intros on video platforms. The filling motion makes a short looping clip that catches the eye on a digital menu board. Motion designers also cut the animation into stories about heat waves, holidays and sweet treats.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2020 · Updated 14 November 2026
