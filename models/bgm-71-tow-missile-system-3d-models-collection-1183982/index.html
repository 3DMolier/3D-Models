About This Model

TOW stands for tube launched, optically tracked, wire guided, and the last part is the interesting one: the missile trails a wire back to the launcher, so the gunner has to keep the sight on the target until impact. It is a weapon that demands the operator stay put, which shaped a generation of infantry tactics.

This is a collection listing rather than a single object, supplying the launcher system and its components as static pieces. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $119 the set suits defence visualization and presentation, documentary and news graphics, simulation, film and game environments. On sale since 2017.
