About This Model

A collection of bathroom bidets in several styles, ready for furnishing modern washrooms, hotel bathrooms and showroom layouts. Each piece shows the low bowl, rim and faucet deck that distinguish a bidet from a toilet at a glance. Gathering several models in one purchase keeps glaze, scale and modeling style consistent across a bathroom series.

All pieces carry CheckMate Pro status, so topology, scale and naming were checked before release. White ceramic needs careful reflections, and the smooth curves here hold a clean highlight under softbox light. Swapping the glaze to matte black or beige takes only a material change, which suits bolder interior palettes.

It joined the catalog in 2018 and remains useful for architects, sanitary ware catalogs and real estate renders. Place a bidet next to a matching toilet with a towel ring above, and the bathroom layout looks complete. A bidet beside a walk-in shower also signals a premium European-style bathroom in property listings.
