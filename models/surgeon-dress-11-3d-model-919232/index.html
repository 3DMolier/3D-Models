About This Model

Priced at $39, the Surgeon Dress 11 is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

The mesh carries 4,537 polygons and 4,637 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 3 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
