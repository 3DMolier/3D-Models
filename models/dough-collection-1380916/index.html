About This Model

A collection of raw dough pieces in several shapes, ready for bakery counters, pizza kitchens and home baking scenes. Soft rounded forms with slight sags and stretch marks give each piece the look of dough that has just been kneaded. Grouped as one purchase, the pieces save time when a kitchen table needs several stages of preparation at once.

The Dough Collection is certified CheckMate Pro, which means clean topology and consistent naming across every piece. A pale, slightly floury surface reads well under warm kitchen lighting and next to wooden boards. Each piece can be moved on its own, so you can arrange them on a tray, in a bowl or along a counter.

Food photographers, cooking app designers and game artists are behind its 14 purchases since 2019. It works as the starting point of a recipe animation, before the bread or pizza reaches the oven. A rolling pin and a dusting of flour around the pieces finish the scene with very little extra work.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Dough Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Can the Dough Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

What is the Dough Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How clean is the geometry on the Dough Collection model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.
