About This Model

The triangular prism is the entire brand, and it creates a modelling problem most chocolate bars do not have: the box is a long triangular tube, the bar inside is a row of peaks, and the two have to agree at every angle. This build carries the folded carton with its end flaps and the ridged bar geometry rather than a flat slab wearing a wrapper texture.

The mesh is 33,536 polygons and 33,858 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for the carton fold lines and the peak edges to stay crisp under a product light, which is where confectionery renders live or die.

At $29 it is aimed at food and confectionery advertising, packaging visualisation, retail and shelf renders, still life and games. On sale since 2021, kept to real-world scale so it sits correctly in a hand or on a shelf beside other packaging.
