About This Model

Priced at $29, the Holy Bible Closed Book is a finished Signage & Decor model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

Geometry weighs in at 4,327 polygons over 4,364 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 6 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
