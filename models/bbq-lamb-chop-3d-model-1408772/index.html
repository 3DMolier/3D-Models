About This Model

The BBQ Lamb Chop is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. You are getting 3,056 polygons and 3,059 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
