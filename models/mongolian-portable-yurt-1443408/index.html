About This Model

Mongolian Yurt is one of the Architecture & Landmarks models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 38,877 polygons and 39,717 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 5 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
