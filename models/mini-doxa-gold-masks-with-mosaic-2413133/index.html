About This Model

Doxa Masks with Mosaic is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Geometry weighs in at 60,974 polygons over 62,433 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 12 x 6 x 10 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
