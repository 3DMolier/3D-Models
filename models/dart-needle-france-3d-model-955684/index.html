About This Model

This steel-tipped dart with a fine needle point, a gripped barrel and tail flights is a classic throwing dart for pub games. It uses 5,434 polygons and 5,491 vertices, much of that budget going into the rounded barrel and the flights. The thin point and long shaft keep a convincing silhouette when the dart is stuck in a board.

No bitmap maps are bundled, so metal, plastic and flight colors are adjusted in materials to match any set. Its non-overlapping UVs leave room to print team logos or bold patterns on the flights and the shaft. TurboSquid granted it CheckMate Pro after a reviewer approved its topology, true size and tidy object naming.

On the marketplace since 2015, the dart serves pub scenes, darts tournament graphics and bar games in VR. Cluster three in the bullseye, and a close-up tells the story of a perfect throw without any words. Game developers can animate it along an arc, since the clean shape spins and lands convincingly in slow motion.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2015 · Updated 13 November 2026
