About This Model

Hibiscus Branch with Flower Red is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. You are getting 42,513 polygons and 42,076 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
