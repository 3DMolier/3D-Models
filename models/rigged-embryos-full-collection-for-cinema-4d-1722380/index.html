About This Model

A series of human embryo and fetus models showing development stages, from an early curled embryo to a nearly formed baby. All figures are rigged, so limbs and head can be posed or gently animated for growth sequences and medical explainers. They suit obstetrics lectures, pregnancy apps, biology classrooms and documentary animation about the beginning of life.

The set was built to the TurboSquid CheckMate specification, so its geometry, scale relationships and naming were checked for clean use. Translucent skin and visible blood vessels depend on subsurface materials, which give each stage a soft, lifelike glow. Warm diffuse lighting works best, recreating the gentle look of an ultrasound-inspired or womb-like environment.

Three versions are offered at $299, holding the same stages rigged for Cinema 4D, Maya or Modo users. Each version keeps controllers native to its package, so posing does not require any conversion or extra setup. Dating from 2021, Rigged Embryos Full Collection for Cinema 4D suits medical education, prenatal care material and science documentaries.
