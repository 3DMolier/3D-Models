About This Model

A set of rigged delivery workers carrying parcels and boxes, built for logistics scenes, city streets and e-commerce explainers. Couriers appear everywhere in modern cities, so having several ready figures fills sidewalks and doorsteps convincingly.

Each man wears a work uniform with cap and gloves, and the packages are separate objects that can change size. Rigging lets them walk, lift and hand over a box, the key gestures any delivery story needs. Different builds and faces keep a group from reading as copies when they share a loading dock.

The pack holds CheckMate Pro certification, which means scale, naming and topology passed the toughest TurboSquid review. Since 2022 it has served warehouse visualizations, shipping app tutorials and animated shorts about online shopping. Recolor the uniforms to match any courier company, and the same figures fit a different story right away. Place a courier at a front door with a parcel held out, and the scene explains itself without captions.
