About This Model

Chimpanzee Light Fur belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 33,738 polygons and 22,999 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
