About This Model

The barbuta is a fifteenth century Italian helmet with a deep bowl and a T-shaped face opening that leaves eyes and mouth exposed. Its 5,196 polygons form the smooth crown, the central ridge and the riveted edge without wasting detail on hidden surfaces. With 5,302 vertices the shape is light enough for game characters yet holds up in a museum style close-up.

Six texture maps at 4096 x 4096 bring hammered steel, light pitting and the darker wear around the rivets. Its unwrap is free of overlapping islands, so you can push the metal toward polished or rusty without fighting stretched pixels. CheckMate Pro approval from TurboSquid confirms the helmet arrives with clean topology and a tidy scene.

First listed in 2017, it suits medieval battle games, historical documentaries and fantasy films with armored knights. Place it on a weapon rack, a castle table or the head of an infantryman in a siege scene. Collectors of arms and armor references also use it to illustrate how Renaissance infantry protected their heads.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2017 · Updated 14 November 2026
