About This Model

The Snowboarder is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 142,259 polygons and 147,326 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 61 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
