About This Model

An apricot kernel with its ridged, textured shell, built for food, botany, nutrition and healthy snack scenes. The Apricot Kernel uses 6,192 polygons to model the almond-like shape, the seam ridge and the subtle surface bumps. That detail makes it hold up in macro food photography, where small irregularities read clearly in sharp focus.

There are 6,194 vertices in a quads-only mesh, which smooths without seams when subdivided further. Two textures at 4000 x 4000 carry the woody brown color and fine pores across the hard shell. CheckMate Pro certification confirms clean topology, sensible naming and real-world scale here.

Listed in 2015 for $9, the kernel suits nut mixes, superfood explainers, cosmetics ingredient visuals and botanical studies. Scattered with dried apricots on a wooden board, it makes an earthy still life for food blogs. Duplicated by the dozen, it can fill a bowl or a glass jar without slowing the scene down.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2015 · Updated 14 November 2026
