About This Model

Looking for a Food & Beverages asset? The Papaya Half Fruit is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 20,421 polygons and 20,707 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
