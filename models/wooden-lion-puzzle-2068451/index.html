About This Model

This Toys & Games model - Wooden Puzzle - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $18 on TurboSquid. You are getting 8,576 polygons and 4,286 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
