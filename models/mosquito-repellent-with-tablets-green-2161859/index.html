About This Model

The Mosquito Repellent with Tablets Green is a production-ready Sports & Recreation 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
