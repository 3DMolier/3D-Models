About This Model

Priced at $79, the Animated Basketball Ball Flies into Ring is a finished Sports & Recreation model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

The mesh carries 53,825 polygons and 54,373 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 15 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
