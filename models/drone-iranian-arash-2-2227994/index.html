About This Model

A loitering munition is a drone designed not to come back, and its economics show in the airframe: a plain delta or straight wing, a simple pusher engine, minimal control surfaces and a warhead where the payload bay would be. Nothing is built to be reused, which is exactly why these aircraft look cruder than reconnaissance drones and are far more numerous.

The mesh is deliberately light at 16,542 polygons and 18,269 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. Weight matters here more than detail: these aircraft are launched in numbers, and a scene may need a dozen in the frame.

Three other builds sit on this page, including rigged versions at $99. Aimed at defence and geopolitical visualisation, news and documentary graphics, training material and games. Built to CheckMate specification, on sale since 2024.
