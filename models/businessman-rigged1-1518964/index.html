About This Model

The Businessman Rigged(1) is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $169 on TurboSquid. You are getting 14,432 polygons and 12,892 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

19 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 3000 x 3000 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
