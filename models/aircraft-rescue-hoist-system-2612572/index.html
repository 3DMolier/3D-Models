About This Model

A helicopter rescue hoist with boom arm, winch housing and cable hook, built for search-and-rescue scenes. The Aircraft Rescue Hoist System uses 39,822 polygons and 41,419 vertices on the winch cover, pivoting boom and hook. It mounts above the cabin door, so it can be added to almost any utility helicopter model.

CheckMate certification means the parts are cleanly separated and named, which makes a rescue sequence easy to animate. The extended boom and hanging hook are what tell viewers a crew is about to lower a rescuer. Bright hook hardware stands out against sea or mountain backdrops, keeping the action readable from far away.

Released in 2026 and priced at $39, it is a small purchase that completes a rescue aircraft. Coast guard animations, mountain rescue films, training simulators and games are the typical destinations. It pairs naturally with helicopters that lack their own winch, turning a transport machine into a rescue one.
