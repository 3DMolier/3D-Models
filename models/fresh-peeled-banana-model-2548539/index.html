About This Model

Fresh Peeled Banana Model is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. You are getting 4,100 polygons and 4,029 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
