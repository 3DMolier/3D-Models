About This Model

A tiger shark animated in a fast swim cycle and rigged for Maya, its tail sweeping hard as it powers through open water. Faded vertical stripes on the gray back and a blunt, wide snout mark the species even in murky shots.

The fast cycle suits chase and attack moments, where speed and body bend matter more than slow drift. Its CheckMate-shaped build keeps the rig organized, so the swim can be retimed or blended in Maya. Since the rig lives natively in Maya, the shark can join a scene without any conversion step.

First published in 2026, the shark is royalty free and suits ocean thrillers, marine documentaries and underwater action games. Cut from a calm reef to this burst of speed and the shot tells viewers a predator has noticed something. Pair it with the slower idle swim and an animator can switch from patrol to strike within one sequence. Low sun rays cutting through water make the striped flank flash as the body flexes.
