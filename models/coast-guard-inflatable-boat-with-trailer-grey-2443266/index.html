About This Model

The Coast Guard Inflatable Boat With Trailer Grey is a production-ready Ships 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX. 2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbour environments in games, and marine simulation.

Geometry weighs in at 723,853 polygons over 747,983 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
