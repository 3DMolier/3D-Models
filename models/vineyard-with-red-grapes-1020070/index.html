About This Model

Priced at $79, the Vineyard with Grapes is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 492,807 polygons over 583,643 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 7 textures, authored at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
