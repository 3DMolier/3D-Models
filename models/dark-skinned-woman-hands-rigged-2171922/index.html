About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Dark Skinned Woman Hands is a finished, render-ready model at $59. Geometry weighs in at 4,698 polygons over 2,719 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.
