This Animals & Creatures model - Rattlesnake Attack - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. The build totals 4,484 polygons across 4,460 vertices. The same count is comfortable in an offline render and demanding in a mobile game, so judge it against your own budget.

Construction is plain and deliberate - quads through the body, triangles only where they are harmless.
