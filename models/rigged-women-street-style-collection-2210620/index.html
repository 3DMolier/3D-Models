About This Model

Rigged Women Street Style Collection gathers fashionable young women in urban outfits, each rigged for posing in city scenes. Oversized jackets, ripped denim and chunky sneakers give the figures the relaxed but deliberate look of modern street fashion. Varied hairstyles and accessories keep a sidewalk crowd from looking cloned when several women share a frame.

The collection is built to CheckMate standards, so clothing, bodies and rigs are named consistently across every character. Rigging lets artists bend arms toward phones, coffee cups or shopping bags without remodeling anything. Buying the group together keeps body proportions matched from one character to the next.

In the range since 2024, the set suits architectural renders, fashion lookbooks and lifestyle animation set in shopping districts. Placed near storefronts or crossings, the women add believable foot traffic to otherwise empty streets. Game developers may also use them as background characters in modern open-world cities.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
