About This Model

A compact bullpup weapon with a translucent magazine laid along the top, modeled for close tactical shots and first-person views. The FN P90 Personal Defense Weapon uses 91,922 polygons and 94,533 vertices, giving the thumbhole grip, optic and sling points real depth. Its rounded shell and stubby profile make the silhouette readable even at small sizes in a busy frame.

Its 21 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 separate polymer housing, metal barrel parts and the clear feed with cartridges inside. An overlap-free unwrap lets you add worn edges on the grip or recolor the body in tan. CheckMate Pro status confirms closed parts and smooth shading across the curved housing.

The license is Editorial Uses Only, which fits documentaries, news graphics, game prototypes and training material. Laid on a tactical vest, slung over a shoulder or racked in an armory, the gun works in special forces and sci-fi scenes. Available since 2017, the model shows the cartridge rows along the top clearly from a side angle.
