About This Model

Bombardier Global 6000 is a detailed Other asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 49,923 polygons and 53,434 vertices, a light load for a scene. Texture work runs to 7 maps, the largest at 8192x8192, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published January 2017·Updated 20 August 2026
