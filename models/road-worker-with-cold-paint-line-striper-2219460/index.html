About This Model

Road Worker with Cold Paint Line Striper is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $229. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 139,613 polygons and 143,927 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
