About This Model

Long-haul tractors and their trailers fill this big collection, ready to line a highway, a truck stop or a distribution yard. Semi Trucks and Trailers Big Collection combines cabs with box, flatbed and tanker style trailers for varied road traffic. Chrome grilles, fuel tanks and exhaust stacks give each tractor its own personality.

Coupling heights, scale and naming are aligned across tractors and trailers alike. Matching dimensions let any tractor hook up visually with any trailer in the bundle, which multiplies the number of possible combinations. Reefer units, side skirts and landing legs on the trailers add realism when a rig is parked at a dock.

New in 2026 at $899, the collection is ready for paid logistics and transport projects. Logistics visualization, driving simulators, highway traffic scenes and freight company explainers all use rigs like these. Wet asphalt reflecting headlights at night is one of the most flattering ways to show them.
