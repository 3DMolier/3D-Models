About This Model

The Electronic Stethoscope Littmann is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 4,101 polygons and 4,345 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 6 textures, authored at 2048 x 2048. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos. It has been on sale since 2018 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
