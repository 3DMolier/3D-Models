About This Model

Priced at $119, the Firefighter is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Straight from the source file: 65,149 polygons, 67,528 vertices. Read that against your engine and your shot: a hero close-up and a background prop have very different budgets.

Included are 18 texture maps, each at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512. Construction is plain and deliberate - quads through the body, triangles only where they are harmless. The outliner reads cleanly: no stray nulls, no leftover helpers, nothing to untangle before work starts.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
