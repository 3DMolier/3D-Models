About This Model

The Pilot Heads Rigged Collection offers a group of helmeted aviator heads for cockpit animation and close crew shots in flight scenes. Flight helmets, dark visors and oxygen masks frame each face, the way pilots actually appear behind canopy glass. A head turning toward the camera in a cockpit is one of the most familiar shots in aviation film.

Thanks to CheckMate Pro certification, scale and topology are clean, so each head seats properly in a cockpit. Masks and hoses are the details that sell realism, and they stay readable from a chase plane angle. Having a range of faces lets a wingman look different from the lead pilot in close shots.

Dating from 2020, the set serves combat flight games, aviation commercials, documentaries and training simulator cutscenes. Pair it with the static heads collection when background crews do not need to move. Reflections on the visors help sell the altitude and the light of the sky outside.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2020 · Updated 12 November 2026
