About This Model

A small plastic dispenser of floss picks with a flip lid, the kind that lives next to a bathroom sink. Of its 33,096 polygons and 33,408 vertices, much goes into the hinge, the lid rim and the rows of picks visible inside. Each pick has a thin floss strand stretched between two prongs, a shape that reads well in close dental shots.

As a CheckMate Pro asset, the box passed checks for topology, real-world scale and object naming. UVs without overlap leave room for printed package graphics on the body and a glossy, clear finish on the lid. Leaving the lid open shows the picks in neat order, which explains the product even at thumbnail size.

There is a plain unbranded box and the CleanStar Dental Floss Toothpicks Box, two versions modeled at one polygon count. The plain version is Royalty Free, whereas the CleanStar variant falls under Editorial Uses Only. Dating from 2021, it fits oral care education, dental clinic interiors and bathroom shelf scenes.

Can the Dental Floss Toothpicks Box model be used in a commercial project?
