About This Model

This Panic Button is a bold red emergency push button set in a sturdy housing, the kind fixed under counters and beside doors. Its large domed cap invites a quick slap of the palm, so the purpose reads instantly even without a label. Its simple shape stays light, so several can be scattered across a control room console.

CheckMate Pro rated, the button comes clean and plainly named, ready for close shots of a hand pressing down. Mounted under a bank teller's desk or in a security booth, it hints at danger and quick reactions. Adding a flashing red light and an alarm sound effect turns a simple press into a tense moment.

Available since 2015, the button suits security system explainers, heist films, industrial safety training and thriller games. Factories use similar buttons for emergency stops, which extends the prop to machinery and workplace safety scenes. Comedy writers love it too, since a big red button always tempts a curious character to press it.
