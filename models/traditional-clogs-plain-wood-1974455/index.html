About This Model

A wooden clog is carved from a single block, which is why it has no seams, no sole join and a thickness that varies with the carving rather than with a pattern. The upturned toe and the hollowed interior are both cut from the same piece, and that construction is entirely unlike any other footwear.

The mesh is minimal at 4,420 polygons and 2,214 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the carved profile and the hollow.

At $15 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits cultural and heritage visualization, tourism and craft material, period film and television, folk museum content, retail renders and games. On sale since 2023.
