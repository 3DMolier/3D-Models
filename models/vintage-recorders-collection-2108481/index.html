About This Model

Vintage Recorders Collection gathers several old audio recorders, the reel and cassette machines that captured music and voices before digital storage. Each recorder keeps its chunky buttons, level meters, reels or cassette door and the speaker grilles of its era.

The collection was built in line with CheckMate specification rules, giving consistent scale and named parts. Brushed metal panels and wood grain cases differ between machines, so a shelf of them never looks repetitive. Knobs and switches are modeled as separate shapes, which helps highlights catch their edges in close renders.

Since 2023 the set has furnished retro studios, detective office scenes and music history documentaries. A radio station from the past comes alive when tape reels sit beside a microphone and headphones. Collectors and museums use these models to plan exhibits about the evolution of sound recording. In a period drama, a recorder on the table can become the key to a secret conversation.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
