About This Model

The Colorado potato beetle is a striped yellow pest that strips potato fields bare, and this rigged set shows it at several life stages. The Colorado Potato Beetle Rigged 3D Models Collection lets farm and science scenes show the insect up close.

Adults carry ten black stripes on their cream wing covers, while the plump reddish larvae have rows of dark spots along each side. Rigs drive legs, antennae and body segments, so the beetles can crawl across leaves instead of sitting frozen. The bundle earned CheckMate Pro status, with clean geometry ready for macro close-ups.

In the range since 2017, the collection suits agricultural education, crop protection presentations and nature documentaries. Agronomy teachers animate larvae chewing a potato leaf to explain why early detection matters so much. Pesticide makers show where the insect hides on the plant and how damage spreads through a field. Game developers use the beetles as small enemies or ambient creatures in farm settings.
