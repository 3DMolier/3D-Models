About This Model

Dental 3d Printer with Surgical Guide is a desktop resin printer shown with a freshly printed guide used for implant surgery. Its 40,696 polygons shape the tinted lid, the build platform, the resin tank and the small guide with drill sleeves. The tinted lid shields resin from stray light while still letting the build be seen.

There are 41,707 vertices on clean UVs, ready for translucent resin materials and printed brand labels. The CheckMate Lite level ensures the parts are named in plain words and grouped sensibly. The surgical guide is small and detailed, so it works well in macro shots beside a dental model.

Choose between two versions, the static printer at $59 or a rigged printer at $79. Since 2020 it has supported dental lab tours, digital dentistry explainers and clinic interior scenes. Next to an intraoral scanner, it tells the full story of guided implant planning in a modern practice.
