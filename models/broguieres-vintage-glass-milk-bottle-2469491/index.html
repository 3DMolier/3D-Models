The Broguieres Vintage Glass Milk Bottle is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $15 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 2,514 polygons and 2,496 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
