About This Model

A straw parasol on a bamboo pole is the shorthand for a warm coastline, and it works because of texture rather than shape. The thatch has to look layered and slightly untidy, the pole has to show its nodes, and the binding at the crown has to be visible - a smooth cone on a smooth stick reads as a party umbrella instead.

The model is built in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so a thatch texture and a bamboo grain land exactly where they are placed, and the parasol takes a new palette without fighting the layout. Built to StemCell specification, so it works in a real-time engine as well as an offline render.

At $29 it suits resort and hospitality visualization, travel and tourism material, exterior and landscape renders, advertising and games. On sale since 2025.
