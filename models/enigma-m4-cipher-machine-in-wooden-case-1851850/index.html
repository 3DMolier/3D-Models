About This Model

The naval rotor cipher machine used aboard U-boats, sitting open in its wooden box with keyboard, lampboard and plugboard exposed. Enigma M4 Cipher Machine in Wooden Case spends 123,091 polygons and 126,488 vertices on its mechanism. Rotor wheels, key caps and patch cables along the front panel get most of that budget.

The lid swings upward and shows the hinges, latches and corner joints of the crate, details that sell the machine in close-ups. Its UV layout avoids overlap, leaving clean space for worn lacquer, stamped letters and scratched metal. The asset passed CheckMate Pro review, so scale and geometry behave on import.

Since 2022 the machine has gone into wartime films, codebreaking documentaries and museum style presentations. Put it on a radio operator desk inside a submarine set, or under a spotlight in a history exhibit. Escape room designers and game makers like it as a puzzle object with obvious mechanical parts to animate.
