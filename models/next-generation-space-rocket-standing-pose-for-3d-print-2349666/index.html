About This Model

This next generation space rocket stands upright on its own base, posed for printing as a desk model or a display piece. The tall body tapers toward the nose, and the standing pose keeps the whole shape balanced on a flat footprint. That vertical orientation suits resin and filament printers, where an upright part can need less support than a rocket lying on its side.

It is made to the CheckMate specification, so the shell is tidy and ready for slicing software without a repair pass. Before printing, scale it to whatever height your shelf or build volume allows, since the rocket is designed as a standalone upright form. Painted after printing, the hull takes white primer, metallic accents and decals much like a classic rocket kit.

On sale since 2025 for $39, it suits hobby printers, space-themed classroom projects and gift figures for engineers. Collectors can pair it with a launch pad diorama, while educators use printed rockets to explain stages and payloads. It also works as a rendered prop for space exhibits when you want a clean, upright rocket rather than a launch scene.
