About This Model

Carved Totem Pole with Tiki Face belongs to our Architecture & Landmarks range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 15,390 polygons and 15,257 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 12.42 x 12.35 x H40.55 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
