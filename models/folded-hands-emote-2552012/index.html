About This Model

Folded Hands Emote belongs to our general range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 1,466 polygons and 1,496 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 5 textures, authored at 1024 x 1024. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
