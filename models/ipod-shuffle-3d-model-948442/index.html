About This Model

Priced at $29, the iPod Shuffle is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 6,461 polygons and 6,558 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 13 maps at 2024 x 2024. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. 2015 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
