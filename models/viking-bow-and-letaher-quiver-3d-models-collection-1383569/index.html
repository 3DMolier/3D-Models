About This Model

A wooden Viking longbow and a leather quiver full of arrows equip Norse warriors, hunters and fantasy archers in historical scenes. The bow's simple self-wood shape and the stitched quiver with its shoulder strap follow early medieval Scandinavian gear. Slung over a character's back or hung on a longhouse wall, the pair adds authentic detail to a Viking setting.

Bow, quiver and arrows come apart, so a character can draw a shaft while the quiver stays on the shoulder. Worn leather, rough hemp string and fletching from goose feathers suit materials with subtle grime and wear. The collection holds CheckMate Pro status, granted by TurboSquid only after a reviewer opened and checked the files.

From 2019 onward, these weapons have armed fantasy role-playing games, museum displays and documentaries about raiders from the north. Reenactment groups preview kit with it, and animators rig the bow to show the draw and release of an arrow. Set beside a round shield and an axe on a wooden table, it completes a classic Norse armory still life.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2019 · Updated 12 November 2026
