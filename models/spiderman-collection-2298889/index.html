About This Model

This collection gathers several figures of the web-slinging hero in his red and blue suit, so one purchase covers more than one moment. The suit pattern is the whole read: the web lines across the chest and the mask lenses decide whether the character looks right.

Built to CheckMate specification, each figure follows clean topology and consistent naming, which keeps a multi-character scene manageable in the outliner. Matching scale across the set means two figures can share a rooftop shot without anyone resizing them by eye. Collecting the figures together also keeps materials consistent, so the red reads the same shade in every shot.

Available from 2024 at $169, the bundle has fan films, comic-style animation and cosplay previews as its natural ground. Perch a figure on a ledge against a night skyline, and the silhouette alone tells viewers who is watching over the street. Rooftops, water towers and neon signs are the usual company for these figures in a finished frame.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
