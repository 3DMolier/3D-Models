About This Model

Looking for a Toys & Games asset? The Teddy Bear Light is a finished, render-ready model at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 33,578 polygons and 33,578 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
