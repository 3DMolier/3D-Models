About This Model

A wooden target with throwing knives, made for circus acts, training yards and medieval fair scenes where blades hit the mark. The round target is cut from a thick log, and the knives sit stuck in the wood at different angles. Several knife designs are included, so a thrower's kit looks varied instead of cloned.

As a CheckMate Pro set, each knife has its pivot at a sensible point, which makes rotating it into the target quick. Steel blades want a sharp highlight, while the end grain of the target takes a soft, diffuse light. Separate knives can also lie on a table beside the target, waiting for the next throw.

Sold since 2023, the collection suits circus animations, sport throwing scenes, fantasy tavern games and stunt show previs. Pinning the knives in a tight cluster near the center tells the viewer the thrower is an expert.
