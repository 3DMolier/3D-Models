About This Model

Handcuffs Collection gathers several pairs of restraints, from classic chain-linked cuffs to hinged and rigid designs. One file covers police stations, crime scenes and escape room props without hunting for separate models.

Every pair carries CheckMate Pro certification, TurboSquid's top mark for clean topology and organized files. Polished steel rings, ratchet teeth and keyholes are the details that make handcuffs read correctly in close ups. Chrome surfaces mirror the room, so an interrogation lamp overhead produces sharp, dramatic highlights.

Offered from 2024, the set suits crime dramas, detective games and law enforcement training material. Lying open on a desk beside a badge and files, a pair tells the viewer an arrest just happened. Escape room designers and magicians also use cuffs as props in puzzles and stage acts. Swapping steel for gold or black finishes moves the same cuffs into music videos or fashion editorials.
