About This Model

A collection of obstacle course walls with climbing ropes, wooden planks, cargo nets and hand holds for training runs. Rough timber faces show bolts and boot scuffs, the wear left by recruits and athletes pushing over the top in the rain. Combining walls of different heights builds a full assault course without modeling each barrier from scratch.

TurboSquid's inspectors found clean structure and logical object names, awarding the walls CheckMate Pro. Wood shaders with mud splashes near the base suit wet weather training days in autumn. Rope materials benefit from a fibrous texture that reads well in close shots of climbing hands.

Offered since 2022, the walls fit military base renders, fitness race events and action game levels with parkour. Lined up across a muddy field, they create the grueling challenge of a boot camp morning before breakfast. Characters vaulting a wall make a strong motion shot for a fitness or recruitment film.
