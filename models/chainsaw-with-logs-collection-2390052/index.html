About This Model

A collection pairing a chainsaw with freshly cut logs, built for forestry, lumber yard and cabin scenes. The saw shows its bar, chain teeth, pull handle and engine housing, while the logs reveal pale rings at the cut ends. Set on a stump in a clearing, the scene suggests a day of hard work in the woods.

TurboSquid's CheckMate rules were followed, so the saw and the logs share one scale and tidy labels. Orange plastic, steel chain and bark sit on independent shaders for quick restyling. Sawdust sprinkled around the logs and a little smoke from the engine sell the moment after a cut.

On sale since 2025, Chainsaw with Logs Collection suits forestry training, outdoor equipment catalogs and survival games. Horror filmmakers have a different use for the saw, propping it in a dark shed for tension. Firewood suppliers picture the stacked logs and the saw in seasonal flyers for winter heating.
