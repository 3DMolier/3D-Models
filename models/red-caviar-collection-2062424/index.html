About This Model

A collection of red salmon caviar servings in jars, tins and small bowls, the glossy delicacy of festive tables and fine dining. Each orange-red egg catches the light, so the set brings instant richness to a buffet or banquet scene. Having several containers in one bundle lets a table look abundant without repeating a single dish.

The pack follows CheckMate specification guidelines, so every item imports at true scale with clean, renderable geometry. Translucent eggs respond well to subsurface scattering, which gives them the juicy glow of the real thing.

Released in 2023, the Red Caviar Collection suits restaurant menus, holiday food features and seafood shop visuals. Pair a tin with blini, sour cream and champagne glasses for a classic celebration spread. Yacht interiors and luxury hotel room service shots also gain polish from a small caviar dish. Spoon a little onto buttered bread on a wooden board for a homely breakfast take.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
