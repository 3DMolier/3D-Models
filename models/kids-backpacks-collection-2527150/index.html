About This Model

A collection of children's school backpacks with cheerful colors, zips and padded straps, sized for young students and kindergarten kids. Front pockets, side pouches, stitched seams and webbing loops give each bag its own personality in close-ups.

The set was built under CheckMate specification, so every bag opens neatly organized and clearly named. Bags can sit on a classroom floor, hang from a hook or ride on the back of a child character. Rounded shapes and short straps make the scale read as children's gear, and any bag recolors to match a uniform. Fabric folds near the straps keep the bags from looking stiff or empty.

The family spans two versions: the first backpack collection and a follow-up with extra designs and colors. Released in 2026, they serve back-to-school scenes, children's animation and classroom visualizations for schools. A row of backpacks by a school door says the first morning of term, especially with books and a lunch box scattered around.
