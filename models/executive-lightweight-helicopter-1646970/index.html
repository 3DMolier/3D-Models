About This Model

Executive Lightweight Helicopter belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 272,953 polygons over 298,098 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
