About This Model

A suede brush is a block with several different surfaces on it: brass or steel bristles on one face, rubber crepe on another, a rubber eraser edge and often a bristle end for corners. Each side does a different job on a nap that cannot be polished the way smooth leather can.

The mesh runs 58,954 polygons and 62,530 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and almost all of that count is bristles, which is exactly where it belongs on a brush.

At $29 it suits footwear care and retail visualization, shoe shop and wardrobe interiors, product and e-commerce renders, lifestyle imagery and games. On sale since 2021.
