About This Model

Mobile Harbour Crane belongs to our Industrial Equipment range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 562,963 polygons and 634,199 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 13.81 x 48.96 x H28.68 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
