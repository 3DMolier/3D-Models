About This Model

Weapon Dragon Movie Camera belongs to our Electronics & Gadgets range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 435,343 polygons over 446,237 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

25 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
