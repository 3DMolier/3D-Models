About This Model

Stone Egyptian Sarcophagus is one of the Kitchen & Tableware models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $49. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 23,824 polygons and 23,763 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. Maps: 12 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
