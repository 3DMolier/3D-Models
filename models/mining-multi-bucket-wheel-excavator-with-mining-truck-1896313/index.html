About This Model

A bucket wheel excavator is among the largest land vehicles ever built, and its scale is impossible to convey without something beside it. Pairing it with a mining truck, itself enormous, is the standard way of showing that - and this build supplies both so the comparison is built in.

It is a very large asset at 4,378,344 polygons and 2,288,165 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the wheel, the boom lattice and the conveyor structure.

This page carries rigged builds from $299 to $359, including versions prepared for Maya and Cinema 4D. Aimed at mining and heavy industry visualization, equipment marketing, documentary work, engineering presentation and games. On sale since 2022.
