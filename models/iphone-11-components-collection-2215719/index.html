About This Model

A collection of iPhone 11 components laid out as separate parts, from the logic board and battery to the camera module and display. Seeing the phone broken into pieces explains how much engineering fits inside a slim glass and metal body.

Each component is its own object, so an exploded view can be animated piece by piece toward the assembled phone. Small connectors and screws make close shots convincing, the kind of detail repair technicians look for. The collection is organized to CheckMate specification, keeping names and pivots consistent across every part. Apple owns the design, so the set is licensed for editorial use only: news, education, repair guides and film work.

Available since 2024, the set suits phone repair tutorials, technology journalism, teardown videos and engineering classes. Laid out on an anti-static mat under bright light, the parts make a clean, satisfying flat-lay image. Every component in the collection comes for $89, ready for a teardown sequence.
