About This Model

School Bus with Teenage Boys Rigged Collection combines a yellow American school bus with rigged teenage boys ready to board. The bus has the familiar flat nose, black trim bands and folding stop arm that make it recognizable worldwide. Backpacks and casual clothes help the boys read as students heading to class rather than generic pedestrians.

Assembled under CheckMate rules, the collection names bus parts and characters clearly for easy scene assembly. Rigs let the boys climb steps, wave or sit inside the bus without remodeling a single limb. Long rows of windows along the side reveal seats inside, useful when the camera passes close.

First offered in 2021, the set suits school safety videos, education explainers and suburban morning scenes in films. Parked at a curb with the stop arm out, it gives a residential street instant narrative purpose. Driver education material also uses such buses to explain stopping rules around children.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
