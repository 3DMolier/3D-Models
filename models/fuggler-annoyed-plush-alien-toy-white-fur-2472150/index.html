About This Model

Fuggler Annoyed Plush Alien Toy Fur belongs to our Toys & Games range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 3,460 polygons and 3,483 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
