About This Model

The Airliner Generic Red Rigged is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 163,404 polygons and 176,553 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Airliner Generic Red Rigged?

10 maps at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Airliner Generic Red Rigged mesh?

163,404 polygons and 176,553 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the Airliner Generic Red Rigged model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
