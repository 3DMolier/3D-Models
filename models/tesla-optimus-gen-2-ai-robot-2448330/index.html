About This Model

A humanoid robot is judged on its hands and its proportions. Optimus has deliberately human limb ratios so it can use tools and spaces built for people, and articulated fingers that are the whole point of the design - a robot that cannot grip is a mannequin. This model carries the hand detail, the joint housings and the panelled torso.

It is built in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so the white panels, the dark joint sections and any branding land cleanly on their own surfaces rather than fighting a shared layout.

At $149 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits robotics and technology marketing, automation and future-of-work presentation, technology journalism, trade show and investor material, and games. On sale since 2025.
