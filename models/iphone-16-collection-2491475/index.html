About This Model

The IPhone 16 Collection brings together the latest Apple handsets in several finishes, from the standard model to the larger variants. The vertical camera layout and the new capture button on the side are both modeled, so the generation is clear. Finishes vary across the set, giving designers a quick choice of color for a scene.

StemCell certification standardizes shading, so glass backs and aluminum frames look the same in any renderer. Apple holds the rights to the design, so these phones may be used for editorial purposes only. Displays are separate parts, ready for launch-day screenshots or interface demos in a hands-on review.

Out since 2025, the IPhone 16 Collection serves tech news, unboxing videos and smartphone comparison articles. Journalists place the phones on a desk or in a hand to illustrate release coverage. Explainer videos rotate them slowly to show where the new button sits on the frame.
