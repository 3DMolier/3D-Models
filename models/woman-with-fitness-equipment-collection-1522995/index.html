About This Model

A collection that pairs an athletic woman with fitness equipment, ready to set up a workout scene in a gym or living room. Sportswear and toned proportions make the figure read as a trainer or an active client rather than a bystander. Having the equipment in the same style keeps the woman and her gear visually consistent in close shots.

It is assembled to CheckMate specification, so the figure and equipment share real-world scale and naming. Equipment colors and the sportswear can be retinted to match a gym chain or a fitness brand. Bright, clean daylight with a large window behind gives the scene the fresh energy fitness content relies on.

On sale since 2020, the set fits fitness app visuals, sportswear advertising, gym interior design and health blogs. Home gym retailers can stage a full workout corner instead of showing equipment on a blank background. Woman with Fitness Equipment Collection saves sourcing a matching figure and gear for every new workout scene.
