About This Model

A serene statue of Guanyin of the Southern Sea, the Buddhist bodhisattva of compassion, prepared for 3D printing. The figure wears flowing robes, and the folds are shaped to print cleanly without fragile overhangs. Calm facial features and a gentle gesture carry the devotional character even at a small print size.

The file was readied under CheckMate specification, so names are clean and the base sits flat on the build plate. Resin printers capture the fine drapery best, while filament prints suit larger garden or shrine pieces. A bronze or jade paint finish after printing brings it close to traditional temple statuary.

In circulation since 2024, it serves home shrine makers, temple model builders and collectors of Asian art. Hobbyists print it as a meditation room ornament or as a gift for festival occasions. Game and film artists can also render it as set dressing for coastal temples in East Asian settings.
