About This Model

Cooling Tower is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and architectural visualization. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

The mesh carries 32,445 polygons and 38,882 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 10 maps, the largest at 8192x8192, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
