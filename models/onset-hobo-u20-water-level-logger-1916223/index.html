About This Model

A slim cylindrical data logger dropped into wells and streams, this instrument records water level and temperature for hydrology studies. The 11,632 polygons shape the sealed metal housing, the pressure sensor end cap and the eyelet used to hang it on a cable. Its 5,816 vertices keep it easy to add to field kits, monitoring wells or lab benches.

UV islands are kept apart, which leaves room for engraved serial text and mud stains from field use. A CheckMate Pro rating confirms correct scale, readable object names and dependable geometry. Fine scratches on the housing hint at repeated deployments in rivers and boreholes.

Two versions are offered in this family: the Water Level Logger and the Black Water Level Logger Black in a darker housing. Since 2022 the logger has fit environmental science explainers, hydrology training, field research scenes and engineering documentation. Suspending it on a thin cable inside a cutaway well makes the measurement principle easy to show.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2022 · Updated 10 November 2026
