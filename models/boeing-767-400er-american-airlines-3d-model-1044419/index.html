About This Model

Priced at $129, the Boeing 767-400ER American is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 469,174 polygons and 479,831 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 34 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
