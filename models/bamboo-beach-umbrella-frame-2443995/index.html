About This Model

This bamboo parasol frame without its canopy shows the central pole and radiating ribs of a tropical beach umbrella. It measures 291.79 x 287.5 x H306.76 cm, with 78,968 polygons and 78,514 vertices spent on lashed joints and natural cane segments. The open skeleton casts striped shadows on sand, which looks striking in midday light.

StemCell readiness lets the frame render consistently in the common engines without material rework. Visible nodes along every bamboo stalk and the rope bindings at the hub give the piece a handmade resort feel. A thatch, fabric or palm-leaf canopy can be added on top, or the bare frame can stand as it is.

Introduced in 2025 at $19, the frame fits beach resort visualizations, tiki bar scenes and island games. Hospitality designers place several along a shoreline to plan lounge zones and walkways for guests. Wedding and event planners also use bare frames like this as decorative arches draped with flowers.
