About This Model

The First Trimester Human Embryos Rigged Collection for Cinema 4D shows how a human embryo develops week by week in early pregnancy. Each stage displays the curled body, the growing limb buds and the large head typical of this early period. Lined up in sequence, the embryos explain growth more clearly than any diagram can.

Each stage was built under CheckMate specification, so the shapes are clean and consistently scaled. Translucent skin shaders with subtle subsurface scattering give the embryos a convincing and gentle biological look. Dark backgrounds with soft rim lighting keep the viewer's attention on the delicate forms.

Three versions cost $179 apiece, delivering this rigged collection for Cinema 4D, Modo and Maya users. The rigs make it possible to animate gentle movement or a slow rotation for lectures and presentations. On offer since 2021, the set suits medical education, prenatal health content, biology classes and science museums.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
