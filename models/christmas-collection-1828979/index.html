About This Model

A festive bundle of holiday props, the kind of decorations that turn an ordinary room into a Christmas scene in minutes. Shapes are kept clean and rounded, so baubles, ribbons and garlands catch reflections the way real glass and satin do. Pieces can be scattered under a tree, along a mantel or across a table, depending on the shot.

TurboSquid placed the collection at CheckMate Pro, its strictest level, after checking geometry, naming and scale by hand. Each item lives in its own file, which keeps the scene light when only a few decorations are needed. Colors are material settings, so classic red and gold can turn into a silver and blue winter palette.

Released in 2021 with a $169 price, the set suits retailers dressing seasonal product shots and studios preparing holiday cards. Animators decorate living rooms for festive shorts, and game artists add the props to winter events in their titles. Interior designers also stage cozy December renders with them for clients and social media posts.
