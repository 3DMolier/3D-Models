About This Model

Priced at $24, the Ground Coffee Bag Paper is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for food photography, film & TV VFX and game assets. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

The mesh carries 5,134 polygons and 5,175 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 4 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
