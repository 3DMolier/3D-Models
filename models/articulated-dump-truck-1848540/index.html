About This Model

Articulated Dump Truck is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $139. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and game assets. Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

The mesh carries 620,286 polygons and 331,774 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
