About This Model

Arabic Antique Silver Tray is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $9. You are getting 732 polygons and 734 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 37.55 x 37.55 x H2.23 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
