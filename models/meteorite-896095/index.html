About This Model

Meteorite belongs to our Space & Sci-Fi range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 26,880 polygons and 26,882 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

3 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Meteorite?

3 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Meteorite mesh?

26,880 polygons and 26,882 vertices, built as polygonal quads only. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the Meteorite model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
