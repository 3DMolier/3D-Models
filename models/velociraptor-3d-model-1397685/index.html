About This Model

Velociraptor is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 9,960 polygons and 10,095 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 15 maps at 4096 x 4096. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
