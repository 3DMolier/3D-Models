This Vehicles model - Tesla Semi Small Trailer - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 65,917 polygons and 73,115 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
