About This Model

The Small Mail Package with Stamps is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. You are getting 2,130 polygons and 1,067 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
