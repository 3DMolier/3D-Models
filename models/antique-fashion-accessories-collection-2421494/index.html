About This Model

This antique fashion accessories collection gathers several period pieces for dressing historical characters and vintage vanity tables. Grouping them saves time when a scene needs believable clutter from another era, such as a parlor or dressing room. Fine shapes and delicate trims give each piece the handcrafted look of older craftsmanship, quite unlike mass-produced modern goods.

The pieces were made to TurboSquid's CheckMate standard, opening cleanly with consistent names and scale. Soft window light on a dressing table shows off fabric, metal and lacquer finishes best. Each accessory works alone as a close-up prop or together as a styled arrangement on velvet or lace.

Released in 2025, the collection suits period dramas, museum displays and vintage boutique interiors with glass cabinets. Scatter the pieces across an antique dresser, and a bedroom immediately feels like another century. Costume designers and game artists also use such sets to enrich wardrobe scenes, fashion history lessons and character inventories.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
