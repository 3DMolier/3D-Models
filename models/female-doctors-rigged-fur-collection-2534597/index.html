About This Model

A collection of female doctors in medical coats, gathered for hospital, clinic and healthcare scenes that need several staff. Fur gives their hair a soft, natural finish, which matters in close portraits where flat hair would look artificial. White coats and stethoscopes are the cues that tell a viewer instantly who these women are.

Having several doctors from one set keeps proportions and material style consistent across a busy ward. Each figure stands as a separate object, so the team can be split across reception, corridor and consulting room shots. The set is prepared to CheckMate specification, with real human scale and readable object names.

Released in 2026, it suits hospital visualization, medical education, healthcare websites and clinic promotion imagery. Bright, even lighting with a soft blue tint gives the clean clinical look viewers expect from medical settings. Fur increases render time, so plan close-ups of a single doctor and keep group shots wider.
