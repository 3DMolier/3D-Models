About This Model

Warehouse buildings, racking systems and storage equipment fill this collection for industrial interior and exterior scenes. Industrial Warehouse Collection gives logistics projects the structures themselves, not only the vehicles moving inside them. Steel portal frames, roller doors and high bay lighting define the classic distribution building seen on every business park.

Shared scale between buildings and props means pallet racks fit the floor plan without manual adjustment. Concrete floors with painted lane markings and safety barriers make interior shots look operational. Long perspectives down the racking aisles are the signature shot for this kind of space, ideally with a forklift halfway down.

Released in 2025 at $439, the set suits commercial industrial and real estate projects. Warehouse leasing brochures, supply chain consulting decks, safety training films and simulation games all use spaces like these. Daylight through roof panels mixed with cool artificial light gives the interior a realistic working atmosphere.
