About This Model

A playful rounded Earth with simplified green continents, bright blue oceans and a separate layer of puffy white clouds. Cartoon Planet Earth Clouds is built with 9,931 polygons over 10,037 vertices, so the globe stays smooth while spinning on loop. The cloud shell floats just above the surface and can rotate at its own speed for livelier motion.

Clean, non-overlapping UVs let you recolor land, add polar ice or paint a city pin for a travel graphic. TurboSquid approved it at CheckMate Pro, so scale and part names are ready for a kids' show or explainer. Soft shapes and flat colors keep it friendly next to cartoon rockets, moons and smiling stars.

Both versions cost $44 at an equal polygon count, the twin being Cartoon Planet Earth and Clouds. Launched in 2022, the planet is Royalty Free for children's lessons, climate explainers and mobile games. Hovering in a starry sky with a tiny rocket circling it, it opens a science lesson warmly.
