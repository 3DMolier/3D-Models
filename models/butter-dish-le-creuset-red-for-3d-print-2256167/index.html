About This Model

A red Le Creuset butter dish prepared for 3D printing, this model can become a real kitchen piece, a decor item or a display sample. The dish has a flat rectangular base and a domed lid with a small knob, matching the classic stoneware shape. Soft rounded edges and a glossy finish give it the friendly character of this well known French cookware.

The design meets the CheckMate specification at TurboSquid, keeping the mesh clean and correctly scaled. As a print-ready model, it is meant to be sliced, printed and finished with paint or glaze in the deep red tone. The lid and base are separate parts, so they print without supports and fit together after finishing.

Released in 2024, the dish suits hobby printing, kitchen decor projects and prototype tests for tableware designs. Printed and painted, it can sit on a shelf as a miniature replica or serve as a tabletop display item. Makers often treat it as a practice piece for testing smooth curved surfaces and tight lid fits.
