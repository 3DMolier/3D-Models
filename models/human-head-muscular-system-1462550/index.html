About This Model

Human Head Muscular System is one of the Medical models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $59. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for character animation, game development and film production. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 35,470 polygons over 35,708 vertices - a light build for its class. Maps: 25 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
