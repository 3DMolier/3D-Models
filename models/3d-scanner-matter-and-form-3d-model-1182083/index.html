About This Model

This desktop 3D scanner folds open into a turntable and a laser head, capturing small objects as point clouds. Its 13,572 polygons over 15,449 vertices describe the clamshell case, the rotating plate and the sensor tower. When closed it looks like a slim box, and the open pose shows how the lid becomes the scanning arm.

Certified as CheckMate Pro, the scanner carries 4 texture maps at 2048 x 2048 on non-overlapping UVs. Matte plastic surfaces dominate, with a darker turntable where an object rests while the laser sweeps across it.

First offered in 2017, it suits maker space interiors, product design studios and tutorials about photogrammetry and scanning. A desk scene with a figurine on the plate explains the whole workflow before any software appears on screen. Engineering classrooms and technology reviews can also place it beside a 3D printer to show a full prototyping bench. Hobbyists planning a workshop layout use such references to judge how much desk space the device really needs.
