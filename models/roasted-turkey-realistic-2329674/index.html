About This Model

Priced at $49, the Roasted Turkey Realistic is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 19,539 polygons and 19,541 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
