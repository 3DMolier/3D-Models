About This Model

This Aircraft model - Airbus A310-300 Generic - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $129 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

The mesh carries 248,114 polygons and 257,489 vertices, which puts it in the mid-weight bracket for this category. Maps: 17 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
