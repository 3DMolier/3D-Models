About This Model

Sleek Electric Fastback White is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2026, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 317,501 polygons and 363,141 vertices, a mid-weight load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
