About This Model

A folded aluminum step ladder with its legs closed together, standing flat as it would rest against a garage wall. The Step Ladder 2 Folded 3D Model saves you from posing an open ladder, since it is already in its stored position. Treads, side rails and the top platform stack neatly, which gives the folded shape a clean vertical silhouette.

Its CheckMate Pro rating means geometry, object names and scale were all reviewed by TurboSquid before release. A dull aluminum shader with a few paint splashes makes it look like a ladder that has seen real jobs. Rubber feet at the base are a good place to add dirt, since they touch the floor every working day.

Garages, storage rooms, renovation scenes and hardware store displays have used the folded ladder since 2015. Prop it beside paint cans and a drop cloth to suggest a room halfway through redecoration on a weekend. Stored ladders also make convincing background clutter in workshops and building maintenance corridors of every kind.
