About This Model

The Medical Gloves on Hands Pink is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
