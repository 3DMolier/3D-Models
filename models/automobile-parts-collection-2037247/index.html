About This Model

Loose car components of the kind found on a mechanic's bench fill a garage, a parts store shelf or an exploded engine view. Machined metal surfaces and rubber seals give each part a distinct read, so a pile of components still looks organized. Arranged on pegboards or scattered on a creeper, the pieces turn an empty workshop into a working one.

Each piece passed CheckMate Pro, TurboSquid's highest tier of technical checks for topology and file structure. Metallic shaders with light oil smudges suit these parts best, and a clean version reads well in catalog-style renders. Rubber hoses and gaskets can take a darker satin finish, keeping them distinct from the steel.

Two versions of the bundle exist: the Automobile Parts Collection at $69 and Automobile Parts Collection 2 at $79 with further components. Mixing both versions lets a parts store shelf hold a wider range without visible copies. Listed since 2023, it is picked for auto repair videos, dealership service areas, driving game garages and engineering tutorials.

Questions About This Model

How heavy is the Automobile Parts Collection mesh?

18,265 polygons and 19,184 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Against a median of about 28,000 polygons across our Model Bundles & Sets models, 18,265 puts this one below the middle of the category.

How clean is the geometry on the Automobile Parts Collection model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.

Does the Automobile Parts Collection model include materials and textures?

The product page states what ships with each format. Textures, when present, come inside the download rather than as a separate purchase. See the listing for the exact contents.
