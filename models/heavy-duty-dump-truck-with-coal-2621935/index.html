About This Model

A heavy off-road dump truck loaded with coal, built for open pit mines, quarries and energy industry visualizations. The massive 2,036,262 polygon budget covers the cab, chassis, huge tires and the heap of coal chunks in the bed. Coal sits in a natural pile above the bed rim, which makes the truck look freshly loaded at the face.

The CheckMate build keeps 1,999,945 vertices in groups for the truck, the dump body and the load. UV coverage is partial, so dust, mud and coal grime are best layered with procedural materials. Remove the coal pile to show the truck returning empty to the excavator.

Offered from 2026 at $149, the model suits mining documentaries, haulage training and heavy machinery games. The truck towers over people and cars, so place figures nearby to sell its scale on a haul road. Tilt the bed in animation to tip the coal at a stockpile and complete the delivery cycle.
