About This Model

A collection of disposable medical hair caps, the soft bouffant covers worn by surgeons, nurses and food workers to keep hair contained. Gathered elastic edges and gentle puffy folds make them read correctly even at small sizes on a character. Food factories and bakeries also require such caps, so the set crosses into industrial scenes.

Each cap is CheckMate Pro certified, so the set shares sizing and fits the same heads. Switch colors between blue, green and white to match hospital, laboratory or kitchen dress codes. Having the variants together saves modeling each cap style separately for a whole staff.

Introduced to the range in 2022, the collection dresses operating room teams, lab technicians and factory hygiene scenes. Put a cap on every figure in a clean room, and the protocol of the space is clear. Infection control trainers use them to illustrate correct preparation before staff enter sterile areas or wards.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
