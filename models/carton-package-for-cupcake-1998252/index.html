About This Model

A carton package for a single cupcake, this asset completes bakery counters, gift scenes and pastry delivery concepts. The box has 7,914 polygons on 3,957 vertices, shaping the folded walls, the insert that holds the cake and the tuck-in lid. A clear window on top shows off the frosting, the feature that makes cupcake boxes so recognizable.

Reviewed under the CheckMate Pro program at TurboSquid, the carton is ready for production renders. The flat unwrap without overlaps gives space for a bakery logo, pastel patterns and ribbon prints. A soft matte cardboard shader with a thin transparent film over the window balances the two materials nicely.

Released in 2022, the carton suits bakery branding mockups, birthday party scenes and patisserie interiors. Tied with a ribbon and placed on a cafe counter, it looks like a small treat ready to take home. Dessert delivery apps show it arriving at the door with a cupcake visible inside.
