About This Model

This model captures the twisted roots of a dragon tree, a thick tangle of trunks and buttresses gripping the ground. A heavy 545,420 polygons over 631,033 vertices goes into the bark ridges, the knotted junctions and the root tips spreading outward. The sculpted mass looks ancient, like the base of a tree that has stood on a rocky island for centuries.

StemCell rules let the bark shading carry over from offline renders to real-time engines without surprises. Overlapping UVs reuse a single bark texture efficiently, and gray-brown weathered wood suits the gnarled forms.

Released in 2024, the roots suit fantasy forests, island landscapes, botanical garden scenes and nature documentaries. Placed in a jungle clearing with mist around it, the tangle becomes a natural landmark for a quest. Film environments can also hide a cave entrance beneath the roots to lead heroes underground. Landscape architects studying old specimens may use it to discuss how such trees anchor themselves on thin soil.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 18 November 2026
