About This Model

Boxing Glove Rigged is one of the Sports & Recreation models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. Geometry weighs in at 8,264 polygons over 8,216 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

2 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
