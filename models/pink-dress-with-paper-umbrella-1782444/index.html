About This Model

Priced at $99, the Pink Dress with Paper Umbrella is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 12,781 polygons and 13,132 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.
