About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Hydrogen Hypercar Black is a finished, render-ready model at $129. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 226,714 polygons over 119,104 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
