About This Model

This Food & Beverages model - Fresh Red Bell Pepper - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 141,904 polygons and 71,050 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX.

2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
