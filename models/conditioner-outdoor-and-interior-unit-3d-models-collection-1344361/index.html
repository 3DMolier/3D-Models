About This Model

This collection covers both halves of a split air conditioner: the wall-mounted indoor unit and the boxy outdoor compressor. Having both parts means one purchase furnishes a bedroom wall and the building facade outside it. Indoor units hang high above a bed or desk, well clear of furniture, curtains and passing heads.

A pass at CheckMate Pro level confirms the louvers, fan grilles and casing seams are cleanly constructed. Outdoor boxes cling to balconies and brackets, where a rusty drip line beneath them adds urban grit. White plastic housings with soft corners stay unobtrusive in bright rooms yet still read as modern equipment.

On offer since 2018, the set is licensed for paid architectural, interior and game work alike. Architectural visualizers use these units so apartments and offices look equipped rather than freshly emptied. Rows of compressors along a facade quickly turn a clean street into a crowded, sweltering city block in summer.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2018 · Updated 11 November 2026
