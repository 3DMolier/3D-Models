About This Model

A steel lifting bar with knurled grip zones and rotating sleeves, ready for gym floors, garages and weightlifting scenes. The Barbell 3D Model spends 17,734 polygons on the collars, the knurling bands and the end caps. Laid across a rack or resting on rubber flooring, it immediately sets a strength training mood.

It arrives with 6 textures at 4000 x 4000 and 3995 x 4000, covering the chrome, the grip and the sleeves. CheckMate Pro certification confirms the topology, and the non-overlapping unwrap spans 17,802 vertices. Chrome reflections need an environment map, and a bright studio HDRI makes the knurling sparkle.

The bar comes in two versions: the straight bar and a curved one for curls and triceps work. Put both on a rack with a bench in front, and a home gym corner looks properly equipped. Sold since 2015 and royalty free, the bar fits fitness apps, sports games, workout videos and gym equipment renders.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2015 · Updated 14 November 2026
