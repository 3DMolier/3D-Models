About This Model

Leather Gloves Black is a detailed Clothing & Accessories asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
