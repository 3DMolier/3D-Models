About This Model

Golf Cart is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $59. The mesh carries 263,010 polygons and 273,245 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 36 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 2272 x 2272. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
