About This Model

The Qin L is a plug-in hybrid saloon built to a price that made western manufacturers pay attention, and its styling reflects that positioning: clean surfaces, a full-width light bar, flush handles and a roofline that runs back into a short deck. In deep blue the body creases show clearly, which is exactly what a car render needs to prove itself.

The mesh is 213,482 polygons and 237,355 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. That is an efficient budget for a full car, and it means several can share a street scene without the render slowing to a crawl.

At $129 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits automotive marketing and configurators, urban and traffic visualisation, dealership and showroom renders, advertising and games. On sale since 2025.
