About This Model

Dart Needles 3D Models Collection gathers throwing darts with slim barrels, steel points and flight fins, ready for pub games and sports scenes. A set of several darts means a board can hold a realistic cluster rather than one copy pasted repeatedly.

Every dart cleared CheckMate Pro review and imports at true scale with tidy naming and clean geometry. Knurled grips and shaped flights are modeled as clear forms, which keeps them readable even when the camera is close. Flight colors can be changed per player, making it simple to stage a match between rivals.

We have offered this collection since 2016, and it still fits bar interiors, game room renders and sports broadcast graphics. Stick a few into the treble ring of a dartboard, or lay them on a scorer's table beside chalk and drinks. Slow-motion throw animations work well too, since each dart is a single compact object easy to keyframe.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
