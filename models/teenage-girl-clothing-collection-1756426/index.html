About This Model

Dressing a crowd of young characters needs range rather than one outfit, because teenagers in a scene never look uniform. Tops, jackets, skirts, trousers and layering pieces have to work in combination, which is exactly what a collection provides and a single garment cannot.

Supplying them as a set also keeps the fit and the topology consistent, so pieces can be swapped between figures without the mismatch that gives away a wardrobe assembled from different sources.

At $179 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits character and costume design for games and animation, fashion and apparel visualization, retail and e-commerce renders, education and youth-focused material, and illustration. On sale since 2021.
