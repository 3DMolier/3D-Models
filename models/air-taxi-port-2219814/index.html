About This Model

This compact air taxi port imagines how electric flying taxis might land in a city, with a raised pad, passenger shelter and boarding area. The 50,240 polygons stay modest, which keeps the structure light enough for urban planning scenes with many buildings. Clear floor markings on the pad show where the aircraft should touch down.

It has 52,738 vertices and non-overlapping UVs, so light baking and wear maps fall neatly onto the deck surfaces. Following CheckMate rules, it arrives with logical naming that helps when you place it on a rooftop or riverside site. Glass panels on the shelter let waiting passengers watch each approach and landing.

Two builds of the Air Taxi Port are sold, one static for $149 and one rigged for $169. The rigged version can animate moving parts, useful when a presentation shows the port preparing for a landing. Available since 2024, it fits future mobility pitches, smart city visuals and science fiction environments.
