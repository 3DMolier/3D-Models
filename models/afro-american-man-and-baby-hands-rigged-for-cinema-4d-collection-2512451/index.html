About This Model

Rigged hands of an African American man and a baby for Cinema 4D, built for the tender grip of an infant around a father's finger. Representation matters in family imagery, and this pair saves the trouble of retexturing generic hands with a different skin tone. The rig lets each knuckle bend, so the hold can be gentle or firm.

Deep brown skin on the adult hand shows lighter palms, creased joints and short clean nails, while the baby hand stays round and soft. Proportions follow CheckMate guidelines, which keeps the adult and infant sizes believable side by side. Warm subsurface shading helps the darker skin glow under soft light instead of turning flat.

This 2026 bundle costs $92 and fits pediatric healthcare, insurance brochures, parenting blogs and inclusive campaigns about family. Place the hands on a patterned blanket in morning light, and the skin tones become the emotional center of the frame.
