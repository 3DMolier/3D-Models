About This Model

A collection of glass bongs in several shapes, from straight tubes to rounded beakers, for smoke shops and lifestyle scenes. Each piece has its own neck height, bowl and downstem, so the lineup looks like a real retail shelf. Thick glass bases keep the silhouettes stable and grounded, even when several stand close together on a shelf.

The whole collection passed CheckMate Pro review at TurboSquid, with clean meshes and consistent scale across pieces. Tinted glass accents give each bong a separate identity once a refractive shader is applied. Because every item is a separate object, you can display one piece alone or fill an entire case.

Since 2022 the collection has filled head shop interiors, cannabis news visuals and lifestyle renders. Arrange the pieces on lit glass shelves, or set a single bong on a dorm room desk. Interior designers working on licensed dispensaries also use the set to plan lit display cabinets and counters.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
