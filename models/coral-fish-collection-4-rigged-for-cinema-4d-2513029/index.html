About This Model

The fourth pack of rigged reef fish, prepared for Cinema 4D and meant for aquarium scenes, ocean documentaries and underwater games. Bright tropical species with flat bodies and fan-shaped fins bring color to any coral garden. A small school weaving between branching corals shows them best, with translucent fin edges glowing against a sunny surface overhead.

The pack passed CheckMate review, so the group stays consistent in scale and naming across species. Saturated scale materials glow under caustic light patterns, which sell the illusion of sun filtering down through shallow water. Striped and spotted patterns differ between species, so a mixed school never looks cloned.

Two versions are sold here: Coral Fish Collection 4 Rigged at $285 for Cinema 4D and a Maya edition with the same species. This 2026 release is licensed Royalty Free, which suits apps and online courses that ship to thousands of users. Aquarium apps, marine biology courses and diving resort promos use the fish to fill reefs with movement.
