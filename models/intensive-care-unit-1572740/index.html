About This Model

Intensive Care Unit is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 64,580 polygons and 65,932 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
