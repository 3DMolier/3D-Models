About This Model

Knight with Severed Dragon Head is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $169. You are getting 114,753 polygons and 119,737 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and VR experiences.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
