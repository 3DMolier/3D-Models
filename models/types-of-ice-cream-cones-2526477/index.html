About This Model

A collection of ice cream cones in several styles, from classic waffle cones to cake cones topped with soft scoops. Each cone shows its own texture, with criss-cross waffle patterns, smooth wafer walls and dripping cream at the rim.

Built to the CheckMate standard, every cone arrives at a natural size for hands, stands and display counters. Pastel scoops and golden wafer surfaces give a cheerful color range that suits summer and dessert themes. Several cone types in one file make it easy to fill a gelato counter or a child's party table without repetition.

Added in 2026, the set suits dessert menus, ice cream shop interiors, summer holiday scenes and food content for kids. The models also print well as display props, which is useful for shop windows and food styling mockups. Hold a cone in a character's hand on a sunny beach, and the scene instantly gains a playful holiday mood.
