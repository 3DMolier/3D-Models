Candy Box Mockup Closed is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $15. You are getting 3,846 polygons and 2,000 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
