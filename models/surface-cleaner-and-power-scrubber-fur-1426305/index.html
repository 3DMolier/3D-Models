About This Model

Priced at $39, the Surface Cleaner And Power Scrubber Fur is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

At 11,908 polygons and 12,635 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Texture work runs to 14 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
