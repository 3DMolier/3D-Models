About This Model

Priced at $139, the Business Jet Bombardier Challenger 604 Generic is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 49,958 polygons over 53,365 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 14 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
