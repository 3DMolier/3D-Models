About This Model

An airless tire with flexible spokes between the hub and a chunky all-terrain tread, so there is no air to leak. The wheel carries 39,424 polygons and 20,152 vertices, spent mostly on the curved spokes and the deep tread blocks. Mount it on a utility vehicle or stand it upright in a garage display.

Being a StemCell asset, it opens in several formats from one master with identical shading. With every UV island kept separate, you can easily dirty the tread or tint the spokes differently. Light passing through the open spoke pattern creates striking shadows, a natural focus for product renders.

Two variants are included: the Michelin All Terrain in clean condition and a Michelin X Tweel All Terrain Dirty edition. The dirty version comes caked with mud, which saves a texturing pass for off-road or farm scenes. Released in 2022, it fits automotive technology content, vehicle design studies and tire showroom renders.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2022 · Updated 14 November 2026
