About This Model

Premium saloons converged on one shape a long time ago, and beige is the colour that admits it. The car is meant to be unremarkable in the best sense: correct proportions, no statement, and a presence that suits a driveway or a hotel forecourt without drawing attention.

The model holds 257,665 polygons and 278,292 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and comes rigged so the wheels and doors can be posed. It carries StemCell certification, and the library also holds a simplified build for background use.

At $129 it suits architectural and street visualisation, automotive marketing, advertising, film backgrounds, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published July 2025·Updated 20 August 2026
