About This Model

This power cable ends in a European Schuko plug with round pins and side earthing clips, the standard across much of Europe. Its 16,925 polygons and 16,978 vertices shape the molded plug body, the grip ridges, the strain relief and the coiled lead. The round plug face and its earth contacts make the cable instantly recognizable to anyone from Germany or France.

CheckMate Pro approval and non-overlapping UVs let the plug and cable carry separate plastic finishes and printed ratings. Black or white matte rubber with slightly glossy pins looks right for household appliances and tools.

Available since 2022, it suits appliance renders, electrical safety guides, home office desks and repair tutorials. Lying unplugged on a floor beside a heater, the cable hints at a fire hazard in a safety lesson. Travel adapter explainers can compare it with British and American plugs to show why tourists need converters. Workshop scenes feel practical when the lead snakes from a wall socket to a drill on the bench.
