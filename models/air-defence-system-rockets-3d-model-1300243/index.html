About This Model

Air defence missiles are almost always seen in groups: loaded in a launcher, stacked on a transporter or laid out on a reload trailer. This asset provides them as a set of rounds, which is how they appear in practice and what a launcher render actually needs behind the tubes.

The mesh is light at 48,672 polygons and 52,572 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with texture maps including one at 8192x8192, three at 4096x4096 and seven at 2048x2048. That large map carries the stencilling and the seeker detail at the nose, where a close camera lands.

At $69 it suits defence visualisation, news and documentary graphics, military training material, games and print. Real-world scale is kept, so the rounds seat correctly in a launcher. On sale since 2018.
