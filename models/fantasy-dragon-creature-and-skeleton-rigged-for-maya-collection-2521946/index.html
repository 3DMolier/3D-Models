About This Model

The winged dragon and its matching skeleton rigged for Maya, aimed at animators in film and game pipelines. Controls open as native Maya rig nodes, so blocking wing beats can begin the moment the file loads.

The skeleton mirrors the creature joint for joint, which makes transitions between living dragon and bones clean. A long neck and tail carry many joints, giving smooth arcs when the dragon turns or lashes out. Membrane wings fold tight at rest and open wide for takeoff, while heavy hind legs keep ground poses from floating.

Dated 2026, the Maya edition serves game cinematics, feature previs and fantasy series production. The bone version fits archaeology scenes in invented worlds and spooky halls of a ruined castle. Pick this edition if Maya drives your animation, since every control was built and tested there. Spiked ridges along the spine make the dragon's back read clearly in silhouette against a stormy sky.
