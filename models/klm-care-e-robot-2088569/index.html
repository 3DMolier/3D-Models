About This Model

Priced at $29, the KLM Care E robot is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 10,300 polygons and 10,659 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
