About This Model

High Mobility Protected Hawkei PMV 4x4 belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 386,435 polygons and 422,572 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

19 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
