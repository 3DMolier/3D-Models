About This Model

Asphalt Milling Machine Clean belongs to our Industrial Equipment range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualisation, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

Geometry weighs in at 713,320 polygons over 553,453 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. Texture work runs to 26 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
