About This Model

A collection of generic power tools such as drills, grinders and saws for workshops, hardware stores and DIY scenes. The tools carry no brand marks, so they fit any setting without license concerns or competing logos. Each one shows recognizable triggers, cables or battery packs that a viewer reads instantly.

Every tool in the set passed CheckMate Pro inspection for geometry and real scale. Parts like chucks, guards and blades are separate, which lets you position them for action shots. Bright plastic housings pair well with sawdust on the bench, and cables can bend along a curve toward a wall socket.

A member of the catalog since 2015, the collection keeps serving set dressers and tutorial makers. Hang the tools on a pegboard, lay them on a workbench, or place one in a worker's hand on a building site. They also suit hardware store shelf scenes and step by step instruction videos for home repairs.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
