About This Model

Priced at $9, the SLV 90 Bollard Light is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 13,128 polygons and 6,530 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
