About This Model

A dwarf hamster scurrying in a walk cycle, its fur groomed and its rig built in Maya for playful pet animation. Quick short steps carry the round body forward, the busy motion owners watch every evening in a cage.

Fur ripples slightly with each step, so the animal keeps its soft look instead of sliding like a toy. Tiny pink paws move in a rapid rhythm, and the head bobs gently as the hamster explores. The walk loops seamlessly, so the hamster can travel along a tube, a table edge or across a carpet.

Introduced in 2026 alongside the eating clip, it lets the same pet move and then stop for a snack. Maya keeps rig and groom together, which spares the setup headaches common with small furry characters. Children's apps, pet product videos and cozy indie games are natural homes for this tiny character. A low camera at floor level makes the small animal feel adventurous as it crosses the frame.
