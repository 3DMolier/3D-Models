About This Model

Mounting ear defenders on the helmet rather than the head solved a compliance problem: separate muffs get taken off and left somewhere. Attached to the shell they swing up out of the way instead, which is the difference between protection and paperwork.

The model is very light at 12,796 polygons and 13,149 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $44 it suits industrial and construction visualisation, protective equipment marketing, training and safety material, character accessories, film props and game environments. On sale since 2022.
