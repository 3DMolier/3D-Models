About This Model

Barn Owl Standing is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $149. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
