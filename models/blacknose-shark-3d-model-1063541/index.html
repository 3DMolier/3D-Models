About This Model

Priced at $79, the Blacknose Shark is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 15,998 polygons over 16,170 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
