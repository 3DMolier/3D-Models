About This Model

Curling Equipment Collection brings together the granite stones, sweeping brooms and delivery aids used on a curling sheet. Each stone has its handle and polished running band, and the brooms carry their fabric heads and long shafts.

The collection is CheckMate Pro certified, confirming reviewed topology, naming and real-world scale. All pieces are separate objects, so a player can hold a broom while stones sit in the house rings at the far end. Polished granite under arena lights gives strong reflections on the ice, which makes the set rewarding to render.

Two versions are available: this collection at $42 and Curling Equipment Collection 2, sold for $59 with additional gear. Arranged along the hack line, the stones form a neat row that explains the sport at a glance. The bundle has been on sale since 2022 and has its first buyer. Winter sports broadcasts, olympic previews, ice arena visualization and sports games all suit it.
