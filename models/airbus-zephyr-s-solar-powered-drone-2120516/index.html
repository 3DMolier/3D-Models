About This Model

The Airbus Zephyr S Solar Powered Drone is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. You are getting 95,998 polygons and 47,562 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
