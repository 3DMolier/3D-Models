About This Model

Tourist Compass is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Geometry weighs in at 14,280 polygons over 14,278 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

6 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

Can the Tourist Compass model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How heavy is the Tourist Compass mesh?
