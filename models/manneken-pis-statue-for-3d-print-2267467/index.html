About This Model

A printable replica of the Manneken Pis, the small bronze boy fountain that has stood on a Brussels street corner for centuries. The model is prepared for 3D printing, so it can become a souvenir, a garden ornament or a scale model. The chubby proportions and playful pose are the whole charm of the figure, and they read clearly at small sizes.

It follows the CheckMate specification, which keeps the geometry clean and the scale predictable for slicing. A closed, solid form matters for printing, because gaps and loose shells are what make prints fail. The figure stands on its small plinth, giving a flat base that prints without extra support.

Available since 2024, it suits souvenir production, tourism visuals of Brussels, city landmark renders and educational material. Painted bronze or left in plain resin, the little statue keeps its humor and recognizability. In a render it can also stand in its corner niche, giving a Belgian street scene its most famous landmark.
