About This Model

The Dark Skin Teenager Fashionable Style Rigged is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $179 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 26,800 polygons and 47,270 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
