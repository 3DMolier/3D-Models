About This Model

Looking for a Food & Beverages asset? The Camping Rolling Cooler Open with Fruits and Beers is a finished, render-ready model at $139. You are getting 220,901 polygons and 224,998 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 50.06 x 50.99 x H108.52 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
