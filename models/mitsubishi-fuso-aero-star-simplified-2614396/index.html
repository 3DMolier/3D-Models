About This Model

This lighter take on the Japanese city bus keeps the full exterior while trimming detail that distant cameras never see. The Mitsubishi Fuso Aero Star Simplified holds 401,418 polygons and 419,484 vertices, making it practical for scenes with several buses. Wheel arches, doors and mirrors stay modeled, so the bus still holds up at mid distance.

Its body length and height match a standard urban route bus parked at a city stop. CheckMate certification guarantees clean naming and real scale even in this reduced build. The rounded roof edge and big glass areas keep the familiar silhouette intact in traffic shots.

Offered as one version since 2026, it costs $139 and carries the Editorial Uses Only terms.
