About This Model

This set of spice jars in clear containers fills a kitchen shelf in one move, each jar showing its colored contents through the glass. Warm reds, yellows and dark herb greens give the row a lively palette that reads well even from across the room. Transparent walls are the tricky part of such props, since refraction exposes every flaw in the shape behind them.

TurboSquid's CheckMate specification guided the build, so each item opens with sensible names, real scale and tidy transforms. Jars can be pulled out one by one and arranged on a counter, a rack or an open pantry shelf. Lids and labels give the eye something to rest on between the glass highlights.

First offered in 2025, the set serves kitchen interiors, cooking videos and food packaging mockups. A few jars in the background of a recipe shot add a lived-in feel without stealing attention from the dish. The Royalty Free license lets the props appear in client work, games and online stores.
