About This Model

A summer farming set gathering field machinery and props, meant for building a harvest season scene on a working farm. The pieces share the same rural scale and look, so a tractor, implements and smaller tools sit together naturally in one meadow.

Every item passes CheckMate Pro inspection, so files open with sensible names, pivots and real-world sizes. Moving parts such as wheels and attachments are split from the bodies, which helps when animating a machine crossing a field. Painted metal and worn surfaces are kept on separate materials, ready for dust and mud layers in late summer light.

The collection went live in 2020 and is used for agricultural visualization, farm simulator games and rural landscape illustrations. Countryside animations gain a lot of life once machinery is parked along hay rows or beside a barn. Educational projects about food production also rely on the set to show how crops move from field to storage.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
