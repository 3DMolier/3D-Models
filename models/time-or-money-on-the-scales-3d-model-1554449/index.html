About This Model

A balance scale weighing a clock against a stack of money, a ready visual metaphor for choices between time and income. The beam rests on a central post with a pan hanging on each side, and the tilt shows which side currently weighs more. It reads clearly at a glance, which makes it handy for thumbnails, slides and editorial illustrations.

The object passed TurboSquid's CheckMate Lite inspection, so it opens with sensible scale and a tidy structure. Metal on the scale and paper on the bills come from separate materials, so the money color or the clock face can be changed quickly. Lit from one side, the brass beam throws a long shadow that adds drama to a plain backdrop.

Out since 2020, Time Or Money On The Scales 3D Model suits business blogs, finance courses and motivational videos about work and life balance. Animators tip the beam back and forth to illustrate a decision, and the motion explains the idea without a single caption.
