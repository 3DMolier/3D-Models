About This Model

This set of rusty steel rebars gives a building site the ribbed reinforcing bars that sit in stacks or poke out of broken concrete. The ribs along each bar are modeled rather than painted, so the bars hold up when the camera passes close.

It is built to the CheckMate specification, which means clean topology, real-world scale and logically named parts. The orange-brown corrosion makes the bars read as abandoned or weather-beaten, perfect for neglected industrial areas. Because each bar is a separate object, they can be rearranged into new piles or laid into a formwork grid.

Available since 2016, the set has been chosen 15 times by construction visualizers, post-apocalyptic game artists and film set dressers. Scattered among broken walls, it suggests a stalled project or a building ruined by war. Bundled neatly on timber, the same bars make a tidy delivery at a fresh foundation. Rebar cages around a column are another quick build, using the straight pieces as they come.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
