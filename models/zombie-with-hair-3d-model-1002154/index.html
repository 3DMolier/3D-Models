About This Model

A shambling undead man with ragged hair, torn clothing and gray decaying skin, built as a ready enemy for horror scenes. Matted strands hang over the face and shoulders, which breaks the silhouette and makes the figure read as a corpse rather than a sick person. The body leans forward slightly, the classic posture of a zombie dragging itself toward the camera.

CheckMate Pro review covers real-world scale and clean topology, so the character stands at adult height next to doors, cars and survivors. Surface wounds, stains and tattered fabric carry most of the gore, which keeps the base shape readable from a distance. Duplicate a few with altered skin tones and you have a small horde for a street shot.

On sale since 2016 at $99, the Zombie with Hair 3D Model serves horror games, Halloween promos, music videos and short films. Light it from below with a cold blue rim, and the hair throws shadows that hide just enough of the face.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2016 · Updated 12 November 2026
