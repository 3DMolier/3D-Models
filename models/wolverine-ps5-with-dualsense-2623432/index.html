About This Model

A PlayStation 5 console in a Wolverine-themed finish with its DualSense controller, ready for gaming rooms and living room setups. The Wolverine PS5 with DualSense uses 47,264 polygons for the console shell, stand and controller buttons.

Its 51,082 vertices keep the curved side panels and controller grips smooth in close shots. Made to CheckMate requirements, the console and controller arrive as separate, correctly scaled objects. The themed panels set it apart from the standard white console at a glance.

Available since 2026 for $59, the console fits gamer bedrooms, esports lounges and tech review scenes. Stand it beside a television on a media unit, or leave the controller on a sofa cushion mid-session. Collectors' rooms gain a special edition piece that stands out among regular consoles on the shelf. The vertical stand keeps the slim console upright, matching how most owners display it next to a screen.
