About This Model

This cafe sign spells the word in blue LED tubing shown switched off, ready for a storefront at closing time or by day. It has 10,236 polygons on 19,794 vertices, nearly twice as many vertices, which suggests split edges along the tubes. The letter strokes are round in section, so they catch a reflection even when the light inside is dead.

Bitmap textures are not part of the package, and the unlit blue glass is a material you can adjust freely. Swap in an emissive shader and the same mesh becomes a glowing sign for a night street scene. UVs stay unoverlapped and the file holds a CheckMate Lite rating, TurboSquid's first level of quality checks.

Available since 2023, the sign fits coffee shop facades, mall interiors and urban streets in games and film. Hang it in a front window above the counter, and passers-by in the scene know at once what kind of place it is. The switched-off state is useful for daylight shots, where a lit tube would look wrong against the bright sky.
