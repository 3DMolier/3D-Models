About This Model

Priced at $19, the Highspeed Rail Section is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 40,706 polygons and 40,864 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 4 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
