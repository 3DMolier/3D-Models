About This Model

A clear plastic takeaway cup with a domed lid and striped straw fits coffee shops, juice bars and food court scenes. The 9,516 polygons and 9,494 vertices define the ribbed wall, the lid opening and the bend in the straw.

Four maps at 4096 x 4096 provide transparent plastic, light scratches, a frosted lid and the colored straw stripes. Thanks to StemCell certification, the clear plastic shades the same way in each format that ships with it. The clean unwrap welcomes a printed cafe logo, and the empty interior accepts any drink you model.

On offer since 2018, the cup suits cafe interiors, smoothie menus, car cup holders and busy street scenes. Fill it with iced coffee or a berry smoothie to turn the empty cup into a beverage close-up. It also illustrates environmental stories about single-use plastics, litter on beaches, ocean waste and recycling habits. Refraction through the ribbed wall rewards a backlight that shows off the clear material.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2018 · Updated 19 November 2026
