About This Model

A two-post parking lift that stacks one car above another, a space saver for home garages and city parking lots. Steel columns, a tilting platform and safety locks hold the upper car, while the lower vehicle parks underneath. The cars included give the lift scale and purpose, so the scene reads instantly as a parking solution.

Built against TurboSquid's CheckMate checklist, the lift and cars open at true size with logical object names. Car paint and lift finishes are material settings, so the scene can match a showroom or a dim garage. Platform and vehicles are separate objects, which lets you animate the lift raising a car to the top.

From 2020 the scene has cost $179, suiting parking equipment makers, garage builders and developers who need to save space. Car dealers show how stock can be stored compactly, and architects present parking options to apartment residents. Classic car collectors also picture their garage with renders like this before installing a lift.
