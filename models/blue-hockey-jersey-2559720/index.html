About This Model

The Blue Hockey Jersey is a production-ready Sports & Recreation 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 1,754 polygons and 1,776 vertices. That sits in low-poly territory, so it stays cheap to instance across a crowd scene or a game level. It ships with 4 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game assets. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
