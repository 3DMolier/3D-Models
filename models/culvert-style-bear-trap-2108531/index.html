About This Model

A culvert trap is a steel tube on a wheeled trailer, and this one shows the drop door that seals a bear inside. The mesh holds 321,348 polygons on 160,096 vertices, so ventilation holes and weld seams survive a close camera. That density lets the barrel stay round in silhouette even when the frame tightens on the trigger mechanism.

It is built to the CheckMate specification, and the non-overlapping UV layout accepts painted rust or decals without stretching. Wildlife agencies tow these traps behind trucks, so the hitch and tongue at the front matter as much as the tube. Galvanized metal suits the shape best, with mud splashed along the lower edge where the wheels throw up dirt.

On sale since 2023, the trap fits forestry training scenes, nature documentaries and survival games set in bear country. Parked at a campground edge with the door raised, it tells a story about a problem animal without any human figure nearby. Biology courses can also show it beside bait buckets to explain how relocation programs move bears away from towns.
