About This Model

A small group of tigers resting beneath a spreading tree, gathered as one collection for jungle and forest-edge scenes. The shade of the tree and the lounging cats set a calm midday mood, quite different from a hunting pose.

Built to CheckMate specification, every piece keeps clean topology and shared scale, so the cats fit beside the trunk without adjustment. Because tree and animals come together, light through the leaves lands naturally on the striped coats. Big cats read through silhouette, and these stay clear even when partly hidden behind roots and leaves.

Added in 2026 at $169, the set fits wildlife documentaries, zoo planning renders and nature games. Add tall grass and a muddy pool nearby, and the scene becomes a believable Asian forest clearing. Each tiger can be moved independently, so the group can spread out or bunch together to fit the frame. The collection also helps conservation campaigns illustrate habitat, since it shows animals in context instead of on a white background.
