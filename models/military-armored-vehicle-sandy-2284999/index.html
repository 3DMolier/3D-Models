About This Model

Priced at $179, the Military Armored Sandy is a finished Military Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 612,058 polygons over 589,112 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags defense simulation, battlefield simulation and war-game environments as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defense training material and military VFX shots.
