About This Model

Phone colors are chosen years in advance and then become the thing people remember about a generation. The hardware differences between models are increasingly invisible, so the finish is doing most of the work of telling one year from another.

The model is light at 21,496 polygons and 11,423 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in green, and carries StemCell certification. Four further colorways are offered separately.

At $34 it suits product and technology visualization, screen and interface mockups, e-commerce imagery, advertising, desk and lifestyle scenes, and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
