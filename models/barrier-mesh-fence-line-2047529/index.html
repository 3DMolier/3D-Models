About This Model

An orange plastic safety mesh strung between metal stakes, this fence line closes off construction sites and roadworks in city scenes. Most of its 533,084 polygons go into the open grid of the mesh, which lets light and background show through realistically. The 262,736 vertices reflect a triangulated net, heavy for a fence but worth it in close shots of the weave.

After a review of topology, scale and naming, TurboSquid granted the fence CheckMate Pro, its top certification. Dirt splashes near the ground and sun fading along the top edge fit onto a UV layout with no overlaps. Run a line of it around a trench with a digger behind, and a roadworks scene is instantly readable.

This orange line is joined by a black security mesh version on the page, and each one costs $19. Dating from 2023, the fence fits construction renders, road safety training and crowd control scenes at festivals. Animate a slight sag between stakes in the wind, and the barrier feels temporary and real.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 14 November 2026
