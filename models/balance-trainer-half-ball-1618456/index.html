About This Model

The Balance Trainer Half Ball is an inflated rubber dome on a flat platform, used for core, balance and rehab workouts. Standing on the dome forces small muscle corrections, which is the whole point of the trainer. Its 17,780 polygons give the dome its soft curve and the platform its rim and side grips.

The rubber surface, hard base and printed branding are painted by five maps at 4096 x 4096. Its 18,309 vertices are unwrapped cleanly, and thanks to StemCell testing the rubber looks believable in each engine. Flipped dome side down, the trainer becomes a wobble board for advanced exercises.

Two companion versions cost $29: a Domyos Home Balance Trainer and a Home Half Dome Balance Ball. A row of these trainers along a studio floor sets up a group class scene quickly. This main trainer costs $29 too and dates back to 2020, serving gym interiors, fitness apps and physiotherapy guides.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2020 · Updated 14 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Balance Trainer Half Ball model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

How heavy is the Balance Trainer Half Ball mesh?

17,780 polygons and 18,309 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Our Sports & Recreation listings sit at a median of about 22,000 polygons, so 17,780 is lighter than the typical one.

What texture resolution ships with the Balance Trainer Half Ball?

5 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.
