About This Model

Priced at $169, the Dip Bars Workout Man is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 54,265 polygons and 56,018 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Real-world footprint is 86.2 x 101.35 x H171.85 cm, so it drops into a scene at correct scale without a rescaling pass. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
