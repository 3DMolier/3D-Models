About This Model

European Woman Street Style Rigged is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $149. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

At 42,364 polygons and 43,958 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
