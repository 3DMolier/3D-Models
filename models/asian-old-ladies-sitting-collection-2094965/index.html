About This Model

A collection of elderly Asian women in seated poses, useful for park benches, waiting rooms, family gatherings and street markets. Relaxed postures, folded hands and comfortable everyday clothing make each figure feel observed from real life. They fill benches and chairs naturally, adding quiet everyday life to the background of a scene.

The pack follows CheckMate guidance for scale and naming, so each woman sits correctly on standard furniture. Soft window light and warm skin tones work well together and keep the mood gentle and respectful. Small differences in hairstyle and clothing color stop a row of seated women from looking repetitive.

Two versions are offered for purchase: Asian Old Ladies Sitting Collection at $249 and the companion Asian Old Ladies Sitting Collection 2. Released in 2023, the set may be used royalty free in healthcare imagery, community projects and games. Senior care providers, hospital architects and filmmakers use these figures to populate lobbies, city parks and traditional tea houses.
