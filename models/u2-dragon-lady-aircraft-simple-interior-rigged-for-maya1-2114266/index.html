About This Model

The Dragon Lady high-altitude reconnaissance jet, with its glider-like wings, slim fuselage and a simple cockpit interior, rigged for Maya. The very long wings droop on the ground and need outrigger wheels on takeoff, a quirk that defines this spy plane.

A rig in Maya controls surfaces and gear, so landings, climbs and taxiing scenes can be animated directly. The simple interior shows the pilot's seat and panel, enough for a canopy shot without heavy detail. Produced to CheckMate norms, the aircraft has clean naming and scale for hangar or runway scenes.

Dated 2023 with no usage limits, it fits Cold War documentaries, aviation history content and military flight simulation. A pilot in a pressure suit walking to the jet at dawn makes a classic reconnaissance mission opener. Chase cars racing behind it on landing are a famous sight, and the long runway suits that scene. Silhouetted against a dark stratospheric sky, the slender wings look almost alien in a wide shot.
