About This Model

Priced at $9, the Flower Pots Collection is a finished Model Bundles & Sets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and architectural visualization. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
