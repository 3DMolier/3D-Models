About This Model

This set gathers several vintage film cameras with leather-wrapped bodies, metal dials and round lenses that recall classic photography. Each camera keeps its advance lever, shutter button and lens barrel as distinct shapes that hold up in close shots. Buying the group at once gives matching cameras instead of collecting single props from separate listings.

The models were made to the CheckMate specification, so scale, naming and geometry follow the inspection rules of TurboSquid. Dressed in worn leather and brushed metal, they suit a photographer's studio, an antique shop window or a collector's desk. Lens caps, straps and small viewfinder windows give each body a slightly different personality on the shelf.

The set has been on the market since 2015 and can be used royalty free. Interior designers use such cameras as decor on bookcases, and film crews place them in period scenes set decades ago. Game artists scatter them through attics and flea markets as pickable props or background clutter.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2015 · Updated 12 November 2026
