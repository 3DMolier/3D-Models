About This Model

Priced at $59, the Mobile Tiered Seating is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 156,660 polygons and 163,998 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
