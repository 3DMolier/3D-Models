About This Model

Pink Albino Axolotl is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 26,338 polygons and 28,880 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
