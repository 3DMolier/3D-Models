About This Model

A Texas vehicle license plate gives cars and trucks in your scene a specific place and a believable local identity. The stamped characters, mounting holes and slightly raised border match the way real plates are pressed from aluminum. Its flat rectangular shape fits front or rear bumpers on almost any vehicle model.

TurboSquid certified the plate at CheckMate Pro, which means correct scale and clean geometry for close shots. The plate surface can carry custom numbers, so every vehicle in a parking lot can be given its own registration. A slight bend or dirt along the bottom edge makes the plate feel weathered by miles of highway driving.

Introduced in 2015, the plate suits car configurators, road trip films, police chase games and street scenes set in Texas towns. Vehicle artists bolt it onto a pickup to place the story firmly in the American South. Scattered on a garage wall with other plates, it becomes decor for a roadside diner or workshop.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
