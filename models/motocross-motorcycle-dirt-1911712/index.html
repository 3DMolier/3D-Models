About This Model

Motocross Motorcycle Dirt is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. The mesh carries 367,238 polygons and 190,791 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.

2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
