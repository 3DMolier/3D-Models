About This Model

An open corrugated packaging box, this shipping carton suits warehouses, unboxing scenes and moving day stories. The 71,640 polygons and 71,642 vertices give the cardboard real thickness with its wavy inner layer visible along the edges. The top flaps stand open and slightly bent, so the box looks freshly cut rather than flat-packed.

Built to CheckMate specification, the carton fits the TurboSquid standard for clean production assets. A non-overlapping unwrap gives each flap its own space for tape, shipping labels and printed arrows. A brown kraft cardboard shader with fine fibers and slight edge wear makes the box look handled by couriers on a long route.

Released in 2025, the box suits e-commerce explainers, logistics renders and moving company scenes. Filled with packing paper and a product inside, it shows an order ready for delivery. Home office and storage room scenes stack it in corners, often half open with paperwork or old toys inside.
