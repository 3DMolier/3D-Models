About This Model

A chipmunk wearing a knitted snow hat, a playful winter character with striped fur and bright eyes. The 97,388 polygons shape the round cheeks, small paws and the woolly knitted hat with its pompom. Fur grooming carries the stripes along the back, so the classic chipmunk pattern reads even in a small frame. Its tail curls up behind the body like a question mark, which balances the silhouette against the bulky hat.

Scale follows CheckMate rules, and the 103,797 vertices are unwrapped without any overlaps. That makes it easy to change the hat color for a holiday theme without touching the groom.

A 2025 release, the character suits holiday greeting animations, children's stories, winter game levels and seasonal decorations. The royalty-free license lets it star in paid holiday projects as well as personal ones. Set it in falling snow with soft backlight, and the fur edges glow while the hat adds a spot of color.
