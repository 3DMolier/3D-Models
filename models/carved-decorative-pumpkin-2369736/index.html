About This Model

A carved pumpkin with a jagged grin and triangle eyes, ready to glow on a porch step or a windowsill on Halloween night. Only 3,648 polygons and 3,505 vertices shape the ribbed shell, the thick cut rim, the openings and the curly stem. Because the cuts go right through the shell, a light placed inside spills out through the face onto the ground.

Clean geometry built to CheckMate rules keeps the pumpkin and stem as separate, tidy pieces. Non-overlapping UVs allow orange skin, pale inner flesh and a slightly scorched rim around the carved face. Surrounded by dry leaves and corn stalks, it brings autumn atmosphere to any front porch render.

Its companion is the Halloween Pumpkinhead, and each of these two versions sells for $29. The pumpkinhead leans toward a costume or character prop, while this carving suits a quiet table centerpiece. Added in 2025, the pumpkin suits holiday animations, seasonal store displays and spooky game levels.
