About This Model

A long radar-guided air-to-air missile with mid-body wings and tail fins arms a fighter for beyond-visual-range combat scenes. The 40,591 polygons and 40,167 vertices go into the slender body, the radome nose, the control fins and the wing roots. Its narrow profile lets fighters carry several under the wings or inside weapon bays.

Mixed UVs separate the white body, the colored bands and the stencils, so markings can be changed easily. With StemCell materials in place, the missile renders without shader rebuilding in any supported package. At 730.22 x 75.04 x H75.04 cm, it fits the wing rails of a modern fighter aircraft.

Two versions cost $59 each: this Active Radar Air Missile and a Raytheon AIM-120 Air To Air Missile. Pick the Raytheon version for a specific weapon and this generic one for fictional air forces. This 2026 missile fits air combat games, defense graphics, flight simulators and military documentaries.
