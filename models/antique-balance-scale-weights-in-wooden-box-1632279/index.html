About This Model

The Antique Balance Scale Weights in Wooden Box is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. You are getting 2,427 polygons and 2,430 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
