About This Model

Vintage gas pumps with rounded tops, glass globes and mechanical dials bring the feel of a mid-century roadside station into a scene. Bright enamel colors and chrome trim make each pump a strong silhouette against desert highways or small-town streets. Lined up under a canopy with a parked classic car, the pumps set an era in one frame.

Each pump holds CheckMate Pro status, so the meshes open cleanly and match real proportions. Faded paint, rust around the nozzles and cracked hoses suit abandoned stations, while glossy finishes suit restored ones. The glass globes on top can glow softly at dusk, a classic image for nostalgic poster art.

The collection has two versions: this one at $69 and Retro Gas Pumps Collection 2 at $89. Mixing pumps from both versions keeps a station forecourt from looking like copies on a grid. Listed in 2024, the pumps serve road movie scenes, diner exteriors, classic car renders and retro games.
