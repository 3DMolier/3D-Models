About This Model

This collection of olive branches gives Mediterranean scenes their silvery leaves and, on some sprigs, small fruit. Several cut branches in one set let you vary arrangements in vases, wreaths and table settings. The slender leaves grow in pairs along thin stems, which is what makes olive foliage easy to recognize.

The sprigs are named neatly under CheckMate rules, so each one can be placed and rotated on its own. Leaves read best with a material that is dusty green on top and paler silver beneath. Backlighting through the foliage creates the warm glow of a Mediterranean afternoon in the countryside.

Available since 2025, the $39 set suits kitchen interiors, food styling scenes and wedding decor renders. Lay a branch beside a bottle of olive oil or tuck a few into a ceramic jug on a rustic table. Royalty-free terms allow commercial use, so the branches also work in packaging and restaurant visuals.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
