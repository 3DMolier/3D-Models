About This Model

Steel electrical conduit is the rigid metal tube that carries wiring along walls and ceilings in factories, garages and plant rooms. This piece shows a straight run of steel pipe with its threaded ends, ready to be joined with couplings and elbows. Its plain cylindrical form looks simple, yet a few runs along a ceiling immediately make a space look industrial.

The file is set up for Cinema 4D, and TurboSquid's CheckMate specification guides its geometry, scale and naming. Metal finishes are defined in the materials, so you can choose bright zinc, dull gray or painted red pipe. Exposed conduit is common in loft renovations, so it also suits interiors where services stay visible by design.

Priced at $19 and around since 2020, it serves warehouse renders, factory walkthroughs and technical training material. Electrical engineers lay it out along walls to plan routes before installation begins on site. Game artists line industrial corridors with it, where repeated pipes guide the eye toward the next doorway.
