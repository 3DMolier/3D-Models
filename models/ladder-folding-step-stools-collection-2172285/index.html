About This Model

This collection brings together folding step stools and small ladders, the kind kept behind a kitchen door or in a garage corner. Most are shown standing open, ready to climb, with treads and braces set at a working angle.

The set passed the CheckMate Lite review on TurboSquid, which checks that files open cleanly and carry sensible names. Different materials such as metal rails, plastic steps and rubber feet give each stool a distinct look in a lineup. Together they save the time of modeling several household ladders when a scene needs variety on the shelves.

The page covers two versions: the first collection at $34 and a second collection of folding stools at $44. Assembled in 2024, the sets serve home interiors, hardware store displays and maintenance scenes in animation. A stool beside a high cabinet or under a light fixture tells the viewer a small repair is about to happen.
