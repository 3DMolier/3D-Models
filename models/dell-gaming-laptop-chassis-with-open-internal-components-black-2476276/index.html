About This Model

Dell Gaming Laptop Chassis with Open Internal Components shows a gaming notebook with its bottom cover removed and internals exposed. The 1,294,460 polygons reveal the motherboard, cooling fans, heat pipes, battery pack and memory slots in fine detail.

UVs across the 1,294,092 vertices overlap in places, a sensible saving for repeated screws and chips. A StemCell build, it keeps identical PBR materials in every format, so the copper heat pipes shine the same way everywhere. Thin copper pipes snake from the processor to the fans, which helps viewers grasp how heat leaves the machine. Hinges and the keyboard deck stay intact, so the laptop still reads as a whole device when flipped.

A second version, Disassembled High-Performance Laptop with Cooling Fans, gives a generic alternative without the brand. Under Editorial Uses Only terms, it suits repair guides, tech reviews, news about hardware and teaching material. New in 2025, the open chassis works best in top-down shots where every component can be labeled.
