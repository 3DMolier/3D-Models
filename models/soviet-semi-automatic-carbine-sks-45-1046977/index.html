About This Model

The Soviet Semi Automatic Carbine SKS 45 is a production-ready Weapons 3D model, priced at $59 on TurboSquid. You are getting 20,098 polygons and 20,682 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
