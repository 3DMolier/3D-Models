About This Model

Great Blue Heron belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $199 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 30,934 polygons over 49,584 vertices - a light build for its class. It ships 44 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
