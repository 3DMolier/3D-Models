About This Model

Great Heron is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $199. Counted at the source, the model is 30,934 polygons and 49,584 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 44 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 402 x 734. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
