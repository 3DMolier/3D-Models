About This Model

A compact audio cassette resting in its clear plastic case, with two tape reels visible through the window. Audio Tape Box holds 29,025 polygons over 29,652 vertices, enough for the hinge, the screw holes and the teeth of the spools. Brown tape stretched between the reels is the detail that makes viewers of a certain age feel instant nostalgia.

Plastic shell, paper label and tape are textured with 6 maps at 2048 x 2048. The label area unwraps on its own, so you can write a handwritten track list or a band name on it. TurboSquid's review placed the cassette at CheckMate Pro, its top certification level for marketplace models.

Three versions are offered: this cassette and a Blank Audio Cassette Tape with Box at $49 each. A Vintage Audio Tape in the Box costs $39 and suits older, worn scenes. It has sold 21 copies since 2018 to radio stations, record shops and retro game designers.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2018 · Updated 14 November 2026
