About This Model

Metal Slatwall Accessories is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $24. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
