About This Model

A spinning reel with an open-face spool, folding handle and bail arm captures the gear anglers clip under a rod for lake and river trips. The Fishing Reel uses 27,262 polygons and 28,235 vertices for the drag knob, rotor and wound line on the spool. The handle folds and the bail arm flips, giving you options for realistic posed shots.

Anodized metal, the rubber grip and printed decals come from 6 maps at 2048 x 2048. A separate shell for the spool lets you change line color or tension without editing the body. The reel was cleared as CheckMate Pro, which vouches for tidy geometry and proper scale on a rod.

Around since 2015, the reel suits fishing shop catalogs, outdoor hobby videos and angling games. Tackle store designers display it on pegboards beside lures and line spools. Mounted on a rod on a misty dock at dawn, it sets a peaceful fishing mood straight away.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2015 · Updated 19 November 2026
