About This Model

A collection of permanent markers together with their retail package, suited to office desks, classrooms and stationery displays. The markers can be scattered across a desk or kept lined up beside the box they came in.

The CheckMate Pro mark confirms the geometry, UVs and naming passed TurboSquid's stricter inspection. Bright barrel colors make a cheerful accent in otherwise gray workspace renders and school interiors. Their short cylindrical bodies read clearly even when the camera sits far back from the desk.

Offered from 2022, the set suits back-to-school campaigns, stationery packaging mockups and office scenes in games. A whiteboard wall behind the markers instantly places them in a meeting room or lecture hall. Designers of packaging also use such sets to preview how a retail box looks on a store shelf. Several identical markers in a row make a tidy product shot, while a messy spread suggests a busy work session.
