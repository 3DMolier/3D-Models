About This Model

A collection of vintage electronic devices, the dials, knobs and chunky cases of a pre-digital household. Every object carries the visual cues of its era: wood veneer, chrome trim, toggle switches and glass displays.

The collection meets the CheckMate specification, so every device keeps a believable size relative to the others. Pieces are separate, letting you place one on a sideboard and scatter the others across a workbench or flea market stall. Materials are split by surface, so faded plastic, dusty wood and scratched metal can be tuned independently.

Available since 2024, it fits period films, retro game interiors and nostalgia-themed product shots for vintage shops. A single old device glowing on a dark shelf can carry an entire mood of memory and time passing. Museums and history channels rely on such collections to illustrate everyday technology from past decades. Antique dealers sometimes use renders like these to stage listings before photographing the real pieces.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
