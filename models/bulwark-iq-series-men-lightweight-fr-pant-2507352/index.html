About This Model

A pair of men's flame-resistant work pants, the lightweight protective trousers worn in oil, gas and electrical jobs. They are modeled in 8,850 polygons and 8,744 vertices, with pockets, seams and a relaxed straight leg.

Laid out, the pants measure 54.46 x 26.13 x H102.74 cm, a full-length adult size that fits an average male character. The branded label makes this an Editorial Uses Only model, delivered under the StemCell standard with mixed UVs.

Out since 2025, the garment suits industrial safety training, refinery worker characters and journalism about workplace protection. Hung in a locker beside a hard hat and gloves, it sets up a plant shift change before workers head out to the units. Safety courses can show it on a mannequin to explain why flame-resistant fabric matters on site. The plain cut keeps attention on the garment's purpose rather than fashion details, which suits instructional material about personal protective equipment.
