About This Model

A pair of worn bast shoes hanging by their cords, the woven bark footwear of traditional Slavic peasants. Frayed weaving, scuffed toes and loose fibers show long use, giving the pair a lived-in, rustic character. Hung from a nail or beam, they become an instant storytelling detail in old village interiors and farm sheds.

The model meets CheckMate requirements, so geometry opens clean and the pair stays easy to place. The plaited bark pattern is carved into the shape, which keeps the weave readable even in simple lighting. The hanging cords let you drape the shoes over pegs, fences, stove corners or a cottage wall.

First offered in 2025, the pair costs $39 and suits folk-themed projects of any size. Ethnographic museums, folk festivals and history lessons use it to show rural life in old Russia and Eastern Europe. Fantasy games, fairy tale animations and period films hang it inside huts, barns and wooden izbas.
