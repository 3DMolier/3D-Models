About This Model

Padded with wide armrests and a low back, this seat fits a cinema hall, a lounge or a waiting room. It uses 8,900 polygons and 4,615 vertices, which keeps a full row of seats affordable in a large interior. The cushions have soft rounded edges that catch light without looking heavy.

Geometry for this seat stays within the CheckMate specification, and each UV island keeps its own room. Fabric on the seat and the harder armrest material can carry different textures, which helps with realism. The chair sits square on the floor, so it lines up easily in rows or grids.

Two versions are on the page, the base Armchair and an Armchair for Cinema in black. Each one goes for $29, and the seat has been around since 2024. Theater interiors, hotel lobbies and office renders are places where this chair saves modeling time.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 14 November 2026
