About This Model

Reef Shark is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. You are getting 16,112 polygons and 16,864 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
