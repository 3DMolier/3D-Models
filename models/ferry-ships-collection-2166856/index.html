About This Model

This collection covers car and passenger ferries, the workhorse vessels behind island crossings, harbor scenes and coastal transport animations. Ferries share a recognizable shape, with tall boxy superstructures, open vehicle decks and bow or stern doors for loading cars. The set keeps those features legible, so a ferry still looks right at the far end of a busy port.

All ships meet CheckMate specification, with geometry, naming and pivots reviewed before the bundle went on sale. Shared scale across the vessels makes it easy to dock several at once along the same pier without manual adjustments.

Available since 2023, the ferries are bought for maritime simulation, port planning visuals, travel videos and ocean game environments. A ferry crossing at sunset, with cars queuing at the ramp, is one of the most familiar coastal images there is. Buying the group spares you the time of modeling these long hulls and layered decks one at a time. Transport planners also drop vessels like these into harbor masterplans to show berth use and passenger flow.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
