About This Model

Futuristic Sci Fi Energy Cell belongs to our Electronics & Gadgets range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualisation and medical sequences in film.

At 9,046 polygons and 9,022 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It ships 6 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
