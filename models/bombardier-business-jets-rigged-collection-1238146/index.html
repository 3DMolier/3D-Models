About This Model

This bundle covers Bombardier business jets with rigged parts, ready for scenes of private flights and executive travel. Slender fuselages, rear-mounted engines and T-tails set these aircraft apart from airliners when they sit on a ramp. Because they are rigged, gear and control surfaces can move during taxi and takeoff sequences without rebuilding the models.

TurboSquid approved the set as CheckMate Pro after reviewing topology, real dimensions and the naming of each part. Matching standards across the jets let a hangar render show several types side by side with equal detail. The aircraft are real products, so they come under Editorial Uses Only terms for news, documentary and educational projects.

At $369 the bundle suits aviation journalism, business aviation reports and training content for ground crews and pilots. Introduced in 2017, it also supports airport planning studies, simulator scenery and explainers about private jet operations. Animators place the jets on a fixed base operator apron to show how corporate flights are prepared before departure.
