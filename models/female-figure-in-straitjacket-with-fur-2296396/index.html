About This Model

The straitjacket is one of the few garments that reads instantly as a story rather than as clothing. Arms crossed and buckled, movement removed: it belongs to psychiatric history, to horror and to stage work, and adding fur turns it from a clinical object into a deliberately unsettling one.

The figure holds 71,133 polygons and 71,456 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplied as a static pose. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $169 it suits horror and thriller film work, game characters, theatre and costume previsualization, editorial illustration and concept art. On sale since 2025.
