About This Model

Twisted from long party balloons, these dog figures capture the classic shape every street performer makes for children. The 21,400 polygons and 21,455 vertices define the bulging segments, the tight twists and the knotted nozzle at the tail. Glossy rubber curves and pinched joints give the dogs a playful, slightly lopsided handmade character.

The 6 textures at 4096 x 4096 deliver saturated colors and the soft sheen of inflated latex. UV islands are separated cleanly, so new color combinations can be painted for each dog. TurboSquid inspected the set and confirmed CheckMate Pro quality for topology, scale and object naming.

On sale since 2016 at $19, the dogs suit birthday party scenes, kids animation and festive decor visuals. Event designers use them to plan tables and photo corners for children's celebrations. Artists inspired by pop sculpture also pose them on pedestals for glossy gallery-style renders.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2016 · Updated 14 November 2026
