About This Model

A film set reads as a film set because of the equipment scattered around it: a camera on a tripod, a monitor cart, light stands, a slate, cases and cable runs, with someone in a director's chair in the middle of it. A single camera model cannot produce that impression. This collection provides the figure and the kit together, which is what a production scene actually needs.

Buying the equipment as a set keeps the scale and the finish consistent across the whole scene, which is the detail that gives away a set dressed from separate sources.

At $199 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits film and television production visualization, studio and rental company marketing, media education material, behind-the-scenes illustration and games. On sale since 2025.
