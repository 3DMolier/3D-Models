About This Model

USA Passports Collection gathers American passport booklets with navy covers and the gold eagle seal, ready for travel and border scenes. Each booklet has the thin page block, rounded corners and embossed cover lettering that make it readable in close-up.

The set follows the CheckMate specification and opens with every passport named and placed for quick selection. Cover materials suit a leathery matte finish, so the gold stamping stands out under a soft desk lamp. Separate objects make it easy to scatter a few passports on a counter instead of placing the whole group.

Two versions are sold here, this set at $79 and a second, larger passport collection at $89. Released in 2024, the passports appear in airport check-in scenes, immigration desks, spy thrillers and travel blog visuals. Tucked into a jacket pocket or stacked beside a boarding pass, they make a scene about travel without words. Because the booklets are small, they also work as background props in hotel rooms and embassy offices.
