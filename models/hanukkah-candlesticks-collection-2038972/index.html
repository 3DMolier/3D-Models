About This Model

Hanukkah candlesticks gathered in one set, including menorah designs whose branches hold candles lit night by night during the festival. The tall central holder, the shamash, rises above the others, a detail any Jewish family would expect to see. Every candlestick is its own object, so one can stand in a window while another decorates the dinner table.

CheckMate Pro review from TurboSquid covered the topology and proportions of each piece. Polished brass or silver shaders suit the metal, and small emissive flames bring the candles to life. Golden light reflecting off the base adds a soft festive shine to any table setting.

Introduced in 2023 for $31, the set fits holiday greeting visuals, synagogue interiors and family celebration scenes. Educational videos explaining the festival often show the candles being lit in sequence from right to left. Placed on a windowsill at night, the glowing menorah becomes the warm heart of the shot.
