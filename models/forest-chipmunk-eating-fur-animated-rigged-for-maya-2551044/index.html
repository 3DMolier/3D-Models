About This Model

A forest chipmunk sitting up on its hind legs and nibbling food, groomed with fur and rigged in Maya for wildlife animation. The eating loop shows the small paws turning a seed while the cheeks puff, a gesture audiences recognize from park visits.

Dark stripes run down the back between pale bands, and the groom keeps them crisp even on such a small animal. The bushy tail rests behind the body as a counterweight, curling slightly upward with each quick bite of food. Controls live in Maya, so the fur and the animation open together in the package where they were built.

Released in 2026, this chipmunk fits nature documentaries, children's educational apps and cozy woodland game scenes. Place it on a mossy log beside fallen acorns, and the shot needs nothing more to tell its story. Its tiny scale also makes it a charming foreground detail in wide forest establishing shots and hiking trail scenes.
