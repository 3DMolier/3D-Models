About This Model

Priced at $99, the Road Bike Cube is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game environments. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.
