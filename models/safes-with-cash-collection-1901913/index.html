About This Model

A collection of steel safes with stacks of cash inside, built for heist, banking and wealth scenes where money needs to be seen. Heavy doors with dials and handles open to show shelves of bundled banknotes, so the content tells the story instantly. Different safe sizes cover everything from a hotel room box to a large office vault.

CheckMate Pro certification means clean, properly scaled geometry and well organized objects across the set. Doors are separate parts, so you can pose them closed, ajar or wide open for the reveal. Dark painted steel contrasts with green and beige money, giving the frame a natural focal point.

The Safes with Cash Collection has been on offer since 2022 with full royalty free rights. Crime films, banking explainers, finance articles and heist games use these safes for robberies and savings themes. Scattering a few bundles on the floor turns a tidy safe into the aftermath of a robbery.
