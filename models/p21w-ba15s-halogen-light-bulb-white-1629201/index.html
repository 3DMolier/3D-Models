About This Model

The P21W BA15S Halogen Light Bulb White is a production-ready Lighting 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags architectural visualization, event visualization and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. 2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits interior and architectural visualization, product rendering and game environment dressing.

At 3,022 polygons and 3,088 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 6 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
