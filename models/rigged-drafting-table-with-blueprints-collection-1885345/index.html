About This Model

A drafting table set with large blueprint sheets spread across the tilted top, ready for an architect's studio. The Rigged Drafting Table with Blueprints Collection brings the table and its paperwork together in one purchase.

The tilting board, the steel frame and the rolled and flat drawings give an office corner its working character. Blueprint sheets can be moved or removed, so the desk can look busy or freshly cleared for the shot. The set is built to TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, which covers clean topology and sensible object names.

It has been offered since 2022 as part of the bundles and sets range. It suits architecture office interiors, engineering classrooms, film sets about designers and game levels with a planning room. A desk lamp angled over the drawings and warm evening light make the scene feel lived in. Drafting tables also work well as background detail in workshops, garages and old newspaper offices.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
