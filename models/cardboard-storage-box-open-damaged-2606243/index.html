About This Model

An open cardboard carton with torn flaps, dents and crushed corners, built for warehouses, moving scenes and abandoned rooms. It uses 16,091 polygons and 16,034 vertices to sculpt the creases and ripped edges that a clean box lacks. The open top shows the interior, so it can hold props or sit empty after unpacking in a new apartment.

StemCell certification keeps the corrugated material consistent across engines and renderers of every kind. Bent flaps and a sagging side tell a story of rough handling, which is perfect for post-delivery or disaster scenes. Several copies rotated differently avoid the repetition of identical cartons stacked in a corner.

Two versions share the $39 price: the main carton and a taller High Corrugated Packing edition. Both arrived in 2026 with Royalty Free usage for any game, film or print project. Storage rooms, garbage alleys, delivery complaint videos and post-apocalyptic game levels all need cartons like these.
