About This Model

This set of ancient priest garments gives temple scenes a full robed outfit, with flowing layers and a ceremonial look. A light 7,744 polygons and 7,789 vertices keep the drapery simple enough for real-time characters. Long sleeves and a floor-length hem give the outfit a solemn vertical line that reads from far away.

Clothing modeled to CheckMate specification keeps clean topology, which helps when fitting the robes to a new body. Non-overlapping UVs leave space for embroidered borders, sacred symbols or aged linen textures. Gold trim against pale fabric is a classic choice that keeps the costume readable in dim temple light.

Ancient Priest Clothing has been offered as a separate garment set since 2024, used for historical films, religious history lessons, museum displays and fantasy games. Draped on a mannequin under torchlight, the robes suggest ritual and authority without a single word of narration. Cloth simulation runs quickly on such a light mesh, letting the hem sway naturally as the character walks through a temple.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2024 · Updated 14 November 2026
