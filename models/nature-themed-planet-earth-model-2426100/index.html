About This Model

The Nature Themed Planet Earth Model is a production-ready Space & Sci-Fi 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. You are getting 600 polygons and 602 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 30.34 x 30.36 x H30.89 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
