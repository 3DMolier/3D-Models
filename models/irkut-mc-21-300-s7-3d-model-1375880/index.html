About This Model

This Aircraft model - Irkut MC 21 300 S7 - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 146,741 polygons and 160,461 vertices. Maps: 12 of them, peaking at 8192x8192, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
