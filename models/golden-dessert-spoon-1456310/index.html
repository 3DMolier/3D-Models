About This Model

This Kitchen & Tableware model - Golden Dessert Spoon - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $7 on TurboSquid. You are getting 484 polygons and 482 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
