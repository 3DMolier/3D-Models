About This Model

Pumpkin Carriage is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. Counted at the source, the model is 64,291 polygons and 67,432 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 5 maps at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
