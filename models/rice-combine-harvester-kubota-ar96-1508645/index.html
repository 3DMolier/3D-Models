About This Model

A rice combine is built for flooded fields, and that changes the machine completely: it runs on rubber tracks rather than wheels so it does not sink, it is narrow enough for paddy bunds, and the header is set low for a crop that grows in standing water. Anyone who has seen one working will spot a wheeled combine dressed up as a rice harvester immediately.

The mesh is 198,198 polygons and 202,998 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 11 texture maps at 4096x4096, carrying the mud, the paint and the manufacturer markings.

A rigged build sits on this page at the same $99 for when the tracks move or the header runs. Aimed at agricultural machinery marketing, farming visualization, agritech presentation, rural scenes and games. Built to StemCell specification, on sale since 2020.
