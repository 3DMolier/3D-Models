About This Model

Tilt rotors solve the argument between hovering and cruising by refusing to choose. The S-A2 concept puts that arrangement on an electric airframe sized for a short city hop, with the lighting treated as part of the design rather than as regulation.

The model holds 148,210 polygons and 151,570 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in white with the lights on. It is built to CheckMate specification, and the library carries several rigged builds around it, including native rigs for Maya and Cinema 4D.

At $119 it suits future mobility visualisation, technology and transport presentation, advertising, science fiction film, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
