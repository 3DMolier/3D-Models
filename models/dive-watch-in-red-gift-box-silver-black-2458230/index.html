About This Model

Dive Watch in Red Gift Box Silver Black is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $89. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2026, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
