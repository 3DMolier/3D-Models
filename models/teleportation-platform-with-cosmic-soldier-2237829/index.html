About This Model

A cosmic soldier standing on a glowing teleportation pad, caught at the moment of arrival or departure. At 59,111 polygons the armored figure and circular platform stay light enough for game levels. Rings of light around the pad frame the soldier and tell the viewer instantly that something extraordinary is happening.

Every one of the 60,875 vertices maps to unique UV space, so emissive panels and armor wear can be adjusted. As a StemCell asset, its PBR materials carry over cleanly between Unreal, Unity and offline renderers. The bulky exosuit and helmet give the soldier a heavy, technological look that contrasts with the clean platform.

This sci-fi platform has been offered since 2024 at $199, royalty free for commercial use. Sci-fi shooters, space opera trailers, VR experiences and concept presentations use teleport moments like this. Add glowing particles rising from the pad and a dark room around it, and the arrival becomes cinematic.
