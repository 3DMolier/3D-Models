About This Model

The Luke Skywalker Lightsaber 2 Used 3D Model recreates a worn saber hilt with scratched metal, grip ridges and an emitter at the top. Wear marks around the activation switch and grip suggest years of use, which gives the hilt a story of its own. The slim cylindrical shape fits naturally into a character's hand or a collector display stand.

A CheckMate Pro rating on TurboSquid ensures correct scale, clean geometry and logical naming for the hilt. A glowing blade can be added with a simple emissive cylinder, while the hilt stays physical and metallic. Check the density in your scene before using several copies in one shot. Fine grooves along the grip section give hands something to hold onto, and they catch rim light beautifully.

In the catalog since 2015, the hilt appears in fan films, cosplay references and collector showcases. Illustrators place it on display stands, desks or in a character's belt when a sci-fi scene needs a recognizable weathered prop.
