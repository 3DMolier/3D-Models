About This Model

A bamboo hairbrush shaped for 3D printing, with a paddle head and a slim handle suited to eco-friendly bathroom scenes. Bamboo brushes have a warm, striped grain and a matte finish, and the shape follows those soft, rounded wooden contours. For printing, the bristle zone needs to stay sturdy, so expect a simplified pattern rather than fine strands.

It meets CheckMate expectations for a clean, closed file with tidy naming and sensible scale. Printed in wood-fill filament, the result looks surprisingly close to natural bamboo after a light sanding. The flat back offers an easy surface to place on the build plate during printing.

Available since 2024, it fits zero-waste lifestyle content, spa product mockups and maker projects at home. Lay it on a towel next to a toothbrush cup and the bathroom shelf reads as sustainable and calm. Beauty bloggers also like it as a neutral prop beside soaps and glass bottles.
