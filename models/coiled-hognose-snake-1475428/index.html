About This Model

The hognose is famous for bluffing. It flattens its neck, hisses, strikes with its mouth closed, and if none of that works it rolls over and plays dead convincingly enough to fool almost anything. The upturned snout it is named for is a digging tool.

The model is extremely light at 4,220 polygons and 4,225 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 6 maps all at 4096x4096, in a coiled pose. It carries StemCell certification, and red and brown builds are offered separately.

At $59 it suits wildlife and nature visualisation, education and documentary work, terrarium and pet material, game creatures, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.
