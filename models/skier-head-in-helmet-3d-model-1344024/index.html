About This Model

Looking for a Sports & Recreation asset? The Skier Head in Helmet is a finished, render-ready model at $69. Counted at the source, the model is 21,920 polygons and 22,958 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

11 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
