About This Model

A set of Tesla signs in several shapes, ready to mark showrooms, service centers and charging locations in a street scene. The Tesla Signs Set totals 8,830 polygons spread across the panels, letters and mounting posts.

With 9,338 vertices, the letterforms keep crisp edges that remain legible even at a distance. Each sign follows StemCell rules, so formats and materials stay consistent from one renderer to the next. The clean sans-serif styling reflects the minimal design language seen at electric vehicle facilities.

Use of the set is limited by its Editorial Uses Only terms, fitting news reports, documentaries and articles. It joined the catalog in 2026 at $49 and helps urban planners and architects model EV districts. Mount the panels on a building facade or line them along a parking area beside charging stations. Mixing several signs on one site makes a dealership feel complete rather than sparsely dressed.
