About This Model

Tesla Supercharger Station belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 978,906 polygons and 988,494 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.
