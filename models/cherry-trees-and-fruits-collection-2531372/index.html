About This Model

Cherry trees in leaf and loose ripe cherries brought together, so an orchard and its harvest come from one consistent set. The trees carry clusters of red fruit on thin stalks, which is the detail that tells a cherry from any other fruit tree. Single cherries with their stems work for close food shots, bowls and baskets, or fallen fruit scattered on the grass below.

Modeling met the CheckMate baseline for scale and organization, so the fruit sits on the branches at the right size for the trees. Repeat the trees in rows to build an orchard, then vary rotation and scale so the canopy never looks stamped. Sunlight through the leaves and the gloss of the fruit are what to light for, especially in a low summer evening.

Released in 2026, the set serves farm visualization, juice and jam packaging scenes and educational material about how fruit grows. Garden planners also show with these trees how a cherry will shade a patio once it reaches maturity in a few years.
