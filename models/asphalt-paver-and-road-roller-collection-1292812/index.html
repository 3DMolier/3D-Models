About This Model

Asphalt Paver and Road Roller Collection pairs the core machines of road surfacing, a tracked paver and a steel drum roller. The paver's hopper and screed spread hot mix, while the roller's heavy drums press it flat behind. Together they show a full paving crew sequence without hunting for matching equipment from different makers.

Both machines carry CheckMate Pro status from TurboSquid, a sign of clean geometry and sensible naming across every part. Drums, screed and conveyors are separate parts, so they can turn or move during a pass. Yellow safety paint and warning decals sit in materials for quick rebranding to any contractor fleet.

First offered in 2018, the collection suits infrastructure visualization, road construction explainers, contractor presentations and city building games. Working along a fresh lane at night under floodlights, the pair captures modern road repair in a busy city. Steam rising from hot asphalt adds atmosphere to low, close ground-level shots.
