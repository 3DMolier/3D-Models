About This Model

A plain laboratory beaker with straight glass walls, a pouring spout and printed volume markings up the side. Beaker 250ml is modeled as a clean glass shell with real wall thickness, so refraction and caustics behave correctly in a renderer. The spout bends gently outward from the rim, which is what makes the vessel read as a beaker rather than a cup.

The model holds a CheckMate Pro certificate for scale and tidy naming. Its simple geometry makes it an easy object to fill with colored liquid or boiling foam in a chemistry animation.

There is a single version on the page, priced at $19 and kept deliberately plain. Since 2015 the glassware has been purchased 18 times by teachers, pharmaceutical marketers and studios producing lab scenes for medical dramas. Group several beakers at different fill levels on a bench, and the experiment tells its own story.
