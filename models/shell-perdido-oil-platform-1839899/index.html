About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Shell Perdido Oil Platform is a finished, render-ready model at $199. Geometry weighs in at 760,470 polygons over 919,381 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
