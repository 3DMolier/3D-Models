About This Model

This emperor stands in the Prima Porta pose, right arm raised in address and weight resting on one leg, prepared as a printable solid. The breastplate carries the carved relief of the original, and the small Cupid on a dolphin beside the leg also braces the figure. Meant for a printer rather than a renderer, the surfaces are closed and the cloak folds stay thick enough to print cleanly.

Its geometry was prepared to the CheckMate rules for clean topology and believable proportions, so slicing software reads the shell without repair. Scale it down for a desk ornament or up for a garden piece, since the detail of the face holds at either size. Printed in resin, the bare feet and the drapery edge show the sculptor's intent better than any texture could on screen.

Dating from 2024, the file serves museum shops, classroom history projects and collectors who want a Roman emperor on the shelf. Miniature painters treat it as a base, adding the faded polychrome that ancient statues once carried in daylight.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
