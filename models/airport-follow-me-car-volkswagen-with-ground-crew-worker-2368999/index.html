About This Model

An airfield follow-me car with a roof sign and beacons, parked beside a ground crew worker in high-visibility gear. Airport Follow Me Car Volkswagen with Ground Crew Worker uses 262,029 polygons and 280,965 vertices across the vehicle body, light bar and figure. The checkered livery and lit FOLLOW ME panel make the car readable even at a distance on the apron. Amber beacons on the roof catch the eye in night scenes on wet tarmac.

Non-overlapping UVs leave clean space for airport insignia, dirt or reflective tape on the bodywork. CheckMate rules were applied to scale and naming, so the car and person sit together correctly on import.

Because the Volkswagen badge is real, the license is Editorial Uses Only, suiting news, documentary and educational work. It came out in 2025 at $289 and fits aviation simulators, airport documentaries and taxiway sequences in films. The worker can stand by the door or wave aircraft in, giving a ground handling shot its human scale.
