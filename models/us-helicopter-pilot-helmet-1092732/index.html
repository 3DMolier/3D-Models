About This Model

This Weapons model - US Helicopter Pilot Helmet - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 19,186 polygons and 19,936 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and aerospace visualization. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
