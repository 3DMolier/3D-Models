About This Model

A handheld inspection camera with a flexible gooseneck probe, the tool plumbers and mechanics push into pipes and engine bays. The rigged probe can bend and coil, so the camera can be posed snaking around a corner or down a drain. A small screen on the handle shows what the lens sees, which gives a clear focal point for close shots.

Construction follows CheckMate requirements for clean naming, scale and grouping, keeping the handle, screen and probe as tidy parts. Rubberized grips and a ribbed cable give the surface texture of a tool built for dirty workshops. Lit from the probe tip, the camera can even light its own close-up inside a dark pipe.

Three rigged versions are available: the Cinema 4D file used for the main build, plus separate rigs for Modo and Maya. Each rig uses that program's own controls, so posing the gooseneck takes minutes instead of rebuilding a bone chain. On the market since 2020, it fits plumbing tutorials, automotive repair guides and tool catalogs for hardware stores.
