About This Model

A grooved aluminum cabin suitcase with a telescopic handle and four wheels, built for airport, hotel and travel scenes. The Rimowa Original Cabin Twist Silver Game Ready stays at 13,484 polygons, light enough to fill an airport with rolling luggage.

Its 13,003 vertices define the parallel ridges across the shell, the detail most people recognize at a glance. StemCell certification gives it several native formats with materials set up once and kept consistent. The silver finish picks up reflections from terminal lights, so it rewards a glossy shader with clear highlights.

This 2026 suitcase model costs $59 and suits travel games, luxury interiors and departure lounge renders. Wheel it beside a traveler at a check-in desk, or rest it next to a hotel bed after arrival. The compact cabin size fits overhead bins, which makes it a natural choice for scenes set aboard a plane. Its rounded corners keep the silhouette friendly even in a low-poly game environment.
