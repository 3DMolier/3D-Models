About This Model

A solid-face padel bat with a perforated hitting surface and short grip, built for sports equipment, court and club scenes. The 12,343 polygons and 12,219 vertices define the teardrop frame, the drilled holes across the face and the wrist cord. Unlike a tennis racket it has no strings, so the drilled pattern carries the whole visual identity of the paddle.

UVs are laid out without overlaps, which helps when repainting frame graphics or adding scuffs from play. The model was built to CheckMate specification, following TurboSquid guidance on topology, scale and file organization. Licensed as Editorial Uses Only, it suits sports journalism, coaching explainers and educational material about the game.

Every one of the four versions costs $39: this blue model, two Padel Tennis Racket builds and a red Professional Padel Racket. The red one gives a warmer accent when several rackets appear together on a bench or wall rack. Introduced in 2024, the racket fits sports news graphics, padel club tutorials and coverage of the fast-growing game.
