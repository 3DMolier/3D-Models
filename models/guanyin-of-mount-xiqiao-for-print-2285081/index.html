About This Model

A printable model of the Guanyin statue on Mount Xiqiao in Guangdong, the towering figure of the bodhisattva of compassion. Flowing robes, a serene face and the lotus motifs typical of Guanyin imagery are carried into the printable shape. Guanyin is one of the most loved figures in Chinese Buddhism, and this statue is a major pilgrimage sight near Foshan.

The figure is set up as a closed, printable body, so a slicer reads it without the gaps that trouble render-only meshes. Fine folds in the robe come out best on resin printers, while filament prints suit larger, display-sized versions. Its geometry respects CheckMate guidance, which keeps the file tidy and quick to open.

On offer since 2024, it suits home altars, Buddhist temple shops, collectors of Chinese landmarks and tabletop dioramas. Finished in gold or patinated bronze, a print brings a calm, devotional presence to a shelf or a meditation corner. The print-ready file costs $89 and works for both physical prints and renders of the mountain shrine.
