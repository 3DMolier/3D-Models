About This Model

The Heavy Duty Construction Lift is a production-ready Industrial Equipment 3D model, priced at $89 on TurboSquid. You are getting 1,291,138 polygons and 1,316,528 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

65 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
