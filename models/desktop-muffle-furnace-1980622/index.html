About This Model

A benchtop muffle furnace with an insulated chamber, a hinged door and a digital controller, the workhorse of small materials labs. The 20,416 polygons shape the vented housing, the door latch and the ceramic chamber lining visible inside. Its 10,481 vertices, about half the faces, come from triangulated geometry that renders fast.

Instead of bundled bitmaps, the enamel housing and refractory lining get their look from editable materials. Every surface sits on its own unwrapped region, so warning labels and a glowing interior can be painted precisely. CheckMate Pro status here reflects a TurboSquid inspection of topology, real proportions and naming.

Arriving in 2022, the furnace fits laboratory interiors, dental workshops, jewelry casting studios and metallurgy lessons. Open the door and add an orange emissive glow to show samples being ashed or annealed at high heat. Lab managers sketch bench layouts with it to check clearance around hot equipment before installation.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2022 · Updated 17 November 2026
