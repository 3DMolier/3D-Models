About This Model

Boxer Man Pose 3 is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. You are getting 23,882 polygons and 25,105 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

17 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
