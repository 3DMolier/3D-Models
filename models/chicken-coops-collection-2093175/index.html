About This Model

A collection of backyard chicken coops, from small raised hutches to larger houses with ramps and wire runs. The Chicken Coops Collection totals 212,022 polygons and 110,588 vertices, with most detail in mesh fencing and roof boards. With far more faces than vertices, the wire grids are triangulated, a practical choice for fine mesh panels.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms clean scale and naming, so each coop can be pulled out of the set alone. Non-overlapping UVs let you weather the timber and add straw around the floors. Hinged doors and nesting boxes are shaped clearly, so close views do not look empty.

On sale since 2023, it suits farm game levels, rural homestead renders and garden design visuals. Place a coop at the edge of a vegetable garden with hens nearby, and the scene becomes a small farm. Grouped together, the coops also fill a poultry yard in an agricultural training video.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
