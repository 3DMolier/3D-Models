About This Model

Portable electric air compressors for workshop, garage and construction scenes, gathered so a tool area can be dressed at once. Tanks, pressure gauges, hoses and carry handles are the parts that make such machines read clearly at a glance. Several units side by side show the variety a real workshop holds, rather than one copy repeated on every bench.

A CheckMate Lite rating from TurboSquid covers the basics: files open properly, objects are named, and scale makes sense. Painted metal tanks and rubber hoses react differently to light, which keeps the compressors convincing in product shots. Each compressor stays independent, so you can drag one beside a car in a garage without bringing the rest.

The $99 set has a second version for $119 that adds further air compressor models to the lineup. Since 2023 the machines have fit tool store displays, DIY tutorials, auto repair scenes and construction site props. Run a hose from one of them to a nail gun or a tire, and the scene tells its own story.
