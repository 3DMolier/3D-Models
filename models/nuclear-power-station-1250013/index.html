About This Model

The Nuclear Power Station is a production-ready Industrial Equipment 3D model, priced at $109 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 248,436 polygons and 257,888 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 21 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2018 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
