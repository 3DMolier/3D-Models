About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Empty Coffee Cup is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Geometry weighs in at 1,361 polygons over 1,362 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
