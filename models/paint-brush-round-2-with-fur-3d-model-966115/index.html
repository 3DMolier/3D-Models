About This Model

A small round brush with a full belly of bristles and a soft point, a staple in every watercolor and acrylic set. Its handle and ferrule come to 816 polygons over 841 vertices, all quads, with fur supplying the bristles. The rounded head holds a lot of paint, and its compact size marks it as a brush for fine to medium strokes.

Fur bristles make the head look soft and absorbent, especially when lit from the side. UV islands on the wooden handle stay apart, so painted wood, chipped lacquer or a logo can be added. The brush earned CheckMate Pro after TurboSquid checked the quad mesh and how the file is arranged.

Its 2015 release has served watercolor lessons, school art rooms and stationery shop scenes. Rinse it in a glass of cloudy water beside a paint box for an honest, everyday studio moment. Royalty Free licensing allows it in commercial product visuals and in personal sketches.
