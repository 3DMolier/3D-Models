About This Model

This Kitchen & Tableware model - Silver Candlestick with Candles - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
