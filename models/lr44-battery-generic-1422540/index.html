The LR44 Battery Generic is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
