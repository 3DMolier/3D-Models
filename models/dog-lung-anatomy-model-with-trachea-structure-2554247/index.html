About This Model

An anatomical model of a dog's lungs and trachea, showing the lobes, the bronchi and the windpipe as one connected system. Its 25,927 polygons and 25,985 vertices shape the cartilage rings of the trachea and the soft rounded surfaces of each lobe. At 19.7 x 48.24 x H34.39 cm, the organs match the respiratory system of a medium to large dog.

The model was produced to CheckMate specification, keeping clean shells, accurate scale and properly labeled anatomical parts for teaching. Mixed UVs give the lungs room for vein detail while the repeating tracheal rings share texture space. Nearly identical vertex and face counts point to a clean quad mesh that subdivides smoothly for close renders.

At $89 it suits veterinary school lectures, animal hospital waiting room screens and pet health articles about breathing problems. Released in 2026, it also helps pharmaceutical companies explain respiratory treatments for dogs in clear visual terms. Veterinary courses, mobile apps and printed clinic charts can all use it, since the license is Royalty Free.
