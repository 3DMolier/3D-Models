About This Model

The River Tunnel with River Tram is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. You are getting 58,495 polygons and 60,168 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
