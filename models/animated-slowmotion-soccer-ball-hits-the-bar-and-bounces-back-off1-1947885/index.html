About This Model

A slow motion shot of a soccer ball striking the crossbar and bouncing back, ready to drop into a sports sequence. The 1,412,346 polygons go into the stitched ball panels and the goal frame around the point of impact.

CheckMate Pro certification puts it in the top quality tier, with clean topology and consistent naming. Non-overlapping UVs let you swap the ball pattern for a club or tournament design. The animation is already keyed, so the deflection plays straight out of the file without extra work. Vertex count sits at 722,114, a heavy file meant for hero shots rather than crowd scenes.

The page holds two versions: this slow motion take and a regular speed animated edition of the same strike. Sold since 2022, it suits sports broadcasts, football game cutscenes and coaching analysis videos. The crossbar moment is the near miss every fan remembers, which makes the clip strong for a title sequence.
