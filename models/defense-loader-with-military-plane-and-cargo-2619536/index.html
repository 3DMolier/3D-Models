About This Model

A full airbase loading scene with a military transport plane, its cargo and a loader working at the rear ramp. Most of the 3,568,994 polygons sit in the aircraft, from engines and landing gear to the open loading bay. Cargo pallets wait on the loader deck, aligned with the open belly of the aircraft.

Built to CheckMate specification, the file holds 3,646,342 vertices split into plane, loader and cargo parts. Gray military paint and tarmac grime are easiest to add with procedural layers, because UVs are incomplete. Separate parts make it simple to swap the plane livery or hide the cargo entirely.

The model joined the catalog in 2026 at $269 and suits defense documentaries, simulation training and flight line environments. The layout covers the full wingspan of the plane together with the ground equipment around its tail. Move the loader away from the ramp to show the moment before or after a cargo transfer.
