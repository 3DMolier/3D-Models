About This Model

A bread box with a selection of loaves in one set, made for kitchen counters, bakery shelves and breakfast scenes. The Rigged Bread Box and Bread Collection gathers the container and the bread so a counter can be dressed in one step.

Different loaf shapes and crust tones give a still life variety, so the counter does not look repetitive. Modeling follows the CheckMate specification, which covers real-world scale and tidy object naming. Because the bread pieces are separate objects, each loaf can be moved, rotated or removed while composing the shot.

Released in 2025, the collection costs $49 and covers both the container and every loaf. Kitchen interior renders, recipe apps and cooking game levels are the usual homes for these props. A bread box with its lid open on a wooden worktop instantly tells the viewer that someone lives there. Pair the loaves with a cutting board and knife to build a breakfast scene without searching for extra assets.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Rigged Bread Box and Bread Collection model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Can the Rigged Bread Box and Bread Collection model be used in a commercial project?

It ships with TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, so a single purchase covers commercial delivery - you do not pay again per project or per seat of the finished work.

What is the Rigged Bread Box and Bread Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Rigged Bread Box and Bread Collection mesh?

25,161 polygons and 25,144 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the roughly 28,000 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs low at 25,161.
