About This Model

An artist's paint brush with soft fur bristles, a crimped metal ferrule and a slim wooden handle. The fluffy tip is the star, catching light the way natural hair does when a brush is dry. It fits easels, art school desks and craft tables where brushes stand in jars or lie across wooden palettes.

CheckMate Pro certification signals that the model meets TurboSquid rules on topology, naming and scale. Dip the tip in a colored shader and the brush instantly looks freshly loaded with paint. A chipped lacquer handle and smudged ferrule add the character of a well-used studio tool.

Paint Brush with Fur was added back in 2015 and serves painting tutorials, art supply shops and studio interiors. Set it beside a half-finished canvas and open paint tubes to suggest an artist just stepped out. Makeup and craft visuals also borrow fur brushes like this one for soft blending close-ups.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
