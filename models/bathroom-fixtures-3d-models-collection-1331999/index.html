About This Model

A collection of bathroom fixtures, the sinks, toilets, faucets and tubs needed to furnish a bathroom from bare walls. Glossy ceramic curves and chrome taps are what a viewer judges first, so those shapes carry the realism.

Buying the fixtures together keeps their style and scale consistent, so a bathroom looks designed rather than collected piece by piece. CheckMate Pro certification ensures real dimensions, which matters when a basin must sit at proper counter height. Reflections in the chrome taps reward close-up shots, where cheaper fixtures would look like plastic.

Released back in 2018, the set has suited architectural visualization, plumbing supplier catalogs, hotel interiors and home renovation planning. Clean white porcelain under soft window light gives a fresh spa feeling in residential and hotel renders. Swapping chrome for brushed brass or matte black finishes adapts the same pieces to a new design trend. Small washroom layouts in offices and restaurants can be furnished just as quickly as a large master bath.
