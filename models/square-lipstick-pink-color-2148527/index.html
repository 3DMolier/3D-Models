About This Model

Square Lipstick is one of the Clothing & Accessories models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 2,546 polygons and 2,537 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
