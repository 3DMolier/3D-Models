About This Model

A rigged woman in everyday clothing at 15,941 polygons, which is game-engine weight rather than hero-render weight: light enough to put a crowd of them in a scene, detailed enough to hold up at conversational distance.

CheckMate Pro certification means correct human proportion and clean topology, which for a character is the difference between a model that deforms properly and one that pinches at the shoulders the first time you raise an arm. Non-overlapping UVs let skin and clothing be retextured independently.

Eleven versions share the page, and they are the practical range: dedicated Maya and Cinema 4D rigs, gardener variants, an elderly Chinese woman in kitchen and gardener styles, A-poses and a seated build. Priced at $199 into advertising, architectural visualisation with people, games and film.
