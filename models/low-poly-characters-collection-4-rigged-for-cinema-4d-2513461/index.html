About This Model

A group of stylized low poly characters gives indie games, mobile apps and explainer animations a ready cast of simple, readable people. Flat-shaded faces and blocky proportions keep the style consistent, so the figures mix well with low poly environments. Varied clothing and body types let a crowd look diverse even at the tiny size of a mobile screen.

Light meshes like these run smoothly in real-time engines, even with many characters on screen at once. The cast meets CheckMate specification with tidy topology, so retargeting animation is straightforward. Flat colors from simple materials keep the look clean and make palette swaps quick.

Both versions cost $64: the main build and the Low Poly Characters Collection 4 Rigged for Maya version. Choosing the version that matches your software saves setup time before animating the cast. Since 2026 the characters have populated casual games, educational videos, infographics and social media animations.
