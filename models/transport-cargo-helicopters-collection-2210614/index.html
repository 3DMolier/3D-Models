About This Model

A collection of heavy transport helicopters for moving troops, vehicles and slung cargo, grouped as one purchase for aviation scenes. Rotor heads, cargo ramps and landing gear define these aircraft, so they read clearly even from far away.

Every helicopter here was built to CheckMate specification, and shared scale keeps a line of aircraft properly proportioned. Separate machines in one pack can be parked, flown or loaded in different states within a single shot. The pack also saves the work of matching shading across aircraft bought from different sources.

Launched in 2024 at $169, the collection serves defense documentaries, disaster relief visuals and flight simulators. Show one lifting a container over a flooded town, and the scene explains humanitarian logistics at a glance. On a carrier deck or forward base, several helicopters together make the location feel active and staffed. Dust clouds under the rotors and a low sun complete the look, since these machines rarely land on clean ground.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
