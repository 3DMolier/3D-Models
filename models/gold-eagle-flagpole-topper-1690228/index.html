About This Model

This Signage & Decor model - Eagle Flagpole Topper - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $69 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 6,884 polygons and 6,787 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.
