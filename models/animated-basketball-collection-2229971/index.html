About This Model

Basketballs with ready animation, bouncing, spinning and swishing through, so a court scene moves without hand keying. The Animated Basketball Collection covers the motions sports graphics need most, from dribbles to free throws. The pebbled orange surface and black seams are what make a basketball read even when it is spinning fast.

The set is built to CheckMate specification, so every ball sits at regulation size beside players and hoops. Baked animation means the ball plays back the same in any scene, which suits broadcast templates. Squash and stretch on impact gives the bounce weight, a detail viewers feel even if they do not notice it.

Available since 2024, the collection serves sports broadcast intros, game menus and team social media clips. A slow spin on a fingertip or a ball dropping through the net makes an instant opening shot. Coaches and sports apps use animated balls to illustrate plays and shooting arcs in short tutorials.
