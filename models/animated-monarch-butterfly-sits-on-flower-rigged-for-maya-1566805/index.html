About This Model

A monarch butterfly with bright orange wings veined in black, resting on a blooming flower in a quiet garden moment. The Animated Monarch Butterfly Sits on Flower Rigged pairs the delicate insect with a simple bloom that frames it nicely. Thin legs grip the petals and the antennae curve forward, just as a feeding monarch holds itself.

White spots along the wing edges are part of the pattern that makes monarchs easy to identify, even from a distance. The pair was built to CheckMate requirements, so butterfly and flower share believable proportions. Thin wings catch backlight beautifully, letting the orange glow when the sun sits behind the subject.

Two versions are available at $149 each, one prepared for Maya and the other for Cinema 4D. The butterfly and the flower are the same in both, so the software you use decides the choice. On sale since 2020, it fits nature documentaries, garden center visuals, biology lessons and spring themed animations.
