About This Model

Priced at $39, the Pilot Control Cabin with Dual Seats Gray is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. First published in 2026, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

The mesh carries 30,348 polygons and 32,482 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
