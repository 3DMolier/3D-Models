About This Model

A winged cherub from the lid of the Ark of the Covenant, sculpted as a solid figure ready for 3D printing. The Ark of Covenant Angel Figure for 3D Print carries 1,033,618 polygons, preserving feathers and folds in fine detail.

There are 516,817 vertices, roughly half the face count, which is typical of a dense triangulated sculpt for printing. Approved to CheckMate standards, the mesh comes in clean and suitable for preparing a print job. The wings sweep forward and upward, the pose described for the two guardians that faced each other above the Ark.

First offered in 2026 at $29, it suits resin printing, miniature painting and religious history displays. Print a pair in mirror image to recreate the full lid arrangement, or paint a single angel in gold leaf tones. Collectors building biblical dioramas gain a centerpiece that holds detail even at small print scales. The high polygon count also makes it a strong hero object in close-up renders of ancient relics.
