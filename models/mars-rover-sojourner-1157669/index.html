About This Model

Looking for a Space & Sci-Fi asset? The Mars Rover Sojourner is a finished, render-ready model at $99. The mesh carries 320,979 polygons and 337,694 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

18 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
