About This Model

A collection of hand-powered lifting tools such as jacks, chain hoists and pulleys, for workshop and construction scenes. Ratchet handles, hooks and heavy chains show how the tools turn muscle into lifting force. Laid out on a garage floor, they make a workspace look ready for a heavy repair.

TurboSquid granted the set CheckMate Pro after reviewing its modeling quality and sizes. Painted steel and bare chain carry their own shaders, so red, blue or rusty finishes are easy. A single work lamp over the hoist hook throws strong shadows that underline the weight involved.

Released in 2022, Hand Powered Lifting Tools Collection suits mechanic training, industrial catalogs and garage games set in old workshops. Animators hang an engine from the hoist to show a lift, step by step, in a repair tutorial. Safety instructors include the tools in guides on lifting loads without injury.
