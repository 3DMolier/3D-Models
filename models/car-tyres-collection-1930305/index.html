About This Model

A collection of car tires with different tread patterns, for garages, tire shops and roadside scenes in visualization and games. Tread patterns differ from tire to tire, so each one shows distinct grooves and blocks. Sidewalls carry raised lettering areas and rim seats, details that read clearly in a close shot.

Under CheckMate Lite certification, each tire opens cleanly at correct scale, ready to mount on a wheel. Black rubber needs subtle roughness variation and a little dust in the grooves to avoid looking flat. Stack a few by a workshop wall, or line them on a rack for a tire store interior.

In the catalog since 2022, the set suits auto service renders, racing games, car configurators and seasonal tire change videos. A worn tire beside a new one makes a clear comparison image for safety and maintenance guides. Several patterns in one purchase spare you modeling treads, which is slow work to do well.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
