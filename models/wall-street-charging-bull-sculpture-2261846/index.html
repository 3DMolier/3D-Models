About This Model

Wall Street Charging Bull Sculpture is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $79. Counted at the source, the model is 14,940 polygons and 14,940 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
