About This Model

Flip Chart with Medicine Education Scheme belongs to our Other range and is offered at $24 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 35,866 polygons and 36,210 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
