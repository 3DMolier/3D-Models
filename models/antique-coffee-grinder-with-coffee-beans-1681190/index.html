About This Model

An antique coffee grinder with a hand crank on top and a drawer below, surrounded by scattered beans, sets a warm vintage table. The scene is heavy at 194,952 polygons and 194,944 vertices, and much of that goes into the individual coffee beans.

Wooden box sides, a cast iron hopper and a brass crank give the grinder its old general store character. The mixed UV layout shares space across the many beans while the grinder body gets its own texture area. TurboSquid reviewed it under CheckMate Lite, confirming that the files open cleanly and carry clear naming.

Two versions are presented, this grinder with beans for $31 and a manual coffee grinder with beans for $49. First sold in 2021, the model suits coffee shop interiors, farmhouse kitchens and barista tutorials. Lit by morning light through a window, the beans and the dark wood make a rich close-up for any coffee story.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2021 · Updated 14 November 2026
