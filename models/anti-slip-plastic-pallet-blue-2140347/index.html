About This Model

A blue plastic pallet with an anti-slip deck, this asset builds believable floors in warehouses, cold storage rooms and loading docks. The heavy 94,044 polygon budget, with 92,296 vertices, covers the ribbed top grid, the rubber grip strips and the ribbed legs underneath. Openings on all sides let a forklift enter from any direction, which matters for animated logistics shots.

TurboSquid certified the pallet as CheckMate Pro, so the topology is clean, the scale is real and the file is tidy. Scuffs, dirt and stenciled warehouse codes fit neatly onto the non-overlapping UV layout of the plastic deck. A slightly glossy blue plastic shader with darker rubber inserts shows the two materials clearly under factory lighting.

Released in 2023, the pallet suits food processing plants, pharmaceutical warehouses and supply chain explainer videos. Stacked in tall piles near a dock door, the pallets give a factory environment real depth and order. Game levels set in distribution centers also rely on them as cover and walkable platforms.
