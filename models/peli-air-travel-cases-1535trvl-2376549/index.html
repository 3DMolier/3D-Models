About This Model

A rugged wheeled hard case of the kind photographers and engineers carry on planes, with latches, a handle and wheels. Its 41,750 polygons and 41,856 vertices describe the ribbed shell, press-and-pull latches and telescopic handle. The squared, tough silhouette tells the viewer that something valuable and fragile travels inside.

Shell, latches and wheels sit on separate non-overlapping UVs, so wear marks can be added precisely. The model meets the CheckMate specification, so it opens at true size with clean geometry. Placed in an airport, a film set or the back of a van, it reads as professional gear.

Four variants share a $49 price: the Peli Air 1535TRVL, a crimson Peli Air case, a red air tight case and a generic travel case. The red ones stand out in a busy luggage scene, while the darker ones blend into technical settings. Introduced in 2025, it suits travel scenes, film crew setups and outdoor equipment visualization.
