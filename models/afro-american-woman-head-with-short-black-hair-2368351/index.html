About This Model

Afro American Woman Head with Short Black Hair is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
