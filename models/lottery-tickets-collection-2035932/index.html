About This Model

A small stack of scratch and draw lottery tickets, the paper slips people pick up at newsstands hoping for a lucky number. The 1,256 polygons give each ticket a thin paper body with slightly bent corners and crisp printed edges. Fanned out on a counter, the tickets read immediately as a bit of hopeful everyday gambling.

With 630 vertices, around half the faces, the set is extremely light for crowded shop scenes. CheckMate Pro certification keeps each slip at real paper size beside wallets, coins and pens. Non-overlapping UVs let artists print any game logo, number grid or scratch-off panel on the fronts.

The collection dates from 2023 and fits convenience store, newsstand and kitchen table scenes. Financial literacy videos, crime dramas and casual games use the tickets as props tied to luck and money. A single winning ticket held up to the light makes a strong storytelling moment in any film.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 13 November 2026
