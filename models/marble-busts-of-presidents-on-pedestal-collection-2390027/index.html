About This Model

A group of white marble busts of presidents, each mounted on its own pedestal, ready for a gallery, a library or a government hall. The carved faces, hair and draped shoulders carry the look of classical portrait sculpture rather than modern figurines. Pedestals lift the heads to eye level, which is how such busts actually stand in a museum corridor.

Marble is all about soft light, so subsurface scattering and a slightly polished surface make the stone believable. The busts follow CheckMate specification, sharing one scale so a row of them lines up without adjustment. Shadows under the brows and chin give each portrait character from a frontal camera angle.

Released in 2025, the set suits history documentaries, education scenes and stately interior visualization. Line them along a corridor or flank a doorway and an ordinary room gains the weight of an institution. Busts can also be separated and placed one at a time on a desk or a bookshelf.
