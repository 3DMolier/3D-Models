About This Model

Priced at $49, the Chocolate Garnished Cake with Physalis is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 72,158 polygons and 71,086 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 40.09 x 40.15 x H9.76 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
