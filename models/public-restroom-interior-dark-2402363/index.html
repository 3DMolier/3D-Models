About This Model

Public Interior is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $89. The mesh carries 327,859 polygons and 337,523 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
