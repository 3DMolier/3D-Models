About This Model

The AW189 is a medium twin-engine helicopter with a wide cabin, sliding doors and retractable landing gear. A dense 589,418 polygons and 647,401 vertices cover the rotor head, engine cowlings, cabin windows and the tail rotor. It is used for offshore crews, search and rescue and corporate transport, so it suits many kinds of scenes.

Livery, glazing and metal surfaces are spread over 13 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. UV islands are laid out without overlaps, so a coast guard, police or airline livery can replace the default scheme. CheckMate Pro inspection backs the geometry, which matters for a mesh this dense.

Of the 6 versions, the static builds cost $149, including corporate transport and medium lift variants. Rigged helicopters for animated flights cost $169 for the medium lift build or $199 for the others. Since 2017 the helicopter has been flown in 26 projects, from offshore rig scenes to flight simulators.
