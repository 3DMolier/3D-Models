About This Model

A rusted naval gun behind a curved armor shield, the kind once mounted on warship decks and coastal batteries. The Rusty Naval Artillery with Shield Simplified uses 225,593 polygons across the barrel, breech, mount and protective plate.

Its 1,399,533 vertices far exceed the face count, a sign of split edges that keep hard corners crisp. The CheckMate build ensures the gun arrives with clean geometry and correct proportions for a deck mount. Corrosion along the shield and barrel suggests a weapon left exposed to salt air long after its service ended.

This 2026 gun model sells for $29 and suits war museums, historical games and abandoned fortress scenes. Mount it on a crumbling coastal platform, or set it on the deck of a decommissioned ship. Simplified modeling lets you line several guns along a battery without overloading a wide shot. History teachers explaining naval warfare can use it to show how gun crews were protected.
