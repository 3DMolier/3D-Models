About This Model

Priced at $39, the Hand of Dark Skinned Woman Middle Finger Sign is a finished model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 3,488 polygons and 1,806 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
