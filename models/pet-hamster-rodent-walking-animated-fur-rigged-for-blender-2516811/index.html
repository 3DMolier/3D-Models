About This Model

A pet hamster with groomed fur trotting along in a walk cycle, rigged for Blender and ready for home scenes. Short legs, a round body and a twitching nose give the hamster the busy, bumbling walk owners know well. The walk loops cleanly, so the pet can wander across a cage floor, a desk or a child's hands.

Hair, rig and walk follow CheckMate specification, opening in Blender as one tidy package. Soft fur reacts well to warm light, which makes close-ups look cuddly rather than stiff. Paths and speed are easy to adjust, so the walk can follow any route through a room.

New for 2026 at $179, it suits pet shop visuals, children's programs and animal care tutorials. Pair it with the idle golden hamster and the wheel runner for a full cage of behaviors. Games set in family homes use small pets like this as charming background activity near the player.
