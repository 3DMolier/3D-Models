About This Model

Looking for a Tools asset? The Empty Plastic Spray Bottle is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The mesh carries 5,950 polygons and 5,960 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
