About This Model

Electric Ship belongs to our Ships & Boats range and is offered at $169 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 535,792 polygons over 558,182 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 31.44 x 130.45 x H24.5 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
