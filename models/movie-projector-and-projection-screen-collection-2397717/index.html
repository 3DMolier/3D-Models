About This Model

A collection of movie projectors and projection screens, covering both vintage reel machines and modern home cinema setups. Projectors with lenses and reels stand alongside screens on tripods or walls, so a full screening can be staged. Reels and lenses show the mechanics of film, which matters in scenes about cinema history.

Made under the CheckMate specification, the collection keeps clean topology and believable proportions. Metal housings, film reels and matte screen fabric give each piece its own surface under projector light. Each object is separate, so a projector can be placed at the back of a room while the screen hangs at the front.

Listed in 2025, the collection suits cinema interiors, school classrooms, home theaters and nostalgic film scenes. A beam of light crossing a dark room toward the screen is one of the most recognizable images in film culture. Event planners also use the set to visualize outdoor screenings in parks and courtyards on summer nights.
