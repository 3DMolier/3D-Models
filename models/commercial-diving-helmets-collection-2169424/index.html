About This Model

Commercial diving helmets in this collection show the sealed shells, face ports and air hose fittings used by underwater workers. Polygon totals are not listed, yet the valves, communication fittings and neck dams are shaped as real parts. The helmets differ from old brass diving helmets, with modern fiberglass shells built for industrial work.

TurboSquid confirmed the bundle under its CheckMate Lite level, which checks that files open and load correctly. Each helmet is its own object, so one can sit on a deck while another goes onto a diver's head. Paint and metal parts can be tuned for a brand new helmet or one worn by years at sea.

Royalty Free since 2023, the set suits offshore industry videos, underwater films and diving training materials. Oil rigs, harbors and salvage operations all need commercial divers, and these helmets show their gear. Museum displays on diving history also use the models to compare old and modern equipment.
