About This Model

Ice cleats are an elastic harness carrying steel spikes or coils, stretched over a boot rather than built into it. Shown mounted, the harness is under tension and follows the sole contour, which is quite different from the flat ring the product is in its packaging.

The mesh is light at 9,350 polygons and 9,494 vertices in quads and tris. The UVs overlap, sensible on a subject built from repeated spikes and a symmetrical harness, and the StemCell build means it works in a real-time engine.

At $19 it suits winter and outdoor equipment visualization, footwear retail renders, safety and workplace material, hiking and mountaineering imagery, and games. On sale since 2026.
