Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Large Bucket-Wheel Excavator Bagger 293 Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $249. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
