About This Model

Looking for a Architecture Landmarks asset? The Baseball Stadium is a finished, render-ready model at $249. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, event visualization and product rendering. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

Counted at the source, the model is 5,622,748 polygons and 5,968,391 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
