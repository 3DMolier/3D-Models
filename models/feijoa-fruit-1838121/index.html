About This Model

This feijoa is the oval green fruit also called pineapple guava, with a waxy skin and a small dried flower tip. Its 8,934 polygons and 4,471 vertices shape the slightly lumpy egg-like body and the crown of sepals at the tip. Its matte dull green color and modest size make it look humble beside showier tropical fruits.

Inspectors assigned the fruit CheckMate Pro status, confirming clean geometry and tidy naming throughout before release. Separate UV islands allow freckled skin detail and a subtle color shift from deep to pale green along the body. Cut a second fruit open beside it, and viewers can imagine the soft cream-colored jelly inside.

Launched in 2022, the feijoa suits exotic fruit guides, smoothie recipes and New Zealand food illustrations. Arrange several in a woven bowl on a kitchen table, and an autumn harvest scene in the southern hemisphere takes shape. Grocery apps and cooking games also add feijoas to fruit shelves for regional variety and color.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2022 · Updated 19 November 2026
