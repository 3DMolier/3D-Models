About This Model

The streaming stick turned any television into a smart one and made the manufacturer's own software irrelevant, which is why the device is sold at a loss. The remote is the actual product: it is what the user touches and where the brand lives.

The set is very light at 10,866 polygons and 11,673 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $39 it suits product and technology visualization, e-commerce and packaging imagery, advertising, living room and interior scenes, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.
