About This Model

Old Dish Cloth Red is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $9. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

Geometry weighs in at 16,664 polygons over 8,334 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
