About This Model

Looking for a Architecture Landmarks asset? The Modern Fire Station Building is a finished, render-ready model at $99. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.
