About This Model

This AI Computing Motherboard is a dense server board with large accelerator chips, memory modules, power stages and heavy cable connectors. Its 311,905 polygons and 356,326 vertices are all quads, describing capacitors, sockets, heatsink mounts and fine pin rows. At 25.43 x 30.5 x H3.34 cm the board matches real server proportions for data center rack scenes.

Assembled to CheckMate specification, the mesh uses mixed UVs, so repeated components share texture space while chips get unique labels. Gold contacts and black chip packages make a striking macro shot under cool blue lighting. Quad-only topology makes the board easy to bevel or subdivide when the camera moves in for extreme close-ups.

An Nvidia GB300 Computing Mainboard is the second version, sold at the same $99. Launched in 2026, the board suits AI infrastructure explainers, tech journalism, data center visualizations and hardware tutorials. Slide it into a rack with glowing status lights to show the engine behind modern artificial intelligence.
