About This Model

This Kitchen & Tableware model - Glass Kettle - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $12 on TurboSquid. You are getting 9,783 polygons and 9,768 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
