About This Model

A collection of human embryos from the first trimester, showing how the body takes shape in the earliest weeks. Each stage differs in size and form, from a curled body with limb buds to a recognizable tiny human outline. Seeing the stages side by side is the point, because growth reads best as a sequence.

CheckMate Pro certification gives every embryo in the set clean topology and consistent naming. Grouping logic stays the same throughout, so a single stage can be isolated for a close shot quickly. The large head relative to the body is the proportion that makes an early embryo read correctly on screen.

Available since 2021, the embryo set suits prenatal education, obstetrics lectures and pregnancy app timelines. Royalty free licensing lets clinics, publishers and documentary makers use it in paid work. Arrange the stages along a timeline and a whole week-by-week development story is ready to animate.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2021 · Updated 13 November 2026
