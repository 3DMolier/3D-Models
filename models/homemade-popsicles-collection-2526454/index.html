About This Model

A set of homemade popsicles on wooden sticks, the kind poured into molds from juice, yogurt and fruit pieces at home. Each pop has a slightly uneven surface and a rounded tip, which keeps the set from looking factory-made. Grouped on a tray or a plate, they make an instant summer dessert scene for a kitchen or a patio.

The bundle follows CheckMate specification, so scale, naming and object structure stay consistent from one pop to the next. Frosted, semi-translucent ice reads best with subsurface scattering, while fruit inside can carry a stronger color accent. The wooden sticks give a matte contrast that keeps the colorful treats grounded in the frame.

The Homemade Popsicles Collection was released in 2026 for food blogs, recipe videos and warm-weather scenes. Drop a few into a cooler or melt one onto a napkin, and the composition gains a playful, lived-in feel. Kids' party tables and beach picnic renders use sets like this to add color without extra modeling.
