About This Model

Plywood Numbers Set is a detailed general asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. The mesh carries 7,776 polygons and 7,414 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
