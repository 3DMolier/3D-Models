About This Model

The mosquito is the deadliest animal on earth by a wide margin, and it is the female doing it: she needs the protein to develop eggs. The proboscis is not a needle but a bundle of tools, some of which saw while others inject an anticoagulant.

The model is very light at 9,270 polygons and 9,288 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 7 maps all at 4096x4096, in the feeding pose. It carries StemCell certification.

At $69 it suits medical and public health visualization, education and documentary work, pest control and pharmaceutical material, advertising, game assets and editorial illustration. On sale since 2017.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2017 · Updated 4 September 2026
