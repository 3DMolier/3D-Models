About This Model

Dark Red Kidney Beans in a Spoon shows a wooden spoon heaped with glossy dried beans, a staple food close-up for recipes and packaging. The spoon and beans use 9,162 polygons and 8,819 vertices, enough for the curved bowl and each bean.

Ten texture maps at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 cover the bean skins, the wood grain and the spoon surface. The spoon set holds CheckMate Pro certification, so the marketplace has signed off on its topology, scale and naming. A mixed UV layout shares texture space between similar beans while keeping the spoon unique.

Two versions are offered at $39 each: this dark variety and the Light Red Kidney Beans in a Spoon Red. Laid on a rustic table with scattered beans around it, the spoon makes a natural hero shot for food content. Food stylists have had access to it since 2020, and one of them has already bought it. Recipe websites, food packaging, nutrition content and rustic kitchen scenes are all natural fits for this spoon.
