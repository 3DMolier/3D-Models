About This Model

The Multipurpose Workbench is a production-ready Tools 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 258,069 polygons over 259,972 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
