About This Model

Tow Trailer Towing Cable is a detailed Tools asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. The mesh carries 8,048 polygons and 8,068 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
