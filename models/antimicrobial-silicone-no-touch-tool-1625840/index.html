About This Model

A pocket no-touch tool with a silicone tip, this small gadget presses elevator buttons, opens door handles and taps screens without fingers. Only 1,072 polygons and 1,070 vertices are needed for the flat key-like body, the hook end and the soft rubber nub. The hook catches door pulls while the tip handles keypads, and both ends read clearly in a close shot.

The tool ships as a TurboSquid StemCell model, with PBR materials prepared once and converted for several major renderers. Five maps at 4096 x 4096 deliver the brass or plastic body, the silicone tip and a faint antimicrobial coating sheen. Non-overlapping UVs make it simple to recolor the tool or print a small company logo on the flat grip.

Created in 2020, the tool suits hygiene explainer videos, public transport scenes and product catalog pages. Shown pushing a lift button or pulling a restroom door, it explains its purpose without any captions. Keychain scenes and everyday carry flat lays also feature it beside keys, cards and a small flashlight.
