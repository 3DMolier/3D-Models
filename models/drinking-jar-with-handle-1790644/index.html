About This Model

A mason-style drinking jar with a molded glass handle, ready for cocktail menus, cafe tables and summer drink close-ups. The 4,316 polygons go into the ribbed body, the threaded rim and the thick join where the handle meets the wall. That join matters, since a thin seam makes the handle look glued on rather than formed together with the glass.

CheckMate Pro inspection confirms the jar was modeled at actual size with tidy topology, so it holds a believable volume beside straws, lemons and bottles. Clean UVs accept tinted glass, frosted finishes or a printed logo panel without remapping the surface. Filled with lemonade or iced coffee, the jar works as a hero object or as background dressing on a bar counter.

The page pairs the plain Drinking Jar with Handle with an Estilo Mason Jar Mug at an equal polygon count. The second build follows a recognizable retail product, which helps when a client asks for a familiar shape. Introduced in 2021, the model serves beverage visualization, restaurant interiors, kitchenware e-commerce and food photography mockups.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2021 · Updated 25 November 2026
