About This Model

A collection of vintage baseballs with faded leather, darkened red stitching and scuffs from decades of play. Old balls carry history, whether displayed in a glass case, on a collector's shelf or in a dusty attic. The raised stitches are modeled, so the seams catch light properly when a ball rotates in a close shot.

Made to CheckMate specification, the balls share one size and clean topology for smooth subdivision. Varying wear levels between balls make a group look like real memorabilia rather than copies.

Three versions are offered across this family: this vintage set, a second collection and a fourth collection of old balls. Combined, they fill a sports museum display, while one set suits a desk or trophy shelf. A worn glove and a wooden bat beside them complete a classic Americana still life. Out since 2025, the balls serve sports memorabilia renders, baseball history documentaries and nostalgic interiors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
