About This Model

The Bowl with Water Gel Beads is a production-ready Toys & Games 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. You are getting 8,928 polygons and 4,750 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
