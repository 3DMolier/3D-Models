About This Model

A collection of colored plasticine sticks, rolled shapes and small sculpting tools, made for craft tables, classrooms and kids' hobby scenes. Bright clay pieces sit beside spatulas and cutters, so the set already tells the story of a modeling session. Some sticks are still wrapped in their neat rows, while others are already squashed and blended by small hands.

Soft rounded forms contrast with the straight tool edges, which keeps the collection readable even from a wide angle. CheckMate Pro certification means each object was checked for scale and naming before release, so pieces drop into a scene at the right size. Every item is separate, so a half-made figure can be built from the loose clay.

Part of the catalog since 2021, the bundle suits art class interiors, preschool learning material and stop-motion inspired visuals. Spread across a plastic mat with fingerprints pressed into the clay, it makes a creative table look busy and genuinely played with.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2021 · Updated 12 November 2026
