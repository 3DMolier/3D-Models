About This Model

The Bicycle Lamp is a front light that clamps to the handlebar and throws a beam ahead on dark roads. Its 8,416 polygons rest on 4,351 vertices, keeping the lens, reflector and mounting bracket crisp even at small size on a handlebar. A bullet-shaped housing makes the lamp read as classic cycling gear rather than a modern LED strip.

Separate UV islands let the chrome reflector, glass lens and painted body each get distinct materials. It passed CheckMate Pro review, so the lamp arrives with clean geometry and real scale. A bright emissive lens turns the lamp into a light source for night riding scenes.

A Bicycle Dynamo joins the lamp on this page, also at $24, as its second version. Together the lamp and dynamo form a complete old-school lighting setup for a city or touring bike. Sold since 2022, it suits bike shop renders, cycling safety guides and urban commuting scenes.
