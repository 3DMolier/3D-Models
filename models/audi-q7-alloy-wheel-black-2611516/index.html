About This Model

A black multi-spoke rim with tire, taken from a premium SUV and ready for configurators or garage scenes. The part uses 10,128 polygons and 10,876 vertices, so the spokes, lug nuts and tire sidewall stay sharp in close-ups. Deep concave spokes give the rim its sporty, three-dimensional face when lit from the side.

CheckMate certification guarantees clean geometry and a centered pivot, which matters when the rim needs to spin. Dark finish on the spokes contrasts with the rubber tread, and the brake gap behind stays readable. It can lean against a garage wall, sit on a tire rack or replace stock rims on another car.

Two versions share the $39 price: the black Audi Q7 piece and the SUV Multi Spoke Alloy Wheel. Both arrived in 2026, and Editorial Uses Only restrictions apply because the design comes from a real Audi. Tire shop interiors, car configurators, automotive journalism and racing games are where it fits.
