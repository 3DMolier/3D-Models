About This Model

Amphibious ATV Rigged belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

Counted at the source, the model is 446,143 polygons and 229,242 vertices, a mid-weight load for a scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
