About This Model

A replica of the English football league trophy, with a golden crown on top and silver lion handles on each side. The Barclays Premier League Trophy has 68,276 polygons over 34,645 vertices, triangulated for the crown and lions.

The base carries a band of malachite-style green stone, which contrasts strongly with the polished silver body. The UVs never overlap, and StemCell certification lets the metal shaders match across several renderers without rework.

It is a branded trophy, so the license covers editorial uses like sports news, previews and documentaries. Lifted above a celebrating squad or set on a plinth in a studio, it anchors end-of-season broadcasts. Football video game menus and fan channel thumbnails also feature it under dramatic spotlights and smoke. Its tall, slender outline photographs well against confetti and stadium lights in a wide celebration shot. Since 2022 the cup has attracted 11 buyers, chiefly sports graphics designers and broadcasters.
