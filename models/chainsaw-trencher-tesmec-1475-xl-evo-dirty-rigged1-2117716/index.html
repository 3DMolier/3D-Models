About This Model

A Tesmec chainsaw trencher cutting deep trenches through rock and soil, finished dirty as it looks after a working shift. The long digging boom with its cutting chain dominates the outline, while tracks carry the weight below. Mud on the tracks and dust on the body make the machine look used, not freshly painted for a showroom.

Being certified at CheckMate Pro level, the trencher arrives at true size for a pipeline or cable laying site. Hard daylight across the boom picks out the chain teeth, which is the detail engineers look at first. Spoil piles along the trench line complete the picture and hide where the cut begins in the ground.

Available since 2023, the machine suits pipeline project presentations, telecom cable visuals, operator training and heavy equipment games. Engineering firms use it to show trench depth and route before crews arrive on site. Pair it with an excavator and a pipe stack, and the whole construction sequence becomes easy to follow.
