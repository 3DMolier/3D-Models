About This Model

A zebra walking at a calm pace gives savanna scenes a relaxed grazing animal, ideal for documentaries and wildlife backgrounds. Its fur adds depth to the striped coat, so the pattern looks soft and natural in both sunlight and shade. Prepared for Blender, the walking cycle suits herds drifting across grassland or gathering at a waterhole.

The asset satisfies CheckMate specification, with topology, naming and structure reviewed by TurboSquid staff. Stripe patterns are unique to each zebra in nature, so varying the texture between copies keeps a herd believable.

Published in 2026, it suits safari park renders, children's nature shows, conservation videos and open world games. A slow walk beside a waterhole at sunset is one of the calmest wildlife shots an editor can place. Pair it with the running version or other plains animals to build a lively African landscape quickly. Teachers explaining camouflage often use zebras, because the stripes confuse predators watching a moving herd.
