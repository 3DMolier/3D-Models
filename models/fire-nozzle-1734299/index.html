About This Model

A brass and aluminum fire hose nozzle with a pistol grip and bail handle, the business end of every firefighting scene. The Fire Nozzle carries 16,906 polygons and 17,846 vertices, so the knurled grip ring and coupling threads stay readable in close shots. The bail lever on top opens and shuts the flow, a shape firefighters recognize instantly in training footage.

UV islands sit apart from each other, ready for polished brass, red anodizing or scuffed chrome. Surface maps are not supplied, which keeps the finish open to match a specific fire department. Holding CheckMate Pro certification, the nozzle loads with consistent topology and plainly named parts.

A 2021 release, it appears in fire safety training, emergency service recruitment videos and rescue game levels. Attach it to a coiled hose beside a hydrant, and a fire truck compartment looks fully stocked. Product designers comparing nozzle types can also render it beside other couplings in a clean studio setup.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2021 · Updated 19 November 2026

Can the Fire Nozzle model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

What is the Fire Nozzle model typically used for?

Stated applications are technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

How clean is the geometry on the Fire Nozzle model?
