About This Model

The Human Leg Lymphatic System is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. You are getting 16,320 polygons and 16,495 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
