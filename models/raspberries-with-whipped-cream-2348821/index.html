About This Model

Raspberries with Whipped Cream is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. You are getting 983,846 polygons and 1,301,320 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
