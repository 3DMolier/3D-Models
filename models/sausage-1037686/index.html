About This Model

A single plump sausage with a smooth, slightly curved casing works as a breakfast side, a grill item or a deli display. Its simple form reads instantly, from a close-up on a fork to a long shot of a diner plate.

The asset meets the CheckMate Lite standard, which confirms it opens cleanly in common packages with sensible naming. The skin shows the gentle wrinkles and pinched tips that real links get after cooking in a pan. A glossy shader with a little subsurface scattering makes it look juicy rather than plastic under warm light.

Priced at $19 it has been in the catalog since 2016 and has found 33 buyers along the way. Food photographers mock up menus with it, while game developers use it to stock kitchens and market stalls. Duplicate the link to fill a frying pan, or slice one open beside mustard for a menu board. Paired with a bun and fried onions, it also completes a hot dog stand at a street fair.
