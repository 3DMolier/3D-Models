About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Lemur Fur is a finished, render-ready model at $139. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 11,072 polygons and 11,495 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
