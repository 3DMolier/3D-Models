About This Model

The Tentacles is a production-ready Animals & Creatures 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 12,163 polygons and 12,164 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. 4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game assets. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
