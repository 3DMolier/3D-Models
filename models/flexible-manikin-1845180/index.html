About This Model

Flexible Manikin is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 42,008 polygons and 21,307 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
