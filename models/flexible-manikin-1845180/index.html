About This Model

Flexible Manikin is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. You are getting 42,008 polygons and 21,307 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2022 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
