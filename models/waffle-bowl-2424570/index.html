About This Model

Waffle Bowl is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

Geometry weighs in at 268 polygons over 270 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
