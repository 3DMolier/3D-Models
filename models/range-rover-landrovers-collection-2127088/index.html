About This Model

Several Range Rover and Land Rover models in one set, covering the boxy luxury SUVs that rule country lanes and city streets alike. The Range Rover Landrovers Collection gives a scene a matching fleet without buying each vehicle separately. High rooflines, floating roofs and clean flanks are the cues that make these vehicles recognizable at a glance.

The set holds CheckMate Lite certification, a basic review that confirms the files open and match their previews. Consistent build quality across the collection means the vehicles sit together naturally in one parking row. Paint colors can be changed per vehicle, which turns the set into a varied fleet for traffic scenes.

Both marks belong to the manufacturer, so the collection is licensed for Editorial Uses Only. Available since 2023, it suits automotive journalism, film backgrounds and country estate driveway visualizations in architecture. Driving games and simulators use these SUVs to populate upscale neighborhoods, ski resorts and rural roads.
