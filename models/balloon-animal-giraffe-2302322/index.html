About This Model

A twisted balloon giraffe with a long neck and bulb-shaped legs, this playful figure brightens birthday parties and children's events. Its 4,112 polygons and 4,123 vertices form smooth inflated segments joined by tight pinched twists. The glossy rubber look and tall neck make it read instantly from across a party room.

Made to TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, the giraffe offers clean geometry and believable size. Each balloon segment has room on the UV layout for spots, color swaps or a slight latex sheen. A soft subsurface shader gives the balloon a pleasing translucency against bright backgrounds, and the glossy latex catches every highlight.

Party planners can pick from two giraffe versions at $19 each, this Balloon Animal Giraffe and the Balloon Giraffe 3D Model. Released in 2024, it fits kids' party scenes, carnival visuals and festive greeting cards. Line several balloon animals in different colors along a table to create a cheerful birthday centerpiece that guests notice first.
