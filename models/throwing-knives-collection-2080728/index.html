About This Model

A set of throwing knives with flat, balanced blades and slim handles, several profiles laid out together for choice. Each knife is cut from a single piece of steel, so blade and handle share one continuous silhouette. Their thin profiles make them light in a scene and easy to fan out across a table.

The knives carry CheckMate Pro certification for clean geometry, consistent scale and clear naming across the set. Separate objects let you stick one into a wooden target while the rest lie flat on a workbench.

The collection has been sold since 2023 at $29 for the whole group. Game designers equip assassins and circus performers with these blades for thrown weapon animations. Film props departments can preview a target board act, with knives framing a silhouette on painted wood. Collectors' display renders also suit them, laid in a neat fan on dark velvet under a single lamp.
