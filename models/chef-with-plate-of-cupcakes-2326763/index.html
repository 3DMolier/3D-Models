About This Model

A pastry chef presenting a plate of decorated cupcakes, ready for bakery, cafe and dessert scenes in film or games. Much of the 103,831 polygons goes into the swirled frosting, the paper liners and the chef's pleated uniform. Frosting is modeled as real piped peaks, so it holds highlights the way buttercream does under a soft light. Each cupcake sits slightly apart from its neighbors, so a hand can lift one off the plate in an animation.

Real-world scale follows CheckMate guidance, so the plate sits naturally at chest height in the chef's hands. The 107,044 vertices are laid out without UV overlaps, letting you recolor the icing or add sprinkles in texture.

Available since 2024, it suits bakery interiors, cooking channels, birthday party scenes and confectionery explainers. Its royalty-free terms keep things simple for client projects ranging from cafe menus to children's games. Pastel lighting and a blurred display case behind the chef finish the sweet, friendly tone.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2024 · Updated 16 November 2026
