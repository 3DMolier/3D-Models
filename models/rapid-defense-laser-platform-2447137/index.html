About This Model

This Weapons model - Rapid Defense Laser Platform - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $139 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 125,598 polygons and 140,807 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 458.02 x 615.48 x H367.32 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
