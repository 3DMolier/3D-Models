About This Model

The Male Surgeon Mediterranean 4 is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $69 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 15,460 polygons over 15,619 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
