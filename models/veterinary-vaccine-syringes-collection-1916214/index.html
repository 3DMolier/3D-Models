About This Model

This collection gathers syringes used for vaccinating animals, the tools a vet or farmer carries through a barn or a clinic. Clear barrels with printed scales, plungers and capped needles give each piece a recognizable medical outline. Some designs suit pets on an exam table, others fit livestock work where a sturdy grip matters.

The CheckMate Lite rating confirms usable geometry, with each syringe ready to place as its own object. Transparent materials work best here, letting light pass through the barrel and pick out the liquid level. A clean white surface and soft overhead light give the scene the sterile feel of an animal clinic.

Released back in 2022, the syringes suit veterinary clinic websites, farm health guides, pet care apps and agricultural training videos. Animators use them in scenes about livestock vaccination, while educators show them in lessons on animal health. Placed beside a vial and a cotton pad, a single syringe makes a clear icon for vaccination reminders.
