About This Model

A young zebra foal with long legs and a fluffy mane, this printable figure makes a sweet figurine or nursery decor piece. Foals have shorter muzzles and oversized ears compared with adults, which gives this figure its gentle charm. The closed, printable body has sturdy legs set apart, a stance that stands steadily on a desk.

The CheckMate specification guided construction, keeping the scale and file structure consistent for slicing software. Painting stripes by hand or using a black and white filament change brings the pattern to life. Digital renders work just as well, with a striped texture and soft fur shading on the mane.

Released in 2025, the foal pairs naturally with an adult zebra print for a mother and young display. Children's rooms, safari-themed parties and biology classes are natural homes for the little animal. Home printing, gifts and digital illustrations all fall under its Royalty Free terms.
