About This Model

A collection of grain staples arranged in small heaps and containers, made for pantry shelves, market stalls and kitchen counters. Each portion shows loose kernels piled into soft mounds, the shape dry goods take when poured from a sack. Owning the group in one file saves hunting for separate cereal props when a food scene needs variety.

TurboSquid rated the bundle CheckMate Pro, meaning the meshes are clean and the scene opens with tidy naming. Kernel colors range from pale cream to golden brown, which helps different grains read apart even in a wide shot. Shaders can be warmed or cooled slightly to match the lighting of a rustic kitchen or a modern store.

Introduced in 2021, the collection serves recipe videos, nutrition infographics and farm to table stories in documentaries. Lined up in glass jars on an open shelf, the grains give a cooking scene a well-stocked, homely feel. Health food store interiors in games and renders also benefit from the bulk-bin look.
