About This Model

Herbivorous Dinosaurs Collection 2 Rigged brings plant-eating giants with long necks, armored backs and horned heads. Each dinosaur has a rig for neck, tail and legs, ready for grazing, walking or defensive animations. Herded across a prehistoric plain or fern forest, they recreate the peaceful side of the Mesozoic world.

CheckMate-level organization gives every creature clean names and joint chains that animators can read at a glance. Wrinkled skin, bony plates and scutes carry enough detail for documentary-style close passes along the body. Earthy greens and browns blend the herd into vegetation while still reading against the sky.

This herd comes in two versions: a Cinema 4D build at $368 plus a Maya edition at $382. Both builds contain the same species, so pick the one that matches your animation software. New in 2026, the dinosaurs suit museum films, paleontology lessons, theme park media and adventure games.
