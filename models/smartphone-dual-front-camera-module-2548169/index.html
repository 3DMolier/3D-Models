About This Model

This Electronics & Gadgets model - Smartphone Dual Front Camera Module - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $39 on TurboSquid. You are getting 21,027 polygons and 22,496 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

6 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
