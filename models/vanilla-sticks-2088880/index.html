About This Model

The Vanilla Sticks is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 6,624 polygons and 6,634 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
