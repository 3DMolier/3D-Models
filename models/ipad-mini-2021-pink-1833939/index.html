About This Model

iPad Mini 2021 is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $34. Counted at the source, the model is 11,997 polygons and 12,344 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and product rendering.
