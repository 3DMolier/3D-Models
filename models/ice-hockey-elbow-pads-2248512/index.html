About This Model

Priced at $49, the Ice Hockey Elbow Pads is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 22,500 polygons and 23,208 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
