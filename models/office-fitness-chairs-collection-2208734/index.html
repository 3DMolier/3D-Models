About This Model

A collection of office fitness chairs, the ball seats and active stools that keep workers moving while they sit at a desk. Stability balls on rolling bases, kneeling chairs and wobble stools show the different approaches to an active workday. Their unusual shapes immediately signal a modern, health-conscious office in any interior render or workplace illustration.

The chairs share realistic proportions, so they slide under standard desks and match seated character height. Rubber balls want a matte, slightly dusty finish, while metal bases and wheels take a brushed sheen. Thanks to CheckMate specification, every chair is a separate, cleanly named object ready for an office layout.

Introduced in 2024, these chairs fit ergonomic office design, coworking renders, wellness blogs and corporate health videos. A row of these seats in a startup office makes the company culture visible before anyone speaks. Physiotherapists can use them to illustrate posture tips and the benefits of moving while working.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
