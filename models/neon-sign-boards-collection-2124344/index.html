About This Model

A collection of neon sign boards with glowing tube lettering and shapes, made for bars, shops and night street scenes. Each sign is a separate piece, so you can hang one over a door or fill a whole alley with them. The tubes follow bent glass paths mounted on backing boards, just like real handmade neon signs.

The set carries CheckMate Pro certification, TurboSquid's highest grade for clean technical files. Neon looks best with an emissive shader and bloom, and the tube color can be changed per sign. A soft glow on the wall behind each board makes the light feel real in a night render.

The collection arrived in 2023 and groups several related signs into one purchase. Cyberpunk games, nightlife district scenes, retro diners and music videos all use neon to set a mood quickly. Flicker one letter off and on, and the sign adds a story to a quiet street.
