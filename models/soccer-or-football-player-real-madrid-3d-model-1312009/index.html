About This Model

Priced at $149, the Soccer or Football Player Real Madrid is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 14,131 polygons over 14,701 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

20 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
