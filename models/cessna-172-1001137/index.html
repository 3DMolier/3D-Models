About This Model

Cessna 172 is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $119. The mesh carries 352,276 polygons and 366,569 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
