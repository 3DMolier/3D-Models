About This Model

A hoodie is defined by how it hangs. The hood collapses into folds behind the neck, the kangaroo pocket pulls the front panel slightly, and the ribbed cuffs and hem gather the fabric in a way that jersey never does elsewhere on the garment. This model builds those folds into the geometry, which is what stops it reading as a flat cutout with a hood attached.

The mesh is deliberately light at 10,014 polygons and 9,999 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 2 texture maps at 4096x4096. Sportswear rarely needs to be heavy: it needs to look right on a figure and to be repeatable across a crowd.

A grey version sits on this page at the same $49. Both suit apparel retail and catalog renders, sportswear advertising, fashion visualization, character set dressing and games. On sale since 2020.
