About This Model

This reconnaissance drone has one of the most unusual wing layouts flying: a joined wing, where the forward and rear surfaces meet at the tips to form a closed diamond. The arrangement is structurally efficient and aerodynamically unusual, and it makes the aircraft identifiable from any angle at a glance.

The mesh is efficient at 71,380 polygons and 75,842 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the wing junction, the intake and the sensor fairings.

A rigged build sits on this page at $99. The static model at $79 covers defence and reconnaissance visualization, news and documentary graphics, strategic analysis material, education and games. On sale since 2022.
