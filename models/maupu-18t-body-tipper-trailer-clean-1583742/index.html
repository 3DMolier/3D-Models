About This Model

Priced at $69, the Maupu 18t Body Tipper Trailer Clean is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 151,369 polygons over 158,557 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
