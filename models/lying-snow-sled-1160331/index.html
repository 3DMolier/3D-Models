About This Model

Looking for a Toys & Games asset? The Lying Snow Sled is a finished, render-ready model at $29. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 13,538 polygons and 13,686 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 17 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
