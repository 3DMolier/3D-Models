About This Model

A snowboarder carves downhill in this animated Maya model, leaning into turns that throw powder from the board's edge. A bulky jacket, snow pants, helmet and goggles give the rider the authentic look of a modern mountain athlete. Knees bent and arms balancing, the pose reads instantly as speed and control on a steep slope.

The rider follows CheckMate specification, with body, board and gear kept as separate pieces in the scene. Maya users can retime the carve or chain it into other moves for a full run down the mountain. Bright alpine sun with deep blue shadows on the snow gives the scene crisp winter clarity.

Royalty free and dating from 2026, the carver suits ski resort films, winter sports games and action sports videos. Resorts and snowboard schools can illustrate technique by slowing the turn and showing body position frame by frame. A follow cam behind the rider as snow sprays toward the lens makes thrilling footage.
