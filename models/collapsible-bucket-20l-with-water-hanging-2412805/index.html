About This Model

Looking for a Containers & Storage asset? The Collapsible Bucket 20L with Water Hanging is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 3,820 polygons and 3,828 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 30.87 x 30.87 x H52.14 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.
