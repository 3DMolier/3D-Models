This Medical model - Portable Medical Oxygen Mask Face Mask - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
