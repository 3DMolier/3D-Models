About This Model

This Food & Beverages model - Traditional Eggnog Bottle - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
