About This Model

Caravans Collection is one of the Model Bundles & Sets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $189. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. The set covers several related objects at once, which saves assembling a scene from separate purchases.
