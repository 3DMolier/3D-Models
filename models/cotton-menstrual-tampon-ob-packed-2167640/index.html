About This Model

Priced at $19, the Cotton Menstrual Tampon OB Packed is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 5,696 polygons and 2,937 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
