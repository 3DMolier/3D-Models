The Russian Air Force Su-34 is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. You are getting 151,636 polygons and 160,987 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
