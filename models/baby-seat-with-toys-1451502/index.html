About This Model

A baby bouncer seat with a soft fabric cover and a toy bar arched over the front, made for nursery and family scenes. Its 37,672 polygons and 39,190 vertices shape the padded seat, the frame and the hanging toys above the child. The reclined angle and the dangling toys are what tell a viewer at once that this seat is meant for an infant.

Eleven texture maps at 4096 x 4096 cover the fabric pattern, plastic toys and the metal frame. Non-overlapping UVs make recoloring easy, so the cover can switch from pastel blue to soft pink in minutes. The seat carries CheckMate Pro status, a sign that TurboSquid checked the mesh and the file before release.

Dating from 2019, it appears in nursery interior renders, parenting app screens and family scenes in animated films. Placed beside a sofa with a blanket on the floor, it makes a living room feel like a home with a newborn. Childcare educators also feature it in slides about safe seating positions for babies in training material.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2019 · Updated 14 November 2026
