About This Model

Priced at $19, the Broken Glass Bottle is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 12,969 polygons and 12,421 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
