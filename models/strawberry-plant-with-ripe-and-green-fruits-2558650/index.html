About This Model

Strawberry Plant with Ripe and Green Fruits is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. You are getting 56,721 polygons and 62,466 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
