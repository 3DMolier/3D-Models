About This Model

Gas Pipeline is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. Geometry weighs in at 40,238 polygons over 39,172 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
