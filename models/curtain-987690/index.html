About This Model

Priced at $29, the Curtain is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 41,638 polygons and 41,586 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 4 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2015 · Updated 4 September 2026

The price is $29 USD. TurboSquid handles payment and delivery; the license is Royalty Free, so the cost does not repeat per use.
