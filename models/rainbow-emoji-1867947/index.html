About This Model

Priced at $24, the Rainbow Emoji is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 966 polygons and 1,006 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
