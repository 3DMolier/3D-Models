About This Model

Boeing 767-400 with Interior Generic is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $349. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. The listing dates back to 2018, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 4,029,426 polygons and 4,142,029 vertices, a heavy load for a scene. Maps: 55 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published August 2018·Updated 20 August 2026
