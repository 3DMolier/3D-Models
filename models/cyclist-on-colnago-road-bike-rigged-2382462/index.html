About This Model

This Characters & People model - Cyclist Road - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $249 on TurboSquid. You are getting 223,309 polygons and 200,132 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
