About This Model

Aardvark Basic Pose is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $79. Geometry weighs in at 16,337 polygons over 13,560 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
