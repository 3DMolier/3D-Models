About This Model

The NUX NDR 5 Atlantic Delay and Reverb is a stompbox that adds echoes and ambient space to an electric guitar signal. Its 13,334 polygons and 13,817 vertices model the control knobs, the footswitches, the jack sockets and the sloped metal enclosure. At 13.52 x 14.63 x H7.3 cm it sits at true size on a pedalboard beside tuners and overdrives.

No UV shells overlap, so the printed control labels and the painted housing stay crisp in a close-up. Made to the CheckMate spec, the pedal arrives with logical part names and accurate scale. Patch cables running in and out of the jacks quickly make it look wired into a live rig.

This pedal and a Stereophonic Guitar Pedal with Delay and Reverb are the two versions, each at $29. The unbranded version helps when a scene needs a generic effects pedal without product labels. Introduced in 2025, it serves music gear catalogs, studio interior renders, rehearsal room scenes and band video sets.
