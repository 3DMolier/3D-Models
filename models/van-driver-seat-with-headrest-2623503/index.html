About This Model

A padded van driver seat with an integrated headrest and side bolsters, ready for cab interiors and vehicle conversions. The Van Driver Seat with Headrest uses 5,647 polygons for the cushions, seat frame and adjustment levers.

There are 5,756 vertices, keeping the cushion seams and curved backrest smooth from the driver's door view. Made to CheckMate requirements, the seat lands at real dimensions with a clean structure for placement in any cab. Firm bolsters along the sides suggest long hours on the road, a detail that suits delivery and work vans.

This 2026 seat sells for $39 and suits van interiors, camper conversions and driving simulator cabs. Bolt it into a delivery van cockpit, or show it on a workshop floor before installation. Upholstery shops can preview fabric choices on the model before cutting new covers. Light geometry lets you fill a fleet of vans with full interiors without a heavy scene.
