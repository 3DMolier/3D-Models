About This Model

A collection of dog houses in different styles, from classic pitched-roof kennels to modern designs for backyards and gardens. Each house has a rounded entrance sized for a dog, so pets in the scene read at the right scale. Several shapes in one file let you vary yards across a street or a whole neighborhood.

TurboSquid certified the set CheckMate Pro, its tougher grade for geometry and file setup. Kennels come as individual objects that can be placed, recolored or weathered one at a time. Wood planks and roof shingles respond well to painted, natural or worn finishes.

It has been available since 2019 at $29 for the full group. It suits backyard renders, real estate visualization, pet product advertising, suburban film sets and family games with pets. Next to a food bowl and a chewed ball, any of these kennels tells the viewer a dog lives here.
