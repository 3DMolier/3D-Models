About This Model

A chihuahua curled in a plush dog bed, with real fur on the tiny body and oversized ears that define the breed. At 11,665 polygons the mesh is light, because the fur system, not the geometry, delivers the softness.

There are 16 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 covering coat color, eyes, nose and the bed's fabric. CheckMate Pro certification confirms real-world scale, so the dog sits at believable size beside sofas and slippers. The 12,096 vertices leave plenty of budget for the fur, which is where render time actually goes.

Priced at $159 since its 2020 release, it suits pet product visuals, veterinary content and cozy interiors. Place the bed by a sunny window, and the dog becomes the warm focal point of a living room. Pet food and accessory brands often favor small breeds like this for friendly packaging imagery. The deep bed rim frames the dog nicely, which helps in square crops for social posts.
