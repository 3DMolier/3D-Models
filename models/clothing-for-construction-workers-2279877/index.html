About This Model

Clothing for Construction Workers is one of the Clothing & Accessories models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $89. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 37,300 polygons and 38,435 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
