About This Model

Several metal sewing buttons in classic shapes, from domed shank buttons to flat four-hole discs, gathered for garment and craft scenes. Together they use 5,408 polygons, enough to bevel every rim, drill each thread hole and round the shank loops. Their 5,448 vertices barely pass the face count, which points to clean, separate closed pieces rather than one merged lump.

Each button unwraps without overlapping islands, so brass patina, engraved crests or brushed nickel finishes can be painted individually. The set meets TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, so scale, naming and the scene hierarchy are already in order when you open it. A polished metal shader with a hint of anisotropy gives the rims the tiny highlights seen on real tailoring hardware.

Released in 2025, the collection supports fashion design mockups, tailoring tutorials and close-up shots of coats, jeans and blazers. Scatter them across a cutting mat beside scissors and thread, or attach them to a jacket placket in a cloth simulation. Game artists use these small parts as loot items, uniform details or sewing kit props in crafting interfaces.
