About This Model

The Apple iMac all-in-one desktop, with a thin aluminum stand, edge-to-edge glass and the computer hidden behind the display. Its tapered profile is famously slim at the edges, a detail that reads well from side angles. The silver chin below the screen and the curved foot instantly identify the machine on any desk.

CheckMate Pro certified, the computer keeps a correct size next to keyboards, mice and desk lamps. Replace the screen image with any interface, photo or text to set the scene you need. Brushed aluminum and glossy glass make the display respond beautifully to soft studio light.

Its Editorial Uses Only license makes it right for tech reviews, news articles and editorial illustrations about computing. In the catalog since 2015, the iMac sets the look of design studios, offices and home workspaces. Writers comparing desktop computers use renders like this to show proportions without arranging a studio photo shoot.
