About This Model

The Women Restroom Symbol is a production-ready general 3D model, priced at $3 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 284 polygons over 288 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
