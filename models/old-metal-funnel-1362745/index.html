About This Model

Priced at $19, the Old Metal Funnel is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 2,078 polygons and 2,094 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 3 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
