About This Model

Priced at $13, the Mockup Classic Tea Bag with String and Label is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 4,429 polygons and 4,413 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 3.96 x 4.88 x H5.03 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography, film & TV VFX and game assets. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
