About This Model

A collection of everyday hair brushes, from paddle shapes to round styling brushes, gathered for bathroom and salon scenes. Each brush shows dense bristle rows and comfortable handles, so the set looks right on a vanity or in a stylist's hand. Having several types in one file saves the work of modeling and matching each brush separately.

The collection carries CheckMate Pro certification, meaning TurboSquid inspected its geometry and organization before sale. Glossy plastic handles and soft rubber cushions respond nicely to bright beauty lighting. Bristles look best with a slight rim light that separates them from a dark backdrop in close beauty shots.

Available since 2018, this second brush collection has 15 sales behind it. Those buyers work on salon interiors, beauty product mockups and bathroom renders of every kind. Lay them out on a towel beside a hair dryer for a busy styling station in a salon.
