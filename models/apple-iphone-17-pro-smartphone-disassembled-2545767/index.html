About This Model

A disassembled phone only works as an asset if the parts are genuinely separate objects that could go back together. Display assembly, battery, logic board, camera module, speakers, shields and screws all exist here as their own bodies, so the layout can be exploded along an axis, laid flat for a teardown spread or animated back into the chassis.

The mesh runs 220,105 polygons and 236,444 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout, with the detail concentrated in the connector housings and the camera assembly where a macro camera lands.

At $129 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits repair and service documentation, technology journalism and teardown content, product and engineering marketing, teaching material and games. On sale since 2026.
