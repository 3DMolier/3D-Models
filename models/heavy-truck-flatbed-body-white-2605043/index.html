About This Model

A white flatbed body for heavy trucks, ready to mount on your own chassis for logistics and construction scenes. The 73,021 polygons cover the deck boards, side rails and headboard that protects the cab during hauls. The clean white finish suits rental fleets and corporate trucks that need a neutral look on busy delivery routes.

StemCell certification makes the white paint and metal trim look the same in every supplied format. Mixed UVs and 76,169 vertices keep the body light enough to reuse across a whole fleet. Tie-down points along the rails let you strap cargo convincingly for highway hauling shots.

Priced at $49 since 2026, the flatbed suits truck customization, freight renders and depot environments at logistics hubs. It measures 357.22 x 820.56 x H350.83 cm, sized for a full heavy truck bed. Fit it on a chassis and load pallets or pipes to complete a working delivery vehicle for your fleet.
