About This Model

A set of beetles with groomed fur on their bodies, gathered for insect close-ups where the texture of the shell matters. Fine hair along legs and thorax catches rim light, which is what makes a macro shot of a beetle believable. Hard wing cases and soft fuzz side by side give each insect two materials to play against each other.

Each beetle follows CheckMate specification, so proportions stay consistent when several species share one log or leaf. Buying the group as one collection saves matching scale and shading across separate purchases from different authors. Segmented legs and antennae are the parts an animator moves first, so they are kept as clean, readable shapes.

Beetle lovers have had this pack since 2024, and $199 buys it under a royalty free license. Natural history films, educational apps about insects and game levels set in forests or caves are where it usually lands. A shallow depth of field with one beetle crawling toward camera shows the fur at its best.

Can the Rigged Beetles Fur Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

How much does the Rigged Beetles Fur Collection 3D model cost?

How clean is the geometry on the Rigged Beetles Fur Collection model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.

Can the Rigged Beetles Fur Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.
