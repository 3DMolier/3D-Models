About This Model

This bundle pairs modern armored ground vehicles with combat aircraft, giving a defense scene both its land and air elements at once. Modern Military Ground and Air Vehicle Collection suits projects where tanks, transports and jets need to appear in the same shot. Desert camouflage or woodland schemes can be applied across the whole set for a unified look.

All models are built to CheckMate specification, so file structure and scale follow one rule across every vehicle. That consistency lets an armored column and an overhead flight share a scene without rescaling or renaming parts.

The collection arrived in 2025 at $1499 and suits commercial as well as broadcast work. Defense simulation, war documentaries, military strategy games and training visuals are its natural destinations. Place the aircraft low over a convoy and the size contrast between jets and tracked vehicles does much of the storytelling.
