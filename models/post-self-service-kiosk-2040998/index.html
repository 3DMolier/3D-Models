The Post Self Service Kiosk is a production-ready Architecture & Landmarks 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 22,426 polygons and 12,370 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
