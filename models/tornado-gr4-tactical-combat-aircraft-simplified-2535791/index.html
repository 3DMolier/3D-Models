About This Model

The Tornado GR4 Tactical Combat Aircraft Simplified is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $119 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 124,158 polygons over 133,776 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 13.89 x 17.37 x H6.15 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
