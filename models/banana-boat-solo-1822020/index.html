About This Model

An inflatable banana boat for a single rider, the bright towed float that beach resorts pull behind a speedboat. Its 29,828 polygons over 15,432 vertices round out the tube, the handles, the footrests and the tow point at the nose. The curved yellow body is unmistakable, so it reads as holiday fun from far across a crowded summer bay.

No texture maps come with it, leaving the vinyl color and any printed stripes to your own shaders. Non-overlapping UVs keep the surface ready for a resort logo or sun-faded patches. It carries CheckMate Pro status, a TurboSquid grade for sound topology and scene order.

Introduced in 2021, the float lands in beach resort renders, summer games and water park planning. Pull it across a turquoise bay behind a jet ski, with spray rising off the nose. It also sits nicely on a wooden dock beside a stack of rental kayaks, waiting for the next guest of the day.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2021 · Updated 14 November 2026
