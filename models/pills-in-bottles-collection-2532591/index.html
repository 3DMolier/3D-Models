About This Model

This bundle gathers pharmacy bottles with loose pills, giving a medicine cabinet, clinic shelf or bathroom counter a ready cluster of small props. Each container stands as its own object, so a designer can spread them along a shelf or bunch them tightly near a sink.

The set was built to the CheckMate specification, which means clean naming, frozen transforms and geometry that opens without repair work. Caps, labels and translucent bodies read as separate surfaces, and that separation makes it simple to recolor a blank label or tint the plastic.

Pills in Bottles Collection went on sale in 2026, so it is one of the newer additions to our health and pharmacy range. Buyers pick it for medical explainer animations, drugstore interiors and close product shots where scattered tablets add believable clutter. Because the pieces are lightweight props rather than hero objects, several copies can fill a long retail aisle without slowing the viewport. A single tablet can also be pulled out and duplicated to build a spill across a table or a dosage diagram.
