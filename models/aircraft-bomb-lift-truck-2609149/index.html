About This Model

A low munitions loader with a lifting boom and cradle, used on flight lines to raise bombs up to aircraft pylons. The Aircraft Bomb Lift Truck spends 467,679 polygons and 510,430 vertices on the hydraulic boom, open chassis and wheels. Its squat profile lets it drive right under a wing, which is the shot most scenes need.

StemCell certification keeps the PBR materials portable between render engines without manual fixes. The cradle at the front of the boom holds ordnance level, and the hydraulic cylinders read clearly from the side. Military airfields and carrier hangars both rely on machines like this, so it fits naval and land air bases alike.

Four versions are offered: two static editions at $89, including the MHU 83 Aircraft Bomb Lift Truck, and two rigged editions at $99. The rigged pair, one of them painted white, lets the boom move during loading sequences. All of them date from 2026 and are Royalty Free, suited to defense visualization, training simulators, games and documentaries.
