About This Model

An aging river barge with a long open hold and low wheelhouse, the workhorse of inland waterways and industrial docks. The River Cargo Barge Old Simplified carries 120,777 polygons, enough for hull plates and deck fittings without heavy interior detail.

There are 337,456 vertices, a higher number that reflects split edges along the weathered hull panels. The vessel meets the CheckMate specification, so the hull arrives clean, closed and at correct scale. Its blunt bow and flat deck suggest a working boat built to haul gravel, coal or timber for decades.

At $129 since 2026, the barge fits port scenes, river cityscapes and logistics animations. Tie it to a quay beside cranes, or float it slowly under a bridge in a wide shot of a city river. The simplified build keeps it practical as background traffic while still holding up at medium distance. Historical documentaries about inland shipping can also use it to show how bulk cargo moved before highways.
