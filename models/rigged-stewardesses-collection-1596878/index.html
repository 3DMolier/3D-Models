About This Model

Rigged flight attendants in airline uniforms, ready to walk the aisle, serve drinks or demonstrate safety procedures. Rigged Stewardesses Collection gives cabin interiors the crew they need, so a passenger plane never looks empty or staged. Fitted jackets, neck scarves and neat hairstyles give the women the polished look that passengers expect from a cabin crew.

The set carries CheckMate Lite certification, confirming clean geometry and sensible file organization. Skeletons are set up for poses like pointing toward exits or pushing a service trolley down the cabin. Smiling, attentive expressions suit a welcome-aboard shot at the aircraft door before departure.

Available since 2020 at $249, the characters cover a role that generic people packs rarely include. Aviation training videos, airport visualizations, travel films and in-flight safety animations use flight attendants most. Several crew members can stand along the galley or by the exit rows to fill a long cabin view.
