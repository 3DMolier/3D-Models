About This Model

This Tools model - Leather Wrist Cuffs Black - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 67,776 polygons over 75,510 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 5 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
