About This Model

Mantel clocks, carved wall pieces and ornate table clocks from past centuries come together here for period interiors and antique shop scenes. Roman numeral dials, decorative pendulum cases and gilded trim give each clock a distinct silhouette, so a wall of them never looks repetitive. Set designers use such clocks to signal a grandparent's study or a Victorian parlor before any character speaks.

The set holds CheckMate Pro status, which TurboSquid grants only after inspecting topology, scale and file organization. Wood grain, polished brass and painted dial faces suggest three separate material families that are simple to assign per object. Because each clock is a standalone object, one can be pulled forward as a hero prop while the rest stay on the wall.

The page pairs two versions: the original group at $45 and Antique Clocks Collection 2 at $69 with a broader spread of styles. First listed in 2021, the collection is sold Royalty Free and suits historical dramas, museum walkthroughs and puzzle games.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Antique Clocks Collection model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Can the Antique Clocks Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

What is the Antique Clocks Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Antique Clocks Collection mesh?

9,108 polygons and 9,108 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the 32,250 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs low at 9,108.
