About This Model

A mackerel lying on its side out of the water, the striped back and silver belly showing the way a fresh catch looks on deck. The pose is the idle of a landed fish, so it fits a pier, a cutting board or a market stall rather than open sea. Packaging targets Blender, which saves a conversion step for teams already working in that package.

The build follows CheckMate specification, so the fish arrives at real-world size and sits correctly in a hand or on ice. Wavy dark bars along the back are the detail that tells a mackerel from a herring, so they deserve a close camera. Wet skin reads best under a hard key light that pulls a sharp highlight along the flank.

Dating from 2026, the model suits fishing documentaries, seafood advertising, cooking shows and survival game scenes where food has to be caught first. A handful on crushed ice makes a fishmonger counter, and a single one on planks sells a harbor morning. Educational clips about marine life can use it to show the body shape up close without filming a real catch.
