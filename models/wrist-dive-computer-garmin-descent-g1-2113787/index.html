About This Model

A rugged wrist dive computer with a round bezel, side buttons and a silicone strap, built for scuba scenes and gear reviews. The watch carries 66,114 polygons over 33,590 vertices, a triangle-dense build that keeps the bezel grip and strap holes sharp.

Non-overlapping UVs keep the display, case and strap apart for custom screen graphics and wear. Holding a CheckMate Pro rating, the watch is ready for macro product shots and close wrist views. Water droplets on the glass and a blue ambient light are all it takes to suggest depth.

Five dive computer versions are offered: this Garmin Descent G1, a solar edition and an electric lime dive computer. Black editions of the G1 and of the G1 Solar complete the line for darker styling. Worn on a diver's wrist beside a coral wall, it becomes a quick storytelling detail. The watch was added to the range in 2023 and suits diving magazines, travel visuals and sports watch renders.
