About This Model

Priced at $24, the Glass Jar with Bay Leaves is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for food photography and film & TV VFX. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
