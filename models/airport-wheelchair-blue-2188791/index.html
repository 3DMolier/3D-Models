About This Model

Airport Wheelchair Blue belongs to our Medical range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 44,852 polygons and 23,518 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
