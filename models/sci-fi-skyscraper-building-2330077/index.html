About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Sci-Fi Skyscraper Building is a finished, render-ready model at $31. You are getting 17,836 polygons and 19,439 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, event visualization and product rendering.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
