About This Model

InSight Mars Lander is a detailed Space & Sci-Fi asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. Geometry weighs in at 192,360 polygons over 203,626 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
