About This Model

An operations control room with curved consoles, rows of computer monitors and a large video wall, ready for monitoring center scenes. The 1,680,633 polygons and 924,989 vertices fill the space with workstations, chairs, screens, cable trays and ceiling fixtures. Tiered desks facing the video wall give operators a clear view, and the layout guides the camera toward the main display.

The control room adheres to the CheckMate specification, and each UV island occupies its own space for clean texturing. Screens can carry any content, from power grid diagrams to traffic feeds, which makes the room adaptable to many industries. Dim ambient light and glowing monitors create the focused night shift mood real control centers keep.

Released in 2024, the room serves energy and transport visualizations, emergency response explainers, sci-fi films and simulation games. Operators of power plants and rail networks use similar scenes to plan console layouts and sight lines. Staff silhouettes against the bright video wall instantly tell viewers that something important is being watched.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 17 November 2026
