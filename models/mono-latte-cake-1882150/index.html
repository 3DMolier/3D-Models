About This Model

Looking for a Food & Beverages asset? The Mono Latte Cake is a finished, render-ready model at $29. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is catalogued for advertising, product visualization and film production. Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualisation, restaurant interiors and game props.

Geometry weighs in at 216,678 polygons over 110,221 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
