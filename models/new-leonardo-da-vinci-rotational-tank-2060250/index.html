About This Model

New Leonardo Da Vinci Rotational Tank is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 192,296 polygons and 101,642 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and defense simulation.
