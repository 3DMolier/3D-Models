About This Model

A yellow collapsible flat rack shipping container with folding end walls, built for loading oversized cargo like machinery and boats. The Flat Rack Collapsible Container Yellow has no side walls or roof, so heavy loads can be lifted on from above. Its folding ends lie flat for empty return trips, which is why stacks of them appear in every busy port.

A CheckMate Lite certificate assures the container opens without errors in mainstream 3D software. Bright safety yellow suits it best, while rust patches and worn decking add the look of years at sea. Corner castings and lashing points are the details that make the rack read correctly beside regular containers.

Offered from 2015, the rack serves port visualization, logistics presentations and shipping yard game levels. Load it with a crated generator or a yacht hull, and the cargo story becomes obvious at once. Freight forwarders can use renders like this to explain how out-of-gauge cargo is secured for transport.
