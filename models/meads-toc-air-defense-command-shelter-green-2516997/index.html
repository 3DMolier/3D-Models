The Air Defense Command Shelter is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $59 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 147,713 polygons over 155,296 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
