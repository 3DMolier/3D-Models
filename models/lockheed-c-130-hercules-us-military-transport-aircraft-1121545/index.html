About This Model

The Hercules has been landing on gravel strips since before most of its crews were born, and the shape explains why: a high wing that keeps the propellers clear of debris, a fuselage that sits low enough to load from a truck bed, and a rear ramp that turns the aircraft into a doorway. The model keeps the ramp, the paratroop doors, the landing gear blisters and the antenna farm that make a C-130 a C-130.

It runs 314,505 polygons and 336,612 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 22 texture maps - 3 at 8192x8192, 12 at 4096x4096 and 7 at 2048x2048. That is a serious texture budget, and it is where the exhaust staining, the walkway markings and the squadron insignia come from.

A rigged build sits on this page at the same $229 for when the propellers turn or the ramp opens. The static model suits film and television, defence visualization, documentary work, games and print. On sale since 2017.
