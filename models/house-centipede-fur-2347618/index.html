About This Model

The house centipede is the fastest thing most people will ever see indoors, and it is genuinely on their side: it eats spiders, silverfish and everything else living in the skirting boards. The legs get longer towards the back so they can overlap without tangling at speed.

The model holds 61,040 polygons and 60,668 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, groomed with fur. It is built to CheckMate specification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $69 it suits nature and education visualization, pest control and domestic material, horror and film props, game creatures, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
