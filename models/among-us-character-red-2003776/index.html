About This Model

Among Us Character is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. You are getting 3,012 polygons and 1,512 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
