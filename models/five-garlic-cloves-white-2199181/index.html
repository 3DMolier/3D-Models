About This Model

Five Garlic Cloves is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $24. The mesh carries 4,640 polygons and 2,330 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
