About This Model

This Kitchen & Tableware model - Stainless Steel Egg Shape Kitchen Timer - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $19 on TurboSquid. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and event visualization. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

Counted at the source, the model is 684 polygons and 708 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
