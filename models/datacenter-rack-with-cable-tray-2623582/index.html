About This Model

A server rack with an overhead cable tray running above it, the kind of detail that makes a data hall feel wired and alive. The 50,397 polygons cover the cabinet, the tray ladder and the bundles of cable dropping into the top. It spans 101.49 x 79.16 x H255.75 cm, with the tray adding width beyond the cabinet itself.

Built to CheckMate rules, the mesh carries 56,938 vertices and keeps tray and rack as separate parts. With UVs present on only part of the mesh, cable jackets and metal surfaces take procedural colors more easily than painted maps.

Three versions come with this page: this rack with cable tray and a rack with overhead cable tray at $79 each. A black rack with overhead cables completes the choice at a lower $69 price. Released in 2026, they fit network operations scenes, colocation tours and tech explainer videos.
