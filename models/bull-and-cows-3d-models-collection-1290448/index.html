About This Model

A collection of a bull and several cows, gathered so farm, pasture and dairy scenes can be filled with one purchase. The bull stands broad and heavy with a thick neck, while the cows are leaner with visible udders and gentler heads. Coat patterns vary between animals, which keeps a small herd from looking like repeated clones in a field.

CheckMate Pro certification means the cattle arrive at real-world scale, so they line up with fences, barns and farmers. Materials and objects are organized per animal, which makes it simple to swap coat colors or hide individuals. Natural daylight brings out the shape of muscles and the sheen of hides, giving the herd a grounded look.

On sale since 2018, the set suits dairy packaging, agricultural presentations, rural architecture renders and farming games. Educational content about livestock and food production also benefits from a believable mixed herd of cattle. Spread across a green hillside, the bull and cows instantly turn an empty landscape into working farmland.
