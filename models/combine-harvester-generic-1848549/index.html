About This Model

A combine does three jobs in one pass: cuts, threshes and separates. That is why the machine looks the way it does - a wide header at the front, a body full of drums and sieves, a grain tank on top and an unloading auger swinging out to the side. Being generic rather than branded makes it usable where a manufacturer's badge would be a problem.

The mesh is heavy at 1,257,805 polygons and 646,664 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 21 texture maps at 4096x4096, carrying the paint, the dust and the chaff staining that agricultural machinery always wears.

At $139 it suits agriculture and agritech visualization, food supply chain presentation, rural landscape scenes, equipment advertising, education material and games. On sale since 2022.
