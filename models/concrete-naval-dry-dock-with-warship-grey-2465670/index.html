About This Model

Priced at $229, the Concrete Naval Dry Dock with Warship is a finished Ships & Boats model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 1,966,491 polygons and 2,040,524 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 149.99 x 510.0 x H54.59 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, naval simulation and game environments. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
