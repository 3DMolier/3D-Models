About This Model

The The Fantasy Creature Basilisk is a production-ready Animals & Creatures 3D model, priced at $59 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for film production, game environments and VR experiences. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 24,768 polygons and 25,685 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
