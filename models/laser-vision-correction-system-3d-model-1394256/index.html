About This Model

Laser Vision Correction System is one of the Medical models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. The mesh carries 44,486 polygons and 46,971 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 18 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
