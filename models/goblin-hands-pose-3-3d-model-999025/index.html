About This Model

A pair of goblin hands frozen in a clawing pose, with knobby knuckles, long fingers and pointed nails ready to grab. The sculpt exaggerates every tendon and wrinkle, giving the hands a gnarled look that suits fantasy creatures. Held in this fixed pose, they slot straight into a still render or a close-up insert shot.

TurboSquid awarded the hands CheckMate Pro, which means the mesh was inspected for clean structure and sensible naming. Green, gray or sickly yellow skin tones can be tried quickly with a simple material change. Adding grime under the nails and scars across the knuckles pushes the creature further toward horror.

Released in 2016, the hands serve fantasy book covers, tabletop game art and creature design studies. Reach them out of a dark cave entrance toward the camera, and the scene gains instant menace and tension. Concept artists also use them as a base for sculpting their own monster hands.
