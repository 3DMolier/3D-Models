About This Model

This animated Tyrannosaurus rex comes rigged for Cinema 4D with a walking cycle ready to loop across a prehistoric landscape. A massive skull, tiny forelimbs and a heavy counterbalancing tail give the predator its iconic posture in motion. Each stride shifts weight from leg to leg, so the walk feels heavy rather than floating on the ground.

TurboSquid passed the dinosaur under its CheckMate specification, which confirms correct scale, clean meshes and organized controls. The walk cycle saves animators days of keyframing and can be retimed for a slow stalk or a faster march. Because the rig stays editable, you can add roars, head turns or a charge on top of the base cycle.

At $179 the model suits dinosaur documentaries, museum exhibits and children's films about the Cretaceous period. Dating back to 2020, it serves Jurassic-themed games, VR experiences and school lessons on paleontology. Paleontologists render the walk to show how a giant theropod probably moved, based on its bones and footprints.
