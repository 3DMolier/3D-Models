About This Model

The Black Hawk is a workhorse rather than a showpiece, and it looks it: a squat fuselage, wide sliding doors, a tail rotor canted over to add lift, and stub wings that carry whatever the mission needs. The model keeps the door tracks, the refuelling probe fitting, the exhaust suppressors and the antenna array that separate it from a generic utility helicopter.

It runs 332,047 polygons and 352,778 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 27 texture maps - 19 at 4096x4096 and 8 at 2048x2048. That budget carries the matt olive finish, the panel wear around the doors and the stencilled unit markings.

A rigged build sits on this page at $289 for when the rotors turn and the gear moves. The static model at $179 covers film and television, defence and emergency services visualization, documentary work, games and print. On sale since 2017.
