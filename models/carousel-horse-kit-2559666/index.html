About This Model

A carousel horse kit with the carved horse, its brass pole and saddle details, ready to dress a fairground ride or nursery. The 28,640 polygons sit on a subdivision-ready cage, so the flowing mane and arched neck smooth out cleanly when refined. Carousel horses are carved mid-gallop, and this one keeps the lifted forelegs that give the ride its motion.

The kit meets CheckMate specification, so its units are true and its parts are named for quick rearrangement. Non-overlapping UVs leave room for hand-painted roses, gold leaf trim and chipped paint on an older ride. Instancing several horses around a central column builds a full merry-go-round in a few minutes.

Debuting in 2026, the kit fits amusement park scenes, children's book art and vintage fairground photography sets. Toy stores and themed cafes also use carousel horses as decor, which widens where the model can go. A slow rotation and warm string lights turn it into a nostalgic evening at the fair.
