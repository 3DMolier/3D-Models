About This Model

Decorated sugar skulls for the Mexican Day of the Dead, with flowers, swirls and bright patterns painted across the bone. The set uses 24,558 polygons and 24,555 vertices, which keeps the rounded crania and deep eye sockets smooth.

Its build follows the CheckMate specification, with clean topology and a natural skull shape that holds up in close candlelit shots. Overlapping UVs let similar areas share texture space, so a varied altar can still be textured efficiently by a single artist. The ornament keeps the skulls festive rather than grim, which suits a family celebration more than a horror scene.

On sale since 2025, the skulls suit altar scenes, festival decorations, Halloween games and cultural documentaries about Mexican traditions. Placed among candles, marigolds and photographs, they turn a table into a respectful ofrenda for remembering relatives. Designers also use them as colorful props in party scenes, where the painted faces carry the theme on their own.
