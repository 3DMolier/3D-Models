About This Model

A truck mounted drilling rig folds its mast down for the road and raises it on site, which is the single fact that shapes the whole machine: the mast pivots at the rear, hydraulic rams lift it, and jacks take the weight off the suspension before drilling starts. This build shows the rig set up and working.

The mesh is efficient at 116,420 polygons and 133,339 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the mast lattice, the drawworks and the jack assemblies.

At $99 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits oil, gas and geological exploration visualization, mining and water well presentation, equipment marketing, site scenes and games. On sale since 2025.
