About This Model

A cable chain is the plainest link pattern there is: identical oval links, each at right angles to its neighbours. That simplicity is what makes it hard, because there is no ornament to distract from a link that is the wrong proportion or a join that does not close.

The mesh is efficient at 6,056 polygons and 6,137 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry entirely in the links.

A gold version sits on this page at the same $19. Both suit jewellery e-commerce and catalog renders, luxury advertising, character set dressing for film, television and games, and product photography. Built to CheckMate specification, on sale since 2024.
