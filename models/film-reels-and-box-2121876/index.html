About This Model

Vintage film reels stacked beside their storage box, a nostalgic prop for cinema lobbies, editing rooms and movie history displays. The Film Reels and Box hold 114,656 polygons over 57,646 vertices, giving the reel spokes and the coiled film edges clean curves. The spoked metal reels show cut-out windows that let light pass through, a detail that sells the vintage look.

Non-overlapping UVs leave room for scratched metal, faded labels and a worn cardboard box. No textures come with the set, so you decide between bright aluminum and tarnished steel. Its CheckMate Pro certification guarantees clean geometry and logical naming for each reel and the box.

Released in 2023, the reels fit cinema interiors, film festival graphics and archive scenes in documentaries. Spill some film across a desk with a splicer nearby and the editing room comes alive. Movie review channels often use such props in their title cards, thumbnails and channel intros.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2023 · Updated 19 November 2026
