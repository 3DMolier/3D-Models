About This Model

The Rigged Russian Military Aircrafts Collection 6(2) gathers a group of Russian combat aviation airframes in one set. Grouping the aircraft saves hunting for matching models when an airbase, parade or flypast scene needs several types.

All models meet the CheckMate specification, which keeps scale, naming and file structure consistent across the set. Camouflage schemes, tactical numbers and weathered panels suit the utilitarian finish typical of military aviation in every season. Consistent proportions mean the aircraft line up correctly on a shared apron without any manual rescaling.

The bundle costs $999 and belongs to a numbered series of Russian aircraft sets from the same author. Introduced in 2023, it serves military history documentaries, defense analysis graphics and strategy game prototypes. Parked wingtip to wingtip on a snowy runway, the aircraft create a strong opening image for a documentary. Staggered in a tight formation above the clouds, they suit dramatic sequences for news explainers and simulation trailers.
