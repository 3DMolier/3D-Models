Bark Shrub is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Geometry weighs in at 74,462 polygons over 76,322 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
