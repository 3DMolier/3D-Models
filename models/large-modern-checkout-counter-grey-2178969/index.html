About This Model

The checkout is where a shop's whole layout terminates, and its dimensions are set by the queue rather than by the till. Belt length, bagging area and the gap a trolley has to pass through are all decided before anything else.

The model is light at 26,568 polygons and 13,325 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in grey, and carries StemCell certification. A red rigged build is offered separately.

At $19 it suits retail and interior visualization, shopfitting presentation, store scenes, advertising, film sets and game environments. On sale since 2024.
