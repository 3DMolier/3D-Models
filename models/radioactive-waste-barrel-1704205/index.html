About This Model

The yellow drum with a trefoil on it is one of the most effective warning designs ever made, and almost none of what it labels is stored that way. The symbol has become shorthand for danger far beyond its actual use.

The model is very light at 8,336 polygons and 8,370 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $29 it suits industrial and environmental visualization, film and television set dressing, game environments, news and documentary graphics, and editorial illustration. On sale since 2021.
