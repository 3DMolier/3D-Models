About This Model

A corvette is the smallest ship a navy still calls a warship: fast, shallow enough for coastal water, armed well beyond its size. Most fleets that cannot afford frigates build corvettes instead, which is why the class turns up in patrol, escort and littoral defence work all over the world.

The hull carries 195,838 polygons and 212,656 vertices in quads and tris, with non-overlapping UVs and StemCell certification. This listing is the static version; rigged builds are offered separately, including native rigs prepared for Maya and for Cinema 4D.

At $199 it fits naval simulation and training, defence presentation, film and television, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
