About This Model

Multirole Strike Fighter belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $179 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 164,723 polygons over 179,416 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

17 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
