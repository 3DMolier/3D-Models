About This Model

A single leaf with a crenate edge, meaning its margin is lined with small rounded teeth like a scalloped hem. The blade has a clear central vein and side veins branching toward each rounded tooth, which makes it useful for botany lessons.

The leaf holds a CheckMate Pro certification for clean structure and naming. Because it is a single element, it can be scattered across a forest floor or arranged on a white background for a herbarium style shot. The gentle curl along the midrib keeps it from looking flat when lit from the side.

Since 2015 it has been downloaded by 13 buyers at $9, mostly teachers, scientific illustrators and environment artists dressing autumn ground cover. Paired with a magnifying glass on a desk, it suits a nature study scene. A few copies rotated and recolored on wet stone also bring a damp autumn mood.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
