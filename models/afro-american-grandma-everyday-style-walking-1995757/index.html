About This Model

Everyday Style is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. The mesh carries 273,486 polygons and 401,068 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
