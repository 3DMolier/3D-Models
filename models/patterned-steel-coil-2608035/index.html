About This Model

A heavy coil of patterned steel sheet stands on its edge, the way rolled metal leaves a mill for transport. The Patterned Steel Coil keeps to 3,746 polygons and 3,879 vertices in quads, making it cheap to repeat across a yard. The hollow center shows the eye of the coil, where lifting hooks or C-hooks engage.

It measures 193.27 x 175.37 x H193.24 cm, a full industrial coil that needs a crane or forklift. CheckMate certification confirms correct units, so the coil sits properly on trucks, racks and saddles. Embossed diamond tread on the outer wrap marks it as checker plate rather than plain sheet.

Offered for $29, the coil is one version that first appeared in 2026. Steel mill interiors, port logistics scenes, manufacturing training videos and truck cargo setups use it as heavy freight. Stack several on a flatbed with chains and the load instantly looks dangerously heavy and realistic.
