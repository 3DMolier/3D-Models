About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Bone Structure Anatomy is a finished, render-ready model at $41. You are getting 10,793 polygons and 10,553 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos.

2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
