About This Model

A red reflective triangle on a folding stand marks a broken-down car and tells the whole roadside story in one prop. The Emergency Warning Triangle is built from 5,506 polygons and 5,458 vertices, including the hinged legs and the reflector frame. Its bright outline stays readable from far away, exactly as it does on a real highway shoulder.

Red reflective strips, orange fluorescent inner panels and black plastic feet come from 5 maps at 2048 x 2048. The prop holds CheckMate Pro status for true scale, and every UV island keeps its own place. At night the reflectors flare in headlight beams, which suits dramatic low-angle shots on wet asphalt.

The triangle went on sale in 2019 and costs $19 as a single version for road scenes. Driving school materials, roadside assistance graphics and accident reconstructions all use this kind of marker. Place it a few car lengths behind a hatchback with hazard lights on, and the breakdown reads instantly.
