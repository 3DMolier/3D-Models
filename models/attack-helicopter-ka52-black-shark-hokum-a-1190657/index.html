About This Model

Priced at $149, the Attack Helicopter KA52 Black Shark Hokum A is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags military simulation, game environments and film production as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

At 418,594 polygons and 451,995 vertices this is a mid-weight asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Maps: 19 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
