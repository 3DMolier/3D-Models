About This Model

A gold necklace with a diamond heart pendant, ready for jewelry catalogs, romantic gifts and bust displays. The 334,558 polygons are spent on the chain links and the set stones, so every link stays round up close. The heart sits on a small bail, so it swings naturally when the chain is posed.

The CheckMate build keeps UVs free of overlaps, which makes it easy to bake reflections or add polish wear. Vertices total 319,039, a density meant for close beauty shots rather than distant background props. The piece spans 26.99 x 1.65 x H70.78 cm laid out, a full length chain with the pendant at the bottom.

This $29 necklace, a 2026 addition, fits jewelry e-commerce renders, fashion editorials and Valentine visuals. Drape it over a velvet neck form to stage a classic jewelry shot. Gold and diamond catch light in contrasting ways, warm on the chain and sharp on the stones.
