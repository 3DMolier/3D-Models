About This Model

The gold iPhone 8 brought a soft champagne finish and a glass back, and this collection gathers that phone in several presentations. Across the iPhone 8 Gold Collection, the rounded aluminum frame, Home button and front bezels match the original handset closely. Front and back views both matter here, because the warm metallic glass reads differently depending on how light hits it.

TurboSquid certified the bundle as CheckMate Pro, its tier for inspected geometry and clean file organization. Separate objects let an artist show the phone face down on a desk while another stands upright beside it. The circular Home button below the display is the detail that dates the phone immediately for anyone who owned one.

This collection has been in our catalog since 2017, costs $39, and its license is Editorial Uses Only. It suits technology journalism, smartphone history articles, repair guides and period scenes set when this handset was new. Gold accents on the side rails pair well with warm interiors, cafe tables and lifestyle photography setups.
