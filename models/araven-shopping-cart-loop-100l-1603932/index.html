About This Model

A plastic bodied shopping trolley is a European supermarket fixture: a moulded basket rather than a wire one, on a steel frame with four castors and a child seat that folds down. The moulded body is quieter, does not rust and takes branding directly, which is why it has largely replaced wire in food retail.

The mesh runs 68,288 polygons and 69,263 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 5 texture maps at 4096x4096 carrying the moulded plastic finish and the frame paint.

At $39 and built to StemCell specification, it suits supermarket and retail interior visualization, store planning presentation, retail marketing, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2020.
