About This Model

Bamboo Grove is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. The mesh carries 1,396,658 polygons and 2,009,654 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 10 maps at 2048 x 2048. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
