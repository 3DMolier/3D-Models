About This Model

Priced at $99, the Soldier in Grey Tactical Gear Rigged is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 192,740 polygons and 197,223 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
