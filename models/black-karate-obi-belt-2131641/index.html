About This Model

Priced at $19, the Karate Obi Belt is a finished Sports & Recreation model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The polygon count is 24,384, spread over 12,194 vertices. Whether that fits depends on where it lands - an architectural still and a real-time walkthrough tolerate very different counts.

Construction is plain and deliberate - quads through the body, triangles only where they are harmless. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
