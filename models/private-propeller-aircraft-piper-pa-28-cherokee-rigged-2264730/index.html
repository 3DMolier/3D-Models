About This Model

Priced at $129, the Private Propeller Aircraft Piper PA-28 Cherokee Rigged is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Geometry weighs in at 47,445 polygons over 49,525 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
