About This Model

This general model - Command Center Tables - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 6,824 polygons over 7,034 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
