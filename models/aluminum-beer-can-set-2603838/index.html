About This Model

A set of plain aluminum beer cans in several sizes, ready to stock fridges, bars and picnic tables. The cans share 17,151 polygons and 17,526 vertices, with detail going into the rims, necks and pull tabs. Each can keeps the rolled top and domed bottom, so it looks right standing up or lying on its side.

StemCell certification keeps the brushed metal shader consistent across render engines, so a fridge full of cans matches. Plain bodies leave room for custom labels, which makes the set a good base for packaging mockups and label design. Light catches the curved shoulders and the thin metal, which sells product close-ups.

The Aluminum Beer Can Set was released in 2026 and sells for $44, licensed Royalty Free. Bar counters, party scenes, packaging presentations and game props are where cans like these usually land. Crushed copies or a few scattered on the floor also dress up messy dorm rooms and garages.
