About This Model

Rigged Crocodiles Collection gives artists several crocodiles with rigs, ready to pose from basking on banks to lunging from water. Bony ridges running down the back and tail form the textured outline that defines these ancient reptiles. Wide jaws lined with teeth can open on the rig, the moment every crocodile scene builds toward. Eyes and nostrils set high on the head let them lurk almost fully submerged.

Every rig and mesh in the set is clearly named for quick posing in any major animation package, to CheckMate standard. Rigging allows the bodies to curve naturally in water or flatten across mud without remodeling.

Since 2024 the crocodile set has served nature films, adventure games and reptile education materials. Several crocodiles basking together on a sandbank make a striking aerial establishing shot for river and swamp documentaries. Horror and survival projects can stage sudden attacks by keying the jaw and tail on the rig.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How heavy is the Rigged Crocodiles Collection mesh?

24,530 polygons and 24,001 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Compared with the roughly 28,000 polygon median of our Model Bundles & Sets catalog, this build runs low at 24,530.

What is the Rigged Crocodiles Collection model typically used for?

Stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

Can the Rigged Crocodiles Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Can the Rigged Crocodiles Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.
