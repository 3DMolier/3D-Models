About This Model

A pile of dried cannabis buds with fine fur strands for trichomes and hairs, made for close-up botanical and documentary shots. It is built from 31,962 polygons and 31,030 vertices, with the fuzz handled by a fur system rather than solid geometry. The dense, sticky look of cured flower is what the fur layer delivers in a macro shot.

CheckMate certification keeps the buds as clean objects that are easy to scatter or rearrange. Orange pistils and frosty surfaces separate them from generic green foliage in close shots. They sit naturally on a scale, in a jar or on a rolling tray in a dispensary interior.

Two versions exist: the loose buds at $49 and the Cannabis in Zip Lock Bag Fur at $59. Both were released in 2026 under Royalty Free terms for commercial use. Dispensary visualizations, crime dramas, medical cannabis explainers and documentary reconstructions are where these buds fit.
