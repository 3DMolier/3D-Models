About This Model

Training mannequins marked with acupuncture points and meridian lines, gathered in one set for medical education and clinic scenes. The Acupuncture Mannequins Collection shows figures in standing poses, with point markings spread over the head, torso and limbs. Each figure reads clearly from the front and side, which is how such models are studied in a classroom.

These mannequins were built to CheckMate specification, so meshes, naming and scale suit further work. Thin meridian lines across a smooth skin surface show best under soft, even studio light. Pale body tones keep the colored point labels easy to read in close renders and diagrams.

The set appeared in 2024 as a digital alternative to the plastic teaching dummies found in clinics. Chinese medicine schools put it in lecture slides and interactive anatomy apps, and wellness clinics show it in reception renders. E-learning teams animate rotating figures to explain where needles go along each channel.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
