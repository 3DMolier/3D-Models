About This Model

Priced at $39, the Long Wallet Open is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 21,611 polygons and 24,310 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

9 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
