About This Model

A male doctor in scrubs, surgical mask and gloves, suited to hospital corridors, operating rooms and public health explainers. With 29,587 polygons and 15,449 vertices, the figure stays light enough to duplicate across a busy ward scene. His gloved hands and covered face read instantly as clinical protection, even at small scale in the background.

UVs are unwrapped without overlap, and TurboSquid graded the character CheckMate Pro for clean, well-named geometry. The loose folds of the scrubs and the mask straps behind the ears add believable detail in medium shots. Cool, even hospital lighting suits him best, matching the fluorescent look of real clinics.

Released in 2023, the doctor serves medical training videos, healthcare apps, pandemic-era documentaries and hospital game levels. Architects designing clinics also place the figure in visualizations to give rooms a sense of scale and purpose. He works well beside a patient bed or a monitor cart, where the role needs no explanation.
