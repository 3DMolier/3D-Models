About This Model

The Barber Scissors is a production-ready Tools 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 4,444 polygons and 2,220 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
