About This Model

An airport equipment collection gathers ground support vehicles such as baggage tugs, loaders, stairs and service carts. The vehicles match in scale, so they can surround an airliner on the apron without any looking out of place. High visibility paint and warning lights give each one the familiar look of real ramp equipment at a busy hub.

TurboSquid checked the pack against its CheckMate specification for structure, naming and consistent real-world scale. Worn yellow paint, rubber bumpers and reflective stripes give the fleet a practical, used look. Doors, ramps and conveyors are separated, which allows the equipment to be animated during loading.

This equipment pack costs $129 and has been in the catalog since 2018, serving airport and aviation projects. Typical uses include airport planning renders, aviation documentaries and flight or ground handling simulation games. Arrange the vehicles around a parked jet at the gate to show a busy turnaround in progress.
