About This Model

Single Post Lift is one of the Tools models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $34. You are getting 282,658 polygons and 282,125 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
