About This Model

A battery-powered hydraulic rescue ram that pushes apart crushed vehicle parts, made for firefighting and road accident rescue scenes. The 42,202 polygons and 22,277 vertices go into the extending piston, the claw tips, the battery pack and the handle. Rescuers wedge it between door pillar and dashboard, so it usually appears inside a wrecked car frame.

Its unwrap keeps every shell separate, which helps when adding grime, hydraulic oil or worn safety stickers. CheckMate Pro certification confirms sound geometry and clear part names for quick scene assembly. Bright safety colors on the housing stand out against twisted metal and dark asphalt.

At $29 apiece, the four versions include the new Holmatro PRA50 and a used Holmatro extrication ram. Two generic tools follow: a Battery Powered Extrication Ram and a used Cordless Rescue Ram Hydraulic. Released in 2022, the ram supports firefighter training materials, rescue documentaries and emergency service simulations.
