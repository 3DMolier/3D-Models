About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Ammunition Loading System is a finished, render-ready model at $49. Geometry weighs in at 206,844 polygons over 208,527 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
