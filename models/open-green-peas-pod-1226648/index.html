About This Model

Open Green Peas Pod belongs to our Food & Beverages range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for food photography and film & TV VFX. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

The mesh carries 4,244 polygons and 4,279 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 5 of them, peaking at 8192x8192, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
