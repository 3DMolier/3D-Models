About This Model

Priced at $59, the Antique High Wheeler is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 634,239 polygons and 63,310 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
