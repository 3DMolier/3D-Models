About This Model

Priced at $39, the Male Head 6 is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 8,762 polygons and 9,307 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
