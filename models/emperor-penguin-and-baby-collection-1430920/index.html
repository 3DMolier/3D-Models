About This Model

An adult emperor penguin with its downy grey chick, the classic pairing of Antarctic nature films. The Emperor Penguin and Baby Collection puts both birds in one purchase, so a colony scene starts with a family rather than a lone figure.

The adult shows the glossy black back, white belly and the orange-yellow patches at the neck and beak. The chick is rounder and fluffier, with the pale face mask that marks a young bird. Both carry CheckMate Pro certification, which confirms closed meshes and clean geometry for close shots.

Placed on sea ice with the chick tucked against the parent's feet, the pair tells a story in a single frame. Priced at $79 the set suits wildlife documentaries, climate explainers and children's education apps. Against a pale snowy backdrop, the dark backs of both birds stand out even in a wide establishing shot. It has been available since 2019 and has found a home in 11 projects so far.
