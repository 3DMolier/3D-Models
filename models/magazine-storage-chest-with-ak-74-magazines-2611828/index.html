About This Model

A large hinged chest stacked with AK 74 magazines, organized in layers like a well-stocked military supply depot. The Magazine Storage Chest with AK 74 Magazines is heavy at 577,823 polygons and 584,260 vertices, rich enough for slow pans. Its open lid reveals rows in depth, so the contents read from above and from the side.

It measures 110.34 x 145.24 x H151.11 cm, big enough to hold ammunition for a whole squad. Every part has its own space on non-overlapping UVs, so wear and stencils never repeat visibly. Delivered as StemCell, the chest keeps the same material setup whichever format you open.

Introduced in 2026, the chest is a single version sold at $39 for game and film. Military shooters, arms depot raids, armory training and Cold War dramas use it as the centerpiece of supply rooms. The ribbed magazines contrast with the flat panels of the chest and give the lighting texture.
