About This Model

Prefabricated Modular Office Two Story Building is a detailed Architecture Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 21,596 polygons over 24,592 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
