About This Model

The A350 is recognisable from the front before you can read the titles: the curved black mask around the flight deck windows and the upswept wingtips are unlike anything else in the sky. In Emirates colors it carries the red tail and the gold Arabic script along the fuselage, both of which have to follow the body curvature correctly to look painted rather than pasted.

The mesh is 320,971 polygons and 332,297 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 20 texture maps at 4096x4096. That budget covers the door outlines, the service stencils and the composite panel joints that a widebody shows along its flank.

A rigged build sits on this page at $149 for when the gear and control surfaces move. The static model at $129 covers airline marketing, airport and terminal visualization, film and television, news graphics and print. On sale since 2017.
