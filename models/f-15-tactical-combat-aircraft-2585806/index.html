About This Model

This twin engine F-15 fighter is a dense, close-up ready model for air combat sequences, flight simulation and defense visualization. The mesh holds 507,450 polygons and 519,672 vertices, enough for the canopy frame, intake ramps and weapon pylons to survive close passes. Twin vertical tails and broad shoulder mounted intakes give the jet its unmistakable profile from almost any camera angle.

StemCell certification means the aircraft ships in several native formats with matching PBR materials rather than one converted file. Its UV islands do not overlap, so squadron markings, weathering and custom camouflage can be painted without seams fighting each other.

Released in 2026, the jet is a new addition for aviation documentaries, military training material and flight game hangars. Its grey air superiority finish suits both bright daylight sorties and moody dusk shots over clouds or desert ranges. Animators often frame this kind of fighter low over a runway or banking hard, where the wing area becomes obvious. Studios building air show recreations can line several copies on a ramp, since the scale stays true across formats.
