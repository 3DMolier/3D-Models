About This Model

Priced at $189, the Tesla Rigged Cars Collection is a finished Model Bundles & Sets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
