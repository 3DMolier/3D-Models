About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Black Jersey is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 3,559 polygons and 3,644 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The object measures 83.3 x 29.74 x H71.78 cm, modelled at true scale. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
