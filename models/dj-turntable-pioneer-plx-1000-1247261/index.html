About This Model

A direct-drive DJ turntable with a heavy platter, an S-shaped tonearm and a long pitch fader running beside the deck. The 200,435 polygons go into the strobe dots on the platter rim, the tonearm counterweight and the start button. Its 206,337 vertices allow slow close-ups of a needle dropping into the groove of a record.

There are 19 textures at 2048 x 2048 for the brushed top plate, rubber slipmat and printed scale markings. The unwrap has no overlapping islands, and the deck passed CheckMate Pro certification.

Two versions cost $39 each: the DJ Turntable Pioneer PLX 1000 under an Editorial Uses Only license and a royalty-free Professional DJ Turntable. The unbranded deck shares the polygon count, so scenes that must avoid a manufacturer lose no detail. Out since 2018, the deck fits club booths, record shops and music school studios. Two decks flanking a mixer form the classic battle setup every hip hop scene expects.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2018 · Updated 18 November 2026
