About This Model

A collection of party hats including paper cones, foil crowns and novelty shapes, ready to bring a birthday or New Year mood to any scene. The cones keep their pompoms, elastic bands and bright patterns, making them instantly read as celebration props.

This collection carries a CheckMate Pro certification, which means files were inspected for topology and scale. Hats are independent objects that can perch on guests' heads, lie on a table or tumble through the air. Glitter, foil and printed paper materials sparkle under festive lights, keeping party scenes cheerful.

These hats have been available since 2018 for party planning visuals, greeting card illustrations and family animation scenes. Scattered across a table with cake and balloons, they finish a birthday setup quickly. Character artists dress avatars with them for festive events in games and apps. Event stores also preview decoration packages with these colorful pieces before ordering stock.
