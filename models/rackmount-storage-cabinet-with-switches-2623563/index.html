About This Model

A server rack cabinet fitted with network switches and patch ports, the core furniture of any data room or office closet. The Rackmount Storage Cabinet with Switches uses 48,342 polygons, spending them on vented doors, mounting rails and port rows.

Its 55,188 vertices give each switch faceplate defined edges, so status lights and sockets stay crisp in renders. CheckMate certification means clean geometry and correct scale when it arrives in your scene. The tall enclosure reads clearly in a wide shot, and the front glass lets viewers see the equipment inside.

New for 2026 and priced at $59, the cabinet fits IT infrastructure visuals, office interiors and cybersecurity films. Line several cabinets in a row to suggest a data center aisle, or tuck one into a corner of a small office. Network engineers preparing training material get a clear view of where switches mount inside a standard rack. Glowing indicator lights added in your renderer make the cabinet feel powered and active.
