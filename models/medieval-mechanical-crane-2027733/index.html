About This Model

Medieval Mechanical Crane is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $28. Counted at the source, the model is 99,318 polygons and 52,209 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
