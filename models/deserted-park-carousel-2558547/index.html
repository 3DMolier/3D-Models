About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Deserted Park Carousel is a finished, render-ready model at $79. You are getting 264,712 polygons and 260,764 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
