About This Model

Floor Toilet Pan Steel New is one of the Furniture & Interior models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. You are getting 4,516 polygons and 2,498 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
