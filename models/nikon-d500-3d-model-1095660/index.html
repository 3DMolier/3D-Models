About This Model

The Nikon D500 is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $69 on TurboSquid. You are getting 111,171 polygons and 115,998 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 8 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
