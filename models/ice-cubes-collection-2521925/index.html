About This Model

This collection of ice cubes gives you several irregular shapes with softened edges, ready to drop into glasses and buckets. Each cube is modeled with slight melting and bevels, so refraction looks natural instead of glassy and perfect.

The pack follows the CheckMate specification, keeping every cube a separate named object for easy scattering. That separation helps physics simulations, where cubes need to tumble into a drink and settle convincingly. A water material with subsurface tint and trapped bubbles brings out their shape.

Collection 2 is the bigger companion version at $49, while this pack sells for $39. Pour them into a whiskey glass under warm bar light, and the drink shot is nearly done. Their varied sizes stop a full glass from looking repetitive, and a few loose cubes on the counter hint at a busy evening. Bar scenes, cocktail recipe videos and summer drink animations have chilled with it since 2026.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026
