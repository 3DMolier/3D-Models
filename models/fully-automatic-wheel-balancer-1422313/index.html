About This Model

Priced at $49, the Fully Automatic Wheel Balancer is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 14,093 polygons and 14,578 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

11 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
