About This Model

This collection brings Arab men and women in traditional and everyday clothing, ready to populate Middle Eastern streets, markets and offices. Flowing robes and headscarves give the figures clear regional identity, which generic crowd characters cannot provide. Placed in a souk, an airport lounge or a business district, the people make scenes feel locally grounded.

The bundle holds CheckMate Pro status, which TurboSquid awards after reviewing topology, proportions and file organization. Fabric colors and patterns are material settings, so you can match festive, formal or casual looks to each scene. Consistent naming across characters makes it easy to duplicate figures, vary outfits and build a crowd quickly.

This first set costs $189, while a second Arab people collection is also available at $249, two versions in all. Offered since 2017, the characters suit architectural renders in the Gulf, travel films, cultural documentaries and city simulation games. A few figures strolling past a mosque courtyard at sunset make a calm, authentic establishing shot.
