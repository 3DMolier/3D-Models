About This Model

A tactical hearing-protection headset with boom microphone, made to sit on soldier, police or shooting-range characters. The Peltor ComTac VI Headset fits into 11,386 polygons and 11,452 vertices, compact enough for game characters. Ear cups, headband and microphone arm are distinct pieces, so the headset can be fitted to different heads.

The model meets TurboSquid CheckMate requirements, with clean quads and readable names for every part. Large rounded cups with side controls are the recognizable feature, and they read well even from a third-person camera. The boom mic can sit forward for a radio pose or swing away for a relaxed look.

Two versions are offered at $49 each: the green main headset and a black tactical edition. Character artists reach for gear like this to finish operator loadouts alongside helmets and plate carriers.
