About This Model

Sci Fi Teleport Machine is one of the Space & Sci-Fi models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. You are getting 39,498 polygons and 40,684 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
