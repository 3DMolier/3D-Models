About This Model

A collection of bamboo furniture for tropical, eco and resort interiors, gathered so a whole room shares one natural material. Bamboo furniture is recognized by its jointed stems and lashed corners, which give each piece a handmade, airy look from any angle. Matching pieces in one purchase keep the stem thickness and color consistent from chair to table.

CheckMate Pro certification guarantees real-world scale, clean topology and well-named materials for every item in the set. Warm daylight filtered through palm leaves suits the material and emphasizes the rhythm of the stems. Sustainable design stories also use bamboo pieces to signal renewable materials and low-impact living.

Available since 2021, the set fits beach resort visualization, eco lodges, patio scenes and tropical game levels. The royalty free license lets furniture shops and hotel designers use it in commercial presentations. Add cushions in bright fabrics and a few potted plants, and a veranda scene comes together quickly.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2021 · Updated 12 November 2026
