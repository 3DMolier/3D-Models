About This Model

Looking for a Collections & Sets asset? The Magnetic Toys Collection is a finished, render-ready model at $49. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
