About This Model

First Essentials Bottle is one of the Clothing & Accessories models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 13,172 polygons and 6,940 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
