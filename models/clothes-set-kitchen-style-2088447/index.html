About This Model

This kitchen-style clothing set dresses a cook or a home chef in an apron worn over practical work clothes. With only 5,136 polygons, the mesh puts its detail into apron ties, pocket edges and fabric folds at the waist.

There are 3,744 vertices, a compact count that keeps the outfit fast in games and crowded restaurant scenes. Non-overlapping UVs let you add stripes, flour stains or a restaurant logo to the apron. TurboSquid checked the set under CheckMate Pro for structure, naming and proper scale.

Released in 2023, the outfit fits restaurant kitchens, cooking show sets, bakery scenes and food-themed games. Put a character beside a stove with a pan in hand to show a dinner service in full swing. The clothes also work for a cafe owner behind a counter or a baker kneading dough at dawn. Rolled sleeves and a tied waist give the figure the busy, practical look of someone mid-shift.
