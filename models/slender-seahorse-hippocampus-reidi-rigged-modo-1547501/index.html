About This Model

A slender seahorse, Hippocampus reidi, rigged for Modo so its curled tail can grip seagrass and its snout can turn toward prey. The long thin body shows the bony rings, the small dorsal fin and the tubular snout typical of the longsnout species.

Rigged segments let the tail coil and uncoil smoothly, which is the motion audiences recognize first in a seahorse. Built along the CheckMate specification, it arrives with orderly naming and structure for a Modo scene. Its native Modo setup means animation can begin immediately, without converting the rig from another package.

Royalty free and available since 2020, it suits aquarium displays, reef documentaries and marine biology lessons. Bright yellow or orange coloring makes this species a favorite subject for macro underwater photography and reef films. Pair two facing each other among corals and the scene hints at the famous seahorse courtship dance. Its small size suits close-up shots where the camera drifts slowly through a seagrass bed.
