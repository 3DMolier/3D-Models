About This Model

A collection of road traffic signs on posts, covering warnings, prohibitions and directions for street and highway scenes. Each sign has a flat face on a slim metal pole, so they can be planted along a curb or grouped at a junction. Bundled together, they save the time of searching for matching signs one by one.

The set earned CheckMate Pro after TurboSquid reviewed its geometry, scale and the way every object is named. Sign faces use their own materials, so local symbols or a different color code can be swapped in for another country. Scuffs on the poles make the props look like they have stood outdoors through several winters.

Available since 2021, Traffic Signs Collection suits driving simulators, city builders and road safety lessons for schools. Architects scatter the signs through street renders so a new development feels connected to real traffic rules. Game levels use them as cheap landmarks that guide players along a route.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2021 · Updated 12 November 2026
