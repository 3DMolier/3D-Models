About This Model

A collection of buildings under construction with cranes and machinery, ready for urban development, real estate and engineering scenes. Exposed floor slabs, scaffolding and rebar give each structure the unfinished look of a site in progress. Tower cranes, excavators and mixers parked around the frames tell the story of active work on a busy site.

Each piece is CheckMate Pro-certified, which assures clean topology and matching scale between buildings and machines. Morning light with long shadows across the site emphasizes the skeletal structure and empty floors. Concrete gray, safety yellow and orange netting give the scene a believable working palette from any angle.

Two versions belong to this series: Building Constructions with Equipment Collection and its sequel pack, Collection 2, sold separately. Sold since 2022 and royalty free, the set suits developers' progress renders, city games and engineering lectures. Construction firms use it to show investors how a project will look midway through the build.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
