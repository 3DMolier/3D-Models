About This Model

Priced at $39, the Classic Rotary Dial Table Phone is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 11,838 polygons and 12,049 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

9 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization and software demos. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
