Crocs Coast Clog Black is a detailed Clothing & Accessories asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. The mesh carries 18,264 polygons and 18,850 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
