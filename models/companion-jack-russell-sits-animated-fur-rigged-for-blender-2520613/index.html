About This Model

A Jack Russell terrier with groomed fur, rigged for Blender and animated sitting down, the calm companion pose of a family dog. A white coat with tan patches, folded ears and a compact body give the breed its instantly familiar look. The sit animation lowers the hindquarters first, the way a real dog settles beside its owner.

Under CheckMate specification rules, the dog opens in Blender with armature, fur and actions linked correctly. Hair particles can be toggled off in the viewport so animation stays responsive while you block shots. A digging version of the same terrier exists separately, useful when a scene needs a more active dog.

New in 2026 at $179, it fits pet food packaging, family animations and veterinary education. Interior renders gain warmth when a small dog sits by the sofa or the front door. Games use companion pets like this as followers that react when the player stops.
