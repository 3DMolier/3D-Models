About This Model

An SPMT is a floor on wheels that can be joined to other floors. Every axle steers and lifts independently, so a load of any shape can be carried level across ground that is not, and modules are simply coupled together until the deck is big enough for a bridge span or a refinery vessel.

The model holds 725,389 polygons and 777,236 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 20 maps all at 4096x4096. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $89 it suits heavy transport and logistics visualization, engineering and construction presentation, equipment marketing, documentary work and game environments. On sale since 2019.
