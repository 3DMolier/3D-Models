About This Model

GOES-R Satellite Double Panel is a detailed Space & Sci-Fi asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 123,485 polygons over 130,219 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
