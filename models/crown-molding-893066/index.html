About This Model

This crown molding profile bridges the joint between wall and ceiling, giving a plain room a finished traditional look. The profile combines a cove, a bead and a stepped fillet, built from quads with 5,516 polygons and 5,522 vertices. Run it along every wall, and the long straight piece repeats cleanly from corner to corner.

Two maps at 2048 x 2048 give the trim a painted white finish with a subtle surface grain. A CheckMate Lite rating confirms working formats, and non-overlapping UVs stay clean along the whole length. Shadows under the cove line make a ceiling look higher in warm evening light.

Priced at $1 since 2015, it is among the cheapest details you can add to an architectural render. Interior designers, real estate visualizers and set builders rely on it to dress living rooms and hallways. Pair it with matching baseboards and paneled doors for a Victorian or Georgian living room.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2015 · Updated 17 November 2026
