About This Model

The Collaborative Robot 2 Rigged is a lightweight cobot arm designed to work safely beside people, with rounded joints and a slim reach. Smooth covers hide the motors, so the arm looks friendly enough for labs, kitchens or assembly benches. Handing a part to a human worker, it illustrates modern human-robot collaboration in one clear shot.

Its geometry was made to CheckMate requirements, giving clean joints and a tidy hierarchy for animation. White shells and dark joint rings make the materials easy to swap for any corporate color scheme. Clean, bright lab lighting suits the glossy white covers and the dark joint rings best.

Three versions are rigged here: this base cobot plus matching versions prepared for Maya and for Modo. Each joint can rotate independently, making pick-and-place loops and handover gestures easy to stage. Introduced in 2022, the cobot serves robotics education, factory automation pitches and short tech explainers for general audiences.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
