About This Model

The X-47B is a flying wing with no cockpit and no tail, designed to launch from and land on a carrier deck without a pilot aboard. This build shows it with the wings folded, which is how the aircraft spends most of its life - parked on a crowded deck, taking up as little width as possible. That configuration is far more useful for a carrier scene than the clean flight pose.

It runs 411,293 polygons and 414,678 vertices, built entirely from quads with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 12 texture maps - 3 at 8192x8192 and 9 at 4096x4096. All-quad geometry subdivides cleanly, which matters on a shape defined by long smooth curves.

Five other builds sit on this page, including a rigged version at $129 and several alternative configurations at $99. On sale since 2016.
