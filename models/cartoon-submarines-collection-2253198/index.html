About This Model

A collection of cartoon submarines with rounded hulls, big portholes and bright paint, made for playful underwater scenes and children's stories. Each sub has its own silhouette, from stubby explorers to longer cruisers, so a fleet does not look cloned. Periscopes, propellers and fins are exaggerated in the cartoon tradition, reading clearly even on small screens.

Every sub follows CheckMate specification, sharing one scale so they can line up together in a harbor. Simple shapes mean low render cost, which suits mobile games and animated series with long shot lists. Colors are bold and easy to swap, so the fleet can match any palette or episode theme.

Since 2024 the collection has served kids' animation, aquarium signage and casual underwater games. Educational apps about the ocean also use friendly vehicles like these to guide young viewers. Add bubbles and a few cartoon fish and the ocean floor turns into a toy box adventure.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
