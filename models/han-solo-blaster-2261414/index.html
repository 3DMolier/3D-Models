About This Model

Han Solo Blaster is one of the Weapons models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $31. Counted at the source, the model is 55,431 polygons and 56,595 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX.
