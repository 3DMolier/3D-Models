About This Model

Woven wicker, perforated plastic and fabric hampers fill this bundle of laundry baskets for bathrooms, bedrooms and utility rooms. Handles, lids and the weave or vent pattern on the sides are what tell the types apart, and each is modeled in full. Filled with folded towels or left empty by the washing machine, a basket makes a home interior feel lived in.

Natural rattan, white plastic and gray canvas are the finishes most scenes call for, and all are simple to adjust in the materials. TurboSquid reviewers gave the bundle CheckMate Pro once they confirmed the meshes, scale and naming were in order. Openwork sides let light pass through in a sunny bathroom, casting a fine pattern on the tiles behind.

Three versions exist for the basket set, costing $43, $49 and $69, each with a different choice of shapes. Around since 2022, the baskets dress real estate interiors, home organization guides and furniture catalogs that show tidy storage. In animated shorts, a tipped basket with socks spilling out is a quick visual joke about a busy household.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
