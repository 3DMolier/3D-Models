About This Model

Priced at $279, the Rigged Workers Collection is a finished Collections & Sets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
