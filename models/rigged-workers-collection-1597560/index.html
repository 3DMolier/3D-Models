About This Model

Rigged construction and industrial workers in safety gear, ready to pose on a site, factory floor or warehouse. Rigged Workers Collection saves building individual characters when a scene needs a crew rather than a single figure. Hard hats, reflective vests and work boots make the trade clear even when the figures are small in frame.

Each character passed the Pro tier of CheckMate, so skeletons, scale and naming agree across the set. A shared skeleton layout lets you reuse animation clips from one worker to the next. Hands are posed open for gripping tools, which helps when you add drills, shovels or clipboards.

The bundle has been on sale since 2020 and costs $279 for the full crew. Construction visualizations, safety training videos, industrial simulations and engineering firm presentations rely on workers to show scale. Real proportions also let an architect check door widths and ceiling heights against a human figure.
