About This Model

A minimalist beige house with a brick chimney, simplified for neighborhood fills, site plans and architectural massing studies. Its 115,364 polygons and 119,337 vertices keep the clean roof planes, window openings and the textured chimney stack readable. The simple gabled form recalls a modern chalet, warm and calm rather than flashy, and suits rural or suburban plots.

UVs here are mixed, so check them before applying brick variation, plaster texture or window reflections. Following CheckMate specification, the building keeps tidy naming for walls, roof and chimney, so materials change quickly. The simplified detail level makes it ideal for repeating along a street without overloading the scene.

New for 2026, the house serves real estate visualization, urban planning layouts and background housing in games. A beige palette sits well with lawns and trees, letting landscaping take the spotlight in garden renders. Planners can line several copies along a road to test density before detailed design begins.
