About This Model

A white polar bear with dense fur is prepared here for animation in Maya, ready to walk across ice or swing its heavy head. White Polar Bear Fur Set Animated Rigged For Maya gives arctic scenes a hero animal that holds up in close shots. The long neck, small ears and broad paws match the real bear's distinctive build.

Clean topology around shoulders and hips, where the fur deforms most, keeps the coat from tearing in strong poses. Maya artists can use the included rig and animation directly instead of rebuilding them. Fur clumps slightly around the legs and belly, which matches how bears look after swimming.

New in 2026 at $569, the bear suits commercial and educational productions. Climate change documentaries, wildlife films, zoo apps and winter games all suit this bear. Low arctic sun and blowing snow bring out the subtle cream tones in the fur, especially against blue ice.
