About This Model

Cucumber Slice is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 1,904 polygons and 1,906 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
