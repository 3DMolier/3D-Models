About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Old Lady in Gardening Outfit is a finished, render-ready model at $129. You are getting 10,578 polygons and 11,427 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

17 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 3000 x 3000 resolution. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
