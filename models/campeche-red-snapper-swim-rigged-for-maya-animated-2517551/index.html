About This Model

A fish swimming is a whole body wave, not a tail flick. Getting that right is the difference between an aquarium shot that reads and one that looks like a toy being dragged through water, and it is why an animated build is worth more than a posed one.

This build is prepared for Maya, rigged and animated with a swim cycle, so it opens as a native scene rather than an import. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $149 it suits underwater and marine visualization, aquarium and nature documentary work, fishing and seafood presentation, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
