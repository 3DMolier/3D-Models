About This Model

The B-21 Raider Sixth Generation Bomber Used is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $229 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 709,862 polygons and 740,258 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 48.29 x 20.4 x H4.94 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
