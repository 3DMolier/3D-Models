About This Model

A set of flags hanging from short wall-mounted poles, the kind fixed above building entrances and on official facades. Several flags come together, so an embassy front, hotel entrance or town hall can show a full row at once. The cloth hangs with soft folds, which reads as real fabric even when no wind simulation is running.

Prepared to CheckMate specification, the poles and brackets keep real proportions for mounting on any facade. Flag designs sit on their own materials, so swapping in a national or corporate flag is a texture change. Brackets and pole tips stay separate from the cloth, which keeps repositioning along a wall quick and tidy.

Released in 2025, the collection suits government building renders, hotel facades, sports events and city scenes in games. Architects use it to dress entrance elevations so the building reads as official or international at a glance. A row of bright flags against stone also adds color to an otherwise neutral street view.
