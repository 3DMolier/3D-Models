About This Model

The Great Sphinx belongs to our Architecture & Landmarks range and is offered at $199 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 194,732 polygons and 98,408 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

Questions About This Model

Can the The Great Sphinx model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

Does the The Great Sphinx model include materials and textures?

Material and texture contents are listed per file on the TurboSquid product page. As a StemCell asset it ships with PBR materials that stay consistent across the delivered formats.

Which industries use the The Great Sphinx model?

Architecture, Film & Video Production and Advertising are the primary industries on this listing, though the Royalty Free license puts no limit on where the model is actually used.

What does StemCell mean for the The Great Sphinx model?

StemCell is TurboSquid's multi-format standard. The model was authored once and delivered in several formats with matching PBR materials, so shading survives the move between a game engine and an offline renderer.
