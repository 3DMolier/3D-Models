About This Model

A wall-mounted fire hose reel with a coiled red hose and nozzle, it equips corridors, warehouses and parking garages. The 44,767 polygons over 23,075 vertices form a triangulated mesh that keeps the drum and the coiled hose smooth. The nozzle hangs ready at the front, so a firefighter character can grab it in one move.

Its unwrap keeps the drum, hose and bracket apart, ready for safety labels and dust on the red paint. Materials control the gloss and color, so the reel can match national safety standards in any country. TurboSquid inspected the reel and granted CheckMate Pro for clean geometry, naming and scale.

Since 2023 the reel has appeared in building safety visuals, fire drill animations and industrial walkthroughs. Architects add it to corridor plans to show where emergency equipment will be fixed on the walls. Game levels also use it as an interactive hose that players unroll to fight a blaze.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2023 · Updated 19 November 2026
