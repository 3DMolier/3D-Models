About This Model

Priced at $19, the White Small Low Box of Burnt Planks is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

The mesh carries 11,808 polygons and 5,980 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
