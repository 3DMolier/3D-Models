About This Model

Assault Rifle Steyr AUG A3 belongs to our Weapons range and is offered at $79 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 48,439 polygons and 49,339 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

10 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
