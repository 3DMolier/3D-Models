About This Model

The Steyr AUG A3 is an Austrian bullpup assault rifle, with the magazine behind the grip and a short overall length. The 48,439 polygons capture the curved polymer stock, the rail on top, the forward grip and the clear magazine. Its rounded, almost futuristic shape has made the AUG a favorite in films and games for decades.

There are 49,339 vertices, unwrapped without overlaps and textured by 10 maps at 4096 x 4096. Those maps show the polymer grain, the metal barrel and wear along the edges where hands hold it. A CheckMate Pro check confirmed clean naming, so the magazine and bolt can be separated for animation.

Two color versions are offered, the standard Assault Rifle Steyr AUG A3 and an Austrian bullpup rifle in green, both $79. First-person shooters, police and military training videos and action films use it as a recognizable service rifle.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2017 · Updated 11 November 2026
