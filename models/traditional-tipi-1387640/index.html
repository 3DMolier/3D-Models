About This Model

Traditional Tipi is a detailed Architecture & Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. The mesh carries 40,685 polygons and 40,788 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 21 maps at 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.
