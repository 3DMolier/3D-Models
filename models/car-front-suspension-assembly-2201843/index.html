About This Model

A front suspension corner with strut, coil spring, control arm, hub and brake disc, for automotive training and technical renders. The Car Front Suspension Assembly uses 59,158 polygons and 31,277 vertices on the strut tower mount, steering knuckle and brake caliper. Parts sit in their installed positions, so the steering and suspension linkages can be traced by eye.

Non-overlapping UVs allow painted springs, bare machined surfaces and rubber boots to carry separate grime. This front assembly is made to CheckMate specification, so parts are named and scaled for rigging. Exploded views are simple to build, since each component is a separate object with its own pivot.

First sold in 2024, it pairs well with rear suspension models for complete chassis studies. Mechanics schools teach wheel alignment with it, and parts makers show how their components fit. Racing and car building games also reveal it during upgrades in garage and tuning menus.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 16 November 2026
