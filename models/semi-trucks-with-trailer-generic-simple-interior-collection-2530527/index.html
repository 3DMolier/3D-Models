About This Model

A collection of generic semi trucks with trailers, giving highway and logistics scenes a varied fleet without brand badges. Tall cabs, exhaust stacks and long box trailers form the classic silhouettes of freight traffic on any road. Simple interiors keep each cab light, which is what background traffic needs when many trucks share a scene.

Each truck follows CheckMate specification, keeping trailers and cabs at believable lengths when they line up at a depot. Because the bodies are unbranded, company liveries and fleet colors can be painted on without licensing concerns. Wheels, couplings and mirrors stay as clean parts, which helps when trucks need to turn or reverse.

This fleet came out in 2026 and is priced at $199, free of royalties for commercial scenes. Logistics visualization, highway planning, driving simulators and open-world games all need believable heavy traffic. A convoy rolling along a desert interstate at sunset is the image this pack was made for.
