About This Model

A traditional burial casket with a padded silk interior, built for funeral homes, period dramas and horror scenes. It uses 42,074 polygons and 42,289 vertices on the panelled lid, handles, lining folds and pillow. The hinged lid shows the soft quilted inside, which is what makes the casket read as ready for a service.

StemCell certification keeps the polished wood and satin lining consistent in every major renderer. Brass-colored handles along the sides suggest how pallbearers would carry it to the grave. Placed on a stand among flowers and candles, the casket anchors an entire memorial room.

Three versions are offered at $39 each: the main casket, a white one with burgundy lining and a black one with silk lining. All of them appeared in 2026 and are licensed Royalty Free for commercial and personal work. Funeral home visualization, vampire films, haunted house games and historical dramas use props like this for strong atmosphere.
