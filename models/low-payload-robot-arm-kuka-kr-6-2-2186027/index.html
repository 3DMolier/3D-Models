Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Low Payload Robot Arm Kuka KR 6 2 is a finished, render-ready model at $59. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.
