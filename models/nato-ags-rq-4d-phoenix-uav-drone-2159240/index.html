About This Model

Priced at $99, the NATO AGS RQ-4D Phoenix UAV Drone is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 250,904 polygons and 137,547 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
