About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Marmoset Monkey Skeleton is a finished, render-ready model at $59. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and medical visualization. 2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 48,738 polygons and 48,846 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
