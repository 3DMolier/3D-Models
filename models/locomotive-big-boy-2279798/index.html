About This Model

Locomotive Big Boy belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 801,724 polygons over 845,584 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
