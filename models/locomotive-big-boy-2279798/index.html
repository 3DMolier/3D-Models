About This Model

Priced at $129, the Locomotive Big Boy is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 801,724 polygons and 845,584 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
