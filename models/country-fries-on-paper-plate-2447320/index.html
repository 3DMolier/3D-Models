About This Model

Thick country fries piled on a paper plate, with golden crusts and skin-on edges, ready for fast food and picnic scenes. The 8,566 polygons shape the uneven fries and the ridged rim of the disposable plate beneath them. At 8,614 vertices, nearly the face count, the dish is light enough for a crowded food court.

The plate spans 20.01 x 20.01 x H6.08 cm, a typical disposable serving size. Grease spots, salt grains and browning stay unique because no two UV islands overlap. It meets the CheckMate standard from TurboSquid, so scale and naming need no cleanup after import.

Out since 2025, Country Fries on Paper Plate suits diner menus, food truck renders and cooking game levels. A ketchup cup and a soda beside it make a classic county fair lunch. Food delivery apps use similar shots to show portion size before an order is placed.
