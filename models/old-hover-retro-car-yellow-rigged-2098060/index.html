Priced at $149, the Old Hover Retro Car Yellow Rigged is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
