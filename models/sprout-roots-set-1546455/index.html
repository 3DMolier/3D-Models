Sprout Roots Set is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
