About This Model

An electronic attack jet hooked to a low carrier tow tractor, staged as it would be moved across a flight deck. The Boeing EA-18G with Shipboard Tow Tractor carries 437,711 polygons and 457,580 vertices across the airframe, jamming pods and the squat tug. Pairing the aircraft with its handler sets a deck scene in one move.

The pair meets TurboSquid CheckMate requirements, with clean geometry and well-named parts throughout. Wingtip pods and the twin tails make the Growler distinct from other Hornet variants, while the tractor sits low under the nose. The tow bar between them shows the exact moment before the jet is repositioned for launch.

Released in 2026 at $199, it is restricted to Editorial Uses Only because it depicts real military aircraft. Naval documentaries, defense news graphics, carrier operation films and flight simulators suit it well. Placed on a deck with crew figures, it gives the busy rhythm of carrier aviation without extra props.
