About This Model

A handful of round smiley face pins in different colors, this set decorates a backpack, a denim jacket or a corkboard. Each pin is simple, and together they use 2,400 polygons and 2,480 vertices, so scattering them costs nothing. The domed faces catch a highlight like enamel badges, and a cluster on a tote strap looks better than a neat grid.

Twelve texture maps at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024 cover the printed faces and the metal backs. CheckMate Pro certification confirms clean topology, and the UVs show no overlaps. You can swap the face artwork easily if a project needs different expressions or a logo.

Two versions sit together here, the assorted pins and a set of round smiley faces with a simpler layout. The pins came out in 2019, and either set is $29 for a whole handful of badges. Fashion close-ups, school scenes and merchandise mockups are where the pins shine.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2019 · Updated 14 November 2026
