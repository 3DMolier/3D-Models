About This Model

A collection of flying concept cars, sleek personal aircraft imagined for cities where traffic moves above the rooftops. Ducted fans, swept canopies and slim fuselages blend car styling with drone engineering in a believable way. Several designs in the set let a skyline fill with varied traffic instead of repeating the same vehicle.

StemCell certification gives these models standardized PBR materials, so glossy paint and glass behave consistently across engines. Each car is a separate object, ready to follow its own flight path in an animated city. Light strips along the bodies glow nicely against dusk skies, the usual setting for future mobility visuals.

Released in 2025, the collection suits urban air mobility pitches, science fiction games and smart city films. Hovering over a rooftop landing pad, a single craft explains a whole transport concept faster than a slide deck could. Startups and city planners use similar visuals to present air taxi routes to investors and residents.
