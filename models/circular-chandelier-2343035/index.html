About This Model

A circular chandelier with a single metal ring and candle-style lamps, the rustic fixture for dining rooms and halls. Only 1,824 polygons and 1,856 vertices are needed for the ring, the hanging chains and the small candle sleeves. Its open, airy shape lets light and shadow fall through, so it never blocks the view of a ceiling.

CheckMate specification guided the build, so the frame arrives at true scale with clean, named geometry. The ring and arms unwrap without overlap, ready for brass, black iron or gold leaf finishes chosen by the designer. Being light, the fixture can repeat along a banquet hall without weighing down a real-time scene.

At $9 it lights farmhouse kitchens, rustic restaurant interiors and castle halls in architectural visualization and games. Since 2025 the chandelier has also hung in wedding venue renders and cozy dining scenes in animation. Royalty Free terms make it usable in commercial interior renders, indie games and online lighting catalogs.
