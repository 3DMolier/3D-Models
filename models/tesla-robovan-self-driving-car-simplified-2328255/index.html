About This Model

Priced at $99, the Tesla Robovan Self-Driving Car Simplified is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
