About This Model

Priced at $4, the White Disposable Paper Plate is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

Geometry weighs in at 5,080 polygons over 2,542 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
