About This Model

Light Helicopter Bell 47 is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. Counted at the source, the model is 144,385 polygons and 154,424 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

9 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
