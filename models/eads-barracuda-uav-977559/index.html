About This Model

This Aircraft model - EADS Barracuda UAV - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2016 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Counted at the source, the model is 45,466 polygons and 46,258 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 9 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
