About This Model

This German euro coin shows the bimetallic design with a silver-colored center and a golden ring around the edge. The national side carries the federal eagle, the emblem Germany stamps on its euro coins, surrounded by stars. Fine relief on both faces and a reeded rim make the coin read as real metal currency in close-up.

Rated CheckMate Lite, the coin comes as a clean object ready for stacking or scattering in large numbers. Nickel brass for the ring and cupronickel for the center give the classic dual-tone look.

Available since 2023, the coin suits finance graphics, shop counters, vending machine scenes and numismatic presentations. Dropped into a tip jar or spread across a cafe table, a few coins show everyday spending in Europe. Economics lessons on the eurozone can explain national designs by placing it beside coins from other countries. Coin collectors' apps and catalog pages also need accurate renders of each national side.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
