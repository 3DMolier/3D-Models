About This Model

A counterbalance lift truck carrying a plastic pallet on its forks, the standard machine of warehouses and loading docks. The mast, the overhead guard and the heavy rear counterweight are all modeled, so the profile reads correctly from any side. Set between tall racks, it brings motion and scale to an otherwise static storage interior.

The pallet is a separate object, so it can be lifted, lowered or swapped for a wooden one. Its build follows the CheckMate rules for clean topology and naming, without a formal TurboSquid inspection. Yellow paint, black tires and the gray plastic deck sit on distinct materials that are easy to restyle.

Offered since 2015, the truck fits logistics animations, warehouse safety training, factory layouts and supply chain explainers. Drive it toward a trailer at the dock with a stack of cartons on board, and the workflow becomes clear. It also fills the background of supermarket stockrooms, distribution centers and busy cargo terminals.
