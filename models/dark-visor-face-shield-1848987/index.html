About This Model

The Dark Visor Face Shield is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are game environments, film production and historical visualization. The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 18,818 polygons and 19,003 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
