About This Model

A bald eagle dives in a hunting flight with its talons extended, animated in Blender for wildlife and patriotic sequences alike. The white head, hooked yellow beak and dark brown body give the raptor its unmistakable American profile from any viewing angle. Primary feathers spread like fingers at the wingtips as the bird adjusts its line of attack on unseen prey below.

Construction follows CheckMate specification, keeping wings, tail and talons logically grouped for quick edits by an animator. Blender handles the rig natively, so the strike can be slowed down for a dramatic moment just above the surface of the water. Hard afternoon sun against a deep blue sky makes the white tail and head almost glow as the eagle banks.

This royalty free eagle went on sale in 2026 and works in documentaries, outdoor games and nature channel title sequences. Pair the dive with a splash over a mountain river, and the classic fishing strike plays out in just a few seconds. Conservation films about recovering raptor populations use the sequence to show the national bird at its most powerful.
