About This Model

The A400M is the odd one out among modern military transports: turboprops rather than jets, chosen so it can lift heavy loads off short and rough strips that a jet would never attempt. Those eight bladed scimitar propellers are the aircraft's signature, and they turn in opposite directions on each wing.

The airframe holds 245,953 polygons and 257,119 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 13 maps, 7 at 8192x8192 and 6 at 4096x4096. It carries StemCell certification. The library also holds the green scheme and rigged builds, including native rigs for Maya and Cinema 4D.

At $179 it suits flight simulation, aviation and defence visualization, documentary and film, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.

Can the Airbus A400M Atlas Turboprop Military Transport Aircraft model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

What is the Airbus A400M Atlas Turboprop Military Transport Aircraft model typically used for?

Stated applications are aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

What does StemCell mean for the Airbus A400M Atlas Turboprop Military Transport Aircraft model?

StemCell is TurboSquid's multi-format standard. The model was authored once and delivered in several formats with matching PBR materials, so shading survives the move between a game engine and an offline renderer.
