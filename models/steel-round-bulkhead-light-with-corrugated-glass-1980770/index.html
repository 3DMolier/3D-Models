About This Model

A bulkhead light is a marine fitting that became an interior design staple: a heavy cast body, a threaded glass dome and a protective cage over it, all built to survive a deck. The corrugated glass is the detail that makes it work indoors, because it breaks the bulb into a pattern instead of glaring.

The mesh is 24,134 polygons and 12,359 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the glass modelled as its own body so a refractive material can produce the ribbed light pattern rather than faking it.

At $29 it suits interior and hospitality visualization, industrial and nautical styling, exterior and marine scenes, lighting retail renders and games. On sale since 2023.
