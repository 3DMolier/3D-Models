About This Model

Gantry Frame is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. You are getting 102,006 polygons and 100,579 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 335.35 x 203.38 x H280.9 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
