About This Model

A collection of Angara launch vehicles, the modular Russian rocket family that grows from light to heavy by adding booster cores. The common core stages and stacked boosters are what define the family, so the set shows how one design scales up.

Grouping the variants keeps proportions consistent, which matters when rockets of different lift classes stand side by side on a pad. CheckMate Pro certification guarantees real-world scale and tidy topology, so the vehicles line up with launch towers and service gantries. Slow orbit shots around the stacked cores show the clustered engines at the base in convincing detail.

Available to buyers since 2021, the set serves space agency explainers, aerospace education, launch documentaries and science fiction sequences. Rendered at the moment of liftoff with steam billowing around the pad, a heavy configuration fills the frame with power. Side-by-side lineups also make useful diagrams showing payload capacity growing with each added booster.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
