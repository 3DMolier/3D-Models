About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Twisted Rocks Glass is a finished, render-ready model at $19. You are getting 1,398 polygons and 701 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
