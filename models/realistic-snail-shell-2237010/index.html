About This Model

Priced at $19, the Realistic Snail Shell is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 978 polygons over 971 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
