About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The US Army Combat Uniform is a finished, render-ready model at $89. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Geometry weighs in at 22,374 polygons over 23,128 vertices - a light build for its class. Texture work runs to 16 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
