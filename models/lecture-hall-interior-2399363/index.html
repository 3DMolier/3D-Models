About This Model

Lecture Hall Interior belongs to our Furniture & Interior range and is offered at $79 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 721,364 polygons and 754,218 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 11.6 x 10.6 x H3.56 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
