About This Model

A set of fire exit doors lets you dress corridors, stairwells and back-of-house walls of a building with consistent emergency hardware. Panic bars, closers and the green running-man sign above the frame are the details that tell a viewer this is an evacuation route. Grouping several doors in one purchase keeps the hinge style and paint finish matching across a whole floor plan.

The collection is built to the CheckMate specification, so the files open without broken references or missing parts in common packages. Steel leaves usually carry a flat industrial coat, which takes scuffs and grime well if you want a worn service corridor. Swap the door color per building zone and the hardware keeps reading as one fire safety system.

The set went on sale in 2026 and costs $24 for the whole group of doors. Architectural visualization teams use doors like these for code-compliant office and hospital interiors, while game artists line warehouse levels with them. Film crews building a stairwell chase scene also need exits that look real when a character slams through the bar.
