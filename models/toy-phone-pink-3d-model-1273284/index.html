About This Model

Toy Phone Pink is one of the Toys & Games models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2018 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
