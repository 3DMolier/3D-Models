About This Model

Priced at $99, the West Highland Terrier is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 26,393 polygons and 25,989 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
