About This Model

A tall clay amphora with a pointed base, held upright by a simple iron ring stand, as such vessels stood in ancient storerooms. The mesh uses only 3,520 polygons and 3,524 vertices, all quads, which keeps the curved shoulders smooth under subdivision. The narrow neck and two looping handles give the silhouette that reads instantly as Greek or Roman pottery.

Its UV layout reuses space across symmetrical parts, which is fine for plain clay and simple painted bands. Vessel and stand are grouped apart for easy material changes, in line with the CheckMate rules TurboSquid applies.

Two versions sit on the page, each $29: the beige clay amphora and the Antiqued Greek Terracotta Amphora on Metal Stand Brown. The brown one reads darker and older, which helps when a scene needs a museum piece rather than a working jar. Released in 2025, the amphora fits Mediterranean interiors, historical film sets and game levels built around an ancient port.
