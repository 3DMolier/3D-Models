About This Model

A hand cranked pasta machine is a set of rollers in a chromed frame, with a clamp that grips a table edge, a thickness dial on the side and a crank that fits into one of two positions. That clamp is what makes it a kitchen tool rather than an appliance - it has no motor and no power lead at all.

The mesh is efficient at 25,320 polygons and 25,515 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the rollers, the dial and the clamp.

A silver version sits on this page at the same $24. Both suit kitchen and culinary visualization, cookware retail renders, recipe and food editorial imagery, restaurant interiors and games. On sale since 2022.
