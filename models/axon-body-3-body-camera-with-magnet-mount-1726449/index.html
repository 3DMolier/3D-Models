About This Model

A police body camera clipped to a magnetic mount, worn at chest height on a uniform to record patrol encounters. Axon Body 3 Body Camera with Magnet Mount spends 37,090 polygons over 39,973 vertices on the lens bezel, buttons and the mount plate. The rugged rectangular housing has rounded corners and a front lens, so it reads clearly on an officer from across a street.

Without bundled image maps, the black housing and status light rely on shaders that are quick to adjust. Every UV island has room of its own for decals, and the camera passed the CheckMate Pro review.

The page carries 2 versions at $39 each: this Axon camera and an unbranded body camera with the same magnet mount. Police training videos, news graphics on accountability and crime dramas explain its 19 sales since 2021. Pinned to a uniform figure, the device tells viewers that every word is being recorded. Because it bears the Axon name, the branded build is meant for editorial work.
