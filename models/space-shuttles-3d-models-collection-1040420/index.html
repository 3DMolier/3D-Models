About This Model

Priced at $99, the Space Shuttles Collection is a finished Model Bundles & Sets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. The set covers several related objects at once, which saves assembling a scene from separate purchases.
