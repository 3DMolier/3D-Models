About This Model

An amphibious combat vehicle has to be two things badly compromised into one: a boat that can climb a beach and an armoured carrier that can drive inland. The result is a hull with a bow plane, water jets at the stern, eight large wheels and a freeboard tall enough to keep the sea out. The model carries the bow plane, the propulsion housings, the wheel arches and the remote weapon station.

It runs 434,120 polygons and 459,684 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, built to StemCell specification for real-time use as well as offline rendering.

Three rigged builds sit on this page at $169, including versions prepared for Maya and for Cinema 4D, for when the wheels turn or the ramp drops. The static model at $149 covers defence visualization, naval and marine corps presentation, documentary work, training material and games.
