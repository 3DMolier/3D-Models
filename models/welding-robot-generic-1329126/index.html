About This Model

Welding Robot Generic is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

Geometry weighs in at 146,796 polygons over 152,284 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 7 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
