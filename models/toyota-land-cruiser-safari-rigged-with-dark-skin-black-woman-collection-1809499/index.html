About This Model

A Toyota Land Cruiser set up for safari pairs with a dark-skinned woman figure, giving an expedition scene both its vehicle and a traveler. Its boxy body and high ground clearance give the cruiser a field-ready silhouette on dusty savanna tracks.

Built to CheckMate specification, both models share clean topology and consistent scale, so the woman stands correctly beside the door. Licensing is Editorial Uses Only, because the vehicle reproduces a real manufacturer's design and badges. Character and vehicle arriving together saves the time of matching scale and lighting between separate purchases.

Offered since 2021 at $169, the collection serves travel documentaries, film scenes and educational material about wildlife tourism. Park the cruiser under an acacia with the woman looking toward the horizon, and the frame reads as a quiet game drive. The pair also suits editorial illustrations about African conservation work and remote fieldwork. Dust effects and long afternoon shadows suit the set, since the vehicle was made for open, dry terrain.
