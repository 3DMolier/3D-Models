About This Model

A fishing collection bringing together the rods, reels and tackle an angler packs for a day on the water. Having the gear as one set means a boat deck or a riverside camp is dressed in a single pass. Long thin rods are the hardest part to get right, and they set the silhouette of any fishing scene.

The kit follows CheckMate specification guidance, so each item scales correctly beside hands, boats and chairs. Predictable loops and no hidden faces mean the pieces render cleanly even when they cross in a tight pile. Pivots sit at the origin, which makes arraying rods along a rack or a shop wall very quick.

Released in 2024, the fishing set suits outdoor game levels, tackle shop interiors and lakeside animation. Its royalty free license fits sporting goods advertising and fishing magazine illustration. Lean a rod against a cooler by the dock and the whole morning story is told.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 13 November 2026
