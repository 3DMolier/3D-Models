About This Model

A bronze Corinthian helmet from ancient Greece, with a full face guard, narrow eye openings and a tall horsehair crest on top. The 11,997 polygons over 12,035 vertices are enough for the flared cheek pieces, nose guard and the crest holder. Its hammered bronze shell covered nearly the whole head, which is why hoplites are so recognizable in art.

Surfacing uses 6 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024 for aged bronze, verdigris and the dyed crest. Paint the plume red, black or white without stretching, since no UV islands overlap. CheckMate Pro grading assures a faithful size when the helmet sits on a warrior character.

Its debut came in 2017, and since then it has armed Spartan characters, history lessons and battle reenactment films. Rest it on a round shield beside a spear and the scene speaks of Thermopylae without a word. Museums use it for virtual exhibits explaining how Greek infantry fought in a tight phalanx formation.
