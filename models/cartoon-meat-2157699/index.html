About This Model

Cartoon Meat is the classic chunk of roast meat on a bone, the round comic food every stylized game hero eats. Only 904 polygons and 470 vertices build it, with a chunky rounded body and a clean white bone at each end. Simple bold forms keep it readable at a distance, which matters when the item floats as a pickup in a level.

StemCell materials give the meat a toon-friendly roasted brown and a bright bone that render alike in many engines. The UVs are laid flat with no overlaps, so you can paint grill marks or a glossy glaze onto the surface. TurboSquid's StemCell program also checks real scale and file structure, which makes the meat quick to drop into scenes.

A 2023 model costing $9, it suits mobile games, animated shorts and fantasy tavern scenes with a cartoon style. Game designers use it as a health pickup that restores energy when a character grabs it in the middle of a run. Animators also pile several onto a feast table to give a cartoon banquet its comic sense of plenty.
