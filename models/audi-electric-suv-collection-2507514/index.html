About This Model

A collection of Audi electric SUVs, smooth crossover bodies with closed grilles, slim light bars and flush aerodynamic wheels. Closed grilles replace the radiator openings of combustion cars, which is the quickest visual cue that these vehicles run on batteries.

Grouping several models keeps paint, glass and tire materials consistent, so a lineup in a showroom looks like one family. Every SUV follows CheckMate guidelines, so each body arrives at real-world size next to chargers, garages and pedestrians. Licensing is Editorial Uses Only, so these vehicles belong in automotive journalism, education, film and design studies. Wet asphalt reflections under the slim headlights give night renders a sleek, premium character.

Since 2025 the collection has fit electric mobility explainers, charging infrastructure visualization, car review illustrations and urban film scenes. Parked at a charging station under evening light, the SUVs make a clean, modern image of electric driving. A row of them in different colors helps comparison articles show the range at a single glance.
