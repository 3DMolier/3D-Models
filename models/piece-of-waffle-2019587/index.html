About This Model

The Piece of Waffle is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. You are getting 5,048 polygons and 2,526 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX.

2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
