About This Model

London Sightseeing Cruise Ship belongs to our Ships & Boats range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 869,496 polygons and 917,843 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX.

2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
