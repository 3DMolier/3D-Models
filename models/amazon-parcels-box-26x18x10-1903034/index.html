About This Model

A cardboard shipping carton with a printed smile logo and taped flaps, ready for delivery and e-commerce scenes. The 12,236 polygons and 6,122 vertices give the corners a slight softness and keep the tape seams visible. Stacked on a doorstep or a conveyor, the carton immediately reads as an online order on its way.

No texture maps are attached, so kraft paper color and the printed artwork are left for you to assign. The unwrap is laid out without overlap, which helps when adding shipping labels, barcodes or handwritten notes. CheckMate Pro certification signals that TurboSquid staff verified the geometry, naming and overall scene structure.

You get two versions at $24 each: the Amazon Parcels Box 26x18x10 and a larger Amazon Parcels Box 41x26x26. Coming out in 2022, the carton suits logistics explainers, unboxing thumbnails, warehouse shelves and apartment hallway scenes. Stack the two sizes together, and the pile looks like a real weekly delivery rather than a single prop.
