About This Model

A set of volcanic mountains with craters and lava-scarred slopes, built for geography lessons, disaster documentaries and fantasy landscapes. Each cone has its own profile, from a broad shield shape to a steep crater rim. A range assembled from the set therefore avoids looking repetitive on the horizon.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms the pieces were checked for clean structure, which matters when terrain is combined with other ground meshes. Lava flows and ash fields can be painted down the slopes, while open craters leave space for smoke or glowing magma. Buying the volcanoes together also keeps the rock style consistent across every peak in a shot.

The collection has been on sale since 2022 and is bought for educational animation, science videos and game environments. Teachers use the cones to show how stratovolcanoes differ from gentler shield volcanoes in cross section. Game artists place them on the horizon of survival maps, where a smoking peak instantly sets a dangerous tone for the level.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
