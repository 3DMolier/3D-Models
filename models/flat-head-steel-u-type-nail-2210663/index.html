About This Model

A U-shaped steel fencing nail with two pointed legs and a flat head bridge, the staple used to fix wire to posts. Its 561 polygons and 552 vertices shape the bent shank, the sharp tips and the flattened top of the bridge. The simple U form reads clearly in close shots of fences, upholstery work or wooden crates being assembled.

Its construction meets the CheckMate specification, giving true scale and clean geometry for close hardware renders and catalogs. The UV layout has no overlapping islands, so galvanized zinc, rust or bright steel finishes apply evenly. Light enough to scatter by the hundred, the nails can fill a hardware bin or spill across a workbench.

At $5 it suits hardware store catalogs, fencing tutorials and DIY guides about fixing wire mesh. Since 2024 it has also helped engineering students illustrate fasteners in woodworking and agricultural building courses. Its Royalty Free license allows commercial use in product catalogs, how-to videos and printed instruction sheets.
