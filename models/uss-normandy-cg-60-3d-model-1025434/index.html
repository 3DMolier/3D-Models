About This Model

An Aegis cruiser built for fleet compositions, named after the beaches where the Allied landings opened the western front. USS Normandy CG-60 3D Model shows the Ticonderoga hull with its flight deck aft, gun mounts at bow and stern, and a tall mast. The tiered superstructure keeps the radar faces angled outward, a shape that reads clearly even in silhouette at dusk.

Unlike several sister ships in this range, it holds CheckMate Pro certification, the stricter TurboSquid standard for topology and scale. That extra review makes it a safer pick when the camera moves close along the hull plating. Gray paint is even and unmarked by heavy rust, matching a ship in active service.

It joined the range in 2016, costs $289 and works for carrier strike group escorts in both games and film. D-Day anniversary documentaries, naval heritage pieces and modern fleet exercises all suit a ship carrying this name. Deck crews and helicopters can be added on the stern pad for a lively harbor departure.
