About This Model

An African acacia tree paired with a white lion, bundled as a savanna vignette for wildlife films, safari visuals and nature games. The flat-topped acacia canopy is the shorthand for the African plains, and a rare white lion makes the scene memorable. Having tree and animal together means scale and lighting already belong to one setting.

Certified as CheckMate Pro, both pieces share real-world scale, so the lion rests convincingly in the shade beneath the branches. Golden-hour light from low on the horizon flatters both the pale fur and the thorny tree silhouette. White lions are a color variant of the African lion, which makes them a favorite subject in conservation stories.

Available since 2021, it suits safari tour visuals, conservation campaigns, zoo signage and educational documentaries. Royalty free terms open it to travel brochures and wildlife charity posters. Add grass and a dusty ground plane and the vignette expands into a full savanna panorama.
