About This Model

Sandwich Triangle is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 64,124 polygons and 37,824 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
