About This Model

A lab-style albino rat with fur and ready animation clips arrives here for Blender, rigged as a complete set. Pink ears, a long bare tail and red eyes set the white rat apart from wild brown rodents. The fur keeps the body soft-edged, so it sits naturally in macro shots with shallow depth of field.

Under CheckMate specification, the file keeps armature and fur system clearly named for quick edits. Animation clips cover typical rodent behavior, saving time on the twitchy, hard-to-key movement of small mammals. Whiskers and small clawed feet hold up well when the camera drops to floor level.

A fresh 2026 release, the rat suits laboratory science explainers, pet care videos and horror scenes in damp basements. Pharmaceutical and research presentations often show white rats to illustrate drug testing stages. Several duplicates scurrying along a wall can fill a sewer or cellar shot quickly.
