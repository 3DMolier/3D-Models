About This Model

Priced at $19, the Vintage PNP Silicon Planar Transistor is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 1,380 polygons and 1,359 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX.
