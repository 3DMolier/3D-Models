About This Model

A retro car with rounded fenders, chrome trim and a simple interior, made for period streets where vintage traffic sets the era. The cabin holds seats, a steering wheel and a dashboard in basic form, enough for shots through the windows. Rounded headlights and a long hood give the body the classic profile viewers link with mid-century roads.

The model carries CheckMate Lite certification, which confirms it opens correctly and delivers what the preview images show. A simplified interior keeps the file light, so several cars can line a street without slowing the scene. Glossy paint and chrome bumpers catch reflections nicely, especially under evening lamps or wet asphalt lighting.

Available since 2020, the Retro Car Simple Interior is licensed for commercial and personal projects alike. Period films, nostalgic game levels, architectural renders of old towns and diner scenes need exactly this kind of background or hero traffic. Swapping the body color is quick, which helps fill a parking lot with varied classics from one model.
