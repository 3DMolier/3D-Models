About This Model

A printable statue of Shiva modeled after the giant seated figure at Murudeshwara, the seaside temple town in Karnataka, India. The god sits in meditation with a serpent around his neck, the trident at his side and the crescent moon in his hair.

Prepared for printing, the mesh is a closed solid without loose shells, so slicers read it without repair. The CheckMate-standard build keeps the file clean and named, which also makes it easy to render digitally. Print it small for a home altar or large for a temple display, and the calm expression holds up at both sizes.

Listed since 2024 for royalty free use in devotional decor, cultural education and Indian travel visuals. Painted in the original's blue and gold, a print becomes a striking gift or souvenir. Temple builders and festival designers can also use it to preview a statue before commissioning a full-size casting.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
