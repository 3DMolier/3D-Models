About This Model

The cup carrier is a piece of packaging that exists entirely because hands run out. It is moulded pulp, it is thrown away in under ten minutes, and it solves a problem that had defeated cafes for decades.

The model is very light at 16,532 polygons and 8,264 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplying the holder with three cups. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $29 it suits food and beverage visualization, packaging and branding mockups, e-commerce imagery, cafe and street scenes, film set dressing and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
