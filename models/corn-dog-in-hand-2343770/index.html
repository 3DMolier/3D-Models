About This Model

The Corn Dog in Hand is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 3,764 polygons and 3,842 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
