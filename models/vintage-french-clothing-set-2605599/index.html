About This Model

A vintage French outfit with a striped sailor shirt, beret, trousers and neck scarf, the classic look of Breton seaside towns. The Vintage French Clothing Set uses 64,272 polygons, giving folds, cuffs and the soft crown of the beret real volume.

Its 64,930 vertices let the fabric wrinkle naturally at elbows and knees when laid over a figure. Built to CheckMate requirements, the garments arrive organized and sized for an adult wearer. Horizontal stripes on the shirt carry the nautical character that made this style famous far beyond the coast.

Priced at $129 since 2026, the set suits period films, fashion visualizations and European travel scenes. Dress a character strolling along the Seine, or hang the pieces on a rack in a vintage boutique. Costume departments can plan a whole ensemble before sourcing fabrics or tailoring real garments. The scarf knotted at the neck adds a dash of color and finishes the outfit with typical Parisian flair.
