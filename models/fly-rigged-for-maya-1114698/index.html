About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Fly Rigged for Maya is a finished, render-ready model at $99. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Which industries use the Fly Rigged for Maya model?

Film & Video Production, Game Development and Advertising are the primary industries on this listing, though the Royalty Free license puts no limit on where the model is actually used.

Can the Fly Rigged for Maya model be used in a commercial project?

It ships with TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, so a single purchase covers commercial delivery - you do not pay again per project or per seat of the finished work.

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.

What is the Fly Rigged for Maya model typically used for?
