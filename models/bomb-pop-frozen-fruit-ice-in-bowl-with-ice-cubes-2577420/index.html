About This Model

Bomb Pop Frozen Fruit Ice in Bowl with Ice Cubes is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $39. You are getting 24,756 polygons and 24,966 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 21.27 x 18.27 x H15.7 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing.
