About This Model

A set of a skier and a snowboarder in full winter gear, ready to populate slopes, lifts and resort promos. Both riders wear helmets, goggles, jackets and gloves, so they read as winter athletes from any angle. They can carve down a run, wait at the lift line or relax outside a mountain lodge with friends.

The Skier and Snowboard Man Rigged Collection is built to CheckMate specification for clean, organized files. Goggles hide the faces, which lets the same figures stand in for many guests on the hill. Bright jackets keep the characters visible against white snow, even in wide drone shots.

Since 2019 the pack has 10 buyers at $189 among resort marketers, sports game studios and travel animators. Pairing both sports in one scene reflects how mixed modern slopes really look in winter. Ski schools also use such figures in safety animations about right of way on busy runs.
