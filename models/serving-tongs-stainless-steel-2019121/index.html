About This Model

A pair of stainless steel serving tongs with scalloped tips, the buffet tool used for salad, bread and grilled food. The 3,602 polygons and 3,424 vertices cover the long spring arms, the flat grip sections and the gently cupped heads. The arms are open at rest, so the tongs look ready to pick up food the moment they enter frame.

No textures are packed with them, and a brushed steel shader with soft reflections is all they need. Non-overlapping UVs allow fine polishing lines along the arms for close product shots. CheckMate Pro status confirms the tongs sit at real scale and open cleanly in any package.

Available since 2023, the tongs suit restaurant buffet scenes, barbecue setups and kitchenware catalog renders. Laid across a salad bowl or beside a grill, they quietly signal that food is about to be served. Their light mesh lets a full buffet line hold several sets without slowing the scene.
