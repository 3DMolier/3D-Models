About This Model

Looking for a Sports & Recreation asset? The Outdoor Badminton Court is a finished, render-ready model at $49. Geometry weighs in at 106,710 polygons over 107,390 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2321 x 2321 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
