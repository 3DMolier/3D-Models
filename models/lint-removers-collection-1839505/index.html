About This Model

This collection brings together several lint removers, the small household tools used to lift fluff and pet hair from clothes. Handheld rollers and fabric shavers share a palm-sized grip, and the set lets you choose the style that fits a scene.

As a CheckMate Pro asset, the set has correct real-world scale and a scene layout that is easy to navigate. Each remover sits as a separate object, so one can be placed on a dresser while the others stay hidden. Sticky roller surfaces and mesh blade guards are the details that make these tools recognizable at close range.

The bundle has been available since 2022 and costs $34, cheaper than gathering similar pieces one by one. Laundry room interiors, wardrobe close-ups and home care product shots are where such small tools usually appear. In a game apartment they add believable clutter to a closet shelf without demanding attention. A lint roller beside a folded coat says more about a character's routine than a line of dialogue.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
