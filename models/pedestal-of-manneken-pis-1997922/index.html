About This Model

The pedestal is the part of the Manneken Pis nobody photographs, and it is most of the monument. The statue itself is famously tiny; the niche, the basin and the stonework around it are what give a small bronze figure the presence of a landmark.

This is supplied as the pedestal, and it carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $99 it suits architectural and historical visualization, museum and exhibition work, urban scenes, film backgrounds, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
