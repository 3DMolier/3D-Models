About This Model

A group of dark skinned teenage boys in everyday clothes, each with his own face, hairstyle and outfit for a diverse cast. Every character is rigged, so they can walk, sit, talk or play sports in school, street and park scenes. They suit high school corridors, basketball courts, bus stops and family stories where young people fill the frame.

Dark Skin Teenage Boys Collection was prepared to the CheckMate specification of TurboSquid, which checks geometry, scale and scene naming. Skin tones and clothing are separate materials, so jackets, sneakers and hoodies can change color without touching faces. Rich dark skin looks natural with warm key lighting and a subtle sheen that keeps facial features readable.

Each of the three versions is $299 and contains the same boys, prepared for Maya, Cinema 4D or Modo. Picking the version for your own package avoids rig conversion and keeps controls exactly as the riggers intended. Released in 2022, the group serves education videos, game crowds, architectural visualization and stories about school and youth culture.
