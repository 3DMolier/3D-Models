About This Model

A pool hoist is the difference between a facility being accessible and merely claiming to be. It is required by law in many places, rarely photographed in brochures, and it is the single piece of equipment that decides whether some people can swim at all.

The model is light at 22,238 polygons and 22,129 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and rigged builds are offered separately, including native rigs for Maya and Cinema 4D.

At $39 it suits accessibility and facilities visualization, pool and leisure centre scenes, equipment marketing, architectural renders, training material and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
