About This Model

Olympic Sleds 3D Models Collection gathers the racing sleds of winter sliding sports, from bobsleigh to luge and skeleton. Each sport uses a very different shape, from enclosed bobsled shells to flat skeleton trays ridden head first. Seeing those sleds together makes it easy to explain how riders sit, lie back or lie face down on the ice.

Before sale the sleds were certified CheckMate Pro, a review of geometry, scale and naming by TurboSquid. Glossy painted bodies and steel runners catch light from stadium floodlights, which helps fast motion shots read clearly.

First offered in 2017 at $59, the sleds serve winter sports broadcasts, games graphics and training simulators. Sports documentaries use them to break down track technique, while museums display them in winter games exhibits. Racing games set on icy tracks can put each sled into its own event with matching physics. Lined up at a start house, the group also makes a neat establishing shot before a race.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2017 · Updated 11 November 2026
