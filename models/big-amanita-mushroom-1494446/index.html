Big Amanita Mushroom is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. The mesh carries 1,477 polygons and 1,474 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
