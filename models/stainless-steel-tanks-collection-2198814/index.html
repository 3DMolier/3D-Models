About This Model

This collection of stainless steel tanks equips brewery, dairy and chemical plant scenes with vessels of several sizes and shapes. Domed tops, conical bottoms and short support legs give each tank the look of real process equipment. Valves, hatches and pipe stubs break up the smooth cylinders and show where lines would connect.

The set carries a CheckMate Pro rating, which means the geometry, file naming and scale passed a full inspection. Brushed metal is where these tanks shine, and smooth curvature keeps reflections flowing without kinks. Each vessel is a separate object, so rows of fermenters can be built from a handful of pieces.

Offered since 2024, the tanks fit factory walkthroughs, food production videos and industrial game levels. Arranged along a tiled floor under bright lamps, they make a convincing craft brewery hall. Under a Royalty Free license, the vessels may appear in commercial training material and client presentations.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
