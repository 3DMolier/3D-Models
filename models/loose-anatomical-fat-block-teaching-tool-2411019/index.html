About This Model

The fat block is a teaching object rather than an anatomical one. It represents a volume of adipose tissue at scale so that a patient can hold it, and its entire purpose is that a number on a chart becomes something with weight.

The model is extremely light at 3,129 polygons and 3,131 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $19 it suits medical and nutrition education, patient explanation, clinic and fitness marketing, health campaigns and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
