About This Model

A Chinese man and woman in traditional dress, collected together for festival, temple and historical drama scenes set in old towns. Silk garments with wide sleeves and embroidered trims are the main read, so the fabric carries most of the detail.

CheckMate Pro review checked both figures for clean geometry, correct scale and tidy material names before release. That keeps the couple matched in height and shading when they stand side by side in a courtyard. Each garment is modeled as its own layer, so the sleeves and skirt hold shape when seen from the side.

Out since 2023 with a $169 price, the pair suits Lunar New Year visuals, cultural education and museum presentations. Stand them under red lanterns at a gate, and the scene reads as a celebration even without crowds. Travel promotions and heritage tours use couples like this to give old streets a human presence. Buying both characters together avoids the mismatched styles that come from separately sourced figures.
