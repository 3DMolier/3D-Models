About This Model

Priced at $11, the Kissing Android Emoji is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 1,152 polygons and 1,147 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering.

2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
