About This Model

Plastic Spring Clamp belongs to our Tools range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 12,856 polygons over 12,812 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
