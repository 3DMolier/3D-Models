About This Model

This bundle gathers blocky, cube-built objects in the spirit of Minecraft, so a whole voxel-style scene can be dressed from a single purchase. Every shape is assembled from square faces and hard right angles, which keeps the geometry honest and light even when many pieces share one frame. The pixel-grid look depends on crisp edges rather than smooth curves, and the set keeps those edges sharp.

The collection passed the CheckMate Pro inspection on TurboSquid, which covers clean topology, sensible naming and files that open without repair. Because the style relies on flat colored faces, a buyer can recolor a whole family of blocks in minutes instead of repainting detailed surfaces. Square forms also line up edge to edge without seams, so ground pieces repeat across a large map with no visible joins.

Priced at $149 the Minecraft Collection has been on sale since 2016 and has found 12 buyers over those years. Most of them reach for it when building fan animations, game prototypes and playful explainer videos where a cubic world sets the tone at once. Teachers also use such sets for lessons about pixel art, since the chunky silhouettes are easy for young viewers to read.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
