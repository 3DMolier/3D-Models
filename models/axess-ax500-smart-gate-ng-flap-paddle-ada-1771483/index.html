About This Model

This Architecture & Landmarks model - Smart Gate Flap Paddle - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 39,669 polygons over 40,318 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
