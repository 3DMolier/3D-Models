About This Model

Kitchen Microfiber Blue is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $9. You are getting 7,692 polygons and 3,848 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and event visualization.
