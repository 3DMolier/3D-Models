About This Model

A rubber tracked dumper exists for ground too soft to carry a wheeled truck. The tracks spread the load so it can work a marsh, a forest floor or a fresh construction site without sinking, and the body sits low and wide over them. That combination gives the machine a squat, deliberate look unlike any road vehicle.

The mesh runs 457,885 polygons and 241,813 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the track links, the tipping body and the cab.

This page is unusually deep, with seven other builds from $99 to $159 including dirty, old and orange variants and rigged versions prepared for Maya and Cinema 4D. Aimed at construction, forestry and utilities visualisation, equipment marketing, site scenes and games. Built to StemCell specification, on sale since 2024.
