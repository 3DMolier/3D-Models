About This Model

A giant oceanic manta ray with broad triangular wings, curled cephalic fins and a short whip-like tail. Manta Ray captures the dark back, the pale belly with its spot pattern and the wide filter mouth at the front. Its flat wingspan glides through open water, so a slow camera beneath it makes a majestic silhouette against the light.

Smooth skin with subtle sheen keeps the animal believable in clear tropical water or murky depths. Gill slits on the underside are modeled as real openings for close shots from below. Inspectors approved the ray at CheckMate Pro, verifying clean topology, logical naming and true proportions.

Four versions are available, with this static ray at $79 and rigged builds at $99 each for general use, Maya and Cinema 4D. Diving resorts, ocean documentaries and aquarium displays explain why the ray has found 19 buyers since 2016. Swimming past a diver above a coral reef, the manta gives any underwater scene a sense of scale.
