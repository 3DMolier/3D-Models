About This Model

Priced at $19, the Middle Finger Emoticon is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 661 polygons and 650 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 1024 x 1024 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
