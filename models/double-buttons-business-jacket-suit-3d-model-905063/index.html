About This Model

A double-breasted business jacket with peaked lapels and a double row of buttons, for office characters and formal wear. The overlapping front panel is what sets this cut apart from a single-breasted blazer at a glance. Shoulders, sleeves and lapel rolls are shaped as if pressed by a tailor, ready for a mannequin or a figure.

Wool suiting reads through subtle weave and soft sheen, so a fine fabric material gives the best result. Passing CheckMate Pro review means the jacket has clean geometry and realistic adult proportions. Pinstripes, checks or plain charcoal can be applied without changing the garment itself, which suits a whole wardrobe.

Since 2015 the jacket has collected 24 sales at $49 from fashion, film and corporate projects. It dresses a banker in a lobby scene, a groom at a wedding or a politician at a podium. Hung on a valet stand in a walk-in closet, it also works as quiet luxury set dressing.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026

Can the Double Buttons Business Jacket Suit model be used in a commercial project?

What is the Double Buttons Business Jacket Suit model typically used for?

Stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.
