About This Model

A row of helicopter troop seats with harnesses, built for military cabin interiors, rescue flights and transport scenes. The 196,420 polygons go into seat frames, cushions and buckles for each passenger along the row. Harness straps cross over each seat back, showing how troops secure themselves before takeoff into a hot zone.

StemCell certification keeps fabric and metal looking the same across every provided file type. Partial UVs and 200,088 vertices allow the row to fill a long cabin wall without strain on the scene. Seats stay separate, so you can remove a few to make space for stretchers on a medical flight.

At $39, launched in 2026, the seats suit military simulation, aviation training, war film interiors and games. The row runs 123.88 x 608.83 x H94.58 cm, matching a full cabin side. Seat soldiers along the row with the rear ramp open to stage an insertion flight at dawn.
