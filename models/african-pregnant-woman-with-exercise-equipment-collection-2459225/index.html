About This Model

A pregnant African woman with exercise gear such as a mat and ball, ready for scenes about staying active during pregnancy. The African Pregnant Woman with Exercise Equipment Collection fills a gap in diverse maternity imagery for health content. The rounded belly and a careful, supported posture show the figure is training with her body in mind.

Every item follows CheckMate specification, so the woman and the equipment share true scale and clean names. Equipment pieces sit as separate objects, which lets a scene move from stretching to sitting on the ball. Soft, natural lighting suits the subject best and keeps the skin tones warm and accurate.

Available since 2025, the collection suits prenatal fitness apps, clinic brochures and wellness blog visuals. Gyms and maternity programs use figures like this to show classes that welcome expecting mothers. Representation matters in health communication, and the set helps African women see themselves in that material.
