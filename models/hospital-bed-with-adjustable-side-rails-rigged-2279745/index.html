About This Model

Priced at $139, the Hospital Bed with Adjustable Side Rails Rigged is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags medical education, training simulation and VR experiences as the intended applications. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualisation, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

At 197,125 polygons and 203,616 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
