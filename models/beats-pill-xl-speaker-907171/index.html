About This Model

A portable wireless speaker shaped like a long rounded capsule, the oversized member of a popular pill-shaped speaker family. Its smooth pill silhouette with a perforated front grille makes it easy to recognize on a shelf or at a beach party. Soft rubber end caps and a single carrying strap complete the familiar form.

The model holds CheckMate Lite certification, so the essential checks on geometry and file structure have been made. A matte body with a fine metal mesh calls for careful lighting, and a gentle rim light separates the grille from the shell. Placed on a picnic blanket or a desk, the speaker fills the frame with a casual lifestyle mood.

Since 2015 the Beats Pill XL Speaker has sold 15 times for tech blogs, electronics catalogs and lifestyle scenes. It suits gadget roundups where several devices appear side by side under studio light. Because it reproduces a real product with its logo, the speaker is limited to editorial projects such as reviews.
