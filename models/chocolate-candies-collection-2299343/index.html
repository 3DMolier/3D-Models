About This Model

This collection groups several chocolate candies in different shapes, from round truffles to filled bonbons, for sweet close-ups and dessert tables. Together they use 1,468 polygons and 1,472 vertices, so a whole box of them still renders quickly on a laptop. Smooth, slightly glossy surfaces suit the tempered look of good chocolate under soft studio lighting or candlelight.

No bitmaps ship with the candies, and milk, dark or white chocolate tones are a quick material change. Each piece keeps its own UV space without overlap, ready for drizzled stripes, cocoa dust or gold leaf. The set was made under TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, which asks for clean geometry, true size and clear names.

Out since 2024, the candies serve chocolatier packaging, dessert menus and festive food scenes in animation and games. Arrange them in paper cups inside a gift box, or scatter a few beside a coffee cup on a saucer. Food brands use such sets to preview new assortments before a single real praline is produced in the kitchen.
