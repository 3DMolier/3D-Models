About This Model

A digital clapboard, this film set essential marks scenes and syncs sound in movie, studio and behind-the-scenes shots. The slate has a hinged striped clapper on top and a bright timecode display in the middle of the board. Writable panels below the display hold scene and take numbers, just like on a professional film set.

The clapboard is a CheckMate Pro model at TurboSquid, the tier for assets ready for production renders. A black acrylic board with white printed labels and glowing red digits reads clearly on camera. Striped black and white bars on the clapper are the instantly recognizable signal of filmmaking.

Available since 2015, the clapboard suits film studio scenes, production company branding and cinema education videos. Held in front of the camera before a take, it opens a scene with authentic cinema energy. Posters for film festivals place it beside cameras, film reels and director chairs for a classic cinema look.
