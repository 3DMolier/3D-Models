About This Model

This Vehicles model - Expedition Mobile Home - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $89 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 436,339 polygons and 486,072 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
