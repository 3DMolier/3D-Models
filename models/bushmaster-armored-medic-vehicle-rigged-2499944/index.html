Priced at $179, the Bushmaster Armored Medic Vehicle Rigged is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 249,411 polygons and 266,089 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
