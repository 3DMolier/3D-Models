About This Model

A collection of transport trolleys and hand carts for moving goods through warehouses, stations and service corridors. Platform carts, wheeled frames and push handles cover the main ways staff move heavy boxes around. Sturdy casters and steel frames make each trolley read as working equipment at a glance.

Each trolley holds CheckMate Pro certification for clean topology and an orderly scene structure. Frames take paint or wear easily, so the set fits both new facilities and worn loading bays. Wheels sit firmly on the floor plane, so the carts can be animated rolling along a corridor without any adjustment.

Around since 2022, the trolleys suit logistics visualization, hospital corridors, airport back areas and warehouse games. Park a loaded cart beside an elevator door and the building feels busy behind the scenes. Empty trolleys lined up against a wall add quiet realism to a delivery area.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How clean is the geometry on the Transport Trolleys Collection model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.

How heavy is the Transport Trolleys Collection mesh?

59,172 polygons and 60,188 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Our Model Bundles & Sets listings sit at a median of about 28,000 polygons, so 59,172 is heavier than the typical one.

What is the Transport Trolleys Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.
