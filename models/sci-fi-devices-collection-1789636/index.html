About This Model

The Sci Fi Devices Collection brings together futuristic gadgets, consoles and handheld tools meant for laboratories, control rooms and spaceship interiors. Glowing panels, sleek housings and exposed cabling give the devices an advanced but believable engineering look. Scattered across a starship bridge or a research lab, they fill empty tables and shelves with small story details.

This collection holds CheckMate Pro approval, ensuring clean geometry and matching scale across the whole set. Emissive materials on the screens and indicator lights do much of the visual work in dark scenes. Cool blue ambient light with bright accents from the devices creates a classic science fiction mood in any interior.

Three versions belong to this family, each a different group of devices at its own price. This set costs $179, while Sci Fi Devices Collection 2 is $219 and Collection 3 is $239. Available since 2021, the props suit space games, science fiction films, escape rooms and futuristic concepts.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How clean is the geometry on the Sci Fi Devices Collection model?

The mesh uses organized, quad-dominant topology at real-world scale, with objects named and grouped rather than merged into one block.

Can the Sci Fi Devices Collection model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
