About This Model

A tuna cut open as an anatomy display, showing heart, gills, stomach, liver and the dark red muscle that defines the fish. Organs sit in their natural positions inside the body cavity, so students can trace how each system connects.

Prepared as a Blender scene, it opens with materials and structure ready for labeling and close inspection. The CheckMate-led build gives organs separate named objects, making it easy to highlight one at a time. Glossy wet surfaces contrast with the silver skin, which reads clearly in educational renders.

Royalty free in 2026, the display suits marine biology courses, fisheries training and seafood industry education. Explode the organs outward in an animation and the internal layout becomes easy to explain on screen. Chefs and fishmongers can also use it to show where prized cuts like otoro come from. Museum displays and science apps benefit from a clean, labeled view of a commercially important fish.
