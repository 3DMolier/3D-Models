About This Model

Mummy with Ankh is a detailed asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $139. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 18,311 polygons and 19,629 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
