About This Model

The Half Open Empty Rectangular Can is a production-ready Containers & Storage 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 11,942 polygons over 11,854 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.
