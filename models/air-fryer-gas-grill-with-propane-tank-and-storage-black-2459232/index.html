About This Model

A backyard gas griddle with a built-in air fryer drawer, this cooking station includes the propane tank, side shelves and a storage cabinet below. It measures 158.99 x 91.21 x H175.12 cm and uses 89,801 polygons with 94,958 vertices for knobs, burner covers and the hood. The lid stands raised, so the cooking surface is visible in a patio shot.

StemCell certification delivers the same PBR materials across formats, keeping the black powder coat and steel accents consistent. The UV layout is mixed, so check the parts you plan to repaint before adding grease stains or heat discoloration.

Two versions appear on the page, the Air Fryer Gas Grill with Propane Tank Black and the Blackstone Air Fryer Gas Grill, both $49. The branded option names the maker for buyers who need that exact appliance. In the catalog since 2025, the grill fits patio design, outdoor kitchen renders, home improvement visualization and summer scenes in games.
