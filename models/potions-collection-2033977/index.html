About This Model

This potions collection gathers small glass flasks and bottles with corks, the kind of magic brews that fill an alchemist's shelf or a game inventory. The whole set uses only 7,684 polygons, so dozens of bottles can sit on shelves across a whole scene.

The 3,853 vertices describe simple closed glass shapes with no hidden geometry inside. Round bellies, square bottles and tall vials give the shelf variety without any extra modeling. Overlap-free UVs make it easy to tint each liquid, add paper labels and age the corks. The CheckMate Pro tier at TurboSquid covers this set, so scale and naming were verified.

Introduced in 2023, the collection suits fantasy games, wizard shops, apothecary counters and Halloween scenes in film and animation. Give each flask a different glowing liquid to mark healing, mana or poison at a glance. Tabletop creators and illustrators also use the set to stage spell components on a cluttered wooden desk.
