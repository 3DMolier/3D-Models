About This Model

Samsung White Front Load Clothes Dryer is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. Geometry weighs in at 92,444 polygons over 96,568 vertices. That is a mid-weight build: detailed enough for a foreground shot, light enough to duplicate freely. 4 texture maps come with it, at 1024 x 1024 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags visualization, advertising and game assets as the intended applications. It is built for interior visualisation, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
