About This Model

Looking for a Sports & Recreation asset? The Dome Camping Tent is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Geometry weighs in at 15,667 polygons over 15,807 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
