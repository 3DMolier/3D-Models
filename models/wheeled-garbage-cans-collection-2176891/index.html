About This Model

This collection of wheeled garbage cans covers the plastic bins found at curbs, alleys and building yards in every city. Hinged lids, molded handles and rear wheels let each bin tilt and roll just like the real thing. Different colors usually mean general waste, recycling and garden waste, so a row instantly reads as municipal service.

CheckMate Lite status confirms clean files and believable sizes across all the bins in the set. Each can is separate, so lids can be left open, closed or knocked over in a messy alley. Scuffed plastic, stickers and a little dirt near the wheels add realism without extra modeling.

Offered since 2024, the bins suit street scenes, waste management explainers and urban game levels. Lined up on a sidewalk on collection day, they bring everyday life into any residential render. Royalty Free terms keep them free to use in commercial games, architectural renders and personal work.
