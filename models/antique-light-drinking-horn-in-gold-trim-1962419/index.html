About This Model

A curved animal horn cup with a metal rim and tip, this drinking horn suits a Viking feast hall or a medieval tavern table. Its 5,568 polygons follow the tapering spiral of the horn and the decorative bands that cap both ends. With 2,788 vertices the shape stays light, so a whole row of horns can line a banquet table.

Non-overlapping UVs let the pale horn grain shift smoothly from base to tip while the gold trim keeps its own space. CheckMate Pro certification confirms the build passed TurboSquid's strictest check for topology and scale. A warm, slightly translucent material helps the light horn glow by candlelight.

The page holds two versions at $12 each, this light horn with gold trim and an Old Dark Drinking Horn with Silver Trim. Available since 2022, they work for historical films, fantasy games and museum reconstructions of Norse life. Mixing both finishes on one table makes a feast look less like a set dressing kit.
