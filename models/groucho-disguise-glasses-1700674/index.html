About This Model

Groucho Disguise Glasses is one of the Clothing & Accessories models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. The mesh carries 6,221 polygons and 6,280 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
