About This Model

A full-detail offshore supply vessel built for close inspection, from bow thrusters to the stern cargo deck. The Offshore Supply model carries 1,407,132 polygons and 1,492,124 vertices, so hatches, ladders and lifeboats hold up in near shots. Anchor handling gear on the aft deck shows the heavy work these vessels do in rough water.

The ship has the proportions of a large platform supply vessel serving remote offshore oil and gas fields. Under CheckMate rules the many deck parts stay neatly grouped and true to scale. A tall superstructure at the bow shields the crew from heavy seas, the trademark shape of these ships.

You can pick between two versions, this vessel and the Offshore Tug Supply Ship. Both reached buyers in 2026, and the main version costs $199 for maritime projects. Offshore training simulators, energy company films, harbor animations and shipping documentaries use it for hero shots alongside drilling platforms.
