About This Model

ISS Leonardo Permanent Multipurpose Module is one of the Space & Sci-Fi models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $79. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

At 57,482 polygons and 60,944 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Maps: 10 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
