About This Model

A man in a thoughtful pose, hand at his chin, standing beside the donkey icon of the Democratic Party for political content. The 86,105 polygons go into the suit, the expressive hand and face, and the solid blue party symbol beside him. The thinking pose is deliberately neutral, so the figure can illustrate a voter weighing options or a strategist planning ahead. Keeping the icon separate means it can be removed or swapped when the shot needs a figure without party symbols.

StemCell certification keeps skin, cloth and the icon consistent between real-time and offline output. An overlap-free unwrap covers 87,664 vertices, so tie color, lapel pins or a party badge can be changed easily.

Created in 2024, it serves election coverage, political explainers, civics lessons and opinion pieces in news media. With a royalty-free license, news outlets and educators can use it without a separate agreement. Set against a dark blue gradient with soft top light, the figure reads as calm and considered rather than partisan.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2024 · Updated 17 November 2026
