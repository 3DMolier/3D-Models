About This Model

A set of Tostitos tortilla chip bags with crinkled foil edges, ready for party tables, pantry shelves and store aisles. The Tostitos Tortilla Chips Bag Set uses 16,929 polygons, shaping puffed bags with sealed crimps at top and bottom.

Its 16,896 vertices give the foil wrinkles enough definition to catch highlights from overhead lighting. The set conforms to CheckMate specification, arriving with correct snack-bag scale and tidy geometry. The bags stand slightly bulged, as if freshly packed with air to protect the chips inside.

From 2026 the set has been priced at $39 and suits supermarket interiors, game night scenes and kitchen renders. Prop one open beside a salsa bowl on a coffee table, or line several on a convenience store shelf. Sports bar and party scenes feel more real with a familiar snack within reach of the guests. Light geometry lets you stock a whole aisle without slowing the scene.
