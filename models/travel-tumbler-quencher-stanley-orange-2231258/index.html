About This Model

Priced at $29, the Travel Tumbler Quencher Stanley is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 7,336 polygons and 7,570 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
