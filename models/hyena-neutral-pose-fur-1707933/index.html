About This Model

Priced at $149, the Hyena Neutral Pose Fur is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 11,344 polygons and 11,890 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
