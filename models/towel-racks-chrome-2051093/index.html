About This Model

This Furniture & Interior model - Towel Racks Chrome - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $9 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 13,832 polygons and 7,041 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
