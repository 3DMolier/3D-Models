About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Electric Car Charging Connector Generic is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 28,558 polygons and 29,595 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

11 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for driving simulation and automotive advertising. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
