About This Model

Teenage Boy Fashionable Style Rigged is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $179. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 8,763 polygons and 9,383 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
