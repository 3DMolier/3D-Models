About This Model

A folded paper fortune teller decorated for Valentine's Day, the playground game that hides sweet messages under each flap. The Valentine Paper Fortune Teller is tiny at 2,004 polygons, keeping the sharp folds and pointed corners crisp.

With 2,053 vertices, the four pockets open just enough to fit a pair of fingers inside. StemCell rules give this paper toy a range of native formats from a single checked master. Its Valentine decoration on the outer panels makes it read as a love note rather than a schoolyard game.

At $9 since 2026, it suits romantic scenes, school settings and craft tutorial illustrations. Leave it on a desk beside a heart-shaped card, or hold it between a child's fingers mid-game. Craft bloggers explaining how to fold one can use it to show the finished shape in three dimensions. The low polygon count makes it suitable even for mobile games with dozens of small collectibles.
