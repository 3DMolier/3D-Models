About This Model

Priced at $49, the Lettuce Leaves with Brazil Nuts is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 30,124 polygons and 29,783 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
