About This Model

This set of tailor measuring tapes brings rolled, coiled and loose tapes to sewing rooms and fashion studio scenes. Each tape shows printed scale markings along a soft band with small metal tips fixed at both ends. Draped around the neck of a dress form or curled on a cutting table, the tapes signal careful handmade work.

Every tape in the bundle passed TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro check for clean shapes, true scale and tidy file structure. Having coiled and draped shapes together lets an artist dress a workspace without modeling any bends by hand. The flexible band reads well in close shots, where the printed numbers become a strong graphic detail in the frame.

Offered since 2015, the set suits tailoring tutorials, fashion school renders and atelier interiors in films and series. Lying beside fabric scissors and pins, a tape completes a believable sewing kit on any workbench or desk. Diet and fitness illustrations sometimes wrap one around a waist to suggest measuring progress over several weeks.
