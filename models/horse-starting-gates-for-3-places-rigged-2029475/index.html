About This Model

The Horse Starting Gates for 3 Places Rigged is a production-ready Sports & Recreation 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 338,500 polygons and 177,705 vertices.
