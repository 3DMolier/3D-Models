About This Model

Stadium Light Tower belongs to our Lighting range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 168,086 polygons over 172,902 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
