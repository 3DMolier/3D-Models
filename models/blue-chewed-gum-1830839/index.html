About This Model

Blue Chewed Gum belongs to our Food & Beverages range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 3,924 polygons and 3,925 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
