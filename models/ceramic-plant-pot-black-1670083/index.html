About This Model

A black ceramic plant pot, this simple container holds succulents, herbs and houseplants in interior and garden scenes. Only 576 polygons and 561 vertices are used for the smooth tapered walls and the slightly rolled rim. The plain cylindrical shape suits minimal interiors, where the plant and not the pot should catch the eye.

The pot reached the CheckMate Pro tier at TurboSquid, which marks models meant for professional work. A clean unwrap without overlaps makes it easy to add matte glaze, speckles or soil stains near the rim. A satin black shader with gentle reflections helps the pot look elegant against light walls.

Created in 2020, the pot suits Scandinavian living rooms, office desks and plant shop visualizations. Planted with a snake plant or a small cactus, it adds a touch of green to any shelf. Low polygon counts make it practical for filling many shelves in a garden center or greenhouse scene.
