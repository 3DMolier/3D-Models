About This Model

An expectant mother surrounded by baby essentials, including a stroller, crib, car seat, bottles and toys, ready for a nursery or shopping scene. The figure stands in a gentle pose with one hand on her belly, which makes the theme clear immediately. Soft shapes and calm colors give the set a warm, reassuring feeling.

Every product is a separate object, so a nursery can be arranged or a store shelf filled without unwanted items. The set conforms to the CheckMate specification from TurboSquid, keeping the mother and each baby item at realistic sizes with clear names. Changing fabric colors on the stroller or crib helps match different brands or nursery themes.

Since its 2025 launch at $149, the collection has suited baby stores, parenting magazines, prenatal education and family lifestyle animation. Clinics and hospitals can illustrate maternity guides with a friendly figure instead of stock photographs. Furniture brands also use such scenes to show how a nursery layout works around a growing family.
