About This Model

The self service kiosk moved work from staff to customers and made it feel like a convenience. The screen angle, the height and the placement of the card reader are all calculated around somebody standing with luggage in one hand.

The model is very light at 6,828 polygons and 7,252 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, so several fit in one scene. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $59 it suits airport, retail and hospitality visualization, equipment marketing, interface mockups, architectural interiors, film sets and game environments. On sale since 2022.
