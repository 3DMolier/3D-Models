About This Model

A dental teaching piece that shows how a titanium screw, abutment and crown replace a lost tooth inside the jawbone. The Education Tooth Implant Model is built from 11,695 polygons and 11,701 vertices, enough to keep the threads and gum line crisp. Neighboring natural teeth flank the implant, so the restoration reads in context rather than floating alone.

Texturing comes from 12 maps at 4096 x 4096, covering enamel, bone, soft gum tissue and the metal post. The unwrap has no overlapping islands, so each surface can be retouched without bleeding into another. It carries CheckMate Pro status, meaning the topology and scale passed the marketplace review.

In the collection since 2019, the model serves dentists explaining treatment, clinic websites and dental school lectures. A cutaway view lets patients see why the post must fuse with bone before the crown goes on. Animators also use it in short procedure videos where the screw slides down into a drilled socket.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2019 · Updated 18 November 2026
