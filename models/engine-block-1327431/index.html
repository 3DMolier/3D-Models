About This Model

A bare cast engine block with open cylinder bores, bolt bosses and a flat deck where the head would sit. It uses 77,870 polygons and 78,116 vertices for the cooling passages, mounting ears and crankcase walls. Seen from above, the row of round bores reads instantly as the core of a car engine.

Five maps at 4096 x 4096 bring rough cast iron, bright machined faces and oil stains in the recesses. Because the UVs never overlap, the casting can be cleaned up as a new part or aged for a junkyard. CheckMate Pro certification lets the block hold up in cutaway animations and tight close renders.

Bolted to an engine stand in a garage, it becomes the centerpiece of a rebuild or tuning scene. It also helps technical animations explain pistons and combustion before the remaining parts are assembled. Available since 2018, the block suits mechanics' training, automotive explainers and garage games.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2018 · Updated 19 November 2026
