About This Model

This bundle gathers several cartoon Santa Claus characters with fur-groomed beards, giving holiday projects a festive cast in one purchase. Round bellies, red suits and fluffy white trim make each Santa instantly readable, even as a small figure in a snowy village. Different builds and styles in the set let one scene show a Santa workshop with variety.

Every character in the collection passed CheckMate checks, arriving with verified scale and clear naming. Fur grooming on the beard and suit trim catches soft warm light from a fireplace or a lit tree. Cartoon proportions suit animated greeting cards, toy store windows and family-friendly games during the season.

Two versions are bundled, the Cartoon Character Santa Clauses Fur Collection at $169 and a second collection at $219. Online since 2024, the set returns every winter for Christmas animations, seasonal e-cards and festive storefront renders. Mixing both sets gives a crowd of Santas for a parade or a busy holiday market.
