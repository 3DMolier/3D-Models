About This Model

A sleek black performance motor yacht with a low wedge profile, a broad deck and a dark tinted cabin. This Motor Yacht uses 78,743 polygons and 82,951 vertices, a lean build for a boat of this length. Its light mesh makes a whole marina of copies affordable, even in real-time water scenes.

Its hull length suits a luxury day cruiser that docks in exclusive marinas along the French Riviera. CheckMate certification keeps the hull, deck and fittings at proper scale with logical grouping. The flat aggressive bow and wraparound glass give it a modern superyacht look in any harbor shot.

Four versions are offered: this black boat, two Wallypower editions in blue, one of them rigged, and a rigged white performance yacht. Every one of them is a 2026 release, and the black edition costs $79 for marine scenes. Marina renders, luxury lifestyle films, racing games and coastal real estate visualizations use it as a premium boat.
