About This Model

This Animals & Creatures model - Firefly Fur Rigged - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
