About This Model

Looking for a Model Bundles & Sets asset? The Fire Trucks Collection is a finished, render-ready model at $199. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. The set covers several related objects at once, which saves assembling a scene from separate purchases.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2015 · Updated 4 September 2026
