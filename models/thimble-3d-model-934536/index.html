About This Model

Thimble is a detailed Signage & Decor asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $14. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2015 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 17,504 polygons and 17,466 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 2 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
