About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Thimble is a finished, render-ready model at $14. The mesh carries 17,504 polygons and 17,466 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 2 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2015 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

Questions About This Model

Can the Thimble model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

Where can I buy the Thimble 3D model?

It is sold through TurboSquid, where this listing has been available since 2015. Purchase, download and licensing are handled by TurboSquid; delivery is immediate after checkout.

What texture resolution ships with the Thimble?

2 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

What is the Thimble model typically used for?

The listing names event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the main applications. In practice it also works anywhere a finished signage & decor object is needed without modeling it from scratch.
