About This Model

A simple wooden dining chair with a slatted back, straight legs and a flat seat, ready to pull up to any kitchen table. Its 2,322 polygons and 2,455 vertices keep the build light, so a full dining set costs almost nothing to render. Clean, timeless lines let the chair fit farmhouse kitchens as easily as modern apartments.

StemCell materials with 4 maps at 4096 x 4096 deliver consistent wood grain across engines and offline renderers. UVs without overlaps let you swap oak for walnut or paint the frame white without visible seams. The StemCell standard also means correct scale, centered pivots and naming that stays tidy in large scenes.

Dining Chair entered our catalog in 2019 for interior design, real estate staging and restaurant layouts. Push several around a round table with plates and a vase to finish a family dining room. Cafe and bistro scenes repeat the chair dozens of times thanks to its modest geometry.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2019 · Updated 18 November 2026
