About This Model

This video intercom camera is the outdoor door station of a home entry system, with a lens, call button and speaker grille. The model spends 18,056 polygons on 18,634 vertices to keep the rounded housing and recessed camera sharp. A slim rain hood over the lens and the infrared ring around it are the details that make it read as a doorbell camera.

TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro grade applies to this unit, and the non-overlapping UVs keep the button, grille and face plate separate. Brushed aluminum or silver plastic suits the plate, with a softly glowing button for night scenes.

First listed in 2021, the camera fits house entrances, apartment lobbies and smart home explainers. Mounted beside a front door in an architectural render, it adds the small security detail that real homes show. A thriller scene can cut to its viewpoint as a stranger presses the call button late at night. Security installers and smart home stores can also show it next to the matching indoor monitor in their guides.
