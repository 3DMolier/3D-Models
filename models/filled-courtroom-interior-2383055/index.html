About This Model

Priced at $249, the Filled Courtroom Interior is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 903,387 polygons over 838,808 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
