About This Model

A locked crate is a narrative device. The lock says somebody decided this needed protecting, which raises a question the scene does not have to answer, and it is the cheapest way to make a prop interesting.

The model is light at 22,438 polygons and 22,324 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in the locked configuration. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits science fiction film and animation, game environments and props, concept art, exhibition work and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
