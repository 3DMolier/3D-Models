About This Model

Priced at $69, the Unmanned Aerial Drone is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 233,550 polygons and 122,551 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
