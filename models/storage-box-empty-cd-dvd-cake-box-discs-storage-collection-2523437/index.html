About This Model

Empty disc storage tubs of the cake box type, round clear containers with a central spindle for stacks of CDs and DVDs. Together they use 9,355 polygons and 9,235 vertices, with the domed lids, spindles and ribbed bases all modeled. Being empty, the tubs can be filled with your own discs or left bare to suggest a finished backup job.

The set was prepared under CheckMate rules on proportion and naming, but it ships without any formal TurboSquid certification. Non-overlapping UVs keep each lid and base apart, so frosted plastic, labels or a brand-free print can be added. Clear plastic shading shows the spindle inside, which is what makes these containers instantly recognizable on a crowded shelf.

Added in 2026, the tubs suit home office scenes, nostalgic computer rooms, data archive visuals and retro studios. Stack them on a desk beside a tower PC, and an older era of burning discs returns in a single shot. For $19 the set fills a gap in technology props that newer gadget packs rarely bother to cover anymore.
