About This Model

An elongated pale mango with a smooth, slightly curved body, this model works for fruit displays and printable food replicas. The Pale Long Mango Fruit for 3D Print uses 2,334 polygons and 2,336 vertices, a closed shape that keeps the beak at the tip. The stem end is slightly flattened, so the fruit rests on a plate without rolling away.

Its unwrap leaves every island separate, so blush tones and tiny skin speckles can be painted across the curve. Prepared under CheckMate guidelines, the mango keeps realistic size relative to knives, bowls and cutting boards. A pale yellow-green base with a faint orange blush suits varieties picked early in the season.

Released in 2024, the mango serves grocery scenes, tropical kitchen renders and printed play food sets. Slice a copy in half for a cutting board shot, or pile several in a woven market basket. Printed and painted versions also make durable classroom props for lessons about tropical fruit.
