About This Model

Autonomous and Rocket pairs a flat drone ship used for rocket landings with a reusable booster standing upright on its deck. Its 195,390 polygons cover the wide landing platform, the thrusters along its sides, and the rocket's legs and grid fins.

The 215,891 vertices share UV space in places, a practical economy for the repeated deck panels. A StemCell asset, the scene arrives with matching materials for each supported format, keeping the scorched deck consistent. Black scorch rings around the landing target show where engines fired during touchdown. Seen from above, the large circle on the deck gives the camera a perfect target for a landing shot.

Two versions are paired here: this one and Autonomous Vessel for Rocket Landings and Nebula 1 Rocket, featuring a named booster. Since its release in 2024, the pair has supported spaceflight news, aerospace explainers and launch sequences in games. Rocking on ocean swells with a booster on deck, it captures the drama of reusable spaceflight.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2024 · Updated 14 November 2026
