About This Model

This Industrial Equipment model - Autonomous and Rocket - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $119 on TurboSquid. You are getting 195,390 polygons and 215,891 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

Questions About This Model

What does StemCell mean for the Autonomous and Rocket model?

StemCell covers both geometry and shading: the model is delivered across formats with materials that stay equivalent instead of needing per-engine conversion.

Which industries use the Autonomous and Rocket model?

This listing is cataloged for Hardware, Simulation, Film & Video Production, Game Development and Event Management. Those are the sectors the model was tagged for on TurboSquid, based on how comparable assets in the Industrial Equipment range are bought.

Does the Autonomous and Rocket model include materials and textures?

The product page states what ships with each format. StemCell delivery means a PBR material set that reads the same across renderers. See the listing for the exact contents.

Can the Autonomous and Rocket model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.
