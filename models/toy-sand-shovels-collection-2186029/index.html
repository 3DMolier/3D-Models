About This Model

The Toy Sand Shovels Collection gathers bright plastic spades and scoops of the kind children bring to the beach or sandbox. Each shovel is a separate object, so they can lie scattered on sand or stand upright in a bucket. Rounded edges and thick handles give the toys their friendly, safe look even from a distance.

Every piece in the set follows CheckMate rules, with clean naming and consistent scale throughout. Glossy primary colors with a little sand dust on the blades make the plastic believable. Half buried beside a sandcastle, a shovel says summer holiday without any other prop in view.

The family has two versions, this set at $21 and Toy Sand Shovels Collection 2 Sand at $22. Since 2024 the toys have suited beach scenes, playground visuals and children's product catalogs for retailers. Using both sets together easily fills a toy shop shelf or a busy family beach day.
