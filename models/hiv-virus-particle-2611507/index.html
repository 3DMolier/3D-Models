About This Model

A spherical HIV virus particle with surface spikes, made for medical animation, biology lessons and public health explainers for wide audiences. The 41,138 polygons describe the round viral envelope and the knobbed glycoprotein spikes spread across its surface. Spikes stand out clearly against the smooth membrane, which helps viewers grasp how the virus attaches to cells.

Its CheckMate review found clean geometry, and the 40,938 vertices keep spikes and envelope as tidy, closed parts. Partial UVs suit translucent membrane shaders and colored spike materials driven by procedural noise and soft lighting. Scale the particle up dramatically for microscopic flythroughs where the camera glides between the spikes like a tiny spacecraft.

Two versions are listed together here at $29 each: the HIV Virus Particle and a Human Immunodeficiency Virus variant. Since 2026 both have served pharmaceutical presentations, immunology courses and science documentaries on television. The model measures 20.71 x 20.65 x H20.43 cm, a convenient size to rescale for any microscopic scene.
