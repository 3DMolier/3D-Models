About This Model

This crushed empty plastic bottle is a white container squashed and dented, with a twisted body and a cap still screwed on. The 6,419 polygons follow every crumple and fold, so it looks genuinely discarded rather than neatly modeled. There are 6,247 vertices, slightly under the faces, which keeps the crushed shell closed and light.

No maps come with it, so the milky white plastic and any printed label are created with your own materials. Clean UV islands without overlap let you bake dirt or grime later, and the model meets CheckMate Pro standards. Sharp creases catch highlights along the folds, which helps it read as trash even in a small corner.

Released in 2021, the Crushed Empty Plastic Bottle White serves recycling campaigns, litter scenes and environmental education. Drop it on a beach, a park path, or into an overflowing recycling bin together with cans and paper. Environmental awareness videos rely on it to show plastic waste without needing a real trash shoot.
