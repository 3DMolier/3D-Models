About This Model

Trade Show Exhibition Booth is one of the Furniture & Interior models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $89. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

At 1,424,176 polygons and 1,479,128 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
