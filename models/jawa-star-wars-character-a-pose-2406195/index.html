About This Model

A hooded desert scavenger is one of the most economical character designs there is: a robe, glowing points where the eyes should be, and nothing else visible. The viewer fills in everything under the cloth, which is why the silhouette works at any size.

The figure is light at 21,578 polygons and 22,158 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplied in an A pose ready for your own rig. It is built to CheckMate specification, and rigged builds are offered separately, including native rigs for Cinema 4D and Maya.

At $89 it suits game characters, science fiction film and animation, concept art, fan illustration and previsualization. On sale since 2025.
