About This Model

A heavy construction dump truck with its bed full of soil, ready for quarry, roadwork and building site scenes. The Construction Dump Truck with Soil carries 746,022 polygons and 765,083 vertices, spread over the cab, chassis, tipper body, wheels and the earth load. The heaped soil makes the truck look mid-job rather than freshly parked at a dealer.

CheckMate certification keeps the cab, chassis and tipper body neatly organized and clearly named. Massive multi-axle wheels and a reinforced tipper wall give the truck its rugged, working silhouette. Removing the soil mesh leaves an empty bed for shots of the truck returning from a dump site.

It has sold for $139 since 2026 under a Royalty Free license. Construction site visualization, mining documentaries, infrastructure animations and city building games suit it well. Paired with an excavator loading the bed, it creates a believable earthworks scene in minutes.
