About This Model

Car Headlight belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $20 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game environments. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

The mesh carries 7,040 polygons and 7,175 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
