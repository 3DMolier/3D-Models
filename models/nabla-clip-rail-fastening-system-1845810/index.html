About This Model

The clip that holds a rail to its sleeper does more than it looks like. It has to grip hard enough to stop the rail creeping under a train, flex enough to absorb the passing load, and be fitted by somebody with a hammer in the dark.

The model is very light at 8,064 polygons and 8,348 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $39 it suits railway engineering visualization, technical documentation, equipment marketing, trackwork scenes, training material and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.
