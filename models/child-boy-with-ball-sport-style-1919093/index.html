About This Model

A young boy in sporty clothes holding a ball, ready for playground, school yard and family park scenes. The ball gives the pose purpose, so the child reads as active and playful rather than simply standing. Child proportions, with a larger head and shorter limbs, keep the figure believable when he stands next to adults.

CheckMate Pro certification ensures true child scale, so he sits naturally beside benches, swings and parents. Bright sportswear colors pop against green grass and pale playground surfaces under clear daylight. A soft afternoon sun from behind adds rim light that suits cheerful lifestyle and family frames.

Listed in 2022, the boy suits school brochures, sports club visuals, residential renders and children's games. Architects add children like him to park plans because a playing kid instantly signals a safe, friendly neighborhood. Swap the ball color to match a team, and the same figure promotes a local football academy.
