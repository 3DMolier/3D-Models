About This Model

A collection of futuristic passenger trains with sleek aerodynamic noses, built for high-speed rail and future city scenes. Long smooth bodies and wraparound windows define the look, and they read well both on a viaduct and in a station. Having several trains in one set lets a transit concept show different lines without mixing design languages.

Each train is a separate object, so cars can be lined up into longer consists or shown as single units. Real-world size and logical naming across all of the trains follow CheckMate specification. Glossy white or metallic finishes respond well to city reflections and long streaks of motion blur.

Released in 2025, it suits urban planning presentations, sci-fi films, transport concept art and city builder games. Elevated tracks between towers at dusk create the classic image of a future transit network. Rail engineers pitching new lines use concepts like this to show passengers what the future service could look like.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2025 · Updated 13 November 2026
