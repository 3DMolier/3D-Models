About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Small Medicine Bottle is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 25,050 polygons and 25,154 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 2 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
