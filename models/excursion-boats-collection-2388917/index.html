About This Model

This set brings together excursion boats of the kind that carry tourists along rivers, harbors and coastal sightseeing routes. Typical features include open upper decks with rows of seats, glazed lower cabins and wide boarding areas near the stern. Those shapes let a single boat sell the idea of a city cruise even at a small size in the frame.

Each hull was checked against CheckMate specification for clean geometry, naming and sensible pivots before the bundle went live. Because the boats come from one author, their materials and scale match, which spares you the usual cleanup between sources.

First released in 2025, the bundle suits waterfront architecture renders, tourism videos, river city animations and marine game levels. Place a couple of boats at a pier and a quay suddenly looks busy, without modeling any vessel from scratch. Wakes, reflections and a low sun across the deck railings give the most convincing result in an evening cityscape. Simulation teams also use passenger craft like these to populate harbor traffic around ferries and larger ships.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
