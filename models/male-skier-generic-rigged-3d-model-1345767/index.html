About This Model

Male Skier Generic Rigged belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $199 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 146,668 polygons and 151,419 vertices, a light load for a scene. Texture work runs to 17 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
