About This Model

Male Skier is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $199. You are getting 146,668 polygons and 151,419 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 17 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 512 x 512.
