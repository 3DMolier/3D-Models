About This Model

A bedside alarm clock radio with a glowing digital display, tuning buttons on top and a speaker grille along the side. Its 35,150 polygons over 36,643 vertices shape the rounded case, every button and the rows of grille holes. A slight backward tilt of the front face makes the numbers easy to read from a pillow at night.

Matte plastic, the lit screen and the grille sit on 7 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Nothing overlaps in the unwrap, so the displayed time can be changed by editing a single region of one map. TurboSquid looked over the radio before granting it CheckMate Pro, its stamp for clean and correctly scaled models.

Three versions cost $39 each: this black radio, a white Magnasonic version and an unbranded generic version. The generic version suits any bedroom where a visible brand would distract from the rest of the scene. Hotel rooms and morning routine scenes have used it since 2019, often with the alarm blinking midnight in the dark.
