About This Model

Wire rope is strands laid around a core, and the lay is what has to be right: the visible spiral, its pitch and the direction it twists all identify the construction. A smooth cylinder with a spiral texture never reads as steel cable, because the surface is genuinely faceted by the strands.

The mesh runs 27,280 polygons and 27,496 vertices in quads and tris. The UVs overlap, which is exactly right for a repeating helical structure, and keeps a long run affordable.

At $15 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits marine and offshore visualization, shipbuilding and rigging presentation, crane and lifting equipment scenes, industrial material and games. On sale since 2025.
