About This Model

A balaclava is a tube of knit with an opening, and its whole character comes from how it sits: gathered under the chin, stretched flat across the crown, and folded slightly around the eye opening where the fabric doubles over. Get that fold wrong and it looks like a painted mask rather than something pulled on.

The mesh is remarkably small at 457 polygons and 479 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 3 texture maps at 4096x4096. That split is the correct one for knitwear: the shape is simple, and every bit of the realism comes from the texture carrying the knit structure.

At $29 it suits winter sports advertising, outdoor and tactical clothing visualization, character set dressing for film, television and games, and retail renders. On sale since 2018.
