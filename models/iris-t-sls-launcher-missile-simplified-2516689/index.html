About This Model

The short range member of this air defence family sits on a lighter chassis than its longer ranged sibling, which changes the whole vehicle: fewer canisters, a smaller erecting frame and a truck that can go places a heavy launcher cannot. The trade is reach for mobility, and that is visible at a glance.

The mesh runs 400,334 polygons and 418,628 vertices with mixed UV layout. The build is described as simplified, which refers to the interior of the cab rather than the launcher itself, and that is the right saving on a vehicle almost always shown from outside.

At $139 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits defence and air defence visualisation, news and documentary graphics, procurement and training presentation, and games. On sale since 2026.
