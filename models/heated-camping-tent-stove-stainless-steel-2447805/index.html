About This Model

Looking for a Sports & Recreation asset? The Tent Stove is a finished, render-ready model at $39. You are getting 67,689 polygons and 67,859 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 85.22 x 161.99 x H347.63 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
