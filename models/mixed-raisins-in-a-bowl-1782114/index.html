About This Model

Mixed Raisins in a Bowl is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $34. You are getting 63,384 polygons and 63,533 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
