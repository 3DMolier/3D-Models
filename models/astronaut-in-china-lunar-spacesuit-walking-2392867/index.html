About This Model

Astronaut in China Lunar Spacesuit Walking shows a crew member striding across the surface in a bulky pressurized suit for future Moon missions. Its 41,552 polygons and 44,166 vertices model the helmet visor, chest control unit, backpack and the creased joints of the suit. At 84.93 x 93.93 x H188.52 cm, the figure stands at full human height with the extra bulk a spacesuit adds.

Unwrapped without overlapping islands, the suit accepts mission patches, flags and lunar dust exactly where needed. The model meets the CheckMate specification, keeping scale correct beside landers, rovers and lunar terrain.

Ten versions add crawling and waving poses, an A-pose for rigging and a modern Moon landing suit alongside the Chinese design. Several come with fur strands, giving fabric surfaces a softer, more tactile look in close renders. Paired with a lander, a walking figure explains the scale of a mission better than any diagram. Released during 2025, the astronaut suits space agency outreach, science documentaries and lunar exploration games.
