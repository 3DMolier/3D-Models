About This Model

A retro open-face bike helmet shaped like a baseball cap, finished in black leather for scooter and city riding scenes. The 13,325 polygons define the rounded shell, the short visor peak and the chin strap with its buckle. With 13,364 vertices the stitched leather seams and soft padding at the edges stay visible in a close-up.

Geometry respects the CheckMate specification, and the clean unwrap gives leather grain and stitching room to breathe. It can sit on a rider's head, hang from handlebars or rest on a shop shelf as a standalone prop. The cap shape keeps the helmet casual, which fits commuters more than racers.

All seven versions cost $29, mixing cap-style and moped helmets in leather or plastic. Colors include black, brown, yellow and pink, so one family covers several riders and personalities. Released in 2024, it suits urban mobility visuals, scooter rental scenes, cafe racer art and sports retail renders.
