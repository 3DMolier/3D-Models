About This Model

The Triple E class ships changed the shape of container vessels: the bridge moved forward, the engine room moved aft, and everything between became cargo. Loaded, the stacks rise well above the deckhouse, and the ship becomes a moving wall.

The model is dense at 1,863,210 polygons and 2,066,912 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplied loaded. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and the library also holds the empty version, an ultra large container vessel, a deckhouse, a lifeboat and its davit crane as separate pieces.

At $149 it suits shipping and logistics visualization, port and infrastructure scenes, documentary and news graphics, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2021.
