About This Model

A collection of empty glass test tubes in various sizes, the rounded-bottom vials used in every chemistry and biology lab. The Empty Test Tubes Collection gathers short and long tubes, some with flared lips for easy pouring. Grouped in a rack, they make a lab bench look busy and ready for work.

Thin clear glass with strong refraction suits the tubes, creating bright highlights and bent reflections when lit from the side. Colored liquids can be added later, and graduation marks or labels can be printed along the glass. TurboSquid gave the bundle its CheckMate Pro seal, so the files open neatly without cleanup.

Science classrooms, research labs and medical explainers have featured these tubes since 2020. Arrange them in a wooden rack beside a microscope, or fill a few with vivid liquids for an experiment. Biotech brochures also use glassware like this to suggest careful scientific research.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
