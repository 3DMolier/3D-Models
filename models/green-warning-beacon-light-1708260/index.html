About This Model

Looking for a Lighting asset? The Beacon Light is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Geometry weighs in at 20,586 polygons over 21,316 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags architectural visualization, event visualization and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits interior and architectural visualization, product rendering and game environment dressing.
