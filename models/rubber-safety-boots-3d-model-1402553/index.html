About This Model

This Clothing & Accessories model - Rubber Safety Boots - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 1,696 polygons and 1,700 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 3 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
