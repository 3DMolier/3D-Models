About This Model

A closed pistachio in its shell, this small food asset fills snack bowls, nut mixes and healthy eating scenes. The nut uses 1,146 polygons and 1,148 vertices for the oval shell with its slight seam where it will split. The shell is tightly shut, so it pairs well with opened nuts to show the difference in a bowl.

TurboSquid approved the nut for CheckMate Pro, the grade it reserves for well made commercial assets. Four maps at 2048 x 2048 plus one at 1024 x 1024 cover the beige shell, small dark spots and the faint pink blush. A matte natural shader with subtle surface noise keeps the shell looking dry and roasted.

Released in 2019, the pistachio suits snack food visualizations, nutrition infographics and bar scenes. Scattered by the handful in a ceramic bowl, the nuts make a casual party snack look real. Grocery store product renders also scatter them around open bags and jars.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2019 · Updated 17 November 2026
