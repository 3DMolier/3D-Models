About This Model

The four presidential faces of Mount Rushmore are carved into this granite cliff, ready to print as a desk sculpture. Its 741,696 polygons and 370,826 vertices hold the features of Washington, Jefferson, Roosevelt and Lincoln in fine, readable relief. Scale it from fridge magnet size up to a mantel centerpiece in the slicer settings.

The mesh skips UVs and textures entirely because it exists to be printed rather than rendered. CheckMate certification keeps the surface clean, so slicing software reads the geometry without errors. Rough rock around the heads gives prints a natural base and hides layer lines well.

The Mount Rushmore National Memorial for 3D Print joined the catalog in 2026 at $24. History teachers, national park gift makers and American road trip fans use it for souvenirs, dioramas and classroom models. Print it in gray resin and a light dark wash brings out every chiseled detail of the faces.
