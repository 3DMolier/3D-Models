About This Model

A complete automatic gearbox with its bell housing, ribbed aluminum case, oil pan and output shaft. It shows the unit as a mechanic would see it on the bench, removed from the car and ready for service. Bolt bosses, cooler line fittings and the shift linkage give the casing the busy surface that real transmissions have.

The gearbox holds CheckMate Pro certification, which confirms clean geometry and a well structured scene. Cast aluminum responds well to a rough metallic shader, and a darker tone for the steel parts adds contrast.

Since 2016 the transmission has been bought 25 times at $79 for technical and educational work. Automotive training courses, repair shop scenes and engineering animations use it to show where power travels after the engine. Placed on a workbench beside scattered tools, it turns a plain garage into a working service bay. Parts catalogs and explainers about vehicle drivetrains also benefit from such a recognizable mechanical shape.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
