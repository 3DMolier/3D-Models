About This Model

This Signage & Decor model - Souvenir Pyramid of Kukulkan Glass Globe - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $39 on TurboSquid. You are getting 5,430 polygons and 5,684 vertices, measured rather than estimated. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and architectural visualization.
