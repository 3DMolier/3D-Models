About This Model

Priced at $169, the Male Centaur Fur is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 22,805 polygons and 23,152 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 58.18 x 157.11 x H214.98 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
