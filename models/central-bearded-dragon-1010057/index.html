About This Model

This Central Bearded Dragon captures the spiny throat pouch, flattened body and banded tail that make the lizard instantly recognizable. Rows of pointed scales run along the flanks, giving close shots the rough texture keepers know from real terrarium animals.

The model holds CheckMate Pro certification, so its geometry, scale and file structure were reviewed by TurboSquid inspectors. Its sandy tan coloring sits well against desert rocks, heat lamps and the dry substrate of a reptile enclosure. The pose keeps the head raised and the forelimbs planted, a stance typical of a basking lizard soaking up warmth.

On the catalog since 2016, it serves pet store renders, zoology lessons and nature documentaries that need an Australian reptile. Game artists also use it as ambient wildlife in outback or desert levels where small creatures add life to the ground. Veterinary clinics find it handy for explaining reptile care with a clear and friendly visual. Its royalty-free license covers commercial and personal projects alike without separate permission requests.
