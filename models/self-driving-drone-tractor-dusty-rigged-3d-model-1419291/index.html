About This Model

Priced at $99, the Self-Driving Drone Tractor Dusty Rigged is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 231,969 polygons and 240,841 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and aerospace visualization. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
