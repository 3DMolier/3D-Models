About This Model

A coiled high-pressure hose for an airless paint sprayer, with threaded swivel fittings at both ends that read clearly in close shots. The mesh counts 8,484 polygons and 8,516 vertices, most of them spent on the round cross-section that follows each loop of the coil. The loose coil lies flat on a floor, so it drops straight into a decorating, renovation or construction scene.

CheckMate Pro certification covers clean topology, real-world scale and a scene free of leftover junk. UVs are laid out without overlaps, which helps when you want a woven rubber texture or a printed pressure rating along the jacket. No maps ship with the hose, so the rubber and brass are shaded with your own materials.

Released in 2021, it accompanies painter rigs, job sites and tool catalogs where the sprayer alone would look incomplete. Contractors explaining spray technique and game teams building a messy work site use it the most often.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2021 · Updated 14 November 2026
