About This Model

A concrete coastal artillery bunker with thick walls and a wide firing embrasure, shown empty without its gun. The Military Coastal Artillery Bunker without Gun uses 73,924 polygons and 72,358 vertices on angled walls, roof slabs and entrance passages. The empty embrasure invites any gun from your library, from field pieces to naval cannons.

Its size suits a full-scale casemate that a crew could walk through, set into a cliff or dune. CheckMate certification keeps the structure organized and at correct real-world scale for environment and level design work. Weathered concrete surfaces and sharp chamfered edges recall Atlantic Wall fortifications from the Second World War.

Its 2026 release comes as a single version priced at $39, gun not included. War games, D-Day films, history documentaries and coastal defense museums use it to build fortified shorelines. Left empty, it also works as an abandoned ruin on a modern beach covered in graffiti.
