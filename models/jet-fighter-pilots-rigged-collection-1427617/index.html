About This Model

Rigged fighter pilots in flight suits and helmets, gathered so a cockpit, flight line or briefing room fills with crew quickly. The figures carry pressure suits, harnesses and oxygen masks, the gear that makes a combat aviator recognizable at a glance. Rigging lets each pilot climb a ladder, settle into an ejection seat or walk across a carrier deck.

Before release, TurboSquid inspected scale and naming across the figures and placed the set in its CheckMate Pro range. Olive flight suits, reflective visors and patches on the shoulders are the details that sell the role. Seen walking toward the camera in front of jets at dawn, the pilots form a classic aviation film image.

The rigged set costs $259, and it entered the range in 2019 for military aviation projects. It serves aviation films, flight simulator cockpits and training documentaries about modern air forces. A pilot lowering a visor in close-up is the beat right before a takeoff that audiences recognize instantly.
