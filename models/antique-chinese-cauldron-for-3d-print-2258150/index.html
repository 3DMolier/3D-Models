About This Model

This print-ready version of an antique Chinese cauldron is prepared for 3D printing, so the ritual vessel can become a real desk object. Walls are thick enough to survive slicing, and the decorative relief is kept bold so it reads after printing. The legs and handles merge cleanly with the bowl, avoiding fragile thin joints that often snap on printed replicas.

The Antique Chinese Cauldron for 3D Print follows CheckMate guidelines, so the file loads at a predictable size. Resin printers capture the fine patterns best, while filament printers suit larger decorative replicas with a bronze paint finish. After sanding, a dark wash in the recesses and metallic dry brushing make plastic look like old bronze.

Released in 2024, the file attracts tabletop gamers, history teachers and makers who want a museum piece on their shelf. Printed small, it becomes an incense holder, and printed larger, it anchors a themed room or stage set.
