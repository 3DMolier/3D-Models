About This Model

Priced at $49, the Electric Walkie Stacker and Wooden Pallet is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 38,492 polygons and 40,419 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 13 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2015 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
