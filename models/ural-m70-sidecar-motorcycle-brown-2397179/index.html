About This Model

A sidecar outfit is not a motorcycle with an attachment; it is an asymmetric three-wheeled vehicle that steers and leans quite differently. The chair sits on its own subframe with its own wheel and suspension, connected by struts that have to be visible and plausible, and the whole machine has a deliberately utilitarian, pre-war look.

The mesh runs 554,160 polygons and 562,359 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the engine, the sidecar frame and the spoked wheels.

Three rigged builds share this page, at $169 and $199 for versions prepared for Maya and for Cinema 4D. The static model at $149 covers motorcycle and adventure travel advertising, period film and television, dealership visualisation, and games. Built to StemCell specification, on sale since 2025.
