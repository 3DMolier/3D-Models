About This Model

Priced at $9, the Bamber Wooden Shoe Horn is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 1,826 polygons and 1,840 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.
