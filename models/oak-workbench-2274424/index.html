About This Model

Oak Workbench is one of the Tools models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 57,780 polygons and 60,591 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
