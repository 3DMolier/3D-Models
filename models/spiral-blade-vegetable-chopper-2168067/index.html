About This Model

This Furniture & Interior model - Spiral Blade Vegetable - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $18 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 83,708 polygons over 42,619 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
