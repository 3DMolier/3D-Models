About This Model

A grain transfer trailer with a tall hopper and folding auger, this machine shuttles harvest from the combine to waiting trucks. This worn black edition uses 331,289 polygons and 336,670 vertices for the hopper walls, the auger tube and the tandem wheels. Scratches, dust and faded paint suit a farm that has seen many seasons, and they make the trailer feel truly working.

A reviewer at TurboSquid inspected the trailer by hand and certified it CheckMate Pro for sound topology and true scale. Its UV layout keeps every surface on separate islands, so mud splashes and rust can be applied panel by panel. Paint colors come from materials, so the black finish can turn red or blue like the other trailers.

Ten versions come in red, blue and black, new or used, with several rigged trailers among them. Rigged versions cost $79, and the static trailers share the $59 price of this edition. Since 2021, the trailer has supported harvest season films, farm simulator games and agricultural machinery presentations.
