About This Model

A demountable grandstand is a scaffold structure with seating decks bolted onto it, a roof over the top and stairs at each end. It exists to be assembled for an event and taken away afterwards, which is why every joint is visible and nothing is clad - and that exposed structure is what makes it read as temporary rather than as a stadium.

The mesh runs 161,794 polygons and 169,478 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the frame members and the seating rows.

At $59 and built to StemCell specification, it suits event and venue visualization, sports and festival presentation, temporary infrastructure planning, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2023.
