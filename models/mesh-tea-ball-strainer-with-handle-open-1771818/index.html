About This Model

Priced at $24, the Mesh Tea Ball Strainer with Handle Open is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

Geometry weighs in at 2,110 polygons over 2,112 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
