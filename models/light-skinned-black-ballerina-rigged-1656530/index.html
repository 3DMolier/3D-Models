About This Model

Light Skinned Black Ballerina Rigged is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $169. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 10,834 polygons over 11,547 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
