About This Model

A collection of silver bullion bars for finance, banking and treasure scenes, ready to stack into gleaming piles. Cast and minted bars differ in shape, and a set lets a vault shelf mix them for a convincing reserve.

Polished silver reflects everything around it, so these bars look best with an HDRI and a dark backdrop. Stamped faces are where the eye lands first, so the top surfaces deserve sharp detail in close-ups. Certification at the CheckMate Pro level means the bars are cleanly built and sized like real ingots. A soft tarnish on older bars adds realism, since silver darkens slightly over time.

Released in 2017, the set fits investment articles, precious metal infographics, coin shop displays and heist scenes in games. Stack bars into a pyramid on a bank counter, or spill a few from an open case for drama. Next to gold bars, silver helps an image show different metals without extra modeling work.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2017 · Updated 12 November 2026
