About This Model

A collection of Super Win lottery tickets, the printed paper slips with bright graphics and rows of numbers picked up at a newsstand. Each ticket is a thin flat card, so the set stays light even when dozens are scattered across a table. Several designs in one purchase let you build a messy pile without obvious repeats, which helps realism.

The set carries a CheckMate Lite rating, a lighter TurboSquid check that still confirms usable structure. Paper can be shown crisp and new or bent and crumpled after a losing draw with a little deformation. Printed artwork sits on the cards themselves, so close shots show the colorful designs clearly.

Since 2023 the collection has cost $9 and suits gambling scenes, convenience store counters and news graphics about jackpots. Fan them out in a hand, pin one to a fridge, or let them spill from a wallet. A crumpled ticket in a trash can tells a whole story in a single quiet frame.
