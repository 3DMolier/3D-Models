About This Model

Priced at $119, the Hockey Player is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 61,172 polygons and 61,909 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 23 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
