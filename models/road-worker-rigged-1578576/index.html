About This Model

Priced at $199, the Road Worker Rigged is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 41,193 polygons and 39,871 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 19 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
