Paper Bread Bag is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $34. The mesh carries 3,262 polygons and 3,208 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
