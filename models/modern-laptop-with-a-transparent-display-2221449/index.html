About This Model

The Laptop with a Transparent Display is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 39,044 polygons and 42,185 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and product rendering.
