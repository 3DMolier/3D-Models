About This Model

Engine Powered Scissor Lift Generic 2 is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $79. The mesh carries 103,347 polygons and 106,501 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 13 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
