About This Model

A pair of laced leather service boots with thick rubber soles, worn by soldiers and sailors on duty. The Military Service Boots use 74,188 polygons and 74,854 vertices, enough for eyelets, laces, welt stitching and deep tread. High shafts wrap the ankle for support, the defining shape of any combat or service boot.

The pair measures 42.6 x 33.45 x H26.4 cm, standing side by side as they would by a bunk. CheckMate certification guarantees real scale, so the boots match adult characters without rescaling. Polished toe caps catch strong highlights while scuffed heels suggest long marches on hard ground.

Three versions come together here: the boots, a white Navy Dress Uniform with Cap and a dark Royal Navy Sailor Uniform. All of them date from 2026, and the boots alone cost $34. Period dramas, military museums, uniform catalogs and war games use them to complete a soldier from the ground up.
