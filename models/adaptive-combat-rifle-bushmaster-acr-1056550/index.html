About This Model

A modular assault rifle with a folding stock, a top rail for optics and a polymer lower receiver, ready for shooters and military scenes. The weapon spends 96,506 polygons and 97,262 vertices on the receiver, the handguard vents and the magazine well. Its squared-off upper and long rail give the rifle a very recognizable side profile.

Eleven maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2272 x 2272 hold the anodized metal, polymer grain and small engraved markings. A CheckMate Pro badge signals that TurboSquid examined its topology and naming at the highest level. Each part has its own unwrap space, so worn edges on the rail never repeat on the stock.

This weapon family has four versions at $49 each, beginning with the black rifle and a Coyote Brown variant. A carbine in Coyote Brown and a black carbine with a shorter barrel complete the set. Sold since 2016, it is used in first-person shooters, tactical training visuals and military films.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2016 · Updated 12 November 2026
