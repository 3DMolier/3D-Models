About This Model

The Model Y shares most of its parts with the saloon below it but raises the roof and the ride height, which changes the proportions completely: a taller glasshouse, a hatchback tail and a longer rear overhang. Those differences are subtle on paper and obvious in silhouette, which is exactly why they have to be right.

The mesh is efficient at 107,776 polygons and 121,063 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 9 texture maps - 7 at 4096x4096 and 2 at 2048x2048.

This page is well stocked, with blue, silver and standard builds at $129 and rigged versions at $149 including builds prepared for Maya and Cinema. This simple interior model at $99 suits urban visualization, background traffic, advertising and games. On sale since 2019.
