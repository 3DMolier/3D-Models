About This Model

A set of dried spices and fresh herbs gives kitchen scenes the small, colorful details that make cooking look real. The Spice Herbs Collection gathers these ingredients, from leafy sprigs to loose seeds and whole pods.

Each item keeps its natural shape, with curled bay leaves, star anise points and thin stems of rosemary or thyme. These pieces can be scattered on a cutting board or heaped in small bowls beside a pestle. The collection follows CheckMate specification, so every element is clean and sized consistently for close food shots.

Introduced in 2025, the set suits recipe videos, food packaging concepts and restaurant interior renders. Food stylists arrange the herbs around a finished dish to frame it on a rustic table. Animators let seeds fall into a pan in slow motion, catching each one as it bounces. Kitchen designers sprinkle a few jars across a counter, which makes a showroom feel lived in.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
