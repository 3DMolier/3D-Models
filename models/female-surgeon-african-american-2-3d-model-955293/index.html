About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Female Surgeon African American 2 is a finished, render-ready model at $129. Geometry weighs in at 9,159 polygons over 9,316 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

16 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
