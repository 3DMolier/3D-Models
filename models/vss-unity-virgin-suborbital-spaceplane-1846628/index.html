About This Model

A suborbital spaceplane is built around one trick: the tail booms fold upward for reentry, turning the aircraft into a shuttlecock that stabilises itself without control input. That feathering mechanism is the vehicle's whole safety argument, and it gives the airframe its twin boom layout and its unusual proportions.

The mesh is efficient at 139,044 polygons and 143,101 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the boom hinges, the cabin windows and the rocket nozzle.

A rigged build sits on this page at $149, which is the one to take if the tail has to feather. The static model at $99 covers commercial spaceflight marketing, space tourism material, news and documentary graphics, education and games. On sale since 2022.
