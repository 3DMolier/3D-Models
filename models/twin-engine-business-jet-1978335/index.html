About This Model

This Aircraft model - Twin Engine Business Jet - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Counted at the source, the model is 194,334 polygons and 197,817 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
