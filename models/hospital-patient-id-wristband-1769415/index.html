About This Model

Hospital Patient ID Wristband is one of the Clothing & Accessories models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $24. You are getting 2,932 polygons and 2,900 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering.
