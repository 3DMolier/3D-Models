About This Model

Priced at $19, the Cleanroom Sticky Mats is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 14,006 polygons over 14,476 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
