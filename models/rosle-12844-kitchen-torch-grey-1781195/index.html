About This Model

Rosle 12844 Kitchen Torch Grey belongs to our Tools range and is offered at $34 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
