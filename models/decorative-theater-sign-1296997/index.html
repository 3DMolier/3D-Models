About This Model

Decorative Theater Sign is a vintage marquee-style board framed by rows of round bulbs, the kind that lights up a cinema facade. Most of the 64,924 polygons are in those bulbs and their sockets, along with the ornate frame edges. Hung above an entrance on a rainy street, the glowing sign announces showtime from a block away.

The 70,602 vertices are unwrapped into separate, non-overlapping islands for clean texture work. Gold paint, chipped enamel and warm bulb glass are drawn from 6 maps at 4096 x 4096. CheckMate Pro certification means the sign fits a building facade at true size, with its parts sensibly named.

Available since 2018, the sign dresses theater districts, retro cinema lobbies and Broadway style title sequences. Animate the bulbs to chase around the frame and the sign instantly brings back old show business glamour. At $29 the sign is an affordable centerpiece for any entertainment district or vintage street.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2018 · Updated 17 November 2026

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Decorative Theater Sign?

6 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Decorative Theater Sign mesh?

64,924 polygons and 70,602 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Our Architecture & Landmarks listings sit at a median of about 53,000 polygons, so 64,924 is heavier than the typical one.

Can the Decorative Theater Sign model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How much does the Decorative Theater Sign 3D model cost?
