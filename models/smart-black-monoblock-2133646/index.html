About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Smart Black Monoblock is a finished, render-ready model at $39. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale.

Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 34,271 polygons over 18,483 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
