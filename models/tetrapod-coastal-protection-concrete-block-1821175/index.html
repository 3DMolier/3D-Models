About This Model

Priced at $24, the Tetrapod Coastal Protection Concrete Block is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 1,496 polygons and 1,506 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
