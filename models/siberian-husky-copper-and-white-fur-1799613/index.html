About This Model

Siberian Husky Fur is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $179. You are getting 11,998 polygons and 12,646 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
