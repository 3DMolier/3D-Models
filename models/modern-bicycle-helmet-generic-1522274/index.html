About This Model

A cycling helmet is mostly air. The vents are structural as well as cooling, the foam is designed to be destroyed once, and the whole object is a calculation about how much protection people will actually agree to wear.

The model is very light at 11,290 polygons and 11,490 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 4 maps all at 4096x4096, in a generic scheme with no branding. It carries StemCell certification.

At $49 it suits sports equipment and retail visualization, e-commerce imagery, character accessories, urban and cycling scenes, game assets and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.
