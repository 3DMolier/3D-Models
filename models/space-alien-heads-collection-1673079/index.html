About This Model

This collection gathers several extraterrestrial heads, each with its own skull shape, eyes and skin, for sci-fi characters and creature design. Heads come as separate objects, so they can sit on display stands, top custom bodies or fill a specimen lab.

Certified CheckMate Pro, the bundle cleared the marketplace's most demanding quality check. Wrinkled skin, bony ridges and large eyes give lighting plenty to grab, which matters for creatures that live in shadow. Different head shapes let one scene show several species meeting at a single table.

Space Alien Heads Collection reached the market in 2021 and fits sci-fi films, game character concepts and Halloween projects. Lined up in glass jars, the heads make a striking secret lab scene for a conspiracy story. Concept artists also use them as quick bases to sketch over when designing new species. A single head under a cold spotlight works as a strong poster or thumbnail image.
