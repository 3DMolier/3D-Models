About This Model

A collection of thumbtacks and push pins with round heads, flat brass caps and sharp steel points in several styles. The short needles and wide heads are the details that make a cork board look genuinely used. Small as they are, the pins tell a story when they hold notes, travel maps and family photos together on a wall.

The set carries CheckMate Pro certification, so every tack and pin arrives at true size with tidy, readable object names. Separate pieces can be pressed into a wall at slight angles to avoid the stamped look of copies.

These thumbtacks have been part of the collection since 2019 and remain an inexpensive scene filler. Detective boards come alive with red string stretched between pins over clippings and photographs. Office and classroom renders use them on notice boards, wall calendars, school timetables and children's drawings. A scattered handful on a desk beside an open box also hints at a project in progress.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
