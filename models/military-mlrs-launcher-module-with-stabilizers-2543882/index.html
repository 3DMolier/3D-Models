About This Model

A multiple rocket launcher module with outrigger stabilizers deployed, ready to fire from a truck or a fixed position. Its 366,650 polygons cover the launch tube pack, the elevating mechanism, hydraulic stabilizer legs and the rotating turntable base. At 403.23 x 532.83 x H404.11 cm the module sits at real scale beside vehicles and crew figures.

StemCell standards give the olive paint and steel parts consistent shading across every delivered format and renderer. Its 400,862 vertices sit on a mixed UV layout, so check the islands before adding stenciled markings. Extended stabilizers show the launcher in firing mode, the pose most defense imagery uses to signal readiness.

Two versions at $99 each give a choice: this module and a multi-mission launcher platform shown deployed. New in 2026, it suits defense analysis graphics, war documentaries, military strategy games and armed forces training simulations. Add smoke trails leaving the tubes in sequence, and a salvo becomes the dramatic centerpiece of the shot.
