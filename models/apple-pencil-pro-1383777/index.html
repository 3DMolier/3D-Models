About This Model

A white digital stylus with a flat side for magnetic charging and a pointed tip for precise drawing on a tablet. The Apple Pencil Pro needs only 660 polygons and 661 vertices, a remarkably lean mesh for a smooth cylindrical tool. Its flat edge stops it rolling off a desk, a small design choice that matters to artists.

A single map at 2048 x 2048 carries the matte white finish and subtle print near the end. StemCell certification keeps the matte finish consistent across the renderers TurboSquid supports. The UVs are free of overlaps, so fingerprints or scuffs can be added near the grip.

Available since 2019, the stylus suits digital art workspace renders, tablet tutorials and design studio scenes. Laid on a tablet screen beside a sketch, it tells viewers an illustrator is at work. Its tiny polygon count lets it appear on every desk in a busy design office scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2019 · Updated 14 November 2026
