About This Model

A stack of folded broadsheet dailies with curled corners, the kind left on a doorstep, a cafe table or a train seat. The pages lie loosely on each other with slight bends, so the pile looks handled rather than freshly printed. Folded edges and offset layers give the bundle believable thickness when seen from a low camera angle.

TurboSquid awarded CheckMate Pro after reviewing the mesh, physical scale and part naming of the set. Every paper is its own piece, which lets you pull one out, open it or roll it for a delivery shot. Front pages accept your own headlines and photos, which avoids showing real mastheads in a film frame.

Available since 2015, the press set suits office desks, morning kitchen scenes and crime board walls in detective stories. Set designers scatter pages across a park bench or a subway floor to suggest a busy city morning. Educators use the pile in explainers on media history, while game artists drop it near newsstands.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
