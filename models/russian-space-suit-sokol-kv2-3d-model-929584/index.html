About This Model

Russian Space Suit Sokol KV2 is one of the Clothing & Accessories models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2015 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

The mesh carries 23,392 polygons and 24,100 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 7 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background.
