About This Model

Glossy party balloons with digit-shaped foil numbers and curling ribbons, ready to mark birthdays, anniversaries and celebrations in any scene. The 51,556 polygons shape the round latex bodies, the tied knots and the puffy seams around each foil numeral. Behind them sit 51,506 vertices, enough to keep each shape smooth and reflective even in close party shots.

Twenty texture maps at 2048 x 2048 give the foil its metallic shine and the latex its soft, slightly translucent color. UV islands keep clear of one another, so names or patterns can be printed onto any of them without distortion. A manual pass at TurboSquid over the structure, the naming and the dimensions gave this decor its CheckMate Pro grade.

Dating from 2018, the decorations cost $39 and suit birthday cards, event planning visuals and celebration animations. Party stores, greeting video makers and game designers decorating festive levels all reach for props that spell out an age. Tie two numbers together with ribbons above a cake, and the scene announces a milestone birthday without any text.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2018 · Updated 13 November 2026
