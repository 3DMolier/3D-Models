About This Model

A Spalding hoop with a glass backboard, orange rim and hanging net, ready for driveway, gym or street court scenes. It carries 99,256 polygons on 49,898 vertices, a triangulated build where net cords and rim brackets take most faces. The woven net tapers naturally, and the mounting arm shows the bolts and plates visible from below.

UV islands are separate, so backboard graphics and rim paint can change without any cleanup. A CheckMate Pro grade places the rim at believable height and scale relative to players, balls and court markings. The frame, spring-loaded rim and net hooks all read clearly from a broadcast angle.

Two versions cost $29 each: Basketball Hoop and Net Spalding, plus an unbranded Basketball Hoop with Net. Since 2024 it has picked up 17 sales from sports games, school gym interiors and suburban backyard renders. The branded version is licensed for editorial use only, while the plain one suits any neutral setting.
