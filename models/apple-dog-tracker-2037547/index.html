About This Model

This dog tracker attaches a small round location tag to a pet collar, so an owner can find a wandering dog by phone. The model uses 14,816 polygons on 7,388 vertices for the disc, the holder and the collar loop. Its smooth white face and rounded edges look like a pebble, which keeps the tag discreet on a furry neck.

Rated CheckMate Pro, the tracker has non-overlapping UVs that keep the glossy disc and the holder material apart. Soft white plastic with a polished metal back and a leather or silicone holder suits the design.

On sale since 2023, it fits pet care scenes, smart gadget explainers and stories about lost animals. Shown on a dog in a park, it hints at technology without pulling attention from the animal. Pet shop interiors and veterinary waiting rooms can also display it on a shelf beside collars and leashes. Tech review videos comparing location tags can rotate it on a turntable to show both sides clearly.
