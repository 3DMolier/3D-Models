About This Model

The tank is the part of a paintball marker that is genuinely regulated. It holds air at a pressure that would matter if it failed, it carries a test date, and it is the one component that cannot be modified by the owner.

The model is extremely light at 5,184 polygons and 5,369 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $24 it suits sports equipment visualization, e-commerce and retail imagery, character accessories, game assets, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2022.
