About This Model

This Vehicles model - Sextant Navigation Instrument - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 16,763 polygons and 17,245 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
