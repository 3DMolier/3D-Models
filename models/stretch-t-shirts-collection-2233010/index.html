About This Model

A set of stretch T-shirts with fitted cuts, ready to dress characters or to present apparel designs on clean product renders. The fabric hugs the torso, showing the soft folds at the waist and the gentle pull across the shoulders. Different cuts and colors give a quick range for wardrobe variety without modeling each shirt from scratch.

Built to CheckMate specification, shirts arrive as separate objects with consistent real-world scale. Prints, stripes or plain graphics can be applied directly when you need quick merchandise mockups. Cloth simulations can also take the shirts as a starting point for animated movement.

First listed in 2024, the collection suits fashion e-commerce, sportswear catalogs and casual characters in games. Laid flat or shown on a mannequin, the shirts work for both catalog and lifestyle imagery. Soft side lighting brings out the stretch fabric and seams, and designers compare necklines and sleeve lengths side by side before production.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
