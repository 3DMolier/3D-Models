About This Model

A group of Soviet ground-attack aircraft in one pack, built for Cold War air combat scenes and military history projects. Armored cockpits, sturdy wings and hardpoints for rockets and bombs define the low-flying strike profile. Heavy, purposeful shapes like these read well in low passes over terrain, where speed blur hides fine detail anyway.

Every aircraft follows CheckMate specification, so wingspans stay in proportion when several planes fly in formation together. Camouflage schemes and national markings can be repainted per aircraft, letting one pack represent several squadrons. Control surfaces such as flaps, rudder and ailerons are the parts animators reach for during a strafing run.

These airframes appeared in 2025, and $199 covers the whole squadron with royalty free terms. Flight simulators, war games, history documentaries and museum explainers are where these attack planes earn their keep. A tight formation skimming a valley at dawn is the kind of shot these airframes suit best.
