About This Model

Priced at $34, the Samsung 4K SUHD JS8500 Series Smart TV 55 inch is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product advertising, hardware presentation and film production. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 22,175 polygons and 27,622 vertices, a light load for a scene. Maps: 6 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
