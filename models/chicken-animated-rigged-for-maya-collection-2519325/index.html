About This Model

Hens, roosters and chicks make up this flock, each rigged and animated for Maya so they can peck, scratch and flap. A farm scene gets a whole flock rather than a single bird repeated many times. Different feather colors and sizes make the group look like a real backyard flock.

Each bird comes with rig and motion, so pecking and wandering cycles can be mixed to avoid visible repetition. Head bobs and quick scratches with the feet are the movements that make chickens instantly recognizable. Golden straw and warm barn light make the feathers glow in an early morning scene.

The flock was released in 2026 and fits commercial and educational projects. Farm explainers, egg carton visuals, children's animation and farming games are natural uses. A rooster on a fence with hens pecking in the dust below opens a farm scene perfectly, with chicks trailing behind their mother.
