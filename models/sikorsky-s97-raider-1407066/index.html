About This Model

Sikorsky S97 Raider belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Geometry weighs in at 81,363 polygons over 86,131 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
