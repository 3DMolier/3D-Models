About This Model

Stick Figure Running Pose Black belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $9 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 1,896 polygons over 954 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
