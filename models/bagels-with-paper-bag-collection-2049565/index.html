About This Model

Fresh bagels with a brown paper bakery bag, a small set for breakfast tables, café counters and takeaway scenes. The ring shapes with their glossy crust and the crumpled bag give a quick sense of morning and freshly baked bread. Several pieces in one purchase let a few bagels spill out of the bag while others rest on a plate.

This collection carries CheckMate Pro, the tougher of the TurboSquid certifications for geometry, naming and organization. The paper bag benefits from a slightly rough shader with soft folds, while the bagels take a warm, faintly shiny baked tone. Because the items come as a group, a close shot can use a single bagel and leave the rest aside.

It was released in 2023 and sells for $34, and commercial use is Royalty Free. Bakery and café interiors, breakfast food photography and New York street scenes in games are common places for it. On a kitchen counter next to a coffee mug, it turns an empty set into a lived-in morning.
