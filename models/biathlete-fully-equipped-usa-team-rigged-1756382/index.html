About This Model

Priced at $249, the Biathlete Fully Equipped USA Team is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 193,714 polygons and 210,142 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
