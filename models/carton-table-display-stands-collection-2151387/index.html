About This Model

Folding cardboard display stands turn an empty store aisle into a promotion point for snacks, books or cosmetics. The set totals 17,202 polygons and 8,615 vertices spread across stands of different heights, tiers and shelf layouts. Thin carton walls, folded lips and tabbed corners keep them reading as printed board rather than plastic fixtures.

No graphics are bundled, which is the point: the blank surfaces wait for your own product art and branding. A clean, non-overlapping unwrap gives each panel its own area, so printed artwork lines up across the fold. TurboSquid rated the collection CheckMate Lite, confirming basic geometry quality and that the files open as expected.

Retail designers have used the set since 2023 to mock up store layouts and in-store displays before anything goes to print. Packaging studios also fill the shelves with their products to preview how a launch will look in a supermarket. Game levels use them as clutter in shops and warehouses, where a few stands quickly fill an empty corner.
