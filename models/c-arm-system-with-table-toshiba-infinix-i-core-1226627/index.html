About This Model

This Toshiba Infinix i Core angiography C-arm with patient table recreates an interventional cath lab for medical renders. The 120,697 polygons and 123,225 vertices describe the curved gantry, the X-ray tube, the flat panel detector and table rails. Swinging over the patient, the arm creates a bold arc that reads well from overhead cameras.

The 21 maps at 4096 x 4096 render white plastic covers, metal joints, padded cushions and small warning labels on clean UVs. StemCell delivery keeps these PBR materials consistent across every supplied file format and renderer, from game engines to offline path tracers.

Dating from 2017 and offered under an editorial license, the system fits hospital planning, cardiology education and documentary work. Surround it with ceiling monitors, lead shields and an injector stand to complete an interventional suite. The setup also helps patient explainers show how a catheter is guided by live X-ray imaging.
