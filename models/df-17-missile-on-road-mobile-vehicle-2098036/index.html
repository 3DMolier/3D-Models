About This Model

DF-17 Missile on Road Mobile Vehicle belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $149 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. 2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defence training material and military VFX shots.

The mesh carries 824,976 polygons and 437,360 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
