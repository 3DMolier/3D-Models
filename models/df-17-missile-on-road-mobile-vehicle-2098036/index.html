The DF-17 Missile on Road Mobile is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 824,976 polygons and 437,360 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
