About This Model

Priced at $49, the Women Sleeveless Pijama is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 1,226 polygons and 1,391 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

8 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 1000 x 1000 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
