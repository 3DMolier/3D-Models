About This Model

A set of rustic wooden storage pieces, including crates, barrels and boxes, ready for warehouses, ships and medieval markets. The Wooden Conteiners Collection covers slatted crates, banded barrels and lidded chests, so a scene can fill quickly with believable cargo. Weathered planks with split ends give each piece an aged, hard-working look after years of travel.

Iron bands, nails and rope handles add small authentic touches that hold up when the camera moves in close. Pieces can be stacked, opened or scattered across docks without extra modeling effort. The collection meets CheckMate Pro, so the files open cleanly with consistent scale throughout.

Part of the catalog since 2015, the containers suit pirate games, fantasy taverns and historical harbor scenes. Pile them in the hold of a sailing ship, or line them along a market street with goods inside. Game level designers use them to break up empty floor space naturally and give players cover.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 12 November 2026
