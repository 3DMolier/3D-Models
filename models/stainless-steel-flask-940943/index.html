About This Model

A slim hip flask in polished stainless steel is a classic personal accessory carried in a jacket pocket. Its curved body follows the shape of the hip, with a screw cap on top and softly rounded edges all around. That gentle curve gives the flask its signature look and catches long highlights in a render.

CheckMate Pro status from TurboSquid confirms the geometry is clean and the size matches a real pocket flask. The metal surface is easy to customize, whether with engraved initials, a leather wrap or a brushed satin finish. Its pivot sits naturally at the base, so placing it on a table or in a hand needs no adjustment.

On the market since 2015, the flask suits detective stories, western saloons, camping scenes and gift product presentations. Character artists slip it into a coat pocket or show it raised for a quick sip on a cold night. Laid beside a pocket watch and a wallet, it also makes a classic menswear still life.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
