About This Model

Sugar Packet is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Geometry weighs in at 1,648 polygons over 1,650 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
