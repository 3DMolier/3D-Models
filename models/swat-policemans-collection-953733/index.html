About This Model

A team of SWAT officers in tactical vests, helmets and gear forms a ready unit for police and action scenes. The figures share a consistent level of detail, so they can stand together in a breach line without any looking out of place. Pouches, knee pads and radios on each officer give the squad a practical, mission-ready silhouette.

Each figure was built to the CheckMate specification and reviewed by TurboSquid for structure. Dark tactical fabric, matte armor plates and reflective visors keep the team readable in night scenes. Patches and lettering on the vests clearly identify the officers as police.

The collection costs $199 and has stayed available in the catalog since 2015, when tactical characters were still rare. It fills action films, police training visualizations and tactical shooter games that need a full unit rather than one officer. Line the officers up behind a shield outside a door, and a hostage rescue moment comes alive.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
