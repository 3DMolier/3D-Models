About This Model

A collection of fruit seeds and stones, from tiny pips to large pits, for botanical and educational close-ups. Seeds are hard to find as ready models, and this set covers the shapes needed for diagrams and food scenes.

Under CheckMate Pro certification, the seeds come with tidy geometry and realistic size relations between them. Smooth shells, ridged pits and glossy coats give each seed a distinct surface under macro lighting. Every seed is a separate object, so a set can be arranged in rows for a chart or scattered across a cutting board.

Released in 2017, the collection serves biology lessons, gardening guides, food illustration and nutrition infographics. Arranged in a neat grid with labels, the seeds form a clear comparison chart for students. Cooks and food stylists also scatter a few beside halved fruit, a small detail that makes a sliced peach or apple look freshly cut.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2017 · Updated 11 November 2026
