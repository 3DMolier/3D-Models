About This Model

A collection of breads in different shapes, from crusty loaves to rolls and baguettes, ready to stock a bakery shelf. Varied crust colors and scoring patterns keep a full bakery display from looking repetitive or copied.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms clean topology, real scale and consistent naming across every piece in the set. Golden crusts with floury tops and cracked edges are what sell bread on screen, and the shapes leave room for that. Warm, soft lighting brings out the crust texture and makes the bread look freshly baked.

In the catalog since 2023, the set suits bakery interiors, breakfast tables, grocery aisles and recipe sites. Placed in wicker baskets or on wooden boards, the loaves bring warmth to kitchen, deli and cafe renders. Game developers use bread as everyday food items in medieval taverns, village markets and survival inventories. Close shots of a loaf sliced open also work for flour and baking product content.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 12 November 2026
