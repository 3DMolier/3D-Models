About This Model

Science fiction cargo containers do the same job as set dressing that real ones do in a warehouse: they establish scale, fill space and imply that something is being moved somewhere for a reason nobody has to explain.

The model is light at 22,438 polygons and 22,324 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in the unlocked configuration. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits science fiction film and animation, game environments and props, concept art, exhibition work and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
