About This Model

Deep Sea Tug is one of the Ships & Boats models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. Geometry weighs in at 529,973 polygons over 551,914 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 21 textures, authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags defense simulation, naval simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
