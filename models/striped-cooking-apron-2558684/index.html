About This Model

The Striped Cooking Apron is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 6,340 polygons and 6,341 vertices. That sits in low-poly territory, so it stays cheap to instance across a crowd scene or a game level. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and game assets. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
