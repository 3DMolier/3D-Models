About This Model

Priced at $18, the Electrical Set Ceramic is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 37,854 polygons and 19,650 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
