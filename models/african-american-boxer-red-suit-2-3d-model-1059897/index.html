About This Model

Priced at $129, the African American Boxer Red Suit 2 is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

At 21,212 polygons and 22,414 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 13 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
