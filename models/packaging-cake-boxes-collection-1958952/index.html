About This Model

These cake boxes are folded cardboard cartons that bakeries use to carry a finished dessert home without crushing the cream. Several boxes come together, which helps a pastry shop counter look stocked rather than repeated. Flat cardboard faces are ideal for testing custom labels, logos or patterns on a new design.

With CheckMate Pro behind it, the thin card walls and folded flaps are built from clean geometry. In film and game scenes, a stack of cake boxes on a kitchen counter quietly signals a birthday party. White, pink and kraft card colors each suggest a different kind of patisserie, from rustic to luxurious.

Released in 2022, the boxes may be used freely in paid packaging and bakery work. Packaging designers, bakery owners planning a brand refresh and food delivery apps are the natural audience here. Boxes tied with ribbon or stacked beside a cafe till make a simple shot feel festive and generous.
