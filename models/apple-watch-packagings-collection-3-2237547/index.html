About This Model

This set collects Apple Watch retail packaging, the long slim boxes and inner trays that hold the watch and its band. Clean white surfaces, rounded edges and pull tabs give the boxes the restrained look the brand is known for. Opened boxes reveal the watch cradle, while closed ones stack neatly on a store shelf.

Every box is a separate, well-named object under CheckMate specification rules, ready to stack or animate. Minimal faces leave room for crisp printed images, subtle embossing and paper textures with a matte finish. Soft top light and gentle shadows recreate the calm mood of an unboxing video.

Out since 2025 for editorial use, the boxes suit tech reviews, unboxing features, retail news and gift guide articles. Set dressers stack them on store counters, while animators lift lids to reveal a watch for a review intro. Placed beside a phone and earbuds, the packaging completes a tidy tech flat lay.
