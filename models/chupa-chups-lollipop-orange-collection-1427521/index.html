About This Model

A collection of Chupa Chups orange lollipops with the round candy head, white stick and twisted wrapper, made for editorial candy and snack imagery. Wrapped and unwrapped pieces show the glossy orange sphere alongside the familiar flower logo on the wrapper. Scatter a handful on a table or stand them in a jar to evoke a sweet shop counter.

The set carries CheckMate Pro certification, covering clean geometry and true candy size beside hands and mouths. A slightly translucent candy shader brings out the fruit color when light passes through the ball. Crinkled foil on the wrapper catches highlights nicely in close macro shots.

Released in 2019, the collection has gathered 27 buyers working on editorial food features, retail planograms and casual games. As a branded product it is meant for editorial use, so it suits news, reviews and documentary content. Its low price makes it an easy extra for any candy aisle or kids' party scene.
