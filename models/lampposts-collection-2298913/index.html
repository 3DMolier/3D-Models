About This Model

A collection of street lampposts for lining roads, parks and plazas, with poles, arms and lantern heads modeled in full. Lampposts Collection is detailed at 244,192 polygons and 274,935 vertices across the set, so each lamp holds up close to camera. Their tall vertical lines frame a shot naturally, which is why they appear in so many city establishing views.

UVs are non-overlapping, ready for painted cast iron, galvanized steel or a chipped municipal green. Built to CheckMate specification, the set keeps every lamp at real height with clear names, making it easy to pick one style. Lantern glass can take an emissive material, turning each head into a light source for night scenes.

On sale since 2024, the collection suits urban visualization, park design renders, street level game maps and film backgrounds. Repeat a single lamp along a sidewalk and the rhythm of the poles gives a street its depth. For distant rows, pair the detailed lamps with lighter stand-ins and save the full mesh for the foreground.
