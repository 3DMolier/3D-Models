About This Model

Candle Lantern Small White is one of the Lighting models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, event visualization and film & TV VFX. Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits interior and architectural visualization, product rendering and game environment dressing.

Counted at the source, the model is 45,496 polygons and 22,817 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
