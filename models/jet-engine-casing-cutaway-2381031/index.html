About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Jet Engine Casing Cutaway is a finished, render-ready model at $41. Counted at the source, the model is 83,031 polygons and 85,636 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
