About This Model

A Ticonderoga-class guided missile cruiser for naval scenes, with the angular deckhouse that carries the flat Aegis radar faces. USS Chosin CG-65 takes its name from the Chosin Reservoir campaign of the Korean War and served much of its career in the Pacific. The hull shows vertical launch cells fore and aft, deck guns at both ends and a tall lattice of masts amidships.

The model passed the CheckMate Lite check, which confirms clean geometry and sensible file structure without the full Pro audit. Haze gray paint and hull number markings make the ship identifiable when it sits among other fleet units. A helicopter deck at the stern gives you a spot to land a Seahawk in a rescue sequence.

Listed since 2016 and priced at $289, the cruiser has a long track record with naval enthusiasts. Military documentaries, Pacific fleet animations, naval strategy games and defense news graphics are its natural homes. Pair it with sister ships of the class to build a carrier escort group.
