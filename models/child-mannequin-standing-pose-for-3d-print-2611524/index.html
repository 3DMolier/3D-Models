About This Model

A standing child mannequin in a neutral pose, prepared as a solid printable mesh for display figures and fitting forms. The Child Mannequin Standing Pose for 3D Print uses 440,944 polygons and 220,641 vertices, keeping curves smooth after printing. Its relaxed arms and balanced stance suit clothing displays, store windows and sewing studios.

It meets CheckMate requirements with clean, closed geometry well suited to slicing software and resin printers. Because the mesh carries no UV layout, it is meant for printing or plain shading rather than detailed texture work. The faceless head and smooth limbs give it the stylized look of retail display forms.

It has been offered since 2026 for $15 under Royalty Free terms, which suits shops and schools. Children's clothing stores, fashion school projects, printed miniatures and window visualizations are where it fits. Printed at small scale, it also works as a posing reference for illustrators and sculptors.
