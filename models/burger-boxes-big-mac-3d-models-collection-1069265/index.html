About This Model

A collection of fast food burger clamshell boxes in the familiar Big Mac design, ready for restaurant counters, delivery and takeaway scenes. Each box is a separate object, so you can stack them on a tray, scatter them or line up a counter. The folded cardboard shape with its rounded lid is instantly recognizable to most viewers.

Under CheckMate Lite, the files are checked to open correctly and work without repair. Printed graphics on the sides carry most of the realism, so soft, even lighting suits these boxes best. Crumpled napkins and a drink cup nearby complete a convincing fast food table scene in seconds.

It is sold as one collection for just $9, without separate versions or sizes. Editorial food stories, street scenes and fast food industry reports explain its 12 sales since 2016. Left on a park bench beside a crumpled paper bag, the boxes clearly say lunch on the go.
