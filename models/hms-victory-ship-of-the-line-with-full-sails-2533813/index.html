About This Model

A first rate ship of the line under full sail is one of the most complex objects ever built by hand: three masts, dozens of sails, and a rigging plan of hundreds of separate lines each with a purpose. Modeling it credibly means the standing rigging has to hold the masts and the running rigging has to reach the sails it controls, or a sailor's eye rejects the whole thing.

The mesh is a very large 2,575,152 polygons and 2,605,991 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout. That density goes into the rigging, the gunports and the stern carvings, which is exactly where a ship like this is examined.

Five other builds sit on this page, including furled sails at $269, a black-sailed pirate variant at $269 and a simplified build at $239. Aimed at maritime history and museum work, period film and television, education, and games.
