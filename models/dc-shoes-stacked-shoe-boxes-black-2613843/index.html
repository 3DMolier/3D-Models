About This Model

A neat stack of black shoe boxes for stockroom shelves, closet floors and sneaker store backdrops. With 6,198 polygons the budget goes to lid edges and box corners, which keeps the stack crisp yet light. Boxes sit slightly offset, so the pile looks hand-stacked rather than perfectly aligned.

Thanks to StemCell delivery, the matte black card shades the same way in each of the supplied formats. Coverage of UVs is mixed, and the 6,166 vertices keep each box as a closed, simple shell. The stack measures 41.1 x 28.44 x H26.69 cm, a believable pile of boxed footwear.

The model went on sale in 2026 for $19 and works as background clutter for retail and home scenes. Duplicate the stack along a wall and you get a full stock room in seconds. Lids sit slightly proud of the box walls, giving each layer a readable shadow line.
