About This Model

A city bus stop with an elderly lady waiting under its shelter, rigged and ready for street scenes and urban daily life. The shelter combines a slim roof, glass side panels and a bench, the familiar layout found on countless suburban roads. The woman wears a coat and carries a handbag, and her patient stance tells the viewer she is waiting for a ride.

TurboSquid placed the set in its CheckMate Pro tier after checking the topology, scale and naming of every part. Rigging lets the lady turn her head, step forward or sit down as a bus pulls up to the curb. Glass panels on the shelter catch street reflections, a small detail that makes night renders feel lived in.

Since 2022 the set has filled city planning visuals, public transport explainers and quiet moments in slice of life animation. Light rain on the pavement and a glowing timetable inside the shelter add mood to an evening stop.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
