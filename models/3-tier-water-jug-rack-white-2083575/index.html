About This Model

Looking for a Containers & Storage asset? The 3 Tier Water is a finished, render-ready model at $24. The mesh carries 64,192 polygons and 32,226 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
