About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Cessna 150 2 is a finished, render-ready model at $99. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale.

Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 142,239 polygons and 142,678 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 26 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
