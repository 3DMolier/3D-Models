About This Model

A collection of miniature shopping trolleys with wire baskets, made for tabletop retail displays, online store icons and playful shop scenes. Each little cart echoes the full-size design, with a push handle, a mesh basket and small swivel casters underneath. Grouped together, the trolleys read as a tidy fleet, the way they wait in a row at a supermarket entrance.

The collection was made to CheckMate specification, so every cart opens at a consistent scale with readable object names. Thin wire grids on the baskets suit a chrome or painted metal shader, and they cast lively shadows under a single light.

Released in 2026, the set serves e-commerce banners, sale promotions, checkout illustrations and toy store windows. A tiny cart filled with gift boxes or coins makes an instant symbol for shopping, discounts or a full basket of savings. Buying the group saves building matching trolleys one by one when a scene needs several sizes or angles.
