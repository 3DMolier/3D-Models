About This Model

A full-size electric SUV with a closed grille, slim LED headlights and a smooth, aerodynamic roofline. The 119,132 polygons and 135,062 vertices cover the body, the wheels and a simplified cabin seen through the windows. With its low interior detail, the SUV suits traffic shots and parking lots rather than dashboard close-ups.

8 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 render the paint, tires, lights and grille pattern. Non-overlapping UVs keep it easy to change body color or apply road dirt along the doors. Its CheckMate Pro certification verifies correct scale and clean surfaces for automotive renders.

Three versions are on offer: this Simple Interior build at $99, a full model at $129 and a rigged one at $149. Since 2019 it has 12 sales from users creating electric mobility scenes, charging station renders and city streets. Parked at a charging post at night, the SUV tells an electric car story without extra props.
