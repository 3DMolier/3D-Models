About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Dwarf Warrior with Hammer Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $199. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and defense simulation. 2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.

The mesh carries 86,007 polygons and 89,453 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
