About This Model

A spiky orange horned melon shown both whole and sliced open, revealing the bright green jelly and pale seeds inside. African Kiwano Fruit Whole and Half is a light mesh of 9,267 polygons and 9,271 vertices, with the horns kept crisp across the rind. The cut half lies face up beside the whole fruit, inviting a close macro shot.

Surface realism comes from 8 maps at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096, carrying the mottled skin and glossy seed pulp. A non-overlapping UV layout keeps peel and flesh apart for easy adjustments to ripeness or color. The fruit holds CheckMate Pro status, meaning its topology and scale were reviewed.

Since 2018 the kiwano has appeared in exotic fruit displays, cooking shows and healthy eating campaigns for shops. Arrange it on a market stall beside dragon fruit, or set the halves on a cutting board with a knife. Food illustrators and teachers use it when introducing unusual tropical produce to curious audiences.
