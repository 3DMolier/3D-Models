About This Model

Plastic Mouse Trap Black is a detailed Tools asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
