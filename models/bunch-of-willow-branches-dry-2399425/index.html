About This Model

Looking for a Nature & Plants asset? The Bunch of Willow Branches Dry is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Geometry weighs in at 93,802 polygons over 94,358 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
