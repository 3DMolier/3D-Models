About This Model

An air traffic control tower with a slim concrete shaft rising to a glazed cab that looks out over the runways. Airport Tower uses 55,573 polygons over 57,441 vertices on the angled cab windows, the roof antennas and the base building. The sloped glass of the cab catches reflections of the sky, a detail that sells the building from far across an apron.

Concrete, tinted glass and metal railings come from 8 maps at 4096 x 4096. Railings and antennas stay crisp in a close shot from a helicopter approaching the field. Islands in the unwrap never overlap, and the tower bears a CheckMate Pro certificate from marketplace reviewers.

Released in 2019, the tower has 19 sales to airport planners, flight simulator makers and aviation film crews. Placed at the edge of a runway with jets taxiing past, it completes an airfield in a single object. At night, with lights in the cab glowing, it becomes a landmark that guides the eye across the scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2019 · Updated 14 November 2026
