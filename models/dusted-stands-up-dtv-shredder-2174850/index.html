About This Model

The DTV Shredder is a stand-up tracked machine, half skateboard and half tank, and this version is covered in trail dust. The rider stands on the deck between twin tracks and steers with a tall handlebar, a layout that looks unlike any other off-road vehicle.

The dusty finish saves you a weathering pass, since the dirt is already settled into the tracks, the deck and the frame. StemCell standardizes the PBR materials, so the mud and worn metal behave the same way across the delivered formats. The machine is a real product, so the model carries an Editorial Uses Only license for news, education and film work.

Listed since 2024, the Shredder fits extreme sports documentaries, off-road game levels and driving simulation scenes. Parked on a muddy hillside with its tracks caked in dirt, it looks like it just finished a hard run. The model costs $89 and arrives ready for close inspection shots of the tracks.
