About This Model

A collection of grass clumps, tufts of blades that break up flat ground in landscapes, gardens and game levels. Each clump grows from a dense base and fans out at the tips, so it reads as a living plant rather than a texture. Clumps of different height and density let you mix lush patches with dry, sparse ones.

Scatter tools in most renderers can spread these tufts across terrain with random rotation and scale. Backlight through the blades gives the translucent glow of real grass at sunrise or sunset. Constructed to CheckMate specification, every tuft is a separate object that instances well in large numbers.

Launched in 2026, the tufts fit architectural landscaping, nature documentaries, open world games and garden design visuals. Clusters placed along paths, fences and rocks hide hard edges where the ground meets other objects. A few tall clumps in the foreground of a wide shot add depth that a flat lawn never gives.
