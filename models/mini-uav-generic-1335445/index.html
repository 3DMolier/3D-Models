About This Model

Priced at $79, the Mini UAV Generic is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

At 56,504 polygons and 58,969 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It ships 5 maps with the top set authored at 8192x8192 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
