About This Model

An animated basketball shot in which a Spalding ball arcs through the air and drops through the hoop, shaking the net. The scene uses 53,825 polygons and 54,373 vertices, shared between the ball, ring, backboard and net. The backboard and orange rim are modeled as a full hoop unit, ready to mount on a gym wall.

Fifteen maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 hold the pebbled leather, black seams and printed markings. Because the motion comes already keyed, the shot plays straight away without any extra animation work. CheckMate Pro status confirms clean geometry, and Editorial Uses Only terms cover sports news and documentaries.

Four animated versions are offered at $79 each, starting with this Spalding shot and a generic ball flying in. The other two show a generic ball bouncing off the ring and a Spalding ball bouncing off it. With both a score and a miss, an editor can cut either outcome, and the set has existed since 2019.
