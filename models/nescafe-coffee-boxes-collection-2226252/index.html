About This Model

A collection of Nescafe coffee boxes, the familiar cartons of instant coffee seen on kitchen shelves and in offices. The boxes keep the crisp folds and printed panels of real retail packaging. Lined up together, the cartons form a recognizable pantry or supermarket shelf display.

Following CheckMate guidelines, the cartons are clean, simple geometry that lines up neatly in any scene. Sharp packaging edges and flat faces keep the printed artwork legible at close range and under strong light. The light geometry lets a whole shelf of boxes sit in a store interior without slowing the viewport.

The license is Editorial Uses Only, so the boxes suit news articles, retail analysis, consumer reviews and documentaries. Since 2024 the set has also served educational scenes about food packaging and coffee culture around the world. A box beside a mug and a kettle on an office counter sets a familiar morning break scene.
