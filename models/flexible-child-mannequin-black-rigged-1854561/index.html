About This Model

Flexible Child Mannequin is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. You are getting 21,467 polygons and 21,503 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
