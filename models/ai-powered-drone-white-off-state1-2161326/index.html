About This Model

This white AI-powered drone is shown in its off state, with rotors still and sensors dark, a clean product view. A smooth shell, compact arms and a camera gimbal under the nose give it a modern consumer tech design. The powered-down look suits packshots and technical explainers where motion would distract from the form.

Built under CheckMate guidance, it imports with tidy object names, clean pivots and believable scale. Glossy white plastic with dark sensor windows contrasts well against both bright and dark backgrounds. Propellers sit as separate parts, so they can be spun or folded when the drone wakes up in another shot.

Available since 2023, the drone suits tech product pages, delivery service concepts and smart city visualizations. Place it on a landing pad at dawn to suggest the moment before an autonomous flight. Startup pitches, tech presentations and games can include the drone freely under its Royalty Free terms.
