About This Model

Priced at $19, the Dried Laurel Leaves is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 13,130 polygons and 12,371 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization and film & TV VFX. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
