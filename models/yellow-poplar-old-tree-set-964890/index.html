About This Model

Yellow Poplar Old Tree Set belongs to our Nature & Plants range and is offered at $49 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for architecture visualization, film production and game environments. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 3,134,133 polygons over 5,999,568 vertices - a heavy build for its class. Texture work runs to 15 maps, the largest at 2048x2048, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
