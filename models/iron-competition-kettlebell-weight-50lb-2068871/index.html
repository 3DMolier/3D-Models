About This Model

A competition kettlebell with a wide flat handle and a uniform bell size, the style used in sport lifting regardless of weight. This competition kettlebell is very light at 556 polygons and 1,056 vertices, so a full rack costs almost nothing. The handle window is wide enough for both hands, which is how swings and goblet squats are usually shown.

CheckMate Pro approval confirms real scale, clean pivots and sensible object names. Non-overlapping UVs make it simple to add the painted color band and the embossed weight marking on the side. The smooth round body takes a matte powder coat or a chipped iron finish equally well.

First listed in 2023, it suits gym interiors, fitness app illustrations, workout tutorials and sports game levels. Line several along a rubber floor and a home gym or competition platform feels ready for training. In a hand-held pose, the bell's weight is implied by the lifter's posture rather than by any detail on the iron.
