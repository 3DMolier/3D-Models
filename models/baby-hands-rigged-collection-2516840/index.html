About This Model

Small baby hands with chubby fingers and soft creases, rigged so each finger can curl, grasp and point. The Baby Hands Rigged Collection covers close-up moments where only a child's hand is in frame. Dimpled knuckles and short, rounded fingers are what make an infant hand read differently from an adult one.

The hands are built to CheckMate specification, so they match real infant proportions and import at true size. Finger controls let an animator wrap the hand around a parent's thumb, one of the most requested shots. Skin shading benefits from subsurface scattering, which gives baby skin its warm, slightly translucent look.

New for 2026, the hands serve baby care product visuals, parenting content and family moments in animation. Medical and pediatric explainers use rigged hands to demonstrate grasp reflexes and early motor development. A tiny hand holding a finger is a universal image, and it works across cultures without words.
