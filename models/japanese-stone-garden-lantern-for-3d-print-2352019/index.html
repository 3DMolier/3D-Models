About This Model

A Japanese stone garden lantern with a wide roof cap and a lamp chamber, prepared for printing as well as rendering. The 7,256 polygons shape the curved roof, the window openings and the stepped pedestal that lifts the light. Its 7,682 vertices exceed the faces a little, typical of hard edges kept sharp for printed stone detail.

It was made to CheckMate specification, so geometry is clean, scale is correct and parts are clearly named. Non-overlapping UVs leave room for weathered granite, moss and lichen growing over the roof. Because the model was prepared for printing, it suits slicers and resin printers as a garden miniature.

Since 2025 it has decorated zen garden renders, tea house scenes and tabletop terrain for miniature gaming. Place it beside a koi pond with raked gravel around it, and the courtyard feels calm and Japanese. Hobby printers turn it into planter ornaments and model railway props for small Asian-style landscapes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2025 · Updated 13 November 2026
