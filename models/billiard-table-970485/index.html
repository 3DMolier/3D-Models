About This Model

Billiard Table is one of the Toys & Games models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $39. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags interior design visualization, advertising and architecture presentation as the intended applications. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualisation, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

At 92,512 polygons and 91,466 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. Texture work runs to 15 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
