About This Model

Boeing 747 Rigged Collection gives animators the jumbo jet with working rigs for gear, flaps and moving control surfaces. The raised upper deck behind the cockpit creates the famous hump, still the most recognizable shape in commercial aviation. Rigged gear bogies fold into the belly, letting takeoff shots end with a clean, smooth fuselage.

Each jet keeps controls and parts clearly named for quick animation, built to CheckMate specification. Buyers receive Editorial Uses Only rights, covering reporting, history programs and aviation education. Leading-edge slats and trailing flaps extend in stages, which helps explain how big jets fly slowly.

First offered in 2019, the rigged set fits documentaries, flight training explainers and news reconstructions of flights. Animating a slow climb-out at dusk captures the grace that made the type famous with travelers. Studios saving time on rig setup can move straight to camera work and lighting.
