About This Model

Priced at $149, the Brown Chicken is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 19,194 polygons and 26,765 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 29 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 922 x 1763. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.
