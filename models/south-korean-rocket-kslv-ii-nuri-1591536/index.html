About This Model

Nuri is South Korea's first entirely domestic orbital launcher, and it looks the part of a modern three-stage vehicle: a clean cylindrical body, a payload fairing at the top and a cluster of engines at the base of the first stage. The national markings run down the side, and the interstage joints are visible where the stages meet.

The model is built with subdivision geometry at 104,463 polygons and 120,465 vertices with non-overlapping UVs. Subdivision suits a launch vehicle: the body is one long smooth surface, and subdivision keeps it clean at any camera distance without carrying the cost up front.

Four other builds sit on this page, from a single stage rocket at $79 to an engine alone at $59. Aimed at space industry visualization, news and documentary graphics, education material, museum displays and games. On sale since 2020.
