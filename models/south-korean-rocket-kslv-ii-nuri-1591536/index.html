About This Model

The South Korean Rocket KSLV II Nuri is a production-ready Space & Sci-Fi 3D model, priced at $89 on TurboSquid. You are getting 104,463 polygons and 120,465 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
