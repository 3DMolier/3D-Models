About This Model

This Cereal Pile heaps small chocolate pillow-shaped pieces into a loose mound, ready for breakfast table shots. Its 17,280 polygons and 17,460 vertices are shared among the individual pieces, each with a crimped edge and a puffy center. Poured beside a bowl and a milk jug, the mound gives a morning table a casual, just-served look.

The non-overlapping UVs leave room for a toasted cocoa color, sugar dusting and crisp highlights on each piece. CheckMate Pro status means the pieces are clean, closed and scaled to real breakfast proportions. Because the pieces are separate, you can pull some out to drop them into a spoon or bowl.

Two $29 versions are offered: the chocolate pile and a variant named Pile of Breakfast Cereal Pads. It has been on the market since 2021 and goes into food photography, nutrition content and kitchen scenes. Scatter a few loose pieces across the tablecloth and the shot gains a playful, unposed quality.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2021 · Updated 25 November 2026
