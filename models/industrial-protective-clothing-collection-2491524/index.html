About This Model

This bundle gathers industrial protective clothing, from coveralls to safety gear, so factory and plant scenes can show properly equipped workers. Protective suits, helmets and high-visibility details tell the viewer a site follows strict safety rules. Garments can hang in a locker room, sit on display stands or dress your own character models.

Garments in the set follow CheckMate specification, keeping scale and naming consistent across the range. Bright orange, yellow and reflective strips read well under harsh industrial lighting in warehouses and plants. Folds and seams on the fabric help the clothing look real in close training and inspection shots.

You can buy two versions, the Industrial Protective Clothing Collection at $169 or a second set at $199. Listed in 2025, the bundle serves workplace safety training, chemical plant visualizations and PPE product renders. Combining both sets covers most protective outfits needed for a full factory floor and its control rooms.
