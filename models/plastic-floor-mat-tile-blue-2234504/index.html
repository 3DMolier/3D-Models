About This Model

Priced at $19, the Plastic Floor Mat Tile Blue is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 34,953 polygons and 34,118 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
