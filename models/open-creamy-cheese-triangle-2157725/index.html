About This Model

Open Creamy Cheese Triangle belongs to our Containers & Storage range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.

At 4,119 polygons and 2,101 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
