About This Model

A California sea lion in an idle animation, rigged in Maya, resting upright on its front flippers and looking around. The sleek brown body, small ear flaps and long whiskered snout match the playful animal seen along Pacific coasts. The idle loop adds breathing, head turns and slight shifts of weight, so the animal looks alive between actions.

Maya rig controls let animators move the flippers, arch the neck or blend into swimming and barking motions. The file complies with CheckMate specification, which keeps names tidy when several sea lions crowd a rock or pier. Wet, glossy skin reflects coastal sunlight and sky, giving the animal a convincing shine straight out of the water.

Released in 2025, the sea lion suits wildlife documentaries, zoo and aquarium visuals and marine education. Game developers populate harbors and beaches with such animals to add charm and motion to coastal levels. Lounging on a dock beside fishing boats, it gives a seaside town a lively, unmistakably Californian character.
