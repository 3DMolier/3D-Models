About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Airbus A350-800 Air France is a finished, render-ready model at $129. The mesh carries 310,974 polygons and 321,888 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 20 maps at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog.
