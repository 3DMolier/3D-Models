About This Model

A collection of drill presses, the column-mounted machines that bore clean, straight holes in metal and wood in any workshop. Each press shows the motor housing, the belt cover, the feed handles and the adjustable table that machinists recognize at a glance.

Several models in one bundle let you fill a school shop or a factory floor without every machine looking identical. CheckMate Lite means the files passed the basic technical check for formats and scale, a lighter review than the Pro level. Stand them beside a workbench with clamps and a vise, and the room instantly reads as a working machine shop.

In the catalog since 2021, the set serves industrial training, manufacturing visualization and garage or factory scenes in games. A close shot of a spinning chuck and curling metal chips is one of the most satisfying details in a workshop sequence. The bundle is priced at $89 for the full range of machines.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2021 · Updated 12 November 2026
