About This Model

Blueberry Lattice Pie With Plate models a homemade pie with a woven pastry lattice, bubbling purple filling and crimped edges on a ceramic plate. The 8,418 polygons and 7,727 vertices shape the pastry strips, the fluted crust and the rim of the dish.

It holds CheckMate Lite certification, which confirms clean geometry and sensible scene setup. No maps are included, but the non-overlapping unwrap suits golden baked pastry, glossy berry filling and glazed porcelain. The gaps in the lattice let filling show through, which is the visual signature of this pie.

Listed since 2022, the pie fits bakery displays, recipe illustrations, holiday tables and cozy kitchen scenes. Set it on a windowsill to cool with a checked cloth beneath, or slice a wedge on a dessert plate. A scoop of ice cream beside a slice adds a classic diner touch. The light mesh lets you fill a whole bakery counter without slowing down.
