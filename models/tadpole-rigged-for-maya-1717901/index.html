About This Model

A rigged tadpole with a round body and a long finned tail, built to swim through pond and biology animation scenes. The simple shape keeps attention on motion, and the tail is the part that carries the whole swimming cycle. A whole school can be duplicated freely, because each swimmer stays small in frame and simple in outline.

CheckMate Pro topology deforms smoothly along the tail when the rig bends it, with no pinching at the joints. The skin reads as wet and dark against sand or weeds, so lighting does most of the work in shots.

Three rigged versions are offered, one per package: Maya for the main file, then Cinema 4D and Modo. Picking the native rig avoids conversion, and controls appear where an animator in that program expects to find them. Placed beside eggs and a young frog, it completes a life cycle diagram in a few minutes. On sale since 2021, the larva serves biology lessons, amphibian explainers and pond scenes in children's animation.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
