About This Model

A textured bath mitt worn on the hand, this exfoliation glove scrubs dead skin away during a shower or a hammam visit. The 5,247 polygons shape the fitted mitten form, the thumb opening and the cuff around the wrist. At 22.77 x 14.9 x H8.97 cm it matches a real adult hand in its fitted pose.

Because no UV islands overlap, the coarse woven texture can run evenly across the palm and back of the glove. It conforms to CheckMate specification, with 5,320 vertices arranged into a clean and correctly scaled shell. Damp fibers look most convincing with a slightly darker, glossier material on the palm side.

Two versions live on this page: the Body Exfoliation Glove Fitted in brown and the Body Peeling Mitt On Hand, posed around a hand. Since 2025 the mitt has worked for spa visualization, skincare tutorials, bathroom shelf dressing and wellness illustrations. Drop it on a wet tile ledge beside a bar of soap, and the shower scene feels complete.
