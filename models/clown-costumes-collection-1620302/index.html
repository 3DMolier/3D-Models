About This Model

A collection of clown costumes, from baggy polka-dot suits to oversized collars and bright wigs, ready to dress performers at a circus or party. Big shoes and exaggerated proportions are part of the joke, and these outfits keep that playful silhouette. Having several costumes together means a whole troupe can appear without looking identical.

A CheckMate Pro inspection checked each outfit at real size, so the costumes fit standard human figures without guesswork. Saturated reds, yellows and blues make the clothing stand out against any background, even in a crowded scene. Ruffles, pompoms and patches are modeled into the shapes, so the costumes look soft and theatrical rather than flat.

Available since 2020 for $89, the set suits circus scenes, children's party visuals, Halloween content and comedic animation. Add a red nose and a rubber horn to one figure, and the troupe is ready for its entrance into the ring.
