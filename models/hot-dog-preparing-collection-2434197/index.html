About This Model

A collection for preparing hot dogs, with buns, sausages, condiments and serving pieces for a street food stand or kitchen counter. The items come as separate pieces, so you can show every stage from an empty split bun to a finished hot dog. Sausages have a glossy, rounded skin, and the soft bread has an uneven golden crust.

Each hot dog piece passes CheckMate checks, so it opens clean and ready for food shaders. Arranging the parts on a cutting board or a grill gives an instant cooking sequence without modeling anything new. Mustard and ketchup lines drawn along the sausages are the details viewers notice first in close food shots.

Introduced in 2025, the kit suits fast food menus, recipe videos, food truck visuals and restaurant interior renders. Game artists use pieces like these to stock a concession stand at a stadium or a fairground. A stack of buns beside a row of sauce bottles makes the counter feel busy at lunchtime.
