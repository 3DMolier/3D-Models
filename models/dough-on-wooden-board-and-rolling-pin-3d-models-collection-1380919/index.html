About This Model

A baking scene set with a ball of dough on a wooden board and a rolling pin, ready for a home kitchen or bakery counter. Several pieces come in one package, so you can arrange board, pin and dough in whatever composition the shot needs. Soft dough next to hard wood gives the scene a pleasing contrast of textures.

CheckMate Pro review means the collection meets TurboSquid standards for topology, naming and scale. A light dusting of flour across the board and pin makes the shot feel freshly worked. Dough looks best with slight subsurface scattering, which keeps it from reading as plastic.

Dough on Wooden Board and Rolling Pin 3D Models Collection dates to 2019 and suits cooking shows, bakery menus and recipe websites. Place it near a warm oven with a tray of fresh cookies to tell a complete baking story. Pizza restaurant interiors and pastry class visuals are other good homes for these kitchen pieces.
