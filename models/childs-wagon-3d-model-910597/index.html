About This Model

This child's wagon is the classic pull-along cart with a deep bed, sturdy wheels and a long handle for tugging. Its 16,744 polygons and 17,412 vertices shape the steel body, the axles, the rubber-tired wheels and the curved pull bar. Children use wagons to haul toys, siblings or garden leaves, so it fits countless backyard stories and neighborhood walks.

Texturing includes 5 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 for painted metal, rubber tires and handle grip. The unwrap keeps islands separate, so scratches, rust spots and stickers can be placed anywhere on the body. The wagon carries CheckMate Pro status, so topology, naming and scale were reviewed before publication on TurboSquid.

Part of the collection since 2015, it suits suburban garden scenes, toy catalogs and family game environments. Fill it with pumpkins or a teddy bear, and a seasonal family scene comes together immediately. Playground planners and preschool designers also include wagons in renders of outdoor play areas and gardens.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2015 · Updated 16 November 2026
