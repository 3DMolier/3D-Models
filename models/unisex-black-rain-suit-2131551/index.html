About This Model

Unisex Black Rain Suit is one of the Clothing & Accessories models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $69. The mesh carries 66,326 polygons and 33,351 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.
