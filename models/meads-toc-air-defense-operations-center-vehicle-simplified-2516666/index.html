About This Model

MEADS TOC Air Defense Operations Center Vehicle Simplified is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $119. You are getting 381,884 polygons and 394,208 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 275.4 x 835.37 x H370.07 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
