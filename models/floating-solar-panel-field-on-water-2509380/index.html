Priced at $59, the Floating Solar Panel Field on Water is a finished Space & Sci-Fi model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2026, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
