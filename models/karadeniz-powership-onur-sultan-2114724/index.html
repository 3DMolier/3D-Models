About This Model

A powership is a power station built on a hull, moored at a port and connected to a national grid by cable. That gives it a deck covered in generator halls, exhaust stacks and switchgear rather than in cargo gear, and it explains why the superstructure looks industrial rather than nautical.

The mesh runs 2,232,979 polygons and 2,494,063 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the generator housings, the stacks and the deck equipment.

An unladen tanker sits on this page at $149. The powership at $249 suits energy and infrastructure visualization, maritime industry presentation, developing markets and utility material, documentary graphics and games. Built to StemCell specification, on sale since 2023.
