About This Model

A blue exhaust extraction duct connected to a car's dual tailpipes, the hose system garages use to pull fumes out while engines run. The 608,872 polygons go into the ribbed flexible hose, the twin rubber nozzles and the tailpipes they clamp over. The corrugated hose bends in a natural sag, which keeps it from looking like a rigid pipe in the shot. Blue makes the hose stand out against dark workshop floors, which is also why many real systems are painted this way.

Workshop-true sizing follows the CheckMate specification, so the nozzles sit snugly over the pipes at the right size. With 543,493 vertices and no UV overlaps, the hose can take on dust, oil smears or a different color.

Issued in 2025, the setup serves auto repair shop visualization, emissions testing explainers and workshop safety training. Royalty-free licensing lets equipment suppliers and garages include it in client presentations.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2025 · Updated 18 November 2026
