About This Model

Tablets, capsules and coated pills of different shapes come together here, covering most of what a pharmacy or medical scene needs on screen. Round tablets carry a scored line across the face, while capsules split into differently colored halves meeting at a thin seam. Small objects like these are often shown in macro, so their soft bevels and rounded edges matter more than polygon thrift.

The Pharmaceutical Pills Collection follows the CheckMate specification, so scale, naming and closed shells meet TurboSquid inspection rules. Separate objects allow you to pour pills from a bottle with a physics simulation or line them up neatly on a white tray. Matte chalky tablets and glossy gel capsules need different shaders, and treating them separately is what makes a close shot convincing.

Released in 2025, the set is mostly picked for medical animation, pharmaceutical explainer videos and healthcare websites. It also works as background dressing on a nightstand, in a first aid kit or beside a glass of water.
