About This Model

Priced at $99, the Transit Bus Flxible New Look 1970 Simple Interior is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 144,078 polygons over 149,631 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

21 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic as the intended applications. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
