About This Model

Ant Pose 3 is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. Geometry weighs in at 38,869 polygons over 39,438 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
