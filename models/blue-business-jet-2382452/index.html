About This Model

The Business Jet is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $299 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 210,331 polygons and 216,746 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Questions About This Model

How heavy is the Business Jet mesh?

210,331 polygons and 216,746 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

What is the Business Jet model typically used for?

Stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

Can the Business Jet model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Can the Business Jet model be modified after purchase?

Yes, modification is covered by the license. Most buyers adjust materials or decimate the mesh for their engine; only redistribution of the source file is off-limits.
