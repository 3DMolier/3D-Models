About This Model

A laptop partly slipped into a leather sleeve, showing both the aluminum lid and the soft protective cover. The Apple Leather Sleeve with MacBook spends 51,113 polygons and 56,359 vertices on the laptop body and the sleeve seams. Having the computer half inside gives the scene a moment of action, as if someone is packing for a trip.

CheckMate certification keeps the laptop and sleeve as clean, separate objects with readable names. The contrast between brushed metal and grained leather is the main visual interest in close shots. It rests nicely on a bed, a cafe table or a desk next to a backpack.

It was released in 2026 at $79, and Editorial Uses Only terms cover the real Apple hardware shown. Tech journalism, lifestyle magazine illustrations, documentaries and films are its natural homes. Pairing it with a coffee mug and notebook gives a quick, believable workspace scene.
