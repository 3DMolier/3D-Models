About This Model

Character Wooden is one of the Toys & Games models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Geometry weighs in at 19,978 polygons over 10,272 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
