About This Model

A mobility aid for foot or ankle injuries: the patient rests a bent knee on a padded platform and steers with handlebars. The Blue Steerable Knee Scooter shows the folding steering column, a hand brake and small wheels that turn with the bars. Its blue frame reads clearly against hospital floors and home interiors alike.

StemCell certification provides PBR materials that keep the painted metal, rubber tires and padded knee rest consistent between formats. That makes the scooter equally usable in a game engine, an offline renderer or a product configurator.

Available since 2023, it fits rehabilitation explainers, medical supply catalogs and recovery scenes in films. Pair it with a patient in a walking boot and the story of a foot injury tells itself. In a clinic corridor or a pharmacy display, the scooter adds a realistic piece of orthopedic equipment. From the side, the low knee platform and tall column make an unmistakable silhouette for a quick read.
