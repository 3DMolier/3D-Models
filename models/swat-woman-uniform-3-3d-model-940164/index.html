About This Model

SWAT Woman Uniform 3 3D Model dresses a female tactical officer in body armor, helmet, gloves and boots for police scenes. The outfit shows load bearing pouches, knee pads and reinforced fabric panels that hold up in close shots.

TurboSquid gave the uniform its CheckMate Pro rating, which confirms clean topology and realistic size. Armor plates sit over a dark field uniform, and straps tighten at the shoulders and waist. The silhouette is tailored to a female figure, so the vest and belt sit correctly on the hips.

Listed since 2015, the uniform suits police dramas, tactical shooters and law enforcement training material. A breaching team scene gains diversity once a female officer stands among the squad. Uniform suppliers use it to show cut and fit for women's tactical gear. The helmet and goggles can be removed to reveal a character face in a cutscene.
