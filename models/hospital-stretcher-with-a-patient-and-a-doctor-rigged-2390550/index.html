About This Model

Hospital Stretcher with a Patient and a Doctor Rigged is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $349. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and medical visualization. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 54,764 polygons over 57,613 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
