About This Model

A wood auger has a screw point at the tip that pulls the bit into the timber, so the operator does not have to push. Behind it a spur scores the circle and a single cutting lip lifts the waste out along a deep flute - a completely different arrangement from a twist drill for metal.

The mesh is minimal at 3,982 polygons and 1,995 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry in the flute, the spur and the lead screw.

A second build sits on this page at the same $9. Both suit workshop and joinery visualization, tool retail and catalog renders, trades and DIY material, and games. On sale since 2023.
