About This Model

Royal Canin Pet Food Collection gathers bags of dry dog and cat food in the brand's familiar packaging, ready to stock a pet store aisle. Each bag stands upright with a flat base and a sealed top, the way the packs sit on a shelf. Package sizes vary across the set, which helps a shelf look naturally assorted rather than cloned.

The collection meets the CheckMate Lite bar, so every bag opens complete and clearly named. Label areas are flat on the fronts, which keeps printed artwork and pet photos crisp. The packs work as hero products on a counter or as filler across long shelves.

The collection has a second version too: this set costs $29 and the Royal Canin Pet Food Collection 2 costs $34. Since 2023 it has filled pet shop shelves, veterinary clinic waiting rooms and kitchen pantry scenes. Put a bowl beside a bag on the floor, and a feeding scene with a pet comes together.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
