About This Model

Mountain Walking Rock Climbing Winter Boots is one of the Sports & Recreation models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. The mesh carries 19,308 polygons and 20,192 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
