About This Model

A bumper car platform with a boy at the wheel, rigged in Cinema 4D for fun fair, amusement park and family scenes. Rounded cars with rubber bumpers and tall contact poles sit on a flat arena floor, exactly like a classic fairground ride. The boy's excited pose adds energy and gives the scene an obvious story.

Prepared with the CheckMate checklist, the ride, cars and child share consistent scale and organized naming. Colored bulbs and glossy paint come alive under evening fairground lighting with lots of small point lights. Sparks at the top of the poles can be added with simple glowing materials for extra charm.

Buyers can pick between two versions: the Bumper Cars Platform with Boy Rigged Collection for Cinema 4D, priced at $239, and a Maya edition. On sale since 2022 with a Royalty Free license, the set suits theme park designers and family entertainment centers. Event planners and animated series producers also use it for birthday party and carnival episodes.
