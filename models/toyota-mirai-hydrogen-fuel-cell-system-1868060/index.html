About This Model

A fuel cell drivetrain is the part of a hydrogen car nobody sees and everybody asks about: the stack itself, the high pressure tanks, the boost converter, the motor and the plumbing between them, laid out in the platform where an engine and fuel tank would otherwise sit. Showing that layout is the entire argument for the technology.

The mesh runs 833,412 polygons and 843,045 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the stack, the tank wrapping and the connector detail where a technical camera lands.

At $179 it suits automotive engineering and technology marketing, hydrogen and clean energy presentation, trade show and investor material, education and training, and games. On sale since 2022.
