About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Stickman With Tie is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The mesh carries 2,826 polygons and 1,417 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
