About This Model

Men's jeans in dark blue denim with a slim cut, a classic pocket layout and contrast stitching along the seams. Laid flat or folded, the trousers show the weight and texture of real denim fabric. Faded areas at the thighs and knees give them the worn-in look shoppers expect from premium jeans.

Quality is backed by CheckMate Pro, TurboSquid's tougher inspection for geometry, scale and naming. The jeans work as a store display item or as clothing on a shelf rather than a simulated garment on a body. Denim materials with a slight twill bump read convincingly even in close product shots.

Since 2023 the jeans have been offered for $29, a fair price for a hero wardrobe piece. It suits fashion e-commerce renders, clothing store interiors, wardrobe scenes, lifestyle visuals, denim lookbooks and character dressing in games. Stacked on a wooden table under warm spotlights, the jeans fit right into a boutique scene.
