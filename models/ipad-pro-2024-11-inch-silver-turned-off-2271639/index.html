About This Model

A silver tablet with its screen switched off gives a clean neutral device for desks, cafes and studio still lifes. The IPad Pro 2024 11 Inch Silver Turned Off keeps the thin aluminum body, flat edges and dark glass of the current generation. With the display dark, the glass reflects the room around it, which helps a render feel grounded in its environment.

The model was built to TurboSquid CheckMate specifications, so scale, naming and structure stay predictable on import. A camera module sits in the corner of the back panel, and its raised lenses catch highlights in close views. Rounded corners and narrow bezels make the tablet readable from a distance, even in wide office shots.

Released in 2024 at $39, it ships under an Editorial Uses Only license because the design belongs to Apple. It works for editorial tech articles, news illustrations, educational videos and interior scenes where a modern tablet lies on a table.
