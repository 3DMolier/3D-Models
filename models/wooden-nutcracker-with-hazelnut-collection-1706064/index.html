About This Model

The Wooden Nutcracker with Hazelnut Collection pairs wooden nutcrackers with hazelnuts, a small ready-made still life for kitchen and holiday scenes. Wood grain and the polished shells of the nuts carry most of the visual interest, so the set rewards close camera work. With several pieces at hand, a table looks naturally scattered rather than built from one repeated object.

The bundle has CheckMate Pro certification from TurboSquid, the inspection tier for clean topology and correct scale. Small round objects like hazelnuts are easy to scatter, so a bowl or a cutting board fills up in minutes. The nutcracker stays a separate object from the nuts, so it can lie on its side or stand beside a bowl.

This set has been on sale since 2021, among the earlier food props in our catalog. It suits Christmas and autumn visualization, recipe content, packaging mockups and cozy kitchen interiors in games and film. Food photographers working in 3D take it for holiday advertising where nuts and wood set a warm mood.
