About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Baby Girl Dark Skin Outdoor Autumn Style A-Pose is a finished, render-ready model at $179. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 1,007,895 polygons and 1,096,217 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
