About This Model

Trash Can Paper is one of the Containers & Storage models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. The mesh carries 762,309 polygons and 726,535 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
