About This Model

Royal Navy Camcopter S-100 with Rocket Pods Grey is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $89. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2026, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbour environments in games, and marine simulation.

The mesh carries 60,780 polygons and 65,317 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
