About This Model

This bundle gathers mobile phones from the touchscreen era, slim slabs with glass fronts, side buttons and camera lenses on the back. Grouped together, the handsets show how phone design converged on large screens and thin rounded bodies over a few short years. Each device can lie on a desk, sit in a hand or stand on a display stand in a shop window.

TurboSquid marked the bundle CheckMate Pro after reviewing geometry, real-world size and the naming of every phone in the scene. Blank screens accept any interface image, so you can show maps, messages or video calls without editing the mesh. Glossy glass and metal frames respond strongly to reflections, which makes softbox studio lighting the best choice for close renders.

First released in 2016, the phone set is priced at $119 and carries Royalty Free terms for commercial work. Tech reviews and app explainers place the handsets on tables, and films use them as props in everyday scenes. Interior designers and game artists also scatter them across offices, cafes and bedrooms to make spaces feel lived in.
