About This Model

Looking for a Tools asset? The Professional Straight Edge Scissors is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Geometry weighs in at 7,057 polygons over 7,023 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
