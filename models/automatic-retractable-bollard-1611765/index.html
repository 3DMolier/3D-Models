About This Model

A steel security bollard that rises from the pavement to block vehicles, built for plazas, embassies and parking entrance scenes. Its 3,404 polygons and 3,502 vertices describe the cylindrical post, the reflective band, the top cap and the ground flange. Lined up across a driveway, the posts read immediately as controlled access, even in a wide street shot.

Surfacing uses 7 maps at 2048 x 2048, covering brushed steel, reflective tape and the flush ground plate. Shells are unwrapped separately, so scuffs from bumpers or rust around the base can be painted in. StemCell delivery keeps these materials consistent across the supplied formats and renderers.

Four versions are priced identically at $19: this bollard, a Retractable Security Bollard and two Kavass models. The Kavass pair suits scenes needing a specific manufacturer look for anti-ram barriers. Available since 2020, the bollard supports urban security planning, architecture renders and city game environments.
