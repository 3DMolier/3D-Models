About This Model

This Vehicles model - Urban Tram Gray - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

The mesh carries 721,551 polygons and 744,717 vertices, which puts it in the mid-weight bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
