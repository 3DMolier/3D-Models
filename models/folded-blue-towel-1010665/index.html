About This Model

A neatly folded bath towel in soft blue, with terry loops on the surface and rounded edges where the fabric rolls over. The Folded Blue Towel adds the small domestic detail that makes a bathroom, spa or hotel room look prepared for a guest.

The pile texture breaks highlights into a soft fuzz, so the towel reads as cotton rather than plastic. A woven border near the hem gives the folded edge a crisp line when stacked with others. Light falling across the folds picks out the layered stack of fabric at the sides. The model carries CheckMate Pro certification, which stands for sealed geometry and orderly construction.

Placed on a bed corner, a sauna bench or beside a sink, the towel signals fresh linen at a glance. Stacked in a pile of three, it fills a shelf in a laundry room or a shop display. Priced at $29 and listed since 2016, the towel has 11 purchases from interior designers and hospitality visualizers.
