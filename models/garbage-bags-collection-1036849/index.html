About This Model

A pile of tied plastic trash bags slumped against each other, bulging with lumpy contents waiting for collection. Garbage Bags Collection gives each bag its own shape, so a heap of them looks like real waste rather than clones. Knotted tops stick up at odd angles, the detail that sells a bag as stuffed and tied by hand. Black plastic catches thin highlights along every fold, which reads well even in a dim alley.

TurboSquid checked the geometry and scale of every sack, giving the pile a CheckMate Pro grade. The plastic shine shows creases and wrinkles that shift with every light you place near them.

The heap is offered as a single set at $49, picked by 20 buyers since 2016. Alley scenes, strike-hit cities and cleanup campaigns use the bags most, along with game levels behind diners. Piled at the curb on collection day, the sacks give a neighborhood a lived-in look.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
