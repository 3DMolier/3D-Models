About This Model

A collection of manual pallet jacks for warehouses, loading docks and retail backrooms, ready to slide under any pallet in the scene. Each truck has the classic pair of long forks, a hydraulic pump unit and a tall steering handle. The low fork profile matters, because the jack must disappear under a pallet without clipping through the boards.

The set carries CheckMate Lite certification, confirming the files are complete and open correctly in common packages. Having several trucks in one purchase avoids the cloned look when a busy warehouse needs more than one in shot. The angled handle and small steering wheels under the pump show clearly how the jack is pushed and steered.

The collection is sold as one bundle at $69, with no separate versions on the page. Since 2015 it has gone to 39 buyers working on logistics visualization, factory games and supermarket stockroom scenes.
