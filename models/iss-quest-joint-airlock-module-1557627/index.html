About This Model

ISS Quest Joint Airlock Module is a detailed Space & Sci-Fi asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 171,630 polygons and 188,264 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 10 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
