About This Model

A tactical combat helmet fitted with a communications headset and protective glasses, ready for soldier and special forces characters. The Tactical Helmet with Headset and Glasses uses 48,395 polygons for the shell, side rails, ear cups and lenses.

Its 49,514 vertices keep the helmet dome smooth and the rail mounts sharply cut. CheckMate approval means the gear arrives clean, scaled to a human head and easy to attach to a character. The glasses rest on the brim, ready to drop over the eyes when a scene calls for action.

The 2026 helmet set costs $89 and suits shooter games, military films and airsoft illustrations. Equip it on a soldier during a briefing, or place it on a crate in a forward operating base. Tactical gear retailers can show how a headset and eyewear combine with a modern helmet. The ear cups sit over the helmet edges, the way real operators wear comms under combat gear.
