About This Model

A volumetric meteor trail stored as a VDB for Blender, giving a ready smoke and fire streak for sky and space shots. Instead of a polygon mesh, the effect is a voxel cloud that renders with real depth, light scattering and soft edges. The trail thickens near the head of the meteor and thins out into wisps toward the tail.

Because it is a volume, there are no UVs or textures to manage; density and emission drive the whole look. The asset meets CheckMate specification, so it loads at a sensible scale and carries clear object naming in Blender. Artists can tint the emission toward orange, green or white to suggest different burning materials entering the atmosphere.

Released in 2026, it fits science fiction films, disaster trailers, astronomy explainers and title sequences that need a falling star. Rotate it against a night sky or a planet limb and it reads as an entry streak without any simulation time.
