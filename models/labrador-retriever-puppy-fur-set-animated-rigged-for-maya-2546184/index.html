About This Model

A Labrador Retriever puppy with soft fur, floppy ears and oversized paws is set up in Maya with its own skeleton and motion cycles. This pup gives family scenes a lovable pet that holds up in close shots. The chunky body and round face capture the clumsy charm of a young retriever.

Fur grooming follows the body shape, lying flat on the back and fluffing around the chest and ears. The rig handles tail wags, head tilts and playful bounces, which carry most of a puppy's personality. Soft natural window light flatters the golden coat and keeps the dark eyes bright and lively.

Released in 2026 at $449, the puppy suits paid and educational productions. Pet food packaging renders, veterinary clinic explainers, family films and pet games are good fits. Set it on a rug next to a chewed toy and the scene feels like home instantly.
