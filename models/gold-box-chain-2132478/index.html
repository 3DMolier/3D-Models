About This Model

Box Chain belongs to our Clothing & Accessories range and is offered at $12 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 130,044 polygons and 65,028 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Questions About This Model

Can the Box Chain model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

How clean is the geometry on the Box Chain model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.

How much does the Box Chain 3D model cost?

The price is $12 USD. TurboSquid handles payment and delivery; the license is Royalty Free, so the cost does not repeat per use.
