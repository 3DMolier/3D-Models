About This Model

A collection of vehicle lifts for auto repair shops, tire centers and garage scenes where a car needs raising off the floor. Lifts like these combine sturdy posts, swing arms and lifting pads, so their mechanical structure reads clearly from any side. All pieces come at real proportions, ready to hold a standard sedan or SUV at a believable working height.

CheckMate Pro status from TurboSquid covers correct scale, tidy topology and logical object naming across the whole set of lifts. Polygon totals are not supplied, so test a lift with a car on it before filling a large service bay. Separate arms and platforms make it practical to animate a car rising, which is the classic workshop establishing shot.

A long-standing set from 2017 at $79, it suits dealership service renders, mechanic training and garage simulation games. Workshop planners lay out bays with it to check clearances between lifts, walls and rolling tool cabinets before construction. Car channels also stage repair videos with a lifted vehicle, showing the underside where most work happens.
