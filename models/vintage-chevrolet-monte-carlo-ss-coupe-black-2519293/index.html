About This Model

A black Chevrolet Monte Carlo SS coupe with a long hood and formal roofline, built for eighties street scenes and lowrider culture. The coupe carries 212,981 polygons and 220,594 vertices, giving its chrome trim, wheels and grille clean, readable edges.

At 203.17 x 532.18 x H199.36 cm it keeps true scale, and the StemCell build opens natively in each included format. UV layout is mixed, pairing unique islands on the body with shared space on repeated parts such as wheels. Black paint mirrors streetlights along the long flanks, which flatters the car in dusk and night renders.

Five versions sit here: this black coupe, a vintage worn variant and three rigged black coupes. One rig is plain, while two are matched with Maya and Cinema 4D. Pick the worn version for a barn find or junkyard story, while the rigged cars cruise a boulevard at night. It came out in 2026 under an Editorial Uses Only license, aimed at classic car journalism and documentary work.
