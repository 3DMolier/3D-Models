About This Model

Helicopter Cockpit Controls is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $89. The mesh carries 66,804 polygons and 72,483 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
