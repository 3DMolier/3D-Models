About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Helicopter Cockpit Controls is a finished, render-ready model at $89. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.
