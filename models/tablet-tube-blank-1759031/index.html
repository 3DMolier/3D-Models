About This Model

Tablet Tube Blank is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 5,632 polygons and 5,572 vertices. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
