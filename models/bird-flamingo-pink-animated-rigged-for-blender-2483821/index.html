About This Model

A pink flamingo set up for Blender, ready to wade, preen or stride through a lagoon shot without building a skeleton yourself. The long S-curved neck and stilt legs are the hardest parts to animate, and here they arrive already rigged and moving. Bird Flamingo Pink Animated Rigged for Blender keeps the backward-bending knee joint readable, which is the detail viewers notice first.

Its build follows the CheckMate specification, so naming, scale and file structure meet a checked baseline before purchase. Plumage reads as a soft coral gradient that deepens toward the wings, where black flight feathers show when the bird opens up. Because the rig lives natively in Blender, there is no conversion step between download and the first test render.

The flamingo went on sale in 2025, sells for $299 and carries a Royalty Free license. Wildlife documentaries, zoo walkthroughs, tropical resort scenes and children's animation are where a single wading bird earns its place in frame.

Questions About This Model

Can the Bird Flamingo Pink Animated Rigged for Blender model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

What is the Bird Flamingo Pink Animated Rigged for Blender model typically used for?

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Bird Flamingo Pink Animated Rigged for Blender mesh?

How clean is the geometry on the Bird Flamingo Pink Animated Rigged for Blender model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.
