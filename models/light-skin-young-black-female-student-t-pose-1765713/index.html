About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Light Skin Young Female Student is a finished, render-ready model at $159. The mesh carries 42,958 polygons and 77,476 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
