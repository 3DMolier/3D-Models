About This Model

A baby boy sitting in a soft folding play pool filled with colorful plastic balls, a familiar sight in nurseries and play corners. The 53,058 polygons cover the child, the padded pool wall and a heap of spheres reaching up to his waist. Vertices come to 62,849, more than the faces because each ball and the child carry their own seams.

Being a StemCell product, the scene opens in several renderers with matching materials, set up by TurboSquid rather than by hand. Every UV island has clear space, so the pool fabric can take a printed pattern while the balls stay glossy plastic. Ball colors are shader values, which makes a pastel or a bright primary palette equally easy.

Dating to 2024 and priced at $179, the scene suits toy stores and baby product makers showing their goods in use. Parenting blogs and daycare websites use it to illustrate sensory play, and family films place it in a living room. Educational apps about early childhood development also show the pool as a safe play area.
