About This Model

A molded cardboard tray with round pockets for each drink, the carrier coffee shops hand over with takeaway orders. Its 30,148 polygons and 15,312 vertices capture the pressed fiber walls, the central handle and the cup wells. Empty or loaded with drinks, it instantly tells the story of a coffee run for the whole office team.

No texture maps are provided, so the gray recycled pulp look comes from a simple rough shader. A clean UV layout leaves room for a stamped recycling symbol or a coffee stain on the edge. CheckMate Pro scrutiny confirmed the tray's geometry and realistic dimensions for standard cups.

Cardboard Four Cup Holder arrived on TurboSquid in 2023 for cafe counters, delivery scenes and office kitchens. Fill the pockets with lidded coffees and place it on a car seat to show a morning pickup. Sustainability graphics about compostable packaging also use the tray as a clear everyday example.
