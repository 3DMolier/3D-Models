About This Model

The BRIT Award Statue Matte is a production-ready Signage & Decor 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 759 polygons and 768 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
