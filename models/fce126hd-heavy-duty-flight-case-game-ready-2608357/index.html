About This Model

A heavy road case with aluminum edges, corner caps and latches, the workhorse of touring bands and film crews. Only 9,815 polygons describe the box, so the game-ready mesh drops into real-time scenes without strain. The boxy shape stacks neatly, which suits towers of gear in a crowded loading bay behind the stage.

StemCell delivery keeps the metal trim and laminate looking identical across file types. Mapping is incomplete, and the 10,898 vertices keep the hardware clean enough for stencil decals. Stencil a crew name on the lid to give the case a story.

Costing $39 since its 2026 debut, the case fits backstage areas, concert loading docks and warehouse game levels. It measures 109.79 x 81.53 x H86.4 cm, big enough for amplifiers or lighting gear, with recessed handles and riveted edges visible in dark corners. Stack a few beside a truck ramp and the scene reads as a show packing out after the encore.
