About This Model

A simplified heavy rescue fire engine, made for traffic scenes, city emergencies and distant fire station views. The 431,378 polygons keep the cab, lockers and roof racks readable while staying lighter than a full hero build. Its boxy rescue body and tall cab give an instantly recognizable emergency silhouette on any street.

Under CheckMate review the engine passed with clean scale, and its 453,869 vertices are grouped for easy editing. UVs cover parts of the mesh only, so livery and reflective chevrons are easiest to add as procedural decals. The lighter mesh makes several engines practical in one large disaster scene.

Available for $149 since 2026, the engine suits city simulations, emergency planning visuals and fire service games. Use it for mid-distance shots where the vehicle crosses a junction with sirens on and traffic pulling aside to let it pass. Place a few of them outside a burning building to fill the street with responders.
