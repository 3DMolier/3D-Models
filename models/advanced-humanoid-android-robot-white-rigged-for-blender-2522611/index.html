About This Model

This advanced humanoid android has a sleek white shell, slender limbs and a smooth faceplate, built for near-future robotics stories. Its human proportions let it stand in labs, homes or factories where it reads as a helper rather than a weapon. Lit with soft studio light, the glossy panels and dark joint gaps give the robot a clean, premium look.

TurboSquid's CheckMate specification governed the build, so geometry stays tidy and every part carries a clear label. Panel lines, status lights and the faceplate glow are material choices you can tune for friendly or eerie moods. Separated limbs and panels make it simple to reveal internal parts or swap colors for a different product line.

This android costs $169, and a walking animated version rigged for Blender is available at $219. New in 2026, the robot suits technology explainers, sci-fi shorts, robotics startup pitches and games about artificial intelligence. Standing quietly in an empty white corridor, the android raises questions about what it means to be human.
