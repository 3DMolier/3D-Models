About This Model

A child dummy is not a scaled adult, and that distinction cost lives to learn. Proportions, mass distribution and neck strength all differ, and child restraint standards exist because the tests were eventually run with the right instrument rather than a smaller version of the wrong one.

The model holds 31,797 polygons and 32,778 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 5 maps all at 4096x4096, and comes rigged for posing or animation. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a T pose build is offered separately.

At $119 it suits automotive safety visualization, crash testing presentation, engineering and training material, child restraint marketing and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.
