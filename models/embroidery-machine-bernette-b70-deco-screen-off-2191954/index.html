About This Model

Machine Screen Off is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. You are getting 27,788 polygons and 14,575 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
