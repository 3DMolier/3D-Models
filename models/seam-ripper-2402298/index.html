About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Seam Ripper is a finished, render-ready model at $11. You are getting 1,066 polygons and 1,069 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 15.27 x 2.1 x H2.1 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
