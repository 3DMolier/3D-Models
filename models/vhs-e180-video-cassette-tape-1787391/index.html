About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The VHS E180 Video Cassette Tape is a finished, render-ready model at $29. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

Counted at the source, the model is 44,225 polygons and 43,302 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
