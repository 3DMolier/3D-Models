About This Model

A print-ready replica of Cristo de la Concordia, the open-armed Christ statue that stands above Cochabamba in Bolivia. The figure keeps the long robe folds, the outstretched arms and the calm face that make the monument recognizable from far away.

Because it is prepared for printing, the shape is a single solid meant to slice cleanly rather than a hollow render shell. The outstretched arms are the fragile part on any print of this statue, so plan supports under them and print at a sensible scale. Geometry is prepared to CheckMate standards, so the mesh opens in a slicer without the usual repair pass.

Available since 2024, the statue suits collectors, souvenir makers, church decor and tabletop dioramas of South American landmarks. Painted in stone gray or bronze, a small print makes a fitting gift for anyone who has visited Cochabamba. It can also stand in a render as a distant landmark on a hilltop skyline. The file costs $89 and is ready to load into your slicer.
