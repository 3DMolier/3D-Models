About This Model

A garage scissor lift with a sedan parked on its runways, a ready workshop scene for service and repair visuals. The lift's crossed arms and low platform are the recognizable shapes, and the car sitting on top proves the scale. Having vehicle and equipment in one file saves time aligning tires with the ramp edges.

Everything meets CheckMate guidelines for naming and real-world size, so the lift merges into a larger workshop without fixes. A raised position shows the underbody, which is where most technician training and inspection shots actually happen. Bare concrete and yellow safety paint around the base suit the lift better than a glossy showroom floor.

Released in 2022, the model fits auto repair explainers, tire shop interiors, mechanic training and garage simulation games. Light it with overhead fluorescent strips, and the workshop mood comes across even in a wide establishing frame. Service centers also use scenes like this to show customers what happens to a car behind the counter.
