About This Model

A print-ready version of the hot chocolate glass with whipped cream, prepared for resin or filament printers rather than rendering. On a printer the transparent glass becomes an opaque shell, so the cream swirl and footed stem carry the whole shape. Painted by hand after printing, the brown drink band and white topping make a convincing miniature for dioramas.

The file follows CheckMate specification rules, which on TurboSquid means clean geometry, real scale and sensible naming. Scaling it down for dollhouse kitchens or tabletop cafe scenes is a common way to use the print. Larger prints work as display props for cafe counters, where a durable fake drink outlasts any real one.

The print file has been available since 2024 and pairs with the render versions of the same glass in the range. Food photography studios also print such pieces as stand-ins, lighting the set before the real drink is poured.
