About This Model

Barbie Doll in Summer Dress is one of the Toys & Games models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $79. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.

Counted at the source, the model is 86,028 polygons and 56,944 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
