About This Model

Barbie Doll in Summer Dress is one of the Toys & Games models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $79. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are product advertising, fashion visualization and game assets. The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Counted at the source, the model is 86,028 polygons and 56,944 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
