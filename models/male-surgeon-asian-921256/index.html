About This Model

Male Surgeon Asian belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $69 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 15,208 polygons and 15,449 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
