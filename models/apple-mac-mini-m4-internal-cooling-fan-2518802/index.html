About This Model

Apple Mac Mini M4 Internal Cooling Fan isolates the small blower fan inside the computer, the part that pulls air through the case. Its 38,287 polygons and 39,054 vertices describe the curved blades, the housing and the mounting tabs that hold it in place. At 10.78 x 11.54 x H2.7 cm the fan is thin and compact, matching the tight space inside the case.

StemCell packaging supplies the fan in many formats, all sharing the same PBR plastic and metal. As an Apple component, the fan is sold strictly for Editorial Uses Only. Curved blades are the detail viewers notice in a teardown, so close shots should favor them.

This part joined our electronics in 2026 as one of several internal computer components. Repair technicians, tech reviewers and engineering courses use it to explain airflow and thermal design. Exploded views and teardown animations place it beside the logic board to show how the machine stays cool.
