About This Model

The Biathlete Fully Equipped Rigged is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $249 on TurboSquid. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 193,714 polygons over 210,142 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
