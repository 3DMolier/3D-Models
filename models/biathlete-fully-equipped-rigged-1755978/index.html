About This Model

Biathlete Fully Equipped is a detailed Characters & People asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $249. You are getting 193,714 polygons and 210,142 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
