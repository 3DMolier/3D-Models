About This Model

Priced at $129, the NYPD ASPCA Mobile Command Center is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 618,129 polygons and 319,932 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
