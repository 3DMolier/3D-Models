About This Model

The Ticket Dispenser Deli Red is a production-ready general 3D model, priced at $24 on TurboSquid. You are getting 16,394 polygons and 8,613 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
