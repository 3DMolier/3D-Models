About This Model

A skier stepping uphill on skis in an animated Maya scene, made for winter resort films, mountain safety guides and snow games. The walking cycle shows the slow, deliberate stride people use when climbing a gentle slope without a lift or skins. Poles plant beside each step, and the body leans forward in the way real skiers do to keep balance on snow.

The animation is set up natively in Maya with a rig, so timing can be adjusted or the cycle looped along a path. A padded jacket, ski pants, helmet and goggles give the figure the full outfit expected on a modern piste. Naming and real world scale in the character follow the CheckMate guidelines, keeping the Maya scene tidy and predictable.

New in 2026, the skier fits ski school tutorials, resort visualizations and winter levels where background figures need to move. Bright sunshine on fresh snow and a line of tracks behind the figure make the climb feel cold and real.
