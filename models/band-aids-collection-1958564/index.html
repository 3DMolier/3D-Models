About This Model

Adhesive bandages of different sizes are grouped here, useful for first aid scenes, pharmacy shelves and medical explainer animations. The strips show the padded center and the vented tape ends, the details that make a plaster read correctly at small sizes. Several shapes appear side by side, from long finger strips to wider patches for knees and elbows.

Skin-tone and printed finishes can be set in the materials, letting one strip look plain while another carries a cartoon print. Wrappers and unpeeled strips help when you need packaging in shot alongside the bandage already applied to a character. A CheckMate Pro inspection by TurboSquid confirmed tidy geometry and sensible scale for every item in the group.

From 2022, the strips have appeared in health app icons, school nurse scenes and pharmacy commercials for small wounds. Animators place one on a character knee after a fall, which tells a small story without any dialogue. Medical educators also use these models in slides on wound care, hygiene and the contents of a home kit.
