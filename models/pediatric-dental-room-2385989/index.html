About This Model

Looking for a Medical asset? The Pediatric Dental Room is a finished, render-ready model at $199. You are getting 263,398 polygons and 268,006 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
