About This Model

The SpaceX Dragon capsule with its trunk section, the reusable spacecraft that carries cargo and crews to the space station. Its 113,442 polygons describe the blunt heat shield, the side hatch, the Draco thruster ports and the solar trunk below. With 122,268 vertices the capsule is ready for close docking sequences against the station ports.

The surface uses 10 texture maps at 4096 x 4096, carrying scorch marks, panel lines and lettering. StemCell certification keeps these maps behaving identically across the major renderers the format supports. Its UV layout is non-overlapping, so new mission markings can be painted without disturbing existing detail.

Released in 2020, the capsule serves commercial spaceflight explainers, launch coverage graphics and orbital game missions. A slow approach toward a docking port, with Earth turning below, is the signature shot for this spacecraft. Splashdown scenes also work, with the scorched capsule bobbing in the ocean under its parachutes.
