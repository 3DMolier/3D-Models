About This Model

A floor-mounted ballet barre with a wooden rail on metal stands, the support dancers use for warmups and stretching. The Floor Mounted Ballet Barre uses 6,968 polygons and 3,820 vertices, keeping the round rail and the sturdy feet clean. Its freestanding design fits rehearsal studios, gyms and home training rooms without drilling into walls.

Light ash wood suits the rail, while the stands take powder-coated white or black metal. The UV islands remain separate without overlaps, so wood grain flows evenly along the rail. TurboSquid confirmed it with CheckMate Lite approval, so the mesh is sound and opens correctly.

Dance studios, fitness centers and ballet schools have used this barre since 2022. Place it along a mirrored wall with dancers in a row, or set it in a living room for home practice. Dance teachers also film barres like this when demonstrating positions, pliés and stretches for online classes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2022 · Updated 20 November 2026

How clean is the geometry on the Floor Mounted Ballet Barre model?
