About This Model

LED Strip Bar is a flexible tape of surface-mounted light chips on a thin printed circuit, the kind stuck under kitchen cabinets. It uses 10,240 polygons and 11,297 vertices of clean quads, with each tiny chip and resistor shaped on the strip. Solder pads and cut marks between segments are visible too, which helps in close product shots or installation guides.

Six maps at 4096 x 4096 cover the copper traces, the white chip housings and the adhesive backing. Each piece has an independent area in the UV layout, letting the chips glow through an emissive map. CheckMate Lite certification confirms a clean, importable file that bends along a curve with a simple deformer.

The page carries two versions at $29 each, both under the LED Strip Bar title with the same strip construction. Bought 16 times since 2019, it lands in kitchen lighting renders, gaming desk setups and electronics tutorials. Bend it behind a TV or along a shelf edge, and the glow sells a modern interior instantly.
