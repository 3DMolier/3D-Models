About This Model

Pearls are knotted between each bead, and the reason is failure planning: if the string breaks, one pearl is lost rather than all of them. That detail is how a real strand is told from a cheap one at a glance.

The model carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $24 it suits jewellery and product visualization, e-commerce and catalog imagery, character accessories, advertising, film costume and editorial illustration. On sale since 2015.
