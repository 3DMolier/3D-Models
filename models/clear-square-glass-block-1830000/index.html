About This Model

Looking for a Architecture Landmarks asset? The Clear Square Glass Block is a finished, render-ready model at $19. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

The mesh carries 1,584 polygons and 1,588 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
