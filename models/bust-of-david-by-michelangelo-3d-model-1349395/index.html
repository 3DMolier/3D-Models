About This Model

Bust of David by Michelangelo is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. You are getting 28,244 polygons and 27,284 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2018, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
