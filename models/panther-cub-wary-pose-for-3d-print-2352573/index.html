About This Model

A panther cub in a wary crouch, ears forward and body low, as if it has just noticed something in the grass. The tense pose gives the figurine more drama than a sleeping cub, suited to a shelf that tells a little story. Its stance is built to rest stably on a printer bed, with paws and belly close to the ground.

Clean geometry made under CheckMate specification lets slicing software process the cub with minimal preparation. Sculpted fur direction and the slight arch of the back keep the form readable even at small print sizes.

Released in 2025, the wary cub fits wildlife collectors, nature centers, tabletop scenery and gift shops. Painted in deep black with a faint rosette pattern, it hints at the leopard markings panthers carry under the dark coat. Placed opposite its lying sibling, it sets up a playful scene of one cub resting while the other keeps watch.
