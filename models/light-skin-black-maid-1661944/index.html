About This Model

The Light Skin Maid is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 16,563 polygons and 17,534 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
