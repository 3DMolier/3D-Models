About This Model

Potato Planter is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. Counted at the source, the model is 375,636 polygons and 384,203 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
