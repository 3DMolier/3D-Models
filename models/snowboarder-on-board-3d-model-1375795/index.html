About This Model

The Snowboarder on Board is a production-ready Sports & Recreation 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. You are getting 142,259 polygons and 147,326 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
