About This Model

Priced at $79, the Tick Stuck in Skin is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 14,646 polygons and 14,752 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.
