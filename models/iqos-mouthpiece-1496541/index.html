About This Model

Iqos Mouthpiece is a detailed general asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Geometry weighs in at 915 polygons over 897 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
