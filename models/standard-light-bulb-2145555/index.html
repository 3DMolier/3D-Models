About This Model

Standard Light Bulb belongs to our Lighting range and is offered at $9 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 7,538 polygons and 7,569 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, event visualization and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits interior and architectural visualization, product rendering and game environment dressing.
