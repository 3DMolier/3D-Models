About This Model

A stacked metal bunk bed with a side ladder, built for barracks, dormitories, hostels and camp cabins. It uses 96,300 polygons and 96,228 vertices on the tubular frame, mattresses, bedding and ladder rungs. The upper berth has a guard rail, which keeps the silhouette accurate for military and institutional rooms.

CheckMate certification gives clean geometry and sensible naming for frame, mattress and bedding parts. Tucked blankets and flat pillows suggest a strict, made-up bed, the way barracks inspections expect. Rows of these beds along a wall turn an empty room into sleeping quarters quickly.

Two versions are offered: the main army edition at $34 and the Prison Bunk Bed with Ladder at $29. Both joined the range in 2026 and carry Royalty Free licensing for games, films and visualization. Military bases, jail cells, summer camps and shelters in survival games all call for beds like these.
