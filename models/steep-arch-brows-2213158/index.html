About This Model

Steep Arch Brows belongs to our Medical range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is catalogued for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 106,317 polygons over 108,532 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
