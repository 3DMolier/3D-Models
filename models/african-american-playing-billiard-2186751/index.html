About This Model

An African American man leaning over a pool table to line up a shot, cue in hand and eyes on the cue ball. The 295,693 polygons cover the figure's clothing and hands, the table with its cushions and pockets, and the balls on the felt.

A CheckMate Pro review confirmed scale and topology, so the player's height matches the table rail exactly. With 390,881 vertices the scene suits close framing, where the bridge hand and cue tip are in sharp focus. The concentration in the pose gives the image narrative, which a table without people never provides.

Released in 2024 at $159, the figure suits bar interiors, leisure campaigns and lifestyle illustrations. Hang a low lamp above the table and leave the room dark, and the shot becomes a classic pool hall moment. Hospitality designers also place players like this in renders to make a games room feel occupied. Billiard rooms in hotels and private clubs are natural homes, as are sports bar scenes for broadcasters.
