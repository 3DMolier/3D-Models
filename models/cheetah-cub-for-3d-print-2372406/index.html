About This Model

The Cheetah Cub for 3D Print is a solid printable figurine of a young cheetah with fluffy mantle fur and oversized paws. The model is prepared as a closed surface, so it can go straight to a slicer for resin or filament printing. Painted and placed on a shelf, the cub makes a charming decor piece or a gift.

CheckMate compliance means the figure has clean geometry and sensible proportions for small-scale printing. Fine details like the tear marks on the face and the spotted coat print well at modest heights. A matte primer coat helps reveal those surface details before you start painting.

Three versions are available at $119 each, all printable cubs in different poses. One shows the cub in a running pose, while another presents it lying down peacefully. Released in 2025, the cubs suit home decor, educational models, tabletop dioramas and wildlife-themed gifts.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
