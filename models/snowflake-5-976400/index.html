About This Model

Snowflake 5 is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. You are getting 35,434 polygons and 35,436 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 4 textures, authored at 2048 x 2048. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
