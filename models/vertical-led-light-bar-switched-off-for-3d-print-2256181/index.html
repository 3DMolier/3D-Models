About This Model

A slim vertical LED light bar shown in its off state, prepared so it can also be sent to a 3D printer. The 19,252 polygons and 9,632 vertices form the long diffuser, the end caps and the mount that holds it upright. Because the light is off, the diffuser reads as frosted plastic rather than a glowing strip.

No textures are included, and a milky white diffuser shader is enough for both renders and print previews. UVs do not overlap, so an emissive map can be painted later if the bar needs to switch on. The mesh is built to CheckMate specification, TurboSquid's standard for clean geometry and true scale. For printing, the closed parts give a slicer solid shells to work with.

Available since 2024, the bar fits gaming room setups, studio backdrops and modern interior lighting plans. Stand a pair of them behind a desk monitor and the scene gains the familiar look of a streamer's room.
