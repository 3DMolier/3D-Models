About This Model

Hand Puppet Characters Collection gathers soft glove puppets with expressive faces for children's theater and storytelling scenes. Each puppet has a hollow body that fits over a hand, with the head and arms shaped to move with the fingers. Bold features such as big eyes and wide mouths make the characters readable from the back of a small stage.

Separate characters can be placed on a puppet theater, scattered in a playroom or held by a performer's hand. Puppets in the set share consistent scale and naming under CheckMate rules. Fabric textures like felt or fleece suit the soft rounded shapes of the bodies.

Released in 2024, the puppets suit kindergarten scenes, toy store displays and animated stories for kids. Teachers use them in language lessons, and game designers bring them into playful puzzle levels. Therapists who work with children can illustrate role play sessions with the friendly characters.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
