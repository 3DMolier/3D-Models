About This Model

A green Vision 60 tactical robot dog moves in this animated Blender set, built for military patrol and security scenes. The olive finish, rugged legs and sensor-laden head give the quadruped a battlefield look distinct from lab prototypes. Its low, stable body lets it navigate rough ground where wheeled vehicles would struggle.

This set adheres to CheckMate specification, keeping joints and panels neatly named for animation work. Blender rigging allows patrol walks, sudden halts and scanning head turns for tense scenes. Overcast daylight on the green panels blends the robot into wooded or desert terrain convincingly.

Released in 2026, the tactical dog is royalty free for defense documentaries, war games and science fiction films. Military trainers can show how quadrupeds scout ahead of infantry or inspect suspicious objects from a distance. Framed beside a soldier in a forest clearing, the robot instantly signals near-future warfare.
