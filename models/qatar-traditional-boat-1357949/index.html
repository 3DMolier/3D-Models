About This Model

A dhow is built shell first, with the planking laid before the frames go in, and that method leaves its mark: a hull with a graceful sheer, a high raked stem and a wide transom. The single lateen sail on a forward raking mast completes a shape that has worked the Gulf for centuries.

The mesh runs 134,564 polygons and 145,441 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 8 texture maps at 4096x4096 carrying the timber grain, the caulking lines and the weathering along the waterline.

Three other builds sit on this page, from a sail alone at $69 to alternative hulls at $99 and $149. Aimed at Gulf heritage and tourism material, maritime history visualisation, cultural and museum presentation, film and television, and games. On sale since 2019.
