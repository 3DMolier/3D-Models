About This Model

Arabian Terrorist Character A-Pose is an armed militant figure in a headscarf and combat clothing, posed neutrally for rigging. The figure measures 125.92 x 43.08 x H185.69 cm, standard adult height with arms held out in the A-pose. Most of the 50,554 polygons go into the clothing layers, scarf folds and tactical vest with its pouches.

Vertices reach 51,795, a touch above faces, a balanced count for a game-ready character in shooters and simulators. Some UVs overlap, sharing texture space across mirrored parts of the outfit such as sleeves and boots. CheckMate rules guided the build, and the neutral stance makes skinning straightforward.

Released in 2025, the character suits counterterrorism training simulations, security exercises, war films and tactical shooter games. Placed in a compound or village set, it gives security drills a recognizable threat profile for trainees. Writers can keep scenes grounded by pairing the figure with civilians and security forces in context.
