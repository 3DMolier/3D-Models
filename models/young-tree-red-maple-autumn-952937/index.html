About This Model

Priced at $29, the Young Tree Red Maple Autumn is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets as the intended applications. The listing dates back to 2015, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

At 197,681 polygons and 249,311 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
