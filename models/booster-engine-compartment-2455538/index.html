About This Model

Booster Engine Compartment is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. First published in 2026, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualisation, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

Geometry weighs in at 20,519 polygons over 21,309 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
