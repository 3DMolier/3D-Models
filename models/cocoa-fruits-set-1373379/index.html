About This Model

Cocoa Fruits Set is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Counted at the source, the model is 3,408 polygons and 3,414 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 2048 x 2048. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.
