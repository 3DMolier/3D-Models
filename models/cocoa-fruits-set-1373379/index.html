About This Model

A small harvest of cocoa pods in several stages of ripeness, from unripe green through yellow to deep orange-brown. The Cocoa Fruits Set needs only 3,408 polygons and 3,414 vertices, all quads, for the ridged pods and their stubby stalks. Lined up side by side, the pods show how the fruit changes color on the trunk as harvest approaches.

Six maps at 2048 x 2048 carry the furrowed skin, color gradients and small dark blemishes on each pod. Non-overlapping UVs make it simple to cut one pod open and paint the pale pulp and beans inside. With CheckMate Pro certification the pods keep sensible names and real size, so they fit a farmer's basket.

Offered since 2019, the set serves chocolate makers, agricultural publishers and food stylists telling the bean-to-bar story. Fair trade explainers use it to show where chocolate starts, long before roasting and grinding at a factory. Pile the pods on a woven mat beside a machete for a plantation harvest scene in dappled shade.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2019 · Updated 17 November 2026
