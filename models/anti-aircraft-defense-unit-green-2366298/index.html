About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Anti Aircraft Defense Unit Green is a finished, render-ready model at $129. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is catalogued for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 637,480 polygons over 691,994 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
