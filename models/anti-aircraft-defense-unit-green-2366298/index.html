About This Model

A green armored anti-aircraft vehicle with radar and launchers raised, set up to defend a column or airfield. The Anti Aircraft Defense Unit Green is a heavy build of 637,480 polygons and 691,994 vertices across hull, turret and sensors. The rotating radar dish and the cluster of launch tubes are what tell a viewer this vehicle hunts aircraft.

StemCell certification keeps the green paint and metal consistent from real-time engines to offline renders. Overlapping UVs spread one texture across matching wheels and road gear, a sensible saving at this density. The turret and radar are separate parts, so they can be animated tracking a target across the sky.

Released in 2025, the unit suits defense simulation, war documentaries and strategy game unit rosters. Parked at the edge of an airfield at dusk, it frames a tense waiting scene before an air raid. Training material for vehicle recognition can show it from several angles with the radar raised and lowered.
