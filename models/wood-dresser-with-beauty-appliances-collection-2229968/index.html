About This Model

A wooden dresser set out with beauty appliances, bundled as a ready vanity corner for bedrooms, salons and lifestyle renders. Hair dryers, straighteners and similar tools tell the viewer about a morning routine without a single character in frame. A dresser with drawers and a flat top gives the appliances a natural home instead of floating props.

Each piece sits at true size with sensible topology, made in the CheckMate manner but not certified. Warm wood against glossy plastic and chrome creates contrast that photographs well under soft window light. Swapping the wood finish from oak to white paint shifts the room from rustic to modern.

On offer since 2024, it suits interior visualization, beauty product imagery, apartment staging and lifestyle content. Its royalty free license allows commercial use, including e-commerce scenes for hair care brands. Add a mirror and a chair and the corner is ready for a morning-routine render, and trailing cables make it feel used rather than staged.
