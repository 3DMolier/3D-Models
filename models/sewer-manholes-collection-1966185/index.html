About This Model

A collection of cast iron manhole covers and frames for streets, sidewalks and parking lots, each with its own grip pattern. Round patterned lids sit next to square drain grates, giving road surfaces the small details that make them feel real. The covers lie almost flat, so they matter most in low camera angles and top-down city views.

Every piece is certified CheckMate Pro and imports as a separate object with consistent scale. That lets you drop several lids along a road and vary them so the pattern never repeats in a long shot. Their low profile also makes them cheap to place in large numbers.

The page offers the Sewer Manholes Collection and the Sewer Manholes Collection 2, a second set with more cover designs. Available since 2022, the sets help city builders, road visualization projects and game environments where the ground is seen up close. A wet asphalt shader around the rims makes them read as heavy cast metal.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
