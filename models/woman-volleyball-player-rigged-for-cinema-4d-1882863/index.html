About This Model

The Woman Volleyball Player Rigged is a fit athlete in a tank top, shorts and knee pads, prepared for spiking and diving. Long legs and a sporty ponytail make her read as a volleyball player on sand or on an indoor court. Placed at the net with teammates, she turns an empty gym into a tense match in full progress.

As a CheckMate Lite model, the file has passed basic inspection for scale and integrity. Team jerseys and numbers can be repainted to match a club, a school or a national squad. Bright beach sun or even arena lighting both flatter the athletic figure and the sports kit.

Three versions sell for $199 apiece: this rigged athlete, a Maya edition and a Modo edition. The skeleton allows jumps, digs and serves, so rallies can be animated without rebuilding joints. Launched in 2022, she fits sports broadcasts, beach holiday scenes and fitness app graphics.
