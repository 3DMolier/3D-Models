About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Exit Emergency Doors is a finished, render-ready model at $19. The mesh carries 15,356 polygons and 7,724 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
