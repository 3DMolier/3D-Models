About This Model

A lion cub sitting upright on its haunches, made as a printable figurine that stands steadily on a flat base. Big paws, a rounded muzzle and oversized ears give the cub the youthful look that separates it from an adult lion. The upright pose suits a shelf or windowsill, where it looks back at the room like a curious young animal.

Made under CheckMate specification, the mesh is clean and solid, so slicing software handles it with little preparation. Fine surface texture suggests downy fur and faint spots, sculpted directly into the form so a printer can reproduce it.

Released in 2025, the figure fits nursery decor, wildlife gift shops, school biology kits and collectible animal sets. Paired with its lying sibling, it makes a small pride for a savanna diorama or a playful bookend set. Rendered in full color, the cub also works as an illustration of big cat babies for children's books.
