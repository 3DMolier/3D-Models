About This Model

This bundle gathers several gym machines into one purchase, so a fitness interior can be furnished without hunting down separate stations. Frames of this kind combine steel tubing, padded seats and cable guides, which gives a camera plenty of hard edges to catch light. A row of these stations along a mirrored wall is often all a commercial gym shot needs in the background.

The set passed TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro inspection, the stricter tier of the marketplace's technical review for clean geometry and file structure. Upholstered pads and metal uprights read as distinct materials, so a lighting artist can separate them when the gym needs a new color scheme.

Three versions share this page: the main Workout Machines Collection costs $79, Collection 2 asks $89 and Collection 3 tops the range at $119. Each numbered set brings a different group of machines, so buying two of them fills a larger hotel gym or a sports club floor. The first set went on sale in 2023 and still suits fitness app renders, health club walkthroughs and sports game levels.
