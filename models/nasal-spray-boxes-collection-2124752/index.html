About This Model

Nasal Spray Boxes Collection is a group of pharmacy cartons sized for spray bottles, ready for decongestant and allergy packaging presentations. The boxes share an upright, slim proportion that mirrors the bottle inside, with tuck flaps at top and bottom. Having several cartons in one file lets a designer compare front panel layouts or show a full product range at once.

Each carton follows the CheckMate specification, so the set matches real pharmacy scale and keeps object names clear. Clean flat panels take printed artwork well, while the edges are softened just enough to catch a highlight in close renders.

On offer since 2023, the boxes suit pharmaceutical branding, pharmacy shelf renders, online drugstore pages and health explainer videos. Lined up with a matching spray bottle in front, a carton completes the classic over-the-counter product shot. Stacked behind a counter, the set also fills pharmacy shelves in hospital and retail interior scenes. Seasonal allergy campaigns often need several variants at once, which is exactly what a collection like this provides.
