About This Model

A marble-style angel figure with folded wings and flowing robes, prepared as a solid model for 3D printing and garden ornaments. The wings sweep back from the shoulders, and the drapery falls in deep folds that hold shadow well on a printed surface. Its calm, downward gaze gives it the quiet character of memorial and church sculpture rather than a playful cherub.

Its CheckMate specification build means the shape is clean enough for slicers to process without extra cleanup. Printed in white resin and lightly sanded, the figure takes on the soft sheen of polished stone. Larger prints in filament can be primed and painted with a marble glaze for an indoor shrine or a garden niche.

Released in 2024, Marble Statue of Angel for 3D Print has reached makers of decor, gifts and tabletop scenery. The Royalty Free license lets you sell the printed figures, which matters for small craft shops. Set it on a stone plinth or among candles to create a quiet memorial corner.
