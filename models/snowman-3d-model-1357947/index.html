About This Model

Snowman belongs to our Other range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2019, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 3,226 polygons and 3,202 vertices, a light load for a scene. Texture work runs to 4 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
