About This Model

Single Juggling Ball is one of the Toys & Games models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $4. Geometry weighs in at 8,008 polygons over 9,826 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
