About This Model

Priced at $169, the ISS Japanese Experiment Module Kibo is a finished Space & Sci-Fi model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 463,366 polygons and 501,989 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

20 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for science-fiction film and TV VFX, space simulation, game environments and VR scenes.
