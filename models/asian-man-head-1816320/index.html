About This Model

This Medical model - Asian Man Head - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $59 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.
