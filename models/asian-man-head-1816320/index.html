About This Model

A realistic head of an Asian man with natural proportions, ready to start characters, portraits and avatars. The Asian Man Head uses just 4,627 polygons and 5,135 vertices, which keeps it fast in real-time engines and easy to sculpt further. The face carries a relaxed, neutral expression, a good base for adding emotions with blend shapes.

Non-overlapping UVs allow skin detail, stubble and subtle color changes to be painted without seams. This head is graded CheckMate Pro, which means inspected topology, tidy naming and complete shading. Soft frontal light shows the cheek and brow structure, while side light brings out the jawline.

Released in 2021, it helps studios build casts that reflect a wider range of faces. Game developers and virtual production teams use it as a character base, and barbers preview haircuts on it. Medical and e-learning projects also rely on it for facial anatomy lessons and patient simulations.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2021 · Updated 14 November 2026
