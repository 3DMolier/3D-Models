About This Model

Cartoon Man in Cavalry Jacket is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 82,571 polygons and 81,005 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 13 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
