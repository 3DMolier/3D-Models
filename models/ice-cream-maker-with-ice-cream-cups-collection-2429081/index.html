About This Model

An ice cream maker paired with a set of filled cups, a ready kit for dessert recipes, kitchen scenes and cafe menus. The machine has a clear bowl and a churning paddle, and the cups hold soft scoops with rounded tops. Together they show both the process and the finished dessert in a single frame. Sprinkles or a wafer stick on top of each cup are easy additions for a more playful look.

Built under CheckMate rules, the dessert kit has every piece as a separate object you can place or remove. Pastel colors on the scoops and a glossy finish on the machine make the set look fresh and inviting.

Launched in 2025, it suits kitchen appliance catalogs, dessert recipe videos and cafe interior renders. Arranged on a counter with spoons and toppings, the set builds a cheerful homemade dessert scene with little effort. Summer promotions for appliance stores also use the machine as a hero shot with a scoop melting beside it.
