About This Model

A modular aluminum access ramp with a landing platform, handrails on both sides and a non-slip deck surface. The 161,570 polygons go into the grip pattern on the deck, the rail posts and the bolted joints between sections. Its modular sections show how an entrance can be made accessible without permanent construction.

Its 163,430 vertices are mapped cleanly, and 5 maps at 4096 x 4096 render brushed aluminum and textured decking. CheckMate Pro conformance keeps each section separate, so the ramp can be extended or reshaped for a site. Matte silver metal reads as clean and modern against brick or painted facades.

Two versions are available at $29 each: this modular set and a simpler single-run Metal Ramp for low steps. Placed at a porch or clinic door, it shows inclusive design in a single object. Since 2019 it has supported accessibility guides, architectural plans, healthcare facility renders and public building visuals.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2019 · Updated 14 November 2026
