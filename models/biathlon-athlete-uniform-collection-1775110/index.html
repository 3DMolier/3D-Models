About This Model

Biathlon Athlete Uniform Collection dresses winter sports scenes in the tight racing suits worn for skiing combined with rifle shooting. A biathlon suit is cut close to the body for speed, with stretch panels along the arms and legs. The collection keeps that slim silhouette, so athletes look fast even when standing at the range.

Bib numbers and team colors are the cues viewers recognize first, which suits broadcast-style graphics well. A CheckMate Pro rating means the garments went through a strict inspection of scale, naming and geometry. Clean fabric surfaces make it simple to swap national colors or add your own patterns.

The uniforms have been on the market since 2021 and work in sports explainers and winter competition games. Game studios fit them onto skier characters, while sports broadcasters animate race recaps and shooting range replays with them. Educational material about Olympic disciplines and museum displays of skiing history are other places the outfits suit.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
