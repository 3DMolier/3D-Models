About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Surveillance Radar is a finished, render-ready model at $79. Geometry weighs in at 122,519 polygons over 142,461 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.
