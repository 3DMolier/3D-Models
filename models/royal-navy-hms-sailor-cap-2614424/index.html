About This Model

A white-topped Royal Navy sailor cap with a black band and HMS tally ribbon, the classic headwear of a British rating. The Royal Navy HMS Sailor Cap uses 10,900 polygons to shape the round crown, stiff band and tally ribbon.

There are 10,912 vertices, and the crown keeps its smooth circular outline when seen from above. Built to CheckMate rules, the cap opens clean and at a real head size, ready to place on a character. The ribbon around the band carries the ship name, the detail that separates a naval cap from a civilian hat.

For $29, this 2026 cap suits naval costumes, historical films and maritime museum displays. Fit it to a sailor model on deck, or place it on a locker shelf beside a folded uniform. Paired with a jumper and bell bottoms, it completes a full Royal Navy rating outfit. Commemorative events and remembrance animations can also use it to honor sailors of past wars.
