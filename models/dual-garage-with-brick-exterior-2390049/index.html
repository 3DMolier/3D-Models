About This Model

Priced at $59, the Dual Garage with Brick Exterior is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 10,089 polygons and 9,952 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
