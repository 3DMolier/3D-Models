About This Model

These whole peanuts in their shells show the familiar waisted pods with a netted surface, ready to scatter across a bar counter. The set uses 1,793 polygons and 1,762 vertices, keeping each pod light enough for large handfuls. Their uneven lengths and slight bends make a pile look natural rather than copied.

The shells get 5 texture maps sized 4096 x 4096, showing ridged fibers and dusty tan color. Non-overlapping UVs let the netting pattern stay crisp and believable right up to a macro lens on a tabletop. Inspectors granted it CheckMate Pro status for clean topology, sensible naming and a scene that opens ready to render.

Offered from 2019, the peanuts suit baseball stadium scenes, snack illustrations, bar counters and nutrition infographics for schools. Tip them out of a paper bag beside a beer glass, and a ballpark mood appears instantly. Farm and agriculture explainers also use them to show the crop after harvest and drying.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
