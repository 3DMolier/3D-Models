About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Wireless Mobile Game Controller is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The mesh carries 24,522 polygons and 12,674 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
