About This Model

Looking for a Model Bundles & Sets asset? The Medical Equipment Collection is a finished, render-ready model at $259. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags game assets, film & TV VFX and medical visualization as the intended applications. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
