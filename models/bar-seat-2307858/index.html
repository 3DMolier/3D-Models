About This Model

The Bar Seat is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $15 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 644 polygons over 697 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.

Questions About This Model

Can the Bar Seat model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What is the Bar Seat model typically used for?

It is cataloged for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

What does StemCell mean for the Bar Seat model?

It means one source asset, several delivered formats, and a PBR material setup designed to read consistently across them - no rebuilding shaders after import.
