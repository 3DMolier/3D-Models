About This Model

A simple bar seat with a round top and slender legs, the stool found at kitchen islands, cafe counters and home bars. The Bar Seat is extremely light at 644 polygons and 697 vertices, so rows of seats can line a long counter with ease. Its clean silhouette suits modern and minimal interiors, from lofts to compact apartments.

Wood, painted metal or leather upholstery all suit the seat, and colors can change to match any interior palette. Non-overlapping UV islands keep wood grain or fabric patterns aligned across the top. As a StemCell model, it keeps consistent materials across supported render engines, which saves time when a scene moves between tools.

Since 2024 the seat has appeared in kitchen designs, restaurant layouts and real estate renders. Line several along a breakfast bar, or tuck them beneath a high cafe table by the window. Game environments also use light seats like this to fill bars and taverns without slowing performance.

Questions About This Model

Can the Bar Seat model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What is the Bar Seat model typically used for?

Stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

What does StemCell mean for the Bar Seat model?

StemCell is TurboSquid's multi-format standard. The model was authored once and delivered in several formats with matching PBR materials, so shading survives the move between a game engine and an offline renderer.
