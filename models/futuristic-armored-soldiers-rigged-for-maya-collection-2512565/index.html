About This Model

Science fiction infantry work needs a squad rather than a hero. Several armoured figures with variation between them are what make a formation look like a unit instead of a cloned line, and every one here arrives rigged and prepared for Maya so posing and animation start immediately.

Building and rigging armoured characters is slow work, because armour plates have to move over a deforming body without intersecting it. Delivered solved, the set removes the longest part of that job.

At $295 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits games, science fiction film and television, cinematic previsualization, concept work and illustration. On sale since 2026.
