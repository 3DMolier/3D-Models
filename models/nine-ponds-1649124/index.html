About This Model

Nine Ponds is a detailed Architecture Landmarks asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $79. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2021 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 85,759 polygons and 92,046 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
