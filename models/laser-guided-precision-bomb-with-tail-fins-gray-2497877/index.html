About This Model

This build shows the weapon with its tail fins deployed, which is the configuration seen in flight rather than on a pylon. The fins are the interesting part: they fold flat for carriage and snap out after release, and modeling them extended means the bomb reads as a weapon in use rather than as stores hanging under a wing.

The mesh is unusually heavy for a munition at 529,081 polygons and 547,436 vertices in quads and tris, and it arrives rigged so the fins and the seeker can be posed. The UVs overlap, which is the sensible economy on a body with four identical fins.

At $69 and built to StemCell specification, it suits defence visualization, news and documentary graphics, military training material, aviation renders and games. Real-world scale is kept. On sale since 2026.
