Douglas DC-3 American belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 71,318 polygons and 77,976 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 16 maps at 8192 x 8192 and 2048 x 2048. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.
