About This Model

Search and Rescue Chopper belongs to our Aircraft range and is offered at $169 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 228,704 polygons over 239,798 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization and flight simulation as the intended applications. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
