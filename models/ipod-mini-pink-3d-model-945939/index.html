About This Model

The iPod Mini is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. You are getting 7,396 polygons and 7,950 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 9 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2015 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
