About This Model

Priced at $79, the Australian Female Hand Rigged is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

The mesh carries 2,958 polygons and 2,715 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 11 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
