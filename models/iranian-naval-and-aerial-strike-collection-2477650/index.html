About This Model

Analysis and news work rarely needs one platform in isolation. A strike package involves aircraft, missiles, launch vehicles and naval units acting together, and a graphic that explains it needs all of them at a consistent scale and finish - which is exactly what a collection provides and single purchases do not.

Supplying them together also keeps the modeling standard consistent, so a wide graphic showing several systems does not have one unit that visibly came from somewhere else.

At $579 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits defence and geopolitical visualization, news and documentary graphics, strategic analysis material, training presentation and games. On sale since 2026.
