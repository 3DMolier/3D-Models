About This Model

A collection of satellite dish antennas, the parabolic reflectors that sprout from rooftops, balconies and broadcast stations. Each dish keeps the curved bowl, the feed arm and the mounting bracket that make the shape recognizable at any distance. Mounted high and angled toward the sky, they cast the oval shadows that make a facade feel inhabited.

Sharing one consistent build, the antennas can be mixed across a housing block to give it a lived-in, slightly cluttered skyline. CheckMate Pro certification applies to every model in the set, so scale, topology and naming match from dish to dish. Brackets attach cleanly to walls and roofs, which saves time when you dress a whole street of apartments.

Available since 2024, the collection works for urban architecture renders, telecom presentations and city levels in games. A cluster of dishes on an old apartment block says more about a neighborhood than most hand-placed props. The full set costs $89, less than buying each antenna as a separate model.
