About This Model

A collection of industrial tow tractors, the compact vehicles that pull cart trains through factories, warehouses and airports. Low bodies, tow hitches and small solid tires are the features that set these tuggers apart from forklifts. Several models in one set let a logistics scene show a fleet rather than a single repeated vehicle.

CheckMate Pro certification keeps each tractor at true scale beside pallets, carts, racks and workers on foot. Bright safety paint and rubber bumpers stand out under warehouse lighting, just as they do in real plants. Tractors and hitches can be positioned freely, so trains of carts can be assembled for any route.

Introduced in 2022, the collection suits intralogistics presentations, factory simulation games and operator safety training. Airport ground scenes can use them to haul baggage carts between parked aircraft and terminal gates. Lean manufacturing consultants also show tuggers like these when explaining milk-run delivery systems on the shop floor.
