About This Model

An uncapped black permanent marker with an ultra-fine tip, the slim pen used for labeling, sketching and signing. Its 5,565 polygons detail the narrow nib, the grip barrel and the pocket clip molded onto the separate cap. The 5,371 vertices sit slightly below the faces, a reasonable ratio for a turned barrel built from tidy rings.

UV islands stay distinct, so printed branding, ink smudges or a worn barrel finish can be painted in place. TurboSquid certified the pen at CheckMate Pro level, verifying topology, scale and naming before it entered the store. With the cap lying beside it, the exposed tip reads clearly even when the marker is small in frame.

Dating back to 2022, it fits office desks, classroom scenes, packaging labels and stationery product renders for stores. Lay it on a notebook beside a half-drawn sketch to suggest work in progress at a designer's desk. Teachers' tables, warehouse labeling stations and crime-board scenes also gain a believable detail with this pen.
