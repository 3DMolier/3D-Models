About This Model

Lego Bricks Pieces belongs to our Toys & Games range and is offered at $24 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 230,679 polygons and 231,998 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

2 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 and 8192 x 8192 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2018 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
