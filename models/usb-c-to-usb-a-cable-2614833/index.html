About This Model

A USB-C to USB-A charging cable with molded connectors at both ends, a small detail that makes desks and gadgets feel real. The USB C to USB A Cable uses 6,030 polygons to shape the plugs, strain reliefs and the flexible cord.

There are 6,194 vertices, enough for the oval USB-C tip and the flat rectangular USB-A housing. StemCell certification supplies the cable in several native formats built around one tested master scene. The cord loops loosely between connectors, ready to lie across a desk or dangle from a charger.

Available from 2026 for $19, the cable suits tech product scenes, workspace renders and electronics tutorials. Plug it into a laptop beside a phone, or coil it on a nightstand next to a power brick. Accessory stores can show which connector fits which device without needing photographs of every cable. Light geometry means you can scatter several cables across an office without noticeable cost.
