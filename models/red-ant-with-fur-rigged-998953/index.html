About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Red Ant with Fur Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $129. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags visualization, advertising and 3D printing as the intended applications. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

At 39,345 polygons and 39,744 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It ships 13 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
