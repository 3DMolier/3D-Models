About This Model

A set of delicate floral lace masquerade masks, ready for carnival balls, costume shops and mystery party scenes. The 55,752 polygons trace the openwork lace pattern, so light passes through the gaps naturally. Eye openings follow a soft cat-eye curve, the shape most people link with masked balls.

The CheckMate build keeps 59,010 vertices organized, with each mask as its own object. Metallic or black lace shaders work well on the cut-out geometry despite mixed UVs. Real geometry instead of an opacity map keeps the lace crisp from every angle.

New in 2026 at $49, the set suits Venetian carnival scenes, fashion renders and theatrical props for opera stages. Together they span 47.54 x 21.34 x H11.39 cm, a group of face-sized masks to lay on a velvet tray in a costume boutique. Worn with candles and long gowns nearby, the lace casts intricate shadows on a face and adds intrigue to portrait lighting.
