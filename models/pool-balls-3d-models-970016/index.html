About This Model

Looking for a Toys & Games asset? The Pool Balls is a finished, render-ready model at $19. The mesh carries 6,387 polygons and 6,417 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

16 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
