About This Model

A collection of golden Chinese dragons set up for Maya, with scaled serpent bodies, flowing manes and antler-like horns. These creatures have no wings, so their grace comes from the long coiling spine and the ripple of the mane. Animators working in Maya can open the files natively and go straight to blocking a flight path.

The collection is assembled to CheckMate specification, which keeps names, pivots and scale aligned across the whole group. Metallic gold catches every light in the scene, so a dark sky with lanterns gives the strongest contrast. Feature and game studios with Maya pipelines are the natural buyers for this edition.

Available from 2026, the dragons suit fantasy cinematics, Asian-themed game levels, temple environments and holiday commercials. A dragon gliding above a pagoda roof or through clouds is the shot most teams build with this kind of creature. Having several matching dragons means a parade or a sky battle can be staged without modeling extra beasts.
