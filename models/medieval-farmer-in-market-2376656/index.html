About This Model

Medieval Farmer in Market belongs to our Characters & People range and is offered at $229 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 261,453 polygons and 261,662 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
