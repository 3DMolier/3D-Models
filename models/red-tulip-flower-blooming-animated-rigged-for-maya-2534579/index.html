About This Model

A single red tulip prepared for Maya, shaped to show the cupped bloom opening above a tall green stem. The petals curve inward at the tips, giving the flower the classic goblet shape gardeners know.

The model is built to the CheckMate specification, so names, structure and import behavior are clean. Saturated red petals against long blade-like leaves give the plant a bold silhouette in any spring scene. Because it is a single stem, it can be multiplied into a whole bed or kept as a lone hero flower.

New in 2026, it serves floral visualization, spring greeting renders and garden scenes in animation. Close shots show the glossy petal surface, while wide shots turn rows of tulips into fields of color. Florists and garden centers also use such flowers in seasonal web banners and newsletters. A vase on a kitchen table with one tulip makes a calm still life that suits lifestyle content.
