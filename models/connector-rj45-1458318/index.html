About This Model

Priced at $19, the Connector RJ45 is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 4,550 polygons and 4,620 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 2048 x 2048. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
