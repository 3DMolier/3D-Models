About This Model

Samurai of Sengoku Period in Fighting Stance Red is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $69. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
