About This Model

Looking for a Weapons asset? The Medieval Military Hammer is a finished, render-ready model at $12. Counted at the source, the model is 11,277 polygons and 11,464 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

16 texture maps come with it, at 12000 x 12000 and 12000 x 8000 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
