About This Model

A tray of assorted Heinz mayonnaise style dips in squeeze bottles, made for restaurant stockrooms and grocery shelf scenes. The 93,838 polygons cover bottle caps, curved bodies and the cardboard tray holding the row together in place. The bottles stand packed tightly in the tray, the way they arrive from a wholesale supplier each morning.

Built to CheckMate rules, the set holds 92,400 vertices with bottles and tray separated for restocking scenes. Partial UVs mean label tweaks are easiest through projected decals on the bottle fronts and caps. Licensing is Editorial Uses Only, so the set fits news, education and documentary use.

At $49 since its 2026 arrival, the tray suits food industry reporting, kitchen training and retail documentary scenes. It measures 43.09 x 29.29 x H18.18 cm, a realistic wholesale case. Set it on a stainless prep table beside a fryer to show a busy kitchen ready for service on a busy Friday night.
