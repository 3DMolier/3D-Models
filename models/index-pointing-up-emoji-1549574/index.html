About This Model

Index Pointing Up Emoji is one of the models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $19. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

Geometry weighs in at 559 polygons over 548 vertices - a light build for its class. Maps: 5 of them, peaking at 1024x1024, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
