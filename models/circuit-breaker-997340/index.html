About This Model

A modular circuit breaker with a toggle lever, rating label and screw terminals is the safety switch found in every electrical panel. The Circuit Breaker uses 53,363 polygons and 55,451 vertices for the housing ribs, terminal screws and DIN rail clip. The toggle can be shown up or down, illustrating on and off states clearly.

Its 11 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 deliver molded plastic, metal terminals and printed markings. Labels have their own UV space, so you can change ratings or wording for different regions. Earning CheckMate Pro tells you the breaker was reviewed for real-world scale and clean topology.

Offered since 2016, the breaker suits electrical training, building services visualizations and product catalogs. Electricians use similar models to explain how panels are wired and how breakers protect circuits. Mounted in a row inside an open distribution board, it creates a convincing technical close-up.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
