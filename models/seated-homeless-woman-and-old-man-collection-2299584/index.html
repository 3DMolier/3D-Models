About This Model

A homeless woman and an old man seated on the ground, wrapped in worn layers, gathered as one set for street scenes. The Seated Homeless Woman and Old Man Collection adds people to a city that is not polished or staged. Slumped postures and patched clothing carry the story, so the figures work even at a distance.

Both figures were produced to CheckMate specification and sit at true human scale against walls and doorways. Their seated pose keeps contact with the ground, which avoids the floating look common in stock figures. Textures can be dirtied or cleaned depending on how harsh the scene needs to feel.

Available since 2024, the collection fits urban drama films, social documentaries and realistic city games. Charity campaigns and journalism use figures like these to talk about homelessness without photographing real people. Placed under a bridge or beside a station entrance, they make an urban street feel honest.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
