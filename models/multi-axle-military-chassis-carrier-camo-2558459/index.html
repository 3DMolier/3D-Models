About This Model

A long multi-axle military carrier chassis in green camouflage, built to haul heavy launchers, radar units or containers across rough terrain. It is a substantial model of 454,474 polygons, concentrated on the armored cab, the many wheel hubs, the suspension and the frame rails. The bare rear deck shows how such heavy platforms are built before a payload is mounted.

Its 504,158 vertices are unwrapped in a mixed fashion that reuses space for repeated wheels while keeping unique panels separate. As a StemCell model, it is delivered in several native formats generated from one source, with camouflage materials that stay consistent. The rows of large tires give the carrier its imposing ground presence, especially from a low camera angle.

New for 2026 at a $149 price, the chassis serves defense presentations, military simulations, documentaries and strategy games that need heavy logistics vehicles. Engineers and analysts can use it to show how missile systems or radars are transported between positions. Because the deck is empty, game teams can mount their own fictional weapons or equipment.
