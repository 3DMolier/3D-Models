About This Model

Priced at $149, the Luxury Car is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 481,251 polygons and 527,637 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
