About This Model

This Wooden Dollhouse is an open-backed toy home with several rooms, a pitched roof and painted window frames for a child's bedroom. Its 23,640 polygons and 23,392 vertices are spent on the floor slabs, stair openings and the cutout windows. Set on a playroom rug with a few toys scattered around, it gives the scene a warm and lived-in feel.

Five maps at 4096 x 4096 show the plywood grain, the printed wallpaper and the colored roof panels. StemCell delivery means those PBR materials are prepared once and carried into each format without manual rebuilding.

Two versions share the same name and the same $29 price, giving you two takes on the toy. Since 2020 the house has gone into nursery renders, toy store displays and family animations. Its open back lets the camera look straight into the rooms, which helps when staging tiny furniture. The roof slopes evenly on both sides, so the silhouette reads as a house even at small sizes.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2020 · Updated 14 November 2026
