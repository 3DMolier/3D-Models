About This Model

Amanita Mushrooms Set is one of the Nature & Plants models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. The mesh carries 2,957 polygons and 2,948 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

6 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
