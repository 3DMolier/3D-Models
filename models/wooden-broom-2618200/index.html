About This Model

A traditional wooden broom with a long handle and bundled bristles, a humble tool for barns, cottages and witch stories. The Wooden Broom spends 472,088 polygons on individual straw strands, so the head looks full and slightly frayed.

Its 485,228 vertices allow each bristle to bend on its own rather than forming a solid block. CheckMate certification confirms clean geometry and a handle length that matches a real broom. Cord binding wraps the bristles tightly at the top, the detail that marks a handmade broom.

The broom became available in 2026 at $29 and suits rustic interiors, farm scenes and Halloween settings. Lean it against a barn door beside a pile of hay, or send a witch flying across a full moon on it. For crowded scenes, consider a lighter broom, since this version is built for close inspection. Folk museums and craft studios can show traditional broom making through close views of the binding.
