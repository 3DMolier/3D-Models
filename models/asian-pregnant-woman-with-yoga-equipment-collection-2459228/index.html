About This Model

An Asian pregnant woman shown with yoga mats, blocks and other props, a collection built around gentle prenatal exercise. The set pairs a calm expectant mother with the equipment she uses, so a full class corner can be staged quickly.

Bought together, the figure and props share scale and style, which keeps a wellness scene from feeling pieced together. Each item is made under CheckMate specification rules, giving clean naming and true dimensions in any scene. A bolster or ball nearby suggests the stretches without needing an animated instructor in the shot.

Since its 2025 release, the collection has suited maternity clinic visuals, yoga studio websites, pregnancy apps and health education. Pastel mats and soft daylight frame the figure in a mood that speaks of care and calm rather than athletics. Representation of Asian mothers in prenatal material is limited, so this set fills a real gap for health providers. Placed by a window with plants behind her, she anchors a peaceful home workout scene.
