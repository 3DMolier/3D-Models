About This Model

Tall-necked brass and copper pitchers with curved spouts and ornate handles fill this collection of traditional Arabic ewers. Engraved bands and bulbous bellies give each vessel a different profile, so a market stall or palace table can hold several without repetition. Lamps, rugs and lanterns from the same cultural palette pair naturally with the pitchers in wide shots.

Construction conforms to CheckMate specification, which keeps shells closed, transforms clean and naming consistent across the set. Hammered metal surfaces benefit from a slightly varied roughness map, and the separate ewers let each take its own patina.

A second collection at $59 extends the original $44 set, so the page carries two versions with further pitcher designs. Released in 2025, the ewers are licensed Royalty Free and suit Middle Eastern film sets, historical games and coffee ceremony illustrations. Placed beside small cups on a low brass tray, one ewer sets up a hospitality scene in seconds. Museum educators also use such vessels to illustrate metalworking traditions from across the region.
