About This Model

This cartoon cat and dog pair brings two friendly characters with big heads and expressive eyes, set up for Cinema 4D. Rounded shapes and simple color blocks give them the clean look of children's animation and mascot work. The cat and dog contrast nicely in silhouette, one slim and curious, the other sturdy and very playful.

Both characters were prepared to the CheckMate specification, keeping file names tidy and proportions consistent. Soft fur-like shading or flat toon materials both suit them, depending on the style of the project. Large eyes and open mouths leave room for strong expressions in posed stills and story frames.

New in 2026, the pair suits children's videos, pet shop mascots and casual mobile games. Pose them side by side on a sofa or chasing a ball, and a short story writes itself. Royalty Free terms cover commercial mascots, apps and educational cartoons made for young viewers.
