About This Model

Star Wars Universe Collection gathers ships, characters and props inspired by the famous space saga in one bundle. Each item is a separate model, so vehicles and figures can be combined into new space scenes and battle layouts. Arranged on a hangar floor or a desert planet, the pieces evoke that galaxy at first glance.

CheckMate specification keeps the bundle tidy, with clear names and consistent scale across items. Weathered panels, scorch marks and worn metal suit the used-universe look the saga is known for across its films. Hard sci-fi lighting with rim highlights brings out the panel lines on every hull.

The saga set has two versions: this collection at $389 and a second universe collection at $449. Combining both collections widens the cast and fleet for bigger fan scenes, dioramas and animated shorts. Released in 2025, the bundle suits fan films, sci-fi dioramas, game mods and collectible displays.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026

Yes. It is sold under TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, which covers commercial use in games, film, advertising and visualization without per-use fees.

How much does the Star Wars Universe Collection 3D model cost?

How clean is the geometry on the Star Wars Universe Collection model?

The mesh uses organized, quad-dominant topology at real-world scale, with objects named and grouped rather than merged into one block.

Can the Star Wars Universe Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.
