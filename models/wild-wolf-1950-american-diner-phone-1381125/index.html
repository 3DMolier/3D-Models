Priced at $39, the Wild Wolf 1950 American Diner Phone is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 16,721 polygons and 17,233 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
