About This Model

AN TPY2 Radar is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $139. You are getting 138,741 polygons and 152,250 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2020 · Updated 20 August 2026
