About This Model

Italian Euro Coin 20 Cent is a detailed asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 27,861 polygons over 27,800 vertices.
