About This Model

Firefighters almost never appear alone. A fire scene needs a crew: someone on a hose, someone at the pump panel, someone carrying equipment, all in full turnout gear with helmets, BA sets and boots. Building and rigging that crew one figure at a time is a long job, and rigging bulky protective clothing is harder than rigging ordinary clothes.

This set arrives rigged and prepared for Maya, so the deformation is already solved in the application the animation will happen in. That preparation is the difference between usable on day one and usable in a fortnight.

Six other builds sit on this page, from $139 for a static collection to $289 for rigged sets prepared for Cinema 4D and Maya. This set at $289 is aimed at emergency services visualization, safety training material, film and television, and games. On sale since 2021.
