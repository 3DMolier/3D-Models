About This Model

Turtles Collection belongs to our Model Bundles & Sets range and is offered at $89 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The set covers several related objects at once, which saves assembling a scene from separate purchases.
