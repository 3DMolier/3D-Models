About This Model

A matching set of red ski gear, with skis, poles, helmet and boots, ready for winter sports and resort scenes. The Red Ski Equipment Collection keeps every piece in the same bold color, so a skier looks fully kitted out. Skis and poles can lean against a lodge wall or rack, the way they usually wait between runs.

The ski set follows CheckMate specification, which keeps each item's name and scale in order. Bright red stands out sharply against snow, which keeps the gear readable in wide mountain shots. Separate pieces make it easy to dress a character partially, with just a helmet or a pair of skis.

Released in 2024, the collection fits modern ski resorts and winter holiday stories. Sports retailers show complete kits with it, and winter games equip their skiers using these pieces. Resort visualizations also scatter skis and poles near lifts and chalets to suggest a busy season.
