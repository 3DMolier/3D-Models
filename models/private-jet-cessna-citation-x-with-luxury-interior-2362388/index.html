About This Model

The Citation X was for years the fastest civil aircraft in the sky short of Concorde, and it looks it: a sharply swept wing, engines set well back on the rear fuselage and a long pointed nose. This build includes the cabin, which is where a business jet is actually sold - the club seating, the tables, the sidewall panels and the galley are all modelled rather than implied behind tinted windows.

The mesh is 179,333 polygons and 191,276 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. Economical for an aircraft with a full interior, which is what lets the same asset serve an exterior ramp shot and a cabin render.

Three rigged builds share this page at the same $299, including versions prepared for Maya and for Cinema 4D. Aimed at business aviation marketing, charter and brokerage presentation, luxury travel advertising, film and television and games.
