About This Model

Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Leather Jacket is a finished, render-ready model at $59. The mesh carries 17,910 polygons and 17,718 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 112.27 x 32.6 x H58.03 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
