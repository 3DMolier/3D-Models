About This Model

A collection of industrial tow tractors, the small tugs that pull carts through factories, airports and warehouses. Low bodies, a single driver seat and a sturdy rear hitch give each tug its compact, practical outline. Several models in one set let a logistics scene show a mixed working fleet rather than a row of clones.

CheckMate Pro certification brings real-world proportions and clean quad-based topology to every tractor in the set. Scene structure is flat but grouped, so each vehicle can be isolated, duplicated or recolored quickly. Their size matches standard carts and pallets, so whole trains of trailers line up without rescaling.

Available since 2022, the tow tractor set suits airport baggage scenes, factory logistics animation and simulations. Royalty free use opens it to material handling advertising, fleet supplier catalogs and training videos. Hook a few baggage carts behind one and a busy airport apron scene starts moving.
