Priced at $79, the Kalmar AGV is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
