About This Model

A two-tier steel bunk bed made up with folded army blankets, ready for barracks and field camp interiors. The Military Bunk Bed with Blankets uses 94,748 polygons and 94,656 vertices, with soft folds in the bedding and crisp metal tubes. Olive drab blankets and pillows make the bed look occupied rather than freshly delivered.

It measures 209.8 x 99.48 x H179.33 cm, a standard military bunk for two soldiers stacked one above the other. StemCell delivery ensures the wool and steel materials stay consistent in each delivered format and rendering engine. Tightly tucked blankets show the inspection-ready neatness drill sergeants demand, a detail that sells authenticity.

This bunk is a 2026 piece that sells for $29 as one version, blankets included. War films, boot camp scenes, survival shelters and military museum dioramas use it to furnish sleeping quarters. Line several along a wall with footlockers and a barracks hall takes shape in minutes.
