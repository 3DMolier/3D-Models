About This Model

Leaves from Banana Tree belongs to our Nature & Plants range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 18,481 polygons and 18,455 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
