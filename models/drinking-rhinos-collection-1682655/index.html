About This Model

A collection of rhinos lowering their heads to drink, the calm watering hole moment that defines the African savanna at dusk. Heavy folded skin, twin horns and stocky legs splayed at the water's edge make the animals instantly recognizable.

Bought as one set, the rhinos share scale and material style, so a small herd drinks together without visual seams. With CheckMate Pro behind each mesh, sizes stay true, which matters when rhinos stand near trees, jeeps or people. Dust on the hides and mud around the legs suggest a long walk to reach the water.

Available since 2021, the collection suits wildlife documentaries, safari lodge visuals, conservation campaigns and nature game levels. Reflections of the animals in still water under an orange sky create a peaceful, painterly scene. Conservation groups often feature rhinos to discuss poaching, and a drinking pose keeps that message gentle. Placed across a wide river bank, a few rhinos fill a cinematic establishing shot without crowding it.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
