About This Model

Beach Stones with Quartz Veins is a set of smooth, sea-worn pebbles crossed by thin white lines of quartz. Each stone has its own rounded shape, polished by waves into soft forms without sharp edges. Scattered along a shoreline or arranged on a spa shelf, the pebbles bring a calm natural texture to the frame.

CheckMate Pro approval stands behind the stones, covering real scale, tidy topology and ready materials. The contrast between dark rock and bright veins is what makes them read well in close tabletop shots. Wet them with a glossy shader and the veins sparkle as if the tide had just pulled back.

First offered in 2017, the stones fill coastal landscapes, zen garden designs and wellness product scenes. Stacked into a small cairn, they also make a quiet symbol of balance for meditation visuals. At $29 the set gives a beach or garden scene natural variety without hand sculpting rocks.
