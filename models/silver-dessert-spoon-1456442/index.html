About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Silver Dessert Spoon is a finished, render-ready model at $7. You are getting 484 polygons and 482 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
