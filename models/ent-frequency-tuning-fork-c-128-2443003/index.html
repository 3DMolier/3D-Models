About This Model

Priced at $19, the ENT Tuning Fork is a finished Musical Instruments model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 6,298 polygons and 6,315 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 4.08 x 18.04 x H3.22 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for event visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It suits music-video and film sets, game props, product rendering and educational material.
