About This Model

Plastic Cap is one of the Kitchen & Tableware models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $19. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for product advertising, fashion visualization and game assets. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualisation, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

The mesh carries 10,052 polygons and 10,887 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
