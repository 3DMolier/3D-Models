About This Model

A Jack Russell terrier with its own dog house and food bowl, the small, lively breed shown at home in the yard. The terrier's white coat with tan patches, upright stance and folded ears are what make the breed instantly recognizable. Its compact body and alert expression suit a dog that is always ready to chase something.

Bundling the dog with its house and bowl means a backyard or porch scene is dressed in one step. CheckMate Pro certification keeps every piece at real-world scale, so the dog fits through the house door as it should. Swap the bowl contents or repaint the house through materials and the set adapts to any garden style.

Listed since 2019, the collection suits pet food advertising, family home renders, children's animation and backyard scenes in games. A terrier peering out of its house on a sunny afternoon adds warmth to any suburban exterior. Dog, house and bowl cost $89 together as a ready backyard vignette.
