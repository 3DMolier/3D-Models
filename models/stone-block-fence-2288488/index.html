About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Stone Block Fence is a finished, render-ready model at $29. The mesh carries 7,339 polygons and 8,594 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
