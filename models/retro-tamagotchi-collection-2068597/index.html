About This Model

Retro Tamagotchi Collection gathers egg-shaped virtual pet toys with small pixel screens and the trio of buttons kids pressed to feed and clean their pets. The shells come in different colors and patterns, so the set captures the playful variety of the original craze.

Every toy meets CheckMate Pro standards, which keeps topology tidy and scale identical across the collection. The keychain loops are part of the shape, ready for a chain or strap to be attached. Screens are separate surfaces, so pixel art of a hungry or happy pet can be dropped in easily.

Scatter a few on a school desk with gel pens and cassette tapes for a childhood throwback. Held in a small hand, one glowing screen tells the story of a pet that needs attention right now.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
