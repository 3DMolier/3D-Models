About This Model

Bottle Bomb belongs to our Weapons range and is offered at $15 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits food advertising, packaging visualisation, restaurant interiors and game props.

Geometry weighs in at 5,143 polygons over 5,168 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
