About This Model

This greyhound captures the lean racing dog with its deep chest, narrow waist and long legs built for sprinting. A fur coat covers the slim body, giving the short haired breed a soft surface that still shows every muscle line. The set is prepared for Blender, so artists working there can bring the dog straight into a scene.

Built to CheckMate specification, the asset cleared TurboSquid checks on topology, scene structure and naming before release. The coat color can be tuned to fawn, brindle, grey or black, the shades most associated with the breed.

Released in 2025, the dog suits racetrack sequences, veterinary education, pet care videos and family animation projects. Greyhounds are also popular as calm house pets, so the model fits a quiet living room shot as easily as a chase. A side view shows off the arched back and tucked belly that make this breed instantly recognizable. Game developers can drop it into park, farm or city levels where a fast, graceful animal adds life to the background.
