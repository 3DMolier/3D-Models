About This Model

Light Skin Sportsmen is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. Geometry weighs in at 8,811 polygons over 9,551 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.
