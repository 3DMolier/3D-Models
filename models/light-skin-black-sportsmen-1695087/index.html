About This Model

This Characters & People model - Light Skin Black Sportsmen - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Counted at the source, the model is 8,811 polygons and 9,551 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
