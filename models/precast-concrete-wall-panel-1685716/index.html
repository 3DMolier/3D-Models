About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Precast Concrete Wall Panel is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 456 polygons and 482 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2021 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
