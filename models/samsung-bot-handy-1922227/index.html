About This Model

Priced at $49, the Samsung Bot Handy is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.
