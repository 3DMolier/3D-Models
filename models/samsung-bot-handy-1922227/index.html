About This Model

Priced at $49, the Samsung Bot Handy is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 67,344 polygons and 35,978 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
