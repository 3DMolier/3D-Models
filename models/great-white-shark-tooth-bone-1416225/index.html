About This Model

A shark tooth is a triangle with serrated edges and a broad root, and the serrations are what do the work: they saw rather than pierce. The enamel is smooth and the root is porous and matt, which gives the object two quite different surfaces meeting at a clear line.

The mesh is minimal at 1,090 polygons and 1,092 vertices, built entirely from quads with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 4 texture maps carrying the enamel, the root and the serration detail.

At $19 and built to StemCell specification, it suits natural history and museum visualization, marine biology and education material, collector and jewellery renders, documentary work and games. On sale since 2019.
