About This Model

A set of workshop car lifts for garages, tire shops and auto service scenes, ranging from post hoists to low platforms. The lifts show hydraulic columns, swing arms and steel bases, the parts mechanics see every day. Floor anchors and safety locks are visible on the posts, which helps close shots of a technician at work.

Each piece meets CheckMate Pro standards, so the meshes arrive tidy, correctly scaled and easy to place on a shop floor. Painted surfaces and bare metal sit on separate materials, which lets you switch the paint colors without touching the geometry.

Three versions are available: the static collection at $79, a rigged collection at $69 and a second rigged collection at $99. The rigged packs let arms swing and platforms rise, useful for repair tutorials and garage animations. Selling since 2015, the Car Lifts Collection fits dealership service bays, auto racing games and mechanic training courses. Raise a car halfway and a viewer instantly reads the space as a working repair shop.
