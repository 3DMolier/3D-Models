Stoneware Trio Pot Sagaform White is one of the Kitchen & Tableware models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $24. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
