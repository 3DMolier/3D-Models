About This Model

The Dark Skinned Bikini Woman Rigged is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and VR experiences. First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 15,997 polygons over 16,750 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
