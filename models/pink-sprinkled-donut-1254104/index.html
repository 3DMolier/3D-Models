About This Model

Pink Sprinkled Donut is one of the Food & Beverages models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for food photography, film & TV VFX and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2018 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
