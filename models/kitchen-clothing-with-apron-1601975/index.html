About This Model

Priced at $69, the Kitchen Clothing with Apron is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 3,402 polygons and 3,734 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 9 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 1000 x 1000. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and event visualization. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
