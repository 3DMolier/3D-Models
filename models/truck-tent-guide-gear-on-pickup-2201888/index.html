About This Model

The Truck Tent Guide Gear on Pickup is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $99 on TurboSquid. You are getting 341,777 polygons and 390,565 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
