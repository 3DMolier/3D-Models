About This Model

The Female Gavel is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $199 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 10,388 polygons and 11,207 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

16 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 3000 x 3000 resolution. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the Female Gavel?

16 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 3000 x 3000, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

Where can I buy the Female Gavel 3D model?

It is sold through TurboSquid, where this listing has been available since 2020. Purchase, download and licensing are handled by TurboSquid; delivery is immediate after checkout.

Can the Female Gavel model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Can the Female Gavel model be used in a commercial project?
