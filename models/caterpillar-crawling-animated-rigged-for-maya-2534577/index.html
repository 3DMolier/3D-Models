About This Model

Caterpillar Crawling Animated Rigged for Maya is a segmented larva with a soft ringed body and stubby prolegs, prepared for Maya artists. The body is built as a chain of rounded segments, so it bends naturally when the creature arches along a leaf or twig. Small head details and fine body hairs help the insect read clearly in macro-style framing.

The CheckMate specification guided the build, which means clean naming, no leftover helpers and a scene that opens predictably. Licensing is Editorial Uses Only, so it belongs in documentaries, news graphics, textbooks and other informational work. Surface patterns can be shifted through shaders, turning a green caterpillar into a striped or spotted species.

Released in 2026, the larva suits nature films, school biology lessons, butterfly life cycle explainers and garden-themed scientific animation. Close shots on a stem with shallow depth of field show it at its best, especially when light passes through the body. It pairs naturally with leaves, flowers and a chrysalis model for a complete metamorphosis sequence.
