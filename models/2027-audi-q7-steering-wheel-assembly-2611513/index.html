About This Model

A detailed steering wheel with spokes, airbag hub and control buttons, made for close-up cockpit renders and driving games. The 2027 Audi Q7 Steering Wheel Assembly needs only 6,824 polygons and 7,492 vertices, light enough for real-time interiors. Because it is a separate assembly, it can be dropped into a custom dashboard or animated to turn.

CheckMate certification keeps the topology tidy and the pivot centered, which is exactly what a rotating wheel needs. The thick rim cross-section and stitched leather look give the part a convincing grip from the driver's viewpoint. Buttons on the spokes catch small highlights and sell the premium feel when the camera sits behind the seat.

On sale since 2026 at $39, the wheel reproduces an Audi design and is therefore sold as Editorial Uses Only. Driving simulators, cockpit configurators, automotive journalism and film inserts are the natural places for it. Pair it with the matching seats to build a believable cabin without modeling a full vehicle interior.
