About This Model

An autonomous humanoid service robot walks, gestures and turns in this Blender-ready set, designed for hotels, stores and offices. Its friendly proportions, rounded head and smooth shell make it look approachable rather than cold and industrial. Articulated arms and hands let it hand over items, point visitors the right way or greet guests.

The robot passes CheckMate review, keeping the hard-surface panels crisp and all of the joints clean. Because the rig is built for Blender, animation plays immediately and can be extended with custom gestures. Glossy white shell panels and a dark face screen look sharp under bright retail lighting.

Royalty free and introduced in 2026, the robot suits hospitality technology pitches, retail visualizations and sci-fi films. A reception desk scene with the robot greeting arriving guests explains service automation in a single image. Pair it with real people in a lobby to show how the machine fits daily life.
