About This Model

Several wooden serving trays in one file give a kitchen or cafe scene ready surfaces for cups, plates and breakfast items. The whole group comes to 4,862 polygons and 5,134 vertices, light enough to scatter across a counter without slowing the viewport. Raised rims and cut-out handles are the shapes that read on camera, and the geometry is spent mostly on those edges.

There are no bitmap textures here, so the grain and the stain are set up in whatever material library your studio prefers. Each tray unwraps onto islands that never touch one another, ready for a baked oak or bamboo pattern. Their construction follows CheckMate rules on proportions, object naming and a scene that loads without warnings.

Released in 2025, the set is a recent addition aimed at food styling, hospitality interiors and lifestyle renders. Cafe and bakery scenes in games use the trays as tabletop dressing, often stacked or tucked under a teapot. Stylists also place one on a bed beside a coffee cup to suggest a slow and quiet morning at home.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2025 · Updated 13 November 2026
