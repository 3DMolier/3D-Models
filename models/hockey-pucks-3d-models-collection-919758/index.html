About This Model

A set of black vulcanized rubber pucks gives an ice rink, locker room or sports shop shelf the small detail that sells the scene. Each disk is a short flat cylinder with crisp edges, so it reads correctly from overhead camera angles and at ice level. Having several pucks in one purchase saves time when you need a practice pile or a scattered warm-up area.

The collection carries the CheckMate specification from TurboSquid, which covers clean geometry, sensible pivots and real scale. Pivots sit at the center of each puck, so sliding, spinning and stacking them in an animation stays simple. The surfaces are plain enough to take a printed league logo or scuffed rubber texture without trouble.

Available since 2015, the pucks fit hockey video games, sports broadcast graphics and short films about junior teams and training. Animators like them for slapshot sequences, where a single disk flies past the goalie and into the net. Set dressers also leave a few on a bench next to sticks and tape to show a team that has just finished practice.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
