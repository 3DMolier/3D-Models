About This Model

The Two Nozzles is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 79,116 polygons and 41,205 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
