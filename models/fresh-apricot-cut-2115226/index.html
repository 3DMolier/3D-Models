About This Model

Apricot Cut is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 24,148 polygons and 15,176 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for food photography and film & TV VFX.
