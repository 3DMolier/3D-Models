About This Model

A red-eyed tree frog with bulging scarlet eyes, a lime green back and bright orange toes, rigged for Blender. The pose controls let the slender limbs fold under the body or stretch across a leaf, with sticky toe pads spread like suction discs. Blue and yellow stripes along the flanks give the animal its famous rainforest look.

The file keeps skin, eyes and controls in clear, named groups, as CheckMate requires, so posing starts straight away. The smooth wet surface catches highlights well, and a little subsurface glow under a rim light makes the thin skin look alive. Close shots work best because the vivid colors carry the whole frame without any extra props.

The Amphibian Red Eyed Tree Frog Rigged for Blender has been available since 2025. It fits rainforest documentaries, biology lessons on amphibians and jungle levels in games, as well as packaging for eco brands. Perch it on a broad leaf beside a raindrop and the scene tells its story without a caption.
