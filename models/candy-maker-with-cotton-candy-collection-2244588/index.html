About This Model

Candy floss is sugar spun into threads by centrifugal force, and the machine is a heated spinning bowl and nothing else. The product is almost entirely air, which is why it costs what it does and why it cannot be packaged well.

This is a collection listing rather than a single object, supplying the machine together with the candy as static pieces. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $32 the set suits fairground and event visualisation, food and retail imagery, advertising, film sets, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
