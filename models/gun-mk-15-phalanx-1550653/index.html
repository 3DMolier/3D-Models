About This Model

The Phalanx is a machine that decides on its own. Radar, gun and control are in one mounting, and once switched to automatic it tracks and engages incoming threats faster than a human could confirm what they are, which is the only way the system works at all.

The model is light at 29,591 polygons and 32,230 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $49 it suits naval and defence visualisation, documentary and news graphics, simulation, film props, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2020.
