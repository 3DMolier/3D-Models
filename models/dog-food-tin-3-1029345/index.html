About This Model

Looking for a Food & Beverages asset? The Dog Food Tin 3 is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 6,928 polygons and 6,929 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

2 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for food photography, film & TV VFX and game assets. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
