About This Model

Magirus has been building fire appliances since the ladder was made of wood, and the name still carries the trade. On an Iveco chassis, the FF160 is the workhorse version of that: a pump, lockers full of kit and a crew cab, sized for European streets rather than American ones.

The model holds 413,278 polygons and 466,832 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification. A rigged build is offered separately.

At $139 it suits emergency services visualization, safety and training material, urban and architectural scenes, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2022.
