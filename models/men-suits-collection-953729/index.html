About This Model

This collection gathers several men's business suits, with jackets and trousers tailored for formal events, office scenes and fashion catalogs. The Men Suits Collection shows the structured shoulders, the lapels and the crisp trouser creases that define tailored menswear. Jackets hang with natural weight, so the cloth drapes over the chest rather than sticking to it.

CheckMate Pro certification of every piece means clean geometry and consistent naming across the whole set of garments. Displayed on mannequins or hangers, the suits fill a showroom rail without any extra modeling. The fabric folds at the elbows and waist, which keeps the garments from looking stiff.

Available since 2015, these suits have reached 11 buyers among fashion retailers, tailoring brands and character artists. At $199 the bundle replaces modeling each suit by hand, and the shared style keeps a store render coherent. Wedding scenes, office crowds and menswear lookbooks are typical places for it, along with movie premieres.
