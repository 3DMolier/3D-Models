About This Model

A combustion flame generator stored as OpenVDB volumes, giving Blender artists a roaring fire source without running a fluid simulation. The flame rolls upward in thick tongues, with denser cores near the base and thinning wisps that fade into smoke.

Since the fire lives in voxel grids, Cycles and EEVEE light it from within, so nearby walls catch a believable orange glow. Scene organization follows the CheckMate guidelines, so the emitter loads centered and named clearly. Temperature and density channels can be remapped in the shader to push the fire toward blue gas or sooty orange.

Launched in 2026, it fits industrial accident scenes, furnace close-ups and burner tests in engineering animations. Place it under a boiler or inside a jet nozzle mockup and the machine suddenly feels powerful and dangerous. Several copies rotated and scaled create a wide wall of fire for disaster sequences. Motion blur on the flame edges sells speed when the camera pushes past quickly.
