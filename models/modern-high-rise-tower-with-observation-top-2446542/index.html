About This Model

A modern high-rise tower crowned with an observation deck, a ready landmark for skylines and tourism scenes. The slender shaft rises to a wider top where glazed viewing levels let visitors look out over the city. That crown is the signature feature, giving the tower a silhouette people recognize from far across town.

Its parts follow CheckMate specification, so the observation level can be lit or textured on its own. Glass bands around the deck reflect the sky by day and glow from inside at night. Clean vertical lines on the shaft keep the tower elegant, whether it stands alone or among other buildings.

First released in 2025, the $179 tower suits city visualization, tourism promotion and urban game maps. Event visualization uses towers like this as backdrops for fireworks, light shows and New Year scenes. Planners also study sightlines with it, checking what a new viewpoint would reveal across the city.
