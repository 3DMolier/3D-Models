About This Model

The SWAT Vehicles Collection groups armored police trucks with heavy bumpers, small windows and ladders for tactical entry teams. These boxy vehicles block streets convincingly, so two or three of them shape a tense standoff scene. Push bars, roof hatches and side steps give each truck a strong silhouette even in night shots.

The bundle complies with the CheckMate specification, so the vehicles share consistent scale and clean topology. Dark matte paint with reflective lettering works best, especially under flashing red and blue light. Tires and glass can be shaded separately, keeping material setups manageable across the whole set.

Three versions are found on this page, each a separate set at a different price. This collection costs $219, the second set is $289 and a further collection under the same name sells for $319. On sale since 2024, the trucks fit police dramas, crime documentaries and urban action games.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
