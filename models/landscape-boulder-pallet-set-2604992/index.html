About This Model

Natural landscaping boulders sit on wooden pallets, the way stone yards and garden centers deliver them to a site. The Landscape Boulder Pallet Set uses 65,154 polygons and 66,320 vertices, built only from quads for clean subdivision of rock surfaces. Each pallet holds a few heavy stones, leaving gaps so the weathered wood remains clearly visible.

The row of pallets covers 413.78 x 129.57 x H68.51 cm, a typical delivery for a garden project. CheckMate certification guarantees metric units, so stones and pallets keep their correct proportion with vehicles and people. Rough, irregular rock faces contrast with the straight slats of the timber pallets underneath.

This single version joined the store in 2026, priced at $39 for landscaping and garden projects. Landscape architects, garden center planners and builder simulation games use it for stone yards and work sites. Placed beside a forklift or skid steer, the set instantly reads as material waiting for installation.
