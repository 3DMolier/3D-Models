About This Model

Priced at $24, the Chair Pad is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 6,238 polygons over 3,405 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

It has been on sale since 2022 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
