Saint Basil Cathedral Snow Globe is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Geometry weighs in at 90,252 polygons over 96,525 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.
