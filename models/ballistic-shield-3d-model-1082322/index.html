About This Model

A shield that stops rifle rounds weighs enough that carrying one is a job in itself. The viewport is the weak point and the reason it exists at all, and everything about the handle geometry is about holding weight at arm's length.

The model is light at 11,842 polygons and 11,986 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 11 maps, 3 at 4096x4096 and 8 at 2048x2048. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $19 it suits law enforcement and defence visualisation, training material, character accessories, film props, game assets and editorial illustration. On sale since 2017.
