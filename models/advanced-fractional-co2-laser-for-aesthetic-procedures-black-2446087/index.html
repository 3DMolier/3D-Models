About This Model

A fractional CO2 laser station for skin resurfacing, with an articulated arm, a handpiece and a touchscreen on a wheeled cabinet. It measures 48.06 x 110.01 x H190.01 cm and uses 73,236 polygons over 77,146 vertices. The jointed delivery arm is the detail that makes it read as a laser rather than an ordinary cart.

Non-overlapping UVs give the white housing, control screen and metal joints their own texture space. It follows the CheckMate specification, so geometry is clean and real-world scale matches the stated size. Rounded panels and a slim screen give it the polished look of a modern aesthetic clinic.

Four versions are on the page: the static laser at $59 and three rigged builds at $69. The rigs include a Maya file, a generic file and a black edition for scar and wrinkle treatment scenes. Released in 2025, it suits dermatology clinic interiors, medical device presentations and cosmetic treatment explainers.
