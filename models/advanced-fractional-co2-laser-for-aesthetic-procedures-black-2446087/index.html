About This Model

Priced at $59, the Advanced Fractional CO2 Laser for Aesthetic Procedures is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 73,236 polygons and 77,146 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 48.06 x 110.01 x H190.01 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
