About This Model

Automatic garage doors in several panel designs, collected so a street of houses does not have to repeat the same door. Each door comes with its frame, so it can be dropped into a wall opening without building trim by hand. Horizontal panel lines and the motor housing above the opening are what make a garage door read as automatic.

Built to CheckMate specification, the collection shares one scale and naming scheme across every door. That consistency matters on a facade, where two doors side by side must line up exactly on the same floor plane. Swap the paint color on a single door and the set covers modern, suburban and industrial frontages.

Released in 2025, the doors serve architectural exteriors, real estate renders and city game levels. A garage door is often the largest single object on a house front, so its proportions carry the whole elevation. Buying them together saves hunting for matching doors one by one when a whole block needs dressing.
