About This Model

A sky lantern is a hot air balloon with a candle in it, and that is exactly the problem: it lands still burning, wherever the wind takes it. Several countries have banned them for that reason, and they remain a fixture of festival imagery.

The model is very light at 10,312 polygons and 10,278 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, in white. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $29 it suits festival and event visualization, night and celebration scenes, advertising, film backgrounds, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
