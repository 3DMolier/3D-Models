About This Model

Vintage Collector Sports Car White is one of the Vehicles models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $129. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.
