About This Model

A transparent car body revealing the full brake system inside, a clear way to show where discs, lines and calipers sit. The Vehicle Brake System on Transparent Car combines 398,658 polygons for the glass-like shell and the hardware within.

Its 425,376 vertices keep both the outer bodywork and the brake parts sharp, even when layered on screen. CheckMate certified geometry keeps every part separated, so you can fade the shell or highlight a single caliper. The see-through body places each component in context, which a loose parts diagram cannot do.

The 2026 assembly costs $149 and suits automotive training videos, engineering explainers and car show displays. Rotate the car slowly while highlighting the brake path from pedal to each wheel. Driving schools and service centers can explain maintenance needs to customers with one clear visual. Ghosted bodywork lets viewers keep a sense of the car's shape while the brakes take center stage.
