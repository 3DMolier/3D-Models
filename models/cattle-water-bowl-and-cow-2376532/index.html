About This Model

This farm scene pairs a cow with a metal cattle water bowl, the automatic drinker bolted to posts in barns and pens. Only 25,884 polygons and 26,263 vertices are needed, which keeps the pair light for repeating across a whole livestock stable. Set side by side, the cow and drinker explain the purpose of the equipment without any caption.

Its modeling respects the CheckMate standard of TurboSquid, with tidy topology, believable scale and clearly named parts. UV islands sit separately with no overlap, ready for hide patterns, mud on the legs or rust on the bowl. Black and white markings or a plain brown coat can be painted to match dairy or beef breeds.

Its 2025 debut came at $119, under a Royalty Free license that covers farm and food projects. Agricultural equipment makers explain watering systems with it, and veterinary courses show how animals access clean water in housing. Farm games and dairy documentaries also place the pair in barns beside feed troughs and straw bedding.
