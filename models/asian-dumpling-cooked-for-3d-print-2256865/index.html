About This Model

A cooked Asian dumpling prepared as a print-ready solid, for food replicas, restaurant display pieces and kitchen education props. The pleated top seam is the defining feature, with each fold shaped like hand-crimped dough pressed together. Cooked dough looks slightly plumper than raw, and the shape follows that softened, rounded profile.

As a printing model it is meant to be closed and solid, so slicers can process it without patching holes. The file conforms to CheckMate specification, which means sensible naming and a scale that loads correctly in common tools. The base is flat enough to stand steadily on a print bed without heavy supports.

Released in 2024 and licensed Royalty Free, it suits makers, food stylists and classroom projects on world cuisine. Painted after printing, it makes a convincing menu replica for the display windows of dumpling shops. Print a handful and arrange them in a bamboo steamer for a desk ornament or a photo prop.
