About This Model

Looking for a Furniture & Interior asset? The Professional Pullout Kitchen Tap is a finished, render-ready model at $19. You are getting 6,604 polygons and 6,778 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are interior visualization, product rendering and event visualization.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
