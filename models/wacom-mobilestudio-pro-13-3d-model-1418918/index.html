About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Wacom MobileStudio Pro 13 is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.

Geometry weighs in at 17,610 polygons over 19,497 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 6 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
