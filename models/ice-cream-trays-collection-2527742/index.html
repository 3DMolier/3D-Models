About This Model

This collection gathers ice cream trays, the rectangular tubs and display pans holding scoopable gelato in shop freezers and cafe counters. Swirled, scooped surfaces show the texture of soft ice cream, which is hardest to model convincingly. Pastel colors and creamy highlights make the trays bright accents on any dessert counter or cafe display.

Produced to CheckMate specification, the trays come with clean geometry and an organized, easy to navigate file.

Joining the range in 2026, the trays are available for commercial food and retail scenes. Gelato shop interiors, dessert menus, summer food scenes and refrigerated display cases are their natural places. Lined up behind curved glass, several trays build the colorful wall of flavors customers expect. Seasonal menus in games and animation can rotate flavors simply by recoloring the trays inside the engine. Food delivery apps and cafe websites can use a single tray as a clean illustration of one flavor.
