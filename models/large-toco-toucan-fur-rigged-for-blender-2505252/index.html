About This Model

A toco toucan with feather fur and a Blender rig, its oversized orange bill the first thing anyone notices in the frame. Black body plumage, a white throat and the blue ring around the eye follow the colors of the real bird. The bill is long and light-looking, with the dark tip that separates this species from other toucans.

As the name says, it is set up for Blender with groomed fur, so it can perch, turn its head or flap without extra preparation. Feathers rendered as fur soften the edges of the body, which keeps the bird from looking like painted plastic. The build follows CheckMate guidelines, so it lands at a believable size beside branches and other jungle props.

First offered in 2025, the toucan suits rainforest documentaries, zoo signage, children's animation and tropical games. Perch it on a mossy branch with soft backlight, and the bill glows like a lantern against the leaves.
