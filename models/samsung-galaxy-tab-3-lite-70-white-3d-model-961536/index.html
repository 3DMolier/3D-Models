About This Model

A compact white Samsung tablet with a slim bezel and a physical home button, sized to hold in one hand. The Samsung Galaxy Tab 3 Lite 7.0 White 3D Model reproduces the rounded corners, speaker grille and rear camera of this early Android tablet. Its small form made it popular for reading, browsing and video calls before larger screens took over.

Being certified under CheckMate Pro, the tablet comes with clean geometry and a tidy scene structure. The screen can take any image as an emissive texture, so a web page or ebook shows up convincingly. White plastic suits bright domestic scenes, where the tablet blends into kitchens and bedrooms.

The license is Editorial Uses Only, so it fits technology journalism, device histories and review articles. Available since 2015, it rests well on a sofa cushion, a kitchen counter or a child's desk. Pair it with a stylus or a cup of tea to suggest a quiet evening of reading.
