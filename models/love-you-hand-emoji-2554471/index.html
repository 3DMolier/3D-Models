About This Model

Love-You Hand Emoji is one of the general models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. The mesh carries 559 polygons and 551 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 5 textures, authored at 1024 x 1024. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
