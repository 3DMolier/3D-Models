About This Model

This Atlantic mackerel gives ocean scenes, fishing visuals and marine biology content a sleek, fast schooling fish. A torpedo body, forked tail and wavy dark stripes over a blue green back make the species easy to identify. Prepared for Blender, the set lets an animator build swimming schools or a single fish turning in open water.

TurboSquid checked the fish against CheckMate specification, approving its topology, naming and organized structure. Iridescent skin needs a shader with subtle color shifts, and the stripe pattern carries much of the realism.

Released in 2026, the mackerel suits fisheries education, seafood supply chain explainers, aquarium visuals and underwater games. A large school swirling into a tight bait ball creates one of the most dramatic sights in the ocean. Light shafts from the surface glinting on silver bellies make close shots of the school especially convincing. Fishing game developers and marine educators both need a reliable mackerel, since the species is so common in northern seas.
