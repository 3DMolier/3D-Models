About This Model

A homeless man in worn layers and a red campaign-style cap, a figure for urban hardship and social commentary scenes. Ragged clothing, a tired posture and an unkempt beard tell a story of life on the street. The cap adds a political note, so the character suits editorial cartoons and documentary reconstructions.

CheckMate guidance kept the proportions realistic, letting the man sit in doorways or on benches at correct scale. Cold, desaturated light suits the subject, emphasizing the hard conditions without turning the image into caricature. Placing a cardboard sign or a shopping cart nearby completes the scene with familiar details.

Offered from 2024, the figure serves news illustration, social issue documentaries, satire, and urban crowd scenes in games. Handle the subject with care, since the strongest images of homelessness respect the person in them. A second, fully measured version of the same character is also available for heavier close-up work.
