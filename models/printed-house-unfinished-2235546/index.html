About This Model

Printed House Unfinished is one of the Industrial Equipment models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $49. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. 2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 28,439 polygons over 33,996 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
