About This Model

A collection of Japanese folding fans, the pleated sensu with slim ribs that open into a half circle of painted paper. Several fans in one set give variety for a shelf, a dancer's hand or a wall display in a single purchase. The pleats are what sell a folding fan on camera, since flat paper would read as a cardboard cutout.

As StemCell assets the fans carry PBR paper and wood materials that render consistently in each supported program. Painted paper and lacquered ribs can be retextured with new patterns, from waves and cranes to plain red. A fan opened behind a cup of tea or spread on a low table gives a room a Japanese accent at once.

The collection came out in 2025 for game props, film and theater sets, cultural exhibitions and interior renders. Animators also use folding fans as hand props for dancers and geisha characters, where a quick flick reveals the pattern.
