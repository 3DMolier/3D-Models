About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The Stainless Steel Manual Honey Extractor is a finished, render-ready model at $39. You are getting 42,768 polygons and 41,786 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
