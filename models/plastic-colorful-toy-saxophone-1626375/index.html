About This Model

This Toys & Games model - Plastic Colorful Toy Saxophone - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. You are getting 8,936 polygons and 9,031 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
