About This Model

A near-future combat suit has to look engineered rather than styled: armour plates sized to the body segments that actually need protection, joints left flexible, cabling routed where it would not snag, and a helmet with a real sensor package rather than a smooth visor. This build follows that logic, which is what separates plausible military science fiction from costume design.

The mesh is 125,228 polygons and 128,832 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. That budget goes into the plate edges and the joint hardware, which is where a suit is examined in close-up.

At $99 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits games, science fiction film and television, previsualization and concept work, illustration and trailers. On sale since 2025, kept to real-world scale so it fits a standard human figure.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier·Published July 2025·Updated 20 August 2026
