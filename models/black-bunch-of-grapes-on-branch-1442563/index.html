About This Model

The Black Bunch Of Grapes On Branch is a production-ready Nature & Plants 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 45,074 polygons and 52,132 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 16 textures, authored at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
