About This Model

This portable speaker has an oval body wrapped in a woven fabric front, with a wooden top panel and a carry strap. The Bang and Olufsen Beosound A5 Riva Speaker uses 10,582 polygons for the curved shell, the grille weave and the strap fittings. At 28.5 cm wide, it sits on a kitchen counter or terrace table like a well-designed piece of furniture.

The speaker meets the CheckMate specification, with 12,103 vertices spread across a UV layout free of overlaps. Warm wood grain against cool aluminum and textile is what gives the object its Scandinavian character. Soft daylight from a window brings out the oak tones without making the metal look harsh.

At $19 apiece, the four versions include this branded model and three generic wooden speakers. The generic ones come in dark wood, light oak panels and a wood finish, handy when a logo is not wanted. It joined the range in 2025 and fits modern living rooms, kitchen scenes and audio product rendering.
