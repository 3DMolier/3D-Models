About This Model

A tic tac toe game set with a grid board and black and gold playing pieces shaped as crosses and rings. The 4,606 polygons and 4,684 vertices shape the raised grid lines and the beveled edges of each piece. Its luxury color scheme turns a children's game into a stylish coffee table object.

Under its Editorial Uses Only license, the set suits lifestyle journalism, design reviews and editorial features. Clean UVs make it easy to add brushed gold on the pieces and a lacquer finish on the board. CheckMate Lite certification confirms usable geometry at realistic tabletop scale for close interior shots.

Released in 2021, the set fits living room renders, game nights and home decor stories. A half-finished match on a marble table hints at two players who just stepped away. Interior features can place it beside design books to show how games become decoration.
