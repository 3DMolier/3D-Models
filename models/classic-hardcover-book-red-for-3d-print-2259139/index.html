About This Model

A red hardcover book ready for 3D printing, this model works as a shelf decor piece, a miniature prop or a hidden storage box. The book has a rounded spine, slightly raised covers and a block of pages set back from the edges. Those classic proportions make it look like an old leather-bound volume from a private library.

TurboSquid lists it as made to the CheckMate specification, so the geometry is sound for printing. The print-ready mesh can be sliced directly, then painted in deep red with gold lines along the spine. Hobbyists can scale it down for dollhouses or enlarge it into a decorative bookend.

New in 2024, the book suits tabletop gaming props, escape room puzzles and film miniature sets. Painted with gold leaf titles, it fits naturally on a shelf among real books as a decorative accent. Makers sometimes hollow it out to create a secret box for keys or small treasures.
