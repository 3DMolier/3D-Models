About This Model

The Amphitheater Stage Setup is a semicircular open-air venue with tiered seating rising around a performance stage, ready for concert and theater scenes. A massive 2,474,362 polygons and 2,476,711 vertices go into the stepped rows, aisles, stage structure and surrounding details. Filled with a crowd at dusk, the bowl shape focuses every eye on the stage.

Matching the CheckMate spec, the venue keeps true architectural scale, so crowd systems and cameras behave predictably. Non-overlapping UVs allow unique weathering on the steps, worn edges near aisles and stains on the stage floor. Warm stage spotlights against a deep blue evening sky give the seating rows a dramatic glow.

Three versions are grouped together: this stage setup, a stone amphitheater with stage and an ancient amphitheater with stage. The ancient variant suits historical reconstructions, while the setup fits modern festivals and open-air shows. Released in 2025, the venue serves event planning, film sets and city park visualizations.
