About This Model

A collection of commercial kitchen appliances for restaurant interiors, catering scenes and food service training visuals. Stainless steel units like these share boxy bodies, control knobs and heavy doors, so the set feels like one coordinated kitchen line. Every appliance comes at real size, so counters, ovens and fridges line up along a wall without adjusting heights.

Assembly follows TurboSquid's CheckMate guidance, keeping every unit separately named and correctly scaled for planning. Face counts are not published for the bundle, so check the largest unit before filling an entire restaurant floor. Brushed steel reflects kitchen lights strongly, so a slightly rough metal material keeps highlights realistic.

Dated 2026 and priced at $79, the set suits restaurant fit-out proposals, cooking show sets and kitchen simulation games. Kitchen designers sketch layouts with it, checking workflow from storage to cooking to the serving pass. Animators also stage busy dinner services here, where steaming appliances frame chefs at work.
