About This Model

A pair of wrought iron fireplace tongs with long scissor arms and curled grips, the tool used to lift logs into a hearth. The 4,344 polygons over 2,176 vertices shape the pivot rivet, the flat jaws and the decorative handle ends. Leaning against the stone of a fireplace, the tongs add an authentic note to any cozy living room.

CheckMate Pro standards mean the tool opens with sensible names and correct proportions. Blackened iron, soot and a touch of rust are easy to paint on the overlap-free layout. The jaws pivot around the rivet, so the tongs can grip a log in an animated sequence.

Released in 2021, the Fireplace Tong Tool suits cabin interiors, historic house tours and winter holiday renders. Firelight flickering across the metal makes even this small tool glint in a dark room. Interior designers styling a hearth and fantasy level makers furnishing a medieval hall place it beside pokers and shovels.
