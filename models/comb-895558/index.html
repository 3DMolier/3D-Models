About This Model

The Comb is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $4 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 2,890 polygons and 2,892 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 3 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Questions About This Model

Can the Comb model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

Where can I buy the Comb 3D model?

It is sold through TurboSquid, where this listing has been available since 2015. Purchase, download and licensing are handled by TurboSquid; delivery is immediate after checkout.

What texture resolution ships with the Comb?

3 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

What is the Comb model typically used for?

The listing names product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX as the main applications. In practice it also works anywhere a finished clothing & accessories object is needed without modeling it from scratch.
