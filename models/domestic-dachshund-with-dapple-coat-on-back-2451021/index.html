About This Model

The dachshund was bred to go down a badger hole, which explains everything about the shape. A dapple coat is the mottled pattern that comes from a single gene, and a dog lying on its back is the pose owners photograph most and modellers supply least.

The animal holds 31,119 polygons and 30,992 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, suited to the repeated coat pattern, and is supplied in the reclining pose. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $139 it suits pet and lifestyle visualisation, veterinary and pet product marketing, interior renders, advertising, animation and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
