About This Model

Bale Wrapper Machine Dirty is a detailed Industrial Equipment asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $69. You are getting 122,766 polygons and 130,651 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
