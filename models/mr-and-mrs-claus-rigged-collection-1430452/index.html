About This Model

Mr and Mrs Claus Rigged Collection brings Santa and his wife together, rigged for festive animation and holiday scenes. Red suits with white fur trim, round spectacles and a full white beard give the couple their classic storybook look. Mrs Claus's apron and bonnet balance Santa's bulk, so the pair reads as partners in one frame.

The pair is certified CheckMate Pro, which confirms clean geometry and organized, clearly named rigs. Rigs let them wave, hug, deliver gifts or bake cookies in the kitchen without remodeling anything. Rosy cheeks, twinkling eyes and smiling faces keep the expressions gentle and friendly for very young audiences.

Offered since 2019, the couple suits Christmas greetings, holiday films, children's stories and seasonal game events. Placed by a fireplace, beside a tree or in a snowy workshop, they create cozy scenes families recognize instantly. Animation studios save precious seasonal crunch time by starting from ready, rigged characters.
