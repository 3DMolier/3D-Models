About This Model

A weathered post topped with arrow-shaped boards points travelers in several directions at a forest trail junction or village crossroad. Its 5,966 polygons give the boards split ends, worn edges and the rough cut of the pointed tips. The 5,972 vertices keep it light enough to place at every crossroads of an open game map.

Gray, sun-bleached wood is easy to build with shaders, because the signpost ships without textures. Painted place names can be added to the boards later, thanks to UVs that do not overlap. Its CheckMate Pro status means the post has clean topology and a believable height.

Two versions share the page, Aged Wooden Arrow Signpost and Old Wooden Arrow Road Sign, priced at $19 each. Planted in tall grass at the edge of a path, the sign gives a fantasy or rural scene its sense of distance. Released in 2023, it goes into adventure games, hiking trail illustrations and countryside landscape renders.
