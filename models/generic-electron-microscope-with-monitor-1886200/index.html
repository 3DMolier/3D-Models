About This Model

Electron Microscope Monitor is one of the Medical models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $99. You are getting 274,282 polygons and 145,090 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos.

The listing dates back to 2022, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
