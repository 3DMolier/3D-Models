About This Model

Beige retail mannequins rigged for Cinema 4D, handy for motion designers who want to pose display figures straight inside their usual software. The figures have smooth, faceless forms, which keeps the viewer's focus on the clothes or the product. Each body can bend at the main joints for natural standing and walking poses.

Neutral skin tones blend into both warm and cool store lighting without color correction. True scale follows CheckMate practice, letting garments built for standard human figures fit cleanly on these bodies. Several mannequins in one set allow a full window arrangement with varied heights and angles. A matte surface avoids harsh reflections, so fabric textures stay the brightest part of the frame.

New in 2026 and sold for $89, the bundle fits fashion animation, retail planning, apparel e-commerce visuals and store window design. Turn one figure slightly toward another, as if mid-conversation, and the display gains a sense of life.
