About This Model

A collection of Boeing 727 airliners, the trijet with its rear-mounted engines and distinctive T-tail. The aircraft share the classic narrow-body fuselage and swept wings that defined short and medium routes for decades. Having several 727s in one set makes it easy to fill an airport apron with period-correct traffic.

Each aircraft meets CheckMate specification, keeping scale, pivots and naming consistent across the whole fleet. Landing gear, flaps and control surfaces are modeled as distinct shapes, which helps a great deal with taxi and takeoff shots. Polished aluminum and painted cheatlines catch the sun on the tarmac, giving the jets a vintage glamour.

Released in 2025 under an Editorial Uses Only license, the set fits aviation documentaries, news stories and flight simulation. Aviation museums and history channels use the 727 to recall the golden age of jet travel in the seventies. Lined up at gates under an evening sky, the fleet brings a retro airport scene to life.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
