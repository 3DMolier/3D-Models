About This Model

This collection gathers classroom furniture into one purchase, from student desks and chairs to the larger pieces that fill a school room. Buying the set saves arranging separate models, and all pieces share a consistent scale and visual style. Arranged in rows facing a board, the desks immediately read as a lesson about to begin.

TurboSquid inspected the bundle to its CheckMate Pro standard, the most demanding grade on the marketplace. Every piece can be moved on its own, so the room can switch from lecture rows to group tables easily. Adding backpacks and books on the chairs is often all a scene needs to feel lived in.

Of the two versions, Classroom Furnishings Collection 2 costs $34 and this original pack costs $29. The second pack widens the furniture choice for schools, colleges and training rooms of different sizes. Offered since 2023, the collection serves architectural school projects, education explainers and children's game levels.
