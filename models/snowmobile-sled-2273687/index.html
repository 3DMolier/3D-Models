About This Model

Snowmobile Sled belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $21 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 52,752 polygons and 53,995 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX.

It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
