About This Model

A Labrador Retriever puppy in an idle loop with groomed fur, set up as a Maya rig for family and pet scenes. Puppies rarely stay still, and the idle captures the small shifts and head tilts that make them so endearing.

Soft fur covers a chubby body with oversized paws, the proportions of a young retriever still growing into itself. Floppy ears and a broad head give the puppy the friendly expression the breed is loved for. Maya controls let animators extend the idle into play, sniffing or sitting without rebuilding the rig.

Released in 2026, the puppy fits family film scenes, pet care videos and cozy home interiors. Set it on a rug by the sofa, and a plain living room instantly feels like a family home. Warm window light brings out the softness of the coat and the shine in the puppy's eyes. Its small size beside adult furniture reminds viewers how young the dog is, which adds warmth to any shot.
