About This Model

A collection of CD jewel cases, the clear plastic boxes with hinged lids that held albums before streaming. Stacked or fanned out, the cases show their spines, trays and front inserts like a real music shelf.

The set follows CheckMate specification, so each case has true size and tidy geometry ready for scenes. Blank insert areas take any album art, letting a project invent bands, mixes or software discs.

New in 2026, the collection fits retro bedrooms, record store interiors, office desks from the past and music documentaries. A tall tower of cases beside a stereo sets a scene firmly in another decade of music. Scattered across a car seat, they suggest a road trip soundtrack chosen by hand for the drive. Glass-like plastic shaders catch reflections, which helps the cases read in dim rooms and night scenes. Game designers can use them as collectible items that unlock songs, clues or hidden levels.
