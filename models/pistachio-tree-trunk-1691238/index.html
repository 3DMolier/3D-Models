About This Model

Pistachio Tree Trunk is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Geometry weighs in at 11,604 polygons over 11,861 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
