About This Model

This white pagoda lantern garden statue stacks tiered roofs over a lantern chamber, the classic stone ornament of Japanese gardens. It is prepared for 3D printing, and its 55,288 polygons keep the curved eaves and openings sharp after slicing.

The 28,376 vertices sit at about half the faces, which supports a closed shell that printers can read. Overlap-free UVs still allow a stone or concrete material for rendering, should you need a finished look on screen. Construction follows the CheckMate specification, so scale and naming arrive ready for a slicer or a scene.

Created in 2024, the lantern fits garden visualizations, tea house scenes and home décor printing projects. Print it in white resin for a modern shelf ornament, or scale it up for an outdoor feature among moss and gravel. Place a candle inside a rendered version to let soft light leak through the openings at dusk.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2024 · Updated 13 November 2026
