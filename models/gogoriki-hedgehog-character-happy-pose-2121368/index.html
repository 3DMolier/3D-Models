About This Model

Character design for young children is a discipline of subtraction. Round shapes, no sharp edges, features large enough to read on a small screen, and a pose that carries the emotion without needing the face to be visible at all.

The figure is light at 33,344 polygons and 17,182 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in a happy pose, and carries StemCell certification.

At $49 it suits animation and children's media, game characters, toy and merchandise visualization, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
