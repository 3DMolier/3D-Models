About This Model

Two neat lines of white powder on a dark surface, a compact prop for crime dramas, nightclub bathrooms and addiction stories. The Cocaine Two Lines model has 19,075 polygons and 20,423 vertices, which goes into the grainy ridges and scattered dust at the edges. Those loose grains around each line are what make the powder look real instead of like painted stripes.

Its CheckMate certification means clean geometry and simple naming, so placement on any surface is quick. The low profile of the lines catches raking light beautifully, which suits tense close-ups lit by a single lamp. It can lie on a mirror, a glass table or a phone screen in a party scene.

At $11 since 2026, this is among the cheapest story props in the range, and buyers may use it Royalty Free. Crime films, addiction awareness videos, police procedurals and narrative games benefit from such a small but loaded detail. A rolled banknote beside it completes the scene with no further explanation.
