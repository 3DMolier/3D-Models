About This Model

A modern surface-to-air missile launcher on a multi-axle truck, with vertical launch tubes mounted on the rear platform. The vehicle uses 646,588 polygons and 728,387 vertices across the wheels, cab, launcher frame and hydraulic supports. Raised missile canisters behind the cab give the launcher a profile that immediately signals air defense.

TurboSquid examined the file and granted it CheckMate Pro for clean, well organized geometry. Non-overlapping UVs let the camouflage pattern, mud and scratches be painted across the body without repeats. Launcher, cab and outriggers are separate parts, so the tubes can be raised into firing position.

Two versions are on this page: the camouflage launcher at $169 and a plain edition at $149. They became available in 2021 and fit defense analysis graphics, military documentaries and strategy game units. The camouflage version suits field scenes, while the plain one is better for parade or showroom shots.
