About This Model

The Ice Cream Balls in Cardboard Cup Collection shows scoops of ice cream in paper cups, in several flavors and colors, ready for dessert scenes. Each scoop has the rough, creamy surface of a real serving, with soft ridges left by the scoop. Set on a cafe counter in the sun, the cups suggest a refreshing summer treat.

Following CheckMate practice, the cups and scoops are separate objects with real-world size. The matte cardboard contrasts with the glossy ice cream, which benefits from subsurface scattering. Soft daylight with a pastel background makes the flavors look appetizing and bright.

Three versions are on sale here: the core assortment and two follow-up volumes with extra flavors and cup designs. Combining them builds a full ice cream shop display without the same scoop appearing twice. Out in 2026, the set serves menu design, food apps and cafe interior renders.
