About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Boeing 737-700 is a finished, render-ready model at $149. The mesh carries 634,873 polygons and 655,781 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

7 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
