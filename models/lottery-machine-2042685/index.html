About This Model

The Lottery Machine is a production-ready Furniture & Interior 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 34,387 polygons over 18,905 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
