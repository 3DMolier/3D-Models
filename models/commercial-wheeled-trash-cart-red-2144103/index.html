About This Model

The Commercial Wheeled Trash Cart is a production-ready Containers & Storage 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. You are getting 5,535 polygons and 5,753 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.
