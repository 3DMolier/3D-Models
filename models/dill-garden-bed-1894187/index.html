About This Model

A garden bed packed with feathery dill plants, ready to fill vegetable patches, allotments and backyard kitchen gardens. The Dill Garden Bed weighs in at 1,306,368 polygons and 1,061,120 vertices, because every thin frond and umbrella-shaped flower head is real geometry. The tall stems sway above a bed of dark soil, giving the planting a soft, airy outline.

Non-overlapping UVs allow fresh green leaves and yellowing tips to be painted without stretching. With no texture maps included, the soil and foliage colors can match any season you are rendering. CheckMate Pro certification signals tidy naming and a scene that opens cleanly despite the dense foliage.

Around since 2022, the bed suits garden design, farm-to-table restaurant visuals and gardening tutorials. Because the count is heavy, it shines in close shots, while distant rows may need a lighter proxy. Pair it with tomato and lettuce beds to build a complete kitchen garden in minutes.
