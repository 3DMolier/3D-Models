About This Model

A transparent tube holding a set of yellow balls for padel, ready for court benches, sports shop shelves and equipment flat lays. It uses 17,424 polygons and 17,434 vertices on the clear cylinder, the lid and the felt-covered balls inside. The see-through wall shows the contents at once, which is what makes this packaging popular on the court.

CheckMate certification keeps the tube, lid and each ball as tidy separate objects with sensible names. Fuzzy felt seams on the balls contrast with the glossy plastic, so a refractive material on the tube really pays off. The lid can come off for a shot of a player pulling a fresh ball out before a match.

Four versions are offered, starting with this clear tube at $29 and a Head racket bundled with a tube at $59. A Quicksand can and a plain tube complete the range at $29 each, all released in 2026 under a Royalty Free license. Padel club interiors, sports store displays, fitness apps and racket sport games suit them well.
