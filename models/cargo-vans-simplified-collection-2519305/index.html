About This Model

A collection of simplified cargo vans, light enough to fill streets, loading bays and parking lots without slowing a scene down. Simplified means the bodies keep their boxy panel shape, wheels and windows while dropping interior detail no camera would see.

Having several van styles in one purchase keeps scale and materials consistent across a fleet parked outside a depot. Each van follows CheckMate specification modeling, so they sit at real size next to trucks, cars and pedestrians. Plain panel sides are ideal for adding courier liveries or a client's logo as a texture decal.

Available from 2026, the vans serve city traffic visualization, logistics presentations, delivery app explainers and background traffic in driving games. Parked in a row at a distribution center, a few vans make a warehouse exterior read as busy and working. Low polygon budgets matter most in long street shots, where dozens of vehicles share the frame at once. Seen from a drone at dawn, a loading yard full of these vans tells a delivery story instantly.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Cargo Vans Simplified Collection model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Can the Cargo Vans Simplified Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

What is the Cargo Vans Simplified Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Cargo Vans Simplified Collection mesh?

307,522 polygons and 320,038 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. 307,522 polygons reads as dense next to the roughly 28,000 median of our Model Bundles & Sets range.
