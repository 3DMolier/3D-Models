About This Model

This general model - MatchBook Cover Open - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. You are getting 5,818 polygons and 5,940 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX and product rendering.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
