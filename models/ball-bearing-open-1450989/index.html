About This Model

Ball Bearing Open belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 9,244 polygons and 9,292 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 5 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
