About This Model

Bunch of Dark Grapes is a detailed Food & Beverages asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $69. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags food photography and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
