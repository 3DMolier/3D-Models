About This Model

The Turbofan Aircraft Scale Model is a production-ready Toys & Games 3D model, priced at $69 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 9,969 polygons and 10,161 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and aerospace visualization.
