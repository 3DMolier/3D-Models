About This Model

A bagel is boiled before it is baked, and that is the only thing that makes it a bagel. The brief moment in water sets the crust, which is why the inside stays dense and why nothing shaped like one but baked straight is the same thing.

The model holds 44,904 polygons and 24,848 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $29 it suits food and bakery visualization, packaging and menu imagery, advertising, cafe and kitchen scenes, film set dressing and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
