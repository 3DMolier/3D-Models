About This Model

A collection of Nike sports water bottles with squeeze bodies, flip caps and the swoosh printed on the side. The bottles vary in color and cap style, the kind runners grab from a gym bag or a bike cage. Soft plastic walls are shaped with grip zones at the middle, so a hand wraps around them naturally.

Being a branded product, the set ships under an Editorial Uses Only license from TurboSquid. Construction follows the CheckMate specification, so scale and naming match marketplace standards. Translucent plastic shaders show a hint of water inside, which makes the bottles look ready for training.

Released in 2023, the bottles fit sports journalism illustrations, fitness documentaries and gym interior scenes for editorial stories. Left on a bench beside a towel and sneakers, a bottle shows the pause between sets at a training session. Reviewers of sports gear can also place them in comparison shots of hydration equipment.
