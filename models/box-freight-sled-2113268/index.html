About This Model

Looking for a Vehicles asset? The Box Freight Sled is a finished, render-ready model at $18. Counted at the source, the model is 34,950 polygons and 18,927 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
