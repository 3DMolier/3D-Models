Looking for a Space & Sci-Fi asset? The Air Taxi Landing Pad is a finished, render-ready model at $69. The mesh carries 639,265 polygons and 357,970 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
