About This Model

A set of dartboards with the classic segmented ring layout, ready to hang on a pub wall or in a basement game room. The board faces show the alternating colored segments, the wire spider that divides them and the bull at the center. Sold as a set, the boards let you dress several lanes of a darts bar without hunting for matching props.

Construction follows the CheckMate specification, so geometry and scene layout match TurboSquid's stated quality standard. Every board keeps the sisal look of a real bristle face, which catches soft light well in close shots. Darts stuck in the boards are a simple addition if the scene calls for a game in progress.

In the catalog since 2015, the set dresses sports bar interiors, man cave renders and casual game scenes. Mount one beside a chalk scoreboard under a hanging lamp, and the corner of a British pub is complete. It also works in a college dorm or a garage workshop where friends gather on weekends.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
