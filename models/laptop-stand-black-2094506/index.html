About This Model

Looking for a Electronics & Gadgets asset? The Laptop Stand Black is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 7,704 polygons and 4,111 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
