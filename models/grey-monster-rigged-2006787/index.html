About This Model

Priced at $169, the Grey Monster Rigged is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2023, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 19,746 polygons and 9,885 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
