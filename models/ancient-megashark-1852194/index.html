About This Model

Priced at $129, the Ancient Megashark is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 10,663 polygons over 10,831 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
