About This Model

A set of American military rotorcraft collected in one package, made for defense, aviation and war film scenes of every scale. Getting several helicopters in one purchase keeps detail, scale and modeling style consistent across a whole fleet. Parked in a row on a flight line, the aircraft turn an empty airbase into an active one.

The collection carries CheckMate Pro certification, so the files passed TurboSquid's manual inspection of topology and naming. Rigging is part of the package, as the name says, so rotors and other moving parts can be animated. Use the aircraft as background traffic over a base or as hero units in close-up action.

At $599 the bundle suits studios that need several airframes rather than a single hero model. First issued in 2018, it fits military documentaries, flight simulators, strategy games, air show recaps and training visualizations. Send a pair low over a desert ridge at sunset, and the shot practically writes its own soundtrack.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2018 · Updated 11 November 2026

How clean is the geometry on the Rigged US Military Helicopters Collection model?
