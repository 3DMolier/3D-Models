About This Model

Priced at $99, the Brown Rhesus Macaque Walking is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 14,768 polygons over 15,389 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
