About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Christmas Ball Wreath is a finished, render-ready model at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 683,168 polygons and 619,394 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
