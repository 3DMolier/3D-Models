About This Model

The Landrace is the long white pig with ears that fall forward over its eyes, and those ears are the identification: no other common breed carries them that way. The body is correspondingly long, bred for bacon rather than for bulk, which gives the animal a quite different profile from the compact breeds.

The mesh is light at 16,029 polygons and 16,205 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 15 texture maps at a mix of sizes up to 4096x4096 carrying the skin, the sparse bristles and the dirt.

This page carries walking and standing poses at the same $79 and a rigged build at $99. Aimed at farming and livestock visualization, food industry and agriculture material, education content, advertising and games. Built to StemCell specification, on sale since 2019.
