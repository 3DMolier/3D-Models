About This Model

Priced at $139, the Experimental Aircraft Martin Marietta X-24B is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 82,247 polygons and 89,373 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 4.9 x 11.23 x H2.58 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
