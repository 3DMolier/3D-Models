About This Model

VHS E180 Cassette with Cover is one of the Signage & Decor models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $29. Geometry weighs in at 45,963 polygons over 45,012 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
