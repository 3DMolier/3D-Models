About This Model

The White Female Hand Rigged is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $99 on TurboSquid. CheckMate certification confirms the mesh passed TurboSquid's manual quality audit: clean topology, sane object naming, correct units and no stray geometry. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Geometry weighs in at 4,698 polygons over 2,719 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
