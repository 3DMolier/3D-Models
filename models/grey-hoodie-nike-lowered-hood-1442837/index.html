About This Model

The Hoodie Lowered Hood is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 10,012 polygons and 10,092 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 4 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
