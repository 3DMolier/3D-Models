About This Model

This rotary lift stacks cars on a vertical carousel, so one small plot of land can hold a whole row of vehicles. The 16 Place Rotary Car Parking Lift System spends 979,425 polygons and 533,824 vertices on its steel frame. That budget also covers the chain drive and the swinging pallets that carry each car around the loop.

Most of the density sits in the uprights and cross bracing, which read clearly when the camera looks up the tower. The structure passed CheckMate Pro inspection, so the mesh arrives clean, at real scale, with nothing hidden inside the columns.

First offered in 2023, the tower suits urban planning scenes where parking density is part of the argument. Architects place it beside apartment blocks to show how a narrow plot can solve a parking shortage. Animators rotate the pallets one by one to explain the mechanism to a planning committee. In busy street scenes, its tall open frame breaks up flat facades with believable industrial detail.
