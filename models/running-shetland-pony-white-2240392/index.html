About This Model

Priced at $99, the Shetland Pony is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 19,100 polygons over 19,178 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.
