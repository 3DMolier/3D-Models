About This Model

Priced at $59, the 4WD Chassis is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 11,051 polygons and 13,065 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2019 · Updated 4 September 2026

Questions About This Model

What texture resolution ships with the 4WD Chassis?

5 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the 4WD Chassis mesh?

11,051 polygons and 13,065 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Can the 4WD Chassis model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How much does the 4WD Chassis 3D model cost?

$59 USD, paid once. The Royalty Free license means no recurring cost and no extra payment when the finished work ships.
