About This Model

Looking for a 3D asset? The Scales Piggy Bank And Coins is a finished, render-ready model at $69. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.

The mesh carries 448,524 polygons and 448,941 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 16 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
