About This Model

This is the rigged edition of a touring caravan with a full interior and front bike rack, made for animated road-trip sequences. The Camping Trailer with Interior and Bike Rack Rigged contains 274,055 polygons and 293,788 vertices across the shell, interior and bicycles. Because it is prepared for animation, the caravan can follow a car down a road instead of sitting still.

CheckMate review confirms the geometry is clean and the parts are named sensibly for animators. Rounded front corners and a wide panoramic window define the classic European touring shape. Inside, a dinette and bed give the camera a reason to cut to the cabin during a stop.

It costs $169 and has been available since 2026 with Royalty Free licensing. Road movies, holiday animations, camping games and motorway simulations all suit an animated caravan like this. Hitched behind a family car, it tells a summer vacation story in a single frame.
