About This Model

A tall straddle grape harvester that drives over vineyard rows, shaking the vines and collecting fruit in side bins. The 163,739 polygons and 174,636 vertices form a subdivision model with a raised cab, a tunnel frame and large wheels. High ground clearance lets it straddle a row of vines, which is the image that makes it recognizable.

The model has no texture maps, and its yellow body and steel parts rely on simple shaders. UVs are spread without overlap, giving space for mud, grape juice stains or dealer decals. Verified to CheckMate Pro, the harvester keeps correct proportions next to vines and tractors.

Four versions are offered, static at $99 and rigged at $139, in branded and generic forms. Wine region documentaries, vineyard visualization and agricultural training account for its 12 sales since 2020. At harvest time on a sunny slope, the machine brings motion to rows of vines.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2020 · Updated 14 November 2026
