About This Model

Pallet Jack and Wooden Pallet Set belongs to our Kitchen & Tableware range and is offered at $34 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. 2015 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

Geometry weighs in at 86,920 polygons over 88,608 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 11 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
