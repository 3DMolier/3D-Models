About This Model

A set of ice cream vending machines with the supplies that surround them, ready to fill a mall corner, station or cafe. The machines carry glass fronts that reveal the frozen treats inside, the single detail that makes a vending kiosk read correctly. Supporting supplies fill the gaps around the units, so the corner looks in use rather than freshly installed.

With CheckMate Pro certification, every unit opens at correct size, with tidy topology and clearly named materials. Each item is a separate object, so machines can be lined up, rotated or swapped without breaking the rest of the arrangement.

On sale since 2018, the collection serves retail interior design, franchise planning and food service presentations. Shopping center visuals and transit hub renders often need exactly this kind of quick, cheerful kiosk to feel populated. Warm interior light reflecting on the glass doors gives the units an inviting glow in evening scenes.
