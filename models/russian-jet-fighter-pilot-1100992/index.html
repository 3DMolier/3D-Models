About This Model

A pilot figure in full kit is a layered build: flight suit, anti-g garment, harness, survival vest, gloves, boots and a helmet with an oxygen mask. Each layer changes the silhouette, and the bulk sits in specific places rather than uniformly, which is what separates a real aircrew figure from a character in a jumpsuit.

The mesh runs 70,625 polygons and 73,918 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and ships with 28 texture maps - 27 at 4096x4096 and one at 512x512, carrying the webbing, the buckles and the patches.

This page carries five other builds, including standing and walking poses at $149 and $119 and a rigged version at $149. Aimed at military aviation visualization, film and television, documentary work, training material and games. On sale since 2017.
