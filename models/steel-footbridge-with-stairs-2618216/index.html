About This Model

A steel pedestrian footbridge with stairs at each end, the kind that carries walkers over railway tracks and busy roads. The Steel Footbridge with Stairs uses 177,670 polygons for the truss sides, treads, landings and handrails.

There are 177,452 vertices, and the near-equal count suggests a carefully closed structural mesh. Following CheckMate rules, the bridge lands in your scene with correct scale and parts that are easy to select. The open truss lets light and views pass through, so the crossing feels airy rather than heavy.

Since 2026 the bridge has carried a $39 price and suits urban renders, train station scenes and city games. Span it over a rail line between two platforms, or across a highway linking a park to a neighborhood. Urban planners can preview how a crossing would look before committing to a design. Pedestrian crowds walking up the stairs bring the structure to life in an animation.
