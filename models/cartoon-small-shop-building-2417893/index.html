About This Model

The Cartoon Small Shop Building is a production-ready Architecture & Landmarks 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. You are getting 200 polygons and 240 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Real-world footprint is 587.62 x 790.93 x H528.03 cm, so it drops into a scene at correct scale without a rescaling pass. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
