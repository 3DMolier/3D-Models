About This Model

A human brain with a microchip implanted in its surface, prepared as a printable piece for sci-fi props and neuroscience displays. The folds of the cortex contrast with the flat, geometric chip, which makes the idea of a brain-computer interface obvious at once. The chip sits in the outer cortex with fine traces running across it, a detail that survives even small prints.

As a print file it is built to come out of a slicer as a solid, watertight object. Print the brain in pink or gray resin and paint the chip metallic, and the piece looks like an artifact from a near-future lab. Its geometry follows CheckMate norms, keeping the file clean and quick to load.

Since 2024 it has found a place in tech conference displays, escape room props, cyberpunk cosplay and science classrooms. It also works as a striking render for articles on neural implants and the ethics of brain technology. Priced at $89 the file goes straight to the slicer without repair work.
