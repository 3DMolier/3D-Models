About This Model

A can in ice rather than a bottle changes the register entirely. It is casual, immediate and unpretentious, which is exactly the positioning a great many drinks brands spend a fortune trying to achieve.

The model holds 35,478 polygons and 36,135 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, supplying the bucket together with the can. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $33 it suits beverage and packaging visualization, e-commerce and retail imagery, advertising, bar and event scenes, film set dressing and editorial illustration. On sale since 2025.
