The Piece of Anchor Ship Chain is a production-ready Tools 3D model, priced at $9 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 3,120 polygons over 3,100 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
