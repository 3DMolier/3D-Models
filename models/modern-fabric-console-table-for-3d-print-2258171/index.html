About This Model

A modern console table with a fabric-wrapped look, prepared as a printable piece for miniatures and dollhouse interiors. The mesh holds 1,620 polygons over 1,646 vertices, a compact count that slices quickly for a desktop printer. Its slim top and solid side panels give a stable base, which matters when the part prints without supports.

UVs are non-overlapping, so the same file also works for interior renders with a woven fabric texture applied. No textures come in the package, letting you choose a woven material or print in a single color. CheckMate review covered the closed geometry, real proportions and naming of the table.

Hallway renders, miniature furniture collections and tabletop game scenes have used it since 2024. Royalty Free terms permit commercial printing projects, interior renders and personal hobby use alike. Place a lamp and a small vase on top, and an entry hall looks furnished in a single step.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2024 · Updated 13 November 2026
