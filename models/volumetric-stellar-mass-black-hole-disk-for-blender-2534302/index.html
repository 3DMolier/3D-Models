About This Model

A volumetric accretion disk around a stellar mass black hole for Blender, made for space documentaries and science fiction close-ups. The glowing ring of gas wraps around a dark center, and as a volume it keeps depth when the camera orbits or tilts. Thin and dense regions of the disk give the swirling look that astronomers' illustrations of black holes usually show.

There is no polygon mesh to manage, so all of the work happens in the volume shader and the render settings. Built to CheckMate specification, the file opens with a clean scene structure, ready to append to an existing Blender project. Raising the emission strength turns the disk white-hot, while a lower value gives a calmer orange glow for wide shots.

New in 2026, it fits astrophysics lessons, planetarium content, game cutscenes and title sequences that need a cosmic event. A slow camera move toward the dark center sells the scale of the object better than any still frame. Pair it with a star field and a distant nebula to build a complete deep space scene.
