About This Model

An adjustable surgical clamp with blue grips, used to hold tendon ends together in position while a surgeon sutures them. The instrument carries 9,854 polygons and 9,839 vertices, enough for the jaw teeth, the slider and the locking mechanism. Its adjustable span lets the clamp be posed open or closed, matching the step being shown in a procedure.

It was produced to CheckMate guidance on size and names, though a TurboSquid inspector has not certified it. UVs that never overlap let steel, blue polymer and fine graduations be textured with precision for close medical shots. Faces and vertices match closely, the sign of a sealed, tidy mesh that holds up under an operating lamp.

A 2025 instrument, it suits orthopedic surgery animations, medical device catalogs, surgical training modules and hand surgery lectures. Place it over a tendon model with sutures, and a repair step becomes clear to students at a glance. For $19, it gives medical animators a specialist tool that generic surgical instrument kits rarely include at all.
