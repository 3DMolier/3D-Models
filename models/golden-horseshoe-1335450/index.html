About This Model

The Golden Horseshoe is a production-ready Industrial Equipment 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 2,026 polygons and 2,004 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 3 maps at 4096 x 4096. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. 2018 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
