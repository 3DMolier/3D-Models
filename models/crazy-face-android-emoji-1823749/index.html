About This Model

Priced at $11, the Crazy Face Android Emoji is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 1,102 polygons and 1,104 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
