About This Model

This slim aluminum soda can carries the bright orange look of the Mirinda soft drink in the compact European size. The rolled lip, the stay-on tab and the domed bottom follow real can construction closely. Its narrow profile stands naturally in fridges, vending machines and picnic coolers next to other drinks.

TurboSquid certified the can as CheckMate Pro for clean geometry, correct scale and tidy naming. The printed label wraps the full body, while the brushed metal top shows the ring pull clearly. Condensation or dents can be added on top of the base shading for chilled or crushed variants.

Released in 2015 at $19, it suits beverage visualizations, convenience store scenes and kitchen interiors. Game artists use cans like this as pickups, trash props and fridge fillers in apartment levels. Packaging studios also compare can sizes and label layouts with models of this kind.
