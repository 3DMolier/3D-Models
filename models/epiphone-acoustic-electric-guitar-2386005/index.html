About This Model

Looking for a Musical Instruments asset? The Epiphone Acoustic Electric Guitar is a finished, render-ready model at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 13,811 polygons and 14,283 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
