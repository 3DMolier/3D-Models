About This Model

Rigged SWAT police officers in tactical gear, gathered for raid scenes, crime dramas and law enforcement training. Helmets, body armor, goggles and holstered equipment are the features that make tactical units read at once. Since the officers are rigged, they can be posed in stacks at doorways or animated during a building entry.

CheckMate rules shaped the collection, so each officer comes at human scale with organized, clearly named parts. Dark uniforms with reflective lettering stand out under flashlights, which suits night operations and tense interior shots. Each figure is separate, so a team can be spread along a corridor or grouped behind a vehicle.

This version is prepared for Maya, and a Cinema 4D version of the same team also costs $99. Dating from 2020, the officers fit police games, crime films, security training and news reconstructions of hostage incidents. Light the scene with red and blue strobes outside, and the arrival of the team feels urgent.
