About This Model

A weathered wooden gallows with steps, crossbeam and trapdoor, this structure sets a grim tone for medieval squares and frontier towns. The 15,001 polygons and 15,788 vertices describe the heavy timber posts, the plank deck, the stair railing and the iron fittings.

At 642.73 x 395.32 x H550.64 cm the platform towers over a crowd, matching the scale of real historical scaffolds. Non-overlapping UVs suit cracked, sun-bleached wood textures, and the timber geometry obeys CheckMate specification. Fog, crows and an overcast sky turn the empty platform into a powerful mood piece without any characters.

Both $39 versions show the same grim structure, one named Ancient Gallows and the other Execution Gallows. Available since 2025 and Royalty Free, the model fits historical dramas, horror games and justice history documentaries. Placed at the edge of a village map, the gallows quietly tells players what kind of world they have entered. Pirate harbor towns and witch trial stories are other settings where such a scaffold belongs.

Questions About This Model

Can the Ancient Gallows model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

How heavy is the Ancient Gallows mesh?

15,001 polygons and 15,788 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. 15,001 polygons reads as economical next to the roughly 53,000 median of our Architecture & Landmarks range.

What size is the Ancient Gallows in real-world units?

642.73 x 395.32 x H550.64 cm. The model is built at true scale, so it lands at the right size next to other objects instead of needing a unit fix on import.
