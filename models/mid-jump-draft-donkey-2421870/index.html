About This Model

Mid-Jump Draft Donkey belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 11,000 polygons over 11,336 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
