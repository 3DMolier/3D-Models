About This Model

A slim folding keyboard cover for a tablet, with a fabric-covered keyboard and a segmented flap that props the screen up. The 23,102 polygons and 23,096 vertices define each key, the fold lines and the magnetic connector strip. Shown folded on a desk or wrapped around a tablet, it reads as a compact mobile workstation.

4 maps at 8192 x 8192 carry the woven fabric texture, so even macro shots of the keys look tactile. As a StemCell model, it keeps the same look in real-time viewers and offline production renders.

This page groups four versions, all at $29, covering two sizes plus rigged builds. Besides this cover you get the Smart Keyboard 12 9 inch 3D Model, the Apple Smart Keyboard 11inch Rigged and a Smart Keyboard Rigged model. The rigged versions let the cover fold and unfold on camera, which suits product demonstration animations. Available since 2018, it serves gadget visualization, workspace scenes and tech tutorials about tablet accessories.
