About This Model

Man Hands 2 is one of the Medical models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $49. The polygon count is 6,512, spread over 6,536 vertices. Whether that fits depends on where it lands - an architectural still and a real-time walkthrough tolerate very different counts.

You get 10 maps at 4096 x 4096 - enough resolution to hold a close shot. Edge flow follows the form rather than fighting it, which is what lets the silhouette hold up when the camera moves in. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
