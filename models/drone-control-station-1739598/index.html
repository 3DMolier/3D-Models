About This Model

Looking for a Toys & Games asset? The Drone Control Station is a finished, render-ready model at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 118,343 polygons and 122,487 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and aerospace visualization.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for product advertising, packaging renders, game props and film set dressing.
