About This Model

Residential Kennel belongs to our Industrial Equipment range and is offered at $34 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 30,923 polygons over 32,322 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
