About This Model

A small cardboard box of rimfire rifle cartridges, with a flip-open lid revealing neat rows of brass rounds inside. The compact printed box is the everyday ammunition of target shooters and small game hunters. Lines of bright brass casings inside catch light, making the open box readable from a distance.

Under its CheckMate Pro certificate, the box sits at true size beside rifles and hands. Print your own label design on the cardboard to fit any setting, country or historical era. Its small size makes it a perfect detail prop for close tabletop shots.

Launched in 2015, the box sits on gun shop counters, shooting range benches and hunting cabin tables. Scatter a few loose rounds beside it on a workbench next to a rifle, and a range day scene is ready. Firearms safety courses use it to show proper ammunition storage, locked away from children and visitors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
