About This Model

The intermediate bulk container standardised liquid logistics the way the shipping container standardised everything else. A fixed footprint that matches a pallet, a cage that stacks, and a valve at the bottom because gravity is free.

The model is light at 37,173 polygons and 18,883 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, in stainless steel. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $39 it suits industrial and logistics visualization, chemical and food processing scenes, equipment marketing, warehouse interiors, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2024.
