About This Model

This collection gathers classical nymph sculptures, graceful female figures in flowing drapery poised on stone bases. Each statue shows soft folds of fabric and relaxed poses typical of classical garden sculpture in European estates. Placed along a path or around a fountain, they give a formal garden its old-world character.

The set passed CheckMate Pro review, so each figure has clean naming and a consistent real-world size. That makes it simple to place a matching row of statues along a park path or a terrace. Raking sunlight in the late afternoon brings out the carved folds and the curves of each figure.

Two versions are available: the Nymphs Sculptures Collection at $99 and a second nymph set at $119. The first bundle was launched in 2023 and is licensed royalty free for any project. Landscape architects, museum planners and fantasy game artists use these figures for gardens, galleries and ancient ruins.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
