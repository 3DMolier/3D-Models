About This Model

Red Fire Extinguisher is one of the Tools models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for technical visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

Geometry weighs in at 7,856 polygons over 7,890 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 4 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
