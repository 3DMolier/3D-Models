About This Model

A long plier-style puller with rubber covered handles, it grabs spark plug boots without tearing the ignition wire. The Automotive Spark Plug Boot Puller with Rubber Grip uses just 2,360 polygons for the jaws, the pivot and the grips.

StemCell construction brings multiple formats from one source and PBR materials that keep the rubber and steel distinct. The unwrap is free of overlaps over 2,386 vertices, leaving room for oil stains or worn grip texture. Lay it on a fender cover beside a socket set for an instant workshop moment.

Two versions share the page: this rubber grip puller and a wire removal plier with a curved jaw. The curved jaw reaches plugs tucked deep in an engine bay, which a mechanic will notice. The tool measures 6.42 x 1.9 x H28.21 cm, the length of a real puller in a technician's hand. Introduced in 2025 and licensed without royalties, it fits repair guides, garage games and auto parts stores.
