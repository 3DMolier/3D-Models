About This Model

Priced at $19, the Handshake Gesture Emoji is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 1,376 polygons over 1,376 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 5 textures, authored at 1024 x 1024. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
