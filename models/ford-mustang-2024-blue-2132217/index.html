About This Model

Ford Mustang 2024 is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. Counted at the source, the model is 477,826 polygons and 253,439 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic.
