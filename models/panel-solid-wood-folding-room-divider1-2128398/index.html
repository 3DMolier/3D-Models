About This Model

This solid wood folding room divider joins tall panels with hinges, so it can zigzag across a room to screen off a corner. The 20,501 polygons describe the panel frames, the inset boards and the hinge hardware between them.

A vertex count of 39,332 runs higher than the faces, which happens when panels are split into separate parts for folding. The UV islands do not overlap, letting warm oak, dark walnut or painted finishes be applied evenly along every panel. The asset met the CheckMate Lite bar at TurboSquid, meaning it imports cleanly into common tools.

Introduced in 2023, the divider suits bedroom interiors, studio apartments and dressing areas in boutiques. Angle the panels to hide a desk or a wardrobe, and the room immediately gains a sense of privacy. Photographers and set dressers also use the screen as a backdrop behind portraits or product shots.
