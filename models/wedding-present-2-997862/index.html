About This Model

Looking for a Signage & Decor asset? The Wedding Present 2 is a finished, render-ready model at $29. You are getting 5,278 polygons and 5,301 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 2 maps at 4096 x 4096. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2016, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
