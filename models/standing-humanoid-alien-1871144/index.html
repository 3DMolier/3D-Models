About This Model

Standing Humanoid Alien belongs to our Animals & Creatures range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. On import you get 7,420 polygons and 7,432 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Parts are separated where separation is useful, so a single component can be hidden, swapped or re-shaded on its own. On this listing the stated applications are film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering.

Its first release was in 2022, and it has earned its place in the catalog since then. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
