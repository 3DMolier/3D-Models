About This Model

A set of sci-fi hydraulic rams with chunky barrels and polished piston rods, ready to drive mech legs, hangar doors and robotic arms. The cylinders come in different lengths and mounting styles, so a large machine can use several without visible repetition. Each piston and barrel are separate parts, which lets you extend the rod for animated pushing motions.

CheckMate Pro certification guarantees correct scale and clean naming across every piece in the kit. Panel lines and bolted end caps give the surfaces enough breakup for a hard-surface look without extra texturing. The proportions follow real industrial rams, so the parts read as plausible engineering rather than pure decoration.

In the range since 2016, the set serves mech designers, spaceship interior artists and game teams building industrial levels. Paired along a loading crane or the knee joint of a walker, the rams immediately suggest heavy force. Kitbashers can also bury them inside larger structures where only the rod ends peek out.
