About This Model

Stone Barbecue belongs to our Furniture & Interior range and is offered at $29 through the TurboSquid marketplace. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualisation, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 32,932 polygons over 35,979 vertices - a light build for its class. It ships 14 maps with the top set authored at 4096x4096 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
