About This Model

This Industrial Equipment model - Electric Hand Mixer 5 Speed - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX. First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
