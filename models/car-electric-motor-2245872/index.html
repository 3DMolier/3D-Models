About This Model

An electric car motor with its housing, cooling channels and output shaft, this unit shows what replaces the engine in an EV. It keeps to 8,743 polygons and 10,141 vertices, enough to define the cylindrical stator casing, mounting brackets and cable terminals. Its compact, rounded form shows viewers how small an electric drivetrain can be compared with a combustion engine.

Made to CheckMate specification, the motor arrives at real scale with logical names in the scene. Non-overlapping UVs give room for a manufacturer plate, warning labels or cutaway shading on the casing. The light mesh keeps it fast in real-time applications such as configurators or VR training.

Launched in 2024, it fits automotive engineering presentations, EV explainer videos, technical schools and mechanic training games. Show it beside a battery pack and an inverter to explain how power flows to the wheels. Garages moving into EV service can use the asset in tutorials before technicians handle a real unit.
