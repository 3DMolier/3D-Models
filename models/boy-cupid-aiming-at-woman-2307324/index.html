About This Model

Boy Cupid Aiming at Woman shows a winged cherub drawing his bow at an unsuspecting woman, a playful staging of love at first sight. The pair use 43,641 polygons and 59,059 vertices, enough for feathered wings, curls and a drawn bowstring without heavy scenes. Cupid floats slightly above the woman, so the arrow's path creates a diagonal that leads the eye.

StemCell status gives buyers native files for the main 3D packages, each with matching physically based materials. UV islands are separate, letting skin tones and fabric be edited cleanly on both figures. The woman's casual stance and unaware expression set up the humor before the arrow lands.

Since 2024 the cupid scene has been at home in Valentine's Day greetings, wedding invitations and dating app illustrations. Its light weight also fits real-time projects such as romantic mini games or AR filters. Soft pink lighting and floating hearts added in post complete the storybook mood.
