About This Model

A collection of tiny houses on wheeled trailers, compact homes for minimalist living, campsites and off-grid scenes in nature. Each house combines a pitched or flat roof, wood or metal cladding and small windows on a sturdy trailer chassis with visible wheels. Varied styles let you build a tiny home village or place a single cabin by a lake.

The collection follows CheckMate standards, keeping each house and trailer neatly organized for quick placement. Porches and steps are modeled, so the homes look lived in rather than freshly delivered. Warm wood tones and large windows glow invitingly when interior lights are turned on at dusk.

The Wheeled Tiny Houses Collection was introduced in 2024 with no royalty fees for commercial projects. Real estate concepts, sustainable living articles, camping games and architecture studies use these homes widely. A forest clearing or mountain view behind them sells the dream of simple living for viewers.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

How heavy is the Wheeled Tiny Houses Collection mesh?

478,663 polygons and 261,709 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Our Model Bundles & Sets listings sit at a median of about 28,000 polygons, so 478,663 is heavier than the typical one.

What is the Wheeled Tiny Houses Collection model typically used for?

The listing names game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the main applications. In practice it also works anywhere a finished model bundles & sets object is needed without modeling it from scratch.

Can the Wheeled Tiny Houses Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

Can the Wheeled Tiny Houses Collection model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.
