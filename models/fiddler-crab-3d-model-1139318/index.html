About This Model

Crab is a detailed Animals & Creatures asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $99. Counted at the source, the model is 10,382 polygons and 10,510 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 3 maps at 4096 x 4096. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.
