About This Model

This office multifunction printer combines a flatbed scanner lid, a control panel, paper trays and an output bin in one boxy floor unit. Its familiar shape tells viewers at once that the scene is a workplace, from a small business to a corporate open space. Paper drawers, hinged covers and the panel screen give the unit enough surface interest for medium shots near desks and printing corners.

Modeling kept to the CheckMate rules from TurboSquid for a clean mesh, sensible names and correct physical scale on import. Plastic panels in light gray and black work well under bright office lighting, where soft shadows outline every tray and cover. Without visible brand marks, the copier fits any fictional company and avoids confusion with a real product on screen.

This unit dates from 2024 and costs $129, licensed Royalty Free for commercial interiors, films and games. Office designers place it in print corners of open floor plans, and training videos show staff how scanning and copying work. Workplace sitcoms, office games and stock imagery also use it as a corner prop beside water coolers and filing cabinets.
