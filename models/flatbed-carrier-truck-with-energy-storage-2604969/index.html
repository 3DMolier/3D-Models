About This Model

A heavy flatbed truck hauling a containerized energy storage unit, built for renewable power and logistics scenes. The 636,156 polygons cover the cab, chassis, wheels and the battery container strapped on the deck. The container sits low on the bed with tie-downs, giving a believable weight to the load.

Built to CheckMate specification, it carries 682,516 vertices with the cargo separate from the truck. Container panels and truck paint take procedural grime easily, which suits the partial UV coverage. You can remove the container to use the truck as an empty flatbed elsewhere in the scene.

Selling at $189 from 2026, the model suits grid storage explainers, energy logistics renders and freight visualization. The long side profile reads well in drone tracking shots, while ribbed container walls catch sunlight in strips. Park it beside a solar farm or substation to show battery delivery in action on a remote site far from the grid.
