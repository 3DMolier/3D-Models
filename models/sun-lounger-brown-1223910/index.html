Sun Lounger Brown is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Geometry weighs in at 12,094 polygons over 12,325 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
