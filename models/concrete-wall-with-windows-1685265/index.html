About This Model

A plain concrete wall section pierced by rectangular windows, a modular piece for building facades and industrial ruins. With 1,884 polygons the wall is almost weightless, spending geometry only on window reveals and edges. Its 1,890 vertices make it easy to tile across a long facade without slowing the viewport.

There are no texture maps, so the concrete look comes from your own shaders or tileable maps. Overlap-free UVs make projecting cast concrete, stains or graffiti onto the panel straightforward. TurboSquid gave the panel CheckMate Pro after checking that its scale, naming and topology were correct.

Out from 2021, the section suits brutalist architecture renders, abandoned factory levels and urban planning blocks. Repeat it along a street and add broken glass in a few frames to build a derelict district quickly. Architecture students often take the panel as a starting point for studying window rhythm on facades.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2021 · Updated 17 November 2026
