About This Model

A collection of wall outlets and power strips with plugs, the small electrical details that make any interior feel properly wired. Wall plates, multi-socket strips and switches cover what an architect or prop artist usually needs along a skirting board.

With CheckMate Pro certification, each piece arrives at true scale with clean geometry in every delivered format. Recessed socket holes and slightly rounded plastic covers keep the objects believable even in tight close-ups. Every outlet is separate, so they can be spaced along walls, desks and kitchen counters as the layout requires.

On offer since 2019, the collection serves interior visualization, home improvement guides and office scenes in games. A power strip crowded with chargers under a desk instantly says that someone works long hours there. Safety trainers also use these sockets in videos about overloaded circuits, where a clear model explains the risk better than words.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
