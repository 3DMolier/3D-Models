About This Model

A set of makeup sponges in several shapes, from teardrop blenders to flat wedges, ready for beauty and cosmetics scenes. Each sponge has the soft rounded form and slightly porous look that tells viewers they are made for blending foundation. Because the pieces are separate, you can scatter them on a vanity or line them up in a row.

The collection passed CheckMate Pro, TurboSquid's top inspection level for topology and scene structure. Sponges like these look best with a soft diffuse shader and a touch of subsurface scattering for the foam. Colors can be changed per piece to match a brand palette or a seasonal beauty campaign.

The set arrived in 2023 and gathers several related cosmetics props in one purchase. Beauty product renders, makeup tutorial thumbnails and bathroom interiors are typical places for these small tools. Next to a compact powder and brushes, they complete a dressing table without extra modeling.
