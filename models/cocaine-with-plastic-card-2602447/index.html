About This Model

A small heap of white powder with a plastic card chopping it into lines, built for crime scenes and dramatic close-ups. It uses 51,246 polygons and 53,003 vertices to sculpt the uneven pile, the card edge and the fine trails it leaves. The card resting at an angle shows action in progress, as if someone just stepped away.

Thanks to CheckMate certification, the powder and card stay separate clean objects that are easy to move or replace. The plain card surface can carry any design, from a hotel key to a credit card belonging to a character. Scattered grains around the pile give the surface a messy, lived-in look under harsh light.

The Cocaine with Plastic Card prop was introduced in 2026 at $24 and carries Royalty Free licensing. Detective series, drug enforcement documentaries, nightclub sets and gritty games are the natural places for it. Framed on a mirror with a lamp overhead, it delivers a familiar cinematic beat in one image.
