About This Model

The Boeing 777-300ER Generic is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. It comes in at 757,593 polygons, with 777,661 vertices behind them. Heavy or light is a question about the scene, not about the model, so check it against the platform you are shipping to.

Surfacing relies on 12 maps authored at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
