About This Model

Priced at $99, the Piper Cub Aircraft is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 69,744 polygons over 71,718 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
