About This Model

A set of Panasonic coin and button cells in common sizes, made for electronics close-ups, repair guides and battery explainers. The Button Cells Battery Panasonic Collection keeps the whole set to 1,060 polygons, with smooth rounded edges and flat faces on each cell. Its 572 vertices make the collection extremely light, so cells can sit inside watches, key fobs, scales and remotes.

Built to CheckMate specification, each cell carries real proportions and a clean name in the scene hierarchy. UVs are laid out without overlaps, ready for printed markings, the positive symbol and brushed steel finishes on both sides.

Released in 2024, the collection suits tech journalism, product reviews and educational content about small batteries and child safety. The license is Editorial Uses Only, so it fits news, reviews and teaching material about consumer electronics. Lay a few cells on a workbench beside tweezers and an open watch back, and the repair scene is ready.
