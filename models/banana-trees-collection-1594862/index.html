About This Model

This collection gathers banana plants with broad, split leaves, the quick way to give a tropical garden or plantation scene its character. Each plant stands alone in the file, so groves can be built with varied heights, spacing and rotation. Wide paddle leaves cast big soft shadows, a signature look of the tropics in daylight renders.

This bundle earned CheckMate Pro marks for topology, naming and accurate scale of every plant. Torn leaf edges and fibrous trunks give the plants believable detail in close views. Bright green foliage pairs naturally with sand, wooden decks, thatched roofs and turquoise water.

Banana Trees Collection has grown in our plant range since 2020, supplying resort designs, jungle games and farming visuals. Planted around a beach bungalow or a pool, a few trees turn a plain lot into a tropical retreat. Wind animation on the large leaves adds gentle motion that makes a scene feel warm, humid and alive.
