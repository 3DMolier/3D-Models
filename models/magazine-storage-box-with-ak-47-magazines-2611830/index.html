About This Model

An open storage box packed with curved AK 47 magazines, the kind found in armories and insurgent hideouts. The Magazine Storage Box with AK 47 Magazines uses 103,727 polygons and 104,956 vertices, giving each magazine its ribbing and lip. The curved banana shape of each magazine is the instantly recognizable signature of the Kalashnikov family.

It measures 73.39 x 98.07 x H100.51 cm, a tall container that fits in a vehicle bay or bunker corner. Non-overlapping UVs give every surface unique texture space for scratches, rust, dust and painted stencil markings. StemCell delivery ensures materials stay consistent across formats for game and film pipelines.

It costs $39 and first appeared in 2026 as one standalone version. Shooter games, war films, police raid scenes and military training content use it to fill armories and supply rooms. Rows of stacked magazines read clearly even from mid distance, which suits third-person gameplay cameras well.
