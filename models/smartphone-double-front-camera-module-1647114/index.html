About This Model

The Smartphone Double Front Camera Module is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $19 on TurboSquid. You are getting 21,027 polygons and 22,496 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

6 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
