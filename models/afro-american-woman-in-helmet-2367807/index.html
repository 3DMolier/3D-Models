About This Model

A young woman wearing a full protective helmet, a character asset for motorsport, tactical or industrial safety scenes. Most of the 22,365 polygons sit in her face, the helmet shell and the visor edge, and her calm focus suits a pre-race moment.

Her build follows CheckMate specification, with skin and hard surface parts each given separate UV islands. A vertex count of 23,904 keeps her practical for real-time use in a game or driving simulator. The helmet can be recolored to match a team or a unit without touching the face texture at all.

The page offers her in two versions: this helmeted woman and an edition wearing a full racing helmet. On sale since 2025, she fits racing games, training visuals and character lineups for diverse casts and crews. Pose her beside a car or motorcycle and the helmet explains her role, while tight framing on the visor adds tension.
