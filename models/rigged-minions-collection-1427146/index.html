About This Model

A group of small yellow characters in goggles and denim overalls, the henchmen made famous by the Despicable Me films. The figures differ in height and eye count, just as in the films, so a crowd of them feels varied. Posed around a desk or a gadget, they bring instant comedy to an animated shot.

Rounded pill-shaped bodies like these need smooth, even topology, and the collection keeps every silhouette clean. Built to CheckMate specification, the files follow TurboSquid's rules for naming, scale and scene structure. Goggle glass and shiny metal frames reward a reflective shader under bright, playful lighting.

This $179 bundle dates from 2019 and goes into fan animations, birthday invitations and editorial illustrations. Because the characters belong to a film studio, they suit editorial and personal projects rather than commercial branding. Birthday party scenes with one of them holding a banana always earn a smile.
