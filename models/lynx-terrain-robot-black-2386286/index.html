About This Model

Priced at $69, the Lynx Terrain Robot is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 125,711 polygons and 132,806 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and architectural visualization. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
