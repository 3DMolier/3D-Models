About This Model

A pair of blue swim fins with soft foot pockets and long ribbed blades equips snorkelers, divers and swimmers in beach and pool scenes. The open blades flare toward the tip, and the side rails channel water, which is the shape swimmers recognize instantly. Lying on warm sand next to a mask and snorkel, the fins promise an underwater adventure before anyone dives in.

Left and right fins can be posed apart, so one can stand upright in the sand while the other lies flat. Translucent blue rubber with a slight gloss suits a fresh pair, while a sandy, scratched finish suits a rental shop. A TurboSquid examiner went through the files for proper scale and structure, so the fins carry the CheckMate Pro tier.

This is one of our early 2015 models and it still serves dive shop catalogs, travel brochures and swimming lessons. Game artists equip divers with them in underwater levels, and lifeguard training uses them to show fin kicking technique. Add bubbles and caustic light patterns around a swimmer's feet to animate the fins in clear tropical water.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2015 · Updated 11 November 2026
