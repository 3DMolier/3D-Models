About This Model

A pair of goblin hands in a fixed pose, with long bony fingers and pointed nails, made for fantasy creatures and horror close-ups. Knobby knuckles and wrinkled skin give the hands a gnarled look that reads instantly as a creature from folklore. The pose is set and ready, so artists can attach the hands to a body or use them alone in a frame.

Skin tone and nail color come from materials, letting the hands turn green, gray or sickly yellow for different stories. Detail around the knuckles and nails holds up when the camera moves close to a menacing gesture. TurboSquid approved them at CheckMate Pro after reviewing the geometry and naming by hand.

Since 2016 the hands have served fantasy games, Halloween posters and creature concept work for film studios. Animators show them reaching out of shadows or clutching treasure in a dark cave scene. Medical artists sometimes study them as exaggerated anatomy references when teaching hand structure.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2016 · Updated 11 November 2026
