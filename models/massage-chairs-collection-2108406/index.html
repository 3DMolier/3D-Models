About This Model

This bundle gathers full-body massage chairs with padded leg rests, side controls and deeply reclining backs for wellness interiors. Buying the chairs together costs less than picking each one separately, and every chair shares the same real-world scale. Placed in a spa lounge or a living room corner, these chairs suggest comfort and modern home technology.

The CheckMate Lite certificate confirms each file opens cleanly and the meshes carry no broken or doubled faces. Curved outer shells, stitched upholstery seams and foot massager wells are what tell one chair from the next. Warm indirect light over a pale floor helps the leather and glossy plastic surfaces read well in showroom renders.

Shoppers can pick from four collection versions priced at $59, $89, $119 and $149 as the sets get bigger. Combining several sets fills an electronics store floor or a shopping mall kiosk with clearly distinct chair designs. Out since 2023, the bundle serves furniture retail visuals, wellness center layouts and interior presentations for clients.
