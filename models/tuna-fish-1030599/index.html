Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Tuna Fish is a finished, render-ready model at $79. Geometry weighs in at 7,334 polygons over 7,272 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
