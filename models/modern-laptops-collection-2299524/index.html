About This Model

A set of laptops in several sizes and finishes, so a desk, a classroom or an office floor gets variety without mixing modeling styles. Each machine has the thin lid, the keyboard deck and the trackpad modeled, with the screens as separate surfaces.

Screens accept any image or video texture, which turns the collection into a quick way to show software on real-looking hardware. Built to the CheckMate spec, the set keeps scale, naming and pivots consistent across every laptop in the pack. Thin bezels and slim aluminum bodies match what people actually carry to work today.

Added in 2024 under royalty free terms, the collection suits office interiors, coworking scenes and educational technology presentations. Scatter them across a meeting table with coffee cups and phones, and the room looks occupied rather than staged. UI designers present app mockups in context this way instead of floating flat screenshots. Because they share one modeling approach, close and distant laptops in the same shot match in quality.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
