About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Great Horned Owl is a finished, render-ready model at $199. Counted at the source, the model is 42,778 polygons and 70,947 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

42 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 200 x 1000 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and VR experiences. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
