About This Model

Comtak Tactical Headset on Head shows a military communication headset fitted over the ears, ready for soldiers, police and pilots. Earcups with protective padding, a boom microphone and a slim headband give the headset its tactical profile. Because it already sits on a head shape, positioning it on a character or a display bust is much quicker.

This headset is rated CheckMate Pro, the stricter TurboSquid tier that reviewers grant only to carefully organized files. Matte rubber and hard plastic shells respond differently to light, which helps the device look convincing up close.

Released in 2022 at $49, the headset suits military training, law enforcement simulations and tactical gear catalogs. Shooter games and airsoft explainers use headsets like this to complete an operator's loadout. Pair it with a helmet and plate carrier to equip a squad for a night raid scene. Defense presentations also use the radio headset to show how teams stay in contact during missions.
