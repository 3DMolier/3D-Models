About This Model

A yellow bamboo hut with a serving counter and a thatched roof, ready to anchor a beach bar or a festival snack stand. The 573,335 polygons and 820,903 vertices are mostly in the straw roof, where layered strands hang over the edges. The counter faces forward with room behind it for a bartender, so the kiosk works as a stage for staff and guests.

The unwrap keeps islands apart, which allows painted signs, stains and worn spots on the posts without bleeding between parts. As a StemCell model, it carries PBR materials prepared to render consistently in several common engines and renderers. Its dimensions of 205.63 x 234.1 x H120.83 cm match a compact stall that fits on a sandy patch.

Released in 2025, the hut suits resort visualization, travel posters, summer event layouts and tropical scenes in film. Level designers drop it onto island maps as a trading post or a quiet spot where characters meet. Event planners also preview pop-up bars with it before booking space at a seaside venue or market.
