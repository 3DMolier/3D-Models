About This Model

The Bronze Bust of Lincoln in Museum Stand is a production-ready Signage & Decor 3D model, priced at $39 on TurboSquid. You are getting 11,352 polygons and 11,241 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are event visualization, product rendering and architectural visualization.

2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works as set dressing for interiors and streets, advertising renders and game environments.
