About This Model

Historical Farm Equipment Collection gathers old agricultural implements such as horse-drawn plows, carts and hand tools from before tractors. Scattered around a barn or along a field edge, the implements give a rural scene the weight of real working history. Wooden frames with iron fittings dominate the look, which reads at once as nineteenth century country life.

CheckMate specification guided each implement, giving believable scale and tidy names that keep a farmyard organized. Weathered wood and rusted metal take dirt and moss textures well, so the tools can look freshly used or abandoned. A cart parked by the barn door and tools leaned against the fence already suggest a family farm at work.

Historical Farm Equipment Collection 2, a bigger version with more implements, costs $89, while the first collection here is $59. Since 2025 the equipment has served period dramas, open-air museum visualizations, history lessons and farming games. Placed in tall grass at dusk, a lone plow can carry a whole opening shot.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
