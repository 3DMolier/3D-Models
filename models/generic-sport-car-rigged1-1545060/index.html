About This Model

A generic sports car with low nose, wide wheel arches and a sloping roof, designed without brand marks for any racing or lifestyle scene. The aggressive stance and large wheels give it a performance look without copying a real model. Because no logos appear, the car fits ads for any product or a game garage.

Made to CheckMate specification, the coupe has real proportions and clean naming throughout the scene. Wheels, doors and lights are separate parts, so it is easy to pose the car on a turntable or a racetrack. Glossy paint shows off the curved body panels, especially under long strip lights in a studio.

Available from 2020, it serves racing game developers, motion designers and automotive concept presenters. Show it drifting around a corner, parked outside a modern villa, or lit in a studio with reflections. It also fills street scenes where a generic car keeps attention on the action.
