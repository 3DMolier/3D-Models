About This Model

This Industrial Equipment model - Kohler Mobile Generator - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $79 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 376,042 polygons and 385,062 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

20 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines.
