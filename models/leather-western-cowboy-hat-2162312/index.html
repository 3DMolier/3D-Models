About This Model

Priced at $39, the Leather Western Cowboy Hat is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 88,608 polygons and 45,824 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
