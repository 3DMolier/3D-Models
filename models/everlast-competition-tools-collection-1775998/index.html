About This Model

A collection of boxing competition gear including gloves, headguards and other fight equipment, ready to dress a gym or a ring corner. Each item comes as a separate model, so a training bench or a locker room can be arranged freely. Stitched leather panels and padded cuffs give the gear a convincing, well used look.

Bold red and black finishes give the set a strong presence under arena lights. The items share a consistent modeling approach, which keeps them looking natural side by side. Every item in the bundle carries TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro mark, earned through a review of topology and proportions.

The Everlast Competition Tools Collection at $89 is the first of three collection versions. A second collection costs $99, and a third collection at $119 extends the gear further. Introduced in 2021, the sets support boxing gym interiors, fight game scenes and sports equipment renders.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published August 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
