About This Model

A tumbleweed is a dead plant doing its last job. The whole above ground structure breaks off at the root specifically so that it can roll and scatter seed, which makes it one of the few organisms whose corpse is part of the design.

The model holds 215,096 polygons and 228,679 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with one map at 4096x4096, because the tangle has to be real geometry. It carries StemCell certification.

At $29 it suits landscape and environment visualisation, western and desert scenes, film and television backgrounds, game environments, advertising and editorial illustration. On sale since 2019.
