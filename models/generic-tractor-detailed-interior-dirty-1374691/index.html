About This Model

Priced at $129, the Generic Tractor is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The build totals 290,880 polygons across 301,033 vertices. The same count is comfortable in an offline render and demanding in a mobile game, so judge it against your own budget.

32 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Edge flow follows the form rather than fighting it, which is what lets the silhouette hold up when the camera moves in. Scene structure is flat enough to navigate and grouped enough to be useful, which is what makes reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for industrial visualization, operator training and game assets. On the marketplace since 2019, with the listing maintained rather than left to age. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
