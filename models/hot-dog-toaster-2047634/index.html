About This Model

Hot Dog Toaster is a detailed Furniture & Interior asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. The mesh carries 49,192 polygons and 25,168 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for interior visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is built for interior visualization, architectural renders, product advertising and game interiors.
