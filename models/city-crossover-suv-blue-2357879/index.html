About This Model

Priced at $129, the City Crossover SUV Blue is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and game environments. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

Counted at the source, the model is 171,108 polygons and 194,029 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
