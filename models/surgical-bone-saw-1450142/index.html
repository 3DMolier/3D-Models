About This Model

Surgical Bone Saw is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.

Geometry weighs in at 6,682 polygons over 6,863 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 6 of them, peaking at 2048x2048, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
