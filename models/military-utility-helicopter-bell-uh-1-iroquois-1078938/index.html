About This Model

The Military Utility Helicopter Bell UH-1 Iroquois is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 1,001,582 polygons over 1,026,912 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 91 maps, the largest at 8192x8192, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
