About This Model

The Military Utility Helicopter Bell UH-1 Iroquois is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for aerospace visualization, film production and simulation. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 1,001,582 polygons over 1,026,912 vertices - a heavy build for its class. Texture work runs to 91 maps, the largest at 8192x8192, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
