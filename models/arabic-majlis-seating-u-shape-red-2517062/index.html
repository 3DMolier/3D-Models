About This Model

A majlis seats people around the edge of the room facing inward, which is a completely different social geometry from furniture arranged toward a screen. Everyone can see everyone, nobody has the head of the table, and the conversation is the point.

The model holds 299,752 polygons and 314,480 vertices, built in quads only with mixed UV layout, in red. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $39 it suits interior and architectural visualization, hospitality and residential presentation, cultural scenes, furniture marketing, film sets and game environments. On sale since 2026.
