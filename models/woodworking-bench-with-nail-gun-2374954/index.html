About This Model

A woodworking bench with a pneumatic nail gun on top, ready to dress workshops, garages and DIY tutorial scenes. The 117,358 polygons and 121,024 vertices cover the bench frame, the work surface and the nailer with its hose fitting. A nail gun reads as serious carpentry rather than hobby work, which sets the tone for a professional shop.

Non-overlapping UVs allow sawdust, glue stains and tool marks to be painted across the bench top individually. It follows CheckMate modeling standards on scale and naming, yet it has not been formally certified. Good overhead light and a few offcuts on the surface make the bench look like work just paused.

Introduced in 2025, it fits tool retailer visuals, construction training, home improvement videos and crafting games. Commercial projects may use it under its royalty free license, from hardware store banners to instruction manuals. Pair it with a compressor and clamps and the corner grows into a full joinery workshop.
