About This Model

Medieval Simple Man Working is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $199. Counted at the source, the model is 1,242,309 polygons and 1,902,555 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
