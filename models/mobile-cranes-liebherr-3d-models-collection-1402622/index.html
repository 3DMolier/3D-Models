About This Model

A collection of Liebherr all-terrain mobile cranes, with telescopic booms, multi-axle carriers and wide outrigger legs for stability. Each crane is a complete model, so one can lift a beam while another travels folded on the road. Set beside a rising building, the cranes make a construction site look busy and real.

Booms, cabins and outriggers are the parts a viewer checks first, and they are modeled with care. Yellow paint, warning stripes and hydraulic cylinders keep the machines faithful to their real counterparts. TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro check stands behind the clean files and real-world scale of every crane.

Selling for $149 since 2019, the cranes support construction planning, engineering presentations and editorial illustrations. Lift planners also use such models to check boom reach around a building before the real crane arrives. Extended booms against an evening sky make one of the strongest silhouettes on any job site.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
