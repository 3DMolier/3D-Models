About This Model

Priced at $129, the Futuristic Urban Flying Vehicle White is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags visualization, advertising and game environments as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

At 71,495 polygons and 74,271 vertices this is a light asset: dense enough for close framing, and honest about what it costs a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
