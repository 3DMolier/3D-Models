About This Model

Priced at $29, the Carnival Top Hat with Buckle Red is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

The mesh carries 2,966 polygons and 2,968 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
