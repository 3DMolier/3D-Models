About This Model

Truck Generic 2 Simple Interior is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $89. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. 2016 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

Geometry weighs in at 663,237 polygons over 681,133 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
