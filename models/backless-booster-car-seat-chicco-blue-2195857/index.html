About This Model

A backless booster cushion that lifts an older child so the adult belt fits, ready for car interiors and family travel scenes. The 40,292 polygons and 20,740 vertices describe the padded seat, the armrests with belt guides and the stitched fabric cover. Its low profile hides easily on a rear bench, yet the bright color makes it a clear story detail when needed.

StemCell delivery means the fabric and plastic materials carry over identically between formats and render engines. Non-overlapping UVs make it simple to recolor the cover or add a safety label on the base. Quilted padding catches soft light along its seams, which helps the seat read as fabric rather than molded plastic.

Four versions come at the same $39 price: this blue Chicco booster, a gray Chicco seat, a pink GoFit and a green generic. The green Child Car Seat Without Backrest suits scenes where a brand name should stay out of view. Released in 2024, the booster is used in car interior visualization, child safety guides and family lifestyle imagery.
