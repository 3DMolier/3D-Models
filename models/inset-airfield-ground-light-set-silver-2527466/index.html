About This Model

A set of flush runway lights in silver housings, made to sit level with the pavement on airport aprons and taxiways. The Inset Airfield Ground Light Set Silver uses 6,162 polygons for the round housings, the raised prisms and the bolted rims. Its 6,150 vertices keep each fixture light, so hundreds can line a runway centerline in an aerial shot at night.

StemCell status means one master was exported to many formats with consistent physically based materials. The UV layout has no overlaps, leaving space for tire marks, grime and a glowing emissive lens on each unit.

Launched in 2026, the set serves airport design renders, flight simulator scenery and aviation safety training for ground crews. Royalty Free terms permit commercial use in simulators, architectural visualizations and broadcast graphics about air travel and airports. Turn on a warm or green emission at dusk, and the lights trace the path for a taxiing aircraft.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2026 · Updated 13 November 2026

6,162 polygons and 6,150 vertices, built as polygonal quads only. Compared with the roughly 53,000 polygon median of our Architecture & Landmarks catalog, this build runs low at 6,162.
