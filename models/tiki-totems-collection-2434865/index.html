About This Model

The Tiki Totems Collection gathers carved Polynesian-style posts with grimacing faces, ready to line a beach bar or jungle path. Each totem stands as its own object, so you can scatter them around a scene at different heights and angles. Deep carved mouths and heavy brows catch side light well, which gives a torch-lit night shot plenty of texture.

The pieces are built to the CheckMate specification, meaning clean geometry and sensible naming across the whole set. Buying the group at once saves hunting for matching carvings from different authors with different modeling styles. Weathered wood materials work best here, with cracks and moss added where the base meets the ground.

There are two versions, this first set and Tiki Totems Collection 2, each costing $29. Combining both sets fills a larger resort garden or island village without the same face appearing twice. First offered in 2025, the carvings suit tropical game levels, themed restaurant concepts and adventure film backdrops.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2025 · Updated 11 November 2026
