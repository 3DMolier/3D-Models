About This Model

A jointed wooden puppet boy with a pointed nose and painted clothes is a beloved fairy tale character for animation. The Pinnochio Wooden Doll Toy Rigged for Maya is set up so the figure can walk, wave and nod inside that program.

Visible joints at the shoulders, elbows and knees show the puppet's handmade nature, with grain running along each limb. A cap, short trousers and a simple jacket give him a classic storybook look. The doll was built to the CheckMate specification, and its rig controls stay tidy in a production scene.

Released in 2023, the toy fits children's animation, holiday window displays and storybook illustration. Animators make him take his first stiff steps across a workshop bench beside carving tools. Toy designers render him on a shelf to preview a wooden collectible before production. Illustrators pose the puppet from several angles to build reference sheets for picture books.
