About This Model

Looking for a Architecture Landmarks asset? The High Voltage Insulator is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
