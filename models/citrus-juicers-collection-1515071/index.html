About This Model

This set gathers several citrus juicers in one file, so a kitchen counter shot gets a matching family of tools. The reamers share the ribbed dome that squeezes a halved lemon or orange, a shape every viewer recognizes instantly.

The collection holds CheckMate Pro status, the stricter of TurboSquid's technical reviews for scale, naming and clean geometry. Each juicer can be pulled out on its own and placed beside fruit halves anywhere along a worktop. Ribbed cones, spouts and drip trays are the details that read in close product shots, and they hold up there.

The bundle went on sale in 2020 and remains available at $29 for the whole group. Buyers use it for kitchen interiors, recipe illustrations, appliance advertising and game props that dress a home scene. Taken together, the pieces cost less than picking separate juicers one by one from different authors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2020 · Updated 12 November 2026
