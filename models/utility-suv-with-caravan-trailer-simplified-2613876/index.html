About This Model

A boxy utility SUV towing a travel caravan, a ready road trip combination for campsites, highways and holiday scenes. The Utility SUV with Caravan Trailer Simplified uses 291,007 polygons across both vehicles, from wheel arches to caravan windows.

Its 306,135 vertices keep the body curves of the SUV and the rounded caravan corners clean. The pair satisfies CheckMate requirements, so the tow hitch, wheels and bodies line up at real scale. A simplified build trims hidden detail, which keeps the combination practical for traffic and camping scenes.

Since 2026 the combo has been offered at $179 for travel visualizations, camping games and caravan park designs. Park it on a grassy pitch beside a picnic table, or follow it along a winding coastal road. Holiday parks planning new pitches can check how a car and caravan fit into each bay. Separating the SUV from the trailer lets you show the caravan parked alone while the car heads out.
