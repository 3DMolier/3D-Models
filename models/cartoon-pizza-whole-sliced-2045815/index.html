About This Model

A whole cartoon pizza already cut into slices, with bright toppings for stylized food scenes, games and colorful delivery menus. Melted cheese, scattered toppings and a thick golden crust are exaggerated just enough to look tasty and playful. Pre-cut slices mean one piece can be lifted away from the rest for a classic, mouth-watering serving moment.

Clean, sensibly organized geometry at a believable scale earned the pizza CheckMate Pro status. Saturated reds and yellows suit cartoon lighting, where flat shading keeps the toppings readable even from far away. Recolor the toppings to turn it into a margherita, veggie or meat lover's version without new modeling.

Catalogued in 2023, it fits fast food ads, delivery app screens and restaurant games. Set it on a checkered tablecloth with a soda cup for an instant pizzeria scene in a cozy corner. In a party game, it doubles as a reward item or a shared table centerpiece at a birthday celebration.
