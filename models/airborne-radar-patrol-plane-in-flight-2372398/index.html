About This Model

The Airborne Radar Patrol Plane in Flight is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $169 on TurboSquid. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for aerospace visualisation, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 282,663 polygons over 291,874 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
