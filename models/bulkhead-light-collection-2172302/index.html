About This Model

A collection of bulkhead lights with protective cages and frosted covers, the rugged fixtures of ships, tunnels and basements. Oval and round housings bolt onto walls, giving industrial spaces a recognizable, utilitarian lighting style. Metal grilles over the glass add a protective detail that reads well at close range.

Under CheckMate Lite certification, the fixtures are usable, well-formed files ready to place on any wall. Covers accept emissive materials, so each lamp can actually light a corridor in the render. Housing colors range from industrial gray to marine white, letting the set suit both a ship deck and a parking garage.

Introduced in 2024, the lights suit ship interiors, industrial games, horror corridors and architectural visualization of utility areas. A flickering bulkhead in a damp passage instantly raises tension in a dark scene. Repeated along a tunnel wall, the lights draw the eye toward a vanishing point.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
