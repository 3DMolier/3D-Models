About This Model

Dark Skin Afro American Businessman is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalogue, listed at $149. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2021, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualisation and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 9,082 polygons over 9,893 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
