About This Model

Modern Flat Screen TV is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.

Counted at the source, the model is 7,505 polygons and 7,785 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
