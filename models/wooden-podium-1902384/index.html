About This Model

Priced at $9, the Wooden Podium is a finished Furniture & Interior model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 670 polygons and 348 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
