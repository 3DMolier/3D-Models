About This Model

Priced at $99, the Flying Red Cockroach is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering as the intended applications. It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

At 46,600 polygons and 24,646 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
