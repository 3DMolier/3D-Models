About This Model

A whaler shark caught mid-swim in a curved pose, its tail swept to one side as it turns through open water. Whaler Shark Pose 2 3D Model shows the streamlined body, tall dorsal fin and long pectoral fins typical of requiem sharks. The bent spine gives the animal motion even in a still frame, which suits posters and underwater stills.

It passed the CheckMate Pro review that TurboSquid applies to its stricter tier of models, covering geometry, scale and naming. The pale belly and darker back follow natural countershading, so the shark reads correctly when lit from above.

In the catalog since 2015, the model fits marine biology lessons, aquarium signage and ocean documentaries. Game developers use posed sharks as background wildlife passing a diver or circling a reef. Several instances at different depths make a convincing patrol around a shipwreck. A blue fog and shafts of light from the surface turn the pose into an instant deep-water scene.
