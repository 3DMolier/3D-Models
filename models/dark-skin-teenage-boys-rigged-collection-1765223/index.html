About This Model

Teenage boys with dark skin tones make up this rigged collection, giving games and films diverse young characters ready for any scene. Everyday casual outfits suit school corridors, city streets and sports settings without any extra wardrobe work from the artist. Lanky adolescent proportions, longer limbs and youthful faces separate them clearly from adult character packs.

Certified CheckMate Pro by TurboSquid, the collection meets the marketplace's strictest technical inspection for usable geometry. Rigging allows quick posing for group conversations, walking scenes or crowds gathered around a neighborhood basketball court. Skin shading benefits from a warm key light and subtle subsurface scattering, which keeps darker tones rich rather than flat.

First sold in 2021, the collection carries a royalty free license for commercial, educational and personal work. School safety videos, youth programs and coming of age films need believable teenagers, and casting them digitally saves weeks. Grouped on bleachers or waiting at a bus stop, the boys bring authentic everyday life into urban scenes.
