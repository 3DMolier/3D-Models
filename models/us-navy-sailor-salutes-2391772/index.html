About This Model

US Navy Sailor is one of the Characters & People models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. Counted at the source, the model is 23,610 polygons and 30,994 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 73.61 x 39.47 x H186.56 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
