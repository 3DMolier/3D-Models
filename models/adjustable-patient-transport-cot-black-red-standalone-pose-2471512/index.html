About This Model

Adjustable Patient Transport Cot Black Red Standalone Pose is an ambulance stretcher with a padded mattress, raised backrest and folding undercarriage. The 67,142 polygons cover the side rails, the telescopic legs, the caster wheels and the safety straps across the mattress. With 67,486 vertices, the frame keeps clean edges when shown next to an ambulance loading bay. Wheeled beside an open ambulance door, it sets an emergency scene without any further props needed.

Some UV islands share space, so identical parts such as the wheels reuse texture detail efficiently. StemCell certification lets the cot move between render engines with its red and black finish intact.

Five versions are offered: this cot and a folded black and yellow Stryker stretcher at $59 each. Three rigged folding ambulance stretchers at $89 come for Maya, Cinema 4D and as a general rig. Out since 2025, the cot fits emergency medicine scenes, hospital corridors and paramedic training videos.
