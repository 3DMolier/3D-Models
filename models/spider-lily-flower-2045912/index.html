About This Model

This Nature & Plants model - Spider Lily Flower - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 40,218 polygons and 20,111 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and game assets.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
