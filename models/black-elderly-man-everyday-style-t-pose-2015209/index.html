About This Model

The Everyday Style is a production-ready Characters & People 3D model, priced at $169 on TurboSquid. You are getting 255,997 polygons and 371,454 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
