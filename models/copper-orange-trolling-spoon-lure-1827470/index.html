About This Model

Copper Orange Trolling Spoon Lure is a detailed Kitchen & Tableware asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.
