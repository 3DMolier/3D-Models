About This Model

The yellow school bus is a regulated color, chosen decades ago because it is the most visible thing on a road at dawn. Everything else about the vehicle follows from carrying children who are not wearing belts: high seat backs, no sharp edges, and a body built to stay upright.

The model is supplied with a simple interior and carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $79 it suits urban and suburban visualization, education and safety material, film and television, advertising, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2016.
