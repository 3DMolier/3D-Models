About This Model

The Office Whiteboard is a production-ready general 3D model, priced at $15 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 5,102 polygons over 5,267 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.
