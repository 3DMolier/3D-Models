About This Model

Box Truck belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $99 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 349,084 polygons and 367,132 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

23 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
