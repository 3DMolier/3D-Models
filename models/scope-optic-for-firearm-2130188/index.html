About This Model

A rifle optic is a tube with a job, and its character sits entirely in the hardware around it: the elevation and windage turrets, the knurled magnification ring, the objective bell, the mount rings and the flip-up caps. Those are what a viewer reads at close range, and this model builds them as geometry rather than as texture detail on a smooth cylinder.

It is 52,529 polygons and 26,903 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, built to StemCell specification so it drops into a real-time engine without a rebuild. Light enough to sit on every weapon in a squad.

At $29 this is an attachment rather than a subject: military and law enforcement visualization, games, film and television armouries, hunting and sport shooting material, and equipment catalogs. Real-world scale is kept, so it mounts at the correct height above a rail. On sale since 2024.
