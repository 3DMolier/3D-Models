About This Model

Red Iron(1) is a household clothes iron with a bright red body, a pointed soleplate and a looped handle on top. The polished metal soleplate contrasts with the glossy plastic shell, which gives the appliance a cheerful look. Standing upright on an ironing board, the iron instantly turns a corner of a room into a laundry space.

The model meets the CheckMate Pro bar for real scale, tidy topology and materials that load with the file. Curved surfaces like the handle and nose show off clean geometry through smooth, unbroken reflections. A red body stands out against white linen, which makes the iron a strong focal point in a domestic shot.

In the range since 2015, the iron furnishes home interiors, cleaning product scenes and housework animations. Next to a basket of folded shirts, it tells a simple everyday story without any extra props. For $39 the iron is a small appliance that adds lived-in detail to an apartment or a dry cleaner.
