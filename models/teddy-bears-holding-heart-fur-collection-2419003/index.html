About This Model

Fluffy teddy bears hugging red hearts, with real fur strands that make them look ready to be given on Valentine's Day. Each bear wraps its paws around the heart, a pose that reads instantly as affection even in small thumbnails. Fur covers the bodies, ears and muzzles, giving the soft fuzzy silhouette that flat textures cannot fake.

As a set made to CheckMate specification, every bear arrives scaled and organized with its heart as a separate part. Fur settings differ between renderers, so plan a short tuning pass for density and clumping. Hearts can be recolored or given satin fabric to match a card or campaign palette.

Released in 2025, the bears suit Valentine greeting cards, gift shop displays and romantic animation scenes. Placed on a pillow with petals scattered around, the bears make a warm close-up for holiday visuals. Toy designers also preview plush concepts with such renders before sewing the first physical prototype.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published June 2025 · Updated 12 November 2026
