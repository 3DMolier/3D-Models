About This Model

Art Mannequins Collection gathers wooden artist figures with jointed limbs, the drawing aids used to study human poses. Each mannequin keeps its ball joints, segmented torso and simplified head, just like the real tabletop figures sold in art supply shops.

Quality reviewers gave the figures CheckMate Pro status, so the topology is clean and the proportions believable. Joints are separate parts, so an animator can rotate limbs into running, sitting or reaching poses. Light wood grain and rounded forms make the figures read well under soft studio lighting from a window.

Since 2023 the collection has served art school scenes, illustration tutorials and creative workspace renders. A desk with sketchbooks, pencils and a posed figure sets the mood for a drawing studio. Instructors use the models to explain gesture, balance and proportion in figure drawing courses. They also work as stylized characters in minimalist animations and puzzle games where faces are not needed.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2023 · Updated 11 November 2026
