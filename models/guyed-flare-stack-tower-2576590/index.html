About This Model

Priced at $69, the Guyed Flare Stack Tower is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 149,357 polygons and 1,059,380 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 107.44 x 92.9 x H115.54 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization and operator training. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
