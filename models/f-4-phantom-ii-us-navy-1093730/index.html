About This Model

F-4 Phantom II US Navy is a detailed Aircraft asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $229. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. Available since 2017, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for aerospace visualization, flight and combat simulation, war-game environments and aviation sequences in film and TV.

Geometry weighs in at 95,876 polygons over 101,834 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 14 of them, peaking at 8192x8192, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
