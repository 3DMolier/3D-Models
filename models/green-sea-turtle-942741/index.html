About This Model

A green sea turtle, supplied plain and rigged. The flippers are the whole animal - a sea turtle flies rather than swims, and the front flippers have a wing-like stroke that only works if the rig allows it.

CheckMate Pro certification covers scale and topology, which for a rigged reptile is what keeps the shell from deforming when the limbs move. The carapace scute pattern and the beaked head are what identify the species.

A rigged version shares the page. Priced at $79 sold 67 times since 2015, into marine documentary work, aquarium and conservation material, travel visualisation and games.
