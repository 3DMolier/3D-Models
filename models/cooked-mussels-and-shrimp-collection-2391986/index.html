About This Model

This collection brings together cooked mussels and shrimp, the seafood pieces chefs arrange on paella, pasta or a platter. Mussels show open dark shells with orange meat, and shrimp keep the curled pink shape of a cooked tail. Both are common garnishes, so the set fills many dishes rather than just one recipe.

Every piece was made to the CheckMate specification, with true scale and orderly object names throughout. Separate items let you compose a seafood plate differently in every shot instead of reusing one fixed arrangement. Glossy shaders on the meat and matte ones on the shells give a fresh, just-cooked look.

Released in 2025, the bundle sells every piece of seafood together for a single $29. It suits restaurant menus, food advertising, cooking show graphics, seaside cafe interiors, market stalls on ice and game kitchens. Scattered over rice with lemon wedges, the pieces build a Mediterranean dish in minutes.
