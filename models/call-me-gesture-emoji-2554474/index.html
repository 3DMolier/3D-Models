About This Model

Looking for a Other asset? The Call Me Gesture Emoji is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Counted at the source, the model is 647 polygons and 641 vertices.

That sits in low-poly territory, so it stays cheap to instance across a crowd scene or a game level. 5 texture maps come with it, at 1024 x 1024 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and game assets. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Geometry weighs in at 647 polygons over 641 vertices - a light build for its class. It ships 5 maps with the top set authored at 1024x1024 - enough resolution to fill a frame rather than sit in the background. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
