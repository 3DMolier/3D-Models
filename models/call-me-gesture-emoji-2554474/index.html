About This Model

Priced at $19, the Call Me Gesture Emoji is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 647 polygons and 641 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 5 textures, authored at 1024 x 1024. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX and product rendering. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.
