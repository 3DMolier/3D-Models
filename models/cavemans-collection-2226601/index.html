About This Model

A collection of caveman characters in fur clothing for prehistoric scenes, museum displays and Stone Age stories. Having several primitive humans together lets a scene show a tribe rather than a lone figure in a landscape.

Each character follows CheckMate standards, keeping names, units and scale consistent across the group. Rough hides, matted hair and weathered skin suit warm firelight and the dim interior of a cave. Consistent proportions mean the figures stand naturally together around a hearth or along a hunting trail through tall grass.

Released in 2024 for $349, it serves education, publishing and entertainment projects alike. It suits history documentaries, natural history museum exhibits, school lessons on human evolution and survival games. Gathered at a cave mouth under a stormy sky, the group creates an instantly readable prehistoric moment. A single figure painting on a rock wall also makes a quiet, evocative scene about the origins of early art.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
