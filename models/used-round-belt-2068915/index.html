About This Model

Used Round Belt is a detailed Tools asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $9. Counted at the source, the model is 3,648 polygons and 1,824 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for technical visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.
