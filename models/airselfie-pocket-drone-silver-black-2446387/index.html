About This Model

Priced at $34, the AirSelfie Pocket Drone Silver Black is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Counted at the source, the model is 53,597 polygons and 54,944 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
