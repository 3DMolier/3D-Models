About This Model

A female figure wearing a delicate white lace eye mask, an elegant subject for masquerade, fashion and portrait scenes. The Woman with Lace Eye Mask White uses 58,461 polygons, shaping the face, hair and openwork lace around the eyes.

There are 79,644 vertices, with extra density in the lace cutouts that frame the eyes. The model follows CheckMate requirements, arriving with a clean structure and human proportions. White lace against skin creates a soft contrast that reads as mysterious rather than theatrical.

Available in 2026 at $69, the figure suits beauty visuals, masquerade ball scenes and fashion editorials. Pose the figure at a candlelit gala, or place it in a studio portrait with a dark backdrop. Book cover designers working on romance or mystery titles can build a striking image around the mask. Soft side lighting picks out the lace pattern and gives the face a gentle, cinematic glow.
