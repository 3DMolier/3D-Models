About This Model

Speculative military design tends to converge on the same features: faceted armour to defeat sensors, a low profile because height is a liability, and wheels rather than tracks because most future conflicts in fiction happen on roads.

The model holds 308,070 polygons and 168,983 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, and carries StemCell certification. Rigged builds are offered separately, including native rigs for Maya and Cinema 4D.

At $79 it suits science fiction film and animation, game vehicles and environments, concept art, defence and technology illustration, and advertising. On sale since 2024.
