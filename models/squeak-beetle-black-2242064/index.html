About This Model

Priced at $59, the Squeak Beetle Black is a finished Animals & Creatures model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. It has been on sale since 2024 and is still maintained as part of the active catalogue. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 14,272 polygons and 17,504 vertices, which puts it in the light bracket for this category. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

Questions About This Model

What does StemCell mean for the Squeak Beetle Black model?

It means one source asset, several delivered formats, and a PBR material setup designed to read consistently across them - no rebuilding shaders after import.

What is the Squeak Beetle Black model typically used for?

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

Can the Squeak Beetle Black model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Can the Squeak Beetle Black model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free licence covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.
