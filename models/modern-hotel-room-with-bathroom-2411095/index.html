About This Model

Looking for a Furniture & Interior asset? The Modern Hotel Room with Bathroom is a finished, render-ready model at $129. You are getting 202,551 polygons and 206,439 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.
