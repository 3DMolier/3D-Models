About This Model

A gas carrier is recognisable from the horizon by the domes on its deck: pressure tanks that hold the cargo cold and contained, sitting proud of the hull because that is the only shape that works. No other class of ship looks like it.

The vessel holds 1,145,448 polygons and 1,160,044 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 40 maps, 10 at 8192x8192, 27 at 4096x4096, one at 2048x2048 and two at 1024x1024. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a generic build without markings is offered separately.

At $159 it suits energy and shipping visualization, port and infrastructure scenes, documentary and news graphics, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2017.
