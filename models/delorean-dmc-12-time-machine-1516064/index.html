About This Model

The stainless steel sports car in its time machine configuration: the brushed body panels and louvered rear deck, the gullwing doors, and the whole machine bolted on behind the cabin, the vents, the cabling and the hardware along the flanks. It is the movie silhouette, and the silhouette is most of the job.

The model ships as StemCell, the pipeline-portable format, which is the practical reason to pick this one: consistent scale, materials and naming wherever you open it, so a car this fiddly does not need a conversion session before you can light it. The detailed geometry and texture figures are not listed on this page, so check the file panel if your shot has a strict budget.

It stands alone on its page, no alternate versions, so the page holds the single build. On sale since 2020, sold 54 times at $129. It goes into retro and 1980s scenes, science fiction and fan art, motion graphics, editorial and event visuals, and games.
