About This Model

The Do 31 is the only jet transport ever built that could take off vertically, and it remains so. Lift engines in pods on the wingtips, cruise engines that vectored their thrust, and a programme cancelled because the fuel cost of hovering a cargo aircraft turned out to be exactly as bad as feared.

The airframe holds 127,277 polygons and 133,180 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 16 maps, 8 at 4096x4096 and 8 at 2048x2048. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $129 it suits aviation history and documentary work, museum and exhibition material, flight simulation, film and game environments. On sale since 2015.
