About This Model

A portable launcher turns a drone into equipment rather than an aircraft. No runway, no crew, no airfield: a case that opens, a rail that fires, and an aeroplane in the air within a minute of somebody deciding they need one.

The model is light at 36,964 polygons and 38,359 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout, supplying the launch system together with the UAV in flight. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $89 it suits defence visualization, documentary and news graphics, simulation, game environments and editorial illustration. On sale since 2026.
