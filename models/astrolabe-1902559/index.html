About This Model

A brass astrolabe with an engraved disc, a pierced star map and a rotating pointer, the classic instrument of early navigators. Its 15,373 polygons and 15,873 vertices describe the fine rete cutouts, the suspension ring and the scale markings along the rim. The open star map throws intricate shadows, which makes the instrument striking on a desk or under a museum spotlight.

Image maps are not part of this file, so aged brass, patina and polish come from a metal shader you adjust. Its UV islands keep apart, which helps if you want to bake engraving detail or add verdigris in the crevices. TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro review checked topology and scale, so the parts line up and the file opens cleanly.

Two more versions mount the instrument on a wooden stand, both at $44, while this bare one costs $39. The stand editions suit study and library scenes, and the bare one fits close-ups in a captain's cabin. Since 2022 it has appeared in history lessons, fantasy game props and scenes of medieval astronomy.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2022 · Updated 14 November 2026
