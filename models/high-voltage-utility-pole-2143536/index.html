About This Model

A transmission pole is a piece of engineering that everyone sees and nobody looks at. The crossarms, the insulator strings, the conductor clamps, the earth wire at the top and the step bolts up the shaft are all there for reasons, and their arrangement identifies the voltage class as clearly as a label would.

The mesh is 143,028 polygons and 72,649 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density in the insulator strings and the hardware where a close camera lands.

At $31 it suits infrastructure and utility visualization, energy sector presentation, rural and roadside scenes, architectural exteriors, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2024.
