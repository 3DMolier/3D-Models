About This Model

A collection of symphonic gongs on stands with padded beaters, built for orchestras, temples and meditation rooms. Large bronze discs hang from sturdy frames, their hammered surfaces catching light in circular patterns. Beaters with soft felt heads rest nearby, ready for a percussionist to strike at the right moment.

This set holds CheckMate Pro status, with clean topology on the broad discs and their sturdy frames. Metallic bronze shaders reward slow camera moves that sweep reflections across each gong face. Cords suspending the discs let a gong swing slightly after a hit, a subtle motion that makes animation feel physical.

Since 2024 the gongs have served concert hall renders, music education, soundtrack visuals and wellness spaces. A single strike in a quiet scene gives an animation a dramatic, resonant beat that viewers almost hear. Arranged in a sound bath studio with mats and candles, the gongs set a calm, meditative mood.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
