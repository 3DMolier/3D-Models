About This Model

Pumpkin on Wooden Barrel with Sack is one of the Containers & Storage models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. You are getting 14,129 polygons and 14,204 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, architectural visualization and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works as warehouse and logistics set dressing, a game prop, or a product-rendering element.
