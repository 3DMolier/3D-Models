About This Model

A gray roadside utility cabinet with double doors, vents and a sloped roof, the box that hides power and telecom gear on sidewalks. Outdoor Electric Cabinet uses 5,384 polygons and 5,521 vertices, enough for handles, hinges and louvers without weighing down a street. Its plain shape blends into backgrounds yet adds the clutter that makes a city scene feel lived in.

The 2 maps at 4096 x 4096 add warning stickers, dust and a few scratches on the painted steel. Overlaps never occur in its unwrap, while CheckMate Pro certification holds it to street-level scale. Mounted on a concrete pad, the cabinet sits correctly next to curbs and lamp posts.

On sale since 2017, the cabinet counts 18 buyers among urban planners, game level artists and telecom presenters. Graffiti or posters can be painted on the doors to age it for grittier streets. At $19 it is one of the cheapest ways to make a sidewalk look real.
