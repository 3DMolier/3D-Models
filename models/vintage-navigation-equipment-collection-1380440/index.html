About This Model

A collection of vintage navigation instruments, such as a brass sextant, compass, telescope and chart tools used by sailors before satellites. Warm brass, worn leather and aged wood give the pieces the feel of a captain's desk on an old ship. Each instrument reflects a real tool of celestial navigation, which keeps educational scenes accurate.

The items hold CheckMate Pro status and share proportions, so they sit together naturally on a map table. Polished metal parts respond well to warm lamplight, revealing engraved scales and fine mechanical details. Grouped in one purchase, the instruments save sourcing matching antiques from scattered files.

Released into the catalog in 2019, the set dresses period films, maritime museum displays and pirate adventure games. Spread an old chart under the sextant with a candle nearby and a voyage begins on screen. History teachers use the pieces to show how sailors found their position using stars and the horizon.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2019 · Updated 12 November 2026
