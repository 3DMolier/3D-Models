About This Model

Looking for a Ships & Boats asset? The Autonomous Surface Vehicle is a finished, render-ready model at $89. You are getting 39,106 polygons and 42,527 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are defense simulation, naval simulation and game environments.

Available since 2024, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Typical uses are naval and maritime scenes: film and TV VFX, harbor environments in games, and marine simulation.
