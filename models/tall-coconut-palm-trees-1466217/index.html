About This Model

Tall Coconut Palm Trees is a detailed Nature & Plants asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 111,559 polygons and 225,652 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 11 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096 and 1024 x 1024. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
