About This Model

A collection of grills loaded with sausages, covering the barbecue setups people fire up for weekends, picnics and garden parties. Sausages sit on the grates with browned skins and grill marks, so the food reads as cooking rather than raw. Each grill keeps its own shape and controls, so the set does not look like one model repeated.

Having several grill types in one pack means a backyard, a campsite and a street food stall can each get the right one. The pack is organized to the CheckMate specification, so sausages can be moved, removed or added. Add a little smoke and glowing coals and the scene smells like summer even in a still render.

Introduced in 2025, the collection serves food advertising, outdoor furniture renders, cooking channel graphics and festival scenes in games. A grill with sausages is the universal symbol of a relaxed weekend, which makes it useful far beyond cooking content. The pack costs $89 for every grill in the collection, sausages included.
