About This Model

A set of Tesla vehicle keys, from the slim key card to the small fob shaped like a miniature car body. The collection gathers several key designs in one file, so a desk scene can show how access to these cars has changed. Each piece is small and smooth, so its shape reads well even on a cluttered table.

The keys are certified to CheckMate specification, which promises clean geometry and true real-world scale. The set is offered under the Editorial Uses Only license, so it fits news coverage, reviews and documentary work. Glossy black plastic and a matte card surface give two very different reflections under the same light.

Released in 2026, the collection suits technology articles, automotive reviews and electric vehicle explainers. Lay the keys beside a phone on a car console, or on a hallway table next to a wallet for a lifestyle shot. A shallow depth of field and a soft rim light make the small fob look like a jewel.
