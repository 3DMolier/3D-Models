About This Model

Firecracker Rocket is a detailed Signage & Decor asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $19. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. The scene is organised rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits battlefield simulation, war-game environments, defence training material and military VFX shots.

Geometry weighs in at 951 polygons over 995 vertices - a light build for its class. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
