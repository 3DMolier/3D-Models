About This Model

An anti-ship cruise missile with a slim body, cruciform wings and tail fins, plus the booster used for ship launches. The AGM UGM RGM 84 Harpoon Anti Ship Missile is lean at 34,462 polygons over 36,217 vertices, spent on fin roots and intake. A small air scoop under the body reveals the turbojet that carries it skimming low over the waves.

Gray paint, stencils and warning stripes are laid out on 15 maps at 4096 x 4096 for the airframe. Islands in the missile's UV layout never collide, and TurboSquid rated the weapon CheckMate Pro after a manual check.

There is one version, sold at $59, and 20 buyers have taken it since its 2019 release. Naval simulators, defense documentaries and strategy games use it most, often hung under a patrol aircraft wing. Racked on a destroyer deck or mounted on a museum cradle, it shows the reach of a modern navy.
