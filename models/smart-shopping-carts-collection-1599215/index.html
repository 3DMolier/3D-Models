About This Model

A smart trolley is a shopping cart with a screen, a scanner and a battery pack, and those additions change its geometry: a console mounted at the handle, a housing under the basket and cable routing along the frame. Retail technology renders need the whole family rather than one unit, because a store shows a nested rank at the entrance and single carts in the aisles.

Buying them as a collection keeps proportion and finish consistent across the shop floor, which is what stops a retail scene from looking assembled out of parts.

A second collection sits on this page at $75. This set at $79 is aimed at retail technology marketing, supermarket and store visualization, smart retail presentation, interior renders and games. On sale since 2020.
