About This Model

A crocodile show arena with a curved arched roof, tiered seating and a water pool where animal shows take place. The 84,336 polygons cover the roof trusses, the spectator stands and the fenced enclosure around the water. The arch shades the audience while leaving the pool open to light, a common layout in tropical zoos.

Being a StemCell asset, the arena carries PBR materials that look the same in every supported format. Non-overlapping UVs give room for painted signage, worn concrete and algae around the water edge. Wide spans and slender columns make the structure feel open, which suits aerial shots of a park.

Released in 2025, the arena fits zoo master plans, tourism visuals and adventure park levels in games. Wildlife documentaries and travel guides about Southeast Asia often feature venues like this one. Add a few crocodiles and a keeper on the platform, and the show is about to begin.
