About This Model

A zippered pencil case surrounded by pens, pencils, an eraser and other desk supplies, ready for school and office scenes. The open case shows its stationery spilling out onto the desk, the familiar look of a student's first day back. Having the case and its contents together means a study table can be dressed in a single step.

The collection follows the CheckMate specification from TurboSquid, a baseline for clean modeling, real size and sensible structure. Colors of the pens and the fabric case can be changed per item to suit a playful or businesslike desk. Small details like pencil tips and pen clips keep the set convincing when the camera looks straight down.

Out since 2025, the set suits back to school graphics, educational app screens and dorm room interiors in games. Placed next to a notebook and a laptop, it makes a home office or a classroom feel occupied. Illustrators of study guides also use it for clean top-down compositions about organization and planning.
