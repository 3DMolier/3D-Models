About This Model

A man recovering from an injury with his arm in a sling, ready for hospital, insurance and first aid scenes. The sling cradles a bent forearm across his chest, a pose that instantly signals a fracture or sprain. He fits clinic waiting rooms, office returns after injury and workplace safety stories about avoiding accidents.

The figure complies with CheckMate rules, so scale, hierarchy and naming stay predictable when he joins an existing scene. Neutral daylight and soft shadows keep the mood sympathetic rather than dramatic or frightening. Casual clothes and a slightly guarded posture show that every movement is being made carefully.

Two versions target different software: the Injured Man Wearing Arm Sling Rigged for Maya and a Cinema 4D build. Sold since 2022 with a Royalty Free license, he suits insurance explainers, physiotherapy content, hospital websites and safety training. Occupational health trainers use him to illustrate the real consequences of preventable workplace accidents.
