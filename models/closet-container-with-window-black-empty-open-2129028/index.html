About This Model

Priced at $12, the Closet Container with Window Black Empty Open is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.

The mesh carries 120,454 polygons and 64,764 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
