About This Model

A rectangular juice or milk carton with a slanted top and a plastic screw cap, the classic aseptic package from grocery shelves. Aseptic Carton Package with Screw Cap uses 4,717 polygons over 4,710 vertices on the folded seams and the threaded cap. Sealed flaps along the top and bottom are modeled, so the box looks properly folded rather than a plain block.

The carton ships without image maps, which makes it a blank canvas for any brand label or nutrition panel. Designers wrap their own artwork around the panels and preview it in a store setting within minutes. The non-overlapping UV layout accepts artwork cleanly, and the package meets CheckMate Lite.

Packaging designers, dairy brands and supermarket scenes account for 19 orders since 2021. Lined up in a fridge door, the cartons give a kitchen render a lived-in breakfast feel. At $19 it is a quick way to mock up a new drink label before printing.
