About This Model

Looking for a Animals & Creatures asset? The Tyrannosaurus Rex is a finished, render-ready model at $179. Counted at the source, the model is 21,680 polygons and 22,268 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

5 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
