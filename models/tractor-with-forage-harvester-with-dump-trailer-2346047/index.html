About This Model

A tractor towing a forage harvester and a dump trailer, made for harvest season scenes, farm machinery visuals and agriculture games. The harvester sits beside the tractor and blows chopped crop through a tall spout into the trailer running alongside. Big rear tires and a high cab give the tractor the commanding stance typical of modern field machinery.

The machines meet CheckMate requirements, so tractor, harvester and trailer load at true scale with clearly named parts and pivots. A tipping trailer body lets the chopped load be dumped at the silage clamp in a short and simple animation. Hitches and drawbars connect the units in a believable chain, ready to follow a path across a field.

Released in 2025, the convoy serves agricultural training, farm equipment presentations and harvesting levels in popular farming simulators. Late summer light over a half cut field of maize gives the scene a warm, productive harvest mood.
