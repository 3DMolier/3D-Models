About This Model

A decorative pattern paint roller with a plastic handle, this tool prints repeating designs on walls in renovation and craft scenes. The 1,986 polygons and 2,059 vertices shape the embossed roller sleeve, the metal frame and the curved grip. The raised pattern on the sleeve transfers paint in a motif, unlike a plain foam roller that leaves a flat even coat.

This StemCell model from TurboSquid ships with materials that work across popular render engines. Because the UVs overlap, the repeating pattern shares texture space, keeping the asset compact and detailed. A rubbery sleeve shader with paint residue and a glossy plastic handle add workshop realism.

Released in 2025, the roller suits interior decorating tutorials, DIY home makeover scenes and hardware store catalogs. Shown rolling a floral pattern onto a fresh wall, it explains the technique in one shot. Craft workshops place it beside paint trays and stencils on a workbench covered with old newspaper.
