About This Model

Priced at $39, the Laurus Nobilis Shrub is a finished Nature & Plants model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 304,459 polygons and 739,242 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for architectural visualization and film & TV VFX.
