About This Model

A carved jack-o'-lantern pumpkin prepared for 3D printing, with a cut face ready to glow from a tealight inside. Its ribbed rind, curled stem and sharp carved openings are shaped to print cleanly as one solid decoration.

Built to CheckMate specification, the model keeps sensible proportions and an orderly structure before slicing. Printed in orange filament, it looks like a real pumpkin, while white or glow-in-the-dark plastic gives a ghostly version.

On sale since 2025, it suits Halloween home decor, party table centerpieces and seasonal maker projects. An LED candle behind the carved eyes throws a flickering face onto the wall in a dark room. Small prints work as place markers on a dinner table, while larger ones greet trick-or-treaters by the door. Schools and makerspaces can print a batch to teach basic 3D printing during the autumn term. Painted with acrylics, a print gains the dark streaks and stem color of a freshly carved gourd.
