About This Model

The Multi Injector 5 Needles Circular Blue is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $12 on TurboSquid. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2024, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

Counted at the source, the model is 16,794 polygons and 16,740 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
