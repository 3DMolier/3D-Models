About This Model

Priced at $49, the Parrot Bebop Quadcopter Drone is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 93,692 polygons over 95,474 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 15 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

The listing flags aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

What texture resolution ships with the Parrot Bebop Quadcopter Drone?

15 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.

How heavy is the Parrot Bebop Quadcopter Drone mesh?

93,692 polygons and 95,474 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Can the Parrot Bebop Quadcopter Drone model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.
