About This Model

This Aircraft model - Parrot Bebop Quadcopter Drone - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. 2017 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Geometry weighs in at 93,692 polygons over 95,474 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 15 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
