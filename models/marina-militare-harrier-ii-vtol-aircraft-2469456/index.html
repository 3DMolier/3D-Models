About This Model

This Aircraft model - Marina Militare Harrier II VTOL Aircraft - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. You are getting 194,496 polygons and 206,192 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 8.82 x 13.31 x H4.36 m, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.
