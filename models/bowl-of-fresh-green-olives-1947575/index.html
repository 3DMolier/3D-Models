About This Model

Bowl of Fresh Olives belongs to our Kitchen & Tableware range and is offered at $39 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 9,156 polygons and 4,634 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2022, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
