About This Model

The Bowl of Fresh Green Olives is a production-ready Kitchen & Tableware 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. Available since 2022, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

Counted at the source, the model is 9,156 polygons and 4,634 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.

Questions About This Model

What is the Bowl of Fresh Green Olives model typically used for?

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and 3D printing - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

Can the Bowl of Fresh Green Olives model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free licence allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.
