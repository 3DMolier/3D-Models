About This Model

A full teardown is only useful if every part is its own object. Display assembly, battery, logic board, camera module, taptic engine, shields, brackets and screws all exist separately here, which is what allows an exploded layout, a flat lay for a teardown spread, or an animation that puts the phone back together.

The mesh runs 325,384 polygons and 168,857 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the density concentrated in the camera assembly and the connector housings where a macro camera lands.

At $99 and built to StemCell specification, it suits repair and service documentation, technology journalism and teardown content, product and engineering marketing, teaching material and games. On sale since 2023.
