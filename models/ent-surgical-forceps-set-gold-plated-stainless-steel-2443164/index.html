About This Model

This ENT surgical forceps set holds fine instruments for ear, nose and throat procedures, each with gold-plated finger rings. Its 45,782 polygons over 46,088 vertices define the slender bayonet shafts, the serrated tips and the ratchet locks. Bent shafts keep the surgeon's hand out of the line of sight, a feature unique to instruments for narrow passages.

Following CheckMate specification, each instrument has non-overlapping UVs separating polished steel from gold plating. Mirror-bright stainless steel against warm gold handles signals a premium surgical tray at a glance.

Released in 2025, the set suits operating rooms, ENT clinics, medical training and surgical supply catalogs. Laid out on a sterile green drape before surgery, the forceps show a team preparing for a sinus procedure. Medical students can study the tips to learn how each forceps grips tissue in tight cavities. Hospital dramas gain a precise touch when the gold rings catch the operating lamp during a close-up.
