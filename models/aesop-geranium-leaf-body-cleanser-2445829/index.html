About This Model

An amber pump bottle with a printed label and dispenser head, this model brings a spa feel to bathroom and vanity scenes. The mesh is light at 5,075 polygons and 4,998 vertices, focused on the pump neck and the rounded shoulder. Real size is 7.07 x 7.09 x H18.17 cm, so it scales correctly next to sinks and towels.

Built to CheckMate specification, the geometry follows TurboSquid's rules for clean structure and accurate proportions. Non-overlapping UVs let the label artwork sit flat and stay easy to edit or swap. The tinted glass invites refraction, which pays off when it stands against a window or a bright tile wall.

Two versions come on this page at $24 each, the AESOP Geranium Leaf Body Cleanser and a generic Bottle with Dispenser and Liquid Mockup. Released in 2025, it fits bathroom interiors, cosmetics shelves and skincare product renders. The blank version is the better choice when a label of your own has to go on the glass.
