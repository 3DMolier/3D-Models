About This Model

The Realistic Chocolate Bar is a production-ready Food & Beverages 3D model, priced at $4 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 6,862 polygons and 6,864 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
