About This Model

A bright yellow feather duster with a fluffy plume, the classic cleaning tool for housework scenes. Soft dyed feathers burst outward from a slim handle, creating a round silhouette that reads instantly in any shot. The plume shape is generous and slightly uneven, which keeps it from looking like a perfect sphere under studio light.

CheckMate Lite certification confirms that the file opens cleanly and the geometry is ready to render. Yellow feathers pop against pale walls and wooden shelves, making the duster a cheerful accent in a neutral interior. Lay it across a sideboard or hold it in a character's hand and the cleaning story tells itself.

Feather Duster Yellow has been available since 2020 for household, hotel and retro comedy visuals. Cleaning service brands, home organization blogs and cartoon housekeeping scenes all use the duster as shorthand for tidying up. It also works as set dressing in vintage maid rooms, antique shops and dusty libraries full of old books.
