About This Model

Wide flared legs and a buttoned front flap give these naval trousers the silhouette sailors wore aboard British warships for generations. The HMS Sailor Bell Bottom Trousers carry 18,800 polygons and 19,334 vertices, built entirely from quads so the fabric folds subdivide smoothly.

Its bounding box of 62.9 x 30.57 x H122.54 cm matches an adult wearer, so it slips onto a standard character without rescaling. As a StemCell asset it ships in several native formats converted from one master scene rather than exported loosely. The dark wool reads well under hard deck lighting, and the flare at each hem catches the wind in motion shots.

The model went on sale in 2026 and costs $39 as a single pair with no alternate cuts. Costume designers, period film teams and naval museum projects use it to dress sailors for scenes set between the wars. Pair it with a jumper and collar to build a full Royal Navy rating for historical reconstructions.
