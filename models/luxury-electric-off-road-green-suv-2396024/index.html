About This Model

Luxury Electric Off Road Green SUV is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $149. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. It has been on sale since 2025 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.

The mesh carries 471,916 polygons and 482,257 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
