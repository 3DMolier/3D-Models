About This Model

The Human Torso Nervous System is a production-ready Medical 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 11,188 polygons over 11,282 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 10 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
