About This Model

This Electronics & Gadgets model - Pentax K-S1 Orange - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $49 on TurboSquid. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review program, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. Available since 2016, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.

The mesh carries 71,101 polygons and 74,783 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 29 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
