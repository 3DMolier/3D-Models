About This Model

A bundle of carved wooden spoons in various lengths and bowl shapes, from slim stirring spoons to wide serving ladles. Every spoon is a separate object, so a jar of utensils, a hanging rack or a single spoon in a pot is easy to build.

The set is put together to CheckMate guidelines, keeping scale and naming consistent across every piece in the bundle. Smooth bowls and tapered handles show the shapes of hand-carved kitchen tools rather than factory plastic. Wood grain textures can run along each handle, adding warmth to rustic kitchens and farmhouse food scenes.

Two set versions are available: the Wooden Spoons Collection and the Wooden Spoons Collection 2 with other shapes. New to the catalog in 2026, it suits cooking videos, kitchen interiors and handmade craft visuals. Stood upright in a ceramic crock beside the stove, the spoons make a counter look well used. Laid out on linen in a neat fan, they also create a clean flat lay for a recipe page.
