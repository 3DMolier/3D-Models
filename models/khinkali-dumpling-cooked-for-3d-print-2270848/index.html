About This Model

A cooked Georgian khinkali with pleated dough gathered into a twisted topknot, prepared as a solid shape for 3D printing. The pleats fan around the crown in regular folds, and the swollen base shows the round belly of a dumpling filled with broth. That silhouette makes it instantly recognizable as khinkali rather than a generic dumpling, even printed in plain gray resin.

The model follows the CheckMate specification, so its scale and structure are tidy enough for slicing software to read without repairs. Printed at small size it becomes a fridge magnet or a menu display piece, while larger prints suit restaurant window decor. The surface carries soft dough wrinkles that paint well with a matte finish after printing.

Released in 2024, Khinkali Dumpling Cooked for 3D Print has reached buyers who need a recognizable Caucasian food shape. Its Royalty Free license covers commercial prints, so a cafe can sell souvenirs or decorate its counter with the result. Pair it with a printed wine cup or a bread board to build a small Georgian table diorama.
