About This Model

A collection of female hands with painted fingernails in different colors and poses, ready for beauty and manicure visuals. Female Hand with Fingernail Polish Collection shows relaxed and posed hands with slender fingers and neatly shaped nails for close-up work. Glossy polish catches highlights along each nail, while skin shows subtle knuckle creases and natural tone variation.

With CheckMate Pro approval, the hands offer clean topology, natural proportions and predictable file naming. Hands can be placed individually, which lets a nail salon scene can mix colors and gestures freely. Polish colors range from soft nudes to bold reds, so a full palette can be shown side by side.

Released in 2022, the collection suits manicure tutorials, cosmetics packaging renders and nail salon interiors. A hand holding a polish bottle under soft daylight makes a clean, inviting shot for a beauty tutorial. Salon owners can preview color charts on realistic hands before printing a menu of shades.
