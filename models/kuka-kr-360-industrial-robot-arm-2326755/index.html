About This Model

Looking for a Industrial Equipment asset? The KUKA KR-360 Industrial Robot Arm is a finished, render-ready model at $79. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are industrial visualization, operator training and software demos. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use.
