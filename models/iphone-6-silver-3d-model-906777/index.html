About This Model

iPhone 6 is one of the Electronics & Gadgets models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. You are getting 16,803 polygons and 18,127 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

7 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. 2015 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
