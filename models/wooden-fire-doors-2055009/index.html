About This Model

A pair of wooden fire doors with vision panels and push hardware, the kind that close off corridors in public buildings. Its 24,136 polygons and 23,453 vertices form the door leaves, the frame, the glazed openings and the handles. Fire doors are thicker and heavier than standard doors, and this pair reflects that solid, institutional look.

UV islands overlap in places, so a single veneer finish across both leaves works better than unique wear. Under TurboSquid's CheckMate Pro tier, the doors were checked for topology, real scale and organized naming. Leaves are separate from the frame, so they can swing open in an animation or a walkthrough of a hallway.

Out since 2023 and free to use commercially, they suit hospital, school and office architecture visualization. Architects place them in corridor renders to show fire compartments, while games use them in hospital levels. Safety training videos also show doors like this closing automatically, explaining why they must never be wedged open.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2023 · Updated 13 November 2026
