About This Model

This collection gathers championship belts, the heavy gold title belts awarded in boxing, wrestling and mixed martial arts. Each belt has a large center plate, side plates and a thick leather strap with engraved metal details. Draped over a shoulder or held overhead, a belt is the clearest symbol of a winner in combat sports.

CheckMate Lite inspection checked the scale and the basic file order of every belt in the set. Gold plates respond well to warm spotlights, which recreate the glare of an arena ring. Laid out on a table, several belts also make a striking display for a trophy room.

The page offers two versions: the Championship Belts Collection for $79 and a second belt set for $119. The belts reached our catalog in 2023 and are free of royalties for creative work. Fighting games, sports broadcast graphics and gym interiors use these belts as rewards and decor.
