About This Model

A golf bag is a portable rack with a strict order to it. Woods at the top where the long shafts have room, irons in the middle, putter wherever the owner has decided, and the whole arrangement is personal enough that players notice immediately when it is wrong.

The model holds 61,829 polygons and 62,054 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured generously with 51 maps at 2048x2048, and supplies the bag together with the clubs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $79 it suits sports and equipment visualization, retail and e-commerce imagery, advertising, club and course scenes, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2016.
