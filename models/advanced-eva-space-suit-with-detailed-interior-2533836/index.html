About This Model

A spacewalk suit is a spacecraft shaped like a person. It has a rigid torso, a life support backpack, a helmet with a gold visor, and layers of insulation that give the arms and legs their quilted look. This build includes the interior, which is unusual and useful: it lets the suit be shown open, being entered, or cut away to explain how it works.

The mesh runs 202,921 polygons and 214,487 vertices in quads and tris with mixed UV layout, with the density in the joint bearings, the backpack fittings and the helmet ring.

At $229 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits space industry visualization, documentary and news graphics, museum and education material, film and television, and games. Real-world scale is kept, so it fits a human figure. On sale since 2026.
