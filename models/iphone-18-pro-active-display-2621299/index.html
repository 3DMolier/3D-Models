About This Model

A smartphone shown with its screen switched on, the iPhone 18 Pro Active Display gives tech scenes a device that looks in use. Its 11,776 polygons and 12,258 vertices shape the rounded frame, the raised camera plateau and the side buttons.

The phone measures 7.19 x 1.36 x H14.98 cm, matching a real handset held in a hand or lying on a desk. Its CheckMate build keeps the frame edges sharp and the scale true, both vital under studio lighting. The lit display is the focus, so an emissive screen material replaces any need for a separate interface render. The lens cluster at the back casts a small shadow that sells the realism in top-down shots.

It is a single version from 2026 priced at $34 and licensed for editorial uses only. News articles, tech reviews, educational explainers and documentary graphics use it to illustrate the latest phone generation. A low budget means it can also sit on tables and in pockets throughout an office scene.
