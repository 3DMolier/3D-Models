About This Model

Cartoon Thunderbolt is one of the Nature & Plants models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $19. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.

The mesh carries 12,210 polygons and 12,190 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
