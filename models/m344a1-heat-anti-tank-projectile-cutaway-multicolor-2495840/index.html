About This Model

Looking for a Weapons asset? The HEAT Cutaway is a finished, render-ready model at $34. Counted at the source, the model is 16,668 polygons and 16,748 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 14.07 x 14.07 x H98.85 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
