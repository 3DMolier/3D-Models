About This Model

The Templars were a banking order that happened to fight, and the white surcoat with its cross was a uniform in an age that had almost none. It is one of the few medieval costumes recognisable to people who know nothing else about the period.

The figure holds 34,036 polygons and 35,733 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 19 maps all at 4096x4096, and is supplied in a T pose ready for your own rig. It carries StemCell certification, and a walking pose is offered separately.

At $129 it suits game characters, historical and fantasy film work, education and museum material, tabletop illustration and previsualisation. On sale since 2020.
