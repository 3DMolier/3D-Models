About This Model

Priced at $129, the Knight Templar is a finished Characters & People model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 34,036 polygons over 35,733 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

19 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications. First published in 2020, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
