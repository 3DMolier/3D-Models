About This Model

The Theatre Masks is a production-ready Clothing & Accessories 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. You are getting 4,953 polygons and 4,942 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2015, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published February 2015 · Updated 4 September 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Theatre Masks model be used in a commercial project?

Commercial use is included. The Royalty Free license allows the model in paid client work, broadcast, published games and print; only redistributing the model file itself is excluded.

Can the Theatre Masks model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

Where can I buy the Theatre Masks 3D model?

It is sold through TurboSquid, where this listing has been available since 2015. Purchase, download and licensing are handled by TurboSquid; delivery is immediate after checkout.

What texture resolution ships with the Theatre Masks?

4 maps at 4096 x 4096, with yes, non-overlapping UVs. That is enough for close framing without resampling.
