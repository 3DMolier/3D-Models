About This Model

An older couple with shovels, rakes and watering cans, ready to bring backyard and community garden scenes to life. Gardening is one of the few everyday activities that shows seniors as active, which makes these figures valuable.

The woman wears a sun hat and apron, while the man leans on a long-handled spade in work boots. The tools are separate objects, so a wheelbarrow or a rake can move to a fence or another figure's hands. Their poses are relaxed and mid-task, which avoids the stiff look of characters staged for a portrait.

First published in 2021, the set still populates residential renders, garden center visuals and retirement community brochures. Place them among raised vegetable beds, and a plain backyard becomes a scene with a story and a season. Earthy fabric colors blend naturally with soil, leaves and wooden planters under afternoon sunlight. Clean geometry built to CheckMate rules lets the couple be scaled and posed without broken faces.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2021 · Updated 11 November 2026
