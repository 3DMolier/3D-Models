About This Model

A collection of breakfast cereal bowls for food styling, kitchen scenes and morning lifestyle renders. Each bowl holds cereal pieces, with milk pooling around golden flakes and rings near the rim. Spoons and bowls can be arranged on a table to stage a family breakfast in minutes, ready for a top view.

Every bowl was produced under the CheckMate specification, keeping scale consistent from one bowl to the next. Milk needs a little subsurface scattering, which keeps it from looking like white paint. Crisp cereal pieces benefit from a warm key light that shows their toasted edges and the drops of milk on them.

This first collection is $49, and the second version, Bowl of Breakfast Cereals Collection 2, sells for $69. Available since 2024, the bowls suit packaging mockups, nutrition lessons and cozy kitchen animations. Shot from above on a wooden table with morning sun, a single bowl makes a strong hero image.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 11 November 2026
