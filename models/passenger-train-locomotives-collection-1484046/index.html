About This Model

A collection of passenger train locomotives, the powered units that haul intercity and commuter services across rail networks. Cab windows, pantographs or exhaust stacks and long side panels give each locomotive its own character and era. Several types in the set let a station platform show realistic variety rather than a single repeated engine.

CheckMate Pro certification confirms real-world scale, so each locomotive matches standard track gauge and platform height. Consistent naming across the collection makes it easy to attach coaches or swap liveries in bulk. Grime along the underframe and bright paint above keep the trains looking like working stock rather than museum pieces.

Available since 2019, the locomotives suit railway games, transport planning presentations, model railway design and travel documentaries. A locomotive leading a train into a station is still the most dramatic image of rail travel. Rail operators also use such collections to show modernization plans to passengers and investors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2019 · Updated 11 November 2026
