About This Model

The Cockroach Spinning is a production-ready Animals & Creatures 3D model, priced at $149 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 14,642 polygons over 14,510 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 6 maps at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

The listing flags film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering as the intended applications. Available since 2019, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
