About This Model

Animated Cockroach Spinning Rigged is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $149. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for film & TV VFX, game assets and product rendering. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It suits game creatures, film and TV VFX, educational material and VR experiences.
