Looking for a Clothing & Accessories asset? The Hanging Lab Coat is a finished, render-ready model at $49. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine.
