About This Model

This Clothing & Accessories model - Space Suit Strizh with SK-1 Helmet - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $149 on TurboSquid. The model is CheckMate certified - a human reviewer checked the wireframe, the material assignments and the real-world dimensions before it went on sale. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Counted at the source, the model is 62,620 polygons and 64,244 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 13 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
