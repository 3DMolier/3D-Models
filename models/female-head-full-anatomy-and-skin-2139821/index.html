About This Model

The head is where anatomy and likeness meet. Every muscle under the skin has a visible consequence on the surface, and modeling both layers together is the only way to explain why an expression looks the way it does rather than simply reproducing it.

The model holds 196,594 polygons and 195,407 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, supplying the full anatomy together with the skin surface. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $149 it suits medical and dental education, surgical and aesthetic visualization, anatomy reference for artists, character work and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
