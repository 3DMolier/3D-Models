Mens Suit Jacket 4 is one of the Clothing & Accessories models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. Geometry weighs in at 14,911 polygons over 15,418 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
