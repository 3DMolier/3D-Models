About This Model

A ready group of charcoal grills lets a backyard or patio scene gain its cooking corner without modeling kettles and grates from scratch. Grills like these are defined by round or boxy fireboxes, wire cooking grates, vent dampers and spindly legs, all of which read well in silhouette.

The bundle meets the CheckMate specification, meaning its geometry and file setup passed the marketplace's baseline technical checks. Painted steel bowls and chrome handles want different shaders, and the separate parts make it simple to assign enamel, soot or rust.

This page offers three versions: the Charcoal Grills Collection at $74, a cheaper Collection 2 for $66 and a pricier Collection 3 for $128. The sets arrived in 2026, so they are among the newest outdoor cooking props in our range. Placed beside a picnic table with a little smoke, any of them turns an empty lawn into a weekend gathering. Typical destinations are garden furniture renders, barbecue product mockups, suburban game levels and summer lifestyle animation.
