About This Model

A baby girl playing inside a playpen surrounded by toys, with groomed hair and a full rig for nursery and family home scenes. Only 52,962 polygons and 54,003 vertices cover the child, the mesh walls of the pen and the scattered toys. Soft padded rails and netting keep the little one safe while she sits among blocks and plush animals.

The rig lets her reach, sit up or clap, so a short clip of play time comes together quickly. Each piece has unique UV space, letting the pen fabric take new colors and the toys receive fresh patterns. The scene meets CheckMate requirements for scale, so the playpen and toys sit in correct proportion to the baby.

Introduced in 2024, the scene works for childcare product visuals, parenting blogs and gentle family animation for young viewers. A sunny living room carpet and soft daylight from a window make the playpen moment feel warm and safe.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 14 November 2026
