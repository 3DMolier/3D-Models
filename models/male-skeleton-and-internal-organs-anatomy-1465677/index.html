About This Model

Bone and organ together is a specific and very common teaching combination: the ribcage over the lungs and heart, the pelvis around the lower organs, the spine behind everything. Showing those two layers without muscle in between is how spatial relationships get taught most clearly.

The mesh runs 486,865 polygons and 488,161 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, and the texture set runs to 646 maps including one at 8192x8192, 85 at 4096x4096, 346 at 2048x2048 and 214 at 1024x1024.

At $379 it suits medical and health education, clinical and patient communication, courseware and textbook illustration, radiology and imaging explanation, and broadcast medical work. On sale since 2020.
