About This Model

Priced at $49, the Arm with Tray is a finished Industrial Equipment model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 20,916 polygons over 21,390 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. The listing flags industrial visualization, operator training and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is useful for industrial and factory visualization, product rendering, game props and technical presentations.
