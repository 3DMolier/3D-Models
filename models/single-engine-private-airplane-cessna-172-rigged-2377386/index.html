About This Model

Single Engine Private Airplane Cessna 172 is one of the Aircraft models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $129. You are getting 352,276 polygons and 366,569 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

The listing dates back to 2025, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
