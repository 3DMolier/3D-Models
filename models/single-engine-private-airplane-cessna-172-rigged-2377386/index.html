About This Model

Looking for a Aircraft asset? The Single Engine Private Airplane Cessna 172 Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $129. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. 2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Geometry weighs in at 352,276 polygons over 366,569 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
