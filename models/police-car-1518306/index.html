About This Model

Priced at $129, the Police Car is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. It carries TurboSquid's CheckMate certification, which means an independent reviewer verified the topology, the naming of objects and materials, and that the geometry is built to real-world scale. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. 2020 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.

Geometry weighs in at 303,840 polygons over 319,307 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 8 maps, the largest at 8192x8192, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
