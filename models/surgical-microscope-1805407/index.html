About This Model

Surgical Microscope is a detailed Medical asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $119. You are getting 125,454 polygons and 131,981 vertices, measured rather than estimated. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and software demos.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
