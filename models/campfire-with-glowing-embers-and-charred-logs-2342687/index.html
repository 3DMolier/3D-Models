About This Model

A campfire with burned logs turns an empty forest clearing into a place where someone has stopped for the night. The Campfire with Glowing Embers and Charred Logs builds that small detail from 92,749 polygons and 93,877 vertices.

The logs lean together in a loose cone, blackened outside and split where heat has cracked the wood open. Between them, a bed of embers gives the light source a believable base. Geometry follows CheckMate specification, so every log stays clean and sized to sit naturally in a clearing.

New in 2025, the fire suits camping scenes, survival games and cabin shots where a fire pit glows outside the window. Lighting artists hide a warm point light inside the stack, so the charred surfaces catch orange highlights. Film teams set it beside tents and backpacks for night sequences in the woods. Game designers place it at rest points, so players recognize a safe spot from across the map.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2025 · Updated 16 November 2026
