About This Model

A set of female hands prepared for Cinema 4D, made for close-ups where a hand holds a product, a phone or a cup. Slim fingers, defined nails and a soft palm line are what make a female hand read correctly in a macro shot. Native files for Cinema 4D mean the materials and hierarchy load as intended, without conversion headaches.

Units and object names follow CheckMate practice, though this collection carries no certification. Manicure work is easy to show, since nails can take any polish color through a simple material change. Choosing the Cinema 4D edition saves time for studios whose motion design pipeline is built around it.

Released in 2026, the hands suit cosmetics visuals, jewelry rings, smartphone demos and motion graphics openers. The royalty free license covers commercial client work, including beauty campaigns and app store videos. A soft key light and a little skin translucency keep the fingers looking alive rather than waxy.
