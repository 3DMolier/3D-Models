About This Model

Priced at $12, the Spotlight Security Camera is a finished Tools model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 14,614 polygons over 7,473 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags technical visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

It has been on sale since 2023 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
