About This Model

The arm is the part of the body people understand least well despite using it constantly. Shoulder, elbow and wrist are three joints of completely different design, and the muscles that drive the hand mostly sit in the forearm rather than the hand itself. A skinless arm makes that visible in a way a textbook diagram does not.

The model holds 44,286 polygons and 44,474 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, showing the muscular system over the bone structure. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $199 it suits medical education and patient explanation, anatomy reference for artists, physiotherapy and sports science material, and character work that needs correct underlying structure. On sale since 2025.
