About This Model

Priced at $9, the Old Ship Anchor is a finished Vehicles model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 2,052 polygons and 2,052 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 4 maps at 4096 x 4096. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for driving simulation, automotive advertising and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2017 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
