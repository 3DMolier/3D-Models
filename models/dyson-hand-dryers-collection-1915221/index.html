About This Model

Dyson Hand Dryers Collection gathers wall-mounted and tap-integrated hand dryers, the sleek appliances found in airport and office restrooms. Blade-style units with a slot for the hands sit beside compact models that blow air from above the sink. A row of dryers beside a line of basins is a quick way to show a busy terminal washroom.

Every dryer carries CheckMate Pro certification, so the files open cleanly at real scale beside sinks and mirrors. Licensing is Editorial Uses Only, which suits news, product reviews, architecture journalism and educational material. Each unit is its own object, ready to be placed at the right height above a counter.

Dyson Hand Dryers Collection 2 is the wider version for $119, while this collection sells for $59. Since 2022 the dryers have appeared in restroom design studies, editorial architecture articles and hygiene education videos. Brushed metal and glossy plastic reflect tile and lights, so reflections matter more than texture here.
