About This Model

A collection of mythical gods, sculpted figures of ancient deities gathered for games, illustration and educational projects about mythology. Divine attributes like weapons, crowns and flowing robes help each figure read as a specific god at a glance. Collecting a whole pantheon in one set keeps proportions and sculpting style consistent across the entire cast.

Every figure follows CheckMate specification, sharing consistent scale and organized geometry for scene assembly. Each deity stands apart in the file, so they can gather in a temple or appear alone in a scene.

Since 2024 the figures have served mythology games, history lessons, fantasy book covers and museum-style displays. Marble shaders turn them into temple statues, while painted skin brings them to life as characters. Teachers can line the gods up to compare how different cultures imagined power, war and nature. A low camera looking up at a single deity makes the figure feel towering and divine.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

What is the Mythical Gods Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Mythical Gods Collection mesh?

91,495 polygons and 48,127 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Our Model Bundles & Sets listings sit at a median of about 28,000 polygons, so 91,495 is heavier than the typical one.

How clean is the geometry on the Mythical Gods Collection model?

It is built as an organized scene, not a single welded mesh - parts are separable, named and sized to real-world dimensions.

Does the Mythical Gods Collection model include materials and textures?

The product page states what ships with each format. Textures, when present, come inside the download rather than as a separate purchase. See the listing for the exact contents.
