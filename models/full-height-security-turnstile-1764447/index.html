About This Model

The Security Turnstile is a production-ready Architecture & Landmarks 3D model, priced at $29 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 15,580 polygons over 17,156 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

First published in 2021, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

Questions About This Model

Can the Security Turnstile model be used in a commercial project?

Yes. It is sold under TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, which covers commercial use in games, film, advertising and visualization without per-use fees.

What is the Security Turnstile model typically used for?

It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How heavy is the Security Turnstile mesh?

15,580 polygons and 17,156 vertices, built as polygonal quads/tris. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

What does StemCell mean for the Security Turnstile model?

It means one source asset, several delivered formats, and a PBR material setup designed to read consistently across them - no rebuilding shaders after import.
