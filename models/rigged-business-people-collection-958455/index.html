About This Model

Rigged Business People Collection is a detailed Model Bundles & Sets asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $649. CheckMate is TurboSquid's manual review programme, and this model passed it: no n-gons where they would hurt, no unnamed objects, no scale surprises on import.
