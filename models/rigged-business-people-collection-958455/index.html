About This Model

This Model Bundles & Sets model - Business People Collection - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $649 on TurboSquid. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

On this listing the stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2015, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. The set covers several related objects at once, which saves assembling a scene from separate purchases.
