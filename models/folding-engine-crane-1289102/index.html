About This Model

A folding hydraulic engine hoist, the shop crane used to lift motors out of cars and a signature garage prop. The Folding Engine Crane uses 59,763 polygons and 59,947 vertices for the boom, the hydraulic ram, the chain hook and the casters. Hinged legs and a telescoping boom are easy to read, so the crane looks believable in a cramped workshop.

There are 17 texture maps at 4096 x 4096, enough to show chipped paint and oily grime on the steel. UV islands do not overlap, and CheckMate Pro certification confirms a clean model ready for scene assembly. Folded upright, it takes little floor space and can lean against a wall in the background of a garage.

On offer since 2018 at $49, the crane suits automotive animators, game artists and restoration documentaries. Mechanic training courses use it whenever an engine has to come out of a vehicle on screen. Swing the boom over an open hood and hang a motor from the chain for a convincing repair scene.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published May 2018 · Updated 21 November 2026
