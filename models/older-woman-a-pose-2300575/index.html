About This Model

Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Older Woman is a finished, render-ready model at $129. Counted at the source, the model is 13,914 polygons and 19,360 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

2024 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for game characters, crowd fills in film and TV, previsualization and VR scenes.
