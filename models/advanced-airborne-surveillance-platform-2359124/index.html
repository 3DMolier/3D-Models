About This Model

Priced at $149, the Advanced Airborne Surveillance Platform is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 156,437 polygons and 171,107 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. Buyers most often take it for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2025, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.
