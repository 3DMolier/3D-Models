About This Model

The R22 is the helicopter most civilian pilots learn on, and it is tiny: two seats, a teetering two-blade rotor, skids and a slim tail boom, with a control system that uses a shared T-bar cyclic rising between the seats. That T-bar is one of the details a pilot looks for immediately.

The mesh runs 230,562 polygons and 243,228 vertices in quads and tris. The UVs overlap, which is a sound economy on a small symmetrical airframe, and the CheckMate build keeps the surfacing clean enough for close inspection.

At $129 it suits flight training and general aviation visualisation, helicopter school marketing, airfield and heliport scenes, film and television, and games. On sale since 2026.
