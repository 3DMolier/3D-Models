About This Model

This gray Phantom alloy wheel with a mounted tire suits modern SUVs and crossovers that need a sporty, clean look. Its 42,568 polygons and 43,312 vertices define the sharp spoke design, the center cap and the tire sidewall. Gray metallic paint sits between bright silver and black, a finish that reads well on many body colors.

CheckMate-conformant geometry keeps rim and tire organized for quick material changes in any renderer. The UVs overlap, so brand stamps or valve stem details should be handled with decals. A reflective environment map is the best friend of this wheel, picking out every edge of the spokes.

Its 2024 debut came with a $39 price, and it fits car configurators, driving games and garage scenes. Mount it on a crossover or display it on a wall rack in a tire shop showroom. A low, rolling camera along the sidewall turns it into a strong hero shot for wheel retailers.
