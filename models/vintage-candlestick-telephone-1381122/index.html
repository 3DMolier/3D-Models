About This Model

Priced at $39, the Candlestick Telephone is a finished Electronics & Gadgets model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh carries 17,472 polygons and 17,705 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

7 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 resolution. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX. It has been on sale since 2019 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
