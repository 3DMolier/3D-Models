About This Model

An eagle ray flies rather than swims. The pectoral fins beat like wings in a slow travelling wave, the body barely moves, and the long tail trails behind as a counterweight. Reproducing that motion is a rigging problem rather than an animation one: the wave has to propagate along the fin, and a rig that simply flaps produces something closer to a bird.

This build arrives rigged and animated in a swim cycle, prepared for Maya, so both the deformation and the motion are already solved in the application it will be used in.

At $269 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits marine documentary and education, aquarium and museum material, underwater film and television, dive tourism visualization, and games. On sale since 2026.
