About This Model

Human Fetus at 24 Weeks belongs to our Medical range and is offered at $79 through the TurboSquid marketplace. It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX. 2021 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalogue since. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualisation and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 15,782 polygons and 15,774 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. Texture work runs to 18 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
