About This Model

This Furniture & Interior model - Public Waiting Seating - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $29 on TurboSquid. Geometry is modelled to real-world proportions with a tidy edge flow, which keeps it usable both as a background element and in closer shots.
