About This Model

Looking for a Kitchen & Tableware asset? The Full Ice Tray with Cover Red is a finished, render-ready model at $19. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX. First published in 2023, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.

Counted at the source, the model is 53,920 polygons and 26,992 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
