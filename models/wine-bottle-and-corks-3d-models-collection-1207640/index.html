About This Model

A wine bottle paired with a handful of natural corks, ready for tasting tables, cellar shelves and restaurant scenes. The bottle shows a classic profile with a long neck, while the corks are short cylinders with softly rounded ends. Corks can be tipped on their sides or stacked in a dish, which adds casual texture to a table.

As a CheckMate Lite asset, the set offers clean, usable pieces at a believable size. Non-overlapping UVs let artists add a custom label, a foil capsule and the porous texture of cork. Glass and liquid shaders do most of the work on the bottle, so lighting matters more than polygons.

The collection has been part of the range since 2017 and remains a handy wine-themed starter kit. Wine bars, vineyard websites and food photographers arrange the corks around the bottle for tasting and pairing scenes. A few corks scattered near a corkscrew suggest a relaxed evening right after the bottle was opened.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2017 · Updated 13 November 2026
