About This Model

A female arm shown in layers, with bones, muscles, blood vessels and the lymphatic network beneath the skin. The 53,783 polygons describe the humerus, radius and ulna, the forearm muscles and the branching arteries and veins. With 54,025 vertices the model is light enough for tablets and web viewers used in anatomy classes.

Tissue colors are assigned through shaders you build, because no texture maps come with the arm. Each layer sits on separate islands of the unwrap, so muscles or vessels can be isolated in a lesson. TurboSquid granted CheckMate Lite after a check that the file loads cleanly in every supported format.

Introduced in 2023, the arm serves physiotherapy explainers, nursing courses and injection training material. Peeling the skin back to the muscles in an animation shows exactly where a nerve or vessel runs. Posed with the palm open under neutral light, the arm reads like a classic atlas illustration.
