About This Model

Priced at $19, the South Korea 5 Won 1983 Coin is a finished general model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 1,408 polygons over 1,410 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.
