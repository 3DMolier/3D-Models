About This Model

Priced at $29, the Stack of Colorful Rubber Bands is a finished model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. As a StemCell asset it comes with standardised PBR materials, so the look holds up whether it lands in a game engine or an offline renderer. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for film & TV VFX and product rendering. The listing dates back to 2020, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It is ready for game assets, film and TV VFX, product rendering and VR experiences.

The mesh carries 4,992 polygons and 4,992 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene. Maps: 5 of them, peaking at 4096x4096, which is where the close-up detail comes from. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
