Looking for a Characters & People asset? The Astronaut Head is a finished, render-ready model at $39. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.

Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. On this listing the stated applications are game characters, film & TV VFX and product rendering. First published in 2016, it is part of the long-running core of the collection.
