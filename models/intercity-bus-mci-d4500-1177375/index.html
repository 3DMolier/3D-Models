About This Model

The MCI coach is the shape of long distance travel in North America. Luggage under the floor, a high floor cabin above it, and a body designed for a driver who will be behind the wheel for eight hours rather than for a city route with a stop every block.

The model holds 259,457 polygons and 274,608 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, textured with 26 maps, 3 at 8192x8192, 11 at 4096x4096, 10 at 2048x2048, one at 1024x1024 and one at 512x512. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification, and a rigged build is offered separately.

At $129 it suits transport and travel visualization, fleet and livery mockups, street and architectural scenes, advertising, film backgrounds and game environments. On sale since 2017.
