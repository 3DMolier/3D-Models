About This Model

This Animals & Creatures model - Running Retriever Dog - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $179 on TurboSquid. You are getting 24,580 polygons and 24,623 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
