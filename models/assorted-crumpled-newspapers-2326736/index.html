About This Model

Several balled-up sheets of newsprint lie in loose heaps here, ready to litter a street corner, a desk or an empty apartment floor. The 20,670 polygons and 20,748 vertices go into folds and creases, so every crumpled page catches light in a different way. Each wad has its own shape, which keeps a scattered group from looking like copies of a single object.

Clean unwrapping without overlaps means you can project real headlines or printed columns onto the paper and keep them readable. There are no bitmap files in the set, so ink, yellowing and grime are added with materials of your own choosing. The file passed the CheckMate specification check, covering tidy geometry, correct size and a scene that opens without cleanup.

From 2024 onward, these papers have dressed alleyways, protest aftermath scenes and abandoned buildings in film and game environments. Wind simulations can push them across a road, adding some motion to an otherwise still night shot. Packing material inside a shipping box or a moving carton is another quiet use for the same crumpled sheets.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2024 · Updated 14 November 2026
