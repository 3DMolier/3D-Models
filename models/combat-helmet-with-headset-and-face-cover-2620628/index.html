About This Model

A modern combat helmet fitted with an electronic headset and a protective face cover, completing a tactical operator look in one piece. The Combat Helmet with Headset and Face Cover uses 55,487 polygons and 56,737 vertices on the shell, rails, ear cups and mesh mask. Side rails and the mount at the front leave room for lights or night vision attachments.

CheckMate certification keeps helmet, headset and cover as clean, separately named parts that fit a character head. The face cover hides the wearer entirely, which gives a faceless, intimidating presence in cinematics. Ear cups sit over the rail adapters exactly as they would on a real high-cut helmet.

It sells for $129 and has been part of the range since 2026, with Royalty Free rights included. Tactical shooters, special forces films, airsoft visualization and military concept art are its natural destinations. Dropped onto a crate or a bench, the helmet also works as a strong still-life prop in an armory.
