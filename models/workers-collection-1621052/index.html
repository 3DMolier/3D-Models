About This Model

This collection of workers in practical uniforms fills construction sites, factories and warehouses with believable people doing real jobs. Hard hats, high visibility vests and sturdy boots make each figure's role obvious even at small sizes in the frame. A varied group means a site render does not repeat one worker across every scaffold and loading bay.

Workers in this bundle passed CheckMate Pro review, so meshes, texture coordinates and file names arrive tidy. Uniform colors and company logos can be retextured to match a contractor, a factory brand or a safety initiative.

Available since 2020, the set suits architectural visualization, industrial safety training, engineering presentations and simulation games. Placing a few figures beside cranes and pallets instantly gives large industrial spaces a readable human scale. Safety trainers rely on such characters to illustrate correct protective equipment and hazard awareness on site. Group them around a site plan or a forklift and the scene suddenly feels like a working morning shift.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2020 · Updated 12 November 2026
