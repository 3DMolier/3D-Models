About This Model

Priced at $24, the Classic Bagels is a finished Food & Beverages model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. You are getting 1,776 polygons and 888 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are food photography and film & TV VFX.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits food advertising, packaging visualization, restaurant interiors and game props.
