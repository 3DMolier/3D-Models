About This Model

A surgical cap is a piece of cloth that has to look like it is doing a job: gathered by elastic around the whole opening, bulging slightly where hair is tucked under it, and sitting further back on the head than a hat would. Those three cues are what separate a scrub cap from a shower cap in a render.

The mesh is 30,534 polygons and 18,427 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, with the geometry spent on the gathered edge and the crown folds where the fabric has to behave like fabric.

At $24 it suits medical and hospital visualization, healthcare advertising and recruitment material, surgical training and education, film and television costume, and games. On sale since 2023, kept to real-world scale so it fits a standard head.
