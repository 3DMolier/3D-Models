About This Model

This Collections & Sets model — Road Barriers Collection — is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $42 on TurboSquid. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done — units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared.
