About This Model

Looking for a Weapons asset? The Battery Center is a finished, render-ready model at $119. Counted at the source, the model is 466,903 polygons and 514,135 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for defense simulation, game assets and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works as a game prop, a film and TV set element, or a reference object in training material.
