About This Model

A Buddhist statue modeled on the figure at Wat Phothikyan Phut Thak Tham in Thailand, prepared as a printable sculpture. The serene face, the flowing robe and the gentle hand gesture are the details a printed copy has to preserve. Made for printing, the model can become a shelf figurine, a meditation corner piece or a garden ornament.

The file follows TurboSquid's CheckMate specification, opening with clean proportions and clear naming. Gold leaf, aged bronze or weathered stone shaders all suit the statue when it appears in renders.

Released to the catalog in 2024 for a $31 price, the statue fits temple visualizations, travel documentaries about Thailand and spa interiors. Makers often print it at a small scale for gifts, while designers place it in calm wellness rooms. Candlelight, incense smoke and a dark backdrop around the figure set a quiet, devotional mood. Travel bloggers also use statues like this to illustrate guides to Thai temple etiquette.
