About This Model

Priced at $39, the Blood Vessel is a finished Medical model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 189,026 polygons and 189,420 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. It is cataloged for medical visualization, medical simulation and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualization and medical sequences in film.
