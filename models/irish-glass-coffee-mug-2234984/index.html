About This Model

Priced at $9, the Irish Glass Coffee Mug is a finished Kitchen & Tableware model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Counted at the source, the model is 1,320 polygons and 1,320 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

First published in 2024, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
