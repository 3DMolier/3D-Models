About This Model

A skier in full winter gear moves through ready Maya animation here, carving turns and racing down a slope. The set includes a helmet, goggles, padded jacket, skis and poles in one character package. The forward lean with bent knees captures the athletic posture of a skilled skier.

The rig lets knees, hips and arms work together, which matters for believable parallel turns. Included animation gives Maya users ready motion for runs that can be adjusted for speed or style. Pole plants and ski tips leaving tracks in the snow add the small details that make shots convincing.

New in 2026 at $429, the skier is suitable for commercial sports and travel work. Ski resort films, winter sports games, equipment explainers and alpine tourism visuals are natural destinations. A wide mountain shot with the skier small against fresh powder shows the scale of the slope.
