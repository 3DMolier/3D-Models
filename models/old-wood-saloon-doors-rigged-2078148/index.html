About This Model

Looking for a Architecture & Landmarks asset? The Old Wood Saloon Doors is a finished, render-ready model at $29. Counted at the source, the model is 31,520 polygons and 32,308 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else. It is cataloged for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
