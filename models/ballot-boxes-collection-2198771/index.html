About This Model

Transparent and solid ballot boxes with slotted lids and seals, the familiar furniture of every polling station on election day. The Ballot Boxes Collection gathers several designs, so a scene can show different countries or different voting rooms. Clear plastic walls reveal whatever is placed inside, which helps when papers need to be visible to the camera.

CheckMate Pro certification means every box passed strict checks for clean topology, correct scale and naming. Glass-like shaders need some care: give clear plastic a slight tint and thickness so it reads in renders. Because the designs share scale, swapping one box for another never breaks the layout of a scene.

In the catalog since 2024, the set suits news graphics, civic education videos and election night broadcast sets. Line the boxes along a table with voting booths behind them and the polling station builds itself. Sealed lids are worth a close shot when a story turns to election security.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
