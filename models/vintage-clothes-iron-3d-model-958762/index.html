About This Model

An old flat iron with a heavy cast body and a curved wooden handle, this household tool recalls the days before electric pressing. Its pointed nose and flat sole show how laundry was smoothed after the tool was heated on a stove. The handle arches high above the base, keeping a cook's hand away from the hot metal.

TurboSquid gave this antique a CheckMate Pro grade for clean topology, realistic scale and tidy naming. The shapes stay simple and solid, which makes the object easy to light and place on shelves or tables in larger scenes. Materials can be pushed toward rusty black metal or polished chrome, depending on the period you need.

Present since 2015, the piece suits farmhouse kitchens, period dramas, antique shop interiors and museum displays. Set dressers stand it on a mantelpiece or windowsill as a nostalgic decorative object. History teachers also use it to show domestic life in the nineteenth century, when pressing a shirt meant heavy, careful work by the fire.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2015 · Updated 12 November 2026
