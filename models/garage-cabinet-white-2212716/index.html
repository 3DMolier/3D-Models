About This Model

Looking for a Furniture & Interior asset? The Garage Cabinet is a finished, render-ready model at $34. The mesh carries 63,035 polygons and 66,121 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

The StemCell build means you are not re-authoring shaders after import - the PBR setup is designed to read the same across engines. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.
