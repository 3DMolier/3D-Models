About This Model

This collection groups playful phone stands with friendly character shapes that keep a phone upright on a desk. The Cute Phone Holders Collection uses just 15,794 polygons and 8,237 vertices across every stand in the set. Their rounded forms and simple faces read as friendly even at small sizes in a frame.

Certified CheckMate Lite, the holders are unwrapped without overlaps for bright plastic or soft resin colors. Each holder is a separate piece, so one can be recolored or placed alone on a shelf.

Published in 2024, the set fits desk and study room interiors, gift shop renders and toy product mockups. Pop a phone into one of them beside a coffee mug and a notebook to stage a cozy workspace. Small designers also print similar stands, so the collection works as reference for home printing ideas. Teen bedroom scenes in games and animations use such accessories to add personality.
