About This Model

A full ballistic face mask is one of the most immediately recognisable pieces of modern protective equipment, largely because it looks like something from fiction. The respirator underneath is the functional part; the shape above it is what everyone remembers.

The model holds 56,013 polygons and 56,350 vertices in quads and tris with overlapping UVs, in black, and carries StemCell certification.
