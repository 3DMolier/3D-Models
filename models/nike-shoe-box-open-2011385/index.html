About This Model

Shoe Box Open belongs to our Kitchen & Tableware range and is offered at $19 through the TurboSquid marketplace. You are getting 5,898 polygons and 2,943 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

It is a StemCell model, so it ships in TurboSquid's multi-format standard with PBR materials that carry over between renderers instead of needing to be rebuilt per engine. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task. On this listing the stated applications are architectural visualization, product rendering and film & TV VFX.

2023 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for interior renders, food and product advertising, and game and film set dressing.
