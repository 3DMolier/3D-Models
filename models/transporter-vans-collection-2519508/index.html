About This Model

The Transporter Vans Collection brings together boxy panel vans with sliding side doors, tall roofs and short sloping noses. These are the delivery workhorses seen on city streets, so a few of them parked along a curb add everyday realism. Rear doors and high cargo bays signal parcel work even when a van is seen from behind.

Every van in the pack is built to the CheckMate specification, keeping proportions believable and topology orderly. Glass, tires and painted panels can be shaded separately, which helps when one vehicle needs a different color. Under overcast light the vans look convincing in loading yards and narrow residential streets.

Three versions are grouped here, from this collection at $237 to two other sets with different vans. Transporter Vans Collection 2 is the lighter option at $199, while Collection 3 is the largest at $469. Offered since 2026, the vans fit urban traffic scenes, logistics explainers and parcel delivery animation.
