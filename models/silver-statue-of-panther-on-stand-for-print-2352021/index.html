About This Model

Built as a decorative figurine, this silver panther crouches mid-stride on its pedestal and is ready for desktop 3D printing. The animal's muscular back and outstretched forelegs create the tension that makes big-cat statues popular as gifts and office ornaments. Surfaces are closed and printable, so the figure can go from download to a sliced job with minimal repair.

CheckMate specification construction keeps the scene uncluttered, with a sculpture and base rather than loose helper objects. In a render, a brushed or mirror silver finish shows off the curve of the haunches under soft studio light. Hobbyists often print it in resin first, then plate or paint it to imitate cast metal.

Available from 2025, the figure fits shop window displays, reception desks and decor catalogs for interior stylists. As set dressing it adds a touch of wealth to penthouse scenes, villain offices and gallery shelves. Pairing it with marble or dark wood plinths in a render emphasizes the metallic sheen of the cat.
