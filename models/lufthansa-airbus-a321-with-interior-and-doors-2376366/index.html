About This Model

Priced at $349, the Lufthansa Airbus A321 with Interior and Doors is a finished Aircraft model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is cataloged for aerospace visualization, flight simulation and film & TV VFX. 2025 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It works for airline and airport visualization, flight simulation, aviation sequences in film and TV, advertising renders and VR training.

Geometry weighs in at 2,405,109 polygons over 2,473,953 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
