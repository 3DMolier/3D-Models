About This Model

Retro white ceramic wall sockets, the rounded porcelain fittings seen in older homes and restored vintage interiors. Their domed covers and simple round outlets carry the charm of mid-century electrical design that collectors still seek today. Mounted on a plaster wall beside cloth-covered wiring, they instantly date a room to an earlier, slower era of living.

Built with CheckMate specification in mind, the file brings tidy naming and true wall-fitting scale. Glossy white porcelain needs a crisp reflection under warm light, which helps the sockets stand out on dark or patterned wallpaper. Retinting the porcelain to ivory or black creates variations for different decades, rooms and decorating styles.

Catalogued since 2023, they suit loft renovations, period film sets and vintage-inspired product renders. Pair them with a twisted fabric cable and a toggle switch to complete an authentic retro wall detail. Interior stylists also use such fittings to add character to minimalist modern rooms where exposed details are welcome.
