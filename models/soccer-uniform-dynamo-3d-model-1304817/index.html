About This Model

The Soccer Uniform Dynamo is a production-ready Sports & Recreation 3D model, priced at $59 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 6,614 polygons and 7,070 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

It ships with 10 textures, authored at 4096 x 4096. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Pivot points sit where you would expect them, which matters as soon as the model needs to be instanced or parented to something else.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. Available since 2018, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It fits sports broadcast graphics, game assets, advertising renders and VR experiences.
