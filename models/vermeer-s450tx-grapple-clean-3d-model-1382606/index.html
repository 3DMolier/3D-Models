About This Model

Vermeer S450TX Grapple Clean belongs to our Industrial Equipment range and is offered at $79 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Counted at the source, the model is 274,366 polygons and 284,672 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

9 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import.
