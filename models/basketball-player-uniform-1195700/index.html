About This Model

Basketball Player Uniform is a detailed Clothing & Accessories asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $49. Counted at the source, the model is 1,933 polygons and 2,010 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

3 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 4096 resolution. The model uses efficient, well-organized geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

It is cataloged for product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. The listing dates back to 2017, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.
