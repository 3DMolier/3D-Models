About This Model

A chicken paired with a rollaway nest box, the kind of setup where laid eggs roll gently out of the hen's reach. The sloped floor and collection tray are the details that explain how the box keeps eggs clean. Together they show a modern backyard or small farm coop rather than a straw-filled barn from the past.

CheckMate Pro certification ensures the hen and box arrive at matching real-world scale with clean topology. Soft feathers against galvanized metal give a pleasant material contrast in daylight or under barn lamps. The pieces can be placed separately, letting the hen wander a yard while the box sits against a wall.

On offer since 2022, the collection suits farming simulation games, poultry equipment catalogs and agricultural education. Homesteading channels and garden blogs also use such visuals to explain egg collection to beginners. A row of boxes with hens nearby turns an empty coop into a working small farm.
