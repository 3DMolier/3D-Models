About This Model

A garden fence of upright bamboo canes lashed to horizontal rails, the kind that encloses a Japanese courtyard or a quiet spa terrace. Its 155,792 polygons and 78,020 vertices round every cane and model the swollen nodes along each stalk. Narrow gaps between the poles let light and foliage show through, so the screen never feels like a solid wall.

The look comes from shaders instead of image maps, so cane color can shift from fresh green to dry straw. Non-overlapping UVs are already in place for anyone who wants to bake moss, rain streaks or a custom bamboo texture. CheckMate Pro certification confirms real-world scale, so the fence lines up with gates, garden lights and people walking past.

A single version is sold for $19, an easy purchase for exterior renders that need an organic border. First offered in 2022, the fence suits landscape architects, resort visualizers and game artists building eastern-style environments. Copying sections along a path or around a hot tub quickly builds a calm, eco-friendly boundary for the scene.
