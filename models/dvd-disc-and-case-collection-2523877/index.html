About This Model

A set of DVD discs and plastic keep cases, open and closed, for shelves of films and old software collections. The cases have hinged spines, inner disc hubs and clear sleeves for cover art, just like the real thing. Shiny disc surfaces show the rainbow sheen of optical media, which reads instantly as early digital era technology.

Built according to the CheckMate specification, the set keeps discs and cases at real size with clear names. Each disc and case is its own object, so they can be stacked, fanned out or slid onto a shelf. Cover art and disc labels are blank surfaces, ready for any invented film title or software logo.

For $14, the collection furnishes living room shelves, video rental shop scenes and nostalgic decor for stories set in the early digital years. Introduced in 2026, the discs also suit tech history lessons and archive scenes about data storage and backups. Commercial renders, games and printed retro posters may use the set freely under a Royalty Free license.

Questions About This Model

Can the DVD Disc and Case Collection model be modified after purchase?

Yes. The Royalty Free license allows editing the geometry, retopologising, changing materials and adapting the asset to a project. What it does not allow is reselling or redistributing the model file itself.

Can the DVD Disc and Case Collection model be used in a commercial project?

Yes - the Royalty Free license covers commercial work, including client projects and released games, with no additional royalties per render or per copy sold.

What is the DVD Disc and Case Collection model typically used for?

Stated applications are game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Because the asset is finished rather than a base mesh, it also holds up as set dressing in scenes it was not specifically built for.

How heavy is the DVD Disc and Case Collection mesh?
