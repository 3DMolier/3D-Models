About This Model

A carved hand drum with a goblet body and tensioned rope lacing brings rhythm and folk craft into music and travel scenes. Its 46,862 polygons over 23,699 vertices go mostly into the rope netting and the carved patterns around the body. The stretched skin head shows the slight dome that real hide forms under tension.

Wood grain and the painted motif can differ on each side of the drum, since the UVs never overlap. Passing CheckMate Pro review confirms that geometry, naming and scale meet high standards. The narrow waist between bowl and foot is the shape players grip between their knees.

African Drum with Folk Motif comes with two other drum versions, all three at $39. You can choose an ornate African djembe with richer carving or a plainer djembe drum. On offer since 2023, it works for music scenes, cultural documentaries, festival sets and souvenir shop interiors.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2023 · Updated 13 November 2026

How heavy is the African Drum with Folk Motif mesh?

How clean is the geometry on the African Drum with Folk Motif model?
