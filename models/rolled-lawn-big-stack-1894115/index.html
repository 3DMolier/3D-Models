About This Model

Rolled Lawn Big Stack belongs to our Nature & Plants range and is offered at $44 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 144,289 polygons over 168,384 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms. The listing flags architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering as the intended applications.

2022 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It fits environment dressing in games, architectural visualization, film backgrounds and VR scenes.
