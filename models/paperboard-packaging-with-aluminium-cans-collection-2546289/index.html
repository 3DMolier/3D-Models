About This Model

This set pairs paperboard multipack cartons with the aluminum cans they hold, ready for beverage packaging mockups. Cartons come with handle cutouts and folded flaps, while the cans keep their rolled rims and recessed bases. Because cans and boxes are separate objects, you can pull a single drink out of a pack for a hero shot.

Clean surfaces on the cartons leave plenty of room for your own label art, nutrition panel or barcode. The models follow CheckMate guidelines, so object names stay readable and pivots sit where a layout artist expects. Cans read well both as metallic hero products and as background clutter on a supermarket shelf.

Added to the catalog in 2026, the collection serves beverage brand presentations, retail shelf planning and packaging design pitches. Stacked on a pallet or a cooler, the cartons also dress convenience stores and warehouse aisles in games. Buying them together means matched scale between cans and cartons, so the pack closes neatly without fiddling.
