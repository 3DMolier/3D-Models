The Arleigh Burke Destroyer OKane DDG-77 is a production-ready Ships & Boats 3D model, priced at $289 on TurboSquid. Counted at the source, the model is 1,558,756 polygons and 1,724,629 vertices. Texturing runs to 26 maps at 8192 x 8192. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.
