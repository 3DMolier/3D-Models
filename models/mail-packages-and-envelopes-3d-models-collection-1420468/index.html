About This Model

Post never arrives as one item. A doormat, a sorting bench or a delivery scene needs a mix: padded envelopes, small boxes, letters of different sizes, a tube, something with tape over a corner. That variety is the whole point of a collection, because repeating one parcel across a pile immediately looks wrong.

Supplying them together also keeps the paper and card materials consistent, so the whole pile reads as coming from the same postal system rather than from three different renderers.

A second collection sits on this page at $89 for a wider selection. This set at $69 is aimed at logistics and e-commerce visualization, delivery and courier marketing, interior and hallway scenes, film and television set dressing, and games. On sale since 2019.
