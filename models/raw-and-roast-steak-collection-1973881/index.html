About This Model

A steak collection that shows beef both raw and roasted, from red marbled flesh to a deeply seared brown crust. Together the pieces use 5,287 polygons, enough for fat edges, uneven thickness and the slight curl of cooked meat. Seeing both states side by side makes the set ideal for explaining cooking and doneness.

Their 5,289 vertices form closed shells that hold up when the camera moves in for juicy detail. CheckMate Pro certification keeps each steak at real plate size, so it sits naturally on boards and grills. Non-overlapping UVs give room for fat marbling, grill marks and a glossy resting juice texture.

The collection has been in the food range since 2022 for restaurant and recipe content. Steakhouses, butcher shops and cooking apps use it in menus, doneness guides and grilling tutorials. Laid on a wooden board with herbs and coarse salt, the steaks look ready for a dinner table.
