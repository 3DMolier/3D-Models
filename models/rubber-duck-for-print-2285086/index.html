About This Model

Rubber Duck for Print is a classic bath duck with a round body and a raised head, prepared as a light mesh for 3D printing. Its 666 polygons and 668 vertices keep the silhouette friendly and simple, which slicers handle easily.

Made to CheckMate specification, the duck has true scale and clean naming for quick setup in any package. Non-overlapping UVs also allow painted eyes, an orange beak and glossy yellow plastic for renders. The low count of quads and triangles still curves smoothly enough for a toy that reads instantly.

Introduced in 2024, the duck fits bathroom scenes, kids animations, toy prints and playful product shots. Float it in a bubble bath and the scene turns cheerful at once. Makers can scale it up for a desk ornament or down for a keychain charm. Programmers know the rubber duck as a debugging buddy, so it suits tech humor too.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published September 2024 · Updated 13 November 2026
