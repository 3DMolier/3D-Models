About This Model

A spot cleaner is a wet vacuum small enough to be carried to the accident. Two tanks, one clean and one dirty, a brush head and a hose, all built around the fact that the stain is usually somewhere a full machine cannot reach.

The model is dense for its size at 554,592 polygons and 285,845 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, which suits close product work. It is built to CheckMate specification.

At $39 it suits appliance and product visualization, e-commerce and retail imagery, advertising, interior and cleaning scenes, film props and editorial illustration. On sale since 2023.
