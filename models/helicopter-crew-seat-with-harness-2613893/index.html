About This Model

A helicopter crew seat with a full harness, made for cabin interiors, rescue missions and military transport scenes. The 23,664 polygons cover the frame, padded cushion and the straps that cross the occupant's chest. Straps hang loosely from the shoulder points, ready for a crew member to buckle in before liftoff.

StemCell delivery keeps the fabric and metal shading stable across every included format. Partial UVs and 24,454 vertices make the seat easy to repeat along a cabin wall beside the door gunner position. Fold the harness over the cushion to show an empty seat between missions on a quiet base.

Costing $34 from 2026 onward, the seat suits aviation training, helicopter interiors and military games set in modern conflicts. It measures 63.58 x 73.95 x H130.17 cm, true to a crew seat inside a rotorcraft. Place it by the open cabin door to frame a crew member watching the ground below during a rescue.
