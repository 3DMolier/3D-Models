About This Model

Priced at $149, the Construction Architect in Yellow Safety Jacket is a finished Clothing & Accessories model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Because it is CheckMate certified, the usual import checks are already done - units, pivots and material names were audited by a reviewer rather than self-declared. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets.

On this listing the stated applications are product rendering, game assets and film & TV VFX. First published in 2017, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It suits fashion visualization, character dressing, product advertising and game assets.

Counted at the source, the model is 40,117 polygons and 40,752 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.
