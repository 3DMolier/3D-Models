About This Model

A rigid steel electrical conduit section with threaded ends, useful for industrial interiors, wiring explainers and construction scenes. The pipe has a smooth galvanized wall and machined threads that accept couplings, elbows and junction boxes. Runs of conduit along walls and ceilings tell viewers that a building is commercial, industrial or still under construction.

The section was modeled to CheckMate specification rules, so scale stays consistent when you join many pieces. A galvanized metal shader with faint zinc spangle gives the pipe its real-world industrial character. Dust and paint overspray near the threads would show conduit installed during a messy renovation.

Electrical tutorials and factory interiors have used the conduit since 2020, with commercial projects covered by its license. Run several sections along a ceiling with straps and boxes to wire a convincing warehouse or parking garage. Bend the path with elbows from your own library to route the pipe around beams and columns.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published April 2020 · Updated 11 November 2026
