About This Model

This Medical model - Knee Muscles and Bones - is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $99 on TurboSquid. The model uses efficient, well-organised geometry - no hidden faces or overlapping shells to clean up before rendering. Objects are grouped and named logically, so isolating a single part for a material change is a two-second job rather than a cleanup task.

On this listing the stated applications are visualization, advertising and 3D printing. The listing dates back to 2021, and it remains one of the models we keep current. It fits medical education, VR anatomy training, patient-facing visualisation and medical sequences in film.

Counted at the source, the model is 42,825 polygons and 42,946 vertices, a light load for a scene. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
