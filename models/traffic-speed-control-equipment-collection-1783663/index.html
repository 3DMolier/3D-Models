About This Model

A set of roadside speed enforcement hardware, the radar units, cameras and warning signs that make drivers lift off the accelerator. Each piece has the housings, mounting poles and lens fronts that people recognize from highway shoulders and city streets. Mounted on poles at the roadside, the units stay readable from the driver's seat and from above.

CheckMate Pro certification covers real-world scale, clean topology and consistent naming, so the whole set lines up with a road scene at once. Collected together, the devices share materials and proportions, which keeps a junction from looking assembled out of mismatched purchases.

Available since 2021, the set serves road safety campaigns, traffic engineering presentations, driving games and city visualization with realistic enforcement. A speed camera half hidden behind a bend is a small detail that makes a driving simulator feel like a real road. Placed along a motorway, a few units give an aerial shot scale and a sense of order. News graphics about fines and speed limits also use them as a quick visual shorthand for enforcement.
