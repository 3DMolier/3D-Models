About This Model

Generic Ambulance Van belongs to our Vehicles range and is offered at $129 through the TurboSquid marketplace. The mesh carries 523,506 polygons and 595,376 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation and automotive advertising. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
