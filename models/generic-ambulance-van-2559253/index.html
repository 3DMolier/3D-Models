About This Model

This Vehicles model — Generic Ambulance Van — is ready to drop into a scene as-is, and sells for $129 on TurboSquid. Geometry weighs in at 523,506 polygons over 595,376 vertices. That is a heavy, close-up-grade build, so plan for it as a hero object rather than background filler. 12 texture maps come with it, at 4096 x 4096 and 2048 x 2048 resolution.
