About This Model

Priced at $79, the Aircraft Maintenance Hangar is a finished Architecture & Landmarks model rather than a base mesh you have to build on. Geometry weighs in at 1,246,676 polygons over 1,401,465 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

12 texture maps come with it, at 8192 x 8192 and 4096 x 4096 resolution. The mesh is modelled at true scale with quad-dominant flow, so subdivision behaves and the object does not need rescaling on import. Everything is placed at the scene origin with sensible pivots, so the asset can be duplicated, arrayed or attached to a rig without hunting for transforms.

The listing flags architectural visualization, aerospace visualization and film & TV VFX as the intended applications. First published in 2019, it is part of the long-running core of the collection. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.
