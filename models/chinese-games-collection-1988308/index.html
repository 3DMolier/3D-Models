About This Model

A collection of traditional Chinese games, such as mahjong tiles, xiangqi pieces and Go stones, for tea house and family scenes. Carved tiles and round wooden pieces carry the characters and markings that give each game its identity. Laid out mid-game on a table, they suggest a lively evening among friends or family.

The pieces follow CheckMate specification, each a separate object that can be moved during an animated match. Polished bone, lacquer and wood surfaces need warm lamp light to bring out their color and glaze. Real-world scale keeps the tiles and stones believable in a player's hand or on a folding table.

On sale since 2022, the games suit Chinese interior renders, cultural documentaries, Lunar New Year visuals and period films. A park table surrounded by elderly players is one of the most recognizable street scenes in China. Board game designers can also reference them when developing modern takes on classic pieces.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published November 2022 · Updated 12 November 2026
