About This Model

Contemporary American military hardware fills this collection, from armored vehicles to aircraft and support equipment. Modern US Military Assets Collection is built for scenes that portray current US forces rather than historical or fictional armies. Tan and green camouflage schemes match the look audiences know from recent news footage.

Scale, naming and structure stay aligned across the whole set, a result of the shared CheckMate build approach. A forward operating base can be assembled quickly because vehicles and equipment already share proportions. Armored transports and helicopters side by side give the base a mix of silhouettes viewers recognize.

New in 2025 at $699, the bundle is ready for commercial defense projects. Defense documentaries, training simulations, tactical games and news graphics are its main audience. A dusty desert base with low afternoon light gives these assets a realistic deployment feel, especially with tents and barriers around them.
