About This Model

Cutting a pumpkin open shows why it is treated as a vegetable in cooking and a fruit in botany. The seed cavity is enormous, the flesh is thin by comparison, and most of what gets carved out is never eaten.

The model is very light at 9,004 polygons and 4,532 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs. It carries CheckMate Lite and Pro certification.

At $29 it suits food and grocery visualization, seasonal and menu imagery, advertising, kitchen and market scenes, film set dressing and editorial illustration. On sale since 2024.
