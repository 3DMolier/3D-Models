About This Model

Aluminium Air Conditioning Duct is one of the Architecture & Landmarks models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $39. You are getting 62,245 polygons and 62,175 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.
