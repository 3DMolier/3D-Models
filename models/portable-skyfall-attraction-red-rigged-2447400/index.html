About This Model

Looking for a Architecture Landmarks asset? The Portable Skyfall Attraction Red Rigged is a finished, render-ready model at $169. Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette.

Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment. Buyers most often take it for architectural visualization, film & TV VFX and product rendering. Available since 2025, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at architectural visualization, city scenes, advertising renders and VR walkthroughs.

The mesh carries 1,040,886 polygons and 1,226,332 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries. The model is jointed, so it can be posed or animated without rebuilding the hierarchy.
