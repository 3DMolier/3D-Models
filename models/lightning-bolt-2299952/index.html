About This Model

A stylised lightning bolt is a symbol rather than a phenomenon: a zigzag with sharp angles and a consistent thickness, nothing like the branching filament of a real strike. That simplification is what makes it readable as an icon for power, speed or electricity at any size.

The mesh is light at 6,622 polygons and 6,630 vertices in quads and tris with non-overlapping UVs, which is the right economy for a graphic form with flat surfaces.

At $15 and built to CheckMate specification, it suits motion graphics and title work, energy and technology branding, interface and app illustration, sports and gaming graphics, and print. On sale since 2025.
