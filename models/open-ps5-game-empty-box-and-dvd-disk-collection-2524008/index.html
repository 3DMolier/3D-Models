About This Model

An opened PlayStation game case with its disc shows the moment a new title comes out of the box in a gaming scene. Case and disc share 10,901 polygons and 10,388 vertices, much of it in the hub clip and the hinged spine. The empty box is posed open, so the inner tray and the disc resting beside it face the camera directly.

Cover art is left blank since textures are not part of the package, so any title design can be applied. UV islands do not share space, which keeps cover prints, spine text and disc labels sharp after baking. Proportions and naming follow TurboSquid's CheckMate guidance, matching the real size of a standard console game case.

Because of Sony's PlayStation branding, this 2026 set is limited to Editorial Uses Only, covering news and reviews. Gaming journalists and YouTubers use it in unboxing thumbnails, release coverage and articles about physical game collections. Teachers and documentary makers also show it when talking about how games are packaged and distributed today.
