About This Model

A collection of foil cat food pouches, the flexible sachets that fill pet store shelves and kitchen cupboards. The pouches show soft crimped edges and a gentle bulge, just like packets filled with wet food. Upright on a shelf or lying flat, they read clearly as pet food packaging.

Built per CheckMate rules, the pouches are clean geometry with tidy names for each item in the scene. Blank areas on the front take custom label art, so any pet brand design can be applied quickly. A metallic foil sheen on the material sells the packaging even when the pouches sit far back on a shelf.

Available since 2024, the pouches fit pet food packaging mockups, supermarket interiors, veterinary clinic scenes and advertising. Set one pouch torn open next to a bowl and a curious cat model for a homey feeding moment. Rows of colorful sachets also make a busy, believable backdrop in pet store aisles.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published October 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
