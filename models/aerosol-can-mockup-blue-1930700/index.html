About This Model

A clean aerosol spray can mockup with a domed cap and nozzle, built for packaging previews of paints and sprays. Its 6,036 polygons and 3,750 vertices shape the rolled rims, the shoulder curve and the actuator under the cap. The plain cylinder gives a label designer a smooth wrap with no awkward breaks or seams.

UVs are non-overlapping, so the body label unwraps into a single flat strip for artwork. The can is CheckMate Pro certified, which guarantees tidy naming and geometry ready to render. Metallic rims and a glossy body give the mockup the bright reflections that real cans show on shelves.

There are three color versions at $19 each: blue, pink and a rainbow edition of the Aerosol Can Mockup. Graffiti scenes also benefit, since a few cans scattered by a wall tell the story clearly. Released in 2022, it fits deodorant, spray paint, hair spray and household cleaner presentations alike.
