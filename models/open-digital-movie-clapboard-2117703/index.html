About This Model

Open Digital Movie Clapboard belongs to our Electronics & Gadgets range and is offered at $12 through the TurboSquid marketplace. Geometry weighs in at 39,128 polygons over 19,678 vertices. That figure reads differently on desktop, on mobile and in WebXR, so weigh it against the target platform and the number of instances in the scene.

Topology is kept deliberately simple where detail would not read on camera, which keeps the scene light without visibly cheapening the silhouette. Naming and hierarchy are consistent, so the model behaves predictably when it is merged into a larger scene alongside other assets. The listing flags technical visualization, software demos and film & TV VFX as the intended applications.

Available since 2023, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
