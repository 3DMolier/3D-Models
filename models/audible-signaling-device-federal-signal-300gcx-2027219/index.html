About This Model

The Federal Signal 300GCX is a heavy-duty industrial horn that warns workers of hazards, shift changes and evacuations in loud plants. A flared projector bell sits on a sturdy mounting base, shaped to throw sound across a factory floor or loading yard. The device holds 30,104 polygons and 15,349 vertices, enough to keep the round horn and mounting bolts clean in close views.

Non-overlapping UVs keep the painted housing, grille and fasteners on separate texture space for decals or grime. CheckMate Pro certification confirms the horn is closed, properly scaled and logically named. Signal red or safety yellow enamel with dust along the top surfaces gives the unit its industrial character.

Offered since 2023 under the editorial license, this Audible Signaling Device Federal Signal 300GCX suits safety training, industrial documentaries and technical manuals. Mounted on a steel column above a production line, it shows viewers where alarms come from in an emergency drill. Fire safety engineers also use such visuals to plan horn placement along long industrial halls.
