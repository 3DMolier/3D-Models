About This Model

The Egyptian Pharaoh portrays an ancient ruler in a striped nemes headdress, a broad collar and a pleated kilt. Detail across the 65,272 polygons and 43,623 vertices goes into the headdress folds, the heavy jewelry and the sandaled feet. Standing between temple columns, the figure brings authority and mystery to any scene set along the Nile.

CheckMate Pro status confirms neat geometry and correct human proportions for this richly dressed historical figure. UVs without overlaps let gold leaf, lapis blue stripes and sun-tanned skin be textured separately. Warm desert light with strong shadows makes the gold accents glow against sandstone backgrounds.

Three versions cost $179, the static ruler plus a Cinema 4D animation setup next to its Maya equivalent. Rigged, the pharaoh can raise a scepter, sit on a throne or walk in procession. Listed since 2023, the character suits history lessons, museum displays and strategy games about the ancient world.
