About This Model

A suspended operator seat for buses and coaches, with a tall backrest and adjustable base designed for long shifts behind the wheel. The model contains 3,765 polygons and 656,821 vertices, with detail in the headrest, armrest and the mechanism beneath. A wide cushion and side bolsters give the seat its supportive, utilitarian shape familiar from depots everywhere.

It complies with the TurboSquid CheckMate standard, keeping geometry clean and parts named for quick edits. The visible suspension under the cushion separates it from a passenger chair at a glance, even in a dim cabin. It fits behind a dashboard, in a depot workshop or on a seat test bench in a factory.

The Bus Driver Seat has been on sale since 2026 at $39 with Royalty Free rights. Bus interiors, transport simulators, fleet configurators and workshop scenes are where it belongs most naturally. Swapping a worn-out seat for this one also makes a simple maintenance animation for training courses.
