About This Model

The Pixel 3 and Pixel 3XL is a production-ready Electronics & Gadgets 3D model, priced at $49 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 6,024 polygons and 6,861 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

4 texture maps come with it, at 2048 x 2048 and 1024 x 1024 resolution. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene. The scene is organized rather than dumped into a single mesh, which makes selective texturing and partial reuse practical.

Buyers most often take it for technical visualization, software demos and product rendering. 2019 is when this one first went up, and it has stayed in the catalog since. It is aimed at product rendering, advertising, UI and app mockups, and game and film props.
