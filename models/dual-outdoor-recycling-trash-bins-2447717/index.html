About This Model

Looking for a Containers & Storage asset? The Dual Outdoor Recycling Trash Bins is a finished, render-ready model at $39. You are getting 36,496 polygons and 36,784 vertices, measured rather than estimated. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.

Dimensions are 90.31 x 38.76 x H105.0 cm, which is what you get on import, with no unit guessing. The mesh is built with clean quad-based topology and correct real-world proportions, so it subdivides predictably and sits at the right size next to other objects in a scene.
