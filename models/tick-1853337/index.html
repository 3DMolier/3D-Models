About This Model

Tick is one of the Animals & Creatures models in the 3D Molier catalog, listed at $59. Counted at the source, the model is 14,174 polygons and 14,278 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
