About This Model

This classic white cable links the wide 30-pin dock plug of older iPhones and iPods to a standard USB port. Its 8,184 polygons and 8,990 vertices shape the flat dock connector, the strain reliefs and the coiled run of cord between them. The wide connector instantly dates a scene to the late iPod era, which helps with period accuracy.

Seven texture maps at 2048 x 2048 give the plastic its soft gloss and the metal contacts their shine. Islands on the unwrap do not overlap, so the plug housing can take printed markings or dust and grime. A CheckMate Lite grade shows TurboSquid verified its core geometry and file setup before release.

Out since 2015 at $4, it is a cheap way to add authentic charging gear to desks and drawers. Tech reviewers and retro gadget channels use it in comparison shots against newer Lightning and USB-C cables. Interior artists also coil it beside an old phone dock to suggest a bedroom from the previous decade.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published March 2015 · Updated 14 November 2026
