About This Model

A brick-shaped aseptic carton like those holding long-life milk, juice or soup, with folded seams at top and bottom. The 1,516 polygons and 1,517 vertices define the flat panels, the sealed fin and the tucked triangular ears. Its clean block shape makes it a natural for supermarket shelves stacked many units deep.

Non-overlapping UVs let you wrap your own packaging artwork across the front, sides and top flap. A CheckMate specification build means closed shells and orderly names, so the carton instances neatly in large shelf arrays. Seen from a low angle, the folded top edge gives the box a crisp silhouette that cheap cubes lack.

Released in 2026, it serves packaging mockups, grocery store scenes and food industry explainer animations. Designers often swap label textures per flavor, turning a single base into a whole juice line. Grocery game levels can fill entire aisles with it because the light mesh barely touches the budget.
