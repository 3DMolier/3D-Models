About This Model

A cardboard package of Arm and Hammer baking soda, the familiar box found in pantries, fridges and laundry cupboards. The 5,114 polygons shape the folded carton, the tuck flap on top and the slightly rounded edges of the pressed board. With exactly 5,114 vertices as well, the box stays light enough to fill a supermarket shelf or a kitchen cabinet.

The carton was built to CheckMate specification, giving clean geometry, a realistic size and well named parts throughout. Every panel unwraps flat without overlaps, so the printed label wraps around the box without seams cutting through the logo. Leave the flap open with a little white powder spilled nearby, and the package suggests baking or cleaning in progress.

Two versions come with this page: the branded Arm and Hammer box and a blank baking soda package mockup. The mockup version lets designers test their own label artwork, while the branded box gives instant recognition in kitchens. Out since 2024, it fits pantry interiors, baking tutorials, home cleaning guides and grocery store shelves in simulation games.
