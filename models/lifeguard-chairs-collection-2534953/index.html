About This Model

This lifeguard chairs collection brings the tall wooden and metal towers that overlook beaches and public pools. A high seat with a ladder, a sunshade and a lifebuoy hook gives each chair its watchful, iconic shape.

Painted wood, weathered rails and rope details suit sandy coasts, while cleaner frames fit municipal pools. Construction keeps to CheckMate specifications, so every tower stands at the height a guard actually climbs. Strong vertical silhouettes make the chairs useful as landmarks along a wide beach panorama. Sunshades and seats are separate parts, so a tower can be shown empty or with a guard at the post.

Two versions are sold for this range: the starting set of chairs at $30 and a second collection at $49. New in 2026, the towers suit summer resort renders, water park designs and beach scenes in games. With a guard watching the surf and a red flag flying, the chair sets the mood of a busy beach day.
