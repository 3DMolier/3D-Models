About This Model

The Pilatus PC-12 NGX Business Plane is a production-ready Aircraft 3D model, priced at $239 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 254,888 polygons and 278,798 vertices. Whether that counts as light or heavy depends on the target platform, the complexity of the scene and how many instances it carries.
