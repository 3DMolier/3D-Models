About This Model

A compact dash camera that mounts behind the windshield records the road ahead for drivers, fleets and car safety scenes. The camera uses 29,929 polygons and 15,358 vertices, shaping the rounded body, the lens ring and the adhesive mount. Its small, minimal body hides behind the rear-view mirror, which is why drivers barely notice it.

The unwrap keeps every part on unshared space for matte plastic, lens glass and small button markings. TurboSquid inspected the camera and assigned CheckMate Pro for clean topology and realistic size. The lens is a separate object, so reflections and glass shaders can be tuned for close-up shots.

Car interior renders, driving safety videos and gadget reviews show the camera mounted on a windshield. With a 2023 release date, the camera works for insurance explainers and road trip scenes that need recording equipment. Seen from inside the car at night with city lights ahead, the camera creates a modern driving scene.
