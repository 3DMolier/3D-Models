About This Model

A collection of antique pocket watches with chains, engraved cases and classic dials, ready for vintage still life and period scenes. Engraved backs and smooth fronts show the craftsmanship that collectors value most. Different case styles and dial layouts keep the set varied when several watches lie together.

Certified CheckMate Pro, the watches arrive as clean meshes with separated chains and cases. Gold, silver and enamel materials are split, so aging or polishing them is a quick shader tweak. Macro shots of the dials show crisp numerals and hands that hold up at very close range.

Since 2022 jewelers, prop makers and illustrators have used the set for antique catalogs and Victorian scenes. Drape a chain over an old letter beside a flickering candle, and the story of a past century appears. Steampunk and detective projects also pick the watches as easy hero props for gentlemen characters.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published July 2022 · Updated 11 November 2026
