About This Model

Vintage Motorcycle is a detailed Vehicles asset built for professional 3D pipelines, available at $129. StemCell is TurboSquid's cross-format standard: one authored source, several delivered formats, and PBR materials that survive the trip between them. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

It is catalogued for visualization, advertising and game environments. Available since 2020, the model has been through several rounds of studio use. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualisation.

Geometry weighs in at 201,841 polygons over 214,008 vertices - a mid-weight build for its class. Texture work runs to 12 maps, the largest at 4096x4096, so the surface holds up when the camera moves in. It is a static build - no rig to strip out if all you need is a rendered object.
