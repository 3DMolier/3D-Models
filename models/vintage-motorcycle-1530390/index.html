About This Model

The Vintage Motorcycle is a production-ready Vehicles 3D model, priced at $129 on TurboSquid. The mesh carries 201,841 polygons and 214,008 vertices. Its suitability for real-time use depends on the target platform, scene complexity and the number of instances.

Texturing runs to 12 maps at 4096 x 4096. StemCell certification means the asset was authored once and delivered across formats with consistent PBR shading, which saves the conversion step when moving between renderers. Transforms are frozen and the hierarchy is shallow, which keeps the asset easy to instance across a large environment.

Buyers most often take it for driving simulation, automotive advertising and game traffic. It has been on sale since 2020 and is still maintained as part of the active catalog. Common uses are automotive advertising, film and TV backgrounds, game traffic and architectural visualization.
