About This Model

A collection of YouTube creator award plaques, the framed play buttons channels receive as their audience grows. Each plaque keeps the metallic play button relief inside a glass-fronted frame, ready to hang on a studio wall. Behind a streamer's chair, one of these tells viewers at once that the channel has grown.

The set carries a CheckMate Lite grade, and every award can be hung, leaned or held on its own. Silver, gold and diamond finishes need only a reflective metal shader and a clear glass layer. A soft key light on the frame makes the button shine without washing out the glass.

Two versions of the awards set are offered: the Youtube Awards Collection and the Youtube Awards Collection 2 with more plaques. Available since 2024, the plaques suit creator studio renders, streaming room setups, social media explainers and celebration animations. Set one on a shelf beside a ring light and a camera for a creator's corner.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published January 2024 · Updated 12 November 2026
