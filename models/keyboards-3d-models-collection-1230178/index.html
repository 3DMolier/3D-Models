About This Model

A collection of computer keyboards in several layouts and styles, from slim office boards to full-size desktop models. Keys are individual shapes with legible rows, so close desk shots do not reveal a flat texture. Different profiles and cable styles help a busy office avoid the cloned look of repeated props in a wide shot.

With CheckMate Pro certification, every keyboard arrives clean and at true size for a standard desk. Individual pieces let you assign one per workstation or swap colors to match a brand. Plastic keycaps with a soft satin finish pick up rim light nicely in close product shots.

Available since 2017, the Keyboards 3D Models Collection fills offices, gaming setups and tech product renders. Software companies place keyboards like these in UI mockups, tutorials and onboarding animations for new users. Game developers dress open-plan offices with them to make each desk look occupied and lived in.

By Andrey Simonenko, 3D Molier · Published December 2017 · Updated 12 November 2026

Questions About This Model

Can the Keyboards Collection model be used in a commercial project?

It ships with TurboSquid's Royalty Free license, so a single purchase covers commercial delivery - you do not pay again per project or per seat of the finished work.

Can the Keyboards Collection model be modified after purchase?

Editing is allowed - remesh it, strip detail for real-time use, or rebuild the shaders. The one restriction is that the model file cannot be resold or given away as an asset.

What is the Keyboards Collection model typically used for?

It is cataloged for game assets, film & TV VFX and product rendering - the sort of work where the object needs to look right on camera but is not the subject of the shot.

How clean is the geometry on the Keyboards Collection model?

Geometry is modelled at true scale with a tidy edge flow, and the scene is split into named objects so partial reuse and material swaps stay simple.
