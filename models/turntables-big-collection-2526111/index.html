About This Model

A large collection of record turntables, from home hi-fi decks to DJ setups, gathered for music rooms and club booths. Tonearms, platters and slip mats are the parts a viewer recognizes first, so they define each deck at a glance.

Built to CheckMate specification, the decks share clean topology and one scale, so they sit correctly on the same shelf or console. Having many variants in one purchase lets a record shop wall or a DJ booth avoid repeating the same model. Close-ups benefit most, since the needle resting on a record groove is a classic macro shot.

Available from 2026 at $169, the set suits music videos, retail interiors and nightlife scenes. Spin a vinyl under a warm spotlight, and a still image suddenly feels like it has a soundtrack. Product designers and audio retailers also use such decks to stage listening rooms in interior renders. Vintage and modern styles together cover everything from a seventies living room to a current club.
