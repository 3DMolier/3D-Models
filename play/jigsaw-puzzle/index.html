About the game

The pictures are product renders of real 3D models from the 3D Molier catalog: food, toys, gadgets, vehicles, animals, rooms and landmarks. Each one is cut into classic jigsaw pieces with tabs and blanks, and the cut is new every time you play.

Four levels: 12, 24, 48 or 108 pieces. When you finish, open the model to see its formats, polygon count and price, or browse the full catalog. More games are on the games page.
