About the game

Every model in the game is a real product from the 3D Molier catalog: 240 of our best-selling models across vehicles, aircraft, medical anatomy, plants, furniture and more. The picture is the model's own render, and the price is the one listed on its page. Click a model's name or picture to open its page.

Prices were taken from the model pages on October 27, 2026. The same model can look simple and still cost more because of an interior, rigging, extra file formats or a set of variants. The model page lists what is included. Browse the full catalog to see everything we make.
